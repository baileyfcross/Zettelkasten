# Computer

A computer is a human or machine that performs calculations and symbol manipulations according to precisely specified rules. Before electronic machines, the word commonly named a person trained to carry out long computations.

An automatic computer performs those rules through physical mechanisms without ongoing human intervention. Speed and repeatability distinguish the machine, but meaning and purpose still come from the people who design and use its computations.
