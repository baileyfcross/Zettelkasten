# Physical Basis of Digital Information

Digital information always depends on a physical arrangement capable of carrying distinguishable states. Signals in circuits and changes in storage media provide the material events that designers interpret as bits, data, and instructions.

Programs may be described abstractly, but execution requires physical mechanisms with timing, energy, noise, and failure constraints. Ignoring that substrate can hide causes of faults that are invisible at the software level.
