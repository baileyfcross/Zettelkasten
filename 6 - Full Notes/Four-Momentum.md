# Four-Momentum

Four-momentum is the product of invariant mass and [[Four-Velocity]], $P=mU$. In time-first notation it is commonly written $P=(E/c,\mathbf{p})$, combining energy with three-dimensional momentum in one [[Four-Vector]].

Its components change under a [[Lorentz Transformation]], so observers in relative motion can disagree about a particle's energy and spatial momentum. Its Minkowski norm does not change and satisfies $P\cdot P=m^2c^2$, which leads to the [[Energy-Momentum Relation]].

For an isolated system, total four-momentum is conserved component by component in every inertial frame. This single covariant rule unifies the separate classical conservation statements for energy and momentum and governs [[Relativistic Collision Conservation]].
