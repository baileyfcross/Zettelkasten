# Roblox World Lighting

Roblox lighting combines world-level illumination with local Light objects. Environment settings influence the entire place, while PointLight, SpotLight, and SurfaceLight instances illuminate areas according to their position, direction, range, brightness, and color.

Lighting is also a compositional system. Bloom, blur, color correction, sun rays, and depth-of-field effects alter the final image and should support the intended mood without obscuring play. [[Roblox Atmosphere]] controls distance and aerial appearance, while [[Roblox Skybox]] supplies the distant visual enclosure behind the scene.
