# Kotlin when Expression

The Kotlin `when` expression selects a branch by matching a subject or evaluating branch conditions. A branch may match individual values, combine alternatives, test membership in a range, or test a type.

When used to produce a value, the chosen branch's final expression becomes the result and an `else` branch covers unmatched input when the cases are not otherwise exhaustive. `when` centralizes a multi-way decision that would otherwise require a longer chain of conditions.
