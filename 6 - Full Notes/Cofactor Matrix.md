# Cofactor Matrix

The cofactor $C_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}M_{ij}$ is the signed determinant of the submatrix obtained by deleting row $i$ and column $j$. Arranging these values in their original positions gives the cofactor matrix.

Expanding a [[Determinant]] along any row or column multiplies entries by their corresponding cofactors. Transposing the cofactor matrix produces the [[Adjugate Matrix]].
