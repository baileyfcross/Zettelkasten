# Combined Heat Transfer Modes

Practical thermal systems commonly conduct energy through solids, convect it through adjacent fluids, and radiate it between exposed surfaces at the same time. All three mechanisms share temperature difference as a driver but use different constitutive relations and geometric information.

Their contributions meet in one [[Energy Conservation in Thermal Continua|energy balance]]. A reduced model may replace fluid motion or radiation with boundary coefficients, while a [[Conjugate Heat Transfer Coupling|multiphysics model]] resolves interacting solid, fluid, and radiative fields when those approximations are insufficient.
