# Physical Game Prototype

A physical game prototype enacts movement, timing, spatial relationships, or social coordination with bodies and ordinary objects. It investigates how an interaction feels before its digital implementation is available.

The prototype abstracts away technology while preserving the relationship under study. Its value comes from testing embodied pacing and roles, not from accurately reproducing every eventual rule or visual detail.
