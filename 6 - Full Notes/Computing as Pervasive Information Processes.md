# Computing as Pervasive Information Processes

The pervasive-information-process view extends computing beyond automating human tasks. Scientists use computation to interpret DNA transcription, chemical reactions, cognition, and physical interactions as transformations of information.

This perspective supports models and new processes that did not exist before computing. It also requires care not to confuse a useful computational description with a claim that the underlying phenomenon literally runs software.
