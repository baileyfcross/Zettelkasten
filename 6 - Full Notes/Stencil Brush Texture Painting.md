# Stencil Brush Texture Painting

A stencil brush overlays an image in the 3D viewport and projects its pixels wherever brushstrokes intersect the model. Moving, scaling, and rotating the stencil positions photographic or designed detail before it is committed to the texture.

The control scheme must provide bindings for those stencil transformations. The book notes that Blender 2.7x's Maya-style controls could leave some bindings undefined, requiring explicit keymap entries before the stencil could be manipulated effectively.
