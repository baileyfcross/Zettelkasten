# Procedural City Extraction from Aerial Images

An aerial photograph can be turned into a rough displacement model when its building and non-building values can be separated. The book first increases contrast between buildings and streets, then extracts areas such as large trees independently so they are not incorrectly pressed down with the streets. The resulting grayscale map raises a subdivided plane, while the original image provides texture. This is a quick shot-dependent approximation, not a substitute for accurately modeling every building when close inspection is required. It extends the terrain method in [[DEM Terrain Displacement for VFX]].
