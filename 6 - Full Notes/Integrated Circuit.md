# Integrated Circuit

An integrated circuit fabricates transistors, passive components, and their interconnections together on one piece of semiconductor material. Treating the circuit as a single manufactured object replaces assembly from many discrete parts.

Integration improves size, power, reliability, and cost at scale. It also allows complex functions to grow from repeated layers of simple logic.
