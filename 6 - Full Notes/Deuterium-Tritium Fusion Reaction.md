# Deuterium-Tritium Fusion Reaction

The deuterium-tritium reaction joins [[Deuterium]] and [[Tritium]] into a helium nucleus and a neutron. The source gives 17.6 MeV released per reaction and favors this pairing over the listed deuterium-deuterium reactions at its chosen operating conditions. Escaping energetic neutrons can transfer heat to a reactor blanket and help breed replacement tritium from lithium.
