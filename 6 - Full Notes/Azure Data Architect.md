# Azure Data Architect

An Azure data architect designs the organization’s data landscape across storage, integration, modeling, quality, analytics, and business intelligence. The role connects raw data to information, knowledge, and decision value rather than selecting a database in isolation. It must coordinate operational and analytical needs, governance, ownership, and the platforms that move data through ingestion, processing, serving, and visualization.
