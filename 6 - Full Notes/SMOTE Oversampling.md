# SMOTE Oversampling

SMOTE oversampling creates synthetic minority observations along line segments joining a rare-class case to selected rare-class neighbors. It expands the minority region without merely copying the same examples.

The method can help a classifier form a broader boundary around sparse positives, but synthetic points may cross into majority territory when classes overlap. Neighborhood choice and the amount of oversampling therefore require validation.
