# Laplace Equation

Laplace equation requires a scalar field to have zero Laplacian, $\nabla^2u=0$. Its solutions are harmonic functions and describe source-free equilibrium fields such as electrostatic potential in a region containing no charge.

Boundary conditions determine the solution inside a region, and harmonic functions cannot possess an isolated interior maximum or minimum unless they are constant. Separation of variables adapts the solution basis to rectangular, cylindrical, or spherical geometry.
