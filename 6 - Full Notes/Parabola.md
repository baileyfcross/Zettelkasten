# Parabola

A parabola is the graph of a quadratic function and is symmetric about the vertical line through its vertex. Its roots, when real, appear at equal horizontal distances from that axis, while the vertex represents the minimum or maximum value of the quadratic.

Plotting a dense input sequence in R makes these relationships visible and lets a scientific quadratic model be checked over the domain that matters. A fitted parabola should not be extrapolated indefinitely when its underlying mechanism is valid only within the observed range.
