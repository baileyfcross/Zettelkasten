# Selection Sort

Selection sort repeatedly finds the minimum item in the unsorted portion of a collection and swaps it into the next final position. Each pass expands the sorted prefix by one item.

For $n$ items, repeated minimum searches give $O(n^2)$ [[Time Complexity]]. The method is easy to understand and implement, but faster loglinear algorithms are preferred when the collection is large.
