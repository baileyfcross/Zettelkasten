# Simulation Geometry Defeaturing

Simulation geometry defeaturing removes small holes, fillets, chamfers, sliver faces, spikes, or other production detail that degrades mesh quality without contributing meaningfully to the modeled physics. Fewer small entities can reduce element count and solver difficulty.

Defeaturing must be selective because a visually minor feature may create a stress concentration, flow separation, contact, or thermal resistance central to the question. The removed feature and justification should remain documented.
