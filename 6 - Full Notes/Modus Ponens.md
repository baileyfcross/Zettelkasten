# Modus Ponens

Modus ponens is the rule that derives q from the premises p → q and p. Its validity follows because the only truth assignment in which both premises are true also makes q true.

In [[Predicate Logic]], a universally quantified implication can first be specialized to an entity a; combining P(a) → Q(a) with P(a) then yields Q(a).
