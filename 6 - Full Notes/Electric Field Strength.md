# Electric Field Strength

Electric field strength at a point is the force per unit positive test charge, $E=F/q$. It is a vector whose direction is the force direction for a positive charge. For a point source charge, $E=(1/4\pi\varepsilon_0)Q/r^2$, directed radially outward for positive $Q$ and inward for negative $Q$.
