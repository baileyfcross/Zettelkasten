# Triangular Membership Function

A triangular membership function rises linearly from zero to a peak membership of one and then falls linearly to zero. Three parameters identify its left boundary, peak, and right boundary.

Its simple shape is easy to implement and interpret for ordered linguistic categories. Adjacent triangles can overlap to express gradual transitions among labels.
