# GPS Space Segment

The GPS space segment is the constellation of satellites that broadcasts synchronized ranging signals and navigation data. Satellites occupy multiple medium-Earth orbital planes so that several are visible from most places on Earth, and on-orbit spares make the service less dependent on any one vehicle. Each satellite carries frequency standards, radio transmitters, and stored orbital and clock information supplied by the control segment.
