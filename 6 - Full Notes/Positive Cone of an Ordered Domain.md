# Positive Cone of an Ordered Domain

The positive cone of an ordered integral domain is the set of elements greater than zero. It is closed under addition and multiplication, and every nonzero element lies in exactly one of the cone or its negative.

Conversely, a subset with these properties determines a compatible order.
