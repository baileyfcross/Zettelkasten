# Camera Exposure

Camera exposure is the amount of light recorded by the sensor, governed jointly by aperture size, shutter duration, and ISO sensitivity. Different combinations can produce similar average brightness while leaving different traces in focus range, motion, and noise.

Forensic exposure analysis compares those visible effects with the values recorded in [[EXIF Metadata]]. An implausible combination can challenge a photograph's stated capture history, although the analyst must allow for post-processing and for multiple settings that can yield comparable brightness.
