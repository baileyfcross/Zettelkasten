# Silhouette Coefficient

The silhouette value for an observation compares its average distance within its assigned cluster with its best average distance to another cluster, normalized by the larger of the two. Values near one favor the assignment, while negative values indicate a poorer fit than a competing cluster.

Averaging point values produces a silhouette coefficient for the clustering. Plotting it across candidate values of k can inform cluster-count selection.
