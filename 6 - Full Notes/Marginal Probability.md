# Marginal Probability

A marginal probability is the distribution of selected variables after the other variables in a joint distribution have been summed or integrated out. It answers a question that does not condition on or retain those omitted variables.

For discrete variables, the probabilities of every compatible value of the removed variables are added. The result remains normalized over the variables that stay in the question.
