# Flash Consistency Analysis

Flash consistency analysis tests whether highlights, brightness falloff, shadows, and reflections agree with illumination from a camera-mounted or nearby flash. A flash produces a strong light source near the viewpoint, so surfaces facing it and objects at different distances should respond predictably.

The analysis combines scene content with any flash field in [[EXIF Metadata]]. Ambient lights, reflective materials, multiple exposures, and off-camera flash complicate the pattern, making explicit assumptions about the lighting setup essential.
