# Depth Lemma

The depth lemma relates depths in a [[Short Exact Sequence]] 0 → M₁ → M₂ → M₃ → 0. Each module's depth is bounded below by a minimum formed from the other two, with a one-degree shift where appropriate.

These inequalities arise from the [[Long Exact Sequence in Ext]]. They let depth information propagate through submodules, extensions, and quotients without constructing regular sequences directly.
