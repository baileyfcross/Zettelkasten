# Vertical Social Networks

A vertical social network serves a particular domain or community instead of a general population. The shared subject determines which profile fields, activities, reputation signals, and integrations are useful. The source identifies developer, finance, health and fitness, travel, neighborhood, gaming, gardening, and book communities as examples.

Domain focus can make interaction more valuable because members share a vocabulary and purpose, but it also makes one generic social API less expressive. A finance service may expose trades and market commentary, while a fitness network may connect wearable measurements and training activities. [[Developer Social Networks]] is one technical vertical, and [[Social Platform Java Client Selection]] must account for each service's domain objects rather than only authentication.
