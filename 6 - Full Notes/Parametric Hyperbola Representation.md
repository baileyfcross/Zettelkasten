# Parametric Hyperbola Representation

One branch of an origin-centered hyperbola can be parameterized with hyperbolic cosine and sine: x equals a cosh theta and y equals b sinh theta. The identity cosh squared theta minus sinh squared theta equals one ensures that the generated points satisfy the implicit hyperbola equation.

Because the curve is unbounded, finite parameter limits must be derived from the portion to be displayed. Hyperbolic angle-addition identities provide an incremental recurrence when the step in theta is fixed. Reflections recover the other branch or quadrants, and affine transforms reposition the canonical curve.
