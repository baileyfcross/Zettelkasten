# Texture Paint Slots and Canvas Images

A texture-paint slot identifies the material image or single image that receives brush strokes. The active canvas must be connected to the object's UV mapping and visible material if the artist expects the painted result to appear on the mesh.

Generated canvases can begin blank or use UV test grids, while opened images bring external artwork into the session. Selecting the wrong slot can modify an unintended image even though the brush appears to operate normally.
