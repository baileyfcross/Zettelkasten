# Nodal Heat Generation Load

A nodal heat generation load distributes internally generated heat to an element's nodes through its shape functions. A uniform source in a two-node line element gives equal consistent contributions to the endpoints.

The source term belongs on the right-hand side of the thermal equation. It raises the temperature field until conduction and boundary heat transfer balance the generated energy.
