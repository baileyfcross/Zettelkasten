# Direct Product of Groups

The direct product of groups G and H uses ordered pairs with componentwise multiplication. Its identity and inverses are also computed coordinatewise.

For finite groups its order is |G||H|, while element orders reflect the orders of both coordinates.

The direct product $C_2\times C_2$ has four elements and three subgroups of order two. The source uses matrix representations of this group to organize quaternion-like four-dimensional algebras and their two-dimensional subspaces. Its proposed physical interpretation is recorded separately in [[Morris's Finite-Group Model of Spacetime]].
