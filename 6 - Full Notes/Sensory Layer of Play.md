# Sensory Layer of Play

The sensory layer is the player's immediate perception of a game through image, sound, touch, movement, and other bodily channels. It determines which signals are available before the player interprets what those signals mean.

Design at this layer directs [[Attention]] through contrast, rhythm, motion, volume, texture, and timing. A crucial state change that cannot be perceived cannot reliably support the higher informational and interactive layers.
