# Photon Flux

Photon flux counts photons crossing a surface perpendicular to a light beam per unit area and time. When average [[Photon Energy]] is known, radiant power per area is photon flux multiplied by that energy. In a solar cell, more photons able to form charge carriers can raise current, but flux alone says nothing about how much of the spectrum the junction can use.
