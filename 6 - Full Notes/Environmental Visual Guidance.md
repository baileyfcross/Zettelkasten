# Environmental Visual Guidance

Environmental visual guidance directs attention through properties already present in a scene. Light attracts the eye; similarity establishes recognizable classes; repeated objects form trails; landmarks organize destination; and line, color, texture, brightness, or directionality can create arrows and contrast.

These cues become part of [[Game Presentation]] rather than a separate overlay. A successful composition makes the intended route readable while remaining credible as a place. Multiple cues can reinforce one another, but excessive contrast everywhere destroys the hierarchy on which guidance depends.
