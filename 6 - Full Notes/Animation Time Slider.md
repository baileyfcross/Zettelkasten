# Animation Time Slider

An animation time slider shows the current frame's temporal position and lets the viewer move directly to another time. It counters the weak temporal reference of an unlabelled loop and supports replaying a transition of interest. In an [[Animated Bubble Chart]], the slider should update in synchrony with every mark. Clear start, end, pause, and current-value states make the control interpretable without guessing the playback position.
