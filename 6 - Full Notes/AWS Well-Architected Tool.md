# AWS Well-Architected Tool

The AWS Well-Architected Tool records structured reviews of workloads against the [[AWS Well-Architected Framework]] and applicable lenses. Teams answer design questions, identify high-risk issues, document milestones, and generate an improvement plan.

The tool supports discussion and tracking; it does not inspect every resource or guarantee that an answer reflects reality. Reviews are most useful when workload owners and domain specialists provide evidence, agree on risk, and repeat the assessment after meaningful change.
