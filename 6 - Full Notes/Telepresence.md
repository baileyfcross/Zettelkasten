# Telepresence

Telepresence allows people in different physical locations to share task space and person space through an interactive system.

In a [[Virtual Environment]], remote users can appear and act in the same three-dimensional space even though their physical spaces differ. A telepresence interface must communicate who is present, where they are, and what they are doing while supporting social cues and coordination.

The concept connects [[Social Computing]], [[Computer-Supported Cooperative Work]], and [[Presence]].

Collaborative AR can create telepresence by placing a remote participant's video, avatar, annotations, or gestures into the local task space. In an asymmetric arrangement, a stationary expert may inspect a remote user's live view and return spatial guidance that appears registered with the user's physical environment.

Telepresence is the perceptual experience of being at the remote site, whereas [[Telerobotics]] is the mechanism for acting there through a robot. Wide-field stereoscopic viewing, spatial audio, viewpoint control, and timely sensor feedback can strengthen that experience, but they do not remove the operational consequences of network delay or incomplete remote sensing.
