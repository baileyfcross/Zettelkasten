# Edible Scaffold

An edible scaffold supports cultured food cells during growth and becomes part of the finished product. Gelatin fibers, textured soy protein, or other food-safe materials can provide attachment, alignment, texture, and nutrition.

Keeping the scaffold eliminates a separation step, but its flavor, cooking response, digestibility, mechanical behavior, and regulatory status must suit the food.
