# Skeletal Muscle Actuator

A skeletal muscle actuator uses aligned myotubes to generate voluntary-style contraction on demand. Electrical or optical stimulation activates the tissue, and attachment across a flexible joint converts shortening into motion.

The actuator can be trained by repeated stimulation and can produce directional control, but it requires maturation, anchoring, and continued access to culture medium.
