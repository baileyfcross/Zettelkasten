# Training Data Poisoning for LLMs

Training data poisoning introduces manipulated examples into pretraining or adaptation data so the resulting model learns degraded, biased, or attacker-chosen behavior. Poisoning may affect general quality or create behavior that appears only for a specific trigger.

Defenses include source provenance, validation, deduplication, anomaly review, controlled data pipelines, and post-training evaluation against targeted behaviors. Model scale makes perfect inspection difficult, increasing the importance of supply-chain accountability.
