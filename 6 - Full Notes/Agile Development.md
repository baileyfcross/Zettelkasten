# Agile Development

Agile development organizes work around short cycles that produce a working [[Product Increment]]. Analysis, design, implementation, integration, and testing recur inside each cycle, so evidence from the product can redirect later work.

Agility is the ability to respond to change while continuing to deliver value. It depends on [[Empirical Process Control]], collaboration, and an ordered [[Product Backlog]], not on eliminating planning or discipline.

The current source frames each iteration as a complete small project containing requirements, design, coding, integration, testing, and an internally deliverable result. Feedback on that integrated result changes the next iteration, making adaptation an evidence-producing cycle rather than a promise to improvise.
