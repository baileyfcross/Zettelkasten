# Normal and Utility Category Loading

An aircraft can have different weight and center-of-gravity limits for normal and utility categories. Utility-category loading permits specified training maneuvers only when the aircraft remains inside the more restrictive envelope. Fuel and payload may therefore need to be reduced before stalls, spins, spirals, steep turns, or slow-flight exercises are authorized.
