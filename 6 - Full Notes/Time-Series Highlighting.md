# Time-Series Highlighting

Time-series highlighting temporarily gives one line or group greater visual emphasis while muting the others. It helps identify an individual path inside a dense [[Superposed Time-Series Plot]] without discarding group context. The selection may come from hovering over a line or legend entry. A persistent visual cue should connect the highlighted line to its name, since color alone may be insufficient among similar or crossing trajectories.
