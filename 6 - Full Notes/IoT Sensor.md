# IoT Sensor

An IoT sensor is a transducer that converts a physical condition into data a connected system can process. Sensors can detect light, sound, temperature, pressure, motion, moisture, magnetic or electrical fields, chemicals, radiation, and biological markers. Their role is to give computation a way to observe conditions that may be continuous, remote, dangerous, or too subtle for direct human inspection.

Many small sensors use microelectromechanical systems, which combine mechanical movement with an electrical signal while consuming little power. A useful sensor installation depends on more than sensitivity: placement, calibration, power life, network reliability, and interpretation determine whether its readings support trustworthy [[Context-Aware IoT Analytics]] or merely generate noise.
