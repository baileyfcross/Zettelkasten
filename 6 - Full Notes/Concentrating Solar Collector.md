# Concentrating Solar Collector

A concentrating collector uses mirrors or lenses to redirect [[Beam Solar Radiation]] onto a smaller absorber. It reaches temperatures suited to process heat and power cycles, but generally needs Sun tracking because diffuse light cannot be focused in the same way. Its useful heat depends on [[Solar Concentration Ratio]], optical efficiency, absorber temperature, and heat losses; greater concentration does not eliminate any of those losses.
