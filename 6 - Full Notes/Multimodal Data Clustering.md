# Multimodal Data Clustering

Multimodal clustering combines representations from sources such as text, imagery, audio, and video. Fusion may occur before clustering through a joint feature space or during clustering through coupled objectives and multiple similarity graphs.

Early fusion is simple but vulnerable to incompatible scales and missing modalities. Model-level fusion can weight evidence separately, though it introduces more parameters and must distinguish complementary signals from redundant or conflicting ones.
