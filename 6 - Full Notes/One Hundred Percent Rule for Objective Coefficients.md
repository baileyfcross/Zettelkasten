# One Hundred Percent Rule for Objective Coefficients

The 100 percent rule gives a sufficient test for simultaneous changes in several objective coefficients. For each change, its magnitude is expressed as a percentage of the allowable increase or decrease in the same direction, and those percentages are summed.

If the total is no greater than 100 percent, the current basis remains optimal and feasible. If it exceeds 100 percent, no conclusion follows from the rule alone; the revised objective row must be checked directly.
