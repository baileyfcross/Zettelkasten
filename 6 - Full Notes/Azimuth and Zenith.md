# Azimuth and Zenith

Azimuth is the horizontal angle between the projection of a direction and a reference direction such as true north. The zenith is the point directly above an observer, opposite the local direction of gravity. Together with elevation, these angles locate an observed object in the local sky and connect celestial observations to terrestrial navigation.
