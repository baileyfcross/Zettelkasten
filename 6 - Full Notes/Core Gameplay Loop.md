# Core Gameplay Loop

A core gameplay loop is the interactive cycle central to the player's attention: intention leads to action, the game state changes, feedback communicates the result, and progress or reward informs the next intention.

Core does not necessarily mean fastest or innermost. A game may shift among several core loops and nest rapid loops inside slower outer loops, connecting moment-to-moment play with [[Player Goal|goals]], learning, and [[Progression System|progression]].
