# Worldline

A worldline is the path traced by an object through spacetime—the ordered set of [[Spacetime Event|events]] at which that object exists. A body moving at constant velocity follows a straight worldline in [[Minkowski Spacetime]], while acceleration bends the path by changing its direction through spacetime.

The tangent to a timelike worldline is the object's [[Four-Velocity]], and the amount of time carried by a clock along the path is its [[Proper Time]]. The restriction that a massive body remain inside the light cone limits the possible slopes of its worldline and prevents it from crossing the light-speed boundary.

Worldlines make comparisons such as the [[Twin Paradox]] geometric: two observers can leave and reunite at the same events but accumulate different proper times because they followed different paths between them.
