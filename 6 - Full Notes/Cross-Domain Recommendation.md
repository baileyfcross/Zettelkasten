# Cross-Domain Recommendation

Cross-domain recommendation improves predictions in one item domain by transferring relevant information from other domains. A user's labels, images, connections, or other activities can reveal preferences not visible in a sparse post history.

The method must preserve both within-domain similarity and cross-domain user-item relationships. Auxiliary evidence is valuable when it reflects stable characteristics shared with the target decision.
