# Waypoint Indicator

A waypoint indicator converts the world position of a destination into a visual cue that helps the player locate it. A visible waypoint can be projected directly onto the screen, while an off-screen target requires an edge marker or directional arrow.

The implementation must account for targets behind the camera and clamp cues to a useful screen region. Distance or contextual labels can supplement direction without overwhelming the view.
