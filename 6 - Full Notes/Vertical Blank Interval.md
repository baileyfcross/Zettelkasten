# Vertical Blank Interval

The vertical blank interval is the short period after a display finishes one image and before it begins scanning the next. Historically it reflected the return of a CRT electron beam to the top of the screen.

Presenting a completed back buffer during this interval prevents [[Screen Tearing]]. Modern display technology differs from CRT hardware, but vertical synchronization preserves the same timing contract between rendering and presentation.
