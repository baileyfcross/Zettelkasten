# Method of Exhaustion

The method of exhaustion approximates a geometric quantity with a sequence of inscribed or circumscribed figures whose error becomes arbitrarily small. Classical calculations of a disk's area use polygons with increasing numbers of sides. The method anticipates the modern idea of a [[Limit of a Sequence]] without requiring a closed-form value at each finite stage.
