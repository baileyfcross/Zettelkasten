# Magnetic Flux Density

Magnetic flux density $B$ characterizes the force-producing strength and direction of a magnetic field. A straight current-carrying conductor of length $L$ perpendicular to the field experiences $F=BIL$, and a moving charge experiences the [[Lorentz Force]]. Its SI unit is the tesla.
