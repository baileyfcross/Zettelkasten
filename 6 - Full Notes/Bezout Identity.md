# Bezout Identity

Bézout's identity states that the greatest common divisor of integers a and b can be written as ax + by for some integers x and y. The coefficients arise by reversing the steps of the Euclidean algorithm.

When the gcd is one, the identity produces modular inverses and proves coprimality consequences.
