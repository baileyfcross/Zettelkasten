# Dimension of a Module

For a nonzero R-module M, its Krull dimension is dim(R/Ann(M)). For a finitely generated module, this also equals the dimension of its [[Support of a Module|support]].

Over a Noetherian local ring, the dimension equals the degree of the [[Hilbert-Samuel Polynomial]] and the minimum number of parameters needed to reduce M to finite length.
