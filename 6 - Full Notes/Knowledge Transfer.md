# Knowledge Transfer

Knowledge transfer reuses structure learned in one domain to improve a related target problem. In social recommendation, auxiliary item domains reveal user characteristics that can compensate for sparse or missing target interactions.

Transfer is mediated through shared users and their relationships rather than copying predictions blindly. Its success depends on whether the auxiliary behaviors express preferences relevant to the target domain.
