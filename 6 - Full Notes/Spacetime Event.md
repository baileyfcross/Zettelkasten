# Spacetime Event

A spacetime event is something localized by one time coordinate and three spatial coordinates. Different [[Inertial Reference Frame|inertial frames]] assign different coordinate values to the same event, but the event itself is the shared physical occurrence those coordinate descriptions identify.

Pairs of events, rather than isolated coordinates, carry much of the physical content of relativity. Their separation determines an [[Invariant Spacetime Interval]], which is the same for every inertial observer and classifies the pair as timelike, lightlike, or spacelike. An object's successive events form its [[Worldline]].

For example, a flash emitted at one location and its later detection at another are two events. Observers may disagree about the spatial and temporal parts of their separation while agreeing on its invariant causal type.
