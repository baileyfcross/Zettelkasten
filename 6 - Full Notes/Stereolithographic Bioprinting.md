# Stereolithographic Bioprinting

Stereolithographic bioprinting selectively exposes a photosensitive cell-compatible polymer to light, solidifying patterned regions one layer at a time. It can produce fine features and change cell or material composition between layers.

Photoinitiator toxicity, light exposure, material switching, resolution, and cell density constrain biological performance.
