# Ionospheric GPS Delay

Ionospheric GPS delay occurs because charged particles change the propagation speed of radio signals passing through the upper atmosphere. The effect varies with frequency, time, location, and solar activity, so an uncorrected pseudorange is longer than the geometric path. Dual-frequency receivers estimate the delay by comparing signals that crossed nearly the same path at different frequencies, while single-frequency users depend more heavily on broadcast or augmentation models.
