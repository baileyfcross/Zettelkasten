# Qualitative Choropleth

A qualitative choropleth fills regions according to nominal categories rather than ordered quantities. Distinct hues should make categories separable without implying that one is larger or better. The number of categories must remain small enough for reliable identification, and adjacent regions need sufficient contrast. If the categories have a natural order, an ordered palette and quantitative mapping may communicate the structure more honestly.
