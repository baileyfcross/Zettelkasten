# Internet of Things Legal Risk

Internet of Things legal risk arises when connected physical devices continuously collect information, exchange it, and use it to affect their surroundings. The same device can implicate privacy, safety, evidence, security, and product responsibility.

Because collection may be ambient and action may be automated, users may not see when a legally significant operation occurs. The activity-based view separates sensing, processing, storage, distribution, and physical action so responsibility can be traced.
