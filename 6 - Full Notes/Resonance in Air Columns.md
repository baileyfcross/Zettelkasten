# Resonance in Air Columns

An air column resonates when its length supports a [[Standing Wave]] consistent with its ends. An open end is approximately a displacement antinode, while a closed end is a displacement node. A tube open at both ends supports integer harmonics; a tube closed at one end supports the odd harmonics in the ideal model.
