# Continuous Speech Recognition

Continuous speech recognition transcribes naturally connected speech without requiring pauses between words. The recognizer must infer boundaries while accounting for pronunciation changes caused by neighboring sounds.

The task requires sequence-aware [[Acoustic Model|acoustic models]], a [[Pronunciation Model]], and linguistic constraints from a [[Statistical Language Model]] to select among competing word sequences.
