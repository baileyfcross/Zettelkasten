# Vertex Groups as Saved Selections

A vertex group stores membership and optional weights for selected vertices of a mesh. The group can later reselect or deselect the same region, turning a laborious component choice into a reusable named set.

Weights extend the set from a binary boundary into graded influence. The same group can therefore organize modeling selections and drive effects such as deformation or particle density, provided its meaning remains clear and consistently named.
