# Team Communication Overhead

Team communication overhead grows faster than headcount because the number of possible person-to-person channels is n(n−1)/2. Adding people therefore creates coordination cost as well as capacity.

Large projects preserve shorter communication paths by organizing small teams around coherent outcomes and using explicit coordination mechanisms between them. More people do not automatically make a late or entangled effort faster.
