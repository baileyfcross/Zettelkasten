# Computational Model

A computational model can describe an abstract machine that executes programs or a mathematical representation of a process that a computer can evaluate. Its meaning depends on whether the context is computer science or a scientific domain.

In simulation, the senses meet: equations representing a phenomenon are translated into the states and operations of an executable machine. The model’s assumptions determine what the resulting computation can legitimately explain.
