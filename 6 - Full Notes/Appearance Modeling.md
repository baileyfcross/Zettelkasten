# Appearance Modeling

Appearance modeling records how a physical or virtual surface should look when rendered. Color, texture, reflectance, transparency, and illumination response may be sampled from the scene or assigned through authoring controls.

Geometry alone can support registration and occlusion, but a convincing reconstruction also needs appearance that changes plausibly with viewpoint and light. The required detail depends on whether the model is used for measurement, visualization, or photometric composition.
