# Solar Clearness Index

The clearness index compares radiation measured on a horizontal ground surface over a period with extraterrestrial radiation expected for that same period. It groups atmospheric absorption, scattering, sun angle, and cloudiness into one ratio. The period may be hourly, daily, or monthly; a design calculation must use the matching timescale. A low index signals little usable site radiation despite the [[Solar Constant]].
