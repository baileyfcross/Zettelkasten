# Uncertain Data

Uncertain data represents values or item occurrences with alternatives, probabilities, or imprecision rather than assuming every recorded fact is definite. Uncertainty can arise from sensing error, incomplete observation, inference, integration, or noisy sources.

Mining must propagate that uncertainty into support and pattern criteria instead of silently converting probabilistic records into certain ones.
