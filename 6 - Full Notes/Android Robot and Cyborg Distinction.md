# Android Robot and Cyborg Distinction

An android is an artificial being designed to resemble a human, whereas a cyborg combines biological and technological components in one embodied system. A robot need not resemble a person at all, and a person using a prosthesis or implanted device may fit the cyborg category without losing human agency.

Popular stories frequently blur these terms because humanlike appearance is narratively useful. Keeping them distinct clarifies whether a discussion concerns artificial embodiment, human augmentation, or a general machine that senses and acts. The categories also become less binary along the [[Human-Robot Partnership Continuum]].
