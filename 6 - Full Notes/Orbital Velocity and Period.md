# Orbital Velocity and Period

In a circular orbit, gravitational attraction supplies the required centripetal force. The orbital speed depends on the parent body's gravitational parameter and the orbital radius, while the period grows with the three-halves power of that radius. These relations assume negligible atmospheric drag and are special cases of the energy relation in [[Vis-Viva Orbital Energy Relation]].
