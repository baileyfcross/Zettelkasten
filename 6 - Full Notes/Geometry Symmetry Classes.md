# Geometry Symmetry Classes

Reflectional symmetry reproduces a geometry across a line in two dimensions or a plane in three dimensions. Rotational symmetry reproduces it after a turn about a point or axis; its order states how many repeated sectors complete a full revolution.

Symmetry describes shape before it justifies model reduction. Materials, sources, loads, and boundary conditions must share the same transformation before one repeated portion can represent the rest.
