# Log Operations Lifecycle

The log operations lifecycle moves from continuous collection to analysis and then response. Collection preserves timely system, application, security, and network events; analysis identifies patterns and anomalies; response uses those findings to repair faults, optimize performance, or contain threats.

An LLM can assist interpretation and response, but it depends on the integrity, structure, and searchable retention of the original telemetry. The lifecycle therefore remains an operational data pipeline rather than a chat-only activity.
