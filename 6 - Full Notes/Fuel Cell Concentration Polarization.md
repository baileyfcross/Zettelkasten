# Fuel Cell Concentration Polarization

Concentration polarization occurs when reactants or ions cannot reach an electrode reaction site fast enough for the demanded current. The source separates slow gas diffusion into porous electrodes from slow ion diffusion on the electrolyte side. As local reactant concentration falls, cell voltage drops sharply. Better transport and reaction-surface design matter especially near the high-current end of the characteristic.
