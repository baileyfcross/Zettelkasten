# Algorithmic Fairness

Algorithmic fairness is the demand that automated decisions distribute treatment, errors, opportunities, and burdens in a justifiable way. It may be evaluated through equal treatment, equal outcomes, comparable error rates, protection of rights, or attention to existing disadvantage.

These aims can conflict, especially when groups differ because of historical conditions. Selecting a metric therefore expresses a theory of justice rather than removing values from the decision. Fairness work must state which people and outcomes matter, explain why a criterion is appropriate, and examine whether the institution using the system is itself legitimate.
