# Smart Home Platform Interoperability

Smart home platform interoperability allows household devices from different manufacturers to participate in one control and automation environment. Low-power technologies such as Z-Wave, ZigBee, and Insteon can connect local devices, while platforms such as Alexa, Google Home, and HomeKit provide user-facing coordination.

Compatibility is not guaranteed merely because products share a radio protocol. Manufacturers can expose different capabilities through their apps and APIs, leaving a device usable alone but unavailable to whole-home scenes or cross-device rules. A fragmented home can therefore require many apps and reduce the very convenience that [[Smart Home Automation]] is meant to create.
