# Angle Between Planes

The angle between two planes is determined by the angle between their normal vectors. If $n_1$ and $n_2$ are normals, then

$$\cos\theta=\frac{n_1\cdot n_2}{\|n_1\|\|n_2\|}.$$

Choosing the acute angle gives the usual dihedral angle; oriented settings may retain a signed or supplementary choice.
