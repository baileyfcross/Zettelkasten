# ROCK Clustering

ROCK, Robust Clustering using Links, is an agglomerative method for categorical data. It finds neighbors under a similarity threshold, counts common-neighbor links between records, and merges clusters with the best positive goodness measure.

The process stops at a requested number of clusters or when links disappear. Sampling can make it practical for large databases.
