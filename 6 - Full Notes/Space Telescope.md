# Space Telescope

A space telescope is an astronomical observatory placed above most or all of Earth's atmosphere. It can avoid atmospheric seeing limits and observe wavelengths absorbed before they reach the ground.

Its advantages create demanding engineering requirements. The spacecraft must point accurately at a small region for long periods, keep instruments within operating conditions, transmit observations, and often work without physical access. Early designers imagined human-operated observatories because remote control seemed impractical; the [[Robotic Spacecraft Revolution]] made autonomous pointing and data return the dominant design.
