# Custom Deployment Target

A custom deployment target publishes an application to infrastructure such as IIS or another destination configured outside a provider-managed default. It gives the team control over the host while also returning responsibility for its operating system, runtime, networking, and maintenance.

The book contrasts such targets with Azure App Service, which manages much of that infrastructure. Target choice therefore determines both the deployment mechanism and who owns continuing operational work.
