# Scene Graph

A scene graph is a hierarchical representation of objects and transformations in a spatial scene. A child inherits the coordinate transformation of its parent, allowing assemblies and attached augmentations to move through changes applied higher in the hierarchy.

AR scene graphs can represent both physical and virtual objects and connect them to tracked frames. Rendering traverses the graph, while application behavior may use the same structure for visibility, grouping, and interaction.
