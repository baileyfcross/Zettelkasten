# Data Binning Thresholds

Binning transforms a numeric field into ranges, such as grouping customer ages into age bands. It can make a coarse distinction more useful than treating every adjacent value as substantively different.

The thresholds are a domain choice. A poorly placed boundary can separate very similar cases or conceal a distinction important to the target. Data transformation therefore needs domain knowledge, exploratory checks, and sometimes trial-and-error evaluation rather than assuming that a convenient round-number cut is neutral.
