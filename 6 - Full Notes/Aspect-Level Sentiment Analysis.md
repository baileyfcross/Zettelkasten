# Aspect-Level Sentiment Analysis

Aspect-level sentiment analysis identifies the feature or attribute being discussed and assigns polarity to the opinion about that aspect. A single product review can therefore be positive about a screen and negative about battery life without forcing both statements into one document label.

The finer unit demands reliable aspect extraction and a way to connect modifiers, negation, and opinion terms to the correct target. It trades the simplicity of [[Document-Level Sentiment Classification]] for more actionable and faithful summaries.
