# Roblox Particle Emitter

A ParticleEmitter creates many short-lived image-based particles from a part or attachment. Its texture, rate, lifetime, speed, spread, size, transparency, color, rotation, and acceleration collectively define effects such as smoke, sparks, fire, dust, or magical energy.

The emitter describes a changing population rather than one persistent object. Small adjustments can multiply across every particle, so rate and lifetime should be balanced against both the desired density and runtime cost. For a continuous effect stretched between two points, [[Roblox Beam Effect]] provides a more direct representation.
