# Lexicon Coverage

Lexicon coverage is the proportion of texts or relevant terms for which an [[Opinion Lexicon]] supplies usable sentiment evidence. A model that returns neutral merely because no entry matched has a coverage failure, not necessarily a genuine neutral opinion.

Evaluation should separate classification quality on covered texts from the percentage left unsupported. A specialized corpus can expose missing jargon, abbreviations, or platform conventions that a general lexicon never encoded.
