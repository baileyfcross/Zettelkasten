# Haptic Compliance Perception

Haptic compliance perception judges how much a surface or object deforms under applied force. Physical compliance is displacement divided by force, and stiffness is its inverse; perceptually these support the soft–hard dimension used to evaluate fruit, cushions, controls, and tissues.

Pressing is the optimal [[Haptic Exploratory Procedure]] for deformable objects because skin deformation supplies critical information. For a rigid control that moves under load, kinesthetic force and motion also matter. Interface designers often add a tactile event at the breakaway force so users know that a key or control has registered.
