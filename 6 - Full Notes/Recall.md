# Recall

Recall is the proportion of actual positive cases that a classifier identifies. It is also called sensitivity or the true-positive rate and falls when false negatives increase.

Thresholds that raise recall often lower [[Precision]], making the tradeoff a decision question rather than a purely technical optimum.
