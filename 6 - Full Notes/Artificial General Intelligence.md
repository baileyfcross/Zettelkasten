# Artificial General Intelligence

Artificial general intelligence is the proposed ability of a machine to perform a wide range of intellectual tasks rather than succeed only within a narrow domain. It is often called strong AI and is contrasted with current systems optimized for tasks such as recognition, prediction, or game play.

General intelligence is central to forecasts of [[Superintelligence]] and a [[Technological Singularity]], but its feasibility and path of development remain uncertain. Ethical attention devoted to this possibility should not replace examination of existing narrow systems, whose limited intelligence can still have broad effects when embedded in social institutions.
