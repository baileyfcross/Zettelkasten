# Truss Element Stress Recovery

Truss element stress recovery projects the global nodal displacement vector onto the member axis and divides the relative axial displacement by length. Multiplication by Young's modulus then yields normal stress.

The resulting scalar indicates the member's tensile or compressive state. It is computed after the unknown global nodal translations have been solved.
