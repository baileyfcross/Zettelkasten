# Time-Harmonic Electromagnetic Field

A time-harmonic electromagnetic field oscillates sinusoidally at a single angular frequency. Phasor notation separates the common exponential time factor from the spatially varying complex field amplitude.

Time differentiation then becomes multiplication by the imaginary frequency factor, converting time-domain Maxwell equations into frequency-domain equations. This form is widely used for steady sinusoidal electrical systems.
