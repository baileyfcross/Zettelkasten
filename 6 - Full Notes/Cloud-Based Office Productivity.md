# Cloud-Based Office Productivity

Cloud-based office productivity delivers word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, and related tools as hosted applications. Users can work from different devices, store documents remotely, and collaborate on shared content without distributing the full software stack to every workstation.

Central updates and reduced endpoint management can lower operating effort. Data protection, open export formats, backup independence, provider availability, and total ownership cost remain important because everyday business work depends on the service.
