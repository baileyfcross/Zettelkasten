# Vacuum Tube Reliability Limit

Vacuum tubes enabled electronic speed but consumed substantial power, produced heat, and eventually burned out. In a machine containing thousands of tubes, one failure could stop the entire computation.

Engineers mounted tubes in sockets for rapid replacement and designed circuits conservatively. The search for a cooler, smaller, more reliable switch drove the development of solid-state electronics.
