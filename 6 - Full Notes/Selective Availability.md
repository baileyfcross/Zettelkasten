# Selective Availability

Selective Availability was the intentional degradation of open GPS accuracy to preserve a military advantage while still supporting civilian navigation. It altered broadcast clock or orbital information so unauthorized receivers obtained a noisier solution. Differential correction largely defeated its practical effect, and policy shifted repeatedly before President Bill Clinton ordered it set permanently to zero in 2000. The episode shows that navigation accuracy can be governed as access, not only engineered as performance.
