# Observability Agent Persona

An observability agent persona defines who is asking, what decision they make, which tools they use, and what operational context they are permitted to see. Developers, testers, SREs, support staff, product owners, and business leaders need different questions and result formats.

Persona design prevents a generic assistant from returning the same dashboard summary to every role. It guides tool selection, access policy, instructions, delivery location, and the level of technical or business explanation appropriate to the user's workflow.
