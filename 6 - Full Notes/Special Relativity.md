# Special Relativity

Special relativity rests on two postulates: the laws of physics have the same form in every inertial frame, and light in vacuum has the same speed for every inertial observer. Space and time coordinates therefore depend on relative motion, while the spacetime interval remains invariant. The theory replaces Galilean transformations with Lorentz transformations.
