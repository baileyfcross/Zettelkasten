# Torque

Torque measures the turning effect of a force about an axis. Its magnitude is $\tau=F d_\perp$, where $d_\perp$ is the perpendicular distance from the axis to the force's line of action; equivalently, $\tau=rF\sin\theta$. Torque is a vector and changes angular momentum just as force changes linear momentum.
