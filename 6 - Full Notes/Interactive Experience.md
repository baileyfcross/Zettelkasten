# Interactive Experience

An interactive experience is an experience whose form depends partly on participant action. Designers inscribe possibilities into rules, media, and technology, but each participant decodes and realizes those possibilities through play.

The term is broader than [[Game]] because interaction does not require a formal goal, conflict, or measurable outcome. It is useful when discussing works that borrow game techniques without fitting a strict game definition. The distinction keeps analysis focused on what the participant can change and how the system responds.
