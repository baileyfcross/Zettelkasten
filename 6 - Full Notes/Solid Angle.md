# Solid Angle

A solid angle extends the planar notion of angle to a surface viewed from a point. It is measured in steradians and can be visualized as the area cut out on a unit sphere by rays from the observation point through the boundary of the viewed surface.

An area element $dA$ at distance $r$, whose normal makes angle $i$ with the viewing direction, subtends $d\Omega=\cos(i)dA/r^2$. Integrating this expression over a surface gives its total solid angle; an entire sphere subtends $4\pi$ steradians at its center.
