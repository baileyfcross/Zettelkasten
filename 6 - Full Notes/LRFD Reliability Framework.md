# LRFD Reliability Framework

Load and Resistance Factor Design treats load effects and resistance as variable quantities rather than exact values. Different load factors reflect differing uncertainty in dead, live, wind, snow, and other actions, while resistance factors reflect uncertainty in strength prediction and the consequences and character of a failure mode. Design is satisfactory when factored demand does not exceed reduced nominal resistance.

The calibration aims for a reasonably uniform reliability level across different members and loading combinations. Predictable dead load receives different treatment from more variable live or environmental load, and ductile yielding receives different treatment from abrupt rupture. LRFD does not eliminate the probability of failure; it controls the overlap between statistical distributions of demand and resistance to a socially accepted, finite level.
