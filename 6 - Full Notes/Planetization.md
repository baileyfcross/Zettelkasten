# Planetization

Planetization is the growth of consciousness that Earth is one planet situated within the solar system and universe. It differs from globalization, which emphasizes connections among societies, economies, and political systems on the world's surface.

Images taken from beyond Earth can produce an emotional perspective of a finite, borderless, life-supporting world isolated in space. [[Earthrise]] and the [[Blue Marble Photograph]] became especially influential symbols of fragility and common planetary identity. They did not create environmental movements by themselves, but their wide reuse shows how spaceflight changed the visual vocabulary through which Earth is understood.
