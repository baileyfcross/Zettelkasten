# Filter Bank

A filter bank divides the frequency spectrum of a speech frame into bands and measures the energy in each band. These measurements approximate the distribution of acoustic energy that distinguishes speech sounds.

The band energies form part of a [[Feature Vector]]. Applying the same bank across successive [[Speech Frame|frames]] produces a time-varying representation for an [[Acoustic Model]].
