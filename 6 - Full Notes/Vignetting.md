# Vignetting

Vignetting is the reduction of image brightness toward the edges of a camera image. It can arise from the lens and camera geometry even when the physical scene has uniform illumination.

If ideal virtual pixels are composited over a vignetted image, their edge brightness can reveal the overlay. Measuring or modeling the falloff and applying it to the generated imagery improves photometric agreement.
