# Aircraft Laser Scanning for Retrofit Design

Laser scanning captures the geometry of an existing airframe as a dense digital measurement rather than assuming nominal drawings match the built aircraft. Importing that geometry into CAD supports accurately fitted wings, cowlings, mounts, and aerodynamic models. Retrofit design benefits because new components must mate with the individual airframe already in service.
