# Wicked Problems Beyond Computation

Wicked problems persist because stakeholders have conflicting values and enough power to block unwanted proposals without enough agreement to establish a shared solution. More data or computing power cannot create the missing social consensus.

Computation can visualize consequences, compare scenarios, and support deliberation, but resolution requires cooperation and collective action. Recognizing this boundary prevents technical models from being mistaken for political decisions.
