# Solar Chimney Power Plant

A solar chimney plant heats ground-level air beneath a transparent cover. Warm air rises through a tall chimney because of its density difference from cooler air, and the resulting flow drives an air turbine and generator. The source calls this semi-direct: sunlight creates the air flow, but a moving turbine still converts its mechanical energy into electricity. Chimney height and collecting area affect output.
