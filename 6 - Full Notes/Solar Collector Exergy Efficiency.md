# Solar Collector Exergy Efficiency

Solar-collector exergy efficiency compares the exergy gained by its heated fluid with the exergy carried by incident sunlight. The source calculates useful gain from fluid mass flow and specific exergy increase. This can be much lower than the collector's heat-energy efficiency, especially for low-temperature output. The distinction helps choose between a [[Flat-Plate Solar Collector]] for heating and a concentrated receiver for high-grade power tasks.
