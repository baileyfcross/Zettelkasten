# Stem-and-Leaf Plot

A stem-and-leaf plot splits each numeric observation into leading digits and a final leaf, arranging the leaves beside their shared stems. It shows distribution shape while preserving enough digits to reconstruct the observed values, which is useful for small datasets.

The stem scale determines the display's resolution. With many observations or a wide numeric range the text becomes crowded, and a [[Histogram]] provides a more compact summary.
