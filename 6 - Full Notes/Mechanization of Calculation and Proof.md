# Mechanization of Calculation and Proof

Mechanization joins numerical calculation and logical inference under explicit rules that a machine can execute without human intervention. This ideal motivated formal methods, models of computation, and the development of automatic digital computers.

Mechanization also revealed boundaries. A machine can reproduce a rule-governed process at extraordinary speed, but some questions are undecidable and many practical computations require approximations, judgment, or domain-specific interpretation.
