# Art and Sound Prototype

An art and sound prototype tests the sensory direction of a game and the process required to produce it. It can explore visual style, animation, audio character, atmosphere, readability, or how those elements change the meaning of an interaction.

The artifact should place representative sensory material in a relevant context. A beautiful isolated asset does not answer whether the style communicates state clearly or supports the intended play experience.
