# Critical Point and Equal Time Point

The critical point, also called the equal time point, is where continuing to the destination takes the same time as returning to departure. Wind shifts it toward the direction from which the wind comes because the return and continuation groundspeeds differ. Unlike the fuel-limited [[Point of Safe Return]], it answers a time-to-alternate question and is especially important where diversion options are sparse.
