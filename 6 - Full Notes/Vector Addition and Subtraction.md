# Vector Addition and Subtraction

Vector addition combines component-wise displacements, while subtraction finds the displacement from one position to another. Adding a velocity-scaled displacement to a position advances an object; subtracting two positions yields the direction and distance between them.

These operations preserve the geometric meaning of their operands only when their coordinate spaces and units agree.
