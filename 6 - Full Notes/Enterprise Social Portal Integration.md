# Enterprise Social Portal Integration

An enterprise social portal combines managed content, application components, identity, collaboration, and external social services in one governed environment. Integration may surface internal communities, let users share portal content outward, ingest external activity, or provide reusable social applications within portal pages.

The source distinguishes this work from simply embedding a public feed. A portal must preserve access control, audience, moderation, identity mapping, and content lifecycle as information crosses systems. Standards and replaceable adapters help keep presentation independent of a single network's API. [[Liferay Portal Social Model]], [[HCL Digital Experience Social Integration]], and [[CoreMedia Social Media Hub]] are three product-level approaches to the same enterprise problem.
