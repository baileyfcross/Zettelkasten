# Disease Progression Model

A disease progression model represents how a patient's latent or observed health state changes over time. It can connect repeated measurements, diagnoses, and interventions to stages or trajectories that support prognosis.

Longitudinal records are irregular and only partially observe the biological process. Visit frequency may depend on illness, and treatment changes the trajectory that the model is trying to describe. A progression model should therefore distinguish observation timing from state change and be validated on future or external patient sequences rather than only reproducing known histories.
