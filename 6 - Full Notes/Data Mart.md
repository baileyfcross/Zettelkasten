# Data Mart

A data mart is a subset of enterprise data restricted to a particular subject area. It uses a consistent model and makes a focused collection available for efficient analysis.

A mart can reduce the amount of data scanned for a specific mining problem, but its narrower scope may omit relationships visible in the wider warehouse. Its boundaries should follow the analytical question rather than departmental convenience alone.
