# Thermionic Emitter

The emitter is the hot cathode of a [[Thermionic Generator]]. Its surface must reach a temperature at which electrons can overcome the [[Surface Work Function]] and leave for the collector. Low work function helps emission, but the electrode also needs to survive the heat and corrosive surroundings. The source identifies expensive high-temperature materials and protective ceramic shielding as design limitations.
