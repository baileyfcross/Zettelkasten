# Collective Communication

Collective communication coordinates a group of distributed workers through operations such as gathering, regrouping, broadcasting, or reducing their partial results. The communication pattern expresses a whole-worker-group exchange rather than many unrelated messages.

In iterative analysis, collectives synchronize centroids, graph edges, or rank values while cached input remains local. This can replace repeated group-by jobs and reduce data-movement overhead.
