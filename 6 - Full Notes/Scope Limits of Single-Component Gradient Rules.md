# Scope Limits of Single-Component Gradient Rules

The generalized theory applies to expectation values differentiated through one gate whose Hermitian generator has exactly two eigenvalues. Other circuit parameters are held fixed, and more general objective functions enter only through the chain rule applied to their expectation-value components.

The paper proves identities, exact bias expressions, and an impossibility result, but does not benchmark an optimizer, run a noisy-hardware experiment, or compare end-to-end runtime. Finite-shot variances are characterized, while device noise and the practical choice of shift remain outside the main result.

Multi-parameter derivative mixtures and pseudo-Newton control are proposed as future directions. The paper therefore establishes the information content and limits of a family of circuit evaluations rather than a general solution to variational quantum optimization.
