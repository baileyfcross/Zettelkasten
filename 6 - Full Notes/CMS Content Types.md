# CMS Content Types

A CMS content type defines the structured shape and editorial purpose of an item. Sites, pages, and posts can have different fields, behaviors, and templates even when they share the same publishing system.

Explicit types turn editorial expectations into a model that software can validate and render. A useful type is broad enough for recurring content but narrow enough that its fields have stable meaning across every instance.
