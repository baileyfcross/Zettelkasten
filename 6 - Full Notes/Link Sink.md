# Link Sink

A link sink is a group of web-graph nodes with incoming links from outside but no outgoing path back to the rest of the graph. Once a path enters the group, its links keep movement trapped within the group.

In PageRank, a link sink can absorb rank even when no individual [[Dangling Node]] exists. [[PageRank Teleportation]] gives the random surfer a probability of jumping anywhere, preventing a closed group from draining importance from every other page.
