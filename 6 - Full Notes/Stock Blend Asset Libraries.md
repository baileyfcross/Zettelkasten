# Stock Blend Asset Libraries

A stock `.blend` library supplies premade objects or complete scenes that can be used directly, studied, or adapted as a starting point. The book presents Blend Swap as an example whose assets were searchable by content and license.

Reuse saves modeling time but does not erase provenance. The artist must verify that the license permits the intended use and inspect topology, dependencies, scale, and materials before treating a downloaded scene as production-ready.
