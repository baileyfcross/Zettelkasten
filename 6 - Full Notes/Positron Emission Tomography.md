# Positron Emission Tomography

Positron emission tomography uses a beta-plus-emitting tracer. Each positron soon annihilates with an electron, producing two gamma photons traveling in nearly opposite directions. Coincident detection identifies a line of response, and many such events reconstruct a three-dimensional map of tracer concentration and metabolic activity.
