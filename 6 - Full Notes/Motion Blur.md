# Motion Blur

Motion blur is the spatial streaking produced when an object or camera moves while the shutter remains open. Its extent and direction depend on exposure time, relative motion, projection geometry, and distance.

Forensic analysis can ask whether a moving subject's blur is plausible for the recorded [[Shutter Speed]] and whether independently inserted elements share the expected camera motion. A sharp object inside an otherwise motion-blurred scene may be suspicious, though masking, flash, and differing object velocities can provide benign explanations.
