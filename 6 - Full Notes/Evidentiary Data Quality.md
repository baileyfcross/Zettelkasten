# Evidentiary Data Quality

Evidentiary data quality is the fitness of information to support a claim, finding, or legal decision. It includes accuracy, provenance, relevance, completeness, and the ability to explain how the record was created and transformed.

An automated output is not self-validating merely because it is quantitative. Its weight depends on the quality of the underlying observations and on whether the processing preserves the meaning required by the decision.
