# Technological Singularity

The technological singularity is a projected point after which accelerating machine intelligence makes social and technological change radically difficult to predict. In AI narratives, an [[Intelligence Explosion]] often drives the transition and produces [[Superintelligence]].

The term marks a limit of prediction rather than a detailed account of what follows. It can support utopian expectations of abundance and human enhancement or catastrophic expectations of loss of control. Because these futures are highly speculative, they should be distinguished from ethical problems created by systems that already classify, recommend, monitor, or decide.

Exponential trends in computing motivate some predicted dates, but a succession of technical growth curves does not guarantee matching progress in the science of intelligence. The important claim is therefore conditional rather than calendrical: if human-level AI can redesign or multiply itself, a discontinuity could follow rapidly enough that institutions have little time to adapt.
