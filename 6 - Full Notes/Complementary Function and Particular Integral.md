# Complementary Function and Particular Integral

For a linear inhomogeneous differential equation, the complementary function is the general solution of the corresponding homogeneous equation. A particular integral is any single solution of the original equation with its forcing term included.

Their sum is the general solution of the inhomogeneous equation. The complementary function carries the arbitrary constants fixed by initial or boundary conditions, while different valid particular integrals differ only by a homogeneous contribution.
