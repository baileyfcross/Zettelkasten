# Model Sheet

A model sheet presents one character in multiple poses, expressions, and views on a single page. It helps a team compare how body language, proportion, clothing, and facial expression remain consistent across situations.

The artifact develops a character beyond one attractive image and gives artists and animators a shared reference. It can expose a design that lacks a readable emotional range before expensive production begins.
