# Jordan Canonical Form

Jordan canonical form is a block diagonal matrix similar to a square matrix whose characteristic polynomial splits into linear factors. Each [[Jordan Block]] has one eigenvalue on its diagonal, ones on its superdiagonal, and zeros elsewhere.

The form extends diagonalization to operators that lack enough independent eigenvectors. Algebraic multiplicity determines the total size of the blocks for an eigenvalue, while the minimal polynomial determines the largest block size.
