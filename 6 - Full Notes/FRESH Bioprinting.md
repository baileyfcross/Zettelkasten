# FRESH Bioprinting

Freeform reversible embedding of suspended hydrogels, or FRESH, extrudes a soft bioink into a support bath that holds each deposited segment in place. The bath prevents deformation while later layers are added.

After printing, warming melts the gelatin-based support so the completed hydrated structure can be released gently into culture medium.
