# Enterprise Social Mashups

An enterprise social mashup combines information or widgets from multiple services into one personalized application. Unlike a portal component tied to a particular server API, a mashup aspires to be language- and platform-neutral. Systems such as Yahoo Pipes, Microsoft Popfly, Google Mashup Editor, IBM Mashup Center, and BEA AquaLogic explored this composition model.

Mashups promise rapid integration, but the source shows that hosted editors and provider APIs can disappear even when applications depend on them. Provider-neutral contracts reduce replacement cost, while proprietary widgets create another form of lock-in. [[Java Portlet Social Interoperability]] represents the enterprise-server approach to composition, and [[OpenSocial Gadget Standard]] tried to make social widgets portable across containers.
