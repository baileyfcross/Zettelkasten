# Regular Sequence

A sequence r₁,…,rₙ is regular on M when r₁ is M-regular, each later rᵢ is regular on M/(r₁,…,rᵢ₋₁)M, and the final quotient is nonzero.

Inside the Jacobson radical, permutations and positive powers preserve regularity under the source's Noetherian finite-generation hypotheses. The maximum possible length in an ideal defines [[Depth of a Module]].
