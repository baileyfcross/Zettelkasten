# Data Protection Accountability

Data protection accountability makes an organization responsible for both following governing principles and being able to demonstrate that it has done so. Policies must be connected to records of decisions, assigned roles, controls, reviews, and evidence of operation.

For big-data projects, accountability follows the full pipeline from collection and combination through model use, sharing, retention, and deletion.
