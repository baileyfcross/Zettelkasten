# No-Cloning Theorem

The no-cloning theorem states that an arbitrary unknown quantum state cannot be copied reliably into another system. A universal unitary copier would have to preserve inner products while mapping every $|\psi\rangle|0\rangle$ to $|\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle$, which is impossible for general nonorthogonal states.

The restriction does not prevent copying a known basis value with a [[Controlled-NOT Gate]], nor does it prevent entangling systems. It blocks the classical strategy of inspecting or duplicating an unknown state without altering the quantum information, which shapes communication, debugging, and [[Quantum Measurement Projection|measurement]].
