# Implicit Partial Differentiation

Implicit partial differentiation finds a partial derivative when dependent and independent variables are related by an equation rather than an explicit function. Both sides are partially differentiated while variables not chosen for differentiation are held constant.

Occurrences of the dependent variable require the [[Multivariable Chain Rule]], producing the unknown partial derivative as a factor. Rearranging the resulting relation yields that derivative without solving the original equation explicitly.
