# Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi provides wireless local-area networking over unlicensed radio spectrum. Access points connect nearby laptops and mobile devices to local networks and through them to the Internet.

Its limited range supports frequency reuse in homes, offices, and public spaces. Built-in laptop and smartphone support turned network access into an expected ambient service.
