# UDIM Texture Tile Workflow

UDIM extends UV layout beyond one zero-to-one square into numbered tiles, each associated with a separate image in one coordinated texture set. Islands can be distributed according to asset region and required resolution.

Major details may receive larger or higher-resolution tiles while secondary regions use less texture memory. Consistent tile naming and material support let the collection behave as one surface system, making the approach useful for large assets that exceed a practical single image.
