# AI Duty of Care

An AI duty of care is an obligation to take reasonable precautions against foreseeable harm when designing, training, supplying, or operating an AI system. It can ground negligence even when nobody intended the harmful outcome.

What counts as reasonable depends on the stakes, available knowledge, and the actor's role. High-risk use may require stronger testing, monitoring, documentation, and human review than a low-consequence application. Uncertainty about a model does not automatically remove the duty; it may instead require limiting deployment. The concept connects legal responsibility with the [[Epistemic Condition for Moral Responsibility]].
