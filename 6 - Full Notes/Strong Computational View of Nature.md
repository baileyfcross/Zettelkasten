# Strong Computational View of Nature

The strong computational view claims that the universe and its living beings are literally computational systems. Space, time, matter, and thought would all arise from underlying information processing rather than merely being modeled that way.

The claim is philosophically ambitious and empirically speculative. Its attraction reflects a recurring habit of interpreting the world through the dominant technology of an era.
