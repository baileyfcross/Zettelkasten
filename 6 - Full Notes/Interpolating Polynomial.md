# Interpolating Polynomial

An interpolating polynomial $p_n$ satisfies $p_n(x_i)=f(x_i)$ at each of $n+1$ distinct data points. Its degree is at most $n$.

The polynomial is unique: if two degree-$n$ polynomials agree at $n+1$ distinct points, their difference has too many roots unless it is identically zero. Different interpolation formulas reorganize the same result.
