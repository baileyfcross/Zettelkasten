# Interpreted Story

An interpreted story arises from the combination of authored material, systemic events, and the player's understanding. Its building blocks may be shared by every player, while their arrangement and meaning vary with choices, attention, and imagination.

This form sits between a fixed [[Authored Story]] and a highly [[Open Story]]. The creator supplies characters, rules, and signals, but the player connects them into a particular account of what happened and why it mattered.
