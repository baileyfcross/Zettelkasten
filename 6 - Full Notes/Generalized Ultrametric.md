# Generalized Ultrametric

A generalized ultrametric replaces a single non-negative number for a pairwise distance with an element of a partially ordered set, such as a subset of attributes on which two objects differ or a shared-prefix set. The comparison then records structured relationships rather than forcing every distinction onto one scalar scale.

The book connects this view to lattices and formal concept analysis. For binary attribute data, the set-valued relation can expose nested or overlapping concepts directly from the input table, sometimes avoiding construction of every ordinary pairwise distance.
