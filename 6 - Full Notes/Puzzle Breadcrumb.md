# Puzzle Breadcrumb

A puzzle breadcrumb is a clue that moves the solver one logical step from a broad [[Puzzle Possibility Space]] toward the solution. A sequence of breadcrumbs supports players with different abilities without removing the entire challenge at once.

Extrinsic breadcrumbs are optional hints supplied outside the puzzle and can grow progressively explicit. Intrinsic breadcrumbs are embedded in the puzzle's structure, such as crossword intersections that reveal letters, and often preserve a stronger sense of independent discovery.
