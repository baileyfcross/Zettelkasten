# Player Expression

Player expression is the communication of preference, style, intention, or identity through choices made in play. It can appear in movement, strategy, character presentation, cooperation, aggression, restraint, or goals the player invents.

Expression does not require unlimited freedom. A focused set of [[Game Verb|verbs]] can support distinctive performances when their relationships create several meaningful ways to proceed.
