# Friend of a Friend Social Graph

Friend of a Friend, commonly abbreviated FOAF, is an early approach to describing people and their relationships in a machine-readable social graph. The source connects it to the broader six-degrees idea that one person's acquaintances lead through a small number of relationships to other people.

FOAF turns a familiar “friend” mechanism into data that is not inherently tied to one platform's private schema. Its significance is less a complete modern social API than an early attempt to make identity and relationships portable across the Web. The same portability goal appears in [[Portable Contacts Interchange]], while later federation standards concentrate more on activities and delivery through [[ActivityPub Federation Model]].
