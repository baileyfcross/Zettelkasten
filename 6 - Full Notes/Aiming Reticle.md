# Aiming Reticle

An aiming reticle is a screen-space HUD element that indicates the direction or point toward which an action is aimed. Its position may be fixed at the center or derived from a world-space targeting calculation.

The visual can communicate weapon spread, target validity, or interaction state by changing size, color, or shape. It should remain legible without obscuring the object the player is trying to select.
