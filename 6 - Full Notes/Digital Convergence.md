# Digital Convergence

Digital convergence combines previously separate media and functions because they share binary representation and programmable processing. A single device can calculate, communicate, photograph, navigate, record sound, and display video.

Convergence depends on components becoming small, inexpensive, and interoperable enough to fit one system. Its direction is also shaped by design, regulation, markets, and user practices.
