# Power Rate Unit Conversion

Power is energy per unit time, so changing a per-second rate to a per-hour rate changes the numerical value even when the energy unit stays the same. The book's Btu/s-to-Btu/h example multiplies by seconds per minute and minutes per hour, giving a factor of 3,600. The units cancel to leave Btu/h. Power must not be confused with the total energy in a kilowatt-hour. See [[Energy Unit Conversion from Power and Time]] and [[Compound Rate Unit Conversion]].
