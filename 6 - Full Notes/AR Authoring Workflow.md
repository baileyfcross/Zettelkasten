# AR Authoring Workflow

An AR authoring workflow is the sequence for creating assets, anchoring them to the real world, defining behavior and interaction, testing the result in context, and packaging it for use. It connects conventional desktop content tools with in-situ inspection or editing.

Rapid iteration matters because spatial relationships are difficult to judge outside the target environment. Previewing the experience through its intended display reveals registration, scale, occlusion, and interaction problems early.
