# Ampere's Law

Ampere's law relates the circulation of magnetic field around a closed path to enclosed current:

$$\oint B\cdot d\ell=\mu_0 I_{enc}.$$

For highly symmetric steady-current arrangements, it provides direct fields for a long straight wire or solenoid. The more general Maxwell form also includes changing electric flux.
