# Constructivist Learning

Constructivist learning treats knowledge as a model the learner builds by connecting new experiences with prior understanding. Learning is therefore shaped by what the player already knows, not delivered intact by an instruction.

A game can support construction by introducing a small concept, allowing the player to act on it, giving feedback, and then combining it with later concepts. This sequence turns a tutorial into an experience of hypothesis and revision rather than a list of rules to memorize.
