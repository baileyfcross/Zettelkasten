# Associative Property

The associative property says that regrouping repeated addition or multiplication does not change the result:

$$a+(b+c)=(a+b)+c,$$

$$a(bc)=(ab)c.$$

It justifies suppressing parentheses when an expression contains only a sequence of additions or only a sequence of multiplications. Associativity does not authorize arbitrary regrouping across different operations, and subtraction or division are not associative in general. The property concerns parentheses; changing order instead uses the [[Commutative Property]].
