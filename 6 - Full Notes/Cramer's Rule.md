# Cramer's Rule

Cramer's rule solves a square [[Linear System]] $Ax=b$ with nonzero determinant by replacing one column of $A$ at a time with $b$. The solution component is $x_i=\det(A_i)/\det(A)$.

The rule clearly connects uniqueness to the [[Determinant]], but repeated determinant evaluation makes it inefficient for large numerical systems.
