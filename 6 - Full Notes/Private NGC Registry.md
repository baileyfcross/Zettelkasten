# Private NGC Registry

A private NGC registry supports organization-controlled storage and distribution of selected or customized GPU software assets. Access policy determines who can publish or pull content, allowing a team to begin with a curated base and keep its application or model inside an internal delivery boundary.

Private placement is only one supply-chain control. Images and models should also be identified by immutable version or digest, scanned, tested, approved, and promoted through environments with evidence that the deployed artifact matches the reviewed one.
