# Finite Difference Summation

Finite difference summation evaluates a series by finding a function whose forward difference equals the summand. If $\Delta F(r)=u_r$, then

$$\sum_{r=a}^{b}u_r=F(b+1)-F(a).$$

The method is the discrete analogue of applying an antiderivative at integration limits. [[Factorial Polynomial|Factorial polynomials]] are convenient because the [[Inverse Difference Operator]] acts on them by a simple degree-raising rule.
