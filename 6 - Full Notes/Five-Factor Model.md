# Five-Factor Model

The Five-Factor Model describes personality through openness to experience, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism. The source uses this model as a basis for relating personality tendencies to domains of play.

The mapping offers design hypotheses rather than fixed player categories. Individuals can seek several experiences, and preference depends on context as well as personality.
