# Software Maintainability

Software maintainability is the degree to which a system can be understood, corrected, tested, and adapted without disproportionate cost or risk. Clear responsibilities, limited complexity, automated evidence, and controlled dependencies support it.

Maintainability matters because deployed software continues to evolve after its initial release. A design that is fast to create but difficult to change transfers today's saved effort into future delivery delay and defect risk.
