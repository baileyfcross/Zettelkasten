# Digital Meme Longevity Fecundity and Fidelity

Longevity, fecundity, and copy fidelity describe how long a meme survives, how many copies it generates, and how accurately those copies preserve it. Digital media can strengthen all three by storing artifacts, enabling near-instant duplication, and connecting large populations of potential transmitters.

High fidelity does not eliminate variation. Online creators can preserve a source perfectly while also producing altered versions, so exact copying and transformation coexist. Searchable archives make both processes visible and allow researchers to observe selection among variants across time.
