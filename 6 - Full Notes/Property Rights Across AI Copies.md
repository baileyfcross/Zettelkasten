# Property Rights Across AI Copies

Property rights become ambiguous when an artificial person is duplicated. Both descendants can claim continuity with the owner who existed before copying, yet assigning the whole estate to both is impossible and dividing it automatically may ignore the predecessor's intentions.

The problem resembles inheritance and separation without matching either one. A legal framework could let the predecessor specify a division, recognize joint ownership, or treat later copies as newly created persons, but every rule changes the incentives to copy or terminate an instance. [[AI Legal Personhood]] therefore needs rules for identity-changing operations, not only rules for ordinary transactions.
