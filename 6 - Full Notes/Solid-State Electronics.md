# Solid-State Electronics

Solid-state electronics controls current within a solid material rather than through moving mechanical contacts or electrons in a vacuum. Transistors and integrated circuits made switching smaller, cooler, more reliable, and easier to mass-produce.

The transition was not an immediate substitution. Materials theory, purification, fabrication, and circuit design had to mature before solid-state computers surpassed tube-based designs.
