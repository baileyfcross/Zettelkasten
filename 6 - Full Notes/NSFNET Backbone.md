# NSFNET Backbone

The NSFNET backbone connected regional and institutional networks through high-speed national links. It advanced from T1 service at roughly 1.5 megabits per second to T3 service at roughly 45 megabits per second.

Contractors such as MCI built and operated the links. The backbone architecture encouraged private providers to create networks that could eventually assume commercial Internet traffic.
