# Iteration Time

Iteration time is the delay between making a change and receiving useful evidence about its effect. Compilation, asset processing, approval, integration, testing, and defect correction can all lengthen this loop.

Measuring the whole delay prevents teams from optimizing one fast step while ignoring waiting elsewhere. Shorter iteration time allows more design learning within the same calendar period.
