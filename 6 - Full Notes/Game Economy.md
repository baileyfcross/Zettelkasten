# Game Economy

A game economy is a system dominated by reinforcing loops in which resources, time, or power are exchanged or converted for a perceived gain in value. Its activity comes from players choosing among immediate use, trade, production, and investment.

Sources, stocks, converters, currencies, and sinks form its flows. The system must remain dynamic while resisting [[Economic Inflation]], [[Economic Stagnation]], and uncontrolled [[Arbitrage]].
