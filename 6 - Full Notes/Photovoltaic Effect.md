# Photovoltaic Effect

The photovoltaic effect converts incident light directly into electrical output in a semiconductor junction. Absorbed [[Photon Energy]] creates electron-hole pairs; the field of a [[P-N Junction]] drives their charge carriers apart, allowing an external load to receive current. No steam, gas turbine, or rotating generator lies between sunlight and this cell-level electrical conversion, although a practical [[Solar Array]] still needs supporting equipment.
