# Genomic Data Quality Control

Genomic data quality control detects technical problems before biological interpretation. Distribution plots, sample comparisons, dimension reduction, and platform-specific diagnostics can reveal failed assays, outlying samples, inconsistent intensity ranges, or unexpected grouping.

Normalization can make samples comparable, but it should not be used to hide a failed experiment. Quality checks must distinguish plausible biological variation from artifacts introduced by preparation, batch, or measurement. A sample excluded after seeing the outcome can bias the study, so criteria and decisions should be recorded explicitly.
