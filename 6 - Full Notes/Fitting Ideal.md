# Fitting Ideal

Fitting ideals of a finitely generated module are generated by minors of a [[Presentation Matrix]]. The j-th Fitting ideal uses minors whose size is determined by the number of presentation generators and j.

Although computed from a presentation, the result is independent of the chosen presentation. Fitting ideals form an increasing chain, commute with base change and localization, and constrain the module's annihilator and support.
