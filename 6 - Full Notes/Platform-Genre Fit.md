# Platform-Genre Fit

Platform-genre fit is the relationship between a game's dominant challenges and the capabilities and use context of its target machine. A genre works well when the platform's input, display, performance, network, physical setting, and session pattern support its central actions.

Fit is not a permanent rule, but adapting a mismatch has costs. A design should explain how it replaces missing controls, preserves essential information, and suits the audience's likely environment.
