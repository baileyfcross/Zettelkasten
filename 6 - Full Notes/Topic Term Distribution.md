# Topic Term Distribution

A topic term distribution assigns a probability to every vocabulary term within a latent topic. The highest-probability terms are normally inspected together to infer a human-readable theme.

No single term defines the topic, and one word can receive probability in several topics. Names, boilerplate, or preprocessing artifacts can dominate the leading terms, so interpretation may motivate a revised [[Custom Stop List]] and a refitted model.
