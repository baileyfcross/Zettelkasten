# Trigonometric Substitution

Trigonometric substitution converts radicals built from quadratic expressions into trigonometric identities. Expressions involving $a^2-x^2$, $a^2+x^2$, or $x^2-a^2$ naturally suggest sine, tangent, or secant substitutions, respectively.

After substitution, the radical and differential are rewritten in the angular variable and the resulting trigonometric integral is evaluated. A right-triangle relation or the inverse substitution then restores the original variable, with attention to the domain and signs of square roots.
