# Streaming Network AI Response

A streaming AI response delivers generated fragments as they become available instead of waiting for the entire answer. For a long security checklist or method of procedure, this reduces perceived delay and lets an interactive client display progress immediately.

Streaming changes transport behavior, not the reliability of the content. The client must assemble fragments in order, detect interruption, and withhold the artifact from downstream automation until completion is confirmed. A partial stream can contain valid-looking opening commands while omitting later validation or rollback steps.
