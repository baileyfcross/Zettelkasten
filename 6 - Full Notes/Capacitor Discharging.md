# Capacitor Discharging

A capacitor discharging through a resistor loses charge exponentially:

$$Q=Q_0e^{-t/RC}.$$

Voltage follows the same decay, while current has the corresponding magnitude and opposite charging direction. The time constant $RC$ is the time for charge or voltage to fall to $1/e$, about 37 percent, of its initial value.
