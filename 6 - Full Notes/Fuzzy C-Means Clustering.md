# Fuzzy C-Means Clustering

Fuzzy C-means begins with a membership matrix, computes membership-weighted cluster centers, updates memberships from distances to those centers, and repeats until the matrix changes by less than a tolerance.

Every observation can belong partly to several clusters. The method extends K-means but remains sensitive to initialization, the selected number of clusters, the distance measure, and outliers.
