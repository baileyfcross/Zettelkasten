# De Moivre's Theorem

De Moivre's theorem states that $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n=\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta)$ for integer $n$. It follows directly by writing the trigonometric expression as a [[Complex Exponential]] and raising the exponential to the power.

The theorem simplifies powers of complex numbers in polar form and provides equations for their roots. It also generates multiple-angle trigonometric identities by expanding the left side and comparing real and imaginary parts.
