# Holistic Social Bot Detection

Holistic social bot detection classifies an account from a broad representation of its behavior and context. It combines many feature families to estimate whether the account as a whole resembles automation.

This view supports general-purpose systems but may hide which particular relationships caused suspicion. It also depends on labels that represent the variety of bots expected at deployment.
