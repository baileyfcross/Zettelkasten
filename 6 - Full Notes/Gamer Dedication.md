# Gamer Dedication

Gamer dedication is a scale describing how much time, knowledge, money, attention, and effort a person invests in games and game culture. It replaces a rigid core-versus-casual binary with several observable dimensions.

Dedication affects tolerance for complexity, learning, long sessions, and specialized equipment, but it does not determine genre taste by itself. A target audience should be described with the dimensions relevant to the proposed game.
