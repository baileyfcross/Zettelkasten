# Roblox Sound Object

A Roblox Sound object references an uploaded audio asset through SoundId and exposes playback properties such as volume, playback speed, looping, and time position. Scripts can play, pause, resume, or stop it and respond to playback events.

Where the Sound is parented affects how it is heard. A sound attached to a part or attachment can behave as positional audio, while a sound placed in a non-spatial container can play globally. The referenced asset remains subject to [[Roblox Asset Identifier and Moderation]], including ownership and availability rules.
