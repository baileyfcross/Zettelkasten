# Blender Scale Cage Tool

The Blender Scale Cage tool surrounds a selected element with a manipulable bounding cage. Handles on its faces and corners allow scaling from a particular side or direction, giving direct spatial control that differs from applying a uniform scale around the current [[Blender Transform Pivot Points|pivot point]].

The cage is useful when one boundary should move while another remains visually anchored, such as changing the extent of a simple architectural form. It does not replace exact numeric transforms, but it makes the intended side and direction visible. For detailed meshes, topology-aware modeling tools may provide more controlled local changes than deforming the whole selection through the cage.
