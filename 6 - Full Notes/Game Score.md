# Game Score

A game score is an evaluation of selected player actions or outcomes. It converts aspects of a performance into numbers, grades, or categories that can be compared across attempts or between players.

Scoring communicates what the designer considers significant, but it is not inherently a [[Reward]] or [[Punishment]]. Players may pursue, reinterpret, or ignore the evaluation, especially in single-player games where no shared competition gives it authority.
