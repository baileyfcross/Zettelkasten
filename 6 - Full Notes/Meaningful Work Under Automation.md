# Meaningful Work Under Automation

Meaningful work under automation concerns how the removal or transformation of tasks affects purpose, skill, social recognition, autonomy, and participation in a shared activity. Employment supplies income, but its value is not exhausted by wages.

Automation can remove dangerous or monotonous tasks and create more room for care, creativity, or leisure. It can also fragment jobs, reduce judgment, or make people supervise systems they cannot influence. A good outcome depends on how work is redesigned and how society distributes the benefits. [[Human-AI Collaboration]] should therefore be assessed by the quality of the human role, not merely by combined output.
