# Vertical Business Process

A vertical business process directly participates in the distinctive business model of an industry or organization. Its workflow helps produce the product or service by which the organization creates value.

Because a vertical process is closely tied to competitive practice and internal stakeholders, it can be harder to standardize or outsource than a [[Horizontal Business Process]]. A cloud implementation must preserve the domain-specific rules and interfaces that make it part of the business.
