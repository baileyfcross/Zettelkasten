# SLES System Registration

SLES system registration associates an installed machine with a SUSE subscription and activates the repositories appropriate to its product and extensions. Registration can occur during installation or afterward, but its success should be verified before relying on supported updates and package resolution.

The operation joins local identity, network reachability, credentials, and [[SUSE Customer Center Entitlement]]. A failure can therefore arise before package management begins. Administrators should inspect the registered product and enabled services instead of assuming that one successful command exposed every required module.
