# Unbounded Interval

An unbounded interval extends indefinitely in at least one direction on the [[Number Line]]. The inequality (x>-1) describes every real number to the right of (-1), while (x\leq2) extends without bound to the left.

An arrow shows the unbounded direction in a graph. The finite endpoint is open for a strict inequality and closed for an inclusive one. Infinity is not a real endpoint that can be included; it indicates that no finite boundary stops the interval.
