# Drone Rotation Matrices

Rotation matrices express a drone's orientation by transforming vectors between body-fixed and space-fixed coordinate frames. Successive rotations about axes are order-dependent, so roll, pitch, and yaw cannot be combined as ordinary scalar addition. Their time derivatives support calculation of angular velocity for control and state estimation.
