# FXyz JavaFX 3D Extensions

FXyz is an open-source library that builds domain-oriented components on JavaFX's foundational 3D APIs. FXyz-Core adds primitives such as prisms, pyramids, tetrahedra, tori, springs, and knots, plus utility meshes for polylines, surfaces, scatter plots, three-dimensional text, SVG extrusion, and curves. Its meshes can use colors, images, patterns, or density maps.

The project also supplies visualization environments such as sky boxes, model importers for formats including OBJ and Maya, and a client sampler for exploring components. The Deep Space Trajectory Explorer is one cited use of the library. FXyz demonstrates how [[JavaFX 3D Shapes and Meshes]], cameras, lighting, and materials can serve as a lower-level platform for specialized scientific visualization.
