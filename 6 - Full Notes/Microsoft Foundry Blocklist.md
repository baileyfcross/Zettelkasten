# Microsoft Foundry Blocklist

A Microsoft Foundry blocklist adds organization-specific terms or patterns to content controls when general safety categories do not capture a local restriction. It can help prevent disclosure of sensitive project names, prohibited phrases, or domain-specific material that the application should not accept or produce.

Blocklists are blunt instruments and require careful scoping. A term may be harmful in one context but necessary in another, and simple matches can create unintended blocks. Teams should test variants, inspect false positives, document why each entry exists, and pair blocking with a clear user response rather than allowing unexplained failures.
