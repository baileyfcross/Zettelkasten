# Programming in the Small

Programming in the small concerns a single programmer or small group constructing a relatively self-contained program. The central challenges are expressing an algorithm, choosing data structures, managing state, and finding errors in code.

Tools such as high-level languages, subroutines, compilers, libraries, and debuggers greatly improve this work. They do not automatically solve the coordination and lifecycle problems that appear when software grows into a production system.
