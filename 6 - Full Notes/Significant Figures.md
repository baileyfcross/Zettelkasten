# Significant Figures

Significant figures communicate the precision justified by a measured quantity. Leading zeros locate the decimal point and are not significant, while zeros between nonzero digits are. A calculated result should not imply more precision than its least precise input; rounding should normally be postponed until the final value so intermediate truncation does not accumulate.
