# Specific Latent Heat

Specific latent heat is the energy per unit mass required for a phase change at constant temperature. The transfer is $Q=mL$. Latent heat of fusion applies between solid and liquid, while latent heat of vaporization applies between liquid and gas. Energy changes molecular arrangement rather than average kinetic energy during the transition.
