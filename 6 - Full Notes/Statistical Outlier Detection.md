# Statistical Outlier Detection

Statistical outlier detection treats extreme observations as unlikely under an assumed or bounded distribution. Normal-theory rules, distribution-free inequalities, and covariance-aware distances provide candidate rejection regions.

The approach is vulnerable when outliers distort the estimated mean and spread. Robust estimators or iterative checking are needed when those parameters are not known independently.
