# Airfoil Geometry and Camber

An airfoil is described by its leading and trailing edges, chord line, thickness distribution, and mean camber line. A symmetric airfoil has coincident chord and camber lines, while a cambered section has different upper and lower curvature. This geometry shapes the relationship among [[Angle of Attack and Critical Stall]], lift coefficient, and pitching behavior.
