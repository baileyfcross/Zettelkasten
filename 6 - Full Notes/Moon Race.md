# Moon Race

The Moon Race was the phase of the Space Race in which the United States and Soviet Union competed through robotic lunar firsts and ultimately a crewed landing. President Kennedy selected landing a person on the Moon and returning safely because its scale offered the United States time to overcome the Soviet lead in launch capability.

The goal reorganized NASA, drove a vast booster and spacecraft program, and directed robotic missions toward landing-site knowledge as well as science. Political competition made the timetable and expense acceptable. Once Apollo succeeded and the Soviet crewed effort failed, the organizing urgency disappeared and sustained human exploration beyond low Earth orbit ended.
