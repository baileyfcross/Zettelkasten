# Probability

Probability assigns a number from zero to one to the chance of an event under a defined random process. Equally likely outcomes permit counting arguments, while a specified probability model supplies event chances when simple enumeration is not appropriate.

Simulation estimates a probability by the long-run proportion of generated trials that satisfy the event. The estimate contains Monte Carlo variation and should be interpreted together with the number of trials and the assumptions of the generator.
