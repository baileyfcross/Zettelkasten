# Carbon-Aware Workload Scaling

Carbon-aware workload scaling uses energy telemetry and workload demand to reduce computing activity that is unnecessary at a given time. Container- and process-level energy estimates can reveal where consumption occurs, while scheduling or sleep mechanisms can remove idle capacity.

An agent can analyze these signals beside cost and performance objectives, but lower energy use is still a constrained optimization problem. The system must preserve required availability and avoid shifting work in ways that merely move emissions or create a later demand spike.
