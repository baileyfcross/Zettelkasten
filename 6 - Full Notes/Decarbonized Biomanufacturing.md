# Decarbonized Biomanufacturing

Decarbonized biomanufacturing powers cell-based production with low-emission energy and reduces carbon-intensive inputs and logistics. This matters because cultured products replace biological methane or land burdens partly with facility electricity.

Environmental forecasts cannot treat today's grid as permanent; cleaner energy can improve future results, while fossil-heavy electricity can erase expected advantages.
