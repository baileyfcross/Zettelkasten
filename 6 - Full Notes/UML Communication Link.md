# UML Communication Link

A communication link in a use case diagram is the line connecting an actor to a use case. It states that the actor participates in or has access to that system capability.

The link does not by itself specify call order, data format, or implementation. Its purpose is to make the distribution of functional responsibilities and access visible at the requirements level.
