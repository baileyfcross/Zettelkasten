# Short Exact Sequence

A short exact sequence has the form 0 → A → B → C → 0. The first map is injective, its image is the kernel of the second map, and the second map is surjective.

It identifies A with a submodule of B and C with the corresponding quotient. If the sequence splits, then B is isomorphic to the direct sum A ⊕ C.
