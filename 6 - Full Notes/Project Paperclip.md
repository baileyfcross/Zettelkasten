# Project Paperclip

Project Paperclip was the U.S. program that imported German and Austrian technical specialists after World War II. It brought Wernher von Braun and other members of the V-2 organization to work with the Army, first around captured missile assembly and testing and later on American ballistic missiles and space launch vehicles.

The program accelerated U.S. capability by transferring experienced personnel along with hardware and documents. It also created a moral and political tension because expertise from a Nazi weapons and forced-labor system was incorporated into a national program while many personal histories were minimized. A parallel Soviet effort compelled German specialists to work in its missile development system.
