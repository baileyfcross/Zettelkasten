# Cloud Adoption Strategy

A cloud adoption strategy connects the organization's current computing landscape to a defined future landscape and a practical plan of action. It identifies which services should move, which should remain outside the cloud, and which dependencies must be redesigned first.

The strategy should be driven by requirements and business processes rather than by the availability of a technology. Provider selection, security, interoperability, continuity, pricing, governance, and organizational responsibility all influence the transition path.
