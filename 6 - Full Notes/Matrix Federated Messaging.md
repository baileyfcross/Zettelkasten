# Matrix Federated Messaging

Matrix is a protocol for secure, decentralized real-time communication. Its servers synchronize room state across a federated network so a room does not depend on one central point of control. The protocol supports extensible messages and optional end-to-end encryption as well as membership operations such as inviting, joining, leaving, kicking, and banning.

The source connects Matrix to instant messaging, voice over IP, video chat, and secure Internet-of-Things communication. Its room-oriented, eventually consistent state model differs from the feed and activity delivery emphasized by [[ActivityPub Federation Model]]. Both belong to [[Fediverse Protocol Diversity|the broader landscape of federated social protocols]], but applications should not treat their entities or delivery guarantees as interchangeable.
