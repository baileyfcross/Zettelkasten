# Militarization and Weaponization of Space

Militarization of space uses orbital systems to support forces and strategy through reconnaissance, communication, navigation, command, and early warning. Weaponization places systems in or against space for destructive attack. A space environment can therefore be heavily military-dependent without routine combat occurring there.

During the Cold War, both superpowers relied on satellites while largely avoiding attacks that could escalate conflict and destroy capabilities they also needed. Anti-satellite tests and ground or electronic threats weakened that restraint. Physical attack is especially dangerous because the resulting [[Space Debris]] can damage unrelated spacecraft and contribute to [[Kessler Syndrome]].
