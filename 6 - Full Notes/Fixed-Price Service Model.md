# Fixed-Price Service Model

A fixed-price service model charges a stated amount for a defined period and scope. The price can combine a one-time setup amount with monthly, quarterly, or yearly recurring charges.

Fixing the price transfers some delivery, staffing, and quality risk to the provider through the [[Service Level Agreement]]. The consumer retains scope risk because it must decide whether the agreed service is sufficient for the work actually required.
