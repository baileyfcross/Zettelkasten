# Flat-Projection Texturing

Flat-projection texturing unwraps only the surface presentation needed for a fixed camera angle. Project From View aligns visible faces to the composition, after which the artist can place them over an image or paint them directly.

The shortcut is well suited to a single still, product view, or visualization whose camera will not move. It deliberately sacrifices coverage from other angles, so it becomes fragile when the object must rotate or the shot becomes an animation.
