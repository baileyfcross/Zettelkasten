# Image Macro Meme

An image macro combines a recurring image with brief overlaid text, often arranged as setup and punchline. The visual template establishes a character, mood, or situation, while the caption supplies the instance-specific claim.

Because the format is simple and recognizable, users can produce many variations with minimal tools. LOLCats, advice animals, and other families turn captioning conventions into shared grammars. Meaning comes from the interaction between the image's established persona and the new text rather than from either component alone.
