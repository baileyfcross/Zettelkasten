# Cluster Reassignment

Cluster reassignment moves an observation from its current group to another when the alternative representative provides a better fit under the objective function. Centroids may then be updated to reflect the changed memberships.

Repeated reassignment drives iterative partition methods toward convergence. The final solution is locally stable under the permitted move, not necessarily globally optimal.
