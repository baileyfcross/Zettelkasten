# Product Increment

A product increment is the integrated, usable result added to everything completed before it. In a game Sprint, it should be playable enough to demonstrate value or reduced risk and satisfy the current [[Definition of Done]].

An increment is evidence about the product, not merely a collection of finished tasks. Its integration enables stakeholder inspection and gives the next planning decision a concrete basis.
