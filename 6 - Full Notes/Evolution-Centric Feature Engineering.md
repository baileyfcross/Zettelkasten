# Evolution-Centric Feature Engineering

Evolution-centric feature engineering maintains populations of feature constructions and improves them through operations such as mutation, recombination, and selection. Candidate fitness is based on predictive or representational utility.

Evolution explores irregular combinations that a fixed greedy order might miss. It can also consume many model evaluations, and its results depend on the representation, operators, population diversity, and fitness estimate.
