# Geocoding

Geocoding associates a place description or record with a geographic identifier. The result may be a precise latitude-and-longitude pair, a street address, a postal area, or a city name. These identifiers differ greatly in specificity: “San Francisco” describes a broad area, while a coordinate can identify a much finer position.

The process must preserve ambiguity and the [[Coordinate Reference System]] behind numeric coordinates. Repeated place names, irregular address systems, trail networks, and rural areas can prevent a text label from identifying one exact point. A geocode is therefore an interpreted location reference, not location precision guaranteed by the presence of numbers.
