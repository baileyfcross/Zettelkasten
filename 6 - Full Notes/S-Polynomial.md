# S-Polynomial

An S-polynomial combines two polynomials so that their leading terms cancel. Multipliers derived from the least common multiple of the leading monomials align those terms before subtraction.

S-polynomials expose conflicts between different possible reductions. [[Buchberger's Algorithm]] repeatedly reduces them; a generating set is a [[Gröbner Basis]] when all required S-polynomials reduce to zero.
