# Primary Decomposition of Modules

A primary decomposition writes a submodule N as a finite intersection N₁ ∩ ··· ∩ Nₛ of [[Primary Submodule]]s. Every proper submodule of a finitely generated module over a [[Noetherian Ring]] has such a decomposition.

The radicals of the component annihilator ideals identify the associated primes of M/N. Decomposition separates behavior supported at different primes while retaining their multiplicity structure.
