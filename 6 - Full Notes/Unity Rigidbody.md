# Unity Rigidbody

A Unity Rigidbody places a three-dimensional GameObject under the physics simulation. It advances velocity and acceleration on fixed physics updates, changes the object's [[Unity Transform]], and works with a [[Unity Collider]] to resolve contacts.

The component can model gravity, drag, forces, and mass. Objects that must move while colliding reliably should normally be moved through the physics model rather than by changing their transforms independently. Unity's physical units use meters, kilograms, and seconds, making consistent scene scale part of stable simulation.
