# Nuclear Fission Power Plant

A fission power plant uses reactor heat to raise steam, then passes that steam through a turbine-generator much like a [[Steam Power Plant]]. The source's comparison concerns the conversion arrangement: nuclear reaction replaces fuel combustion as the heat source, but thermal, mechanical, and electrical stages remain. This differs from the separate proposed [[Nuclear Fusion]] heat source discussed later in the book.
