# Control Remapping

Control remapping lets players assign game actions and menu operations to preferred keys, buttons, or devices. It accommodates physical needs, learned habits, device differences, and conflicts among default assignments.

A remapping screen should show current assignments, unassigned actions, and available controls together. It should warn when required actions are missing, save profiles, and offer a way to restore defaults.
