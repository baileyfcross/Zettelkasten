# Natural Logarithm

The natural logarithm is the inverse of the exponential function with base $e$. It converts multiplication into addition and exponential relationships into linear ones: if $y=e^x$, then $\ln y=x$ for positive $y$.

R's logarithm function uses the natural base by default. The domain restriction matters in data work because zero and negative inputs do not produce finite real logarithms and require a scientific explanation rather than silent removal.
