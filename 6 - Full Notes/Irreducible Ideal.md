# Irreducible Ideal

An irreducible ideal is an [[Ideal]] I that cannot be written as I = I₁ ∩ I₂ with both I₁ and I₂ strictly larger than I. Any such intersection forces one component to equal I.

In a [[Noetherian Ring]], every ideal is a finite intersection of irreducible ideals. The source uses this finiteness as a step toward [[Primary Decomposition of Ideals]].
