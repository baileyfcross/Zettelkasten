# Low Barrier to Scientific Contribution

A low barrier to scientific contribution makes the required effort, procedure, and available tasks understandable before a volunteer commits. Small modules, clear protocols, summary lists of work, and a contact who matches contributors to tasks reduce uncertainty and startup cost.

Lowering the barrier does not mean lowering quality. Documentation and templates can make standards easier to follow while freeing participants to focus on the unique scientific work. Combined with [[Crowdsourced Research Modularity]] and [[Skill-Matched Research Contribution]], this design lets a large collaboration draw useful work from people with different amounts of time and expertise.
