# Generalized Wilcoxon Survival Test

The generalized Wilcoxon survival test compares event experience across groups while weighting times by the size of the risk set. It is also known as the Gehan–Breslow test and generally gives greater influence to earlier events than the [[Log-Rank Test]].

Different weighting schemes can lead to different sensitivity when curves separate early or late. The chosen test should match the scientific comparison and be specified before inspecting which weighting gives a smaller p-value.
