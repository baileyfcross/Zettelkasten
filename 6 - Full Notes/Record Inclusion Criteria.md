# Record Inclusion Criteria

Record inclusion criteria state which observations belong to the target analytic population. They may specify demographic membership, valid exposure and outcome values, an observation window, or the availability of measurements required by a chosen model.

Applying these rules determines the population to which results can be generalized. Criteria should be decided before model fitting, expressed in executable code, and paired with counts so the effect of every filtering step remains visible.
