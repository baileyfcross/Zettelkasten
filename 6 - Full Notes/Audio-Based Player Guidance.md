# Audio-Based Player Guidance

Audio-based player guidance uses music or sound effects to influence attention and behavior. Musical style can prepare a player for stealth, investigation, or forceful action, while a localized or conditional sound can indicate that an interactable object or reward is nearby.

Audio can guide without requiring the object to be visible, making it useful outside the current camera frame. A cue should have a stable relationship to its meaning so players can learn it, and it should remain sparse enough that important signals are not masked by ambient sound.
