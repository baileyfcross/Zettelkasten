# Invertible Linear Transformation

A transformation $T:V\to W$ is invertible when a map $T^{-1}:W\to V$ satisfies $T^{-1}T=I_V$ and $TT^{-1}=I_W$. For a linear transformation, the inverse is also linear.

Invertibility requires both a trivial [[Kernel of a Linear Transformation|kernel]] and an image equal to the codomain. In finite equal dimensions either condition alone implies the other, making $T$ a [[Linear Isomorphism]].
