# Floating-Point Exponent

The floating-point exponent scales the mantissa by a power of the representation base. Its range determines the smallest and largest magnitudes the system can encode.

Exponent limits concern range rather than precision: too large a result overflows, while an extremely small result may underflow. The [[Mantissa]] determines how closely values within that range can be represented.
