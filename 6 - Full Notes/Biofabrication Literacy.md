# Biofabrication Literacy

Biofabrication literacy is the practical understanding needed to evaluate how living-cell technologies are made, what they can and cannot do, and which economic, environmental, and ethical choices accompany them. It is relevant to citizens and consumers as well as specialists.

Shared vocabulary enables meaningful public participation, adaptive education, and governance before products and labor markets have already changed.
