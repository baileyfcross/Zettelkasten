# Bisection Method

The bisection method finds a root of a continuous function on an interval whose endpoints have opposite signs. It repeatedly halves the interval and keeps the half that still brackets a sign change.

The method converges reliably under its assumptions and has a predictable error bound based on interval width. Its convergence is slower than derivative-based methods but does not require a derivative.
