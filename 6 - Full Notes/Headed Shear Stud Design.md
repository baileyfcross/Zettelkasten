# Headed Shear Stud Design

Headed shear studs are welded to a beam or girder flange to transfer horizontal shear between structural steel and a concrete slab. Their total strength determines how much slab compression can participate in [[Composite Beam Action]]. Stud resistance depends on connector size, steel strength, concrete properties, deck rib orientation and geometry, and whether the stud is placed in a favorable rib position.

Design also imposes minimum length, spacing, edge cover, and flange-thickness constraints so studs can be installed and develop reliable behavior. Studs are distributed along the span in relation to the change in longitudinal force, with practical placement coordinated to deck ribs. Partial composite construction intentionally supplies fewer connectors than full action requires when the reduced strength still satisfies demand more economically.
