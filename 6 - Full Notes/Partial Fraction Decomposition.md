# Partial Fraction Decomposition

Partial fraction decomposition rewrites a [[Proper Rational Function]] as a sum of simpler rational terms determined by the denominator's linear and irreducible quadratic factors. Repeated factors require a term for each power. Multiplying by the common denominator and matching coefficients determines the unknown numerators, preparing the expression for later integration and other operations.
