# Subspace Density Clustering

Subspace density clustering searches for dense connected regions within selected subsets of dimensions. It addresses data in which a group is coherent only when irrelevant attributes are ignored.

The method must explore both spatial connectivity and a combinatorial feature space. Density monotonicity and pruning can reduce that search, but reported clusters may overlap in objects or dimensions and require redundancy control.
