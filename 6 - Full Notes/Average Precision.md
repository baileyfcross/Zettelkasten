# Average Precision

Average precision evaluates a ranking with binary relevance by averaging precision at the positions where relevant items occur. A relevant item contributes more when it appears before many irrelevant items.

The metric combines retrieval quality across the list rather than judging a single cutoff. It is useful when items can be labeled relevant or not but cannot be assigned reliable graded scores.
