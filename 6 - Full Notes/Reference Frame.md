# Reference Frame

A reference frame establishes what positions, orientations, and movements are measured relative to.

An egocentric frame is relative to the user's body, such as the eyes, head, torso, hands, or legs, and corresponds to a first-person view. An exocentric frame is relative to an object or the world and corresponds to an outside or third-person view.

In a [[3D User Interface]], changing between egocentric and exocentric views can help different tasks but may also confuse the user. [[Navigation]], [[System Control]], and [[3D Manipulation]] should use a frame that makes their mappings easy to predict.
