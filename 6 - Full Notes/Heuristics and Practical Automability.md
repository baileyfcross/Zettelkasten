# Heuristics and Practical Automability

A heuristic can make an intractable task practically automatable by finding a good answer quickly without guaranteeing the optimum. The value of the approximation depends on the domain’s tolerance for error and the quality of the heuristic.

This tradeoff changes the question from whether an exact solution exists to whether a useful result can be produced within resource limits. Skilled designers make the approximation and its uncertainty explicit.
