# Direct Product of Modules

The direct product ∏Mᵢ consists of all tuples with an entry in each module, without requiring finite support. Its module operations are performed componentwise.

For a finite family it equals the [[Direct Sum of Modules]]. For an infinite family the product is generally larger because it permits infinitely many nonzero coordinates.
