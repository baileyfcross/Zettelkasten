# Universality Loss under Quantum Gate Symmetrization

A symmetrized gateset can fail to generate every unitary that respects the symmetry even when none of its generators vanishes. Projecting the customary one- and two-qubit gates may leave only a proper subset of the symmetry-preserving transformation group.

The failure arises because universality is a property of the Lie closure of the surviving generators, not a property guaranteed by projecting a universal unconstrained set. In fermionic quantum-chemistry encodings, for example, higher-locality operations can be necessary to span all transformations compatible with the conserved quantities.

A useful validation therefore checks both commutation and reachability. [[Equivariant Quantum Gateset]] establishes the first condition; the generated algebra must be analyzed separately to establish the second.
