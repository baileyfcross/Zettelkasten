# Self-Inductance

Self-inductance measures a circuit's tendency to oppose changes in its own current. The induced back emf is $\mathcal{E}=-L\,dI/dt$, and the unit of $L$ is the henry. An inductor stores magnetic energy $U=\tfrac12LI^2$, so abrupt current change requires a correspondingly large voltage.
