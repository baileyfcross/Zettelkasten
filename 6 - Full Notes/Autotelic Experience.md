# Autotelic Experience

An autotelic experience is pursued because the activity and its goals are rewarding in themselves. The motivation is intrinsic to taking part rather than supplied only by an external necessity or prize.

[[Flow]] often makes an activity autotelic by aligning challenge, skill, goals, and feedback. Games also support autotelic play when players create personal [[Player Goal|goals]] that remain meaningful within the available systems.
