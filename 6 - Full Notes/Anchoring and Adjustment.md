# Anchoring and Adjustment

Anchoring and adjustment is the tendency to begin from an initially presented value and revise away from it too little. Even a number unrelated or implausible to the decision can pull later estimates upward or downward.

Ordering prices can exploit this effect: showing an expensive virtual-currency bundle first makes later bundles feel cheaper by comparison. Because the anchor changes perceived value without changing the goods, its deliberate use can be manipulative.
