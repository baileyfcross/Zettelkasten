# Formative Evaluation

Formative evaluation is performed during evolving stages of design to identify problems and improve the design. Representative users perform task-based scenarios while evaluators observe interaction and collect evidence.

It may be informal and qualitative, recording critical incidents, comments, and reactions, or more formal, adding measurements such as task time and errors. Findings feed directly into redesign and another evaluation cycle.

Formative evaluation helps form a [[Prototype]] into a more usable system. It differs from [[Summative Evaluation]], which usually assesses or compares a design after it is substantially complete.
