# Amazon S3 Bucket Policy

An Amazon S3 bucket policy is a resource-based JSON policy that permits or denies actions on a bucket and its objects for named principals and conditions. It can grant cross-account access, restrict transport, or support controlled public delivery.

The resource ARNs must distinguish the bucket from the objects inside it. Public access settings and other policy layers still participate in evaluation, so a policy that appears to allow access may remain blocked or be overridden by an explicit deny.
