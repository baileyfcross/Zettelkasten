# Static, Dynamic, and Stagnation Pressure

Static pressure is the local thermodynamic pressure of the air. Stagnation, or total, pressure is the pressure reached when a moving flow is brought to rest, and dynamic pressure is represented by the difference between total and static pressure. A pitot-static system uses that difference to infer [[Indicated, Calibrated, Equivalent, and True Airspeed]].
