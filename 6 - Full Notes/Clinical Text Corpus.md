# Clinical Text Corpus

A clinical text corpus is a collection of medical narratives prepared for language research, often with annotations for concepts, relations, or contextual attributes. It provides examples for training and a reference standard for evaluation.

Corpus construction is constrained by confidentiality and annotation cost. Agreements among annotators reveal whether the task itself is well defined, while the document mix determines what a model can generalize to. A high score on one specialty's carefully curated corpus does not establish performance on differently formatted records from another institution.
