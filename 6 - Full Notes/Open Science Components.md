# Open Science Components

Open science combines open data, open-source software, open access, and open standards. Data permit verification and new analysis; source code exposes computational methods; access makes the scholarly argument available; and standards make these objects easier to exchange and combine.

The components reinforce one another but are not interchangeable. A readable paper without its data and code may remain computationally unverifiable, while downloadable files in incompatible formats may be technically open but costly to reuse. [[Research Licensing and Reuse]] supplies the legal permissions that allow access to become practical reuse.
