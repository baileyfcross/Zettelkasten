# Spatial Tooltip

A spatial tooltip displays a short attribute value or label when the pointer reaches a map feature. It provides detail on demand without permanently covering nearby points or regions. Because tooltips disappear, they are poorly suited to comparing many locations or communicating essential categories. Selection tolerance, touch support, and unambiguous feature highlighting help users understand which geometry supplied the displayed information.
