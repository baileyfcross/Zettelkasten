# Cultural Mechanics

Cultural mechanics are rule-related practices and consequences that move beyond a bounded play session. Player-created mods can alter a game's inscribed rules, while emergent strategies can change how the public understands the game or the behavior it appears to reward.

This category exposes a designer's responsibility for systemic implications. If a reward structure predictably encourages undesirable conduct, the resulting behavior is not detached from the original mechanic merely because players discovered it. Cultural reception can reveal what the formal system communicates when optimized and discussed at scale.
