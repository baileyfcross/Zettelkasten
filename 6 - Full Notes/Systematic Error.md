# Systematic Error

Systematic error biases measurements consistently because of calibration, procedure, or an unrecognized environmental effect. Repeating the same method does not remove it and may produce a tightly clustered but inaccurate result. Detection requires independent calibration, altered methods, or comparison with an external standard rather than averaging alone.
