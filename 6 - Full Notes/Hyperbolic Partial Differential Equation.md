# Hyperbolic Partial Differential Equation

A hyperbolic partial differential equation describes disturbances that propagate with finite characteristic speeds. The wave equation is the central example, with second derivatives in time and space carrying opposite signs in its standard form.

Solutions require appropriate initial displacement and velocity data along with boundary conditions. Characteristic curves or surfaces trace the paths along which information travels, defining which earlier data can influence a later event.
