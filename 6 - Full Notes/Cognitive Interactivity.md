# Cognitive Interactivity

Cognitive interactivity consists of deliberate reasoning about tactics, strategy, planning, prediction, learning, and mastery. Short-term cognitive loops guide immediate choices, while long-term loops organize plans and progression.

It depends on understandable goals, sufficiently predictable consequences, and [[Systemic Depth]]. If the game supplies neither useful uncertainty nor a coherent model to learn, the player has little reason to think through its decisions.
