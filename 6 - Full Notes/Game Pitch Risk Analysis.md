# Game Pitch Risk Analysis

Game pitch risk analysis identifies the assumptions that could prevent a proposed experience from being built or succeeding. Scope, team size, schedule, technology, market, production cost, and dependence on unproven interactions should be addressed alongside the creative opportunity.

A pitch should explain what the player does, why that activity is compelling, what distinguishes the proposal, and how obvious risks will be tested or reduced. Ignoring risk makes exciting possibilities less credible rather than more persuasive.
