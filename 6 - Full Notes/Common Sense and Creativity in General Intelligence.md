# Common Sense and Creativity in General Intelligence

General intelligence requires both common sense and creativity. Common sense supplies an everyday model of physical and social consequences, while creativity proposes actions, explanations, or designs that are not already explicit in that model.

The capabilities complement one another. Creativity without common sense produces possibilities with no regard for feasibility or relevance; common sense without creativity can evaluate familiar options but cannot generate a useful response when a situation is novel. A serious [[Artificial General Intelligence]] therefore needs mechanisms for both constrained prediction and open-ended search.
