# Welch t-Test

The Welch t-test compares two independent means without assuming equal population variances. It adjusts the standard error and uses Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, which may be non-integer.

Many statistical systems use this test as the default two-sample t-test. When the equal- and unequal-variance analyses lead to the same substantive conclusion, the choice may not change the inference, but the reported method should remain explicit.
