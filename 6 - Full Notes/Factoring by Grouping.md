# Factoring by Grouping

Factoring by grouping partitions a polynomial into smaller groups, factors each group, and looks for a repeated expression. For example,

$$ax^2+a+bx^2+b=a(x^2+1)+b(x^2+1)=(a+b)(x^2+1).$$

The first grouping may require terms to be rearranged through the [[Commutative Property]]. Success depends on producing the same factor from each group. Once that repeated expression appears, the [[Distributive Property]] is used backward again to complete the factorization.
