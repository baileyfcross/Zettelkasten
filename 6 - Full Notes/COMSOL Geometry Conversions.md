# COMSOL Geometry Conversions

COMSOL geometry conversions change how existing entities are represented or grouped. Operations can create composite curves, surfaces, or domains; split an object into lower-level entities; retain input objects during conversion; or translate geometry from a CAD kernel into the COMSOL kernel.

Conversion changes topology even when the visible boundary appears unchanged. Entity selections and downstream physics must be reviewed because identifiers and domain relationships may be rebuilt.
