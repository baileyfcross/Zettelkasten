# Homomorphic Encryption Bootstrapping

Homomorphic encryption bootstrapping refreshes a ciphertext whose accumulated noise is approaching the decryption limit. By homomorphically evaluating a decryption-related procedure, the system produces an encryption of the same plaintext with a renewed capacity for further operations.

This refresh turns a bounded-depth construction into a path toward [[Fully Homomorphic Encryption]], but it is computationally expensive. The source describes efficient bootstrapping as a central area of improvement because reducing its time and resource cost makes long encrypted computations more practical while controlling [[Ciphertext Noise in Homomorphic Encryption]].
