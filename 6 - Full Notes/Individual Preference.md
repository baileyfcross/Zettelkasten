# Individual Preference

Individual preference describes how well an item's topics or semantics match a user's interests. It can be estimated by comparing the item's topic distribution with the distributions of items the user previously adopted.

Preference is one contextual factor in [[Social Recommendation]]. A user may still adopt a weakly preferred item because influential friends sent it, or reject a preferred item when it lacks visibility or trusted endorsement.
