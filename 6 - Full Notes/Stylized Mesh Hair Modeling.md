# Stylized Mesh Hair Modeling

Stylized mesh hair modeling represents locks as deliberately shaped polygon forms instead of simulating individual strands. A scalp-derived shell establishes the fit, and extruded tapered locks create the main rhythm of the hairstyle with predictable geometry and rendering cost.

Natural variation comes from changing heights, directions, overlap, and spacing, then duplicating selected locks to cover gaps. Geometry permanently hidden by a cap or another accessory can be omitted, because unseen detail adds production work without contributing to the shot.
