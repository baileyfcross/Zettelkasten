# Invariant Subspace

A subspace $W$ is invariant under a linear operator $T$ when $T(W)\subseteq W$. The restriction of $T$ to $W$ is then itself a linear operator, so the original problem can be studied on a smaller space.

Eigenspaces and [[Generalized Eigenspace|generalized eigenspaces]] are invariant. A basis adapted to an invariant subspace gives the operator matrix a block upper-triangular form; a direct-sum decomposition into invariant subspaces gives block diagonal form.
