# Molten Rock Solidification Model

The molten-rock case represents hot material flowing through cooler solid rock and losing energy to the surrounding environment. Temperature-dependent basalt properties and a transition interval describe the change between molten and solid states rather than treating melting as an abrupt single temperature.

An axisymmetric solid-fluid model combines inlet and outlet flow, conductive exchange with rock, convection and radiation to ambient, and local mesh refinement. Liquid-phase fraction and heat-flux plots show where cooling leaves a remaining molten region.
