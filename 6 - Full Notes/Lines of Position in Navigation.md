# Lines of Position in Navigation

A line of position is the set of possible locations consistent with a navigation observation. Depending on the measurement, it may be represented by a straight line, circle, arc, or hyperbola. Intersecting independent lines reduces ambiguity, while a single line often requires the supporting estimates used in [[Pilotage and Dead Reckoning]].
