# Polar Form of a Complex Number

The polar form of a nonzero complex number is $z=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$, where $r=|z|$ and $\theta$ is an argument giving its direction.

This form turns multiplication into multiplication of moduli and addition of arguments. Powers and roots therefore become simpler than in rectangular form.
