# Systemic Model

A systemic model represents parts, their internal state, and the interactions that form feedback loops. It exposes how aggregate behavior is produced rather than describing only an input-output equation.

Like a mathematical model, it is an abstraction rather than a complete copy of reality. Its value lies in choosing a [[System Boundary]] and level of detail that reveal the dynamics relevant to the design question.
