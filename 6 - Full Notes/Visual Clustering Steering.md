# Visual Clustering Steering

Visual clustering steering presents projections, cluster summaries, and uncertain boundaries through which an analyst can guide the search. Actions such as selecting a region, merging groups, or adjusting emphasis are translated into model updates.

The visualization must preserve enough structure for decisions to be informed rather than decorative. Projection distortion can make separated points look close or conceal overlap, so coordinated quantitative diagnostics remain important.
