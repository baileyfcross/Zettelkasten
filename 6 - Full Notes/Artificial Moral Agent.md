# Artificial Moral Agent

An artificial moral agent is a machine designed to select actions using moral rules, values, or learned evaluations. The design ambition ranges from systems that obey safety constraints to machines expected to resolve conflicts among values.

The central difficulty is that human ethics is not always a complete set of explicit rules. Moral judgment is also embodied, socially embedded, and sensitive to context. An artificial system may therefore display [[Functional Morality]] without possessing full [[Machine Moral Agency]]. Keeping that distinction clear prevents engineered compliance from being mistaken for moral understanding or responsibility.
