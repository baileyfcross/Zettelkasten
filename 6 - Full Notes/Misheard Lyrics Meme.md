# Misheard Lyrics Meme

A misheard lyrics meme pairs a song with alternative words that resemble the original sounds. Subtitles or images make the reinterpretation visible, converting an accidental or playful hearing into a repeatable audiovisual joke.

The genre depends on tension between phonetic similarity and semantic absurdity. Creators preserve the timing and sound of the source while replacing its apparent meaning, so the audience experiences both recognition and surprise. Language differences can intensify the effect when unfamiliar lyrics are heard as phrases in another language.
