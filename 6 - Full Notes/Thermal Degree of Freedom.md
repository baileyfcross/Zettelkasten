# Thermal Degree of Freedom

A thermal degree of freedom is the nodal temperature used as the primary unknown in a heat-conduction finite element model. A two-node one-dimensional thermal element carries one temperature value at each endpoint.

Prescribed temperatures constrain selected degrees of freedom. Solved nodal temperatures are interpolated inside elements and differentiated to obtain temperature gradients and heat fluxes.
