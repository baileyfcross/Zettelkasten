# Electric Potential

Electric potential is electric potential energy per unit positive charge. Potential difference is the work done per unit charge in moving between two points, $V=\Delta U/q$. It is a scalar, so potentials from several source charges add algebraically. The electric field points in the direction of decreasing potential.
