# User Story

A user story describes desired value from a user's point of view, commonly as “As a role, I want a goal, so that a reason.” It is a concise placeholder for a conversation, not a complete specification.

The form keeps attention on who benefits and why. [[Acceptance Criteria]] add testable detail when the story approaches implementation, while the Development Team retains freedom to discover the solution.
