# Heat Conduction

Heat conduction is energy transfer through a material without net motion of its mass. The heat-flow rate is governed by thermal conductivity, cross-sectional area, and the spatial temperature gradient.

Finite element conduction matrices convert this continuum relationship into nodal temperature and heat-flow equations. Material interfaces are handled by assembling elements with their own conductivities and dimensions.
