# AI Existential Risk

AI existential risk is the possibility that artificial intelligence could cause human extinction or permanently destroy humanity's future. A common scenario combines [[Superintelligence]] with an objective that is only partly aligned with human values, allowing the system to transform the world while competently pursuing the wrong goal.

The seriousness of the possible outcome does not establish how probable the scenario is. Ethical priority setting must compare speculative long-term danger with documented harms from current AI, including surveillance, discrimination, unsafe infrastructure, and environmental cost. Treating one horizon as the only real AI risk produces an incomplete policy agenda.

The proposed mechanism combines a general optimizer, a misspecified objective, and enough capability to acquire resources or resist correction. The [[Paperclip Maximizer]] illustrates how competence can turn a harmless-looking goal into a threat without hatred or conscious intent. Even an uncertain probability warrants investigation when failure would permanently eliminate humanity's future options.
