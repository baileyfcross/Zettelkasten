# Biological Autonomy

Biological autonomy is a system's ability to maintain and organize its own continued operation rather than relying on an external operator for every essential condition. Metabolism alone is insufficient if an incubator, media supply, and fabricated scaffold perform the broader self-maintenance.

Autonomy exists by degree, making it useful for distinguishing living cells from an independently living biofabricated organism.
