# Rage Comic

A rage comic is a short, user-assembled comic built from recurring crudely drawn faces that represent recognizable emotions and reactions. Panels turn an everyday frustration, triumph, or social encounter into a compact sequence with a conventional emotional payoff.

The faces form a shared visual vocabulary, allowing creators with limited drawing skill to narrate personal experiences. Variation occurs in the situation and arrangement, while the recurring expressions provide continuity and subcultural recognition. The genre combines autobiographical storytelling with a reusable symbolic system.
