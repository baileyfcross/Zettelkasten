# AI Self-Regulation

AI self-regulation occurs when companies, researchers, or professional organizations create and enforce their own principles and practices without relying primarily on new government rules. It can move quickly, use specialized knowledge, and influence design before formal law catches up.

Its weakness is the conflict between ethical restraint and competitive incentives. Voluntary principles may lack independent verification, remedies, or penalties, and firms with concentrated digital power may shape the rules in their own interest. Self-regulation can contribute to governance, but high-impact uses also require external boundaries and public accountability.
