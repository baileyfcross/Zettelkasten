# Kasiski Examination

The Kasiski examination estimates the length of a repeating key in a [[Vigenere Cipher]]. Repeated plaintext fragments encrypted at equivalent key positions can produce repeated ciphertext fragments, and the distances between those repetitions contain evidence about the key period.

After a likely period (n) is found, the analyst groups every (n)th ciphertext character. Each group was transformed by one consistent shift, reducing the polyalphabetic problem to several Caesar-cipher analyses. The method exploits repetition in key use rather than attempting every full keyword directly.
