# Tacton

A tacton is a tactile icon: a patterned stimulus assigned to a word, status, or concept. It can combine body location, rhythm, duration, frequency, intensity, and perceived roughness so the message is recognized as a unit rather than decoded letter by letter.

Good tactons are easy to distinguish, learn, and remember. Location and rhythm are often more usable than fine intensity differences, and several independent dimensions make patterns more separable. Practical vocabularies remain modest because identification capacity is limited; visual or auditory context can reduce ambiguity when many concepts must be represented.
