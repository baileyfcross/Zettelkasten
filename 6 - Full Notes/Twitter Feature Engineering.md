# Twitter Feature Engineering

Twitter feature engineering converts posts, accounts, media, metadata, and network relations into variables for social-media analysis. The platform's structured records combine short text with timestamps, locations, user profiles, and interaction fields.

Different applications require different evidence. Profile classification may emphasize users, crisis response may emphasize intent and location, and social analysis may join thematic, spatial, and temporal features.
