# Tangent Line Approximation

Near a differentiable point $x=a$, a function is approximated by its tangent line

$$f(x)\approx f(a)+f'(a)(x-a).$$

The derivative supplies the slope of the best local linear approximation. The error shrinks faster than the horizontal displacement as $x$ approaches $a$, distinguishing tangency from merely passing through the same point.
