# Heated Pipe and Nozzle Flow Models

The pipe exercises compare a constant-wall-temperature approximation with conjugate heat transfer for water, air, and gas-pipeline conditions. Symmetry reduces the geometry, while temperature-dependent density and viscosity determine whether a selected inlet velocity remains laminar.

The converging-diverging nozzle extends the workflow to pressure-driven acceleration and possible choking. Cross-sectional area, inlet and outlet pressure, and gas properties couple velocity, pressure, and temperature, illustrating why [[Coupled Pressure Velocity and Temperature Fields|field coupling]] must match the intended flow regime.
