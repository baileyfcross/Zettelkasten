# Projected Clustering

Projected clustering assigns each cluster its own subset of relevant dimensions. Objects are partitioned while the algorithm estimates which coordinates make each group compact relative to the rest of the data.

Allowing cluster-specific subspaces captures structures hidden in the full space and permits different explanations for different groups. The objective must prevent tiny dimension sets from making every cluster appear artificially tight.
