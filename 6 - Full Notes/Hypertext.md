# Hypertext

Hypertext is text containing references that connect one part of a document to another document or location. These references are [[Hyperlink|hyperlinks]] that a reader can follow while navigating related information.

On the web, linked documents collectively form a [[Web Graph]]. The link structure is not merely navigational; [[PageRank]] interprets it as evidence about which pages other authors considered important enough to reference.
