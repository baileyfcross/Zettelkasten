# Downward Causality

Downward causality is the influence of an emergent whole on the parts that produce it. Once a higher-level pattern exists, it changes the environment, constraints, or opportunities within which lower-level parts act.

Together with [[Upward Causality]], it makes causation in a [[System Hierarchy]] reciprocal across levels. A game system can emerge from many local actions while its resulting state then reshapes each player's next decision.
