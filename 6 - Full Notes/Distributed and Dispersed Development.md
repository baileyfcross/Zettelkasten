# Distributed and Dispersed Development

Distributed development places whole teams at different locations, while a dispersed team separates members of the same team across locations. Both add communication delay, but dispersed membership disrupts the dense daily collaboration inside a team more directly.

Keeping teams locally cohesive where possible, overlapping working hours, making work visible, and investing in rich communication reduce—but do not eliminate—the distance cost.
