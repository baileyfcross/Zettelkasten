# Cyberattack Attribution

Cyberattack attribution is the process of determining who is responsible for an attack. Technical traces can reveal infrastructure, tools, timing, or methods, but attackers can reuse code, route activity through other systems, and deliberately plant misleading evidence.

Attribution therefore combines technical evidence with context and intelligence about a possible [[Threat Actor]]. The source cautions that identifying an apparent origin is not the same as establishing responsibility with certainty.
