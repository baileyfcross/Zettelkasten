# Manhattan Distance

Manhattan distance sums the absolute differences between corresponding coordinates. It models movement along coordinate axes and is also called city-block distance.

Compared with squared Euclidean geometry, absolute differences reduce the special emphasis placed on large single-coordinate deviations. The resulting neighborhoods and therefore the clusters can differ even when the same attributes are used.
