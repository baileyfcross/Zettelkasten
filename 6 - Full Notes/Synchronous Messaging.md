# Synchronous Messaging

Synchronous messaging makes a sender wait for reception or processing before it proceeds with dependent work. The communication therefore blocks one participant until the required interaction completes.

This provides a simple visible sequence but couples availability and response time: a slow or unavailable receiver delays the sender. [[Asynchronous Messaging]] removes that immediate wait by letting a queue hold the message for later retrieval.
