# Grover Amplitude Amplification

Grover search alternates a [[Grover Phase Oracle]] with the [[Grover Diffusion Operator]] starting from an equal superposition. Each pair of reflections transfers probability amplitude from unmarked states toward the marked state.

After on the order of $\sqrt N$ oracle uses for one marked item among $N$, measurement returns the target with high probability, with exact variants available under appropriate knowledge of the search space. The speedup is in oracle-query count; constructing the oracle and preparing the search space remain application costs.
