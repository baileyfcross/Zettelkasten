# Information Storage and Distribution

Information storage and distribution covers retaining data and making it available across systems, organizations, or jurisdictions. Storage affects duration, security, and later reuse, while distribution changes who can interpret or act on the information.

Treating this as a separate activity exposes risks that are hidden when collection and analysis receive all the attention. Copies, remote services, and cross-border transfers can place one dataset under several technical and legal regimes.
