# Local Extrema Annotation

A local extrema annotation identifies peaks and troughs in a [[Time Series]] and marks them on its display. These points can direct attention to unusually high or low observations without requiring the reader to scan every value. Because local extrema depend on their neighborhood and smoothing choices, the detection rule should be consistent. Labels should be sparse enough to clarify the pattern rather than cover the line.
