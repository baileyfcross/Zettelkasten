# N-Effect in Competition

The N-effect is the tendency for competitive motivation to decrease as the perceived number of competitors increases. A person can feel less able to win against a large field even when the mathematical chance of receiving a prize is unchanged.

Games can reduce this intimidation by comparing players with a small peer group, a nearby percentile, or opponents of similar power instead of only displaying a global leaderboard. Competition is not a universal motivator, so its scale and presentation should be tested with the intended audience.
