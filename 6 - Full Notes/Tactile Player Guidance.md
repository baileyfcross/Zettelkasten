# Tactile Player Guidance

Tactile player guidance uses the form or feedback of a physical interface to suggest meaning and action. A specialized controller establishes expectations through its shape, while vibration can communicate boundaries, surface changes, danger, or proximity without occupying the visual display.

The cue is strongest when its physical character matches the modeled event. Irregular vibration when a vehicle leaves the track can communicate loss of traction directly through the hands. Because tactile signals share a channel with control, their intensity and timing must remain distinguishable from decorative feedback.
