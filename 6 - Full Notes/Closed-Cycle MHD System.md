# Closed-Cycle MHD System

A closed-cycle MHD system reheats and recirculates its working fluid instead of exhausting combustion gas after one duct passage. The source describes argon or helium heated through a heat exchanger, then seeded to conduct or mixed with conductive liquid metal. After electrical extraction, the fluid is slowed, cooled, compressed, and reheated. This separates combustion products from the generator flow but adds loop equipment.
