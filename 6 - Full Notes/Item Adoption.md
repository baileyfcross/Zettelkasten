# Item Adoption

Item adoption occurs when a user accepts or acts on content received through a social network, such as reading, sharing, retweeting, or applying a label. Adoption can be represented as a positive user-item link, with rejection represented separately when observed.

Its probability can depend on item semantics, the receiver's history, the sender, and the social tie. Adoption therefore serves as both a behavior to explain and a signal for later recommendations.
