# Simulation

A simulation executes a [[Computational Model]] to track how a represented system changes over time or across states. Variables, equations, parameters, and transition rules determine the behavior that can emerge.

Simulation makes complex models explorable, but it produces evidence about the modeled system rather than direct observation of reality. Credibility requires verification of the implementation and validation against independent knowledge of the phenomenon.
