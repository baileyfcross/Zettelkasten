# Angle Addition Identities

The angle-addition identities express a combined rotation through separate angles:

$$\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta,$$

$$\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta.$$

Changing the sign of $\beta$ gives the subtraction identities. These formulas generate [[Double-Angle Identities]] and many exact trigonometric values.
