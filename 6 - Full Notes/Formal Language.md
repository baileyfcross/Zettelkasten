# Formal Language

A formal language over an [[Alphabet]] Σ is any subset of Σ*, the set of all finite strings over that alphabet. A language may be finite or infinite and need not resemble a human language.

Because languages are sets, union, intersection, and complement apply to them. Operations derived from strings, including concatenation and [[Kleene Star]], give formal language theory additional ways to construct languages and compare their expressive complexity.
