# Cycle Time

Cycle time is the elapsed time from when work actually begins on an item until that item is completed. It includes active work and waiting that occurs after the start.

Tracking cycle time reveals how quickly the system finishes what it starts. Smaller batches, fewer handoffs, and lower work in progress can reduce it by limiting queues and feedback delay.
