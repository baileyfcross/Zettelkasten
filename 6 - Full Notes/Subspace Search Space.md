# Subspace Search Space

With d attributes, the number of nonempty axis-parallel subspaces is exponential in d. If orientations are also allowed, candidate projections form a continuous and even larger search space.

Pruning, randomized search, monotonicity, local modeling, and integrated partitioning make the problem tractable. These approximations trade exhaustive coverage for speed, so evaluation should report stability and redundant or missed views.
