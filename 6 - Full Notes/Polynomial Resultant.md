# Polynomial Resultant

The resultant of two positive-degree polynomials is the determinant of their [[Sylvester Matrix]]. It is a polynomial expression in their coefficients and vanishes exactly when the polynomials have a common factor.

The resultant can also be expressed as Af + Bg for suitable polynomials A and B. It eliminates one variable and is therefore useful in implicitization and solving polynomial systems.
