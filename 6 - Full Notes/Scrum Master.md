# Scrum Master

A Scrum Master helps a team use and improve Scrum. The role coaches self-management, facilitates events, exposes impediments, protects the team from disruptive interference, and helps the wider organization create conditions for effective work.

The Scrum Master does not assign the team's tasks or act as its status collector. Authority comes from service, facilitation, and improving the system in which the team operates.
