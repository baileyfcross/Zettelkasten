# Climb Performance Modeling

Climb charts relate pressure altitude and temperature to climb speed, rate, time, distance, and fuel required. For the Cessna 172M example, rate and climb speed decrease as pressure altitude rises, while time, distance, and fuel to climb increase. Weight and starting altitude remain essential assumptions when applying those relationships in [[Climb, Cruise, and Leg Fuel Planning]].
