# Electrical Resistance

Electrical resistance is the ratio $R=V/I$ for a component at a specified operating condition. An ohmic conductor has a linear current-voltage relation with constant resistance. For a uniform wire, $R=\rho L/A$, linking resistance to material resistivity, length, and cross-sectional area. Temperature can change resistivity and invalidate a constant-$R$ approximation.
