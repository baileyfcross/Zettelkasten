# Crout Decomposition

Crout decomposition is an [[LU Decomposition]] convention in which the diagonal entries of $U$ are all one. Entries of $L$ and $U$ are computed column by column so their product reproduces $A$.

Like other LU forms, it separates the reusable factorization from the two inexpensive triangular solves required for each right-hand side.
