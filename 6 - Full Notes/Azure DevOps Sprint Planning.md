# Azure DevOps Sprint Planning

Azure DevOps sprint planning assigns selected backlog items and their tasks to an iteration. The backlog, capacity, and planning views help the team balance desired value with the work it can plausibly complete during the sprint.

Dragging an item into an iteration changes a plan, not physical progress. Estimates and assignments must remain open to evidence discovered while the work is being performed.
