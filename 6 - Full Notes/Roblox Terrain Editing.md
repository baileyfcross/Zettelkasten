# Roblox Terrain Editing

Roblox terrain is voxel-based world geometry edited with tools for generating, adding, subtracting, growing, eroding, smoothing, painting, and manipulating regions. These operations create large natural surfaces without assembling the landscape from thousands of ordinary parts.

Generation provides a broad starting form, while brush and region tools support local revision. Material painting distinguishes rock, grass, sand, water, and other surfaces. For externally designed landscapes, [[Roblox Height and Color Map Terrain]] can transfer elevation and material information from images before the terrain is refined in Studio.
