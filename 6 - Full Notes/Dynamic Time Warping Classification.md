# Dynamic Time Warping Classification

Dynamic time warping classification compares two sequences after finding a low-cost alignment that may stretch or compress their time axes. Dynamic programming accumulates local matching costs subject to monotone path constraints.

The resulting distance lets similar shapes match despite different speeds. Broad warping windows increase flexibility and computation but can align unrelated events, so constraints often limit how far the path may depart from the diagonal.
