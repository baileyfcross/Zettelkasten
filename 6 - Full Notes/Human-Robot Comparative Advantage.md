# Human-Robot Comparative Advantage

Human-robot comparative advantage assigns parts of a shared task to the partner that performs them better. Robots offer repetition, strength, precision, tirelessness, and consistent execution; people contribute flexible perception, dexterity, context, improvisation, and social judgment.

The division is not fixed. Better sensors or software can move a task toward the machine, while unexpected environments can restore the need for human intervention. Good design preserves meaningful human authority and makes handoffs clear. This task-level view supports [[Human-Robot Centaur Team]] and avoids treating collaboration as a temporary stage before total replacement.
