# Programming as Art and Science

Programming became a central academic concern as higher-level languages, operating systems, and methods matured. It combined creative judgment about representations with formal efforts to prove correctness and analyze computational cost.

Calling programming both art and science recognizes that formal rules do not determine every good design choice. Clear notation, decomposition, testing, and experience are needed to make an algorithm understandable and dependable.
