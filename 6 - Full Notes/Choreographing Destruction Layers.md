# Choreographing Destruction Layers

A convincing digital explosion is a timed relationship among fractured structural pieces, fire and smoke, expelled interior debris, and surviving damaged material. The book compares this to a practical set explosion whose different charges are fired in sequence. Showing a whole model until destruction, then swapping to fractured geometry, prevents premature gaps. Fire footage on depth-staged cards can sit behind, between, and in front of fragments; particle-driven debris suggests contents that were inside the destroyed object. See [[Rigid Body Fracture and Collision]] and [[Geometry-Instanced Particle Debris]].
