# Acceptable Use Policy

The NSF acceptable use policy limited commercial traffic on publicly funded network infrastructure. Companies could connect for research or educational purposes but could not simply use the backbone to conduct ordinary profit-making business.

The boundary was increasingly difficult to enforce as commercial mail and private networks joined. Relaxing the policy helped open the Internet to broad commercial use.
