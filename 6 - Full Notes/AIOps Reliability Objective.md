# AIOps Reliability Objective

An AIOps reliability objective aggregates measures such as response success, latency, accuracy, guardrail behavior, and safe completion over time. One fast answer is a performance observation; repeated conformance to an agreed target is evidence that users can depend on the system.

The objective should reflect the task's consequence. An incident assistant may require not only acceptable response time but also a validated diagnosis rate and zero unauthorized writes, because a consistently quick unsafe answer is not a reliable operational service.
