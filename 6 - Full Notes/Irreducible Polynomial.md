# Irreducible Polynomial

An irreducible polynomial is a nonconstant polynomial that cannot be factored into nonconstant polynomials of smaller degree over the chosen coefficient ring or field.

Over a [[Field]], an irreducible polynomial that divides a product divides at least one factor. This prime-like behavior supports the [[Unique Factorization of Polynomials]]. Irreducibility always depends on the coefficient domain.
