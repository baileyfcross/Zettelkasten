# Web of Trust

A web of trust derives confidence through a network of participants who vouch for one another. Direct trust can be extended along paths to estimate whether an unknown contributor or item should be believed.

Trust propagation must limit path length, cycles, collusion, and the influence of uncertain intermediaries. A dense connection graph is not automatically evidence of honest behavior.
