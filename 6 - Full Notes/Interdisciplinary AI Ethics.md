# Interdisciplinary AI Ethics

Interdisciplinary AI ethics combines technical, legal, philosophical, social-scientific, and practical knowledge to evaluate intelligent systems. No single field can understand both how a model works and how it changes institutions, rights, behavior, and power.

Transdisciplinary work extends the collaboration beyond academic fields to professionals, users, and affected communities. The challenge is not merely placing experts in the same room; they need shared methods and enough mutual understanding to connect abstract principles with concrete development. [[AI Ethics Education]] builds this capacity, while stakeholder participation prevents expert collaboration from excluding lived consequences.
