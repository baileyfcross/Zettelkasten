# Unmanned Ground Vehicle

An unmanned ground vehicle carries sensors, tools, cargo, or weapons without an onboard driver. Military variants range from large drive-by-wire trucks to small wheeled, tracked, legged, or throwable platforms used for reconnaissance and work in dangerous spaces.

Ground vehicles confront obstacles, rubble, stairs, soft surfaces, and human-built environments at close range. Their locomotion and size must fit the mission: a heavy vehicle can carry supplies, while a small robot can enter a building or culvert. [[Bomb Disposal Robot]] demonstrates how remote ground action reduces direct exposure to explosives.
