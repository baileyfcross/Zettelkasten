# Mammalian Cell Culture

Mammalian cell culture maintains animal cells outside the body under controlled laboratory conditions. Temperature, humidity, carbon-dioxide concentration, pH, nutrients, physical support, and sterility must remain within ranges compatible with the selected cell type.

Culture protocols turn these requirements into repeatable handling, feeding, expansion, and observation procedures for biofabrication.
