# IoT Remote Monitoring and Management

IoT remote monitoring and management provides centralized visibility and control for large groups of connected endpoints. It can inventory sensors and devices, inspect diagnostics, manage configuration and access, distribute updates, detect failures, and maintain protected connections across buildings, factories, retail systems, and field installations.

The capability is necessary because an IoT deployment can contain thousands or millions of physically dispersed devices that cannot be serviced individually for every change. Central reach also concentrates authority, so enrollment, authentication, staged updates, rollback, and auditable access are necessary to prevent a management tool from becoming a mechanism for fleet-wide failure or compromise.
