# Medium Earth Orbit

Medium Earth orbit is the orbital region between low and geostationary altitudes, with navigation constellations concentrated around 11,000 to 12,000 miles. Circular orbits there have periods of approximately twelve hours.

This altitude lets a modest constellation provide broad coverage while signals remain substantially stronger than they would be from geostationary distance. GPS and other navigation systems distribute satellites among several orbital planes so receivers can observe enough geometry for continuous positioning and time. The region is therefore strongly associated with global navigation rather than one stationary service area.
