# Cardiac Tissue Patch

A cardiac tissue patch is a sheet-like engineered construct placed over damaged heart muscle. It aims to replace contractile cells, support the injured region, or provide signals that improve repair.

Useful integration requires electrical coordination with surrounding myocardium and rapid access to blood vessels; otherwise the patch may remain weak, ischemic, or rhythmically incompatible.
