# Altair 8800

The Altair 8800 was a low-cost microcomputer kit announced by MITS in 1975. Its front-panel switches and modular enclosure echoed minicomputers, but a microprocessor made comparable ownership affordable to hobbyists.

The response to its magazine debut helped launch the personal-computer market. It also created demand for software, expansion hardware, and user communities.
