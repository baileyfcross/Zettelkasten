# Truss Support Reaction

A truss support reaction is the force supplied by a restraint to maintain equilibrium. Reactions are associated with prescribed nodal degrees of freedom and are not independent applied loads.

After solving free displacements, reactions are recovered from the original global stiffness relation. Their resultant should balance the externally applied truss loads.
