# Datafication

Datafication transforms activities, relationships, and experiences into quantified records that can be stored and analyzed. Mobile devices accelerate this process by continuously producing location, communication, search, purchase, and application-use data.

Once behavior is represented as data, it can be aggregated for prediction and targeting beyond the immediate service. Datafication therefore expands both the convenience of personalization and the reach of [[Dataveillance]].
