# Return on Investment

Return on investment compares the financial benefit produced by an initiative with the capital committed to it. For a big-data project, value comes from improved decisions, lower cost, new revenue, or faster processes rather than the quantity of data collected.

Timing, risk, dependencies, and the opportunity to fund a different project make simple benefit-minus-cost totals incomplete.
