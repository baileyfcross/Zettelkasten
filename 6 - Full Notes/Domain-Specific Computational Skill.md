# Domain-Specific Computational Skill

Computational skill becomes powerful when joined to the concepts and practices of a domain. A learner may understand loops and data structures yet remain unable to formulate a meaningful model in physics, biology, or history.

Education should therefore connect computational methods to authentic disciplinary questions. Transfer is more plausible when teachers explicitly bridge representations, assumptions, and evidence between contexts.
