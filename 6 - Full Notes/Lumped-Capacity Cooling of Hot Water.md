# Lumped-Capacity Cooling of Hot Water

A lumped-capacity model treats the water and immersed spoon as spatially uniform thermal masses that exchange heat through effective convection. Their transient temperatures depend on mass, specific heat, area, heat-transfer coefficient, and the initial difference from the surroundings.

The calculation provides a transparent benchmark for more detailed finite-element models. It captures overall equalization but cannot resolve internal circulation or local gradients, which motivates the later [[Axisymmetric Water and Spoon Model|axisymmetric model]].
