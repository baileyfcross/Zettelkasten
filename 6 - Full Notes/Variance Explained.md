# Variance Explained

Variance explained is the fraction of a centered dataset's total sum of squares attributed to a component or collection of components. For singular-value and principal-component methods, each component's share is its squared singular value divided by the sum of all squared singular values.

Individual and cumulative plots help choose a representation size. A large percentage summarizes dominant variation, but the highest-variance direction is not automatically the scientifically relevant one.
