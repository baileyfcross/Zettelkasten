# Complex Conjugate

The complex conjugate of $z=a+bi$ is $\bar z=a-bi$. It reflects the point across the real axis.

Multiplying a complex number by its conjugate gives the real value $z\bar z=a^2+b^2$. Conjugation is also required in a [[Complex Inner Product]] and the transpose-like operation defining a [[Hermitian Matrix]].
