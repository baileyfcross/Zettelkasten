# Logically Equivalent

No matter what the prepositions are, we can change them and it will logically stay the same outcome or conclusion

Two formulas are logically equivalent when they have the same truth value under every permitted interpretation. Equivalence can be checked with a [[Truth Table]] or proved through a chain of [[Boolean Algebra]] laws, and it licenses replacing one formula with the other inside a larger expression.
