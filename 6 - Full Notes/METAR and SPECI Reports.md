# METAR and SPECI Reports

A METAR is a standardized routine aerodrome weather observation, commonly issued hourly. A SPECI is an unscheduled special report produced when specified significant changes occur between routine observations. Both describe current conditions rather than the future conditions addressed by [[Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts]].
