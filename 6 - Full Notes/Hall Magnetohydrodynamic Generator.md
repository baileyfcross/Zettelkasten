# Hall Magnetohydrodynamic Generator

A Hall [[Magnetohydrodynamic Generator]] extracts the component of induced current along the duct's gas-flow direction rather than the cross-duct Faraday component. Charged particles in the moving plasma experience the magnetic field and can develop this longitudinal current. The source treats Hall and Faraday extraction as distinct electrical arrangements; both still need a fast conductive fluid, a field, and a load.
