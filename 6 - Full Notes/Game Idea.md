# Game Idea

A game idea is an initial possibility for an activity, situation, mechanic, fantasy, or experience that might become a game. Ideas can come from dreams of doing, other media, observed systems, existing games, or unexpected parts of ordinary life.

An idea is not yet a [[Game Concept]]. It becomes actionable only after the designer explains what the player does, who the game serves, how it progresses, and why its structure can produce the intended experience.
