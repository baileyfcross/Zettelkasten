# Babbage Difference Engine

Charles Babbage designed the Difference Engine to calculate and print mathematical tables from difference formulas. Its purpose was to replace error-prone hand calculation with a repeatable mechanical process.

The machine’s thousands of precisely interacting parts exceeded the manufacturing capabilities available to Babbage. Its failure illustrates that a sound computational plan must still be realizable in the physical technology of its time.
