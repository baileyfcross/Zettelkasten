# Conceptualize-Prototype-Playtest-Evaluate Cycle

Iterative game design moves through four recurring activities: conceptualize an idea, make a prototype that gives part of it form, playtest the artifact with people, and evaluate the resulting evidence. Evaluation changes the concept and begins another pass.

The cycle is not a one-time pipeline. Different questions can be at different stages simultaneously, and later discoveries can reopen decisions that once seemed settled.
