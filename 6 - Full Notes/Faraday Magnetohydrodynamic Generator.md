# Faraday Magnetohydrodynamic Generator

A Faraday [[Magnetohydrodynamic Generator]] extracts current across the duct in the direction perpendicular to both fluid flow and magnetic field. Electrodes on opposing walls collect the induced current as the conducting fluid crosses field lines. It differs from the Hall arrangement, which draws current along the flow direction. The electrode geometry is therefore part of how induced electrical power is collected.
