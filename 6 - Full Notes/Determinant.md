# Determinant

The determinant assigns a scalar to a [[Square Matrix]]. It changes sign when two rows are interchanged, scales when a row is scaled, and is unchanged when a multiple of one row is added to another.

A matrix is [[Nonsingular Matrix|nonsingular]] exactly when its determinant is nonzero. The determinant also appears in [[Cramer's Rule]] and the [[Characteristic Equation]], and for a triangular matrix it equals the product of the diagonal entries.
