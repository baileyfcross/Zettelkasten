# Cloud Billing and Reporting

Cloud billing and reporting translate measured service use into statements that consumers can understand and reconcile with contracts. Reports should connect price units, usage, service performance, and time periods rather than presenting an unexplained total.

Common processes and formats make costs from several providers easier to consolidate and compare. Frequent reporting also lets consumers detect unexpected demand or a misconfigured service before the cost grows unnoticed.
