# Cultural Layer

The cultural layer describes a game beyond individual play sessions, where player communities and society interpret, modify, circulate, and respond to it. Influence runs in both directions: culture shapes how a game is made and received, while the game can affect language, behavior, art, technology, and public debate.

Ownership is more distributed here than in the [[Inscribed Layer]]. Developers remain responsible for foreseeable implications, but players control many community practices and meanings. Its elements—[[Cultural Mechanics]], [[Cultural Aesthetics]], [[Cultural Narrative]], and [[Cultural Technology]]—therefore have blurrier boundaries than their inscribed equivalents.
