# Induced Fuzziness

Induced fuzziness transforms numeric or crisp attribute values into graded linguistic memberships before further analysis. A continuous height, for example, can have partial membership in several labels instead of one abrupt category.

The transformation uses a family of membership functions and should preserve distinctions relevant to the application. Its thresholds and shapes become part of the data representation.
