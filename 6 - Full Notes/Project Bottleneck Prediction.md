# Project Bottleneck Prediction

Project bottleneck prediction analyzes work retention time, handoffs, queues, dependencies, and recurring delays to identify stages likely to constrain delivery. It can highlight where tasks accumulate before the overall schedule has visibly failed.

The prediction should lead to investigation rather than automatic blame or reassignment. Process design, approval rules, resource scarcity, and data-quality problems may all produce the same surface signal.
