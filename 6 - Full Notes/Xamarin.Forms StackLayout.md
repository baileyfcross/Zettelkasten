# Xamarin.Forms StackLayout

A Xamarin.Forms `StackLayout` arranges its child views one after another in a vertical or horizontal sequence. It is useful when the interface expresses a simple ordered flow and each child can determine its size along the stacking direction.

Nested stacks can describe small compositions, but excessive nesting adds measurement work and obscures relationships better represented by a grid or another purpose-built layout.
