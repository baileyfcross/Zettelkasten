# CAD-to-FEM Geometry Exchange

CAD-to-FEM geometry exchange translates a product-oriented model into a representation that an analysis tool can turn into valid domains and a mesh. The transfer must preserve scale, topology, interfaces, and the features that influence the investigated physics.

The analysis copy often needs simplification, partitioning, capping, or repair even when the source CAD model is acceptable for manufacturing. Exchange is therefore a modeling step rather than a neutral file-opening operation.
