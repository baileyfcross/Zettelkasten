# Blackbody Radiation

A blackbody is an ideal absorber and emitter whose thermal spectrum depends only on temperature. Classical theory predicted an unphysical ultraviolet divergence, while Planck reproduced the observed spectrum by quantizing oscillator energy in multiples of $hf$. Increasing temperature raises total emitted power and shifts the spectral peak to shorter wavelength.
