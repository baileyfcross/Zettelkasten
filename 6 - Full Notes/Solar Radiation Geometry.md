# Solar Radiation Geometry

Solar-radiation geometry relates a site's latitude, time-of-day hour angle, and seasonal declination to the Sun's altitude, zenith, and azimuth. These angles set how much [[Beam Solar Radiation]] falls on a horizontal or tilted surface. The source uses them to estimate inclined-collector irradiation and to guide fixed tilt or tracking, while clouds and atmospheric attenuation remain separate influences.
