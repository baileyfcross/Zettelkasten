# Homogeneous Element

A homogeneous element of a [[Graded Ring]] or graded module belongs to one degree component. Multiplying homogeneous elements adds their degrees.

Every element is a finite sum of homogeneous components. Homogeneous generators let ideals, modules, and resolutions be studied degree by degree rather than only as undifferentiated algebraic objects.
