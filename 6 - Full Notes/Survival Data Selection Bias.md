# Survival Data Selection Bias

Survival data selection bias arises when eligibility for time-to-event analysis depends on having a recorded event time or follow-up measurement, and that availability differs systematically across participants. Removing most event-positive records can leave a highly unrepresentative analytic sample.

A missingness audit should quantify the loss before modeling. Imputing nearly all event times would substitute assumptions for evidence, so a demonstrative analysis may proceed only with an explicit warning while a substantive study may need a different outcome or dataset.
