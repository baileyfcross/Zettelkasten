# Inscribed Aesthetics

Inscribed aesthetics are the sensory materials placed into a game: visual art, character models, animation assets, sound, music, packaging, and any designed qualities of touch, smell, or taste. Here, aesthetics means perceivable form rather than the emotional categories called aesthetics in the [[Mechanics-Dynamics-Aesthetics Framework]].

The distinction matters because an asset is not identical to its experienced effect. A threatening musical cue is inscribed, while the tension it produces in a particular performance belongs to the dynamic layer.
