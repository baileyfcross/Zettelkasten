# Cultured Meat Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Cultured meat greenhouse gas emissions arise mainly from energy and supply-chain inputs rather than from maintaining and digesting within livestock. Comparisons are sensitive to whether carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide are combined into one equivalent measure.

Methane's shorter atmospheric behavior and carbon dioxide's accumulation make short- and long-term conclusions differ, especially as electricity systems decarbonize.
