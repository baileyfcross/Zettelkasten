# Centripetal Acceleration

Centripetal acceleration points toward the center of a circular path and has magnitude $a_c=v^2/r=r\omega^2$. It exists even in uniform circular motion because the velocity vector changes direction. A real inward resultant force must supply this acceleration; centripetal force is not an additional force separate from gravity, tension, friction, or another interaction.
