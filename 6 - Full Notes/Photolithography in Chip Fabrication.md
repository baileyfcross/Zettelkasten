# Photolithography in Chip Fabrication

Photolithography transfers miniature circuit patterns onto a semiconductor wafer using light-sensitive materials and controlled exposure. Successive steps define doped regions, conductors, and other device features.

The technique scales circuits by printing them rather than wiring each component by hand. It made dense, repeatable, mass-produced integrated circuits practical.
