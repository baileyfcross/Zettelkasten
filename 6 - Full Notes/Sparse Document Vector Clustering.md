# Sparse Document Vector Clustering

A sparse document vector represents a text using weights for only the terms that occur, commonly after frequency and inverse-document-frequency transformations. Most vocabulary coordinates are zero, making sparse storage and computation essential.

Cosine-like similarity often focuses on term distribution rather than document magnitude. High dimensionality, synonymy, polysemy, and common background language can still obscure the topical structure sought by clustering.
