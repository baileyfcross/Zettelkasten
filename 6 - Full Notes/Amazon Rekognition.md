# Amazon Rekognition

Amazon Rekognition applies managed computer-vision models to images and video. It can identify objects, scenes, text, faces, unsafe content, and activities without requiring the caller to build and operate the underlying vision infrastructure.

The service returns detected features and confidence values that an application must interpret according to its purpose. Human review, privacy, bias, and acceptable confidence thresholds remain design responsibilities, especially when results affect people or sensitive decisions.
