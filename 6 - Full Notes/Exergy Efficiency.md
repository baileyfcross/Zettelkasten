# Exergy Efficiency

Exergy efficiency compares useful-product [[Exergy]] with the exergy entering a system: $\epsilon=E_{useful}/E_{in}=1-E_{destroyed}/E_{in}$. It is a second-law performance measure because it tracks whether energy quality survives conversion, not merely whether heat or electrical energy is accounted for. A device may have respectable energy efficiency yet low exergy efficiency when it delivers heat at a temperature poorly matched to its source.
