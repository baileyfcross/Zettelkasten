# Player Action

A player action is a behavior permitted by the game's rules. Many actions are tools for overcoming challenges, while others support exploration, expression, communication, experimentation, or simple pleasure.

The action set defines what the player can actually do, not merely what the fiction claims is possible. A small group of reusable actions can support many challenges when their consequences combine consistently.
