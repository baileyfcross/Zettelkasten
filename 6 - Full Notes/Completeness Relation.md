# Completeness Relation

A completeness relation states that a basis contains enough independent functions to reconstruct every function in the intended space. For a discrete orthonormal basis, a sum over products of basis functions acts like a Dirac delta kernel.

Completeness is stronger than orthogonality: functions can be mutually orthogonal without spanning the full space. When completeness holds, expansion coefficients preserve all of the original function's information within the convergence conditions of the basis.
