# Marker Tracking

Marker tracking estimates camera or object pose from a deliberately designed visual target. A square marker provides a high-contrast boundary for detection, an interior pattern for identity and orientation, and four known planar corners for geometric estimation.

After detection, the system estimates a homography and derives pose using the calibrated camera model. Markers simplify correspondence but require the physical environment to be prepared and the target to remain visible.
