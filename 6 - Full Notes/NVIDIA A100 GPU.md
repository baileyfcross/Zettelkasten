# NVIDIA A100 GPU

The NVIDIA A100 is an Ampere-generation data center GPU presented in the source as a mature general-purpose accelerator for training, inference, and analytics. Representative configurations use 40 GB or 80 GB of HBM2e and support [[NVIDIA Multi-Instance GPU|MIG]] for partitioned service delivery.

Its value lies in a balance of software maturity, broad workload support, high-bandwidth memory, multi-GPU integration, and flexible sharing. Exact memory, form factor, power, and interconnect specifications must be checked for the deployed PCIe, SXM, cloud, or system configuration.
