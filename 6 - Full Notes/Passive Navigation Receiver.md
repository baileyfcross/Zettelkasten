# Passive Navigation Receiver

A passive navigation receiver listens to external signals but does not transmit in order to obtain a fix. LORAN, Omega, Transit, and GPS all used this property, which lets many users share a service without consuming return-channel capacity or disclosing their positions to the navigation provider. Passivity improves scalability and operational security, although a receiver still depends on the availability and authenticity of the signals it observes.
