# Final Regression Model

A final regression model is the specification selected after candidate terms have been evaluated, discarded terms reconsidered, and relevant diagnostics reviewed. It is the adjusted model used to answer the prespecified hypothesis and support the main presentation table.

“Final” does not mean uniquely true. Another defensible selection path may produce a different specification, so the report should disclose the candidate set, selection process, reference groups, fit information, and sensitivity of the substantive conclusion.
