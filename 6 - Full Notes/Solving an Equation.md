# Solving an Equation

Solving an equation is the process of finding every value that makes the equation true. The usual method transforms the original into simpler [[Equivalent Equations]] until the [[Unknown Variable]] is isolated or the possible factors are exposed.

Each transformation must preserve equality and account for restrictions. Linear equations use inverse operations on both sides, while quadratic equations may use factorization, completing the square, or the quadratic formula. The final values are checked by [[Substitution]] into the original equation because intermediate operations can conceal invalid candidates.
