# Data Loss Prevention

Data loss prevention is the coordinated discovery, identification, monitoring, management, and protection of sensitive data throughout its life cycle. Its scope includes creation, transmission, active use, storage, backup, archival, retention, and destruction.

Controls must cover [[Data at Rest]], [[Data in Motion]], and [[Data in Use]]. Encryption protects contents, checksums help verify integrity, monitoring records access, and retention management removes stale data when it should no longer be held.
