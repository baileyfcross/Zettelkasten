# Solar Panel Tilt

Solar panel tilt sets the angle at which a fixed [[Solar Array]] receives sunlight. The source chooses it with site latitude in mind and adjusts it toward the time of highest load or desired seasonal collection. Changing tilt changes incident beam radiation, so array capacity cannot be judged separately from orientation and local solar geometry. A tracker is an alternative to a fixed panel.
