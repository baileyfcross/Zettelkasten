# Degree of Field Extension

The degree [E:K] of a field extension is the dimension of E as a vector space over K. A finite basis lets every element of E be expressed uniquely as a K-linear combination.

Degrees multiply in a tower: [E:K] = [E:L][L:K] when the relevant degrees are finite.
