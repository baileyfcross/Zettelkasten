# Gusset Plate Design by Uniform Force Method

A gusset plate connects diagonal braces or truss members to beams and columns while resolving axial member force into the adjoining frame. The uniform force method selects connection dimensions so beam and column interface forces are arranged consistently and undesirable moments at the joint are minimized.

Design includes bolt or weld strength, Whitmore effective width for tension, yielding, rupture, block shear, and gusset buckling in compression. The brace work point and available edge distances shape the force path, while seismic bracing may require space for predictable gusset rotation and brace inelasticity. A plate that is strong in a simple net-section check can still be too slender or geometrically restrained for the deformation the system demands.
