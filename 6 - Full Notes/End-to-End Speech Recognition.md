# End-to-End Speech Recognition

End-to-end speech recognition trains a neural system to map speech features or waveforms directly to output symbols with fewer separately engineered components than a conventional pipeline.

The approach can learn acoustic, alignment, and linguistic regularities jointly from large datasets. It simplifies component boundaries, but it does not remove the need for diverse data or solve every accent, noise, and far-field problem.
