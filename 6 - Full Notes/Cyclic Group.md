# Cyclic Group

A cyclic group consists of all integer powers, or integer multiples, of one element. It is written ⟨a⟩, where a is a generator.

Every cyclic group is abelian, and every subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic.

The order-two cyclic group $C_2$ can be represented by the identity permutation matrix and the matrix that exchanges two objects. The source uses this representation as the starting point for exponentiating matrix variables into circular and hyperbolic rotation forms. Its further claim that $C_2$ alone generates the physical structure of spacetime is an author-specific proposal summarized in [[Morris's Finite-Group Model of Spacetime]].
