# Game Composer Demo

A game composer demo is music submitted to demonstrate creative fit and production quality to a prospective developer. It may be a reel of relevant previous work or a new mockup written for the project, and it is often delivered alongside a [[Game Music Production Estimate]].

The surrounding proposal should explain how the demo connects to the game's creative direction rather than leaving the client to infer its relevance. Before sharing prior commissioned work, the composer must also confirm that the applicable [[Game Music Project Contract]] permits promotional use.
