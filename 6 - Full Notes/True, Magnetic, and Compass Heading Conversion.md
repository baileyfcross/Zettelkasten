# True, Magnetic, and Compass Heading Conversion

True track is measured from geographic north. Applying wind correction produces a true heading, applying magnetic variation converts it to magnetic heading, and applying compass deviation produces the heading to steer on that instrument. Keeping the stages separate prevents the direction reported by weather, the runway's magnetic label, and the compass indication from being mixed.
