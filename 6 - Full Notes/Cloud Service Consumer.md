# Cloud Service Consumer

A cloud service consumer is the person or organization that uses a [[Cloud Service]]. The consumer evaluates candidates by the value they deliver, including cost, quality, timeliness, scope, security, and the ability to satisfy a real requirement.

A service catalog can record approved choices and their commitments. Before selecting a service, the consumer should compare its [[Service Level Agreement]], pricing basis, data obligations, continuity provisions, and dependency on its provider.
