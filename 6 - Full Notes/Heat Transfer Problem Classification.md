# Heat Transfer Problem Classification

Thermal processes can be classified by what remains constant or what crosses the boundary. Isothermal processes hold temperature constant, isobaric processes hold pressure constant, isochoric processes hold volume constant, and adiabatic processes exclude energy transfer across the selected boundary.

Isentropic and isenthalpic descriptions instead hold entropy or enthalpy constant. These labels are modeling constraints, not interchangeable synonyms, and they help determine the governing equations, properties, and conditions required by a [[Thermal System and Control Volume|thermal system or control volume]].
