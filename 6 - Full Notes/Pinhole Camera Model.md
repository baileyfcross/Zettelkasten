# Pinhole Camera Model

The pinhole camera model represents a camera as a center of projection and a flat image plane separated by a focal length. Light from each scene point travels through the center to one sensor location, producing a compact geometric account of image formation.

Real lenses introduce distortion, blur, and other effects, but the model captures the perspective relationships needed by many forensic tests. It provides the common foundation for [[Vanishing Point]] analysis, scene reconstruction, and reasoning about apparent scale.
