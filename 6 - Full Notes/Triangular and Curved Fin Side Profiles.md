# Triangular and Curved Fin Side Profiles

Triangular and curved fin side profiles vary thickness or edge position along the fin length instead of using a constant rectangular outline. Concave, convex, triangular, and trapezoidal-concave profiles can be defined with parameterized points and curves.

The profile controls both volume and exposed area, so comparisons should normalize the quantity relevant to the design. Smooth appearance alone does not establish better thermal performance or easier meshing.
