# Distance Preservation

Distance preservation measures how well a reduced representation retains the pairwise distances present in the original data. If most variation lies along a small number of directions, projecting onto those directions can approximate the original geometry with far fewer coordinates.

Preservation is approximate whenever discarded dimensions contain nonzero variation. Residual reconstruction error and comparisons of original with reduced distances reveal what the simplification loses.
