# Unclassed Choropleth

An unclassed choropleth maps each numeric value directly to a position on a continuous color scale. It preserves more of the variable's ordering than a [[Classed Choropleth]] and avoids artificial interval boundaries. However, viewers cannot reliably distinguish many fine color differences or recover exact values. The continuous legend must show the numeric range, and outliers can compress most regions into a narrow visual span.
