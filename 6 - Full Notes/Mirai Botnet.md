# Mirai Botnet

The Mirai botnet infected Internet-connected devices by exploiting weak or default credentials and coordinated them for large denial-of-service attacks. Its scale came from many ordinary embedded devices that were reachable, poorly administered, and easy to compromise automatically.

Mirai connects [[Internet of Things Security]] to broader network consequences: a device with little local value can still supply attack capacity as a [[Zombie Computer]] in a [[Botnet]].
