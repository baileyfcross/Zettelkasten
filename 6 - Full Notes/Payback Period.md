# Payback Period

The payback period is the time required for the benefits of an investment to recover its cost. If a service produces a measurable monthly gain, the accumulated gain can be compared with the committed service cost to estimate when the investment pays for itself.

Shorter payback is attractive, but the method ignores the changing value of money over time and benefits that arrive after the cutoff. [[Net Present Value]] adds a discount rate when timing materially affects the decision.
