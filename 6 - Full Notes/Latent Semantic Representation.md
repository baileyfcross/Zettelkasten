# Latent Semantic Representation

A latent semantic representation maps observed words and documents into hidden dimensions intended to capture recurring concepts or topics. Matrix factorization and probabilistic topic models reduce sparse vocabulary space by explaining co-occurrence through a smaller set of latent factors.

The representation can connect documents that discuss similar ideas with different words and reduce noise from synonymy or sparse counts. Its dimensions are learned from the corpus, so their stability and interpretation depend on the model, data, and chosen dimensionality.
