# Difference of Squares Identity

The difference of squares identity is

$$a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b).$$

The two factors are conjugate binomials. When expanded, the cross terms (-ab) and (ab) cancel, leaving only the two squares.

The identity can be used in either direction for expansion, factorization, or exact calculation. Compound expressions may serve as (a) and (b), and repeated use can continue a factorization, as in (x^4-16=(x^2-4)(x^2+4)=(x-2)(x+2)(x^2+4)).
