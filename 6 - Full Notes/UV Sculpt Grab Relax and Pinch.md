# UV Sculpt Grab Relax and Pinch

UV Sculpt tools edit coordinates with a brush. Grab moves UV vertices, Relax redistributes them more evenly to reduce local distortion, and Pinch draws them toward the brush center.

Radius, strength, falloff, pressure sensitivity, border locking, and island-aware behavior control the influence. These tools refine an existing unwrap continuously, but they do not replace the structural choices made by seams and projection.
