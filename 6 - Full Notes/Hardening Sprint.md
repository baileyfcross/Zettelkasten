# Hardening Sprint

A hardening Sprint closes the gap between the quality required of each ordinary increment and the broader conditions required for a release. It can address final certification, deployment preparation, accumulated polish, and defects that were not unfinished story work.

It should not become a recurring container for integration or basic quality that every Sprint was expected to provide. A large hardening burden signals a weak [[Definition of Done]].
