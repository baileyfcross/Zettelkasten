# Thin-Film Solar Cell

A thin-film solar cell deposits a photovoltaic semiconductor layer on a supporting surface rather than slicing a thick crystalline wafer. The source describes amorphous silicon deposition and also lists cadmium telluride and copper-indium-selenide materials. Less wafer processing can lower manufacturing cost, but its examples have lower conversion efficiency than crystalline silicon modules. See [[Solar Cell]].
