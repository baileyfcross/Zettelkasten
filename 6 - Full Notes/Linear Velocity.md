# Linear Velocity

Linear velocity is the rate of change of [[Displacement]]. Average velocity is $\Delta s/\Delta t$, while instantaneous velocity is $v=ds/dt$. Because it is a vector, a change in direction is a velocity change even if speed remains constant. The gradient of a velocity-time graph is [[Acceleration]].
