# Collective Data Protection

Collective data protection addresses harms that arise for groups or society even when no single person can demonstrate an individual privacy injury. Profiling can create categories, influence opportunities, or intensify surveillance across a population.

Because these effects are distributed, individual consent and complaint mechanisms may be insufficient. Socio-ethical and multiple-risk assessment can evaluate how a system changes power, behavior, and treatment at the collective level.
