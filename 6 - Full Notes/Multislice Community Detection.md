# Multislice Community Detection

Multislice community detection finds groups across a sequence of related network snapshots. Intralayer resolution controls communities within each time slice, while interslice coupling encourages a node's community assignment to remain comparable across adjacent times.

Varying those parameters exposes stable membership and genuine transitions. Applied to session-level co-sponsorship, it can reveal [[Party Switching]] that a single aggregate graph conceals.
