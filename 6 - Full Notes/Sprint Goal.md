# Sprint Goal

A Sprint Goal is the coherent outcome a team intends to achieve during a [[Sprint]]. It gives the selected backlog work a shared purpose and supports trade-offs when details change.

For a game team, a strong goal describes playable value or the reduction of an important risk. Completing unrelated discipline tasks is weaker because it postpones integration and obscures whether the player experience improved.
