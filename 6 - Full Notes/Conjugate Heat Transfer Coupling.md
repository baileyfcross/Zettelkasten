# Conjugate Heat Transfer Coupling

Conjugate heat transfer solves conduction in solids together with heat transport and motion in adjacent fluids. A shared temperature field and interface conditions connect the domains so neither the solid heat flux nor the fluid temperature is prescribed independently at their contact.

The coupling is needed when the flow pattern and solid temperature influence one another strongly. It replaces an assumed [[Convective Heat Transfer Coefficient|convection coefficient]] with resolved fluid behavior, at the cost of additional equations, material data, mesh demands, and nonlinear convergence work.
