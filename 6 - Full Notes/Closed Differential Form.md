# Closed Differential Form

A [[Differential Form]] $\omega$ is closed when its exterior derivative vanishes: $d\omega=0$.

Every [[Exact Differential Form]] is closed because applying the exterior derivative twice gives zero. The converse depends on the domain: on a suitable star-shaped region a closed form is exact, while domains with holes can support closed forms that are not exact.
