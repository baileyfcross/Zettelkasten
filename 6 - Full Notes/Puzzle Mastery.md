# Puzzle Mastery

Puzzle mastery occurs when a solver correctly performs the solution. It completes the sequence from understanding the goal through exploration, insight, and attempt, but its emotional weight depends on how much uncertainty or execution difficulty preceded it.

An immediate solution may provide a small confirmation, while a difficult action puzzle can make mastery feel like a learned physical skill. Once the relevant pattern and execution have been internalized, later performance can remain strong even after a long interval. Mastery is therefore both an outcome and evidence of a changed player capability.
