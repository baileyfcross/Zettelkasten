# Academic Formation of Computer Science

Computer science departments emerged in universities amid doubts that computing had a distinct intellectual subject. Founders had to show that programs, machines, and information processes presented phenomena and design problems not reducible to mathematics or electrical engineering.

The first department was established at Purdue in 1962, after which the field grew steadily. Its institutional identity was shaped by research, employer demand, disciplinary politics, and the need for a coherent curriculum.
