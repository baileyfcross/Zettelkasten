# Cluster Result Interpretation

Cluster result interpretation assigns substantive meaning to the groups, boundaries, and relationships produced by an algorithm. It examines characteristic attributes, within-group variation, separation, overlap, and exceptions.

Interpretability depends on the method: a K-means cluster is organized around a centroid, while a density cluster or probabilistic component has a different meaning. Validation evidence and domain knowledge must accompany the label.
