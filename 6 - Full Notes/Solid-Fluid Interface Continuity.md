# Solid-Fluid Interface Continuity

At an ideal solid-fluid interface, the coupled model enforces a common temperature and balances heat flux leaving one side with heat flux entering the other. This closes the energy exchange without inventing an arbitrary intermediate boundary temperature.

Contact resistance or an imperfect interface can modify that relationship and create a temperature jump. Geometry repair and [[COMSOL Domain and Boundary Selections|boundary selection]] must preserve the intended shared surface, because disconnected or duplicated boundaries can silently break the coupling.
