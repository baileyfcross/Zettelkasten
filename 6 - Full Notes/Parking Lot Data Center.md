# Parking Lot Data Center

A parking lot data center combines the onboard computers and storage of parked vehicles into a temporary computing facility. Long-term parking provides more stable membership than moving traffic, and physical spaces can supply power and wired networking.

The design must predict departures, isolate owners' systems, coordinate workloads, and offer incentives worth the use of vehicle resources.
