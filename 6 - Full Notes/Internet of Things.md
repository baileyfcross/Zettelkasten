# Internet of Things

The Internet of Things is a distributed framework in which physical or digital objects use identifiers, processors, sensors, communications, and software to exchange data and participate in computation. It is not a single network designed by one organization. It is a confederation of devices, platforms, protocols, databases, clouds, and applications that interoperate to varying degrees.

Its distinctive value comes from connecting observation to interpretation and action. An [[IoT Sensor]] can turn a physical condition into data, an [[Internet of Things Architecture]] can transport and analyze that data, and an [[IoT Actuator]] can change the physical environment in response. Combining many such loops exposes relationships and supports automation that an isolated device cannot provide.
