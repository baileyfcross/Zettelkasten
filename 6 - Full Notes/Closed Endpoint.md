# Closed Endpoint

A closed endpoint is a boundary value included in an [[Interval]]. It corresponds to an inclusive inequality such as (x\leq2) or (x\geq-1).

On a [[Number Line]], inclusion is shown with a filled point at the boundary. In interval notation, a bracket is used for a finite endpoint. The equality portion of (\leq) or (\geq) is what admits the boundary. A strict comparison instead produces an [[Open Endpoint]].
