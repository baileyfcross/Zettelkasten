# Forward Difference Operator

For equally spaced arguments with step $h$, the forward difference operator is

$$\Delta f(x)=f(x+h)-f(x).$$

Repeated application forms higher differences, such as $\Delta^2f(x)=f(x+2h)-2f(x+h)+f(x)$. The operator is linear, and its leading differences form the left edge of a forward [[Difference Table]].
