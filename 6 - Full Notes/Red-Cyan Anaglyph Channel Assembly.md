# Red-Cyan Anaglyph Channel Assembly

A red-cyan anaglyph combines the red channel from one eye view with the green and blue channels from the other. Correspondingly colored filters then present the intended channel combination to each eye. The book demonstrates the assembly by disabling green and blue in the left image layer and red in the right layer. This is a presentation method for a stereo pair, not a way to invent depth from a single identical view. The two viewpoints must first satisfy [[Left- and Right-Eye Image Separation]].
