# Personal Health Cloud

A personal health cloud stores and organizes one person's health records and measurements across devices and services. Wearables or home sensors can contribute measurements such as weight, heart rate, or blood pressure, while authorized people can receive selected information.

Automated monitoring can alert the person, family, or clinician when a threshold is crossed. Because the data is personal and sensitive, consent, access, retention, location, and the uncertainty of predictions must be explicit.
