# Blender Metaball Objects

Metaballs are implicit surfaces whose elements merge as they approach one another, resembling droplets joining into a larger drop. Their influence can also be subtractive, cutting into another metaball surface.

Because the blended surface updates as elements move, metaballs are useful for fluid, organic, or rapidly changing forms. The artist controls when elements combine and how strongly each contributes to the shared surface.
