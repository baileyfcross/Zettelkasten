# Game Team Role Clarity

Game-team role clarity makes visible who owns each responsibility, who contributes expertise, and who makes a final decision. It reduces duplicated effort and prevents essential work from being assumed to belong to someone else.

Roles describe accountability rather than rigid creative borders. Designers, programmers, artists, writers, and audio specialists still collaborate because a change in one discipline alters the player's combined experience.
