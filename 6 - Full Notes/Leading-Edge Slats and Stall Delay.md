# Leading-Edge Slats and Stall Delay

Leading-edge slots and slats route energetic air to the upper surface and help the flow remain attached at a higher angle of attack. This delays stall and increases usable lift for low-speed, short-field operation, while also changing pitching moment and structural loading. The benefit therefore requires aerodynamic calibration and strength verification rather than geometric extension alone.
