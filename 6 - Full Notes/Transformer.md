# Transformer

A transformer transfers alternating electrical energy between coils through changing magnetic flux in a shared core. For an ideal transformer, $V_s/V_p=N_s/N_p$ and input power equals output power, so increasing voltage decreases current. High-voltage transmission reduces resistive losses for a given delivered power.
