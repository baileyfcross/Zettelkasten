# Computers as Computational Agents

A computer is an agent that carries out calculations and symbol manipulations according to specified rules. The agent may be a person or a machine, although electronic computers perform long sequences much faster and with less distraction than human computers.

Calling a machine a computational agent does not imply understanding or intelligence. Its reliable behavior depends on representations, programs, and physical mechanisms designed by people.
