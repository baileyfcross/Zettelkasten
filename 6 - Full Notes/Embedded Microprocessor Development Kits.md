# Embedded Microprocessor Development Kits

Early semiconductor companies sold development kits containing a microprocessor, memory, power, input controls, and basic interfaces. Customers used them to prototype embedded controllers before ordering chips for production.

Although marketed as engineering tools, the kits were genuine small computers. Hobbyists recognized their independent potential before many manufacturers imagined a consumer market.
