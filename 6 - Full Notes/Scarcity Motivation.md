# Scarcity Motivation

Scarcity motivation is the increase in perceived value and urgency produced by limited time, quantity, or availability. An object can feel more desirable simply because few remain or because access will soon disappear.

Games use scarcity through expiring offers, limited-use helpers, rare items, and constrained opportunities. Because scarcity changes perceived value rather than the object's underlying function, it can motivate effort while also pressuring players into decisions they would not make without the limitation.
