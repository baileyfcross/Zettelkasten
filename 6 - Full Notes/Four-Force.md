# Four-Force

Four-force is the proper-time derivative of [[Four-Momentum]], $F=dP/d\tau$. It combines the ordinary spatial force with a temporal component related to the rate at which energy is transferred.

For invariant mass that remains constant, four-force is proportional to [[Four-Acceleration]]. Its components are commonly expressed using the three-force $\mathbf{f}$ and power $\mathbf{f}\cdot\mathbf{v}$, showing that force and power transform as parts of one spacetime object.

The Minkowski product of four-force with [[Four-Velocity]] vanishes when rest mass is constant, paralleling the orthogonality of four-acceleration and four-velocity. The electromagnetic example is especially important: the [[Electromagnetic Field Tensor]] acting on four-velocity yields the covariant form of the [[Lorentz Force Law]].
