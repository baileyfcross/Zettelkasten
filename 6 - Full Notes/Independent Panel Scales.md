# Independent Panel Scales

Independent panel scales give each variable in a multi-panel [[Time Plot]] its own vertical range. They preserve visible variation when variables have very different units or magnitudes, but equal vertical distances no longer mean equal numerical changes across panels. Clear labels and aligned time axes are therefore essential. Shared scales are preferable when direct magnitude comparison is the goal; independent scales are preferable when within-series shape is the goal.
