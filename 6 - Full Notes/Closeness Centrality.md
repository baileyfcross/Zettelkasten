# Closeness Centrality

Closeness centrality summarizes how short a node’s paths are to all other reachable nodes. A node with high closeness can reach the rest of the network through comparatively few steps.

The measure becomes less discriminating in a very dense, low-diameter graph because most nodes are already close. Disconnected components and the interpretation of weights also require an explicit convention before values can be compared.
