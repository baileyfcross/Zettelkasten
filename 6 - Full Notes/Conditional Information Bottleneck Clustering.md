# Conditional Information Bottleneck Clustering

Conditional information bottleneck clustering compresses observations into cluster labels that preserve information about a target representation while conditioning out information already captured by a known clustering. The objective formalizes a distinct yet informative view.

Tradeoff parameters balance compression, relevance, and novelty. Estimating mutual information from limited or high-dimensional data can be difficult, and an information-theoretic difference still needs domain interpretation.
