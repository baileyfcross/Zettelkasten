# Physical Ergonomics

Physical ergonomics concerns how the human musculoskeletal system affects safe, comfortable, and effective interaction. Relevant factors include posture, reach, grip, device weight and balance, muscle groups, movement range, repetition, and how long a pose must be held.

An interaction can be accurate in a short test yet become tiring or painful during realistic use. A device should therefore be evaluated with representative tasks, grips, poses, and durations. In handheld systems, balance and control placement may matter as much as total weight.

Physical ergonomics is a major part of [[Human Factors]] and contributes directly to [[Usability]].
