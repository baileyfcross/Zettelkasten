# Social Bot Temporal Feature

A social bot temporal feature summarizes when and how regularly an account acts. Posting intervals, daily rhythms, bursts, and response timing can expose scheduling or machine-speed behavior.

Automation does not always mean constant or perfectly regular activity, and humans can also show routines. Temporal evidence is therefore more reliable as part of a multi-family representation.
