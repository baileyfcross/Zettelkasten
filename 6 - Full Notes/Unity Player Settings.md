# Unity Player Settings

Unity Player Settings configure how a built game identifies and presents itself across target platforms. Cross-platform properties include the product name, icons, and cursor, while platform-specific sections expose options such as supported resolutions and presentation behavior.

Defaults permit quick builds, but advanced changes can cause platform-specific failures when their consequences are unclear. Player Settings should be reviewed alongside [[Unity Build Settings]] and tested in the resulting player rather than judged only inside the editor.
