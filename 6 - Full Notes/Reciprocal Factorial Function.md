# Reciprocal Factorial Function

A reciprocal factorial function is the inverse of a rising or falling product of equally spaced linear factors. It provides a discrete counterpart to negative powers in ordinary calculus.

Finite differences of reciprocal factorials follow compact reduction rules, making them useful when rational expressions must be differenced repeatedly. The notation keeps the changing sequence of denominator factors visible.
