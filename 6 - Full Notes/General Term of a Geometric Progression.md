# General Term of a Geometric Progression

The general term of a geometric progression gives the value at position (n) from the first term (a_1) and [[Common Ratio]] (r):

$$a_n=a_1r^{n-1}.$$

The exponent is (n-1) because the first term has not yet been multiplied by the ratio. For (6,12,24,48,\ldots), the formula is (6\cdot2^{n-1}). This expression condenses repeated multiplication and permits any position to be evaluated directly.
