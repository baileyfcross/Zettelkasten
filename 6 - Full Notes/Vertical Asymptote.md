# Vertical Asymptote

The line $x=a$ is a vertical asymptote when a function becomes unbounded as $x$ approaches $a$ from at least one side. In a [[Rational Function]], an uncancelled denominator zero commonly creates this behavior. Left- and right-hand limits may approach different infinities, so each direction must be examined separately.
