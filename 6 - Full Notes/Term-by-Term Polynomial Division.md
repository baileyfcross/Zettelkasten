# Term-by-Term Polynomial Division

Term-by-term polynomial division divides each term of a polynomial numerator by the same [[Monomial]] denominator:

$$\frac{a+b+c}{d}=\frac{a}{d}+\frac{b}{d}+\frac{c}{d},$$

where the quotients are defined. Coefficients divide and exponents on common bases subtract. The method applies to a sum divided by one term, not to a product or to an expression whose divisor has several terms. A multi-term divisor generally requires [[Polynomial Long Division]] or factorization.
