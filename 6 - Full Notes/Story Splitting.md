# Story Splitting

Story splitting divides an epic into Sprint-sized stories that each preserve a testable increment of value. Useful split dimensions include player progression, scenarios, conjunctions, acceptance conditions, and knowledge gained through research or prototyping.

Splitting by discipline creates components that may remain unusable until late integration. Vertical, value-oriented splits instead create earlier feedback about the whole experience.
