# Clinical Timeline Visualization

A clinical timeline visualization arranges encounters, diagnoses, tests, treatments, and measurements along time so a viewer can inspect the patient's course. It can align heterogeneous events and expose sequences, overlaps, and long gaps that tables make difficult to see.

Dense records require aggregation, zooming, and semantic grouping to remain legible. The display should distinguish an instantaneous event from an interval and preserve uncertainty about dates. A useful timeline supports both overview and drill-down without implying that every recorded change caused the next one.
