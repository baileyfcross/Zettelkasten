# Zero Product Property

The zero product property states that if a product of real numbers equals zero, at least one factor equals zero:

$$ab=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=0\text{ or }b=0.$$

For several factors, each factor supplies a possible equation. This property connects [[Polynomial Factorization]] to equation solving. It applies only when the product equals zero; from (ab=c) with (c\neq0), the factors cannot be set separately equal to (c) or zero.
