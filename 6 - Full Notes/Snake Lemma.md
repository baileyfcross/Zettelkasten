# Snake Lemma

The Snake Lemma starts from a commutative diagram with exact rows and constructs a connecting homomorphism between a kernel and a cokernel. These objects fit into one longer exact sequence.

It systematically relates the failures of injectivity and surjectivity of the vertical maps. The connecting map is obtained by following representatives through the diagram and checking that choices do not change the resulting class.
