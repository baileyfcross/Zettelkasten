# Microservice Honeycomb Architecture

The honeycomb analogy depicts a microservice application growing by joining autonomous hexagonal services around one another. Each cell retains its own ports, adapters, deployment, and data while participating in a larger business system through standard interactions.

The analogy emphasizes evolutionary addition rather than enlarging one service indefinitely. It does not remove dependencies; it makes each dependency cross an explicit service boundary that must tolerate independent availability and change.
