# Digital Footprint and Data Shadow

A digital footprint is the collection of traces associated with a person's activities across physical and online systems. The book distinguishes data knowingly made public from a data shadow: records gathered about a person without their knowledge, consent, or awareness.

Even deliberate sharing offers limited control once records are combined or repurposed. Likes, purchases, locations, and call metadata can support inferences about traits never disclosed explicitly. Privacy analysis must therefore consider what a dataset enables others to infer, not only what the original field appears to reveal.
