# Timebox

A timebox is a fixed maximum duration for an activity. When the limit is reached, participants use the best available result, stop, or explicitly replan instead of allowing the activity to expand indefinitely.

Timeboxing creates focus and a dependable inspection rhythm. Sprints, planning, Daily Scrums, reviews, and retrospectives use different timeboxes appropriate to their purpose.
