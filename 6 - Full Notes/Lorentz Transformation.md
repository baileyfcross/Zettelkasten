# Lorentz Transformation

A Lorentz transformation relates the spacetime coordinates assigned to the same events by inertial observers in relative motion. For motion along one spatial axis, it mixes that coordinate with time while leaving the transverse coordinates unchanged. In one common sign convention,

$$t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right),\qquad x'=\gamma(x-vt).$$

The transformation replaces the Galilean assumption of a universal time. It preserves the [[Invariant Spacetime Interval]], the light-speed boundary, and therefore the causal class of every event pair. [[Time Dilation]], [[Length Contraction]], and the [[Relativity of Simultaneity]] are coordinate consequences of this single rule.

Geometrically, the transformation is a [[Lorentz Boost]] or [[Hyperbolic Rotation]] in a time-space plane.
