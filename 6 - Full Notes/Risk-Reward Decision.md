# Risk-Reward Decision

A risk-reward decision pairs a desirable outcome with uncertainty or a possible loss. The decision becomes meaningful when the player can compare the magnitude and likelihood of outcomes with a safer alternative.

Risk should be communicated well enough to support judgment without making the result certain. Different players may choose differently because of their goals and [[Risk Attitude]], so a balanced risk-reward structure can sustain several viable strategies.
