# Assistant Initiative

Assistant initiative is the degree to which the system controls the direction and timing of an interaction. Asking the next question, suggesting an action, or starting a notification are progressively stronger forms of initiative.

High initiative can reduce user effort in a narrow task, but it can also interrupt or constrain the user. [[Mixed-Initiative Dialog]] distributes control according to what each participant knows and needs.
