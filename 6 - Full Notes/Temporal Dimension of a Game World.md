# Temporal Dimension of a Game World

The temporal dimension of a game world defines how time behaves relative to the real world and across parts of the game. Time may run continuously, pause, accelerate, skip uneventful periods, or move at different rates in different modes.

Temporal rules shape pacing, causality, and strategic opportunity. A design should distinguish game-world time from the player's real time and communicate when either can be controlled.
