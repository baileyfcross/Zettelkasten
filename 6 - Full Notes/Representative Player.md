# Representative Player

A representative player is a hypothetical person who typifies the intended target audience. The designer uses this figure to evaluate whether a proposed feature, challenge, interface, or theme serves the experience being built.

The representative is a decision aid rather than a stereotype or claim about every customer. It should be grounded in audience research and revised when observation contradicts the assumptions behind it.
