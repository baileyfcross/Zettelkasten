# LUIS Intent

A LUIS intent is a named category representing what an utterance is trying to accomplish or discuss. The bot project defines categories such as greeting, family, feelings, and weather and codes its response lookup against those names.

Intent boundaries determine what the classifier can distinguish. More fine-grained categories can support more specific replies, but every category needs representative training phrases and a fallback for messages that fit none well.
