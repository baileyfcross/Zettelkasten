# Virtual Embodiment for Emulated Minds

Virtual embodiment places an emulated mind in a simulated world with simulated senses and actions. This avoids building a physical robot while still closing the perception-action loop required for situated experience.

Because both body and environment are software, their scale, speed, and physical rules can be altered. Communities of emulations could interact in a shared virtual world or carry out experiments faster than physical time. The flexibility creates research opportunities but also makes control of the simulation an ethical form of power over its inhabitants.
