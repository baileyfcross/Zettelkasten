# Auto-Merge Topology Reduction

With vertex snapping and AutoMerge Editing enabled, moving one vertex onto another can combine them automatically. Repeating the operation along straight or over-tessellated regions removes edges that do not materially contribute to silhouette or deformation.

This is manual simplification guided by shape, not uniform decimation. The artist chooses which vertices are redundant, preserving important corners and contours while reducing avoidable game-mesh complexity.
