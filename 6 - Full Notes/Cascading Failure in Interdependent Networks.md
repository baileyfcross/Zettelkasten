# Cascading Failure in Interdependent Networks

A cascading failure in interdependent networks begins when a failed node disables dependent nodes in another layer, whose failures can then propagate back. Power, transportation, and communication systems can therefore amplify one local disruption.

Repeated dependency loss may shrink the mutually connected giant component that remains functional across all layers. Resilience depends on interlayer structure as well as robustness within each network.
