# K-Nearest Neighbor Outlier

A K-nearest-neighbor outlier has an unusually large distance to its kth nearest neighbor or an unusually large sum of distances to its k nearest neighbors. The score ranks observations by local isolation.

The choice of k defines the neighborhood scale. Computing all pairwise distances is expensive, so randomized ordering and pruning can reduce work.
