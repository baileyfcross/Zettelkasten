# George Stibitz Digital Computing

George Stibitz built relay-based calculators at Bell Laboratories and recognized the value of binary switching for arithmetic and control. At a 1942 fire-control meeting he suggested the term digital for pulse-based numerical methods.

His work connected telephone switching technology with computation. It also demonstrated remote use when a calculator in New York was operated through a Teletype from another location.
