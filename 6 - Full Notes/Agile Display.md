# Agile Display

An agile display is a movable display whose changing physical pose participates in the interaction. A handheld screen, tracked panel, or other repositionable surface can reveal different registered views as the user aims or carries it through the environment.

The movement connects display output to spatial exploration, but it also requires continuous tracking and ergonomically manageable use. The device can act as a window, a lens, or a movable control surface depending on how augmentation is placed.
