# Jack Kilby Integrated Circuit

Jack Kilby demonstrated at Texas Instruments that all components of a circuit could be made from semiconductor material. His 1958 oscillator used a single germanium wafer, with fine wires connecting its elements.

The experiment established the integrated-circuit concept. Its wiring method was less suited to mass production than the planar approach developed soon afterward in California.
