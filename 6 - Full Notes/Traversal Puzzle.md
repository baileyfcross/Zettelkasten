# Traversal Puzzle

A traversal puzzle makes a visible or known destination difficult to reach. The solution may require discovering a route, opening gates, changing the environment, taking a detour, or executing an efficient line through space.

Traversal combines spatial understanding with movement capability. [[Environmental Visual Guidance]] can establish the destination without disclosing the route, while checkpoints and feedback can distinguish a bad plan from poor execution. Racing lines are a time-sensitive form in which route choice and precise control jointly define the solution.
