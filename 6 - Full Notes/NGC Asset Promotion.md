# NGC Asset Promotion

NGC asset promotion moves a selected catalog artifact through scanning, testing, controlled registry storage, approval, development, staging, and production. Version or digest pinning preserves the identity of what was evaluated, while checkpoints and prior artifacts support rollback.

The process treats NGC as the beginning of a software supply chain rather than a direct production feed. Organization-specific code, model behavior, host compatibility, security policy, and service performance must be validated after the base asset is selected.
