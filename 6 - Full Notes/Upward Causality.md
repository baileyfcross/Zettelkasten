# Upward Causality

Upward causality is the production of a higher-level property by distributed actions and interactions at a lower level. No single part contains or directs the aggregate result.

It is the generative direction of [[Emergence]]: local behaviors form loops, and those loops create the behavior of the whole. [[Downward Causality]] describes the complementary influence of that whole back on its parts.
