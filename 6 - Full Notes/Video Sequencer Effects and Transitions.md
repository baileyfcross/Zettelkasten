# Video Sequencer Effects and Transitions

Effect strips combine or modify selected strips, and the selection order can determine which input controls the result. Blend operations may be represented as dedicated effects or as a strip's blend mode.

Preset transitions include sound crossfades, visual crossfades, gamma-corrected dissolves, and wipes. They package common animated changes so the editor can focus on timing rather than keying every transition value manually.
