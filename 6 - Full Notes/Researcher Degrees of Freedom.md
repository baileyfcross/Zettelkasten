# Researcher Degrees of Freedom

Researcher degrees of freedom are the analytic choices available in variable definition, exclusions, transformations, model form, covariate selection, interactions, and reporting. When these choices are made after seeing results, they can steer an analysis toward a preferred conclusion.

Prespecification, a restricted candidate set, versioned code, and [[Model Metadata]] make this flexibility visible. Judgment remains necessary, but transparent constraints help distinguish model development from [[P-Hacking]].
