# Generalized Reduced Gradient Method

The generalized reduced gradient method separates variables into dependent and independent groups so equality constraints remain satisfied while the objective improves.

At each iteration it solves constraint equations for dependent-variable changes and uses a reduced gradient to choose movement in the remaining variables. The method repeatedly restores feasibility and searches along a feasible direction.
