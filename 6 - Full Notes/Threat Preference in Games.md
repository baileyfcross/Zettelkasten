# Threat Preference in Games

Threat preference describes a player's attraction to danger, frightening content, and other deliberately unpleasant emotions. In the source's model, the response relates to neuroticism in a less intuitive way: players who are less easily distressed may seek stronger fictional threat.

Threat should be calibrated to the intended audience. Surprise, vulnerability, imagery, and uncertainty can produce excitement for one player and exclusion for another.
