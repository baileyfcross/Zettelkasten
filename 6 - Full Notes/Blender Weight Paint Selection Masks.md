# Blender Weight Paint Selection Masks

Weight Paint can restrict editing with face masks or vertex selection. Activating these controls exposes direct, box, circle, and lasso selection tools so weight changes can be confined to a chosen region.

This is important where nearby parts of a mesh should respond to different systems. A selection boundary prevents a broad brush from accidentally assigning influence to unrelated vertices.
