# Triangulation

Triangulation determines an unknown position or distance from a known baseline and measured directions to the target. The baseline and two angles define a triangle whose remaining sides can be calculated with trigonometric relationships.

The method turns angular observations into distance estimates, but small angle errors can create large positional errors when the geometry is nearly parallel. R calculations should therefore preserve units and explore sensitivity rather than report more precision than the measurements support.
