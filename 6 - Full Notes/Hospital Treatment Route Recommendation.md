# Hospital Treatment Route Recommendation

Hospital treatment route recommendation orders a patient's required examinations or treatment tasks using predicted service and queue times. Independent tasks can be rearranged to reduce total waiting, while prerequisite relationships restrict feasible routes.

The recommendation must update as queues change and must remain subordinate to urgent clinical priorities and professional judgment.
