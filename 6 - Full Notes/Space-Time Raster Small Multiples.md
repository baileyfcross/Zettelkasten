# Space-Time Raster Small Multiples

Space-time raster small multiples arrange a sequence of map snapshots in a regular panel grid. A fixed extent, color scale, and panel design let viewers compare both location and time by scanning corresponding cells. They are especially effective after [[Monthly Raster Aggregation]]. The number of panels must remain small enough to preserve spatial detail, and labels should make temporal order unmistakable.
