# Interactive Voice Response System

An interactive voice response system conducts automated telephone interactions through spoken prompts and user responses. Traditional systems often accept keypad input or restricted speech and use a controlled call-flow to route or complete a service task.

An IVR is a form of [[Virtual Agent]]. Its constrained [[Directed Dialog]] makes recognition and recovery more manageable than the broad, open requests expected by a [[Personal Virtual Assistant]].
