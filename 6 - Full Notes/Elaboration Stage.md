# Elaboration Stage

The elaboration stage is the longest design stage, during which a team adds most details and refines the game through prototypes, documentation, and playtesting. Abstract intentions become specific rules, interfaces, content requirements, and production plans.

The stage is iterative rather than a single pass. Evidence from playable versions can revise details until the schedule requires [[Feature Lock]] and movement into the [[Tuning Stage]].
