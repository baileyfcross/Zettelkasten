# Linear Difference Equation

A linear difference equation contains the sequence and its shifts only to the first power and does not multiply them together. A constant-coefficient example is

$$a_0y_n+a_1y_{n+1}+\cdots+a_ry_{n+r}=g(n).$$

The solution is the sum of a [[Complementary Function of a Difference Equation]] and a [[Particular Integral of a Difference Equation]]. When $g(n)=0$, the equation is homogeneous.
