# Cyber Actions on Objectives

Cyber actions on objectives are the effects an attacker pursues after establishing access, such as stealing data, disrupting service, changing information, surveilling users, or expanding control. They are the final outcome-oriented stage of the [[Cyber Kill Chain]].

Defenders should identify these likely outcomes in advance. Controls that preserve essential functions, constrain privileges, detect data leaving the environment, and support recovery can reduce harm even after intrusion.
