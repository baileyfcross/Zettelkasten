# Character Color Scheme Iteration

Character color scheme iteration tests multiple palettes over the same established design before surface production begins. Separating skin, hair, clothing, and accessories into editable layers lets an artist compare alternatives without repainting the drawing or confusing color decisions with shape changes.

The chosen palette should reinforce context, personality, and focal hierarchy while remaining feasible for the final materials. Early comparisons reveal whether adjacent areas need more contrast and whether a distinctive accent can make the character readable without adding geometry.
