# Observability-Driven Development

Observability-driven development treats observable health as a software requirement defined before deployment. A team identifies the external signals consumers experience, the internal leading indicators that warn of degradation, how those values will be measured, and where the resulting evidence must be available.

The practice resembles test-driven development in its timing: instrumentation and health criteria are part of design, not an operational retrofit. Adoption can begin with a lighthouse project whose successful self-service feedback demonstrates the value of expanding observability left into engineering work.
