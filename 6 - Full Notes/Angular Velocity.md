# Angular Velocity

Angular velocity is the rate of change of angular displacement, $\omega=d\theta/dt$. For circular motion at radius $r$, tangential speed is $v=r\omega$. Its vector direction lies along the rotation axis according to the right-hand rule. Constant angular velocity still gives changing linear velocity because the direction of motion continually turns.
