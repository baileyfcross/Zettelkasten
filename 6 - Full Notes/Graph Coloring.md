# Graph Coloring

Graph coloring assigns labels called colors while preventing selected adjacent parts of a [[Graph]] from sharing a color. The colors can represent any mutually incompatible resource, such as tournament days rather than literal visual colors.

[[Vertex Coloring]] constrains connected vertices, while [[Edge Coloring]] constrains edges that meet at a vertex. Finding the minimum number of colors can be difficult, so a [[Greedy Algorithm]] may provide a practical coloring without guaranteeing the global optimum.
