# Space Science Fiction

Space science fiction imagines travel, societies, encounters, and technologies beyond Earth. Nineteenth-century works responded to industrial transportation and modern astronomy by treating the Moon and planets as places that might be reached rather than supernatural realms.

The technical mechanisms were often impossible, but increasingly realistic stories encouraged readers to ask how flight might actually work. Later fiction adopted rockets as real theorists and experiments made them credible. The genre both influenced spaceflight pioneers and absorbed the imagery, heroes, and anxieties of actual programs, making imagination and engineering mutually reinforcing.
