# Terrestrial Solar Radiation

Terrestrial solar radiation is sunlight reaching Earth's surface after atmospheric scattering and absorption. It combines direct beam and diffuse components and varies with time, season, location, and cloud cover. A site's measured irradiation, not the extraterrestrial [[Solar Constant]], determines what a [[Solar Collector]] can actually receive. Its diffuse portion also affects whether a concentrating device will work well.
