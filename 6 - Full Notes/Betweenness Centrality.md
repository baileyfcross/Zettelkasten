# Betweenness Centrality

Betweenness centrality measures how often a node lies on shortest paths between other node pairs. A high value identifies a bridge that connects otherwise separated portions of a network, even when the node is not among the most frequent.

In a tag network, general activities such as debugging or testing can bridge technology-specific communities. The result depends on how edge weights are translated into path lengths and whether an approximation truncates the paths considered.
