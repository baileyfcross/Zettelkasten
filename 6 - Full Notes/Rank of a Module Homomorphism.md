# Rank of a Module Homomorphism

For a homomorphism represented by a matrix A and an R-module M, the rank on M is the largest r for which the ideal generated by the r-by-r minors of A is not contained in Ann(M).

This determinantal definition works over rings where ordinary row reduction is insufficient. Rank conditions combine with depth of minor ideals in the [[Buchsbaum-Eisenbud Exactness Criterion]].
