# Stereo Parallax and Convergence

Because eyes occupy different positions, a nearby object appears to shift farther between left and right views than a distant one. This positional difference is stereo parallax. Convergence describes the location where the paired views are brought into alignment, influencing which depth appears to lie at the physical screen. The book's finger experiment makes the changing parallax visible by alternating eyes while moving a finger away. A stereo pair must preserve enough difference for depth without making its alignment uncomfortable. See [[Stereo Window Placement]].
