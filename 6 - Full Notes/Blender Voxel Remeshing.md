# Blender Voxel Remeshing

Voxel remeshing rebuilds a sculpted object into more uniformly distributed geometry. The voxel size sets the resolution: smaller voxels preserve finer forms with more geometry, while larger voxels produce a coarser and lighter mesh.

Remeshing is useful after sculpting stretches vertices and leaves uneven density. Because the operation reconstructs topology, fine details below the chosen voxel scale may disappear and should be added or refined afterward.
