# Lower Triangular Matrix

A lower triangular matrix is a [[Square Matrix]] whose entries above the main diagonal are zero. Its determinant is the product of the diagonal entries.

A lower triangular system is solved by [[Forward Substitution]]. The factor $L$ in [[LU Decomposition]] has this form, and [[Cholesky Decomposition]] uses a lower triangular matrix with its transpose.
