# Evaluation-to-Task Translation

Evaluation-to-task translation turns a chosen design revision into changes to the design document and schematics, then into assignable work. It closes the gap between knowing what should change and constructing a prototype that embodies the decision.

Tasks should preserve their reason by linking back to the evidence and value they serve. Otherwise implementation details can drift while still appearing to complete the requested work.
