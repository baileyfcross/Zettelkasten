# Constant Descent Final Approach

A constant descent final approach follows a continuous descent from the final-approach segment toward the threshold or missed-approach point without intermediate level-offs. A typical path is near three degrees, but procedure design and aircraft category determine the authorized gradient. The method supports a [[Stabilized Approach Criteria]] by keeping configuration, power, speed, and vertical path predictable.
