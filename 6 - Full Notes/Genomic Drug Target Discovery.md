# Genomic Drug Target Discovery

Genomic drug target discovery uses variants, expression patterns, pathways, and molecular networks to identify biological components whose modification may affect disease. The analysis can connect disease-associated changes with genes or processes that are potentially actionable.

Association alone does not establish that altering a gene will be safe or therapeutic. Network position, tissue context, causal direction, and experimental evidence influence target value. Genomic analysis narrows and prioritizes hypotheses, while laboratory and clinical studies determine whether the proposed mechanism can support a drug.
