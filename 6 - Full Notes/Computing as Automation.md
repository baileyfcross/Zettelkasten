# Computing as Automation

The automation view treats computing as the use of machines to carry out processes with minimal human intervention. It once offered a unifying question for the field: what can be automated efficiently?

The view captures an important purpose of programs but is incomplete. Simulations, communication systems, and investigations of natural information processes can create new capabilities rather than replace a preexisting human procedure.
