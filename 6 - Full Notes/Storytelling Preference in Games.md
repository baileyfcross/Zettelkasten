# Storytelling Preference in Games

Storytelling preference is the importance a player assigns to characters, plot, and dramatic context. Some players treat story as the main reason to continue, while others prefer games where social or mechanical activity needs little narrative support.

Genres differ in how naturally they use story. The design should integrate narrative with the intended play rather than adding it as an obligation that interrupts the player's preferred activity.
