# Transverse Mercator Projection

The transverse Mercator rotates the ordinary Mercator cylinder so its tangency follows a meridian rather than the Equator. Scale is true along that central meridian and remains acceptable within a limited band on either side. This makes the projection well suited to regions whose north-south extent is much greater than their east-west width.
