# Active Touch

Active touch is perception produced by deliberately moving the body to obtain tactile and kinesthetic information. Sliding a finger reveals texture, pressing tests softness, and lifting reveals weight more precisely than passive contact with the same object.

The movement is part of the sensing process, so active touch is bidirectional: action changes the sensory input and may even change the object being examined. This coupling distinguishes active touch from stimulation delivered to a stationary observer and makes it the operating principle behind [[Haptic Exploratory Procedure|haptic exploratory procedures]].
