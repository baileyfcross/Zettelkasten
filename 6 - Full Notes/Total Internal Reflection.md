# Total Internal Reflection

Total internal reflection occurs when a wave travels from a higher-index medium toward a lower-index medium at an incidence angle greater than the critical angle. At the critical angle the refracted ray runs along the boundary; beyond it no propagating refracted ray emerges. Optical fibers confine light through repeated internal reflection.
