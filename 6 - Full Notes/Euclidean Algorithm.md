# Euclidean Algorithm

The Euclidean algorithm repeatedly replaces a pair of integers by the divisor and remainder from division. When the remainder reaches zero, the last nonzero remainder is the [[Greatest Common Divisor]].

Working backward expresses that divisor as an integer linear combination of the original numbers.
