# Inscribed Angle Theorem

An inscribed angle has its vertex on a circle and its sides along chords. Its measure is half the measure of the central angle subtending the same arc. Consequently, inscribed angles standing on the same chord are equal, and an angle subtended by a diameter is a right angle.
