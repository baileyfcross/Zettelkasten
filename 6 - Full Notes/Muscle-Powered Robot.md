# Muscle-Powered Robot

A muscle-powered robot attaches living contractile tissue to a skeleton whose geometry directs the resulting force. Asymmetry can turn equal contractions into crawling, while films, tails, fins, or tubes create pumping and swimming motions.

Performance depends on tissue strength, stimulation, attachment, skeleton stiffness, friction, fluid mechanics, and the health of the culture environment.
