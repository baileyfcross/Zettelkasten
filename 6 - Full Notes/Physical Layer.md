# Physical Layer

The physical layer carries raw bits as electrical, optical, or radio signals over a medium. It concerns the mechanics and signaling needed for a device to place bits on a channel and receive them from that channel.

Higher layers can describe frames, addresses, and protocols only after this layer supplies an actual transmission path. Signal strength and the characteristics of the medium can therefore affect integrity and throughput above it.
