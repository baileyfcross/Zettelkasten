# Memetic Video Success Factors

Videos with strong memetic potential often feature ordinary people, imperfect or failed performances, humor, simplicity, repetition, and whimsical content. These traits create a recognizable model that viewers can imitate without requiring professional resources.

Flawed masculinity is one recurring pattern: performers visibly fail to meet conventional masculine ideals, creating comic tension and an accessible role for parody. More generally, a video becomes remakeable when its structure is easy to grasp and leaves room for participants to substitute their own bodies, settings, and interpretations.
