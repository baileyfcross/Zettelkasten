# Hidden Layer

A hidden layer is a [[Neural Network Layer]] that is not directly exposed as the network's external input or final output. It transforms signals received from an earlier layer and passes its activations onward.

Multiple hidden layers let a [[Deep Neural Network]] build increasingly abstract representations. In image recognition, earlier layers may respond to local edges while later layers combine those patterns into larger structures.
