# Roblox Developer Product

A Roblox Developer Product represents a repeatable purchase, often used for consumable currency, boosts, or other benefits that a player may buy more than once. Receipt processing must grant the outcome exactly once even if delivery is retried.

The authoritative server verifies and records fulfillment before treating the transaction as complete. Client-side purchase prompts are presentation, not proof that the benefit should be awarded. Developer products complement the one-time entitlement model of [[Roblox Game Pass]] and contribute to revenue data in [[Roblox Experience Analytics]].
