# Industrial Robot

An industrial robot is a programmable machine that performs repeatable physical operations such as transferring parts, welding, painting, or assembly. Traditional examples are fixed, powerful, specialized, and separated from people by safety cages.

Their strength is consistency across structured tasks and environments, not general understanding. Reprogramming and tooling allow some variation, but the workflow is usually designed around predictable motion. Newer collaborative systems alter this arrangement by limiting force and simplifying instruction, as described in [[Collaborative Industrial Robot]], but they still depend on a clearly engineered task.
