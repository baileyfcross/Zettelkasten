# Interactive Story

An interactive story combines a credible sequence of dramatically meaningful events with player participation. Player events contribute to the experience, and [[Dramatic Action|dramatic actions]] may alter the future plot.

Interactivity does not require every action to change the story. The design decides which choices are dramatically significant while preserving gameplay as the reason the audience is playing rather than watching.
