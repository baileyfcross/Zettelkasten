# Expected Support

Expected support is the sum of the probabilities that an itemset occurs across uncertain transactions. It generalizes deterministic support when individual items are present only with stated probabilities.

An itemset is expected-frequent when this value reaches the threshold. The expectation is efficient to calculate under the model's independence assumptions but does not express the full probability distribution of support.
