# Meaning Representation

A meaning representation is a structured form that captures the actionable content of an utterance. It abstracts away from wording so that requests with different surface forms can drive the same system behavior.

Assistant representations commonly combine an [[Intent]] with named arguments filled by [[Entity|entities]]. The [[Dialog Manager]] uses this structure to decide what information is missing and what action should follow.
