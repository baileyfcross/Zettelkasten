# Donkey Space

Donkey space is the tangle of recursive anticipation created when multiple players try to infer one another's plans while knowing that the others are doing the same. Each participant reasons about what the others know, expect, and may pretend to intend.

This layered mind-reading expands [[Yomi]] into a shared structure of uncertainty. It helps explain why competitive human play can generate forms of [[Multiplayer Complexity]] and surprise that fixed rules alone do not supply.
