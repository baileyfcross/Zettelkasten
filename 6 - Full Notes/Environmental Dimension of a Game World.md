# Environmental Dimension of a Game World

The environmental dimension describes what occupies the game world's space and the atmosphere those contents create. It includes cultural context, geography, architecture, weather, art, sound, and other sensory properties.

These elements are not merely decorative. They tell players what kind of place this is, what behavior is plausible, and which objects or regions may matter to play.
