# Domain-Adapted Machine Translation

Domain-adapted machine translation tunes its vocabulary, phraseology, rules, or learned parameters to a bounded field such as weather forecasts, patents, medicine, or technical manuals. Restricting the domain reduces ambiguity and makes recurring expressions predictable.

The Météo system illustrates why this can work: weather reports have limited structures, regular terminology, and repeated daily content, so a specialized system can require little post-editing. The same design may fail on open-domain prose. Successful adaptation therefore depends on matching training material and terminology to actual use, then evaluating the system on that domain rather than assuming general quality.
