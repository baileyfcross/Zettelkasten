# Play

Play is a nonessential human activity usually undertaken voluntarily and often for recreation. It can be structured or unstructured, solitary or social, and it does not require a formal victory condition.

A [[Game]] is one organized form of play. It adds a pretended context, at least one arbitrary and nontrivial goal, and rules that constrain how participants pursue that goal.
