# Integer

An integer is a whole number, including zero, a positive natural number, or the [[Opposite Number]] of a positive natural number:

$$\mathbb{Z}=\{\ldots,-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,\ldots\}.$$

Integers extend the natural numbers so subtraction always has an integer result. Their signs can be interpreted as opposite directions on a [[Number Line]] or as gains and losses. Addition moves in the direction indicated by each sign, while multiplication by a negative reverses direction. Two sign reversals make a positive product; one makes a negative product.
