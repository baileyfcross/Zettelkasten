# Lagrange Interpolation

Lagrange interpolation writes the interpolating polynomial as $p_n(x)=\sum_{i=0}^n L_i(x)f(x_i)$. Each [[Lagrange Basis Polynomial]] is one at its own node and zero at every other node.

The formula works for equally or unequally spaced points and gives a direct expression. Adding a new point generally requires rebuilding all basis polynomials.
