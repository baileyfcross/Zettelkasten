# Speech Synthesizer

A speech synthesizer generates the acoustic waveform for a planned sequence of speech sounds and prosodic targets. It may concatenate recorded units, predict acoustic parameters, or generate audio directly with a neural model.

The synthesizer is the final computational stage of [[Text-to-Speech]] before digital-to-analog conversion and playback. Its output quality depends on the linguistic and prosodic information supplied upstream.
