# Inscribed Mechanics

Inscribed mechanics are the authored systems that define how a player and game can interact. They include explicit [[Game Rule|rules]], objectives, resources, boundaries, player interaction patterns, and other formal elements fixed by the design.

These mechanics specify possibilities rather than actual behavior. A scoring rule can encourage a strategy, but the strategy itself belongs to [[Dynamic Mechanics]]. Separating the two lets a designer compare intended incentives with the procedures and outcomes that players actually produce.
