# Puzzle Mechanic Sequencing

Puzzle mechanic sequencing introduces a concept through its simplest isolated use, increases the difficulty of using it, and finally combines it with concepts the player already understands. This ordering separates learning a rule from solving complex situations that depend on it.

Good sequencing supports text-free teaching because the order of problems demonstrates what changed. It also gives difficulty a diagnosable structure: if players fail when two known concepts are combined, the issue differs from failing to understand either concept alone. [[Playtesting]] is needed because designer familiarity makes the intended progression appear easier than it is.
