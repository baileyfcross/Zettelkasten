# Vehicular Cloud Taxonomy

A vehicular cloud taxonomy classifies deployments by factors such as whether vehicles are moving or parked, who controls the cloud, how participants connect, and what services they provide. The categories expose different assumptions about stability and available infrastructure.

Classifying a proposal prevents performance claims from a long-residency wired setting from being applied uncritically to a short-lived wireless one.
