# Downward Closure Property

The downward-closure property states that every subset of a frequent itemset is also frequent. Equivalently, if an itemset is infrequent, every superset containing it must also be infrequent.

This monotonic relationship supports efficient pruning: failed candidates eliminate entire branches of the combinatorial search.
