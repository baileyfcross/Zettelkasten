# Buoyancy-Driven Thermal Flow

Heating changes fluid density, and gravity then produces a body force that can drive circulation. Warmer, less dense fluid rises while cooler, denser fluid descends, transporting energy in addition to molecular conduction.

This natural-convection feedback appears in a cup or kettle even without an imposed inlet velocity. A coupled model needs gravity, a consistent density law, and suitable walls or free surfaces to resolve the circulation rather than replacing it with a fixed [[Convective Heat Transfer Coefficient|boundary coefficient]].
