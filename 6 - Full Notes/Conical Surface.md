# Conical Surface

A conical surface is formed by rays from a fixed apex through every point of a generating curve. Scaling a point away from or toward the apex therefore remains on the same generator.

A familiar circular cone is one example, but the definition permits other generating curves. A parameterization uses one parameter on the generating curve and a second scaling parameter along the rays.
