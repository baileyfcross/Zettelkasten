# Parallel Corpus

A parallel corpus contains source texts paired with their translations. A bilingual aligned pair is a bi-text; a collection aligned across more than two languages can be called a multi-text. Alignment may connect documents, paragraphs, sentences, words, or smaller units.

Parallel corpora preserve translation choices in context, making them richer than isolated bilingual dictionaries. They support translation memories, example retrieval, and the training of statistical and neural systems. Their value depends on provenance and quality: the original language should be known, loose adaptations can corrupt alignments, and domain mismatch can teach correspondences that fail on the intended material.
