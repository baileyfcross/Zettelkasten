# Stirling Interpolation Formula

Stirling's interpolation formula averages the Gauss forward and backward formulas to obtain a symmetric central expansion. Odd differences enter as averages of neighboring values, while even differences lie on the central line.

It is best suited to equally spaced data when the desired argument is very near the chosen center, so the normalized displacement $p$ is close to zero. Symmetry makes it especially useful for estimating values and derivatives at a central tabular point.
