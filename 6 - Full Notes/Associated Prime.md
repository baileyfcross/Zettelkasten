# Associated Prime

A prime ideal p is associated to an R-module M when p is the annihilator of some element of M. Equivalently, M contains a cyclic submodule isomorphic to R/p.

The set Ass(M) identifies primes directly visible through module elements. Over a [[Noetherian Ring]], a nonzero module has an associated prime, and a finitely generated module has only finitely many.
