# Koszul Complex

The Koszul complex of elements r₁,…,rₙ is a finite complex whose terms are exterior powers of a free module and whose differential contracts with the chosen sequence.

It encodes algebraic dependence among the elements. If they form a [[Regular Sequence]], positive [[Koszul Homology]] vanishes and degree-zero homology is the quotient by their generated ideal.
