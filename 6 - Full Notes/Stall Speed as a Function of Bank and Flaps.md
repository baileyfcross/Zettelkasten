# Stall Speed as a Function of Bank and Flaps

Stall speed rises with bank angle because a level turn requires a higher load factor and therefore more lift. Extending flaps increases camber and can lower stall speed, while center-of-gravity location also changes the measured value and recovery behavior. These trends connect performance tables to the force balance in [[Bank Angle and Load Factor]].
