# Fisher-Jenks Classification

Fisher-Jenks classification partitions ordered numeric values into classes that minimize variation within classes and maximize separation between classes. The resulting natural breaks follow gaps and clusters in the observed distribution rather than fixed-width or equal-count rules. It can support a [[Graduated Symbol Map]] or [[Natural Breaks Map Classification]], but class boundaries may change when the dataset changes, limiting comparison between separate maps.
