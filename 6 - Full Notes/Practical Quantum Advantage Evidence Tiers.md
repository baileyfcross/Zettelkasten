# Practical Quantum Advantage Evidence Tiers

The framework distinguishes four levels of evidence for practical quantum advantage. Provable PQA requires superiority over the best classical algorithm backed by complexity-theoretic arguments. Robust PQA compares against the best available classical algorithms, although a later classical improvement can still overturn the result.

Potential PQA compares genuinely against a credible set of state-of-the-art classical methods when the best classical algorithm is not agreed upon. Limited PQA is weaker: it compares a classical method only with its promoted quantum counterpart, such as a restricted Boltzmann machine against a quantum Boltzmann machine.

These tiers separate the strength of the comparison from the practical value of the task. The paper targets potential PQA by racing a QCBM against RNN, Transformer, VAE, and WGAN baselines, but its synthetic simulation remains evidence toward that tier rather than a conclusive PQA demonstration.
