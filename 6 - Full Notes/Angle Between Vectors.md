# Angle Between Vectors

For nonzero vectors $u$ and $v$, the angle $\theta$ between them is determined by

$$\cos\theta=\frac{u\cdot v}{\|u\|\|v\|}.$$

The formula connects the [[Dot Product]] to Euclidean geometry. A positive dot product gives an acute angle, a negative one gives an obtuse angle, and a zero dot product gives [[Orthogonal Vectors|orthogonal vectors]].
