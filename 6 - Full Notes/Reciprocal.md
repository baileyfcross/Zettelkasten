# Reciprocal

The reciprocal of a nonzero number is the value that multiplies with it to produce the multiplicative [[Identity Element]] one. For a nonzero fraction,

$$\left(\frac{c}{d}\right)^{-1}=\frac{d}{c}.$$

Thus the reciprocal of (3/5) is (5/3). Division by a nonzero fraction is multiplication by its reciprocal:

$$\frac{a}{b}\div\frac{c}{d}=\frac{a}{b}\cdot\frac{d}{c}.$$

Zero has no reciprocal because no number multiplied by zero equals one.
