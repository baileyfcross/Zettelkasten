# Scalar

A scalar is a single numerical value, distinguished from a [[Vector]] or [[Matrix]]. In a linear combination, scalars multiply vectors or matrix columns and determine the contribution of each component.

Linear-model coefficients are scalars collected into a coefficient vector. Their numerical values are unknown parameters or estimates, while the predictor columns they weight are treated as known quantities in the design.
