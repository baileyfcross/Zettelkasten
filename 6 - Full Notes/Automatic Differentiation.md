# Automatic Differentiation

Automatic differentiation is a family of techniques for evaluating derivatives numerically through the operations that compose a function. It avoids manually deriving and coding a separate symbolic formula for every derivative.

Neural networks contain large numbers of connected calculations and parameters. Efficient derivative evaluation makes [[Backpropagation]] practical by supplying the information needed for a [[Neural Network Parameter Update]] throughout the network.
