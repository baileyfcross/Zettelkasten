# Primary Gameplay Mode

The primary gameplay mode is the mode in which the player spends the largest share of play time. It combines the central actions and challenges with the ordinary camera and interaction models.

Defining it during concept development forces the proposal to describe the actual experience rather than only its setting or story. Secondary modes should support the primary activity instead of fragmenting the design.
