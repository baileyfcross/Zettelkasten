# AWS CloudTrail Trail

An AWS CloudTrail trail configures continuous delivery of selected CloudTrail events to an S3 bucket and optionally to CloudWatch Logs. A multi-region trail can preserve an account-wide record of management activity rather than relying only on the short event-history window.

Log-file validation, restricted bucket access, encryption, retention, and centralized account design help protect the audit record. The trail records actions; it does not by itself decide whether those actions were authorized, safe, or compliant.
