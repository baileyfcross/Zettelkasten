# Semantic Knowledge

Semantic knowledge describes what tracked physical and virtual entities mean and how they relate, beyond their geometric shape and pose. An AR object may be identified as a machine component, destination, control, person, or task step with associated properties and actions.

This knowledge lets the system select relevant information, enforce interaction rules, and attach authored content to meaningful referents. Geometry answers where an entity is; semantics helps determine what the application should do with it.
