# Performance-Based Pricing

Performance-based pricing ties part of a service's charge to a measurable benchmark or result. The metric should have a clear connection to a business process or outcome so that the payment encourages behavior valuable to the consumer.

The approach can align provider and consumer goals, but attributing an outcome to the cloud service may be difficult. [[Outcome-Based Pricing]], [[Business-Linked Pricing]], and [[Gain-Share Pricing]] use different forms of that relationship.
