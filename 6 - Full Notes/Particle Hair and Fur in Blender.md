# Particle Hair and Fur in Blender

Blender's Hair particle type treats particle paths as strands growing from a surface. A large set of those strands can represent hair, fur, or similar fibers.

Particle Edit mode lets an artist comb and customize the generated strands after the base settings are established. Edited hair can be reset to its procedural state if the manual changes do not work.

Hair systems separate a simulated parent population from optional children that increase visible density. Strand shape controls root and tip radius, while vertex groups can vary density, length, clumping, kink, roughness, and twist across the emitter. Hair Dynamics adds mass, bending stiffness, damping, air drag, and volume interactions when the strands must move rather than remain groomed static paths.
