# Automatic Carry in Mechanical Calculators

Automatic carry lets a calculating machine transfer overflow from one digit position to the next, as when 999 plus 1 becomes 1000. Mechanizing this operation distinguishes a true adding mechanism from a device that only records quantities.

Carry mechanisms made reliable multi-digit arithmetic possible but required intricate coordination of moving parts. Their complexity illustrates why electronic switching eventually transformed calculation.
