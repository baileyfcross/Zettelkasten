# Contextual Fit Beyond Requirements

Contextual fit means software aligns with the existing practices, norms, and environment of its users rather than merely checking off formal requirements. The system feels like a coherent extension of work people already know how to do.

Achieving fit requires immersion and feedback. Designers must understand the relationships and constraints that users may find too obvious to mention in a requirements interview.
