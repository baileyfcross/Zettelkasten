# Hub-and-Spoke Level Layout

A hub-and-spoke level layout uses a central, relatively safe area connected to several outward paths. Players leave the hub to pursue challenges and return after completing or abandoning them.

The hub provides orientation, recovery, services, and repeated social or narrative contact. Spokes can be unlocked or attempted in different orders while the stable center limits navigational complexity.
