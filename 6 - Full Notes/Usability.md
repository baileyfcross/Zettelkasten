# Usability

Usability is the set of qualities of a system or artifact that affect a person's use of it. Three central qualities are effectiveness, efficiency, and satisfaction.

- Effectiveness concerns whether users achieve their goals completely and accurately.
- Efficiency concerns the time, effort, and other resources required.
- Satisfaction concerns whether the system is comfortable and acceptable to use.

Learnability, retention, error rate, speed, ease of use, and comfort also contribute. Usability is part of [[User Experience]], but it is not the same as usefulness: a system can be easy to use yet provide no function that a person needs.
