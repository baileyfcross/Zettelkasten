# Design Against Negative Consequences

Design against negative consequences anticipates ways software could cause disruption, loss, misuse, or irreversible mistakes. It adds safeguards, reversibility, monitoring, and repair paths before users encounter the breakdown.

This level goes beyond implementing requested features. Designers actively explore harmful interactions and remain available to correct problems discovered after delivery.
