# Commutative Property

The commutative property says that changing the order of two inputs does not change the result. For real numbers,

$$a+b=b+a\qquad\text{and}\qquad ab=ba.$$

This property permits terms and factors to be rearranged when simplifying an [[Algebraic Expression]]. It does not apply automatically to every operation. In general, (a-b\neq b-a) and (a/b\neq b/a), so reversing subtraction or division changes the relationship. Commutativity concerns order, whereas the [[Associative Property]] concerns grouping.
