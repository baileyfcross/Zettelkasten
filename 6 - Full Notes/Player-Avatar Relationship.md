# Player-Avatar Relationship

The player-avatar relationship is the psychological and mechanical connection between a player and the character representing them. A blank or customizable avatar encourages projection, while a strongly authored protagonist asks the player to guide a distinct personality.

The relationship should match the game's story and control. Forcing choices that contradict an established character or withholding actions implied by the role can make the player feel detached from both avatar and plot.
