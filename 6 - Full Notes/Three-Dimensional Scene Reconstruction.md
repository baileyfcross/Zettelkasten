# Three-Dimensional Scene Reconstruction

Three-dimensional scene reconstruction builds a geometrically plausible arrangement of cameras, objects, support surfaces, and bodies that could project to the observed photograph. Known sizes, contact points, articulation limits, and perspective cues turn otherwise ambiguous image measurements into testable spatial relationships.

The method can reveal that a person would need an implausible size or pose to occupy the claimed location. Its conclusion depends on the assumed dimensions and camera model, so sensitivity analysis is needed to show that the inconsistency persists across reasonable alternatives.
