# Rotini Fin Geometry

Rotini fin geometry twists an extended surface along its length, creating a helical or pasta-like form with greater surface complexity than a straight cylindrical or rectangular fin. A parameterized sweep or comparable construction controls pitch, cross section, length, and total twist.

The twist changes surface area and heat-flow paths while making meshing more demanding. Equal-volume comparison with a simpler fin separates geometric distribution from the trivial effect of adding more material.
