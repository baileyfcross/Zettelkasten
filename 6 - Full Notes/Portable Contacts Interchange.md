# Portable Contacts Interchange

Portable Contacts attempted to provide a common way to exchange address-book and social-contact data. Plaxo developed it around the idea that users should be able to carry a set of digital business cards between services instead of recreating relationships inside every provider.

The source presents commercial resistance and incompatible provider APIs as reasons the broader effort faded, while vCard remained in use as a contact format. The history illustrates a recurring limit of social interoperability: a technically useful schema does not become portable if dominant platforms decline to expose or import their data. [[Friend of a Friend Social Graph]] addressed relationships, while [[OpenSocial Gadget Standard]] combined contact ideas with cross-provider applications.
