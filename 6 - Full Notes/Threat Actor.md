# Threat Actor

A threat actor is a person, group, or organization capable of directing harmful activity against a system. Actors differ in goals, resources, access, persistence, and tolerance for risk, so the same technical weakness may attract very different adversaries.

Understanding motive and capability informs [[Threat Assessment]] and helps interpret evidence during [[Cyberattack Attribution]]. Defenses should address plausible actors rather than relying on a single generic image of an attacker.
