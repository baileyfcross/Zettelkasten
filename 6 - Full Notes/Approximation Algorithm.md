# Approximation Algorithm

An approximation algorithm seeks a solution that may not be optimal but is acceptably close to the optimum. It is useful when an exact solution exists mathematically yet the required [[Computational Complexity]] makes exact search impractical.

The tradeoff is deliberate rather than accidental. For an [[Intractable Problem]] such as the [[Traveling Salesman Problem]], a near-optimal route found in reasonable time can be more useful than an exact method that cannot finish on realistic input.
