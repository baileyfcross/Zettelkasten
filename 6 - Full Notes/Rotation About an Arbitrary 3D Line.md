# Rotation About an Arbitrary 3D Line

Rotation about an arbitrary spatial line is reduced to rotation about a principal axis. A known point on the line is translated to the origin, then principal-axis rotations align the line's direction with an axis. The desired rotation is applied using one of the [[3D Axis Rotation Matrices]].

The alignment rotations and translation are then inverted in reverse order to restore the line to its original position and orientation. Direction cosines derived from the line endpoints supply the alignment angles. The full product rotates objects around the specified line without changing the line itself.
