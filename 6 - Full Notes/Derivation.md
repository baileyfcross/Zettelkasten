# Derivation

A derivation is a sequence of [[Production Rule|production-rule]] applications that begins with a grammar's start symbol and produces another string. A terminal-only result belongs to the language generated by the grammar.

A left derivation always replaces the leftmost nonterminal, while a right derivation always replaces the rightmost. Restricting the order removes irrelevant differences and makes ambiguity and parsing procedures easier to analyze through a [[Parse Tree]].
