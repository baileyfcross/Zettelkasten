# Low-Quality Image Placeholder

A low-quality image placeholder transfers a tiny, heavily compressed or reduced-resolution preview before the final image. It reserves visual space and communicates the composition while the higher-quality asset loads.

The placeholder adds its own bytes and processing, so its value comes from improving perceived completeness rather than reducing the final image cost. It can be embedded or fetched, blurred or scaled, but should not compete significantly with critical resources. See [[WebP Progressive Loading Tradeoff]].
