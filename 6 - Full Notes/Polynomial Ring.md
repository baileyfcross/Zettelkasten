# Polynomial Ring

A polynomial ring K[x₁,…,xₙ] consists of finite sums of monomials whose coefficients lie in a base ring or [[Field]]. Addition combines coefficients and multiplication uses the exponent laws, making the collection a [[Commutative Ring]].

When the coefficients form a field, the polynomial ring is an [[Integral Domain]]. Its ideals encode systems of polynomial equations and can be studied computationally through a [[Gröbner Basis]].
