# Stacked Autoencoder

A stacked autoencoder builds a deep representation by placing several encoders in sequence. Layers may be pretrained one at a time as autoencoders before the entire stack is fine-tuned.

Successive hidden layers transform the input into increasingly abstract features. Bottlenecks, sparsity, denoising, and supervised objectives can be combined to shape the learned representation.
