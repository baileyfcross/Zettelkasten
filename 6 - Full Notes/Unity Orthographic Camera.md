# Unity Orthographic Camera

A Unity orthographic camera renders objects without perspective foreshortening: an object's apparent size does not shrink merely because it is farther from the camera. This makes it a common choice for flat games and diagram-like views.

Orthographic Size controls half the vertical span visible in world units, so increasing it shows more of the scene and decreasing it shows less. Depth can still organize objects, but [[Unity Sorting Layer|sorting layers]] are the clearer mechanism for deciding which sprites appear in front.
