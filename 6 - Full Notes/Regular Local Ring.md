# Regular Local Ring

A Noetherian local ring (R,m,K) is regular when its Krull dimension equals the vector-space dimension of m/m² over the [[Residue Field]] K. Equivalently, the maximal ideal needs exactly dim(R) generators.

Those generators form a [[Regular System of Parameters]]. The homological characterization says R is regular exactly when its residue field has finite [[Projective Dimension]].
