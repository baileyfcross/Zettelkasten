# Medical Ultrasound Imaging

Medical ultrasound imaging sends short high-frequency sound pulses into the body and detects echoes from boundaries with different acoustic impedance. Echo delay determines depth, while echo amplitude contributes contrast. Coupling gel removes air between transducer and skin, and electronic arrays steer and focus the beam without ionizing radiation.
