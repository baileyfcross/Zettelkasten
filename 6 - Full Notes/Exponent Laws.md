# Exponent Laws

Exponent laws summarize repeated multiplication. For a nonzero base, $a^ma^n=a^{m+n}$, $a^m/a^n=a^{m-n}$, $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$, and $a^{-n}=1/a^n$. These rules preserve the base while changing how many factors remain, and they extend naturally to [[Scientific Notation]] and [[Rational Exponent]]s.
