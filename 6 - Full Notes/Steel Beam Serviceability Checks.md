# Steel Beam Serviceability Checks

Steel beam serviceability limits deformation and response under unfactored or service-level loads. Vertical deflection can damage ceilings, partitions, glazing, roofing, drainage, or sensitive finishes; it can also create poor appearance or interfere with equipment. Limits are commonly expressed as a fraction of span and may distinguish live-load deflection from total-load deflection.

Serviceability is not automatically satisfied by a strength-efficient section because stiffness depends on elastic modulus and moment of inertia rather than yield strength alone. Long-span, shallow beams can be strong yet too flexible. Camber can offset predictable dead-load deflection but does not increase stiffness or solve vibration and ponding. The check must use the appropriate load duration, construction stage, composite action, and support assumptions.
