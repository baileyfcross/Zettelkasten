# Chinese Room Argument

The Chinese room argument challenges the claim that executing a formal program is sufficient for understanding. A person who does not know Chinese could follow rules for transforming Chinese symbols and produce convincing answers without knowing what any symbol means. The room would exhibit correct syntax without semantic understanding.

Applied to AI, the argument distinguishes successful symbol manipulation from intentionality and meaning. Critics respond that understanding might belong to the larger system rather than the person following instructions, or that mental activity itself can be explained computationally. The argument therefore identifies a contested assumption behind claims about [[Artificial General Intelligence]] rather than offering a simple performance test.
