# Polynomial Least Squares

Polynomial least squares fits data with a polynomial while minimizing squared residuals rather than requiring exact interpolation.

The unknown polynomial coefficients enter linearly, so powers of each input form the columns of a design matrix and the coefficients follow from a [[Linear Least Squares]] problem. The polynomial degree controls flexibility.
