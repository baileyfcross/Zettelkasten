# Health Data Swapping

Health data swapping exchanges selected attribute values among records before publication. Marginal distributions can remain similar even though the released combinations no longer correspond exactly to the original individuals.

The protection depends on which values are swapped, how often, and within what strata. Too little swapping leaves linkable records; too much can destroy multivariable relationships or create implausible combinations. Because an analyst cannot infer privacy from the released table alone, the publisher should document the mechanism and evaluate the resulting [[Privacy-Utility Tradeoff]].
