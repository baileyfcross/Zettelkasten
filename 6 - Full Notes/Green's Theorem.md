# Green's Theorem

Green's theorem converts a positively oriented line integral around the boundary of a planar region into a double integral over the region. For a one-form $\omega=P\,dx+Q\,dy$,

$$\oint_{\partial M}\omega=\iint_M\left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)dx\,dy.$$

It is the two-dimensional one-form case of the [[Generalized Stokes Theorem]] and links boundary circulation to accumulated interior rotation.
