# Kotlin Kandy Visualization

Kandy builds visualizations from Kotlin data in a notebook. A plot maps DataFrame columns to visual roles such as the x-axis, y-axis, color, or fill, then selects a layer such as lines, points, or bars.

The book's temperature example uses month and temperature for position and city for color, allowing several series to be compared in one chart. The visualization therefore depends on an explicit mapping from the data schema to marks and visual encodings rather than on the chart type alone.
