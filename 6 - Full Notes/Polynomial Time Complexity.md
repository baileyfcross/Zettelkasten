# Polynomial Time Complexity

Polynomial time complexity has the form $O(n^k)$ for some constant exponent $k$. These algorithms are generally considered tractable because their growth, although sometimes steep, remains far below [[Exponential Time Complexity]] for large inputs.

Finding a polynomial-time solution is often a practical success. Selection sort's quadratic $O(n^2)$ behavior is polynomial, though faster loglinear sorting algorithms make it unattractive for very large collections.
