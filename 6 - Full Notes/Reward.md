# Reward

A reward is positive feedback that pulls a player toward an action, goal, or further engagement. It can mark a milestone, confirm that the player is on a productive path, or open new parts of the game's [[Possibility Space]].

The feeling of reward depends on the effort and context leading to it, not only on celebratory images or sound. Reward signals are most meaningful at significant moments rather than after every routine action.
