# Parametric Sweep in COMSOL

A parametric sweep repeats a COMSOL study over a declared list or range of parameter values. It can reveal how geometry, source strength, ambient conditions, or a heat-transfer coefficient changes the solution without manual model duplication.

The sweep produces a family of solutions that can be plotted or evaluated consistently. Parameter ranges should reflect plausible conditions, and outputs should be chosen in advance so the sweep supports [[Thermal Management Goals|an engineering question]] rather than generating unstructured variation.
