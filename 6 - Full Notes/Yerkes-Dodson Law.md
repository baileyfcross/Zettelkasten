# Yerkes-Dodson Law

The Yerkes-Dodson law describes performance as rising with arousal to an optimal point and then falling as arousal becomes excessive. More complex tasks tend to reach their optimum at a lower level of arousal than simpler tasks.

Growing skill can expand the range a player tolerates, but continuously high intensity produces fatigue. Alternating high- and low-arousal loops helps maintain [[Engagement]] without overwhelming the player's [[Interactivity Budget]].
