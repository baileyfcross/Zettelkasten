# Elliptical Orbits and Kepler's Laws

Kepler's first law describes an orbit as an ellipse with the parent body at one focus. The second law states that the line from parent to orbiter sweeps equal areas in equal times, so orbital speed varies around the ellipse. The third law relates the square of orbital period to the cube of orbital size, connecting geometry to [[Orbital Velocity and Period]].
