# Nuclear Fusion

Nuclear fusion joins light nuclei into heavier products and releases energy when the product mass is lower than the reactants' mass. The source relates the difference to $\Delta E=\Delta mc^2$. Useful terrestrial power requires nuclei energetic enough to overcome repulsion, a hot confined plasma with enough reactions, and equipment to capture the released heat. This is distinct from splitting heavy nuclei in fission.
