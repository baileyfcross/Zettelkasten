# Systemic Game Design

Systemic game design creates a desired player experience by moving among the whole, the feedback loops that produce it, and the parts that implement those loops. It uses [[Systems Thinking]] to decompose and recompose the game without losing either level.

The designer directly specifies parts and relationships, then iterates through prototypes and playtests to observe the emergent [[Game-Player System]]. This makes design a [[Designer Loop]] rather than a one-pass act of specification.
