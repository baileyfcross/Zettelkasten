# Systemic Reward

A systemic reward feeds back into the playable system by enabling new actions or opportunities. It may grant a new [[Game Verb]], provide a [[Resource]], reveal new objects, or unlock a new area.

Because it changes what the player can do next, a systemic reward connects past accomplishment to future play. It differs from a [[Dead-End Reward]], which may be pleasurable but does not alter the system being played.
