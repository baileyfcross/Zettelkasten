# Neural Feature Visualization

Feature visualization seeks inputs that strongly trigger a selected behavior inside a neural network, such as a hidden unit's activation. Kelleher describes starting with a generated input and iteratively changing it until the chosen response increases, then viewing the result to investigate what the unit may detect.

The method can make learned representations more inspectable than raw weights alone. However, the constructed input is evidence about the model's response, not necessarily a typical real-world example or a full explanation of the model's final decision. It complements [[Neural Attribution Map|attribution]] and other views of the [[Neural Network Explainability Gap]].
