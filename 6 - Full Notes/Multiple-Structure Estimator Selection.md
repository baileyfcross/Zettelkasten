# Multiple-Structure Estimator Selection

Multiple-structure estimator selection chooses among procedures designed for different possible organizations of the signal, such as coordinate sparsity, group sparsity, or variation sparsity. It treats the structural class itself as unknown rather than tuning only one estimator inside a fixed class.

Candidate model families and their weights can be combined into one selection criterion. An oracle-style guarantee then compares the result with the best estimator across all included structures. This approach formalizes adaptation but remains limited by whether the candidate collection contains a useful approximation to the data-generating signal.
