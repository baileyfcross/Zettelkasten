# Gene Sequencing Accelerator

A gene sequencing accelerator moves compute-intensive sequence operations from a general CPU to a parallel platform. The book's FPGA prototype implements KMP and BWA matching as hardware cores controlled by host software.

The accelerator is useful when repeated pattern searches dominate execution, but its end-to-end value includes preprocessing, data transfer, driver overhead, and the portion of the workflow that remains on the processor.
