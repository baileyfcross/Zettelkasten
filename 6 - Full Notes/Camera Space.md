# Camera Space

Camera space expresses world geometry relative to the camera as though the camera were at the origin with a standard orientation. The view transformation effectively moves and rotates the world by the inverse of the camera's transform.

This common viewpoint makes projection consistent. Geometry proceeds from camera space into [[Projection Space]] before it is mapped to the screen.
