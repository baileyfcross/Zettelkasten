# Water-Gas Shift Reaction

The water-gas shift reaction converts carbon monoxide and steam into carbon dioxide and additional hydrogen: $CO+H_2O\rightarrow CO_2+H_2$. In the source's natural-gas hydrogen route, catalytic high- and lower-temperature stages reduce CO before CO2 scrubbing. This step increases recovered hydrogen and changes the gas composition; it does not create hydrogen without an upstream feedstock and heat supply.
