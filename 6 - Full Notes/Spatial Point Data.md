# Spatial Point Data

Spatial point data represent features whose relevant location can be expressed by a coordinate pair at the map's scale. Stations, cities, and sampled observations are typical examples. Attributes attached to each point may be encoded by a [[Proportional Symbol Map]] or used as inputs to [[Point-Surface Interpolation]]. The coordinates are interpretable only with a [[Coordinate Reference System]] that states how they relate to the Earth.
