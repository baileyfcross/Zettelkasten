# Blender Scene Workflow

Blender's start-file categories are workflows as well as collections of workspaces. Their tabs are ordered roughly in the sequence an artist follows, so moving across the interface can mirror the progression from layout and creation to output.

The General workflow begins in Layout because arranging the scene comes early in a typical 3D process. Workspaces can be renamed after customization, and descriptive names become important when a project is reopened after time away.

The source's final project illustrates a scene workflow as a composition of previously learned systems: model and shade a landscape and dome, append an aircraft, shape a curve path, animate traversal, then layer particle-driven fragmentation and smoke-fire simulation at chosen frames. The scene becomes complex through relationships between reusable components rather than one monolithic operation.
