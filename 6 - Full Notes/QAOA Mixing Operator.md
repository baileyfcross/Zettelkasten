# QAOA Mixing Operator

A QAOA mixer moves amplitude among candidate solutions between applications of the cost-dependent phase separator. The standard choice is generated by a transverse field, $H_M=\sum_j X_j$, giving $U_M(\beta)=e^{-i\beta H_M}$ and allowing independent bit flips.

The standard mixer explores the full bit-string space and can leave a constrained feasible set. The quantum alternating-operator formulation replaces it with a problem-specific unitary that preserves feasibility while still connecting the feasible configurations needed for search.
