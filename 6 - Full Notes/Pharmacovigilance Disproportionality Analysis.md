# Pharmacovigilance Disproportionality Analysis

Pharmacovigilance disproportionality analysis tests whether a drug-event pair is reported more often than expected relative to other reports in a [[Spontaneous Reporting System]]. A contingency table compares reports with and without the drug and event.

A high disproportionality score is a signal, not an estimate of population risk. Reporting biases, co-medications, indication, and media attention can change the table. Minimum-count rules and uncertainty intervals help avoid unstable signals, while clinical review determines whether the association merits deeper study.
