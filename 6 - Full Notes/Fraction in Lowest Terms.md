# Fraction in Lowest Terms

A fraction is in lowest terms when its [[Numerator]] and [[Denominator]] share no whole-number factor greater than one. It is the reduced representative of its family of [[Equivalent Fraction]]s with the smallest positive denominator.

Reduction divides both components by common factors until none remain. For example,

$$\frac{104}{120}=\frac{26}{30}=\frac{13}{15}.$$

Different valid cancellation sequences reach the same reduced form. This uniqueness depends on the fact that natural numbers have unique prime factorizations.
