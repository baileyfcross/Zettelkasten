# VOR Navigation

Very High Frequency Omnidirectional Range navigation lets an aircraft determine its bearing, or radial, relative to a ground station. The station transmits a reference signal and a direction-dependent rotating pattern; their phase relationship identifies direction from the facility. Networks of VOR stations became waypoints for established air routes, but their VHF signals remain limited largely to line of sight and therefore depend on ground infrastructure and terrain clearance.
