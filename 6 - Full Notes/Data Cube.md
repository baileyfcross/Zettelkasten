# Data Cube

A data cube is a multidimensional arrangement of measures indexed by analytic dimensions. It makes common summaries and cross-sections efficient to retrieve from a large body of organizational data.

Users can slice, dice, pivot, drill down, or roll up the cube to explore patterns at different resolutions. These operations guide questions and preparation but do not replace statistical or machine-learning validation.
