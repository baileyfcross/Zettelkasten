# COMSOL Geometry Units

COMSOL geometry settings define length and angular units for interpreting dimensions, offsets, rotations, and measurements. These settings give numerical coordinates physical scale and must agree with imported files, parameters, and the quantities used by physics interfaces.

Changing a displayed unit is not a substitute for verifying scale. Known dimensions should be measured after import or rebuild because an apparently plausible shape can be uniformly wrong by orders of magnitude.
