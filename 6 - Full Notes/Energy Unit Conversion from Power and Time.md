# Energy Unit Conversion from Power and Time

Energy can be expressed as power accumulated over time. Pryor converts kilowatt-hours to joules by changing kilowatts to watts and hours to seconds, leaving watt-seconds, which equal joules. The factor chain produces $3.6\times10^6\ \mathrm{J}$ for one kW·h. This illustrates why a time-bearing energy unit is not converted with a power factor alone. The book also lists other energy, work, and heat units in the same category. See [[Power Rate Unit Conversion]] and [[Conversion-Factor Chains]].
