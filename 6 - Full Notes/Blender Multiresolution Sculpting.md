# Blender Multiresolution Sculpting

The Multiresolution modifier supplies several subdivision levels for one sculpt. Lower levels support broad proportional changes, while higher levels expose enough geometry for fine detail.

This avoids permanently subdividing the entire base mesh at the outset. High levels still consume substantial memory, so the usable detail is constrained by system resources and the cost of storing dense geometry and undo history.
