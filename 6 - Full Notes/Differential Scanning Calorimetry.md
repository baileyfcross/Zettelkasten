# Differential Scanning Calorimetry

Differential scanning calorimetry compares the heat required to drive a sample and a reference through the same temperature program. The difference reveals heat capacity as a function of temperature and can expose energetic transitions.

Differential temperature analysis uses a related comparison but records temperature differences to identify endothermic and exothermic behavior. The resulting measurements can populate the [[COMSOL Analytic and Interpolation Functions|functions]] used for realistic thermal properties and phase behavior.
