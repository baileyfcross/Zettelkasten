# Klein-Gordon Probability Current

The Klein-Gordon current is a conserved four-current constructed from a complex scalar field and its spacetime derivatives. The Klein-Gordon equation and its complex conjugate combine to give a continuity equation for this current.

Unlike the nonrelativistic probability density, its time component is not guaranteed to be positive. This prevents a straightforward interpretation as a one-particle probability density and instead supports interpreting the conserved quantity as a charge density in relativistic field theory.
