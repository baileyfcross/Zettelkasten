# Curl of a Vector Field

The curl of a vector field is a vector measure of local circulation. Its direction follows the axis of infinitesimal rotation according to the right-hand rule, while its magnitude records the circulation density about that axis.

A field with zero curl in an appropriate simply connected region can be represented as the gradient of a scalar potential. Stokes theorem relates the surface integral of curl to the circulation of the original field around the surface boundary.
