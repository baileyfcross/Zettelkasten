# Framed Beam Shear Connections

A framed beam connection transfers an end reaction through the supported beam web while permitting enough rotation to be modeled as simple. Common details include double angles, single plates, and seated connections using shop welds and field bolts in various arrangements. Although called shear connections, their geometry can produce eccentricity and localized web demands.

Design checks the bolts or welds, connected plate or angles, supported beam web, supporting member, block shear, bearing, tearout, and any cope. The connection must also fit erection clearances and avoid excessive restraint that contradicts the analysis assumption. Standard manual tables accelerate selection, but their limits on material, geometry, hole type, edge distance, and loading must match the actual detail before tabulated capacity is used.
