# Deformation-Oriented Edge Flow

Deformation-oriented edge flow directs mesh loops along the forms and movements that an animated surface must express. Loops around the mouth or eyes can expand and compress with those features, whereas an unrelated horizontal-and-vertical grid tends to pinch or produce a boxlike expression.

The correct flow is therefore defined partly by intended motion, not only by the rest shape. A topology review should test likely poses and expressions early, when redirecting loops is less costly than repairing a fully textured and rigged character.
