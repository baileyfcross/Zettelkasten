# Complete Game Prototype

A complete game prototype embodies the entire intended play experience from entry through completion, including all major systems and representative presentation. It exists to evaluate the design as a whole before full production treats its structure as settled.

Complete does not mean shippable. Assets and implementation may remain provisional, but a tester should be able to encounter the complete sequence and relationships whose design must be validated.
