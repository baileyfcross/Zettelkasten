# Heat Transfer in Solids and Fluids Interface

A heat-transfer interface spanning solids and fluids uses conduction in every participating domain and adds fluid energy transport where motion exists. Domain-specific material properties and sources enter one shared temperature formulation.

Used alone, the interface can approximate a fluid as a conducting domain or accept prescribed velocity information. Coupling it to a flow interface creates [[Conjugate Heat Transfer Coupling|conjugate heat transfer]] in which velocity and temperature are solved consistently.
