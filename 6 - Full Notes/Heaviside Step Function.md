# Heaviside Step Function

The Heaviside step function switches between two constant values at a chosen point, usually zero before the point and one after it. It provides a compact way to turn a forcing term or signal on at a specified coordinate or time.

In the generalized-function sense, its derivative is the Dirac delta. Shifted and combined step functions can represent piecewise functions, making them especially useful in transform methods for discontinuous inputs and initial-value problems.
