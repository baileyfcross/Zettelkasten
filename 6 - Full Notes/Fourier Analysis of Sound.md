# Fourier Analysis of Sound

Fourier analysis represents a complex sound as a combination of sinusoidal frequency components. A spectrum shows how intensity or amplitude is distributed among the fundamental and harmonics. Fourier synthesis reverses the process by combining selected components, explaining why instruments playing the same pitch can have different timbres.
