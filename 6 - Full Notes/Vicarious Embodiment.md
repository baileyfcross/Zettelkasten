# Vicarious Embodiment

Vicarious embodiment is the acquisition of grounded knowledge from records of other agents interacting with the world. Video, language, sensor traces, and action histories can let a system learn regularities that an embodied learner would otherwise have to discover through its own body.

This is not identical to direct [[Embodied Intelligence]], because the system does not bear the risks or needs that organized the original experience. It nevertheless offers a route for a disembodied AI to connect symbols with patterns of perception, action, and consequence at a scale no single body could encounter.
