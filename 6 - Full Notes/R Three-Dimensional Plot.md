# R Three-Dimensional Plot

An R three-dimensional plot projects points or a surface defined by three coordinates onto a two-dimensional device using a chosen viewing angle and scale. It can reveal a plane, curved response, or spatial path that is difficult to recognize from a single pairwise view.

Perspective can also hide points and distort distance. Rotated views, pairwise projections, or contours should accompany the 3D image when the conclusion depends on structure that one camera position may conceal.
