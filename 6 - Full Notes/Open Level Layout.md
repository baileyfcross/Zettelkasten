# Open Level Layout

An open level layout gives the player largely unconstrained movement across a broad space. Obstacles and restricted regions may exist, but the player is not forced through one dominant route.

Open layouts support exploration and strategic positioning while making sequence and pacing harder to control. Landmarks, goals, terrain, and information must help players choose direction without requiring a fixed path.
