# Rumor Source

A rumor source is the node from which a particular diffusion process begins. It may be the creator of the claim or simply the earliest origin represented inside the observed network.

The source is usually hidden once only a later [[Infection Snapshot]] is available. Missing nodes, delayed observation, heterogeneous transmission, and multiple origins make reconstruction uncertain.
