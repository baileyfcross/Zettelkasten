# Big Data Competition Risk

Big data competition risk arises when control of extensive, difficult-to-reproduce datasets strengthens market power or creates barriers to entry. Data can be an input whose value increases when it is combined with infrastructure, analytical capability, and access to users.

The competitive effect depends on substitutability, collection costs, network effects, and whether rivals can obtain data of comparable scope and quality.
