# DEM Terrain Displacement for VFX

A digital elevation model stores sampled elevation in a grid whose values can drive displacement of a subdivided plane. A dark-looking DEM may hold useful values in a narrow range, so levels can reveal its heights before it is applied. Subdivision must be increased cautiously: too little looks jagged, while excessive detail burdens the machine. The book notes that radar-derived elevation may represent the first reflected surface, including treetops or buildings, rather than bare ground. A matching aerial image can texture the resulting terrain. See [[Manual Perspective-Plane Scene Extraction]] for another source-image-to-scene method.
