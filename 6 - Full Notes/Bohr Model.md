# Bohr Model

The Bohr model places the hydrogen electron in discrete stationary orbits with quantized angular momentum. Radiation is emitted or absorbed only during transitions, with photon energy equal to the difference between allowed levels. The model explains the hydrogen line spectrum but is superseded by the wavefunction description for general atoms.
