# Navigation

Navigation combines the motor and cognitive work of moving through an environment. Its motor component is [[3D Travel]], and its cognitive component is [[Wayfinding]].

Travel changes the viewpoint or location. Wayfinding determines where to go and how to get there. An efficient travel method is insufficient when the user lacks the [[Spatial Knowledge]] needed to choose a route, while a good route plan is useless without a controllable way to move.

Designers can integrate the two through maps, landmarks, view overviews, or techniques that reinforce a [[Cognitive Map]] while the user travels.
