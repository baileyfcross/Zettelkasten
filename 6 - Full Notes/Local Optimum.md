# Local Optimum

A local optimum is a solution better than all solutions reachable by one neighboring move, but not necessarily the best solution over the entire problem space. It resembles a plateau or smaller peak that appears final from the immediate surroundings.

Methods such as [[Hill Climbing]] and a [[Greedy Algorithm]] are vulnerable to this distinction because they make choices from local information. Reaching the [[Global Optimum]] may require a move that temporarily appears worse.
