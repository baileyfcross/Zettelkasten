# Takeoff and Landing Performance Planning

Runway planning uses aircraft weight, pressure altitude, temperature, wind, slope, surface, flap setting, braking, and obstacle requirements to estimate takeoff and landing distance. The calculated requirement must be compared with available runway length while preserving a practical margin. Current runway condition can make a nominally adequate length unusable, linking the check to [[Runway Friction and Crosswind Limits]].
