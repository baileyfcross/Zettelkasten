# Fourier Sine Series

A Fourier sine series expands a function using only sine modes. Since sine is odd, this is the natural Fourier representation of an odd periodic function.

For data given on a half interval, an odd extension produces a sine series without requiring the original function to be odd on its domain. Sine modes often arise in separated boundary-value problems where the field itself must vanish at both endpoints.
