# Cloud Asset Optimization

Cloud asset optimization reduces the need to own computing capacity sized for a workload's highest anticipated demand. Permanently overprovisioned equipment consumes capital, space, power, and maintenance even when business activity is low.

Elastic services can supply capacity during peaks and release it as a product declines. The organization then avoids being left with obsolete or unnecessary infrastructure whose life cycle no longer matches the product it supports.
