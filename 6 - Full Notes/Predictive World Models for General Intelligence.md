# Predictive World Models for General Intelligence

A predictive world model represents how situations change and what consequences are likely to follow an action. Learning such a model turns accumulated data into a basis for planning rather than merely recognizing patterns in the present.

Prediction alone is not agency. An [[Artificial General Intelligence]] also needs goals and a method for comparing possible actions by their expected consequences. In this architecture, learning builds the model and optimization searches it for a course of action that scores well under a [[Reinforcement Learning Reward Function]].
