# Event Timeline

An event timeline orders [[Domain Event]]s according to how facts occur in a business process. Sequence shows necessary precedence, while parallel branches show activities that can proceed independently. Building the timeline can reveal that one apparent process crosses several systems or organizational areas. The ordering is a reasoning tool rather than a decorative chronology: participants can ask what happens before, after, or when an expected event fails to occur.
