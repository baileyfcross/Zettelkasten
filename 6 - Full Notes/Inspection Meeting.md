# Inspection Meeting

An inspection meeting is a time-bounded session in which prepared reviewers follow a reader's paraphrase and report defects to a recorder. The moderator maintains the pace and scope, while the author answers clarification questions without directing the interpretation.

Defect repair is deferred to later rework so the session remains a search activity. If all material cannot be inspected within the meeting limit, the remainder is scheduled separately instead of being rushed.
