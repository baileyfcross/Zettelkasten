# Recursive Language

A recursive language is a synonym for a [[Turing-Decidable Language]]. Membership is determined by a computation that always halts with either acceptance or rejection.

A language is recursive exactly when both it and its complement are [[Recursively Enumerable Language|recursively enumerable]]. Running their accepting machines in alternating steps guarantees that one halts and reveals the correct decision for each input.
