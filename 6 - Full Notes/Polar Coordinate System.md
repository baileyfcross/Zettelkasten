# Polar Coordinate System

The polar coordinate system locates a point by its distance $r$ from an origin and an angle $\theta$ from a reference direction. Conversion to Cartesian coordinates uses $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y=r\sin\theta$, while distance and angle recover the polar representation.

Polar equations are natural for circular and orbital shapes whose radius varies with direction. R can evaluate the radius over an angle sequence, convert the coordinates, and plot the resulting path on equal axis scales.
