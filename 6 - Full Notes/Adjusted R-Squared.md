# Adjusted R-Squared

Adjusted R-squared estimates the proportion of outcome variation explained by a linear model while penalizing the inclusion of additional predictors. Unlike ordinary R-squared, it need not increase when an unhelpful term is added.

It can help compare linear models fit to the same outcome data, but a larger value does not establish better causal control or validate assumptions. A low value may still accompany a meaningful estimate when individual outcomes are inherently variable.
