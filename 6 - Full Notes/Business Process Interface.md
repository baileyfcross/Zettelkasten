# Business Process Interface

A business process interface defines how one process interacts with another. It identifies the information shared, the event or control signal that starts the exchange, the expected result, and any timing or service conditions.

Most processes are not independent. A payroll process, for example, needs changes from human resources and approved overtime from management, so its interfaces must be specified before the process can be outsourced or automated safely.
