# Redundant Fuel Systems in Aircraft Conversion

The Wilga conversion increased fuel capacity and distributed tanks into structures around the landing-gear legs. Electrical pumps in the gear-leg assemblies moved fuel toward a mechanical pump near the engine, providing redundancy rather than a single transfer path. Reserve placement also had to leave the aircraft inside its legal center-of-gravity range as fuel was consumed.
