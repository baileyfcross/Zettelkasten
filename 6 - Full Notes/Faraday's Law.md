# Faraday's Law

Faraday's law states that an induced electromotive force equals the negative rate of change of magnetic flux linkage:

$$\mathcal{E}=-\frac{d(N\Phi)}{dt}.$$

Flux can change through field strength, area, orientation, or relative motion. Faster change or more turns produces a larger induced emf.
