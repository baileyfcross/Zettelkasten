# Dynamic Time Warping

Dynamic time warping aligns two time sequences that express a similar pattern at different speeds. Early speech recognizers used it to compare an input word against stored acoustic templates despite differences in speaking rate.

Template matching worked best for small, often speaker-dependent vocabularies. Statistical sequence models such as the [[Hidden Markov Model]] later provided a more scalable treatment of variation.
