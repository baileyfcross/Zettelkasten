# Platform Composability

Platform composability is the ability to connect and replace components through stable interfaces with little custom translation and minimal effect on the rest of the platform. It counters architectures in which scripts, serverless functions, or undocumented data transformations permanently bind one tool to another.

A composable component can be orchestrated in the target environment, integrated through documented interfaces, configured through automation, and observed well enough to operate independently. Composability does not remove dependencies; it makes them explicit so that specialized teams can evolve their capabilities without forcing the entire platform to change at once.
