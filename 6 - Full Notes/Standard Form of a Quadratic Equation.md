# Standard Form of a Quadratic Equation

The standard form of a quadratic equation is

$$ax^2+bx+c=0,$$

with (a\neq0). Terms are collected on one side in descending powers, and the other side is zero.

Standard form exposes the coefficients used by the [[Quadratic Formula]] and permits factorization to use the [[Zero Product Property]]. An equation such as (8x^2-2x=15) becomes (8x^2-2x-15=0). Distributing, combining like terms, or clearing denominators may be required before the form is visible.
