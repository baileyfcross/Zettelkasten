# Unity Terrain Texture Painting

Unity terrain texture painting blends surface layers across a [[Unity Terrain]]. Instead of assigning one uniform appearance, an author can paint materials such as grass, soil, rock, or sand where they visually fit the landscape.

Convincing terrain depends on how layers respond to form: steep areas often read differently from flat ground, and abrupt repetition reveals the underlying texture. Painting is therefore both an artistic and structural pass performed after broad sculpting and refined as the level is playtested from the player's viewpoint.
