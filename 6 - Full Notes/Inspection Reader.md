# Inspection Reader

An inspection reader paraphrases the work product during a formal inspection while the other participants follow the material and report defects. Restating what the code or document does forces an interpretation that can expose ambiguity between the artifact and its intended behavior.

The reader is distinct from the author so the meeting does not depend solely on the creator's explanation. The author may clarify facts, but the reader's independent account helps reveal what another qualified person can actually understand from the artifact.
