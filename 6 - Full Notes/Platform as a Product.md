# Platform as a Product

Platform as a product treats an internal delivery platform as a service designed for identifiable users rather than a one-time infrastructure project. A platform team discovers developer needs, defines supported journeys, measures adoption and outcomes, and maintains a lifecycle and roadmap.

This mindset prevents central tooling from becoming a mandatory collection of features that does not reduce delivery friction. The [[Internal Developer Platform]] should provide coherent paved paths while accepting feedback and evolving like any other product.

Unlike a deadline-bound project, a platform must continue adapting to user demand, security patches, vendor changes, and the evolution of its own components. Its purpose, hypotheses, adoption evidence, and roadmap remain active after the first release, and operations are part of the product lifecycle rather than a reduced maintenance phase.
