# Sweep Surface

A sweep surface is formed by moving a generator curve along a directing path. One parameter traverses the generator and another advances it along the path, allowing the construction to describe shapes such as extrusions, tubes, ducts, and springs.

The frame attached to the path determines how the cross-section translates and rotates during the sweep. A straight path produces a translational extrusion; rotating a profile around an axis produces the special case [[Surface of Revolution]]. When a closed planar domain rather than only its boundary is swept, the operation constructs a solid.
