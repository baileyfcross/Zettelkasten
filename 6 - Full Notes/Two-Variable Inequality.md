# Two-Variable Inequality

A two-variable inequality describes a region of the Cartesian plane. Its boundary is the graph obtained by replacing the inequality sign with equality. A solid boundary includes points satisfying equality, while a dashed boundary excludes them. Testing a point on one side identifies the half-plane or more general region that satisfies the inequality.
