# Robot Structure Design

A robot's structure must support its sensors, processors, power source, payload, and actuators while remaining strong, stable, and appropriate to its task. Added size can produce a disproportionate increase in mass, which then requires larger motors, more energy, and stronger supporting members.

Human-facing structure also communicates. Shape, color, moving parts, and visible affordances can signal where a person should stand or how the robot may move. Structural design therefore balances mechanics, power, safety, maintainability, economics, and social interpretation rather than merely enclosing components.
