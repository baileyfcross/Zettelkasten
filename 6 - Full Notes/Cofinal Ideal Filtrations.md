# Cofinal Ideal Filtrations

Two decreasing chains of ideals are cofinal when every term of either chain contains some term of the other. They eventually probe the same neighborhoods even if their indices differ.

Cofinal powers produce the same direct-limit local cohomology. In particular, replacing an ideal by another with the same radical does not change the resulting [[Local Cohomology]] modules.
