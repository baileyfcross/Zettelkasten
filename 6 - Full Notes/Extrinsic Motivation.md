# Extrinsic Motivation

Extrinsic motivation drives an activity through a reward or consequence separate from the activity itself. Points, badges, grades, virtual goods, and unlocks can all make behavior instrumental: the player acts in order to obtain something else.

External incentives can direct attention and start a behavior, but they can also displace [[Intrinsic Motivation]] when the reward becomes the reason for acting. The distinction matters most when the game expects participation to continue after the reward is removed.
