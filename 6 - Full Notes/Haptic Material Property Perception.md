# Haptic Material Property Perception

Haptic material property perception identifies qualities such as texture, compliance, temperature, friction, and weight through coordinated touch and movement. These properties are often more efficiently judged haptically than geometric properties because direct contact exposes deformation, heat flow, vibration, and resistance.

Each property favors a different [[Haptic Exploratory Procedure]]: lateral motion for texture, pressure for compliance, static contact for temperature, and unsupported holding for weight. Material judgments can interact, so temperature, surface friction, shape, and volume may bias the perceived heaviness of an otherwise identical object.
