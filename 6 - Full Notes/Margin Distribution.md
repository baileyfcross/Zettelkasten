# Margin Distribution

In ensemble classification, a margin expresses how much support the correct class receives over competing classes. The margin distribution across examples describes both the strength and reliability of the ensemble rather than only its average accuracy.

Random-forest optimization can seek to improve these margins, especially for observations near the decision boundary.
