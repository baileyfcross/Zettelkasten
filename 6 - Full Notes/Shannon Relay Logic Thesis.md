# Shannon Relay Logic Thesis

Claude Shannon showed that Boolean algebra could describe networks of on-off relays. Logical expressions and electrical switching were therefore two representations of the same control relationships.

His 1938 work supplied a theoretical foundation for designing digital circuits. It transformed ad hoc switching practice into a systematic method for combining binary logic at scale.
