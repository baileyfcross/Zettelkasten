# Fundamental Parallelogram

For two [[Linear Independence|linearly independent]] planar vectors $u$ and $v$, the fundamental parallelogram is the set

$$\{au+bv:0\leq a\leq1,\ 0\leq b\leq1\}.$$

It is the region spanned by allowing each coefficient in the [[Linear Combination]] to range through one unit interval. Translations by integer combinations of $u$ and $v$ tile the plane with copies of this region.
