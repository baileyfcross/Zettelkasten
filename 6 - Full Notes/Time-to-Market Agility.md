# Time-to-Market Agility

Time-to-market agility is the ability to make a product or service available sooner by reducing the delay required to obtain and prepare computing environments. On-demand infrastructure and platforms can replace part of the procurement and installation sequence.

The benefit can be expressed as elapsed time or as revenue available during the months gained. Speed has value only if the service still meets its functional, security, continuity, and quality requirements.
