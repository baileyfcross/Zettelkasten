# CSS Flex Items

Flex items are the boxes generated by the direct children of a [[CSS Flex Container]]. They can be reordered, aligned, and flexed independently, and their margins do not collapse with one another or with the container.

An item's automatic minimum size can prevent it from shrinking below its content unless sizing or overflow rules change that constraint. Auto margins can absorb free space before alignment properties distribute what remains.
