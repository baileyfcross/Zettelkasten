# Radio Direction Finding

Radio direction finding estimates the bearing to a transmitter whose location is known. An early aircraft receiver could rotate a loop antenna until the received signal reached a null, using the antenna's orientation as the direction of the station. One bearing produces a [[Lines of Position in Navigation|line of position]] rather than a complete fix; another bearing or independent observation is needed to resolve location.
