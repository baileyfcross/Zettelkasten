# Post-Reward Resetting

Post-reward resetting is the decline in desire for another similar reward immediately after one has been received. Anticipation may have carried much of the value, while possession reduces the motivational pull of the next equivalent prize.

A progression system that simply repeats the same reward can therefore lose force after payout. A new goal needs renewed meaning, distance, or variation rather than assuming that the previous anticipation will continue unchanged.
