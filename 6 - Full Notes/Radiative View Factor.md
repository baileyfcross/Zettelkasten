# Radiative View Factor

A radiative view factor is the fraction of energy leaving one surface that reaches another. It is determined by surface area, orientation, separation, visibility, and shadowing rather than by the surfaces' temperatures or material properties.

Convex exterior surfaces cannot see one another in the same way that concave interior surfaces can. [[Surface-to-Surface Radiation|radiation models]] integrate angular contributions over all mutually visible surfaces so that geometric exposure and occlusion are represented in the energy exchange.
