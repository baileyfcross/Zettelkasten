# Blender Active Camera

Rendering requires an active camera because Blender needs a defined viewpoint from which to convert the scene into an image. A camera object can be added to the scene and selected as the active camera through the View menu.

The Outliner helps locate a camera in a complex file. Looking through the active camera makes composition, framing, and the visible effect of background images available before a final render begins.
