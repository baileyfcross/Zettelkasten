# Kotlin String Templates

A Kotlin string template embeds a value in text with `$name` or embeds a larger expression with `${expression}`. The expression is evaluated and its result becomes part of the resulting string.

Templates keep the fixed text and substituted values together, avoiding a chain of concatenation operations. They can incorporate properties and calculations as well as simple local variables, provided braces make the expression boundary clear.
