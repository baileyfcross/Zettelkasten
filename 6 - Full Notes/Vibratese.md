# Vibratese

Vibratese was a tactile language whose basic elements varied vibration amplitude, duration, and location across the chest. Forty-five combinations represented letters, numerals, and common short words, demonstrating that trained users could receive extended symbolic messages through the skin.

The design avoided treating frequency and amplitude as independent because changes in amplitude can also change perceived pitch. It also avoided relying on waveform differences that touch resolves poorly. Although never broadly adopted, Vibratese established both the promise and training cost of an explicit [[Tactile Vocabulary]].
