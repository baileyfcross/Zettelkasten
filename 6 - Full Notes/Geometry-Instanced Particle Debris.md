# Geometry-Instanced Particle Debris

When an explosion sends pieces toward or past the camera, real 3D debris models can replace particle placeholders. Instances inherit simulated positions and motion, while the source models supply shape, surface detail, and rotation. The book centers debris pivots, scales models in relation to the scene, and varies model complexity according to how close a piece will be seen. Their forms should resemble damaged parts of the object being destroyed. Motion blur helps connect the rendered pieces with the shot. See [[Sprite Particle Rendering]] for the cheaper card-based alternative.
