# Precision Medicine

Precision medicine adapts prevention, diagnosis, or treatment to characteristics of an individual or biologically meaningful subgroup. Genomic variation can be combined with clinical and environmental information to estimate risk or likely treatment response.

The promise depends on representative evidence, validated associations, interpretable decisions, privacy protections, and clinical context rather than sequence volume alone.
