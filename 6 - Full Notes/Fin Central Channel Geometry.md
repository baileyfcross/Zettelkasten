# Fin Central Channel Geometry

A fin central channel is a void created by subtracting an internal solid from the surrounding fin geometry. Its cross section and placement alter material volume, exposed surface, conduction paths, and the topology that must be meshed.

The subtracting object should be parameterized and aligned through explicit coordinates or a work plane. Measuring the completed channel and remaining tip features verifies that the Boolean difference produced the intended opening rather than an enclosed cavity or broken domain.
