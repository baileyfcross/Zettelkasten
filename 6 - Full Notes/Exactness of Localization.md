# Exactness of Localization

Localization is an exact operation on modules. If the image of one map equals the kernel of the next, the same equality holds after every module and map is localized.

Equivalently, S⁻¹R is a [[Flat Module]] over R. This permits kernels, cokernels, and exact sequences to be studied prime by prime without losing their relationships.
