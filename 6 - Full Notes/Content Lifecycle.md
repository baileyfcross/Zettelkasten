# Content Lifecycle

A content lifecycle is the time pattern through which an item gains, sustains, and loses attention. Different kinds of social content can decline immediately, remain steady, rebound after an initial drop, or burst and disappear quickly.

Lifecycle features allow a model to distinguish transient trends from durable interest. Early observations become more informative when interpreted against the characteristic shape of the content type.
