# Local Noise Inconsistency

Local noise inconsistency occurs when one image region has a noise level or sensor-pattern correlation that does not fit comparable regions in the same photograph. Compositing, resizing, blurring, sharpening, or denoising can change these signals and create a spatial boundary.

Interpretation must control for brightness, texture, saturation, and depth-dependent processing because authentic regions need not have identical apparent noise. Heavy JPEG compression and downsampling may destroy the evidence, while an informed editor can add synthetic noise to conceal a mismatch.
