# Decision Visualization

Decision visualization presents data and analytical results in forms that help people compare alternatives, detect patterns, and understand consequences. Its purpose is not only to display output but to incorporate evidence into the reasoning process.

A useful visual representation matches the decision, exposes uncertainty and scale, and avoids hiding important variation through aggregation. The display mediates between an analytical pipeline and human judgment.
