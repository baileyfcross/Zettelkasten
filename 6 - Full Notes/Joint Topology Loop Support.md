# Joint Topology Loop Support

Joint topology loop support places enough edge rings around an articulation for the surface to bend without collapsing or stretching across too few faces. A simple limb joint often benefits from a central loop near the bend and supporting loops on both sides.

More geometry should be concentrated where shoulders, elbows, knees, hips, eyelids, or the mouth require a wide range of deformation. Extra loops are useful only when their flow contributes to the motion; uniform subdivision everywhere increases work without necessarily improving the pose.
