# Dish-Sterling Solar Power Plant

A dish-Sterling plant tracks the Sun with a point-focus mirrored dish and heats a receiver at its focus. A hot-gas Sterling engine converts the receiver heat into shaft work and electrical output, making the arrangement useful for autonomous generation in the source. Hydrogen or helium can be the engine's working fluid. Tracking and optical interception determine the heat reaching the engine.
