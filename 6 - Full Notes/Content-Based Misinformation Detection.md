# Content-Based Misinformation Detection

Content-based misinformation detection uses properties of the message itself, such as words, topics, sentiment, style, or repeated URLs. These features are transformed into a representation from which a classifier estimates a label.

Content can expose recurring deceptive patterns but may be imitated or change rapidly. Combining it with network and account evidence can reduce dependence on any single clue.
