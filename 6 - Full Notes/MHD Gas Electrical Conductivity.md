# MHD Gas Electrical Conductivity

Electrical conductivity lets a hot gas carry induced current between the electrodes of an [[Magnetohydrodynamic Generator]]. Pure gas requires extreme temperature for useful ionization; even a small ionized fraction can help, and [[MHD Gas Seeding]] raises it at less severe conditions. As gas cools through the channel, electrons and ions recombine, limiting further output and making downstream heat recovery valuable.
