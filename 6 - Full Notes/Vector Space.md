# Vector Space

A vector space is a nonempty set whose elements can be added and multiplied by scalars while satisfying closure, associativity, commutativity of addition, distributive laws, and identity and inverse axioms.

The familiar space $\mathbb{R}^n$ is one example, but vectors may also be polynomials or functions. [[Basis]] and dimension describe the independent directions available in the space.
