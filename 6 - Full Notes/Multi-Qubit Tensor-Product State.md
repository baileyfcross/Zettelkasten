# Multi-Qubit Tensor-Product State

Quantum state spaces combine by the tensor product. If one qubit has basis $\{|0\rangle,|1\rangle\}$, two qubits have basis $\{|00\rangle,|01\rangle,|10\rangle,|11\rangle\}$, formed from every ordered pairing of the single-qubit basis vectors.

Tensor-product dimensions multiply rather than add. This rule distinguishes combining quantum subsystems from merely listing alternatives, and it provides the representation in which local gates, multi-qubit gates, [[Separable Quantum State|separability]], and [[Entangled Quantum State|entanglement]] are defined.
