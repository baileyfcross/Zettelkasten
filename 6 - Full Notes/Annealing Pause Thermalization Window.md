# Annealing Pause Thermalization Window

Pause effectiveness depends on the relation among the system's relaxation time $t_r$, the Hamiltonian-change timescale $t_H$, and the pause duration $t_p$. Near a narrowing gap, fast relaxation can drive nearly instantaneous thermalization. Later, $t_H<t_r\lesssim t_p$ creates a window in which holding the Hamiltonian permits additional relaxation toward low energy.

After freeze-out, $t_p\ll t_r$, even a long-looking pause is short relative to relaxation and has little effect. The best pause location is therefore a dynamical property of the device and embedded problem, not simply the point of minimum gap.
