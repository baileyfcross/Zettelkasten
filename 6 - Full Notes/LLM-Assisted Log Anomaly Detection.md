# LLM-Assisted Log Anomaly Detection

LLM-assisted log anomaly detection learns or applies sequence representations to distinguish normal log behavior from unusual events. Transformer-based models can capture contextual relationships across log sequences and transfer shared patterns among systems with different formats.

The model augments rather than replaces parsing, storage, search, and alerting infrastructure. Detection quality depends on representative normal and abnormal data, stable templates, domain adaptation, and evaluation against operationally meaningful incidents.
