# Intersection of Sets

The intersection of sets contains exactly the elements common to all of them. Intersections preserve many closure properties, so intersecting subgroups, subrings, or subfields again produces the corresponding substructure.

This makes intersection a natural way to define the smallest structure satisfying stated containment requirements.
