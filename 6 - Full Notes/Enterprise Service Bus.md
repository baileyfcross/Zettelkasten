# Enterprise Service Bus

An enterprise service bus is an integration layer that mediates communication among applications and business processes. It can hide lower-level transport and translation concerns behind shared interfaces so each participant does not need a custom connection to every other participant.

The bus supports [[Data Integration]] and [[Process Integration]], but semantic agreement is still required. Centralizing connectivity without clear ownership can merely move coupling into the bus.
