# Syzygy Module

The syzygy module Syz(f₁,…,fₜ) is the submodule of Rᵗ containing every [[Syzygy]] among the listed generators. It is the kernel of the map Rᵗ → M that sends standard basis vectors to those generators.

Generators of this kernel become columns of a [[Presentation Matrix]]. Higher syzygy modules continue the relations-among-relations structure of a free resolution.
