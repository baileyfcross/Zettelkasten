# Design Discussion Queue

A design discussion queue records questions and disagreements that need collective judgment but are not yet actionable tasks. Keeping them separate prevents unresolved decisions from being disguised as assignments.

Each entry should name the issue, the evidence or constraint behind it, and when a decision becomes necessary. The team can then use meeting time on choices that actually block progress.
