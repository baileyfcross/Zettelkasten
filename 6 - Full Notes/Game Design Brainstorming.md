# Game Design Brainstorming

Game design brainstorming is a group method for generating many ideas before judging them. Participants emphasize quantity, postpone criticism, welcome unusual proposals, and combine or improve one another's suggestions.

Separating generation from evaluation protects momentum and participation. After the session, the team can compare ideas against player, platform, scope, market, and gameplay requirements.

Brainstorming is especially useful after a [[Game Design Problem Statement]] has identified the underlying question. Reframing the problem and inviting unusual uses can counter [[Functional Fixedness in Game Design|functional fixedness]], while the later evaluation phase preserves focus.
