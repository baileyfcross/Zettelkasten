# Solar Cell Fill Factor

Fill factor compares a cell's real maximum electrical power with the rectangle formed by its open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current: $FF=P_{max}/(V_{oc}I_{sc})$. A better-shaped [[Solar Cell Current-Voltage Curve]] has a larger factor. The source uses it in estimating $P_{max}$, so the two endpoint measurements alone are not enough to determine deliverable peak power.
