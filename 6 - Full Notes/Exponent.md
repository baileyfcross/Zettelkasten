# Exponent

An exponent is the raised value in a [[Power]] that specifies repeated multiplication or an extension of that pattern. In (a^n), (n) is the exponent and (a) is the base.

A positive integer exponent counts copies of the base. A [[Zero Exponent]] makes a nonzero base equal one, and a [[Negative Exponent]] denotes the reciprocal of a positive power. Parentheses determine what the exponent acts on: ((-a)^2) squares the negative quantity, while (-a^2) takes the opposite after squaring (a).
