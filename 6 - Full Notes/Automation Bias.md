# Automation Bias

Automation bias is the tendency to give an automated recommendation more authority than its evidence warrants. A person may accept the output because the system appears mathematical, consistent, or complex, even when contextual information points to an error.

Nominal human oversight does not protect against harm when the reviewer is expected to approve the machine quickly or lacks the expertise and authority to disagree. Effective review requires time, relevant information, training, and responsibility for the final judgment. [[Explainable AI]] can help, but an explanation will not correct organizational incentives that reward deference.
