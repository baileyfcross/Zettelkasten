# AI Workplace Monitoring

AI workplace monitoring uses data and automated analysis to observe employees, evaluate performance, or predict behavior. Continuous measurement can turn ordinary activity into a source of managerial judgment while employees have little knowledge of or control over the model.

The practice affects privacy, autonomy, and power at work. A numerical score may ignore context while encouraging workers to optimize visible metrics rather than perform meaningful tasks. Ethical use requires a legitimate purpose, proportionate collection, intelligible criteria, and a way to challenge errors. Worker participation is necessary because apparent consent may not be meaningful within an unequal employment relationship.
