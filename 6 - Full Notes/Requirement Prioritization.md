# Requirement Prioritization

Requirement prioritization orders desired outcomes by value, urgency, risk, dependency, and delivery cost. It helps a team decide which capabilities belong in the next increment when time and budget cannot satisfy every request simultaneously.

Priority is a planning decision, not a statement that lower-ranked needs are invalid. Architectural work must still notice a deferred requirement that would make today's structure prohibitively expensive to evolve.
