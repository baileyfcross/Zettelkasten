# Descriptor Matching

Descriptor matching compares feature descriptors to propose which observed image points correspond to known model points. Similarity or distance in descriptor space supplies candidates, often with a test that rejects matches that are not sufficiently distinctive.

Appearance similarity can still produce false pairs in repeated or ambiguous texture. A robust geometric estimator tests whether a subset of matches agrees with one camera pose and discards inconsistent outliers.
