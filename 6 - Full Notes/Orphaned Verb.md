# Orphaned Verb

An orphaned verb is a [[Game Verb]] with little or no relationship to the rest of a game's interactive vocabulary. It may be used for one isolated action and then forgotten because other rules do not reinforce or develop it.

Orphaned verbs increase [[Cognitive Load]] without creating much depth. A design can avoid them by making its important verbs serve multiple purposes and interact with multiple objects, rather than adding a new control for every special case.
