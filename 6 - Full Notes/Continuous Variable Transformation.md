# Continuous Variable Transformation

A continuous variable transformation applies a mathematical function, such as a logarithm or square root, to every valid value. Analysts may use transformations to reduce skew, stabilize variance, or make a modeled relationship closer to linear.

The transformed distribution must be rechecked rather than assumed to improve. A log or square-root transformation can leave strong skewness intact, and its new scale may complicate interpretation even when it improves a diagnostic plot.
