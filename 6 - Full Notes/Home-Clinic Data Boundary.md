# Home-Clinic Data Boundary

The home-clinic data boundary is the point where observations from everyday life enter professional healthcare. Most health management occurs outside appointments, so home records can supply context that clinical measurements do not capture.

Crossing the boundary changes the standard applied to the data. A personally useful note may need documented units, reliable timing, validated measurement, secure transfer, and a clear connection to a care decision before a clinician can rely on it. The clinical system must also determine who reviews the stream and how urgent findings are handled.

The goal is not to turn the home into a miniature clinic. It is to preserve daily context while making selected information trustworthy and actionable enough for the particular clinical purpose.
