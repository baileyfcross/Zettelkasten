# Augmented Paper

Augmented paper combines a physical paper artifact with registered digital information or controls. Printed content provides a stable, portable surface, while tracking lets an AR system place imagery or interpret marks and pointing relative to the page.

The paper retains familiar handling and high-resolution static information. Digital augmentation can add change, animation, computation, or links, provided the system preserves alignment as the page or viewer moves.
