# Ethernet AI Fabric

An Ethernet AI fabric uses the broad Ethernet ecosystem for accelerator-cluster storage, inference, management, and distributed-compute traffic. Its strengths include vendor interoperability, familiar routing and monitoring practices, integration with enterprise services, and support for high link rates.

Ethernet can also carry RDMA through [[RDMA over Converged Ethernet]] when congestion and packet behavior are engineered appropriately. It is not inherently limited to light AI work, but tightly synchronized clusters require evidence that the topology, effective bandwidth, latency, and loss controls meet the workload objective.
