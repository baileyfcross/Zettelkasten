# LLM-Assisted Compliance Monitoring

LLM-assisted compliance monitoring compares policies and regulatory language with operational records, configurations, and evidence to identify possible gaps. Language models can map varied terminology and summarize which controls or documents need review.

The model cannot establish legal compliance by assertion. Results need traceable evidence, versioned requirements, jurisdiction-aware interpretation, and approval by accountable security, legal, or audit personnel.
