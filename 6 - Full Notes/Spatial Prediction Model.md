# Spatial Prediction Model

A spatial prediction model estimates an unobserved location, condition, or future event from past observations and geographic relationships. It goes beyond a [[Spatial Query]], which selects facts already stored, by using patterns involving nearby features, environment, time, or movement to infer what is likely.

Applications include forecasting rainfall, hurricane landfall, disease spread, habitat, crop suitability, and neighborhood change. The model's usefulness depends on relevant historical data and on accounting for [[Spatial Autocorrelation]] and [[Spatial Heterogeneity]] rather than assuming that every observation is independent and drawn from one uniform population.
