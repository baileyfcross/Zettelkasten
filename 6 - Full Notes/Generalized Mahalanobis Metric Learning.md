# Generalized Mahalanobis Metric Learning

Generalized Mahalanobis metric learning estimates a positive semidefinite matrix that stretches informative directions and contracts unhelpful ones. The resulting distance between two observations is their quadratic difference under that learned matrix.

Because the matrix can be factored into a linear projection, the learned distance is equivalent to ordinary Euclidean distance after transforming the data. Labels or pairwise constraints shape that projection for the classification task.
