# Time-Series Small Multiples

Time-series small multiples repeat the same graphical design for different variables, groups, or periods in aligned panels. Shared time coordinates support position-based comparison while separation avoids the crossings of a [[Superposed Time-Series Plot]]. Consistent scales improve cross-panel magnitude judgments; [[Independent Panel Scales]] instead emphasize each series' shape. Compact designs such as a [[Horizon Graph]] make it possible to compare a larger number of panels.
