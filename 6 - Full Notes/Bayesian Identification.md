# Bayesian Identification

Bayesian identification supplies values or distributions for model parameters from observed data. It completes parts of a description that were structured by the specification but not numerically fixed by preliminary knowledge.

The learned result depends on the chosen variables, decomposition, forms, and dataset. Identification transforms some incompleteness into represented uncertainty; it does not remove the unmodeled influences outside the description.
