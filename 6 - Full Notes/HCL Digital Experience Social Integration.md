# HCL Digital Experience Social Integration

HCL Digital Experience brings portal and content capabilities together with social integrations for enterprise sites. It can use social data or services within a managed digital experience while preserving the portal's content, identity, and presentation framework.

The integration problem is bidirectional: a portal may surface community information internally and may also publish selected managed content to external networks. Those paths require different credentials, audience rules, and operational monitoring. Separating provider connections from authored content keeps a network change from rewriting the content model. [[HCL Social Media Publisher]] focuses on the outbound publishing path, while [[Enterprise Social Portal Integration]] provides the broader architectural context.
