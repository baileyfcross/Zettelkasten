# Konrad Zuse Z3

Konrad Zuse completed the Z3 in Berlin in 1941 as a program-controlled binary calculating machine built with electromechanical relays. Instructions were encoded on punched movie film.

The Z3 realized important elements of Babbage’s programmable-machine vision independently of British and American projects. Wartime isolation and destruction delayed recognition of its place in computing history.
