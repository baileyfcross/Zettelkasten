# BITNET

BITNET began in 1981 as a network connecting IBM mainframes at universities. It provided messaging, file transfer, and Listserv discussion groups to communities that often lacked access to ARPANET.

Connections with other networks eventually brought its users into the Internet. BITNET broadened network culture beyond technical and military research groups.
