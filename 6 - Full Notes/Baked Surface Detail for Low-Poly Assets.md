# Baked Surface Detail for Low-Poly Assets

Baking transfers aspects of a high-resolution surface into texture maps used by a lower-resolution mesh. Normal or bump information can make lighting suggest small relief without retaining every sculpted polygon at runtime.

The low-poly mesh still needs clean silhouette and shading topology, while the high-poly source supplies fine surface character. A compatible UV layout and consistent projection relationship are necessary for the baked detail to align after export.
