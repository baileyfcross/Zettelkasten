# MeasureIt Add-On

MeasureIt extends Blender with visible dimensions for segments, angles, arcs, and areas in both Object and Edit modes. Its controls also expose mesh indices and display data, while separate settings govern measurement colors, typography, and rendering.

Unlike Edit-mode-only [[Blender Mesh Measurement Overlays]], MeasureIt can keep dimensional information visible while whole objects are arranged. This makes it useful for presentation and checking across an architectural scene.
