# Commutative Ring

A commutative ring is a set with addition and multiplication in which addition forms an abelian group, multiplication is associative, multiplication distributes over addition, and the order of factors does not change a product. The source assumes rings also have a [[Ring Identity]].

Commutativity lets left and right multiplication be treated uniformly. [[Ideal]]s, [[Quotient Ring]]s, and [[Module]]s are the principal structures used to study such a ring.
