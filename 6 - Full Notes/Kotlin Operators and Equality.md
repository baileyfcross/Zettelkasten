# Kotlin Operators and Equality

Kotlin operators express arithmetic, assignment, comparison, Boolean logic, increments, ranges, and membership. Precedence and parentheses determine how a compound expression is grouped, while the operand types determine which operations are meaningful.

Equality has two distinct questions. Structural equality with `==` asks whether values are equal according to their equality behavior; referential equality with `===` asks whether two references identify the same object. Keeping those questions separate prevents object identity from being mistaken for value equivalence.
