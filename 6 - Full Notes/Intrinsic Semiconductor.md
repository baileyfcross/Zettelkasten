# Intrinsic Semiconductor

An intrinsic semiconductor is a semiconductor in its pure form, before controlled impurities are added. Its [[Semiconductor Band Gap]] keeps most electrons out of the conduction band at low temperature. Thermal energy can promote some electrons across the gap, creating mobile electrons and corresponding holes. Deliberate [[Semiconductor Doping]] changes this behavior far more strongly for device construction.
