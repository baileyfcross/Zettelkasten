# Sequence Control Structure

A sequence control structure executes a series of steps one after another in a specified order. The output or changed state produced by one step is then available to the steps that follow it.

Sequence is the simplest [[Control Structure]], but order still matters. Reversing two operations may change the result, especially when later work depends on values calculated or data transformed earlier in an [[Algorithm]].
