# Technical Confounding

Technical confounding occurs when a processing variable is associated with the biological outcome of interest, making their effects difficult or impossible to distinguish. A gene-expression difference attributed to population, treatment, or sex may instead reflect processing date or batch.

When the alignment is perfect, no statistical adjustment can recover the separate effects from that dataset. Randomizing biological groups across batches prevents the design failure more reliably than post hoc correction.
