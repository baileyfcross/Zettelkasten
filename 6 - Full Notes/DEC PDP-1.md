# DEC PDP-1

The PDP-1 was Digital Equipment Corporation’s first computer, introduced in 1960 as a programmed data processor. Its transistorized design supported direct interactive use outside the traditional batch-oriented computer room.

Researchers used it for graphics, music, games, and experimental interfaces. The machine helped establish a culture of hands-on computing that fed later networking and personal systems.
