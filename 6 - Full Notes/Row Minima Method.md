# Row Minima Method

The row minima method builds an initial transportation plan one row at a time. Within the current row it allocates as much as possible to the lowest-cost uncrossed cell, adjusts supply and demand, and continues in that row until its supply is exhausted.

Because it considers costs only inside the active row, its result depends on row order and tie choices. It usually uses more cost information than the [[Northwest Corner Method]] but less global information than the [[Least-Cost Transportation Method]].
