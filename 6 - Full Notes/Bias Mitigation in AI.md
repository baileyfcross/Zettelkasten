# Bias Mitigation in AI

Bias mitigation in AI is the effort to identify and reduce unjust patterns in data, models, and deployment. Measures can include improving [[Dataset Representativeness]], examining labels and proxy variables, comparing outcomes across groups, changing objectives, diversifying teams, and monitoring a system after release.

No purely technical method can remove every contested value. Mitigation must begin with a definition of the relevant harm and a defensible account of fairness. It must also consider whether the application should exist at all; making a discriminatory system somewhat more balanced does not justify an illegitimate institutional use.
