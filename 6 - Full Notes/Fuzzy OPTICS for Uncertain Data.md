# Fuzzy OPTICS for Uncertain Data

Fuzzy OPTICS constructs a reachability ordering from uncertain or graded neighborhood relations. Reachability values then express both density scale and uncertainty in the connections among observations.

The ordering can reveal possible clusters across several thresholds without forcing early crisp assignments. A final interpretation must distinguish valleys supported strongly across uncertainty models from those created by permissive fuzzy connections.
