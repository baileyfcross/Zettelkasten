# Azure Boards

Azure Boards is the Azure DevOps area for organizing work items, backlogs, boards, sprints, and queries. It gives a team shared views of planned, active, and completed work without making the tool itself responsible for team communication or sound prioritization.

The value comes from maintaining current relationships and status. A board that does not reflect actual work creates reporting confidence without delivery evidence.
