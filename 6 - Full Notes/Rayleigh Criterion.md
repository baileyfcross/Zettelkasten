# Rayleigh Criterion

The Rayleigh criterion says that two point sources are just resolved when the central diffraction maximum of one coincides with the first minimum of the other. For a circular aperture, the limiting angle is approximately $1.22\lambda/D$. Larger apertures and shorter wavelengths therefore resolve finer angular detail.
