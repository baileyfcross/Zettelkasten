# Wind Barbs and Windsock Interpretation

A wind barb shows the direction from which wind comes and encodes speed with short feathers, long feathers, and triangular pennants for 5, 10, and 50 knots. A windsock gives an immediate local estimate of direction and strength through its orientation and degree of extension. Both are observations that must be converted into the components in [[Crosswind and Headwind Components]].
