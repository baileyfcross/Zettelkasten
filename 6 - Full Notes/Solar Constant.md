# Solar Constant

The solar constant is the mean intensity of the Sun's beam measured normal to its direction just outside Earth's atmosphere. The source uses approximately 1.353 kW per square meter and notes a yearly variation from Earth's changing distance to the Sun. It is an incoming extraterrestrial reference, not the guaranteed radiation at a collector after clouds and atmospheric absorption.
