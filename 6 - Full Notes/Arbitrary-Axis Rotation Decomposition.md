# Arbitrary-Axis Rotation Decomposition

Arbitrary-axis rotation decomposes a difficult three-dimensional motion into elementary translations and principal-axis rotations. A point on the axis is moved to the origin. Rotations about coordinate axes then eliminate transverse components of the axis direction until it coincides with a principal axis.

The required turn is performed about that axis, after which the alignment rotations and translation are undone. Each alignment angle can be represented by sine and cosine values derived from direction ratios, avoiding an unnecessary explicit angle. The decomposition is a practical application of [[Coordinate Frame Conjugation]].
