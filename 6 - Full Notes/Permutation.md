# Permutation

A permutation is a rearrangement of a collection's elements into a different order. The elements themselves and their multiplicities remain unchanged.

The result of a [[Sorting Algorithm]] must be a permutation of its input. Ordering the records correctly is not sufficient if an item is lost, duplicated, or altered during the process.
