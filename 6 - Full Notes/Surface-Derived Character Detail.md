# Surface-Derived Character Detail

Surface-derived character detail begins by duplicating edges or faces from an existing body or garment so a new eyebrow, badge, earpiece, or clothing trim inherits the base surface's position and curvature. The separated geometry can then be reshaped without manually reconstructing its fit.

A [[Blender Modifier Stack|modifier stack]] can project the detail back to the body, add thickness, mirror it, and smooth it in a controlled order. Projection should precede thickness when the goal is to keep the base conforming while preserving a raised final form.
