# Regulatory Timing for AI

Regulatory timing for AI concerns when policy should intervene in relation to technological development and deployment. Acting too late allows systems and business practices to become entrenched, while acting with little knowledge may impose rules poorly matched to the technology or its effects.

Proactive policy can state rights, responsibilities, and unacceptable uses before adoption becomes difficult to reverse. Because consequences remain uncertain, early rules should be paired with monitoring and revision. The relevant time horizon also matters: policy focused on speculative distant systems may neglect current applications, while purely immediate rules may fail to establish durable principles.
