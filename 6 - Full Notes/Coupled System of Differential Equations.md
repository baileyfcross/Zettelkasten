# Coupled System of Differential Equations

A coupled system contains multiple dependent variables whose rates of change depend on one another. Writing the variables as a vector converts a linear first-order system into the matrix form $\mathbf{x}'=A\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{f}$.

For a constant matrix, eigenvalues and eigenvectors organize the homogeneous modes and determine growth, decay, or oscillation. Coupled systems also provide a first-order representation of higher-order equations and a natural starting point for phase-space analysis.
