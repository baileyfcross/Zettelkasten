# Metagaming

Metagaming consists of actions, rules, knowledge, or relationships that extend beyond one immediate play session or cross the [[Magic Circle]]. Examples include planning between matches, community norms, or strategic information learned outside the game.

Metagaming can become an outer loop that changes later decisions inside the game. It therefore belongs to the broader [[Game-Player System]] even when it is not encoded directly in the game's internal model.
