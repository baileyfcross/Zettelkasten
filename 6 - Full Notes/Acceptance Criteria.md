# Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria are testable conditions that clarify when a user story provides its intended behavior or value. They capture important details discovered through conversation without turning the story into an implementation script.

Criteria help the Product Owner, developers, and testers form a shared understanding before work begins. They complement the team's general [[Definition of Done]] with conditions specific to one item.

For a read-only network-agent pilot, acceptance criteria should measure evidence quality, prevention of unsafe command execution, controlled error behavior, common-path latency, audit completeness, operator usefulness, scope rejection, and disable readiness. Each criterion needs an observable target and a measurement method, preventing a few impressive model responses from substituting for repeated operational evidence.
