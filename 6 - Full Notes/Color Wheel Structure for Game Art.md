# Color Wheel Structure for Game Art

A color wheel arranges hues by their relationship around a circle, making primary, secondary, and tertiary combinations visible. The exact primaries depend on whether the system describes emitted light, pigment, or another color model.

For environment art, the wheel is a planning device rather than a rule that selects colors automatically. It helps the artist build [[Analogous Environment Palette|neighboring]], complementary, triadic, and tetradic relationships while still testing them in the intended lighting and context.
