# Bivariate Normal Distribution

A bivariate normal distribution jointly describes two normally distributed variables through their two means, two standard deviations, and a correlation coefficient. Under this model, conditional expectations follow a line whose slope depends on the correlation and relative scales.

The compact five-parameter description explains why [[Pearson Correlation Coefficient|correlation]] is informative in this setting. When the joint shape is not approximately bivariate normal, the same coefficient may conceal nonlinear structure, outliers, or poor agreement.
