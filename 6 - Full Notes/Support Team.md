# Support Team

A support team supplies a capability used by many development teams, such as build infrastructure or specialized services. Its work often arrives continuously from multiple requesters and is well suited to visible flow policies.

The team needs a clear service boundary and ordering mechanism. Without them, urgent requests and hidden dependencies fragment attention and turn the team into a bottleneck.
