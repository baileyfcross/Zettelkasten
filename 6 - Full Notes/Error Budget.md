# Error Budget

An error budget is the amount of unsuccessful or degraded service behavior permitted by a [[Service Level Objective]] during its measurement window. If an objective requires 99.9 percent success, the remaining 0.1 percent is the budget available for failures and risky change.

The budget connects reliability to delivery decisions. A healthy remaining budget supports continued release velocity, while rapid consumption justifies slowing releases and prioritizing resilience work. It is not permission to cause avoidable outages; it is a shared quantitative boundary that prevents both reckless change and an unproductive demand for perfect reliability.
