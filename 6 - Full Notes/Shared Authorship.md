# Shared Authorship

Shared authorship occurs when players extend, rearrange, or create material within a game rather than only consuming what its original creators supplied. Level editors, scenario tools, role-playing facilitation, and modding can turn players into designers.

The original game still provides rules, examples, and a world that shape the new work. Sharing authorship therefore creates a continuing conversation between the initial design and what players imagine and build from it.
