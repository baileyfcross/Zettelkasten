# Grand Challenge Problems in Science

Grand challenge problems are scientifically important questions whose established models require massive computation. Researchers can connect anticipated hardware and algorithmic progress to the point when a previously unreachable simulation becomes feasible.

The framing helped organize computational science and investment in high-performance computing. It made computational capacity part of long-range scientific planning rather than an afterthought to theory.
