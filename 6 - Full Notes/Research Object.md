# Research Object

A research object aggregates the paper, data, methods, tools, workflows, and contributor information that together constitute a body of research. Relationships and metadata make the parts discoverable as a coherent unit rather than a collection of unrelated downloads.

The concept generalizes a [[Research Compendium]] to include executable workflows, people, and other citable digital objects. A useful research object can be shared, preserved, and inspected by both people and software. Its value depends on persistent identifiers, explicit relationships, interoperable formats, and licenses that permit the intended reuse.
