# DEC PDP-8

The PDP-8 was a compact, relatively inexpensive 12-bit minicomputer introduced in 1965. Standard transistor modules and automated wire-wrap construction reduced its size and cost.

DEC shared design information so customers could embed the machine in laboratories, factories, telephone systems, and medical instruments. This openness made general-purpose computing adaptable to specialized work.
