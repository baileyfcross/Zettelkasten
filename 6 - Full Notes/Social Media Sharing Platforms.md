# Social Media Sharing Platforms

Media-sharing platforms organize participation around audio, images, or video. Audio services may distribute music, podcasts, or audiobooks; image services combine storage, backup, publication, and messaging; video services let individuals publish clips or build audiences around recurring channels.

The source uses the Live Music Archive and Napster to show different distribution models, Instagram and photo-storage services for image sharing, and YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, and Dailymotion for video. The category is defined by the primary content and workflow, not by the absence of social relationships. Enterprise APIs therefore need to account for media upload, metadata, permissions, comments, and publishing status, as in [[Meta Business SDK for Java]].
