# Numerical Inference

Numerical inference evaluates a probabilistic question using concrete sums, integrals, optimizations, or samples. It is required when symbolic simplification does not produce a usable analytic form.

The method chosen depends on whether variables are discrete or continuous, the dimension of the free-variable space, and whether the goal is a complete distribution, an expectation, or a most probable assignment.
