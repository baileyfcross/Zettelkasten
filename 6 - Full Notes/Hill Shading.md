# Hill Shading

Hill shading simulates illumination over a digital elevation model by deriving slope and aspect and calculating how each surface faces an assumed light source. The resulting grayscale relief can sit beneath a translucent thematic raster or support a [[Physical Map]]. It communicates terrain form rather than measured brightness. Light direction and vertical exaggeration affect the apparent shape, so the shading should remain context instead of competing with the mapped variable.
