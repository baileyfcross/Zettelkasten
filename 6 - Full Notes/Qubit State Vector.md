# Qubit State Vector

A qubit is represented by a normalized vector in a two-dimensional complex state space. After choosing the computational basis, its pure state has the form

$$
|\psi\rangle=\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle,
\qquad |\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1.
$$

The coefficients are probability amplitudes rather than simultaneous classical bit values. Their magnitudes determine measurement probabilities, while their relative phase can change the result of later [[Unitary Quantum Gate|unitary operations]] even when the computational-basis probabilities initially match.
