# Encrypted Communication Channel

An encrypted communication channel protects data moving between endpoints from being read or altered easily in transit. The peers establish cryptographic parameters and keys, commonly using an identity certificate to help start a trusted exchange.

Channel encryption protects the route between the participating endpoints. An application may additionally encrypt the data itself when protection must persist after the channel ends or across intermediate systems.
