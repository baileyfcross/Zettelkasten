# Spatial Heterogeneity

Spatial heterogeneity means that a process or distribution differs across locations or regions. Climate zones, terrain, population, and local institutions can make the relationship observed in one place unlike the relationship observed elsewhere.

It violates the identically distributed part of the usual independent-and-identically-distributed assumption. A single global average can therefore combine distinct local populations and describe none of them well. Spatial models address this by allowing location, regions, or local conditions to change the expected values or relationships.
