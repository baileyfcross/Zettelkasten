# Design Thinking

Design thinking is a human-centered way to investigate a problem before converging on a solution. It emphasizes empathy with users, reframing the problem, generating alternatives, prototyping, and learning from feedback.

During requirements work, the approach helps separate a stakeholder's underlying need from the first requested feature. A prototype can reveal usability or workflow assumptions while changes are still cheaper than modifications to a completed system.
