# Time-Series Interval Feature

A time-series interval feature summarizes a selected contiguous segment with measures such as its mean, standard deviation, or slope. Different intervals expose localized changes without requiring an exact pattern match.

Candidate intervals may be sampled or searched at multiple lengths and positions. Many overlapping intervals create redundancy, so selection or ensemble methods are commonly used to retain useful regional information.
