# Social Virtual Assistant

A social virtual assistant is designed to participate in relationship-oriented interaction as well as complete instrumental tasks. It uses conversational style, memory, and an [[Assistant Personality]] to create continuity across encounters.

Social behavior can improve engagement, but apparent empathy or companionship may encourage users to attribute abilities the system does not possess. Design should keep the assistant's actual limits legible.
