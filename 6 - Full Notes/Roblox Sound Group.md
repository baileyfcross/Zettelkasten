# Roblox Sound Group

A SoundGroup lets multiple Roblox Sound objects share volume control and audio effects. Assigning music, ambience, dialogue, or effects to distinct groups creates category-level mixing without editing every source independently.

Group organization supports both authoring and player settings. A master control can change overall loudness while category controls preserve relative balance, and effects can give a group a consistent treatment. The structure connects individual [[Roblox Sound Object]] instances to larger systems such as [[Roblox Soundtrack Playback]] and [[Roblox Ambient Sound]].
