# Feature-Based Forecasting

Feature-based forecasting uses characteristics of a time series to choose, combine, or configure forecasting models. Features describe properties such as trend, seasonality, dependence, entropy, and forecastability.

Instead of assuming one forecaster is best for every series, the feature space relates series behavior to model performance. An instance-space view can also reveal regions where current forecasting methods succeed or leave gaps.
