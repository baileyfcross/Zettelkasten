# Computational Thinking Transfer Hypothesis

The computational thinking transfer hypothesis claims that learning programming improves general problem-solving ability across unrelated domains. It has often been used to justify universal computing education.

Research has not provided strong evidence for broad transfer. Programming is itself a network of specialized skills, and competence acquired in one context does not automatically become expertise in another.
