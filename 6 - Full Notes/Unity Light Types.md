# Unity Light Types

Unity provides light types suited to different spatial roles. Directional lights illuminate a scene as if rays came from a distant source, point lights radiate from a position, spot lights project within a cone, and area-style lighting represents emission across a surface where supported.

The type determines how position, direction, range, and angle affect the result. Color and intensity shape mood, but every active light also contributes rendering cost. A scene should therefore use the simplest arrangement that communicates form and atmosphere to its [[Unity Camera Component|cameras]].
