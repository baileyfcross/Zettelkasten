# Digital Matte Painting with 2.5D Cards

A matte painting can be separated into foreground, middle, and background images and staged as cards in depth. A camera move then gives their separation visible parallax while animation on individual cards supplies moving cars or other elements. In the book's environment examples, this arrangement can be mixed with genuinely modeled CG; the rendered environment may also be reduced to a single background plate when the camera angle permits. Choosing which features need real geometry is an efficiency decision governed by the desired shot, not by the source image's apparent complexity. See [[2.5D Depth Cards and Parallax]].
