# Biological Signal

A biological signal is structured variation in measurements associated with the biological condition the study intends to learn about. It may appear as a mean difference, expression pattern, class boundary, or coordinated variation across related features.

Technical adjustment should preserve this target structure while removing unwanted variation. The source evaluates correction methods partly by whether known sex-chromosome expression differences remain detectable after batch effects are addressed.
