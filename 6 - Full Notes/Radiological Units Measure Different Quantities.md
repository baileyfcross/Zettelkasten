# Radiological Units Measure Different Quantities

Pryor gives radionuclide activity, absorbed dose, dose equivalent, and X/gamma-ray exposure separate conversion categories. Activity is represented by curie or becquerel and has inverse-time units; absorbed dose by rad or gray has energy-per-mass units; dose equivalent by rem or sievert is another named category; exposure by roentgen or coulomb per kilogram is charge-per-mass. A factor valid inside one pair does not convert a different quantity merely because both belong to radiology. See [[Algebraic SI Derived Units]] and [[Dimensional Homogeneity in Equations]].
