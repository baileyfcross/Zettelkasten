# Linear Level Layout

A linear level layout requires spaces to be experienced in a fixed sequence, with movement mainly to the next or previous area. The geometric shape need not be a straight line.

Linearity gives strong control over teaching, pacing, and story order but offers little route choice. One-way transitions can prevent backtracking, while excessive constraint can make the player feel carried through the level.
