# Real-Time Stream Processing

Real-time stream processing analyzes events while they are arriving so results can influence a situation before their value expires. Operators maintain bounded state, transform records, detect patterns, and emit alerts or updates without waiting for a complete dataset.

IoT streams intensify the problem through many geographically distributed producers, high rates, diverse formats, and limited device resources.
