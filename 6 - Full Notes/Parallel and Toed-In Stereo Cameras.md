# Parallel and Toed-In Stereo Cameras

Two stereoscopic cameras can be horizontally separated while pointing in parallel, or angled inward toward a chosen subject. The book calls the second arrangement toeing in and compares the resulting left- and right-eye parallax with the parallel arrangement. Both generate distinct views, but their alignments are not interchangeable when foreground and background objects occupy different depths. The final composite still needs a deliberate convergence choice, as described in [[Stereo Window Placement]].
