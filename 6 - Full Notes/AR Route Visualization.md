# AR Route Visualization

AR route visualization registers a path or sequence of waypoints directly with the environment through which the user travels. The route is shown from the traveler's current egocentric viewpoint rather than only on a separate map.

This reduces the mental transformation from map coordinates to visible streets or corridors. The design must keep the path legible despite occlusion, perspective, tracking error, and nearby visual clutter.
