# Connective Action

Connective action is political mobilization organized through personalized content shared across digital networks rather than through a single collective identity imposed by a formal organization. Individuals join by adapting broad frames to their own experiences.

Memes support this logic because a common template can coordinate participation without requiring identical messages. Each contribution remains personally meaningful while links, hashtags, and visual conventions make it part of a larger action. The network connects diverse expressions into a recognizable public campaign.
