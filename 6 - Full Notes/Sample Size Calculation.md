# Sample Size Calculation

A sample size calculation solves for the number of observations required after the analyst specifies the planned test, effect size, significance level, target power, and allocation structure. The result may refer to each group or to the total sample, depending on the function.

The computed number should be rounded up and adjusted for expected attrition, missing measurements, clustering, or unequal outcome groups. It is a design estimate based on assumptions, not a guarantee that the completed study will yield a significant result.
