# Tensor Product of Modules

The tensor product M ⊗_R N is an R-module equipped with a universal [[Bilinear Map]] M × N → M ⊗ N. Every bilinear map from M × N to another module factors uniquely through it.

Tensor products support [[Scalar Extension for Modules]] and test [[Flat Module|flatness]]. They are right exact but need not preserve injections; the [[Tor Functor]] measures that failure.
