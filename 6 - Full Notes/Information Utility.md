# Information Utility

Information utility is the property that information is in a usable and useful form for its intended user and purpose. Data may be confidential, correct, authentic, controlled, and available yet still fail if its format, key, context, or representation makes it unusable.

Utility is one of the six properties in the [[Parkerian Hexad]]. Protecting it requires attention to formats, applications, decryption capability, retention, and the consumer's actual information need.
