# Prosthetic Sensory Feedback

Prosthetic sensory feedback returns information about contact, force, or device movement from an artificial limb to its user. Sensors on the prosthesis measure interaction, a processor transforms those signals, and a display or neural interface stimulates remaining tissue.

Closing this loop reduces dependence on vision and can support more stable, economical grasp. The mapping must be discriminable, timely, and learnable; adding many sensor channels is not useful if their feedback cannot be localized or interpreted. [[Peripheral Nerve Cuff Feedback]] is one approach for referring sensations toward the missing hand.
