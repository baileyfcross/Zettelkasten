# Enhanced LORAN

Enhanced LORAN is a proposed modernized terrestrial navigation and timing service based on high-power, low-frequency transmitters. Its signals are much stronger at the receiver than GNSS signals and follow a different propagation path, making it a dissimilar backup that is difficult to jam in the same way. It requires costly ground infrastructure and offers different accuracy and coverage, but those differences are precisely what make it valuable for resilience.
