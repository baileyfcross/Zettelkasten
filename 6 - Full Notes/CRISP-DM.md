# CRISP-DM

CRISP-DM is a cross-industry process model organized into business understanding, data understanding, data preparation, modeling, evaluation, and deployment. Each phase contains practical tasks such as defining goals, checking quality, constructing attributes, designing tests, and planning monitoring.

Its business and technical phases connect modeling choices to an operational purpose. Evaluation can redirect the work to an earlier phase rather than serving only as final approval.

Kelleher and Tierney present the six phases as an iterative, vendor-independent route from a practical question to deployment. Business and data understanding inform each other: examining available records may force a better definition of the goal, while the goal determines which records and attributes are worth preparing. Evaluation asks whether the result meets the business objective as well as whether a model fits data; deployment includes using and monitoring the result, not just saving a fitted model.
