# Bounded Complex

A complex is bounded below when all sufficiently low-degree terms are zero and bounded above when all sufficiently high-degree terms are zero. It is bounded when both conditions hold.

Boundedness limits the number of active homological positions. Finite projective and injective resolutions are bounded complexes whose lengths define projective or injective dimension.
