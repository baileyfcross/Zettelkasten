# Cryptographic Separation

Cryptographic separation protects selected memory or stored regions with encryption so processors or contexts without the key cannot interpret their contents. It creates a logical boundary even when the protected information shares physical hardware with other work.

The boundary depends on secure key handling and on limiting exposure when the data is decrypted for use. It complements rather than replaces [[Process Isolation]] and hardware memory controls.
