# Graphical Solution of Matrix Games

A graphical matrix-game solution applies when one player has exactly two strategies, giving a $2\times n$ or $m\times2$ payoff matrix. Expected payoffs are plotted as linear functions of the probability assigned to one of those two strategies.

For an $m\times2$ game, the lowest point on the upper envelope gives the column player's minimax choice; for a $2\times n$ game, the highest point on the lower envelope gives the row player's maximin choice. Active lines reduce the problem to a smaller $2\times2$ game.
