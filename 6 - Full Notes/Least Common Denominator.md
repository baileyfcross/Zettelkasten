# Least Common Denominator

The least common denominator is the smallest positive [[Common Denominator]] for a group of fractions. It is the least common multiple of their denominators.

Using the least common denominator minimizes the size of intermediate numerators and often reduces later simplification. It is convenient rather than mandatory: a larger common multiple still gives a correct sum or difference when every fraction is scaled consistently. For denominators (5) and (7), the least common denominator is (35), although (70), (105), and other common multiples also work.
