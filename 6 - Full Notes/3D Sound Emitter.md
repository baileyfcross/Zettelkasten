# 3D Sound Emitter

A 3D sound emitter is the world-space source of a spatial sound. Its position and possibly its velocity determine how the sound reaches the [[3D Sound Listener]].

Distance can control attenuation, horizontal direction can influence stereo placement, and relative motion can produce a Doppler shift. Geometry between emitter and listener may also cause occlusion or obstruction.
