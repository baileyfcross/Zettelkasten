# Twisted-Fin Thermal Performance

The rotini-shaped fin case compares a twisted aluminum fin with a cylindrical fin of equal volume. Their different surface areas and conduction paths produce different temperature decay, transient response, and heat transfer at the wall-fin interface.

Surface integrations quantify conductive power while cut lines compare temperature along the fin length. The case shows that a larger surface-to-volume ratio can improve heat rejection, but [[COMSOL Derived Values|integrated flux]] and base temperature are more reliable design measures than shape appearance alone.
