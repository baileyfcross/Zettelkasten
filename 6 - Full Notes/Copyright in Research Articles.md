# Copyright in Research Articles

Copyright in a research article protects the author's original expression of ideas, including the written presentation, rather than the underlying scientific facts themselves. In the United States account described by the source, these rights arise by default and can be transferred to a publisher.

Because the copyright holder controls reproduction and derivative works subject to legal exceptions, access and reuse depend on the publishing agreement and license. Retaining rights or granting an attribution license can support sharing, archiving, and adaptation. [[Green and Gold Open Access]] addresses access routes, while licensing determines what downstream users may lawfully do with the article.
