# Assisted Living Analytics

Assisted living analytics uses pervasive sensing and learned behavior patterns to support people who need help living independently. It can monitor routine, detect possible emergencies, and identify changes that merit attention from family or care providers.

The objective is not merely to classify activity but to connect an observation to proportionate assistance. A deviation may reflect a harmless schedule change rather than decline. Personalized baselines, user consent, and escalation policies help prevent a supportive system from becoming intrusive or generating unnecessary interventions.
