# Service Level Objective

A service level objective is a measurable target used to judge one characteristic of a service. Examples include uptime, throughput, available capacity, response time, recovery time, or delivery time.

Several objectives can support a broader [[Service Level Agreement]]. The provider uses them to monitor whether internal performance is likely to satisfy the value promised to the consumer, even when every internal metric is not part of the external contract.
