# Spatial Fourier Transform

A spatial Fourier transform decomposes a field into plane-wave components labeled by wave vector rather than temporal frequency. Each component records variation at a particular spatial scale and direction.

Spatial derivatives become multiplication by components of the wave vector, so partial differential equations with constant coefficients can simplify substantially in wave-number space. The inverse transform recombines those plane waves to reconstruct the field in physical space.
