# Radial Popularity Coordinate

A radial popularity coordinate places more popular or higher-degree network nodes closer to the center of a hyperbolic embedding. Newer or less connected nodes are positioned farther outward, and radial movement can represent popularity fading over time.

Because node degree and density vary exponentially with radius, radial position compactly expresses hierarchy and influence. It works with the [[Angular Similarity Coordinate]] to determine hyperbolic proximity.
