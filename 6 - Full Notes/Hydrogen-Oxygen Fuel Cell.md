# Hydrogen-Oxygen Fuel Cell

A hydrogen-oxygen [[Fuel Cell]] combines hydrogen with oxygen to produce water and electrical work. Hydrogen loses electrons at one electrode, and the external circuit carries them to the oxygen-side reaction; ions move through the [[Fuel Cell Electrolyte]] to maintain charge balance. For one mole of hydrogen consumed, the ideal reaction produces one mole of water. Real output is below the reversible limit because of polarization losses.
