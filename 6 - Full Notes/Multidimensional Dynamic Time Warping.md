# Multidimensional Dynamic Time Warping

Multidimensional dynamic time warping aligns two vector-valued sequences by finding a low-cost path through their pairwise temporal positions. The path permits local stretching and compression while preserving order.

A joint local cost keeps the dimensions synchronized during alignment, unlike warping each coordinate independently. Constraints on the path reduce computation and prevent implausible matches that align distant events solely to lower numerical cost.
