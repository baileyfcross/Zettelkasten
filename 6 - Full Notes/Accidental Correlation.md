# Accidental Correlation

An accidental correlation is an observed association that does not arise from a stable causal relationship and cannot reliably support prediction or manipulation. It may result from coincidence, selection, limited data, or an unrecognized pattern in background conditions.

Large datasets can contain many such associations because the number of possible comparisons is enormous. Causal methodology is needed to separate them from relationships that remain useful beyond the observed sample.
