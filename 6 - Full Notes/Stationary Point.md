# Stationary Point

A stationary point of a differentiable function is a point where its [[Gradient]] is the zero vector. It is a candidate for an extremum, but it need not be a maximum or minimum.

The local classification depends on curvature. The [[Hessian Matrix]] can identify a [[Local Minimum]], [[Local Maximum]], or [[Saddle Point]] when its definiteness is conclusive.
