# R Variance Homogeneity Test

An R variance homogeneity test evaluates whether several groups have comparable variability. Classical variance-ratio procedures are sensitive to non-normal data, while tests based on absolute deviations from group centers offer more robust alternatives.

The test should support, not replace, graphical review of group spread. Heterogeneous variance can affect standard errors and group comparisons even when mean structure is modeled correctly, and the appropriate response may be a transformed outcome or a model that allows group-specific variance.
