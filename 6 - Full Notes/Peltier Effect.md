# Peltier Effect

The Peltier effect exchanges heat at the junction of two materials when electrical current crosses it. Reversing current reverses whether that junction releases or absorbs heat, making it a reversible thermoelectric effect. Its heat rate is proportional to current through the Peltier coefficient. In a [[Thermoelectric Generator]], this heat exchange belongs in the hot- and cold-junction energy balances.
