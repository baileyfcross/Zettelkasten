# Palm Pilot

The Palm Pilot was a handheld organizer introduced in 1996 with a deliberately simple interface. It replaced paper address books, calendars, task lists, and notebooks with synchronized digital records.

Careful attention to a limited set of common activities made the device approachable. Its success established personal digital assistants as a path toward smartphones.
