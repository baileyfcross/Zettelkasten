# Transaction-Based Pricing

Transaction-based pricing charges for discrete units of work such as invoices processed, information requests, application operations, or another countable business event. A unit price can be estimated by dividing the service's cost by its expected transaction volume.

The model works best when transactions are clearly defined, their volumes are predictable, and the volume follows the provider's cost drivers. It is particularly suitable for information and business-process services.
