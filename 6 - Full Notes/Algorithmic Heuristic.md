# Algorithmic Heuristic

An algorithmic heuristic is a strategy for choosing among alternative moves while constructing a solution. It narrows the search by applying a practical rule rather than exploring every possible continuation.

A [[Greedy Algorithm]] uses the heuristic of selecting the best-looking immediate option. The result may be excellent, approximate, or trapped at a [[Local Optimum]], so a heuristic should be evaluated by the guarantees and typical performance it provides for its specific problem.
