# Sterile Cockpit Discipline

Sterile cockpit discipline excludes nonessential duties and conversation during critical phases of flight. Taxi, takeoff, landing, and non-cruise operations below the specified altitude demand attention to aircraft control, communication, and checklists. The rule converts lessons from distraction-related accidents into a shared boundary on crew activity.
