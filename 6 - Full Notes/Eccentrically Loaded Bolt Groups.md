# Eccentrically Loaded Bolt Groups

When a load does not pass through a bolt group's centroid, the connection resists direct shear plus a moment. An elastic analysis distributes direct shear equally and moment-induced shear in proportion to each bolt's distance from the group centroid, with force directions tangent to that radius. Vector addition then identifies the most highly loaded bolt.

An instantaneous-center method can represent the nonlinear strength distribution at ultimate load more accurately by locating a rotation center and enforcing equilibrium. Eccentric load can also create simultaneous bolt shear and tension depending on connection geometry. Increasing group depth, adding bolts farther from the centroid, or shortening eccentricity usually improves resistance more efficiently than adding fasteners close to the center.
