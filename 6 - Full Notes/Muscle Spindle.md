# Muscle Spindle

A muscle spindle is a sensory receptor within muscle that reports muscle length and the rate at which length changes. Its signals contribute to perceiving limb position, movement direction, amplitude, and velocity.

Signals from one spindle can be ambiguous because a muscle or tendon may cross several joints. The nervous system resolves limb state from converging patterns across spindles, skin-stretch receptors, and joint receptors. Strong spindle activation by tendon vibration can also produce an illusory limb movement, demonstrating the spindle's central role in [[Kinesthesia]].
