# Steepest Descent Method

The steepest descent method moves opposite the [[Gradient]], the direction of greatest first-order decrease of a differentiable objective.

Each iteration selects a step length along that direction and then recomputes the gradient. The method is conceptually simple but can progress slowly along narrow curved valleys.
