# Apple iPad

The iPad extended Apple’s touch interface and mobile operating environment to a larger tablet display. It combined Wi-Fi or cellular networking with a form suited to reading, media, browsing, and direct manipulation.

Tablet computers had existed earlier, but the iPad achieved broad adoption through an established application ecosystem and coherent user experience. It illustrates how timing and integration can matter more than being first.
