# Linear ODE with Constant Coefficients

A homogeneous linear ordinary differential equation with constant coefficients is solved by testing exponential functions. Substitution produces a characteristic polynomial whose roots determine the independent solution modes.

Distinct real roots give separate exponentials, repeated roots require powers of the independent variable multiplying an exponential, and complex-conjugate roots produce exponentially weighted sine and cosine terms. A nonhomogeneous equation adds a particular solution to this complementary solution.
