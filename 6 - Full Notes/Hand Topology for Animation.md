# Hand Topology for Animation

Hand topology for animation gives the thumb a partially forward origin, staggers the finger bases along a knuckle arch, and provides loops at finger articulations and the wrist. These relationships make a stylized hand read naturally and provide geometry where posing will compress or stretch the surface.

A practical workflow models one finger, duplicates and reshapes it, then joins the bases to prepared hand loops. Palm and tendon lines can guide cuts, but the result should be judged in curled and spread poses rather than only as a flat open hand.
