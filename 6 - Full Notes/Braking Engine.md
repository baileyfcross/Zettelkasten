# Braking Engine

A braking engine is a predominantly [[Balancing Loop|balancing]] structure that drains a resource or reduces its future rate of gain. It creates friction that regulates progress and counteracts a reinforcing engine.

Brakes can preserve competition and pacing, but excessive friction leaves the player unable to act and can stop the game. They are therefore usually subsidiary loops within a [[Boosting Engine]], economy, or progression system.
