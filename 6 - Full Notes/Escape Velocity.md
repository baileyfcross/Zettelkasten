# Escape Velocity

Escape velocity is the minimum ideal launch speed that lets an object reach indefinitely large distance with no remaining speed under gravity alone. It follows by equating initial kinetic energy with the gravitational potential-energy change and decreases as launch distance from the planet's center increases. For Earth it is about 11.2 kilometers per second near the surface when atmospheric resistance and rotation are neglected.
