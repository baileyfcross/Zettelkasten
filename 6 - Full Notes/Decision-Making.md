# Decision-Making

Decision-making is the process of choosing between alternatives with real or imagined consequences. It depends on recognizing the current situation, identifying possible actions, and considering their outcomes.

Its speed is affected by conflict, complexity, and uncertainty. Normative models emphasize rational choice, behavioral models account for human limitations and heuristics, and naturalistic models examine decisions made during realistic complex tasks.

In [[Information Processing]], decision-making connects perception and knowledge to action. [[Situation Awareness]] supplies an internal model of the environment, while learned behavior and [[Skill Acquisition|skills]] can make common responses faster and more accurate.
