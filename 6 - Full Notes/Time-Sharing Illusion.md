# Time-Sharing Illusion

The time-sharing illusion is each user’s perception of having a powerful computer exclusively available even though many people share it. Fast switching hides the intervals when the processor serves others.

The illusion depends on low response time and isolation. Delays or interference reveal the shared infrastructure and break the conversational quality of interaction.
