# Zariski Closure

The Zariski closure of a subset is the smallest closed algebraic set containing it. It consists of all points satisfying every polynomial equation that holds throughout the original subset.

A [[Projection of an Affine Variety]] need not itself be closed. The [[Closure Theorem for Elimination]] identifies the variety of an elimination ideal with the Zariski closure of that projection.
