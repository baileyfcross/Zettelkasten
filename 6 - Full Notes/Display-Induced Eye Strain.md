# Display-Induced Eye Strain

Display-induced eye strain is visual fatigue or discomfort caused by the optical, geometric, or temporal demands of a viewing system. In XR headsets, likely contributors include sustained near focus, poor eye-lens alignment, image distortion, flicker, blur, and [[Vergence-Accommodation Conflict]].

Symptoms such as headache, difficulty focusing, or blurred vision may overlap with [[Cybersickness]] without being identical to it. An incorrect [[Interpupillary Distance]] can add distortion and convergence error, while low frame rate or high pixel persistence can make motion harder to view comfortably.

Comfort therefore cannot be inferred from resolution alone. Evaluation should include adjustment across users, the optical path, content depth, session duration, and recovery after use. The long-term effects of repeated extended viewing should be treated as an empirical safety question when evidence is incomplete.
