# Action Game

An action game centers its gameplay on physical coordination challenges. Timing, aim, movement, reaction, and rapid control normally matter more than extended planning or symbolic reasoning.

The label describes dominant challenges rather than setting. Shooters, fighting games, and platformers are action subgenres, and an action-adventure hybrid adds story and puzzles without removing the action core.
