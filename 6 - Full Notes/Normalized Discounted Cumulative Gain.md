# Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain

Normalized discounted cumulative gain evaluates a ranked list by rewarding relevant items more when they occur near the top. Logarithmic discount reduces the contribution of relevance found at lower positions.

Dividing the observed discounted gain by the ideal ordering yields a value between zero and one. This normalization permits rankings with different relevance scales to be compared at selected cutoff positions.
