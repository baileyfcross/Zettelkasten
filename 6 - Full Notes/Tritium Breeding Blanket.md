# Tritium Breeding Blanket

A breeding blanket surrounds fusion plasma with lithium-containing material. Neutrons from the [[Deuterium-Tritium Fusion Reaction]] interact with lithium to make replacement [[Tritium]] and release heat. The source places this blanket outside the plasma wall in its tokamak arrangement, with subsequent tritium recovery. It addresses limited natural tritium supply while also linking fuel production to reactor heat extraction.
