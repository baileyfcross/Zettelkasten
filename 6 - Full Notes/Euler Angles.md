# Euler Angles

Euler angles describe a three-dimensional orientation by a sequence of rotations about coordinate axes. Each angle supplies one elemental [[Rotation Matrix]], and multiplying the matrices produces the combined rotation.

The sequence is part of the definition because three-dimensional rotations do not commute. Reordering the same angle values can therefore produce a different final orientation.
