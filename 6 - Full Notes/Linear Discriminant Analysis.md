# Linear Discriminant Analysis

Linear Discriminant Analysis constructs a linear projection in which samples from different known classes are well separated. It compares between-class covariance with total or within-class covariance and selects projection directions associated with the strongest separation criterion.

LDA is a supervised [[Feature Extraction]] method: its output coordinates are combinations of the original variables rather than a subset of them. For $c$ classes, the between-class structure has rank at most $c-1$, so the discriminant space contains at most $c-1$ independent directions.
