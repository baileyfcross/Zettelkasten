# Aviation Gasoline Grades and Lead

Aviation gasoline grades distinguish anti-knock performance and lead content, with 100LL identified by blue dye in the source. Lead improves knock resistance but is toxic and can leave deposits during prolonged low-power operation. Hot deposits and fouled spark plugs can promote abnormal ignition, making correct fuel grade and engine handling part of combustion reliability.
