# Data Protection Impact Assessment

A data protection impact assessment examines a proposed processing operation before or during deployment to identify effects on people, justify the purpose, and select safeguards. It treats privacy risk as a property of the complete system rather than of isolated fields.

For large-scale analytics, the assessment must include linkage, inference, opaque processing, secondary uses, and the possibility of group-level harm. It complements individual notice when affected people cannot foresee every later analytical use.
