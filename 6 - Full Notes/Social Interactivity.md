# Social Interactivity

Social interactivity arises from relationships among players or between players and socially legible agents. It includes communication, grouping, reciprocity, complementary roles, competition, and cooperation.

These loops often develop more slowly than moment-to-moment action and can extend outside a single play session through [[Metagaming]]. Social systems remain engaging while relationships and group positions can still change meaningfully.
