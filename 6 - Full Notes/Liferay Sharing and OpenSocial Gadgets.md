# Liferay Sharing and OpenSocial Gadgets

Liferay can expose sharing actions around portal content and can host OpenSocial-style gadgets as reusable applications. Sharing connects a governed portal resource to a selected social destination, while a gadget packages interface and behavior so it can run inside a compatible container.

Both mechanisms cross a boundary that must be explicit. Shared content may leave portal access controls behind, and a gadget may request identity or social data from its container. Permission prompts, audience selection, and provider-specific adapters should therefore be part of the design. [[OpenSocial Gadget Standard]] explains the portable gadget model; [[Liferay Portal Social Model]] supplies the users, sites, and resources with which the integration interacts.
