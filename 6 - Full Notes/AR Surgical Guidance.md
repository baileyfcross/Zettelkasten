# AR Surgical Guidance

AR surgical guidance places selected patient or procedure information into a clinician's view of the worksite. Possible augmentations include registered medical imagery, vital signs, anatomical boundaries, instrument guidance, or a [[Situated Annotation]] that would otherwise require attention to shift toward a separate monitor.

The aim is not to display every available datum but to improve [[Situation Awareness]] at the moment a decision is made. Information filtering, legibility, and placement are therefore as important as registration: an overlay that obscures tissue, arrives late, or directs attention away from the patient can increase rather than reduce risk.

Because the real procedure remains primary, the system should support the practitioner's established workflow. [[Spatial Registration]], sensor reliability, hygiene, and a clear fallback when the display fails are safety requirements rather than cosmetic refinements.
