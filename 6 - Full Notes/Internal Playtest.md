# Internal Playtest

An internal playtest is performed by the designers or development team. It supports fast iteration on an unfinished artifact and checks whether a build is coherent enough to expose to people outside the project.

Familiarity makes internal testers efficient but biased. They know the intended controls and goals, so an internal success cannot establish that a new player will understand the same experience.
