# Commercialization of Internet Backbone

Internet backbone commercialization transferred national traffic from a publicly funded research service to competing private carriers. By 1995 commercial entities operated the backbone infrastructure.

Legal changes allowed uses in addition to research and education when they strengthened the network’s capabilities. The transition expanded access while reducing the National Science Foundation’s operational role.
