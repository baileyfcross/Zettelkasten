# Asynchronous Play

Asynchronous play lets participants act at different times without requiring simultaneous connection. Players may join, take a turn or perform an activity, and leave while the shared game continues.

This structure reduces scheduling pressure but needs durable state, notifications, deadlines, and rules for inactivity. Challenges should remain meaningful when response may arrive much later.
