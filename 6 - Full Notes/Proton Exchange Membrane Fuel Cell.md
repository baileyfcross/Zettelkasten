# Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cell

A proton exchange membrane fuel cell uses a thin polymer [[Fuel Cell Electrolyte]] that passes hydrogen ions while electrons follow the external load. The source describes porous carbon electrodes with platinum catalyst, relatively low operating temperature, high power density, and quick output changes. Those properties suit vehicle propulsion and building supply, but catalyst cost and sensitivity to fuel impurities are practical concerns.
