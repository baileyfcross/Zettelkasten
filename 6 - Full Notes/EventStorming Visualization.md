# EventStorming Visualization

EventStorming visualization assigns stable colors and shapes to modeling concepts so everyone can interpret the wall consistently. Orange notes represent [[Domain Event]]s, and other colors distinguish uncertainties or outside systems. Once an idea is visible, participants can point to it, move it, question it, or add alternatives instead of relying on different private interpretations of spoken words. The notation should remain simple enough for domain experts to use without learning a technical diagramming language.
