# Development Team

A Development Team is the group that selects how to turn backlog items into a completed [[Product Increment]]. It plans and monitors its own work, adapts daily, and demonstrates the result rather than receiving a detailed task plan from a manager.

A game Development Team needs the disciplines required to deliver integrated, playable value. Shared accountability applies to the increment even when individual members retain specialties.
