# Industry 4.0

Industry 4.0 describes an industrial stage built around connected machines, cyber-physical systems, pervasive data, and increasingly autonomous production. Sensors and networked equipment allow processes to be monitored and adjusted through analytics.

The concept links physical operations to [[Business Intelligence and Analytics 4.0]]. Automation therefore depends on both machine connectivity and methods capable of converting streams of operational data into decisions.

Its technical scope combines smart sensors, machine-to-machine communication, robotics, drones, positioning, additive manufacturing, augmented and virtual reality, machine learning, and distributed computing. These are joined to pursue information transparency, interconnected operations, and increasingly decentralized decisions. The goal is not automation as an isolated machine upgrade but a reorganization of the production system around continuous data and coordinated physical action.
