# Sturm Sequence

A Sturm sequence is a recurrence of polynomials used to count how many eigenvalues of a symmetric tridiagonal matrix lie below a chosen value.

Comparing sign variations brackets eigenvalues and supports interval refinement. It locates individual eigenvalues without first forming all roots of a [[Characteristic Polynomial]].
