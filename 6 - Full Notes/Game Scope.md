# Game Scope

Game scope is the total amount of people, skills, time, systems, features, and content required to realize a design. It connects the desired experience to the practical cost and risk of creating it.

Scope is controlled through clear priorities and willingness to remove elements that do not support the [[Game Concept]]. Unreviewed additions create [[Scope Creep]], while a scope that omits core systems cannot deliver the intended experience.
