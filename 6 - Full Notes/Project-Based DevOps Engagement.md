# Project-Based DevOps Engagement

A project-based DevOps engagement commits to a defined technical outcome for an agreed scope and price, such as building a deployment pipeline or codifying an environment. It gives the client cost and deliverable clarity when the problem can be bounded before implementation begins.

The model depends on a strong [[Statement of Work Scope Boundary]] because ambiguity transfers hidden risk to both parties. Acceptance evidence, dependencies, exclusions, milestones, and change handling should be agreed before work starts. Open-ended diagnosis or evolving architecture may fit hourly consulting better, while ongoing maintenance may fit a [[DevOps Consulting Retainer]].
