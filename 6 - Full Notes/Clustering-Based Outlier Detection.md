# Clustering-Based Outlier Detection

Clustering-based outlier detection treats observations in very small or sparse clusters, or outside all normal clusters, as anomaly candidates. A hierarchical approach can cut a dendrogram and inspect the smallest groups.

The result depends on the cut level, distance, and clustering method. Normal points can form small clusters, while outliers can be absorbed when the grouping is too coarse.
