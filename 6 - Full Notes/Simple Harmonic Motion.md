# Simple Harmonic Motion

Simple harmonic motion occurs when acceleration is directed toward equilibrium and proportional to displacement: $a=-\omega^2x$. Its displacement is sinusoidal, with constant angular frequency in the ideal model. Velocity is greatest at equilibrium, acceleration magnitude is greatest at the turning points, and energy alternates between kinetic and potential forms.
