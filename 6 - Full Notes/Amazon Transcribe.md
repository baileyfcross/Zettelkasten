# Amazon Transcribe

Amazon Transcribe converts recorded or streaming speech into text. It can produce timestamps, identify speakers in supported workflows, and apply domain vocabulary to improve recognition of specialized terms.

The transcript is probabilistic output and may require review when audio quality, accents, overlapping speech, or uncommon terminology matter. Transcribe supplies speech-to-text capabilities, while [[Amazon Polly]] synthesizes speech from text.
