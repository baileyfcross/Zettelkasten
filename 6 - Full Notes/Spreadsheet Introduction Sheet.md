# Spreadsheet Introduction Sheet

A spreadsheet introduction sheet is the first tab of a complex or shared workbook and acts as its local operating guide. Often named Instructions, StartHere, or Help, it explains the purpose of each sheet, documents the color convention, and tells readers which values they should or should not change.

The amount of explanation should fit the audience, but even an individual designer benefits when returning to an old workbook. Clear instructions are especially valuable because overwriting a formula or reference can silently break downstream results. The introduction sheet makes safe use part of the workbook itself rather than relying on outside memory.
