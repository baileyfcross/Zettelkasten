# Digital Surveillance

Digital surveillance is the systematic monitoring and analysis of behavior through connected devices, platforms, sensors, or records. AI expands surveillance by recognizing patterns and identities at a scale that would be impractical for human observers.

The harm is not limited to disclosure of private facts. Continuous observation can change how people speak, move, associate, or work, especially when they cannot see the system or challenge its conclusions. The distribution of power matters: employers, companies, and states may gain detailed knowledge of individuals while remaining opaque about their own models and decisions.
