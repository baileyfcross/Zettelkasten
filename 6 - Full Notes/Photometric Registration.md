# Photometric Registration

Photometric registration aligns the lighting and appearance of virtual content with the physical scene. It estimates relevant illumination, material, exposure, and color relationships so generated objects respond to light in a manner compatible with the captured image.

Geometric alignment can place an object correctly while inconsistent brightness, shadow direction, or reflection still exposes it as synthetic. Photometric registration supplies the information needed for common illumination and coherent compositing.
