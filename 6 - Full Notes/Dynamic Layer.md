# Dynamic Layer

The dynamic layer is generated when players encounter and operate the [[Inscribed Layer]]. It contains both deliberate player actions and the consequences that emerge when those actions interact with authored rules, assets, narrative, and technology.

Its four elements are [[Dynamic Mechanics]], [[Dynamic Aesthetics]], [[Dynamic Narrative]], and [[Dynamic Technology]]. Because simple rules can combine into complex and surprising behavior, the dynamic layer cannot be specified completely in advance. Repeated [[Playtesting]] is how designers compare predicted play with actual play.
