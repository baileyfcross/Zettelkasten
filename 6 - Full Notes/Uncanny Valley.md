# Uncanny Valley

The uncanny valley is the drop in comfort that can occur when an artificial face, body, or animation becomes highly humanlike but retains small mismatches in movement, texture, gaze, or timing. Increasing visual detail can therefore make a character or robot feel less acceptable rather than more realistic.

The effect shows that resemblance is not a monotonic design goal. A clearly stylized social robot may communicate intention more effectively than an almost-human face that raises expectations it cannot satisfy. Form should match behavior and capability, especially where [[Social Robot]] interaction depends on trust and emotional interpretation.
