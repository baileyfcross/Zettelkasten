# Post-Marketing Pharmacovigilance

Post-marketing pharmacovigilance monitors medical products after release to detect adverse effects and interactions that may not have appeared in pre-approval studies. Spontaneous reports, electronic health records, literature, and patient-generated data provide complementary evidence.

Observed associations are shaped by reporting behavior, confounding, prescribing patterns, and the underlying disease. Analytics can generate early safety signals, but causality requires further investigation. Combining sources is valuable because a limitation in one source may be addressed by timing, population, or clinical detail from another.
