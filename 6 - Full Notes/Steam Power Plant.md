# Steam Power Plant

A steam power plant releases fuel heat in a boiler to raise high-pressure steam, expands that steam through a turbine, and drives an electrical generator. Its energy path passes from chemical to thermal to mechanical to electrical form. The source uses coal combustion as an example and notes that heat rejected in the condenser and stack limits overall conversion. See [[Combined-Cycle Power Plant]].
