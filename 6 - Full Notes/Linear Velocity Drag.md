# Linear Velocity Drag

Linear velocity drag is a resistive force proportional to and opposite the instantaneous velocity. In one-dimensional motion it adds a term of the form $-bv$ to Newton's equation.

Under a constant applied force, the resulting first-order velocity equation approaches a terminal value exponentially. The ratio of mass to drag coefficient sets the characteristic relaxation time, separating the initial transient from the later steady motion.
