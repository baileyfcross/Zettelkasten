# SAS Procedure

A SAS procedure, conventionally invoked with a `PROC` statement, is a packaged operation for analysis, reporting, plotting, or data management. Procedures such as PROC FREQ, PROC UNIVARIATE, and PROC LOGISTIC each expose their own options while sharing a broad call-and-argument structure.

Procedures contrast with the [[SAS Data Step]], which edits records, and often rely on the [[SAS Output Delivery System]] when results must become reusable datasets or presentation files. This division means that a complete SAS analysis may require familiarity with several related command systems.
