# First-Order Linear Differential Equation

A first-order linear differential equation has the standard form $y'+P(x)y=Q(x)$. Multiplying by an integrating factor $\mu(x)=e^{\int P(x)dx}$ turns the left side into the derivative of the product $\mu y$.

Integrating that product derivative gives a general solution containing one arbitrary constant. Initial data determine the constant, while the term involving $Q$ supplies the particular response and the remaining term describes the homogeneous behavior.
