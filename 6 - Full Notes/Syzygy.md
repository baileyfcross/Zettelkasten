# Syzygy

A syzygy is a relation among generators f₁,…,fₜ of a module: a tuple (r₁,…,rₜ) such that r₁f₁ + ··· + rₜfₜ = 0.

All first syzygies form the [[Syzygy Module]]. Repeating the construction on generators of that module produces higher syzygies and the successive kernels in a free resolution.
