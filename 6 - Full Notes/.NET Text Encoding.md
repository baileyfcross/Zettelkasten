# .NET Text Encoding

Text encoding defines how characters are represented as bytes. .NET strings use Unicode characters internally, but reading from or writing to files and networks requires an explicit or implied encoding such as UTF-8 or UTF-16.

The sender and receiver must agree on the encoding or text can be corrupted even when the bytes arrive intact. The `Encoding` APIs perform conversion and expose byte and character counts, making the representation boundary visible to the program.
