# Excess Classification Risk

Excess classification risk is the difference between a classifier's misclassification probability and that of the optimal Bayes classifier. It measures the avoidable error due to approximation and learning rather than the irreducible ambiguity of the class distribution.

For a finite or controlled-complexity hypothesis dictionary, the excess risk can be decomposed into how closely the dictionary approaches the Bayes rule and how accurately data select within it. This distinction prevents a low training error from being mistaken for closeness to the best possible classifier.
