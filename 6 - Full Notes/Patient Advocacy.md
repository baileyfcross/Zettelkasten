# Patient Advocacy

Patient advocacy is action taken by patients, families, or allies to secure access, recognition, resources, and influence within healthcare. In data-intensive care, advocacy includes the right to obtain measurements generated by one's body and to participate in decisions about their meaning and use.

Advocates can turn a personal obstacle into a public issue. Campaigns around implanted-device data, continuous glucose records, and patient-built tools reveal contradictions between asking people to manage their health and denying them the information needed to do so.

Advocacy also builds communities that exchange practical knowledge and pressure institutions to redesign systems. It complements individual self-tracking by addressing the policies and power relationships that one person's experiment cannot change.
