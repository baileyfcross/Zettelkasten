# Verb Relationship

A verb relationship exists when two [[Game Verb|game verbs]] affect how one another can be used. Moving can determine where a shot is aimed, for example, while dodging may place the player where firing is temporarily unsafe.

Related verbs create choices that neither verb would produce alone. Their coordination can be introduced simply and then developed into more demanding combinations, allowing skill and understanding to grow without continually adding new controls.
