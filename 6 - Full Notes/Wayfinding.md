# Wayfinding

Wayfinding is the cognitive process of determining and following a route between an origin and a destination. It is the planning and decision-making component of [[Navigation]].

The user determines the current location, selects a path, maintains orientation, and builds or consults a [[Cognitive Map]]. Wayfinding aids support this mental work rather than moving the user directly. Examples include maps, signs, trails, compasses, reference objects, and [[Landmark]]s.

In a large or unfamiliar [[Virtual Environment]], wayfinding aids can be essential even when the [[3D Travel]] technique is efficient. Integrated aids can reduce [[Cognitive Load]] and strengthen [[Spatial Knowledge]].
