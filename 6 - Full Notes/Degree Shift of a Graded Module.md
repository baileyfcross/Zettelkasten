# Degree Shift of a Graded Module

A degree shift M(d) reindexes a [[Graded Module]] by defining its degree-t component to be M_{d+t}. The underlying module does not change; only the recorded degrees move.

Shifted copies of a ring are the building blocks of a [[Graded Free Module]]. Their shifts record the degrees of generators and appear in graded Betti numbers and Hilbert-series calculations.
