# Multi-Similarity Drug Interaction Prediction

Multi-similarity drug interaction prediction combines several views of drugs and targets, such as chemical structure, sequence, side effects, expression, or network neighborhoods. The combined evidence can recover interactions that no single similarity exposes.

Integration requires deciding how much each view contributes and how to handle missing or conflicting evidence. A flexible model can overfit the known interaction network, especially when positives are much better documented than negatives. Validation should hold out interactions and, when possible, entire drugs or targets to test genuine generalization.
