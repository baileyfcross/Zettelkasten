# Optical Tracking

Optical tracking estimates the position or orientation of cameras, objects, or users from captured light. A system may observe prepared markers, active light sources, reflective targets, or natural image features.

Cameras are readily available on mobile AR devices and can relate measurements directly to visible scene structure. Performance depends on illumination, visibility, feature quality, camera calibration, computation, and the ability to recover after the target leaves view.
