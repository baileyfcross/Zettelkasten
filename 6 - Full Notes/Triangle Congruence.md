# Triangle Congruence

Two triangles are congruent when they are equal in size and shape, so corresponding sides and angles agree. Standard sufficient criteria include side-side-side, side-angle-side, and angle-side-angle; right triangles also admit hypotenuse-leg reasoning. Congruence turns a limited set of measurements into conclusions about every corresponding part.
