# Survival Curve

A survival curve plots estimated survival probability against time. Its downward steps occur at observed events, while censored observations reduce the later risk set without directly causing a probability drop.

Curves may be drawn overall or stratified by an exposure or confounder. Axis scaling is important when the event is rare, because a default zero-to-one probability axis can make meaningful differences near one visually disappear.
