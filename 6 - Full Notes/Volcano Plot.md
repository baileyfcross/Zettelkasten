# Volcano Plot

A volcano plot displays an effect estimate on the horizontal axis and negative log p-value on the vertical axis for many tested features. Large effects appear far from the center, while small p-values appear high on the graph.

The display prevents significance from being viewed without magnitude and can expose asymmetry or unusual groups of features. It does not correct for multiple testing; thresholds and q-values must be interpreted separately.
