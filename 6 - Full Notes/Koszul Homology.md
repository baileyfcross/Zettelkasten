# Koszul Homology

Koszul homology is the homology of a [[Koszul Complex]] after its differential is defined by a sequence of ring elements. It measures the failure of that sequence to behave regularly.

For a regular sequence on a ring, every positive-degree Koszul homology module is zero and H₀ is the quotient by the sequence. Patterns of vanishing can be used to extract regular subsequences.
