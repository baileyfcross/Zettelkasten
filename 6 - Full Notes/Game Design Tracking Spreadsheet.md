# Game Design Tracking Spreadsheet

A game design tracking spreadsheet keeps the evolving work visible alongside the design document and schematics. It can separate decisions awaiting discussion, tasks at different horizons, recurring responsibilities, required assets, and completed work.

The structure connects design intent to execution without pretending every future step is known. Updating it after evaluation turns a chosen revision into accountable work for the next prototype.
