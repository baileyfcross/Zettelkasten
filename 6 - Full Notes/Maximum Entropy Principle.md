# Maximum Entropy Principle

The maximum entropy principle selects, among the distributions satisfying stated constraints, the distribution with greatest entropy. It represents the supplied information while avoiding additional distinctions that the constraints do not justify.

The constraints and observable functions remain modeling choices; maximizing entropy does not create knowledge absent from them. The resulting exponential form makes explicit which expectations or macroscopic quantities were imposed.
