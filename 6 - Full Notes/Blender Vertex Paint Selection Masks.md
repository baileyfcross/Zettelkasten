# Blender Vertex Paint Selection Masks

Vertex Paint can limit brush effects through face masking or vertex selection. A face mask can derive from components selected in Edit mode, while vertex selection exposes box, circle, lasso, and direct selection tools inside the painting mode.

Masking separates the decision about where paint may land from the brush stroke itself. It protects neighboring topology and makes sharply bounded attribute edits possible even when the brush overlaps excluded areas.
