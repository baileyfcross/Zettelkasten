# Acyclicity Lemma

The acyclicity lemma gives conditions under which a finite complex of modules over a Noetherian ring is exact. It combines depth bounds on the modules with dimension bounds on any nonzero homology modules.

The principle is that homology cannot persist when its possible support is too small relative to the depth available in the neighboring terms. It leads to determinantal exactness criteria for free complexes.
