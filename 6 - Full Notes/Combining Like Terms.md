# Combining Like Terms

Combining like terms replaces a sum of [[Like Terms]] with one equivalent term by adding their coefficients. The variable factor remains unchanged:

$$5a-8a+4a=(5-8+4)a=a.$$

The method is the [[Distributive Property]] used in reverse. Before combining, terms may be rearranged through the [[Commutative Property]] and regrouped through the [[Associative Property]]. Unlike terms remain separate because they do not share a common variable factor with identical exponents.
