# Sensor Conflict

Sensor conflict occurs when different observations support incompatible states or values. The disagreement can result from ordinary measurement uncertainty, shared hidden influences, failure, a false alarm, or an incorrect model of sensor dependence.

Bayesian fusion does not require selecting one reading immediately. A joint model assigns relative support to the competing explanations, while explicit reliability and coherence variables can prevent conflict from producing unwarranted certainty.
