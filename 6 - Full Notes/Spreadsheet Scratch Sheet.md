# Spreadsheet Scratch Sheet

A spreadsheet scratch sheet is a deliberately disposable workspace for quick calculations, pasted values, and one-off experiments. Keeping this work on a far-right tab prevents temporary fragments from obscuring authoritative data and makes it clear that its contents may be overwritten or deleted.

Scratch work should not quietly become infrastructure. If a calculation proves useful or begins feeding other parts of the workbook, it should move to a labeled reference, data, or analysis sheet where its purpose and dependencies can be maintained. The scratch sheet therefore supports exploration without weakening the permanent workbook structure.
