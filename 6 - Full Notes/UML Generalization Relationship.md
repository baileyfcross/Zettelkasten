# UML Generalization Relationship

A UML generalization relationship represents inheritance from a more specific classifier to a more general one. It is drawn as a solid line ending in a hollow triangle that points toward the superclass.

The notation communicates an is-a relationship: instances of the subclass are intended to satisfy the contract of the superclass while adding or specializing behavior. It should not be used merely because two classes share a few fields.
