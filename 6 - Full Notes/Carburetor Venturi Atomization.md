# Carburetor Venturi Atomization

A carburetor accelerates intake air through a narrowed Venturi throat, lowering static pressure and drawing fuel through jets. The fuel is broken into fine droplets and mixed with air, while a butterfly throttle controls the resulting mixture flow to the engine. The same pressure-velocity relationship also explains Venturi-driven vacuum instruments.
