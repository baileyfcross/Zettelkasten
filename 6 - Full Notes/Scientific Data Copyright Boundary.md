# Scientific Data Copyright Boundary

The scientific data copyright boundary separates raw facts from creative expression in selecting or arranging them. The source explains that, under the United States framework it discusses, raw facts are not copyrightable, while an original selection or arrangement of facts may carry a residual copyright; other jurisdictions can treat databases differently.

Because cleaning and organizing a dataset may create uncertainty about rights, open research should not assume that factual content is automatically free of every restriction. A [[Public Domain Dedication for Research Data]] can waive exercisable rights, while privacy, confidentiality, contracts, and third-party interests still require separate treatment.
