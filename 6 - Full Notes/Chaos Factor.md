# Chaos Factor

The chaos factor is the residual unpredictability that remains even when a game contains no explicit random mechanism or a model strongly favors one result. Players vary from day to day, rare probability streaks occur, external conditions intervene, and software defects can change play.

Because no finite test completely removes these influences, designers should not treat one result set as certainty. Quantitative evidence narrows uncertainty and supports better decisions, but judgments about the feel of a game still require repeated observation and human interpretation.
