# Coordinated Healthcare Fraud

Coordinated healthcare fraud involves multiple actors whose linked actions create or conceal improper claims. Each participant may appear only mildly unusual, while the group shares patients, referrals, timing, or complementary billing patterns.

Network analysis helps expose this distributed structure, especially when combined with claim and episode features. Coordination must be distinguished from legitimate multidisciplinary care. Evidence is stronger when the relationships are statistically unusual for comparable providers and align with specific questionable services or temporal behavior.
