# Call-to-Action Goal Hierarchy

A call-to-action goal hierarchy connects an explicit long-term purpose to progressively smaller objectives the player can perform now. The long-term goal gives direction, medium-term prerequisites organize progress, and short-term tasks turn that structure into concrete action.

The hierarchy prevents local activity from feeling arbitrary. Collecting resources, learning a route, or speaking with a character makes sense because the player can see how it contributes to the next prerequisite and ultimately to the larger objective. Each layer can therefore guide without replacing the player's execution decisions.
