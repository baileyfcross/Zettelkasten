# Space-Charge Barrier

Electrons already occupying the gap between a thermionic emitter and collector repel additional emitted electrons. This accumulated negative charge creates a retarding potential, reducing collected current below the surface's emission capability. A narrow gap or ionized [[Cesium Vapor Thermionic Gap]] can lessen the barrier. It is a transport limitation distinct from the energy needed to release electrons from the emitter itself.
