# Generalizing Specialist

A generalizing specialist has deep capability in one discipline and enough understanding of adjacent work to collaborate, anticipate constraints, and help outside a narrow role. The person remains a specialist while identifying first with the product team.

This reduces dependency stalls and improves cross-discipline decisions. It does not require every developer to perform every craft at production quality.
