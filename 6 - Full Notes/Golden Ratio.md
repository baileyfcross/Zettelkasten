# Golden Ratio

The golden ratio is the positive proportion obtained when a rectangle's longer side relates to its shorter side as the whole relates to the longer remainder. Setting the shorter side to one gives $x/1=1/(x-1)$ and hence the quadratic equation $x^2-x-1=0$.

The positive solution is $(1+\sqrt5)/2$. R can calculate the numerical value and plot the corresponding quadratic, connecting the geometric proportion with its two algebraic roots.
