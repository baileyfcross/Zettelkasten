# Bayesian Question

A Bayesian question requests a conditional distribution from a description by partitioning its variables into searched, known, and free sets. It has the form $P(Searched \mid Known)$, with free variables marginalized out.

Instantiating the known variables with evidence yields one particular inference. Changing the partition can turn the same model from a predictive description into a diagnostic or control calculation.
