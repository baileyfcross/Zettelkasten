# Legal Attribution and Scientific Citation

Legal attribution and scientific citation serve related but distinct purposes. A software license may require preservation of authorship and notices in distributed files, while scientific citation records intellectual dependence in the scholarly literature and contributes to academic credit.

Meeting the license condition does not necessarily satisfy the citation norm. Software and data used in research should be cited in the reference list with enough information to identify the authors, version, release or access date, and location. This distinction allows [[Permissive Software License for Research|permissive reuse]] while preserving transparency and reward for digital contributions.
