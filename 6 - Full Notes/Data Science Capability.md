# Data Science Capability

Data science capability is the combined organizational ability to obtain data, use appropriate tools, apply analytic judgment, and deliver reproducible knowledge. It depends on people, process, infrastructure, and governance rather than on software alone.

Capability grows when exploratory work can progress into validated models and clear reports without losing provenance or repeatability.
