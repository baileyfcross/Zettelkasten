# Unintended Consequence

An unintended consequence is an effect that arises outside the immediate purpose of an action. It often appears when a local fix participates in a larger or delayed [[Feedback Loop]] that was not considered.

A change may reduce a visible problem in the short term while strengthening the conditions that recreate it later. [[Systems Thinking]] helps reveal these effects by widening the boundary and timescale of analysis.
