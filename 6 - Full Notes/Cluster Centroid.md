# Cluster Centroid

A cluster centroid is the component-wise mean of the observations assigned to a numerical cluster. It serves as the center used by K-means and related methods for distance comparisons.

A centroid summarizes location but not spread, density, or shape, and it need not be an observed data point. Outliers and attribute scale can move it substantially.
