# Exporting UV Maps Normals and Materials

Mesh geometry alone does not preserve an asset's surface appearance. UV coordinates place images, normals influence shading, tangents support normal mapping, vertex colors carry per-corner or per-vertex data, and material records associate those channels with a surface.

Interchange formats support different subsets and may store images internally or by external path. Export settings and receiving-tool importers must therefore be checked together rather than assuming that visible Blender shading will transfer automatically.
