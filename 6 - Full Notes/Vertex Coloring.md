# Vertex Coloring

Vertex coloring assigns colors to the vertices of a [[Graph]] so that vertices joined by an edge never share a color. The colors represent categories or resources that must differ across every direct connection.

It is one form of [[Graph Coloring]]. The related [[Edge Coloring]] problem places the constraint on edges meeting at the same vertex instead of on adjacent vertices themselves.
