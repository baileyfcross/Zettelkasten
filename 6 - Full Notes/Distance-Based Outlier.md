# Distance-Based Outlier

A distance-based outlier has at most a specified proportion or count of observations within a chosen radius. It can be isolated from most of the data by a neighborhood under the selected metric.

The method requires distance and frequency thresholds and does not inherently rank anomalies. It can perform poorly when different regions of the data have different densities.
