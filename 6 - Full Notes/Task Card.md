# Task Card

A task card records a concrete piece of work needed to implement a backlog item. Cards are created and revised by the team during [[Sprint Planning]] and development, then moved across a visible board as their state changes.

A card is a coordination aid, not proof of product value. Progress is ultimately judged by completed backlog items and the integrated [[Product Increment]].
