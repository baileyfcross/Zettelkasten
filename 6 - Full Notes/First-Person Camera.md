# First-Person Camera

A first-person camera places the viewpoint at or near a character's eyes and derives its target from the viewing direction. Translation follows the character, while yaw and pitch control where the camera looks.

The design produces an immersive perspective but must manage rotation limits, input sensitivity, and field of view carefully. Visual geometry for the controlled body may require special treatment because it is unusually close to the camera.
