# Mosaic Browser

Mosaic was a graphical Web browser developed at an NSF-supported supercomputer center in Illinois. It integrated text, images, mouse navigation, and icons into an accessible interface.

Its creators later founded Netscape and helped bring the Web into commercial use. Mosaic demonstrated how interface design could unlock the value of open network protocols.
