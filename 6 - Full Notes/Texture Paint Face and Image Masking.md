# Texture Paint Face and Image Masking

Face masking restricts painting to selected mesh faces, connecting surface selection with the pixels those faces occupy. Image or stencil masks instead restrict the stroke according to a two-dimensional pattern.

These mechanisms solve different targeting problems and can be combined. Face selection protects unrelated object regions, while an image mask shapes detail inside the permitted area without requiring every boundary to follow mesh topology.
