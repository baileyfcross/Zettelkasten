# Tactile Vocabulary

A tactile vocabulary is a learned set of skin-stimulation patterns associated with symbols, commands, or concepts. Candidate dimensions include body location, rhythm, duration, frequency, intensity, apparent motion, and modulated roughness.

Discrimination between two patterns is easier than identifying one pattern from memory, so a large physically distinct signal set does not automatically become a large usable vocabulary. Codes should emphasize salient dimensions, limit easily confused levels, and exploit intuitive mappings where possible. [[Tacton|Tactons]] represent whole concepts to avoid the slow process of spelling every word tactilely.
