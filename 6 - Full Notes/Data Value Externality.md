# Data Value Externality

A data value externality occurs when an organization captures benefits from data while shifting some costs or risks to people who generated, supplied, or are represented by it. Privacy loss, unfair profiling, and weakened bargaining power may remain outside the firm's price calculation.

Recognizing the externality explains why possession alone may not justify unrestricted use. Liability, access rights, compensation, or governance duties can be considered alongside ownership.
