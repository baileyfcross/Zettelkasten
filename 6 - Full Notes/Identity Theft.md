# Identity Theft

Identity theft is the unauthorized use of information associated with another person to impersonate them or obtain benefits. User-generated platforms expose names, locations, relationships, routines, and preferences that can be combined into convincing fraudulent profiles.

Protection involves minimizing unnecessary disclosure, detecting abnormal account behavior, strengthening authentication, and preventing sensitive attributes from being inferred from public context.

Immersive communication raises the credibility of impersonation because a fraudulent participant can appear as a familiar avatar, voice, or holographic person inside a shared space. A request for money or credentials may therefore arrive with social and spatial cues that normally signal trust. Strong authentication must bind the represented identity to the session instead of relying on lifelike appearance alone.
