# Corpus Linguistics

Corpus linguistics is the computer-mediated study of language structure and use in collections of naturally occurring text. It searches for patterns, associations, historical changes, meanings, and sentiment while retaining attention to the contexts in which forms appear.

The method combines quantitative rigor with qualitative investigation. Rather than assuming a word has one stable meaning, it asks how the word behaves across genres, periods, and neighboring language.
