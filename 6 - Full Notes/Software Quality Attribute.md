# Software Quality Attribute

A software quality attribute describes a measurable property of how a system operates, such as performance, scalability, robustness, security, usability, interoperability, or maintainability. It cuts across individual features and often shapes the system's major boundaries and technology choices.

A useful attribute is tied to a scenario and target. The architecture cannot meaningfully optimize “quality” in general; it must decide which properties matter for the expected users, load, environment, cost, and future change.
