# Descriptive Analysis

Descriptive analysis characterizes the observations in an [[Analytic Dataset]] before multivariable modeling. It summarizes distributions, reports the prevalence or average of outcomes, and examines how exposures and candidate confounders vary across relevant groups.

Its main deliverables combine tables, graphs, and bivariate tests. Description is not merely preliminary decoration: it can reveal implausible codes, sparse cells, missingness, skewness, or confounding patterns that change how the later model should be specified.
