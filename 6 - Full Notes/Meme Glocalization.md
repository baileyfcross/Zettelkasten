# Meme Glocalization

Meme glocalization is the adaptation of a globally circulating meme to local languages, identities, issues, and cultural conventions. The resulting artifact is simultaneously recognizable across borders and meaningful within a particular setting.

Glocalization is more than literal translation. Creators may replace characters, rewrite social stereotypes, change the target of a joke, or shift the meme's stance. The process reveals which parts of a formula are portable and which require cultural substitution to remain intelligible.
