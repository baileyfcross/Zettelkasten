# Closed Parametric Curve

A [[Parametric Curve]] $r:[a,b]\to\mathbb{R}^n$ is closed when its initial and terminal points coincide: $r(a)=r(b)$. The parameter therefore returns to its starting point after traversing the trace.

Closedness alone does not prevent the curve from crossing itself or visiting other points more than once. Adding the appropriate simplicity condition produces a [[Jordan Curve]].
