# Four-Stroke Aircraft Piston Engine

A four-stroke piston engine completes intake, compression, combustion and expansion, and exhaust strokes. Compression requires work on the mixture, while expansion after ignition produces useful work through the connecting rod and crankshaft. This repeating mechanical sequence is represented thermodynamically by the [[Otto Cycle Engine Efficiency]].
