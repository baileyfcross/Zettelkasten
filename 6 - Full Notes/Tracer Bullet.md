# Tracer Bullet

A tracer bullet is a thin, representative path through a larger body of work. By implementing one realistic slice across relevant layers, a team learns about integration, performance, workflow, and cost before estimating or committing to the full set.

Unlike a disposable research [[Spike]], a tracer bullet is working product evidence. Its purpose is not breadth or polish but reducing uncertainty with an end-to-end example.
