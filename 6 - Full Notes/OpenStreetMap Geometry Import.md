# OpenStreetMap Geometry Import

A historical Blender OpenStreetMap add-on imported an exported OSM region and generated flat geometry corresponding to its street and building layout. The result provided real-world plan geometry that could be extruded or refined into a cityscape.

Imported height information was limited to what the map data recorded, so the geometry was often largely planar. OSM data and the add-on's installation and import procedure are version-specific, and the source data's license and completeness remain part of the workflow.
