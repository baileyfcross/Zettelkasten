# Propensity Modeling

Propensity modeling estimates the likelihood that an individual will take a specified future action, such as responding to an offer, defaulting on a loan, or leaving a service. Its output is useful when a business can prioritize a timely response rather than treating all customers identically.

The target event, observation window, prediction horizon, and intervention cost must be set together. Ranking likely churners is valuable only if there is a feasible offer or contact process for the customers selected and if the model's score is used within that process.
