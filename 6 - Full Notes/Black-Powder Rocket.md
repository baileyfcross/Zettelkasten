# Black-Powder Rocket

A black-powder rocket uses a slow-burning gunpowder mixture of saltpeter, sulfur, and charcoal as solid propellant. Invented in China around 1100 CE, it remained the principal rocket form for roughly eight centuries and was used for fireworks and warfare.

Improved casings made rockets competitive with artillery during the Napoleonic Wars, but rifled guns later surpassed their accuracy. The black-powder form could not approach the efficiency needed for orbital flight. Its history nevertheless established the self-contained reaction device from which modern [[Solid-Propellant Rocket]] technology developed.
