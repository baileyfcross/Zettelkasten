# Workload Redistribution

Workload redistribution changes processor assignments after an application finishes or a failure makes the current allocation unbalanced. It can give newly idle capacity to the remaining tasks and reduce the completion time of the pack.

Redistribution is worthwhile only when its migration and reconfiguration costs are smaller than the time saved by the new allocation.
