# Trajectory Clustering

Trajectory clustering groups moving objects from sequences of spatial positions, possibly augmented with time, speed, direction, or context. Similarity may compare complete paths, aligned subtrajectories, stops, or common route segments.

Different applications care about different invariances: two travelers may share a route at different times, or occupy the same places in a different order. The representation should make the intended notion of shared movement explicit.
