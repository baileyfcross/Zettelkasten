# Mobile Privacy Boundary

A mobile privacy boundary is the practical line a user draws around personal information when deciding what an application or service may know and share. The boundary is shaped by context, perceived benefit, trust, and expectations about who will receive the data.

Cross-platform exchange makes the boundary difficult to maintain because a disclosure to one application can enter a much larger profile. Effective control requires visibility into those secondary flows.
