# Video Game

A video game is a [[Game]] mediated by a computer. Software implements the rules, maintains state, presents the game world, accepts player actions, and calculates their consequences.

Computer mediation enables rapid bookkeeping, hidden information, complex simulations, artificial opponents, networked play, and audiovisual feedback. It also means that permitted actions are limited to what the software and interface implement.
