# Finite Element Temperature Distribution

A finite element temperature distribution is the interpolated thermal field obtained from solved nodal temperatures. Within a linear one-dimensional element, temperature varies linearly between its endpoint values.

The distribution reveals gradients that drive conductive heat flux. Comparing nodal results with analytical solutions provides a direct check on discretization, material assignment, and thermal boundary conditions.
