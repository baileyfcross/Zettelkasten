# Laplacian Regularized Metric Learning

Laplacian regularized metric learning supplements labeled constraints with a graph built from labeled and unlabeled observations. A Laplacian penalty encourages nearby points on the data manifold to remain close after transformation.

The supervised term separates classes, while the graph term preserves local geometry inferred from abundant unlabeled data. Their relative weight controls whether labels or manifold structure dominates the learned distance.
