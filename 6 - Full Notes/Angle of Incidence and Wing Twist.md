# Angle of Incidence and Wing Twist

Angle of incidence is the fixed angle between a wing reference line and the aircraft's longitudinal reference. Wing twist changes that incidence along the span: washout reduces it toward the tip, while wash-in increases it. Washout can help the root stall first and preserve aileron effectiveness as the aircraft nears [[Angle of Attack and Critical Stall]].
