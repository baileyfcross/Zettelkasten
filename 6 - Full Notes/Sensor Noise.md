# Sensor Noise

Sensor noise is the variation added when a camera converts light into digital measurements. It includes changing random components as well as stable differences in how individual sensor elements respond to the same illumination.

Forensic analysis uses both properties. Random-noise level should vary plausibly with brightness and camera processing, while the stable component can identify a device. Denoising, sharpening, compression, resizing, saturation, and low exposure all alter the observable signal.
