# Unity Renderer

A Unity Renderer makes a GameObject's visual data visible in the Scene and Game panes. For ordinary 3D geometry, a Mesh Renderer combines the mesh supplied by a [[Unity Mesh Filter]] with materials, shaders, lighting, and camera state to produce screen pixels.

Rendering is separate from collision and physics. An object can be visible without being solid, or solid without being visible, depending on its component set. This separation lets a designer choose simplified collision geometry and change presentation without redefining physical behavior.
