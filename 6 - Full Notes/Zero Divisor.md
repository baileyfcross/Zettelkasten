# Zero Divisor

A zero divisor is a nonzero element a of a ring for which another nonzero element c satisfies ac = 0. Zero divisors cannot occur in an [[Integral Domain]].

Every nonzero [[Nilpotent Element]] is a zero divisor because some lower positive power is nonzero while its product with a further power is zero. Zero divisors also motivate the module-specific notion of a [[Zero Divisor on a Module]].
