# Template-Based NLG

Template-based natural language generation fills variable values into predefined sentence patterns. It gives designers precise control and produces reliable language for recurring service messages.

Templates need alternatives and grammatical logic to handle number, gender, context, and repetition. Without such variation, an assistant sounds rigid even when its [[Dialog Manager]] behaves correctly.
