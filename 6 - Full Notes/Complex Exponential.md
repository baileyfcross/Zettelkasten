# Complex Exponential

The complex exponential extends the exponential power series to complex arguments. Separating the even and odd powers gives Euler's relation $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$.

Consequently, $e^{x+i\theta}=e^x(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$ has modulus $e^x$ and argument $\theta$. Complex exponentials compress oscillatory sine and cosine calculations into algebra and make multiplication correspond to adding phases.
