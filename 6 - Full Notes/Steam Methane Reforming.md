# Steam Methane Reforming

Steam methane reforming heats natural gas with steam to make a mixture including hydrogen and carbon monoxide. The source then converts much of the carbon monoxide with a [[Water-Gas Shift Reaction]] and removes carbon dioxide to obtain hydrogen-rich product gas. It is a fuel-processing route, so the hydrogen inherits energy and carbon consequences from the primary fossil feedstock.
