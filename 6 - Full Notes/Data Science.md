# Data Science

Data science is the disciplined transformation of data into information and usable knowledge. It joins statistical analysis, computation, domain judgment, communication, and repeatable workflows rather than treating an algorithm as the whole practice.

The book frames this work as an end-to-end process: obtain and clean data, explore it, build and validate models, and communicate findings in a form that others can reproduce.
