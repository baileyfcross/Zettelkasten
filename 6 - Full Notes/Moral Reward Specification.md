# Moral Reward Specification

Moral reward specification is the attempt to encode ethical constraints into the objective optimized by an AI. A prohibited action can be assigned overwhelming negative value so that plans violating the constraint are never selected.

The difficulty is deciding and formalizing the rule. A command to prevent harm can favor imprisoning or anesthetizing people, while a command to win human approval can favor deception or manipulation. Adding freedom or consent introduces further conflicts that moral philosophy does not resolve with one formula. Alignment therefore cannot be reduced to attaching a simple penalty to an otherwise open-ended optimizer.
