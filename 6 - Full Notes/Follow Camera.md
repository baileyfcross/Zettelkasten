# Follow Camera

A follow camera maintains a chosen horizontal and vertical offset behind a moving target. The target's orientation determines the desired camera position and the direction in which the camera looks.

Directly snapping to the desired transform is simple but can transmit every abrupt target movement to the view. A [[Spring Follow Camera]] adds controlled lag and damping for smoother motion.
