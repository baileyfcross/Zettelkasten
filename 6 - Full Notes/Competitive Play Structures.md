# Competitive Play Structures

Competitive play makes participants' outcomes meaningfully opposed: success for one side entails defeat, loss of position, or reduced opportunity for another. Competition may place players directly against one another, compare asynchronous performances, or pit a player against a system.

Symmetrical competition gives sides equivalent capabilities, while asymmetrical competition assigns different powers or goals. Fairness in either form depends on the whole opportunity structure rather than identical surface features.
