# Correlation Clustering Subspace

A correlation subspace is a set of directions along which a group follows a strong linear relationship rather than merely occupying a small axis-aligned box. Points may vary widely on individual attributes while remaining close to a lower-dimensional manifold.

Correlation clustering measures deviation from that manifold and groups points sharing a relationship. It must distinguish genuine local correlation from chance alignments and from one global trend spanning the dataset.
