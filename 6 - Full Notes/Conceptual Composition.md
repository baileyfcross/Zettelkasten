# Conceptual Composition

Conceptual composition combines the meanings of words and phrases into the meaning of a larger expression. The interpretation depends not only on individual words but also on their syntactic and semantic relationships.

An assistant uses composition to build a [[Meaning Representation]] with an [[Intent]] and linked arguments. This is harder than detecting isolated keywords because order and structure can change who did what to whom.
