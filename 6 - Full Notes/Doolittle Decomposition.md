# Doolittle Decomposition

Doolittle decomposition is an [[LU Decomposition]] convention in which the diagonal entries of $L$ are all one. The remaining lower-triangular entries and the entries of $U$ are determined successively.

After factorization, [[Forward Substitution]] solves $Ly=b$ and [[Back Substitution]] solves $Ux=y$. Zero pivots may require row permutation.
