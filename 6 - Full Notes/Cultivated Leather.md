# Cultivated Leather

Cultivated leather is a leather-like material produced from cells and their secreted collagen rather than from a complete animal hide. Fibroblast sheets can be layered, matured, and processed with adapted tanning methods.

Uniformity and predetermined shape reduce waste, but collagen content, thickness, tear strength, and compatibility with stitching must reach the demands of finished goods.
