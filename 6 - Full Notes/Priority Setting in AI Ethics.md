# Priority Setting in AI Ethics

Priority setting in AI ethics asks how attention, money, policy, and technical effort should be allocated among AI risks and other urgent social or environmental problems. The severity of a possible harm, the quality of evidence, the time horizon, and the opportunity cost of action all matter.

Speculative danger from future superintelligence may be grave but uncertain, while discrimination and surveillance are already present. AI itself may also distract from poverty, war, or climate change unless it contributes to addressing them. Ethical priority setting makes these comparisons explicit rather than allowing technological novelty or powerful institutions to define urgency by default.
