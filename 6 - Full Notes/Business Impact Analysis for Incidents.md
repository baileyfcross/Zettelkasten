# Business Impact Analysis for Incidents

Business impact analysis for incidents determines which users, transactions, objectives, revenue, or contractual promises are affected by a technical problem. It moves prioritization beyond the size of a CPU spike or error count toward the consequence of not acting.

Observability can connect an anomaly with active users, failed purchases, claim-processing time, or an SLO and its remaining error budget. This supports a top-down response in which the most urgent incident is the one threatening important outcomes, not necessarily the one producing the most alerts.
