# COMSOL Point Line and Surface Evaluations

Point evaluations sample a field at a location, line evaluations trace spatial profiles, and surface or volume evaluations integrate or average over geometric regions. Each operation converts a distributed numerical field into a quantity tied to an engineering question.

The evaluation geometry and units must match the expression: heat flux integrated over area yields power, while an average temperature remains a temperature. Reusing named evaluations across [[Parametric Sweep in COMSOL|sweep cases]] makes comparisons consistent and auditable.
