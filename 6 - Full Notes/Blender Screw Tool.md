# Blender Screw Tool

The Blender Screw tool creates helical mesh geometry by rotating and duplicating a selected profile around an axis while offsetting successive copies along that axis. The 3D cursor and current view establish the center and orientation of the operation.

In the demonstrated workflow, a circular profile supplies the screw's cross section and a separate line on the axis supplies its pitch. Step count controls smoothness, and the turns setting controls how many revolutions are generated. The setup therefore separates profile shape, axial rise, and rotational repetition.
