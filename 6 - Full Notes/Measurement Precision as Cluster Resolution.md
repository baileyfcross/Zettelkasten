# Measurement Precision as Cluster Resolution

In a longest-common-prefix hierarchy, the number of retained digits sets cluster resolution. Sharing one digit creates broad bins; sharing several creates more selective bins. Reducing recorded precision can thus make values equal at a coarse level and induce a more visibly hierarchical representation.

The book demonstrates this with chemical fingerprints recoded at different precisions. The effect is partly a consequence of encoding, not a discovery independent of the measurement process. A coarse bin can aid search or preprocessing while hiding distinctions needed for scientific interpretation.
