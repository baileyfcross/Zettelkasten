# Radioactive Decay

Radioactive decay is a spontaneous, random nuclear transformation. The time of one nucleus's decay cannot be predicted, but a large population follows $N=N_0e^{-\lambda t}$ with constant decay probability per unit time. Activity is $A=\lambda N$, so it falls with the same exponential law.

The student companion computes and graphs the remaining quantity across time, showing that equal time steps remove a constant fraction rather than a constant amount. The R curve also makes the connection between the decay constant and [[Half-Life]] directly testable.
