# Game Design Schematic

A game design schematic maps how screens, scenes, states, or moments connect across the player experience. It turns a verbal description into a visible structure that exposes required content, transitions, decisions, and feedback.

The schematic can combine flow diagrams, wireframes, and storyboards according to the current question. Its abstractions should make the next prototype easier to plan rather than imitate finished art.
