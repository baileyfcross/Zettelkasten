# Semiconductor Band Gap

A band gap is the forbidden interval between occupied valence states and available conduction states in an [[Electron Energy Band]] picture. An electron must gain enough energy to cross it before it can contribute to conduction. The source gives silicon's gap as about 1.1 eV; its size helps determine which [[Photon Energy]] can generate carriers and which light passes without useful conversion.
