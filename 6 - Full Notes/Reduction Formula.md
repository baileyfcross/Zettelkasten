# Reduction Formula

A reduction formula expresses one member of a parameterized family of integrals in terms of a simpler member with a lower parameter value. Repeated application eventually reaches an integral that can be evaluated directly.

Such formulas are often obtained through [[Integration by Parts]] after rewriting the integrand to reproduce the original family. The recurrence does not evaluate every case at once; it supplies a controlled chain from the requested case to a known base case.
