# Industry 4.0

Industry 4.0 describes an industrial stage built around connected machines, cyber-physical systems, pervasive data, and increasingly autonomous production. Sensors and networked equipment allow processes to be monitored and adjusted through analytics.

The concept links physical operations to [[Business Intelligence and Analytics 4.0]]. Automation therefore depends on both machine connectivity and methods capable of converting streams of operational data into decisions.
