# Skin Modifier Edge Modeling

The Skin modifier builds volumetric mesh geometry around a network of vertices and edges. Editing the edge skeleton changes the generated form, and per-vertex radii control how thick the skin becomes at different points.

This makes a sparse graph an efficient structural model for branching or limb-like shapes. A subdivision modifier can smooth the blocky generated surface, while the underlying edge network remains the simpler editable representation.
