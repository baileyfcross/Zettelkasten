# High-Tech Profiling

High-tech profiling assembles detailed information from surveillance and data-gathering systems to classify a person or group. The profile can combine self-tracking records with other sources and infer conditions, behavior, identity, or risk beyond what the person directly disclosed.

The danger lies in both error and power. A profile may rely on a misleading correlation, yet even an accurate inference can be used in a context where it produces discrimination or removes meaningful choice. The person may never know which data or model affected the decision.

Civil-rights analysis therefore asks who builds the profile, what opportunities or burdens follow, whether protected traits are reproduced through proxies, and how a person can inspect or challenge the result.
