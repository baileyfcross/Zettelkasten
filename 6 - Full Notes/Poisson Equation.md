# Poisson Equation

Poisson equation relates the Laplacian of a scalar field to a specified source distribution. It generalizes Laplace equation by allowing charge, mass, or another density to create curvature in the potential field.

A complete solution combines the influence of the sources with conditions imposed on the boundary. Green-function or integral approaches express the field as a superposition of responses to point sources, while separation methods work when the geometry and source permit them.
