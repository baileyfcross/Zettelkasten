# Contextual Sentiment Visualization

Contextual sentiment visualization pairs an aggregate view of prominent words with a display of the phrases surrounding a selected term. A [[Word Cloud]] can identify candidates for inspection, while a [[Word Tree Visualization]] reveals whether their recurring contexts express praise, criticism, or a mixture.

The combination helps explain a classifier or review collection without pretending frequency alone determines polarity. Analysts should inspect positive and negative subsets separately when a shared term occurs in both classes.
