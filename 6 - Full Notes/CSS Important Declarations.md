# CSS Important Declarations

Appending `!important` to a CSS declaration raises its explicit weight above normal declarations. Importance applies to the individual declaration, not to every declaration in the same rule.

The cascade compares important and normal groups before it compares [[CSS Specificity]]. Reader important styles have special precedence in the source's cascade order, preserving the reader's ability to enforce essential presentation needs over author styles.
