# Tutorial Level

A tutorial level teaches controls, actions, challenges, and rules through an intentionally arranged early experience. It lets the player learn by doing rather than requiring all instructions to be read in advance.

New demands should be introduced with clear feedback and enough safety to experiment, then recombined to confirm understanding. A tutorial succeeds when later play can rely on what the player learned.

An effective tutorial behaves as [[Instructional Scaffolding]] rather than a detached lecture. It integrates instruction with the relevant action, adapts or becomes skippable for experts, and makes learning itself feel like ordinary play.
