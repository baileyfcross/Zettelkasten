# Platform Team Buy-In

Platform team buy-in is the practical commitment of the people who will build, operate, support, and evolve a chosen capability. An architect may make the initial decision without performing daily maintenance, so technical merit alone cannot make an unsustainable choice succeed.

Buy-in matters most when a decision requires new skills, language, operating practices, or on-call obligations. Collaboration can expose hidden effort and reduce burnout; where full consensus is impossible, the team can disagree and commit after concerns and consequences are recorded. A solution the responsible team cannot or will not sustain creates immediate technical debt.
