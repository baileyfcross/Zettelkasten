# User-Item Interaction

A user-item interaction records a person's relationship with an item, such as viewing, adopting, rejecting, posting, or sharing it. A matrix can place users on one axis, items on the other, and observed behaviors in its entries.

Recommendation predicts missing entries from observed interactions and contextual evidence. Because the matrix is often sparse, social relations and auxiliary item domains can supply additional structure.
