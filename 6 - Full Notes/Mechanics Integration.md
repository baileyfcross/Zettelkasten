# Mechanics Integration

Mechanics integration teaches how a newly learned action combines with systems the player already understands. After isolated instruction and practice, the game places the mechanic in a situation where an explicit explanation or an observable consequence reveals a useful interaction.

Integration prevents onboarding from becoming a collection of disconnected tutorials. A player who learns that two effects amplify one another can carry that relationship into later problems and discover new applications. The lesson therefore expands the player's systemic model rather than merely adding another control.
