# Skill Acquisition

Skill acquisition is the process through which repeated performance makes actions faster, more accurate, and less dependent on conscious attention.

The cognitive stage relies on explicit instructions and deliberate thought. The associative stage tunes behavior and reduces errors. In the autonomous stage, actions can be executed rapidly and accurately with little conscious control. Acquired skills are stored as procedural knowledge in [[Long-Term Memory]].

For training in a [[Virtual Environment]], an important question is whether a skill learned through the interface transfers to performance in the physical world.
