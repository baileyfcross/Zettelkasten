# Identity Matrix

The identity matrix is a [[Square Matrix]] with ones on the main diagonal and zeros elsewhere. It is the multiplicative identity: $IA=AI=A$ whenever the dimensions are compatible.

It appears in the definitions of a [[Matrix Inverse]] and the [[Characteristic Equation]]. Subtracting $\lambda I$ from a matrix shifts its diagonal entries when forming an eigenvalue problem.
