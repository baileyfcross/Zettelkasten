# Maximin and Minimax Principle

The maximin principle makes the row player choose the row whose worst payoff is largest. The minimax principle makes the column player choose the column whose worst possible loss, represented by its largest row payoff, is smallest.

These conservative choices create a lower and upper bound on the game value. When the bounds coincide, their common matrix entry is a [[Saddle Point in a Matrix Game]] and both selected pure strategies are stable.
