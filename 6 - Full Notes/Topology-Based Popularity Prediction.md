# Topology-Based Popularity Prediction

Topology-based popularity prediction uses the positions and connections of users who encounter or propagate content. Network paths, community reach, and the influence of early adopters can distinguish otherwise similar activity counts.

Diffusion through one narrow community may plateau, while adoption through several independent regions can expand further. Structural features therefore complement content and temporal behavior.
