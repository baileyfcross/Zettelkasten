# Data Integrity

Data integrity is the property that information remains accurate, complete, and consistent with its intended use. An accidental change, deliberate substitution, transmission error, or unauthorized modification can all violate integrity.

Integrity must be protected across the full data journey, including endpoints, communication channels, active processing, storage, backup, and archival. Authentication of endpoints, encryption, checksums, monitoring, and controlled modification each address a different part of that journey.
