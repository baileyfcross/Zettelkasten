# Numerical Feature

A numerical feature takes quantitative values for which ordering and differences can carry meaning. Examples include age, length, count, or a continuous sensor measurement.

Numerical domains support comparisons and notions of nearness that many analytic methods use directly. Their scale still matters: a ratio-scaled feature permits meaningful multiplicative statements, while other numerical measurements may support differences without making ratios meaningful.
