# Muscle-on-a-Chip

A muscle-on-a-chip forms engineered muscle between flexible anchors or pillars. Electrical stimulation causes contraction, and measured pillar deflection can be converted into force using the pillar's known geometry and material properties.

Cells carrying a disease-associated mutation let the platform compare contractility and screen compounds intended to restore muscle function.
