# Refreshable Braille Display

A refreshable braille display uses individually controlled pins to raise the dot patterns for a changing line of digital text. After the reader finishes the displayed characters, the pins reconfigure to present the next segment from a computer or mobile device.

The actuator array must reproduce braille geometry within fingertip [[Haptic Spatial Acuity]] while fitting many cells into a compact, reliable device. Unlike speech output, it preserves spelling, punctuation, layout cues, and the active scanning process of [[Braille Tactile Reading]], but the number of simultaneously available characters is limited by physical size and cost.
