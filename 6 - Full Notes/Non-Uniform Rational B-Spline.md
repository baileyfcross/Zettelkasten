# Non-Uniform Rational B-Spline

A non-uniform rational B-spline, or NURBS, extends a B-spline by assigning weights to control points and dividing the weighted basis sum by the total weighted basis. The nonuniform knot vector controls parameter intervals and continuity, while weights adjust how strongly individual control points attract the curve.

Rational coordinates allow exact representation of conic sections that ordinary polynomial cubic pieces can only approximate. NURBS retain the B-spline advantages of piecewise construction and local control, while supporting circles, ellipses, and other engineering curves within one general representation.
