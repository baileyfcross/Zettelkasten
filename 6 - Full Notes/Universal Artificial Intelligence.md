# Universal Artificial Intelligence

Universal artificial intelligence is a theoretical ideal that combines probabilistic model building with action selection that maximizes expected future reward. It describes what a perfectly general learner would do without claiming that the ideal can be implemented efficiently.

Its value is architectural rather than practical. It separates three questions that every engineered agent must answer: what objective supplies reward, what evidence and prior structure support learning, and what optimization method can search the resulting possibilities. These questions expose why more data or computation alone does not settle the design of [[Artificial General Intelligence]].
