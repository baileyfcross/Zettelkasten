# Carbon-Aware Workload Scheduling

Carbon-aware workload scheduling chooses execution times or locations partly according to the emissions associated with available electricity. It can favor periods of abundant renewable generation or regions with a cleaner power mix.

The schedule remains constrained by deadlines, resource capacity, transfer costs, and reliability. Lower emissions are useful only if the work still completes within its service requirements.
