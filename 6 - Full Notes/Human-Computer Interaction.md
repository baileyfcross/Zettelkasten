# Human-Computer Interaction

Human-computer interaction studies the relationship and communication between people and interactive technology. People communicate actions, goals, and queries to a system, while the system communicates its state and responses through a [[User Interface]].

The field places human needs first and draws on knowledge of [[Perception]], [[Cognition]], and physical action. It includes both understanding existing interactions and designing new ones, from devices and gestures to computer-mediated collaboration. Effectiveness depends on the purpose: task performance may dominate a work system, while engagement or emotional impact may matter more in a game or artwork.

HCI design therefore involves tradeoffs rather than a single universally optimal solution.
