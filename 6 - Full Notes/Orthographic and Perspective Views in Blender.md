# Orthographic and Perspective Views in Blender

Perspective projection resembles ordinary vision: equal-sized objects appear smaller with distance. Orthographic projection removes that size change, so equal dimensions remain visually equal regardless of depth.

Perspective is helpful for judging the final spatial impression, while orthographic views are useful for matching blueprints and controlling proportions. Blender can toggle between them and can jump directly to front, side, top, bottom, or camera views.
