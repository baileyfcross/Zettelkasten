# GPS Phase I Validation

GPS Phase I was the concept-validation period in which experimental transmitters, satellites, and receivers tested whether the proposed architecture worked as a system. Ground-based pseudo-satellites at New Mexico test ranges exercised code ranging before launch, and the NTS and early Block I satellites validated spaceborne clocks, signals, and user equipment. Validation reduced the risk of committing to a full global constellation while revealing receiver and signal problems that required redesign.
