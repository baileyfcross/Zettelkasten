# EventStorming

EventStorming is a collaborative modeling technique that visualizes how a business behaves using a large surface and color-coded notes. Participants begin with [[Domain Event]]s and arrange them along an [[Event Timeline]], allowing many people to contribute simultaneously. The visible model exposes ambiguous terminology, missing steps, disagreements, and boundaries that a verbal meeting can hide. Its primary output is shared understanding, not a permanent diagram or a mechanically generated implementation.
