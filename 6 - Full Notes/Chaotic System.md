# Chaotic System

A chaotic system is deterministic but highly sensitive to its initial conditions. Very small differences in starting state can produce dramatically different later behavior.

Chaotic unpredictability is therefore distinct from a [[Random System]]. A designer may know all the rules of a chaotic system while still being unable to make reliable long-range predictions about its outcomes.
