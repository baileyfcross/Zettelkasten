# Decider

A decider is a logical branch that routes a flow according to internal state, available resources, or external conditions. It represents a decision point inside a system model.

The conditions governing a decider should remain near the same organizational level and operational context as the decision. Excessive dependence across distant levels makes a [[System]] harder to understand and more brittle.
