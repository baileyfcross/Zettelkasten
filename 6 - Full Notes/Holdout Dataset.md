# Holdout Dataset

A holdout dataset is a subset excluded from a particular fitting stage so it can estimate performance on unseen cases. Depending on the workflow, a holdout may serve as validation or final testing.

Its interpretation depends on randomization, representativeness, and a strict record of which decisions were allowed to use it.
