# Social Robot

A social robot is an embodied system that interacts with people through speech, movement, gaze, expression, and spatial behavior. Its physical presence makes [[Nonverbal Communication]] part of the interface.

Unlike a screen-only assistant, it must locate speakers, orient sensors, coordinate motion with dialog, and behave safely in shared space. Embodiment intensifies both social expectations and technical failure modes.
