# Gray-Level Co-Occurrence Matrix

A gray-level co-occurrence matrix counts pairs of intensity values found at a defined displacement and direction in an image. Statistics derived from the matrix describe texture through local adjacency rather than isolated pixels.

Changing the offset or orientation reveals texture at different scales and directions. The representation is sensitive to quantization and parameter choices, so several matrices or summarized measures may be needed for robust characterization.
