# Filtering

Filtering estimates the present hidden state from observations available through the present time. A recursive filter alternates prediction through the transition model with correction through the observation model.

The operation answers the time-indexed question with no offset between the searched state and the latest evidence. It differs from prediction of future states and smoothing of past states.
