# Internet as Network of Heterogeneous Networks

The Internet is an internetwork that links independently designed networks through common protocols. Participants can retain different local media, hardware, and administrative structures while exchanging packets globally.

Open interconnection distinguishes it from a single proprietary service. Its technical unity comes from TCP/IP and shared addressing rather than one owner or one physical network.
