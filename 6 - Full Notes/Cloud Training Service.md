# Cloud Training Service

A cloud training service treats enrollment, content delivery, exercises, assessment, progress, and certification as a scalable process rather than only placing lecture material online. It can serve learners beyond the physical capacity and location of one institution.

Integrating identity and testing makes training closer to a complete business-process service. Customer-centered delivery should adapt the timing and path to the learner while preserving credible evidence of participation and achievement.
