# Finned Central Channel Geometry

Finned central channel geometry adds smaller extended surfaces to the walls of a void passing through a larger fin. The nested features increase geometric complexity because they must remain connected to the host while preserving a continuous open channel.

A robust construction uses parameters, repeated transforms, Boolean operations, and sectional measurements. The added detail is justified only when the physics being modeled can respond to the additional surface and altered flow or conduction path.
