# First-Time User Experience

The first-time user experience is a player's initial encounter with a game, including entry, orientation, tutorial, and the first core actions. It forms the player's first impression and begins construction of a [[Player Mental Model]].

Too much friction, explanation, or simultaneous novelty can overwhelm the [[Interactivity Budget]], while too little guidance can leave goals and actions opaque. Behavioral drop-off and [[Playtesting]] help locate where the experience loses players.
