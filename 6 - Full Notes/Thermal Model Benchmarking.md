# Thermal Model Benchmarking

A thermal benchmark is a case with an analytical solution, trusted numerical result, or controlled experiment against which a model can be compared. Simple geometry and conditions isolate errors that are difficult to diagnose in a complete multiphysics system.

The comparison should use the same properties, dimensions, initial state, and boundary assumptions. Agreement with a [[Lumped-Capacity Cooling of Hot Water|lumped model]], for example, supports only the regime where its uniform-temperature assumption is valid and does not validate unrelated flow or radiation features.
