# Three-Stub Tuner

A three-stub tuner is a microwave matching component that places three adjustable [[Tuned Stub|tuned stubs]] at different positions along a main transmission path. Each stub couples electromagnetically to the main guide and changes the combined transmission and reflection behavior.

The modeled device uses three hollow rectangular stubs attached orthogonally through apertures in the upper wall of a [[Rectangular Waveguide]]. The stubs are short-circuited at their outer ends, and their heights act as the tunable parameters.

Using three separated adjustments allows the response to be shaped across a band rather than at only one geometric location. The model evaluates that response through [[Scattering Parameters]] and [[Voltage Standing Wave Ratio]].
