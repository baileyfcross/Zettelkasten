# GPS Spoofing

GPS spoofing presents counterfeit navigation signals that cause a receiver to compute a plausible but false position or time. It is more deceptive than jamming because the receiver may continue reporting a solution. Differential services address integrity with monitoring, error-detecting codes, and central alerts, while protected military codes add authentication. Cross-checking other constellations, inertial behavior, maps, clocks, or independent sensors helps expose inconsistencies.

Spoofing can redirect a vessel or unmanned system without making the receiver appear offline. Authentication and antispoofing signals raise the difficulty, but resilience still benefits from comparing satellite output with dissimilar evidence rather than trusting one apparently coherent position stream.
