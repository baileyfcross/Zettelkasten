# Beta Milestone

The beta milestone is reached when the game's features, systems, and assets are in place and the build is stable enough for broader release-oriented evaluation. Work centers on balancing, bug fixing, polishing, infrastructure, and continued player testing.

It follows the [[Alpha Milestone]] and precedes commercial release, but its exact public visibility varies. The essential distinction is that the design is no longer expanding materially; the team is validating and preparing the integrated product.
