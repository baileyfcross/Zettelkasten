# Polynomial Module of a Linear Operator

A linear operator $A$ on a vector space over a field turns that space into a module over the polynomial ring by defining $f(x)\cdot v=f(A)v$. Polynomial multiplication then represents repeated application and linear combinations of powers of the operator.

Relative to the standard generators, $xI-A$ is a [[Relation Matrix]]. Reducing it over the polynomial PID connects its invariant factors to the operator's polynomial and canonical-form structure.
