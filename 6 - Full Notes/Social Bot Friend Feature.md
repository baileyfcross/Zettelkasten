# Social Bot Friend Feature

A social bot friend feature summarizes properties of the accounts connected to a focal user. Aggregates over followers or friends can expose an unusual social environment even when the focal profile appears ordinary.

These features extend detection beyond self-reported attributes. Sampling limits, private accounts, and coordinated manipulation can affect the reliability of friend-based summaries.
