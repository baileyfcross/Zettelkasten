# Blender Shader Nodes

A shader describes how a surface interacts with light. Shader nodes expose those behaviors as inputs and outputs that can be combined with colors, values, textures, and other shaders.

The network separates material logic into visible stages. This makes it possible to inspect how a texture or numeric factor changes a surface and to reuse the same data in several branches before they converge at Material Output.
