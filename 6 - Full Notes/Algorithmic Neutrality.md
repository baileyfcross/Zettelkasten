# Algorithmic Neutrality

Algorithmic neutrality is the claim that an automated system merely applies objective mathematics and is therefore independent of social values. The claim is misleading because choices about data, categories, targets, error costs, and deployment determine what the system represents and whose interests it serves.

An algorithm can apply a rule consistently while reproducing an unjust institution or unequal history. Recognizing non-neutrality does not mean that every result is arbitrary. It means assumptions should be documented and justified rather than hidden behind technical language. [[Algorithmic Accountability]] begins where the appearance of inevitability ends.
