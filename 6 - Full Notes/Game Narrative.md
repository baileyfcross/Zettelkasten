# Game Narrative

Game narrative is a connected sequence of events made meaningful to the people involved. It can be authored directly, arise from play, or combine a fictional premise with systemic events chosen by the player.

Highly prescribed narrative narrows the [[Play Space]], while a premise can scaffold interpretation without fixing one path. Systemic narrative gains authenticity from player agency but gives the designer less precise control over the resulting story.

An [[Authored Story]] is deliberately carried by the game, while an [[Emergent Story]] is the account produced through a particular act of playing. Games can combine both, letting authored context and systemic events inform how the player interprets the experience.
