# Simplex Tableau

A simplex tableau stores the constraint coefficients, objective-row coefficients, current basic variables, right-hand-side values, and ratio calculations of a linear program in one array. It is the tabular form of the equations used by the [[Simplex Method]].

The tableau exposes whether the current basis is feasible and whether a nonbasic variable can improve the objective. After choosing entering and leaving variables, row operations update every coefficient while preserving an equivalent system.
