# Microprocessor Support Chip Ecosystem

A microprocessor becomes a usable computer when paired with memory, input-output controllers, clocks, and other support circuits. Offering a compatible family of chips lets customers configure one processor for many products.

Intel used this ecosystem to answer the commonality problem. Standard building blocks reduced custom hardware work while preserving application-specific behavior in software and peripheral choices.
