# Fuel Efficiency Unit Conversion

The book's fuel-efficiency quantities are distance per fuel volume: mi/gal and km/L. Converting mi/gal to km/L multiplies by kilometers per mile and by gallons per liter, the latter being the reciprocal orientation of a liters-per-gallon factor. Miles and gallons cancel separately. This is a compound ratio conversion, not merely a conversion of miles; both parts of the measure must preserve their positions. See [[Reciprocal Conversion Factors]] and [[Compound Rate Unit Conversion]].
