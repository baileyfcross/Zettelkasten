# Cascaded Thermoelectric Generator

A cascaded thermoelectric generator puts stages in successive temperature ranges. Heat rejected from an upper, hotter stage becomes heat supplied to a lower stage, allowing each to use material with a favorable [[Thermoelectric Figure of Merit]] at its own temperature. The stages shrink as available heat falls. Cascading can improve total utilization, but each stage must still manage electrical resistance and thermal conduction.
