# Character Rig Component Roles

A character rig separates bones by role. Deform bones transmit weighted motion to the mesh, control bones present animator-facing handles, and helper bones implement mechanical relationships that should remain hidden from ordinary posing.

Hierarchy and constraints connect those roles into one system, while custom shapes make controls recognizable. Naming, layer or collection organization, and locked transforms communicate which operations are valid so an animator does not accidentally edit the rig's internal machinery.
