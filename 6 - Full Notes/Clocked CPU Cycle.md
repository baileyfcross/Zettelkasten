# Clocked CPU Cycle

A clocked CPU uses regular timing pulses to separate one computational step from the next. Each interval allows signals to propagate through logic circuits and settle before their outputs are captured in registers.

The clock turns an abstract sequence of algorithmic steps into coordinated physical events. If the interval is too short, unstable signals may be recorded and corrupt later operations.
