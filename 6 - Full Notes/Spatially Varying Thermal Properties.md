# Spatially Varying Thermal Properties

Thermophysical properties can vary by domain, direction, or location within one geometry. A conductivity defined as $k(x,y,z)$ represents a continuously inhomogeneous material, while separate domain assignments represent distinct layers or components.

Spatial variation must correspond to the model's coordinate system and selections. It can create localized gradients even under simple boundary conditions, making [[COMSOL Domain and Boundary Selections|correct entity assignment]] and targeted mesh refinement essential.
