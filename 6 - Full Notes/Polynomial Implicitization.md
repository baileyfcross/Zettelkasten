# Polynomial Implicitization

Polynomial implicitization converts a polynomial parametrization xᵢ = fᵢ(t₁,…,tₘ) into equations in the x-coordinates that define the [[Zariski Closure]] of its image.

One forms an ideal generated by xᵢ − fᵢ and eliminates the parameter variables. The resulting [[Elimination Ideal]] contains exactly the implicit polynomial relations satisfied by every parametrized point.
