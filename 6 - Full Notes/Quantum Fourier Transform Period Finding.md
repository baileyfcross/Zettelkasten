# Quantum Fourier Transform Period Finding

Quantum period finding evaluates a periodic function coherently over a superposition of inputs and applies the quantum Fourier transform to make its reciprocal-period structure observable. Measurement produces frequency information from which the period can be recovered by classical postprocessing.

In [[Shor Period-Finding Reduction]], the periodic function is modular exponentiation. The QFT does not factor the integer by itself; it exposes the hidden period, while random base selection, modular arithmetic, continued-fraction reasoning, and greatest-common-divisor calculations surround the quantum subroutine.
