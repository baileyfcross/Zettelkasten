# Tissue Bioreactor

A tissue bioreactor maintains engineered tissue while controlling media flow, temperature, gases, biochemical composition, and sometimes mechanical or electrical stimulation. It can improve nutrient transport and reproduce aspects of the body's operating environment.

The chamber and flow path must support sterile handling while exposing the tissue to consistent, measurable conditions.
