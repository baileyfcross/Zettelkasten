# EHR Pharmacy Data

EHR pharmacy data represents medication orders, dispensing, and related drug information within the clinical record. Standard drug vocabularies help connect a prescribed product to normalized ingredients and strengths, while timestamps place medication exposure in the patient's care trajectory.

An order is not automatically evidence that a drug was dispensed or taken. The meaning of a field depends on the workflow that produced it. Analyses of outcomes or adverse effects should distinguish intended therapy from actual exposure and connect pharmacy events to diagnoses, laboratory results, and follow-up observations.
