# Spherical Law of Sines

For a spherical triangle,

$$\frac{\sin a}{\sin A}=\frac{\sin b}{\sin B}=\frac{\sin c}{\sin C}.$$

Lowercase letters denote angular side lengths and uppercase letters the opposite vertex angles. Like the plane [[Law of Sines]], the relation connects opposite parts, but all six quantities here are angles.
