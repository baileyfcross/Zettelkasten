# Rater Policy

A rater policy is the functional relationship a judge appears to use when converting a profile of predictor values into an overall decision. Judgmental analysis represents that relationship through a multiple-regression equation.

Comparing equations reveals whether judges share a policy even when their individual ratings differ. The result preserves both the direction and relative importance of the predictors.
