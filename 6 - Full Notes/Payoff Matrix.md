# Payoff Matrix

A payoff matrix lists the outcome for every combination of a row player's strategy and a column player's strategy. Entry $a_{ij}$ is the maximizing row player's payoff when the row player chooses strategy $i$ and the column player chooses strategy $j$.

For a two-person [[Zero-Sum Game]], the minimizing player's matrix is the negative of this matrix. Row minima and column maxima provide the quantities used by the [[Maximin and Minimax Principle]].
