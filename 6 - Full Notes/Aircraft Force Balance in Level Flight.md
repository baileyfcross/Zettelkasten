# Aircraft Force Balance in Level Flight

In steady straight-and-level flight, lift balances weight and thrust balances drag. A climb, descent, acceleration, or turn changes these vector relationships rather than creating new fundamental forces. This balance provides the reference state for understanding [[Bank Angle and Load Factor]] and the conversion of engine power into motion.
