# Regional Health Data Platform

A regional health data platform integrates healthcare information across institutions within a geographic area. It can connect hospital, primary-care, public-health, and administrative records for broader management and analysis.

Integration improves continuity and population-level insight, but it also concentrates sensitive information. Shared definitions, access controls, legal authority, and accountability are necessary for the platform to be trustworthy.
