# Space Science

Space science uses rockets, spacecraft, remote instruments, and related ground observations to study Earth, near space, the solar system, and the wider universe. It includes [[Space Physics]], [[Space Astronomy]], planetary science, life science, and Earth observation.

The field grew from short postwar sounding-rocket experiments into satellites and interplanetary missions. Cold War military needs and prestige supplied much of the initial funding, but the resulting laboratories, agencies, international partnerships, and discoveries made research increasingly self-sustaining. Scientific value depends on engineering systems that can launch, point, power, communicate with, and preserve an instrument long enough to collect interpretable data.
