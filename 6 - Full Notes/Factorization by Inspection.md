# Factorization by Inspection

Factorization by inspection searches possible factor pairs and tests which product reproduces a polynomial's coefficients. For a quadratic trinomial, signs and the factors of the leading and constant coefficients constrain the possible binomials.

Inspection is systematic trial rather than unsupported guessing. Each candidate is checked through [[Polynomial Expansion]], and symmetry can reduce the cases. It works efficiently when coefficients are small and integer factors exist. A quadratic that does not factor conveniently over integers may require [[Completing the Square]] or the [[Quadratic Formula]].
