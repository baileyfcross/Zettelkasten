# Conversational Reference Resolution

Conversational reference resolution identifies what a referring expression points to in the current interaction. Pronouns and phrases such as “that one” or “there” often depend on entities introduced in earlier turns or shown on a display.

Resolution uses [[Conversation Context]] and sometimes multimodal state. When several referents remain plausible, the [[Dialog Manager]] should use a [[Clarification Question]] rather than silently choose a risky action.
