# Player Competitiveness Preference

Player competitiveness preference is the degree to which an audience wants its performance measured and compared. A noncompetitive experience may omit scoring and rankings; a moderately competitive one may rank only the current session; a highly competitive one may preserve detailed statistics and global leaderboards.

The preference affects more than whether players can oppose each other. It determines which feedback is welcome, how long performance persists, and whether comparison motivates or alienates the audience. Competitive tracking should therefore be planned with the target profile instead of added as a neutral feature.
