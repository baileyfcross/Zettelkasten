# Two-Point Threshold

The two-point threshold is the smallest separation at which two simultaneous contacts on the skin are perceived as two points rather than one. It provides a behavioral measure of [[Haptic Spatial Acuity]].

Thresholds are much smaller on fingertips than on the back or limbs because receptor density and receptive-field size differ across the body. The measure is useful but not complete: pressure, orientation, stimulation method, and response criterion can affect the result, so it should not be treated as a universal resolution number for the entire tactile system.
