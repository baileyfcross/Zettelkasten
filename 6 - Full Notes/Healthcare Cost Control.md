# Healthcare Cost Control

Healthcare cost control uses data about services, utilization, payments, and outcomes to identify inefficiency and improve allocation. Insurance and institutional records can show where spending concentrates and how processes might change.

Lower cost is not the only objective. An analysis must preserve access and quality, distinguish necessary care from waste, and avoid incentives that shift burdens to patients whose needs are poorly represented.
