# Spaceflight Movement

The spaceflight movement was a transnational network of enthusiasts, theorists, experimenters, writers, and societies that promoted rockets as a practical route beyond Earth. Organizations arose in the Soviet Union, Germany, Austria, Britain, and the United States during the 1920s and 1930s.

Members exchanged publications, built small experimental rockets, and used popular media to make an apparently fantastic goal credible. Political repression and military secrecy later broke much of the open network apart, while wartime states absorbed rocket expertise. The movement's main achievement was cultural and technical legitimacy: it supplied ideas and committed people before governments supplied the scale of resources.
