# Word Cloud

A word cloud maps a term statistic, usually frequency or weight, to visual prominence. It offers a quick overview of dominant vocabulary and can reveal obvious artifacts that should be added to a [[Custom Stop List]] or investigated before modeling.

The display suppresses document context, word order, and precise comparisons between similarly sized terms. It is therefore best used as an exploratory diagnostic alongside counts or contextual displays rather than as evidence that a prominent word has a particular meaning or sentiment.
