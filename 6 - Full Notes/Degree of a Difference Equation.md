# Degree of a Difference Equation

After a difference equation has been made polynomial in its shifted dependent variables, its degree is the highest power of the term having the greatest shift. Radicals and fractional powers must first be removed when that can be done unambiguously.

Order and degree describe different features: order measures the span of shifts, while degree measures the algebraic power of the highest-shifted term. A [[Linear Difference Equation]] always has degree one.
