# Poincare Disk Model

The Poincare disk model represents the infinite hyperbolic plane inside a finite Euclidean unit disk. Its boundary is infinitely far away in hyperbolic distance, and its geodesics are disk diameters or circular arcs perpendicular to that boundary.

The model supports focus-plus-context visualization because regions near the center appear large while increasingly more space is compressed near the edge. Network nodes can be assigned radial and angular coordinates inside the disk.
