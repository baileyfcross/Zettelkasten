# Electric Charge from Current and Time

Electric charge has the unit relationship coulomb equals ampere-second. The book converts ampere-hours to coulombs by keeping current in amperes and changing hours to 3,600 seconds; one A·h therefore gives 3,600 C. The chain makes the time contribution explicit instead of treating ampere-hour as a standalone name with an unexplained numerical factor. Its SI derived-unit table expresses C as s·A. See [[Algebraic SI Derived Units]] and [[Energy Unit Conversion from Power and Time]] for another quantity built from a rate and time.
