# Directed Prompt

A directed prompt asks a focused question whose likely answers belong to a known set or semantic type. It communicates what information is needed and constrains the next recognition and understanding task.

Directed prompts can improve recovery by offering alternatives or examples. Overuse, however, turns a flexible assistant into a rigid [[Directed Dialog]] that prevents users from volunteering useful information.
