# Tissue Playtester

A tissue playtester is a participant whose unrepeatable first encounter is reserved for evaluating material that depends on naïveté. Once someone has seen the tutorial, difficulty progression, plot twist, ending, or emotional arc, prior knowledge permanently changes later sessions.

The tester is “one use” for that particular first-impression question, not disposable as a person. Designers should decide when each participant's fresh perspective will be most valuable while continuing to recruit other newcomers for earlier iterations. Saving every trusted tester until late would deprive the design of essential early evidence.
