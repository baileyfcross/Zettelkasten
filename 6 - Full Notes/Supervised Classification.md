# Supervised Classification

Supervised classification learns from records whose class labels are known. The training data provide examples of the relationship between attributes and the target category.

A complete workflow constructs the model, evaluates it on separate or withheld cases, and applies it to unseen records. The evaluation estimates whether the learned relationship generalizes.
