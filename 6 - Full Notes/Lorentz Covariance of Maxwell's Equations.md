# Lorentz Covariance of Maxwell's Equations

Lorentz covariance of Maxwell's equations means that their mathematical form is preserved when electromagnetic quantities and coordinates are transformed between inertial frames. This contrasts with Galilean transformations, under which the equations do not retain the same form.

Covariance becomes transparent when the sources are written as the [[Four-Current]] and the fields as the [[Electromagnetic Field Tensor]] and its dual. Tensor equations that hold in one inertial coordinate system then hold in every Lorentz-related system, even though individual electric, magnetic, charge-density, and current components change.

The property explains why every inertial observer obtains the same electromagnetic wave speed from [[Maxwell's Equations]]. It also requires electric and magnetic fields to mix under a boost, supporting [[Electric and Magnetic Field Unification]].
