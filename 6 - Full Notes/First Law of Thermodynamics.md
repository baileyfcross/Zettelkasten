# First Law of Thermodynamics

The first law of thermodynamics applies energy conservation to a thermodynamic system. With the convention that $W_{on}$ is work done on the system,

$$\Delta U=Q+W_{on}.$$

Heating and mechanical work are transfer processes, while internal energy is a state property. A gas that expands and does external work may gain less internal energy than the heat supplied.
