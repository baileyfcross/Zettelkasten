# AI Black Box

An AI black box is a system whose internal path from input to output is difficult for people to understand. [[Deep Neural Network]] models are a central example because behavior is distributed across many learned parameters rather than expressed as a short set of readable rules.

Opacity can exist at several levels. A developer may not know why a particular prediction occurred, an operator may not understand the model's limits, and an affected person may not even know that automation was used. The ethical problem is strongest when an opaque output determines rights, opportunities, safety, or access to essential services.
