# Generalized Eigenspace

For an eigenvalue $\lambda$, a generalized eigenspace is the null space of a sufficiently high power $(T-\lambda I)^r$. It contains ordinary eigenvectors and the additional vectors needed to form Jordan chains.

When the characteristic polynomial splits, the space decomposes into the direct sum of its generalized eigenspaces. On each one, the operator acts as $\lambda I$ plus a nilpotent part represented by [[Jordan Block|Jordan blocks]].
