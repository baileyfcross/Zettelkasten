# P-Value

A p-value is the probability, under a specified [[Null Hypothesis]], of obtaining a test statistic at least as extreme as the observed one. It measures incompatibility with the null model rather than the probability that the null hypothesis is true.

The value is itself a [[Random Variable]] across repeated studies. It should be reported with an [[Effect Size]] and uncertainty because statistical significance alone does not establish scientific importance.
