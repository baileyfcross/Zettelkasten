# Receiver Clock Bias

Receiver clock bias is the offset between a navigation receiver's local clock and the satellite-system time scale. The offset adds nearly the same apparent range error to all simultaneously observed satellites, because electromagnetic signals travel about 300 meters in one microsecond. Rather than equipping every receiver with an atomic clock, GPS includes clock bias in the navigation equations and estimates it from an additional satellite measurement.
