# Airworthiness Documents and Defect Logs

Airworthiness depends on current certificates, registration, operating information, weight-and-balance records, journey logs, insurance, crew credentials, and required procedures. Defects recorded as snags must be evaluated and addressed by authorized maintenance personnel before flight unless a valid provision permits operation. New approved documents supersede older versions rather than silently coexisting with them.
