# Avatar

An avatar is the fictional character, vehicle, or other entity through which a player is represented in the game world. The player may identify with it as an imaginary self even when it is not human.

Avatar design affects both mechanics and interpretation. Its available actions define what the player can do, while appearance, voice, history, and behavior shape the kind of identification the game invites.
