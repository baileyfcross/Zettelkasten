# Anticipating Software Breakdowns

Anticipating software breakdowns means imagining failures and harmful consequences before they occur. Designers examine user mistakes, attacks, organizational disruption, lost data, and changes in the surrounding environment.

Useful responses include undo, continuous backup, safe defaults, graceful degradation, and post-delivery repair. The practice treats recovery and care as parts of the product rather than exceptions left to users.
