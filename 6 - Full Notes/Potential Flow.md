# Potential Flow

Potential flow is an irrotational flow of an ideal incompressible fluid. It can be formulated with a velocity potential or a stream function, chosen partly according to how easily boundary conditions can be imposed.

Finite element potential flow solves nodal scalar values and differentiates the field to recover velocity and volumetric rates. Flow around a cylinder or airfoil provides representative applications.
