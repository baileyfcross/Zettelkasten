# Cyber Weaponization and Delivery

Cyber weaponization prepares a malicious capability for a chosen target, while delivery brings it to the target through a message, website, removable medium, network service, or another channel. Together they connect reconnaissance to an attempted compromise.

In the [[Cyber Kill Chain]], filtering, safe content handling, user verification, and endpoint controls can interrupt delivery before the payload reaches [[Cyber Exploitation and Installation]].
