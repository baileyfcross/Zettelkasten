# Turboprop Conversion of the Wilga

The Wilga DRACO conversion replaced a 260-horsepower piston engine with a much more powerful, lighter PT6 turboprop installation. The change supported high climb performance and short takeoff and landing operation, but it also required new propeller, fuel, cooling, structure, control, electrical, and cockpit solutions. The project shows that an engine swap is an aircraft-system redesign rather than a single-component substitution.
