# Proportion Power Calculation

A proportion power calculation plans a comparison involving one or two proportions. Its effect measure reflects the expected difference in prevalence, and its sample-size arguments may assume equal groups or permit one group size to be fixed.

The unequal-group form is useful when the number of outcome-positive observations is constrained by the available data. A small prevalence difference or severe imbalance can require a much larger comparison group to maintain the desired power.
