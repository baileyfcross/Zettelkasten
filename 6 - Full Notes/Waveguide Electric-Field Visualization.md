# Waveguide Electric-Field Visualization

Waveguide electric-field visualization maps the solved field magnitude through selected planes of a three-dimensional guided-wave model. It makes the spatial distribution inside the main guide and coupled cavities visible rather than reducing the solution to port-level response numbers.

The book replaces a default three-plane multislice display with nine slice planes so that the field distribution through all three tuned stubs can be inspected. The color scale reports electric-field magnitude while the geometry locates regions of concentration and weak response.

This view complements [[Scattering Parameters]] and [[Voltage Standing Wave Ratio]]. Port curves quantify transmission and reflection across frequency; field slices help show where the corresponding electromagnetic energy pattern resides within the component.
