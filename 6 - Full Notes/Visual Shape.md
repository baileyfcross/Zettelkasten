# Visual Shape

Visual shape is the representational meaning created by arranging ordinary game objects into a recognizable larger form. A collection of walls, gates, and water can read as a castle and moat, giving the player context for the obstacle and its goal.

This differs from [[Scene Shape]], which describes the structure of choices over time. The two can reinforce each other when the image formed by a scene suggests how the player should interpret and navigate its possibilities.
