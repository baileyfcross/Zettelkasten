# Real Part of a Complex Number

For a complex number $z=a+bi$, the real part is $\operatorname{Re}(z)=a$.

Geometrically it is the horizontal coordinate of the point representing $z$ in the complex plane. Together with the [[Imaginary Part of a Complex Number]], it uniquely determines the number.
