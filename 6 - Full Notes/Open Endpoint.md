# Open Endpoint

An open endpoint is a boundary value excluded from an [[Interval]]. It corresponds to a strict inequality such as (x<2) or (x>-1).

On a [[Number Line]], exclusion is shown with an unfilled circle at the boundary. In interval notation, a parenthesis is used. Values may approach the endpoint arbitrarily closely while the endpoint itself remains outside the set. This differs from a [[Closed Endpoint]], which is included through an equality option.
