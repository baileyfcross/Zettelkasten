# Recommendation Choice Architecture

Recommendation choice architecture is the design of how suggested options are arranged, framed, explained, and made actionable. Ranking order, imagery, defaults, social proof, and the number of alternatives can influence a decision even when the underlying candidate items do not change.

This makes the recommendation interface part of the intervention rather than a neutral display. A defensible architecture preserves [[Individual Autonomy]] through understandable options, transparent intent, and an easy ability to decline. Personalized nudges may help users act on their interests, but hidden decoys or friction that serves only the platform turn guidance into manipulation.
