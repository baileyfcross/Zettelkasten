# Energy Benefit-Cost Ratio

A benefit-cost ratio scales an energy project's discounted net savings to its capital investment. The source presents both a savings-to-investment measure and a net-benefit divided by investment measure. Ratios can help compare the return per invested unit, but they do not replace the absolute value of savings: a small project can have a high ratio yet contribute less total benefit than a larger one.
