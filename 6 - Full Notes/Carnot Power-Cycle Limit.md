# Carnot Power-Cycle Limit

For an ideal reversible heat engine operating between hot and cold reservoirs, the maximum cycle efficiency is $1-T_{cold}/T_{hot}$ with absolute temperatures. Real [[Steam Power Plant]] and turbine cycles perform below it because entropy is generated and components lose energy. Raising the hot temperature may help, but boiler tubes and turbine materials limit it, while cooling-water temperature constrains the cold side.
