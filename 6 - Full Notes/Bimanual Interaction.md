# Bimanual Interaction

Bimanual interaction coordinates both hands to perform a task. It may use two devices, two tracked hands, or different controls assigned to each hand.

Symmetric interaction gives both hands similar roles; asymmetric interaction gives them different but coordinated roles. Timing can also be synchronous or asynchronous. For example, one hand may establish a frame or region while the other performs a more precise action inside it.

Bimanual design can support integrated [[3D Manipulation]] and [[Progressive Refinement]], but the mapping should respect hand roles, device ergonomics, and the flow between actions rather than merely duplicating controls.
