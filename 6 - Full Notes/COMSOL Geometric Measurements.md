# COMSOL Geometric Measurements

COMSOL geometric measurements report distances, lengths, areas, volumes, and cumulative sizes for selected entities in the active unit system. They can compare a constructed or imported object with its known design values and quantify how later operations changed it.

Measurement is both a construction aid and a verification tool. Copying reported values directly into calculations or tables reduces manual transcription, but the selected entity set and whether it refers to source objects or finalized geometry must remain explicit.
