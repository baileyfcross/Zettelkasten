# Undefined Rational Expression

A rational expression is undefined at any value that makes its denominator zero. Before simplifying

$$\frac{x^2-1}{x-1},$$

the value (x=1) must be excluded. Factoring and cancelling gives (x+1), but the original expression still has no value at (x=1).

Restrictions should therefore be identified before cancellation, multiplication, or cross multiplication. A transformation can preserve equality only on the domain where every expression and division used in the step is defined.
