# Initial Cluster Partition

An initial cluster partition is the starting allocation of observations or seed representatives used by an iterative method. It can be chosen randomly, constructed from ordered data, or refined through a preliminary algorithm.

Different initial partitions can lead to different local optima. Initialization is therefore part of the model specification and should be varied or justified rather than treated as an invisible implementation detail.
