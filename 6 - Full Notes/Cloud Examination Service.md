# Cloud Examination Service

A cloud examination service delivers identity checking, authorization, question selection, supervision, scoring, and result handling as an integrated process. A large question bank can vary test instances while keeping the assessment rules consistent.

Remote delivery requires credible evidence that the authorized person completed the examination under the intended conditions. The process should integrate with training rather than treating assessment as an unrelated final event.
