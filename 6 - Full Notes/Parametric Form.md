# Parametric Form

A parametric form specifies how one probability factor in a Bayesian decomposition can be computed from a known family and a set of parameters. Tables, deterministic relationships, and named probability families are examples of forms used by the book.

A factor can also take the form of a question to another Bayesian program. The selected form determines which parameters must be supplied or identified and which inference operations remain feasible.
