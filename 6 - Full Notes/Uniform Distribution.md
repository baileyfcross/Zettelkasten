# Uniform Distribution

A continuous uniform distribution assigns constant density across a bounded interval, making equal-length subintervals equally probable. Its lower and upper bounds determine both the range and the scale of generated values.

R uniform draws can represent measurements under this model or act as the raw random input for other simulations. Comparing a draw with a threshold creates a [[Bernoulli Trial]] whose success probability equals the corresponding fraction of the interval.
