# Half-Angle Identities

Rearranging the cosine [[Double-Angle Identities]] yields

$$\sin^2\frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{2},\qquad
\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{1+\cos\theta}{2}.$$

Taking square roots requires a sign chosen from the quadrant of the half-angle. The identities reduce functions of half an angle to functions of the original angle.
