# Fitts's Law

Fitts's law models the time required to make an aimed movement to a target:

$$
MT = a + b\log_2(2D/W)
$$

$MT$ is movement time, $D$ is distance to the target, $W$ is target width, and $a$ and $b$ are fitted constants. Movement takes longer as distance increases or the target becomes narrower.

The logarithmic term is the index of difficulty. Dividing that index by movement time gives an index of performance in bits per second, which can compare [[Input Device]]s. The law captures an important speed-accuracy relationship in [[Motor Control]] and [[Object Selection]].
