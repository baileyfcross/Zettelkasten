# Categorical Attribute Similarity

Categorical attribute similarity measures how strongly two records resemble one another through equal values or shared co-occurrence patterns. A simple matching ratio can treat all attributes alike, while a cardinality-aware measure gives rarer agreements more influence.

The chosen definition determines neighbor relations and every later cluster. It should not impose a numeric order that the categories do not possess.
