# Apollo Guidance Computer IC Demand

The Apollo guidance computer used integrated circuits to meet stringent spacecraft limits on size, weight, power, and reliability. NASA purchases followed military demand and consumed a large share of early chip production.

The program provided both a market and a demanding validation environment. Its success demonstrated that integrated electronics could control safety-critical missions beyond Earth.
