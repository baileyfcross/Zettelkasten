# Medical Image Visualization

Medical image visualization presents two- or three-dimensional biomedical data for interpretation. Cross-sectional images can be shown in axial, sagittal, and coronal views, while volume rendering and surface extraction reveal spatial structure across slices.

The display is a transformation of measured data. Windowing, color mapping, interpolation, viewpoint, and segmentation can emphasize or hide features. Clinical use therefore requires consistent controls and a way to return from a derived 3D view to the underlying slices when a finding must be verified.
