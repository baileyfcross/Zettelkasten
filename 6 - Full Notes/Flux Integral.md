# Flux Integral

A flux integral measures how much of a vector field passes through an oriented surface. Its integrand is the dot product of the field with the differential area vector, so only the component normal to the surface contributes.

The sign depends on the chosen orientation: outward flow is positive for a closed surface under the usual convention. Flux connects local fields to total transport and is the surface quantity related to divergence by the divergence theorem.
