# Fuel Cell Reversible Voltage

Reversible voltage is the ideal electromotive force if a [[Fuel Cell]] converts the reaction's available work without internal losses. The source computes it as $E=-\Delta G/(nF)$, where $\Delta G$ is [[Gibbs Free Energy]] change per mole, $n$ is transferred electrons per mole, and $F$ is Faraday's constant. For its room-temperature hydrogen-oxygen example, the ideal voltage is about 1.23 V; operating voltage falls below it.
