# Evacuated-Tube Solar Collector

An evacuated-tube collector surrounds its absorbing element with an evacuated enclosure to reduce heat loss. The source places it between flat-plate and strongly concentrating designs in achievable fluid temperature and lists water heating, space heating, cooling, and process heat among its tasks. Vacuum insulation does not increase incident sunlight; it preserves more of the captured heat as the absorber becomes hotter.
