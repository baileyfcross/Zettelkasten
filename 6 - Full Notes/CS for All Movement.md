# CS for All Movement

CS for All frames computer science as part of general education rather than a specialty reserved for future programmers. It aims to broaden access to computational concepts, creative practice, and participation in a society shaped by digital systems.

Achieving the aim requires qualified teachers, inclusive curricula, institutional support, and more than device access. It also requires honest expectations about what introductory study can and cannot provide.
