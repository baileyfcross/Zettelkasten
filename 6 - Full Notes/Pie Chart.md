# Pie Chart

A pie chart represents category proportions as angles or areas of a circle. It is frequently requested for categorical summaries, but people compare angles and areas less accurately than positions along a common scale.

Small categories also produce crowded labels and nearly invisible slices. A [[Bar Chart]] is usually easier to read, while a legend may be required when the pie must be used despite these limitations.
