# Basic and Extended Regular Expressions

Basic and extended regular expressions are two Unix pattern dialects that share literals, anchors, character classes, and repetition but assign different syntax to grouping, alternation, and related operators. A tool option or invocation name selects which dialect interprets the pattern.

A pattern copied between grep, sed, awk, and another engine may therefore require changed escaping or may support features outside either standard dialect. Portability begins by naming the engine and mode in which the expression will run.
