# Feature Standardization for Clustering

Feature standardization transforms numerical attributes to comparable scales before proximity is computed. Without it, a variable measured in large units can dominate Euclidean or squared-error methods regardless of its substantive importance.

Standardization improves scale comparability but does not make all attributes equally meaningful. Its transformation should match the intended interpretation.
