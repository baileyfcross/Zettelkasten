# Diagonalizable Matrix

A matrix is diagonalizable when it is similar to a [[Diagonal Matrix]]. Equivalently, it has enough linearly independent eigenvectors to form a basis.

If the eigenvectors form the columns of $Q$, then $Q^{-1}AQ=D$, where the diagonal of $D$ contains the corresponding eigenvalues. Repeated eigenvalues do not guarantee diagonalizability.
