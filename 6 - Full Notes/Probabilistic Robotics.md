# Probabilistic Robotics

Probabilistic robotics represents uncertain states, sensors, movements, and actions with probability distributions. Rather than committing prematurely to one world state, a robot updates competing hypotheses as evidence and controls arrive.

Localization, sensor fusion, behavior recognition, and decision models share this foundation. Their descriptions differ, but each makes incompleteness explicit and asks an inference question suited to the task.
