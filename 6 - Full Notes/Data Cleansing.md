# Data Cleansing

Data cleansing analyzes fields, corrects or removes erroneous values, fills missing information where justified, and enforces domain and business rules. It is the activity used to improve data quality before and during analysis.

Cleaning is continuous because codes, systems, and real-world conditions change. Every correction should preserve provenance and distinguish error repair from deletion of a valid rare case.
