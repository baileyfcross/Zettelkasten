# Heterogeneous Transfer Learning

Heterogeneous transfer learning moves predictive knowledge between domains with different feature spaces, label spaces, or both. Directly reusing observations or coefficients is therefore impossible without a learned correspondence.

Shared latent representations, cross-domain relations, or paired examples can connect the spaces. The mapping becomes part of the learning problem and must preserve information relevant to the target rather than only reconstructing source structure.
