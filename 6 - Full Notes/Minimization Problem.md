# Minimization Problem

A minimization problem asks for the permitted solution that gives an objective function its smallest value. The function converts the quality of a candidate into a quantity that can be compared with other candidates.

Neural-network training treats prediction error as a [[Loss Function]] and changes weights and biases to reduce it. The network does not need to know the minimum in advance; local derivative information indicates changes that make the current loss smaller.

A minimization may be unconstrained or limited to [[Feasible Solution|feasible solutions]] by equations and inequalities. A [[Local Minimum]] is best only in a neighborhood, while the global solution is best over the entire feasible set; convexity can make those notions coincide.
