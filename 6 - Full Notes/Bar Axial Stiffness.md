# Bar Axial Stiffness

Bar axial stiffness is the force required per unit axial extension. For a uniform linear elastic member, it is proportional to Young's modulus and cross-sectional area and inversely proportional to length.

This scalar rigidity factor multiplies the two-node bar's signed coefficient matrix. Changes in material, area, or element length therefore rescale the local relation between nodal forces and displacements.
