# Accelerator Energy Efficiency Ratio

An accelerator energy efficiency ratio compares the work completed per unit energy, or the energy consumed for the same workload, across platforms. It combines runtime with power rather than treating low instantaneous power as sufficient.

A short high-power GPU run can use less total energy than a much slower low-power accelerator. Fair measurement therefore includes data movement, active duration, and identical algorithmic work.
