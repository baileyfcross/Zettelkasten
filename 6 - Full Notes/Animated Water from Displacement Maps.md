# Animated Water from Displacement Maps

Still water in an image can be made to move by displacing a masked copy of the plate with an animated grayscale map. The book constructs wave-like noise with blur, directional blur, contrast adjustment, and scaling to match the pictured ripple pattern. The map drifts slowly in the apparent wave direction; its contrast or the displacement control governs strength. A roto limits the displaced copy to water, while the original still plate remains intact underneath. Large movement breaks the illusion, so subtlety is part of the method.
