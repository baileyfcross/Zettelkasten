# Long Tail Distribution

A long tail distribution has a small number of extremely frequent or popular items and a large number that each receive little attention. Social-network content and user activity often exhibit this imbalance.

The tail matters because collectively it contains many items even though no single one dominates. Prediction and evaluation must avoid being governed entirely by the few head items.
