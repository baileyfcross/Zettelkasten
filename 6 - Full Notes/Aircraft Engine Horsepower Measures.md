# Aircraft Engine Horsepower Measures

Indicated horsepower describes idealized power developed by cylinder pressure before mechanical friction losses. Brake horsepower is measured at a piston engine's crankshaft, while shaft horsepower describes power delivered through an output shaft to a propeller or turbine load. Distinguishing these measures prevents gross, transmitted, and useful power from being treated as the same quantity.
