# Tactile Situation Awareness System

The Tactile Situation Awareness System is a torso-worn array that translates vehicle motion and orientation into spatial vibration cues. A helicopter prototype used a vest of pneumatic actuators connected to aircraft sensors so pilots could perceive velocity direction and magnitude through the skin.

Flight tests showed that tactile cues could support hovering and reduce workload even amid vehicle vibration and restricted visual information. The system illustrates how a [[Tactile Navigation Display]] can offload vision and audition when its body-centered mapping corresponds directly to the external direction that must be controlled.
