# Spectral Radius

The spectral radius of a square matrix is the largest absolute value among its eigenvalues: $\rho(A)=\max_i|\lambda_i|$.

For an [[Iteration Matrix]] $T$, the linear iteration converges from any initial vector exactly when $\rho(T)<1$. The quantity also connects matrix powers with the long-run growth or decay of repeated transformations.
