# Smart Utility Grid

A smart utility grid uses connected meters, sensors, controls, and analytics to observe and coordinate energy production, distribution, and consumption. Real-time measurements make demand patterns visible and allow rates, incentives, or equipment behavior to respond to changing load.

At the building level, connected thermostats can optimize climate control from occupancy and outside temperature. Across the grid, aggregation helps utilities balance resources and users shift consumption toward off-peak periods. The same connectivity makes resilience and cybersecurity critical because disruption or manipulation can affect many customers and physical systems simultaneously.
