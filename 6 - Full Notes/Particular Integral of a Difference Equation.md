# Particular Integral of a Difference Equation

A particular integral is any one solution of a [[Nonhomogeneous Difference Equation]]. In operator form it is written symbolically as $F(E)^{-1}g(n)$ and evaluated according to the forcing sequence.

Polynomial, exponential, and trigonometric forcing each have convenient shift-operator rules. If the forcing duplicates a complementary term, the trial expression must be multiplied by a sufficient power of $n$ to remove the resonance.
