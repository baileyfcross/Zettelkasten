# RUR Robot Labor Metaphor

Karel Capek's 1920 play R.U.R. introduced the word robot through artificial workers whose name derives from terms for forced labor and servitude. The original robots were biological artificial humans rather than metal machines, and the story used them to criticize dehumanized work, conformity, and industrial modernity.

The labor metaphor became embedded in robotics before the engineering field existed. Later images of robots as obedient servants, exploited workers, or rebelling creations all inherit part of this framing. That history connects the cultural meaning of robots to [[Robotics Labor and Political Economy]] rather than treating automation as a purely technical idea.
