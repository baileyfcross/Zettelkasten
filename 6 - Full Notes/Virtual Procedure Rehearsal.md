# Virtual Procedure Rehearsal

Virtual procedure rehearsal lets a learner or practitioner repeat a consequential task in simulation before performing it on a person or physical system. It is especially valuable when real practice is dangerous, costly, rare, or ethically constrained because failure can become part of learning without producing the normal consequence.

A medical rehearsal can combine stereoscopic anatomy, instrument tracking, [[Bimanual Interaction]], tissue simulation, and a [[Haptic Display]] so the trainee practices both decisions and coordinated movements. An instructor can introduce complications, observe performance, and require repetition across a structured sequence of tasks.

Transfer depends on matching the important perceptual and motor demands of the real procedure. A headset or controller that is absent from actual practice may teach compensations specific to the simulator, so display and interaction choices should follow the skill being trained.
