# Laplacian Eigengap Robustness

The stability of a Laplacian eigenvector under perturbation depends on both the size of the perturbation and the separation of its eigenvalue from neighboring eigenvalues. A larger eigengap limits how far the eigenvector can rotate when noise changes the graph operator.

Leading directions of a graph with well-separated clusters often have larger useful gaps than densely packed tail directions. Spectral selectors can therefore improve robustness by emphasizing leading nontrivial eigenpairs and discarding unstable tail components rather than treating every spectral direction equally.
