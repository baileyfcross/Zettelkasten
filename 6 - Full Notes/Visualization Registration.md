# Visualization Registration

Visualization registration determines the physical frame to which displayed information is attached. A locally registered visualization follows a nearby object or surface, while a globally registered one is positioned within a larger world coordinate system.

The choice communicates meaning: a label attached to a machine part expresses a different relationship from an overview fixed to the room. Tracking and calibration error must be judged relative to the scale and task of that relationship.
