# Result-to-Computation Traceability

Result-to-computation traceability is the recoverable link between a reported figure, table, or number and the exact computation that produced it. The link includes the relevant code version, inputs, parameters, dependencies, and execution record rather than only the final artifact.

Automation can preserve this relationship by generating reports from authoritative sources, attaching source identifiers to outputs, or binding figures to workflow versions. Without it, manually copied results can diverge from the current analysis. Traceability makes an [[Executable Research Publication]] inspectable even before a reader attempts a full rerun.
