# Training Performance Bias

Training performance bias is the optimistic error estimate produced by evaluating a model on the observations used to fit it. Flexible learners can adapt to sample-specific noise and appear nearly perfect without generalizing.

A [[Validation Dataset]] or [[Test Dataset]] is required to estimate out-of-sample behavior.
