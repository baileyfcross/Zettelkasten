# Immersive Engineering Design Review

Immersive engineering design review presents a product, machine, facility, or process as an interactive three-dimensional model that multidisciplinary teams can inspect together. The shared visualization gives specialists a common spatial language for discussing clearances, assembly sequences, human access, and relationships that are easy to miss on a two-dimensional display.

Because engineering geometry often originates in computer-aided design and manufacturing systems, the review can occur before expensive physical fabrication. Participants can compare alternatives, identify conflicts, rehearse procedures, and use [[3D Manipulation]] to inspect difficult viewpoints. This can shorten decisions and reduce errors when the source geometry and simulation assumptions are trustworthy.

The method complements rather than replaces analysis. A visually plausible model does not establish structural, thermal, manufacturing, or safety performance unless those properties are represented and evaluated separately.
