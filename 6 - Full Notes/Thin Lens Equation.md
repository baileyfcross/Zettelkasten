# Thin Lens Equation

The thin lens equation relates object distance $u$, image distance $v$, and focal length $f$ through a consistent sign convention:

$$\frac{1}{f}=\frac{1}{u}+\frac{1}{v}.$$

Ray diagrams determine whether the image is real or virtual and its orientation, while magnification follows from the ratio of image to object dimensions.
