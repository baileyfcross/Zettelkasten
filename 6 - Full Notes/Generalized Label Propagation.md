# Generalized Label Propagation

Generalized label propagation spreads label or membership information across a graph under smoothness and constraint objectives. Labeled vertices anchor the process while nearby unlabeled vertices acquire compatible scores.

Extensions can handle multiple classes, soft labels, negative relations, and heterogeneous graphs. If the graph reflects nuisance similarity, propagation confidently amplifies the wrong relation, making graph construction and uncertainty reporting essential.
