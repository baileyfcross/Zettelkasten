# Lead Time

Lead time is the elapsed time from identifying a desired feature until it is deployed. Unlike [[Cycle Time]], it includes time the item spends waiting in a backlog before active work begins.

For a live game, lead time measures responsiveness to players and the market. Reducing queues, batch size, low-value backlog inventory, and deployment delay can matter as much as faster development.
