# Bottleneck

A bottleneck is the constraint that most limits a system's throughput. Work accumulates before it while downstream capacity may be starved, making it visible through queues and rising cycle time.

Improvement focuses on one dominant constraint at a time: protect it from avoidable interruption, help it complete valuable work, and prevent excess upstream production. Once relieved, another constraint becomes limiting.
