# High-Dimensional Statistical Regime

A high-dimensional statistical regime is one in which the number of variables or parameters $p$ is comparable to, or exceeds, the number of observations $n$. Classical asymptotics that hold $p$ fixed while sending $n$ to infinity can then give misleading guidance.

The regime changes what is identifiable, which matrices are invertible, and how errors scale. Analysis must state its dependence on both $n$ and $p$, along with the assumed [[Low-Dimensional Statistical Structure]]. A method may succeed for sparse signals with $n$ proportional to sparsity times $\log p$ while remaining hopeless without that restriction.
