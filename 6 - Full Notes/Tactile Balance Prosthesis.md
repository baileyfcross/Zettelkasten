# Tactile Balance Prosthesis

A tactile balance prosthesis converts measured head or body tilt into stimulation on the skin for a person with vestibular impairment. Accelerometers and gyroscopes estimate orientation and angular velocity, and a worn motor array signals when sway exceeds a threshold associated with falling.

The activated body location indicates the direction of excessive tilt, giving the user an intuitive corrective cue. The device does not restore the damaged vestibular receptors; it supplies substitute feedback that can reduce sway when the wearer learns to integrate the tactile signal with vision and remaining proprioception.
