# Belief Exchange Model

A belief exchange model represents misinformation diffusion as repeated changes in users' degrees of belief. Two regular users move toward a shared value, while an interaction with a [[Forceful Individual]] changes the regular user much more than the forceful one.

Iterated exchanges can converge toward group consensus. The final belief depends on initial values, influence asymmetry, connectivity, and whether communities are bridged or isolated.
