# Microsoft Responsible AI Principles

Microsoft’s responsible AI framework organizes system design around fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and security, inclusiveness, transparency, and accountability. These principles are not a final checklist applied after a model is built; they influence data selection, evaluation, user experience, access control, documentation, and operational ownership throughout the lifecycle.

The principles become actionable only when translated into evidence and controls. Teams need segmented fairness tests, safety thresholds, privacy boundaries, explanations of limitations, accessible interaction paths, and named decision owners. Microsoft Foundry supplies evaluation and guardrail mechanisms, but the organization remains responsible for defining acceptable behavior and responding when the system falls short.
