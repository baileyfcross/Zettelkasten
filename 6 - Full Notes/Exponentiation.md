# Exponentiation

Exponentiation is the operation that raises a base to a power. For a natural number (n\geq1),

$$a^n=\underbrace{a\cdot a\cdots a}_{n\text{ factors}}.$$

Here (a) is the [[Base of a Power]] and (n) is the [[Exponent]]. Exponentiation abbreviates repeated multiplication, just as multiplication abbreviates repeated addition for natural numbers. It is evaluated before multiplication and addition unless [[Grouping Symbols]] specify another structure. Its inverse operation is expressed through a [[Root]].
