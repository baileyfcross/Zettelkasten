# Agile Manifesto

The Agile Manifesto expresses four preferences: individuals and interactions over processes and tools, working software over comprehensive documentation, customer collaboration over contract negotiation, and responding to change over following a plan.

The items on the right still have value. The preference means that when they conflict, teams protect collaboration, working results, and adaptation because these create faster and more reliable learning.

The book connects these values to frequent delivery, daily cooperation between business and development, motivated self-organizing teams, technical excellence, simplicity, and regular reflection. Those principles make the value choices observable in work rather than leaving them as slogans.
