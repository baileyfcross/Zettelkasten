# Particle-Density Discrepancy between XEB and Fidelity

In the diffusion-reaction picture, ideal random circuits approach a homogeneous particle density of $3/4$. Weak homogeneous noise mainly reduces the total weight while leaving this normalized shape close to equilibrium, so the different XEB and fidelity boundary vectors give nearly the same result.

Strong noise lowers the particle density before it can re-equilibrate, and localized omitted gates create spatial depressions around sink positions. Because XEB gives the vacuum sector more relative weight than fidelity does, these depleted distributions make XEB systematically larger. The discrepancy is therefore controlled by both total loss and the normalized particle profile, not by an error count alone.
