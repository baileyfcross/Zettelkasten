# Player-Based Balancing

Player-based balancing uses observed play and player reports to revise a game's balance. It closes the [[Designer Loop]] by comparing the intended experience with what players actually understand, choose, and feel.

Players are strong evidence about the problems they experience but are not necessarily reliable designers of solutions. The designer interprets their feedback alongside behavior and the game's systemic goals.
