# Dead-End Reward

A dead-end reward acknowledges or entices the player without feeding back into the main game system. A noninteractive story segment or platform-level [[Achievement]] may be rewarding while leaving the player's available verbs, resources, and choices unchanged.

These rewards can still provide rest, meaning, or motivation, but they risk eclipsing the pleasure of play when the player treats gameplay only as a barrier to the next payoff.
