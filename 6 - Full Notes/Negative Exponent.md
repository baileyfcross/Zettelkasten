# Negative Exponent

A negative exponent denotes the [[Reciprocal]] of the corresponding positive power. For nonzero (a),

$$a^{-n}=\frac{1}{a^n}.$$

This interpretation follows from (1=a^0=a^{n-n}=a^na^{-n}). It also means that taking a reciprocal reverses the sign of an exponent: (1/a^{-n}=a^n). Negative exponents do not make the value itself negative; for example, (2^{-3}=1/8). They describe position between numerator and denominator rather than arithmetic sign.
