# Cultural Technology

Cultural technology covers technology crossing the boundary between a game and society. Game techniques can be reused for non-game purposes, third-party hardware can alter the play experience, and community-built tools or mods can expand what the original artifact permits.

The category highlights that a platform is not culturally neutral. Different controllers, access to modification tools, and new technical uses can redistribute advantage or authorship. As communities appropriate technology, they can change both the game's practical possibility space and the wider meaning of game technology.
