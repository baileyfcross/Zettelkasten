# Spatial Line Data

Spatial line data represent extended features through ordered coordinate sequences. Roads, rivers, routes, and administrative boundaries can be stored as lines even though their physical width may be ignored at the chosen scale. Lines often supply context in a [[Map Layer Overlay]], but they can also carry attributes such as category or flow. Their geometry and other layers must use compatible coordinate reference systems.
