# Cinematic Bullet-Hit Compositing

A screen bullet hit follows the visual convention and tone of the production, which may differ from a literal entry wound. The book times a desaturated, darkened burst to the performer's reaction and places a second elongated burst behind the body for an exit effect, using roto to prevent the rear element from crossing in front. A still spray image on a perspective-aligned card can be revealed with a moving soft matte. The composite is built from timed layers rather than one footage overlay.
