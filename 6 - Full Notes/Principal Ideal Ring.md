# Principal Ideal Ring

A principal ideal ring is a nonzero commutative ring in which every ideal is a [[Principal Ideal]]. Unlike a [[Principal Ideal Domain]], it may contain zero divisors.

The distinction matters when transferring divisibility arguments: domain assumptions permit cancellation and unique-factor behavior that are not available in every principal ideal ring.
