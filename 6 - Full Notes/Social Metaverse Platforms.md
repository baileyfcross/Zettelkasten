# Social Metaverse Platforms

A social metaverse platform places participants together in a navigable virtual environment rather than presenting only a page or feed. Avatars, shared rooms, voice, and immersive media become part of the social interface, making the environment itself a context for conversation and activity.

The source treats services such as AltspaceVR, Bigscreen, and Couch as this category and connects the wider direction to Meta's corporate emphasis on the metaverse and Apple's Vision Pro. The model expands social integration beyond posts and profiles: identity, presence, spatial context, and device capabilities may all matter. It remains distinct from [[Social Messaging Services]], even when voice or group conversation is available in both.
