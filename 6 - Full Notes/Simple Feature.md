# Simple Feature

A simple feature is a standardized representation of a geographic geometry and its attributes. In R's `sf` model, an `sf` data frame holds ordinary attribute columns plus an `sfc` list-column of geometries, each of which is an `sfg` object. This tabular organization makes subsetting and attribute operations familiar while retaining geometry and a [[Coordinate Reference System]] for spatial transformation and plotting.
