# Thermionic Emission

Thermionic emission releases electrons from a heated solid surface once some electrons gain enough energy to cross its [[Surface Work Function]] barrier. The emitted electrons can move through a vacuum or conductive vapor gap to a cooler collector. This makes emission a direct heat-to-electron-current mechanism, although a working [[Thermionic Generator]] also needs an external return path and manageable space charge.
