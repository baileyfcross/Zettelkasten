# Ethical Dimension of a Game World

The ethical dimension defines the moral assumptions and consequences operating in a game world. It concerns which actions are permitted, condemned, rewarded, ignored, or treated as ordinary by characters and systems.

Ethical meaning is communicated through mechanics as well as dialogue. If the story condemns conduct that the reward system encourages, the world sends conflicting signals about what its values actually are.
