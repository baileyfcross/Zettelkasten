# UNIVAC

UNIVAC was a commercial general-purpose electronic computer developed by J. Presper Eckert and John Mauchly after ENIAC. Its name emphasized universality and automatic operation across scientific, government, and business applications.

Presper Eckert used conservative electrical loads to improve vacuum-tube life, giving the machine notable reliability. Although early sales were modest, UNIVAC demonstrated that electronic computing could become a commercial product.
