# Natural Feature Tracking

Natural feature tracking estimates pose from distinctive image structure already present in the environment instead of requiring artificial markers. Interest points are detected, described, and matched to a prepared model or to features observed in earlier frames.

The method allows less intrusive AR, but feature quality changes with viewpoint, scale, illumination, blur, and occlusion. Robust pose estimation must separate geometrically consistent matches from false correspondences.
