# Xerox Alto

The Xerox Alto was an experimental personal workstation integrating a bitmapped display, mouse, graphical interface, local networking, and rich text and image software. It was completed at Xerox PARC in the 1970s.

The system made a coherent interactive environment from innovations previously explored separately. Its ideas reached consumers through later Apple and Microsoft products.
