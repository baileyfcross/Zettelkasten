# Cost Optimization Pillar

The cost optimization pillar delivers required business outcomes at the lowest appropriate lifecycle cost. It emphasizes financial accountability, visibility, elimination of waste, demand-aware resource supply, and continuing evaluation of service and pricing choices.

Cost is balanced with the other Well-Architected qualities rather than minimized in isolation. Ownership, cost allocation, usage data, right-sizing, managed services, and informed commitments help teams make spending an architectural signal instead of a surprise after deployment.
