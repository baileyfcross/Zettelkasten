# Entropy

Entropy is a state quantity that measures energy dispersal and, statistically, the number of microscopic arrangements compatible with a macrostate. For reversible heat transfer, $dS=\delta Q_{rev}/T$; statistically, $S=k_B\ln\Omega$. The entropy of an isolated system tends toward a maximum as overwhelmingly probable macrostates are approached.

For occupation probabilities $P_i$, counting arrangements and applying the large-number approximation produces the information form $S=-Nk_B\sum_i P_i\ln P_i$. In canonical and grand-canonical descriptions, entropy can also be derived from the temperature dependence of the corresponding thermodynamic potential once the partition function is known.
