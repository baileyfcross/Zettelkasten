# Radiographic Image Formation

A radiographic image records spatial variation in transmitted X-ray intensity after the beam passes through the body. Differences in thickness, density, and attenuation coefficient create contrast. Lower photon energy can improve contrast but increases absorption and dose, while scatter degrades the image; filtration, collimation, and contrast media manage these tradeoffs.
