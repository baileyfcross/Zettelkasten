# Cluster Cohesion

Cluster cohesion measures how closely related the observations within one cluster are. A common point-level definition uses the average distance from an observation to the other members of its assigned group.

Low within-cluster distance indicates strong cohesion under the selected metric. It is combined with [[Cluster Separation]] in the silhouette measure.
