# Known Batch Covariate Adjustment

Known batch covariate adjustment represents recorded processing variables explicitly in a statistical model. A month, laboratory, instrument, or reagent-lot indicator can absorb its systematic contribution while the model estimates the biological contrast.

Recorded labels are often only surrogates for the true technical causes and may be too coarse. Residual sample correlation after adjustment suggests that additional known covariates or a [[Hidden Factor]] remain.
