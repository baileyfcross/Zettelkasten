# Intel 4004

Intel introduced the 4004 in 1971 as a microprogrammable computer on a chip. Designed initially for a calculator system, it placed the central processing functions of a small general-purpose computer into one integrated circuit.

Memory and input-output chips allowed customers to build complete systems around it. The 4004 and its successors established the microprocessor as a reusable foundation rather than a single fixed-purpose circuit.
