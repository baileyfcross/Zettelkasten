# Steel Building Drift Criteria

Drift is the lateral displacement of a level relative to another level, usually evaluated as story drift and total building movement. Strength alone does not control it: excessive drift can damage cladding and partitions, impair doors and services, create occupant discomfort, and magnify second-order effects even while steel members remain below their resistance limits.

Wind drift is commonly checked under service-level wind for functional and comfort criteria. Seismic story drift is calculated from code-level analysis and adjusted according to the lateral system's expected inelastic response. Limits depend on occupancy and nonstructural detailing. Because drift is a system property, changing member size, bracing arrangement, connection rigidity, diaphragm action, or frame location may be more effective than strengthening an isolated member.
