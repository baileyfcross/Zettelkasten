# 3D Axis Rotation Matrices

Three-dimensional rotation about a principal axis leaves the coordinate on that axis unchanged and performs a two-dimensional sine-cosine rotation in the perpendicular coordinate plane. Rotation about x acts in the yz plane, rotation about y acts in the xz plane, and rotation about z acts in the xy plane.

Using a right-handed coordinate system, a positive rotation is counterclockwise when viewed along the axis toward the origin. The three homogeneous 4 by 4 matrices provide the elementary factors used to build [[Rotation About an Arbitrary 3D Line]].
