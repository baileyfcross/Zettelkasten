# Equivalent Equations

Equivalent equations have the same solution set even though their expressions differ. Adding the same quantity to both sides or multiplying both sides by the same nonzero quantity produces an equivalent equation.

For example, (3x-5=28), (3x=33), and (x=11) are equivalent. Not every manipulation is reversible on the full domain. Multiplying by an expression that can be zero or cancelling a variable factor may add or remove cases. Recording restrictions and using [[Equation Verification]] protects the equivalence chain.
