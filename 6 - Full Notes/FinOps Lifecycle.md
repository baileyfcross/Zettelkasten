# FinOps Lifecycle

The FinOps lifecycle is a repeating sequence of informing stakeholders about allocation and usage, optimizing resources and purchasing decisions, and operating the resulting controls as part of everyday work. Later measurements reveal new opportunities, so the process loops rather than ending after one savings exercise.

The inform phase depends on accurate ownership and cost data. Optimize can include [[Cloud Resource Right-Sizing]], removing waste, and choosing commitments. Operate turns decisions into automation, budgets, and team practices. This cycle gives [[FinOps]] a feedback structure that links financial signals to accountable engineering action.

On an internal platform, the cycle is implemented through allocation metadata, consolidated billing and utilization evidence, process and pricing review, autoscaling policy, retention, and user-facing cost feedback. Each iteration should verify that a saving preserves the service and developer outcomes the platform exists to provide.
