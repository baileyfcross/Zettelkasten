# First-Order Separable Differential Equation

A separable first-order differential equation can be rearranged so that every factor involving the dependent variable lies with its differential and every factor involving the independent variable lies on the other side. Both sides can then be integrated independently.

The resulting relation may be explicit or implicit. Dividing during separation can discard constant solutions when the divided factor vanishes, so those possibilities must be checked against the original equation.
