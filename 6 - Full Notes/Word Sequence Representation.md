# Word Sequence Representation

A word sequence representation tokenizes text into an ordered list of word occurrences. It moves beyond raw characters while preserving the order needed by language models, parsers, and other methods that infer relationships between neighboring or hierarchically combined words.

Tokenization and vocabulary decisions affect the result. Normalization and stop-word handling may reduce variation, but deleting or merging tokens can also remove grammatical or domain-specific information needed by a downstream task.
