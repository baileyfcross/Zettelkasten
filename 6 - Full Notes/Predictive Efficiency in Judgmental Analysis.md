# Predictive Efficiency in Judgmental Analysis

Predictive efficiency in judgmental analysis is the degree to which a regression equation reproduces a judge's criterion ratings from the supplied predictors. The squared multiple correlation, R-squared, is used as its summary.

A high value suggests that the judge expressed a consistent policy through the measured variables. An extremely low value may indicate inconsistency, missing predictors, or a judge who should not be retained in the study.
