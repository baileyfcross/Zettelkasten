# Healthcare Information Need

A healthcare information need is the gap between what a clinician, patient, or researcher knows and what is required for a decision. It may concern diagnosis, treatment, prognosis, prevention, policy, or background knowledge.

Retrieval begins by translating that need into a query, but the vocabulary of the question may not match the vocabulary of the literature. Time pressure and task context also change what counts as a useful answer. Evaluation should therefore consider whether retrieved information supports the user's decision, not only whether documents contain the query words.
