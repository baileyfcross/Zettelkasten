# OStatus Protocol Suite

OStatus combined several existing web specifications into a federation stack for social updates. The source lists Atom for entries, Activity Streams for activity vocabulary, WebSub for update notification, Salmon for federated comments, and WebFinger for discovering information associated with an account.

StatusNet and later GNU social adopted the stack, but many federated microblogging projects moved to [[ActivityPub Federation Model|ActivityPub]] after encountering limitations. The source identifies Friendica as a remaining OStatus user at the time of publication. OStatus shows one way federation can emerge by composing narrow standards, but also how the seams among them can motivate a more integrated successor.
