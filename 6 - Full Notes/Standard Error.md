# Standard Error

A standard error is the standard deviation of a statistic's [[Sampling Distribution]]. It measures how much the estimate would vary if the data-generating and sampling process were repeated.

For a sample mean under the [[Central Limit Theorem]], the population standard deviation is divided by the square root of sample size. In practice the unknown population spread is estimated, which introduces the t-distribution in small samples.
