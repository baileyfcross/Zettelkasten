# Controlled Medical Terminology

A controlled medical terminology organizes preferred concepts, synonyms, and relationships so that different expressions can be indexed and searched consistently. Hierarchies such as a disease tree let a query expand from a broad category to narrower concepts.

Controlled terms improve semantic consistency but do not eliminate ambiguity. A vocabulary may lag new language, represent some specialties more deeply than others, or impose a granularity that does not fit the question. Effective retrieval combines terminology with free-text search and makes query expansion visible to the user.
