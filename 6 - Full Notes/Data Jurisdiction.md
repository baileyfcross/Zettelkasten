# Data Jurisdiction

Data jurisdiction identifies the laws and regulatory authority that apply to data and the systems or people handling it. A cloud provider, user, application host, storage location, and support team may each be located in a different country.

The applicable jurisdiction should therefore be established before a service is purchased, not inferred only from the consumer's location. Geographic movement caused by elastic provisioning must remain compatible with the service's legal and compliance requirements.

A shared virtual space can place participants in the same apparent room while their bodies, accounts, platform operator, and stored evidence remain in different legal jurisdictions. Harassment, contracts, property disputes, or prohibited content may therefore be experienced together but governed differently. Platform rules cannot remove that conflict; they must state which authority and process will handle it.
