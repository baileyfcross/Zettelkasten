# Analyst-First Principle

The analyst-first principle designs data-science systems around the person investigating the problem. Tools should make data, assumptions, transformations, and results visible so the analyst can exercise judgment rather than merely accept automated output.

The book uses this human-centered stance to connect flexible open-source tooling with accountable analytic practice.
