# Validated Entry and Conversion in the MATLAB App

In the book's unit-converter app, the user chooses the category and units, enters a From value, and clicks Enter before Convert becomes active. Enter validates the value's format and evaluates its significant digits. Convert then displays the result, the multiplicative factor, and whether that factor is exact or derived. Temperature conversions are shown differently because the app calculates them with formulas rather than a single multiplicative factor. The sequence makes input validation a prerequisite to reporting a conversion. See [[Exact and Derived Conversion Factors]] and [[Temperature Readings versus Intervals]].
