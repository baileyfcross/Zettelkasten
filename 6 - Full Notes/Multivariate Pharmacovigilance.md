# Multivariate Pharmacovigilance

Multivariate pharmacovigilance models several patient, drug, and event variables together instead of evaluating one drug-event pair in isolation. It can account for co-medications and measured confounders and can search for interactions among exposures.

The increased detail brings sparse combinations and model instability. Multi-drug adverse effects may be important precisely where few reports exist, and missing variables can leave residual confounding. Regularization, careful feature definition, and independent evidence are needed before a complex signal is treated as clinically credible.
