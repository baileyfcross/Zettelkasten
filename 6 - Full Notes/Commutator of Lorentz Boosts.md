# Commutator of Lorentz Boosts

The commutator of two Lorentz boosts measures the difference between applying infinitesimal boosts in opposite orders. When the boosts point in different spatial directions, their commutator is proportional to a spatial rotation generator rather than to another pure boost.

This result reflects the noncommutative structure of the [[Lorentz Group]]. Collinear boosts share one time-space plane and can be combined by adding [[Rapidity|rapidities]], but non-collinear boosts mix different planes. Reversing their order changes the final spatial orientation.

The associated finite rotation appears physically as Thomas–Wigner rotation, including the Thomas-precession correction in spin dynamics. The source uses the same boost–boost–rotation relation to compare Lorentz generators with mixed symmetric and skew-symmetric matrix algebras.
