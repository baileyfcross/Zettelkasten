# Emotion as a Design Value

An emotion design value names the feelings a game should make possible and connects them to the play that may produce them. Tension, tenderness, embarrassment, triumph, uncertainty, grief, or delight require different patterns of action, consequence, timing, and presentation.

The value is an intention rather than a guaranteed output because players bring different histories and interpretations. Playtesting asks whether the designed conditions repeatedly support the emotional direction, not whether every person reports an identical feeling.
