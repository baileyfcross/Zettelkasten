# Polynomial Multiplication

Polynomial multiplication distributes every term of one polynomial across every term of the other. Coefficients multiply, powers with a common base combine through the [[Laws of Exponents]], and like terms are collected after all products are formed.

The method works for any number of terms. For two binomials, the [[FOIL Method]] is a mnemonic for the same complete distribution. An omitted cross-product changes the result, which is why ((a+b)^2) equals (a^2+2ab+b^2) rather than (a^2+b^2).
