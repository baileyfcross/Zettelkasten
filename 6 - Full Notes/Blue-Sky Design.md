# Blue-Sky Design

Blue-sky design is an expansive ideation phase in which a group generates many possibilities before evaluating them. Quantity, combination, twists on familiar ideas, and a temporary “yes, and” posture help the group move beyond its first clichés.

The ideas are later curated against the [[Game Concept]], audience, feasibility, and scope. Constraints can strengthen creativity, while loud voices, groupthink, and pressure to settle everything in one sitting can narrow the search prematurely.
