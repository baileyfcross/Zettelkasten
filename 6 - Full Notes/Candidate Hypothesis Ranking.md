# Candidate Hypothesis Ranking

Candidate hypothesis ranking orders competing interpretations or assistant skills by estimated plausibility. It can combine the utterance with recognition confidence, [[Conversation Context]], [[Personal Context]], and current device or display state.

The top candidate can be executed when confidence and risk permit. Close or consequential alternatives should trigger a [[Clarification Question]] instead of an invisible guess.
