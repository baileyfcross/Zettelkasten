# Social Computing

Social computing describes systems that serve as a medium or focus for social relationships. As a medium, a system supports existing social conventions and contexts. As a focus, it creates new forms of social activity that would not otherwise occur.

Because conversations and conventions are situated in social reality, social computing is a form of [[Embodied Interaction]]. It is closely related to [[Computer-Supported Cooperative Work]], which concentrates on supporting collaborative activities and their coordination.

In three-dimensional environments, social computing can place remote users in a shared virtual space through [[Telepresence]].
