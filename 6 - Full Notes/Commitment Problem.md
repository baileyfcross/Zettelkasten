# Commitment Problem

A commitment problem occurs when a promised future action is advantageous to announce now but disadvantageous to perform later. Other players can use [[Backward Induction]] to recognize that the promise or threat is not credible and adjust their earlier choices.

A contract, enforced rule, or irreversible move can change the game by making the commitment credible. The mechanism does not merely communicate intent; it changes later payoffs or available actions so following through becomes rational.
