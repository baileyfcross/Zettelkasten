# Humanism in AI

Humanism in AI places human reason, dignity, freedom, or flourishing at the center of judgments about intelligent technology. It rejects the idea that technical capability or efficiency should determine its own goals and insists that machines should serve human purposes.

The position protects people from being subordinated to automated systems, but it can treat a historically specific picture of the human as universal. It may also neglect nonhuman life and environmental interests. [[Posthumanism]] and [[Anthropocentrism in AI Ethics]] expose these limits without eliminating the practical need to defend people against concentrated technological power.
