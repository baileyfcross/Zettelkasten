# Residual

A residual is the difference between an observed outcome and the value predicted by a fitted model. Residuals represent the portion of each observation not explained by the model's current terms.

Their distribution and relationship with fitted values or predictors support assumption checks. Systematic curvature, changing spread, or extreme residuals can reveal misspecification that a high-level fit statistic conceals.
