# Site Reliability Engineering

Site reliability engineering applies software-engineering methods to the reliability, scalability, performance, and operational efficiency of production systems. SRE work combines monitoring, alerting, fault recovery, resilient design, change risk, capacity, and operational automation.

The discipline balances delivery speed with system stability by turning reliability needs into engineering requirements and reviewable practices. It participates before launch as well as after incidents, ensuring observability, rollback, emergency plans, documentation, and risk controls are ready.
