# Camera Model

A camera model defines the ordinary viewpoint from which the virtual camera displays the game world and the rules governing how that view moves. It includes fixed perspectives and cameras that respond to player input, avatar motion, or context.

The camera is part of a gameplay mode because it controls what information and spatial relationships are visible. Its behavior must support the interaction model without causing disorientation or hiding important action.
