# Lifted Condensation and Convection Levels

The lifted condensation level is where a rising parcel first reaches saturation. The convective condensation level describes saturation reached through surface heating and convection, while the level of free convection marks where a parcel becomes buoyant enough to rise without continued forcing. The equilibrium level caps that positive-buoyancy region, whose energy is summarized by convective available potential energy.
