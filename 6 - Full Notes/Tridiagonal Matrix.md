# Tridiagonal Matrix

A tridiagonal matrix is a banded square matrix whose nonzero entries lie only on the main diagonal, superdiagonal, and subdiagonal.

Its restricted structure makes [[Gaussian Elimination]] and [[LU Decomposition]] much cheaper than for a dense matrix. Such systems occur in discretized differential-equation and heat-conduction models.
