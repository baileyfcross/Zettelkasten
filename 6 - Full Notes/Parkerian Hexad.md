# Parkerian Hexad

The Parkerian hexad expands the [[CIA Triad]] into six information-security properties: [[Information Confidentiality]], [[Information Possession and Control]], [[Data Integrity]], [[Information Authenticity]], [[Information Availability]], and [[Information Utility]].

The properties are intended to expose non-overlapping ways in which information can fail its intended user. For example, correct and confidential data can still be unusable, unavailable, held by an unauthorized party, or falsely attributed to an untrustworthy origin.
