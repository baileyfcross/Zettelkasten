# Napier's Rules

Napier's rules organize the five non-right parts of a right [[Spherical Triangle]] in a circular diagram, using complements for the two parts adjacent to the right angle and for the opposite side. The sine of any selected middle part equals the product of the tangents of its adjacent parts or the product of the cosines of its opposite parts.
