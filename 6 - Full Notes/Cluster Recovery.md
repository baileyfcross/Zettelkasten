# Cluster Recovery

Cluster recovery is the extent to which an algorithm reconstructs a known or simulated grouping from observed data. Recovery studies vary sample size, cluster count, dimensionality, separation, noise, and method, then compare captured and true partitions.

An external validity index quantifies the match. Strong recovery under controlled conditions reveals capability but does not guarantee performance on an unknown real-world structure.
