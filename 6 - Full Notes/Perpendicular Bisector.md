# Perpendicular Bisector

The perpendicular bisector of a segment passes through its midpoint and meets the segment at a right angle. In coordinates, first average the endpoint coordinates to find the midpoint, then use a perpendicular slope to construct its line equation. Every point on this bisector is equidistant from the two endpoints.
