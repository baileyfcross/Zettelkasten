# Laurent Series

A Laurent series expands a complex function in integer powers about a point and allows both positive and negative exponents. It converges on an annular region bounded by singularities rather than necessarily on a disk.

The nonnegative-power part is the analytic portion, while the negative-power principal part classifies the isolated singularity at the center. The coefficient of the inverse first power is the residue used in contour integration.
