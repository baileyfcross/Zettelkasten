# Poincare Section

A Poincare section reduces a continuous phase-space trajectory to the sequence of points where it crosses a chosen lower-dimensional surface in a specified direction. The resulting return map exposes recurrent structure that may be obscured in the full flow.

Periodic motion produces a finite set of repeated points, quasiperiodic motion produces organized curves, and chaotic motion produces a more intricate scattered structure. The method therefore distinguishes dynamical regimes while lowering the dimension of the data.
