# Genomic Data Transfer

Genomic data transfer moves large raw and derived files between instruments, computing centers, repositories, and researchers. Dataset size makes bandwidth, resumable movement, integrity checks, and proximity to computation significant design concerns.

Human genomic data also require authentication, authorization, encryption, and auditable sharing policies during movement.
