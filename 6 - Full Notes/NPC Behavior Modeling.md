# NPC Behavior Modeling

NPC behavior modeling teaches by letting the player observe another character perform an action and experience its consequence. A non-player character can demonstrate a safe route, reveal a hazard by failing, or pause to show the timing required to cross an obstacle.

The method communicates rules in-world and allows imitation without a textual explanation. Emotional attachment can strengthen the guidance by making a player want to follow, protect, or pursue the character. The modeled behavior must be readable and causally clear or the player may copy the wrong feature of the demonstration.
