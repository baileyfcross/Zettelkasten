# Social Review Platforms

A social review platform collects user evaluations of products, services, locations, or organizations and makes those judgments visible to later users. Its network is formed around the reviewed object as much as around relationships among reviewers.

For an enterprise, reviews are both public feedback and operational data. Integrating them requires retaining the association among rating, text, author, target, and time rather than flattening every contribution into a generic post. The source distinguishes review sites from broad social networks because their traffic and influence follow search and purchase decisions. A portal or content system may surface this material alongside other feeds through an [[Enterprise Social Portal Integration]].
