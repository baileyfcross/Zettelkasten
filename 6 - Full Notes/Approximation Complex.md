# Approximation Complex

An approximation complex is built from cycles, boundaries, or homology of a [[Koszul Complex]] tensored with a symmetric algebra. It reorganizes syzygy information into a graded complex.

Its zeroth homology can recover the symmetric algebra of an ideal or of its conormal module. Acyclicity conditions connect linear-type ideals, Rees algebras, and determinant formulas used in implicitization.
