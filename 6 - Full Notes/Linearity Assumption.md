# Linearity Assumption

The linearity assumption states that the expected continuous outcome is an additive linear function of the predictors as represented in the model. It concerns the mean structure, not whether each raw variable has a normal distribution.

Residual patterns and scatter plots can expose curvature or thresholds. Transformations, splines, interactions, or categorical representations may address departures, but each alternative changes what the coefficients mean.
