# K-Partite Clique

A K-partite graph divides its vertices into K sets and permits edges only between different sets. In categorical clustering, the sets can represent attribute domains and vertices their values.

A clique selects mutually connected values across the parts. CLICK uses maximal supported cliques to describe combinations that form categorical clusters.
