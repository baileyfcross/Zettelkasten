# Microcode for Early Microprocessors

Microcode is a detailed program that coordinates the internal operations used to realize a processor’s instruction set. Early microprocessor systems stored these low-level routines in read-only or random-access memory.

A simple processor could therefore provide useful behavior through carefully designed control programs. The balance between hardware and microcode helped make a computer architecture just barely realizable on an early chip.
