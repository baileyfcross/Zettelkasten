# AI in the Anthropocene

AI in the Anthropocene concerns the role of intelligent systems in an era when human activity has become a powerful force shaping Earth's climate and ecosystems. AI can help study complex environmental change, but it can also intensify extraction, energy use, waste, and attempts to manage the planet as a controllable resource.

Delegating environmental management to a supposedly superior machine would not escape human hyper-agency; it could extend it through automated control. The ethical challenge is to use intelligence while recognizing dependence on embodied, social, and ecological relationships. [[Sustainable AI]] must therefore consider nonhuman life and limits, not only human efficiency.
