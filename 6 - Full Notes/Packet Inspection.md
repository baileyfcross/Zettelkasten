# Packet Inspection

Packet inspection examines captured network frames and decodes their link, network, transport, and application protocol fields. It can reveal addresses, ports, flags, sequence information, payload sizes, and timing that application logs omit.

Inspection is diagnostic evidence rather than an explanation by itself. Captures should be filtered to the relevant conversation and handled carefully because payloads can contain credentials or sensitive application data.
