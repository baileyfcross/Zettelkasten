# Cloud Service Broker

A cloud service broker is an intermediary between consumers and one or more cloud service providers. It can reduce provider lock-in, aggregate several services into one offering, and choose or orchestrate providers according to cost, quality, and timeliness.

The broker negotiates upstream prices and [[Service Level Agreement|service agreements]], then presents a consolidated relationship to the consumer. A broker may maintain a [[Cloud Service Catalog]] and perform integration so the consumer does not have to coordinate every supplier separately.
