# Hypothesis-Driven Development

Hypothesis-driven development states what the team believes a change will cause, for whom, and within what constraint, then builds the smallest test that can challenge the belief. A useful hypothesis connects an intervention to an observable result.

The goal is fast learning rather than proving the team correct. Deployment, measurement, and interpretation are part of the feature, not activities postponed until after implementation.
