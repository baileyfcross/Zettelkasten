# Microsoft Entra Workload ID

Microsoft Entra Workload ID applies identity governance and protection to applications, service principals, and managed identities rather than human users. These non-human identities authenticate software to protected resources and can hold powerful permissions even though no employee directly signs in as them.

The service brings workload sign-ins under Conditional Access, risk detection, Continuous Access Evaluation, access reviews, and health recommendations. [[Azure Managed Identity]] and [[Azure Workload Identity Federation]] reduce stored secrets, while monitoring and lifecycle automation address unused applications, excessive roles, and expiring credentials. Workload identity security therefore combines credential elimination, least-privilege authorization, formal ownership, and removal when the software is retired.
