# Column Base Plates and Anchor Rods

A column base plate spreads steel column force into concrete so bearing pressure remains acceptable. Plate area is established from axial load and concrete bearing strength, while plate thickness resists bending in the projections beyond the effective column footprint. Grout provides uniform bearing and leveling nuts or shims establish erection position.

Anchor rods hold the column during erection and resist specified tension, uplift, shear, or moment rather than automatically carrying gravity compression. Their design can involve steel tension, concrete breakout, pullout, and edge-distance effects. Base shear may be transferred by friction, anchor rods, or a shear lug. Combined axial load and moment produce nonuniform bearing and anchor tension, making equilibrium and a clear load-transfer detail essential.
