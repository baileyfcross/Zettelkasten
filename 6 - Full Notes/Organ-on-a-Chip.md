# Organ-on-a-Chip

An organ-on-a-chip is a small engineered tissue model designed to reproduce selected structures and functions of an organ within a controlled device. Microfluidic flow and mechanical or biochemical inputs create conditions difficult to maintain in an ordinary culture dish.

The model is intentionally partial: it is useful when the reproduced function matches the biological question or therapy being tested.
