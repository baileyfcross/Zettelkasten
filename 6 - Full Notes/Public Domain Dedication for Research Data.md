# Public Domain Dedication for Research Data

A public domain dedication for research data states that the dataset creator will not exercise copyright or similar ownership rights they may hold in the data. The book recommends CC0 as a way to address uncertainty about residual rights in selection and arrangement while maximizing downstream use.

The dedication is legal permission, not a claim that scholarly credit is unnecessary. A dataset should also provide a preferred citation so users can acknowledge its creators. Nor does the dedication override privacy, confidentiality, or rights supplied by collaborators. Those constraints belong in the [[Data Sharing Plan]].
