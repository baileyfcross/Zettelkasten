# Composite Cloud Business Process

A composite cloud business process combines applications, information services, infrastructure, human activity, and other workflows into one delivered business function. The components can come from several providers while an orchestrated flow presents one outcome to the consumer.

A tax-planning service, for example, can join current tax information, calculation applications, personal data entry, scenario analysis, and reporting. Contracts between the components determine whether the complete process stays current and dependable.
