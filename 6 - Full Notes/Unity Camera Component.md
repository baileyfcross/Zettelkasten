# Unity Camera Component

A Unity Camera component renders a view of the scene from its GameObject's position and orientation. Its projection, field of view or orthographic size, clipping planes, viewport rectangle, depth, and layer mask determine what portion of the world becomes the Game view.

Multiple cameras can create split-screen or picture-in-picture compositions by rendering to different viewport regions. Camera culling also connects to [[Unity Tag and Layer|layers]], allowing a view to omit selected groups without removing them from the scene.
