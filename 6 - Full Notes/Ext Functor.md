# Ext Functor

Extⁿ_R(M,N) is a derived form of the [[Hom Module]]. It can be computed by applying Hom to a projective resolution of M or an injective resolution of N and taking cohomology.

Ext⁰ equals Hom. Positive-degree Ext records higher extension obstructions and is used by the source to characterize depth, grade, and injective dimension.
