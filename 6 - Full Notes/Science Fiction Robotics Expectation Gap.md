# Science Fiction Robotics Expectation Gap

Science fiction described socially capable and autonomous robots long before engineers could build them. As a result, public expectations often begin with characters that reason, converse, manipulate objects, and form intentions, while real systems struggle with doors, uneven ground, perception, and narrow context.

This expectation gap can make genuine technical progress look underwhelming and distant speculative threats look immediate. It also encourages people to infer self-awareness from outward behavior. Clear discussion should separate present capabilities from the [[Robot Cultural Archetypes]] used to narrate them and distinguish narrow systems from general intelligence.
