# Lean Development

Lean development improves the whole stream by delivering quickly, reducing waste, building quality into the work, and using the knowledge of the people doing it. Local efficiency is secondary to the movement of value through the complete system.

Its practices include [[Continuous Improvement]], smaller [[Batch Size|batches]], shorter [[Cycle Time|cycle times]], and exposing constraints instead of hiding them with excess inventory or parallel work.

The software-development source organizes lean practice around eliminating unfinished work, unnecessary processing, speculative features, waiting, context switching, motion, and defects; building quality in; preserving created knowledge; deferring consequential commitments; delivering fast; respecting the people doing the work; and optimizing the complete value stream.
