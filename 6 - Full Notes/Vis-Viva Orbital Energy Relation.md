# Vis-Viva Orbital Energy Relation

The vis-viva relation gives orbital speed from the current radius and the orbit's semi-major axis. It follows from conservation of mechanical energy and applies to elliptical motion, where speed is greatest near periapsis and lowest near apoapsis. Circular-orbit speed appears when the current radius equals the semi-major axis.
