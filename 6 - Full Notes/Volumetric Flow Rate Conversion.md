# Volumetric Flow Rate Conversion

Volumetric flow combines volume with inverse time, such as ft³/s or L/day. The book converts cubic feet per second to cubic meters per second with a volume ratio because the seconds already match. When the time unit differs, a second factor must be oriented to cancel the denominator as well. The app lists per-second, per-minute, and per-day choices, illustrating why a flow result cannot be obtained from a volume factor alone in every case. See [[Volume Unit Scaling]] and [[Compound Rate Unit Conversion]].
