# Inference Complexity

Inference complexity is the computational cost of answering a question from a probabilistic description. It is shaped by the searched and free variables, their domains, and the dependencies created during marginalization.

A compact model is not automatically easy to query. Good decompositions expose conditional independences that keep intermediate calculations small, while an unfortunate query or elimination order can still cause rapid growth.
