# Cycle in a Chain Complex

An n-cycle is an element of the kernel of the differential dₙ: Cₙ → Cₙ₋₁. It is sent to zero by the next map in a [[Chain Complex]].

Every [[Boundary in a Chain Complex]] is a cycle, but a cycle need not be a boundary. The quotient of cycles by boundaries is the n-th [[Homology Group]].
