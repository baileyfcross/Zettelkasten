# Principle of Moments

The principle of moments states that an object in rotational equilibrium has zero resultant moment about any point. In planar problems, the sum of clockwise moments equals the sum of counterclockwise moments. Complete static equilibrium also requires zero [[Resultant Force]], because balanced torques alone do not prevent translation.
