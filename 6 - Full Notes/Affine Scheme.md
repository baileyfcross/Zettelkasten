# Affine Scheme

An affine scheme is the [[Spectrum of a Ring]] together with its [[Structure Sheaf]]. Its points are prime ideals, and its local functions are obtained by localization.

Ring homomorphisms reverse direction to produce morphisms of affine schemes. This contravariant correspondence makes commutative rings and affine schemes two descriptions of the same structure: one algebraic and one geometric.
