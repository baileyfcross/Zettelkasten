# Similar Triangles

Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides. Angle-angle information is sufficient to establish similarity, while side-angle-side and side-side-side proportionality provide other tests. Similarity preserves shape under scaling and supplies the geometric reason that [[Trigonometric Ratios]] depend only on an angle.
