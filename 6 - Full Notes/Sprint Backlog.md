# Sprint Backlog

The Sprint Backlog is the selected set of [[Product Backlog Item|Product Backlog Items]] and the team's evolving plan for meeting the [[Sprint Goal]]. It makes the current work visible and belongs to the Development Team.

As knowledge changes during the Sprint, tasks and implementation details can change. The backlog is therefore a live coordination artifact rather than a contract frozen at planning.
