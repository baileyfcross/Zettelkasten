# Data-Driven Culture

A data-driven culture expects claims and organizational choices to be informed by accessible evidence. It combines technical capability with routines for asking questions, challenging assumptions, sharing results, and revising action.

Culture matters because an accurate analysis has little effect if it is isolated from authority and workflow. Evidence must be understandable, timely, and connected to the people who can act on it.
