# Free-Form Modeling

Free-form modeling creates geometry through unconstrained spatial strokes, surfaces, or direct manipulation rather than fitting only predefined primitives. AR lets the creator draw or shape virtual content while viewing the physical environment around it.

The method favors expressive creation over exact reconstruction and can tolerate lower precision than measurement-oriented modeling. Clear depth cues, viewpoint control, and feedback are needed because a two-dimensional display can make three-dimensional input ambiguous.
