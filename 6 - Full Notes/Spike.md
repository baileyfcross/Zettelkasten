# Spike

A spike is a timeboxed backlog item whose primary result is knowledge. A team uses it to research, experiment, or prototype when uncertainty prevents responsible estimation or design.

The spike answers a stated question and ends at its time limit. Any production feature revealed by the work is planned separately, preventing investigation from silently becoming unbounded implementation.
