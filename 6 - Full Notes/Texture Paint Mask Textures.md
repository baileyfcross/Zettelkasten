# Texture Paint Mask Textures

A mask texture varies how strongly the brush affects each part of a dab. Light and dark mask values admit or suppress paint, allowing scratches, pores, broken edges, or other patterned boundaries to emerge from a uniform brush color.

Mask mapping and pressure settings operate separately from the brush's color texture. Combining them lets one image determine what is painted while another determines where and how strongly it is applied.
