# Product Backlog Item

A Product Backlog Item is a brief description of behavior, value, knowledge, or work that may improve a product. Game PBIs can represent player features, content, tools, defects, technical changes, or [[Prototype|prototypes]].

Items near implementation are refined until they are small and understood enough to fit a Sprint. The description leaves implementation choices to the Development Team.
