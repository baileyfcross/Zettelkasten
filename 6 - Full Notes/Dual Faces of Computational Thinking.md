# Dual Faces of Computational Thinking

Computational thinking has a backward-looking face and a forward-looking face. The first explains how machines, algorithms, and software work; the second designs new systems that answer human purposes and concerns.

The two faces reinforce one another. Understanding mechanisms makes designs feasible, while attention to use exposes what a technically correct computation must accomplish beyond merely executing its instructions.
