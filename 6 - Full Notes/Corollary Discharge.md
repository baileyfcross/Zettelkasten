# Corollary Discharge

A corollary discharge is a copy of an outgoing motor command sent to sensory areas of the brain. Because its magnitude reflects the command used to contract a muscle, it provides a feed-forward estimate of effort before peripheral consequences are fully returned.

Comparing this internal signal with feedback from [[Golgi Tendon Organ|Golgi tendon organs]], [[Muscle Spindle|muscle spindles]], and skin helps distinguish expected from unexpected movement and force. A mismatch—such as contact with an obstacle—can trigger correction and contributes to the integrated force perception of [[Kinesthesia]].
