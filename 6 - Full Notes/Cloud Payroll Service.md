# Cloud Payroll Service

A cloud payroll service delivers salary calculation, payment, payslip processing, and required tax transfer as a repeatable hosted process. Its per-record workload and common use across industries make it a horizontal business-process service.

The process depends on interfaces to human resources and management for events such as unpaid leave, overtime, and departure dates. Standard delivery improves consistency only when those inputs remain accurate and timely.
