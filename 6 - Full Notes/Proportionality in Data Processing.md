# Proportionality in Data Processing

Proportionality in data processing asks whether the amount, sensitivity, duration, and intrusiveness of processing are justified by its legitimate objective. A useful result alone does not establish that every available input or analytical technique is necessary.

The principle encourages narrower collection, shorter retention, and less invasive alternatives when they can meet the same purpose with lower risk.
