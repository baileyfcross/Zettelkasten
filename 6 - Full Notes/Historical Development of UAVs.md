# Historical Development of UAVs

Unmanned flight progressed from nineteenth-century weapon-carrying balloons to powered concepts, radio-controlled targets, jet reconnaissance vehicles, and armed or civilian platforms. Advances in radio guidance, engines, electronics, batteries, cameras, and control systems repeatedly expanded the feasible mission set. The history shows that a UAV is shaped as much by communication and sensing technology as by aerodynamic design.
