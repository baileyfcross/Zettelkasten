# Flatness Criterion via Tor

An R-module M is flat exactly when Torᵢᴿ(N,M) vanishes for every R-module N and every positive degree i. It is enough to test the first obstruction in standard formulations.

The criterion turns preservation of all exact sequences into a homological vanishing condition. Nonzero Tor identifies where tensoring with M destroys injectivity.
