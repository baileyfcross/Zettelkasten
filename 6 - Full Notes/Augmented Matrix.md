# Augmented Matrix

An augmented matrix places the [[Coefficient Matrix]] and right-hand-side vector of a [[Linear System]] side by side, written $[A\mid b]$.

[[Elementary Row Operation|Elementary row operations]] on this matrix represent equivalent operations on the equations. Its echelon form reveals consistency, free variables, and the solution.
