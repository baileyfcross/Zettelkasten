# Numerical Integration

Numerical integration approximates a definite integral from sampled values of its integrand. In one dimension the process is also called quadrature.

A common construction replaces the integrand by an [[Interpolating Polynomial]] and integrates that polynomial exactly. The accuracy then depends on the interpolation remainder, the spacing of the nodes, and the smoothness of the original function.
