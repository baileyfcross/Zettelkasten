# Randomization

Randomization selects among outcomes using apparent chance, through devices such as dice, shuffled cards, or software-generated values. Perfect physical randomness is unnecessary when the result is functionally unpredictable to players.

Games use randomization to represent detail below the chosen modeling level and to create uncertainty. The resulting probabilities must still support perceived fairness, because players often judge short sequences by intuition rather than by statistical expectation.
