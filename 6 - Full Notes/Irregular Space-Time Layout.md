# Irregular Space-Time Layout

An irregular space-time layout contains measured position-time pairs with no shared grid or obvious repeated organization. Locations, times, or both may differ from one observation to the next. STI and [[STIDF Object|STIDF]] represent this form in the `spacetime` package. Analysis and visualization cannot assume a complete array, so observations must be selected or aggregated through their explicit coordinates and time indexes.
