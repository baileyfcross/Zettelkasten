# Dirac Probability Current

The Dirac probability current is a conserved four-vector formed from a spinor and its Dirac adjoint. Applying the Dirac equation and its adjoint produces a continuity equation relating the current's spacetime divergence to zero.

Its time component is proportional to the spinor's positive sum of squared component magnitudes, allowing a probability-density interpretation. The spatial components describe the associated probability flow and transform together with the density as a relativistic current.
