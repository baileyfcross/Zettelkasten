# Correlated Predictor Removal

Correlated predictor removal reduces a group of inputs that contain nearly duplicate information. It can improve numerical stability, reduce computation, and simplify interpretation, although some algorithms tolerate correlation better than others.

The retained variable should be chosen with measurement quality and domain meaning in view, not correlation alone.
