# Tactile Differential Threshold

A tactile differential threshold is the smallest change in a suprathreshold stimulus that produces a perceptible difference. It is also called a just noticeable difference and is often expressed as a fraction of the original magnitude.

The fraction depends on the judged property. Force or weight changes can be detected at a smaller proportional difference than changes in compliance or viscosity, where movement and force cues must be integrated. A differential threshold describes discrimination between stimuli; it differs from the absolute threshold at which any stimulus first becomes detectable.
