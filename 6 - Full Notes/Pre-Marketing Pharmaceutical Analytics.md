# Pre-Marketing Pharmaceutical Analytics

Pre-marketing pharmaceutical analytics supports the discovery and development stages before a product is widely used. Chemical, biological, genomic, and experimental data can be mined to propose targets, predict interactions, identify biomarkers, and generate hypotheses about efficacy or toxicity.

The evidence is incomplete and selective: known positive interactions are recorded more often than confirmed noninteractions. A model must not interpret every absent database link as a negative example. Predictions are candidates for laboratory and clinical evaluation rather than substitutes for those evaluations.
