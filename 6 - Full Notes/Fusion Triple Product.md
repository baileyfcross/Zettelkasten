# Fusion Triple Product

The fusion triple product combines plasma density, confinement time, and temperature into one indicator of whether reactions can release enough energy. Higher temperature helps overcome the [[Fusion Coulomb Barrier]], but a plasma too sparse or too briefly confined still produces inadequate reaction output. The source uses the measure when describing its projected [[Tokamak Fusion Reactor]] and the need for a positive net energy balance.
