# Von Mises Stress

Von Mises stress combines components of a multiaxial stress state into one equivalent scalar. It is commonly plotted in finite element post-processing to compare spatial stress intensity and identify critical regions.

For a plate with a hole, the distribution highlights the concentration around the boundary. Numerical maxima should be checked against analytical stress-concentration estimates and equilibrium.
