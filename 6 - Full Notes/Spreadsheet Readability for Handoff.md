# Spreadsheet Readability for Handoff

Spreadsheet readability for handoff means organizing a workbook so that a teammate—or the original author months later—can understand what its data represents, why its values exist, and which cells may be changed. Unlabeled numbers and unrelated tables crowded onto one sheet make a workbook difficult to debug or reuse.

Designing from an outsider's perspective encourages explicit labels, short explanations, consistent formatting, and visible separation of object types and calculations. This is not cosmetic polish: a workbook that communicates its own structure reduces onboarding time and prevents accidental changes to formulas or fixed reference data.
