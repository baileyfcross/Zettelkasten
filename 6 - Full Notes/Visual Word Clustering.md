# Visual Word Clustering

A visual-word representation quantizes local image descriptors into a learned codebook and describes an image by the frequency or arrangement of codewords. This converts variable sets of local features into vectors suitable for clustering.

Codebook size controls the tradeoff between discrimination and robustness. Quantization can discard spatial relationships and make visually distinct patches share a word, so extensions add spatial layout or soft assignments.
