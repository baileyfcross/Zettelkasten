# Game Balance

Game balance is a dynamically stable relationship among parts, systems, progression, and players. A balanced game avoids an inherent or insurmountable advantage, preserves several viable strategies, and lets players recover from reasonable risks.

Balance is a property of the whole [[Game-Player System]], not only of numeric equality between parts. It combines designer judgment, [[Playtesting]], player analytics, and mathematical models while keeping the [[Play Space]] perturbable and meaningful.

The source distinguishes competitive balance from player-versus-environment difficulty management. A balanced competitive game avoids dominant strategies and preserves the relevance of skill despite chance; a balanced environmental game keeps perceived difficulty within a credible range as challenge, player power, and experience change.
