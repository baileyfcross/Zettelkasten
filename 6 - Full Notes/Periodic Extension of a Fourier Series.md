# Periodic Extension of a Fourier Series

A Fourier series fitted on a finite interval represents a periodic extension when evaluated outside that interval. The values repeat with the period fixed by the expansion, even if the original function was specified only on the initial interval.

A half-range sine series creates an odd extension, while a half-range cosine series creates an even extension. These different continuations agree with the supplied data on the half interval but can have different endpoint continuity and convergence behavior elsewhere.
