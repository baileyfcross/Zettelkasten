# Radical Ideal

A radical ideal is an [[Ideal]] I satisfying I = √I. If a power rⁿ belongs to a radical ideal, then r already belongs to it.

Because the [[Radical of an Ideal]] is the intersection of all [[Prime Ideal]]s containing that ideal, radical ideals record which prime components occur while ignoring their multiplicities. Vanishing ideals of affine algebraic sets are radical in the classical setting developed by the source.
