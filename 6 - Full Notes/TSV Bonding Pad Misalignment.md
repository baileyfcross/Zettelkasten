# TSV Bonding Pad Misalignment

TSV bonding pad misalignment occurs when pads on stacked tiers do not overlap as intended. The reduced contact area raises resistance and current density; large offsets can also force current through a right-angle path that further worsens electromigration.

The defect appears during bonding, so it requires post-bond resistance sensing rather than only a pre-bond TSV fill test. Its location becomes an input to [[TSV-SAFE Framework]] protection.
