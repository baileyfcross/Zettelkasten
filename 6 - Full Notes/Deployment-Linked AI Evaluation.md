# Deployment-Linked AI Evaluation

Deployment-linked evaluation tests the endpoint, workflow, or agent version that users will actually invoke. Linking the evaluation to the deployed asset reduces the gap between an offline prompt experiment and production behavior, where tool access, orchestration, retrieval, model configuration, and safety controls may all alter the response.

The linkage also makes comparison operationally useful. A team can evaluate a candidate deployment before promotion, retain results with its version, and compare it with the current release. This creates evidence for rollback or advancement rather than treating deployment and quality review as unrelated activities.
