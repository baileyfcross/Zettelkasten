# Inscribed Layer

The inscribed layer contains the parts of a game that exist before and apart from a particular play session. Developers place rules, assets, scripted material, and enabling technologies into the artifact, defining the initial possibility space from which play can emerge.

Its four elements are [[Inscribed Mechanics]], [[Inscribed Aesthetics]], [[Inscribed Narrative]], and [[Inscribed Technology]]. An inscribed analysis therefore describes what the team actually supplied, not everything players will do with it or every meaning a community will later construct.
