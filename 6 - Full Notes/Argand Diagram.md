# Argand Diagram

An Argand diagram represents a [[Complex Number]] $z=x+iy$ as the point $(x,y)$ in a plane. The horizontal coordinate is the real part and the vertical coordinate is the imaginary part.

This picture turns complex addition into vector addition and makes multiplication interpretable through changes of length and angle. Distance from the origin is the modulus, while the directed angle from the positive real axis is an argument of the number.
