# Cultured Meat Sensory Properties

Cultured meat sensory properties are the appearance, aroma, flavor, texture, juiciness, tenderness, and mouthfeel through which a consumer experiences the product. They emerge from cells, fat, matrix composition, fiber alignment, maturation, storage, and cooking.

A nutritionally adequate tissue may still fail as food if its post-cooking behavior does not match or intentionally improve the expected eating experience.
