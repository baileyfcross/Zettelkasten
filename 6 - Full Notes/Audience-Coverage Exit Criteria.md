# Audience-Coverage Exit Criteria

Audience-coverage exit criteria require successful playtests with target-audience members, people new to the game, and experienced players. The groups expose different risks: relevance, first-use comprehension, and sustained depth.

No single session proves readiness. Coverage means the design has survived repeated observation across these perspectives with no unresolved failure that threatens the core experience.
