# Ultrametricity Triangle Coefficient

The book measures inherent ultrametricity by examining triples of points before imposing a clustering tree. In a Euclidean embedding, a triple is counted when its triangle is approximately equilateral or isosceles with a small base; the coefficient is the proportion of counted, nondegenerate triples.

Its implementation samples triples when exhaustive enumeration is too costly and uses a chosen angular tolerance, so the value depends on both the embedding and approximation rule. It can compare datasets or encodings for hierarchical tendency without assuming that a particular linkage algorithm's fitted dendrogram is the ground truth.
