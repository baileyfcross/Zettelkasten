# Oracle Risk Bound

An oracle risk bound compares a data-driven estimator with the best inaccessible estimator in a specified collection. Its typical form says that the selected risk is no larger than a constant times the candidate's approximation error plus variance and model-complexity terms, minimized over the collection.

The bound clarifies both adaptation and cost. It shows whether one procedure can adjust to unknown sparsity, rank, or tuning parameters, while revealing the penalty paid for searching many alternatives. A leading constant near one gives a particularly sharp comparison, as in [[Gibbs Estimator Aggregation]].
