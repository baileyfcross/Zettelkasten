# Laplace Equation Finite Difference Method

The finite difference method for $u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0$ replaces both second derivatives by centered differences on a mesh. With equal spacing, the result is the [[Standard Five-Point Formula]].

Known boundary values enter the equations for adjacent interior nodes, producing a simultaneous linear system. The solution approximates the harmonic field, and a finer mesh reduces discretization error at the cost of more unknowns.
