# Spatial Knowledge

Spatial knowledge is remembered information about an environment and its arrangement. It forms a [[Cognitive Map]] and supports [[Wayfinding]].

Three important forms are:

- Landmark knowledge: visual characteristics and distinctive objects.
- Route knowledge: sequences of actions needed to follow paths between places.
- Survey knowledge: map-like knowledge of locations, distances, orientations, and topology.

People often begin with landmark information, learn routes between landmarks, and gradually develop survey knowledge. Travel methods, viewpoints, maps, and [[Reference Frame]]s can change which forms are acquired and how easily they are used.
