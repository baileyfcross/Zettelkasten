# Challenge Preference in Games

Challenge preference is the degree to which a player seeks effort, precision, accomplishment, and difficult goals. The source relates it to conscientiousness and desire for control.

High-challenge players may enjoy demanding mastery, while low-challenge players may prefer sandbox activity or experiences without compulsory achievement. Difficulty options alone do not replace designing for the intended preference.
