# Random Mesh Selection

Random selection chooses a configurable percentage of mesh components, with a seed controlling which particular components are included. It is useful for introducing distributed variation before operations such as extrusion, material assignment, or deletion.

Percentage controls density while the seed explores alternative arrangements without changing that density. Because randomness can obscure intent, the generated selection should be judged visually and saved when later steps depend on reproducing it.
