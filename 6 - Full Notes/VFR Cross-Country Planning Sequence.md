# VFR Cross-Country Planning Sequence

A VFR cross-country plan proceeds from weather and aerodrome information to route selection, headings, performance, time, fuel, loading, frequencies, and plan filing. The order matters because later values depend on earlier choices such as altitude, true airspeed, wind, and alternates. Rechecking the complete plan exposes inconsistencies before they become in-flight workload.
