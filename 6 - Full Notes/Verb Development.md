# Verb Development

Verb development is the process of deepening the player's relationship with a [[Game Verb]]. A developed verb gains new responsibilities, participates in more relationships, and must be applied in increasingly nuanced situations.

Development does not require adding more controls. Designers can vary timing, combine existing verbs, or introduce objects that change the consequences of an established action. This preserves focus while expanding the [[Possibility Space]].
