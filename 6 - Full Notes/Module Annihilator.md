# Module Annihilator

The annihilator Ann(M) of an R-module M is the [[Ideal]] of ring elements r such that rM = 0. The annihilator of one element m consists of scalars that kill that element.

The quotient R/Ann(M) acts faithfully on M. The annihilator determines [[Dimension of a Module]] and helps describe its support and associated primes.
