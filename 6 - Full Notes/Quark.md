# Quark

A quark is an elementary particle carrying fractional electric charge and participating in the strong interaction. Six flavors occur in three generations. Quarks are confined within composite hadrons: three-quark combinations form baryons such as protons and neutrons, while quark-antiquark pairs form mesons.
