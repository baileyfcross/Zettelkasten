# One-Point and Two-Point Tracking

One tracked feature supplies the changing position needed for a translation match. Two features also expose how their relative angle and spacing change, permitting rotation and apparent scale to be matched. This still describes a planar image transform, not a full camera reconstruction. The book's tracking examples show why the number and arrangement of points should be chosen according to the movement the replacement element must follow. A surface that changes perspective may need [[Four-Point Corner Pin Tracking]].
