# Space-Wide Time Series

A space-wide time series stores each spatial zone or location as a separate variable and each time as a shared observation row. It converts a space-time dataset into a multivariate temporal form, enabling conventional line and horizon plots. The representation preserves temporal comparison among locations but treats geographic adjacency as metadata rather than visible position. Maps are needed to restore that spatial relationship.
