# Texture Paint Brush Radius and Strength

Brush radius controls the area affected by a texture-paint stroke, while strength controls how much each application changes the image. Pen pressure can drive either property when a compatible tablet is available.

Large soft brushes establish broad color fields and smaller brushes place detail, but texture resolution sets the finest result the image can store. Radius and strength should therefore be judged on the painted asset rather than by cursor size alone.
