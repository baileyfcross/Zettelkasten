# Categorical Cross-Entropy

Categorical cross-entropy is a [[Loss Function]] that measures the difference between two probability distributions. In multiclass classification, one distribution is the network's predicted probabilities and the other is the target with probability one on the correct class and zero on the others.

A [[Softmax Function]] produces the predicted distribution at the output layer. Training reduces cross-entropy so probability moves toward the correct label rather than merely matching one independent scalar value.
