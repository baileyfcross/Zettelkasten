# Conservation of Angular Momentum

The total angular momentum of a system remains constant when the resultant external torque is zero. A rotating body can therefore spin faster when its [[Moment of Inertia]] decreases, because $I\omega$ must remain unchanged. Internal torques redistribute angular momentum among parts of the system but cannot change the total.
