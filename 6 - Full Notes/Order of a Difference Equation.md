# Order of a Difference Equation

The order of a difference equation is the largest separation between sequence subscripts appearing in it after simplification. Thus a relation involving $y_{n+2}$ and $y_n$ but no more distant term has order two.

An order-$r$ recurrence normally requires $r$ independent conditions to select one solution from its general family. The order also equals the degree of the auxiliary polynomial for a constant-coefficient [[Linear Difference Equation]].
