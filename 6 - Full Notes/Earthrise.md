# Earthrise

Earthrise is the color photograph of Earth above the lunar horizon taken by Apollo 8 astronaut William Anders on December 24, 1968. Earlier robotic images had shown a similar geometry, but the sharp color view and its connection to the first human lunar-orbit mission gave this image exceptional reach.

The small bright Earth against black space and the barren Moon became a symbol of planetary beauty, isolation, and vulnerability. Its circulation in media and environmental campaigns made it central to [[Planetization]], demonstrating how a mission aimed at the Moon could transform cultural perception by looking back at home.
