# Observation Model

An observation model gives the probability of a measurement conditioned on a hidden state. It expresses how an inaccessible state is expected to appear through an imperfect sensor or other evidence source.

During filtering, this likelihood reweights the predicted state distribution. An informative observation concentrates belief on states that could plausibly have produced the measured value.
