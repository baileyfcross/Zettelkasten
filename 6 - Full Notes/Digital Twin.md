# Digital Twin

A digital twin is a computer representation of a physical machine, asset, or system whose state is informed by data from its real-world counterpart. Sensor streams let the model reflect how the object operates across time rather than serving only as a static design drawing.

Engineers can use the twin to simulate conditions, compare expected and observed performance, test changes, and identify likely failure before intervening in the physical system. Its usefulness depends on how faithfully its data and assumptions represent reality; a detailed model fed by incomplete or inaccurate measurements can still produce misleading conclusions.
