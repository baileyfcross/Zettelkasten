# Unmanned Maritime Vehicle

An unmanned maritime vehicle operates on or below water for tasks such as surveillance, mapping, mine detection, or cargo delivery. The environment makes autonomy difficult: saltwater corrodes hardware, waves and currents disturb navigation, weather stresses sensors, and radio communication is limited underwater.

Underwater vehicles may need long-duration power, local decision making, and a way to surface for communication. Surface craft can maintain richer links but remain exposed to capture or interference. These constraints make mission architecture a balance among endurance, payload, communication, recoverability, and independent operation.
