# Stress Concentration Factor

A stress concentration factor is the ratio of maximum local stress to a nominal reference stress. Holes and other geometric discontinuities raise local stress above the value predicted from net or gross section averages.

Analytical factors offer a benchmark for finite element results. Accurate numerical estimation requires a mesh fine enough around the discontinuity to resolve the steep gradient.
