# Electrical Impedance

Electrical impedance combines resistance and reactance into the complex ratio of AC voltage to current. Its magnitude determines current amplitude, while its phase determines the offset between current and voltage. In a series RLC circuit, $|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}$, with resonance when inductive and capacitive reactances cancel.

The book expresses the transition from DC resistance to AC impedance as $Z=R+j(\omega L-1/(\omega C))=R+jX$. This complex form keeps resistive loss and frequency-dependent energy storage in one quantity, which is necessary when both amplitude and phase affect a microwave response.
