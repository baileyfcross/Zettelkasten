# Quantum Tunneling in Annealing

Quantum annealing uses a transverse driver to permit transitions between configurations that are separated in the problem-energy landscape. Tunneling can make a narrow barrier accessible even when climbing over it thermally would require a large energy increase.

This mechanism motivates annealing as an alternative to a purely classical local search that becomes trapped in a basin. It is not a guarantee of reaching the global minimum: barrier width, minimum spectral gaps, decoherence, temperature, schedule duration, and the encoded landscape determine whether the transition is useful.
