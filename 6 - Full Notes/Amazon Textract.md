# Amazon Textract

Amazon Textract extracts printed or handwritten text and document structure from scanned documents and images. In addition to characters, it can identify relationships in forms and tables, turning visual documents into machine-readable fields and cells.

This differs from plain optical character recognition because the output includes structural context. Applications should validate uncertain fields, preserve links to source documents, and apply appropriate protection when documents contain personal or confidential information.
