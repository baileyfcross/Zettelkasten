# Microphysiological System

A microphysiological system is an engineered in-vitro platform that reproduces selected physical and biological functions of human tissue at small scale. It often combines living cells, three-dimensional matrices, microfluidic flow, sensing, and controlled stimulation.

Its validity is specific to the modeled mechanism; no small platform should be assumed to represent every response of the source organ.
