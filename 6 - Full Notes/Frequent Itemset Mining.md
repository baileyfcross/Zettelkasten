# Frequent Itemset Mining

Frequent itemset mining finds groups of items that occur together in at least a chosen proportion or number of transactions. Support provides the basic frequency measure, while candidate generation or pattern-growth methods search the combinatorial space.

The minimum threshold controls both usefulness and cost: a low threshold can produce an enormous number of patterns.
