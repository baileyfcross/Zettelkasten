# Diminishing Returns in Geometry Refinement

Diminishing returns in geometry refinement occur when additional detail or tighter repair produces less improvement in the decision-relevant result than the time, instability, and mesh cost it adds. The stopping point depends on the model's accuracy requirements and other sources of uncertainty.

A refined fillet is not valuable merely because it is more exact. Effort should move to material data, boundary conditions, validation, or another dominant uncertainty once geometry changes no longer affect the chosen outputs appreciably.
