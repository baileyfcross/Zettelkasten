# Game Genre Hybridization

Game genre hybridization combines substantial challenge and action patterns from more than one established genre. A hybrid can create a distinctive experience when the parts serve a common player role and premise.

Combining genres increases design and development work and does not automatically combine their audiences. The mixture should be playtested early because players attracted to one pattern may dislike the other.
