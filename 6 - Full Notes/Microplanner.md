# Microplanner

A microplanner turns a response plan into detailed linguistic decisions. It can choose sentence structure, referring expressions, aggregation, and the level of explicitness appropriate to the context.

Its output guides [[Lexicalization]], which realizes the structure as words and inflected forms. Together these stages separate what the assistant should say from exactly how it says it.
