# Optical See-Through Display

An optical see-through display combines a direct view of the physical world with generated light by means of an optical combiner. The user sees real objects through the display while virtual imagery is reflected or projected into the same line of sight.

Because the real view does not pass through a camera, it retains natural resolution and low visual delay. The virtual camera must be calibrated to the user's eye, and the system has limited control over real-world brightness, focus, and occlusion.
