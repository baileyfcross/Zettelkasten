# AWS CloudOps Financial Management Pillar

The CloudOps financial-management pillar connects engineering consumption to planning, visibility, and control. Cloud removes long procurement cycles but makes spend highly sensitive to resource configuration and ongoing usage. Teams therefore need forecasts, allocation, budgets, optimization practices, and accountable owners as part of normal operations. Financial feedback belongs inside the operating model because architecture and deployment decisions continuously change cost.
