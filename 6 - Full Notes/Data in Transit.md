# Data in Transit

Data in transit is information moving between systems, services, or network locations. During transmission it can be observed, altered, redirected, replayed, or blocked if the communication path is not adequately protected.

An [[Encrypted Communication Channel]] can provide confidentiality and integrity for this state, while endpoint authentication helps establish who is communicating. These protections complement the separate controls needed for [[Data at Rest]] and [[Data in Process]].
