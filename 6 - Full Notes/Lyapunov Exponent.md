# Lyapunov Exponent

A Lyapunov exponent measures the average exponential rate at which nearby trajectories in phase space separate or converge. A positive exponent indicates growth of small perturbations and therefore sensitivity to initial conditions.

A dynamical system can have a spectrum of exponents corresponding to different perturbation directions. The largest exponent is especially diagnostic: a positive largest value is a quantitative signature of chaotic dynamics, while negative values describe local contraction.
