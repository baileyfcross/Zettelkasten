# Food Bioprinting

Food bioprinting deposits edible pastes or cell-containing materials in controlled layers to create shape, composition, and internal structure. Multiple nozzles can place distinct ingredients, while targeted heating may cook each region during or after deposition.

The technique enables culinary forms unavailable through conventional preparation, though speed and sanitation must scale beyond demonstrations.
