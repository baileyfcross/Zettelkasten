# Orthogonal Clustering View

An orthogonal clustering view seeks a projection whose directions carry little or no overlap with those supporting a prior clustering. Clusters found in that projection emphasize variance hidden from the original view.

Geometric orthogonality provides a clear diversity criterion for numeric data, but it is stronger than semantic difference and may discard useful shared information. The new projection still requires an independent cohesion and stability assessment.
