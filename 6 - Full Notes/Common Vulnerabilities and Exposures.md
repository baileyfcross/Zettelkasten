# Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures

Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures, or CVE, is a standardized naming system for publicly known cybersecurity vulnerabilities. A stable identifier lets vendors, defenders, tools, and reports refer to the same disclosed weakness without relying on inconsistent descriptions.

A CVE identifier catalogs a vulnerability; it does not by itself measure the organization's [[Security Risk]]. Prioritization still requires context about exposure, plausible threats, available controls, and potential impact.
