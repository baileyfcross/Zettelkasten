# Standard Model

The Standard Model organizes matter into quarks and leptons and describes electromagnetic, weak, and strong interactions through exchanged gauge bosons. The Higgs field accounts for particle masses within the theory. Gravity is not included, and observations such as dark matter show that the model is highly successful but not a complete account of nature.
