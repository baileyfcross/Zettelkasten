# Structural Connection Design Responsibility

Steel connection design can be completed by the engineer of record, selected from standardized details by the fabricator, or delegated to a licensed engineer working for the fabricator. Each arrangement changes who supplies dimensions, loads, restrictions, calculations, and final details, but it does not remove the engineer of record's duty to communicate the structural intent and review conformance to it.

Ambiguity is dangerous because a connection is the interface between separately designed members. Contract documents should identify design method, factored or service-level forces, required connection type, moments or axial effects, seismic or fatigue demands, and any geometric constraints. Delegated design needs enough information to be engineered rather than guessed, while complete engineer-designed details must be constructible and coordinated with fabrication practice.
