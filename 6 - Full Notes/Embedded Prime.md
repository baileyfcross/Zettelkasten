# Embedded Prime

An embedded prime is an [[Associated Prime]] that is not minimal among the associated primes of a module. It represents a component contained inside the support associated with a smaller prime.

In a primary decomposition, minimal associated primes describe isolated components, while embedded primes reflect additional algebraic structure that is not a separate minimal component of the underlying support.
