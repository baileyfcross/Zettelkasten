# Logistic Omitted Variable Bias

Logistic omitted variable bias occurs when an unmodeled determinant of a binary outcome alters the scale or interpretation of coefficients in the fitted model. In nonlinear models, omitted variables can affect comparisons even when they are not strongly related to the included predictors.

Measuring relevant continuous variables and avoiding unnecessary categorization can help, but an existing dataset may not contain the needed information. Odds ratios should therefore be presented as model-dependent adjusted associations rather than invariant effects.
