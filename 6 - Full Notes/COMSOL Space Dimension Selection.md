# COMSOL Space Dimension Selection

COMSOL space-dimension selection establishes whether a component uses zero-, one-, two-, two-dimensional axisymmetric, or three-dimensional geometry. The choice controls which primitives, physics interfaces, boundary entities, and dependent-variable formulations are available.

Dimension should follow the relevant variation in the physics rather than the visual complexity of the object. A lower-dimensional model is efficient only when omitted directions can be represented by properties or symmetry without changing the sought result.
