# Human Computing Teams

Before electronic computers, teams of trained human computers carried out large calculations by following organized procedures. Managers divided work into stages, passed intermediate values along an assembly line, and relied on trained operators to detect errors.

These teams anticipated modern distributed systems. Their practices exposed the importance of decomposition, communication patterns, synchronization, and verification long before those ideas were implemented in electronic hardware.
