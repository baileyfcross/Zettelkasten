# Early Popularity Prediction

Early popularity prediction estimates final attention from observations collected soon after publication. Initial views, comments, votes, tweet rates, or adopter structure provide a partial signal before the full lifecycle unfolds.

Earlier predictions are more useful but have less evidence, creating a timeliness-accuracy tradeoff. Lifecycle and network features can help separate temporary bursts from growth likely to continue.
