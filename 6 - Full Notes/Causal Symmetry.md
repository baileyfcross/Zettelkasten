# Causal Symmetry

A causal symmetry exists when relabeling outcomes leaves their probability distribution unchanged while the collective conditions that determine the phenomenon's causal structure remain the same. Outcomes related by that invariant structure must receive equal probabilities.

The concept provides an objective route from causal conditions to probability values. It avoids treating observed frequency alone as the source of probability.
