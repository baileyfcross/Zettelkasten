# Net Wind Uplift on Steel Roof Framing

Net roof uplift is the upward wind effect after downward permanent load is included with the correct load factor. Because dead load stabilizes rather than aggravates uplift, a load combination may use less than the full dead load. The resulting sign reversal can place a roof beam or joist's normally compressed region in tension and can reverse reactions at supports.

Uplift design must check more than member bending. Deck fasteners, joist seats, beam connections, collectors, column splices, base plates, anchor rods, and foundations all participate in the upward path. A member adequate for downward gravity load may have insufficient unbraced length or connection capacity when force reverses, so both directions must be modeled and detailed continuously.
