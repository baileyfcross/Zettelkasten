# Fuel-Air Mixture Control

Mixture control adjusts fuel flow so the fuel-air ratio remains suitable as air density and engine demand change. Climbing reduces the number of air molecules in a fixed volume, so a mixture set for lower altitude becomes progressively richer and may need leaning. The best operational setting balances power, fuel economy, temperature, and the combustion relationships in [[Stoichiometric Aviation Fuel Combustion]].
