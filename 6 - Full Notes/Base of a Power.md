# Base of a Power

The base of a power is the quantity repeatedly multiplied in an exponentiation. In (a^n), (a) is the base and (n) is the [[Exponent]].

The base can be a number, variable, product, or grouped expression. Its boundaries are determined by notation: in ((-2)^4), the base is (-2), while in (-2^4), the base is (2) and the negative sign is applied afterward. When the base is zero, zero and negative exponents require special care because reciprocal interpretations would involve division by zero.
