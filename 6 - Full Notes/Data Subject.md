# Data Subject

A data subject is the person to whom [[Personal Data]] relates. The concept ties information-processing duties to an individual even when the dataset uses an indirect identifier rather than a name.

In large combined datasets, identifying the subject can be uncertain because records may be linked or reattributed later. Rights and safeguards therefore depend on realistic possibilities of identification, not only on the label attached to a field.
