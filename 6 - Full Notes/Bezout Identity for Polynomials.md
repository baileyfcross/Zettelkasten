# Bezout Identity for Polynomials

If polynomials f₁,…,fₙ over a [[Field]] are coprime, there are polynomials g₁,…,gₙ such that f₁g₁ + ··· + fₙgₙ = 1. Their generated ideal is therefore the whole polynomial ring.

The identity follows from the fact that every one-variable polynomial ideal is principal. It certifies [[Coprime Polynomials]] and supports the common-factor interpretation of a [[Polynomial Resultant]].
