# Arbitrarily Oriented Subspace Clustering

Arbitrarily oriented subspace clustering finds groups near lines, planes, or other low-dimensional manifolds that need not align with original axes. Correlations among attributes define the relevant directions.

Covariance, local linear models, or projected residuals can reveal this structure. The added flexibility increases estimation cost and makes solutions harder to summarize because relevance is expressed through combinations of features.
