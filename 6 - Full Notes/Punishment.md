# Punishment

A punishment is a consequence that signals an action or situation the game discourages. It can increase resistance, remove a resource, give an opponent an advantage, or send the player backward through the experience.

Punishment differs from a rule that merely blocks an action. It changes the player's situation after failure or error, and its design determines how much time, progress, and opportunity the player must lose before trying again.
