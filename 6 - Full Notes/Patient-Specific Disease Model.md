# Patient-Specific Disease Model

A patient-specific disease model uses donor-derived cells to reproduce biological characteristics of one person's condition in the laboratory. Induced pluripotent stem cells can supply otherwise inaccessible tissue types while retaining relevant genetic variation.

The model can compare therapies before treatment, although it cannot capture every influence of the patient's complete body, history, or environment.
