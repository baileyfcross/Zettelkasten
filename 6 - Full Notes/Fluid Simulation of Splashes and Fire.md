# Fluid Simulation of Splashes and Fire

Fluid dynamics handles interacting material that cannot be convincingly represented by a simple cloth or isolated particle path. The book moves a sphere through a simulated liquid container to generate wake, foam, splash, and ripples, then describes converting the solved motion to a mesh for surfacing. A voxel-based gas example changes fuel and temperature controls to move from smoke toward ignition and fire. Such effects require specialized interaction calculations beyond the basic forces in [[Particle Motion Forces and Lifetime]].
