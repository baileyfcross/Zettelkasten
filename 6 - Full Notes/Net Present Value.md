# Net Present Value

Net present value sums future cash inflows and outflows after discounting them to a common present time. A positive value indicates that expected discounted benefits exceed costs under the chosen rate.

For interdependent subprojects, changing the running order changes the dates of costs and benefits and can therefore change the portfolio's value.

NPV can compare a stream of recurring cloud-service payments with the up-front cost of owned infrastructure by expressing both at the same present time. It can also convert a capital amount into an equivalent recurring stream. A positive NPV indicates that discounted benefits exceed the initial and continuing costs under the chosen discount rate.
