# Cast Shadow Consistency

Cast shadow consistency tests whether the directions and shapes of shadows are compatible with a shared arrangement of objects and light sources. In a perspective image, lines connecting an object's points to corresponding points on its shadow constrain the projected light position.

Multiple objects supply independent estimates that should agree when one dominant source illuminates the scene. Uneven terrain, area lights, multiple lamps, and uncertain point correspondences can broaden the valid solution, so disagreement is meaningful only after those conditions are considered.
