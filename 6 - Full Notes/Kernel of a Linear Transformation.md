# Kernel of a Linear Transformation

The kernel of $T:V\to W$ is $\ker T=\{v\in V:T(v)=0\}$. It is a [[Vector Subspace]] of the domain and contains exactly the directions that the transformation collapses to zero.

A linear transformation is injective precisely when its kernel contains only the zero vector. The kernel's dimension is the transformation's nullity and participates in the [[Rank-Nullity Theorem]].
