# Biofabrication Supply Chain

A biofabrication supply chain moves cell banks, media ingredients, scaffolds, equipment, living intermediates, and finished products among specialized facilities. Temperature, sterility, time, provenance, and biological viability impose constraints beyond ordinary manufactured goods.

Distributed production can shorten final transport but requires standardized inputs, skilled operators, monitoring, and reliable cold or controlled-environment logistics.
