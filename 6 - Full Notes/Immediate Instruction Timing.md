# Immediate Instruction Timing

Immediate instruction timing presents information at the moment a player first needs to apply it. A control explanation attached to the relevant obstacle is easier to understand and remember than a complete control diagram shown long before the actions become meaningful.

Timing can also connect explanation to safe practice. Requiring a newly learned action to leave a low-risk room makes recall part of the interaction immediately before the action is needed under pressure. The instruction therefore becomes situated knowledge rather than an isolated fact.
