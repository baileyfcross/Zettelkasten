# AI Adoption Maturity Model

The AI adoption maturity model describes a progression from informal use to enterprise optimization. Early experimentation establishes capability; defined guardrails constrain it; enforced approval controls consequential work; auditing and compliance make participation provable; and optimized adoption uses evidence to refine cost, quality, and scale.

Maturity is not the number of AI tools deployed. It is the degree to which use is bounded, reviewed, traceable, and connected to organizational outcomes. Teams should expand only after the previous level's controls are effective, rather than treating broad access as proof of readiness.
