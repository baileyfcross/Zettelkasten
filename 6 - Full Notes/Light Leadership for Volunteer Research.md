# Light Leadership for Volunteer Research

Light leadership for volunteer research combines open participation with enough coordination to turn discussion into decisions. Leaders articulate goals, maintain timelines, integrate opinions, and recommend action without relying on authority to compel unpaid contributors.

Participants should be able to influence project design without each carrying the full burden of governance. Public documentation, milestones, and frequent communication make expectations visible and allow asynchronous work. As a collaboration grows, dedicated administration may be necessary even when the scientific modules remain volunteer-driven.
