# INVEST Criteria

INVEST is a quality check for user stories: Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Sized appropriately, and Testable. The criteria reveal stories that are overly coupled, prescriptive, unclear in value, too uncertain, too large, or impossible to verify.

They are diagnostic rather than rigid rules. A story can temporarily violate a criterion while the team uses refinement, a [[Spike]], or conversation to improve it.
