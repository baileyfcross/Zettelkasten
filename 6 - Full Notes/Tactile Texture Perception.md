# Tactile Texture Perception

Tactile texture perception organizes surface feel along dimensions that include rough–smooth, hard–soft, and sticky–slippery. Coarse roughness is strongly related to the spacing of surface elements, while very fine texture is conveyed through high-frequency vibrations generated as a finger moves across the surface.

Tangential force is especially important for stickiness and slipperiness. The result also depends on scanning speed and contact force, so texture is not a fixed sensation produced by geometry alone. [[Active Touch]] selects movement parameters that reveal the surface at a useful scale.
