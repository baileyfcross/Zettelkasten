# AWS Trusted Advisor

AWS Trusted Advisor checks account resources and usage against AWS recommendations in areas including cost optimization, performance, security, fault tolerance, and service limits. Each check identifies conditions worth reviewing and suggests a related improvement.

The findings are advisory snapshots, not a replacement for workload-specific architecture review. Available checks and refresh behavior depend on support arrangements, and teams should assess operational risk before applying changes that affect production resources.
