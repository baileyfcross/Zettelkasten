# Matrix Diagonalization

Matrix diagonalization factors a diagonalizable matrix as $A=QDQ^{-1}$, where the columns of $Q$ are eigenvectors and $D$ contains their eigenvalues.

The representation simplifies powers, difference equations, and systems of differential equations because $D^k$ and functions of $D$ act entry by entry. A real symmetric matrix permits [[Orthogonal Diagonalization]].

A matrix is diagonalizable exactly when its minimal polynomial splits into distinct linear factors. Equivalently, the dimensions of its eigenspaces add to the matrix size, providing enough independent eigenvectors for the columns of $Q$.
