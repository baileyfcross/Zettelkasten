# Stochastic Neural Network Hardware

Stochastic neural network hardware represents values as probabilistic bit streams and performs approximate arithmetic with compact logic. Multiplication and accumulation can use fewer conventional arithmetic resources, improving area or energy efficiency.

Shorter streams increase variance and may lower accuracy. Hybrid designs keep some operations binary and retrain or rescale the network to compensate, making the representation a joint algorithm-and-hardware decision.
