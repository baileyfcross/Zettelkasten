# AI Electronic Waste

AI electronic waste is discarded hardware produced as intelligent systems depend on and accelerate replacement of devices, sensors, servers, and other computing equipment. The environmental burden includes raw-material extraction, manufacturing energy, toxic substances, transport, and disposal.

Waste is easy to omit when AI is described only as an algorithm or online service. A sociotechnical view follows the material infrastructure and asks where obsolete equipment goes and who handles it. [[Sustainable AI]] therefore includes durability, maintenance, reuse, and production impacts as well as efficient model operation.
