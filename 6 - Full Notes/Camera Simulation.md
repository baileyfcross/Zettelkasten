# Camera Simulation

Camera simulation applies characteristics of the real image-formation pipeline to virtual imagery before compositing. Lens distortion, depth-dependent blur, sensor noise, vignetting, chromatic aberration, mosaic artifacts, and tone mapping can all make camera pixels differ from ideal rendering.

Matching selected effects prevents virtual content from appearing unnaturally clean or geometrically perfect beside the captured scene. The simulated parameters must correspond to the actual camera and its current settings.
