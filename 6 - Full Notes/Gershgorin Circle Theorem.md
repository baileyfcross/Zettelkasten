# Gershgorin Circle Theorem

The Gershgorin circle theorem places every eigenvalue of a square matrix inside at least one disk centered at a diagonal entry. The disk radius is the sum of the absolute off-diagonal entries in that row.

These disks bound where eigenvalues can occur before an iterative calculation. Disjoint groups of disks can also reveal how many eigenvalues lie in separated regions.
