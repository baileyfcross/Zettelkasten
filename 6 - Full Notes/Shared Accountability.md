# Shared Accountability

Shared accountability means a team owns its collective result rather than treating each specialty as responsible only for local tasks. Members surface impediments, help where they can, and judge progress by integrated value.

Individual expertise and responsibility still matter, but they operate inside a common goal. This reduces the tendency to declare one component done while the product remains unusable.
