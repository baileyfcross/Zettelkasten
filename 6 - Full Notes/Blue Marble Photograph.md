# Blue Marble Photograph

The Blue Marble photograph is the fully illuminated view of Earth made by the Apollo 17 crew after launch in December 1972, with Africa and Arabia prominently visible. Also called Full Earth, it became one of the most widely reproduced images of the planet.

Unlike a map divided by political borders, the image presents one atmospheric and oceanic world suspended in space. It became a flexible symbol for environmentalism, global unity, and the uniqueness of Earth. Its influence should not be mistaken for the sole origin of those movements, but its ubiquity shows how human spaceflight supplied a new planetary icon.
