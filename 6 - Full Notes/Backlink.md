# Backlink

A backlink is an incoming hyperlink pointing to a web page; by extension, the source page containing that link can also be called a backlink. In graph terms, it is an incoming edge and the vertex from which that edge originates.

[[PageRank]] derives a page's importance from its backlinks. A backlink contributes a share of its source page's rank, divided across all pages to which that source links.
