# Publisher Data Availability Policy

A publisher data availability policy states what supporting data, code, protocols, and materials authors must disclose or deposit. Policies can range from an implied willingness to share, through a published availability statement, to public archiving as a condition of publication.

The strongest policy defines acceptable repositories and persistent links, explains consequences for noncompliance, and checks evidence rather than relying only on a promise. Journals can act as a final enforcement point because publication is valuable to authors, but policy language alone does not ensure compliance. A [[Reproducibility Review]] can test whether submitted materials actually work.
