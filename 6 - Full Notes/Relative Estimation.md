# Relative Estimation

Relative estimation compares the size and complexity of one backlog item with known examples instead of predicting an exact duration. Teams use analogy and triangulation to place items on a shared scale.

Comparison is often more reliable than absolute forecasting under uncertainty. The estimate supports planning, while actual throughput remains the evidence used to revise future forecasts.
