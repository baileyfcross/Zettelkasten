# Tokamak Fusion Reactor

A tokamak is a toroidal reactor arrangement for [[Magnetic Fusion Confinement]]. Magnets guide the charged plasma inside an evacuated chamber, while escaping fusion neutrons transfer energy to surrounding structures and a lithium blanket can breed fuel. The source's diagram also shows heat removal, helium handling, and tritium separation. The magnetic chamber is only one part of a potential electricity-generating plant.
