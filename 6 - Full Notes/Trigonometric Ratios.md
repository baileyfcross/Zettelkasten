# Trigonometric Ratios

For an acute angle in a right triangle, sine is opposite over hypotenuse, cosine is adjacent over hypotenuse, and tangent is opposite over adjacent. Their reciprocal ratios are cosecant, secant, and cotangent. Similarity makes these ratios depend on the angle rather than the triangle's size, allowing angle and side measurements to determine one another.
