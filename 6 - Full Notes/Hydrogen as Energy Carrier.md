# Hydrogen as Energy Carrier

Hydrogen is a secondary fuel produced using energy from another source, not an energy source obtained ready-made for use. Electricity, fossil-fuel processing, or sunlight can make it; it can then be stored, moved, burned, or supplied to a [[Fuel Cell]]. The source emphasizes high energy per unit mass but difficult bulk storage, distribution cost, and special safety requirements. Its production route determines the whole-system energy balance.
