# GPS Satellite Atomic Clock

A GPS satellite atomic clock supplies the stable time reference from which a receiver infers signal travel time. Putting accurate frequency standards aboard each satellite removed the need to synchronize every transmission continuously from the ground, the key architectural insight tested by the Navy's Timation program. Ground monitoring still measures clock behavior and uploads corrections because even atomic standards have offsets and drift that matter at navigation precision.
