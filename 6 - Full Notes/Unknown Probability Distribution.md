# Unknown Probability Distribution

An unknown probability distribution marks a place where the model has not specified how plausibility is allocated across a variable's values. If that variable is always known evidence in every intended question, its prior factor may cancel during normalization and need not be supplied.

Otherwise the model must represent the available knowledge with an explicit form, such as a justified uniform distribution or a parameterized family. Calling the distribution unknown does not by itself define an inference.
