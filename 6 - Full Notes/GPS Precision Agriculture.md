# GPS Precision Agriculture

GPS precision agriculture uses repeatable position to coordinate field operations with spatial data. Corrected receivers can guide tractors along closely spaced passes, reduce overlap, and associate soil, yield, fertilizer, or pesticide information with particular parts of a field. The value comes from returning machinery and measurements to the same coordinate framework, so improved relative accuracy and dependable availability often matter more than a consumer receiver's standalone location display.
