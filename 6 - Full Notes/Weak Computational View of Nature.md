# Weak Computational View of Nature

The weak computational view holds that computational models are useful tools for studying the world without claiming that the world itself is a computer. A phenomenon may behave as if it were an information process for the purposes of explanation and prediction.

This position evaluates computational interpretations by their evidence and usefulness. It preserves a distinction between a model and the reality the model represents.
