# Clustering Solution Space

A clustering solution space contains candidate partitions produced by varying algorithms, parameters, initializations, features, or constraints. Exploring that space treats clustering as selection among defensible organizations rather than generation of one unquestioned answer.

Distances or transitions among solutions help an analyst locate stable regions and meaningful alternatives. The display must summarize a very large space without implying that nearby solutions are equally useful for the application.
