# Dominance Method in Matrix Games

The dominance method removes a strategy that can never outperform another. For the maximizing row player, a row dominated componentwise by another row is discarded; for the minimizing column player, a column with componentwise larger payoffs than another is discarded.

A strategy may also be dominated by an average of other strategies. Repeated elimination reduces the payoff matrix, often to a form solvable by mixed-strategy formulas or the [[Graphical Solution of Matrix Games]].

For game design, a dominated option consumes implementation and content effort even though a rational player should never select it. Dominance can be used deliberately to steer behavior, but central decisions are usually more interesting when every option has some strategic circumstance in which it is chosen.
