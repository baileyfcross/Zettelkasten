# Player Time Investment Profile

A player time investment profile distinguishes desired session time from desired total time. Session time is the duration of a self-contained play period that feels satisfying to leave; total time is the duration of a complete experience, whether completion is authored or defined by the player through goals such as maximum level or all achievements.

The two dimensions can vary independently. A game may offer short sessions indefinitely, or long sessions within a finite campaign. Naming both early helps designers choose pacing, save structure, content scope, and suitable playtesters for the audience they intend to serve.
