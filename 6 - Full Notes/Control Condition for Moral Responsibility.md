# Control Condition for Moral Responsibility

The control condition for moral responsibility holds that a person can be responsible only when the relevant action or outcome was sufficiently under that person's control. AI complicates this condition because models adapt, systems interact, and automated decisions may occur faster than a human can intervene.

Control need not mean selecting every output personally. Organizations can exercise control through design choices, testing, access limits, monitoring, override procedures, and decisions about whether a system should be deployed. Designing these mechanisms is one way to prevent a [[Responsibility Gap]] rather than using autonomy as an excuse after harm occurs.
