# PROCLUS Clustering

PROCLUS is a projected clustering method that selects representative medoids and estimates a relevant dimension set for each resulting cluster. Distances are computed in the cluster-specific subspaces during assignment and refinement.

The method avoids exhaustive enumeration of all subspaces and returns a compact partition with interpretable dimensions. Its result depends on the requested numbers of clusters and average dimensions, as well as the quality of initial medoids.
