# Vehicular Resource Sharing

Vehicular resource sharing lets owners contribute otherwise idle processor, storage, network, or sensing capacity to a pooled service. Compensation may include parking, charging, payment, or access to other services.

A workable mechanism must measure contribution, enforce limits, protect owner data, account for wear and energy, and remain safe for the vehicle's primary functions.
