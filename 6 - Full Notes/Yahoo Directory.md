# Yahoo Directory

Yahoo began as a manually organized guide to Web sites created by Jerry Yang and David Filo. Categories gave users a human-curated route through an otherwise flat and rapidly expanding information space.

Advertising funded its evolution into a broader portal. Manual classification became difficult to sustain as the Web’s size accelerated.
