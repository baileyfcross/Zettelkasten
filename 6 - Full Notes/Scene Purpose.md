# Scene Purpose

Scene purpose is the specific idea, rule, relationship, or feeling that a [[Game Scene]] is constructed to develop. A designer should be able to state what the scene contributes and how established game vocabulary produces that contribution.

Purpose keeps a scene from becoming unrelated spectacle or clutter. Tension, timing, discovery, or a strategic insight should arise from [[Game Rule|rules]] and objects the player can understand, so the experience continues the game's existing conversation.
