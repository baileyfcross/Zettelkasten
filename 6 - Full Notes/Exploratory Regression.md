# Exploratory Regression

Exploratory regression searches for a useful predictive or descriptive model without treating one prespecified exposure–outcome hypothesis as the sole target. Candidate variables may be compared for their contribution to fit or prediction.

Exploration is legitimate when it is labeled and validated as such. Presenting a model discovered through extensive searching as if it tested an a priori hypothesis understates [[Researcher Degrees of Freedom]] and inflates confidence in chance findings.
