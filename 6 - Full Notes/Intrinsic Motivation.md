# Intrinsic Motivation

Intrinsic motivation comes from the value and satisfaction of the activity itself. In creative work it is supported by autonomy over meaningful choices, growing mastery of a craft, and a clear purpose connecting effort to a larger result.

External rewards and controls can support necessary boundaries, but excessive task assignment and surveillance weaken ownership. Self-managing teams give motivation a structure in which it can become coordinated value.
