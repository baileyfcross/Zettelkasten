# Induced Pluripotent Stem Cell

An induced pluripotent stem cell is a mature donor cell reprogrammed into a state capable of generating many specialized cell types. It allows researchers to derive tissue-relevant cells while retaining the donor's genetic background.

Producing, maintaining, and directing these cells is expensive and technically demanding, and incomplete differentiation can complicate a fabricated tissue.
