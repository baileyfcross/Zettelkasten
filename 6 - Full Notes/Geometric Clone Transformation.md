# Geometric Clone Transformation

A geometric clone transformation relates the source and pasted regions of a copy-move operation. Simple duplication produces a translation, while an editor may also rotate or scale the copied material to fit a new location.

Matched feature pairs vote for transformation parameters, and a dense cluster of consistent votes identifies a candidate clone. Estimating the transformation also lets the analyst align the regions for pixel-level verification and distinguish one deliberate copy from unrelated repeated patterns.
