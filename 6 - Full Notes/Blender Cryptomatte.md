# Blender Cryptomatte

Cryptomatte produces masks for objects, materials, or parented asset groups without requiring manual pass-index assignment. It also handles motion blur, depth of field, and transparency more robustly than simple object or material indices.

In the Compositor, the artist can pick rendered elements to include or remove from the matte. The resulting mask limits later color changes or effects to selected parts of the image.
