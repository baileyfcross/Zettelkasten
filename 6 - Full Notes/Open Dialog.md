# Open Dialog

Open dialog allows users to express requests without being led through a fixed sequence of narrowly constrained prompts. It supports natural wording and user initiative but creates a much larger interpretation problem.

A broad [[Personal Virtual Assistant]] often begins with an [[Open Prompt]], uses [[Intent Triggering]] to select a domain, and then may shift into more directed task completion.
