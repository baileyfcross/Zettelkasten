# Privacy by Design

Privacy by design makes privacy a requirement throughout the engineering process rather than a feature added after data practices are fixed. It can influence collection limits, default settings, access control, retention, combination, user information, and the ability to erase or correct data.

The approach is especially important for AI because models often depend on large datasets and may infer information beyond what was directly supplied. General commitments must be translated into concrete decisions about purpose and risk. [[Meaningful Digital Consent]] remains important, but privacy-preserving defaults reduce the burden placed on individuals to understand every technical consequence.
