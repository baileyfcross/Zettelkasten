# Aviation Radio-Wave Propagation and Line of Sight

Ground waves follow the surface at low frequencies, sky waves return from the ionosphere with limited reliability, and higher-frequency space waves support navigation and line-of-sight communication. Mountains and buildings can block space waves even though atmospheric refraction extends their range slightly beyond geometric sight. Increasing either antenna's height increases the available radio horizon.
