# Experimental Unit

An experimental unit is the entity to which a condition is applied and from which an outcome measurement is obtained. In the source's diet example, each mouse is an experimental unit and contributes one row to the [[Design Matrix]].

Correct identification matters for independence and sample size. Several measurements from one unit are not automatically several independent units, even when they occupy separate data records.
