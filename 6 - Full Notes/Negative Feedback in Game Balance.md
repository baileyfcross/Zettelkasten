# Negative Feedback in Game Balance

Negative feedback in game balance makes success increase later resistance or makes failure reduce it. It narrows differences among players or keeps challenge near a target level.

Moderate negative feedback can sustain uncertainty and recovery. If it is too strong, leading becomes a disadvantage and outcomes swing artificially, weakening the connection between decisions and results.
