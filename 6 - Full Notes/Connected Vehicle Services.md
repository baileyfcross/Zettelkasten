# Connected Vehicle Services

Connected vehicle services combine onboard sensors and computers with smartphones, positioning, networks, and cloud data. They support navigation, diagnostics, remote locking, media, payments, Wi-Fi access, maintenance alerts, and driving assistance such as adaptive cruise control, lane warnings, and automated braking.

The vehicle becomes part of a wider transportation environment when it exchanges traffic, road, parking, or safety information. This creates benefits beyond the car, including better routing and infrastructure use, but it also makes latency, software updates, privacy, and fallback behavior important because a communications or automation failure can affect physical safety.
