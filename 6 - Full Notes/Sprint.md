# Sprint

A Sprint is a fixed one-to-three-week interval in which a [[Scrum Team]] pursues a [[Sprint Goal]] and produces a usable [[Product Increment]]. Its stable duration creates a regular opportunity to inspect the product and adapt both plans and practices.

A Sprint contains planning, daily coordination, development, review, and retrospective work. It is not merely a deadline for a batch of disconnected tasks.
