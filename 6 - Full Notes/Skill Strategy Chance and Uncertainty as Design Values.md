# Skill Strategy Chance and Uncertainty as Design Values

A design should state the intended balance among practiced skill, forward-looking strategy, randomized events, and uncertainty from hidden or interacting causes. Each produces a different basis for success and a different emotional rhythm.

Making the balance explicit prevents one source from erasing the others. Excessive randomness can make developed skill irrelevant, while complete predictability can eliminate the uncertainty that gives strategic commitment its tension.
