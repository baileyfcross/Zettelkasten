# Hyperbolic Network Embedding

A hyperbolic network embedding assigns coordinates in [[Hyperbolic Space]] to graph nodes. One approach approximates observed graph distances, while another chooses coordinates that make the observed network likely under a hyperbolic growth model.

Once embedded, coordinate distance can accelerate shortest-path estimates, centrality calculations, missing-link prediction, routing, and visualization. The value of the embedding depends on both its computational cost and its structural distortion.
