# Robert Noyce Planar Integrated Circuit

Robert Noyce proposed an integrated circuit built with the planar silicon process at Fairchild Semiconductor. Deposited conducting paths connected flat components while silicon oxide isolated them.

The design was well suited to photographic patterning and mass production. Patent decisions ultimately divided credit for the integrated circuit between Noyce and Jack Kilby.
