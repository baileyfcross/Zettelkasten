# Biohybrid Robot Exercise Adaptation

Biohybrid robot exercise adaptation is the increase in engineered muscle performance after repeated stimulation. Training signals impose recurring contraction and loading, prompting living tissue to reorganize and generate greater force.

The response distinguishes a biological actuator from a fixed synthetic one, while also making prior use, recovery, fatigue, and culture conditions part of device calibration.
