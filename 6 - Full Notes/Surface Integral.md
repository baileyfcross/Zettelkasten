# Surface Integral

A surface integral accumulates a scalar or differential form over an oriented smooth surface. A [[Surface Parameterization]] converts it to a double integral over the parameter domain, with a surface-area factor accounting for local stretching.

Orientation matters for signed two-form and [[Flux Integral|flux]] integrals: reversing the surface orientation reverses their sign. When only scalar surface area is accumulated, the area element is nonnegative.
