# Cloud Compliance

Cloud compliance is adherence to the laws, industry regulations, policies, and contractual standards that apply to a cloud service and its data. General services may face broad rules, while specialist services can inherit additional industry and local requirements.

Every interoperating component within a regulated process must meet the applicable obligations. Provider location, hosting location, users, data subjects, and service purpose can all affect the compliance scope.
