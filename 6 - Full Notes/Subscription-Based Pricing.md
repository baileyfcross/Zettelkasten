# Subscription-Based Pricing

Subscription-based pricing gives access to a service for a recurring charge, usually independent of how fully the consumer uses the allocated capability. The agreement may include a minimum term or a notice period before cancellation.

The model makes spending predictable but can leave paid capacity unused. It can be applied at any service abstraction or deployment model when the access period is a more useful unit than individual resource consumption.
