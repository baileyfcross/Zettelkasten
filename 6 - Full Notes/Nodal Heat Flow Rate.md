# Nodal Heat Flow Rate

A nodal heat flow rate is the thermal quantity analogous to nodal force in a structural element. Its sign records heat entering or leaving the element according to the adopted convention.

Element heat flows assemble at shared nodes, enforcing conservation across the mesh. Boundary convection, prescribed flux, and internal generation add contributions to the global thermal load vector.
