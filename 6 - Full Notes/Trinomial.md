# Trinomial

A trinomial is a [[Polynomial]] with three terms. A common one-variable form is

$$ax^2+bx+c,$$

called a quadratic trinomial when (a\neq0). A trinomial may be the expansion of two [[Binomial]] factors, but not every trinomial factors over integers.

Recognizing coefficients and signs supports [[Quadratic Trinomial Factorization]]. A perfect square trinomial has the special structure (a^2\pm2ab+b^2), while a general quadratic may require completing the square or the quadratic formula.
