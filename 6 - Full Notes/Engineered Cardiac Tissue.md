# Engineered Cardiac Tissue

Engineered cardiac tissue organizes cardiomyocytes into a construct that contracts with coordinated electrical activity. Cell alignment, gap-junction coupling, matrix compliance, nutrient transport, and electrical conditioning influence force and synchrony.

Thicker tissue requires perfusion or vascular growth, while clinical use requires the construct to couple safely with the host heart.
