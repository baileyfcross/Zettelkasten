# Eulerian Circuit

An Eulerian circuit is an [[Eulerian Path]] that begins and ends at the same vertex. It is also called an Eulerian tour.

Because the route returns to its start and traverses every edge exactly once, each visited vertex must support paired arrivals and departures. [[Hierholzer's Algorithm]] constructs such a circuit by forming tours from unused edges and splicing them together.
