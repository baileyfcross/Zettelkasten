# Quotient Ring

Given an [[Ideal]] I in a ring R, the quotient ring R/I consists of additive cosets of I. Two elements represent the same coset when their difference lies in I, so every element of I becomes zero in the quotient.

The natural map R → R/I is a surjective [[Ring Homomorphism]] with kernel I. Quotients translate ideal properties into ring properties: prime ideals yield [[Integral Domain]] quotients and maximal ideals yield [[Field]] quotients.
