# Blender 3D Cursor

The 3D cursor is a movable reference point within Blender's scene. New objects appear at its location, and it can serve as the center for rotation or scaling.

The Snap menu connects the cursor to exact placement. Objects can snap to the cursor, grid, or world origin, while the cursor can snap to selections, the grid, or the active object. This makes the cursor a bridge between free spatial work and deliberate coordinates.
