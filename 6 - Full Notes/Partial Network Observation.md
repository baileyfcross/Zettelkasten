# Partial Network Observation

Partial network observation occurs when only a fraction of nodes report their state or transmission history. Complete snapshots are often impractical in networks containing millions of accounts.

Source detection must use distances and states from the available observers without assuming unobserved nodes are inactive. Observer placement and network heterogeneity determine how well candidate origins can be distinguished.
