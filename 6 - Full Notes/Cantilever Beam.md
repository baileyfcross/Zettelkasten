# Cantilever Beam

A cantilever beam is fixed at one end and free at the other. The fixed support prescribes both transverse displacement and slope, while the free end can translate and rotate.

Finite element recovery yields the fixed-end reaction force and moment after solving the remaining degrees of freedom. Concentrated and distributed loads produce distinct deflection and internal-force patterns.
