# Segmenting Moving Roto Shapes

An articulated subject is easier to isolate with several shapes for moving parts than with one large spline around the whole body. Each segment can follow its own movement, and overlapping shapes can be combined into a single matte. Fewer control vertices make each shape easier to maintain from frame to frame. This paper-puppet approach appears throughout the book, including its advice for motion colorization. It complements [[Sparse Rotospline Keyframing]], which governs when those shapes should be revised.
