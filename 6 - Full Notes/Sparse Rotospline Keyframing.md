# Sparse Rotospline Keyframing

Rotoscopy does not need a new hand-shaped outline on every frame. Place keyframes at important motion changes or extrema, let the spline interpolate between them, and inspect the resulting edge. Whole-shape translation or rotation can handle motion that does not require reshaping. Too many independently adjusted keyframes may make the contour chatter, while too few may let it drift away from the subject. The method works best after the subject is divided into manageable pieces as in [[Segmenting Moving Roto Shapes]].
