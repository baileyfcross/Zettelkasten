# Hermitian Matrix

A complex square matrix is Hermitian when it equals its conjugate transpose. This generalizes a real [[Symmetric Matrix]].

Hermitian matrices have real eigenvalues and orthogonal eigenvectors associated with distinct eigenvalues. Their quadratic forms and spectral properties make them central to complex linear algebra.
