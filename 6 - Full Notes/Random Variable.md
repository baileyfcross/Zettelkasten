# Random Variable

A random variable assigns a numerical value to the outcome of a random process. Its realized value changes across repeated samples or experiments even when the underlying population and procedure remain fixed.

The source treats sample means, mean differences, test statistics, and p-values as random variables. Recognizing this variability is essential because an observed estimate is one realization from a [[Sampling Distribution]], not the population quantity itself.

The student companion introduces random variables through computational experiments whose outcomes are numeric counts or measurements. R distribution functions can evaluate probabilities or generate realizations, keeping the theoretical model distinct from the particular simulated values observed in one run.
