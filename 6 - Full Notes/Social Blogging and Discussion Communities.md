# Social Blogging and Discussion Communities

Blogging and discussion communities make authored posts or threaded conversation the main social object. Blogging services emphasize publication and following, while forums organize replies around questions, topics, or communities. A single platform can participate in both patterns, but its interface and moderation tools usually reveal which interaction it prioritizes.

The source lists Tumblr, Medium, Pinterest, Instagram, and Facebook among writing or blogging environments, and Reddit, Quora, Facebook, and LinkedIn among discussion sites. Enterprise integration may consume or publish content, but it must also preserve thread context, author identity, and moderation state. [[W3C Social Web Protocols]] attempts to standardize some of these create, subscribe, and interaction operations across services.
