# Filtered Ring

A filtered ring has a descending sequence R = R₀ ⊇ R₁ ⊇ R₂ ⊇ ··· such that RₘRₙ is contained in Rₘ₊ₙ. The index records increasing order or depth in the filtration.

Passing to successive quotients Rₙ/Rₙ₊₁ produces the [[Associated Graded Ring]]. Powers of an ideal give the important [[I-Adic Filtration]].
