# Fourier Heat Conduction Law

Fourier's law relates conductive heat rate to thermal conductivity, area normal to the flow, and the temperature gradient. In one dimension, the negative sign in $q=-kA\,dT/dx$ records that heat flows toward decreasing temperature.

Thermal conductivity is the material proportionality factor, while geometry and the temperature field supply the remaining terms. The law becomes part of the [[Energy Conservation in Thermal Continua|energy equation]] when conductive inflow and outflow are balanced against generation and storage.
