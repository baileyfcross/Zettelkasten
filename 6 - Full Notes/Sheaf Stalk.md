# Sheaf Stalk

The stalk of a [[Sheaf]] at a point collects sections defined on neighborhoods of that point, identifying two sections when they agree on some smaller neighborhood. It records only the local behavior near the point.

For the [[Structure Sheaf]] of Spec(A), the stalk at a prime p is the local ring A_p. Stalks therefore connect sheaf-theoretic locality with [[Localization at a Prime Ideal]].
