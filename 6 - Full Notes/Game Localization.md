# Game Localization

Game localization adapts content for sale in another country or cultural market. It includes language and idiom as well as symbols, humor, laws, ratings, controls, payment expectations, and sensitivities about violence, sexuality, religion, or politics.

Literal translation is therefore insufficient. A localized game must remain understandable and acceptable without erasing the experience or introducing conflicts between altered presentation and unchanged mechanics.
