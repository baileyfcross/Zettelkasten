# Document-Level Sentiment Classification

Document-level sentiment classification assigns one polarity to an entire review, post, or message. It is most defensible when the document discusses a single entity and its overall orientation is the intended target.

The approach becomes coarse when one document evaluates several entities or mixes praise and criticism. In those cases, [[Aspect-Level Sentiment Analysis]] can preserve which opinion belongs to which feature instead of collapsing all evidence into one label.
