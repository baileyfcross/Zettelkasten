# AI Capacity Forecast

An AI capacity forecast uses recent time-series data to describe whether device utilization appears to be approaching a limit over a stated horizon. It can identify recurring peaks, list devices needing attention, and explain the assumptions behind an expected trend.

The forecast should not present a language model's narrative as a calibrated statistical prediction. Thresholds, horizon, and source window must remain visible, and conventional forecasting or additional history may be needed for quantitative commitments. Capacity decisions require confirmation with live data and known demand changes.
