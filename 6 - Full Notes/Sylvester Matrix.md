# Sylvester Matrix

The Sylvester matrix of two positive-degree one-variable polynomials is formed by shifted rows of their coefficients. Its size is the sum of their degrees.

The determinant of this matrix is the [[Polynomial Resultant]]. A zero determinant means the coefficient equations admit a nontrivial relation, which occurs exactly when the two polynomials share a common factor.
