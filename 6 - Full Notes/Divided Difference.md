# Divided Difference

A divided difference is a recursively defined coefficient based on function values at distinct nodes. The first divided difference is $f[x_i,x_{i+1}]=(f(x_{i+1})-f(x_i))/(x_{i+1}-x_i)$.

Higher orders compare lower-order divided differences over wider node intervals. A divided-difference table supplies the coefficients for [[Newton Divided Difference Interpolation]].
