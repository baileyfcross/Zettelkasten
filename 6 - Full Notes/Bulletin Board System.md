# Bulletin Board System

A bulletin board system was a computer that callers reached by modem to exchange messages and files. Many were operated locally by individuals or small groups and could run on an ordinary personal computer with storage.

Long-distance costs encouraged boards to forward material to one another during cheaper hours. Their communities anticipated later forums and social networks.
