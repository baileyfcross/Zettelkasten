# Cophenetic Correlation Coefficient

The cophenetic correlation coefficient is the product-moment correlation between original pairwise proximities and the cophenetic proximities encoded by a dendrogram. A value near one indicates that the hierarchy preserves the pairwise structure well.

Only one triangle of symmetric proximity matrices is needed for the calculation. A high coefficient measures faithful distance representation, not necessarily domain correctness.
