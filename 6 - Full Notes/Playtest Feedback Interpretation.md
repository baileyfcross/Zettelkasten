# Playtest Feedback Interpretation

Playtest feedback is strongest as evidence of a participant's problem and weakest as a ready-made solution. A request to increase attack speed may express an underlying feeling that the game is unresponsive; implementing the request directly could miss that cause.

The facilitator asks why, gathers observations without solving during the session, and later compares patterns across players. Participants are usually credible when they report that something did not work for them, but the design team remains responsible for diagnosis and remedy.
