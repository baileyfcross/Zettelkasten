# Trust in Mobile Commerce

Trust in mobile commerce is a user's willingness to rely on a service when its data practices and future behavior cannot be observed completely. Trust can lower perceived privacy risk and make disclosure more likely.

Trust is not a substitute for governance. A user may trust the visible service while being unaware of advertisers, analytics providers, or brokers that receive the information, so transparent boundaries and enforceable duties remain necessary.
