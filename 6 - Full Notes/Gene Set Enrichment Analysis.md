# Gene Set Enrichment Analysis

Gene set enrichment analysis asks whether members of a predefined biological group tend to occur toward one end of a ranked genomic result. It shifts interpretation from isolated genes to pathways or coordinated processes.

The method can detect a distributed signal even when no individual gene crosses a strict threshold. Its conclusion depends on the ranking statistic, gene-set definitions, overlapping membership, and the background population. Enrichment indicates coordinated association with the phenotype, not that every member changes or that the pathway is causally responsible.
