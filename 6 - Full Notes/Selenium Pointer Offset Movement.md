# Selenium Pointer Offset Movement

Selenium pointer offset movement moves the cursor by x and y pixel values from its current position, from an element, or from a viewport origin. Positive and negative offsets determine horizontal and vertical direction.

Offsets are useful when no distinct target element represents the desired point, but they depend on geometry and viewport state. Element-centered actions are generally more stable when the target can be located semantically.
