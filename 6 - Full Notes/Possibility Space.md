# Possibility Space

A possibility space is the set of actions, choices, strategies, and outcomes that a game's rules make available. Its boundaries come from the [[Game Verb|verbs]], objects, relationships, and constraints designed into the system.

The space may be narrow at one moment and wide at another. Rich possibility does not require unrestricted action; a compact set of strongly interacting rules can create more meaningful variety than a large set of unrelated options.
