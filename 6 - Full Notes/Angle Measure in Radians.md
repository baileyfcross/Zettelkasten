# Angle Measure in Radians

The radian measure of a central angle is the length of its intercepted arc on the [[Unit Circle]]. More generally, an arc of length $s$ on a circle of radius $r$ subtends $\theta=s/r$ radians. A full revolution is $2\pi$ radians, so radians make trigonometric and calculus formulas independent of an arbitrary degree scale.
