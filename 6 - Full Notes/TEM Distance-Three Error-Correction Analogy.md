# TEM Distance-Three Error-Correction Analogy

At or above its threshold bond dimension, TEM represents every contribution linear in the learned local noise rates. The remaining effective noise is therefore quadratic in those rates, replacing the leading error scale $\epsilon$ by a residual scale of order $\epsilon^2$.

This is analogous to a distance-three quantum error-correcting code, which corrects any single physical error while leaving events with two or more simultaneous errors. The resource is different: TEM spends additional measurements and classical contraction rather than redundant physical qubits. The analogy suggests a hybrid path in which TEM mitigates residual errors from early error-corrected processors whose logical encoding cannot yet be recursively enlarged.
