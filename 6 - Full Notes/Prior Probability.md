# Prior Probability

Prior probability represents knowledge about a variable or parameter before the selected evidence is incorporated. It is part of the broader preliminary knowledge encoded by the modeler, not the whole of Bayesian modeling.

The prior combines with a likelihood to produce a posterior. When later data arrives sequentially, the previous posterior can serve as the next prior, preserving the information accumulated so far.
