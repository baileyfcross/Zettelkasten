# Dexterity and Timing Puzzle

A dexterity and timing puzzle makes the required action apparent but difficult to perform with the necessary precision or rhythm. The player solves it by practicing the mapping between perceived cues, planned movement, and control input.

This form often follows an intellectual puzzle: first determine the correct route or response, then execute it. Difficulty should arise from a learnable pattern rather than unreliable controls. Attempts need fast, specific feedback so practice refines performance instead of merely repeating failure.
