# Crowd Replication with Animated Cards

A short running cycle can be keyed onto a card, looped, and copied at different positions, depths, and starting times to build a moving crowd. A virtual camera gives the cards parallax and can add motion blur, but the translation of each card must agree with the runner's gait or the feet slide across the floor. The book contrasts hand-arranged 2.5D card groups with a particle-emitter version that distributes the same sprite and its shadow. See [[Procedural Contact Shadows for Cards]].
