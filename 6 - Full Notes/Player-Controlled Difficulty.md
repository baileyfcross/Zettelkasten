# Player-Controlled Difficulty

Player-controlled difficulty lets players alter the resistance they encounter through choices made inside play. A player might advance into a more dangerous area, retreat to safer space, accept an optional power, or choose when to approach a difficult challenge.

Unlike a fixed [[Difficulty Mode]] selected before the system is understood, these decisions can be grounded in experience. They make pacing part of the player's performance while preserving concrete rules and consequences.
