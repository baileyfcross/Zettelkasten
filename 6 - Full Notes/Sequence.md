# Sequence

A sequence is an ordered list of values whose members are called terms. Each term is associated with a position, usually a [[Natural Number]], so the same value appearing at different positions still counts as a different occurrence.

A [[Law of Formation]] describes the ordering. It may specify a constant additive change, producing an [[Arithmetic Progression]], or a constant multiplicative change, producing a [[Geometric Progression]]. A formula for the (n)th term permits a distant position to be evaluated without constructing every earlier term.
