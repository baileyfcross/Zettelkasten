# Capacity-Aware Task Allocation

Capacity-aware task allocation matches candidate work with team availability, expertise, workload, dependencies, and development goals. Historical performance and current commitments can inform task size and difficulty recommendations.

Automated assignment can encode stale measurements or overload the most experienced people, so managers and assignees should review the suggestion. Fairness, learning opportunities, preferences, and work that is missing from tracking systems are part of the real capacity picture.
