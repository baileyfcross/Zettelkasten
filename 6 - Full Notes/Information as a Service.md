# Information as a Service

Information as a service delivers data that has been transformed or selected so that it is meaningful to a consumer's situation. Raw storage is not sufficient; software must add context such as current location, applicable rules, relevant prices, or a timely interpretation.

An information service builds on lower cloud layers and exposes defined interfaces to other applications. Its quality depends on currency, accuracy, relevance, privacy, and the consumer's ability to understand how the supplied information should be used.
