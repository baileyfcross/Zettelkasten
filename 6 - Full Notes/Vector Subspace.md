# Vector Subspace

A vector subspace is a nonempty subset of a [[Vector Space]] that remains a vector space under the inherited operations.

It is enough to check closure under [[Vector Addition]] and [[Scalar Multiplication of a Vector]]. Null spaces, column spaces, and [[Eigenspace|eigenspaces]] are important examples.
