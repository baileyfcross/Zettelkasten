# Concept Stage

The concept stage is the first major stage of game design. It turns an idea into a stable outline of the player, platform, genre, business context, and central experience.

Its result should guide later work without specifying every detail. Decisions that define the identity and feasibility of the project are established here so the [[Elaboration Stage]] can explore how to realize them.
