# Bootstrap Aggregating

Bootstrap aggregating, or bagging, trains base classifiers on samples drawn with replacement from the original training set and combines their outputs by voting or averaging. Resampling makes otherwise identical learners see different evidence.

Bagging is most effective for unstable algorithms whose fitted boundaries change substantially under small data perturbations. Averaging reduces that variance when the resulting errors are not perfectly correlated.
