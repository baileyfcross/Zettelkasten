# Computer Operator

A computer operator manages the physical and procedural flow of jobs through a shared installation. Duties historically included mounting tapes, feeding card decks, handling printers, and responding to machine conditions.

Operators controlled access to expensive mainframes while programmers and end users remained outside the computer room. Interactive systems later automated or redistributed much of this intermediary work.
