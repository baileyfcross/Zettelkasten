# Inspection Rework and Follow-Up

Inspection rework is the author's correction of the significant defects recorded during an inspection. Follow-up is the moderator's verification that each correction addresses the finding and that the resulting change does not demand a new inspection.

Extensive rework changes the artifact enough that the original inspection evidence may no longer apply, so another inspection can be required. A smaller change can be verified with a walkthrough, but every recorded major defect still needs a confirmed disposition.
