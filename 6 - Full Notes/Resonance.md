# Resonance

Resonance is the large steady response of a driven oscillator when the driving frequency is near its natural frequency. Energy is then transferred efficiently on successive cycles. Weak damping produces a tall, narrow resonance peak, while stronger damping reduces the maximum amplitude and spreads the response across a broader frequency range.
