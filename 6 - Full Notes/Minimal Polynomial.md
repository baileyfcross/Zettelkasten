# Minimal Polynomial

The minimal polynomial of an algebraic element c over K is the unique monic irreducible polynomial in K[x] having c as a root. Every polynomial over K that vanishes at c is divisible by it.

Its degree equals the degree of the simple extension K(c) over K.
