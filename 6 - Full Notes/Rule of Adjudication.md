# Rule of Adjudication

A rule of adjudication identifies who may decide whether a legal rule has been violated and how that decision is made. It supplies procedures and authority for resolving disputes about primary obligations.

The rule is secondary because it organizes application and enforcement. Effective privacy protection requires more than a written duty; it also requires an institution able to determine violations and provide a remedy.
