# Cyber Risk Formula

The source expresses cyber risk conceptually as threat multiplied by vulnerability multiplied by impact. The relationship emphasizes that severe risk requires a plausible harmful force, an exploitable weakness, and meaningful consequences.

The formula is a reasoning aid rather than a promise of exact arithmetic. It structures [[Cybersecurity Risk Assessment]] by showing that risk can be reduced through threat deterrence, vulnerability remediation, or controls that limit impact.
