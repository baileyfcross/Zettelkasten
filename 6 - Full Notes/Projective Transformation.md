# Projective Transformation

A projective transformation of projective $n$-space is induced by an invertible $(n+1)\times(n+1)$ matrix acting on homogeneous coordinate vectors. Scalar-equivalent inputs remain scalar-equivalent after the transformation, so the action is well defined on projective points.

Projective transformations preserve incidence, collinearity, concurrency, tangency, and algebraic curve degree. An affine transformation is the special case that preserves the [[Hyperplane at Infinity]].
