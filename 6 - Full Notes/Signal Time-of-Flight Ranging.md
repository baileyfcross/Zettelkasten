# Signal Time-of-Flight Ranging

Signal time-of-flight ranging estimates distance by multiplying propagation time by the speed of the signal. In GPS, a receiver compares a satellite's coded timestamp with a locally generated replica and interprets their offset as travel time. Because receiver time is not initially synchronized to the satellite clocks, the raw result is a pseudorange containing a common clock-bias term as well as atmospheric and other errors.
