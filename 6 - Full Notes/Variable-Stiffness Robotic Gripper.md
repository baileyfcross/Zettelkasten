# Variable-Stiffness Robotic Gripper

A variable-stiffness robotic gripper changes between a compliant state that adapts to object shape and a rigid state that resists motion. One implementation encloses granular material in an elastic membrane and applies vacuum after contact so the material jams and stiffens around the object.

Separating conformation from load-bearing lets the gripper acquire shapes that a fixed rigid mechanism would need to model in advance. The transition must be controlled without damaging the object, and sensors are needed to verify contact and holding force before stiffness is increased.
