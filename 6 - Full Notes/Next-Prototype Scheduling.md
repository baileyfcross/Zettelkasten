# Next-Prototype Scheduling

Next-prototype scheduling assigns ownership and deadlines to the smallest set of revisions needed for another meaningful playtest. The interval may be hours or months depending on scope, but it ends with a testable artifact rather than an arbitrary volume of completed work.

A schedule grounded in actual team capacity makes the iteration cadence explicit. Revising estimates is evidence for future planning, not a reason to leave commitments undefined.
