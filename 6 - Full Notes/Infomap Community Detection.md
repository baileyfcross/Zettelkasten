# Infomap Community Detection

Infomap detects communities by seeking a compact description of a random walk through a network. Regions in which a walker tends to remain can be encoded as modules, producing groups with dense internal movement and fewer transitions between them.

The method can reveal finer, coherent communities in a dense tag graph where a greedy modularity partition remains coarse. Because it includes randomness, seeds and repeated runs should be used to assess whether the substantive groupings are stable.
