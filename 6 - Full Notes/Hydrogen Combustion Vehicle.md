# Hydrogen Combustion Vehicle

A hydrogen-combustion vehicle burns hydrogen with air in an engine rather than turning it into electricity in a [[Fuel Cell]]. The source describes manifold mixing or direct injection and altered spark timing because hydrogen flames travel rapidly. Water is the fuel's carbon-free combustion product, but hot air combustion can still form nitrogen oxides. On-board gas or hydride storage adds volume or weight constraints.
