# Projection Stereolithography

Projection stereolithography exposes an entire two-dimensional light pattern at once using a digital micromirror device rather than tracing it with a single laser. Demagnified patterns can produce microscale features rapidly in a photosensitive hydrogel.

Layer-wise changes in material and cell type enable complex internal organization, while optical chemistry must remain compatible with living cells.
