# Facial Feature Loop Topology

Facial feature loop topology builds concentric and redirected edge flows around the eyes, mouth, nose, and cheeks so the mesh can represent both form and expression. The mouth needs geometry that follows the lips and continues into an interior cavity, while eyelid loops must close and slide over the eyeball.

Loops from separate features must join without creating abrupt density changes or uncontrolled poles in heavily deforming regions. The rest pose should be inspected under subdivision, then tested with likely blinks, smiles, and mouth openings before surface work proceeds.
