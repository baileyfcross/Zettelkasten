# Poisson Distribution

The Poisson distribution models a nonnegative event count through a rate parameter. Its mean and variance both equal that rate under the basic model, connecting the expected count to its sampling variability.

The source uses it for rare lottery wins, genome palindrome counts, and RNA-sequencing molecule counts. Biological variability can produce more dispersion than the basic model permits, so replication and model checking remain important.
