# Enterprise Social Privacy Exposure

Social applications concentrate profiles, relationships, messages, locations, and media that users may have disclosed in different contexts. Combining those fragments can reveal more than any one field does alone, so privacy risk depends on aggregation and secondary use as well as on whether a single item was public.

The source treats privacy as an architectural concern rather than a settings-page feature. An enterprise integration should request only the data it needs, explain the purpose, control retention, and keep authorization boundaries intact when information moves between systems. Provider access does not remove the application's responsibility for the copied data. [[Social Platform Data Breach Risk]] adds the operational danger of compromise, while [[Jakarta Authorization Least Privilege]] limits routine exposure.
