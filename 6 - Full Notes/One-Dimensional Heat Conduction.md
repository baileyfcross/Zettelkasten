# One-Dimensional Heat Conduction

One-dimensional heat conduction assumes temperature varies primarily along one coordinate. Line elements can represent layered walls or slender regions using conductivity, area, length, sources, and endpoint boundary conditions.

A composite wall is assembled from elements with different conductivities and thicknesses. Continuity at shared nodes produces a common interface temperature and conserved heat-flow rate.
