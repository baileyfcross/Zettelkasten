# Face Mask Nonisothermal Flow Model

The face-mask case models oxygen flow and temperature during inhalation and exhalation through an imported mask geometry. Time-dependent analytic functions represent the changing breathing direction and flow rate over one breath cycle.

Temperature-dependent oxygen properties, laminar-flow assumptions, inlet and outlet changes, and fine tetrahedral meshing couple respiratory flow to heat transfer. Spatial cuts and time histories reveal how the thermal field reverses with the [[Nonisothermal Flow|breathing cycle]].
