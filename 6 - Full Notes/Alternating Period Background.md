# Alternating Period Background

An alternating period background uses contrasting light blocks to separate consecutive years, months, or other intervals behind a [[Time Plot]]. The blocks expose period boundaries without adding another axis or breaking the line into panels. Because they supply context rather than encode the measured value, the colors should remain visually subordinate. This technique is a specific form of [[Layered Time-Plot Annotation]].
