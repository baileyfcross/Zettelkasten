# Opportunity Cost

Opportunity cost is the value forgone when resources are committed to one choice instead of the best available alternative. Capital tied up in computing capacity cannot be used for another investment, even if the purchased capacity remains idle.

Cloud services can reduce this cost by converting some up-front acquisition into flexible operating expense. The comparison must also include the opportunity loss caused when insufficient capacity prevents the organization from meeting demand.
