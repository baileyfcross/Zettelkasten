# Map Popup Graphic

A map popup graphic embeds a small chart, image, or structured summary in a panel attached to a selected feature. It extends a [[Spatial Tooltip]] when one text line cannot express a local time series or distribution. The popup should maintain a clear link to its location and avoid hiding the surrounding pattern. Consistent chart scales are necessary if readers will compare popups from different features.
