# Algorithmic Auditability

Algorithmic auditability is the capacity of an AI system and its surrounding process to be examined by an authorized reviewer. An audit may evaluate training data, model construction, performance across groups, security, records of use, and whether required procedures were followed.

Auditability must be designed in advance because evidence that was never recorded cannot be reconstructed reliably after harm. It differs from public [[AI Transparency]]: some audits require protected access to sensitive data or proprietary systems. The ethical value comes from connecting evidence to standards, consequences, and actors able to require change.
