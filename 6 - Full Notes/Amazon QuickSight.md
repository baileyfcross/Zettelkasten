# Amazon QuickSight

Amazon QuickSight is a managed business-intelligence service for building interactive analyses, dashboards, and visual reports from cloud or external data sources. Authors assemble datasets and visualizations, then publish dashboards for viewers without managing visualization servers.

QuickSight is the presentation layer of an analytics workflow rather than the primary data store. It can consume results exposed by services such as Athena, Redshift, and RDS, turning governed data into shareable metrics and exploratory views.

Datasets may query a source directly or be imported into [[Amazon QuickSight SPICE]] for fast, concurrent in-memory analysis. SPICE improves dashboard responsiveness and reduces repeated source load, but its refresh schedule becomes the boundary that determines how current the displayed data is.
