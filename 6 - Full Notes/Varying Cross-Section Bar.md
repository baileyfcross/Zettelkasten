# Varying Cross-Section Bar

A varying cross-section bar has an area that changes along its length, so axial stress and stiffness are position dependent. A uniform-bar formula cannot represent the complete member with one constant rigidity.

Finite element treatment approximates the geometry with elements assigned representative areas or uses higher-order interpolation. Refining the subdivision improves the representation of the area and displacement variation.
