# Scene Shape

Scene shape is the overall arrangement of choices and constraints within a [[Game Scene]]. It describes how the available paths widen, narrow, branch, and converge without requiring the designer to predict every exact action.

Alternating broad choice with focused demands creates [[Game Pacing|pacing]]. A scene can guide play toward a particular point while preserving [[Player Performance|individual performance]], much as walls define a room without determining every movement inside it.
