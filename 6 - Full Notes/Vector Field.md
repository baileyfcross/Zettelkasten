# Vector Field

A vector field assigns a vector to every point in a region. Its components depend on position and may describe a fluid velocity, a force field, or an electric or magnetic field.

The field's local behavior is characterized by differential operators. Divergence measures net outward spreading from a point, curl measures local circulation, and line or surface integrals connect the field to motion along curves and transport through surfaces.
