# Sustainable AI

Sustainable AI develops and uses artificial intelligence in ways compatible with long-term social and environmental well-being. It includes using AI to understand environmental systems while also reducing the energy, materials, waste, and unequal burdens created by AI itself.

The goal requires life-cycle thinking. A system is not sustainable merely because its application has an environmental purpose; production, infrastructure, and rebound effects still matter. Sustainability also includes human autonomy and justice, since an efficient solution that imposes authoritarian control or transfers pollution to vulnerable communities does not resolve the larger ethical problem.
