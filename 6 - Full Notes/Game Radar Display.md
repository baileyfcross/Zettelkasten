# Game Radar Display

A game radar display maps nearby world entities into a compact two-dimensional HUD region centered on or oriented around the player. Relative world positions are transformed, scaled, and clipped to the radar bounds.

The display may rotate with the player or preserve a fixed world orientation. Symbols compress spatial information, so filtering and prioritization are necessary when many entities occupy the same area.
