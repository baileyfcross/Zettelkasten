# Inverse-Square Law

An inverse-square law has the form $y=k/x^2$. Doubling the distance variable reduces the dependent quantity to one quarter. Plotting $y$ against $1/x^2$ produces a straight line through the origin when the relationship holds. Such laws commonly arise when a conserved influence spreads across spherical area proportional to $x^2$.
