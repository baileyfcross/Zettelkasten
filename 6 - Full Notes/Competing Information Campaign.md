# Competing Information Campaign

A competing information campaign spreads accurate information through a network to contain an existing misinformation campaign. Reached users may be corrected after exposure or immunized before the false claim arrives.

The main optimization question is which limited set of users should seed the truthful campaign. Community structure and campaign budget can make local influencers more useful than apparently central bridges.
