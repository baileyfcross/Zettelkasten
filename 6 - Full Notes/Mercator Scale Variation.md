# Mercator Scale Variation

Mercator scale is true at the Equator in the simple tangent form and increases with the secant of latitude. Areas at high latitude therefore appear much larger than their actual proportions, even though local shape remains conformal. The increasing distortion makes the projection best suited to a limited equatorial or otherwise carefully bounded band.
