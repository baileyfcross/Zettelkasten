# Wireframe Geometric Model

A wireframe model represents a three-dimensional object through vertices and edge curves without storing enclosing faces. It is economical to construct, requires relatively little memory and computation, and exposes interior structure because no surface hides the rear edges.

That same transparency makes complex models ambiguous: edges behind the object remain visible through the front, and the representation cannot reliably supply volume, mass properties, manufacturability, or complete intersection information. Wireframes are therefore useful as lightweight structural descriptions, but [[Surface Geometric Model]] and [[Solid Geometric Model]] add information needed for realistic display and engineering analysis.
