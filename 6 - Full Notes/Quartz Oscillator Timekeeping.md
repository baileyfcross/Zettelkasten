# Quartz Oscillator Timekeeping

A quartz oscillator uses the crystal's piezoelectric resonance as a stable frequency reference. Its rapid, regular vibration permits more precise portable timekeeping than a mechanical balance wheel, which made accurate radio navigation and early satellite transmitters practical. Quartz is not perfectly invariant: temperature, contamination, shock, and aging can shift its frequency, so demanding systems monitor or correct its drift against a better standard.
