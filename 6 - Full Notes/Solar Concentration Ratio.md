# Solar Concentration Ratio

Concentration ratio is a focusing collector's receiving aperture area divided by its absorber or receiver area. A larger ratio can produce a more intense beam and higher useful fluid temperature at the smaller receiver. The source contrasts line-focus parabolic troughs with point-focus dishes and heliostat fields. Ratio alone is not delivered heat: mirror reflection, interception, absorption, and receiver losses also count.
