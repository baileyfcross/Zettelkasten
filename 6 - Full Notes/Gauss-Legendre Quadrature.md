# Gauss-Legendre Quadrature

Gauss-Legendre quadrature applies [[Gaussian Quadrature]] on $[-1,1]$ with unit weight. Its nodes are the roots of the Legendre polynomial $P_n(x)$, and symmetric nodes receive symmetric weights.

An integral on $[a,b]$ is transformed to $[-1,1]$ before the rule is applied. With $n$ nodes, the formula is exact for polynomials of degree at most $2n-1$.
