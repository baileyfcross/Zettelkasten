# Trade-Off Choice

A trade-off choice gives every option a distinct advantage and disadvantage. Selecting one benefit means accepting its cost and forgoing the strongest feature of another option, creating an opportunity cost rather than a universally correct answer.

Trade-offs remain interesting when their value changes with the player's goal, current state, or expectations. If one package dominates across all relevant conditions, the apparent trade-off collapses into an [[Obvious Game Decision]].
