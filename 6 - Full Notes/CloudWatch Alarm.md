# CloudWatch Alarm

A CloudWatch alarm evaluates a metric or metric expression over specified periods and changes state when its threshold conditions are met. State changes can notify people, invoke automation, or influence an Auto Scaling policy.

The alarm requires a meaningful signal, threshold, evaluation period, and missing-data policy. A noisy or weakly correlated alarm creates distraction, while an alarm tied to an actionable symptom helps operators identify and respond to real service risk.
