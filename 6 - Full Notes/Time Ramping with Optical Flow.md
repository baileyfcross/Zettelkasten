# Time Ramping with Optical Flow

Time remapping compresses or stretches sections of a clip between keyframes to make action speed up or slow down. Slow motion needs new intermediate frames; simple frame blending can ghost moving subjects. Optical flow estimates pixel motion to synthesize the in-between images, but crossing or overlapping limbs can confuse it and create smears. The book suggests isolating troublesome components with mattes, ramping them separately, and recombining them when a severe speed change breaks the automatic interpolation. Temporal repair is another form of constrained scene finishing.
