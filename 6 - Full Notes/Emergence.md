# Emergence

Emergence is the appearance of a relatively stable property or behavior at the level of a whole that is qualitatively different from the properties of its individual parts. It is produced by distributed interactions rather than directed by one component.

Local, generic [[Modular Behavior|behaviors]] and feedback loops make emergence possible. The resulting whole can in turn affect its parts through [[Downward Causality]], while the parts create it through [[Upward Causality]].
