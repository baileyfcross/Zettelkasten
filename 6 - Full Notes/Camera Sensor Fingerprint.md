# Camera Sensor Fingerprint

A camera sensor fingerprint is an estimate of a device's [[Photo-Response Non-Uniformity]] pattern derived from multiple reference photographs. Correlating that estimate with a questioned image can support or reject attribution to the same physical camera.

The method is stronger than camera-type attribution because it targets manufacturing variation within one sensor. Reliable matching depends on adequate resolution, unsaturated content, limited compression, and compatible cropping or scaling; deliberate fingerprint insertion is also a possible countermeasure.
