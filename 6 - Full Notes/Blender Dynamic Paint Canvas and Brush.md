# Blender Dynamic Paint Canvas and Brush

Blender Dynamic Paint assigns complementary roles to interacting objects. The canvas stores the changing surface result, while a brush contributes paint, displacement, weight, or wave influence where it intersects or approaches that canvas.

The canvas needs enough geometric or image resolution to record the desired detail, and its frame range must cover the animation in which contact occurs. Brush material can supply color, while alternate surface types can turn the same interaction into ripples or raised and depressed geometry. These roles make [[Blender Dynamic Paint]] an animated data-transfer system rather than only a painting tool.
