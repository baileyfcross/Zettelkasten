# Hydrogen Safety

Hydrogen systems need deliberate leak, ignition, flame, pressure-surge, and explosion controls because hydrogen-air mixtures can ignite over a wide range and the flame can be difficult to see. The source lists pressure-rated equipment, relief provisions, flame traps, and rapid closing as design concerns. This note describes the book's engineering principles, not a current safety code or complete operating procedure.
