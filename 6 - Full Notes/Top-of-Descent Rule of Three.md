# Top-of-Descent Rule of Three

For a path near three degrees, multiply the desired altitude loss in thousands of feet by three to estimate descent distance in nautical miles. Losing 7,000 feet therefore calls for a top of descent about 21 nautical miles before the target. The approximation is slightly shallower than the exact tangent relationship and should be refined when procedure constraints demand it.
