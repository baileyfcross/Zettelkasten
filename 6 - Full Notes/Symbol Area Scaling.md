# Symbol Area Scaling

Symbol area scaling makes a map symbol's area, rather than its radius, directly proportional to a data value. For circles this means radius grows with the square root of the value. Scaling radius directly would make area grow quadratically and greatly exaggerate large observations. Correct mathematical scaling establishes an honest geometric mapping, although [[Perceptual Symbol Scaling]] may still be considered because viewers tend to underestimate area differences.
