# Mixture Likelihood

Mixture likelihood evaluates each observation under the weighted sum of all component densities and multiplies those values across the data set. Membership is latent and integrated through the sum.

Maximizing this likelihood estimates parameters for overlapping components. It provides a basis for comparing fitted model structures when complexity is also controlled.
