# Information Systems Research Methods

Parent topic: [[Reproducible Computational Research]]

Information systems research selects methods by the kind of claim being made, then connects that choice to a defensible problem, conceptual framework, design, evidence, analysis, and report. The topic includes empirical, observational, constructive, axiomatic, and simulation-based approaches, together with the reliability, validity, ethics, sampling, and communication practices that make their results credible.
