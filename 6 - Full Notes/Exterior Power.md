# Exterior Power

The n-th exterior power ∧ⁿM is obtained from the n-fold tensor product by imposing alternating relations, so tensors with repeated factors vanish and swapping factors changes sign.

Exterior powers encode oriented multilinear combinations. The [[Koszul Complex]] of a sequence is built from exterior powers of a free module, with differentials determined by the sequence elements.
