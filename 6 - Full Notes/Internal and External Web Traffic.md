# Internal and External Web Traffic

Internal and external web traffic distinguishes activity originating within an organization from visits by outside users. The split can prevent employee testing or routine internal use from being mistaken for public engagement.

Classification depends on available signals such as network, referrer, or account information and should be treated as an operational rule.
