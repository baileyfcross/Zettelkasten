# EC2 Instance Families

EC2 instance families group virtual-server shapes around general-purpose, compute-optimized, memory-optimized, storage-optimized, accelerated, and other workload profiles. A size within a family changes the amount of CPU, memory, networking, and related capacity.

Selection should follow measured workload constraints rather than choose the largest type by default. Rightsizing balances performance and price, while a family change may alter processor architecture, local storage, network limits, or software compatibility.
