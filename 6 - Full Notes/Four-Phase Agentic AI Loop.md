# Four-Phase Agentic AI Loop

The four-phase agentic AI loop consists of perceive, reason, act, and learn. Perception gathers operational evidence; reasoning forms an explanation or plan; action uses external tools to change code, configuration, or workflow state; and learning evaluates whether the action produced the intended outcome.

Observability participates at both ends. It supplies the signals and context used to perceive a problem, and after an action it shows whether performance, vulnerability, SLO, or resource behavior actually improved. This closes the loop with measured feedback rather than a model's confidence alone.
