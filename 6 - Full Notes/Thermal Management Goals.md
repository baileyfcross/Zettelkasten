# Thermal Management Goals

Thermal management uses analysis to keep temperatures, gradients, and heat-transfer rates within acceptable limits. The objective may be to remove waste heat, preserve warmth, control a process cycle, prevent damage, or make a design more energy efficient.

The required output should be stated before model construction: a maximum temperature, heating time, spatial uniformity, heat loss, or another measurable quantity. That objective guides [[Dominant Physics Selection for Thermal Problems|physics selection]], resolution, validation evidence, and the level of detail worth adding.
