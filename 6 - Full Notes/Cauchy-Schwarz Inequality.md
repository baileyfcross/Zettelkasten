# Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality

The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality states that $|u\cdot v|\leq\|u\|\|v\|$ for Euclidean vectors. It bounds the magnitude of the [[Dot Product]] by the product of the two vector lengths.

The inequality follows from the cosine formula for the [[Angle Between Vectors]], since $|\cos\theta|\leq1$. Equality holds precisely when the vectors are linearly dependent, including the case in which one vector is zero.
