# Blender Transform Hotkeys

Blender begins its primary transforms with memorable keys: G grabs or moves, R rotates, and S scales. Left-click or Enter confirms the active transform, while right-click or Escape cancels it.

The same interaction pattern appears in editors beyond the 3D Viewport, including animation-curve editing. Axis keys and coordinate-orientation choices can constrain the operation, combining fast initiation with explicit spatial control.
