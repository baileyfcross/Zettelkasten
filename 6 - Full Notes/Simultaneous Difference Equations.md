# Simultaneous Difference Equations

Simultaneous difference equations couple two or more sequences through their shifted values. A linear system can be written with polynomials in the [[Shift Operator]] acting on each dependent sequence.

Eliminating all but one sequence produces a higher-order scalar recurrence. After solving it, substitution recovers the other sequences, and the supplied initial values determine the constants across the coupled solution.
