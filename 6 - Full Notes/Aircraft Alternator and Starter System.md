# Aircraft Alternator and Starter System

In a small piston aircraft, the starter motor turns the crankshaft to initiate engine operation, while the alternator supplies direct-current electrical power and charges the battery after start. Belts and shafts connect this equipment to the engine's rotating assembly. Electrical generation supports avionics and systems but remains distinct from the combustion power that drives the propeller.
