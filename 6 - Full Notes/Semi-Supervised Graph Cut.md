# Semi-Supervised Graph Cut

A semi-supervised graph cut partitions a similarity graph while using seed labels or pairwise constraints to favor particular sides of the cut. Guidance can appear as fixed terminals, modified edge weights, or penalty terms.

The graph propagates sparse supervision through neighborhoods and can follow nonlinear structure. Performance depends on graph connectivity; weak links may isolate unlabeled regions, while spurious bridges may spread guidance across true boundaries.
