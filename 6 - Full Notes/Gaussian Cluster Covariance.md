# Gaussian Cluster Covariance

Gaussian cluster covariance describes the spread, orientation, and correlations of one mixture component. In several dimensions, constant-density surfaces are ellipsoids determined by the covariance matrix.

Constraining covariance matrices across components defines different model families, from equal spherical groups to clusters with distinct volumes, shapes, and orientations.
