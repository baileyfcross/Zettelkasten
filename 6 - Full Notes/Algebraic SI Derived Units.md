# Algebraic SI Derived Units

A derived SI unit is built by multiplying or dividing base units. The book's Table 2-2 writes force as the newton, $\mathrm{N}=\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}$, and pressure as the pascal, $\mathrm{Pa}=\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$. Special names make frequently used combinations shorter without changing their dimensional structure. Radian and steradian are listed with a unit expression of one because matching lengths or areas cancel. See [[Seven SI Base Units]] and [[Dimensional Homogeneity in Equations]].
