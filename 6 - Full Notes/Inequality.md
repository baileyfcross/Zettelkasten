# Inequality

An inequality compares two expressions by order rather than asserting that they are equal. The relations less than, greater than, less than or equal to, and greater than or equal to are written (<), (>), (\leq), and (\geq).

An inequality with a variable usually describes a [[Solution Set of an Inequality]] containing many values. Equality-preserving methods still help isolate the variable, but multiplication or division by a negative value reverses the relation. The result can be represented as an [[Interval]] and graphed on a [[Number Line]].
