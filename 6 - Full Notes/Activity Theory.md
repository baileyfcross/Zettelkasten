# Activity Theory

Activity theory is a framework for understanding interaction within complex real-life contexts. An activity relates a subject—a person or group—to an object that motivates or meets a need.

Activities form hierarchies: conscious, goal-directed actions are made from more routine operations. External operations can become internalized through practice, while internal ideas can be externalized into artifacts. Tools can mediate the relation between subject and object, and activities develop as mediating tools change.

For interface design, the framework expands attention beyond a user and computer to include community, rules, division of labor, context, and the outcome of the activity.
