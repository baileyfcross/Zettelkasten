# Xerox PARC

Xerox established the Palo Alto Research Center in 1970 and hired researchers whose defense funding had become restricted. The laboratory combined work on personal workstations, graphical interfaces, networking, and printing.

PARC produced technologies that defined later computing, including the Alto, Ethernet, laser printing, and integrated screen graphics. Xerox struggled to convert many of them into successful mass-market products.
