# Circle Equation

A circle with center $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ consists of the points satisfying

$$(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2.$$

The equation follows from holding the [[Distance Between Points]] from $(x,y)$ to the center constant. Expanding it gives a general quadratic equation whose equal squared coefficients reflect the circle's symmetry.
