# Ranking Prediction

Ranking prediction orders items by expected popularity, influence, or relevance instead of estimating an exact value. Evaluation must consider both whether relevant items appear and how highly they are placed.

Metrics such as [[Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain]] support graded relevance, while [[Average Precision]] is useful when relevance is binary. The metric should reflect the positions users or decisions actually inspect.
