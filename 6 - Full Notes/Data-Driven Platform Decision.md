# Data-Driven Platform Decision

A data-driven platform decision uses operational, delivery, adoption, cost, and user-experience evidence to compare options and identify technical debt. Telemetry already collected for incidents can also reveal toil, unused capabilities, capacity patterns, recurring policy failures, and components that prevent service objectives from improving.

Evidence informs judgment rather than replacing it. The team should define the question and decision threshold before choosing metrics, avoid treating correlation as causation, and include qualitative context. A measure is useful when it changes a concrete architecture or product choice, not merely because the platform can collect it.
