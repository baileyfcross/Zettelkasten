# Time-Series Characterization

Time-series characterization describes a series through interpretable properties such as distribution, dependence, predictability, stationarity, and model fit. The resulting feature vector permits conventional learning algorithms to operate on whole time series.

Characterization is especially useful for heterogeneous collections because series of unequal length can be compared in a common feature space. Its success depends on selecting measures that reflect the temporal behaviors relevant to the task.
