# Wavelet Thresholding

Wavelet thresholding modifies or removes small wavelet coefficients after a [[Wavelet Transform]] to reduce noise. Coefficients below a selected threshold are treated as less informative than the larger components that represent stronger signal structure.

The threshold controls a tradeoff: aggressive removal simplifies the signal but may discard useful detail, while a weak threshold leaves more noise for the forecasting model.
