# Graphical Model Interpretation

Graphical model interpretation uses diagnostic and coefficient plots to understand a fitted model before writing conclusions. Residual plots reveal assumption violations, while a [[Coefficient Plot]] shows which estimates lie above, below, or near the no-association value and how uncertain they are.

The graph is a reasoning aid rather than independent evidence. Its scale and transformations must match the model: a logistic coefficient plot on the log-odds scale has a reference of zero, whereas an odds-ratio display has a reference of one.
