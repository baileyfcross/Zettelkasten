# AWS Multi-Account Strategy

An AWS multi-account strategy separates environments, teams, workloads, security functions, and billing into distinct account boundaries. A compromised or misconfigured development account then has less direct reach into production than it would inside one shared account.

The strategy needs centralized identity, policy, logging, networking, and cost allocation so separation does not become unmanaged sprawl. [[AWS Organizational Unit|Organizational Units]] and service control policies apply common guardrails to groups of accounts.
