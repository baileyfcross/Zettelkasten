# Stimulation Preference in Games

Stimulation preference concerns the amount of social and sensory activity a player wants. The source associates it with extraversion: some players seek lively multiplayer interaction, while others prefer solitary play with fewer external demands.

The preference affects competition modes, communication features, pacing, and presentation. More stimulation is not automatically better when it overwhelms the audience the game intends to serve.
