# Parabolic Trough Solar Collector

A parabolic trough is a line-focus [[Concentrating Solar Collector]]. Its curved mirror directs sunlight onto a receiver tube running along the focal line, where flowing fluid gains heat. The trough follows the Sun about one axis. Many trough modules can send their heated fluid to a [[Parabolic Trough Solar Power Plant]]; the individual collector's aperture, tracking, and receiver losses set the heat delivered.
