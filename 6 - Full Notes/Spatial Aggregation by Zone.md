# Spatial Aggregation by Zone

Spatial aggregation by zone reduces a raster layer to summaries over geographic bands or other regions. Repeating the operation through time turns a three-dimensional [[Space-Time Raster]] into a matrix whose rows or columns represent zones and times. The direction and summary function determine which pattern survives. Latitude means, for example, conceal longitudinal variation but can expose broad seasonal gradients.
