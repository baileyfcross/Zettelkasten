# Unit Circle

The unit circle is the circle of radius one centered at the origin. The point reached by an angle $\theta$ has coordinates $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$, extending [[Trigonometric Ratios]] beyond acute angles. Its circumference also defines [[Angle Measure in Radians]] and makes the signs and periodicity of sine and cosine geometrically visible.
