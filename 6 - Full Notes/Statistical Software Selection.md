# Statistical Software Selection

Statistical software selection matches an analytic environment to the data volume, existing code, team expertise, cost, support needs, output requirements, and organizational constraints of a project. No platform is universally superior across all of these dimensions.

R can offer accessible licensing, flexible graphics, and a broad package ecosystem, while SAS may be favored by legacy workflows, institutional support, very large datasets, or supplied SAS-format assets. The decision should be recorded as part of project design because it affects reproducibility and staffing.

A broader evaluation also considers total cost, operational history, supported platforms and environments, program design, documentation, installation and maintenance effort, and the supplier's continuing support. Regardless of platform, the analyst remains responsible for choosing a method whose assumptions fit the design and for interpreting the output rather than accepting a software default as a research decision.
