# User Experience

User experience is a person's entire relationship with an artifact. It includes [[Usability]] and usefulness as well as emotional effects such as enjoyment, frustration, elegance, novelty, engagement, and pride of ownership.

UX extends beyond the visible [[User Interface]] to include the system's social context, the surrounding ecology of tools, and what the user brings to the interaction, such as training, culture, abilities, and emotional state. A functionally complete interface can still produce a poor experience if people are uncomfortable or unwilling to use it.

[[User Experience Evaluation]] assesses selected parts of this relationship rather than reducing the whole experience to one score.
