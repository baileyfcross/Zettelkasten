# Mathematical Generality

The idea that we can take some of the properties of what we're looking at and examining just those specific properties without relying on other properties from the items that our properties came from. 

In algebra, mathematical generality is achieved by replacing particular numbers with a [[Variable]]. The equation (a+b=b+a) captures the commutative behavior illustrated by indefinitely many numerical cases. A symbolic statement can therefore express a reusable relationship without knowing the variables' particular values. This abstraction must still preserve the properties relevant to the problem.
