# Computational Fluid Dynamics Mesh

Computational fluid dynamics represents the space around an object with a three-dimensional mesh and approximates airflow equations at its points. Mesh cells are made smaller where flow changes rapidly, such as near an aircraft surface.

A supercomputer advances the field through time and visualization converts numerical values into interpretable patterns of flow and stress. The method can reduce dependence on wind tunnels and physical test flights.
