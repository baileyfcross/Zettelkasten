# Digital Paradigm

The digital paradigm represents information, calculation, and control with discrete binary states. Arithmetic values, logical decisions, instructions, and transmitted messages can all be encoded as patterns of zeros and ones.

Its power comes from using a common representation across previously separate technologies. Once information is digital, the same electronic machinery can store, transform, copy, and communicate it.
