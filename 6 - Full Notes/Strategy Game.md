# Strategy Game

A strategy game emphasizes planning, resource use, positioning, and decisions whose consequences unfold over time. The player manages forces or systems to achieve objectives against an opponent or environment.

Good strategy preserves several viable approaches and provides enough information for plans to be formed and revised. Real-time and turn-based structures change the amount of time pressure without eliminating the need for strategic choice.
