# Euclidean Inner Product

The Euclidean inner product of real coordinate vectors is $u^Tv=\sum_i u_iv_i$. It is the standard dot product.

It produces the Euclidean [[Vector Norm]] and determines the familiar angle and perpendicularity relationships. For complex vectors, the corresponding inner product uses conjugation.

Relative to an orthonormal basis, a vector's coordinates are its inner products with the basis vectors, and the squared Euclidean norm is the sum of the squared coordinate magnitudes.
