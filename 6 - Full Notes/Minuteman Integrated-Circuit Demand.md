# Minuteman Integrated-Circuit Demand

The Minuteman missile guidance program required circuits that were compact, light, rugged, and highly reliable. United States Air Force purchases created a major early market for integrated circuits.

Government demand helped manufacturers refine fabrication and lower costs before a broad commercial market existed. Military requirements therefore accelerated the technology’s path into general computing.
