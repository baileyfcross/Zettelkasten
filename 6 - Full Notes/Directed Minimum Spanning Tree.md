# Directed Minimum Spanning Tree

A directed minimum spanning tree restricts a learned Bayesian network to a tree-shaped dependency structure. Pairwise relationship weights identify a connecting tree, which is then oriented to obtain a directed probabilistic decomposition.

The restriction makes structure identification tractable and exposes important dependencies, but it cannot represent every pattern of interaction. Its simplicity is both the computational advantage and the modeling limitation.
