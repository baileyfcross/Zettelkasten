# Iteration Control Structure

An iteration control structure repeats a sequence of steps while a continuation condition holds or until a stopping condition is reached. It is also called a loop.

Iteration lets an [[Algorithm]] apply the same operation to successive data or progressively improve a result. Its condition must support [[Algorithm Termination]]; otherwise the procedure can enter an infinite loop and fail to produce its intended output.
