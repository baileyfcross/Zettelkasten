# Initial and Boundary Conditions

Initial and boundary conditions select one physical solution from the family represented by a differential equation's general solution. An $n$th-order ordinary differential equation generally introduces $n$ arbitrary constants and therefore requires the same number of independent conditions.

Initial conditions specify the function or its derivatives at one value of the independent variable, commonly an initial time. Boundary conditions specify behavior at distinct positions or limits; in either case, the conditions are applied to the complete general solution rather than separately to its homogeneous and particular parts.
