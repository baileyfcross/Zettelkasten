# Mixed Strategy in Matrix Games

A mixed strategy assigns probabilities to two or more pure strategies and chooses among them randomly. It is used when a matrix game has no saddle point, preventing the opponent from exploiting a predictable fixed action.

The row player chooses probabilities to maximize the minimum expected payoff, while the column player minimizes the maximum expected loss. At the optimal distributions, both quantities equal the [[Value of a Matrix Game]].
