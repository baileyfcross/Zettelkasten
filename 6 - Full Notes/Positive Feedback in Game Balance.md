# Positive Feedback in Game Balance

Positive feedback in game balance makes success increase the likelihood of further success. A player who gains a lead may acquire additional resources, position, or power that widens the lead.

This can reward good play and help a match reach resolution, but excessive feedback creates runaway advantage. Limits, vulnerability, or compensating costs can preserve the value of later decisions.
