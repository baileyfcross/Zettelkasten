# Sputnik 1

Sputnik 1 was the first artificial satellite, launched by the Soviet Union on October 4, 1957. It proved that humans could accelerate an object to orbital velocity and made long-range Soviet rocket capability visible to a global public.

The satellite's scientific and technical simplicity did not limit its political effect. International press attention turned a planned [[International Geophysical Year]] experiment into a symbolic Soviet victory and helped define the [[Space Race]]. Its radio signal also prompted an important navigation insight: if Doppler measurements could determine a satellite's orbit, a known orbit could be used to determine a receiver's position on Earth.
