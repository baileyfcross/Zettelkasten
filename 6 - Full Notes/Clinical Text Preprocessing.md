# Clinical Text Preprocessing

Clinical text preprocessing converts raw narrative into units suitable for extraction or learning. It may identify sections and sentences, tokenize text, normalize variants, expand or disambiguate abbreviations, and attach part-of-speech or lexical information.

Ordinary language assumptions often fail in clinical reports. Punctuation can carry formatting meaning, abbreviations can be specialty-specific, and fragments may be intentional. Preprocessing should therefore be adapted to the document type and measured by its effect on downstream tasks rather than judged only by generic linguistic correctness.
