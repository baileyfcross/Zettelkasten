# Skew Lines

Skew lines are two lines in three-dimensional space that are neither parallel nor intersecting. Unlike distinct nonparallel lines in a plane, they lie in different planes and pass one another without meeting.

Testing for skewness requires both direction and position information. Nonparallel direction vectors rule out parallelism, while failure of the two parametric equations to share a point rules out intersection.
