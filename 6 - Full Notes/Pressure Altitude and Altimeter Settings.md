# Pressure Altitude and Altimeter Settings

Pressure altitude is the altitude indicated when the altimeter is referenced to standard pressure. QNH is a local setting that makes the instrument indicate elevation above mean sea level on the ground, while the standard setting supports flight levels and a common vertical reference. Pressure altitude also serves as an input to [[Density Altitude and Aircraft Performance]] and approved performance charts.
