# Judgmental Analysis Method

Judgmental analysis captures how individual judges combine predictor information and then groups judges whose decision equations are similar. Each judge's ratings are modeled with multiple regression, and a hierarchical procedure merges policies while tracking the loss of predictive efficiency.

The method produces both interpretable policy equations and evidence about agreement. It can identify several policy groups or, when supported, a combined consensus policy.
