# Inclusive AI Development

Inclusive AI development brings varied social positions, disciplines, and affected communities into the design and evaluation of an AI system. Homogeneous teams are more likely to overlook assumptions, missing populations, and harms outside their own experience.

Inclusion is more than adding demographic diversity to a technical team. People who bear the consequences need meaningful influence over problem definition, acceptable risk, and deployment. Their knowledge can reveal failures that aggregate performance measures hide. The practice connects [[Bias Mitigation in AI]] with [[Stakeholder Participation in AI Governance]].
