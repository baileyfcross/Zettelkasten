# Elliptical Utterance

An elliptical utterance omits words whose meaning can be recovered from context. After asking about restaurants in Boston, a user might say “How about Cambridge?” without restating the full request.

The [[Dialog Manager]] combines the fragment with [[Conversation Context]] to reconstruct the intended [[Meaning Representation]]. Treating the utterance in isolation would often produce an incomplete or incorrect intent.
