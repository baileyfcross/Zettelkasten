# AI Paternalism

AI paternalism occurs when an intelligent system directs people's behavior for their claimed benefit while reducing their freedom to judge or choose. Personalized nudges, automated resource allocation, or environmental controls may be presented as benevolent because the system predicts what is good for individuals or society.

Even accurate guidance can undermine autonomy when goals are imposed, influence is hidden, or there is no meaningful refusal. A machine that manages people for planetary efficiency could combine care with authoritarian control. Ethical assistance should support self-chosen aims, make interventions visible, and preserve human responsibility for political questions about values and priorities.
