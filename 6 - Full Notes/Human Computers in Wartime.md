# Human Computers in Wartime

Wartime human computers, many of them women, followed detailed procedures on mechanical calculators to produce firing tables and other numerical results. They chose branches from interim results and checked one another’s work.

Their labor supplied control and judgment that available machines lacked. The title computer later migrated from these people to the automatic electronic devices that replaced much of their routine calculation.
