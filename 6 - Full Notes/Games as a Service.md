# Games as a Service

Games as a service treats deployment as the beginning of an ongoing product cycle. The team repeatedly plans, develops, deploys, supports, measures, and learns from changes used by a live player population.

This operating model connects product development with service reliability. Release speed has value only when the game can be observed, supported, and changed safely after deployment.
