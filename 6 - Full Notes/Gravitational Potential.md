# Gravitational Potential

Gravitational potential is gravitational potential energy per unit mass. Taking zero at infinity, the potential outside a spherical mass is $\phi=-GM/r$. Potential is scalar and adds algebraically for multiple masses. Gravitational field points toward decreasing potential, and moving a mass through a potential difference changes its potential energy by $m\Delta\phi$.
