# Open Resistance

Open resistance gives players substantial control over where, when, and why they encounter challenge. Openness may come from several routes through a world, a larger palette of verbs, optional goals, or a system in which players invent goals of their own.

An open game is still shaped by rules rather than being a blank canvas. Its [[Possibility Space]] determines what can be built or pursued, while players decide which possibilities become their particular experience.
