# ARPANET

ARPANET was a packet-switched research network funded by the United States Advanced Research Projects Agency. Its first computers were connected in 1969, linking university and government-supported computing centers.

The network demonstrated that heterogeneous computers could exchange data through shared protocols. Its technologies and research community became major ancestors of the Internet.
