# IoT Security Externalities

IoT security externalities are harms created by an insecure connected product that fall partly on people and systems other than its owner or manufacturer. A compromised camera, appliance, vehicle, or industrial endpoint can expose its user, become infrastructure for broader attacks, or provide a path into another network.

Market incentives can underprovide protection when low price and rapid release are visible to buyers but maintenance, secure defaults, and long-term update support are not. The resulting risk is distributed across an ecosystem. Effective security therefore depends on device design and lifecycle management as well as network segmentation, monitoring, and accountability beyond the individual purchaser.
