# Access Control

Access control ensures that only entitled users, applications, or systems can reach a resource or perform an operation. It combines identity, authentication, authorization, and enforcement at boundaries such as applications, queues, networks, and data stores.

Controls should follow least necessary access and apply to senders as well as receivers. For example, protecting a message queue requires deciding who may place messages on it and who may retrieve them.
