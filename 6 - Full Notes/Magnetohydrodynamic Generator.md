# Magnetohydrodynamic Generator

A magnetohydrodynamic generator moves an electrically conducting gas or liquid through a strong magnetic field to induce a voltage and collect current at electrodes. A hot seeded gas can serve as the conductor, so no rotating turbine-generator is needed for this electrical stage. The moving fluid loses energy as power is extracted; its speed, conductivity, and magnetic field set the available output.
