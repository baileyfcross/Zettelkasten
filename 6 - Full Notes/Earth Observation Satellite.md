# Earth Observation Satellite

An Earth observation satellite collects images or other measurements of Earth's surface, atmosphere, oceans, weather, or environment. Different wavelengths and resolutions support mapping, resource study, environmental science, forecasting, and military intelligence.

Civil and military development have repeatedly overlapped. Sensors created for reconnaissance or missile detection informed civilian instruments, while weather and Earth-science data also served defense planning. Polar-orbiting spacecraft can build global coverage over successive passes, whereas geostationary systems provide continuous views of large regions.
