# COMSOL Temperature Contours

Temperature contours map equal-temperature values across a surface, slice, or volume. They expose gradients, hot spots, penetration depth, and symmetries that a single maximum or average cannot communicate.

Contours should be paired with units and a deliberate range. A full scale preserves the global extrema, while a restricted scale can reveal modest variations in the operational range; showing both avoids the distortions that can arise from [[Thermal Result Scale Selection|scale selection]].
