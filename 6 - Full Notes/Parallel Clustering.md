# Parallel Clustering

Parallel clustering distributes distance calculations, assignments, or summary updates across several processors or machines. Workers can process different observations while coordinating shared cluster representatives.

Scalability depends on balanced partitions and the size and frequency of exchanged state. Iterative methods may become communication-bound when each round performs little local computation.
