# Sybil Attack

A Sybil attack occurs when one adversary creates or controls many apparently independent identities. The identities can distort voting, popularity, recommendation, reputation, or routing systems that assume each account represents a separate participant.

Defenses use stronger identity costs, social-graph structure, behavioral similarity, resource tests, and rate limits, but each technique trades openness and privacy against resistance to fake identities.
