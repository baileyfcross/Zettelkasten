# Microsoft Entra Access Review

A Microsoft Entra access review asks an accountable reviewer to recertify whether identities should retain an assignment. Reviews can cover group membership, application access, access packages, or privileged roles and can run once or recur on a schedule.

The reviewer may be an owner, manager, delegated reviewer, guest sponsor, or the users themselves. Entra can provide recommendations based on signals such as recent activity, but the decision remains a governance action. Multi-stage reviews separate perspectives, and automatic application of decisions removes denied or unreviewed access according to policy. A useful review has a clear population, evidence, deadline, and consequence; merely collecting approvals without removing stale access does not enforce least privilege.
