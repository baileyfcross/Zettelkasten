# Haptic Exploratory Procedure

A haptic exploratory procedure is a stereotyped hand movement optimized for learning a particular object property. People spontaneously use lateral motion for texture, unsupported holding for weight, pressure for compliance, static contact for temperature, enclosure for global shape and volume, and contour following for exact shape.

The procedure is not mechanically fixed. Contact force and scanning speed change with the surface and discrimination task. A grasp-and-lift action efficiently combines several procedures to obtain coarse geometric and material information before a more specific movement is chosen.
