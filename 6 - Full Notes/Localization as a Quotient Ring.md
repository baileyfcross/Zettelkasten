# Localization as a Quotient Ring

For a ring R and element f, the localization R_f is isomorphic to R[y]/(fy−1). The new variable y represents the inverse of f, and the quotient relation forces fy to equal the [[Ring Identity]].

This presentation converts fractions into ordinary polynomial classes. It is useful when localization must be handled with ideal and polynomial computations.
