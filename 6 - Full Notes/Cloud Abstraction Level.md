# Cloud Abstraction Level

A cloud abstraction level indicates how much of the underlying technology a service consumer must specify or manage. At a low level, the consumer chooses infrastructure details; at a high level, the consumer asks for information or a completed business outcome.

Increasing abstraction can reduce technical management and let the consumer focus on purpose. It also transfers more control and dependency to the provider, making interfaces, service commitments, data access, and portability more important.
