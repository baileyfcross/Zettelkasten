# CS Unplugged

CS Unplugged teaches computing concepts through games, puzzles, magic tricks, and physical activities that do not require a computer. It makes ideas such as representation, algorithms, and information accessible in resource-limited or introductory settings.

Unplugged activity is a teaching method, not evidence that machines are irrelevant to computation. Learners still need to connect the activity to the behavior and constraints of actual computing systems.
