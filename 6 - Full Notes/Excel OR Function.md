# Excel OR Function

The Excel OR function evaluates several conditions and returns TRUE when at least one condition is true. It models alternatives, such as accepting either of two category labels or flagging a record when any one of several risk conditions appears.

OR often becomes the logical test of an [[Excel IF Function]] or a conditional formatting formula. Its broad acceptance can hide which particular condition succeeded, so complicated rules may be easier to audit when their component tests are placed in helper cells. OR expresses union: a record qualifies if it belongs to any specified set.
