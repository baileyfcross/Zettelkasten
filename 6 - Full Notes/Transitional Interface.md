# Transitional Interface

A transitional interface animates the change between two related viewpoints or representations instead of replacing one abruptly. In navigation, it can connect an egocentric AR view to an exocentric overview while showing how position, scale, and orientation transform.

The visible transition preserves context and reduces the mental effort required to match objects across views. Its duration and path should explain the relationship without delaying the user's task unnecessarily.
