# Emotional Interactivity

Emotional interactivity occurs when a player's actions and the game's responses create changing motivations and emotions within meaningful context. It goes beyond momentary startle or sensory pleasure to involve consequences the player cares about.

Authored scenes can target emotion precisely, while systemic emotion is less controllable but can feel more authentic because it follows from [[Player Goal|goals]] and agency. The supporting systems must let player choices genuinely matter.
