# Developer Observability Service

A developer observability service presents each product team with the signals it owns and can act on: application performance, availability, logs, exceptions, failed delivery jobs, deployment history, and delivery metrics. It removes unrelated platform and tenant data so the interface is both safer and easier to interpret.

Self-service includes the ability to define relevant metrics and dashboards within platform standards, not merely view a central screen. Access controls, ownership metadata, and tenant-aware querying ensure that teams see the correct evidence. The service should integrate with their normal workflows and link an operational symptom back to the version and lifecycle events that produced it.
