# Parametric Circle Representation

An origin-centered circle of radius r can be represented by x equals r cosine theta and y equals r sine theta as theta ranges through a full turn. Uniform increments of theta produce an ordered sequence of points around the circumference without solving separately for two branches of y.

Angle-addition identities yield an incremental form: each new point is obtained by rotating the preceding point through the constant parameter step. The sine and cosine of that step need be computed only once. Translation moves the center, while scaling an origin-centered unit circle changes the radius before translation.
