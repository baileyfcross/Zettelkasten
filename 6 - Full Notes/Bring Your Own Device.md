# Bring Your Own Device

Bring your own device is an operating model in which people use personally chosen computers or mobile devices to reach organizational applications and information. The organization must support device diversity rather than managing one homogeneous hardware and software image.

Cloud applications make this model practical by moving shared services away from the individual device. Protected local storage, identity controls, endpoint security, remote data access, and clear user responsibility are necessary because the organization does not fully control the client.
