# Aggregated Biomedical Evidence

Aggregated biomedical evidence combines information from multiple publications into higher-level resources such as reviews, evidence summaries, or synthesized databases. It can reduce the work required to interpret a large and changing literature.

Aggregation adds judgment rather than merely copying documents. Inclusion criteria, appraisal, and update timing determine which evidence is represented and how conflicts are resolved. Retrieval systems should make those methods visible because a concise summary can be easier to use while also hiding uncertainty or becoming outdated.
