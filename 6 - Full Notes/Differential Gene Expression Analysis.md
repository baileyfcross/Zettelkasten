# Differential Gene Expression Analysis

Differential gene expression analysis identifies transcripts whose measured abundance differs between biological conditions. It estimates both the direction and magnitude of change while accounting for variability across samples.

Thousands of genes are tested together, so unadjusted significance produces many false discoveries. The analysis requires normalized data, an appropriate comparison design, and multiple-testing control. A statistically different transcript is a candidate biological signal; pathway context and independent validation are needed before it becomes a clinical biomarker.
