# Exclusionary Game Material

Exclusionary game material is content or a feature that drives away people who might otherwise enjoy the game's central activity. Unnecessary stereotypes, hostility, sexualization, confusing conventions, or incompatible side content can narrow the audience without strengthening the premise.

The test is not whether any person could object. It is whether the material undermines the experience for the intended audience while contributing little to the design.
