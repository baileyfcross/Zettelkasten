# Richardson-Dushman Equation

The Richardson-Dushman equation estimates the maximum electron current density thermally emitted by a hot surface. Its temperature-squared factor is multiplied by an exponential penalty set by [[Surface Work Function]] relative to thermal energy. Thus a change in emitter temperature or surface material can greatly change available current. Actual [[Thermionic Generator]] current may be lower because electrons in the gap create space charge.
