# Column Minima Method

The column minima method is the column-wise counterpart of the [[Row Minima Method]]. Starting with the first demand column, it assigns as much as possible to the lowest-cost available cell, adjusts the remaining quantities, and advances when that demand is fulfilled.

The method produces a feasible starting allocation but not necessarily an optimal one. Its cost can differ from the row-wise result because each procedure gives priority to a different traversal of the tableau.
