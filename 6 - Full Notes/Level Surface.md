# Level Surface

A level surface of a [[Scalar Field]] is the set of points satisfying $f(x,y,z)=k$ for one constant value $k$. In two dimensions the analogous sets are level curves, such as contour lines, isobars, or isotherms.

Moving within a level surface produces no first-order change in the field. Consequently, tangent directions are perpendicular to the field's gradient, and a family of level surfaces visualizes both the value distribution and the directions in which it changes fastest.
