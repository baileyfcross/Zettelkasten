# Moment of Inversion

A moment of inversion is a sudden change in a game's dominant relationship, such as the hunted becoming the hunter or a background rule moving to the foreground. It changes the shape of play and often functions as a climax or release.

Inversion creates contrast within [[Game Pacing|pacing]] by briefly changing roles, priorities, or power. Because it reuses established rules in a different relationship, it can feel surprising while remaining understandable.
