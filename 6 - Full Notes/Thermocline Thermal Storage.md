# Thermocline Thermal Storage

Thermocline storage uses one tank with a packed bed, such as rocks and circulating oil, to separate hot and cool temperature regions. Solar-heated fluid charges the bed; during discharge, flow reverses and transfers stored heat to the fluid sent to the plant. This arrangement uses one container rather than separate hot and cold tanks, but temperature mixing and heat-transfer effectiveness affect usable output.
