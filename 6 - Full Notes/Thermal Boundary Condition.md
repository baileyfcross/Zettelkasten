# Thermal Boundary Condition

A thermal boundary condition specifies how a model exchanges energy with its surroundings. Common forms prescribe temperature, heat flux, convection to an ambient, radiation, insulation, symmetry, or continuity across an interface.

Each condition acts on selected geometric entities and must match the physical boundary being represented. An omitted or misapplied condition changes the energy balance, while mutually inconsistent conditions can make the finite-element problem overconstrained or physically meaningless.
