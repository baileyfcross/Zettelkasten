# Inter-Attribute Summary

An inter-attribute summary stores strongly connected value pairs drawn from two different categorical attributes together with their supported co-occurrence counts. Weak pairs below the threshold are omitted.

CACTUS can construct these sparse summaries in a scan of the data and use them during later projection and clustering phases.
