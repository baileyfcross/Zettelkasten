# Cogeneration Power Plant

A cogeneration plant supplies electricity and useful process heat from one fuel and conversion system. Steam can be extracted from a turbine at a suitable pressure, or gas-turbine exhaust and diesel-engine exhaust or cooling water can serve the heat demand. The source emphasizes industries and buildings needing both products at once. Total useful-energy efficiency rises when recovered heat displaces separate heat production.
