# Quadratic Form

A quadratic form is a scalar expression of the form $q(x)=x^TAx$. Only the symmetric part of $A$ affects its value, so it can be represented by a [[Symmetric Matrix]].

The sign of $q(x)$ for nonzero vectors classifies $A$ as positive definite, semidefinite, negative definite, or indefinite. This classification connects eigenvalues and the [[Hessian Matrix]] to curvature and extrema.
