# Guided Immersive Learning

Guided immersive learning uses a virtual or augmented environment as part of an instructor-led sequence rather than as an unguided novelty. The teacher directs attention, supplies questions and explanations, and connects what students experience to explicit learning objectives.

A virtual field trip can place a class in a museum, distant location, historical reconstruction, or model of the solar system. Simply allowing students to roam may produce distraction; a guided tour can instead compare specific features, establish spatial relationships, and coordinate discussion across the class.

The technology amplifies the pedagogy already present. [[Presence]] and interactivity can increase attention, but they do not guarantee understanding. Curriculum design, instructor familiarity, accessibility, assessment, and an appropriate relationship between exploration and explanation determine whether the experience improves learning.
