# Contactless IoT Payment

Contactless IoT payment lets a connected phone, watch, card, vehicle, or other device authenticate a purchase through a short-range reader or an embedded service. Near-field communication is especially useful because its few-centimeter range permits a deliberate tap while reducing accidental communication with distant readers.

Payment can become part of a larger system rather than a separate checkout step. A transit network can record rides and apply the best fare, while a connected vehicle can pay for fuel, parking, or food through its infotainment interface. This integration improves convenience but joins identity, location, consumption, and financial records into a consequential data trail.
