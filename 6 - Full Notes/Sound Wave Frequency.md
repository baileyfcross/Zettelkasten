# Sound Wave Frequency

Sound wave frequency is the number of waveform cycles that occur per second, measured in hertz. It is perceived primarily as pitch: faster oscillation generally sounds higher and slower oscillation lower.

Digital game audio represents a complex sound as sampled changes in air pressure containing many frequency components. Playback systems can modify or filter those components through digital signal processing.
