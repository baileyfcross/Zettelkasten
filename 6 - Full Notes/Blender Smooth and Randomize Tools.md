# Blender Smooth and Randomize Tools

The Smooth tool averages selected vertex positions with their neighbors, reducing abrupt local variation in the surface. Repeated application progressively relaxes the form and can soften an uneven region without adding topology.

Randomize moves selected components by bounded random offsets, introducing irregularity that counters obvious repetition. Magnitude and per-axis controls should remain small enough to support the intended material or terrain rather than destroy the underlying silhouette.
