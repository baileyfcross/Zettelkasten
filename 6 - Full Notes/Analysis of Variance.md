# Analysis of Variance

Analysis of variance compares the residual variation of nested linear models after accounting for their difference in complexity. An F statistic assesses whether the reduction in residual sum of squares from the added terms is larger than expected under the smaller model.

The source uses this omnibus test for a block of interaction terms. A significant result indicates that the larger block contributes information, but it does not identify which individual group comparison is responsible.
