# Silicon Valley Spin-Off Culture

Silicon Valley developed a culture in which engineers frequently left established semiconductor firms to create nearby competitors. Fairchild Semiconductor itself came from defectors, then became the source of many further companies.

Employee mobility spread knowledge, investment, and manufacturing skill through the region. Short-term disruption produced a dense network that accelerated innovation.
