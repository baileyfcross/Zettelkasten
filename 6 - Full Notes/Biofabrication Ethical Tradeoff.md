# Biofabrication Ethical Tradeoff

A biofabrication ethical tradeoff compares competing benefits and harms when no option satisfies every value. A more complex tissue model may improve medical prediction while increasing moral concern; customization may improve care while raising cost and unequal access.

Making the tradeoff explicit identifies whose benefits count, who bears risk, what uncertainty remains, and which alternatives were excluded.
