# Network Method of Procedure Review Gate

A network MOP review gate is the explicit decision point between a generated procedure and an approved change. Reviewers compare the plan with current topology and policy, validate commands in a lab or safe parser, confirm backups and rollback, and ensure monitoring and communication are ready.

The gate should be recorded rather than implied by the existence of a polished document. A complete-looking [[AI-Generated Method of Procedure]] can still contain a subtle platform or sequence error. Human ownership, peer review, and change-control evidence make clear who accepted the risk and under what conditions execution may proceed.
