# Plane Stress

Plane stress is a two-dimensional idealization for thin, flat bodies loaded within their plane. Stress components normal to the plane and transverse shear stresses are assumed negligible.

The in-plane displacement field still varies over the surface, and the constitutive matrix is specialized to the plane-stress assumption. Thickness enters element force and stiffness calculations even though out-of-plane stress is omitted.
