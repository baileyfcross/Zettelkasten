# Multimodal Interaction

Multimodal interaction combines multiple input or output channels in one interface. A person may switch between modalities or use them together, such as speaking a command while pointing to its spatial arguments.

Multiple channels can decouple [[System Control]] from a primary task, resolve ambiguous speech or gestures, support complementary strengths, and give users alternatives when one channel is blocked. They can also increase [[Cognitive Load]] or disrupt the flow of action if switching and coordination are poorly designed.

The task should be matched to suitable modalities, transitions should be clear, and combined performance should be evaluated rather than assumed to improve automatically.
