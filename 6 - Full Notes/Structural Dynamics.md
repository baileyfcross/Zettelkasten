# Structural Dynamics

Structural dynamics studies systems with mass and components capable of relative motion. Loads and inertia make displacement vary with time, so stiffness alone is insufficient to describe the response.

Finite element dynamic models add mass and, when needed, damping matrices to the assembled stiffness system. Modal, harmonic, and transient analyses answer different questions about the resulting motion.
