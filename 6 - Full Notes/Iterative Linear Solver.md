# Iterative Linear Solver

An iterative linear solver begins with an approximate vector and repeatedly applies an update intended to approach the solution of $Ax=b$. It produces usable approximations before exact arithmetic would finish.

These methods are attractive for large [[Sparse Matrix|sparse matrices]], where direct elimination may require excessive work or destroy sparsity. Convergence depends on the coefficient matrix and the chosen iteration.
