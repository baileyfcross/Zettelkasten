# Project Risk Analysis

Project risk analysis identifies conditions that could prevent a software effort from meeting its objectives and plans how the team will detect and respond to them. Examples in the source include schedule slips, excessive defect rates, incomplete requirements, dependency failures, staff changes, and inadequate skills or equipment.

A useful risk is tied to an observable signal and an action, not merely recorded in a list. Frequent status communication, continuous integration, short iterations, and early technical experiments shorten the time between a risk becoming real and the team discovering it.
