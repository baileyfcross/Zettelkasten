# Immersive Real Estate Walkthrough

An immersive real estate walkthrough lets a prospective buyer experience the scale and layout of a property through a navigable digital representation. It can present an existing remote property or a building that has not yet been constructed, reducing reliance on a viewer's ability to infer space from plans and still images.

The experience works best when viewpoint height, movement, room scale, and [[Field of View]] support natural spatial judgments. Photographic capture can emphasize visual realism, while a modeled environment can show alternate finishes, furnishings, or design options.

The walkthrough is a communication aid rather than a substitute for verification. [[Presence]] can make a representation persuasive even when capture gaps, simplified geometry, lighting, or navigation conceal important physical details.
