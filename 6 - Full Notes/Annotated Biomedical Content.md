# Annotated Biomedical Content

Annotated biomedical content enriches documents with structured concepts, entities, relationships, or indexing terms. Manual curators or automated text-mining systems can connect language in the document to controlled biomedical knowledge.

Annotations support concept-based search across synonyms and can expose relationships that keyword matching misses. Their usefulness depends on coverage and reliability: inconsistent manual labels and automated extraction errors can both distort retrieval. The system should preserve the annotation source and confidence so users can judge how strongly it supports a result.
