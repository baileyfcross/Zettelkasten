# Flat Module

A module M is flat when tensoring an exact sequence with M preserves exactness. Since tensor product is already right exact, flatness specifically prevents injections from being destroyed.

Every [[Projective Module]] is flat, and every localization S⁻¹R is flat over R. The [[Flatness Criterion via Tor]] characterizes flatness by the vanishing of higher Tor groups.
