# Convolution Integral

A convolution integral combines an input function with a response function by shifting one relative to the other, multiplying their overlapping values, and integrating. In one dimension it has the form $C(x)=\int f(x-x')g(x')dx'$.

The output records how the response spreads every point of the input. For example, convolving a sharp signal or image with a Gaussian response produces broadening or blur, modeling the finite resolution of a measuring system.
