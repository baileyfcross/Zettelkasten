# Viscous Flow

Viscous flow retains nonzero fluid viscosity and the relationship between shear stress and deformation rate. It can develop boundary layers and frictional losses that an inviscid model omits.

The primer contrasts this regime with its ideal potential-flow examples. Choosing a viscous or inviscid formulation changes the governing equations, element variables, and necessary boundary conditions.
