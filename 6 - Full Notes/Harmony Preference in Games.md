# Harmony Preference in Games

Harmony preference is the desire for cooperative, agreeable, and socially supportive play. It is associated with agreeableness in the source's model and contrasts with attraction to direct interpersonal conflict.

A harmony-oriented design can reward assistance and shared success without eliminating meaningful tension. Competitive games should not assume that every socially motivated player wants adversarial interaction.
