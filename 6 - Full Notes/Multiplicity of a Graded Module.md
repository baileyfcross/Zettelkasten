# Multiplicity of a Graded Module

For a non-Artinian finitely generated graded module M of dimension d, its multiplicity is obtained from the leading coefficient of its [[Hilbert Polynomial]] after multiplying by (d−1)!.

Multiplicity measures the leading rate of graded growth rather than the full Hilbert function. The associative formula decomposes it into contributions from top-dimensional prime components.
