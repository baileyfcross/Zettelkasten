# Recursive Self-Improvement

Recursive self-improvement occurs when an AI uses its current abilities to redesign its software or hardware so that it becomes better at making further improvements. Better programming, modeling, or engineering can feed back into the next redesign cycle.

The process is the proposed engine of an [[Intelligence Explosion]], but rapid growth is not guaranteed. Improvement can encounter bottlenecks in hardware, data, experimentation, or the difficulty of understanding the system's own architecture. Safety must nevertheless address the possibility because modifications could amplify both competence and defects in the objective being pursued.
