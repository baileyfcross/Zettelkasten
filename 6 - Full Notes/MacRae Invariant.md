# MacRae Invariant

The MacRae invariant associates an invertible fractional ideal to a finitely generated, coherently unfaithful module with a suitable finite resolution. It is formed as an alternating product of Fitting ideals from the resolution terms.

The invariant is independent of the chosen admissible resolution under the source's hypotheses. For certain positive-grade ideals of finite homological dimension, it recovers a greatest common divisor through the quotient module.
