# Robot Cultural Archetypes

Popular culture repeatedly casts robots into a small set of roles: threatening creation, faithful servant, comic companion, superior intellect, exploited worker, or aspiring person. These archetypes make unfamiliar technology emotionally legible, but they also import assumptions about rebellion, obedience, consciousness, and human uniqueness.

Real robots usually lack the motives, general understanding, and flexible competence implied by these characters. When policy and design discussions borrow the archetype without examining it, spectacular fictional dangers can displace immediate questions about safety, labor, privacy, or control. The mismatch is central to the [[Science Fiction Robotics Expectation Gap]].
