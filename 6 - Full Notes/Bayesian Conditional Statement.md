# Bayesian Conditional Statement

A Bayesian conditional statement uses a choice variable to condition which probabilistic model applies. Unlike a classical branch whose condition has one known value, the choice may itself have a probability distribution.

Marginalizing the choice makes every branch contribute in proportion to its probability. The construct therefore produces a mixture when the condition is uncertain and a single selected branch when it is known.
