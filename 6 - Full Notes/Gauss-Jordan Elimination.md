# Gauss-Jordan Elimination

Gauss-Jordan elimination continues row reduction above as well as below each pivot until the coefficient matrix reaches [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]].

The method can expose a solution directly without [[Back Substitution]]. Applying the same operations to $[A\mid I]$ reduces the left block to $I$ and produces a [[Matrix Inverse]] in the right block when $A$ is nonsingular.
