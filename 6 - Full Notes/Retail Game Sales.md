# Retail Game Sales

Retail game sales distribute a finished physical product through publishers, manufacturers, wholesalers, and stores. The model gives access to shelves and large marketing campaigns but divides revenue among several organizations.

Retail financing tends to favor projects with a predictable chance of repaying high development, manufacturing, and promotion costs. A funding publisher may therefore exercise substantial influence over audience, platform, scope, and content.
