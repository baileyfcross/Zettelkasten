# t-Distribution

The t-distribution describes a standardized sample mean when the population is normal but its variance is estimated from the same sample. It has heavier tails than the normal distribution because the denominator contains additional sampling uncertainty.

Degrees of freedom control the tail thickness. As sample size grows, the variance estimate stabilizes and the t-distribution approaches the normal distribution used by the [[Central Limit Theorem]].
