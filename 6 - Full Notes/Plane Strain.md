# Plane Strain

Plane strain is a two-dimensional idealization in which out-of-plane strain components are constrained to zero. It is appropriate for bodies whose geometry and loading vary little along a long constrained direction.

Plane strain and plane stress use similar mesh procedures but different constitutive matrices. Choosing between them changes the relationship among in-plane stress, strain, and Poisson effects.
