# Complex Matrix

A complex matrix is a matrix whose entries may be [[Complex Number|complex numbers]]. The usual rules of matrix addition and multiplication still apply.

Transpose alone is not enough for many geometric properties; one normally combines transpose with complex conjugation. This conjugate transpose defines [[Hermitian Matrix|Hermitian matrices]] and complex inner products.
