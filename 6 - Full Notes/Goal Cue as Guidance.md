# Goal Cue as Guidance

A goal cue places something associated with a player's current objective where it draws action toward a desired route. Visible resources, crafting possibilities, or signs of progress can make one path attractive without issuing a command.

The technique can also help players form their own goals. When a system exposes useful materials and recipes, it suggests projects that lead to exploration and further acquisition. Guidance then emerges from the relationship among player motivation, visible opportunity, and the rules that make the opportunity valuable.
