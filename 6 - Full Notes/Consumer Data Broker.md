# Consumer Data Broker

A consumer data broker aggregates information from multiple sources and resells or applies the combined records. Mobile applications and websites can supply behavioral data that brokers connect with other databases.

The resulting profiles may be used for identity verification, marketing, or fraud detection. Aggregation increases usefulness while making it harder for a person to know which data exist, how they were interpreted, and who can act on them.
