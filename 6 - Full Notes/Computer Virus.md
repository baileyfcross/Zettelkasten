# Computer Virus

A computer virus is malicious code that attaches to a host and reproduces by infecting additional files or programs when the host executes. Unlike a [[Computer Worm]], it generally depends on a carrier and some action that activates or transfers the infected host.

The [[Virus Component Model]] separates replication, triggering, and payload behavior, making clear that a virus's method of spreading is distinct from the harmful action it eventually performs.
