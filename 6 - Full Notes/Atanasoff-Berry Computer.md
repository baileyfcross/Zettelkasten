# Atanasoff-Berry Computer

The Atanasoff-Berry Computer was an experimental electronic binary machine designed to solve systems of linear equations. Vacuum tubes performed arithmetic while a rotating mechanism governed its fixed sequence of operations.

John Atanasoff and Clifford Berry demonstrated that electronic calculation at high speed was feasible, but the machine was neither completed nor general-purpose. It represents one path from specialized calculation toward electronic computing.
