# Acoustic Variability

Acoustic variability is the change in a speech signal caused by speakers, accents, rate, emotion, microphones, distance, noise, and room conditions. The same word can therefore produce substantially different feature sequences.

A robust [[Acoustic Model]] needs training examples that cover the expected variation. [[Far-Field Speech Recognition]] is especially difficult because reverberation and competing sounds distort the target voice.
