# Cultural and Contextual Color Meaning

Color meaning is not universal. A hue can suggest danger, celebration, purity, authority, nature, or mourning depending on culture, surrounding symbols, and the situation in which the player encounters it.

Environment artists should treat associations as hypotheses to test with the target audience. Shape, value contrast, text, and sound should reinforce critical signals so progress or hazards do not depend on color perception alone.
