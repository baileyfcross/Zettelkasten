# Modus Tollens

Modus tollens derives ¬p from the premises p → q and ¬q. If p were true, [[Modus Ponens]] would force q, contradicting the second premise.

The rule applies equally after a universally quantified implication is specialized in [[Predicate Logic]]. It must not be confused with denying the antecedent, which does not form a [[Valid Argument]].
