# Electric Current

Electric current is the rate of charge flow, $I=dQ/dt$. Conventional current follows the direction a positive charge would move, which is opposite electron drift in a metal. In a conductor, current also depends on charge-carrier density, carrier charge, cross-sectional area, and drift speed.
