# Direct Player Guidance

Direct player guidance explicitly tells a player what action, destination, or objective is relevant. Instructions, dialogue, diagrams, contextual prompts, mission assignments, maps, and route indicators all make the guidance recognizable as guidance.

Its quality depends on immediacy, scarcity, brevity, and clarity. Information should appear when it becomes useful, include only what must be understood now, and explain consequences that are not obvious. Directness can remove uncertainty efficiently, but excessive guidance competes with play and can train the player to follow prompts instead of reading the game world.
