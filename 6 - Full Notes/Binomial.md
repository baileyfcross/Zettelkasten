# Binomial

A binomial is a [[Polynomial]] with two terms, such as (x+3) or (a^2-b^2). The terms are joined by addition or subtraction at the outermost level.

Products of binomials can be expanded by distributing every term, sometimes remembered through the [[FOIL Method]]. Particular binomial structures generate important identities: conjugates (a+b) and (a-b) produce a difference of squares, while cubed binomials produce four-term expansions. Binomials also commonly appear as factors of quadratic polynomials.
