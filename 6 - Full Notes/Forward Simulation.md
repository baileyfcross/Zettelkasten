# Forward Simulation

Forward simulation samples or predicts effects from specified causes using a probabilistic description. Known initial conditions and inputs are propagated through the model to produce a distribution over possible outcomes.

The operation is not a different kind of model from diagnosis or control. A Bayesian program can support all three by changing which variables are known, searched, and marginalized in the question.
