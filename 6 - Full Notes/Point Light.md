# Point Light

A point light emits illumination outward from a particular location in the world. The direction to the light therefore changes from one surface point to another, and its contribution can weaken with distance.

Real-time shading compares the light direction with a surface normal and view direction through a reflection model such as the [[Phong Reflection Model]].
