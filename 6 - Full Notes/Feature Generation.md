# Feature Generation

Feature generation creates new variables that describe data in ways not already present in the original feature set. Image descriptors, mined sequence patterns, graph neighborhoods, and domain-specific measurements are examples.

Generation may be manual, automated, or learned. Because a large candidate pool can increase computation and overfitting, it is commonly paired with [[Feature Selection]] or an objective that favors compact, relevant representations.
