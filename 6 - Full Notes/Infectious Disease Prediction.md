# Infectious Disease Prediction

Infectious disease prediction uses health records and surveillance observations to estimate where or when disease activity may increase. Combining sources can reveal temporal and geographic patterns earlier than isolated reports.

Predictions depend on timely reporting, consistent definitions, and awareness of bias in who is observed. A model supports monitoring and response but does not replace epidemiological interpretation.
