# Robotic Companionship Ethics

Robotic companionship uses responsive movement, sound, touch, or appearance to produce comfort and engagement, especially for people who are isolated or living with dementia. A device such as an animal-like care robot can respond to voice and stroking without the practical demands of a living animal.

The benefit creates an ethical tension. A person may genuinely feel calmer while the relationship remains technologically staged, and families or institutions may use the device to justify less human contact. Evaluation should ask whether the robot supplements care and connection or becomes a convenient substitute for them.
