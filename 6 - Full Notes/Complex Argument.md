# Complex Argument

The argument of a nonzero complex number is the directed angle from the positive real axis to its point in the complex plane. For $z=x+iy$, the ratio $y/x$ helps determine the angle, but the quadrant must also be considered.

Arguments are not unique because adding an integer multiple of $2\pi$ gives the same direction. Choosing a standard interval defines a principal argument, while the unrestricted family is used when computing powers, roots, and polar products.
