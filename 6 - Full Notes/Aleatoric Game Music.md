# Aleatoric Game Music

Aleatoric game music introduces controlled chance into musical pitch, rhythm, articulation, form, or performance. The composer defines a field of acceptable possibilities rather than fixing every event in one immutable sequence.

If an aleatoric performance is recorded as a single audio file, its variability becomes fixed on playback. An interactive system preserves the idea more fully by selecting among fragments or instructions at runtime. The result can reduce obvious repetition, but its constraints must still protect the score's style, continuity, and dramatic purpose.
