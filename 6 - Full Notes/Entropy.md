# Entropy

Entropy is a state quantity that measures energy dispersal and, statistically, the number of microscopic arrangements compatible with a macrostate. For reversible heat transfer, $dS=\delta Q_{rev}/T$; statistically, $S=k_B\ln\Omega$. The entropy of an isolated system tends toward a maximum as overwhelmingly probable macrostates are approached.
