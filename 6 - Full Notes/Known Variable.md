# Known Variable

A known variable is a variable assigned an observed or imposed value when a Bayesian question is instantiated. These values form the evidence on the right side of the conditional expression.

Known does not mean the variable is always observable in every use of the model. Its role belongs to the current question, and the same variable can become searched when the inference direction changes.
