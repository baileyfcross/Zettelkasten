# Interlingual Machine Translation

Interlingual machine translation converts the source into a language-independent representation of its meaning and generates the target language from that representation. In principle, adding a new language requires one analyzer and one generator rather than a separate transfer system for every language pair.

The difficulty lies in constructing an interlingua rich enough to preserve everything a translation may need. Ambiguity, context, style, and culture resist a complete formal representation, while deriving that representation requires reliable syntactic and semantic analysis. The idea remains the apex of the [[Vauquois Triangle]], even when practical systems stop at lower levels.
