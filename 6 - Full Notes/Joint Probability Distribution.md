# Joint Probability Distribution

A joint probability distribution assigns probabilities to combinations of values across several variables. It represents every probabilistic relationship the description makes available and is normalized over the full joint state space.

Bayesian programs rarely store an unrestricted joint table. They decompose it into smaller conditional factors using justified independence assumptions, then reconstruct the terms required for a particular question.
