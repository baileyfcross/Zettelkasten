# Arc Length of a Parametric Curve

For a differentiable [[Parametric Curve]] $r(t)$ on $[a,b]$, its arc length is

$$L=\int_a^b\|r'(t)\|\,dt.$$

In the plane this becomes $\int_a^b\sqrt{(x'(t))^2+(y'(t))^2}\,dt$. The integrand is the speed of traversal, so a regular reparameterization that preserves the same trace does not change the geometric length.
