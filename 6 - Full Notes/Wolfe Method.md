# Wolfe Method

Wolfe's method solves a quadratic program by expressing its optimality and complementary-slackness conditions as a modified linear-programming tableau.

Artificial variables establish an initial basis, while restricted pivot choices preserve the paired slackness relationships. Under the method's convexity or concavity assumptions, the final feasible stationary point is optimal.
