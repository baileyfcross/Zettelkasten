# Blender Push and Pull Tool

Push/Pull moves selected components radially toward or away from a common center, producing a scale-like deformation without being identical to ordinary scaling. The result depends on the selected pivot and the spatial distribution of the vertices.

Its operator settings expose distance and proportional falloff after the initial drag. The tool can taper or broaden a region, while [[Blender Shrink and Flatten Tool|Shrink/Flatten]] instead follows local surface normals.
