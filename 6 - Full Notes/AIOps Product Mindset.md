# AIOps Product Mindset

An AIOps product mindset begins with an operational problem and measurable outcome rather than with a desire to deploy AI. Work is organized around users, prioritized use cases, costs, benefits, feedback, and a technical roadmap that evolves from a small validated implementation.

Centralizing related efforts reduces duplicated experiments and unmanaged technical debt. Objectives and key results communicate value such as higher availability, observable releases, faster recovery, or safe automatic rollback even when the system does not directly generate revenue.
