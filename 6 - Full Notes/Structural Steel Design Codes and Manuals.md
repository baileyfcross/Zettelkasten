# Structural Steel Design Codes and Manuals

Steel building design operates through a coordinated set of documents rather than one isolated formula collection. The building code establishes legal minimums, the load standard defines environmental and occupancy actions, the steel specification states strength and stability requirements, and the steel construction manual supplies section properties, design tables, and practical connection aids.

These documents divide responsibilities but must be read together. A member selected from a manual table is only adequate when its demand was formed with the proper load combinations, its applicable limit states were checked, and its detailing follows the governing specification. Welding and bolting bring additional standards, while seismic systems require special provisions. Code compliance is therefore a traceable chain from hazard definition through analysis, member design, detailing, fabrication, and inspection.
