# Art Direction Versus Resolution Switching

Resolution switching supplies several encodings of essentially the same visual composition so the browser can choose enough pixels for a slot and device density. Art direction supplies meaningfully different crops or compositions because layout changes what should be shown.

The browser can automate resolution choice from descriptors and conditions, but it cannot infer the editorial intent of a crop. Mixing both use cases is valid: a selected art-directed source can itself expose multiple resolution candidates.
