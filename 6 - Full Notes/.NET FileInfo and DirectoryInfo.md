# .NET FileInfo and DirectoryInfo

`FileInfo` and `DirectoryInfo` are object-oriented views of individual files and directories. Their properties expose metadata such as existence, length, timestamps, and parent locations, while their methods perform operations on the represented path.

The instance-based model is useful when several facts or operations concern the same resource because the path travels with the object. It complements the static `File` and `Directory` classes rather than replacing them; the best choice depends on whether the work is resource-centered or a single convenience operation.
