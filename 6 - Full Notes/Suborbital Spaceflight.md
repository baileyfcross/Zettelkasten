# Suborbital Spaceflight

Suborbital spaceflight follows a trajectory that reaches space but does not gain the horizontal velocity required to remain in orbit. The vehicle rises, provides a short period of microgravity or high-altitude observation, and returns to Earth along the same overall flight.

Because it avoids orbital velocity, suborbital flight can use a smaller vehicle and became the initial target for private passenger ventures. The X-Prize required a nongovernment craft capable of carrying three people to above 100 kilometers twice within two weeks, encouraging SpaceShipOne. Lower energy does not remove the safety and cost challenges of crewed rocket flight.
