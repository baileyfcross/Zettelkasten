# Latitude and Longitude Graticules

Latitude measures angular position north or south of the Equator, and its parallels form circles around Earth. Longitude measures angular position east or west of the prime meridian, and its meridians join the poles. One minute of latitude corresponds historically to one nautical mile, while the surface distance represented by a degree of longitude shrinks as the meridians converge poleward.
