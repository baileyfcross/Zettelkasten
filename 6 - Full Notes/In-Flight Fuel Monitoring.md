# In-Flight Fuel Monitoring

Fuel monitoring compares actual remaining fuel with the planned profile as the flight progresses. Plotting or computing the consumption trend toward the destination reveals whether the aircraft is expected to land with its alternate and reserve requirements intact. If the projection falls below the safe threshold, the crew must shorten, divert, or terminate the trip before the reserve becomes ordinary trip fuel.
