# Inspection Preparation

Inspection preparation is the required individual reading performed before a formal inspection meeting. Reviewers examine a bounded artifact with a checklist, record suspected defects, and report the time spent so the meeting begins with independent evidence.

The source recommends limiting the review to an amount that can be examined carefully and canceling the meeting when preparation was not completed. This prevents a scheduled gathering from substituting for the close reading that finds most defects.
