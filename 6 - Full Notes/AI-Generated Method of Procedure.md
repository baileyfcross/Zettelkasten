# AI-Generated Method of Procedure

An AI-generated method of procedure turns a described network change into an ordered operational document. A useful MOP includes purpose and scope, prerequisites, implementation commands, verification checks, rollback instructions, communication points, and criteria for stopping the change.

Generation accelerates drafting and can expose missing steps, but the model does not know the live environment unless that evidence is supplied. Device syntax, interface names, dependencies, timings, and rollback feasibility must be confirmed. [[Network Method of Procedure Review Gate]] keeps the draft from becoming an implicit execution authorization.
