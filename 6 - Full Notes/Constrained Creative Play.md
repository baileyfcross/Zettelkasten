# Constrained Creative Play

Constrained creative play invites creation under explicit physical, aesthetic, economic, or rule-based limits. The restrictions turn making into a problem whose solutions can be compared or evaluated.

Constraints can focus imagination and connect expression to the larger game. They should produce meaningful tradeoffs rather than arbitrarily preventing actions the creative premise appears to promise.
