# Good-Enough Robot Substitution

A robot does not need to equal the full range of human ability to change a job. It only needs to perform a bounded task well enough once the environment and surrounding process are redesigned around its strengths and weaknesses.

People can supply exceptions, perception, or recovery while customers perform self-service steps and one worker supervises several machines. As organizations learn this arrangement, the threshold for useful automation falls. The comparison is therefore not a general robot versus a general person, but a configured system versus the cost and quality of the previous workflow.
