# Computational Design Across Disciplines

Computational design applies knowledge of representations, methods, and machines within any field where a computation can create value. The practitioner need not be a computer scientist but must understand both the technology and the domain being served.

The work is increasingly centered on systems, prototypes, and community concerns rather than low-level coding. Domain-specific tools can expand participation while making judgment about context even more important.
