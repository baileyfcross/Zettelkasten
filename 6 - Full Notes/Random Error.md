# Random Error

Random error produces unpredictable scatter among repeated measurements because small uncontrolled influences vary from trial to trial. Repetition and averaging can reduce its effect on an estimate, while the spread of the observations helps quantify uncertainty. Random error primarily limits precision rather than shifting every measurement in the same direction.
