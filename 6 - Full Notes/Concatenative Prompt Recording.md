# Concatenative Prompt Recording

Concatenative prompt recording builds spoken responses by joining prerecorded words or phrases. The pieces retain a human voice, but joins can sound unnatural when pitch, timing, or recording conditions do not match.

The method works best for predictable templates and carefully recorded fragments. [[Prompt Tapering]] can make fixed and variable pieces connect more smoothly.
