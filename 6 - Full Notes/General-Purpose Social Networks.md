# General-Purpose Social Networks

A general-purpose social network combines identity, relationship, publishing, messaging, and media features for a broad audience rather than one profession or interest. The source places Facebook, Twitter/X, Mastodon, Myspace, and WeChat in this category even though their ownership and architectures differ.

The broad feature surface makes these networks common integration targets, but it also exposes applications to changing provider policies and APIs. A client that hard-codes one platform's concepts may be expensive to migrate after an acquisition, shutdown, or protocol change. [[Open Social Standards and Federation]] addresses that risk through portable formats and protocols, while [[Vertical Social Networks]] deliberately narrows the community instead.
