# Rational Expression

A rational expression is a quotient of algebraic expressions, commonly polynomials:

$$\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)},\qquad Q(x)\neq0.$$

It generalizes a numerical [[Fraction]] while retaining the restriction that its denominator cannot be zero. Operations follow fraction rules, but polynomial factorization is often required before cancellation or construction of a common denominator. Equivalent-looking simplified forms must preserve the original excluded values because cancelling a factor does not make the original denominator defined there.
