# Gibbs Phenomenon

The Gibbs phenomenon is the persistent overshoot and undershoot produced by a finite Fourier sum near a jump discontinuity. Adding more terms narrows the region containing the oscillation but does not drive the peak overshoot to zero.

Away from the jump the approximation improves, while at the discontinuity the complete Fourier series converges to the average of the one-sided limiting values under the usual conditions. The effect distinguishes pointwise convergence from uniform visual agreement near sharp edges.
