# Quadrature Formula

A quadrature formula approximates an integral by a weighted sum of function values,

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\approx\sum_{i=0}^{n}w_i f(x_i).$$

The nodes $x_i$ and weights $w_i$ determine which polynomials are integrated exactly. [[Newton-Cotes Formula|Newton-Cotes formulas]] prescribe equally spaced nodes, whereas [[Gaussian Quadrature]] chooses both nodes and weights to obtain greater algebraic exactness.
