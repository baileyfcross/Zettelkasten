# Digital Game Distribution

Digital game distribution delivers a finished game electronically through an online store or portal. It removes physical inventory and retailer shelf constraints, allowing smaller teams and niche products to reach buyers directly.

The platform still takes a share, and the developer may have to fund discovery and marketing. Lower expected prices and a crowded catalog can offset the greater creative and logistical freedom.
