# Linear Regression Coefficient

A linear regression coefficient is the estimated change in a continuous outcome associated with a one-unit increase in a predictor while the other model terms remain fixed. For an [[Indicator Variable]], it is the adjusted mean difference from the reference group.

Its sign gives direction and its magnitude remains in outcome units. The standard error, confidence interval, and p-value describe estimation uncertainty, while subject-matter context determines whether the change is important.
