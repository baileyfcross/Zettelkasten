# Nonnegative Matrix Factorization Clustering

Nonnegative matrix factorization approximates a nonnegative data matrix as the product of two lower-rank nonnegative matrices. One factor represents observations in a latent component space, while the other describes how original features contribute to those components.

The nonnegativity constraint produces additive representations and often makes components easier to interpret than signed factors. Cluster assignments can be taken from dominant component weights or coupled directly to a partitioning objective.
