# Thermoelectric Figure of Merit

Figure of merit $Z$ combines the useful [[Seebeck Coefficient]] with unwanted electrical resistance and thermal conduction of a thermoelectric pair. The source expresses it as a squared differential coefficient divided by the product of total electrical and thermal paths; higher $Z$ improves attainable efficiency. The dimensionless $ZT$ includes operating temperature. No single material property can be optimized in isolation.
