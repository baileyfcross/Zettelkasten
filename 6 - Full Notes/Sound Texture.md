# Sound Texture

Sound texture is the background layer of audio that shapes the player's immediate relationship to a game world. Adding, removing, muffling, or foregrounding a layer can change tension and focus without conveying a discrete message.

A tune that stops when movement stops can turn silence into suspense. Muting the score while movement sounds become prominent can instead emphasize embodiment, speed, or a state of [[Flow]].
