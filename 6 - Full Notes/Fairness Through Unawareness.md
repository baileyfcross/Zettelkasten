# Fairness Through Unawareness

Fairness through unawareness is the strategy of withholding a protected characteristic from an algorithm so the system cannot explicitly use it. The approach aims to make the decision formally blind to traits such as race or gender.

Unawareness is insufficient when other variables act as proxies or when historical outcomes already reflect unequal conditions. It can also prevent measurement of whether results disadvantage a protected group. Fairness may require knowledge of group effects and, in some cases, corrective action. The strategy therefore addresses direct discrimination but not the full problem of [[Algorithmic Bias]].
