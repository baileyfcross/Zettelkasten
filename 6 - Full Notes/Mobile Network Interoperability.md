# Mobile Network Interoperability

Mobile devices often use both Wi-Fi and cellular networks, but the two systems differ in access, pricing, coverage, and governance. Switching between them does not fully realize the open interoperability envisioned for the Internet.

Carrier lock-in and incompatible policies can constrain a technically universal computer. Network convergence therefore remains incomplete even when one device speaks both technologies.
