# Seebeck Coefficient

The Seebeck coefficient relates a material's induced potential gradient to its imposed temperature gradient, with units of volts per kelvin. For a p-type and n-type pair, their differential coefficient determines the junction voltage across a temperature range. The source notes opposite signs for the two types, helping their useful voltages add. See [[Seebeck Effect]] and [[Thermoelectric Figure of Merit]].
