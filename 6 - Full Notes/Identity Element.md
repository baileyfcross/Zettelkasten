# Identity Element

An identity element leaves every value unchanged under a particular operation. Zero is the additive identity because

$$a+0=0+a=a,$$

and one is the multiplicative identity because

$$a\cdot1=1\cdot a=a.$$

Identity is operation-specific: zero does not act as the multiplicative identity, and one does not act as the additive identity. These elements support inverse operations. An [[Opposite Number]] combines with its original through addition to reach zero, while a [[Reciprocal]] combines through multiplication to reach one.
