# Data Reidentification

Data reidentification is the process of connecting records that were treated as anonymous back to an identifiable person. Linkage with auxiliary datasets, rare combinations of attributes, or recognizable behavioral patterns can restore an identity without a direct name.

Reidentification makes anonymity a matter of degree and context. A release must be evaluated against the other information and analytical capabilities likely to be available, not only against identifiers removed from the released table.
