# Constant-Acceleration Equations

For one-dimensional motion with constant acceleration, the variables displacement $s$, initial velocity $u$, final velocity $v$, acceleration $a$, and time $t$ satisfy equations including $v=u+at$, $s=ut+\tfrac12at^2$, and $v^2=u^2+2as$. Each equation omits one variable, so the known and required quantities determine which form is useful.
