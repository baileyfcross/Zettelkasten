# Log-Normal Survival Distribution

The log-normal survival distribution assumes that the logarithm of event time follows a normal distribution. Its hazard can change over time, making it more flexible than the constant-hazard exponential model.

A lower AIC than an exponential fit favors it relatively, but visual agreement with Kaplan–Meier curves and scientific plausibility still matter. A good relative fit does not correct unreliable or selectively observed event times.
