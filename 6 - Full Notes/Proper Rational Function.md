# Proper Rational Function

A proper rational function is a quotient of polynomials whose numerator has lower degree than its denominator. If the numerator's degree is at least as large, [[Polynomial Long Division]] first separates a polynomial part and a proper remainder. Properness is the starting condition for [[Partial Fraction Decomposition]].
