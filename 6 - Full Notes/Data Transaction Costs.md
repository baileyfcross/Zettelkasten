# Data Transaction Costs

Data transaction costs are the resources required to find relevant data, identify a rights holder, evaluate quality and permission, negotiate terms, and enforce an agreement. These costs can prevent beneficial exchange even when both sides would gain.

Standardized property forms can reduce some uncertainty, but creating new exclusive rights can also multiply the permissions that must be cleared. The design question is whether a rule lowers coordination costs across the whole market.
