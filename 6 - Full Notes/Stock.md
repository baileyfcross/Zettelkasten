# Stock

A stock is a container that accumulates and holds a [[Resource]]. Its state is the amount currently stored, and it may have a maximum capacity or be effectively unbounded.

Stocks make time visible in a system because inflows and outflows change their contents at particular rates. Bank balances, health pools, inventories, and populations can all function as stocks in a systemic model.
