# Error Sum of Squares Clustering

Error sum of squares clustering measures the squared deviations of observations from their cluster means. Ward's method chooses each merge to produce the smallest increase in this error.

The criterion rewards compact groups and is affected by measurement scale. Standardizing attributes can materially change the resulting hierarchy.
