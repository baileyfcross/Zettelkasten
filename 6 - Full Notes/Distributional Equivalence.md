# Distributional Equivalence

Two rows or columns are distributionally equivalent when their normalized profiles are identical. In correspondence analysis, identical profiles occupy the same position and have zero profile distance. Adding their counts together preserves their profile and therefore does not alter the mutual distances that depend on that profile.

This permits exact-profile aggregation before an expensive analysis and can make a display easier to interpret. Near-equivalent profiles require an approximation judgment: combining them changes the data slightly, so the accepted loss should be checked against the study's resolution.
