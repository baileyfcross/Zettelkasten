# Intertwined Game Attributes

Intertwined game attributes produce their meaningful effect jointly, so evaluating either one alone gives an incomplete comparison. Weapon damage and attack frequency, for example, combine into damage per unit of time; pallet capacity and trip duration combine into throughput.

The designer converts the component attributes to compatible units and derives a shared comparison measure. A cooldown may first become attacks per minute, then combine with damage. The derived measure is often more useful for internal balance and weighting than for player display, especially when it produces decimals or hides the distinct feel created by each component.
