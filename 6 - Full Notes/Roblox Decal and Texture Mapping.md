# Roblox Decal and Texture Mapping

A Roblox Decal places one image on a selected face of a part, whereas a Texture repeats an image across the surface. Both use an uploaded asset identifier, but they serve different visual purposes: decals work well for signs and single markings, while textures cover larger areas with a tileable pattern.

The image remains distinct from the part's geometry and material, allowing the visual layer to be changed without rebuilding the object. Because uploaded images enter Roblox's asset system, their identifiers and availability also depend on [[Roblox Asset Identifier and Moderation]].
