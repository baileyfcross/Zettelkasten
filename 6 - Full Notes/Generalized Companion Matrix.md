# Generalized Companion Matrix

A generalized companion construction replaces a scalar polynomial with a polynomial matrix $P(t)=C_0+C_1t+\cdots+C_dt^d$. Block matrices $A$ and $B$ are arranged so that a nonzero vector in the null space of $P(t)$ corresponds to one in the null space of $A-tB$.

The construction linearizes a polynomial rank problem. Values of $t$ at which $P(t)$ loses row rank become generalized eigenvalues of the associated [[Matrix Pencil]].
