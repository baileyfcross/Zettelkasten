# Planning Poker

Planning Poker is a group estimation technique in which participants reveal estimates simultaneously, then discuss the assumptions behind the largest differences. The group repeats the process until it reaches enough shared understanding to choose an estimate.

Simultaneous revelation limits anchoring by the first or most senior speaker. The valuable output is often the conversation that exposes different interpretations, risks, and implementation knowledge.
