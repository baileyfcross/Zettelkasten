# Composite Cowling Fabrication

Custom shell components were formed by laying carbon or glass reinforcement over molds, impregnating the layers with resin, vacuum-bagging them against the tooling, and curing them with controlled heat. Layer count and thickness varied with the part's structural need. Sanding and secondary resin work finished the shapes that enclosed the new engine and ducts.
