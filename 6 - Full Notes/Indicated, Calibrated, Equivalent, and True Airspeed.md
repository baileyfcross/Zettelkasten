# Indicated, Calibrated, Equivalent, and True Airspeed

Indicated airspeed comes directly from the airspeed indicator's pitot-static measurement. Calibrated airspeed corrects instrument and position error, equivalent airspeed further corrects compressibility effects, and true airspeed corrects for the actual atmospheric density. The sequence is often remembered as ICET, and true airspeed becomes ground velocity only after the wind vector is included through [[Navigation Vectors and Ground Velocity]].
