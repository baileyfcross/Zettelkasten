# Cancer Genomics Hub

The Cancer Genomics Hub was a large repository and distribution system for cancer-related genomic data. It supported controlled access to sequence collections used by major research programs and moved large datasets to authorized analysts.

The case demonstrates that scientific repositories need scalable transfer, metadata, access control, monitoring, and long-term stewardship in addition to storage capacity.
