# Satellite Ephemeris

A satellite ephemeris is the set of orbital parameters from which a receiver computes the satellite's position and velocity at a particular time. GPS ground stations estimate these parameters from observations and predict the orbit forward before uploading the data for broadcast. The prediction must account for effects such as Earth's equatorial bulge, lunar gravity, and solar radiation pressure. A range measurement is useful only when the transmitting point in space is also known accurately.
