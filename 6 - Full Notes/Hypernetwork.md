# Hypernetwork

A hypernetwork generalizes an ordinary graph by allowing one hyperlink to connect more than two nodes. The network is defined by a node set and a family of nonempty node subsets that serve as its hyperlinks.

This representation is useful when a relationship belongs to a group as a whole rather than decomposing naturally into independent pairs. Pairwise projection can lose that joint structure.
