# Flat Ring Homomorphism

A ring homomorphism R → S is flat when S is a [[Flat Module]] over R through that map. Extending scalars from R to S then preserves exact sequences of R-modules.

Flatness is transitive: if S is flat over R and an S-module M is flat over S, then M is flat over R. Localization maps are fundamental flat homomorphisms.
