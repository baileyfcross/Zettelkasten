# Sprint Review

The Sprint Review is an inspection of the working [[Product Increment]] by the Scrum Team and relevant stakeholders. In game development, participants play or observe the game, discuss what changed, and use the result to adapt the Product Backlog.

Acceptance is based on demonstrated behavior and the applicable [[Definition of Done]], not on reports that component tasks are nearly complete.
