# Axis-Parallel Subspace Clustering

Axis-parallel subspace clustering searches for groups that are dense or compact in subsets of the original attributes. Each discovered cluster can be described by the dimensions on which its members agree.

The representation is interpretable because selected dimensions retain their original meaning. It cannot directly capture a group aligned with an oblique linear combination unless that structure also appears in an axis-aligned projection.
