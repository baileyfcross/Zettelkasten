# Immersive Analytics

Immersive analytics uses an interactive three-dimensional environment to explore data through spatial arrangement, movement, and direct manipulation. It expands the representational palette beyond a flat chart, allowing an analyst to move around a structure, examine relationships from different viewpoints, and coordinate multiple visual scales.

The additional dimension is not automatically informative. Abstract, high-dimensional, varied, or very large data can still produce [[Data Overload]], occlusion, and misleading spatial metaphors. A useful design must decide which variables deserve position, size, color, time, or interaction rather than filling space merely because it is available.

Other modalities can carry complementary dimensions: [[Sonification]] can reveal patterns outside the current view, and a [[Haptic Display]] can make thresholds or structure tangible. The central problem remains analytical—choosing representations and interactions that lead to insight when the user may not yet know what pattern to seek.
