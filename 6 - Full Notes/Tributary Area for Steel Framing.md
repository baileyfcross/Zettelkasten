# Tributary Area for Steel Framing

Tributary area assigns a portion of a loaded surface to each supporting member. A beam generally receives load from half the spacing to the adjacent member on each side, converting an area load into a line load. Its end reactions then become concentrated loads on girders, whose tributary area extends through the beams they support. Columns accumulate the reactions from connected framing over successive levels.

The method follows the actual [[Gravity Load Path in Steel Buildings]], so irregular bays, edges, openings, cantilevers, and transfer framing require geometry-specific boundaries. Tributary area is also used for live-load reduction because the chance of maximum live load occupying a large influence area is lower than for a small area. Load tracing must preserve both magnitude and point of application so eccentricity is not lost.
