# Beam Bearing and Web Limit States

Concentrated loads and reactions can create local failure near a steel beam's flange and web even when global bending and shear are adequate. Relevant limit states include flange local bending, web local yielding, web crippling, web compression buckling, and web sidesway buckling. Which checks apply depends on whether force acts through one flange or both and on distance from the member end.

Increasing bearing length spreads force and can raise local resistance. When geometry cannot provide enough strength, transverse bearing stiffeners transfer load through a more direct compression path and must themselves be designed as attached plates or column-like elements. The detail also needs adequate welds and fit. These checks explain why a high-capacity beam can still require reinforcement at a support or point load.
