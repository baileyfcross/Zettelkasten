# Crab Angle from Wind Components

Crab angle is the difference between aircraft heading and desired ground track required to counter crosswind. For a fixed track, it can be obtained from the inverse sine of crosswind component divided by true airspeed. The aircraft points into the wind so the sideways air-velocity component cancels drift while the remaining component advances along track.
