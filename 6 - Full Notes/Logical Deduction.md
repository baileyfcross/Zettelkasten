# Logical Deduction

A kind of computation that can be derived from a premise and lead into a conclusion

A formal sequence of justified deductions is a proof.

More precisely, deduction preserves truth from accepted [[Premise|premises]] to a [[Conclusion]]. Rules such as [[Modus Ponens]] can be chained into a formal proof, where each line is either a premise or follows logically from earlier lines.
