# Open Story

An open story is an emergent narrative with relatively few controlled outcomes and substantial room for players to create their own experiences. The creator facilitates a space in which stories can arise rather than determining which story must be delivered.

Open stories trade authorial control for variety and unpredictability. They may emerge from complex systems, player-created goals, shared authorship, or multiplayer interaction, and they need not resemble a polished traditional plot to be worth retelling.
