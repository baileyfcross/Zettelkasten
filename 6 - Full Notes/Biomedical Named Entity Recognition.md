# Biomedical Named Entity Recognition

Biomedical named entity recognition locates and classifies mentions such as genes, proteins, diseases, chemicals, and anatomical structures in scientific text. It converts spans of language into typed entities that can support indexing and later relation extraction.

Biomedical terminology is difficult because names vary, overlap, and change with context. Dictionary methods offer coverage of known terms, rule-based methods encode local clues, and machine-learning methods can learn patterns from annotated corpora. Recognition quality should be measured at the correct span and type, since a partially identified name can break downstream linking.
