# Lorentz Force

The magnetic force on a charge $q$ moving with velocity $v$ is $F=qv\times B$, with magnitude $|q|vB\sin\theta$. It is perpendicular to both motion and field, so it changes direction without doing work or changing speed. Combined electric and magnetic fields give the full force $q(E+v\times B)$.
