# Scrum Team

A Scrum Team combines a [[Product Owner]], [[Scrum Master]], and [[Development Team]] around one product goal. The roles divide accountability for value, process improvement, and delivery without dividing ownership of the outcome.

In game development, the team is cross-discipline so that playable value can move from idea through implementation, art, audio, design, and testing inside the same Sprint.
