# Blender Surface Objects

Surface objects describe two-dimensional parametric sheets embedded in three-dimensional space. Their control structure can form smooth patches without requiring the artist to manipulate every final display polygon.

They are useful while a form benefits from mathematical continuity, but mesh conversion may be required for common game workflows. The conversion freezes the evaluated surface into explicit topology for UV mapping, optimization, and interchange.
