# Compressed Hydrogen Storage

Compressed-gas storage holds hydrogen in high-pressure cylinders or other pressure-rated vessels. The source describes pressures in the tens of megapascals and notes that tanks can be bulky and expensive for the useful energy held. Pressure lets gas be carried without cryogenic cooling, but vessel mass, compression work, leakage control, and [[Hydrogen Safety]] shape the system comparison.
