# Positive AI Ethics

Positive AI ethics asks what good lives and good societies intelligent technology should help create. It complements a negative ethics focused on preventing harm, banning unacceptable uses, or complying with minimum constraints.

The question cannot be answered by technical experts or efficiency measures alone because ideas of flourishing and meaningful life are plural and politically contested. Democratic participation is needed to decide which capabilities deserve support and which forms of dependence or inequality should be avoided. Positive ethics also treats regulation as potentially enabling: rules and incentives can direct innovation toward socially valuable ends rather than merely restrict it.
