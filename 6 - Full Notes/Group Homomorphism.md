# Group Homomorphism

A group homomorphism preserves the operation: f(ab) = f(a)f(b). It necessarily preserves the identity, inverses, and integer powers.

Its kernel is a normal subgroup, its image is a subgroup, and its quotient by the kernel is isomorphic to the image.
