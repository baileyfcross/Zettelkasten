# Healthcare Fraud

Healthcare fraud is intentional deception within the financing or delivery of care for unauthorized benefit. It can involve claims, providers, beneficiaries, services, or coordinated networks and must be distinguished from mistakes, waste, and abuse.

Unlike many payment-card settings, the person receiving a service may not be the perpetrator, and a claim can look plausible in isolation. Detection therefore uses clinical, billing, temporal, and relational context. An analytic flag is evidence for review rather than proof of intent, because unusual care can also reflect a legitimate complex case.
