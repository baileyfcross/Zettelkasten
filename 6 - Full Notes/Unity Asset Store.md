# Unity Asset Store

The Unity Asset Store distributes reusable models, textures, scripts, tools, and other project content. Store items may be free or paid and may arrive as individual resources or coordinated collections.

Acquiring an item does not automatically make it suitable for a project. Its license, rendering setup, scale, dependencies, and import contents still require review. In the workflow described by the book, store acquisitions are downloaded and imported through package management before appearing as usable [[Unity Asset|assets]].
