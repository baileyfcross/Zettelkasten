# Game Music Contract Negotiation

Game music contract negotiation is a collaborative search for terms that let the project obtain appropriate music while keeping the composer's work sustainable. When a budget cannot support the initial [[Game Music Production Estimate]], the parties can reduce nonessential assets, shorten cues, replace original themes with variations, or reduce costly interactivity.

Other tradeoffs include royalties, a limited license, future work, publishing interests, promotional rights, and prominent credit. These alternatives have different risks and should be written into the [[Game Music Project Contract]]. Early communication matters especially when scope grows, because presenting unannounced overages at the end damages both the budget and the relationship.
