# Loose Coupling and High Alignment

Loose coupling and high alignment describes teams that share goals and boundary expectations without sharing every implementation decision. A cross-functional team can own a [[Bounded Context]] and work independently inside it, while context contracts coordinate the wider system. Organizing teams only by technical specialty creates handoffs, waiting, and conflicting schedules. Applying the same responsibility principles used for services to teams supports [[Team Autonomy]] and reduces coordination cost.
