# Automated Decision Accountability

Automated decision accountability assigns responsibility for the design, operation, review, and consequences of decisions produced with computational systems. It asks who can explain a result, correct an error, and answer for an unjustified effect.

Responsibility cannot disappear inside a model or supply chain. Organizations must connect the purpose of processing, the quality of evidence, the choice of procedure, and the authority to act on the output.
