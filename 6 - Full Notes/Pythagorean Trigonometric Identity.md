# Pythagorean Trigonometric Identity

The fundamental Pythagorean identity is

$$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1.$$

It is the [[Circle Equation]] $x^2+y^2=1$ applied to the unit-circle point $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. Dividing by either squared trigonometric term produces the related identities $1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$ and $1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta$ where defined.
