# Weak Positive Regression Dependency

Weak positive regression dependency is a condition describing how the joint distribution of p-values responds when a true-null p-value becomes larger. It formalizes a class of positive dependence under which a step-up procedure can retain false-discovery-rate control.

The condition matters because p-values from high-dimensional measurements are rarely independent. The [[Benjamini-Hochberg Procedure]] remains valid under this kind of dependence, but not under arbitrary joint behavior. When the condition is unjustified, the more conservative [[Benjamini-Yekutieli Procedure]] offers a broader guarantee.
