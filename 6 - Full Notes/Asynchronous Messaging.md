# Asynchronous Messaging

Asynchronous messaging lets a sender place a message for later processing without waiting for the receiver to finish. A [[Message Queue]] separates the time at which the producer creates work from the time at which a consumer handles it.

The separation improves decoupling and resilience but moves responsibility into delivery rules. Persistence, acknowledgments, ordering, retries, and the consequences of duplicate or delayed work must be defined.
