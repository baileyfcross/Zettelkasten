# Jordan Block

A Jordan block $J_k(\lambda)$ is a $k\times k$ upper-triangular matrix with eigenvalue $\lambda$ along the diagonal, ones immediately above it, and zeros elsewhere. A block of size one is simply $[\lambda]$.

The off-diagonal chain records the failure of diagonalization. Vectors in that chain are generalized eigenvectors, and the largest block for $\lambda$ determines the exponent of $(x-\lambda)$ in the [[Minimal Polynomial of a Linear Operator]].
