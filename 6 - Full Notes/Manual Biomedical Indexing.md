# Manual Biomedical Indexing

Manual biomedical indexing assigns controlled concepts to a document through human review. An indexer interprets the article's central subjects and can distinguish a major topic from a passing mention.

Human judgment produces valuable semantic labels but is costly, slow, and not perfectly consistent. Indexing policy and expertise affect which terms are chosen. Manual labels are therefore strongest when their scope is clear and can also serve as training or evaluation data for [[Automated Biomedical Indexing]] rather than being treated as infallible ground truth.
