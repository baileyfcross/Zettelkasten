# Positional Notation

Positional notation is a numeral system in which a symbol's contribution depends on its location. Adjacent positions differ by a constant multiplier called the base, and each position represents a power of that base.

In base ten,

$$1234=1\cdot10^3+2\cdot10^2+3\cdot10+4.$$

The same digit therefore denotes different quantities in different positions. Positional notation makes arithmetic far more compact than a purely additive sign-value system and explains how a finite collection of digit symbols can represent arbitrarily large numbers and decimal fractions.
