# Transfer-Based Machine Translation

Transfer-based machine translation first analyzes a source sentence into a linguistic structure, applies rules that map this structure to a corresponding target-language structure, and then generates the target text. The transferred representation may be morphological, syntactic, or semantic.

Compared with [[Direct Machine Translation]], transfer rules can preserve relations between words that surface in different orders or forms. They also demand parsers and pair-specific rule sets, so an error in analysis can propagate into generation. The approach sits between direct mapping and the language-independent ambitions of [[Interlingual Machine Translation]].
