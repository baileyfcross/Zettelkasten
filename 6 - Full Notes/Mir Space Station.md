# Mir Space Station

Mir was a Soviet modular space station launched beginning in 1986. Its multiple docking adapter allowed new laboratory and support modules to be added, enabling long stays and more sophisticated experiments than earlier Salyut stations.

After the Soviet Union collapsed, severe funding shortages made international support important. Shuttle visits and long American stays created operational experience for Russian-American cooperation, even as fires, a docking collision, and aging systems exposed risk. Mir was deorbited in 2001 after serving as both a national station and a bridge to the [[International Space Station]].
