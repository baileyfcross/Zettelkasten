# Social Bot Content Feature

A social bot content feature is derived from the language and symbols used in an account's posts. Word usage, parts of speech, entities, hashtags, links, and repetition can characterize automated production.

Content features capture what an account says, but language varies by topic, community, and time. Robust detection separates automation signals from ordinary topical differences.
