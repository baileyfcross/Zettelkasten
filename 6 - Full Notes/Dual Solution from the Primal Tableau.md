# Dual Solution from the Primal Tableau

An optimal primal simplex tableau contains the information needed to recover an optimal dual solution. The objective-row entries associated with the original slack-variable columns identify the dual-variable values under the tableau's sign convention.

This connection avoids solving the dual independently. It also explains why reduced costs are the residuals of dual constraints and why primal feasibility plus dual feasibility certifies optimality.
