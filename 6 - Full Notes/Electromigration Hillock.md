# Electromigration Hillock

An electromigration hillock is an accumulation of displaced metal atoms at the receiving region of an interconnect. Continued growth can protrude into a neighboring conductor and cause a short circuit.

Voids and hillocks are complementary outcomes of the same mass transport. Reliability protection must therefore limit directional atomic movement rather than checking only whether a conductor is currently open.
