# Lithotripsy

Extracorporeal shock-wave lithotripsy breaks kidney stones with focused high-intensity pressure pulses delivered from outside the body. Imaging locates the stone, and the acoustic focus concentrates stress there while limiting exposure elsewhere. Repeated pulses fragment the stone into pieces small enough to pass through the urinary tract.
