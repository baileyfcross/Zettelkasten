# Engineered Liver Tissue

Engineered liver tissue maintains hepatocytes in a three-dimensional environment that preserves liver-like metabolism and protein production. Hepatocytes often lose specialized function in ordinary culture, making matrix compliance, cell density, coculture, and perfusion important.

Controlled media flow improves transport and supplies mechanical cues, while dense cell contact supports multicellular liver organization.
