# Action Selection

Action selection chooses among possible controls using beliefs about state, consequences, and goals. In a probabilistic model, uncertainty over the current situation and future transitions is carried into the comparison of actions.

A decision process can rank actions by expected cumulative reward, while a Bayesian control question can infer actions compatible with a desired outcome. Both make the criterion for choosing behavior explicit.
