# Single-Provider Cloud Usage Model

A single-provider cloud usage model places one IT service provider between the organization's business units and delivered services. A central IT department controls contracts and service agreements, while the provider may subcontract parts of delivery.

The arrangement gives a clear accountability path but tightly couples business demand to the central function and provider. That control can make it slow to adopt a different service when requirements change.
