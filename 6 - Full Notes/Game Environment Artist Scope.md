# Game Environment Artist Scope

A game environment artist constructs the visible world and its atmosphere. The work can include terrain, architecture, set dressing, surface appearance, lighting, and the arrangement that communicates the game's setting to the player.

The exact scope depends on studio size. A large team may divide concept art, props, modeling, texturing, and lighting among specialists, while an independent team may ask one artist to move across most of the asset pipeline.
