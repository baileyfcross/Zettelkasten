# Bessel Function

Bessel functions solve Bessel's differential equation and describe radial behavior in problems with cylindrical symmetry. For integer order, the first-kind solution is regular at the origin while the second-kind solution is singular there.

Boundary conditions select allowed zeros and coefficients, producing orthogonal radial modes on a finite interval. Bessel functions therefore arise in vibrating circular membranes, cylindrical waves, diffusion, and potential problems expressed in cylindrical coordinates.
