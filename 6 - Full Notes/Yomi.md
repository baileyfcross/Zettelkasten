# Yomi

Yomi is the attempt to read an opponent's intentions and predict what they will do. A player chooses while considering the opponent's likely plan, the opponent's model of the player's plan, and the possibility of deliberate deception.

It becomes important in competitive systems where unpredictability is an advantage and several viable strategies can answer one another. Yomi adds a human layer of recursive reasoning to [[Multiplayer Complexity]].
