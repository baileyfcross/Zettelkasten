# Branch Policy

A branch policy defines conditions that a proposed change must satisfy before it can enter a protected branch. Required reviews, successful builds, passing tests, linked work items, and resolved comments are common conditions.

The policy makes integration rules consistent and auditable. It should protect important branches without encouraging developers to combine many changes simply to avoid an excessively slow or fragile validation process.
