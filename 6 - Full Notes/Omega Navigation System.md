# Omega Navigation System

Omega was a global, passive radio-navigation system built around eight synchronized very-low-frequency transmitters. Its long wavelengths propagated through the Earth-ionosphere waveguide, allowing continuous worldwide coverage with relatively few stations. A receiver compared signals to obtain a two-dimensional position, but accuracy was only about two kilometers and the remote, high-power transmitter network was expensive to maintain. Omega demonstrated global coverage while also exposing the limits of terrestrial infrastructure.
