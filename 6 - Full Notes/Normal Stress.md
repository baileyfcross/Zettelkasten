# Normal Stress

Normal stress is internal axial force divided by the cross-sectional area over which it acts. Tensile stress is positive when it tends to lengthen a member, while compressive stress is negative when it tends to shorten it.

Because stress scales force by member size, it permits comparison among rods with different areas. In a bar element, stress is recovered from nodal displacement through the material modulus and strain-displacement relation.
