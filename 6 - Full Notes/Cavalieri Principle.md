# Cavalieri Principle

Cavalieri's principle states that planar regions with proportional cross-sectional lengths at every height have proportional areas, and solids with proportional cross-sectional areas at every height have proportional volumes.

The principle works because area and volume can be accumulated by integrating corresponding slices. It can transfer a difficult measurement to a simpler comparison object, such as deriving a sphere's volume from a cylinder with a cone removed.
