# Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment

Dynamic difficulty adjustment changes rules or resources in response to the player's performance. It may provide more help when the player struggles or increase resistance when the player succeeds, with the aim of keeping the experience near a desired [[Flow|flow state]].

Subtle adjustment can prevent prolonged boredom or frustration. Strong adjustment can make the system feel unstable or "mushy," because the apparent structure changes whenever the player pushes against it and mastery becomes harder to evaluate.
