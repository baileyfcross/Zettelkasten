# Game Sound

Game sound communicates through a channel that does not require the player to look away from current action. Distinct effects can identify objects, signal a state change, confirm success or failure, and indicate where an unseen threat has appeared.

Audio may reinforce visual information, clarify an ambiguous interaction, or deliberately oppose what is shown. Changes in pitch, loudness, rhythm, and timbre can also represent progress and influence player choice.
