# Roblox Height and Color Map Terrain

A height map encodes terrain elevation in image brightness, allowing Roblox Studio to import a landscape whose high and low areas follow the source image. A corresponding color map can classify areas so the imported terrain receives intended materials or colors.

The maps must align in dimensions and geography for their information to coincide. Import is best treated as the first construction pass: after scale and vertical range are chosen, [[Roblox Terrain Editing]] can correct artifacts, improve traversability, and add local detail that the raster maps cannot express.
