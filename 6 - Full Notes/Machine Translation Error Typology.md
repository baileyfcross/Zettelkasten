# Machine Translation Error Typology

A machine translation error typology groups failures into recurring classes such as unknown words, wrong lexical choices, incorrect word forms, mistranslated idioms, word-order problems, omissions, and unjustified insertions. The distribution of errors depends strongly on the language pair, morphology, domain, and system architecture.

Categorization turns a vague quality complaint into diagnostic evidence. A rule-based system may permit a direct correction to a dictionary or transfer rule, while a statistical or neural system usually requires new data and retraining, with no guarantee that one local error will disappear. Error counts therefore complement aggregate metrics by showing what kind of improvement a score conceals.
