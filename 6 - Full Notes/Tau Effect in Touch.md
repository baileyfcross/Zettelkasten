# Tau Effect in Touch

The tau effect in touch is a spatial distortion in which the perceived distance between skin stimuli depends on their timing. Events close together in time tend to be judged as closer together in space, while a longer interval expands the perceived separation.

The illusion demonstrates that tactile location is constructed jointly from spatial and temporal evidence rather than read from the skin as an independent map. A display designer who varies pulse timing may therefore change apparent spacing even when actuator locations remain fixed, which can either distort a code or deliberately enrich it.
