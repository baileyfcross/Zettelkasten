# Gradual Neuronal Replacement

Gradual neuronal replacement is a thought experiment in which biological neurons are exchanged one at a time for functionally equivalent artificial units. If each replacement preserves every relevant causal relation, no single step appears to provide a principled point at which consciousness or identity must vanish.

The argument supports functional continuity across a change of substrate. Its force comes from the absence of a sudden behavioral or organizational break, not from direct observation of another being's experience. It therefore makes [[Mind Uploading and Personal Identity]] more plausible while leaving open whether functional equivalence is sufficient for phenomenal consciousness.
