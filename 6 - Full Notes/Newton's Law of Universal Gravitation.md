# Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

Newton's law of universal gravitation gives the attraction between point masses:

$$F=G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2}.$$

The forces are equal, opposite, and directed along the line between the masses. Spherically symmetric bodies act externally as if their mass were concentrated at the center.
