# Local Ring

A local ring is a ring with exactly one [[Maximal Ideal]]. Every element outside that maximal ideal is a [[Ring Unit]], and the quotient by it is the [[Residue Field]].

Local rings focus algebra on one part of a spectrum. [[Localization at a Prime Ideal]] produces a local ring whose maximal ideal is the localized prime, making global questions accessible through pointwise analysis.
