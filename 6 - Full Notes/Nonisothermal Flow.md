# Nonisothermal Flow

Nonisothermal flow couples a fluid's pressure and velocity equations to its temperature equation. Fluid motion advects energy, while temperature can change density, viscosity, and therefore the motion itself.

The coupling matters in heated liquids, gases, breathing flow, pipes, and other systems where the temperature range is not negligible. Consistent reference temperature, pressure, and property definitions are required for [[Temperature-Dependent Fluid Properties|temperature-dependent behavior]].
