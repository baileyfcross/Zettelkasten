# Autocorrelation Feature

An autocorrelation feature measures the association between a time series and lagged versions of itself. Values at selected lags reveal repetition, persistence, and characteristic temporal scales.

A collection of lag correlations can distinguish dynamics even when two series have similar marginal distributions. Interpretation must account for trend and nonstationarity, which can create high correlations without a stable repeating mechanism.
