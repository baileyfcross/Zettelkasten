# Direct Pattern-Space Classification

Direct pattern-space classification maps each object into a vector indexed by selected patterns and trains a conventional classifier in that space. The patterns are explicit derived variables.

This pipeline separates representation construction from predictive modeling and allows many standard learners. Its performance depends on candidate quality, mapping scale, sparsity, and regularization.
