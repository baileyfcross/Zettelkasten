# P-Value Histogram

A p-value histogram summarizes the distribution of p-values across many hypothesis tests. When all null hypotheses are true and tests are calibrated, the values should be approximately uniform; a concentration near zero can indicate a subset of real effects.

Other shapes can reveal model failure, dependence, confounding, or overcorrection. The plot is therefore a diagnostic of the entire analysis rather than a substitute for a multiple-testing procedure.
