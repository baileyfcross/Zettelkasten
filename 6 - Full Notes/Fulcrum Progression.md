# Fulcrum Progression

Fulcrum progression extends a [[Game Data Fulcrum]] across levels or other advancement stages. A single central object is insufficient when every object has weaker and stronger versions; the reference itself needs a tested low point, midpoint, and high point.

Starting at the middle often makes it easier to establish the intended ordinary feel, then remove power toward the minimum and add it toward the maximum. Once that gradient is sound, other objects can be balanced against the midpoint and inherit parallel progression lines. This preserves their relative differences while keeping the whole advancing set anchored to one tested structure.
