# Solar Thermal Energy Storage

Thermal storage holds heat collected during sunny periods for later delivery to a [[Solar Thermal Power Plant]]. It closes part of the timing gap between variable solar input and electricity demand. The source distinguishes sensible, latent, and chemical heat storage and describes packed-bed [[Thermocline Thermal Storage]] and [[Two-Tank Thermal Storage]] as practical solar-plant arrangements. Capacity and heat-transfer path determine useful discharge.
