# Player Agency

Player agency is the capacity to act on one's own behalf and affect the state of a game. It depends on more than the number of available inputs: the player must care about the decision, understand enough of the alternatives to act intentionally, and receive consequences that make the action matter.

More agency is not automatically better. A designer should spend agency on decisions that serve the intended experience and remove choices that add effort without meaningful consequence. The quality of agency is therefore connected to [[Meaningful Decision]], not to raw option count.
