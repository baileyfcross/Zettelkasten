# Balancing Loop

A balancing loop counteracts deviation and moves a system toward an equilibrium or acceptable range. The result is often dynamic balance rather than a motionless state.

In games, balancing loops can constrain a leader, help a trailing player, regulate progression, or prevent resource accumulation. If made too strong they can stop meaningful change, so good [[Game Balance]] remains perturbable rather than static.
