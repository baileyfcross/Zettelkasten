# Open Standard for Scientific Interoperability

An open standard for scientific interoperability defines a documented, reusable way for data, software, or publications to interact without discriminatory access or restrictive licensing. Reference implementations and permissive terms make it easier for independent groups to exchange and combine contributions.

Standards do not merely permit reproduction; they lower its cost. Common formats can support cross-dataset analysis, replaceable workflow components, and machine-readable literature. A standard can also define a known experimental state, such as a benchmark dataset or platform. It must still evolve carefully so stability does not prevent necessary innovation.
