# AI-Assisted Postmortem Draft

An AI-assisted postmortem draft synthesizes incident timelines, alerts, changes, tool output, approvals, and remediation results into a reviewable first version. It can reduce clerical work and highlight missing intervals, but the underlying evidence remains authoritative and operators remain responsible for causal claims.

The draft should distinguish observed facts, inferred causes, unresolved questions, and proposed follow-up work. Human review is especially important where telemetry is incomplete or several failures overlap, because a coherent narrative can conceal uncertainty just as easily as it can clarify it.
