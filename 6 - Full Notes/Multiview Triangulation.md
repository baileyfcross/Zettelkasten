# Multiview Triangulation

Multiview triangulation reconstructs a three-dimensional point from corresponding image observations in two or more calibrated cameras. Each observation defines a viewing ray, and the desired point is estimated where those rays meet most consistently.

Noise and calibration error usually prevent an exact intersection. Additional views can supply redundancy, but the result still depends on correct correspondence and camera geometry with a useful baseline.
