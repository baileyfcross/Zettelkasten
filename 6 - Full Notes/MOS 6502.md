# MOS 6502

The MOS Technology 6502 was an inexpensive microprocessor introduced in 1975. Its low price made it attractive to hobbyists and manufacturers competing with systems based on Intel and Motorola chips.

The processor powered the Apple I and II, Commodore machines, and game systems. A common affordable architecture enabled a broad software and hardware ecosystem.
