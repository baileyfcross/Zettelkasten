# Tit-for-Tat Strategy

Tit-for-tat is a strategy for an [[Iterated Game]] that cooperates on the first round and then repeats the opponent's previous action. It is initially nice, immediately retaliatory after defection, and immediately forgiving after renewed cooperation.

In Robert Axelrod's tournament of repeated Prisoner's Dilemma strategies, this short rule outperformed more elaborate submissions. Its success came from enabling long mutual cooperation without allowing persistent exploitation, although that tournament result does not prove it is optimal in every environment.
