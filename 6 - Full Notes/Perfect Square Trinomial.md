# Perfect Square Trinomial

A perfect square trinomial is a three-term polynomial that is the square of a binomial:

$$a^2+2ab+b^2=(a+b)^2,$$

or

$$a^2-2ab+b^2=(a-b)^2.$$

Its first and last terms are squares, and its middle term is twice the product of their square roots with the appropriate sign. Recognizing this structure permits immediate factorization. [[Completing the Square]] creates the structure when the original quadratic lacks the needed constant term.
