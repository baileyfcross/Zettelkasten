# Hidden Factor

A hidden factor is an unobserved variable that induces coordinated variation across several measured variables or samples. Its presence can be inferred from correlation structure even when the underlying cause was not recorded in the study metadata.

In high-throughput experiments, hidden factors may represent biological structure or unmeasured technical batches. [[Factor Analysis]] estimates their shared patterns, but contextual evidence is needed before interpreting or removing them.
