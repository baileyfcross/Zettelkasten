# AdChoices Icon

The AdChoices icon is a notice attached to behaviorally targeted advertising that is intended to signal data-based personalization and provide access to information or choices. It attempts to make an otherwise hidden advertising process visible at the point of use.

Its effectiveness depends on recognition and comprehension. A symbol cannot support meaningful control when users do not know what it means, what tracking produced the ad, or what an offered opt-out changes.
