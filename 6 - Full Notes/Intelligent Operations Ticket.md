# Intelligent Operations Ticket

An intelligent operations ticket augments an incident or service record with automated summarization, context retrieval, status interpretation, and handling guidance. It can present different information to requesters, operators, and managers while keeping the underlying ticket as the system of record.

Useful assistance requires ticket fields, identity, business rules, history, operational knowledge, and APIs rather than text generation alone. Stable templates and reviewable evidence help prevent flexible model output from changing official status or action without authority.
