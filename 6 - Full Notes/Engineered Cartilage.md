# Engineered Cartilage

Engineered cartilage combines chondrocytes or cartilage-forming stem cells with a shaped scaffold or hydrogel. Because cartilage lacks an internal blood supply, it avoids some vascularization problems faced by other replacement tissues.

The construct must still retain patient-specific geometry, produce suitable extracellular matrix, and withstand the repeated mechanical loads of joints or other anatomical sites.
