# Body Tracking

Body tracking estimates the pose or motion of a user's limbs, hands, head, or full body so bodily action can become interface input. The tracked body may control virtual content directly or provide context for interpreting gesture and contact.

AR makes this difficult because the body also belongs visibly to the real scene and may occlude virtual objects. Tracking range, self-occlusion, precision, latency, and the absence of markers all shape which interactions are practical.
