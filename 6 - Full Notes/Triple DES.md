# Triple DES

Triple DES strengthens the [[Data Encryption Standard]] by applying the DES transformation three times to each data block. The source presents the encryption-decryption-encryption sequence with three keys and a two-key variant in which the first and third keys are the same.

The construction preserved DES's block size and much of its structure, making it an easier transition for systems already built around DES. That compatibility also retained legacy costs: repeated operations are slower, and the design remains tied to an older 64-bit block cipher. The book therefore treats Triple DES as a legacy bridge rather than the successor role now filled by the [[Advanced Encryption Standard]].
