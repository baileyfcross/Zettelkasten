# Autonomous Vehicle Pose Estimation

Vehicle pose describes position and orientation, including pitch, yaw, and roll. An autonomous car needs this state because sensor measurements are taken from a platform that tilts, turns, accelerates, and brakes.

A roof sensor aimed far ahead will observe a different region when braking makes the car's nose dip. Wheel rotation, inertial measurements, and other self-sensors therefore help transform environmental readings into the correct frame and predict near-term motion. Pose estimation is the vehicle-specific form of [[Robot Self-Monitoring]] and a prerequisite for reliable path control.
