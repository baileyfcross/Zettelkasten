# Space Tourism

Space tourism sells a spaceflight experience to private passengers rather than selecting crews primarily for government missions. Early orbital tourists purchased rare Soyuz seats and underwent extensive training, restricting access to a handful of very wealthy and physically qualified customers.

Suborbital ventures aimed to lower cost and preparation by providing a brief journey above the accepted boundary of space. Delays, development expense, and fatal test accidents showed that carrying paying passengers safely remained harder than optimistic markets assumed. A tourist accident could also alter demand and trigger stricter regulation.
