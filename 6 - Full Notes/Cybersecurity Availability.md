# Cybersecurity Availability

Availability ensures that information and services are usable by the right people when they are needed. A denial-of-service attack violates this goal by exhausting or disabling the resources that legitimate users require.

Redundant servers, backups, resilient networks, and recovery plans preserve availability when a component fails or is attacked. Security controls must also avoid causing unnecessary downtime while responding to another security problem.
