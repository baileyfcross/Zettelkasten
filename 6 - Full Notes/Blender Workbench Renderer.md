# Blender Workbench Renderer

Workbench is Blender's renderer for clear, responsive viewport inspection rather than physically based final imagery. It emphasizes forms, cavity, outlines, studio lighting, and display colors so modeling decisions remain legible without the cost of Eevee or Cycles shading.

Choosing Workbench is therefore a workflow decision: it removes material and light complexity when the immediate question concerns shape, topology, or scene organization.
