# Acoustic Model

An acoustic model estimates the relationship between speech observations and phonetic units or states. It must tolerate differences caused by speakers, accents, rate, microphones, noise, and surrounding sounds.

Traditional systems combine features with [[Hidden Markov Model|hidden Markov models]], while newer systems use neural networks. Its scores are joined with a [[Pronunciation Model]] and [[Statistical Language Model]] during recognition.
