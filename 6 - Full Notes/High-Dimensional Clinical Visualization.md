# High-Dimensional Clinical Visualization

High-dimensional clinical visualization displays many variables at once through techniques such as parallel coordinates, matrices, glyphs, or linked views. It helps analysts examine relationships that are hidden when each measurement is plotted separately.

Additional dimensions increase visual clutter and the risk of accidental patterns. Ordering, scaling, color, and interactive filtering determine which structure becomes visible. The display is strongest as an exploratory interface paired with statistical validation, not as proof that a visually striking cluster or association is real.
