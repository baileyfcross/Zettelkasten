# Circulation Integral

A circulation integral is the closed line integral of the tangential component of a [[Vector Field]] around an oriented loop. When the field represents force, the circulation is the work done in carrying a body once around that loop.

The sign depends on the direction of traversal, and reversing the loop reverses the integral. [[Stokes Theorem]] relates this boundary circulation to the surface integral of the field's curl across any consistently oriented spanning surface.
