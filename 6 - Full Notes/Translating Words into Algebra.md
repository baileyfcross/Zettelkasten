# Translating Words into Algebra

Translating words into algebra represents a verbal relationship with variables and operation symbols. Terms such as sum, difference, product, quotient, square, and reciprocal indicate operations, while phrasing determines their order.

Order matters for noncommutative operations. "A number decreased by seven" is (x-7), whereas "seven decreased by a number" is (7-x). Grouping also matters: tripling a number and then adding five gives (3x+5), while tripling the result after adding five gives (3(x+5)). A correct translation models the relationship, not merely the vocabulary.
