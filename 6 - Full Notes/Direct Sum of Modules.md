# Direct Sum of Modules

The direct sum ⊕Mᵢ consists of tuples with one entry in each module and only finitely many nonzero entries. Addition and scalar multiplication are componentwise.

Finite direct sums agree with direct products, but infinite sums retain the finite-support condition. A [[Free Module]] is a direct sum of copies of its base ring.
