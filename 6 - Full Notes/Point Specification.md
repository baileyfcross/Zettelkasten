# Point Specification

Point specification records a three-dimensional location as the basic input to an AR model. A tracked pointer, ray, image observation, or intersection with known geometry can supply the location in the model's coordinate system.

Individual points can mark landmarks, vertices, or measurements and can be combined to infer lines, planes, and volumes. Tracking and pointing error directly limit the precision of the resulting geometry.
