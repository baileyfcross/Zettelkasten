# Streaming Feature Engineering

Streaming feature engineering adapts representation construction to data that cannot be stored and revisited as a fixed batch. Algorithms must update features in one pass, under limited memory, while the stream's length and future content remain unknown.

The term covers both arriving observations and arriving dimensions. These settings demand incremental statistics, compact sketches, bounded computation, and decisions that remain useful as the available data or feature space changes.
