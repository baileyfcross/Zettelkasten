# Rumor Source Detection

Rumor source detection estimates the origin of a diffusion from the network, a spreading model, and observed affected nodes. Candidate sources are compared by how plausibly their paths and timing could have produced the snapshot.

Many estimators assume homogeneous infection and recovery, but real networks and observations are incomplete. The result is therefore conditional on both the diffusion model and the available observer set.
