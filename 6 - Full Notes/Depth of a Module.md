# Depth of a Module

The depth of a module M with respect to an ideal I is the maximum length of an M-regular sequence contained in I. Over a Noetherian local ring, depth usually refers to the maximal ideal.

The source also characterizes depth as the smallest degree in which Ext_R(R/I,M) is nonzero. Depth cannot exceed dimension and is linked to finite projective dimension by the [[Auslander-Buchsbaum Formula]].
