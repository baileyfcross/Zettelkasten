# Physics-Preserving Geometry Simplification

Physics-preserving geometry simplification removes regions or detail that do not materially influence the quantity being studied while retaining features that control loads, flow, heat paths, interfaces, or boundary conditions. The analysis question, not visual complexity, determines relevance.

The effect of simplification should be tested by comparing defined outputs against a more complete baseline. Guessing that a baffle, exterior wall, or distant region is irrelevant is not equivalent to demonstrating it.
