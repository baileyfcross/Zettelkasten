# Sensing Data Stream

A sensing data stream is a continuous sequence of measurements emitted by sensors or mobile devices. Individual records may have a short useful lifetime, while the stream's rate and duration make storing everything before analysis impractical.

Wireless links and resource-constrained producers expose authenticity, integrity, confidentiality, loss, and synchronization problems throughout collection.
