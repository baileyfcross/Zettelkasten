# Contractual Data Control

Contractual data control uses agreements to define who may access, copy, process, disclose, or transfer data. It can allocate responsibilities without creating a property right enforceable against everyone.

Contracts are flexible but normally bind only their parties, and bargaining power may determine their terms. They can therefore support data exchange while leaving collective interests or third-party effects unresolved.
