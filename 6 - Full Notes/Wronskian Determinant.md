# Wronskian Determinant

The Wronskian is a determinant formed from a collection of functions and their successive derivatives. For two functions it is $W=y_1y_2'-y_2y_1'$ and provides a practical test of linear independence for solutions of a linear differential equation.

A nonzero Wronskian at a point guarantees that the functions are linearly independent there. For solutions of a well-behaved homogeneous linear equation, the Wronskian's evolution is constrained, so independence at one point extends across the interval of the equation.
