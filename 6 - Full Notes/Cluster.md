# Cluster

A cluster is a group of observations that are similar to one another with respect to selected attributes and comparatively different from observations in other groups. In cluster analysis, neither the number of groups nor their defining characteristics must be supplied in advance.

A cluster is therefore defined operationally by the representation, the [[Proximity Measure]], and the grouping rule. Changing any of those choices can change which observations appear to belong together.
