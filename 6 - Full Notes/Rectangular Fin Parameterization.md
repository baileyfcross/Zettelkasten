# Rectangular Fin Parameterization

Rectangular fin parameterization defines length, width, thickness, base dimensions, and relevant offsets as named quantities rather than fixed sketch coordinates. A profile and block can then be rebuilt consistently when any dimension changes.

The parameterized construction supports one-, two-, and three-dimensional comparisons and systematic sweeps. Derived measurements such as tip area, perimeter, and volume provide checks that the rebuilt geometry matches the intended proportions.
