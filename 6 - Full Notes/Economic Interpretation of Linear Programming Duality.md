# Economic Interpretation of Linear Programming Duality

In a resource-allocation primal model, each objective coefficient represents revenue from an activity and each constraint records consumption of a limited resource. The corresponding dual variable assigns an imputed worth to one unit of that resource.

A dual constraint requires the imputed resource cost of an activity to cover its unit revenue. The difference is a reduced cost, which indicates whether expanding a currently inactive activity is economically attractive. At optimum, total primal revenue equals the dual valuation of available resources.
