# Production Debt

Production debt is the amount of content and production work still required for a game to meet its commercial goals. It becomes meaningful after core mechanics, tools, pipelines, quality standards, and asset budgets are stable enough to forecast repeated work.

Treating uncertain preproduction estimates as fixed production debt creates false predictability. The debt should be revised from actual throughput and changes to the minimum viable game.
