# Thermal Boundary Layer

Near a solid wall, fluid temperature changes from the surface value toward the bulk-flow value across a finite region. This thermal boundary layer concentrates temperature gradients and therefore strongly influences convective heat flux.

Wall orientation, buoyancy, roughness, and imposed velocity alter the layer and the effective [[Convective Heat Transfer Coefficient|heat-transfer coefficient]]. A numerical mesh must resolve this near-wall variation finely enough that further [[Thermal Mesh Refinement|refinement]] does not materially change the reported flux.
