# Search Query Disease Surveillance

Search query disease surveillance tracks the frequency and geography of symptom- or disease-related web searches as a proxy for population health. Aggregated queries can provide rapid observations at a scale that is difficult to obtain from individual clinical reports.

The mapping from a query to illness is uncertain: users may search because of news, concern for another person, or general curiosity. Query vocabularies and platform behavior also change. Models need regular recalibration and should be treated as complementary signals rather than replacements for clinical and laboratory surveillance.
