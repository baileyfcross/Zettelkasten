# User-Generated Globalization

User-generated globalization is the cross-border circulation of cultural forms driven by ordinary Internet users as well as professional media industries. People translate, imitate, subtitle, remix, and redistribute artifacts for their own communities.

The process does not erase local difference. A globally visible template becomes raw material for situated expression, and many adaptations travel through informal networks rather than centralized distributors. Internet memes make this bottom-up globalization visible because their versions preserve traces of both a shared model and local intervention.
