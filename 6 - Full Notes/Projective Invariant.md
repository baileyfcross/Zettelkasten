# Projective Invariant

A projective invariant is a property unchanged by an invertible [[Projective Transformation]]. Examples include collinearity of points, concurrency of lines, tangency, incidence, and the degree of an algebraic curve.

Lengths and angles are not projective invariants because perspective can distort them. Projective reasoning therefore focuses on relational structure that survives a change of viewpoint.
