# Reflection Consistency

Reflection consistency checks whether an object and its image in a mirror or other planar reflective surface obey the geometry of reflection. Corresponding points should be related by a common mirror plane, and the reflected scene should preserve the appropriate reversed viewpoint.

A composite may place a plausible-looking duplicate in the mirror without satisfying those shared constraints. Reliable use requires identifying the reflective plane and corresponding features; curved, warped, or imperfect surfaces demand a different model.
