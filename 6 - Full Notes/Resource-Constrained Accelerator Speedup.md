# Resource-Constrained Accelerator Speedup

Resource-constrained accelerator speedup is the improvement obtained by a prototype whose lane count, clock frequency, memory bandwidth, and board resources are limited. It should be interpreted against the exact CPU or GPU baseline and must include relevant data-transfer time.

An FPGA may lose absolute performance to a larger GPU yet offer better energy use. Conversely, a speedup against a low-frequency embedded CPU does not imply the same gain against a high-end processor.
