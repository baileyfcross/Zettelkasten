# Cayley-Hamilton Theorem

The Cayley-Hamilton theorem states that every square matrix, or finite-dimensional linear operator, satisfies its own characteristic polynomial. If $p_T(x)=\det(xI-T)$, then $p_T(T)=0$.

Consequently, the [[Minimal Polynomial of a Linear Operator]] divides the characteristic polynomial. Higher powers of an operator can therefore be reduced to combinations of lower powers, and polynomial identities constrain its possible canonical forms.
