# Stakeholder

A stakeholder is a person or group with a meaningful interest in a product's outcome. Players, publishers, platform holders, business leaders, and dependent teams can each supply constraints, feedback, or value judgments.

Stakeholders collaborate through the [[Product Owner]] and inspect working results at the [[Sprint Review]]. Their participation grounds decisions in product evidence without turning the Development Team into a committee-controlled task pool.
