# Somatosensory Submodality

The somatosensory system is not one undifferentiated sense. Its cutaneous submodalities include touch, temperature, pain, and itch, with proposed affective-touch pathways contributing pleasant social sensations. Signals from muscles and joints add limb position and movement.

Each submodality has different receptors and functions. Touch and temperature discriminate events on the body, nociception warns of potentially damaging mechanical, thermal, or chemical input, and itch follows a pathway distinct from pain. [[Haptic Sensing]] depends primarily on touch and [[Kinesthesia]] while interacting with thermal and affective cues.
