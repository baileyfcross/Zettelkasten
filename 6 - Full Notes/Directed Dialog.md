# Directed Dialog

Directed dialog structures an interaction around narrowly framed questions and expected answers. The assistant controls the sequence so that each turn supplies a known slot or decision.

The approach improves predictability for a [[Virtual Agent]] and can constrain a [[Speech Recognition Grammar]]. It becomes frustrating when users want to provide information out of order or take initiative.
