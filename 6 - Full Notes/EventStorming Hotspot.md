# EventStorming Hotspot

An EventStorming hotspot marks uncertainty, conflict, or missing information that the workshop cannot resolve productively at that moment. A bright note makes the issue conspicuous without forcing participants to settle it through speculation. This lets the group continue mapping the wider process while preserving a concrete follow-up question. A wall containing many hotspots is not a failed model; it is evidence that the workshop exposed areas where further [[Knowledge Crunching]] is needed.
