# Spherical Harmonic

A spherical harmonic is an angular basis function on the surface of a sphere. It combines an associated Legendre function in the polar angle with a complex exponential in the azimuthal angle and is labeled by two integer indices.

Spherical harmonics are orthonormal over solid angle and are eigenfunctions of the angular part of the Laplacian. They separate angular dependence in three-dimensional potential, wave, and quantum problems and organize fields by angular momentum structure.
