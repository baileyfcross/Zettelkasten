# Causal Order in Special Relativity

Causal order in special relativity is protected by the invariant light-speed limit. If one event can influence another through a timelike or lightlike path, every inertial observer places the cause before the effect. A [[Lorentz Transformation]] may change their measured time separation, but it cannot reverse their order.

Spacelike-separated events are different. Their coordinate-time order can reverse between frames because no signal moving at or below light speed can connect them. The reversal therefore changes no causal chain. This is why the [[Relativity of Simultaneity]] is compatible with cause and effect.

The [[Light Cone]] displays the rule geometrically: possible causes lie in the past cone, possible effects lie in the future cone, and causally disconnected events lie outside both. Crossing from one region to another would require changing the sign of the invariant interval, which no Lorentz transformation does.
