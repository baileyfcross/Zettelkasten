# SVG Curve Import to Blender

Blender imports scalable vector graphics as curve objects, allowing shapes authored in Inkscape or another SVG-capable tool to enter a 3D scene without first being rasterized. The imported curves can retain editable outlines and later be converted into mesh geometry.

Conversion produces triangles or quads that follow the visual shape but may not provide ideal edge flow. Extrusion or rendering can proceed quickly, while deformation-sensitive work may require retopology after conversion.
