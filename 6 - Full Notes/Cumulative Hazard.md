# Cumulative Hazard

Cumulative hazard accumulates instantaneous event risk over time. It starts at zero and increases as risk is experienced, providing a complementary representation to the declining survival probability.

Plotting cumulative hazards by group helps assess the [[Proportional Hazards Assumption]]. Curves that remain roughly proportional or parallel support a constant relative effect more than curves that cross or diverge irregularly.
