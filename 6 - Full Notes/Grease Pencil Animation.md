# Grease Pencil Animation

Grease Pencil supports a traditional hand-drawn workflow and a rigged workflow closer to 3D character animation. Hand-drawn work creates and refines new stroke states over time; rigging reuses controls and deformations.

The choice depends on the desired visual style and production method. Both approaches retain access to Blender's camera, timeline, materials, compositing, and three-dimensional scene, so they can also be combined.
