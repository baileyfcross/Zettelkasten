# Fluid Finite Element

A fluid finite element represents pressure, velocity, temperature, potential, or related variables over a small flow-domain region. Two-dimensional elements may use three triangular nodes or four quadrilateral nodes.

Material properties, nodal coordinates, body loads, and surface conditions define the local equations. Assembled results can provide nodal pressures and velocities for steady or transient fluid systems.
