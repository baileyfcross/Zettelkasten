# Treatment Task Queue

A treatment task queue is the ordered set of patients waiting for an examination, inspection, pharmacy service, or other hospital operation. Its delay depends on both the number of people waiting and their predicted service durations.

Queue estimates should change when patients arrive, leave, receive priority, or take longer than predicted.
