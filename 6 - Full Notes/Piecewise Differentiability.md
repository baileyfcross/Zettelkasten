# Piecewise Differentiability

A piecewise-defined function can be differentiable within each interval on which one formula applies even when its behavior changes at the boundaries between formulas. The derivative must therefore be evaluated separately on each open piece.

At a joining point, continuity does not guarantee differentiability: two pieces may meet while having different one-sided slopes. Absolute value is the standard example, with derivatives of opposite sign on either side of its corner and no derivative at the corner itself.
