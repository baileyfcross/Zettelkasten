# Perspective Projection in Computer Graphics

Perspective projection maps a three-dimensional point to a view plane using projectors that meet at a finite center of projection. Its visual effect resembles a camera or the human eye: projected size varies inversely with distance from the center, producing perspective foreshortening.

Parallel world lines not parallel to the view plane can converge at vanishing points. These cues make the result look realistic, but the image does not preserve true dimensions and is unsuitable for taking exact measurements. The finite center distinguishes perspective from [[Parallel Projection in Computer Graphics]], whose projectors remain parallel.
