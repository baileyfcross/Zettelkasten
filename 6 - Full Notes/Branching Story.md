# Branching Story

A branching story lets player choices select among multiple authored paths. Each branch may change events, available choices, or endings, allowing players to pursue different versions of the narrative.

Branches remain constrained by material the creators prepared. Wide divergence can require a large amount of content and make it difficult for every path to feel complete, so many designs use [[Reconverging Branches]] or concentrate variation near the ending.
