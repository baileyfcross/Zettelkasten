# Logistic Model Fit Comparison

Logistic model fit comparison uses likelihood, deviance, degrees of freedom, or information criteria to choose between plausible specifications. A likelihood-ratio calculation is appropriate for nested models, while AIC can compare a broader set fit to the same outcome data.

The method is useful for deciding whether a predictor should be continuous or represented by category indicators. Better relative fit does not excuse post hoc searching or guarantee that the chosen representation is clinically meaningful.
