# Transverse Wave

A transverse wave has oscillations perpendicular to its direction of energy transfer. The displacement can occur in any direction within the plane normal to propagation, which makes [[Polarization]] possible. Electromagnetic waves and waves on a stretched string are transverse, although their physical disturbances are different.
