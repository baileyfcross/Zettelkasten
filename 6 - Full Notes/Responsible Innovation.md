# Responsible Innovation

Responsible innovation is an approach to research and development that anticipates consequences, reflects on values, includes stakeholders, and responds to new evidence. It shifts ethical attention from repairing harm after deployment to shaping the direction and conditions of innovation itself.

The approach requires both process and substance. Inclusive discussion helps reveal who bears risk, while design methods translate values into technical and organizational choices. Responsibility also requires an option not to proceed. If innovation is treated as inevitable, participation can become a way to secure acceptance rather than a real opportunity to alter or stop the project.
