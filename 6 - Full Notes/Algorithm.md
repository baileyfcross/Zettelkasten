# Algorithm

An algorithm is a precise set of steps for solving a problem or carrying out a task. It can be followed by a person with pencil and paper or expressed as a [[Program]] for a computer; computers make algorithms fast, but they do not define what an algorithm is.

An algorithm may receive an [[Algorithm Input]], transforms that data through operations arranged by a [[Control Structure]], and produces an [[Algorithm Output]]. Its steps must be unambiguous, effective, and guaranteed to finish after a finite amount of work.
