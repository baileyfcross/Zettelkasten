# Stacked Area Time-Series Plot

A stacked area time-series plot accumulates component series so the outer boundary represents their total. It is meaningful when the variables are additive parts of a global quantity and supports reading contribution and total change together. Only the first component has a stable baseline; internal layers are harder to compare precisely. Layer order therefore affects interpretation, and components that do not form a meaningful sum should not be stacked.
