# Experience as a Design Value

An experience design value states the central activity available to the player and the physical or emotional quality that activity should produce. It answers both what the player gets to do and what doing it should feel like.

This value keeps the project centered on play rather than its feature inventory. Two implementations can contain similar mechanics yet support different experiences through timing, framing, feedback, and consequence.
