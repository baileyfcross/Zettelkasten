# Summative Evaluation

Summative evaluation assesses a substantially complete interface against target values or compares two or more designs, components, or techniques. It helps sum up the quality and consequences of design choices.

Representative users perform consistent task scenarios while evaluators collect qualitative and quantitative results. Formal summative experiments vary independent variables, measure dependent variables, and control other factors so observed effects can be attributed to the design conditions.

Summative evaluation complements [[Formative Evaluation]]. Formative work refines the design; summative work measures the resulting design or compares alternatives.
