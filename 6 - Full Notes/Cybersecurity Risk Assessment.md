# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

A cybersecurity risk assessment identifies valued assets, relevant [[Cyber Threat|threats]], exploitable [[Cyber Vulnerability|vulnerabilities]], existing controls, and the likely impact of harmful events. It turns a broad concern about security into a prioritized account of what could happen and why it matters.

The result supports decisions about treatment, acceptance, transfer, or avoidance of risk. Because systems and threats change, assessment is a continuing process rather than a one-time checklist.
