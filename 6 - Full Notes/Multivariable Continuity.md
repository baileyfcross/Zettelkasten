# Multivariable Continuity

A multivariable function is continuous at $a$ when it is defined there and its [[Multivariable Limit]] as $x\to a$ equals $f(a)$. Continuity on a domain means this condition holds at every point of the domain.

Sums, scalar multiples, products, and suitable quotients of continuous functions remain continuous. [[Differentiability in Euclidean Space]] implies continuity, but continuity alone does not guarantee differentiability.
