# Torus

A torus is the [[Surface of Revolution]] obtained by rotating a circle around a coplanar axis that does not meet the circle. If the circle has radius $r$ and its center lies distance $a$ from the axis, a standard parameterization is

$$((a+r\cos\alpha)\cos\theta,(a+r\cos\alpha)\sin\theta,r\sin\alpha).$$

One angle moves around the generating circle and the other moves that circle around the axis.
