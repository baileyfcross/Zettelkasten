# Growth Factor Cost

Growth factor cost is the expense of purified signaling proteins added to culture media to maintain proliferation or guide differentiation. These proteins can account for most of a defined medium's cost.

Large-scale food production may use lower-purity, more efficient, or alternative formulations than therapeutic manufacturing, provided they remain safe and consistently produce the required cells.
