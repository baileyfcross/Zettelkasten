# Cybersecurity Integrity

Cybersecurity integrity is the trustworthiness and reliability of systems and data. A user needs evidence that a system has not been compromised and that information has not been changed without authorization.

The source separates [[System Integrity Verification]] from [[Data Integrity Verification]] because trustworthy data depends on the environment that stores and processes it. Hashes, secure boot, identity checks, and protected storage provide different parts of that evidence.
