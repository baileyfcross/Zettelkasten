# Simple Module

A simple module is a nonzero module whose only submodules are zero and itself. It cannot be decomposed further through a proper nontrivial submodule.

Every factor in a [[Composition Series]] is simple. Over a commutative ring, simple modules are represented by quotients R/m for [[Maximal Ideal]]s m.
