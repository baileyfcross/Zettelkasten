# Beam Nodal Slope

Beam nodal slope is the rotation degree of freedom associated with the derivative of transverse deflection at a beam node. It accompanies nodal displacement in the bending element vector.

Including slope permits a smooth cubic interpolation of deflection inside the element. Applied nodal moments act through the rotational degree of freedom.
