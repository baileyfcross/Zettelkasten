# Quadratic Discriminant

For $ax^2+bx+c=0$, the discriminant is $\Delta=b^2-4ac$. Its sign classifies the roots visible in the [[Quadratic Formula]]: positive gives two distinct real roots, zero gives one repeated real root, and negative gives a conjugate pair of nonreal complex roots. It therefore predicts the quadratic graph's intersections with the horizontal axis.
