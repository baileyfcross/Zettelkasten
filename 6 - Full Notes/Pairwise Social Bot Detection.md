# Pairwise Social Bot Detection

Pairwise social bot detection analyzes relationships between two accounts, such as similarity, interaction, or synchronized behavior. The pair becomes the unit of representation instead of an isolated user.

Pairwise evidence can reveal coordinated automation that individual profiles conceal. The number of possible pairs is large, so candidate generation and meaningful relationship definitions are essential.
