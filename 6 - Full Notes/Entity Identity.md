# Entity Identity

Entity identity uniquely distinguishes one [[Domain Entity]] from all others of the same type throughout its life cycle. It can come from an external domain identifier or be generated by the application, but it should not force construction to depend unnecessarily on a database. A strongly typed identity [[Value Object]] prevents IDs for different entity types from being accidentally exchanged and makes the intended role of a value explicit in method signatures.
