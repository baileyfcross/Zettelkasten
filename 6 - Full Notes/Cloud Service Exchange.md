# Cloud Service Exchange

A cloud service exchange is a proposed marketplace in which standardized cloud services could be discovered, priced, bought, and sold in real time. Supply and demand would help establish price, while service patterns would classify comparable offerings.

The idea depends on strong [[Cloud Service Interoperability]] so one provider's service can replace another without a long migration. Lower-level infrastructure and platform services are easier to commoditize this way than specialized business processes.
