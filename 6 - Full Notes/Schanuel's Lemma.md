# Schanuel's Lemma

Schanuel's lemma compares two projective presentations of the same module. If P/M and P′/M′ are isomorphic with P and P′ projective, then P ⊕ M′ is isomorphic to P′ ⊕ M.

The lemma shows that kernels of different projective presentations are stably equivalent. It supports the independence of invariants derived from a chosen [[Projective Resolution]].
