# Map Scale and Representative Fraction

Map scale is the ratio of a length on the chart to the corresponding distance in the represented world. A large-scale chart shows a smaller area with more detail, while a small-scale chart covers more area with less resolution. The scale is exact only where the chosen projection makes it so, and its variation is part of selecting a suitable aeronautical chart.
