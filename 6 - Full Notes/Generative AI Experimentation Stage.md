# Generative AI Experimentation Stage

The generative-AI experimentation stage compares candidate foundation models, prompts, data preparations, and adaptation strategies against the business objective. Jupyter notebooks support interactive exploration, while experiment tracking preserves parameters, metrics, code, and artifacts.

Versioning notebooks and datasets separates a finding from an unreproducible session. An experiment promoted toward production should become a parameterized job whose environment and output can be recreated without the original researcher's live notebook.
