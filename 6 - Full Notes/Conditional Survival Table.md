# Conditional Survival Table

A conditional survival table lists event times, the number at risk just before each time, the number of events, the estimated survival probability, and often its standard error. It is the numerical structure behind a Kaplan–Meier curve.

The table shows why survival probability is not a fixed-period prevalence. Participants can leave the risk set through events or censoring, causing the denominator for each conditional step to change.
