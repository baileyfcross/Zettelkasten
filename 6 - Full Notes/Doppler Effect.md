# Doppler Effect

The Doppler effect is the change in observed frequency caused by relative motion between a wave source and observer. Approach compresses successive wavefronts and raises the received frequency; recession spreads them and lowers it. For sound the medium defines wave speed, while electromagnetic Doppler shifts require relativistic treatment at high relative speed.
