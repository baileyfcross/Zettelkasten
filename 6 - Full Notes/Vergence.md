# Vergence

Vergence is the rotation of the eyes that allows their images to fuse at different viewing distances. The eyes converge for near objects and diverge for farther objects, and muscular feedback from this rotation acts as a [[Depth Cue]].

In natural viewing, vergence and [[Accommodation]] generally agree about distance. A stereoscopic display can draw images that drive vergence toward a virtual depth even though the physical screen keeps accommodation fixed at another depth. This mismatch is an important limit on visual [[Display Fidelity]] and can contribute to discomfort.
