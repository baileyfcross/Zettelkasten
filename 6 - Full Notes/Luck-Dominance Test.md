# Luck-Dominance Test

A luck-dominance test asks designated players to make the worst available decisions and attempt to lose. In a game where skill strongly controls the outcome, intentional losing should produce a clear and consistent defeat, especially when another player is trying to win.

If the intended loser cannot reliably cause that result, luck is likely dominant despite the presence of better and worse decisions. The test is a practical diagnostic rather than a universal formula; it examines whether agency has enough leverage to overcome the game's random structure in an individual session.
