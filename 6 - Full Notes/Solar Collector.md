# Solar Collector

A solar collector absorbs incident sunlight and transfers useful heat to a fluid such as water or air. Heat can serve a task directly or feed a later power cycle. The source groups collectors by operating temperature and distinguishes flat-plate units, which can use diffuse light, from focusing units that concentrate [[Beam Solar Radiation]]. Useful heat is incident absorbed energy minus optical and thermal losses.
