# Spearman Rank Correlation

Spearman rank correlation computes a correlation after replacing measurements with their ranks. It measures monotonic association and reduces the leverage of an extreme magnitude because only the observation's order is retained.

The statistic is useful when a [[Pearson Correlation Coefficient]] is dominated by outliers or when a relationship is monotonic but not well summarized as linear. Ranking also discards distance information, so the two coefficients answer different questions.
