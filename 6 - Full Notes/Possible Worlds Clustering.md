# Possible Worlds Clustering

Possible-worlds clustering treats an uncertain dataset as a distribution over deterministic realizations. A clustering property is evaluated across those worlds, allowing probabilities or expected qualities to reflect alternative values.

The semantics are direct but exact enumeration is usually infeasible because worlds multiply across uncertain objects. Sampling, bounding, or analytic expectations approximate the distribution and introduce their own accuracy-cost tradeoffs.
