# XAML Resources and Templates

XAML resources give names to reusable values such as brushes, styles, and templates at application, page, or control scope. Resource lookup lets many elements share a visual definition rather than duplicate property settings.

Templates define the visual structure used to present a control or data item. Together, resources and templates separate consistent presentation rules from individual instances and allow a theme to change without rewriting the underlying data or behavior.
