# Cellular Handoff

Cellular handoff transfers an active call or data session from one base station to another as the device moves. The system compares signal conditions and changes the serving cell without ending the connection.

Handoff requires subscriber databases, measurement, signaling, and coordinated switching. It turns many local radio cells into the experience of one continuous mobile network.
