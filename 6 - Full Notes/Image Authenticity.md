# Image Authenticity

Image authenticity is the claim that a photograph represents an unaltered output of its stated capture process. Forensic examination cannot prove this claim absolutely: a clean result means only that the applied techniques did not find evidence of tampering under their operating conditions.

Negative findings carry different weight for different files. A high-resolution image with intact metadata and strong physical cues is more testable than a compressed, downsampled, or stripped copy. Authentication is therefore an evidence-weighting judgment, not a binary property supplied by one tool.
