# Golden Path Evolution

Golden path evolution keeps supported workflows aligned with current tools, rules, and user needs. Adoption changes, broken templates, new regulations, and shifts in preferred technology are evidence that a once-effective path needs repair, replacement, or retirement.

The platform should observe which paths users follow and provide direct channels for feature and improvement requests. Golden paths are product features, so each needs ownership, versioning, feedback, and a lifecycle. Platform engineers also benefit from golden paths for creating and updating the user-facing paths themselves, reducing the bottleneck of one team manually maintaining every template.
