# Blender User Preferences

The Preferences editor controls Blender-wide behavior rather than the contents of a single scene. It can replace any editor in an area or appear in its own window through the Edit menu.

Preferences cover a large range of options, including interface behavior, input, add-ons, and keymaps. Factory settings and restore controls provide a route back when extensive customization makes the application difficult to understand or follow alongside standard instructions.
