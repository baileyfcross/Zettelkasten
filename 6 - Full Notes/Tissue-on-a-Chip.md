# Tissue-on-a-Chip

A tissue-on-a-chip recreates a focused tissue unit or process at microscale, such as connective-tissue wound closure or muscle contraction. The platform controls geometry and stimuli while allowing repeated imaging and measurement.

Compared with an organ model, its narrower target can simplify interpretation, though it omits interactions that emerge only across several tissues.
