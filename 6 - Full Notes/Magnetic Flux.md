# Magnetic Flux

Magnetic flux through a surface is $\Phi=\int B\cdot dA$; for a uniform field and flat area it is $BA\cos\theta$. It measures the normal magnetic field crossing the surface and has SI unit weber. A coil with $N$ turns has flux linkage $N\Phi$.
