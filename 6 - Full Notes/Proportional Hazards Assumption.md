# Proportional Hazards Assumption

The proportional hazards assumption states that the hazard ratio between comparison groups is constant over time. Their baseline hazards may change, but the multiplicative relationship should remain stable.

Roughly parallel cumulative-hazard curves provide a visual diagnostic. Clear crossings or changing separation suggest that a single Cox hazard ratio may conceal time-varying effects and that another specification is needed.
