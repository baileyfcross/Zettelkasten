# Goal-Gradient Effect

The goal-gradient effect is the tendency for effort to accelerate as a person perceives that a goal is near. Visible progress can therefore change behavior even when the remaining work is objectively unchanged.

Progress bars, partially filled experience meters, and initial "free" progress can make a reward feel closer and increase persistence. The effect depends on perceived distance, so feedback that hides or resets progress can weaken it.
