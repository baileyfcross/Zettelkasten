# Method of Difference

The method of difference compares situations that vary in a candidate cause while other relevant circumstances are held as similar as possible. If the effect changes with that factor, the contrast supports a causal claim.

Real cases rarely differ in only one respect, and causes can depend on combinations of conditions. The method therefore motivates more rigorous [[Variational Induction]] rather than functioning as an infallible rule.
