# Constant Time Complexity

Constant time complexity, written $O(1)$, means that an operation needs no more than a fixed amount of work regardless of [[Input Size]]. Checking whether the last digit of an integer is odd or even is constant time because the rest of the digits need not be examined.

Constant time is the fastest complexity family discussed in the source. The notation does not require one literal step; it permits any fixed number of steps that does not grow with $n$.
