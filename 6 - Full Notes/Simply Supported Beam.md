# Simply Supported Beam

A simply supported beam is restrained against transverse displacement at its supports while remaining free to rotate. The support conditions create reactions but do not impose end moments in the ideal model.

In finite element equations, the supported displacement degrees of freedom are prescribed as zero and rotational degrees remain unknown. Symmetric loading and geometry can produce symmetric reactions and deflection.
