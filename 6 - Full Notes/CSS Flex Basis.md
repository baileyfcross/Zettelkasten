# CSS Flex Basis

The `flex-basis` property establishes an item's starting main-axis size before [[CSS Flex Grow and Shrink|growth or shrinkage]] distributes free space. It can use a length, percentage, content-based value, or `auto` behavior that consults the relevant width or height.

A zero basis asks items to divide space mainly according to flex factors, whereas an automatic or content basis preserves more of their starting size. The `flex` shorthand combines basis, growth, and shrink factors.
