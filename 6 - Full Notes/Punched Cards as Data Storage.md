# Punched Cards as Data Storage

Herman Hollerith used holes in standardized cards to encode attributes of census records. Cards could be sorted, counted, reused, and carried among machines, turning information into a durable machine-readable object.

The same physical medium later stored business records, programs, and intermediate results. Its value came from separating coded information from the machine that processed it.
