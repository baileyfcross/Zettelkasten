# Sonification

Sonification is the process of turning information into sound. It is the auditory counterpart of visualizing data.

A mapping assigns data properties to audible properties such as pitch, intensity, timing, or spatial location. For example, moving a hand through a fluid-flow dataset could produce sounds whose frequencies vary with flow speed. This lets an [[Auditory Display]] communicate patterns without requiring continuous visual attention.

The mapping must remain understandable and should complement rather than overload other information channels.
