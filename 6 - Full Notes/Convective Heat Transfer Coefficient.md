# Convective Heat Transfer Coefficient

Newton's law of cooling expresses convective heat rate as $q=Ah(T_s-T_\infty)$. The coefficient $h$ packages the effect of fluid velocity, orientation, surface condition, and other flow characteristics on exchange between a wall and the surrounding bulk fluid.

Because $h$ represents a coupled transport process, it is not generally a fixed material property. Correlations or experiments supply an effective value, and [[Thermal Model Verification and Sensitivity|sensitivity analysis]] should test its influence when flow conditions are uncertain.
