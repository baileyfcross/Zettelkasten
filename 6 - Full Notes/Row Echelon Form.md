# Row Echelon Form

A matrix is in row echelon form when zero rows are at the bottom, every nonzero row begins with a leading one, and each leading one lies to the right of the leading one above it.

As an [[Augmented Matrix]], this form exposes pivots and free variables. The corresponding [[Linear System]] can be solved from the last nonzero row upward by [[Back Substitution]].
