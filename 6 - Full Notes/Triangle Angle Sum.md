# Triangle Angle Sum

The interior angles of a Euclidean triangle sum to $180^\circ$. Drawing a line through one vertex parallel to the opposite side transfers the other two angles by alternate-angle relationships; the three then form a straight angle. The result depends on Euclidean parallel geometry and does not hold unchanged for a [[Spherical Triangle]].
