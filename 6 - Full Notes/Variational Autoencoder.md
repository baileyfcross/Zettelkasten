# Variational Autoencoder

A variational autoencoder learns a probabilistic latent representation by encoding each input as a distribution and decoding samples back into data. Its objective balances reconstruction with regularization toward a chosen latent prior.

The regularized space supports smooth interpolation and generation as well as feature extraction. The probabilistic assumptions and tradeoff in the objective influence how much detail and disentanglement the latent variables contain.
