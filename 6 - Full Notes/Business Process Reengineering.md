# Business Process Reengineering

Business process reengineering redesigns work at the process level instead of automating every existing step unchanged. It identifies necessary processes and workflows, removes or changes those that do not contribute, and implements a more efficient future arrangement.

A cloud transition creates an opportunity for this redesign because applications, information services, and complete business-process services can replace different portions of the old flow. The technology choice should follow the redesigned process rather than define it prematurely.
