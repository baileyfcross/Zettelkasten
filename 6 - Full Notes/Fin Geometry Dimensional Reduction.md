# Fin Geometry Dimensional Reduction

Fin geometry can be represented as a one-dimensional line with cross-sectional properties, a two-dimensional profile with an assigned thickness, or a complete three-dimensional solid. Each added dimension retains more spatial variation at greater meshing and solution cost.

The reduced model must preserve the quantities used by the physics, such as conduction area and exposed perimeter. Comparing corresponding outputs across dimensions shows whether the simpler representation is adequate for the design question.
