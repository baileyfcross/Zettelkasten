# Rational Inequality

A rational inequality compares a [[Rational Expression]] with zero or another expression. Zeros of the numerator and denominator divide the number line into sign intervals, but denominator zeros are always excluded. A sign chart avoids unsafe cross-multiplication by a quantity whose sign may be unknown.
