# XPath Logical Locator

An XPath logical locator combines conditions with `and`, `or`, or `not`. `and` requires every condition, `or` accepts alternatives, and `not` excludes elements with a specified attribute or value.

Logical expressions make an [[Attribute-Based XPath Locator]] more precise without depending on a full structural path. The conditions should remain understandable; a locator that embeds too much page logic becomes difficult to diagnose when it fails.
