# Cloud Service Provider

A cloud service provider supplies a cloud service to consumers and orchestrates the resources required to activate it. The provider maintains the service offering, measures its operation, and agrees on a price and service commitment with the consumer.

The provider distinguishes cost from price: cost is what it bears to create and operate the service, while price is what it charges. A provider may build its own services as a [[Cloud Service Creator]] or combine services obtained elsewhere.
