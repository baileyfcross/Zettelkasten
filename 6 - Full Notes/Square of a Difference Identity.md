# Square of a Difference Identity

The square of a difference identity is

$$(a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2.$$

It follows from the [[Square of a Sum Identity]] by substituting (-b) for (b), or by directly distributing ((a-b)(a-b)). The first and last terms are positive squares, while the cross terms combine to (-2ab).

The identity is reversible: a trinomial with this exact structure factors as ((a-b)^2). It should not be confused with the [[Difference of Squares Identity]], which has no middle term.
