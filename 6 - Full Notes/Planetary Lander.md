# Planetary Lander

A planetary lander is a robotic spacecraft designed to reach and operate on the surface of another world. It must survive arrival and descent, tolerate uncertain terrain and environmental conditions, and carry instruments able to answer questions without a laboratory crew.

Early lunar landers demonstrated that proposed landing regions could support the weight of a spacecraft and returned close views needed for human planning. Mars landers later examined surface chemistry and searched for evidence related to life. A successful landing trades the broad coverage of a flyby or orbiter for sustained measurements at one location.
