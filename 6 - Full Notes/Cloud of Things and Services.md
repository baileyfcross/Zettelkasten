# Cloud of Things and Services

A cloud of things and services combines connected devices with the cloud-hosted workflows that monitor and support them. The book uses this term for a specialist cloud joining the Internet of Things with the [[Internet of Services]].

Automation is its central premise: sensed conditions can trigger maintenance, information, billing, or control at the place where a device is located. The cloud can use public, private, community, or hybrid deployment according to the devices and consumers involved.
