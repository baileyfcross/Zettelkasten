# Dominant Physics Selection for Thermal Problems

A model should include the mechanisms that materially affect its intended result. Conduction may dominate inside a solid, fluid motion may make convection essential, surface temperatures may make radiation significant, and phase change may add latent-energy behavior.

Adding every possible effect increases data requirements and numerical coupling without guaranteeing a better prediction. A defensible selection connects the [[Thermal Management Goals|question of interest]] to expected scales, available evidence, and a planned sensitivity check for effects initially treated as negligible.
