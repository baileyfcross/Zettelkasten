# Intel Founding

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore founded Intel in 1968 after leaving Fairchild Semiconductor, later joined by Andrew Grove. The company initially focused on semiconductor memory rather than processors.

Intel’s position within Silicon Valley’s spin-off network combined manufacturing knowledge with a strategy for high-volume standardized chips. That foundation supported its later microprocessor business.
