# Simulation Tail Risk

Simulation tail risk is the danger carried by rare, extreme outcomes that lie far from the common results. A system can appear to deliver small steady gains in most trials while an infrequent event erases all of them.

Evaluating only typical samples or the proportion of successful trials can therefore be misleading. A game simulation should inspect the full distribution and the impact of low-probability failures, especially when one exceptional player or economic event can destabilize the wider system.
