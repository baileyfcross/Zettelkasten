# 2D Reflection Transformation

A two-dimensional reflection produces a mirror image across a selected line. Reflection across the x-axis negates y, reflection across the y-axis negates x, and reflection through the origin negates both coordinates. These axis cases can be regarded as signed scaling transformations.

Reflection across an arbitrary line is composed from simpler steps: translate the line to pass through the origin, rotate it onto a principal axis, reflect across that axis, and then apply the inverse rotation and translation. The construction illustrates how an unfamiliar transform becomes a sequence of familiar affine transforms.
