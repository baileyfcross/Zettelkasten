# Steel Floor Vibration Control

Vibration can be reduced by separating the source, increasing structural stiffness, increasing participating mass, increasing damping, or changing the layout so sensitive uses are farther from excitation. Deeper beams or girders raise natural frequency, added mass can reduce acceleration but may lower frequency, and partitions, ceilings, and furnishings can add damping that a bare-floor calculation lacks.

Each measure changes more than one response parameter, so mitigation must be reanalyzed rather than selected by intuition. Moving an exercise area or isolating equipment can be more efficient than strengthening an entire floor. After construction, tuned damping or supplemental framing may work but is disruptive and expensive. Early coordination of span, beam spacing, composite action, occupancy, and finish assumptions is therefore the most dependable control strategy.
