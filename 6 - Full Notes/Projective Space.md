# Projective Space

Real projective $n$-space consists of the lines through the origin in $\mathbb R^{n+1}$. Equivalently, each projective point is an equivalence class of nonzero vectors under multiplication by a nonzero scalar.

This construction models perspective: all points on a viewing ray project to one image point. [[Projective Coordinates]] record a representative of the equivalence class rather than a unique coordinate vector.
