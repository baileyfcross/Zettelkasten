# Social Contextual Factor

A social contextual factor is relationship or interaction information that conditions a user's behavior. The book groups rich social evidence into [[Individual Preference]] and [[Interpersonal Influence]], derived from content, user-item activity, user-user activity, and explicit relations.

Context makes recommendation more than a lookup of similar items. It explains how an identical item can receive different responses depending on receivers, senders, and their prior history.
