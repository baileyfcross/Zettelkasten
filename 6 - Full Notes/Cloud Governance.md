# Cloud Governance

Cloud governance is the organizational oversight used to keep cloud services aligned with business needs, standards, security policy, regulation, and financial constraints. It defines who may choose services, which controls apply, and how performance and risk are reviewed.

Interoperating services should remain under compatible governance rather than falling between separate owners. A governance board, catalog, audit process, and reporting practices convert policy into recurring decisions and evidence.
