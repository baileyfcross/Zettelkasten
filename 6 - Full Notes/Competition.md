# Competition

Competition is a form of play in which participants pursue mutually exclusive outcomes. One player's success prevents another from achieving the same result, so rules must define how the conflict is resolved.

Competition does not require hostility. It can coexist with cooperation inside teams, and a fair competitive design gives participants a credible opportunity to win through the permitted actions.
