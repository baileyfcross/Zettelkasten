# Seebeck Effect

The Seebeck effect produces an electrical potential when a conductor has a temperature gradient, even before current flows. In a pair of different materials, the difference between their responses gives a voltage across hot and cold junctions. That voltage is the direct heat-to-electricity basis of a [[Thermoelectric Generator]]; it is not heat released by current through resistance.
