# Permutation Test

A permutation test approximates a [[Null Distribution]] by repeatedly shuffling group labels and recalculating the statistic. If labels are exchangeable under the null, these rearrangements represent results that could occur without a group effect.

The procedure is useful when a standard analytical approximation is unavailable or doubtful. Its validity depends on the randomization structure, while the number of permutations determines the resolution and Monte Carlo error of the estimated tail probability.
