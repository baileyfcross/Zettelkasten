# Differential Equation Order and Degree

The order of a differential equation is the order of its highest derivative. It indicates how many independent conditions are generally needed to select one solution from a family of solutions.

When the equation is polynomial in its derivatives, its degree is the exponent of the highest-order derivative after radicals and fractions involving derivatives have been removed. Order and degree classify the form of the equation but do not by themselves determine whether it is linear.
