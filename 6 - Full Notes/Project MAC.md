# Project MAC

Project MAC was an MIT research initiative begun in the 1960s to explore multiple-access computing and machine-aided cognition. Defense research funding supported work on time-sharing, operating systems, artificial intelligence, and interactive use.

The project gathered researchers around a vision of computers as responsive intellectual tools. Its successes and difficulties influenced later personal and networked computing.
