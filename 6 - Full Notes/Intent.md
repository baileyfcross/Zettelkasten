# Intent

An intent is the action or goal an assistant assigns to a user's utterance. Examples include requesting weather, sending a message, or reserving a table, independent of the exact words used.

An [[Intent Classification|intent classifier]] selects among the intents defined in an [[Intent Schema]]. Associated [[Entity|entities]] supply the arguments needed to carry out the goal.
