# Blender Properties Editor

The Properties editor modifies attributes of a scene and its elements. Its contexts move from broad settings such as rendering, output, view layers, the scene, and the world toward more specific object, constraint, data, material, and texture settings.

Available contexts depend on the active selection. A camera, mesh, material, or armature exposes different controls, so the editor acts as a context-sensitive view of the selected data rather than a fixed form.
