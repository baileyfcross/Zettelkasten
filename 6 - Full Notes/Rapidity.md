# Rapidity

Rapidity is the hyperbolic angle that parameterizes a collinear [[Lorentz Boost]]. For speed $v$, it is defined by

$$\tanh\chi=\frac{v}{c},\qquad \cosh\chi=\gamma,\qquad \sinh\chi=\gamma\frac{v}{c}.$$

Unlike ordinary velocity, rapidity is unbounded even though $|v|<c$. As $|\chi|$ increases, $\tanh\chi$ approaches one without reaching it. This converts the limiting-speed behavior into a property of the hyperbolic functions.

Successive boosts along the same axis add rapidities: $χ_{total}=χ_1+χ_2$. Translating that sum back into velocity gives [[Relativistic Velocity Addition]]. Rapidity is therefore the natural additive measure of relative motion in [[Minkowski Spacetime]].
