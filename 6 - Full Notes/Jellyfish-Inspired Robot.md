# Jellyfish-Inspired Robot

A jellyfish-inspired robot reproduces the body geometry and circumferential muscle organization that generate propulsive swimming in a jellyfish. Cardiomyocytes arranged on a flexible synthetic film contract synchronously and bend its lobes.

The example demonstrates that biological architecture can guide engineering alongside the direct use of biological material.
