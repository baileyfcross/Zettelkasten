# Multivariable Taylor Expansion

A multivariable Taylor expansion approximates a function near a chosen point using its partial derivatives there. The first-order terms give the local tangent plane, while second-order terms include pure and [[Mixed Partial Derivative|mixed partial derivatives]] that describe local curvature.

Keeping only finitely many terms produces a polynomial model valid near the expansion point. At a [[Stationary Point]], the first-order terms vanish, so the quadratic terms provide the leading information used to distinguish a maximum, minimum, or saddle.
