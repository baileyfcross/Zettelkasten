# Disinformation

Disinformation is intentionally false information created or distributed to deceive, cause anxiety, or obtain an improper benefit. Its defining distinction from [[Misinformation]] is the spreader's purpose rather than the truth value alone.

Later participants may forward the same claim sincerely, so intent can vary within one cascade. Detection may therefore combine content evidence with account behavior and network structure.
