# Cloud Monitoring and Alerting

Cloud monitoring and alerting observe service health, use, capacity, and failures and notify responsible people or automated processes when action is needed. Interoperating services benefit from common alert formats and routes so one team can interpret them consistently.

Proactive stress and security tests can supplement operational signals. Monitoring supports remediation only when thresholds relate to service requirements and alerts lead to owned, timely response procedures.
