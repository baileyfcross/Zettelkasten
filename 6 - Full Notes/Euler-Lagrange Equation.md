# Euler-Lagrange Equation

The Euler-Lagrange equation is the stationarity condition for a functional whose integrand depends on a function and its first derivative. It balances the partial derivative with respect to the function against the derivative of the partial derivative with respect to the function's slope.

In mechanics, applying it to the action built from a Lagrangian produces the equations of motion in generalized coordinates. A separate Euler-Lagrange equation holds for each independent coordinate.
