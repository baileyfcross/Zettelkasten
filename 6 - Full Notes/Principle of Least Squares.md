# Principle of Least Squares

The principle of least squares chooses model parameters that minimize the sum of squared residuals between observed and calculated values:

$$E=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\hat y_i)^2.$$

Squaring prevents positive and negative residuals from canceling and penalizes larger discrepancies more strongly. Setting the parameter derivatives of $E$ to zero produces [[Normal Equation|normal equations]] once the curve's functional form has been chosen.
