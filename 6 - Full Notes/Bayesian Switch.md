# Bayesian Switch

A Bayesian switch activates or deactivates part of a probabilistic model through a variable included in the decomposition. When active, the selected coherence or conditional relationship constrains the connected variables; when inactive, that relationship no longer contributes in the same way.

Because the switch can be uncertain, inference may average over active and inactive cases instead of making a premature hard choice. This provides conditional structure inside the probability model itself.
