# Parameter Prior

A parameter prior represents knowledge about model parameters before the selected observations are incorporated. It can favor plausible ranges, express symmetry, or regularize identification when data is limited.

Bayesian estimation multiplies this prior by the data likelihood and normalizes the result. As evidence accumulates, its influence usually becomes smaller unless the observations are weak or ambiguous.
