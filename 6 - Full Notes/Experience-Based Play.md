# Experience-Based Play

Experience-based play makes encountering a designed situation more important than winning or mastering a difficult action. Its value may come from exploration, the unfolding of a story, contemplation, or communal participation.

Removing a conventional victory condition does not remove design. The designer still structures attention, pacing, interpretation, and available movement so that players can discover the intended qualities of the experience.
