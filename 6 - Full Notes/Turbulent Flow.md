# Turbulent Flow

Turbulent flow contains irregular eddies and strong mixing rather than stable parallel streamlines. It develops when inertial effects overwhelm viscous damping above a geometry-dependent critical Reynolds number. Turbulence increases drag and energy loss, so formulas derived for [[Laminar Flow]] cannot generally be carried into the turbulent regime unchanged.
