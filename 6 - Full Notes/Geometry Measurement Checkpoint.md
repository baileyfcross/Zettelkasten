# Geometry Measurement Checkpoint

A geometry measurement checkpoint records known distances, areas, volumes, or counts before and after import, repair, simplification, or parameter changes. Agreement within an appropriate tolerance shows that the operation preserved the intended scale and material extent.

The checkpoint belongs in the workflow even when the shape looks simple. Visual inspection alone rarely exposes a uniform scale error, a small missing face, or a Boolean operation that removed the wrong region.
