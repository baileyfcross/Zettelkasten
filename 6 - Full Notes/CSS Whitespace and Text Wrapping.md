# CSS Whitespace and Text Wrapping

The `white-space` property controls whether source whitespace collapses, whether line breaks are preserved, and whether lines wrap automatically. Related wrapping and hyphenation properties affect how long tokens may break when they exceed available inline space.

These controls operate on rendered text rather than stylesheet source formatting. They can preserve preformatted material, permit emergency word breaks, or keep a sequence on one line depending on the chosen behavior.
