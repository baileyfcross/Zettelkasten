# Developer Experience Framework

The developer experience framework evaluates whether engineers can enter and sustain flow, understand the systems they use, and receive timely feedback. These dimensions capture the lived experience that delivery-throughput and activity measures can miss.

For a platform team, the framework turns developer experience into a product outcome rather than a vague promise. Surveys and interviews can reveal cognitive friction, unclear interfaces, slow feedback, and interruptions; operational data can then show where those perceptions correspond to specific workflows. Used beside [[DORA Metrics]] and the [[SPACE Framework]], it balances system performance with user experience.
