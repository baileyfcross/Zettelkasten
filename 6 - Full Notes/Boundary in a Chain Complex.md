# Boundary in a Chain Complex

An n-boundary is an element in the image of the differential dₙ₊₁: Cₙ₊₁ → Cₙ. It is produced by applying the preceding map in a [[Chain Complex]].

Since dₙdₙ₊₁ = 0, every boundary lies in the kernel of dₙ and is therefore a [[Cycle in a Chain Complex]]. Homology identifies cycles that differ by a boundary.
