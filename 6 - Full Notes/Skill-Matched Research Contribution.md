# Skill-Matched Research Contribution

Skill-matched research contribution assigns work according to the resources and expertise a participant can supply. A collaborator without access to the population needed for a replication may still code studies, review methods, analyze data, maintain documentation, or provide specialized consultation.

The approach turns diversity of skill into project capacity instead of treating the central experimental task as the only valuable contribution. Explicit roles and credit are important so supporting work remains visible. Matching also lowers the [[Low Barrier to Scientific Contribution|barrier to entry]] by giving prospective volunteers a tractable way to participate.
