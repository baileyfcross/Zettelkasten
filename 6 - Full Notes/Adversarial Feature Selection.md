# Adversarial Feature Selection

Adversarial feature selection evaluates variables in the presence of manipulated inputs or strategic attacks. A highly predictive feature may be undesirable if an attacker can cheaply alter it.

Robust selection favors evidence that remains informative under plausible perturbations. This connects feature relevance to a threat model rather than assuming that training and deployment data are passively generated.
