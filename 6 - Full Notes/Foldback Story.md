# Foldback Story

A foldback story is a branching interactive plot whose paths reconverge at planned inevitable events before branching again. It gives players local variation while limiting the combinatorial growth of wholly separate plot lines.

Reconvergence reduces production cost and preserves major dramatic beats. The branches still need distinct consequences or perspectives; otherwise the player learns that choices are cosmetic.
