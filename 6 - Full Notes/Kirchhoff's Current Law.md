# Kirchhoff's Current Law

Kirchhoff's current law states that the sum of currents entering a circuit junction equals the sum leaving it. The law follows from conservation of [[Electric Charge]]: charge cannot accumulate indefinitely at an ideal junction. Assigning consistent current directions turns the statement into an algebraic node equation.
