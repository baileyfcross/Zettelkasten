# P-Hacking

P-hacking is the use of flexible analytic choices to obtain or emphasize statistically significant results. It can include trying many variables, exclusions, transformations, interaction terms, or model specifications without accounting for the search.

The problem is not exploration itself but presenting searched-for evidence as a single prespecified test. Defenses include an [[A Priori Hypothesis]], a documented analytic plan, honest labeling of exploratory findings, and validation in independent data.
