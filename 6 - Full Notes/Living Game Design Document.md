# Living Game Design Document

A living game design document records the current concept, design values, player experience, and operational rules of a game. It changes when prototypes and playtests replace assumptions with better knowledge.

Its purpose is shared clarity, not speculative completeness. The document is valuable when a collaborator can use it to understand the present design and make the next artifact, and harmful when obsolete detail appears more authoritative than the playable game.

The appropriate format depends on the [[Game Design Document Audience]]. A programmer, artist, publisher, and tabletop player may need different views, references, and levels of precision; the document should communicate features in the form its actual implementers prefer.
