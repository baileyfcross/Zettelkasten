# Noetherian Ring

A Noetherian ring is a ring in which every [[Ideal]] is finitely generated. Equivalently, every nonempty collection of ideals has a maximal member and every ascending chain of ideals eventually becomes stationary.

This finiteness condition supports decomposition and computation. Quotients of Noetherian rings remain Noetherian, and the [[Hilbert Basis Theorem]] extends the property from a ring to its polynomial rings.
