# GNSS Interoperability

GNSS interoperability is the ability of independently operated constellations and augmentations to be used together without harmful interference. Shared or coordinated frequency bands and compatible signal structures make common receivers practical, while published relationships among time and coordinate systems support combined solutions. Interoperability improves service and encourages cooperation, yet each provider retains political control and can preserve protected military capabilities.
