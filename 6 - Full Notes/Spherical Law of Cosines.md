# Spherical Law of Cosines

For a unit-sphere triangle with side lengths $a,b,c$ opposite angles $A,B,C$, the spherical law of cosines is

$$\cos c=\cos a\cos b+\sin a\sin b\cos C.$$

It replaces the plane [[Law of Cosines]] when the triangle's sides are great-circle arcs and solves spherical side-angle relationships.
