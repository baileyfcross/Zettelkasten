# Fractal

A fractal is a form with self-similar structure across different scales of observation. Zooming into part of the form reveals patterns related to those visible at larger scales.

Computational iteration can generate fractals from compact rules, as in the [[Mandelbrot Set Simulation]]. Their repeated structure also provides efficient ways to model irregular natural forms and visualize scale-dependent behavior.
