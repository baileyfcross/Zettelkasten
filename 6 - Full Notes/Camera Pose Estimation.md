# Camera Pose Estimation

Camera pose estimation determines the camera's position and orientation relative to a known target or map. The estimate connects image measurements to a world coordinate system so the renderer can generate virtual content from the observed viewpoint.

Planar markers can yield pose from a homography, while noncoplanar point correspondences can use a perspective-n-point method. Calibration supplies the camera's internal parameters, and robust estimation helps reject incorrect matches.
