# Hyperbolic Network Visualization

Hyperbolic network visualization places a large hierarchy or graph in a finite view whose available space expands rapidly away from the center. Moving a region toward the center enlarges its detail while compressing distant context toward the boundary.

The technique can show global hierarchy, centrality, and community structure while retaining local neighborhoods. The geometric transformation supports an adaptive focus instead of permanently discarding off-center nodes.
