# Large-Scale Replication Sampling Frame

A large-scale replication sampling frame defines the population of published studies from which replication targets are selected. A documented rule—such as journals, publication period, article order, and a default study within each article—limits discretionary selection after researchers know which cases are convenient or appealing.

Feasibility still shapes the achieved sample because specialized populations, expensive instruments, or long follow-up can exclude studies. Coding both selected and unselected candidates makes that limitation analyzable. The eventual replication rate must therefore be interpreted as applying to the sampled and feasible frame, not automatically to an entire discipline.
