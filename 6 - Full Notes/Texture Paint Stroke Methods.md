# Texture Paint Stroke Methods

Texture Paint can generate strokes as continuous dots, spaced dabs, an airbrush, a draggable single dab, an anchored mark, a straight line, or a curve. Each method converts pointer movement into paint samples differently.

Spacing, jitter, input smoothing, and pressure response shape the distribution within the selected method. Choosing the method first produces more predictable marks than trying to force every edge, spray, or stamp through one freehand stroke behavior.
