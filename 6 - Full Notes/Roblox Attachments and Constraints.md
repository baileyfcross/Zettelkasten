# Roblox Attachments and Constraints

An Attachment marks a position and orientation inside a part. A Constraint connects two attachments and applies a physical relationship between the parts, such as keeping points aligned or permitting movement around a controlled axis.

Constraint behavior depends on attachment placement, orientation, and whether the participating parts are anchored. If an expected mechanism does not move, the attachment axes and anchoring state should be checked before its force values. Specialized examples include [[Roblox Hinge and Spring Constraints]] and the powered behavior of [[Roblox Motorized Constraint]].
