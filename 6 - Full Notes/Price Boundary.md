# Price Boundary

A price boundary is a designed floor or ceiling that limits how far market prices can move. A guaranteed low export price and higher import price can create a channel within which players trade freely.

The channel prevents a seller or buyer from completely controlling access while preserving dynamic price discovery. Linking its bounds to item rarity or power lets the viable price range expand coherently with progression.
