# Regression Robustness

Regression robustness is the stability of a substantive conclusion across reasonable model specifications, coding choices, and covariate sets. If the exposure estimate remains far from significance or retains a similar magnitude after defensible adjustments, the conclusion is less dependent on one arbitrary formula.

Robustness should be demonstrated with planned sensitivity analyses rather than declared from a single large model. A stable estimate can still be biased when all specifications share the same flawed measurement or selection process.
