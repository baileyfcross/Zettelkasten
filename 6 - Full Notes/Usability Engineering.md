# Usability Engineering

Usability engineering is an iterative process for creating and refining a [[User Experience]]. A representative lifecycle moves through analysis, design, implementation, and evaluation, with iteration within and between stages.

Analysis identifies users, tasks, context, and requirements. Design turns those findings into interaction concepts. Implementation realizes concepts as [[Prototype]]s or working systems. [[User Interface Evaluation]] tests whether the design meets its goals and supplies evidence for revision.

The process can pursue [[Usability]], usefulness, and emotional impact. Iteration matters because guidelines and designer experience cannot predict every problem before people use the system.
