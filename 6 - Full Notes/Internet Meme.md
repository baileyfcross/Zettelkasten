# Internet Meme

An Internet meme is a group of digital items that share common characteristics of content, form, or stance; were created with awareness of one another; and were circulated, imitated, or transformed by many users. The unit of analysis is therefore the family of versions rather than one exceptionally popular item.

This definition separates a meme from [[Viral Content]], which may spread mainly as unchanged copies. It also makes user interpretation and modification constitutive rather than incidental: an Internet meme exists through the visible relationships among multiple artifacts and the people who remake them.
