# Direct Time-Series Labeling

Direct time-series labeling places a series or group name beside its visible mark instead of requiring repeated lookup in a separate legend. Labels are especially helpful when many colored [[Temporal Polyline|temporal polylines]] cross or end at different locations. Automatic placement must avoid collisions and ambiguous attachment. A subset of endpoints or representative observations is usually more legible than labeling every point on every path.
