# AI Techno-Solutionism

AI techno-solutionism is the belief that data and intelligent technology can provide a decisive solution to problems whose central difficulties are also ethical, political, or social. It turns contested questions about goals and distribution into optimization tasks.

AI can contribute useful prediction and coordination, but it cannot make value conflicts disappear. Climate change, inequality, and public health involve institutions, behavior, power, and priorities that are not resolved by recognizing more patterns. Treating the technology as the main answer can delay political action, expand surveillance, and strengthen the same forms of control that helped create the problem.
