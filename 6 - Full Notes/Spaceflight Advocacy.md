# Spaceflight Advocacy

Spaceflight advocacy is organized persuasion that travel and activity beyond Earth are feasible, valuable, and worthy of public or state support. Advocates publish technical explanations, build societies, stage demonstrations, use popular media, and connect future visions with national, scientific, or economic goals.

Advocacy was essential when most people considered space travel absurd. It helped create a constituency and a pool of expertise, but did not by itself finance operational launch systems. War and political competition supplied the resources. Later advocates continued to frame new programs as steps toward the future even when governments supported them for prestige or institutional reasons.
