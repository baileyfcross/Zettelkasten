# Surface Geometric Model

A surface model joins surface elements to represent the skin of a three-dimensional object. A [[Wireframe Geometric Model]] can be promoted to a surface model by defining faces over its edges. Analytic elements include planes, ruled surfaces, surfaces of revolution, and tabulated cylinders; synthetic elements include Hermite, Bezier, B-spline, Coons, and Gordon surfaces.

Surface models can express smooth varying shapes and support visual or structural inspection, but they require more computation and do not necessarily define a valid interior. A surface description becomes a solid representation only when its boundaries and inside-outside relationships are complete and consistent.
