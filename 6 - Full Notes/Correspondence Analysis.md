# Correspondence Analysis

Correspondence analysis maps a table of non-negative occurrences or frequencies into a factor space where relationships among rows and columns can be viewed geometrically. Rows may be scenes or documents, while columns may be words or attributes. Their profiles are compared using a chi-squared weighted distance before the factor mapping.

The method does not begin by imposing predefined classes. It uses row and column masses, principal axes of inertia, and the dual relation between observations and attributes to reveal how a record is positioned relative to the overall pattern. The book uses this platform to map semantic context and then adds hierarchical clustering to examine change.
