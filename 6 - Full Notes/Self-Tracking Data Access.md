# Self-Tracking Data Access

Self-tracking data access is the practical ability to retrieve and use records generated by a personal device or service. Access is not a single yes-or-no condition. Viewing a proprietary score, downloading processed observations, obtaining raw sensor output, and connecting through a [[Personal Data API]] support different questions.

Deeper access can enable a person to combine sources, inspect an unexplained calculation, build an adaptive medical system, or preserve records when a company closes. Restrictions determine who can ask new questions and who receives the benefit of the answers.

Access must be paired with security and understandable documentation. A usable right provides a safe means of retrieval and explains the units, intervals, transformations, and missingness needed to interpret the data.
