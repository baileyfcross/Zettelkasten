# Community of Practice

A community of practice connects people who share a discipline or technical interest across delivery teams. Members exchange techniques, standards, tools, and learning without forming another team with its own competing Sprint Goal.

This lateral network preserves craft coherence while cross-discipline teams remain focused on features. Participation and influence come from shared practice rather than a separate command hierarchy.
