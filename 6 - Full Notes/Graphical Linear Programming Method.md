# Graphical Linear Programming Method

The graphical method solves a linear program with two decision variables by plotting each constraint, identifying the common feasible region in the nonnegative quadrant, and evaluating the objective at its extreme points. The best corner gives an optimum when one exists.

A second approach moves a constant-objective [[Isoprofit and Isocost Line]] across the feasible region until the last contact for maximization or first contact for minimization. The picture also makes infeasibility, unboundedness, redundancy, and tied optima visible.
