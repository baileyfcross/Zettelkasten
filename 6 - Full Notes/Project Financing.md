# Project Financing

Project financing determines how much capital is committed, when it becomes available, and what cost or constraints accompany it. A technically feasible big-data initiative can still be unattractive if it consumes scarce funds too early or exposes the organization to excessive risk.

Financing analysis connects the implementation schedule to cash needs, expected benefits, and alternatives competing for the same capital.
