# Trained Rather Than Programmed Systems

A trained system acquires internal parameters from examples or interaction rather than having every decision rule written by a programmer. The training algorithm defines how parameters change, while data and objectives shape the resulting behavior.

This shifts computational design toward dataset construction, evaluation, and control of generalization. The absence of hand-written rules does not remove human responsibility for the model, training process, or deployment context.
