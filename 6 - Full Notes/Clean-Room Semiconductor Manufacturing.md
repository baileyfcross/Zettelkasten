# Clean-Room Semiconductor Manufacturing

A semiconductor clean room filters air and controls contamination because tiny particles can ruin microscopic circuit features. Workers wear protective garments and follow tightly documented procedures.

Missile reliability programs helped institutionalize this discipline. Traceable production records allow defective batches to be identified and removed when later tests reveal a problem.
