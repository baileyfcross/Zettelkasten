# Derivative Closure of Two-Eigenvalue Quantum Costs

Expectation values generated by an $r$-gate have a restricted trigonometric dependence on the gate parameter. Their repeated derivatives obey a two-step recurrence, so every [[Higher-Order Derivative]] can be reduced to a known linear combination of the first and second derivatives.

This closure explains why the [[Generalized Parameter-Shift Rule]] contains enough information to represent derivatives of arbitrary order within the two-eigenvalue setting. Repeated applications of the centered parameter-shift rule provide another route to the same higher-order information.

The result is structural rather than a statement about arbitrary smooth functions. It follows from the spectrum of the gate generator and does not extend unchanged to circuits whose differentiated generator has more than two distinct eigenvalues.
