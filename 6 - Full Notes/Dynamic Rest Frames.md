# Dynamic Rest Frames

Dynamic rest frames preserve a stable visual reference while other parts of a virtual scene move. A cockpit, vehicle frame, grid, or temporarily reduced view can remain stationary relative to the user even while the exterior world depicts rapid travel.

The stable region gives the visual system an anchor and can reduce the sensory disagreement that contributes to [[Cybersickness]]. Unlike a permanently narrow view, a dynamic frame can fade in during acceleration or turning, adapt to the user's motion, and then recede when the scene becomes stable. It may also guide attention by emphasizing the region in which the next action will occur.

The technique is a mitigation rather than a cure. Its usefulness depends on the movement, the person's susceptibility, and agreement with other cues described by [[Sensory Conflict Theory]].
