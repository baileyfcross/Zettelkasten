# Temperature Effects on Barometric Glidepaths

A barometric altimeter interprets pressure through a standard-atmosphere model. When outside temperature differs from standard, the true vertical path associated with indicated altitudes can differ from the nominal descent angle. Temperature correction is therefore important when terrain clearance and stabilized approach geometry depend on barometric guidance.
