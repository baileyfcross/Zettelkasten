# Censorship-Evasion Meme

A censorship-evasion meme uses indirect language, homophones, images, or absurd symbols to communicate criticism while avoiding prohibited terms. Shared cultural knowledge allows participants to recognize the political reference beneath an apparently innocuous surface.

Chinese memes such as the Grass Mud Horse and River Crab illustrate the tactic by turning wordplay and invented creatures into vehicles for discussing censorship and official harmony. Their ambiguity supports circulation, but their effectiveness also depends on a community able to decode the euphemism.
