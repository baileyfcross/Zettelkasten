# Beam Solar Radiation

Beam radiation reaches a surface in essentially straight rays from the Sun without first being scattered by the atmosphere. Its direction can be followed by a tracking collector and focused by mirrors or lenses. This makes it the useful part for a [[Concentrating Solar Collector]], whereas a fixed [[Flat-Plate Solar Collector]] can also receive diffuse light. Incidence angle changes the beam energy per unit collecting area.
