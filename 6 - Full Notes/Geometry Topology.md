# Geometry Topology

Geometry topology describes how vertices, edges, faces, and volumes connect independently of their exact sizes and curvatures. Two models can look nearly identical yet have different topology if one contains a gap, duplicate edge, disconnected face, or unintended internal boundary.

Finite-element preprocessing depends on this connectivity to form domains and transfer fields continuously across shared entities. Import repair and virtual operations often target topology even when the visible shape changes little.
