# Excel PRODUCT Function

The Excel PRODUCT function multiplies numeric arguments and the numeric contents of referenced ranges. It provides a concise alternative to a long multiplication chain and can combine individual factors with entire ranges in one expression.

Multiplication is sensitive to zero because one zero makes the entire product zero. The range should therefore be checked for placeholders or unintended zeros, as well as for missing factors outside its boundary. PRODUCT performs scalar multiplication across its inputs; calculations that need corresponding elements multiplied and then added use [[Excel SUMPRODUCT Function]] instead.
