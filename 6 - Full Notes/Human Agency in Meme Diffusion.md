# Human Agency in Meme Diffusion

Human agency in meme diffusion means that people do not merely transmit cultural units as passive hosts. They notice, interpret, select, alter, and strategically circulate artifacts according to social relationships, purposes, and norms.

Biological metaphors can obscure this activity by portraying memes as viruses or genes that control their carriers. Internet meme analysis corrects that tendency by examining visible creative decisions across versions. Even apparently faithful copying is a communicative act through which a person chooses an audience, timing, platform, and stance.
