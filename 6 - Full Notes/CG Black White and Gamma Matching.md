# CG Black White and Gamma Matching

A rendered object can have correct hue but still differ from the photographed plate in its shadow density, highlight density, or midtone contrast. Per-channel levels adjustments let the compositor compare and move black, white, and gamma values toward nearby plate material. Adjusting only overall brightness can leave colored shadows or highlights visibly wrong. This matching is most useful after shape, perspective, and lighting are plausible; it cannot disguise a camera mismatch. See [[Matching CG Key Fill and Rim Lights]].
