# Overlapping Clusters

Overlapping clusters permit an observation to participate in more than one group. They are useful when categories have gradual boundaries or when the same case legitimately exhibits characteristics of several clusters.

A fuzzy method represents overlap through membership values rather than forcing a single label. The interpretation must distinguish partial membership from probability: a point can belong to several fuzzy clusters at the same time.
