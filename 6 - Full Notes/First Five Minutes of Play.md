# First Five Minutes of Play

The first five minutes of play is a concept-document technique that describes what a new player sees, understands, and does immediately after starting. It converts a broad pitch into a concrete account of the opening experience.

The description can expose a concept that has setting and story but no clear activity. It also connects the primary gameplay mode, player role, onboarding, and initial promise before detailed level design begins.
