# Thermogravimetric Thermal Analysis

Thermogravimetric analysis records a sample's mass as temperature changes through a prescribed heating cycle. A decline in mass can identify degradation or evaporation and thereby establish an upper processing-temperature limit.

Coupling the measurement with evolved-gas analysis can also identify the composition and timing of released vapor. These observations define material transitions that a [[Temperature-Dependent Thermophysical Properties|temperature-dependent model]] should not silently extrapolate through.
