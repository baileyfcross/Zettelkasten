# Software Design Principles Patterns and Hints

Software designers draw on principles, patterns, and hints at different levels of generality. Principles guide choices toward system qualities, patterns describe recurring situations and proven structures, and hints capture judgments learned through experience.

None of the three is a mechanical recipe. Their value depends on recognizing when a situation fits, understanding the tradeoffs, and adapting the guidance to the system’s users, risks, and constraints.
