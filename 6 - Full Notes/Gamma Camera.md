# Gamma Camera

A gamma camera maps photons emitted by a [[Radioactive Tracer]]. A collimator accepts selected directions, a scintillation crystal converts gamma energy to light, and photomultipliers estimate event position and energy. Accumulating accepted events forms an image of tracer distribution and therefore physiological function rather than only anatomy.
