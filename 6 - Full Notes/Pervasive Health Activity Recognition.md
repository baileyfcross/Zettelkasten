# Pervasive Health Activity Recognition

Pervasive health activity recognition maps streams from body-worn and ambient sensors to activities such as walking, sleeping, eating, or taking medication. Models can use supervised examples, learned clusters, or sequential context to interpret the sensor pattern.

Activity complexity ranges from short motions to extended routines composed of smaller actions. Recognition should account for person-to-person variation and changes in the environment. The output is most useful when uncertainty is preserved, because an incorrect label can trigger an inappropriate health assessment or intervention.
