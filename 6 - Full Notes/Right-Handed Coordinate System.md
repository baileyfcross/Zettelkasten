# Right-Handed Coordinate System

A right-handed Cartesian coordinate system orients its three axes so that curling the fingers of the right hand from the positive $x$-axis toward the positive $y$-axis makes the thumb point along the positive $z$-axis.

This handedness fixes the sign convention for the [[Cross Product]]: $\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j}=\mathbf{k}$. Reversing the handedness reverses orientation-sensitive quantities even though distances and angles remain unchanged.
