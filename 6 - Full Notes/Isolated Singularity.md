# Isolated Singularity

An isolated singularity is a point where a complex function fails to be analytic even though it is analytic throughout some punctured neighborhood around that point. Its Laurent series determines the singularity's type.

A removable singularity has no genuine principal part, a pole has finitely many negative-power terms, and an essential singularity has infinitely many. This classification predicts local behavior and guides the evaluation of contour integrals.
