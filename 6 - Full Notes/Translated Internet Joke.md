# Translated Internet Joke

A translated Internet joke carries a humorous digital text into another linguistic community by rewriting its words while attempting to preserve the comic relationship among setup, reference, and punchline. Successful translation may require cultural replacement rather than word-for-word equivalence.

The circulation of translated jokes shows that humor can cross borders, but not frictionlessly. Language-specific puns, stereotypes, and background knowledge may limit portability. Comparing versions reveals how translators act as cultural producers who reconstruct the joke for a new audience.
