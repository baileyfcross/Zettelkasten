# Timation Program

Timation was a Naval Research Laboratory program for transferring precise time and testing satellite ranging. Its first two satellites used quartz oscillators and side-tone ranging, while the third mission evolved into Navigation Technology Satellite 1 and carried atomic clocks. Timation demonstrated the core architectural idea that synchronized clocks could reside in medium-Earth-orbit satellites and broadcast timing from which users determined range.
