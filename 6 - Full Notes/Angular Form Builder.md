# Angular Form Builder

Angular FormBuilder is an injectable helper for creating reactive form groups, controls, initial values, and validators with less repetitive construction code. It produces the same kind of explicit [[Form Model]] as direct `FormGroup` and `FormControl` construction.

The builder improves declaration convenience but does not change the separation between model-driven form logic and the HTML template.
