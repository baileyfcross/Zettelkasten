# Game Pitch

A game pitch communicates a game's vision persuasively to obtain a particular response, such as funding, validation, coverage, or team participation. Its content and depth change with the audience, context, and requested outcome.

An effective pitch opens with a concise [[Concept Statement]], shows the core experience with appropriate evidence, and limits detail to what the audience needs to build confidence. It ends with a clear call to action and is refined through practice and feedback.
