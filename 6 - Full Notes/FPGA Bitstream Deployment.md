# FPGA Bitstream Deployment

FPGA bitstream deployment loads the synthesized and routed hardware configuration onto the programmable device. It follows behavioral verification, timing analysis, IP packaging, and system integration.

The bitstream must match the driver, memory map, and board design used by the runtime. Deployment is therefore the final materialization of a specific hardware-software contract, not an interchangeable executable independent of its platform.
