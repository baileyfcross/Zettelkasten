# Situation Awareness

Situation awareness is an internal model of the current state of a user's environment. It includes perceiving relevant elements across time and space, understanding their meaning, and projecting how their status may change in the near future.

The model can include spatial relationships, other users, and task states, making it a basis for [[Decision-Making]]. In [[Navigation]], situation awareness depends on both spatial orientation—knowing one's location and viewing direction—and [[Spatial Knowledge]] stored in a [[Cognitive Map]].

A 3D interface can support situation awareness with overviews, landmarks, maps, and clearly managed viewpoint changes.

Mobile AR can add registered information about relevant objects while allowing the user to continue looking at the physical surroundings. This can improve discovery and awareness, but excessive or poorly placed augmentation can also cover the evidence the user needs to perceive the situation directly.
