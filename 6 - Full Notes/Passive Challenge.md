# Passive Challenge

A passive challenge is created by a static obstacle or spatial arrangement that does not require additional mechanics to operate. A maze can challenge navigation even when its walls never change.

Passive does not mean trivial. Layout, visibility, memory demand, and route structure can produce substantial difficulty without an adversary or continuously executing process.
