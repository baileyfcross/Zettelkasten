# Behavioral Search Log Limitation

A behavioral search log records actions exposed by a particular system, not a direct account of a user’s intention or understanding. Queries, clicks, timing, and reformulations are observable proxies shaped by the interface, collection rules, and population using the service.

Patterns may not generalize to another search system or user group. Interpretations should therefore be framed as behavior within the logged environment and compared against an explicit model of information-seeking rather than treated as mental states.
