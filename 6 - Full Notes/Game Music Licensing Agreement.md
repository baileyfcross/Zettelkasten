# Game Music Licensing Agreement

A game music licensing agreement permits a developer or publisher to use music under defined conditions without necessarily transferring ownership. The grant can specify the game, media, exclusivity, territory, term, renewals, compensation, and whether the client may create derivatives or reuse the music in promotion.

Licensing is common for existing music and can also replace a full buyout when a project's budget or lifespan makes a [[Game Music Work-for-Hire Agreement]] unattractive. At the end of a time-limited license, rights may revert to the composer unless the parties renew the license or exercise a negotiated buyout.
