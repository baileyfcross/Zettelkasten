# Exponential Growth

Exponential growth occurs when a quantity changes at a rate proportional to its current size, producing $N(t)=N_0e^{rt}$ for constant positive rate $r$. Equal time increments multiply the quantity by the same factor rather than adding the same amount.

R can generate a time sequence, evaluate the growth curve, and compare it with observed measurements. The model assumes the proportional rate remains constant and therefore becomes implausible when resources or other feedback impose a limit.
