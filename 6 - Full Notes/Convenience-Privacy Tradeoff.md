# Convenience-Privacy Tradeoff

The convenience-privacy tradeoff occurs when a mobile service reduces effort or provides faster information in exchange for access to personal data. Location-aware results and personalized services can make disclosure feel immediately worthwhile.

The tradeoff is difficult to evaluate because convenience is present and visible while privacy costs may be delayed, uncertain, or distributed across future uses. This asymmetry contributes to the [[Consumer Privacy Paradox]].
