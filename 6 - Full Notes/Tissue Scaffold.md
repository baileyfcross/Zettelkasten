# Tissue Scaffold

A tissue scaffold is a temporary or persistent structure that gives cells a three-dimensional surface or volume in which to attach, organize, and deposit their own matrix. Its pores and mechanics influence transport and cell behavior.

An effective scaffold supports early function and degrades or integrates at a rate compatible with formation of new tissue.
