# Fulcrum Lock

A fulcrum lock is the decision to stop changing a tested [[Game Data Fulcrum]] except for a catastrophic defect or a major project shift. Once locked, the fulcrum becomes the measuring stick for every other object of its type and a stable benchmark for testing connected content.

New objects should initially inherit the fulcrum's values, verify that non-data factors do not alter their behavior, and then deviate while being retested against the unchanged reference. Locking also lets level and content teams build around known movement, size, combat, or equipment expectations before the full object set is complete.
