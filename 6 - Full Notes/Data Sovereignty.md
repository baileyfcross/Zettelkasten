# Data Sovereignty

Data sovereignty concerns which party owns data and which legal authority governs that ownership and use. The question becomes difficult when creators, processors, consumers, storage systems, and service providers reside in different countries.

A cloud contract should state ownership, permitted processing, storage location, and the jurisdiction for resolving a breach. Transforming or augmenting data can add contributors without automatically settling who controls the resulting information.
