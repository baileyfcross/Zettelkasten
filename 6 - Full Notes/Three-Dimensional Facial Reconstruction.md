# Three-Dimensional Facial Reconstruction

Three-dimensional facial reconstruction fits a parameterized face model to landmarks and shading visible in a two-dimensional portrait. The fitted shape and camera pose allow an analyst to estimate surface orientation and how a proposed light source should illuminate the face.

Faces in one photograph can then be compared in a common three-dimensional lighting model. Incompatible illumination may indicate compositing, but model fit, skin reflectance, expression, occlusion, and multiple lights all affect the inference.
