# Equivalent Lateral Force Distribution

The equivalent lateral force method distributes seismic base shear over the building height in proportion to story weight and a height-dependent exponent. Upper levels receive a larger share because their elevation contributes more strongly to overturning and modal response. The story forces accumulate downward into story shear, overturning moment, and forces in each participating lateral frame.

Horizontal distribution then assigns diaphragm force to lateral-force-resisting elements based on diaphragm behavior, stiffness, location, and torsion. Accidental eccentricity and differences between centers of mass and rigidity can increase demand on edge frames. Vertical and horizontal irregularities may limit use of the simplified procedure or require additional analysis and detailing, because a nominal story-force pattern cannot capture every concentration of inelastic response.
