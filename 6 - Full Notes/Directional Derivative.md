# Directional Derivative

A directional derivative measures the instantaneous rate of change of a multivariable function in a chosen unit direction $u$. For a differentiable function, it is $D_u f(x)=\nabla f(x)\cdot u$.

The [[Gradient]] supplies all such first-order directional information. Its direction gives the steepest local increase, while its negative gives the direction used by the [[Steepest Descent Method]].
