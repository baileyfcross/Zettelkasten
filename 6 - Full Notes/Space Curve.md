# Space Curve

A space curve is a [[Parametric Curve]] in three-dimensional space, written $r(t)=(x(t),y(t),z(t))$. Its trace may be given directly or arise as the intersection of two surfaces.

Its length is obtained from $\int\|r'(t)\|dt$, using all three coordinate derivatives. A cylindrical helix, for example, combines circular motion in two coordinates with linear motion in the third.
