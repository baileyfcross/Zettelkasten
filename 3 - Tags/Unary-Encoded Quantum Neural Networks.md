# Unary-Encoded Quantum Neural Networks

Parent topics: [[Circuit-Centric Quantum Classification]] · [[Quantum Computing]]

Quantum neural-network constructions that encode one real feature per qubit in the single-excitation subspace, use reconfigurable beam-splitter circuits for linear algebra, and connect their measured outputs to classical neural-network operations.
