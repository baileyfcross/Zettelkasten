# Stress-Strain Curve

A stress-strain curve plots force per unit area against fractional deformation. Its initial linear gradient is [[Young's Modulus]]. The end of proportional behavior, elastic limit, yield region, ultimate tensile stress, and fracture point distinguish stiffness, recoverability, strength, and ductility. The curve describes material behavior independently of the specimen's original dimensions.
