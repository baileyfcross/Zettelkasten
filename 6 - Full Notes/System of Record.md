# System of Record

A system of record is the authoritative source for a defined body of business data. It prioritizes durability, integrity, transactions or consistency appropriate to the domain, and auditable change. Relational, document, file, or object services can play this role depending on the data and access pattern. Calling a store authoritative also assigns ownership, retention, recovery, and compliance responsibilities that a cache or analytical copy does not inherit.
