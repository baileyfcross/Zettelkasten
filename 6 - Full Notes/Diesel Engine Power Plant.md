# Diesel Engine Power Plant

A diesel-engine plant burns fuel and converts hot gas energy into piston motion inside its engine cylinders. The engine rotates a generator to make electricity. The source places this arrangement in local backup service and lower-demand areas away from long grid lines. Exhaust and cooling-jacket heat can also be recovered in a [[Cogeneration Power Plant]].
