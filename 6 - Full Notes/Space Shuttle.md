# Space Shuttle

The Space Shuttle was the U.S. reusable orbital transportation system built around a winged orbiter, external propellant tank, and solid rocket boosters. It launched satellites and science payloads, carried laboratories, serviced the Hubble Space Telescope, and assembled most non-Russian parts of the International Space Station.

The vehicle was a technical achievement but did not provide routine low-cost access as promised. Its complex refurbishment and design made each mission expensive, and the absence of a launch escape system contributed to exceptional crew risk. After the Challenger and Columbia losses, station construction became the program's principal justification until retirement in 2011.
