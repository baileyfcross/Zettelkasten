# Misinformation Detection

Misinformation detection estimates whether a social message or claim is inaccurate. A learning system derives a compact representation from raw content, trains against identity labels, and updates its estimator to reduce classification loss.

Evidence may come from language, sentiment, timing, users, or diffusion structure. Reliable evaluation depends on labels whose collection process and class imbalance are made explicit.
