# SAS Output Delivery System

The SAS Output Delivery System, or ODS, routes procedure output into destinations such as HTML, report files, or SAS datasets. It is often required when a table displayed by a [[SAS Procedure]] must be captured for later processing or exported to a spreadsheet.

ODS adds a distinct layer of syntax to an analysis. The captured dataset may contain every statistic generated by the procedure in a structure unlike the desired journal table, so further reshaping is usually necessary before presentation.
