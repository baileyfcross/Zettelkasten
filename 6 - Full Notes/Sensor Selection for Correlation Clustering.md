# Sensor Selection for Correlation Clustering

Sensor selection identifies a subset of streams that preserves the correlation structure needed for clustering. Redundant sensors can be omitted when their behavior is well represented by selected neighbors or latent patterns.

Reducing the monitored set lowers communication, storage, and computation costs. Selection must be revised as relationships drift, because a sensor that was redundant in one period may become informative in another.
