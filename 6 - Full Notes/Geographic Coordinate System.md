# Geographic Coordinate System

A geographic coordinate system identifies positions on Earth with angular measurements, most commonly latitude and longitude. Latitude measures position north or south of the equator, while longitude measures position east or west of a chosen prime meridian. These values become meaningful only through a [[Coordinate Reference System]] that states the modeled shape and reference frame of Earth.

The coordinates provide global coverage at fine precision, but identical numbers in different datums can identify different points on the surface. A location service must therefore preserve the reference system along with the numbers rather than treating latitude and longitude as self-interpreting.
