# Structural Load Combinations

Load combinations represent credible simultaneous actions rather than simply adding every maximum load. Dead load is persistent, occupancy live load varies, and wind, snow, rain, ice, seismic, and other actions have different probabilities and directions. Strength combinations apply factors appropriate to LRFD, while service or allowable combinations support ASD and serviceability checks.

The controlling combination can change with the response being checked. Downward gravity may govern beam strength, net uplift may govern a roof connection or base plate, and reduced dead load may be unfavorable when it is the only stabilizing action. Designers must evaluate signs, orthogonal directions, reversals, and companion loads so the envelope captures both maximum demand and minimum stabilizing effect along a continuous load path.
