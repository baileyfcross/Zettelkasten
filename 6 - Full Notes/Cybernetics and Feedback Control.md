# Cybernetics and Feedback Control

Cybernetics studies communication and control through feedback in machines, organisms, and organizations. Norbert Wiener developed its mathematical foundations while considering how a system could track a moving target despite noise and evasive behavior.

Feedback compares observed behavior with a goal and adjusts subsequent action. The concept influenced computing even though it did not depend on the stored-program digital architecture.
