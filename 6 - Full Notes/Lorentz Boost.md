# Lorentz Boost

A Lorentz boost is a [[Lorentz Transformation]] between inertial frames that differ by constant velocity but not by a purely spatial rotation. It mixes time with the spatial coordinate parallel to the motion and preserves the [[Invariant Spacetime Interval]].

In a single time-space plane, a boost has the matrix form of a [[Hyperbolic Rotation]], with diagonal entries $\cosh\chi$ and off-diagonal entries $\sinh\chi$ up to sign and unit conventions. The parameter $\chi$ is [[Rapidity]].

Collinear boosts combine into another collinear boost. Boosts in different spatial directions do not generally commute; their composition contains a spatial rotation, a feature encoded in the [[Lorentz Group]] and summarized by the [[Commutator of Lorentz Boosts]].
