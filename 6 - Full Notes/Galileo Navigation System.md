# Galileo Navigation System

Galileo is the European Union's satellite-navigation system, created to provide an independently governed civil capability despite free access to GPS. Its medium-Earth-orbit architecture, onboard atomic clocks, and coded signals broadly resemble GPS, while its governance, service definitions, hydrogen-maser standards, and safety-of-life features reflect European priorities. Galileo demonstrates that constellation ownership is also a question of industrial capacity and political autonomy.
