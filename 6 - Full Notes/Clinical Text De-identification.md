# Clinical Text De-identification

Clinical text de-identification detects and removes or replaces protected identifiers in narrative reports before data is shared. Names, dates, contact information, locations, record numbers, and other identifying expressions may appear in prose rather than in predictable database fields.

The process balances privacy and utility. Missing one identifier can expose a patient, while removing every rare or specific phrase can destroy clinical meaning. Evaluation must examine both recall of sensitive information and preservation of the contextual content needed for research.
