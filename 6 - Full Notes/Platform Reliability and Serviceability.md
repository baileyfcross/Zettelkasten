# Platform Reliability and Serviceability

Platform reliability is the ability of users to reach and successfully use shared capabilities despite component failures and changing demand. Serviceability is the ability of the platform team to diagnose, repair, upgrade, and support those capabilities without excessive disruption or specialized heroics.

The two qualities shape usability together. Isolation, resource requests and limits, priority, disruption budgets, and multi-tenant boundaries protect reliability, while observability, documented interfaces, repeatable maintenance, and replaceable components improve serviceability. A system that is available only because experts constantly intervene is not a durable developer platform.
