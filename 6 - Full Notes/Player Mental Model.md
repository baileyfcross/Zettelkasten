# Player Mental Model

A player mental model is the player's evolving understanding of the game's state, rules, goals, and likely outcomes. It includes remembered decisions, predictions, learned skills, and both explicit and self-created goals.

The player tests this model through action and revises it from [[Feedback]]. A coherent relationship with the [[Game Model]] enables planning and mastery, while effects that seem arbitrary or incomprehensible weaken engagement.
