# Computational Exploration in Science

Computational exploration uses models and simulations to investigate a phenomenon when analytical solutions or direct experiments are insufficient. Researchers vary conditions, visualize behavior, and search for recurrences that suggest new explanations.

The activity differs from merely calculating a known answer. It treats computation as an environment for discovery while requiring scientific judgment about which simulated patterns deserve confidence.
