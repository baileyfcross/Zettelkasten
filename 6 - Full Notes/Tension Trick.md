# Tension Trick

The tension trick changes the emotional effect of a game state by choosing numbers that are easier or harder for the player to calculate, even when the underlying outcome is equivalent. A character who survives three attacks and dies on the fourth may feel safer at five remaining hit points than at two, despite facing the same next-hit result.

Clean divisibility and familiar units create calm and predictability; awkward quantities and conversions create dissonance and stress. This is useful only when it matches the intended feeling. Numeric friction can heighten action, but it can also make a reflective or relaxing system needlessly unpleasant.
