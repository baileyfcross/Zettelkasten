# Semiconductor Doping

Semiconductor doping introduces controlled impurities into a crystal to change how electrical charge moves through it. P-type material supports mobile positive holes, while N-type material supports mobile electrons.

Joining differently doped regions creates the electrical behavior needed for transistors and other devices. Precise control of impurity concentration is essential for repeatable chip production.
