# Economic Stagnation

Economic stagnation occurs when too little currency or resource flows through a game economy. Scarce income, high maintenance costs, or excessive caution make players hoard rather than exchange, so the economic loop stops functioning.

Stagnation can result from overcorrecting for [[Economic Inflation]]. A healthy [[Game Economy]] needs enough gradient for both parties to perceive gain and enough liquidity for interactions to continue.
