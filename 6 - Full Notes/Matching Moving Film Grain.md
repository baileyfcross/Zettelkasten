# Matching Moving Film Grain

A grain-free render or retouched patch can reveal itself against a moving photographed plate. Grain must resemble the plate's distribution, density, color, and temporal change, not just add random dots to a still frame. The book describes inspecting darker and midgray areas, creating colored noise, and adjusting blur and blend to make new material fit. Adding grain only to the new element preserves the original plate; de-graining and re-graining the whole shot can soften it, and grain over the whole composite can double what the plate already has.
