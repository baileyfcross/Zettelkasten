# Classification Permutation Test

A classification permutation test evaluates a performance difference against a null distribution generated by exchangeable rearrangements. Depending on the design, labels or paired classifier outcomes are permuted while the comparison statistic is recomputed.

The method avoids relying on a parametric sampling distribution whose assumptions may fail across datasets. Validity depends on permuting only units that are exchangeable under the null and repeating the entire relevant evaluation procedure.
