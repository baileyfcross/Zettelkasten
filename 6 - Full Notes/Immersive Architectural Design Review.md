# Immersive Architectural Design Review

Immersive architectural design review places participants inside a full-scale representation of a proposed building so they can judge geometry and spatial relationships from an occupant's viewpoint. A [[Virtual Environment]] can externalize a structure that is difficult to understand from plans or a desktop model and let architects, engineers, builders, and clients discuss the same visible space.

Review is most valuable before construction, when discovering a blocked sightline, cramped room, misplaced fixture, or conflict between building systems still permits inexpensive design changes. Shared large displays can also support collaborative inspection because several disciplines can point to and discuss the same feature rather than translating among separate drawings.

The method does not make a model correct by itself. Its decisions remain limited by model completeness, scale fidelity, navigation design, and the [[Display Fidelity]] of the review system.
