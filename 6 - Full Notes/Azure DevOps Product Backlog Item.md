# Azure DevOps Product Backlog Item

An Azure DevOps product backlog item represents a deliverable increment of product behavior in the Scrum process template. It carries description, priority, acceptance information, state, and relationships to its feature and implementation tasks.

Backlog refinement makes the item small and clear enough to plan. Completion should be judged by its accepted behavior, not solely by closing the child tasks.
