# Azure Cross-Cutting Concern Maturity

Azure cross-cutting concern maturity describes the staged improvement of monitoring, delivery, identity, security, networking, and governance around a workload. Not every concern can reach its desired state at the first release. Defining current and target maturity makes gaps visible, lets stakeholders agree on acceptable interim risk, and turns later controls into roadmap commitments instead of leaving them as vague future work.
