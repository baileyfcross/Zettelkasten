# Inverse Power Method

The inverse power method applies the [[Power Method]] to $A^{-1}$ without explicitly forming the inverse. Each iteration solves a system with coefficient matrix $A$.

Because the eigenvalues of $A^{-1}$ are reciprocals of those of $A$, the method converges toward the eigenvector associated with the smallest-magnitude eigenvalue of $A$, under the required dominance conditions.
