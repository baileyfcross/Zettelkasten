# Network Seeding with Hubs and Bridges

Network seeding introduces content through nodes positioned to accelerate diffusion. Hubs have many connections and can expose a large immediate audience, while bridges connect otherwise separated groups and can carry an item into new communities.

Effective seeding depends on more than choosing the most famous account. A hub may saturate one cluster without crossing community boundaries, and a bridge may reach a valuable new network despite having fewer contacts. Content features and structural position therefore work together in viral and memetic success.
