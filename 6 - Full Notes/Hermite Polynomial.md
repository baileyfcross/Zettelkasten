# Hermite Polynomial

Hermite polynomials are polynomial solutions of Hermite's differential equation and are orthogonal on the real line with a Gaussian weight. They can be generated by a differentiation formula or related through recurrence identities.

They appear whenever a differential equation combines a quadratic coordinate dependence with Gaussian decay. The stationary states of the quantum harmonic oscillator are a central example: each wavefunction is a Hermite polynomial multiplied by a Gaussian envelope.
