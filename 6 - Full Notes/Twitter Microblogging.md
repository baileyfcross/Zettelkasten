# Twitter Microblogging

Twitter developed as a short-message service shaped by mobile screens and keyboards. Its constrained posts favor rapid updates, public conversation, and circulation through follower networks.

The format differs from long-form blogging even though both publish chronologically. Its growth illustrates how an interface constraint can become the organizing principle of a social medium.
