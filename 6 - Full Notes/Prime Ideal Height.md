# Prime Ideal Height

The height ht(p) of a [[Prime Ideal]] p is the supremum of lengths of strict prime chains ending at p. It measures the dimension below that point of the spectrum.

The Krull height theorem bounds the height of each minimal prime over an ideal generated by n elements by n. Localization at p preserves this height as the dimension of the local ring R_p.
