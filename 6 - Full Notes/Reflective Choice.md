# Reflective Choice

A reflective choice asks the player to consider values, identity, or attitude even though the selected option does not change the plot or game state. Its purpose is to focus attention on the act of choosing and on what that choice means to the player.

Because meaning can arise from interpretation rather than only from mechanical consequence, a reflective choice may affect the experience without altering the outcome. It can ask who the player or character is within the story when no decision can produce a perfect result.
