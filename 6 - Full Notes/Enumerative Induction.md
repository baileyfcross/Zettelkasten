# Enumerative Induction

Enumerative induction infers a generalization from repeated observations of the same kind of event. More confirming instances increase confidence, but repetition alone does not reveal why the relationship holds.

This limitation is central to the classic problem of induction. The source contrasts enumeration with [[Eliminative Induction]], which varies circumstances to identify factors that make a difference.
