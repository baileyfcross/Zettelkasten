# Greatest Common Factor of Terms

The greatest common factor of algebraic terms is the largest [[Monomial]] that divides every term. Its numerical coefficient is the greatest common divisor of the coefficients, and each shared variable uses the smallest exponent appearing across the terms.

For (20a^2b^3+24a^3b^2), the greatest common factor is (4a^2b^2). Variables missing from any term cannot appear in the common factor. Extracting this factor first simplifies later [[Polynomial Factorization]] and reveals the remaining structure.
