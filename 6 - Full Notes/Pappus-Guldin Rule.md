# Pappus-Guldin Rule

The Pappus-Guldin rule relates a [[Surface of Revolution]] to the path of a centroid. The lateral area equals the generating curve's length times the distance traveled by that curve's center of gravity during a full revolution.

Likewise, the volume equals the area of the generating plane region times the distance traveled by the region's center of gravity. The generating curve or region must lie on one side of the axis used for the revolution.
