# Confidence Interval

A confidence interval combines a [[Point Estimate]] with a margin determined by its [[Standard Error]] and a reference distribution. It reports a range of parameter values compatible with the procedure's sampling uncertainty.

The confidence level is a long-run property: across repeated samples, the stated proportion of intervals will contain the fixed parameter. A particular interval does not assign that probability to the parameter after the data are observed.
