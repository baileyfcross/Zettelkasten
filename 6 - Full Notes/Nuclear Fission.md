# Nuclear Fission

Nuclear fission splits a heavy nucleus into two medium-mass fragments, neutrons, and released energy. Neutrons from one event can trigger further fissions, producing a chain reaction. A reactor controls neutron population and removes heat, while an uncontrolled supercritical assembly releases energy rapidly.
