# COMSOL Internal Geometry Construction

COMSOL can construct geometry from elementary points, curves, surfaces, and solids combined by transforms and Boolean operations. Work planes define two-dimensional profiles that can be extruded or revolved into three-dimensional features.

An internal geometry sequence keeps dimensions connected to [[COMSOL Global Parameters and Variables|model parameters]] and makes reconstruction reproducible. It is well suited to idealized analysis geometry, whereas a detailed production shape may be better handled through [[COMSOL CAD Geometry Import|CAD import]].
