# Probabilistic Clustering Membership

Probabilistic clustering membership assigns each observation a distribution over clusters, so its membership values sum to one. Increasing membership in one group necessarily reduces the amount available to the others.

When interpreted as fuzzy degrees, this formulation remains fundamentally partition-oriented. It represents uncertainty about assignment among alternatives.
