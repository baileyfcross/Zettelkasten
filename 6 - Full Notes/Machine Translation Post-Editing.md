# Machine Translation Post-Editing

Machine translation post-editing is the human revision of automatically generated output into an acceptable target text. It can increase productivity when the system is adapted to a repetitive domain and already produces reliable terminology, phraseology, and sentence structure.

Poor output reverses the advantage: diagnosing and rewriting a misleading sentence can take longer than translating it directly. The relevant performance measure is therefore not raw system output alone but the total human effort needed to reach the required quality. Corrections can also become feedback for future training or a [[Translation Memory]], provided confidentiality and validation are handled deliberately.
