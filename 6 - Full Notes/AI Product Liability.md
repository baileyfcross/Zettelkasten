# AI Product Liability

AI product liability assigns responsibility for damage to the company that produced or supplied an AI-enabled product, potentially without proving that a particular employee intended or negligently caused the harm. It offers one response to outcomes produced by complex systems with many contributors.

The approach can ensure that an injured party is not left without remedy simply because the precise internal cause is opaque. Its design must decide what counts as a defect, how learning or later modification affects responsibility, and which organization in a supply chain can best prevent harm. It complements rather than replaces [[AI Duty of Care]].
