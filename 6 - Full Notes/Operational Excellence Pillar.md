# Operational Excellence Pillar

The operational excellence pillar focuses on running and monitoring systems effectively while continually improving processes and procedures. Its practices favor operations as code, frequent small reversible changes, anticipated failure, observable outcomes, and learning from events.

The pillar treats operations as part of product design. Teams prepare runbooks, automate repeatable work, define meaningful metrics, and feed incident lessons into architectural change rather than relying on heroic manual response.
