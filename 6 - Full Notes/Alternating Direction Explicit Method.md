# Alternating Direction Explicit Method

The alternating direction explicit method advances a two-dimensional heat equation by finite differences on a rectangular mesh. Each new value combines the current value with its four axial spatial neighbors from the preceding time plane.

Computation proceeds point by point across one level and then plane by plane in time. The method extends the explicit heat-equation idea from one spatial coordinate to two while preserving a local stencil.
