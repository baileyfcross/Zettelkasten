# Earth-Rate and Transport Wander

Earth-rate error arises because a fixed gyro axis is observed from a rotating planet; its apparent drift varies with the sine of latitude. Transport wander appears when the aircraft moves east or west across converging meridians and depends on easterly groundspeed and latitude. The total directional error combines these apparent effects with real wander and compensation settings.
