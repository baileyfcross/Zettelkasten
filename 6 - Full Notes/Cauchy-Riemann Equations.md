# Cauchy-Riemann Equations

The Cauchy-Riemann equations relate the partial derivatives of the real and imaginary parts of a complex function. For $f=u+iv$, they require $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$.

They arise by demanding that the complex difference quotient give the same derivative along real and imaginary directions. With appropriate continuity assumptions, they provide a test for analyticity; they also imply that both component functions satisfy Laplace equation.
