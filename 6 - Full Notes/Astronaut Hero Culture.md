# Astronaut Hero Culture

Astronaut hero culture presents spacefarers as exemplary representatives of their nation, political system, and human courage. During the Space Race, American astronauts and Soviet cosmonauts were portrayed as disciplined pilots, model citizens, family figures, and peaceful envoys even though most came from military backgrounds.

The image made human missions more emotionally powerful than robotic achievements. It also favored a masculine test-pilot ideal and marginalized scientist-astronauts and other possible identities. Governments helped construct the cult, but public identification and entertainment media made it durable beyond official propaganda.
