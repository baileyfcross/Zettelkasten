# Triclustering

Triclustering extends biclustering to a third dimension such as time, patient, or experimental replicate. A result identifies a coherent subarray rather than a single global partition of each axis.

The extension can reveal context-specific temporal expression, but the search space grows rapidly and overlapping patterns become common. Statistical significance and redundancy controls are needed to distinguish useful structures from flexible fits.
