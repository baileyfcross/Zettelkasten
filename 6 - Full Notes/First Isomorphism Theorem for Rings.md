# First Isomorphism Theorem for Rings

For a [[Ring Homomorphism]] f: R → S, the image of f is isomorphic to the [[Quotient Ring]] R/ker(f). Elements of R that differ by an element of the kernel have the same image, and no further identifications occur.

The theorem converts a mapping problem into an ideal-and-quotient problem. In particular, every surjective ring homomorphism presents its target as the source divided by its kernel.
