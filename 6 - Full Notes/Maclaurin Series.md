# Maclaurin Series

A Maclaurin series is a [[Taylor Series]] expanded about zero. Its coefficient of $x^n$ is the $n$th derivative of the function at zero divided by $n!$, provided the function is represented by the resulting series in the region considered.

Truncating the expansion gives a polynomial approximation for small $x$. The method is especially useful near an equilibrium or when a physical result depends on a small parameter, because higher powers become progressively less important within the range of validity.
