# Separation of Privilege

Separation of privilege requires more than one independent condition before granting a sensitive capability. Examples include two-person approval or authentication that combines distinct evidence.

The principle reduces the chance that one stolen credential, compromised component, or mistaken decision can authorize a critical action. Its cost is additional coordination and recovery complexity.
