# Strategic Transparency from Reconnaissance Satellites

Strategic transparency from reconnaissance satellites is the reduction of uncertainty when rivals can repeatedly observe military forces, missile sites, and other preparations from orbit. Better information can strengthen targeting capability, but it can also make surprise attack harder and expose misleading claims about an opponent's strength.

This creates a stabilizing effect alongside the military one. States that depend on their own satellites also have incentives not to begin attacks that could destroy shared orbital conditions or invite retaliation. Transparency is therefore not peace by itself, but a way infrastructure can reduce some dangers even while supporting war planning.
