# Game

A game is a voluntary activity organized within a distinct rule-bound context, with goals, meaningful choices or conflict, and a recognizable outcome. Individual examples may not exhibit every trait equally, so games are better treated as a family of related forms than as a rigid checklist.

A functioning game is not only rules or content. It is a [[Game-Player System]] in which a player acts on a dynamic [[Game Model]], receives [[Feedback]], and builds a [[Player Mental Model]] through play.
