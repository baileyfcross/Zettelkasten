# Sensor-Based Decision Analytics

Sensor-based decision analytics converts continuous physical observations into evidence for operational or longer-range decisions. It can combine live measurements with historical records, external conditions, simulations, and predictive models to detect a change, forecast demand, or recommend an intervention.

The method replaces occasional snapshots with a motion picture of a process. An earthquake warning network can distinguish strong seismic motion from trucks or distant events and issue a short but actionable alert, while a retailer can combine weather and past sales to adjust inventory. The value comes from contextual discrimination and timely action, not from collection volume alone.
