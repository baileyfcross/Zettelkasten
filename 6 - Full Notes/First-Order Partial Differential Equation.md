# First-Order Partial Differential Equation

A first-order partial differential equation relates a multivariable function to its first partial derivatives. Unlike an ordinary differential equation, its solution is generally constrained by data supplied along a curve or surface rather than at one isolated point.

Characteristic curves reduce many such equations to ordinary differential equations by following paths along which the derivatives combine into a total derivative. The geometry of those paths determines how boundary or initial data propagate through the domain.
