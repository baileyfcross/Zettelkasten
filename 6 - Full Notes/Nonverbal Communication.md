# Nonverbal Communication

Nonverbal communication conveys interactional meaning through gaze, facial expression, gesture, posture, movement, timing, and spatial orientation. In an embodied assistant, these signals can show attention, turn readiness, emphasis, or uncertainty.

Signals must agree with speech and dialog state. A [[Social Robot]] that speaks to one person while looking at another can create confusion even when every word is correct.
