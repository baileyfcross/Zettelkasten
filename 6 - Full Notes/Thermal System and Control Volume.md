# Thermal System and Control Volume

A closed thermal system permits energy as heat or work to cross its boundary while retaining its mass. Its boundary may move or deform, but the same collection of matter remains the object of the balance.

A control volume occupies a defined region through which both mass and energy may flow. Selecting between these viewpoints determines whether advected enthalpy must appear alongside conduction, generation, work, and storage in the [[Energy Conservation in Thermal Continua|energy balance]].
