# Weight Painting for Modifiers

A modifier can use a vertex group as an influence mask, and Weight Paint supplies the group's values spatially. In the book's displacement example, low-weight regions receive little or no relief while highly weighted regions receive more of the modifier's effect.

This separates the modifier's operation from its distribution. The same nondestructive modifier remains adjustable while the painted weight field determines where and how strongly it acts.
