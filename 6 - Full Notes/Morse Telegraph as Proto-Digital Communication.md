# Morse Telegraph as Proto-Digital Communication

The Morse telegraph sent messages as discrete electrical pulses chosen from a code. Frequently used letters received shorter patterns, allowing text to travel quickly and accurately with minimal apparatus.

Although the term digital came later, the telegraph anticipated binary communications and variable-length encoding. Its networks also established long-distance information infrastructure later reused by computing.
