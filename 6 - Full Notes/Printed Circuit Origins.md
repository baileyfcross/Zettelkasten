# Printed Circuit Origins

A printed circuit forms electrical connections by depositing conducting paths on an insulating board instead of joining every component with separate wires. Wartime miniaturization work on the proximity fuse helped advance the technique.

Printed boards reduced size, assembly labor, and wiring errors. Their planar organization also anticipated the patterned interconnections of integrated circuits.
