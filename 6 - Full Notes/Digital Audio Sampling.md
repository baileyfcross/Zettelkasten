# Digital Audio Sampling

Digital audio sampling measures a continuous sound waveform at regular time intervals and stores each measurement with finite precision. Sample rate determines how often measurements occur, while bit depth determines the available amplitude resolution.

Higher values can reproduce more detail but require more storage and transfer bandwidth. A game audio system balances fidelity, memory, decompression cost, and the importance of each asset.
