# Approximate Search

Approximate search looks for items similar enough to a target rather than requiring an exact match. The search therefore needs a way to measure or rank similarity and a rule for deciding which candidates are acceptable.

A spellchecker illustrates the distinction: an unrecognized spelling may not occur in its dictionary, so it searches for nearby words that could have been intended. This changes both the meaning of success and the work required from a [[Search Algorithm]].
