# Wave Equation

The wave equation relates the second time derivative of a field to its spatial Laplacian multiplied by the square of a propagation speed. It models small disturbances in strings, acoustic media, and electromagnetic fields.

In one dimension, traveling profiles can move in either direction without changing shape in an ideal nondispersive medium. Boundaries restrict the allowed spatial modes, producing standing waves whose temporal frequencies are tied to the spatial eigenvalues.
