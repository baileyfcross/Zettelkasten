# Production Rule

A production rule specifies that one string may be rewritten as another. In a [[Context-Free Grammar]], every production has a single nonterminal on its left side and a string of terminals and nonterminals on its right side.

Applying a production replaces the chosen nonterminal wherever it occurs, independent of surrounding symbols. A sequence of such replacements forms a [[Derivation]].
