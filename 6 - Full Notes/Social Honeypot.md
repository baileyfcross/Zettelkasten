# Social Honeypot

A social honeypot is a legitimate-looking account created to attract suspicious interactions. Accounts that send deceptive information or malicious links to it become candidates for labeling and later supervised detection.

Honeypots obtain labels cheaply but passively, so collection may take a long time. They also emphasize malicious positive examples and require another method to represent normal accounts accurately.
