# Remote Collaboration

Remote collaboration connects participants who occupy different physical locations but work on a shared task or representation. AR can combine live video, reconstructed geometry, virtual objects, annotations, pointing, and speech to make one participant's environment accessible to another.

The collaborators may have different devices, views, and roles. The system must communicate what each person can see or control and keep shared spatial references synchronized across the network.
