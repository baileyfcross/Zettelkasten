# Steel Moment Connection Classification

Steel frame connections are classified as simple, fully restrained, or partially restrained according to their moment-rotation behavior. A simple connection is modeled as permitting rotation with negligible moment transfer. A fully restrained connection maintains the angle between connected members sufficiently for rigid-frame analysis, while a partially restrained connection has measurable stiffness and strength between those ideals.

Moment transfer creates a tension-compression couple through beam flanges plus shear through the web region. Flange plates, end plates, direct flange welding, column stiffeners, continuity plates, panel zones, and beam or column splices may participate. The analysis assumption must match demonstrated connection behavior. Seismic moment frames add qualification and detailing requirements because cyclic ductility, not static strength alone, governs acceptable performance.
