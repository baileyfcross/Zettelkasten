# Release Approval Gate

A release approval gate pauses automated promotion until an authorized person or policy confirms that required evidence and timing conditions are satisfied. It is commonly placed before a production deployment or another high-consequence stage.

Approval should answer a defined risk question rather than add ceremony. The approver needs test results, artifact identity, change scope, and operational readiness to make the decision meaningful.
