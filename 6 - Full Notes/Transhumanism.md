# Transhumanism

Transhumanism supports using science and technology to overcome limits of the human condition, including limits on cognition, health, lifespan, and bodily capability. AI appears in transhumanist visions as an enhancement, a partner with which humans might merge, or a route toward intelligence and life no longer constrained by the biological body.

The vision raises questions about access, inequality, and what counts as improvement. It can also express a desire to escape embodiment and earthly dependence. [[Posthumanism]] challenges human centrality from another direction: instead of perfecting humanity, it questions why human traits should define intelligence and value in the first place.

Responses to machine superintelligence can range from using AI as an external tool to integrating computation directly with the brain. The more intimate path aims to make added capabilities part of a person's cognitive process rather than a separate adviser. Radical life extension and [[Mind Uploading and Personal Identity|mind uploading]] extend the same project from improving a life to changing the substrate and duration in which it continues.
