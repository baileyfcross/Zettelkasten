# Evidence-Based Decision Making

Evidence-based decision making uses systematically collected and analyzed information to support a choice. In a big-data setting, the evidence emerges through a chain of extraction, transformation, integration, cleaning, storage, exploration, and presentation.

A large dataset does not make a decision evidence based by itself. The information must be relevant and trustworthy, and analytical results must be incorporated into the actual decision process.
