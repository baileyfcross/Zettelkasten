# Epsilon Neighborhood

An epsilon neighborhood of a point contains observations whose distance from that point is less than a specified positive radius. With Euclidean distance it forms a disk in two dimensions and a ball in three dimensions; other metrics produce different shapes.

Neighborhood radius and metric jointly determine local membership. A radius that is too large can join separate structures, while one that is too small can fragment a coherent group.
