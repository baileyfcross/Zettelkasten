# Concave Function

A function is concave when its negative is a [[Convex Function]]. Its graph lies on or above the chord joining any two points in its domain.

For a differentiable concave function, a local maximum is also a global maximum. A twice-differentiable function is concave where its [[Hessian Matrix]] is negative semidefinite.
