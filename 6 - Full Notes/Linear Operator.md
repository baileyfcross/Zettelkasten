# Linear Operator

A linear operator is a [[Linear Transformation]] from a vector space to itself. Because the domain and codomain coincide, one basis can be used for both sides of its matrix representation.

Operators can be composed, added, and multiplied by scalars. Their [[Invariant Subspace|invariant subspaces]], eigenvalues, and canonical forms reveal directions or blocks on which their action becomes simpler.
