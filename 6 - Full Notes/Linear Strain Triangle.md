# Linear Strain Triangle

A linear strain triangle is a higher-order triangular structural element whose displacement interpolation permits strain to vary linearly within the element. Midside nodes provide more degrees of freedom than a constant strain triangle.

It can represent curved boundaries and changing stress fields more accurately with fewer elements. The additional nodes enlarge the element matrix and computational work.
