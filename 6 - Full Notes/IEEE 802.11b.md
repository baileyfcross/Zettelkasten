# IEEE 802.11b

IEEE 802.11b was an early widely adopted wireless networking standard. It specified interoperable radio communication for local-area networks and helped move Wi-Fi from ad hoc products into mainstream devices.

Standardization let equipment from different vendors connect through common access points. Intel’s integration of Wi-Fi into laptop chipsets further accelerated adoption.
