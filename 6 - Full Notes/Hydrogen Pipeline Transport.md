# Hydrogen Pipeline Transport

Pipeline transport carries gaseous hydrogen over distance and needs compression to maintain flow and pressure. The source counts pipeline, compressor, and compressor-energy costs and highlights material embrittlement and seal penetration as engineering problems. Transport therefore is not cost-free merely because hydrogen is a light gas. It connects production sites to storage and end users within a larger [[Hydrogen as Energy Carrier]] system.
