# Behavior-Based Robotics

Behavior-based robotics replaces a long sense-model-plan-act sequence with repeated local loops that sense, act, sense again, and adjust. Simple behaviors such as moving, avoiding obstacles, and reacting to proximity can combine into activity that appears intelligent without a complete internal model of the world.

The approach is useful when building a detailed map would take so long that the environment changes before the robot acts. It does not eliminate planning in every task, but it shows that coherent behavior can emerge from situated feedback. [[Robot Path Planning]] remains necessary when goals and obstacles require more deliberate coordination.
