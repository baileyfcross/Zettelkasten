# Work in Progress Limit

A work-in-progress limit caps how many items may occupy a workflow state or the system as a whole. When the limit is reached, the team helps finish or unblock existing work instead of introducing more.

The limit exposes a [[Bottleneck]] and changes behavior from individual busyness toward collective flow. It should be adjusted from measured results rather than used as an arbitrary quota.

Across software teams, excess work in progress often appears when one change requires coordination among several specialized groups. Items wait for prerequisites, are abandoned temporarily, and impose context-switching costs when resumed. Clear [[Bounded Context Ownership]] reduces those dependencies, while the limit makes remaining queues visible and encourages teams to finish work before starting more.
