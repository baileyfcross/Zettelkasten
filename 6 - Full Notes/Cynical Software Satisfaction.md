# Cynical Software Satisfaction

Cynical satisfaction occurs when users trust enough of a product’s claims to keep using it while expecting bugs, workarounds, weak support, and security problems. Their commitment is shallow and may disappear when a better option arrives.

This level often results from pressure to release quickly and shift liability to customers. It produces functional software without the disciplined care needed for durable trust.
