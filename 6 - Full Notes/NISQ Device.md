# NISQ Device

A noisy intermediate-scale quantum device has enough controllable qubits to explore nontrivial circuits but lacks the scale and error correction needed for long, fully fault-tolerant computation. Noise and limited depth make algorithm structure, compilation, measurement count, and hybrid classical control central constraints.

Such devices create an experimental platform for quantum and hybrid heuristics even when they do not yet solve broadly useful problems better than classical computers. Evidence from a NISQ experiment must separate the ability to run a circuit from useful solution quality and from a demonstrated end-to-end advantage.
