# Quantum Wavefunction Normalization

Quantum wavefunction normalization requires the integral of the probability density over all allowed space to equal one. For an ordinary nonrelativistic wavefunction, the density is the squared magnitude $|\psi|^2$.

If an unnormalized solution has finite total squared magnitude, multiplying it by an appropriate constant enforces the condition. Time evolution under the Schrodinger equation preserves normalization when the probability current and boundary behavior conserve total probability.
