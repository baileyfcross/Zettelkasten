# Mutual Inductance

Mutual inductance measures magnetic coupling between two circuits. A changing current in one coil changes flux linkage in the other and induces an emf $\mathcal{E}_2=-M\,dI_1/dt$. The value depends on geometry, turn counts, core material, and the fraction of one coil's flux that links the other.
