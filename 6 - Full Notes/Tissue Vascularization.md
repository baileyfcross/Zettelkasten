# Tissue Vascularization

Tissue vascularization creates blood-vessel-like networks that deliver oxygen and nutrients and remove waste throughout a thick construct. Passive diffusion reaches only a short distance, leaving central cells in a large solid tissue without adequate support.

Printed perfusable channels are an intermediate solution, while successful implantation ultimately requires connection with the host's circulation.
