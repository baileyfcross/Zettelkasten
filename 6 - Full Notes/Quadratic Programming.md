# Quadratic Programming

Quadratic programming optimizes a quadratic objective subject to linear constraints. Its derivative is linear, so the first-order conditions can be combined with feasibility and complementary-slackness relationships.

The problem is especially well behaved when the quadratic objective has the appropriate convexity for minimization or concavity for maximization.
