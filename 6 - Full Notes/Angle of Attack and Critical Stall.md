# Angle of Attack and Critical Stall

Angle of attack is the angle between an airfoil's chord line and the relative airflow. Increasing it usually increases lift coefficient until the critical angle is reached, after which widespread separation causes lift to decrease and drag to rise. Stall recovery therefore requires reducing angle of attack, regardless of the aircraft's attitude relative to the horizon.
