# Differential Form

A differential $k$-form assigns an alternating, multilinear quantity to $k$ tangent vectors. In coordinates it is a sum of coefficient functions multiplied by elementary products such as $dx_i\wedge dx_j$.

Zero-forms are differentiable scalar functions, one-forms integrate along oriented curves, and two-forms integrate over oriented surfaces. This common language allows line, surface, and volume integration to be expressed with the same structural rules.
