# Descent Gradient, Angle, and Rate

Descent gradient is altitude lost per unit of horizontal distance, while descent angle is the arctangent of that geometric ratio. Multiplying gradient by groundspeed and converting hours to minutes gives the required vertical rate. These three forms describe the same path, but each matches a different planning input or cockpit display.
