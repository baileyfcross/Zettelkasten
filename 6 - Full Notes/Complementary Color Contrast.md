# Complementary Color Contrast

Complementary colors occupy opposing positions on a color wheel and create strong hue contrast when placed together. A warm color against a cool counterpart can make a focal object separate sharply from its surroundings.

Equal amounts of fully saturated complements can become exhausting or visually unstable. Environment design often lets one color dominate and uses the other as a smaller accent, with value contrast supporting the intended emphasis.
