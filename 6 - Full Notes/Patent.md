# Patent

A patent grants a time-limited right to exclude others from exploiting a qualifying invention without the holder's consent. In exchange, the inventor publicly discloses the invention, which must meet requirements such as novelty, non-obviousness, and practical utility.

The right is jurisdictional and can be licensed, sometimes in exchange for royalties. When the term ends, the invention enters the [[Public Domain]] and may be practiced without the former holder's permission.
