# Cultural Interactivity

Cultural interactivity is the long-timescale reflection and dialogue a game provokes about cultural values, assumptions, or practices. It may continue after play and outside the game's immediate [[Magic Circle]].

Unlike fast action loops, its feedback may appear through conversation, interpretation, and later behavior. It can therefore become an outer loop around [[Social Interactivity]] and the player's evolving meaning of the experience.
