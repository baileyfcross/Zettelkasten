# Quality Gate

A quality gate is an automated decision that allows or blocks progression based on defined evidence such as successful tests, acceptable analysis results, or bounded defect measures. It turns a quality policy into a repeatable delivery checkpoint.

The gate should focus on risks the team is prepared to act on. Passing it is evidence that declared criteria were met, not a guarantee that the release is correct, usable, or safe in every untested situation.
