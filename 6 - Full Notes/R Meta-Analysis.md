# R Meta-Analysis

R meta-analysis combines study-level effect estimates while weighting their uncertainty. A fixed-effect model treats the studies as estimating one common effect, whereas a random-effects model includes between-study heterogeneity and therefore represents a distribution of underlying effects.

The effect scale, standard errors, and direction must be harmonized before pooling. A forest plot displays individual and combined estimates, but heterogeneity and study design determine whether the numerical summary is substantively meaningful.
