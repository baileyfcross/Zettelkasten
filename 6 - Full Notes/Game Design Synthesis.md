# Game Design Synthesis

Game design synthesis combines ideas and constraints from several disciplines into a consistent playable vision. Programming, art, audio, writing, production, publishing, budget, hardware, and schedule can pull a design in different directions.

Synthesis is not simple compromise. The designer identifies relationships among concerns and finds a solution that still serves the intended player while giving collaborators meaningful ownership of the result.
