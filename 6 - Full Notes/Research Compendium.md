# Research Compendium

A research compendium treats the explanatory narrative, the code, and the data as one scholarly unit. The article explains the scientific question and interpretation, while the executable materials expose the operations and parameter settings that produced the claims.

This unit is more complete than a paper accompanied by disconnected files. Its parts should identify one another, carry enough metadata for use, and preserve a [[Result-to-Computation Traceability|traceable path]] from reported evidence back to its inputs. A compendium can be distributed as an archive, repository, or richer [[Research Object]].
