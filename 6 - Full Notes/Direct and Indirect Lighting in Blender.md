# Direct and Indirect Lighting in Blender

Blender scenes combine direct illumination from light objects with indirect illumination from the World. Point, sun, spot, and area lights establish local or directional sources, while World shaders contribute surrounding color, reflections, and environment light.

The two systems should be designed together. A sun can establish a clear direction and shadow, while an HDRI or procedural sky supplies the broad ambient response that reaches surfaces outside the direct beam.
