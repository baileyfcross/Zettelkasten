# Tracking Accuracy

Tracking accuracy describes how close a measured pose or quantity is to its true value. It is primarily limited by systematic errors in the sensor, its model, installation, or calibration.

High precision does not guarantee high accuracy: repeated readings may agree closely while all remain biased. Better calibration can reduce that bias, but the effort and cost must be justified by the registration demands of the application.
