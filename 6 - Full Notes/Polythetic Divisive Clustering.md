# Polythetic Divisive Clustering

Polythetic divisive clustering uses the values of all selected variables when deciding how to divide a cluster. It contrasts with a monothetic or homothetic split driven by one feature.

Using the full representation can capture multivariate structure, but it increases computational and interpretive complexity and remains sensitive to irrelevant or poorly scaled attributes.
