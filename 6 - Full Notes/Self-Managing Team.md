# Self-Managing Team

A self-managing team chooses backlog work, plans its approach, monitors progress, and adapts how it will meet a shared goal. Decisions are made close to the knowledge needed to act rather than routed through a manager who assigns each task.

Self-management develops through trust, mentoring, clear boundaries, and shared accountability. It transfers control of the work without removing responsibility for results.
