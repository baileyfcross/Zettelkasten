# Collaborative Platform Threat Modeling

Collaborative platform threat modeling identifies what is being changed, what can go wrong, what controls can reduce the risk, and whether the result is good enough for the platform’s context. The questions are repeated as the system, users, and threat surface evolve.

Platform engineers, security specialists, operators, and affected product teams should participate because technical and sociotechnical risks cross their boundaries. Collaboration reveals credential handling, overly permissive roles, unsafe defaults, human manipulation, and workflow bypasses that a component-only review can miss. The output should assign mitigations and residual risks to owners rather than remain a diagramming exercise.
