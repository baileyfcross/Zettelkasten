# Internal Developer Platform

An internal developer platform is a curated set of tools, interfaces, and automated workflows that product teams use to provision environments, deliver applications, and observe services. It presents supported capabilities as a coherent product instead of requiring each team to integrate cloud primitives and pipeline components independently.

The platform commonly exposes a [[Golden Path]] through a portal, API, templates, or command-line interface. [[Developer Self-Service]] remains bounded by policy, quotas, and secure defaults. A successful platform preserves escape hatches for legitimate exceptions while making the routine, compliant path the easiest path to use.
