# LORAN Hyperbolic Navigation

LORAN determined position from the difference in arrival time of synchronized pulses sent by separated ground transmitters. A constant time difference defines a hyperbolic line of position, and intersecting measurements from multiple transmitter pairs yield a fix. The receiver remained passive and the system worked in poor weather, but coverage depended on powerful staffed stations and propagation through or around the ionosphere could limit accuracy.
