# Plate Lighting Reference for CG

Before lighting a rendered element, inspect the filmed plate for the direction, color, intensity, and softness of its sources. Shadows and reflections help infer the key light, while sky and ground colors suggest fills and bounce. The book repeatedly uses such observed cues to construct CG lighting, including a sun key, sky fills, and ground bounce for aircraft. A physically plausible render can still look pasted on if it is plausible for a different scene. See [[Matching CG Key Fill and Rim Lights]].
