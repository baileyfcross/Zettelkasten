# Truss Direction Cosine

A truss direction cosine is the cosine of the angle between a member axis and a global coordinate direction. In two dimensions, cosine and sine factors project local axial deformation and force into global x and y components.

These factors populate the oriented truss stiffness matrix. Reversing element orientation changes signed transformation terms while preserving the physical member response when node ordering is handled consistently.
