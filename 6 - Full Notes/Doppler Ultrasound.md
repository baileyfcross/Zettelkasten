# Doppler Ultrasound

Doppler ultrasound measures blood-flow velocity from the frequency shift of sound reflected by moving blood cells. The shift depends on transmitted frequency, flow speed, sound speed, and the angle between beam and motion. Incorrect angle estimation can strongly bias the calculated speed, especially as the beam approaches perpendicular incidence.
