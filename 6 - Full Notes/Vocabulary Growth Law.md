# Vocabulary Growth Law

A vocabulary growth law relates the number of distinct terms observed to the number of tokens read from a corpus. The rate normally slows as more text is processed because common words recur while genuinely new terms become less frequent.

The relationship helps estimate how feature dimensionality will expand with collection size and provides a diagnostic for comparing corpus segments. Tokenization, normalization, and domain vocabulary all affect the curve, so it should be interpreted with the preprocessing definition that produced it.
