# Magnetic Fusion Confinement

Magnetic confinement holds very hot ionized fusion fuel away from physical reactor walls while reactions occur. The source describes a toroidal [[Tokamak Fusion Reactor]] using large superconducting magnets around an evacuated chamber. Confinement must last long enough, at suitable plasma temperature and density, to sustain useful reactions. It contrasts with [[Inertial Confinement Fusion]], where a small pellet is compressed briefly instead.
