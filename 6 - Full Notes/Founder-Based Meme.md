# Founder-Based Meme

A founder-based meme is a meme family in which one original or early artifact remains highly visible as the reference point for later derivatives. Remakes gain meaning through their relationship to that source, even when they substantially transform it.

This structure combines viral and memetic dynamics. The founder accumulates concentrated attention, while participants create a distributed field of imitations, parodies, and remixes. Comparing its popularity distribution with an [[Egalitarian Meme]] reveals how cultural authority can remain centralized even within participatory circulation.
