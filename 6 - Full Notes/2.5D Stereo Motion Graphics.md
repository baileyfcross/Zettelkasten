# 2.5D Stereo Motion Graphics

Flat artwork can still contribute to a stereo sequence when separate cards are arranged at different depths. The book stages parchment, silhouettes, and text cards in a 3D scene, animates them, and records the result with offset left and right virtual cameras. The images themselves remain two-dimensional, but the cards' placement gives them binocular parallax. The method works especially well for titles and stylized artwork that do not require a camera orbit around volumetric forms. See [[2.5D Depth Cards and Parallax]].
