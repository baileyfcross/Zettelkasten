# Young's Modulus

Young's modulus is the proportionality between normal stress and axial strain in the linear elastic range. It is a material property with units of force per area and corresponds to the slope of the initial stress-strain relation.

Together with area and length, the modulus determines axial element stiffness. A larger modulus makes the same geometry resist elongation more strongly under a given load.
