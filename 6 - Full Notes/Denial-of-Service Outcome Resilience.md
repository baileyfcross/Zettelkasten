# Denial-of-Service Outcome Resilience

Denial-of-service outcome resilience focuses on preserving useful service when an attacker attempts to exhaust resources, disrupt dependencies, or overwhelm communication. It treats [[Cybersecurity Availability]] as the protected result rather than assuming every hostile request can be identified in advance.

Capacity planning, distribution, graceful degradation, rate controls, failover, and recovery can limit the outcome. Detection still matters, but continued function and restoration are the decisive measures.
