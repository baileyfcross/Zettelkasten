# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is the effort to build systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as recognizing speech, interpreting language, choosing actions, and learning from experience. The label covers both explicitly programmed methods such as an [[Expert System]] and data-driven methods such as [[Machine Learning]].

In a [[Virtual Assistant]], intelligence is distributed across specialized components rather than located in the conversational surface alone. Fluent language can make a system appear broadly intelligent even when its understanding and available actions remain narrow.
