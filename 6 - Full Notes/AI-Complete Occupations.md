# AI-Complete Occupations

An occupation is AI-complete when automating it competently would require the broad common sense and creativity associated with human-level general intelligence. Professional work such as scientific research, programming, management, law, or psychiatry cannot be reduced to one stable classification rule because it continually confronts novel physical and social situations.

The category distinguishes task automation from replacement of a whole role. Specialized systems can remove portions of an occupation long before [[Artificial General Intelligence]] exists, while complete substitution requires integrating many capabilities and responsibilities. Labor forecasts should therefore identify which tasks are vulnerable and which depend on still-missing general capacities.
