# BSurfaces Grease Pencil Retopology

The historical BSurfaces add-on could turn Grease Pencil strokes drawn across a dense model into retopology patches. Cross and follow controls determined how generated surface density followed the drawn guides.

The method lets an artist sketch intended edge flow directly over the reference form rather than first constructing every polygon. The generated result still requires inspection, because stroke placement and density settings determine whether the new surface is clean enough for its downstream use.
