# Arbitrary-Plane Reflection Decomposition

Arbitrary-plane reflection reduces a general plane to a principal plane. A known plane point supplies the translation to the origin, and the normalized plane normal supplies rotations that align it with a coordinate axis.

Once aligned, reflecting across the corresponding principal plane requires only negating one coordinate. The inverse alignment then returns the result to the original frame. The product illustrates why the normal vector, not merely a point on the plane, is necessary: the point fixes position while the normal fixes orientation.
