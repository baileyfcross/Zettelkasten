# Patient-Facing Health Visualization

Patient-facing health visualization presents personal measurements, progress, or treatment information in a form intended to support comprehension and self-management. It can turn repeated observations into trends and make goals or changes easier to discuss.

The audience may not share a clinician's vocabulary or statistical training. Labels, scales, uncertainty, and reference ranges must be explained without creating false reassurance or alarm. The display should support an action or conversation and should be tested with patients rather than merely simplifying a professional dashboard.
