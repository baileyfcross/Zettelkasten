# Laguerre Polynomial

Laguerre polynomials solve Laguerre's differential equation and form an orthogonal family on the nonnegative real axis with an exponential weight. Associated Laguerre polynomials extend the family by including an additional index.

Their domain and weight make them suited to radial problems whose solutions decay exponentially. In quantum mechanics, associated Laguerre functions occur in the radial part of hydrogen-like bound states after the dominant asymptotic behavior is removed.
