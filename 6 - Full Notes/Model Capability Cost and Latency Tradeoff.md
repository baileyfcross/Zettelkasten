# Model Capability Cost and Latency Tradeoff

A network AI model should be selected for the work it must perform rather than by size or reputation alone. Complex troubleshooting may justify a slower, more capable model, while routine classification or formatting may be served by a smaller model with lower latency and token cost.

The tradeoff must be measured on representative network questions. A fast answer that invents syntax is expensive in operational terms, while a high-quality answer whose delay prevents interactive use may also be a poor fit. [[Task-Specific Network Prompt]] evaluation makes capability, response time, and price comparable for the intended task.
