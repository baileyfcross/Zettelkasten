# SAGE Air Defense System

SAGE was a network of large computers built to combine radar data and calculate interception paths for incoming bombers. Its development funded advances in memory, displays, communications, and interactive computing.

The system was operational as ballistic missiles reduced the relevance of its original mission. Its technical legacy nevertheless influenced IBM computers and later human-computer interfaces.
