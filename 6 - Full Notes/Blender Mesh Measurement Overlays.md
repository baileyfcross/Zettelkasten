# Blender Mesh Measurement Overlays

Mesh measurement overlays display edge lengths, edge angles, face areas, and face angles directly on selected geometry in Edit mode. They update as the mesh changes, turning numerical inspection into part of the modeling feedback loop.

These overlays are especially useful for mechanical and architectural work because they expose dimensional consequences while components are edited. Their Edit-mode scope distinguishes them from persistent object-level measurement systems such as [[MeasureIt Add-On]].
