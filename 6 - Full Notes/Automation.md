# Automation

Automation uses a machine to perform or control a process with minimal human intervention. It can replace repetitive physical control, execute knowledge-work procedures, or carry out a computation that people previously performed.

Automation is limited by what can be specified, computed, and safely delegated. A system may assist judgment without automating it, and deciding what should be automated remains a human and social question.
