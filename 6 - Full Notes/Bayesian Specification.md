# Bayesian Specification

A Bayesian specification states the preliminary knowledge supplied directly by the modeler. It selects the variables, decomposes their joint distribution, and assigns a computable form to every factor in that decomposition.

These choices answer what the model discusses, how its knowledge is structured, and how each part is represented mathematically. Parameters not fixed by the specification can be learned during identification.
