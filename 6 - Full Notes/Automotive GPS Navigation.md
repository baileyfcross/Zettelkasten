# Automotive GPS Navigation

Automotive GPS navigation combines a satellite-derived position with a digital road database and route-selection software. The useful product is not merely latitude and longitude but map matching, turn-by-turn instructions, estimated arrival, and recovery after a missed turn. Cellular traffic data and local services later enriched the same position layer, while inertial sensors can bridge short signal losses such as tunnels when their measurements are integrated effectively.
