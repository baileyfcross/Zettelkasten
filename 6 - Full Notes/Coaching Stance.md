# Coaching Stance

A coaching stance combines neutrality, attention to the client's agenda, principle-guided action, and compassion. The coach listens for what the team is trying to achieve and helps it examine choices without covertly imposing a preferred answer.

Neutrality does not require indifference to harmful behavior or Scrum principles. It separates respect for the people from curiosity about the system and its results.
