# Conservation of Linear Momentum

The total linear momentum of a closed system remains constant when the net external impulse is zero. During an internal interaction, [[Newton's Third Law]] gives equal and opposite impulses to the participants, so their momentum changes cancel. Momentum conservation holds whether kinetic energy is conserved or transformed into other forms.
