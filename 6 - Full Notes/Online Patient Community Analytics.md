# Online Patient Community Analytics

Online patient community analytics examines discussions in condition-focused forums and support networks. Posts can reveal treatment experiences, information needs, coping strategies, and questions that may not appear in a clinical record.

Community norms and membership shape the evidence. Active posters are not a random sample of patients, and a single person can contribute many messages. Analysis should respect privacy expectations, distinguish users from posts, and interpret reported outcomes as patient-generated observations rather than verified clinical endpoints.
