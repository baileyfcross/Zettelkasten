# Blender Spin Duplicates

Spin Duplicates copies selected mesh geometry repeatedly around an axis instead of joining the steps into a swept surface. The 3D cursor provides a convenient rotational center, while angle and step count control the arrangement.

The result is suited to radial repetition such as teeth, spokes, or ornaments that should remain disconnected at the component level. It differs from [[Blender Spin Tool|Spin]], which normally builds connected topology between successive steps.
