# Player Motivation

Player motivation is the pattern of desired activity and reward that draws a person into play. The source groups recurring motivations as action, social connection, mastery, achievement, immersion, and creativity, with players often combining several.

Motivations guide the [[Target Audience]] description and the interaction loops a game emphasizes. They are more useful than a generic assumption that all players want the same form of fun.
