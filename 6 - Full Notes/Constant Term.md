# Constant Term

A constant term is an [[Algebraic Term]] whose value does not depend on a variable. In (3x^2-5x+7), the term (7) is constant.

Constants behave as like terms with other constants, so they can be combined during [[Algebraic Simplification]]. Their role depends on the surrounding form. In a linear expression (ax+b), (b) shifts the value independently of (x); in a polynomial, the constant is the term of degree zero. Writing it explicitly can also preserve place structure during [[Polynomial Long Division]].
