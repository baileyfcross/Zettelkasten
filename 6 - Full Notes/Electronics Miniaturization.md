# Electronics Miniaturization

Electronics miniaturization reduces the size, weight, and power demands of circuits while increasing functional density. Hearing aids, proximity fuses, missiles, and spacecraft created strong early demand for compact components.

Miniaturization changed system possibilities as well as packaging. Smaller reliable circuits could be embedded in places where room-sized computing equipment was impossible.
