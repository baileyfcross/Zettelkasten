# Community Cloud

A community cloud serves several entities that share an interest or operating concern. The shared boundary can be based on industry, geography, regulation, security needs, business model, or a common group of users.

Members may share infrastructure, software, information, or processes according to the service level. The model can spread the cost of specialist capability while preserving requirements that a general [[Public Cloud]] may not satisfy.
