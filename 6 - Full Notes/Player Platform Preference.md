# Player Platform Preference

Player platform preference records where the intended audience is willing and able to play, such as PC, console, mobile, virtual reality, arcade hardware, a tabletop, or pen and paper. Platform alone is a weak description of an audience because many genres and player types now appear on most hardware.

It remains a practical constraint because controls, display, physical context, and hardware strengths shape the experience. A target profile should name required platforms when they affect access or use, while avoiding unsupported stereotypes about the people who use each device.
