# Atmospheric Lapse Rates and Stability

A lapse rate describes how temperature changes with altitude. Comparing the environmental lapse rate with the dry or saturated adiabatic response of a displaced parcel indicates whether the parcel tends to return, remain neutral, or continue rising. Potential-temperature gradients and measures such as convective available potential energy extend this comparison in [[Thermodynamic Weather Diagrams]].
