# Middleware

Middleware is software that connects applications, data, and lower-level computing facilities. It sits between operating systems and business applications and supplies common communication, integration, database, messaging, or management capabilities.

The book groups middleware as message-oriented, data-oriented, or object-oriented according to what participants share. In a cloud platform, middleware forms part of the operating environment supplied above the raw infrastructure.
