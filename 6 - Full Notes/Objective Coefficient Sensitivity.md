# Objective Coefficient Sensitivity

Objective coefficient sensitivity asks how far an activity's unit profit or cost may change before the current basis stops being optimal. A change to a nonbasic variable affects its reduced cost directly, while a change to a basic variable can alter several objective-row coefficients.

The allowable interval is defined by maintaining the appropriate optimality signs. Feasibility is unchanged because the constraints and right-hand sides have not moved, but crossing the interval means simplex reoptimization may be required.
