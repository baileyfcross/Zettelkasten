# Treatment Contrast

A treatment contrast expresses a comparison among fitted group means as a linear combination of model coefficients. It turns the parameterization of a [[Design Matrix]] into the scientific difference an investigator wants to estimate or test.

Changing a [[Regression Reference Group]] changes the displayed coefficients but not a properly specified contrast among the same group means. Explicit contrasts are especially useful when factors and interactions make the desired comparison span several terms.
