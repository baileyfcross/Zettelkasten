# Trigonometric Periodicity

A periodic function repeats its values after a fixed positive interval. Sine and cosine have period $2\pi$, while tangent has period $\pi$. These periods follow from repeated positions or slopes on the [[Unit Circle]] and allow infinitely many angles to share the same trigonometric value.
