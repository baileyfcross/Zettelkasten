# Tuckman Model

The Tuckman model describes team development as forming, storming, norming, performing, and adjourning. Members first orient to the group, encounter conflict, establish workable norms, develop effective coordination, and eventually separate or transform.

The sequence is a diagnostic model rather than a guaranteed linear schedule. Membership or context changes can return a mature team to earlier coordination needs.
