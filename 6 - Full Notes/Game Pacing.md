# Game Pacing

Game pacing is the rate and rhythm at which activity, challenge, reward, and progression unfold. It can be measured through actions, completed challenges, attention, or elapsed time rather than through levels alone.

Pacing balances early success with lasting goals and alternates intensity to avoid fatigue. [[Progression Curve|Progression curves]] set the broad rate, while nested interaction loops and playtests reveal how that rate feels to the player.
