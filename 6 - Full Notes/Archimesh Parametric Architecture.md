# Archimesh Parametric Architecture

Archimesh generates adjustable architectural objects such as rooms, doors, windows, cabinets, shelves, columns, stairs, roofs, and common decorative props. Objects are inserted at the 3D cursor and retain parameters that can be edited from the sidebar after creation.

The add-on accelerates scene blocking by encoding familiar building elements as reusable generators. Its output still requires design judgment, proportion checks, and adaptation to the specific environment.
