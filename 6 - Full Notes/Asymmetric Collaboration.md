# Asymmetric Collaboration

Asymmetric collaboration gives participants different interfaces, information, mobility, or roles within one shared task. A mobile worker may stream a first-person AR view while a stationary expert uses a desktop or immersive overview to inspect the wider scene.

The differences can complement each role, but they also create unequal context and control. Cross-view cues must connect the expert's annotations or route suggestions to what the mobile participant can perceive and act upon.
