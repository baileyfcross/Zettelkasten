# Sequence-Constrained Hierarchical Clustering

Sequence-constrained clustering permits a merge only between adjacent segments of an ordered series. In the book's screenplay and tweet analyses, scene or message chronology is preserved while complete-link agglomeration builds a nested hierarchy.

The resulting dendrogram can expose where consecutive passages cease to resemble one another. Without the sequence constraint, a conventional clusterer might put distant but similar scenes together and erase the narrative position of a transition. The constraint changes what the tree means, not merely how it is drawn.
