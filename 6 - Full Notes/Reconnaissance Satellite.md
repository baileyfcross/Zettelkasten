# Reconnaissance Satellite

A reconnaissance satellite gathers intelligence from orbit through imaging, signals collection, or other remote sensing. Early photographic systems returned exposed film in reentry capsules because electronic transmission could not yet provide the required resolution.

Orbital reconnaissance made broad regions accessible without aircraft overflight and supplied repeated evidence about military forces and installations. The demanding sensors, launch vehicles, and data systems also influenced civilian observation technology. Although the spacecraft increased military capability, the information they provided could reduce uncertainty and challenge exaggerated claims about an opponent's strength.
