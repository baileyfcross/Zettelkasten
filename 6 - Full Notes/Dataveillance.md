# Dataveillance

Dataveillance is surveillance carried out through the collection and analysis of data about people's communications and activities. Digital systems allow governments, corporations, and data brokers to monitor behavior at a scale not possible with isolated paper records.

The practice often works through ordinary transactions and mobile services rather than visible observation. Its power comes from combining records over time to infer identity, habits, relationships, and likely future behavior.
