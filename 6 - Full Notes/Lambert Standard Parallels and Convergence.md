# Lambert Standard Parallels and Convergence

Standard parallels are the latitudes where the Lambert projection cone touches or intersects the reference globe and local scale distortion is least. Scale is reduced between two such parallels and increased outside them. Chart convergence is proportional to longitude change and the sine of the reference latitude, producing a constant convergence factor for the chart.
