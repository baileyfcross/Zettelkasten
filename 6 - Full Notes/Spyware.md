# Spyware

Spyware secretly observes a user or system and collects information for another party. It may record activity, credentials, communications, browsing behavior, or device details, undermining [[Cybersecurity Confidentiality]] without immediately disrupting normal operation.

Because stealth is central to its value, spyware may prioritize persistence and quiet data transfer. Endpoint monitoring, least privilege, application control, and [[Extrusion Detection]] help reveal collection and unauthorized outbound movement.
