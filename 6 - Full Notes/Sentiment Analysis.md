# Sentiment Analysis

Sentiment analysis assigns an opinion orientation or score to text about an entity, event, or aspect. A system may distinguish positive, negative, and neutral language or produce a continuous measure that can be aggregated across many documents.

The result depends on the unit of analysis, available labels, domain vocabulary, and treatment of cues such as negation, emoticons, and hashtags. Automated polarity is an inferred feature of language, not a direct measurement of a writer’s private emotional state.
