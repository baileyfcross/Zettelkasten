# High-Dimensional Estimation Frontier

The high-dimensional estimation frontier separates structural regimes where accurate estimation is possible from those where no estimator can succeed uniformly. It is identified by comparing achievable risk bounds with [[Minimax Statistical Risk|minimax lower bounds]].

In sparse regression, the scaled prediction risk has order $D\log(p/D)\sigma^2/n$ under suitable design conditions. Accuracy is plausible when this quantity is small and impossible uniformly when it is large. The frontier makes explicit that ambient dimension can grow rapidly only if sample size, signal structure, and noise remain in a favorable balance.
