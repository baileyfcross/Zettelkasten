# Context-Specific Language

Context-specific language gives a term one unambiguous meaning within a defined setting. A product can mean sales price and margin in sales, stock and availability in inventory, or storage size and location in a warehouse. Forcing all meanings into one universal object creates optional fields, tangled dependencies, and constant mental switching. A [[Ubiquitous Language]] should therefore remain consistent inside a context while allowing another context to use the same word differently.
