# MHD Generator Duct

The generator duct guides fast ionized gas through the field and holds electrodes where current can be taken off. Its walls must tolerate heat, corrosion, electrical isolation, and erosion from seeded plasma. The source contrasts insulating ceramics with water-cooled metal segments separated by ceramic material. A long-lived smooth duct matters because wall damage and pressure drop reduce useful conversion.
