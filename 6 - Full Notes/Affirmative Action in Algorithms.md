# Affirmative Action in Algorithms

Affirmative action in algorithms intentionally considers disadvantage or group membership to counter exclusion and unequal starting conditions. It rejects the idea that identical formal treatment is always fair when the data and institutions were shaped by earlier injustice.

The approach is ethically contested because it distributes opportunities differently in order to pursue a more equitable result. Its use requires a stated theory of justice, clear scope, and evaluation of effects. Unlike [[Fairness Through Unawareness]], it makes the corrective value choice visible rather than hiding it behind a claim of neutrality.
