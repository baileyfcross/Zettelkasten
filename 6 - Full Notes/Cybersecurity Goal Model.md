# Cybersecurity Goal Model

The source organizes cybersecurity around six goals: [[Cybersecurity Confidentiality]], [[Cybersecurity Integrity]], [[Cybersecurity Availability]], [[Authentication]], [[Authorization]], and [[Nonrepudiation]]. The first three form the commonly recognized confidentiality-integrity-availability group, while the other three make identity, access, and source evidence explicit.

These goals describe desired outcomes rather than particular products. One incident can damage several at once, so controls should be judged by the security properties they preserve across people, systems, applications, and data.
