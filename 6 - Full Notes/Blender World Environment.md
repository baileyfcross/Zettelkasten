# Blender World Environment

World Properties describe the environment surrounding a Blender scene. They control the background and can contribute illumination, so changing the World color affects both the visible sky and the color cast of objects and shadows.

The environment establishes whether a scene appears indoors, outdoors, in daylight, at night, or in an abstract void. Node-based World settings can create more complex backgrounds and lighting than a single flat color.
