# Numerical Coefficient

A numerical coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying the variable part of an [[Algebraic Term]]. In (13a^2b^3), the coefficient is (13); in (-ac), the unwritten coefficient is (-1).

Coefficients describe how many copies of the same variable structure occur. The [[Distributive Property]] therefore permits coefficients of [[Like Terms]] to be added: (5x-2x=(5-2)x=3x). A missing written coefficient of a positive term is one, not zero.
