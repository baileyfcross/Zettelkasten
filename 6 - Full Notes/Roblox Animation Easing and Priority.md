# Roblox Animation Easing and Priority

Animation easing controls how motion accelerates and decelerates between Roblox keyframes. Linear interpolation maintains a constant rate, while other styles can ease into or out of a pose and make the same keys feel heavier, softer, sharper, or more mechanical.

Priority determines which animation wins when several tracks try to control the same joints. A critical action can override idle or movement animation, while lower-priority tracks remain available when the stronger track stops. Easing shapes motion within [[Roblox Pose Keyframes]]; priority resolves competition among complete animation tracks.
