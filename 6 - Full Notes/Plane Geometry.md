# Plane Geometry

A plane in game collision geometry is represented by a normalized surface normal and a signed distance from the origin. Substituting a point into the plane equation determines which side of the plane contains it and how far away it lies.

The normal also supplies the orientation needed for reflection and collision response. Line and segment intersection solves for the parameter at which their points satisfy the plane equation.
