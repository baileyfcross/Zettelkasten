# Formal Dramatic and Dynamic Elements Framework

The Formal, Dramatic, and Dynamic Elements framework separates design into the rule-bound structures supplied by the game, the narrative and emotional material that gives those structures significance, and the behavior that appears during play.

Its value is diagnostic. A designer can isolate objectives, resources, procedures, characters, premise, conflict, or emergent behavior without assuming that one category explains the whole experience. The framework also makes narrative part of game design rather than treating it as decoration added after mechanics.
