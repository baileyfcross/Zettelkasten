# Remainder Theorem

When a [[Polynomial]] $f(x)$ is divided by $x-c$, the remainder is $f(c)$. The statement follows from polynomial division: $f(x)=(x-c)q(x)+r$, where the remainder $r$ is constant; substituting $x=c$ gives $f(c)=r$. The theorem turns substitution into a shortcut for testing a proposed divisor.
