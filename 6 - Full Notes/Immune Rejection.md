# Immune Rejection

Immune rejection occurs when a recipient's immune system recognizes an implanted tissue or its materials as foreign and attacks them. Donor-cell identity, matrix chemistry, contaminants, and degradation products can all contribute.

Using autologous cells can lower risk, but a construct's complete biological and synthetic composition must be evaluated rather than assuming the cells alone determine compatibility.
