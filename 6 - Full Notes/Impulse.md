# Impulse

Impulse is the time integral of force and equals the change in [[Linear Momentum]]:

$$J=\int F\,dt=\Delta p.$$

For constant force, $J=F\Delta t$. On a force-time graph, impulse is the signed area under the curve. Extending the interaction time reduces the average force needed for the same momentum change.
