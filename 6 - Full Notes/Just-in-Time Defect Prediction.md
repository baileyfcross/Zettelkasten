# Just-in-Time Defect Prediction

Just-in-time defect prediction estimates the risk that an individual code change introduces a defect. Features describe the change, its author, affected files, timing, and recent history.

Making a prediction at commit time supports rapid review and testing. The representation must use only information available then and distinguish risky complexity from ordinary large or experienced changes.
