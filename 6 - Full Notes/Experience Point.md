# Experience Point

An experience point is a numerical marker of progress toward a threshold, commonly a new level. Unlike an expendable [[Game Currency|currency]], experience points are usually accumulated automatically and converted by the system when the threshold is reached.

Crossing that threshold may grant resources, new [[Game Verb|verbs]], or access to new areas. The points therefore represent progress toward a later [[Systemic Reward]] rather than a flexible resource the player can spend immediately.
