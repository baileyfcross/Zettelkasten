# Quasi-Static Electromagnetic Field

A quasi-static electromagnetic field varies with time slowly enough, or occurs in a conductor good enough, that displacement current can be neglected relative to conduction current. The remaining equations retain selected time-varying effects without the full wave coupling.

This approximation is useful in conducting media over frequency ranges where charge and current respond much faster than electromagnetic propagation effects. Its validity depends on material and scale.
