# Image Forensics Countermeasure

An image forensics countermeasure is a manipulation intended to erase, imitate, or avoid a detector's expected trace. A skilled forger can alter metadata, reinsert noise, equalize compression, or adapt generated imagery after learning how a forensic method works.

This creates a continuing contest between authentication and fabrication. New methods expose regularities that current tools overlook, while new forgeries are designed around published tests. The possibility of countermeasures is why a clean result should not be treated as conclusive proof of authenticity.
