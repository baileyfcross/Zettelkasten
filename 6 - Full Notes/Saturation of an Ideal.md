# Saturation of an Ideal

The saturation (J:I∞) consists of elements r for which Iⁿr is contained in J for some nonnegative integer n. It is the union of the ideal quotients (J:Iⁿ).

Saturation removes components supported where I vanishes. The source computes it through polynomial elimination by adjoining variables that encode inverses, linking it to [[Ring Localization]] and [[Elimination Ideal]]s.
