# Magic Circle

The magic circle is the conceptual boundary that separates play from ordinary activity. Inside it, the game's own [[Game Rule|rules]], meanings, goals, and consequences organize what actions count.

The boundary can be porous: [[Metagaming]] may carry information, relationships, or consequences across individual sessions and beyond the immediate play context. Even so, the game remains an arbiter of the shared state within the circle.
