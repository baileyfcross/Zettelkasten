# Chinese Human Spaceflight Program

The Chinese human spaceflight program, known bureaucratically as Program 921, was established to develop independent crewed spacecraft and a space station as signals of national scientific and technological power. Yang Liwei's 2003 Shenzhou 5 flight made China the third country to orbit a person with its own launch vehicle and spacecraft.

The program used Russian-derived experience and technology while proceeding at a deliberate pace through uncrewed tests, increasing crew sizes, spacewalking, and docking. Its military-civilian organization and exclusion from U.S.-led ISS cooperation reinforced an independent path. National prestige and capability-building were central even without the urgency of a two-power race.
