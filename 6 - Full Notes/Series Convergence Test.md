# Series Convergence Test

A convergence test determines whether the partial sums of an infinite series approach a finite limit. Necessary conditions, comparison arguments, and ratio- or root-based tests reveal convergence without requiring a closed form for the sum.

For a power series, convergence usually depends on distance from its center. The ratio test identifies a radius inside which the series converges absolutely and outside which it diverges; the endpoints must be checked separately because the ratio test is inconclusive there.
