# Sampling-Based Clustering

Sampling-based clustering derives a smaller representative subset, clusters it, and uses the result to characterize or assign the full dataset. The reduced problem can fit in one machine and finish much sooner.

The approximation is reliable only when the sample preserves rare groups, density differences, and boundary structure important to the analysis.
