# Cell-Laden Hydrogel

A cell-laden hydrogel contains living cells dispersed within a hydrated polymer network. The liquid precursor can be molded or printed into a shape and then gelled to hold cells in a three-dimensional support environment.

Cell density, matrix stiffness, crosslinking conditions, transport distance, and biological ligands jointly determine whether the cells survive and organize.
