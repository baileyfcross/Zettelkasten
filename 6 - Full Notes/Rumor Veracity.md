# Rumor Veracity

Rumor veracity is the truth status eventually assigned to an initially unverified claim. Possible outcomes include true, false, disputed, complex, or still unknown rather than only a simple binary label.

Propagation size and speed do not determine veracity because the same communication mechanisms carry accurate and inaccurate information. Evidence from content, sources, user reactions, and outside confirmation must be evaluated separately.
