# EDSAC

EDSAC was a practical stored-program computer completed under Maurice Wilkes at Cambridge in 1949. Unlike an experimental demonstration, it became a useful scientific computing service for university researchers.

Its routine operation helped establish libraries of reusable program routines and a disciplined approach to programming. EDSAC showed how stored programs could support a continuing community of users.
