# Engagement

Engagement is an ongoing cognitive and emotional state marked by vigor, dedication, and absorption. It persists when a player has reasons to continue attending, acting, learning, and pursuing goals.

Games build engagement through understandable feedback, meaningful decisions, appropriate challenge, and nested interaction loops. [[Fun]] is one positive form of engagement, but a compelling experience can remain engaging without being conventionally fun.
