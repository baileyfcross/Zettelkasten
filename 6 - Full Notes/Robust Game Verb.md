# Robust Game Verb

A robust game verb produces an observable response whenever the player reasonably expects an interaction. The response may show success or refusal, but it should still communicate what happened.

Because players use [[Game Verb|verbs]] to investigate other rules, silence can make the system seem unresponsive or inconsistent. Timely [[Feedback]] lets even a failed action teach the relationship between the verb and a [[Game Part|game object]].
