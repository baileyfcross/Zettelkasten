# JPEG Signature

A JPEG signature is a collection of encoding choices that can characterize a camera or software pipeline. Quantization tables, embedded-thumbnail properties, and the order and contents of marker segments are particularly useful because different devices and applications package JPEG data differently.

A mismatch between a file's signature and its claimed source suggests that the image was resaved or did not come directly from that pipeline. A match is not proof of origin, since signatures can be shared, copied, or changed by firmware and export settings.
