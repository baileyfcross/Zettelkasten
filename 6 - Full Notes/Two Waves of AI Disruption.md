# Two Waves of AI Disruption

AI disruption can be divided into two waves. The first comes from specialized systems that exceed people in bounded tasks and reorganize work, markets, and daily decisions. The second would follow the creation of human-level general intelligence and possibly [[Superintelligence]], with a much wider and less predictable scope.

Separating the waves prevents distant scenarios from obscuring nearer changes. Specialized automation can cause displacement, concentration of power, and dependence without an intelligence explosion. At the same time, successfully governing the first wave may determine the institutions, incentives, and technical infrastructure through which more capable systems are later developed.
