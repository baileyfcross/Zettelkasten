# Aseptic Cell Culture

Aseptic cell culture prevents bacteria, fungi, and other contaminants from entering a living-cell experiment. Work is performed in a sterile-air biosafety hood, with gloves, containers, and tools disinfected before they cross into the working area.

Contamination can kill cells or silently alter their behavior, making sterility part of experimental validity rather than mere cleanliness.
