# Group-Structured Feature Selection

Group-structured feature selection treats predefined sets of variables as related units. Selection can retain or discard a group together when domain knowledge says its members describe one source, pathway, sensor, or representation.

This structure changes the optimization target from isolated feature relevance to coordinated inclusion. It can produce more coherent models and exploit known relationships, although a poorly defined group may force useful and noisy variables to move together.
