# Pronunciation Model

A pronunciation model describes how words map to sequences of speech sounds. It connects the word hypotheses favored by a [[Statistical Language Model]] with the phonetic evidence scored by an [[Acoustic Model]].

The model may include multiple pronunciations for one spelling to cover accents and ordinary variation. It is often represented as a lexicon whose entries contain [[Phoneme|phoneme]] sequences.
