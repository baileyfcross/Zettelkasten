# Lagrangian Mechanics

Lagrangian mechanics describes motion through a scalar function of generalized coordinates, generalized velocities, and time. For many conservative systems, the Lagrangian is kinetic energy minus potential energy.

Requiring the action to be stationary produces the Euler-Lagrange equations. This formulation incorporates constraints through the choice of coordinates, exposes conserved quantities through symmetries, and avoids resolving every force into Cartesian components.
