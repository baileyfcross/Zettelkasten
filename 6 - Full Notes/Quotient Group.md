# Quotient Group

Given a normal subgroup H of G, the quotient group G/H consists of cosets with multiplication (aH)(bH) = abH. Its identity is H and the inverse of aH is a⁻¹H.

The natural projection from G onto G/H is a surjective homomorphism with kernel H.
