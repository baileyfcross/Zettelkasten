# Null Proportion Estimate

A null proportion estimate approximates the fraction of tested features whose [[Null Hypothesis|null hypotheses]] are true. The source estimates it from the share of p-values above a relatively high threshold, where most observations are assumed to come from true nulls.

Using this information can make false-discovery estimates less conservative than assuming every null is true. The estimate depends on threshold choice and on the expected p-value behavior under the null.
