# Misinformation

Misinformation is false or inaccurate information that misleads people and may be spread without an intention to deceive. Open social platforms allow it to propagate rapidly through ordinary sharing and trust relationships.

It is distinguished from deliberate [[Disinformation]], although real cascades may mix both intentions. Analysis can target the content, the accounts spreading it, its diffusion structure, or interventions that limit its effects.
