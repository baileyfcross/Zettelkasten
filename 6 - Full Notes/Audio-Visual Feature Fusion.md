# Audio-Visual Feature Fusion

Audio-visual feature fusion joins measurements from sound and image streams before the combined representation is classified. Temporal alignment ensures that features describe corresponding moments or events.

Early fusion lets the learner model interactions between modalities directly, but incompatible scales and missing channels can distort the joint vector. Dimensionality reduction or modality-specific normalization may be needed before training.
