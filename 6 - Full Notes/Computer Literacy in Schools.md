# Computer Literacy in Schools

Computer literacy courses teach people to use common digital tools such as word processors and spreadsheets. They can improve practical access but offer little understanding of how computation, programs, and information systems work.

For computational-thinking advocates, literacy was too shallow to prepare students to shape a digital world. Its limited appeal helped motivate later movements toward computational fluency and programming.
