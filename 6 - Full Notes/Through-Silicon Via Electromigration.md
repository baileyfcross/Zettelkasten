# Through-Silicon Via Electromigration

Through-silicon via electromigration is the transport of conductor atoms under sustained current in a TSV. Higher current density and thermomechanical stress can shorten the vertical interconnect's lifetime, especially when a manufacturing defect narrows the current path.

The risk is evaluated through a [[TSV Mean Time to Failure Model]]. Because a chip can be limited by its weakest damaged via, the mitigation workflow identifies defects, records a [[TSV Defect Fault Map]], and balances current direction during operation.
