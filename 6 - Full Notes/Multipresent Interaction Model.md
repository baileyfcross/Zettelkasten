# Multipresent Interaction Model

A multipresent interaction model allows the player to influence many areas of the game world at the same time. Strategy and management games use it when control is not confined to one avatar's position.

The interface needs scalable selection, camera movement, overview information, and alerts from places outside the current view. The challenge is to make broad authority legible without presenting every option at once.
