# Character Polygon Density Management

Character polygon density management keeps the editable mesh as simple as the required silhouette, detail, and deformation permit. Lower density makes proportional changes easier, while selected areas receive added loops when a corner, facial feature, or joint needs more control.

Under subdivision, sparse loops allow a surface to relax and move farther from its cage; closely spaced loops hold a sharper form. Density should therefore be treated as tension control, not as a uniform measure of quality, and the artist should add it where the intended shape or motion justifies it.
