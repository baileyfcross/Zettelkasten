# Akaike Information Criterion

The Akaike information criterion, or AIC, compares models by combining lack of fit with a penalty for the number of estimated parameters. Among models fit to the same outcome data, a smaller AIC indicates a preferable balance under the criterion.

AIC is relative: it has no universal good or perfect value. Unlike a likelihood-ratio comparison, it can compare some non-nested models, but it does not establish causal validity or repair a poorly defined outcome, exposure, or sample.
