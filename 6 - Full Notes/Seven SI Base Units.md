# Seven SI Base Units

The book lists seven base quantities in the International System of Units: length (meter, m), mass (kilogram, kg), time (second, s), electric current (ampere, A), thermodynamic temperature (kelvin, K), amount of substance (mole, mol), and luminous intensity (candela, cd). Their unit symbols provide the building blocks for the expressions of derived units. A base unit is not simply a commonly used unit; it is part of this chosen foundation.
