# Egalitarian Meme

An egalitarian meme is a meme family whose popularity is spread across many versions rather than organized around one dominant original. Participants recognize a shared formula, but no single artifact necessarily defines the whole phenomenon.

The distributed structure makes collective variation the primary cultural object. Individual versions may be short-lived or modestly viewed while the format remains highly visible in aggregate. This distinguishes egalitarian circulation from both a viral item and a [[Founder-Based Meme]], where the source retains disproportionate authority.
