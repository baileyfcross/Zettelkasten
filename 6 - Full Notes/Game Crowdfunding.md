# Game Crowdfunding

Game crowdfunding raises development money from many prospective players before the game is finished. Support commonly functions as preordering with rewards rather than as ownership investment.

A successful campaign needs a credible team, persuasive demonstration, clear promises, and continuing communication. It can free a niche project from publisher and retailer pressure while creating direct obligations to funders.
