# Homebrew Computer Club

The Homebrew Computer Club was a Silicon Valley community where enthusiasts shared microcomputer designs, components, and software. Its collaborative culture connected countercultural ideals of personal access with local semiconductor expertise.

The club helped turn the microprocessor from an industrial component into a personal medium. Apple’s founders were among the participants who converted shared experiments into commercial products.
