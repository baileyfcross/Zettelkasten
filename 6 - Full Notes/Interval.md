# Interval

An interval is a set of real numbers containing every real number between any two of its members. It represents a continuous portion of the [[Number Line]] and therefore contains infinitely many values even when its length is finite.

Intervals may be [[Bounded Interval]]s with two finite boundaries or [[Unbounded Interval]]s extending indefinitely. Each finite boundary can be included or excluded, represented by a [[Closed Endpoint]] or [[Open Endpoint]]. Linear inequality solution sets commonly form intervals or unions of intervals.
