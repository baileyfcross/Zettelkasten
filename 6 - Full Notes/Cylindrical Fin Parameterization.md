# Cylindrical Fin Parameterization

Cylindrical fin parameterization defines radial and axial dimensions, base placement, and any internal channel through reusable parameters. Cylinders, blocks, work planes, partitions, and deletion operations can then construct the final solid.

Named dimensions make the relationship between surface area, material volume, and heat path auditable. They also let a family of cylindrical designs be rebuilt without manually editing every geometric feature.
