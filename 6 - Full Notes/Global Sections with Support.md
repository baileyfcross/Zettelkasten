# Global Sections with Support

For an ideal I and module M, the submodule Γ_I(M) consists of elements annihilated by some power of I. These are the algebraic sections supported on the closed subset V(I).

Applying Γ_I to an injective resolution and taking cohomology produces [[Local Cohomology]]. The degree-zero local cohomology module is Γ_I(M) itself.
