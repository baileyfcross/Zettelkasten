# Factor Inertia

In correspondence analysis, inertia is the mass-weighted squared distance of a point cloud from its center. Eigenvalue decomposition splits that total inertia among ordered factors, so the early factors capture larger portions of the table's variation.

A two-dimensional plot is an approximation when it displays only two factors. The book repeatedly distinguishes visual interpretation from calculations in the full factor space: a low percentage of displayed inertia should make an analyst cautious about claims based solely on a planar diagram.
