# Data Protection Transparency

Data protection transparency requires an organization to make its collection and use of [[Personal Data]] understandable to the people concerned. Meaningful notice identifies the responsible party, purposes, categories of data, important recipients, and ways to exercise rights.

Big-data reuse makes transparency difficult because later combinations and inferences may not be predictable at collection time. That uncertainty is a reason to document evolving purposes, not a reason to abandon explanation.
