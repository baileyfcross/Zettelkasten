# Economic Inflation

Economic inflation in a game occurs when resources enter an economy faster than meaningful sinks remove them. Players accumulate more currency or goods than they can use, so each unit loses perceived value.

Rising rewards can create a temporary sense of power, but they remain engaging only while new spending choices matter. Balancing sources and sinks, controlling [[Arbitrage]], and providing durable uses for value limit runaway inflation.
