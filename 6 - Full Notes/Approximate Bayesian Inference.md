# Approximate Bayesian Inference

Approximate Bayesian inference replaces an intractable exact calculation with a controlled estimate of the requested distribution or statistic. Sampling and optimization are common ways to trade precision for feasible computation.

The approximation should be interpreted as part of the answer, not as a hidden implementation detail. Its error depends on the model, proposal mechanism, sample count, convergence, and the particular question being asked.
