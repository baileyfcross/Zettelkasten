# Fisher Exact Test

Fisher's exact test evaluates association in a contingency table by calculating probabilities under fixed marginal totals. It does not rely on the large-sample chi-squared approximation and is therefore useful when cells are small.

The test provides evidence about dependence between the categorical variables, not the size or practical importance of the association. Frequencies and proportions should still accompany its p-value.
