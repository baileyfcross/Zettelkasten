# Tactile Display

A tactile display presents controlled stimulation primarily to the skin through vibration, static indentation, electrical pulses, or tangential stretch. It can alert, encode direction, or reproduce a surface feature without necessarily moving the user's limb.

This distinguishes it from a [[Haptic Display]], which also exchanges forces or positions through the kinesthetic system. Display design must match actuator spacing, frequency, intensity, and timing to the sensitivity of the target body location. A technically distinct signal is not useful if neighboring elements or intensity levels cannot be identified reliably.
