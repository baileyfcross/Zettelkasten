# Cross-View Interaction

Cross-view interaction lets a user move information, attention, or control between displays that show different views of a shared augmented space. An action begun on a handheld overview may identify or affect an object seen through a head-worn or environmental display.

The technique needs a mapping among coordinate systems and representations. Visual cues and feedback should show where an item came from, where it went, and how the views remain related.
