# Electromagnetic Field Tensor

The electromagnetic field tensor is an antisymmetric rank-two spacetime tensor that combines the electric and magnetic field components. It is obtained from antisymmetrized derivatives of the [[Electromagnetic Four-Potential]], so its diagonal components vanish and opposite off-diagonal components have opposite signs.

Different inertial observers decompose the same tensor into different electric and magnetic three-vectors. The tensor itself transforms covariantly, making [[Electric and Magnetic Field Unification]] explicit rather than treating the two fields as independent objects.

Contracting the tensor with [[Four-Velocity]] and electric charge produces the covariant [[Lorentz Force Law]]. Taking its spacetime divergence yields the sourced half of [[Maxwell's Equations]], while applying the corresponding operation to its dual yields the source-free half.
