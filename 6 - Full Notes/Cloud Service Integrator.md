# Cloud Service Integrator

A cloud service integrator coordinates a portfolio of services so that it meets business needs consistently and efficiently. The role joins services across providers and connects those services to the organizational units that consume them.

The integrator may also consolidate contracts, reporting, and billing. Unlike a systems integrator focused on constructing a technical system, this role manages the continuing relationship and interoperability among delivered services.
