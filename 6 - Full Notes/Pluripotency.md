# Pluripotency

Pluripotency is the capacity of a stem cell to differentiate into many cell types of the body. The property provides a flexible starting material for biofabrication when the desired mature cell is difficult to harvest or expand directly.

Pluripotency is potential, not finished function; a controlled sequence of biochemical and physical cues must guide maturation into the target lineage.
