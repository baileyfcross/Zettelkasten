# Multi-Horizon Game Task List

A multi-horizon task list separates immediate actions from short-, medium-, and long-range work. The distinction lets a team preserve future needs without allowing distant speculation to crowd out the next testable step.

Near-term tasks should be concrete enough to assign and complete. Longer-range items can remain less detailed until evidence from intervening prototypes makes their requirements clearer.
