# Computing Education for Wisdom

Computing education for wisdom prepares learners to deliberate about what should be automated, who bears risks, how jobs and institutions change, and how digital systems affect society. Technical competence alone cannot answer these questions.

Curricula need interdisciplinary and intergenerational conversations alongside algorithms and software construction. Their purpose is not to force computational solutions, but to develop judgment, care, and shared understanding about technology’s consequences.
