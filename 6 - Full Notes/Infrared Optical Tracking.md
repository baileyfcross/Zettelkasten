# Infrared Optical Tracking

Infrared optical tracking uses cameras and controlled infrared illumination to observe active or reflective targets while suppressing much of the visible scene. Bright image regions can be detected as blobs and associated with known target points.

Two or more calibrated cameras provide rays whose intersection estimates a point in three dimensions. A rigid arrangement of several points then establishes an object's pose relative to the global tracking frame.
