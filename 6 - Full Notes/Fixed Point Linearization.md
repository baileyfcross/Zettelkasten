# Fixed Point Linearization

Fixed point linearization approximates a nonlinear dynamical system near an equilibrium by retaining only first-order deviations. The derivatives of the system's evolution functions form a Jacobian matrix evaluated at the fixed point.

The Jacobian's eigenvalues determine the local behavior: negative real parts imply decay toward the point, positive real parts imply growth away from it, and imaginary parts introduce rotation or oscillation. Zero real parts can make the linear approximation inconclusive.
