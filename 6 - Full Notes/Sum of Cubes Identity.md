# Sum of Cubes Identity

The sum of cubes identity factors two cubes as

$$a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2).$$

The sign in the linear factor matches the sum, while the middle term of the quadratic factor has the opposite sign. Expansion verifies that the mixed cubic terms cancel.

The identity applies whenever both terms are recognizable cubes, including coefficients and variable powers. It can be combined with other methods; for example, (x^6-1) first becomes a difference of squares and then its cubic factors can be decomposed separately.
