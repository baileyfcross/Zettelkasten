# Crisis Intent Feature

A crisis intent feature represents what a social-media message is trying to communicate during an emergency, such as requesting help, reporting conditions, offering resources, or expressing reaction. Lexical, entity, and contextual cues support the distinction.

Intent is more actionable than topic alone because responders need to know the message's function. Ambiguity, rapidly changing vocabulary, and scarce labels make domain adaptation important.
