# Live Support Team

A live support team protects a deployed game's availability and integrity by responding to crashes, exploits, degraded metrics, and release failures. It monitors signals, coordinates incidents, and restores a safe service state.

Support knowledge feeds development through incident learning, test additions, and pipeline improvements. Treating support as a separate cleanup function prevents the product teams from improving the conditions that create failures.
