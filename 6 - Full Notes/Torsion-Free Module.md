# Torsion-Free Module

A torsion-free module has no nonzero element annihilated by a non-zero-divisor of the ring. Over an [[Integral Domain]], rm = 0 with r and m nonzero cannot occur.

For a module over a Noetherian integral domain, torsion-freeness corresponds to the zero ideal being its only [[Associated Prime]]. It is weaker than freeness but preserves cancellation by nonzero scalars.
