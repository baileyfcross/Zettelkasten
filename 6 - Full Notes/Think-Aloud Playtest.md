# Think-Aloud Playtest

A think-aloud playtest asks the participant to narrate goals, interpretations, feelings, and reasoning while playing. The method exposes why an action occurs, distinguishing similar visible behavior driven by different mental models.

Challenge naturally makes players stop talking, so the facilitator uses brief neutral prompts such as asking what the player is trying to do. The prompt should restore narration without suggesting the intended solution or answering the player's question.
