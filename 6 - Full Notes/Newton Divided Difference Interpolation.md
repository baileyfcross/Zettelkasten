# Newton Divided Difference Interpolation

Newton divided-difference interpolation represents a polynomial incrementally using products $(x-x_0)\cdots(x-x_{k-1})$ and coefficients from [[Divided Difference|divided differences]].

Its main advantage over the Lagrange form is extensibility: adding one new data point adds one new term rather than forcing a complete recalculation.
