# Interlayer Edge

An interlayer edge connects nodes belonging to different network layers. It may identify the same entity across contexts, represent a dependency, or express an interaction between different systems.

Interlayer meaning cannot be inferred from its existence alone. Dependency edges propagate failure differently from ordinary interaction edges, while identity edges couple copies of one node through time or relationship layers.
