# Designer-Based Balancing

Designer-based balancing uses the designer's learned heuristics and intuition to judge whether relationships and progression feel appropriate. It is especially useful early, before enough playable material or behavioral data exists.

Intuition can guide promising hypotheses but is unreliable as sole evidence. Competing intuitions are better resolved through a fast [[Prototype]], mathematical model, or [[Playtesting|playtest]] than through prolonged argument.
