# Data Veracity in Law

Data veracity in law is the reliability and truthfulness required when information supports a legal determination or automated decision. More records do not compensate for uncertainty about their accuracy, provenance, meaning, or completeness.

Veracity is especially important when an analysis is presented as evidence. Decision makers need to know how observations were produced and whether the analytical conclusion remains justified under the applicable evidentiary standard.
