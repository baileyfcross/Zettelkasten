# Technology Adoption S-Curves

A technology adoption curve often begins with accelerating growth, reaches an inflection point, and slows as performance or market penetration approaches saturation. Plotted over time, this pattern resembles an S.

Business strategy must distinguish growth within the current curve from a jump to a new curve. Extrapolating early exponential growth indefinitely ignores physical, economic, and social limits.
