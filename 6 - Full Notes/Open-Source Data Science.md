# Open-Source Data Science

Open-source data science uses freely inspectable languages, packages, and workflows to perform analysis. Source visibility and a shared package ecosystem support learning, adaptation, peer review, and reproducibility.

The book treats R and its package community as a practical foundation for this approach while noting that dependency and version management remain part of responsible use.
