# Swarm Robotics

Swarm robotics coordinates multiple robots through simple local rules rather than a single commanding machine. Like insect or bird groups, the collective can cover an area, maintain spacing, relay information, or adapt when an individual unit fails.

Distributed coordination offers redundancy and parallel work, but it adds communication, collision, and emergent-behavior risks. Designers must understand what global pattern the local rules produce under loss, noise, or congestion. A swarm extends the feedback logic of [[Behavior-Based Robotics]] from one situated machine to a population sharing space and objectives.
