# Gaze Awareness

Gaze awareness is the ability of collaborators to understand where another participant is looking or directing attention. In person this is partly conveyed by head and eye orientation; mediated AR may need to preserve or explicitly visualize the cue.

Knowing a partner's focus helps resolve references such as “this part” and coordinates turn-taking around shared objects. Approximate gaze can be useful, but the interface should not imply more measurement precision than the tracker provides.
