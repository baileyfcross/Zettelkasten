# Data Protection in AI

Data protection in AI is the set of rights and controls governing how personal data are collected, stored, processed, accessed, corrected, and erased. It treats privacy as an enforceable relationship between a data subject and the organization using the data.

Automated decision making adds a need for meaningful information about the logic and likely consequences of processing. Erasure, access, and objection rights can restore some control, while [[Privacy by Design]] builds protection into the system rather than depending only on later complaints. Legal compliance remains a minimum; an allowed use can still be manipulative or unjust.
