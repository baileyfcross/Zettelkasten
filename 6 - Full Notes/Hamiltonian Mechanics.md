# Hamiltonian Mechanics

Hamiltonian mechanics represents a system using generalized coordinates and their conjugate momenta as independent phase-space variables. A Legendre transformation converts the Lagrangian into the Hamiltonian.

Hamilton's equations give paired first-order evolution equations for each coordinate and momentum. For many conservative systems the Hamiltonian equals total energy, and its phase-space formulation provides a direct bridge to statistical and quantum mechanics.
