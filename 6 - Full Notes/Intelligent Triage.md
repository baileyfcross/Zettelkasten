# Intelligent Triage

Intelligent triage uses data and predictive models to help prioritize or route patients through care processes. Operational predictions can reduce avoidable waiting and overcrowding by anticipating task duration and queue conditions.

The model supports rather than replaces clinical triage. Medical urgency, safety, accessibility, and exceptional circumstances can outweigh the route with the shortest predicted time.
