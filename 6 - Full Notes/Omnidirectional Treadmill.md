# Omnidirectional Treadmill

An omnidirectional treadmill supports walking or running in any horizontal direction while keeping the user inside a bounded physical footprint. A virtual-reality system tracks the direction and pace of the body and maps them to movement through a much larger [[Virtual Environment]].

The device can make locomotion more bodily than a joystick or teleport command and can support forward, backward, and lateral travel. Harnesses, low-friction surfaces, or actively moving platforms impose their own gait and balance constraints, so the resulting movement is not automatically identical to ordinary walking.

Whole-body motion may improve the consistency between intended travel and muscular activity, but it does not reproduce every acceleration or vestibular cue. An omnidirectional treadmill can therefore reduce some locomotion mismatches without guaranteeing freedom from [[Cybersickness]].
