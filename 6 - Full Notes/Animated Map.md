# Animated Map

An animated map presents successive spatial states as frames so movement and change can be perceived over time. Effectiveness depends on similarity between consecutive frames: smooth enough transitions support object continuity, while abrupt unrelated frames are difficult to integrate into one story. Stable extent, color scale, and geographic context are essential. A [[Static Small-Multiple Complement]] preserves positions that transient motion makes hard to compare.
