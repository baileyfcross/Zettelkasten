# Fuel Cell Activation Polarization

Activation polarization is voltage lost while an electrode reaction overcomes its electron-transfer energy barrier. A larger driving potential is needed to make current flow at the desired rate. The source describes reducing this loss with better catalysts, more effective electrode reaction area, or a higher operating temperature. It differs from resistance loss in the bulk path and from reactant shortage at high current.
