# Information Gain

Information gain is the entropy of a parent node minus the weighted entropy of the child nodes produced by an attribute split. It is the expected reduction in uncertainty gained by knowing that attribute's value.

ID3 chooses the feature with the largest gain. This often reduces later branching but does not guarantee the globally smallest tree.
