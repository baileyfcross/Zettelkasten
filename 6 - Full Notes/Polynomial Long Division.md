# Polynomial Long Division

Polynomial long division divides a polynomial by a multi-term polynomial through repeated leading-term cancellation. Arrange both expressions by descending exponent, divide the leading dividend term by the leading divisor term, place that result in the quotient, multiply it by the divisor, subtract, and repeat.

The process ends when the remainder has lower [[Polynomial Degree]] than the divisor. The result has the form

$$\text{dividend}=(\text{divisor})(\text{quotient})+\text{remainder}.$$

Missing powers should be treated as zero-coefficient placeholders so columns remain aligned.
