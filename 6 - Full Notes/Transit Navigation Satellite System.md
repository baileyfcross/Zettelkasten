# Transit Navigation Satellite System

Transit was a US Navy satellite-navigation system developed to update the inertial positions of ballistic-missile submarines. Its polar-orbiting satellites broadcast stable frequencies and orbital data; a receiver inferred location from the Doppler curve during a pass. Transit supplied latitude and longitude rather than continuous three-dimensional navigation, yet its long operational life proved that a passive space-based service could support both military and unanticipated civilian users.
