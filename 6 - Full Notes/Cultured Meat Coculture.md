# Cultured Meat Coculture

Cultured meat coculture grows multiple cell types to reproduce the composition of animal tissue. Muscle, fat, connective-tissue, smooth-muscle, and endothelial cells contribute different structural, nutritional, and sensory properties.

The cells may also release signals that suppress one another's maturation, so separate culture followed by assembly can be preferable to simultaneous growth.
