# UML Lifeline

A UML lifeline represents a participant in an interaction, such as an object, interface, controller, or external system. Its header names the participant, and a vertical dashed line extends downward through the sequence.

Messages connect lifelines at the points where participants communicate. A lifeline can begin later when created during the scenario or end when a destroy message terminates it.
