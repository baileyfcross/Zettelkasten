# Human Accountability in AI DevOps

Human accountability in AI DevOps means production responsibility never transfers to a model. AI may propose a command, configuration, risk assessment, or remediation, but an accountable engineer remains responsible for validating and approving consequential work.

The operating model is: let AI assist, let humans decide, let deterministic automation execute, and validate the result. Autonomous self-approval increases blast radius because a plausible error can move through environments before anyone notices. Explicit approval gates, audit trails, and named ownership preserve the ability to explain who authorized an action and why.
