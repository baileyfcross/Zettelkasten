# Rational Exponent

A rational exponent connects powers and roots: $a^{m/n}=(\sqrt[n]{a})^m$ when the real root is defined. The denominator selects the root and the numerator selects the power. These definitions extend the [[Exponent Laws]], but even roots impose nonnegative-radicand restrictions when calculations remain in the real numbers.
