# Two-Tank Thermal Storage

Two-tank solar storage keeps hot and cold heat-transfer fluid in separate insulated reservoirs. Charging pumps fluid from the cold tank through a receiver into the hot tank; discharge draws hot fluid through a steam generator and returns it cool. The source lists molten salt or oil as candidate media. Liquid level and temperature difference set stored sensible heat, while pumps and piping add losses.
