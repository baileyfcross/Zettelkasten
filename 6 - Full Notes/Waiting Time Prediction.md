# Waiting Time Prediction

Waiting time prediction estimates how long a person will remain in a queue before service begins. The hospital approach sums predicted treatment durations for patients ahead while accounting for the current task state.

Uncertainty grows with queue length because individual duration errors accumulate, arrivals and priorities change, and real operations do not follow a fixed schedule exactly.
