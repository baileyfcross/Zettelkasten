# Row Proportion

A row proportion divides a cell in a [[Contingency Table]] by the total for its row. It answers questions framed within the row category, such as what proportion of veterans report asthma.

Row and column proportions are not interchangeable. The grouping variable that supplies the denominator should follow the scientific question, and the table's orientation must be documented so readers do not reverse the conditional comparison.
