# COMSOL Probe Time Histories

A probe records a field or expression at a chosen location or region as a transient solution advances. Temperature probes reveal delays, peaks, equalization, and approach to steady behavior without requiring the full field to be inspected at every time.

Probe placement determines what the history means. Using points on a source, inside a material, and at an exposed surface can separate local heating from delayed response, as in the [[Heated Seat Transient Thermal Model|heated-seat case]].
