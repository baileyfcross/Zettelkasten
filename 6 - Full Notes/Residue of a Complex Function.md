# Residue of a Complex Function

The residue of a complex function at an isolated singularity is the coefficient of $(z-z_0)^{-1}$ in its Laurent expansion about that point. It is the part of the local expansion that contributes to a closed contour integral.

At a simple pole, the residue can often be found by multiplying by $z-z_0$ and taking the limit as $z$ approaches the pole. Higher-order poles require derivatives or an equivalent series expansion.
