# Krull Dimension

The Krull dimension of a ring is the supremum of the lengths of chains of distinct [[Prime Ideal]]s. It measures how many strict prime specializations the spectrum can contain.

The [[Prime Ideal Height]] measures chains below one prime, while a module's dimension is the dimension of the ring modulo its annihilator. The [[Hilbert-Samuel Polynomial]] gives another description of dimension in a Noetherian local setting.
