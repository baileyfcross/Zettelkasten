# Story as Exertion

Story as exertion makes progress through a narrative depend on the player's work within a game system. Quests, puzzles, or combat become the means by which the next authored event is reached.

Effort can strengthen attachment because the player has helped move the story forward. It can also turn play into a chore when repetitive or frustrating tasks become barriers and the story reward is more compelling than the process used to reach it.
