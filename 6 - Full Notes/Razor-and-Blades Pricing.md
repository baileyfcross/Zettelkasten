# Razor-and-Blades Pricing

Razor-and-blades pricing discounts or gives away a base component and earns revenue from the recurring service or consumable required to make it useful. A connected device can be inexpensive while its cloud storage, analysis, or monitoring carries the ongoing charge.

The model shifts attention from the acquisition price to the total dependency between components. A consumer should evaluate the continuing service price and the consequences of switching before treating the subsidized component as inexpensive.
