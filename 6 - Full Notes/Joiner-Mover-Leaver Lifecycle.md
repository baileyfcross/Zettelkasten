# Joiner-Mover-Leaver Lifecycle

The joiner-mover-leaver lifecycle models how access should change as a person enters an organization, changes responsibilities, and departs. Joiner processes establish identity and birthright access; mover processes add newly required access while removing obsolete privileges; leaver processes disable entry points, revoke sessions, remove assignments, and preserve required records.

The mover stage is easy to neglect because the person remains employed, yet accumulated access across several roles is a major least-privilege failure. Lifecycle design should use authoritative human-resources attributes, explicit effective dates, approvals for non-birthright entitlements, and auditable removal. [[Microsoft Entra Lifecycle Workflow|Lifecycle Workflows]], entitlement policies, and access reviews address different parts of this continuous process.
