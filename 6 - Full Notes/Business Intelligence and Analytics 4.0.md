# Business Intelligence and Analytics 4.0

Business intelligence and analytics 4.0 is an ultra-intelligent stage of organizational analytics intended to support or make optimal decisions automatically. It draws on large, diverse data resources and evaluates results with sophisticated quality measures.

Automation does not remove the need for domain understanding. The analytical system must connect its objective, input data, learning method, and evaluation to the business decision it is supposed to improve.
