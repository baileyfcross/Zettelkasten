# Surface-to-Surface Radiation

Surface-to-surface radiation models exchange among boundaries separated by a transparent or partially participating medium. Each surface emits according to its temperature and emissivity and receives energy from other surfaces according to their [[Radiative View Factor|view factors]].

The model must distinguish exterior surfaces facing an ambient from enclosed surfaces that see one another. Opacity, diffuse or specular behavior, wavelength dependence, and shadowing determine whether a simple ambient-radiation condition is adequate or full enclosure exchange is needed.
