# Computational Fluency

Computational fluency combines practical capability with concepts and skills for expressing ideas through information technology. It goes beyond operating applications toward adapting tools, understanding systems, and creating computational artifacts.

The fluency movement offered a richer educational goal than literacy but did not by itself produce universal computing courses. Its concepts nevertheless helped prepare the later computational-thinking movement.
