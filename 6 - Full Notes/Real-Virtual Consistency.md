# Real-Virtual Consistency

Real-virtual consistency is the requirement that software state remain compatible with the physical objects and processes it represents. A real object may move, appear, disappear, or be changed by a person outside the application's control.

The AR system must sense relevant changes and update virtual behavior without violating expected physical relationships. This consistency extends beyond appearance to interaction, semantics, timing, and the consequences of acting on real objects.
