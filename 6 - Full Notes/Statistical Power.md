# Statistical Power

Statistical power is the probability that a study will reject a false null hypothesis under a specified effect, sample size, significance level, and analysis design. Higher power reduces the chance of missing an effect of the assumed magnitude.

Power is conditional rather than an intrinsic property of a dataset. Changing the test, outcome prevalence, allocation ratio, or anticipated [[Effect Size]] changes the sample size required to achieve a target such as 80 percent.
