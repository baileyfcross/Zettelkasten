# Year-Panel Comparison

A year-panel comparison gives each year its own panel while holding the graphical mapping constant. The layout reveals how a relationship or distribution changes across years without the crossings produced by a single long [[Temporal Polyline]]. Shared scales preserve positional comparability. The method sacrifices a continuous trajectory between years, so it is most useful as a complement to an overall path or [[Animated Bubble Chart]].
