# Space-Time Horizon Graph

A space-time horizon graph gives each aggregated geographic zone a compact temporal strip and encodes deviations from a reference through layered color bands. It allows many zonal series to be aligned vertically, revealing when and where values depart from the local average. The technique depends on [[Spatial Aggregation by Zone]] and sacrifices exact magnitudes and within-zone geography for a dense comparison of temporal patterns.
