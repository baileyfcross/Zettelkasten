# Time-Series Feature Representation

Time-series feature representation turns an ordered sequence of observations into variables suitable for comparison, prediction, or classification. It may summarize an entire series, describe selected intervals, or record the presence of local patterns.

No single representation preserves every useful aspect of temporal data. Global summaries capture overall behavior, while shapelets and pattern dictionaries retain localized structure that aggregate statistics can miss.
