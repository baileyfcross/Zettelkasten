# Wave Reflection and Transmission

When a wave reaches a discontinuity between two media, the solution is divided into incident, reflected, and transmitted parts. Continuity of displacement and the appropriate derivative or force condition at the boundary determine their amplitudes.

Reflection and transmission coefficients compare energy fluxes, not merely squared displacement amplitudes when the two media have different wave speeds or wavenumbers. Conservation requires the reflected and transmitted fractions to sum to the incident fraction in the lossless case.
