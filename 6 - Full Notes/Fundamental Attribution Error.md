# Fundamental Attribution Error

The fundamental attribution error is the tendency to explain another person's behavior through enduring traits while explaining one's own comparable behavior through circumstances. A player may call an opponent careless but blame their own mistake on lag, unclear feedback, or bad luck.

This asymmetry affects multiplayer judgment and playtest interpretation. Knowing the bias exists does not reliably remove it, so designers should prefer recorded behavior and multiple perspectives over a single participant's causal story.
