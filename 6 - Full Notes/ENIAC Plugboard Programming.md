# ENIAC Plugboard Programming

ENIAC was programmed by wiring its functional units together with cables and setting switches for each problem. Reconfiguring the plugboards could take days even when the resulting computation ran in minutes.

The method matched electronic calculation speed without requiring an unavailable high-speed program memory. Its inflexibility made the need for internally stored instructions unmistakable.
