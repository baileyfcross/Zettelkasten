# Soft-Body Cloth Collision

A pliable object should deform as forces act on it rather than behave like an unyielding fragment. The book demonstrates this by holding the top edge of a textured plane and animating a sphere into and away from it as a collider. The cloth simulation supplies the resulting bend and rebound without hand-keyframing every fold. Soft-body dynamics suit flags, fabric, and other compliant forms; complex liquid or gas interaction calls for [[Fluid Simulation of Splashes and Fire]].
