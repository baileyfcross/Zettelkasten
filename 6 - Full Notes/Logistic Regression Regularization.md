# Logistic Regression Regularization

Logistic regression regularization adds a coefficient penalty to the likelihood objective. The penalty discourages large weights and stabilizes estimates when predictors are numerous, correlated, or weakly supported by the training set.

Quadratic penalties shrink coefficients smoothly, while absolute-value penalties can set some coefficients to zero and perform embedded feature selection. The regularization strength balances training fit against a simpler model expected to generalize better.
