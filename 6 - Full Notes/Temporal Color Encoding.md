# Temporal Color Encoding

Temporal color encoding maps observation time or a time-derived category to color. In a [[Time-Series Scatterplot Matrix]], it can reveal whether an apparent relationship belongs to a season, year, or transition. Ordered periods call for an ordered palette, while recurring categories such as months may require a cyclic or carefully arranged palette. Color supplements position but should not carry distinctions too numerous or subtle to discriminate reliably.
