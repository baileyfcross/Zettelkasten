# Portable Game Platform

A portable game platform is designed for play while being carried, including dedicated handheld systems and mobile devices. Convenience, short access time, built-in controls or touch input, battery life, and a small display shape the experience.

Portable games often trade complexity for immediacy. Interruptions and changing surroundings favor readable interfaces, resumable sessions, and interactions that remain usable without a living-room setup.
