# Autonomous Photovoltaic Plant

An autonomous photovoltaic plant serves a local network without relying on a central grid as its main supply. The source's layout combines a [[Solar Array]], storage battery, charge controller, inverter, and standby diesel generator. Solar generation, battery discharge, and backup generation must together cover load and internal losses. Remote homes, pumps, and telecommunications sites are example applications.
