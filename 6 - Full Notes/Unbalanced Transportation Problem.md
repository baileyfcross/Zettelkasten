# Unbalanced Transportation Problem

A transportation problem is unbalanced when total supply differs from total demand. If supply is greater, a dummy destination absorbs the excess; if demand is greater, a dummy source supplies the shortfall.

The dummy row or column normally has zero unit shipping cost and converts the table to a [[Balanced Transportation Problem]]. Its allocations represent unused capacity or unmet supply supplied by the modeling device, so their business meaning must be interpreted explicitly.
