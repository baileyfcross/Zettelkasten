# Right to Explanation

The right to explanation is a claimed entitlement to meaningful information about an automated decision that affects a person. Its purpose is to make hidden judgment contestable by showing the logic, evidence, and anticipated consequences relevant to the decision.

The scope of the right is disputed. General information about a model does not necessarily explain an individual outcome, while a full technical account may be incomprehensible. A useful right must specify who owes the explanation, what level of detail the affected person needs, and what process allows correction or appeal. It therefore links [[Explainable AI]] with [[Algorithmic Due Process]].
