# Combined-Cycle Power Plant

A combined-cycle plant uses a hot gas turbine as the first power stage and recovers its exhaust heat to raise steam for a second turbine. Electricity is produced in both stages from a wider temperature range than the gas turbine alone uses. The source's arrangement treats the steam plant as a bottoming cycle; its extra equipment is justified by additional fuel-energy utilization.
