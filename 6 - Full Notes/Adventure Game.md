# Adventure Game

An adventure game is an interactive story in which the player usually takes the protagonist's role and advances by exploration, dialogue, and conceptual puzzle solving. Physical coordination challenges are limited or absent.

The genre depends on meaningful narrative context and intelligible problems rather than defeating an opponent through reflex. Its interface should help the player reason about possibilities without reducing puzzles to accidental guessing.
