# HPStream

HPStream combines fading cluster summaries with projected clustering for high-dimensional streams. Each cluster maintains a centroid, temporal weight, and a set of dimensions considered relevant to its current members.

By updating both summaries and projected dimensions, the method can follow groups that evolve in location and subspace. Its forgetting rate and dimensionality controls determine the balance between responsiveness and stability.
