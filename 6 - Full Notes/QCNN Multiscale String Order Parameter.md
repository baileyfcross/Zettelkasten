# QCNN Multiscale String Order Parameter

The exact phase-recognition QCNN can be viewed in the Heisenberg picture as measuring a multiscale string order parameter. When its convolution-pooling layers are commuted through the final local observable, the resulting operator becomes a weighted sum of products of exponentially many ordinary string operators.

This aggregate observable remains sharp closer to the phase boundary than a single conventional string order parameter. At depth (d), its support can probe correlations over a length on the order of (3^d), matching the circuit's repeated three-to-one coarse graining.

The sharper output reduces the number of copies needed to distinguish the topological phase from the neighboring phase. In the numerically accessible regime, the paper reports that the improvement in sample complexity over individual string-order measurements grows exponentially with QCNN depth, subject to finite-size effects near criticality.
