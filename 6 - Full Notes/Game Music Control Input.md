# Game Music Control Input

A game music control input is a message or parameter from gameplay that causes an interactive score to change. Inputs can represent discrete events, such as entering combat, or continuously varying conditions, such as a character's speed or level of danger.

Designing the input requires two coordinated decisions: what game information is musically meaningful, and what the music should do when that information changes. The mapping may switch cues through [[Horizontal Resequencing]], alter layers through [[Vertical Remixing]], or drive a continuous property through [[Real-Time Game Music Tempo Control]].
