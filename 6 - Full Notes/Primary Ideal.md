# Primary Ideal

A proper ideal q is primary when xy ∈ q and x ∉ q imply that some positive power of y belongs to q. Equivalently, every [[Zero Divisor]] in R/q is [[Nilpotent Element|nilpotent]].

The radical √q of a primary ideal is a [[Prime Ideal]]. Primary ideals act like prime powers in a decomposition, retaining multiplicity information that a radical alone discards.
