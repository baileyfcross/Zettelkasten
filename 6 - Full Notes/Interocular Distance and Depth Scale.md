# Interocular Distance and Depth Scale

The horizontal distance between the cameras of a stereo pair is its interocular distance. Increasing or decreasing that separation changes the amount of parallax and thus the apparent depth scale, even when the 3D geometry stays the same. The book notes that nearby subjects show stronger differences than remote ones at a human-like separation and that deliberately unnatural camera spacing can exaggerate depth or create a miniaturized impression. Camera spacing should be chosen with the shot's composition and [[Stereo Window Placement]] in mind.
