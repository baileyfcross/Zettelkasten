# Thermal Equilibrium and Temperature Difference

A temperature difference is the driving force for conduction, convection, and radiation. Net transfer diminishes as temperatures equalize, and thermal equilibrium is reached when no temperature difference remains to sustain spontaneous heat flow.

Thermal equilibrium alone is narrower than thermodynamic equilibrium. The broader condition also requires mechanical and chemical equilibrium, so no unbalanced internal force, reaction, or matter redistribution remains alongside the absence of net heat transfer.
