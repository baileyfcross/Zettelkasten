# Task Codification and Automation

A task becomes easier to automate as its steps, inputs, exceptions, and success conditions become understood and codified. People initially handle new or variable work because they can improvise, but repeated analysis can turn parts of that work into a stable procedure for a machine.

Automation therefore acts on tasks before it necessarily eliminates occupations. A job may be reorganized around the activities that remain difficult to specify, while routine middle-skill work is especially exposed. This mechanism connects technical feasibility to [[AI Job Displacement]] without assuming a one-for-one replacement of worker by robot.
