# N-Type Semiconductor

An n-type semiconductor has donor impurity levels close to the conduction band. Electrons can be promoted from these levels into available conduction states at ordinary temperatures, making electrons its main mobile carriers. Paired with a [[P-Type Semiconductor]], it forms a [[P-N Junction]] whose field can separate light-generated carriers. The impurity amount affects conductivity.
