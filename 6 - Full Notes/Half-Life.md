# Half-Life

Half-life is the time required for the number of undecayed radioactive nuclei, or their activity, to fall to half its initial value. For decay constant $\lambda$, $t_{1/2}=\ln2/\lambda$. Each successive half-life removes half of what remains, producing exponential rather than linear decline.

In the student companion's R treatment, substituting the half-life relation into the decay equation provides both a calculated value and a graphical checkpoint. Repeated half-lives create the sequence one-half, one-quarter, one-eighth, making the multiplicative pattern visible.
