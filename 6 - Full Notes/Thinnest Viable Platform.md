# Thinnest Viable Platform

A thinnest viable platform is the smallest platform capability that is good enough to solve a real internal user problem and test a product hypothesis. It adapts the minimum viable product idea to an internal market: the platform team is the product team, employees are the users, and the proposed capability is valuable only if it changes their work for the better.

The team selects a narrow use case with meaningful impact, reasonable confidence, and feasible effort, then exposes early prototypes to power users and expands to early adopters once the path works. A build-measure-learn loop makes the TVP a vehicle for evidence rather than an excuse for a permanently incomplete platform.
