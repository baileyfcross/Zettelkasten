# Display Fidelity

Display fidelity is the degree to which sensory stimuli produced by a display correspond to those that would be present in the physical world.

It is not one score. Visual components include [[Field of View]], [[Field of Regard]], stereoscopic quality, [[Refresh Rate]], [[Spatial Resolution]], color quality, and [[Latency]]. Auditory fidelity includes sound quality, spatiality, and delay, while haptic fidelity includes resolution, delay, and the quality of force or touch.

One display has strictly higher fidelity than another only when all relevant components are at least as strong. More often, displays trade one component for another, so the task determines which profile is preferable.
