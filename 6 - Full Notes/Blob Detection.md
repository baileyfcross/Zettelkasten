# Blob Detection

Blob detection identifies connected image regions that differ from their surroundings according to intensity or another selected property. In infrared tracking, thresholding isolates bright reflections and each region's centroid supplies a two-dimensional target observation.

Size, shape, and brightness tests can reject implausible regions. Detection alone does not identify a three-dimensional point; observations must still be matched across cameras and triangulated.
