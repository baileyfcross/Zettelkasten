# Least Squares Plane

A least squares plane fits observations $(x_i,y_i,z_i)$ with a model such as $z=a+bx+cy$. Its coefficients minimize the sum of squared vertical residuals.

Placing columns of ones, $x$ values, and $y$ values into a matrix turns the problem into [[Linear Least Squares]], so the same normal-equation or factorization methods apply.
