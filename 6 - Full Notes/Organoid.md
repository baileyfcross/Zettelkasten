# Organoid

An organoid is a three-dimensional tissue model produced when stem cells differentiate and self-organize into structures that reproduce selected features of an organ. Developmental programs create architecture rather than a printer placing every cell directly.

Organoids capture useful multicellular behavior but vary between samples and remain incomplete representations of mature whole organs.
