# Euclidean Axiomatic Geometry

Euclidean geometry derives the properties of plane figures from definitions, common notions, postulates, and previously proven propositions. A proof makes each construction and inference explicit rather than relying only on a diagram's appearance. The parallel postulate distinguishes this geometry from spherical and other non-Euclidean geometries.
