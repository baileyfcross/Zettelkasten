# Game Lore

Game lore is optional story material that fills in the background of a game's world, characters, and history. It commonly appears through readable objects, conversations, artifacts, and environmental details discovered during exploration.

Lore supports the main narrative by adding flavor and context rather than necessarily advancing the plot. If understanding it becomes essential to the central story, treating it as entirely optional can make important meaning easy to miss.
