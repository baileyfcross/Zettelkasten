# Boolean Algebra for Circuit Design

Boolean algebra represents true-false variables combined by operations such as AND, OR, and NOT. Claude Shannon showed that the same expressions could describe switching circuits, making logic an effective design language for digital hardware.

The abstraction supports simplification and verification of circuit functions. Physical propagation delays can still create [[Race Condition|race conditions]], so a correct Boolean formula alone does not guarantee reliable hardware behavior.
