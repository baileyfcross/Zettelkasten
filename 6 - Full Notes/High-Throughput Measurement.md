# High-Throughput Measurement

High-throughput measurement produces thousands or millions of related features for each biological sample. Microarrays, RNA sequencing, mass spectrometry, and similar technologies transform a small collection of specimens into a high-dimensional dataset.

The scale creates opportunities for joint exploration and information sharing, but it also makes one-test-at-a-time interpretation unreliable. Multiplicity, dependence, quality variation, and technical batch structure become central parts of the analysis.
