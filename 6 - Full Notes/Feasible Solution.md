# Feasible Solution

A feasible solution satisfies every constraint and sign restriction of an optimization problem. The collection of all such points is the feasible region.

Feasibility is separate from quality: a feasible point need not be an [[Optimal Solution]]. If no point satisfies all constraints, the model is infeasible.
