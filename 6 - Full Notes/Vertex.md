# Vertex

A vertex is one of the objects represented in a [[Graph]]. It is also called a node. What it stands for depends on the model: a landmass, person, web page, task, or fragment of a biological sequence can all become vertices.

An [[Edge]] records a selected relationship between two vertices. The number of edges adjacent to a vertex is its [[Vertex Degree]], while direction and weight can add further meaning to the connection.
