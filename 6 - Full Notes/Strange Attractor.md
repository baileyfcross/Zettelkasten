# Strange Attractor

A strange attractor is a bounded invariant set toward which nearby trajectories evolve while continuing to display aperiodic, sensitive dynamics. Unlike a fixed point or limit cycle, it has intricate structure rather than an ordinary smooth geometric shape.

Trajectories on the attractor separate rapidly when begun from nearby initial states even though they remain confined to the attracting region. Its geometry is often characterized by a noninteger fractal dimension.
