# EHR Pharmacovigilance

EHR pharmacovigilance searches longitudinal clinical records for adverse outcomes associated with medication exposure. Orders, diagnoses, laboratory results, and notes can provide timing and patient context that spontaneous reports often lack.

The record was not created as a randomized safety study. Indication, disease severity, co-treatment, and uneven follow-up can confound an association, while an order may not prove that a drug was taken. Temporal design and careful phenotype definitions are essential before comparing exposed and unexposed patients.
