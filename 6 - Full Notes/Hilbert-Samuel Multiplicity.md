# Hilbert-Samuel Multiplicity

The Hilbert-Samuel multiplicity e(I,M) is the factorial-normalized leading coefficient of the [[Hilbert-Samuel Polynomial]] for ℓ(M/IⁿM). It measures the leading growth of lengths along powers of an ideal of definition.

For a local ring or module, this multiplicity refines dimension with quantitative information about the local algebra. It also appears in formulas for parametrized surfaces with base points.
