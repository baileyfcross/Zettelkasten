# Field Adjunction

Field adjunction constructs K(c) by adding an element c to K and closing under field operations. When c is algebraic with minimal polynomial p, K(c) is isomorphic to K[x]/⟨p(x)⟩.

Successive adjunctions produce fields written K(c₁,...,cₙ).
