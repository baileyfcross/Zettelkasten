# F-Distribution

The F-distribution is a positive, right-skewed probability family parameterized by two degrees of freedom. It arises from ratios of scaled variance estimates and supplies the reference distribution for many analysis-of-variance statistics.

The source also uses a scaled F model for gene-specific sample variances in an empirical Bayes hierarchy. This permits a shared distribution to stabilize many noisy variance estimates.
