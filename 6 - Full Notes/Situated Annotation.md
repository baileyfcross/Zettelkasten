# Situated Annotation

A situated annotation attaches explanatory information to the physical object, place, or feature it describes. Registration reduces the need to translate between a separate document and the observed environment.

The annotation may be text, graphics, or a link to additional content. Its placement must remain legible as viewpoint and occlusion change, and its visual relationship must make the intended referent unambiguous.
