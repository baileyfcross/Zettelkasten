# Clinical Report Analyzer

A clinical report analyzer processes the structure and content of a medical report to recover sections, observations, diagnoses, and other clinically useful information. It can use both the language and the document's reporting conventions.

Reports differ by specialty, institution, and author, so headings and layouts are not universally stable. The analyzer must handle free prose, lists, templates, and pseudo-tables while retaining the distinction between history, findings, and interpretation. That structure helps a later [[Clinical Context Extraction]] step decide what a mention means.
