# Geometric Dimension

Geometric dimension identifies the independent spatial directions retained by a model. A point is zero-dimensional, a line or interval is one-dimensional, a planar region is two-dimensional, and a volume is three-dimensional.

Reducing dimension can remove enormous computational cost when variation through an omitted direction is negligible or represented by a property such as thickness or cross-sectional area. The reduction is valid only if the governing physics and boundary conditions remain meaningful in the lower-dimensional representation.
