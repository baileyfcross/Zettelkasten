# Selenium Wheel Scrolling

Selenium wheel scrolling brings off-screen content into view or scrolls by a specified horizontal and vertical amount. A scroll origin can be the viewport, a located element, or an offset from an element.

Scrolling is useful for lazy-loaded and infinite content as well as elements outside the current viewport. The test should wait for content triggered by the scroll rather than assuming that pixel movement makes it immediately interactive.
