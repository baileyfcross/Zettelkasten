# Bidirectional Pairs in the MATLAB Unit Converter

The book's Enhanced Unit Converter app organizes its 1,316 bidirectional factors into topical major tabs and 44 minor sections. The user chooses a quantity category, then selects From and To units in separate pull-down lists. The app can therefore present a conversion in either direction within a supported pair instead of asking the user to construct an inverse ratio manually. This describes Pryor's MATLAB R2017b/R2018a-era app, not a general built-in MATLAB command. The underlying unit logic is explained in [[Reciprocal Conversion Factors]].
