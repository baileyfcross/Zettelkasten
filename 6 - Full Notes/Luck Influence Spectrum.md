# Luck Influence Spectrum

The luck influence spectrum classifies an activity as pure luck, luck dominant, luck influenced, or pure ability according to how strongly player decisions and skill predict outcomes. Pure luck gives agency little or no effect; luck-dominant play permits decisions without making individual results predictable; luck-influenced play rewards skill over many trials; pure-ability play has no built-in random mechanism.

The categories describe influence rather than the visible number of random events. Performance variation and uncertainty can make even an ability game unpredictable, while repeated low-impact random events can average out enough for skill to dominate long-run results.
