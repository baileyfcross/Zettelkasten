# Editing Versus Compositing in Blender

Editing arranges shots in sequence, changing their order and timing along a timeline. A nonlinear editor permits an earlier cut to be revised without rebuilding every later edit.

Compositing combines visual elements within an image or shot, often by stacking and processing them. A production commonly alternates between the two: editing determines temporal structure, while compositing creates or enhances each resulting image.
