# Cultural Aesthetics

Cultural aesthetics are player- and community-controlled sensory creations associated with a game, such as fan art, musical remixes, costume play, and other transformative visual or performative works.

Control distinguishes this category from authorized transmedia. A licensed film or product remains an artifact commissioned by the intellectual-property owner and forms its own inscribed work. Cultural aesthetics instead show players taking expressive ownership of game material and circulating new forms around it.
