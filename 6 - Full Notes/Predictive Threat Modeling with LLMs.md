# Predictive Threat Modeling with LLMs

Predictive threat modeling with LLMs combines historical incidents, current indicators, unstructured intelligence, and system context to propose plausible attack paths and emerging risks. It shifts some analysis from reacting to alerts toward preparing for likely threats.

The output is scenario generation rather than foreknowledge. Assumptions, data recency, and uncertainty should be explicit, and security teams should test the resulting hypotheses against architecture and observable evidence.
