# Second Law of Thermodynamics

The second law of thermodynamics gives a preferred direction to macroscopic change. Heat does not spontaneously flow from colder to hotter bodies, and no cyclic engine can convert all absorbed heat into work. Equivalently, the total [[Entropy]] of an isolated system does not decrease, explaining why many energy-conserving processes are irreversible.
