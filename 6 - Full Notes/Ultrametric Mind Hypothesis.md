# Ultrametric Mind Hypothesis

The ultrametric mind hypothesis treats nested classes of stimuli or concepts as a possible representation for some unconscious generalization. Inside one cluster, items are equivalent at that resolution; a higher branch groups several such classes into a broader one.

The book connects this structure to Matte Blanco's account of symmetric reasoning and suggests that hierarchical computation could make pattern matching efficient. This is an exploratory analogy and research proposal. Measuring an ultrametric tendency in text does not by itself prove that a particular brain process uses a tree-distance algorithm.
