# External-Internal Index

The external-internal index compares links crossing an attributed group boundary with links remaining inside the group. It subtracts internal ties from external ties and divides by their total, producing values from negative one to positive one.

Negative values indicate stronger within-group connection, while positive values indicate more outward connection. In a legislature, the grouping attribute can be party, seniority, or political position.
