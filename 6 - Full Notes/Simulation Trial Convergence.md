# Simulation Trial Convergence

Simulation trial convergence is the stabilization of summary results as the number of random trials increases. If averages or rates change substantially between otherwise identical runs, the simulation may not yet contain enough trials for a dependable estimate.

Convergence should be checked rather than assumed. A stable mean can also hide important distributional behavior, so trial counts need to be paired with inspection of spread, inputs, and rare outcomes.
