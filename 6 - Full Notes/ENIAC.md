# ENIAC

ENIAC was a general-purpose electronic numerical machine built by J. Presper Eckert and John Mauchly for United States Army firing-table calculations. Its roughly 18,000 vacuum tubes performed decimal arithmetic and storage at electronic speed.

The machine demonstrated that ambitious electronic computing could work reliably enough to solve real problems. Its difficult programming method motivated the [[Stored-Program Principle]].
