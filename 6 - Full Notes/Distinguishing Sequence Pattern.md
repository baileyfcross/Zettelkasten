# Distinguishing Sequence Pattern

A distinguishing sequence pattern occurs differently across classes or target groups and is selected for its discriminative signal. Its usefulness depends on contrast, not only high overall support.

Class-aware criteria can expose rare but diagnostic orderings that ordinary frequent-pattern mining would miss. Statistical reliability and redundancy control remain necessary when many candidates are evaluated.
