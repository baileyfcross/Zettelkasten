# Roblox Beam Effect

A Beam renders a texture between two attachments. Width, color, transparency, curve, segmentation, texture length, speed, and facing behavior determine whether the result looks like a laser, cable, trail, lightning arc, or other continuous effect.

Because its endpoints are attachments, a beam follows their movement and inherits the spatial logic of [[Roblox Attachments and Constraints]]. It differs from a [[Roblox Particle Emitter]] by maintaining a connected strip between two defined locations rather than emitting many independent particles into the world.
