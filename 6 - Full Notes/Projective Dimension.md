# Projective Dimension

The projective dimension of a module is the minimum length of a finite [[Projective Resolution]], or infinity when no finite resolution exists. A projective module has projective dimension zero.

Projective dimension measures how many layers of projective modules are needed to resolve the module. Over a Noetherian local ring, the [[Auslander-Buchsbaum Formula]] relates finite projective dimension to depth.
