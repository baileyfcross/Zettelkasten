# Mixture Distribution Clustering

Mixture distribution clustering represents the population density as a weighted sum of component densities. Each component corresponds to a latent group and contributes according to its mixture weight.

Learning estimates the component parameters and the probability that each observation arose from each group. Gaussian components are common for numerical data.
