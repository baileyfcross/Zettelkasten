# Nuclear Binding Energy

Nuclear binding energy is the work required to separate a nucleus into free protons and neutrons. The bound nucleus has less mass than those separated nucleons; the mass defect corresponds to energy through $E=mc^2$. Binding energy per nucleon compares stability and explains energy release from both fission of heavy nuclei and fusion of light nuclei.
