# Rule-Learning Tolerance

Rule-learning tolerance describes how willing a target player is to learn unfamiliar rules before and during play. It can range from refusal, through resistance and neutrality, to active acceptance or enthusiasm for learning systems.

This preference constrains how novel or complex a game can be, how much onboarding it needs, and whether familiarity with a genre or real-world activity can carry the early experience. High tolerance permits larger barriers to entry but does not guarantee long-term retention; some enthusiasts leave once they believe they have fully understood the rules.
