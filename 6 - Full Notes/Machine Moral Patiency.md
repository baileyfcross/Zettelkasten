# Machine Moral Patiency

Machine moral patiency is the possibility that an artificial system can be the object of moral duties or can itself be wronged. Capacity-based accounts look for properties such as sentience, suffering, consciousness, or interests. Present machines do not clearly establish these properties.

Other approaches do not wait for proof of an inner experience. [[Indirect Duties to Machines]] focus on how treatment of machines affects people and character, while [[Relational Moral Status]] examines the social relationship that forms around an artificial being. These approaches explain why machine treatment can matter even when machine consciousness remains uncertain.
