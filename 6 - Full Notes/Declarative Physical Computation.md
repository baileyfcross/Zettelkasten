# Declarative Physical Computation

Declarative physical computation states the scientific entities, functional relationships, parameters, and desired operation rather than burying the method inside a program's procedural implementation. An execution engine supplies the mechanics for evaluating or optimizing the declared model.

The separation makes the scientific specification inspectable and potentially implementable by another system without hidden domain conventions. Reusable functional forms and semantic data can also support [[Workflow Parameter Sweep|parameter sweeps]]. The approach depends on [[Computable Scientific Semantics]] and thorough validation; declarative syntax alone cannot remove ambiguity from undefined terms.
