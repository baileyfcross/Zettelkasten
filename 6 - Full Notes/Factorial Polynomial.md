# Factorial Polynomial

A falling factorial polynomial is

$$[x]^n=x(x-h)(x-2h)\cdots(x-(n-1)h).$$

It is adapted to finite-difference calculus because $\Delta[x]^n=nh[x]^{n-1}$, mirroring the derivative rule for $x^n$. Every ordinary polynomial can be expressed in factorial-polynomial form, where repeated differencing becomes especially simple.
