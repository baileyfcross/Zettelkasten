# Ongoing Responsibility Register

An ongoing responsibility register identifies work that must recur throughout development rather than finish once, such as maintaining builds, updating documentation, organizing tests, or checking asset integration. It names an owner without reducing the responsibility to a single checkbox.

Separating recurring duties from ordinary tasks prevents essential maintenance from disappearing each time the immediate task list is reprioritized.
