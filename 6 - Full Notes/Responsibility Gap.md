# Responsibility Gap

A responsibility gap appears when an AI system causes a morally significant outcome but no actor seems to satisfy the usual conditions for full responsibility. The machine may operate with some autonomy while lacking moral understanding, and the people involved may neither control nor foresee its particular action.

The gap is a governance problem, not proof that nobody should answer. It can be reduced by assigning duties before deployment, maintaining oversight, documenting the [[Data Science Pipeline]], and ensuring that organizations can investigate and remedy harm. Treating the system itself as responsible may instead widen the gap by shielding human decision makers.
