# Replication and Computational Reproducibility

Replication and computational reproducibility test different parts of a scientific claim. Replication independently implements the method, often with newly collected data, while computational reproducibility uses the original data and described procedures to regenerate the reported result.

The second test is narrower but diagnostically valuable: it checks whether the published computation is internally recoverable. Passing it does not replace independent replication, and failing it may reveal missing inputs, undocumented transformations, or a broken [[Result-to-Computation Traceability|result-to-computation link]].
