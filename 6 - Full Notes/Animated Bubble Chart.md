# Animated Bubble Chart

An animated bubble chart represents two variables with horizontal and vertical position, a third with bubble area, groups with color, and time through successive frames. Stable object identities let viewers follow movement rather than interpret every frame as a new scatterplot. An [[Animation Time Slider]] supplies temporal reference, while a static [[Temporal Polyline]] can preserve paths that motion alone makes difficult to remember.
