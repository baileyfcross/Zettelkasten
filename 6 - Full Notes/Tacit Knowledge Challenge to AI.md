# Tacit Knowledge Challenge to AI

The tacit knowledge challenge to AI holds that much intelligent behavior relies on practical know-how that people cannot fully state as explicit rules. Everyday action depends on recognizing what matters in a situation against a background of embodied experience and social practice.

This creates a problem for systems that assume intelligence can be captured by formal representations alone: every rule requires relevance conditions that may themselves need more rules. Data-driven systems can learn patterns without hand-coding every instruction, but learned correlation does not automatically provide human meaning or situated judgment. The challenge therefore remains relevant to both symbolic and statistical AI.
