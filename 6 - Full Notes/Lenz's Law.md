# Lenz's Law

Lenz's law states that an induced current flows in the direction whose magnetic effect opposes the change in flux that produced it. This opposition is the meaning of the negative sign in [[Faraday's Law]]. A reinforcing response would create energy without input, so the law is a consequence of energy conservation.
