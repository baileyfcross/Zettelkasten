# EHR Administrative Data

EHR administrative data describes the operational context of care: patient registration, admission, discharge, transfer, scheduling, billing, and insurance information. These fields connect clinical events to an encounter and make it possible to study utilization and costs as well as disease.

Administrative records are not neutral labels. They are collected to run and reimburse care, so their definitions and completeness reflect institutional workflows. An analysis should therefore distinguish an operational code from a direct clinical observation and verify that encounter boundaries match the question being asked.
