# Conscious Technical Debt

Conscious technical debt is an accepted shortcoming chosen after the team understands what problem is being solved, what is not yet needed, the cost to adopt and operate the choice, its maintenance demands, expected return, and the team’s ability to sustain it.

Acceptance includes a compensating plan rather than vague hope. The team records the constraint, decides how it will be monitored, and identifies the condition that triggers remediation or replacement. Collaborative assessment reduces the chance that one architect’s preference silently becomes long-term toil for the people operating the platform.
