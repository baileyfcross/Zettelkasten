# Commodore PET and 64

The Commodore PET was an early integrated personal computer using the MOS 6502, while the later Commodore 64 paired a related processor with strong graphics and sound at mass-market scale.

These machines show how standardized microprocessors supported differentiated systems through surrounding hardware and software. The Commodore 64 became one of the most widely used early home computers.
