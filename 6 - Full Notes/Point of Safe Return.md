# Point of Safe Return

The point of safe return is the farthest point an aircraft can reach outbound and still return to its departure point with the planned usable endurance. Groundspeeds differ on the outbound and inbound legs when wind is present, and reserve, contingency, alternate, and ground fuel are excluded before safe endurance is computed. Stronger wind reduces the reachable PSR distance even when it shortens one of the two legs.
