# NVIDIA L40S GPU

The NVIDIA L40S is an Ada Lovelace-generation GPU with 48 GB of GDDR6 in the configuration discussed by the source. It combines enterprise AI inference with graphics rendering, simulation, virtual desktop infrastructure, and mixed AI-visual workloads.

Its selection criterion is not simply lower or higher training throughput than another data center GPU. A service that needs low-latency inference and strong graphics or media capability may value this balance more than the characteristics of a training-focused accelerator.
