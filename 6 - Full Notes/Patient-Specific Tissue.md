# Patient-Specific Tissue

Patient-specific tissue is engineered to match an individual's anatomy, cells, or disease characteristics. Imaging can define implant geometry, while donor-derived cells can make biological behavior and immune compatibility more personal.

Customization raises manufacturing, validation, scheduling, and regulatory challenges because each construct may differ rather than belonging to one identical production batch.
