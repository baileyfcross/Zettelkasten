# GPS Ground Tracking Network

The GPS ground tracking network observes satellite signals from accurately surveyed sites equipped with synchronized time references. These observations reveal orbital position, velocity, clock performance, and signal health. Estimation filters account for incomplete observations and predict future satellite states, after which updated ephemeris and clock data are uploaded for rebroadcast. The network is therefore the measurement foundation beneath the autonomy of the space segment.
