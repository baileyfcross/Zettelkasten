# Azure Container Platform Quality Attribute Selection

Azure container platform selection should compare the effort required to achieve quality attributes such as scalability, resilience, security, portability, observability, and cost efficiency. A platform may permit a capability without providing it natively; for example, custom automation can scale Container Instances, while another service supplies scaling directly. The meaningful comparison is therefore built-in fit plus operational friction, not a binary claim that an outcome is possible.
