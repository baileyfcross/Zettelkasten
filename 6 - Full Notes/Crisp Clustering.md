# Crisp Clustering

Crisp clustering assigns every observation to exactly one cluster. The clusters are nonempty, their union covers the data set, and distinct clusters do not overlap.

This exclusivity makes a crisp partition easy to store and interpret, but it can misrepresent gradual boundaries. [[Fuzzy Partition]] models replace the zero-or-one assignment with degrees of membership when observations plausibly belong to more than one group.
