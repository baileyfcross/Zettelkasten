# High-Throughput Drug Screening

High-throughput drug screening tests many compounds, concentrations, or combinations in parallel. Miniaturized tissue models reduce the cells and reagents required for each condition while automated measurements rank candidates for further study.

Throughput is valuable only when the assay predicts relevant safety or efficacy; a rapid but biologically weak model can efficiently select the wrong compounds.
