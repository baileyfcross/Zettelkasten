# Health Topic Modeling

Health topic modeling uses recurring word co-occurrence patterns to summarize themes in large collections of health-related posts. A document can be represented as a mixture of latent topics, allowing researchers to discover or compare concerns without assigning every message a predefined label.

The topics are statistical patterns, not automatically coherent medical categories. Short posts, slang, and changing terminology can make interpretation unstable. Researchers should examine representative messages, compare solutions, and avoid treating a topic proportion as a direct measure of disease prevalence.
