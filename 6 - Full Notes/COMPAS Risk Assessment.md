# COMPAS Risk Assessment

COMPAS risk assessment is an example of algorithmic prediction used in criminal justice to estimate a person's likelihood of reoffending. Debate around it shows how an opaque score can influence liberty while different groups experience different patterns of error.

The case illustrates [[Competing Fairness Criteria]] because calibration, false-positive rates, and equal treatment can point in different directions. It also raises a procedural issue: a person affected by a risk score needs to know the basis of the judgment and have a way to challenge it. Predictive accuracy alone cannot settle whether the decision process is just.
