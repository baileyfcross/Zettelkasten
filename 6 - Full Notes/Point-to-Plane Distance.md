# Point-to-Plane Distance

If a plane through point $a$ has nonzero normal vector $n$, the perpendicular distance from point $b$ to the plane is

$$d=\frac{|(b-a)\cdot n|}{\|n\|}.$$

The [[Dot Product]] extracts the displacement component along the normal, and division by the normal's length removes any scaling of the [[Plane Equation in Three Dimensions|plane equation]].
