# Hyperplane at Infinity

In the projective completion of affine $n$-space, the hyperplane at infinity consists of points whose final homogeneous coordinate is zero. These points encode directions that have no finite affine representative.

A [[Projective Transformation]] restricts to an affine transformation exactly when it preserves this hyperplane. This condition separates ordinary affine maps from projective maps that can send finite points toward infinity or vice versa.
