# Behavior-Driven Memetics

Behavior-driven memetics locates memes in observable practices and artifacts rather than hidden mental representations. A repeated phrase, gesture, image, or technique is analyzable because its copies and variations can be documented in public behavior.

This makes the approach well suited to digital research, where posts and remixes leave visible traces. Its limitation is that similar behavior may carry different meanings in different contexts, so analysis must consider [[Memetic Stance]] as well as surface resemblance when deciding whether artifacts belong to the same meme.
