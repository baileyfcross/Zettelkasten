# Public Health Surveillance Data

Public health surveillance data are records collected by specialized institutions to monitor diseases, population health, and events that may require coordinated response. Their value comes from coverage across individuals and locations rather than from one clinical encounter.

Timeliness and consistent reporting are critical when the data support outbreak detection or prediction. Weak sharing and incompatible systems can prevent local observations from becoming a regional warning.
