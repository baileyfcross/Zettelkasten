# Choropleth Map

A choropleth map fills geographic regions according to an attribute associated with each polygon. It is most appropriate for rates, proportions, or other normalized values that can be treated as uniform within relatively homogeneous regions. Raw totals often reflect region size or population more than the intended phenomenon. Color may be continuous or classed, and the regional boundaries remain part of the interpretation rather than mere decoration.
