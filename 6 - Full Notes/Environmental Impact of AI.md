# Environmental Impact of AI

The environmental impact of AI includes the energy, raw materials, infrastructure, and waste associated with producing and operating data-driven systems. The apparent immateriality of software can hide data centers, devices, electricity generation, extraction, manufacturing, and disposal.

Impact must be evaluated across the system's life rather than only at the point of use. An AI application may improve the efficiency of another process while its own resource demands offset part of the benefit. The distribution of costs also matters because pollution and extraction can burden communities that receive little of the resulting technological value.
