# Spurious Correlation

A spurious correlation is a statistical association that does not reflect the causal relationship a decision maker assumes. Variables may move together by chance, because both depend on another factor, or because the data collection process created the pattern.

Machine learning can exploit such associations successfully within training data while failing when conditions change. The ethical risk grows when correlation is used to judge a person, allocate an opportunity, or justify intervention. High predictive performance does not by itself establish that the inferred relationship is meaningful, fair, or appropriate for action.
