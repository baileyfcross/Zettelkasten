# Streamgraph

A streamgraph represents additive time-series components as flowing colored bands arranged around a varying baseline. Like a [[ThemeRiver]], it emphasizes changes in composition and the overall shape of the total. The organic layout can make emergence and decline easy to notice, but position changes and curved boundaries impede precise comparison. It should therefore use a restrained qualitative palette and provide labels or interaction when individual components must be identified.
