# Bessel Interpolation Formula

Bessel's interpolation formula is a central-difference expansion centered between two tabulated arguments. It averages their function values and arranges the remaining even and odd differences symmetrically.

The formula is most effective when the normalized argument lies near one half, especially between about $0.25$ and $0.75$. At the exact midpoint, several odd-difference contributions simplify or vanish.
