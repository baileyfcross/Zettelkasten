# Density Altitude and Aircraft Performance

Density altitude is pressure altitude adjusted for nonstandard temperature so that the actual air density is expressed as an equivalent standard-atmosphere altitude. Warm air, high pressure altitude, and added water vapor reduce density and raise density altitude. A high value reduces engine, propeller, and wing performance, increasing the importance of [[Takeoff Ground Roll and Obstacle Clearance]] calculations.
