# Game World Realism

Game world realism is a continuous, multivariate relationship between a designed world and familiar reality. Graphics, physics, scale, behavior, causality, social rules, and consequences can each be more abstract or representational.

Realism should be selected separately for each aspect according to player expectations and gameplay needs. A simulation may simplify controls for accessibility, while a stylized game may preserve realistic consequences where they make choices meaningful.
