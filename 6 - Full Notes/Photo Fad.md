# Photo Fad

A photo fad is a coordinated pose or visual action that many participants reproduce in their own photographs. The body, setting, and local details change, while a recognizable compositional rule links the images.

Unlike a reaction Photoshop, a photo fad usually requires reenactment in physical space rather than editing an existing image. Its appeal comes from balancing conformity with personalization: participants prove they know the convention and then display themselves, their group, or their location through a distinctive instance.
