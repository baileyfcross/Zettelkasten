# Game Concept

A game concept is the single driving idea that unifies what players do, how they progress, and what the experience is meant to feel like. It provides a shared reference for selecting systems, content, theme, and style.

A focused concept does not prescribe one player path. It guides [[Second-Order Design]] by defining the intended whole while leaving room for a broad [[Play Space]] and discovery through prototyping.
