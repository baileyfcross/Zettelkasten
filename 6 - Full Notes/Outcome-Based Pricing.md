# Outcome-Based Pricing

Outcome-based pricing rewards a provider when an agreed service outcome is achieved. A bonus might be tied to reduced time to market, improved availability, or another result that represents the consumer's reason for using the service.

This differs psychologically from a penalty for missing a service level: it pays for success instead of only punishing failure. It is often combined with a fixed base price so the outcome component supplements a stable service contract.
