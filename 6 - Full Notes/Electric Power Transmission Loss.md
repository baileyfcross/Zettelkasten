# Electric Power Transmission Loss

Centralized electricity generation must deliver output through transmission and distribution infrastructure before users receive it. Line resistance, voltage drop, outages, and construction or maintenance costs reduce the practical value of power produced at the plant. The source contrasts this with [[Captive Power Plant]] and [[Autonomous Power Plant]] arrangements that generate near demand. Avoiding transmission does not remove losses inside the local plant.
