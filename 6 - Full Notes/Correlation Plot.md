# Correlation Plot

A correlation plot visually encodes the pairwise coefficients in a [[Correlation Matrix]]. Color, size, or shape communicates the direction and magnitude of each relationship, and clustering can reorder variables with similar patterns.

The display helps find groups of related variables and potential redundancy, but it does not establish causation. Labels, significance information, and the treatment of missing observations must remain consistent with the matrix from which the plot was produced.
