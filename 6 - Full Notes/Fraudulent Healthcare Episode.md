# Fraudulent Healthcare Episode

A fraudulent healthcare episode is a suspicious pattern that emerges only after related claims and services are grouped into a course of care. The episode view can expose implausible sequences, excessive intensity, or combinations that individual claim lines do not reveal.

Episode construction is therefore part of detection. The analyst must decide which services belong together, over what time window, and around which patient or condition. Poor grouping can label fragmented legitimate care as anomalous or conceal a scheme by distributing its claims across artificial boundaries.
