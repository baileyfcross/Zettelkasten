# Color Filter Array

A color filter array is a repeating mosaic of red, green, and blue filters placed over a digital camera sensor. Each sensor element records only the light passed by its filter, so a raw capture initially contains one color measurement at each spatial location rather than a complete RGB pixel.

The camera reconstructs the two missing channels through [[Demosaicing]]. Because the filter pattern and interpolation are regular, they introduce local correlations that can serve as evidence of camera capture and can reveal regions whose pixels were later replaced.
