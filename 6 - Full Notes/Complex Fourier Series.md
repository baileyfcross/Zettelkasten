# Complex Fourier Series

A complex Fourier series writes a periodic function as a sum of complex exponentials $e^{in\omega_0t}$. Euler's relation makes this representation equivalent to the real sine-and-cosine form while combining each harmonic into one compact basis.

The coefficients are projections onto orthogonal exponential modes over one period. For real-valued functions, coefficients at positive and negative indices are complex conjugates, ensuring that the summed result remains real.
