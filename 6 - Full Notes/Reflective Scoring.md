# Reflective Scoring

Reflective scoring describes a player's pattern of choices without ranking one pattern as universally better. Several measures can show tendencies such as directness, restraint, independence, or collaboration rather than collapsing the experience into one high-to-low scale.

This form of [[Game Score|score]] turns evaluation into a mirror. It encourages the player to consider the style of performance created through cumulative decisions instead of optimizing toward a single approved result.
