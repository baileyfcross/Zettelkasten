# Thermal Property Unit Conversion

Thermal-property units combine energy or power with area, length, mass, and temperature intervals in different positions. The book writes thermal conductivity in W/(m·K), heat-transfer coefficient in W/(m²·K), heat capacity in kJ/K, and specific heat capacity in kJ/(kg·K). These are not one interchangeable heat unit: the extra length, area, or mass changes the dimension. In compound conversions, Fahrenheit-to-kelvin interval ratios are multiplicative; the absolute-temperature offset is not inserted into a denominator. See [[Temperature Readings versus Intervals]] and [[Compound Rate Unit Conversion]].
