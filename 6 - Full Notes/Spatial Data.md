# Spatial Data

Spatial data associate observations with locations or geographic geometries and a [[Coordinate Reference System]]. The source distinguishes discrete objects, represented through [[Spatial Point Data|points]], lines, or polygons, from continuous [[Spatial Field|fields]], commonly represented as rasters. This distinction influences storage, analysis, and visualization: objects have identifiable boundaries or paths, while fields assign a potential value throughout a region.
