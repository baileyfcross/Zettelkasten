# GPS Aviation Navigation

GPS aviation navigation replaces dependence on a chain of nearby ground beacons with global three-dimensional position and route guidance. Aviation adoption required more than raw accuracy: users needed integrity, rapid warning of faulty data, dependable coverage, and adequate vertical performance for approach operations. Augmentation systems compare GPS against surveyed ground sites and broadcast corrections and status, allowing smaller airports to gain guidance without installing a complete local precision-approach system.
