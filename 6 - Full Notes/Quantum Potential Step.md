# Quantum Potential Step

A quantum potential step divides space into regions with different constant potential energies. Stationary solutions of the Schrodinger equation are written as incident, reflected, and transmitted matter waves and matched at the boundary by continuity conditions.

When particle energy exceeds the step, the transmitted wavenumber is real and probability flux is divided between reflection and transmission. When the step exceeds the energy, the transmitted wavenumber becomes imaginary, producing an evanescent wave in the higher-potential region and total reflected flux for an ideal semi-infinite step.
