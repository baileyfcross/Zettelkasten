# Nilradical

The nilradical is the [[Radical of an Ideal]] for the zero ideal. It consists exactly of the [[Nilpotent Element]]s of a ring and is the intersection of every [[Prime Ideal]].

Quotienting a ring by its nilradical yields a [[Reduced Ring]]. This operation removes nilpotent elements without changing the underlying set of prime ideals that determines the ring's spectrum.
