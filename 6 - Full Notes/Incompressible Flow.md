# Incompressible Flow

Incompressible flow assumes a fluid parcel has no volume change, so density remains constant and the velocity field has zero divergence. Liquids are often approximated this way in potential-flow analysis.

The constraint links spatial velocity components and permits scalar potential or stream-function formulations under additional assumptions. It does not by itself imply zero viscosity or zero rotation.
