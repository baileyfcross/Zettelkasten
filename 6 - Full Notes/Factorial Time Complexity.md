# Factorial Time Complexity

Factorial time complexity, written $O(n!)$, grows according to the product of every positive integer through $n$. It often appears when an algorithm enumerates every possible ordering or route.

The growth is even faster than [[Exponential Time Complexity]]. A brute-force solution to the [[Traveling Salesman Problem]] can examine all tours, making even a few dozen cities beyond the reach of straightforward exhaustive search.
