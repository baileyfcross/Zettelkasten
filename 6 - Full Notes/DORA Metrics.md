# DORA Metrics

DORA metrics describe software-delivery performance through deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, and time to restore service. Together they connect delivery speed with stability and recovery, making them useful for comparing a platform’s intended improvements with the actual flow of changes into production.

The measures require traceable release and incident data. Commits must be associated with deployed versions, production failures must be connected to the releases that caused them, and incident systems must record detection and restoration times. DORA data can reveal whether a platform improves delivery, but it should be combined with developer-experience and collaboration evidence rather than used as a complete productivity score.
