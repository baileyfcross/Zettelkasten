# Blind Game Decision

A blind game decision asks the player to choose without information that distinguishes the options. The result may be uncertain, but the immediate decision contains no basis for reasoning, so success is effectively a guess.

Blind choices can become useful when an early guess creates information for later decisions or when the experience intentionally emphasizes uncertainty. Without that later learning or thematic purpose, the choice gives the appearance of [[Player Agency]] without the deliberation of a [[Meaningful Decision]].
