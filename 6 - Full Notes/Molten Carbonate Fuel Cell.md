# Molten Carbonate Fuel Cell

A molten carbonate fuel cell carries carbonate ions through a hot liquid carbonate electrolyte held in a matrix. The source describes roughly 650°C operation with hydrogen, natural gas, or other gaseous fuels. Its electrode reactions require carbon dioxide circulation as well as fuel and oxygen supply. The high-temperature exhaust can serve [[Fuel Cell Cogeneration]] rather than being treated solely as a loss.
