# Computational Science Revolution

The computational science revolution occurred when powerful computers and improved numerical methods made large simulations practical across scientific fields. Researchers could explore grand challenges and discover patterns unavailable to conventional theory or laboratory work.

The change was not simply the importation of computer science. Scientists developed domain-specific computational practices within physics, biology, chemistry, engineering, the humanities, and other fields.
