# Transform Orientations in Blender

Blender places objects on a Cartesian grid whose X, Y, and Z axes conventionally represent side-to-side, front-to-back, and vertical movement. It uses a right-handed coordinate system with Z as the up axis.

Transform orientations change how that grid is interpreted relative to the scene, view, cursor, or active object. Global, Local, Normal, Gimbal, View, and Cursor orientations let the same translation, rotation, or scale follow different frames of reference.
