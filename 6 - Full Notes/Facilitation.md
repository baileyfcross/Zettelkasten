# Facilitation

Facilitation structures a group's participation so it can explore information, make decisions, and reach workable agreement. A facilitator manages the process and access to the conversation while participants retain ownership of the content and result.

Clear purpose, visible information, balanced participation, and explicit decision rules reduce domination and premature closure. Facilitation is especially useful when expertise is distributed across disciplines.
