# Operations Knowledge Document Chunking

Operations knowledge document chunking divides varied files into retrieval units while preserving headings, local context, and relationships among sections. Hierarchical splitting is useful for structured reports and tutorials because it keeps semantic boundaries that arbitrary fixed lengths may destroy.

Chunks should remain small enough for retrieval yet complete enough to support an operational answer. Metadata linking each chunk to its service, version, section, and source lets the [[Operations Knowledge Base]] filter and cite results accurately.
