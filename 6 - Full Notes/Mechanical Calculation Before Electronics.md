# Mechanical Calculation Before Electronics

Mechanical aids such as counting boards, the abacus, Pascal’s adding machine, Leibniz’s multiplier, the Comptometer, and the Burroughs adding machine progressively automated arithmetic. Their gears and levers recorded numbers and carried digits between columns.

These devices reduced manual effort but remained specialized and mechanically slow. They supplied techniques and commercial practices later absorbed by electronic computers.
