# Cube Projection UV Mapping

Cube Projection assigns faces according to their orientation toward six box-like projection directions. It quickly maps buildings, crates, and other forms whose major surfaces align approximately with perpendicular axes.

Cube size changes how the projection fills texture space, while aspect and bounds settings control fitting. Curved or diagonally oriented regions may split or distort, so the resulting islands still need inspection and adjustment.
