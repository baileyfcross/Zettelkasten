# Trend-Type Landing Forecasts

A trend-type landing forecast is a short-range forecast, typically valid for about three hours, appended to an aerodrome observation. For that short period it supersedes the corresponding portion of the longer [[Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts]]. Its placement beside current observed weather makes imminent operational changes easier to interpret.
