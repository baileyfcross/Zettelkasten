# Minimal Free Resolution

A minimal free resolution uses the smallest possible number of generators at every stage. Over a Noetherian local or graded local ring, its differentials have entries in the relevant maximal ideal.

Minimal resolutions are unique up to isomorphism under the source's hypotheses. Their free ranks and degree shifts give [[Graded Betti Number]]s, projective dimension, and Castelnuovo-Mumford regularity.
