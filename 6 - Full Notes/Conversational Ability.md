# Conversational Ability

Conversational ability is a system's capacity to exchange turns in natural language while preserving enough meaning and context to move an interaction forward. It depends on more than recognizing words: the system must interpret intent, manage state, choose actions, and express responses.

Conversational fluency is not a reliable measure of general intelligence. A system can produce socially plausible language while having little knowledge or utility beyond a narrow [[Intent Schema]].
