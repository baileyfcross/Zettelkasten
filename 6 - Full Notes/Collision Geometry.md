# Collision Geometry

Collision geometry is a simplified mathematical shape used to test physical relationships without comparing every triangle of a visible model. Spheres, axis-aligned boxes, and oriented boxes trade geometric fit against the cost of intersection tests.

A game may combine several shapes around one object to improve approximation. The representation should be detailed enough for believable interaction but inexpensive enough to test repeatedly during simulation.
