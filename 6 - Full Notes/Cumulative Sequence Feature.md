# Cumulative Sequence Feature

A cumulative sequence feature summarizes all matches of a pattern across an entire sequence. Binary presence and total occurrence count are common cumulative mappings.

This produces a fixed-length representation independent of sequence length and match positions. It is efficient for standard learners but discards when events occurred and how their occurrences were distributed.
