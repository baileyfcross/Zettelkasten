# Forensic Technique Aggregation

Forensic technique aggregation combines multiple analyses whose evidence arises from different parts of the imaging pipeline. Metadata inspection, lighting geometry, compression history, resampling detection, and camera-sensor analysis can each test a different necessary condition of the proposed photograph.

Agreement among independent tests increases confidence because a forger must preserve many subtle regularities at once. Aggregation still does not prove [[Image Authenticity]]: correlated tests may share assumptions, benign processing may disturb several traces, and an informed forger may deliberately conceal them.
