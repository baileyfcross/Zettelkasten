# Climb, Cruise, and Leg Fuel Planning

Flight fuel is calculated by segment rather than by distance alone. Climb tables supply time, distance, and fuel to altitude; cruise data provide true airspeed and flow at the planned power setting; wind converts that speed to leg time; and each leg's duration determines its fuel. Adding ground and reserve categories produces the total described by [[Ramp, Trip, Reserve, and Alternate Fuel]].
