# Dry-Bulb and Wet-Bulb Temperature

Dry-bulb temperature is the ordinary air temperature measured without deliberate evaporative cooling. Wet-bulb temperature is measured with a wetted sensor exposed to airflow, so evaporation lowers its reading toward the adiabatic-saturation state. Their difference helps describe atmospheric moisture and complements [[Relative Humidity and Saturation]].
