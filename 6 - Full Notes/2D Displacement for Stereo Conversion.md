# 2D Displacement for Stereo Conversion

A depth map can displace a copy of a single image sideways to synthesize another eye view. The two views then differ in image-space position according to estimated depth, giving a limited stereo effect. The book notes that this simple method can work in some shots but that displacing actual 3D geometry may yield a more natural result. The map itself is still an interpretation made from roto, procedural extraction, a depth sensor, or their combination. See [[Roto-Gradient Depth Maps for Stereo]] and [[Rubber-Sheet Stereo Conversion]].
