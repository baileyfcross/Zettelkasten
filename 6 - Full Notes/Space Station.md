# Space Station

A space station is a crewed orbital facility intended for repeated or long-duration habitation rather than a single flight. Docking ports, living quarters, power, life support, laboratories, logistics, and visiting vehicles allow crews and experiments to continue across missions.

Stations became the main form of sustained human spaceflight after lunar competition declined. Soviet Salyut and Mir missions developed long-duration experience, while the International Space Station expanded the model into a large modular partnership. A station can support research and preparation for deeper travel, but its expense and institutional role often exceed the scientific return alone.
