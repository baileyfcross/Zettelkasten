# Azure Enterprise Architect

An enterprise architect aligns technology initiatives and the overall IT landscape with business strategy. The role maintains a broad, cross-business view and guards coherence among programs rather than designing every Azure component. Azure-specific choices normally reach this level as principles, capabilities, standards, and portfolio consequences; deeper platform or workload decisions are delegated to domain and solution architects.
