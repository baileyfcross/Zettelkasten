# Muscle Tetanus

Muscle tetanus is sustained contraction produced when stimuli arrive so rapidly that the tissue cannot relax between them. Individual twitches merge into a stable force while high-frequency stimulation continues.

Biohybrid devices can use tetanus to hold a grip or position, but prolonged activation increases fatigue and should not be confused with the infectious disease also called tetanus.
