# Parabolic Trough Solar Power Plant

A parabolic-trough plant connects many [[Parabolic Trough Solar Collector]] modules that heat a circulating fluid along their receiver tubes. Hot oil can be stored and used in a boiler to raise steam for a turbine, then returned cool to the collector field. The source also describes direct steam generation and a backup-fired boiler. Temperature limits of the primary fluid shape operating conditions.
