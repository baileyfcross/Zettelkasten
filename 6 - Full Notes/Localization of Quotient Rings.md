# Localization of Quotient Rings

Localization commutes with forming a quotient. If I is an ideal of R and S maps to a multiplicatively closed set in R/I, then S⁻¹R divided by S⁻¹I is isomorphic to the localization of R/I.

This identifies two routes—first localize and then quotient, or first quotient and then localize. Both make the same denominators invertible and turn the same ideal elements into zero.
