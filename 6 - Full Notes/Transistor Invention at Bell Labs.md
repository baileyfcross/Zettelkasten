# Transistor Invention at Bell Labs

William Shockley, Walter Brattain, and John Bardeen developed the first successful transistor at Bell Laboratories in 1947. The solid-state device could switch or amplify current without a vacuum or heated filament.

Bell Labs disclosed and licensed the invention rather than keeping it classified. Years of further materials and manufacturing work were required before transistors transformed computers.
