# ENCODE

ENCODE is a collaborative project that catalogs functional elements in the human genome through many experimental assays and cell contexts. Its shared datasets connect sequence regions with evidence about transcription, regulation, chromatin, and binding.

The project illustrates how standardized metadata, public access, large computational workflows, and integration across experiments support reuse beyond the original study.
