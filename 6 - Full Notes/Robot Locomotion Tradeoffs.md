# Robot Locomotion Tradeoffs

Robot locomotion can use wheels, tracks, legs, wings, propellers, or hybrid mechanisms. Wheels are energy efficient on smooth surfaces, tracks spread load and cross rougher ground, and legs can step through irregular terrain at the cost of greater control complexity and power use.

No locomotion form is best in every environment. A rescue robot may need to survive rubble and recover after overturning, while a warehouse robot benefits from a controlled floor. Choosing movement therefore begins with terrain, speed, payload, endurance, stability, and maintenance requirements rather than a preference for biological resemblance.
