# Mechanical Calculation as Codified Expertise

A computational method captures expert practice as a sequence that others can follow. Once the essential operations are expressed precisely, a nonexpert can reproduce a result without possessing the intuition that originally discovered the method.

Codification spreads expertise and makes calculation more consistent, but it does not eliminate the need for experts. Their judgment is still required to formulate the method, identify its limits, and improve it when new situations expose gaps.
