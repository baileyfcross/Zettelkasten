# Change-in-Estimate Criterion

A change-in-estimate criterion retains a candidate confounder when adding it changes the exposure coefficient by more than a chosen relative amount, often ten percent. It focuses on distortion of the target association rather than the confounder's own p-value.

The threshold is a practical rule, not a statistical test for confounding. Its result depends on the coefficient scale and the current model, so substantive knowledge and the broader selection strategy still govern the decision.
