# Inductive Reactance

Inductive reactance is the opposition an ideal inductor presents to sinusoidal current, $X_L=\omega L$. It increases with frequency because rapidly changing current induces a larger back emf. In an ideal inductor, current lags voltage by one quarter cycle, and energy is stored and returned rather than dissipated on average.
