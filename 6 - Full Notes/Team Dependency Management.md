# Team Dependency Management

Team dependency management makes cross-team needs visible, identifies when they must be satisfied, and gives both sides responsibility for coordination. Dependencies can be represented on shared boards and discussed before they block a Sprint Goal.

The first response is to reduce the dependency through team design, shared skills, or a thinner feature split. Scheduling a handoff manages the remaining cost but does not remove it.
