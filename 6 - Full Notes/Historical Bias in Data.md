# Historical Bias in Data

Historical bias in data occurs when records encode earlier discrimination, exclusion, or unequal access. A model can learn this pattern accurately and then present its continuation as an objective prediction.

The problem remains even if collection and computation are technically correct. Data describing the world as it was do not decide whether the world should remain that way. In hiring, policing, or lending, past outcomes may reflect unequal opportunity rather than differences relevant to future decisions. Addressing the bias requires normative choices about correction, not merely a larger sample of the same history.
