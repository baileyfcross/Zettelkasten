# Parallel and Perpendicular Lines

Distinct nonvertical parallel lines have equal slopes. Two nonvertical, nonhorizontal lines are perpendicular when their slopes are negative reciprocals, so $m_1m_2=-1$. Horizontal and vertical lines form the exceptional perpendicular pair. These slope relationships convert geometric direction into algebraic conditions.
