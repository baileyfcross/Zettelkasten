# R Quantitative Method Agreement

R quantitative method agreement analysis extends paired-method assessment to several measurement methods. It estimates method effects and the variation attributable to subjects and residual measurement error, allowing pairwise differences and repeatability to be examined within one model.

The design must preserve which observations belong to the same subject and method. A high association among methods is insufficient because agreement concerns the magnitude and consistency of their differences, as illustrated for two methods by an [[R Bland-Altman Agreement Plot]].
