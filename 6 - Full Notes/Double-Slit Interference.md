# Double-Slit Interference

Double-slit interference occurs when coherent waves from two narrow openings overlap. Bright or strong maxima occur where path difference is an integer multiple of wavelength; minima occur at half-integer multiples. For slit separation $d$, maxima satisfy $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$. The pattern demonstrates the wave behavior of light and matter.
