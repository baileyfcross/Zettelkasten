# Cultural Narrative

Cultural narrative consists of stories that communities make from or about a game. It includes fan fiction, fan-made films, characters and premises added through mods, critical interpretations, and public accounts that praise or condemn the game's meaning.

These narratives are neither the developer's [[Inscribed Narrative]] nor the event sequence of one [[Dynamic Narrative|play session]]. They arise when a game becomes material for collective storytelling and debate. Their existence shows that narrative ownership continues after release even when the original text never changes.
