# P-N Junction

A p-n junction brings [[P-Type Semiconductor]] and [[N-Type Semiconductor]] regions into contact. The resulting electric field separates electrons and positive holes created when suitable photons are absorbed. With a load connected, their separation gives a usable current. In the source's silicon-cell example, an individual cell supplies roughly half a volt while current depends on receiving area and illumination.
