# Dominant Strategy

A dominant strategy is so effective that a player has no meaningful reason to use an alternative. Its existence narrows the decision space and makes other mechanics strategically irrelevant.

Balance work should change costs, risks, counters, circumstances, or rewards so several strategies remain situationally useful. A strategy available to only one side can also make an asymmetric game unfair.
