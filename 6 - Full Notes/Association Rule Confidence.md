# Association Rule Confidence

Association rule confidence is the support count of the union of antecedent and consequent divided by the support count of the antecedent. It estimates how often the consequent appears when the antecedent appears.

A confidence threshold filters rules generated from frequent itemsets. Confidence should be interpreted alongside prevalence because a common consequent can produce a high value without a distinctive relationship.
