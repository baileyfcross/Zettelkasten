# Personal Data Co-Production

Personal data co-production is the idea that a record is made through contributions from both the person described and the organization operating the system. The person's body, behavior, entries, and circumstances generate signals, while the organization supplies sensors, categories, algorithms, storage, and presentation.

This joint production complicates a simple ownership claim. Possessing the server does not erase the person's relationship to data about their life, and being described does not erase the organization's work in making a usable system. The relevant governance question is which continuing rights and responsibilities follow from each contribution.

Co-production supports access and accountability models that are richer than treating data as an ordinary object transferred completely to one side.
