# Auxiliary Equation for a Difference Equation

For a constant-coefficient homogeneous recurrence $F(E)y_n=0$, substituting $y_n=m^n$ gives the algebraic equation $F(m)=0$. This is the auxiliary equation.

Each distinct root $m$ contributes a term $Cm^n$ to the complementary function. A root repeated $s$ times contributes $(C_0+C_1n+\cdots+C_{s-1}n^{s-1})m^n$, and complex roots can be recombined into real trigonometric sequences.
