# Internal Economy

An internal economy is the part of a game's core mechanics that governs numeric relationships among entities and how those relationships change over time. It tracks resources as they are produced, consumed, converted, moved, or exchanged.

The economy is broader than money. Health, ammunition, time, labor, territory, and other quantified entities can participate when mechanics change their amounts.
