# Wald Test

A Wald test evaluates whether an estimated model coefficient differs from a hypothesized value, usually zero, by comparing the estimate with its standard error. Logistic regression output commonly reports a z or chi-squared form of this test.

The test supports variable-selection decisions but can behave poorly for unstable or extreme estimates. Confidence intervals and model comparisons provide additional context, especially near separation or with sparse categories.
