# Structural Weld Symbols

Weld symbols communicate joint requirements through an arrow, reference line, weld-type symbol, dimensions, and optional tail. Placement above or below the reference line identifies the other or arrow side of the joint. Size appears to the left of the weld symbol, while length and spacing appear to the right; supplementary marks identify all-around and field welds.

A symbol should state the necessary result without prescribing an impractical operation. Groove preparation, penetration, contour, finish, intermittent length, and special process information may all need coordination with access and member geometry. Misreading the arrow side or confusing weld size with effective throat changes capacity. Clear drawings therefore pair standardized notation with details where the three-dimensional joint cannot be understood from a symbol alone.
