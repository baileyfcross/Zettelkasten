# Domain Knowledge

Domain knowledge is understanding of the field in which software will operate, including its terminology, constraints, processes, and goals. It lets developers speak productively with experts rather than relying only on a requirements document. No single expert necessarily possesses the whole truth, so knowledge should be collected from people at different levels and in adjacent areas. This collaborative learning process is [[Knowledge Crunching]] and supplies the material for a [[Ubiquitous Language]].
