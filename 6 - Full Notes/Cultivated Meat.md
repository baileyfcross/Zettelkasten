# Cultivated Meat

Cultivated meat is edible animal tissue grown from cultured cells outside a whole animal. Muscle cells are central, but fat, connective tissue, vascular cells, matrix, and processing contribute to the resulting product.

Manufacturing must expand enormous cell numbers, use food-compatible inputs, and reproduce enough nutrition, texture, appearance, aroma, flavor, and cooking behavior to serve as meat.
