# Hyperbolic Complex Numbers

Hyperbolic complex numbers, also called split-complex numbers, have the form $a+jb$ with $j^2=+1$. A real $2\times2$ matrix representation is

$$a+jb\;\longleftrightarrow\;\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&a\end{pmatrix},$$

whose determinant is $a^2-b^2$.

Exponentiating the non-real generator gives a matrix containing $\cosh\chi$ and $\sinh\chi$. This matrix preserves the determinant, so the algebra models the two-dimensional hyperbolic geometry used for a [[Lorentz Boost]]. The identity $\cosh^2\chi-\sinh^2\chi=1$ is the preserved norm condition.

The source treats these numbers as the algebra of a time-space plane and uses them as an alternative to the conventional [[Four-Vector]] presentation. That identification is a modeling choice; standard special relativity requires the Lorentzian geometry, not the book's broader finite-group interpretation of it.
