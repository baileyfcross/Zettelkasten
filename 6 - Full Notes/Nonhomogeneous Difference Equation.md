# Nonhomogeneous Difference Equation

A nonhomogeneous difference equation has a nonzero forcing sequence, such as $F(E)y_n=g(n)$. Its general solution combines the homogeneous complementary function with one particular solution responding to $g(n)$.

The forcing form guides the calculation of the [[Particular Integral of a Difference Equation]]. Initial values then determine the arbitrary constants in the complementary part without changing the forcing response.
