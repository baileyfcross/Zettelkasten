# Image Provenance

Image provenance is the history connecting a digital photograph to its source, capture device, and subsequent transformations. [[Reverse Image Search]] can expose earlier appearances or altered variants, while [[EXIF Metadata]], JPEG signatures, and sensor traces can supply evidence about the device or software involved.

No single trace normally reconstructs the entire history. Metadata can be removed, search indexes are incomplete, and a file can be repeatedly transcoded. A provenance judgment is strongest when independent content, format, and capture clues converge on the same account.
