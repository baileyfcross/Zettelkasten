# Viral Content

Viral content is a single cultural item that spreads rapidly and widely through a population, usually through many transmissions of substantially the same artifact. Viewers may comment on or recommend it, but its recognition depends on one dominant version.

Virality should not be treated as identical to memetic circulation. An [[Internet Meme]] is a collection of related versions created through imitation and transformation. A viral video can become the basis of a meme, yet widespread exposure alone does not guarantee that users will remake it.
