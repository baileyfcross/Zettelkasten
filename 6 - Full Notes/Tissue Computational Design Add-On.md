# Tissue Computational Design Add-On

The Tissue add-on creates computational-design forms by repeating or transforming a component across another mesh. Its tessellation workflow derives a new structure from the relationship between a base surface and a component, while other tools support reaction-diffusion and related patterned geometry.

Because the generated form follows source geometry, changing the sources can produce families of related architectural or product-design surfaces. The method treats repetition as a rule rather than a collection of manually placed copies.
