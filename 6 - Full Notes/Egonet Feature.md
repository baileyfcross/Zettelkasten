# Egonet Feature

An egonet feature is computed from the subgraph induced by a focal node, its neighbors, and the edges among them. Counts of nodes, internal edges, boundary edges, and local density summarize the focal node's social or structural environment.

Egonets balance interpretability with more context than degree alone. Anomalous relationships among egonet measures can also reveal unusual nodes or local structures.
