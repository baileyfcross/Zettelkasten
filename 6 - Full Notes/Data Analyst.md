# Data Analyst

A data analyst converts organized data into summaries, comparisons, and explanations that answer defined questions. The work emphasizes correct measures, suitable visualizations, and interpretation within the problem domain.

In the book's capability progression, analysis builds on sound data preparation and precedes the broader pattern discovery associated with a [[Data Miner]].
