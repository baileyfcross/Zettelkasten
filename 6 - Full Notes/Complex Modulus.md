# Complex Modulus

The modulus of $z=a+bi$ is $|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$. It is the Euclidean distance from the origin to the point $(a,b)$ in the complex plane.

The identity $|z|^2=z\bar z$ links the modulus to the [[Complex Conjugate]]. Moduli multiply under complex multiplication.
