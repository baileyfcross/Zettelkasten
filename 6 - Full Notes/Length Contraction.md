# Length Contraction

Length contraction reduces the measured dimension of an object parallel to its motion. If $L_0$ is proper length in the object's rest frame, an observer seeing it move at speed $v$ measures $L=L_0/\gamma$. The comparison requires simultaneous endpoint measurements in the observer's frame, linking contraction to the relativity of simultaneity.

The effect follows directly from Lorentz-coordinate differences evaluated at equal times in the measuring frame. Transverse dimensions are unchanged, and the proper length remains the endpoint separation in the object's rest frame rather than an absolute size shared by every observer.
