# Solid Geometric Model

A solid geometric model represents the occupied three-dimensional portion of an object, not merely its edges or visible skin. It provides a computer model that can be viewed from different directions and treated as the counterpart of a manufactured or physical object.

Common construction schemes include stored primitives, [[Constructive Solid Geometry]], [[Boundary Representation Model]], and sweeping a closed planar domain. Because a valid solid distinguishes interior from exterior, it supports calculations and consistency checks that a [[Wireframe Geometric Model]] cannot provide. Solid models are used in engineering design, architecture, entertainment, and medical visualization.
