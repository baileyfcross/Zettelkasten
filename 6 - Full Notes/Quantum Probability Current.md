# Quantum Probability Current

Quantum probability current is the flux paired with probability density in the continuity equation. For the Schrodinger wavefunction, it depends on the antisymmetric combination of the wavefunction and its spatial derivative.

The continuity equation states that a local decrease in probability density is balanced by current flowing out of the region. Integrating it over space relates conservation of total probability to the boundary flux, giving physical meaning to wavefunction flow.
