# ANT Landscape Add-On

ANT Landscape procedurally deforms a tessellated plane with noise constrained by terrain-oriented parameters. It quickly produces hills, rock-like surfaces, fields, and other landscape foundations without requiring the artist to sculpt every elevation.

Subdivision controls geometric density, smoothing changes surface character, and a random seed explores different formations under the same general settings. The generated terrain is a starting point whose polygon cost and silhouette should be matched to the scene.
