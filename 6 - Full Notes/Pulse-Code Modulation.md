# Pulse-Code Modulation

Pulse-code modulation samples a continuous signal and encodes each measurement as a discrete numerical value. Bell Laboratories used the technique during World War II to transmit secure digitized speech.

Digital encoding allows a signal to be regenerated and processed without preserving every analog variation in the transmission medium. It later became fundamental to modern telephony and audio systems.
