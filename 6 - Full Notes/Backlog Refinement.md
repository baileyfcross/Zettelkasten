# Backlog Refinement

Backlog refinement is the recurring work of adding, clarifying, splitting, estimating, reordering, and removing Product Backlog Items. It prepares a small amount of likely near-term work while preserving uncertainty in distant work.

Refinement is continual planning rather than a one-time requirements phase. It incorporates what working increments, stakeholders, and technical experiments reveal.
