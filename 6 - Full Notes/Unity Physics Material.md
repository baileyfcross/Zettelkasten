# Unity Physics Material

A Unity physics material configures how a collider behaves at contact, especially through friction and bounciness. Assigning different materials can make surfaces behave more like ice, rubber, wood, or another interaction profile without changing their visible [[Unity Material]].

Physical and visual materials are separate because appearance does not determine simulation automatically. A shiny floor may still have high friction unless its collider receives the intended physics material. This separation supports deliberate gameplay tuning and reuse across differently rendered objects.
