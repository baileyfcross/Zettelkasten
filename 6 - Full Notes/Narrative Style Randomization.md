# Narrative Style Randomization

Randomizing the order of narrative units supplies a comparison for observed tempo, rhythm, and movement through a semantic factor space. The book analyzes Casablanca scenes and beats against repeated reordered alternatives rather than declaring every visual pattern distinctive by inspection.

The test depends on what is randomized and which style statistic is measured. A result unusual under shuffled order says that sequence matters for that statistic; it does not establish the aesthetic quality or causal meaning of the story by itself.
