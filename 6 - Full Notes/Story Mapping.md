# Story Mapping

Story mapping arranges stories in two dimensions: a player or product narrative across one axis and priority or forecasted delivery slices across the other. A narrative backbone preserves why the pieces belong together while smaller stories fill planned increments beneath it.

The map gives a release a visible big picture that a one-dimensional backlog cannot. Teams can move stories, dependencies, and Sprint boundaries as the product emerges while retaining coherent player-facing goals.
