# Perception

Perception gives sensed stimuli a meaningful interpretation. It is the first major stage of [[Information Processing]] and supplies the information that [[Cognition]] uses to choose actions.

A [[3D User Interface]] can communicate through visual, auditory, haptic, chemical, and vestibular channels. Each channel has different strengths, limits, and cues. Designers need to understand these mechanisms because display choices can strengthen, omit, or conflict with the information people normally use in a physical environment.

Perceptual evaluation can combine observation, performance measures, self-report, and methods such as eye tracking.
