# EC2 On-Demand Pricing

EC2 On-Demand pricing charges for instance use without a long-term commitment. It is appropriate for new, short-lived, unpredictable, or interrupt-intolerant workloads whose future baseline is not yet known.

Flexibility carries a higher unit price than commitment discounts. On-Demand capacity should not become an unquestioned permanent choice; measured steady use may justify a Reserved Instance or Savings Plan, while fault-tolerant flexible work may use Spot capacity.
