# Polycrystalline Silicon Solar Cell

A polycrystalline silicon cell is made from a block containing many small silicon crystals and sliced into wafers. It still uses semiconductor junction conversion, but its crystal structure and manufacturing route differ from a [[Monocrystalline Silicon Solar Cell]]. The source reports a lower module efficiency for its example; system comparison must therefore use module performance, not only cell material names.
