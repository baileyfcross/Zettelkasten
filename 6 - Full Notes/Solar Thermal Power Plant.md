# Solar Thermal Power Plant

A solar thermal power plant collects sunlight as heat, transfers it to a working fluid, and runs a thermodynamic power cycle. It is different from a [[Solar Cell]], which generates electricity in a junction without a heat-engine stage. The source's high-temperature plants use concentrating collectors, receivers, heat transport, and often storage to handle variable solar input and demand.
