# Mass-Spring Oscillator

An ideal mass-spring oscillator obeys [[Hooke's Law]], so displacement from equilibrium produces the restoring force $F=-kx$. Its angular frequency is $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$ and its period is $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$. Increasing stiffness shortens the period, while increasing the oscillating mass lengthens it.
