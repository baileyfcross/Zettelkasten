# Population Parameter

A population parameter is a fixed numerical property of the population about which an analysis seeks information, such as a population mean, variance, or difference between group means. It is conceptually fixed even though it is usually unknown.

A sample supplies a [[Point Estimate]] of the parameter. Repeated sampling changes the estimate rather than the parameter, which is why uncertainty is described through a sampling distribution and interval estimate.
