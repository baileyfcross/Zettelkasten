# Information Confidentiality

Information confidentiality ensures that information is disclosed only to people, applications, or systems authorized to receive it. The required protection depends on the data: intellectual property, financial records, health information, and credentials have different audiences and consequences.

Access control and encryption can support confidentiality, but they apply at different boundaries. Access control decides who may obtain the data, while encryption reduces what an interceptor or unauthorized holder can understand.
