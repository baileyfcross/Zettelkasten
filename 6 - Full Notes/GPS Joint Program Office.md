# GPS Joint Program Office

The GPS Joint Program Office was established in 1973 to replace competing service-specific navigation projects with one Defense Department program. Led initially by Air Force Colonel Bradford Parkinson, it reconciled Navy timing and orbit concepts, Air Force ranging codes, service requirements, receiver development, and cost limits. Its consensus design became NAVSTAR GPS, showing that the system's architecture was as much an organizational integration as a single technical invention.
