# Shape Function

A shape function interpolates a field inside an element from its nodal values. Linear, quadratic, and higher-order polynomials provide progressively richer variation while remaining tied to the element's degrees of freedom.

Useful shape functions equal one at their own node and zero at the other nodes. This interpolation makes nodal unknowns the coefficients of the approximate field and supplies the derivatives used to calculate gradients, strain, or flux.
