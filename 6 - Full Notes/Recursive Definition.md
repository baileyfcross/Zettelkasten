# Recursive Definition

A recursive definition specifies initial values and then defines later or larger objects in terms of earlier or smaller ones. The factorial definition fixes 0! = 1 and defines n! = n(n − 1)! for n > 0; the Fibonacci sequence requires two initial values before applying its recurrence.

Because the definition builds an infinite family from finite rules, facts about recursively defined objects are commonly proved with [[Mathematical Induction]] or [[Strong Induction]].
