# HDR Image-Based Lighting for CG

Image-based lighting uses the colors and intensities of an environment image to illuminate CG. A high-dynamic-range image retains exposure differences beyond those of an ordinary display image, so a bright sun region can contribute far more light than shaded surroundings even when the monitor cannot display that full range. The book distinguishes an HDRI from the global-illumination method that uses it: an image can be a lighting source without lighting the scene globally. See [[Light Probe Reference for CG]] and [[Radiosity as Diffuse Interreflection]].
