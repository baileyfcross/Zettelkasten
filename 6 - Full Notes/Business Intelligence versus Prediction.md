# Business Intelligence versus Prediction

Business intelligence integrates and summarizes data for reports and decision support, often using a warehouse and multidimensional queries. Prediction instead trains a model from historic examples to estimate an unknown target for new instances. A report describing past sales is not by itself a forecast of future customer behavior.

The two activities can reinforce each other. A warehouse may supply cleaned historical data and shorten model preparation, while scored predictions can return to dashboards or applications. Their distinct goals still require different evaluation: a correct aggregation and a model that generalizes to unseen cases are not the same accomplishment.
