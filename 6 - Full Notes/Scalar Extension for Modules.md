# Scalar Extension for Modules

Given a ring homomorphism R → S and an R-module M, extension of scalars forms the S-module S ⊗_R M. The factor S supplies the new scalar action.

If M is finitely generated over R, its scalar extension is finitely generated over S. [[Localization of a Module]] is the special case obtained by extending scalars to a localized ring.
