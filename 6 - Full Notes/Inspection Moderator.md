# Inspection Moderator

An inspection moderator coordinates a formal inspection. The moderator selects participants, receives and distributes materials, schedules the meeting, provides the defect checklist, keeps the review focused, and follows up with the author afterward.

The role requires technical understanding as well as process training. A moderator must recognize when preparation is insufficient, prevent the meeting from turning into solution design, and verify that recorded defects are addressed through rework or another review.
