# Graded Betti Number

The graded Betti number β_{i,j}(M) counts generators of degree j in the i-th free module of a [[Minimal Free Resolution]]. It records both homological position and internal degree.

The ranks of syzygy modules are obtained by summing over degrees at a fixed position. The largest shifts j−i determine [[Castelnuovo-Mumford Regularity]].
