# Health Data Sharing Mechanism

A health data sharing mechanism defines how institutions make healthcare resources available to authorized users. It includes technical interfaces, common standards, access rules, responsibilities, and procedures for opening or restricting data.

Sharing can reduce fragmentation and enable public-health or research uses, but openness without governance can expose patients and institutions. Effective mechanisms combine interoperability with privacy protection, legal authority, and traceable use.
