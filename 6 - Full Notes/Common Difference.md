# Common Difference

The common difference of an [[Arithmetic Progression]] is the constant change between consecutive terms. If the terms are (a_1,a_2,a_3,\ldots), then

$$d=a_{n+1}-a_n$$

has the same value for every permitted (n). A positive difference produces an increasing progression, a negative difference a decreasing one, and zero a constant sequence. Together with the first term, the common difference determines the entire progression and its direct (n)th-term formula.
