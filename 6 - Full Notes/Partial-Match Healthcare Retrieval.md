# Partial-Match Healthcare Retrieval

Partial-match healthcare retrieval ranks documents by estimated relevance rather than requiring every condition to be satisfied exactly. Term weighting and document statistics allow results that match different portions of the query to be ordered.

Ranking is useful when vocabulary is uncertain or the collection is large, but a high score is not proof of clinical usefulness. System-oriented evaluation measures properties such as precision and recall, while user-oriented evaluation asks whether the results support the information-seeking task. Both are needed because relevance depends on the user's context.
