# Advanced Chess

Advanced Chess is a form of chess in which each human player consults a computer before choosing moves. It was proposed after machine victories to explore whether a coordinated human-computer team could exceed either unaided player.

Results show that teaming quality matters more than simple access to a strong engine. A person must interpret suggestions, manage time, and integrate machine analysis without adding confusion or delay.
