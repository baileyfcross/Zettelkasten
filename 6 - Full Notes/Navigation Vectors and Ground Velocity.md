# Navigation Vectors and Ground Velocity

An aircraft's velocity through the air and the wind velocity are vectors with both magnitude and direction. Adding their Cartesian components produces the ground-velocity vector, whose magnitude is groundspeed and whose direction is track. The difference between heading and track is the wind-correction or crab angle developed further in [[Crab Angle from Wind Components]].
