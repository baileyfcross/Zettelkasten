# Backus-Naur Form

Backus-Naur Form is a notation for describing context-free aspects of language syntax. Meaningful nonterminal names appear in angle brackets, ::= introduces a production, vertical bars mark alternatives, brackets mark optional material, and repeated brackets describe zero or more occurrences.

These conveniences can be translated into ordinary [[Context-Free Grammar]] productions by splitting alternatives and introducing recursive nonterminals. In programming-language definitions, the terminals are usually tokens rather than individual characters.
