# Gas Turbine Power Plant

A gas-turbine plant compresses air, burns fuel to heat it, then expands the hot working gas through a turbine that drives both a compressor and an electrical generator. The source presents natural gas or oil as fuels. Exhaust leaving the turbine still carries substantial heat, so a [[Combined-Cycle Power Plant]] can use it to raise steam instead of simply discharging it.
