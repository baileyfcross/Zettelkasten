# Game Design Document Audience

A game design document should be written for the people who will use it to implement, evaluate, or communicate the feature. Its vocabulary, level of detail, examples, and medium should match what that audience needs rather than an abstract ideal of completeness.

Documentation succeeds when the implementer can act without inventing missing behavior or decoding irrelevant prose. The writer should identify what the audience already knows, what must be explicit, and whether a diagram, table, reference, or playable example communicates the design most efficiently.
