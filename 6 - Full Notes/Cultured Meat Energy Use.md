# Cultured Meat Energy Use

Cultured meat energy use includes maintaining sterile temperature-controlled reactors, mixing, pumping, producing media and scaffolds, cleaning, refrigeration, and transport. It may compare favorably with beef while performing less favorably against poultry or pork.

Efficiency improvements and low-carbon electricity change both the amount and environmental meaning of this demand.
