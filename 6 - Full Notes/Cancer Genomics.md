# Cancer Genomics

Cancer genomics studies the inherited and acquired genomic changes associated with tumor formation, progression, and treatment response. Comparing tumor and normal samples can identify mutations, copy-number changes, expression patterns, and affected pathways.

Tumor heterogeneity and evolving cell populations mean that one sample may not represent every clone or stage of the disease.
