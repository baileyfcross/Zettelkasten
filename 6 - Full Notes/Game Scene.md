# Game Scene

A game scene is the smallest useful unit of [[Game Pacing|pacing]] in which a distinct development of rules or play occurs. Depending on the game, a scene may be called a level, stage, round, wave, board, mission, or screen, but those labels do not determine its actual boundaries.

A scene arranges [[Game Verb|verbs]] and [[Game Part|objects]] so the player can encounter, understand, and apply particular relationships. One conventional level may contain several scenes when its focus or choice structure changes.
