# Basic Promise Fulfillment

Basic promise fulfillment means the producer delivers the functions and qualities explicitly agreed upon. Standards-oriented engineering, testing, and disciplined development support this first level of genuine integrity.

Fulfilling promises is necessary but not sufficient for excellent design. Written commitments may omit contextual needs, future breakdowns, or opportunities that users could not articulate beforehand.
