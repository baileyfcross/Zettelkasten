# Social Engineering

Social engineering manipulates people into disclosing information, granting access, or performing an unsafe action. Instead of defeating a technical control directly, the attacker exploits trust, urgency, authority, curiosity, or another predictable human response.

Technical defenses alone cannot eliminate this attack path. Verification procedures, training, limited privileges, and multiple layers of control help keep one persuaded user from compromising an entire system.
