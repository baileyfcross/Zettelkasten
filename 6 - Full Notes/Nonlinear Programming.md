# Nonlinear Programming

Nonlinear programming optimizes an objective when the objective or at least one constraint is nonlinear. Nonlinearity may come from powers, products of variables, trigonometric functions, exponentials, or logarithms.

Unlike [[Linear Programming]], an optimum need not occur at an extreme point. Solution methods use derivatives, nonlinear equations, local searches, and constraint conditions, so convexity strongly affects whether a local result is globally optimal.
