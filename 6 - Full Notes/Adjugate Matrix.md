# Adjugate Matrix

The adjugate of a square matrix is the transpose of its [[Cofactor Matrix]]. It satisfies $A\operatorname{adj}(A)=\operatorname{adj}(A)A=\det(A)I$.

When the determinant is invertible, $A^{-1}=\det(A)^{-1}\operatorname{adj}(A)$. This formula establishes the link between cofactors and invertibility, though elimination or factorization is usually more efficient computationally.
