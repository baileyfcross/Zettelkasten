# Matrix Equivalence over a Ring

Two matrices over a ring are equivalent when one can be written $B=PAQ$ with invertible matrices $P$ and $Q$. Left multiplication changes the generating relations, while right multiplication changes the generators of the presented module.

Equivalent [[Relation Matrix|relation matrices]] therefore present isomorphic modules. Over a [[Principal Ideal Domain]], this freedom reduces every matrix to [[Smith Normal Form]].
