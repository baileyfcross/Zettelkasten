# Target Audience Composite

A target audience composite combines several measurable player attributes into a coherent profile for whom a game is being made. Useful dimensions include [[Rule-Learning Tolerance]], challenge tolerance, desired session and total time, pace, competitiveness, platform, required skills, thematic preferences, and the value the project expects from participation.

The composite is a design reference, not a claim that only one kind of person may play. Teams can use it to evaluate feature proposals, choose among competing directions, recruit appropriate testers, and orient new collaborators. Giving the composite a simple persona can make tradeoffs easier to imagine, provided the profile remains grounded in relevant design attributes rather than stereotypes.
