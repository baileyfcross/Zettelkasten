# Regression Models for Aircraft Performance

Regression fits a mathematical relationship between an aircraft performance response and selected predictors. Pressure altitude, temperature, engine speed, weight, and configuration can be evaluated as critical variables, while residuals and probability plots show how closely the fitted model follows the data. A high confidence level does not remove the operational limits stated in [[Pilot Operating Handbook Performance Data]].
