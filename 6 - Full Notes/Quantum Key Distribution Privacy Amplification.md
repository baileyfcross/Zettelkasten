# Quantum Key Distribution Privacy Amplification

Quantum key distribution privacy amplification shortens a reconciled key so that any partial information an eavesdropper may have gained becomes less useful. It occurs after incompatible measurement positions have been discarded and the remaining sample has passed error checking.

The shorter output trades key length for stronger secrecy. It complements [[Quantum Key Distribution Eavesdropping Detection]]: error estimation determines whether the exchange should continue, while privacy amplification reduces residual knowledge that may remain even when the measured disturbance is within the accepted range. In [[BB84 Protocol|BB84]], both steps occur before the undisclosed bits become the final shared key.
