# Amazon Lightsail

Amazon Lightsail packages virtual compute, storage, networking, and predictable monthly pricing for simple workloads. Preconfigured blueprints can launch common websites or development environments without exposing the full complexity of EC2 design.

The simplicity trades away some flexibility and advanced integration. Lightsail is appropriate for small, straightforward deployments; workloads needing granular network, scaling, or service architecture controls may graduate to other AWS services.
