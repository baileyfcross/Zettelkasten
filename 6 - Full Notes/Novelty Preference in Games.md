# Novelty Preference in Games

Novelty preference is the desire for variety, surprise, unfamiliar settings, and new rules or experiences. It is associated with openness to experience in the source's player-motivation model.

Players low in novelty seeking may prefer familiar structures and dependable repetition. A design can signal how much learning and uncertainty it expects rather than assuming every player values constant change.
