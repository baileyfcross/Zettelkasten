# Game Context

Game context is the collection of images, words, animation, sound, and timing that gives abstract rules a recognizable meaning. Numerical positions and collisions become characters, weapons, dangers, and goals that players can reason about.

Context is not merely decoration around mechanics. It supplies metaphors that help players anticipate how [[Game Part|objects]] behave and why a [[Game Rule]] exists, turning mechanical feedback into an intelligible experience.
