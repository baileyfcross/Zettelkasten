# Time Series Classification

Time series classification assigns a label to an ordered sequence of measurements. Useful comparisons must account for both observed values and their temporal arrangement rather than treating positions as an unordered feature set.

Methods may classify through whole-series distances, local shape patterns, decision trees, or fitted generative models. Sampling rate, phase shifts, unequal length, noise, and temporal distortion determine which representation and similarity measure are appropriate.
