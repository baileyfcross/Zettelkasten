# GPS Geometric Dilution of Precision

Geometric dilution of precision describes how satellite arrangement magnifies or suppresses ranging errors in the final position solution. Satellites clustered in one part of the sky produce nearly parallel constraints, so a small pseudorange error can move their intersection substantially. Satellites spread across different directions provide stronger geometry. Dilution of precision does not measure signal error itself; it measures the leverage that geometry gives existing errors.
