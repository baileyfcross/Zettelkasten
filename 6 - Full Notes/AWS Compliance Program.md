# AWS Compliance Program

An AWS compliance program aligns AWS controls and independent assurance with a legal, regulatory, or industry framework. The applicable programs vary by service, region, workload, and customer requirement, and their reports can be obtained through [[AWS Artifact]].

Provider participation is an input to compliance, not an inherited verdict for the customer's system. The customer must confirm scope and implement its portion of controls under the [[AWS Shared Responsibility Model]], including governance, configuration, evidence, and continuing oversight.
