# Physical Dimension of a Game World

The physical dimension of a game world defines its simulated space, including dimensionality, scale, boundaries, and the relative size and speed of objects. These properties determine where entities can move and what spatial actions are possible.

Physical design may distort real proportions or travel times to serve play. The important requirement is internal clarity: players must be able to build a reliable model of reach, movement, obstruction, and distance.
