# CSS Sticky Positioning

A sticky-position box participates in normal flow until scrolling would cross a declared offset within its scroll container. It then behaves like a constrained fixed box, remaining at that inset until the boundary of its containing region forces it onward.

At least one relevant offset is needed to create the sticking threshold. The box keeps its original flow space, unlike an absolutely or fixed-positioned box.
