# Roblox Part Assembly

Roblox construction begins with Parts: geometric building blocks that can be positioned, rotated, scaled, colored, and assigned materials. Several parts can be grouped into a model so they are easier to select, duplicate, organize, and move as one authored object.

Anchoring determines whether physics may move a part, while collision settings determine whether it blocks other objects. A stable structure usually anchors its fixed geometry and leaves only intentionally dynamic pieces free. Visual surface detail can then be added with [[Roblox Decal and Texture Mapping]], while motion between pieces is defined by [[Roblox Attachments and Constraints]].
