# Bayesian Model Averaging Ensemble

A Bayesian model averaging ensemble approximates the Bayes-optimal prediction by combining sampled hypotheses with weights based on posterior support. Models can be generated from random training subsets when the full hypothesis space cannot be enumerated.

Predictions are averaged rather than selecting one fitted model with certainty. In practice, likelihood estimates and prior assumptions determine whether the weighting truly combines models or becomes dominated by one model favored by training error.
