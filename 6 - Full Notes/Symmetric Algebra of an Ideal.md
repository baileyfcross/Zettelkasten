# Symmetric Algebra of an Ideal

The symmetric algebra Sym(I) is the graded algebra generated formally by the elements of an ideal I, subject to the linear relations among those generators. It maps naturally onto the [[Rees Algebra]].

The Rees algebra also enforces nonlinear relations coming from actual products in I. When the natural map is an isomorphism, I is an [[Ideal of Linear Type]].
