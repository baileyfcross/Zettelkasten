# Logic App Recurrence Trigger

A recurrence trigger starts a logic app on a repeated schedule. The campaign workflow uses it to check an Excel table at a chosen interval for rows whose intended publication time has arrived.

A shorter interval makes the automation more responsive but causes more workflow executions. Scheduling is therefore both a behavior decision and an operational-cost decision, even when most runs discover no work.
