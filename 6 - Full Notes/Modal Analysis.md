# Modal Analysis

Modal analysis determines a structure's natural frequencies and corresponding mode shapes. It solves an eigenvalue problem formed from the global stiffness and mass matrices without an arbitrary time-dependent forcing history.

Each mode describes a characteristic deformation pattern, while its eigenvalue determines frequency. Boundary conditions and discretization strongly influence the spectrum and must match the physical support conditions.
