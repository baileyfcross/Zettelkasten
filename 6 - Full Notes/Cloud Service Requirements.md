# Cloud Service Requirements

Cloud service requirements state the capabilities and constraints against which a service and provider will be evaluated. Functional requirements include maturity, features, interoperability, and the usage model; nonfunctional requirements include security, availability, resilience, and network capacity.

Business requirements cover pricing and value, risk, continuity, support, reporting, and billing. Turning these needs into measurable commitments prevents a technology label from substituting for evidence that the service fits its intended purpose.
