# Random Forest Variable Importance

Random forest variable importance ranks predictors by their contribution to the ensemble, commonly through split improvement or the loss caused by permuting a feature. It helps identify influential inputs in a model whose individual trees are numerous.

Importance is not causal evidence and can be distorted by correlated predictors, scale, or category count.
