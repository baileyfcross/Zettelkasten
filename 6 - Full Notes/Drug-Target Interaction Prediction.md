# Drug-Target Interaction Prediction

Drug-target interaction prediction ranks drug and protein pairs by their likelihood of interacting. Computational screening can focus laboratory validation on promising pairs, including possible new uses for known compounds or new targets for existing drugs.

The task is difficult because verified interactions are sparse and an unrecorded pair is not necessarily a true negative. Methods therefore combine the observed [[Drug-Target Interaction Matrix]] with drug similarity, target similarity, and models such as [[Neighborhood-Regularized Logistic Matrix Factorization]].
