# Quadratic Equation

A quadratic equation in one unknown has a squared variable as its highest power and can be written

$$ax^2+bx+c=0,$$

where (a\neq0) and the coefficients are real numbers. The equation may have more than one solution because different numbers can produce the same square.

Common solution methods are [[Solving Quadratics by Factoring]], [[Completing the Square]], and the [[Quadratic Formula]]. Before applying them, simplify the equation and place it in [[Standard Form of a Quadratic Equation]].
