# Requirements Review

A requirements review examines a proposed specification with stakeholders and delivery participants before implementation proceeds. Reviewers look for ambiguity, contradiction, omission, infeasibility, and statements that cannot be verified.

The review is not merely proofreading. It checks whether the documented system still reflects stakeholder needs and whether developers, testers, and operators share the same interpretation of its behavior and constraints.
