# Fraction Operations

Fraction operations preserve the meanings of addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division while expressing results as quotients. Addition and subtraction first use a [[Common Denominator]]:

$$\frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad+bc}{bd},\qquad
\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad-bc}{bd}.$$

Multiplication combines corresponding components, ((a/b)(c/d)=ac/bd). Division by a nonzero fraction multiplies by its [[Reciprocal]], ((a/b)\div(c/d)=ad/bc). Common factors may be cancelled before or after an operation to simplify the result.
