# Markov Cluster Algorithm

The Markov Cluster Algorithm simulates flow on a graph by alternating expansion and inflation of a stochastic matrix. Expansion spreads transition probability along longer paths, while inflation strengthens high-probability flows and suppresses weak ones.

Repeated operations separate regions in which random walks circulate readily. The inflation parameter controls granularity: stronger inflation tends to produce more and smaller communities.
