# Structural Text Representation

A structural text representation describes syntactic or semantic relationships among words, phrases, and sentences. Parse-derived constructions can capture sentence organization that a [[Bag-of-Words Representation]] discards.

These features are useful when style, grammatical competence, authorship, or semantic roles matter. Their greater linguistic depth usually brings language dependence, parsing error, and computational cost, so shallow representations may remain preferable for robust topic-oriented tasks.
