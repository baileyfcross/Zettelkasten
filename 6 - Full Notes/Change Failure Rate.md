# Change Failure Rate

Change failure rate is the proportion of production changes that cause degraded service and require remediation such as rollback, hotfix, or incident response. It evaluates the stability of the delivery process rather than counting all application failures regardless of whether a change introduced them.

The team needs a consistent definition of both a deployment and a failed change so comparisons remain meaningful. A lower rate can reflect better automated tests, smaller changes, safer release strategies, and faster feedback. It should be interpreted with deployment frequency and recovery time so teams do not improve the number simply by shipping less often.
