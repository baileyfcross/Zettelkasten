# Persistent World

A persistent world is a large online game world with no single beginning or ending that continues while individual players join, act, and leave. State and community outlast any one session.

Persistence creates ongoing design and service obligations: economies evolve, new and veteran players coexist, content is consumed, social norms develop, and operators must maintain security and support.
