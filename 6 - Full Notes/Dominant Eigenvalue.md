# Dominant Eigenvalue

A dominant eigenvalue has strictly greater absolute value than every other eigenvalue of a matrix. Repeated multiplication then makes its eigenvector component dominate a generic starting vector.

This behavior supports the [[Power Method]]. If the largest magnitudes are tied or the starting vector has no component in the dominant eigendirection, the expected convergence may fail.
