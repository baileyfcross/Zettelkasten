# Inverse Fourier Transform

The inverse Fourier transform reconstructs a function from its continuous spectrum. It integrates all frequency components with their complex amplitudes and phases, using the sign and normalization convention paired with the forward transform.

The forward and inverse operations together show that the original and spectral descriptions contain equivalent information when the transform conditions hold. Different conventions may distribute factors of $2\pi$ differently, but the paired operations remain mutually inverse.
