# Squeeze Theorem

If $g(x)\leq f(x)\leq h(x)$ near $a$ and both bounding functions approach the same limit $L$, then $f(x)$ also approaches $L$. The squeeze theorem determines limits that resist direct substitution, including foundational trigonometric limits used to derive derivatives of sine and cosine.
