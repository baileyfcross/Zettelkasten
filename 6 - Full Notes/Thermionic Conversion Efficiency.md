# Thermionic Conversion Efficiency

Thermionic conversion efficiency is electrical load power divided by heat supplied to the emitter. It remains below the ideal heat-engine limit set by hot-emitter and cool-collector temperatures because emission, collector rejection, gap transport, radiation, and other losses consume part of the input. The source's worked example contrasts a much lower actual device efficiency with its Carnot limit. High current density alone therefore does not establish high efficiency.
