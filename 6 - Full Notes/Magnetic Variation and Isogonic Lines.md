# Magnetic Variation and Isogonic Lines

Magnetic variation, or declination, is the angle between magnetic north and true north at a location. It changes geographically and slowly over time as the magnetic pole moves. Isogonic lines join equal variation, an agonic line marks zero variation, and related chart lines can show equal annual change, dip, or magnetic intensity.
