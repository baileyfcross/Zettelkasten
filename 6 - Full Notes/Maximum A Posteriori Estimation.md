# Maximum A Posteriori Estimation

Maximum a posteriori estimation selects the value that maximizes the posterior distribution after prior knowledge and evidence are combined. It returns a point estimate rather than preserving every alternative and its probability.

The prior distinguishes it from maximum likelihood estimation. MAP is useful when a decision requires one assignment, but it can hide uncertainty, multiple modes, and the probability mass surrounding the chosen maximum.
