# Photometric Unit Conversion by Area

Photometric quantities in the book include luminous intensity, flux, luminance, and illuminance, each with a different unit expression. Luminance can be given in cd/in² or cd/m², while illuminance can be given in footcandles or lux. In both of its displayed examples the area lies in the denominator, so converting square inches or square feet requires an inverse area factor. The illuminance example gives about 10.76 lux per footcandle. See [[Area Unit Scaling]] and [[Algebraic SI Derived Units]].
