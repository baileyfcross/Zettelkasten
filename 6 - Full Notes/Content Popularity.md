# Content Popularity

Content popularity is the amount of attention or interaction a social item receives. It may be operationalized through views, visits, votes, comments, shares, retweets, or another behavior appropriate to the platform.

The measure is time-dependent: a high final count, a rapid early burst, and sustained attention describe different outcomes. A prediction must therefore state both its popularity measure and its forecast horizon.
