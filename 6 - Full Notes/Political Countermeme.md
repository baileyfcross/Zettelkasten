# Political Countermeme

A political countermeme adopts the form of an existing political meme while reversing or contesting its message. The response gains intelligibility from the shared template, making disagreement visible as a direct relationship between versions.

The 53 percent images that answered Occupy's 99 percent testimonials reused handwritten personal narratives but changed their explanation of economic responsibility. Such opposition shows that a meme is not a unified ideological voice. Its format can become a contested arena in which rival groups appropriate the same participatory grammar.
