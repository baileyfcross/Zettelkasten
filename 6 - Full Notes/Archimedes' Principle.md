# Archimedes' Principle

Archimedes' principle states that a body wholly or partly immersed in a fluid experiences an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of fluid it displaces. The force results from greater [[Hydrostatic Pressure]] on lower surfaces than on upper ones. Floating equilibrium occurs when buoyancy equals the object's weight.
