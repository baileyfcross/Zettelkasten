# Proactive AI Ethics

Proactive AI ethics considers moral and social consequences during research, design, and policy formation rather than waiting for a widely deployed system to cause harm. Early work can influence goals, data practices, architecture, and whether an application should proceed at all.

Forecasting is imperfect because new technologies have unintended and context-dependent effects. Concrete scenarios, stakeholder participation, staged testing, and continued monitoring help without pretending the future is fully knowable. Proactivity is therefore an iterative responsibility: anticipate what can be anticipated, preserve the ability to revise, and avoid making harmful infrastructure difficult to reverse.
