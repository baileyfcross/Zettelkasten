# Deontological Ethics

Deontological ethics evaluates an action through duties and moral rules that should apply consistently, emphasizing the intention to act from a defensible obligation rather than only the result that happens to follow. A proposed rule must be capable of guiding comparable cases, not merely excuse a preferred exception.

In a software decision, this view asks what the professional is obligated to disclose, protect, or refuse even when violating the duty might appear convenient. It complements [[Consequentialism]], which instead foregrounds the expected benefits and harms of the available actions.
