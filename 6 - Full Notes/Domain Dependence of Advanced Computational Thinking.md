# Domain Dependence of Advanced Computational Thinking

Advanced computational thinking depends on the application domain. Programming skill alone does not supply the fluid dynamics needed for aircraft simulation, the biology needed for genome analysis, or the institutional knowledge needed for hospital software.

Productive collaboration therefore requires learning enough of the domain to recognize meaningful variables, constraints, and evidence. Computational methods add power to domain expertise; they do not replace it.
