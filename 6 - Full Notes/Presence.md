# Presence

Presence is the feeling of being in a [[Virtual Environment]] rather than in the physical world. It is an important but incompletely understood part of [[User Experience]] in virtual systems.

Presence can be assessed through rating scales and questionnaires, responses to controlled changes in the environment, physiological reactions, behavior toward virtual events, memory, or performance on tasks that depend on feeling present. Each method captures only part of the concept, and there is no universally accepted single measure.

Display characteristics, natural movement, [[Spatial Audio]], consistent [[Feedback]], and interaction quality can all influence presence.
