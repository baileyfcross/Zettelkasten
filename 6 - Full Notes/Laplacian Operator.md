# Laplacian Operator

The Laplacian of a scalar field is the divergence of its gradient, written $\nabla^2 f$. In Cartesian coordinates it is the sum of the second partial derivatives with respect to the spatial coordinates.

It compares a field's value with its local spatial variation and appears in equations governing potentials, diffusion, waves, and quantum states. Its coordinate expression changes in cylindrical or spherical systems even though the underlying geometric operator is the same.
