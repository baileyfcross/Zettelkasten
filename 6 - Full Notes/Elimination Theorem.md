# Elimination Theorem

The elimination theorem states that if G is a Gröbner basis in lexicographic order x₁ > ··· > xₙ, then the elements of G involving only xₗ₊₁,…,xₙ form a Gröbner basis for the ℓ-th [[Elimination Ideal]].

The theorem turns variable removal into basis selection after one computation. Its geometric counterpart describes the Zariski closure of a projected affine variety.
