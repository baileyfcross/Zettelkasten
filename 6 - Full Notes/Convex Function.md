# Convex Function

A function is convex on a [[Convex Set]] when every chord between two points on its graph lies on or above the graph. Equivalently, for $0\leq t\leq1$, $f(tx+(1-t)y)\leq tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)$.

For a differentiable convex function, local minima are global minima. A twice-differentiable function is convex where its [[Hessian Matrix]] is positive semidefinite.
