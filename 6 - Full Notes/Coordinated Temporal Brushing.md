# Coordinated Temporal Brushing

Coordinated temporal brushing selects an observation, object, or interval in one view and highlights the corresponding records in another. A point chosen in an [[Animated Bubble Chart]] might reveal its trajectory or values in an accompanying [[Interactive Time-Series Plot]]. Coordination links complementary encodings without forcing them into one crowded panel. It depends on stable record identities and a consistent selection state across all views.
