# R Strip Chart

An R strip chart draws individual one-dimensional observations along a numeric axis. Jittering or stacking separates repeated and nearby values so the display retains sample size and clusters that a summary statistic would hide.

The method is most readable for small or moderate samples. Grouped strip charts can compare distributions, but heavy overlap signals that a [[Box Plot]] or density-oriented display should accompany the points.
