# Adaptive Mesh Refinement Clustering

Adaptive mesh refinement clustering begins with a coarse spatial grid and subdivides only cells whose density or variation warrants closer inspection. Computational effort is concentrated near complex cluster boundaries and dense regions.

Adaptive refinement can retain more detail than a uniform grid with a similar cell budget. The refinement criterion must be designed carefully because an early coarse summary can hide a small structure that would have justified subdivision.
