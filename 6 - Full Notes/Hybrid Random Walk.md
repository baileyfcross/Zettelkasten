# Hybrid Random Walk

A hybrid random walk moves within user or item domains and across user-item links in a star-structured graph. Steady-state probabilities estimate user tie strength, item relevance, and the likelihood of adoption or rejection.

Updates combine routes through similar users, similar items, labels, and observed cross-domain behaviors. This transfers auxiliary knowledge into a target recommendation while addressing [[Behavioral Data Sparsity]] and the [[Cold-Start Problem]].
