# LLM Security Policy Recommendation

An LLM security policy recommendation synthesizes observed risk, architecture, incidents, and existing controls into a proposed change to security rules or procedures. It can translate technical evidence into policy language and identify gaps across large document collections.

Recommendations must be reviewed for operational impact, legal authority, conflicts, and unintended denial of service. The model may advise, but approved policy-as-code and accountable owners should enforce the final decision.
