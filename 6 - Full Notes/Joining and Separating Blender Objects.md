# Joining and Separating Blender Objects

Joining combines selected compatible objects into one object, while separating moves selected components of a mesh into a distinct object. These operations change object boundaries rather than merely changing selection.

When geometry is separated, its origin may remain at the source object's origin. Setting the origin to geometry recalculates a center for the new object, making later rotation and scaling behave around the separated form itself.
