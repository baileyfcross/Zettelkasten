# Ideal Quotient

For ideals I and J in a ring R, the ideal quotient (I:J) consists of elements r such that rJ is contained in I. It measures which multipliers force all of J into I.

The special case (0:J) is the annihilator of J. Replacing J by successive powers produces the [[Saturation of an Ideal]], which collects elements that enter I after multiplication by a sufficiently high power.
