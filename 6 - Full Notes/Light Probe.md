# Light Probe

A light probe captures illumination arriving from many directions at a point in a real scene. A reflective sphere, calibrated camera arrangement, or related method can turn the observed light into an environment representation for virtual rendering.

The probe samples illumination at one place and time, so moving lights, outdoor change, and spatially varying conditions require new observations or a richer model. Its purpose is to make virtual shading and reflections agree with the environment.
