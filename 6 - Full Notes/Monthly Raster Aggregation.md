# Monthly Raster Aggregation

Monthly raster aggregation groups the layers of a daily [[Space-Time Raster]] by calendar month and summarizes each cell, commonly with a mean. It reduces hundreds of snapshots to twelve panels that can fit on one page and highlights seasonal structure. The operation discards daily extremes and within-month sequences, so the aggregate should be presented as a reduction rather than a replacement for the original time-indexed layers.
