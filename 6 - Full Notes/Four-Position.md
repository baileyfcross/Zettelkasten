# Four-Position

Four-position is the [[Four-Vector]] that locates a [[Spacetime Event]]. It combines the event's spatial coordinates with a time component scaled by the speed of light, commonly written $X=(ct,\mathbf{x})$ or with the components in another fixed order.

The Minkowski norm of the difference between two four-positions is their [[Invariant Spacetime Interval]]. Although a [[Lorentz Transformation]] changes the temporal and spatial components, it leaves this norm unchanged.

Differentiating four-position along an object's [[Worldline]] with respect to [[Proper Time]] produces [[Four-Velocity]]. This makes four-position the starting point for relativistic kinematics just as ordinary position is the starting point for Newtonian velocity and acceleration.
