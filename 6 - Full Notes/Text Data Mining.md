# Text Data Mining

Text data mining applies semiautomated computational methods to organize, classify, cluster, retrieve, and discover patterns in large text collections. It often converts language into numerical representations so algorithms can compare documents or tokens.

The approach increases scale and speed, but its results inherit the assumptions used to remove words, define similarity, and represent order. It is strongest when those reductions match the research question and are checked against linguistic context.

A practical workflow moves from corpus construction through [[Text Processing Pipeline Order|ordered enrichment and preprocessing]] to a [[Document-Term Matrix]], model fitting, evaluation, and visualization. Search logs, reviews, news pages, and tag co-occurrences show that “text” may include behavior and relationships as well as prose, so the representation must match the claim being made.
