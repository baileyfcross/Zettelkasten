# Projection of an Affine Variety

Projection sends a point in Kⁿ to the coordinates that remain after selected variables are removed. Applied to an [[Affine Variety]], it records which remaining coordinate values can be extended to a full solution.

The image may omit boundary points and therefore fail to be Zariski closed. Its [[Zariski Closure]] is described by the corresponding [[Elimination Ideal]].
