# Cloud Storage Service

A cloud storage service supplies durable storage over a network as an infrastructure capability. It can hold media, documents, correspondence, backups, and application data while letting authorized users or applications reach them from multiple places and devices.

Providers can operate redundant disks and monitoring at a scale that is difficult for a small consumer to reproduce. The consumer must still plan encryption, access control, backup independence, restoration, retention, and the consequences of losing the provider.
