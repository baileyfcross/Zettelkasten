# Perceived Difficulty

Perceived difficulty is how hard a challenge feels to a particular player. It combines intrinsic skill, stress, power supplied by the game, and the player's in-game experience with similar challenges.

Because native talent and prior gaming experience also differ, designers cannot predict perception exactly. Difficulty modes, gradual teaching, observation, and testing with representative players help manage the range.
