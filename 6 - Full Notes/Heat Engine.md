# Heat Engine

A heat engine operates cyclically between a hot reservoir and a cold reservoir. It absorbs heat $Q_H$, converts part to useful work $W$, and rejects the remainder $Q_C$, so $W=Q_H-Q_C$. Its efficiency $W/Q_H$ is always below one and is bounded by the reservoir temperatures through the [[Second Law of Thermodynamics]].
