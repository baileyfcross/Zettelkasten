# Runge-Kutta Method

A Runge-Kutta method combines several evaluations of $f(x,y)$ within one step so its update matches a Taylor expansion to a chosen order without calculating higher derivatives explicitly.

The first-order member is [[Euler Method]], the modified Euler formula is second order, and [[Runge Method]] is third order. These self-starting methods work for both linear and nonlinear equations and are commonly used to generate values for multistep predictors.
