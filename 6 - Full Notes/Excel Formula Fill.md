# Excel Formula Fill

Excel formula filling copies one calculation across a selected row, column, or range while adjusting relative references. A user can drag the fill handle or select a destination range and enter the formula with Ctrl+Enter. This avoids rebuilding the same logic for every record.

Filling is reliable only when the formula's relative, absolute, and mixed references express the intended movement. After filling, checking the first, middle, and last copied formulas is a useful audit because boundary errors and unintended reference shifts may not be visible from displayed results alone. [[Excel Relative Cell Reference]] and [[Excel Absolute and Mixed Cell Reference]] provide the underlying rules.
