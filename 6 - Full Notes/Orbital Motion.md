# Orbital Motion

An orbit is continuous [[Free Fall]] in which gravity bends motion around a central body. For a circular orbit, setting gravitational force equal to the required centripetal force gives $v=\sqrt{GM/r}$ and $T^2=4\pi^2r^3/(GM)$. Higher circular orbits have lower speed but longer period.
