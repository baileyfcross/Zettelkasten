# Hubble's Law

Hubble's law relates the recession speed of a sufficiently distant galaxy to its distance: $v=H_0d$. Spectral redshift estimates recession, while calibrated distance indicators estimate $d$. The relationship shows cosmic expansion rather than ordinary motion away from a unique central point in space.
