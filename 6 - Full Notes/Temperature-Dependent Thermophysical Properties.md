# Temperature-Dependent Thermophysical Properties

Density, conductivity, heat capacity, viscosity, and emissivity may change over the temperature range traversed by a model. Replacing those relationships with constants can hide feedback between the evolving temperature and the coefficients governing its evolution.

Tables, interpolation functions, or analytic functions can represent measured dependence. The added nonlinearity may make convergence harder, so the model should compare the variable-property result with a justified constant-property baseline and inspect [[Thermal Model Uncertainty from Input Properties|input uncertainty]].
