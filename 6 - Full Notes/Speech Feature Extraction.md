# Speech Feature Extraction

Speech feature extraction converts a digitized audio signal into a sequence of compact measurements suited to recognition. It divides speech into short [[Speech Frame|frames]] and summarizes the spectral energy in each interval.

The result is a sequence of [[Feature Vector|feature vectors]] that preserves information useful for distinguishing sounds while discarding much of the raw waveform's size and irrelevant detail.
