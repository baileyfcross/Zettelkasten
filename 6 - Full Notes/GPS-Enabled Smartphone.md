# GPS-Enabled Smartphone

A GPS-enabled smartphone combines satellite measurements with cellular assistance, digital maps, inertial sensors, network data, and application software. Assisted GPS can provide approximate time, location, or satellite data that reduces acquisition time, while Wi-Fi and cell-site observations help when satellite signals are weak. The social impact comes from this convergence: navigation, traffic, nearby services, ride sharing, photography, and location sharing all reuse one continuously available position layer.
