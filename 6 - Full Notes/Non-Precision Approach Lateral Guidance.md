# Non-Precision Approach Lateral Guidance

A non-precision instrument approach provides lateral guidance without a fully coupled vertical path. The crew must manage altitude constraints, step-down fixes, and the transition toward the runway while avoiding controlled flight into terrain. A computed continuous path such as [[Constant Descent Final Approach]] can replace repeated level-offs when procedure and conditions allow.
