# Zariski Topology

The Zariski topology on [[Spectrum of a Ring|Spec(R)]] declares V(I) = {p : I ⊆ p} closed for each ideal I. A basic open set D(f) consists of primes that do not contain f.

This topology is designed around polynomial vanishing rather than metric distance. The [[Structure Sheaf]] assigns the localized ring R_f to D(f), linking open regions to algebraic inversion.
