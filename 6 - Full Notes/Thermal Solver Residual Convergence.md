# Thermal Solver Residual Convergence

A numerical solver substitutes an approximate field into the assembled equations and measures the remaining imbalance as a residual. Iteration continues until the residual meets a defined tolerance or another stopping condition is reached.

Convergence only shows that the discretized equations were solved to the chosen criterion; it does not prove that the equations, mesh, properties, or conditions represent reality. Residual plots therefore complement, rather than replace, [[Verification versus Validation in Thermal Modeling|verification and validation]].
