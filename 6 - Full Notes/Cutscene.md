# Cutscene

A cutscene is a section of a game devoted primarily to showing noninteractive story material. It can use animation, dialogue, still images, or other presentation and often appears between playable sections as an intermission.

Cutscenes can provide context and rest from [[Game Resistance|resistance]], but they place playing and watching beside one another rather than fully integrating them. Requiring timed input inside a cutscene adds exertion without necessarily making its choices meaningful.
