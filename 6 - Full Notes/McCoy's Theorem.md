# McCoy's Theorem

McCoy's theorem gives a determinantal condition for a homogeneous linear system over a commutative ring to have a nontrivial solution relative to a module. The relevant maximal minors must have a nonzero common annihilator on that module.

The theorem replaces field-based rank reasoning with ideals of minors and annihilators. It provides a foundation for exactness tests involving maps of free modules.
