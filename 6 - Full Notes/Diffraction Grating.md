# Diffraction Grating

A diffraction grating contains many equally spaced parallel slits. Their diffracted waves interfere to form narrow principal maxima satisfying $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$, where $d$ is slit spacing. More illuminated slits sharpen the maxima, allowing wavelength measurement and separation of spectral components with greater resolving power.
