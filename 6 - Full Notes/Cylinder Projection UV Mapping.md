# Cylinder Projection UV Mapping

Cylinder Projection unwraps side faces around a cylindrical direction while mapping end regions separately. It suits columns, pipes, bottles, and other assets dominated by one rotational axis.

Projection direction, alignment, seam location, radius, aspect correction, and bounds determine the island arrangement. A seam is still needed to open the side wall, and end caps may overlap or fall outside the desired texture area if left unchecked.
