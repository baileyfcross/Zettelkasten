# Virtual Assistant Pipeline

A virtual assistant pipeline transforms a user's signal through a sequence of specialized modules. Audio passes from conversion and [[Automatic Speech Recognition]] to [[Natural Language Understanding]] and a [[Dialog Manager]], after which [[Natural Language Generation]] and [[Text-to-Speech]] can produce a spoken reply.

The pipeline is also connected to external services that supply information or carry out actions. Its modular form allows individual components to be improved while keeping their input and output contracts stable.
