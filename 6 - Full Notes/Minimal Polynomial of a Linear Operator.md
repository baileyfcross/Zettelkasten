# Minimal Polynomial of a Linear Operator

The minimal polynomial $\mu_T$ of a finite-dimensional linear operator is the unique monic polynomial of least degree satisfying $\mu_T(T)=0$. Every other polynomial that annihilates $T$ is divisible by $\mu_T$.

It has the same distinct roots as the characteristic polynomial, though possibly lower multiplicities. Its factor exponents determine the largest Jordan blocks, and the [[Cayley-Hamilton Theorem]] guarantees that it divides the characteristic polynomial.
