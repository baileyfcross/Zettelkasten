# Thermoelectric Generator

A thermoelectric generator places p-type and n-type elements between a hot and a cold junction. The [[Seebeck Effect]] makes an electrical potential from that temperature difference and a connected load receives power. Heat also leaks by conduction, and current incurs [[Joule Heating]] and other thermoelectric heat exchanges. Material properties and load resistance jointly determine efficiency and output.
