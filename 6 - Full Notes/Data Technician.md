# Data Technician

A data technician prepares the foundations of analysis by acquiring, storing, cleaning, and organizing data. Reliable technical handling matters because errors in formats, types, or provenance can invalidate every later model.

This role anchors the first stage in the book's progression from raw data through analysis and mining to data science.
