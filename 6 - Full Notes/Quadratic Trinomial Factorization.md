# Quadratic Trinomial Factorization

Quadratic trinomial factorization rewrites (ax^2+bx+c) as a product of two [[Binomial]]s when such factors exist. In the monic case,

$$x^2+bx+c=(x+p)(x+q),$$

so the needed numbers satisfy (p+q=b) and (pq=c). For (x^2+5x+6), the numbers are (2) and (3), giving ((x+2)(x+3)).

When (a\neq1), possible factor pairs of both (a) and (c) must be considered. Expansion verifies the chosen factorization.
