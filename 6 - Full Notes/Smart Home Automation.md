# Smart Home Automation

Smart home automation connects lighting, climate controls, locks, security devices, appliances, irrigation, entertainment, and other household systems so that they can be monitored or controlled through schedules, sensor events, apps, and voice commands. The objective is not merely remote control but coordinated behavior that improves convenience, safety, or energy efficiency.

An irrigation controller illustrates the difference: instead of following only a timer, it can combine plant and soil settings with current weather and optional moisture sensors, then decide when and how long to water. Useful automation therefore depends on context, dependable rules, and [[Smart Home Platform Interoperability]], not just adding a network connection to each appliance.
