# Alpha Milestone

The alpha milestone is a checkpoint at which a game is largely playable and its major systems, features, and assets are present. Important bugs may remain, but the core and secondary gameplay have been tested beyond the development team.

After alpha, emphasis shifts from adding major features toward balancing, testing, and fixing. Treating alpha as a [[Stage Gate]] forces the team to judge the game's actual experience rather than continue solely because much work has already been invested.
