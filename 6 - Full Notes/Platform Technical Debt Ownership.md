# Platform Technical Debt Ownership

Platform technical debt ownership means that the obligations and risks created by architecture choices are visible to a responsible team. Ownership includes routine maintenance, unexpected dependency failures, security updates, user migration, and the operational cost of keeping a component alive.

Debt should be integrated into normal planning rather than held in a list that always loses to feature work. A team can review it during iterations, reserve capacity for reduction, and rank it beside product work according to risk and toil. The architect makes the debt legible, but sustainable ownership is collective rather than heroic.
