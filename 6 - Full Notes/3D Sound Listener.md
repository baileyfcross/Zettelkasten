# 3D Sound Listener

A 3D sound listener represents the position and orientation from which the audio scene is heard. It is commonly associated with the camera or player, though the desired auditory viewpoint may differ from the visual one.

The audio engine compares the listener with each [[3D Sound Emitter]] to calculate direction, distance, relative velocity, and environmental effects for playback.
