# Damping

Damping removes mechanical energy from an oscillator through resistive forces, causing amplitude to decrease with time. Light damping leaves several oscillations before decay; critical damping returns the system to equilibrium as quickly as possible without oscillation; overdamping returns more slowly. Damping also broadens and lowers the response near [[Resonance]].
