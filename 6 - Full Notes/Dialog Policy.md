# Dialog Policy

A dialog policy maps the current conversational state to the assistant's next action. The policy encodes choices such as whether to ask, confirm, execute, wait, or recover.

A policy can be hand-authored as a [[Finite-State Dialog Manager]] or learned through [[Reinforcement Learning]]. In either case, it must balance task completion, efficiency, error risk, and the user's interaction expectations.
