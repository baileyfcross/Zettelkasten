# Robust Statistical Summary

A robust statistical summary changes relatively little when a small number of observations are extreme. Medians, interquartile ranges, ranks, and rank correlations can remain representative when means, variances, and ordinary correlations are pulled sharply by an [[Outlier]].

Robustness protects against limited contamination rather than making data quality irrelevant. Extreme observations should still be investigated because they may reveal an error, a distinct population, or a scientifically important event.
