# Social Platform Data Breach Risk

A social-platform breach can expose more than account credentials because a platform often holds identity details, relationship graphs, private communication, and behavior history. The source uses major platform incidents to show that centralized collections become valuable targets and that weaknesses in connected applications can expand the affected population.

An enterprise application should therefore treat data received from a social API as sensitive after it leaves the provider. Data minimization, protected transport and storage, narrowly scoped tokens, revocation, monitoring, and incident preparation reduce the consequences of a compromised integration. [[Enterprise Social Privacy Exposure]] concerns legitimate collection and reuse; breach risk concerns unauthorized access to the same accumulated information.
