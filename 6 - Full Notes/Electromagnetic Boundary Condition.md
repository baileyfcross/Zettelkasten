# Electromagnetic Boundary Condition

An electromagnetic boundary condition specifies how field components behave across an interface between materials. Maxwell's integral laws determine continuity or jumps in tangential electric and magnetic fields and normal electric and magnetic fluxes.

Surface charge and surface current create specific discontinuities. Perfect conductors impose stronger conditions, including zero internal electric field, that shape the neighboring solution.

Practical RF models can assign different boundary roles within one geometry. The book applies an [[Electromagnetic Impedance Boundary Condition]] to finite-conductivity waveguide walls while reserving the two end faces for numeric ports, so wall confinement and loss remain distinct from wave excitation and measurement.
