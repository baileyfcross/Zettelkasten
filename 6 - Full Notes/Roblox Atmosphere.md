# Roblox Atmosphere

The Atmosphere object changes how a Roblox world appears across distance. Density, offset, haze, color, glare, and decay shape the amount, placement, tint, and directional character of the aerial effect.

Atmosphere is useful for establishing scale and mood because distant objects can fade or shift color rather than remaining as visually sharp as nearby geometry. It should be tuned together with [[Roblox World Lighting]] and [[Roblox Skybox]]: the three systems share the final view, and extreme settings in one can overwhelm the contribution of the others.
