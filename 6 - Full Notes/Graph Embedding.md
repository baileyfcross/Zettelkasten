# Graph Embedding

A graph embedding maps nodes or other graph objects into dense vectors while preserving selected structural relationships. Distances or directions in the vector space then become usable by standard machine-learning methods.

Different objectives preserve neighborhoods, random-walk co-occurrence, global proximity, or labels. An embedding is therefore not a neutral compression: its training signal determines which graph similarities survive.
