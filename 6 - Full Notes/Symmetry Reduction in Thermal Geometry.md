# Symmetry Reduction in Thermal Geometry

Geometric symmetry can reduce a model to a fraction of the physical object when temperature and heat flux repeat across the omitted sectors. A symmetry boundary prevents normal heat flow across the cut plane and represents the mirrored continuation of the domain.

The reduction is invalid if an asymmetric source, material, flow, or boundary condition breaks the repeated pattern. When applicable, it permits finer [[Thermal Mesh Refinement|local resolution]] or shorter solution time without discarding distinct physical behavior.
