# Localization of a Module

The localization S⁻¹M of an R-module M consists of fractions m/s with s in a multiplicatively closed set. It is naturally a module over the localized ring S⁻¹R.

There is a canonical isomorphism S⁻¹M ≅ S⁻¹R ⊗_R M. This identifies module localization as [[Scalar Extension for Modules]] and explains its exactness.
