# Software Development Life Cycle

A software development life cycle organizes a product's passage through conception, requirements, design, coding and debugging, testing, release, maintenance or evolution, and eventual retirement. A process may visit these activities once in sequence or repeat subsets of them between releases, but omitting an activity leaves a corresponding quality question unanswered.

The life cycle is distinct from any one methodology. [[Plan-Based Software Development]] places stronger phase and sign-off boundaries around it, while [[Agile Development]] repeats a small version of the cycle to deliver and learn from working increments.
