# Individual View

An individual view presents shared augmented content from one participant's physical viewpoint and may also contain information private to that participant. Head or device tracking generates the correct perspective even when several users surround the same objects.

Individual views support role-specific detail and avoid forcing everyone to see one projected image. Collaboration cues must still reveal relevant actions and attention so private presentation does not remove awareness of the group.
