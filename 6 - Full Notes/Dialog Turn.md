# Dialog Turn

A dialog turn is one participant's contribution before control passes to the other participant. In a voice interface, turn boundaries are inferred from speaking, silence, prompts, and interruption rather than explicit message submission.

The [[Dialog Manager]] uses turn state to decide when to listen, respond, wait, or accept an [[Interjection]]. Poor turn handling can make an accurate assistant feel slow or unresponsive.
