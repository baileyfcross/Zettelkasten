# Blender Motion Tracking Workspace

Blender's VFX start file opens in a Motion Tracking workspace centered on the Movie Clip Editor. Related Masking, Compositing, and Rendering workspaces follow the same shot through later stages.

Loading a clip makes tracking tools available, and Set Scene Frames can match the Blender frame range to the footage. Keeping a shot short limits unnecessary calculation and concentrates the solve on the portion that will receive visual effects.
