# Action-Feedback Interactivity

Action-feedback interactivity is the fastest loop between a player's physical action and the game's sensory response. It operates at subsecond timescales and supports game feel, reflexive attention, and moment-to-moment control.

Feedback below roughly one tenth of a second tends to feel immediate, while longer delays weaken the felt connection between action and effect. This loop can sit inside slower [[Cognitive Interactivity|cognitive]] and [[Core Gameplay Loop|core]] loops.
