# Continuum Flow and Knudsen Number

The Knudsen number compares molecular mean free path with a characteristic geometric length. A very small value supports treating air as a continuous fluid, while larger values move through slip flow toward free-molecular behavior. The continuum assumption underlies ordinary aircraft analyses of [[Boundary Layers and Flow Separation]], pressure, lift, and drag.
