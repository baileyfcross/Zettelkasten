# Nodal Degree of Freedom

A nodal degree of freedom is an independent field component assigned to a finite element node. A bar node may translate along one axis, a truss node may translate in two directions, and a beam node may carry both transverse displacement and slope.

The degree-of-freedom definition determines the size and ordering of element and global vectors. Boundary conditions prescribe or constrain selected degrees of freedom before the unknown system is solved.
