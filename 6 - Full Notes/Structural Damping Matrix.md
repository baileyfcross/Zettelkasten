# Structural Damping Matrix

A structural damping matrix represents mechanisms that dissipate vibrational energy and oppose motion. It contributes a force proportional to nodal velocity in the standard linear dynamic equation.

The primer identifies damping as an additional matrix needed beyond stiffness and mass for general vibration analysis. Its presence limits resonance and governs decay in transient motion.
