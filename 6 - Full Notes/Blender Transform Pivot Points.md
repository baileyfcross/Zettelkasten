# Blender Transform Pivot Points

The transform pivot determines the center around which a selection rotates or scales. Blender can use a median point, individual origins, the active element, the 3D cursor, or a bounding-box center, producing different motion from the same selected objects or components.

Pivot choice separates what is transformed from where the transformation is anchored. This is useful for radial arrangements, independent scaling of many elements, hinge-like rotations, and transformations aligned to a deliberately placed cursor.
