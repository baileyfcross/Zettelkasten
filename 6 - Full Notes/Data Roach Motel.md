# Data Roach Motel

A data roach motel is a service into which personal data can enter but from which it cannot meaningfully leave. The provider accepts continuous contributions yet offers no adequate export, interface, or way to recover the record in a reusable form.

The arrangement creates risk when the provider discontinues a product, changes its business model, or denies access needed for a new question. It also raises switching costs because leaving means abandoning a history created through the user's activity and attention.

The term draws attention to control rather than storage alone. [[Data Portability]] and documented access prevent lock-in only when the exported record includes enough detail to preserve its use outside the original platform.
