# Divisive Clustering

Divisive clustering begins with the complete data set in one cluster and repeatedly splits groups until every observation is separate or another stopping condition is reached.

A divisive method must choose both which cluster to divide and how to divide it. Binary-feature methods and full-variable methods impose different structural assumptions on that choice.
