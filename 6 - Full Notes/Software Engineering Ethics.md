# Software Engineering Ethics

Software engineering ethics concerns the decisions a computing professional makes when software can affect an employer, client, user, coworker, or wider society. Technical authority creates opportunities both to prevent harm and to conceal risks that other stakeholders cannot independently evaluate.

Ethical practice therefore goes beyond obeying a direct instruction. It includes disclosing material defects and conflicts, respecting privacy and property, treating people fairly, accepting responsibility for supervised work, and balancing loyalty to an employer with obligations to the public.
