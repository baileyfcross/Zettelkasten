# Internet Governance Transition

Internet governance shifted from United States defense management to the National Science Foundation and later toward commerce agencies and specialized coordinating institutions. Responsibilities included addressing, names, standards, and backbone policy.

The transition reflects that the Internet is a political and institutional system as well as a technical one. Control of shared identifiers and interconnection rules affects who can participate.
