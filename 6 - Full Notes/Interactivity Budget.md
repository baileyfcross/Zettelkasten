# Interactivity Budget

An interactivity budget is the finite amount of perceptual, cognitive, social, and emotional effort a player is willing and able to devote at a moment. Fast reflexive interactions often consume capacity before slower reflective ones can operate.

Designers allocate this budget by deciding which loops are intense at the same time and by separating competing demands across time. Exceeding it creates confusion or exhaustion; using too little can produce boredom and weak [[Engagement]].
