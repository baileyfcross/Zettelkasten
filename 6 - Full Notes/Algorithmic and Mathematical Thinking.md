# Algorithmic and Mathematical Thinking

Algorithmic thinking differs from mathematical thinking by emphasizing executable processes, information structures, changing state, and reductions of complex tasks into connected simple operations. It also moves deliberately among levels of abstraction.

Mathematical proof remains important, but a computational designer must additionally invent representations and notations that a machine can act on. The concern is not only whether a relationship is true, but how a process realizes it.
