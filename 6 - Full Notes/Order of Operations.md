# Order of Operations

The order of operations makes an unparenthesized expression unambiguous. Grouped expressions are evaluated first, followed by exponents, multiplication and division from left to right, then addition and subtraction from left to right. Parentheses can override this convention and expose which subexpression should be treated as a single quantity.
