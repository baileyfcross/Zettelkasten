# Particle in a Box

The particle-in-a-box model confines a quantum particle to a finite interval with impenetrable walls. The wavefunction vanishes at the boundaries and outside the box, so only standing-wave solutions satisfying both endpoint conditions are allowed.

Those conditions quantize the wave number and make the energy proportional to the square of a positive integer. The lowest energy is nonzero, and the normalized stationary states form an orthogonal basis for more general states inside the box.
