# Data Ownership Right

A data ownership right would give a person or organization property-like control over data, potentially including powers to exclude others, transfer access, or seek remedies against interference. The source finds no general, specific property right in data under the Swiss and European rules it examines.

The term is still useful in a broader sense because control can arise through contracts, confidentiality, intellectual-property rules, tort remedies, or technical access. Each mechanism supplies different powers and binds different parties.
