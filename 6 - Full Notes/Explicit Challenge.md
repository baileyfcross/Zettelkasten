# Explicit Challenge

An explicit challenge is one the game states directly to the player. Victory conditions, mission objectives, and immediate instructions commonly make a desired outcome unambiguous.

Explicitness helps players plan, but it does not require explaining the solution. The interface should distinguish the goal from optional guidance and from the actions available to pursue it.
