# Algorithmizing as a General Thinking Tool

Algorithmizing is the effort to express a process precisely enough that an automatic computer can perform it. Early computing educators argued that coding a process forces deeper understanding by exposing ambiguity and hidden assumptions.

The discipline is valuable within suitable domains, but it is not a universal route to knowledge. A precise procedure still depends on correct domain concepts and may omit judgment that cannot be formalized.
