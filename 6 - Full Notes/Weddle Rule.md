# Weddle Rule

Weddle's rule is a seven-point closed quadrature formula spanning six equal subintervals:

$$\int_{x_0}^{x_6}f(x)\,dx\approx\frac{3h}{10}(y_0+5y_1+y_2+6y_3+y_4+5y_5+y_6).$$

A composite application therefore requires the number of subintervals to be a multiple of six. The formula comes from retaining finite-difference terms through the sixth order with a small coefficient adjustment.
