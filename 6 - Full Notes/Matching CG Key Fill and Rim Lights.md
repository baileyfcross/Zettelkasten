# Matching CG Key Fill and Rim Lights

Key, fill, and rim lights should be placed for the photographed shot rather than for an attractive standalone CG image. Match their apparent direction, contrast, color, and shadow quality to the plate. Background light reflected from a nearby surface may demand another fill even if the real source is outside frame. The book advises controlling foreground lighting separately from a keyable background during shooting so reflected screen color does not compromise extraction. The analysis begins with [[Plate Lighting Reference for CG]].
