# Fixed-Interval Reinforcement Schedule

A fixed-interval reinforcement schedule rewards the first qualifying response after a known amount of time. Activity therefore tends to be low after a reward and rise sharply as the next interval approaches.

An auction deadline illustrates the pattern: attention and repeated checking accelerate near the closing time. The idle period after each reward can also encourage extinction, making fixed intervals poorly suited to behaviors that should remain steady.
