# AIOps as Action Intelligence

AIOps turns operational information into actions by applying algorithms and AI to context-enriched observability data. It can detect abnormal behavior, connect related evidence, explain a likely cause, estimate impact, and recommend or trigger a bounded response.

The useful unit is not a model-generated alert but a decision supported by signals, dependencies, objectives, and change history. Poor telemetry merely lets automation produce uncertain conclusions faster, so AIOps begins with observability quality and ends with measurable operational outcomes.
