# Core Mechanics

Core mechanics are the algorithms and data that implement rules operating throughout a game. They maintain state, accept actions, produce consequences, present active challenges, detect success or failure, control mode changes, and support artificial opponents and story events.

The source groups major mechanical work into physics, internal economies, progression, tactical maneuvering, and social interaction. Level-specific scripts or behavior unique to one temporary character are not necessarily core mechanics.
