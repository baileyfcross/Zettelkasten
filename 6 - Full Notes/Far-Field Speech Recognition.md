# Far-Field Speech Recognition

Far-field speech recognition handles speech captured at a distance from the microphone. The signal is weakened and mixed with room reverberation, background noise, and voices or sounds from other directions.

Microphone arrays and [[Beamforming]] can improve the target signal, but distance remains a major source of [[Acoustic Variability]] for home assistants and social robots.
