# Behavior Recognition

Behavior recognition infers which candidate behavioral model best explains an observed sequence of actions or states. A model-choice variable indexes the alternatives, and evidence updates its posterior probability.

Recognition can remain uncertain when several behaviors explain the same partial sequence. As more observations arrive, recursive or mixture-based inference revises the relative support for each model.
