# Weak Regular Sequence

A weak regular sequence satisfies the successive non-zero-divisor conditions of a [[Regular Sequence]] but does not require the final quotient M/(r₁,…,rₙ)M to be nonzero.

The distinction matters under scalar extension: flatness preserves weak regularity, while an additional nontriviality condition is needed to obtain a regular sequence. Faithful flatness reflects both properties.
