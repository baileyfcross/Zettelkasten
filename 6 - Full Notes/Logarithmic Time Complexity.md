# Logarithmic Time Complexity

Logarithmic time complexity, commonly written $O(\log n)$, occurs when each step reduces the remaining problem by a constant factor. Repeatedly halving a search space produces a base-two logarithm.

This growth is very slow: [[Binary Search]] can locate an item among roughly one billion ordered items in about thirty probes. Logarithmic algorithms are therefore close to [[Constant Time Complexity]] in practical scalability.
