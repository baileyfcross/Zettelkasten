# Parametric Ellipse Representation

An origin-centered ellipse with semi-axes a and b is represented parametrically by x equals a cosine theta and y equals b sine theta. Advancing theta from zero through a full turn sweeps the complete closed curve.

For a fixed number of perimeter points, a constant parameter step and angle-addition identities create an efficient recurrence. Because a and b scale the coordinate directions differently, uniform parameter spacing does not generally equal uniform arc-length spacing. A rotated and displaced ellipse is obtained by applying a rotation and translation to the generated points.
