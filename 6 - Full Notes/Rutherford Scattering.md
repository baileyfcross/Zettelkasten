# Rutherford Scattering

Rutherford scattering directs alpha particles at a thin foil and measures their deflections. Most pass nearly straight through, while a small fraction scatter through large angles. The pattern shows that atoms are mostly empty space and that positive charge and most mass are concentrated in a compact nucleus rather than spread throughout the atom.
