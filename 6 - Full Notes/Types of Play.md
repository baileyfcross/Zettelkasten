# Types of Play

Caillois's framework describes four recurring forms of play: agon centers on competition, alea on chance, mimicry on role-playing, and ilinx on altered perception or vertigo. A game may combine several rather than belonging exclusively to one.

The framework also places play on a continuum from ludus, which is structured by formal rules, to paidia, which is spontaneous and improvisational. These dimensions help describe the experience more precisely than a broad [[Game Genre]] label alone.
