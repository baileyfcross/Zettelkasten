# Data Ownership Attribution

Data ownership attribution is the rule used to decide who would receive a property-like claim in data. Possible candidates include the person described, the collector, the creator of a database, the party that invested labor or money, or a group whose information produced the value.

Different criteria reward different contributions and can conflict when several parties participate. Attribution must therefore be justified explicitly rather than inferred automatically from physical possession or technical control.
