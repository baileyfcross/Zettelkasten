# Commercial Human Spaceflight

Commercial human spaceflight uses private firms and market-oriented contracts to develop or operate vehicles that carry people. It includes tourism as well as government-funded crew transportation, so commercial organization does not necessarily mean that passengers or missions are privately financed.

Entrepreneurial companies sought lower costs through new production structures and reusable designs, while NASA used competition to develop cargo and crew services for the ISS. Government development money, contracts, safety requirements, facilities, and demand remained essential. The model changes who designs and operates a vehicle without eliminating the public institutions behind human access to orbit.
