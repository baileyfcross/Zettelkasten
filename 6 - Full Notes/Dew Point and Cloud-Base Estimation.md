# Dew Point and Cloud-Base Estimation

Dew point is the temperature to which air must be cooled at constant pressure to become saturated. The spread between air temperature and dew point indicates how close the air is to condensation; a shrinking spread suggests cloud or fog formation. With an assumed lapse relationship, the spread can also provide a practical estimate of cloud-base height related to the [[Lifted Condensation and Convection Levels]].
