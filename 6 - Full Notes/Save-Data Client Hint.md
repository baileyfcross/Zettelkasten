# Save-Data Client Hint

The Save-Data hint expresses a user preference to reduce transferred data. For images, a service can respond with smaller dimensions, stronger compression, fewer optional images, or a lower quality tier while keeping the content usable.

The preference is an explicit signal, not a license to remove essential information or accessibility. It should feed a documented policy alongside layout and format capabilities. See [[Image Quality Tier Selection]] and [[Critical Image Eager Loading]].
