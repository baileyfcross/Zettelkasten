# Center of Pressure and Center of Gravity

The center of pressure is the effective point through which the resultant aerodynamic force acts, while the center of gravity is the balance point of aircraft mass. Their relative locations create pitching moments and affect longitudinal stability and stall recovery. Loading decisions therefore connect aerodynamic behavior to [[Aircraft Center-of-Gravity Calculation]].
