# Thermodynamic Weather Diagrams

Stüve diagrams, emagrams, tephigrams, and skew-T log-P diagrams plot atmospheric temperature and pressure in forms chosen to make parcel processes easier to interpret. A vertical sounding can show environmental temperature, dew point, adiabatic paths, and moisture relationships on one chart. These diagrams support identification of [[Lifted Condensation and Convection Levels]] and layers of stability or instability.
