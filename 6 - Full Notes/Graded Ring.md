# Graded Ring

A graded ring decomposes as a direct sum R = ⊕Rₙ whose multiplication sends RᵢRⱼ into Rᵢ₊ⱼ. Elements belonging to one component are [[Homogeneous Element]]s of that degree.

The grading records algebraic size or degree. Polynomial rings have their standard grading by total degree, and [[Homogeneous Ideal]]s preserve the decomposition in quotient constructions.
