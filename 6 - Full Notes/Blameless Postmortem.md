# Blameless Postmortem

A blameless postmortem reconstructs an incident to understand how system conditions, incentives, information, and safeguards shaped the outcome without reducing the explanation to individual fault. It records impact, timeline, contributing factors, detection and response behavior, and concrete follow-up work.

Blamelessness does not remove accountability; it redirects accountability toward learning and system improvement. Actions should have owners and be prioritized by risk, while the review examines why each decision was reasonable with the information available at the time. Repeated themes can guide [[Continuous Improvement]] across teams and delivery systems.
