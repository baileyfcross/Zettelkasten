# Presentation Layer

The presentation layer of the OSI model converts application data into a representation that another host can interpret. Its concerns include serialization, encoding, compression, and encryption rather than the business meaning of the message.

This layer separates a shared wire representation from each application's in-memory structures. Both endpoints must agree on the transformation or the delivered bytes cannot be reconstructed correctly.
