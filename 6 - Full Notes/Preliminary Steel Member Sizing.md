# Preliminary Steel Member Sizing

Preliminary sizing supplies plausible member depths and areas before a complete analysis is available. Span-to-depth rules, approximate column capacities, typical bay proportions, and experience with joists, beams, trusses, and plate girders help establish a workable structural layout and expose likely conflicts with architecture and building services.

Rules of thumb are starting points, not design checks. The preliminary section must later be tested for every applicable strength, stability, connection, and serviceability limit state under the governing load combinations. A heavier preliminary choice can sometimes improve economy by reducing iteration or connection complexity, but repeated sizes and simple details often matter more than small weight reductions. Iteration converts the first estimate into a coordinated final system.
