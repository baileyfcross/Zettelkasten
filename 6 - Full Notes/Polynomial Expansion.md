# Polynomial Expansion

Polynomial expansion removes products and grouping symbols by applying the [[Distributive Property]]. Every term in one factor multiplies every term in the other factors, after which [[Like Terms]] are combined.

For example,

$$(2x+3)(5x+7)=10x^2+14x+15x+21=10x^2+29x+21.$$

Expansion and [[Polynomial Factorization]] are reverse views of the same equality. Expansion exposes coefficients and like terms, while factorization exposes multiplicative structure and possible zeros.
