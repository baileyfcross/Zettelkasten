# Quantum Kernel and Image-Scale Sensitivity in Quanvolution

The HQNN-Quanvolution sweep compared patch sides of two and three, corresponding to four- and nine-qubit patch circuits. Their validation-accuracy distributions were similar, with only a small tendency favoring the larger patch. A side-five patch would require a 25-qubit simulated circuit and was left unevaluated because of computational cost.

The same study found similar accuracy distributions for $16\times16$ and $32\times32$ inputs. This suggests that the aggressively downsampled images retained enough information for the tested task, not that image resolution or receptive-field size is generally irrelevant. Both resolutions are small, the larger patch regime is missing, and the classical back end can compensate for some early-layer differences. The result defines a narrow sensitivity check rather than a scale-invariance property of quanvolutional networks.
