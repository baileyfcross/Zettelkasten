# Data Processing Legitimacy

Data processing legitimacy asks whether an organization has a defensible basis for collecting and using personal data. It is distinct from technical feasibility: the ability to combine or analyze records does not itself authorize that operation.

Legitimacy must be considered together with [[Purpose Limitation]], proportionality, transparency, and the rights of the affected person.
