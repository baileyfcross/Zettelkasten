# System of Intelligence

A system of intelligence applies models or rules to support near-real-time decisions and operational actions. It differs from a historical system of insight by placing prediction, classification, recommendation, or anomaly detection in the path of current work. These capabilities often enrich a system of engagement, but model latency, confidence, fallback behavior, monitoring, and authority over the resulting action must remain explicit.
