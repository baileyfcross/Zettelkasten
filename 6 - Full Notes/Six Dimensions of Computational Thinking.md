# Six Dimensions of Computational Thinking

Computational thinking can be viewed through six dimensions: methods, machines, computing education, software engineering, design, and computational science. Each highlights a different practice through which people formulate, execute, teach, scale, evaluate, or investigate computations.

The dimensions overlap without collapsing into one another. Together they connect the ancient history of calculation with modern computing systems and show why computational thinking is richer than programming alone.
