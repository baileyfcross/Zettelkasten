# Scene Composition

Scene composition is the visual arrangement of elements that directs attention and gives a playable space a particular character. Framing, contrast, repetition, symmetry, and directional shapes can show what matters before the player understands every rule.

Composition can also convey mood or function: symmetry may suggest an artificial structure, irregularity a natural landscape, and an open view a pause from danger. Its visual guidance should support the [[Scene Purpose]].
