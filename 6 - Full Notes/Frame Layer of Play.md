# Frame Layer of Play

The frame layer is the broader interpretation through which a player understands an activity: as competition, fiction, social ritual, performance, critique, training, or something else. The same mechanics can mean different things under different frames.

Theme, setting, cultural knowledge, and the social context of play all help establish this layer. Designers shape the frame, but players also bring expectations and experiences that can reinforce or redirect it.
