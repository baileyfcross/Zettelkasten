# Required Player Skill

A required player skill is an ability the audience must bring to a game or develop quickly enough to participate. Depending on the game, requirements can include reading, motor dexterity, hand-eye coordination, endurance, reflexes, pattern recognition, spatial awareness, probability calculation, or planning future moves.

Listing these requirements early reveals whom the design attracts or excludes and informs onboarding, controls, accessibility, and playtest recruitment. Skills should be described in relation to actual play rather than assumed from broad demographic categories, because players of similar age or platform may have very different capabilities.
