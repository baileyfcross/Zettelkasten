# Biofabrication Regulatory Pathway

A biofabrication regulatory pathway defines the evidence, manufacturing controls, classification, and oversight required before a living-cell product reaches patients or consumers. Existing rules may have been designed for human implants, drugs, food, or animal tissue rather than the new combination.

Regulation must establish identity, origin, safety, consistency, labeling, traceability, and the consequences of process changes.
