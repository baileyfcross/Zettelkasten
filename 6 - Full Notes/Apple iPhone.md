# Apple iPhone

Apple introduced the iPhone in 2007 as a touch-centered convergence of phone, networked computer, media player, camera, and later an application platform. Its interface placed direct manipulation ahead of physical keys.

The product triggered rapid smartphone adoption and a large software ecosystem. Its impact came from coordinated hardware, software, networking, and design rather than a single new component.
