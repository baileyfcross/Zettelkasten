# Domain and Range

The domain of a [[Function]] is the set of permitted inputs, while its range is the set of outputs the function actually attains. A formula can impose restrictions: denominators cannot be zero and an even root of a real expression requires a nonnegative radicand. The range is determined by both the rule and the chosen domain.
