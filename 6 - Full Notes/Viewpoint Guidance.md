# Viewpoint Guidance

Viewpoint guidance gives perceptual cues that help a user move or aim the display toward a useful view. The target may be an off-screen object, a physical location, or a particular camera pose required to inspect or capture something.

Arrows, frames, paths, and changing visual indicators can encode direction and remaining difference. Feedback should distinguish translation from rotation and remain interpretable while the target is outside the current field of view.
