# Sensitive Data Inference

Sensitive data inference derives protected or intimate information from fields that appear nonsensitive on their own. Correlations among behavior, location, purchases, or relationships can reveal health, political, or other sensitive characteristics without asking for them directly.

The inference matters because the harm comes from what a system can conclude and use, not only from what a [[Data Subject]] intentionally disclosed. Governance must therefore examine analytical outputs as well as raw inputs.
