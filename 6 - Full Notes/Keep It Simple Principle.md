# Keep It Simple Principle

Keep It Simple Stupid (KISS) treats unnecessary complexity as a design risk. A solution should contain enough structure to meet the present requirement, but avoid extra layers, conditions, or abstractions that make its behavior harder to understand and maintain. Simplicity does not mean ignoring essential error handling or future change; it means keeping each chosen mechanism proportional to the problem it actually solves.
