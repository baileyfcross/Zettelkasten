# Poynting Vector

The Poynting vector is the cross product of electric and magnetic field intensity and represents electromagnetic power density and flow direction. Integrating its normal component over a surface gives power crossing that surface.

For time-harmonic fields, the real part of a complex field product gives average power. Poynting's theorem balances radiated power, stored field energy, and resistive dissipation.
