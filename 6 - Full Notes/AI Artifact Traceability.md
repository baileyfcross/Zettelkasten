# AI Artifact Traceability

AI artifact traceability places generated code, prompts, configurations, reports, and release documents in the same versioned governance path as human-authored work. The repository preserves the change, pull-request history records review, and deployment records connect the accepted artifact to its operational result.

Traceability should include the model or service used, relevant input or input reference, validation result, reviewer approval, and final disposition when practical. This evidence does not prove the artifact is correct, but it makes influence attributable and supports auditing, rollback, incident analysis, and later evaluation.
