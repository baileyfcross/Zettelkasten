# Data Market Failure

Data market failure occurs when ordinary exchange does not produce socially useful creation, access, or use of data. Proposed causes include uncertainty about value, weak bargaining positions, externalized costs, and difficulty excluding or coordinating users.

A new ownership right is only one possible response. Regulation, contracts, access duties, liability, standards, or collective arrangements may address a particular failure with different effects on innovation and reuse.
