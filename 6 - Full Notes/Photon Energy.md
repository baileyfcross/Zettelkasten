# Photon Energy

A photon carries a discrete amount of radiant energy. Its energy rises with frequency and falls with wavelength: $E=h\nu=hc/\lambda$, where $h$ is Planck's constant, $\nu$ is frequency, $c$ is light speed, and $\lambda$ is wavelength. In a [[Photovoltaic Effect]], the photon must provide enough energy to promote an electron across the material's [[Semiconductor Band Gap]].
