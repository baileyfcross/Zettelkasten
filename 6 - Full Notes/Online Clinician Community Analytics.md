# Online Clinician Community Analytics

Online clinician community analytics studies professional discussions, questions, and shared resources among physicians and researchers. These communities can expose emerging information needs, practice concerns, and patterns of collaboration.

Professional participation is selective, and public messages may omit the context available in a clinical setting. Identity, specialty, and expertise can also be difficult to verify. Analyses should distinguish communication behavior from clinical effectiveness and avoid treating popularity or repetition as evidence that a medical claim is correct.
