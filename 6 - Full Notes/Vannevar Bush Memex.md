# Vannevar Bush Memex

Vannevar Bush proposed the Memex as a personal machine for storing and retrieving a large body of information. It would preserve what people remember poorly while supporting associative trails that match how people connect ideas.

The machine was never built, but its vision influenced later [[Hypertext]] and the World Wide Web. It framed computing as an aid to human thought rather than merely a calculator.
