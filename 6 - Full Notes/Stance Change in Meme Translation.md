# Stance Change in Meme Translation

Stance change in meme translation occurs when a cross-cultural version alters the attitude, participation structure, or communicative purpose of its source. The translated artifact may preserve form and topic while becoming more approving, critical, ironic, or locally confrontational.

This shows why translation cannot be evaluated only by semantic accuracy. A meme's social meaning also lies in how it addresses an audience and positions its creator. Tracking content, form, and stance separately reveals adaptations that would be invisible in a word-for-word comparison.
