# Procedural Contact Shadows for Cards

A duplicated, darkened extraction of a performer can provide a shadow tied to the performer's own movement. In the book's crowd example, the lower part of that dark copy is blurred and directionally spread to make a moving core contact shadow, while a feathered dark shape supplies wider falloff. Stacking these contributions makes feet meet the floor without keyframing each footfall. Additional gradients can darken subjects behind nearer copies, suggesting mutual occlusion. The technique is especially useful for [[Crowd Replication with Animated Cards]].
