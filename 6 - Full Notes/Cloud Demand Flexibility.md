# Cloud Demand Flexibility

Cloud demand flexibility is the ability to change computing capacity as a product or service moves through development, testing, launch, promotion, ordinary use, and decline. Each stage can create a different peak or trough in demand.

[[Rapid Elasticity]] lets spending and capacity follow those changes rather than requiring permanent overprovisioning. The value appears as both avoided idle investment and a lower chance of losing business during unexpected peaks.
