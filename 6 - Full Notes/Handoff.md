# Handoff

A handoff transfers work and responsibility from one person or discipline to another. Each handoff can introduce a queue, information loss, delayed feedback, and ambiguity about who owns the outcome.

Cross-discipline teams and smaller batches reduce these costs by increasing direct collaboration and keeping work centered on integrated features. Remaining handoffs should be visualized and measured as part of the flow.
