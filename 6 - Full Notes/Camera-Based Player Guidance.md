# Camera-Based Player Guidance

Camera-based player guidance frames or reveals the next meaningful destination as the player moves. A pan, change of angle, or controlled reveal can transfer attention from the current action to the next jump, route, or landmark without displaying an instruction.

Because the camera also controls what information is available, its movement can reduce doubt at moments when scenery changes or an apparently unsafe action is actually intended. The technique must preserve orientation and agency; a forceful camera that fights player input can create confusion instead of guidance.
