# Multipurpose Internet Mail Extensions

Multipurpose Internet Mail Extensions expands email messages with typed content, multipart bodies, encodings, and attachments. It gives a recipient the metadata needed to interpret content that cannot be represented as one plain text body.

MIME is a message-format extension rather than a replacement for SMTP. SMTP transports the message, while MIME describes the organization and representation of its parts.
