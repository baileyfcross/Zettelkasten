# Shrinkwrap Retopology Projection

Shrinkwrap projects a simpler mesh onto the surface of a detailed reference mesh, allowing the low-resolution topology to follow the high-resolution form. The two objects must occupy compatible world-space positions for projection to produce a useful result.

The modifier preserves an editable low-poly base while using the dense surface as a geometric target. It can accelerate retopology, but the artist still decides edge placement, density, and whether the projected topology will deform cleanly.
