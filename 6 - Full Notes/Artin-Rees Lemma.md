# Artin-Rees Lemma

Let I be an ideal in a Noetherian ring, M a finitely generated module, and N a submodule. The Artin-Rees lemma says that for some k, IⁿM ∩ N = Iⁿ⁻ᵏ(IᵏM ∩ N) for all n ≥ k.

Thus the filtration induced on N eventually follows a fixed rule. The result controls intersections with ideal powers and supports [[Krull Intersection Theorem|Krull's intersection theorem]].
