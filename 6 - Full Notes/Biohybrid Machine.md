# Biohybrid Machine

A biohybrid machine combines living biological components with synthetic structures, electronics, or materials to perform a designed function. The biological part may provide actuation, sensing, adaptation, or processing that is difficult to reproduce synthetically.

Its operation depends on both mechanical interfaces and life-support conditions, so maintenance of the cells is part of maintaining the machine.
