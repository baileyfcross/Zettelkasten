# Prop Art within Environment Production

Prop art focuses on individual objects that populate a game world, such as furniture, dishes, tools, or architectural details. Those assets make an environment specific and believable by showing how its spaces are used.

In a specialized studio, prop artists may own these objects while environment artists assemble the larger setting. On a smaller project the roles merge, so asset standards for scale, style, polygon complexity, and export must remain shared across both kinds of work.
