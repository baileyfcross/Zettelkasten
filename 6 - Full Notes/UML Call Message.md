# UML Call Message

A UML call message represents one participant invoking behavior on another participant. It is drawn as a directed message from the caller's lifeline to the receiver and labels the requested operation or information.

Call messages form the forward path of a sequence scenario. Their order shows how responsibility passes through the participants before results return to earlier callers.
