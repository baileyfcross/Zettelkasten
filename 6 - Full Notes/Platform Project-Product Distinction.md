# Platform Project-Product Distinction

A project is organized to reach a bounded target within a fixed time and budget, after which attention and staffing commonly move elsewhere. That model fits a feature-complete implementation poorly when the result must continually absorb upgrades, security patches, user feedback, and changes in the surrounding toolchain.

A platform is therefore better managed as a product with an ongoing lifecycle, identifiable users, a roadmap, and continuing investment. The distinction is operational rather than semantic: treating a platform as a project tends to separate construction from maintenance, while [[Platform as a Product]] keeps discovery, delivery, support, and improvement within one enduring responsibility.
