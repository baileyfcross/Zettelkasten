# Physiological Pulse Signal Detection

Physiological pulse signal detection measures tiny periodic facial-color changes caused by blood circulation. By tracking a face through video and combining subtle temporal variations, an analysis can recover a pulse-like signal that is normally invisible to direct viewing.

A rendered or synthesized face may lack this living physiological rhythm even when its appearance and motion seem realistic. The cue is not permanent protection against fakery: motion, illumination, compression, and skin visibility affect measurement, and a generator can be modified to reproduce a plausible signal.
