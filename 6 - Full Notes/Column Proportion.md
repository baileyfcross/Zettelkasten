# Column Proportion

A column proportion divides a cell in a [[Contingency Table]] by the total for its column. It describes the composition of a column-defined group, such as what proportion of people with asthma are veterans.

It conditions on a different denominator from a [[Row Proportion]], so the two values can differ sharply even for the same cell. Reporting which variable forms the columns prevents a common reversal of interpretation.
