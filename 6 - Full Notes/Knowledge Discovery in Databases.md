# Knowledge Discovery in Databases

Knowledge Discovery in Databases is an iterative process for turning stored data into useful, understandable knowledge. It includes application understanding, data selection and preparation, mining, interpretation, and incorporation of results into practice.

Data mining is the algorithmic pattern-finding stage inside this broader process. The distinction prevents a technically interesting pattern from being mistaken for a complete discovery outcome.
