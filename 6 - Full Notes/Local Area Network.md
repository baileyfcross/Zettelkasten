# Local Area Network

A local area network connects computers across a limited space such as an office, laboratory, or campus department. High local bandwidth supports shared files, printers, servers, and interactive applications.

Local networks connect end users to regional and backbone infrastructure. Ethernet became their dominant implementation as personal computers and workstations spread.
