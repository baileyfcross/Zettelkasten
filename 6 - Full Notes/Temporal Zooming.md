# Temporal Zooming

Temporal zooming narrows an [[Interactive Time-Series Plot]] to a selected interval and rescales the view to expose local variation. It allows a long series to provide both overview and detail without a separate figure for every period. The control should make the active interval visible and provide a reliable reset. Zooming changes what is shown, so comparisons made at different zoom levels must account for altered scales and context.
