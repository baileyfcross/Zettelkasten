# R Polynomial Regression

R polynomial regression extends a linear model with powers of a quantitative predictor so the fitted mean can bend while remaining linear in its coefficients. Raw powers can be entered explicitly, while polynomial-basis functions can produce numerically better-behaved terms.

The degree should be justified by the observed range and validation rather than increased until the training data are followed closely. Polynomial predictions can change rapidly outside the observed predictor range, making extrapolation especially hazardous.
