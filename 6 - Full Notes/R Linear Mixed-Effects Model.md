# R Linear Mixed-Effects Model

An R linear mixed-effects model combines population-level fixed effects with random effects that represent variation among sampled groups, subjects, or clusters. Random intercepts and slopes allow observations within the same unit to share modeled dependence rather than being treated as independent replicates.

The random-effects structure should follow the sampling and repeated-measure design. Serial correlation can be modeled in addition to random effects when measurements closer in time remain more alike than the random-effects structure alone explains.
