# Tracking Jitter

Tracking jitter is rapid random variation in reported pose even when the tracked object is still or moving smoothly. In an augmented display, that noise makes virtual content appear to shake relative to its physical reference.

Temporal filtering can suppress high-frequency variation, but it introduces delay and can attenuate real motion. Filter strength must therefore be chosen with both visual stability and registration latency in mind.
