# Imaginary Part of a Complex Number

For a complex number $z=a+bi$, the imaginary part is $\operatorname{Im}(z)=b$, the real coefficient multiplying the [[Imaginary Unit]].

It is the vertical coordinate in the complex plane. The imaginary part itself is a real number, not the term $bi$.
