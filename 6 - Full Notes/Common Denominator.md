# Common Denominator

A common denominator is a shared [[Denominator]] used to express two or more fractions in comparable units. Fractions with denominators (b) and (d) can always be rewritten over (bd):

$$\frac{a}{b}=\frac{ad}{bd},\qquad \frac{c}{d}=\frac{bc}{bd}.$$

Once the units match, numerators can be added or subtracted. The chosen denominator need not be the smallest possible one; any common multiple works and can be reduced afterward. The smallest choice is the [[Least Common Denominator]].
