# Rayleigh Quotient

For a nonzero vector $x$ and square matrix $A$, the Rayleigh quotient is $R(x)=x^TAx/(x^Tx)$. If $x$ is an eigenvector, the quotient equals its eigenvalue.

Applied to an approximate eigenvector, it supplies an eigenvalue estimate. For a symmetric matrix its values are bounded by the smallest and largest eigenvalues.
