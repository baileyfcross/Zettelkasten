# Microscopic Meaning of Temperature

Temperature represents the energetic activity of a material's elementary particles. Adding internal energy increases translational, rotational, and vibrational motion, while removing energy reduces those modes of molecular activity.

The material state changes how that activity transports energy. Closely packed particles favor [[Fourier Heat Conduction Law|conduction]], mobile fluid particles add bulk transport, and electromagnetic radiation can carry energy without an intervening material medium.
