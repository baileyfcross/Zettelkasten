# Exergy

Exergy describes the usable quality of an energy flow relative to an environment, rather than only its energy quantity. As irreversibility raises entropy, some ability to produce useful work is lost even if energy is conserved. The source uses exergy analysis to locate losses in conversion systems and to compare a low-temperature solar heater with higher-temperature equipment chosen for power production.
