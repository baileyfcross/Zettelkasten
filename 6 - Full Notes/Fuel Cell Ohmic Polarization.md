# Fuel Cell Ohmic Polarization

Ohmic polarization is the operating-voltage drop caused by internal electrical and ionic resistance in a [[Fuel Cell]]. Electrodes, electrolyte, and their contact interfaces all contribute. Reducing path resistance or electrode spacing can lower this loss, while different electrolytes change its temperature dependence. It tends to appear across the middle region of a cell's current-voltage characteristic, unlike activation and concentration polarization.
