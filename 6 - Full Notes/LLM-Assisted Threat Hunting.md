# LLM-Assisted Threat Hunting

LLM-assisted threat hunting converts an analyst's hypothesis into queries, correlates evidence from multiple security sources, and summarizes suspicious patterns for further investigation. It can reduce the language and tool friction involved in exploring large telemetry collections.

The model must not invent observations or treat an absent query result as proof of safety. Read-only access, query review, preserved result sets, and explicit investigation stop conditions keep the hunt evidence-driven.
