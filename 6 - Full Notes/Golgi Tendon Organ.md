# Golgi Tendon Organ

A Golgi tendon organ is a force-sensitive receptor located where muscle fibers meet tendon collagen. It reports tension generated by muscular contraction and therefore supplies peripheral feedback about force.

Force perception does not depend on this receptor alone. The brain also receives an internal copy of the outgoing motor command through [[Corollary Discharge]], allowing intended effort to be compared with sensory consequences. Together these feed-forward and feedback sources contribute the force component of [[Kinesthesia]].
