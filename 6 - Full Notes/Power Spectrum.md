# Power Spectrum

A power spectrum describes how the squared magnitude of a signal's Fourier amplitude is distributed across frequency. It discards phase information while retaining the relative strength of spectral components.

Peaks identify dominant periodicities or characteristic scales, while a broad spectrum indicates contributions from many frequencies. For spatial data, the same construction uses wave number and reveals how variation is apportioned among different spatial wavelengths.
