# Game System Design Document

A game system design document explains one system's name, purpose, desired player experience, looping structure, player interactions, and relationships to subsystems and peer systems. Diagrams make the important parts and behaviors visible.

Each system should have a brief separate document linked to the rest of the design rather than being buried in a monolithic bible. The document is living evidence of why the [[Game System]] has its current structure and must be updated as the design changes.
