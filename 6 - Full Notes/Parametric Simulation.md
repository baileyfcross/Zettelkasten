# Parametric Simulation

A parametric simulation generates artificial observations from a chosen probability distribution using specified or estimated parameters. It replaces unavailable population data with a model of the data-generating process.

The source uses estimated means and standard deviations with a normal model to study test behavior. The simulation can evaluate finite-sample approximations, but its conclusions remain conditional on the selected parametric family and parameter values.
