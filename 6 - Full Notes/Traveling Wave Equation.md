# Traveling Wave Equation

A sinusoidal wave traveling in the positive $x$ direction can be written

$$y(x,t)=A\cos(\omega t-kx+\phi_0).$$

Here $A$ is amplitude, $\omega=2\pi f$, and $k=2\pi/\lambda$. A surface of constant phase moves at $v=\omega/k=f\lambda$. Reversing the sign before $kx$ reverses the propagation direction under this convention.
