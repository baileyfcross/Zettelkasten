# Homogeneous Difference Equation

A homogeneous linear difference equation has zero forcing term. For constant coefficients it can be written $F(E)y_n=0$, where $E$ is the [[Shift Operator]].

Trying $y_n=m^n$ produces the [[Auxiliary Equation for a Difference Equation]]. Its roots determine the complementary solution, with repeated roots introducing polynomial factors in $n$ just as repeated characteristic roots do for linear differential equations.
