# Self-Defining Play

Self-defining play lets players choose, customize, or construct the avatar or representation through which they appear in the game. Appearance, identity, equipment, abilities, and role can become statements about an imagined self.

The available choices communicate what forms of identity the world recognizes. Cosmetic options support expression, while functional choices also reshape mechanics and must be balanced as gameplay decisions.
