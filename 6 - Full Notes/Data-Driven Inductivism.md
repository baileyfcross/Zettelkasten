# Data-Driven Inductivism

Data-driven inductivism is the view that general patterns, predictions, or causal claims can be developed from observations with relatively little prior theory. Data science renews this position by making very large collections and powerful pattern detection available.

The view does not imply that data interpret themselves. The source argues that a defensible inductivism needs methods for distinguishing useful causal structure from accidental correlation and for improving claims as further evidence arrives.
