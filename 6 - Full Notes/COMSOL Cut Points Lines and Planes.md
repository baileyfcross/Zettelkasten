# COMSOL Cut Points Lines and Planes

Cut points, lines, and planes sample a higher-dimensional solution on lower-dimensional geometric objects. They reveal local histories, gradients along a path, and internal cross-sections that an exterior surface plot cannot show.

The cut location should be defined from the physical question and kept identical across comparison cases. A point near a heat source, a line through layered material, or a plane across a flow channel can then support [[COMSOL Point Line and Surface Evaluations|quantitative evaluations]] and plots.
