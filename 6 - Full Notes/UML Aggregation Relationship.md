# UML Aggregation Relationship

Aggregation is a whole-part association in which the part can exist independently of the whole. UML marks the whole end of the association with a hollow diamond.

A computer and replaceable components illustrate the intended lifetime distinction: the whole refers to its parts, but destroying one object does not define the destruction of the other. Aggregation should be used only when that shared, independent ownership is important to the model.
