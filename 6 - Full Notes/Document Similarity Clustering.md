# Document Similarity Clustering

Document similarity clustering groups texts from a representation of their lexical or semantic proximity. Choices include term vectors, latent factors, graph links, language-model scores, and combinations of content with metadata.

The similarity definition determines whether clusters reflect topic, style, source, or another property. Evaluation should therefore inspect representative terms and documents rather than relying only on an internal geometric score.
