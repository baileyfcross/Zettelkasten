# HiveQL

HiveQL is the SQL-like query language of [[Apache Hive]]. Its statements describe tables, filters, joins, grouping, aggregation, and nested transformations over distributed data.

Hive parses a query, checks it against the metastore, optimizes a logical plan, and generates a physical plan for Hadoop execution. The familiar relational syntax lowers the barrier for analysts accustomed to SQL.
