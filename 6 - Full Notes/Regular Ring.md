# Regular Ring

A Noetherian ring is regular when every localization R_p at a [[Prime Ideal]] is a [[Regular Local Ring]]. Regularity is therefore defined pointwise across the spectrum.

For a regular local ring, the residue field has finite projective dimension equal to the Krull dimension. Polynomial rings over a field satisfy the corresponding finite-resolution bound through the Hilbert syzygy theorem.
