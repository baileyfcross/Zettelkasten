# Long Exact Sequence in Ext

A short exact sequence in either argument of Ext produces a long exact sequence of Ext modules, with direction determined by the contravariance of the first argument and covariance of the second.

Connecting homomorphisms link adjacent Ext degrees. This sequence lets vanishing or bounds for two modules constrain the third and underlies depth comparisons in short exact sequences.
