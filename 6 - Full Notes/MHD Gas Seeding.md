# MHD Gas Seeding

Gas seeding introduces easily ionized material into the hot working gas of a [[Magnetohydrodynamic Generator]]. Cesium or potassium can supply charge carriers at temperatures lower than a pure gas would need for comparable conductivity. The improved electrical path raises possible output but creates costs and corrosion problems; an open-cycle plant also needs a way to recover and reuse its seed material.
