# Exact-Match Healthcare Retrieval

Exact-match healthcare retrieval returns documents that satisfy explicit Boolean conditions on terms, fields, or controlled concepts. Operators let the searcher require, combine, or exclude criteria and can produce a reproducible set.

The method gives the user direct control but requires a well-formed query. Synonyms and spelling variants can be missed, while broad expansions can overwhelm the result. Exact matching is valuable for systematic searches when the strategy is carefully documented, but its binary decision does not naturally rank marginally relevant documents.
