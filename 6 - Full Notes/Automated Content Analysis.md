# Automated Content Analysis

Automated content analysis uses computational procedures to classify or measure documents from their language. A hand-coded sample or coding dictionary can train a system that applies categories across a much larger corpus.

The method extends quantitative content analysis but does not eliminate interpretation. Category definitions, coding decisions, preprocessing, and validation determine what the automated counts actually represent.
