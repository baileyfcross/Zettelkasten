# Nonknowledge-Based Clinical Decision Support

Nonknowledge-based clinical decision support learns decision patterns from data rather than applying a hand-authored medical rule base. Neural networks, genetic algorithms, and other statistical methods can model relationships that are difficult to specify explicitly.

The learned system inherits the coverage and bias of its training data, and its internal reasoning may be harder to explain. Validation must therefore test new cases and clinically relevant subgroups. The absence of an explicit rule base does not remove the need for expert oversight, workflow integration, or monitoring after deployment.
