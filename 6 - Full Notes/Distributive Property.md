# Distributive Property

The distributive property connects multiplication with addition:

$$a(b+c)=ab+ac,$$

and similarly ((a+b)c=ac+bc). A factor outside parentheses multiplies every term inside them.

Distribution is the basis of [[Polynomial Expansion]] and [[Combining Like Terms]]. Used in reverse, it becomes [[Polynomial Factorization]] because a repeated factor can be taken outside a sum. The rule does not imply that every operation distributes over addition; for example, ((a+b)^2) is generally not (a^2+b^2) because expansion also produces the cross term (2ab).
