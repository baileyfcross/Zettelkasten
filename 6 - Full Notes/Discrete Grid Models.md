# Discrete Grid Models

A discrete grid model replaces continuous space or time with a finite set of sample points. Difference equations relate the value at each point to neighboring values, allowing a computer to approximate differential equations step by step.

Grid spacing controls a central tradeoff. Finer meshes can represent local change more accurately but demand more computation and may require careful numerical treatment to remain stable.
