# Space Complexity

Space complexity describes how much storage an [[Algorithm]] requires as its [[Input Size]] grows. It complements [[Time Complexity]] because two methods with similar running times may demand very different amounts of memory.

Representation can strongly affect this resource. A [[Sparse Matrix]] avoids storing every zero in a large matrix and instead records the positions and values of its comparatively few nonzero entries.
