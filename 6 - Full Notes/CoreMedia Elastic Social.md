# CoreMedia Elastic Social

CoreMedia Elastic Social adds community participation features to a content-driven digital experience. It supports interactions such as comments, ratings, reviews, and user contributions around managed content, making social engagement part of the site rather than only an outbound link to a public network.

User-generated content requires a lifecycle distinct from editorial content: submission, moderation, publication, abuse handling, and removal all need policy and tooling. The integration must also connect community identity and permissions to the content being discussed. [[Social Review Platforms]] describes the review pattern at a service level, while Elastic Social embeds that pattern in an enterprise publishing system. [[CoreMedia Social Media Hub]] addresses aggregation across external channels.
