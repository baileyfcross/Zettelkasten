# Parabolic Partial Differential Equation

A parabolic partial differential equation combines first-order evolution in one variable with second-order smoothing in spatial variables. The heat and diffusion equations are standard examples.

Initial data specify the starting distribution, while boundary conditions control exchange at the domain edges. As evolution proceeds, sharp spatial variations are smoothed and the solution tends toward lower spatial-frequency structure, reflecting irreversible diffusion rather than wave-like propagation.
