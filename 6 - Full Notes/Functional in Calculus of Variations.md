# Functional in Calculus of Variations

A functional assigns a number to an entire function or path rather than to a finite list of variables. A common form integrates an expression depending on a trial function, its derivative, and the independent variable.

Calculus of variations asks which admissible path makes the functional stationary under small variations that respect the endpoint conditions. This generalizes ordinary optimization and provides the mathematical basis for action principles in mechanics.
