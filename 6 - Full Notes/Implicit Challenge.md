# Implicit Challenge

An implicit challenge is not announced directly but must be inferred from rules, observation, experimentation, or the relationship among explicit goals. It can emerge from the system rather than being authored as a discrete objective.

Implicit challenges reward understanding, but they become unfair when essential information cannot be discovered. Feedback must let players recognize the pattern and learn from unsuccessful attempts.
