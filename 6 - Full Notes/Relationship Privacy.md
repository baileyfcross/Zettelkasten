# Relationship Privacy

Relationship privacy protects the existence, type, or strength of connections between people. Even when profiles hide sensitive fields, friendship, communication, co-location, and interaction patterns can reveal communities or personal circumstances.

Protecting one person's relationship also affects the other endpoint, so disclosure choices cannot always be modeled as independent individual settings.
