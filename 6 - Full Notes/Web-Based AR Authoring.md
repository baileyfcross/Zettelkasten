# Web-Based AR Authoring

Web-based AR authoring applies web content and linking ideas to registered spatial information. A physical artifact can be associated with virtual content, while markup, style, and localization attributes describe what appears and how it is arranged.

An AR browser can combine several content channels rather than allowing one application to monopolize the display. Composition and view management must resolve competition for limited visual space and preserve the source and meaning of each channel.
