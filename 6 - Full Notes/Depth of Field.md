# Depth of Field

Depth of field is the range of scene distances that appear acceptably focused. It changes with aperture, focal length, focus distance, and acceptable blur: a smaller aperture generally extends the focused range, while a wider aperture makes it shallower.

Because depth-dependent blur should vary coherently across a real scene, it can test both exposure metadata and a composite's geometry. Inserted objects whose sharpness does not fit their apparent distance may violate the camera model that explains the rest of the photograph.
