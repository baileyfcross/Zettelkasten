# Spectrum of a Ring

The spectrum Spec(R) of a ring R is the set of its [[Prime Ideal]]s equipped with the [[Zariski Topology]]. Closed subsets have the form V(I), consisting of primes that contain an ideal I.

The spectrum treats prime ideals as generalized points. Pairing it with the [[Structure Sheaf]] produces an [[Affine Scheme]], whose local rings encode algebra near each prime.
