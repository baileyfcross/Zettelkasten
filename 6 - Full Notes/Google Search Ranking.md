# Google Search Ranking

Google improved Web search by ranking a page partly through the links it received from other pages, not only through keyword frequency. The method adapted citation-based ideas to the structure of the Web.

Better relevance combined with a sparse, uncluttered interface. Search quality became Google’s primary product rather than a feature designed mainly to keep users inside a portal.
