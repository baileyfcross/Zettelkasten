# Game Resistance

Game resistance is the push and pull between a player's actions and the system's response. The player pushes through [[Game Verb|verbs]] and choices; the game pushes back through rules, obstacles, consequences, and limits, or pulls forward through goals and rewards.

Resistance includes more than difficulty. It also includes how widely the game opens its choices, how firmly rules refuse an action, how the experience changes over time, and whether the player can influence the pace or purpose of play.
