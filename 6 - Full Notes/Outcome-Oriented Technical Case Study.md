# Outcome-Oriented Technical Case Study

An outcome-oriented technical case study translates an implementation into changes the client values. Instead of stopping at a list of tools, it reports effects such as shorter deployment lead time, more frequent releases, reduced deployment downtime, recovered engineering hours, or lower operating cost.

The evidence should be tied to a credible baseline and measurement rather than presented as an invented percentage. The connection between decision and result helps a reader evaluate the consultant's impact: automation matters because it changes speed, reliability, risk, or cost, not merely because Terraform or a pipeline appears in the architecture.
