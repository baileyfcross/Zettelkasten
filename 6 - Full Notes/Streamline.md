# Streamline

A streamline is a curve whose tangent at every point aligns with the instantaneous local velocity vector. In steady flow, the streamline pattern does not change with time and traces particle trajectories.

Stream functions label streamlines by constant values in two-dimensional incompressible flow. Around an impermeable body, the surface itself becomes a streamline because normal velocity is zero.
