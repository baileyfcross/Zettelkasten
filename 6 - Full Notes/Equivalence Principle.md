# Equivalence Principle

The equivalence principle states that the local effects of a uniform gravitational field are indistinguishable from those of an accelerating reference frame. An observer in free fall experiences local weightlessness, while an accelerating enclosure can imitate weight. This insight connects gravity to curved spacetime in general relativity.
