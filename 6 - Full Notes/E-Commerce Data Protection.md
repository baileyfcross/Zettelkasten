# E-Commerce Data Protection

E-commerce data protection applies confidentiality, integrity, authentication, and authorization to online transactions. Buyers need confidence that they are communicating with the intended service, that payment and personal information remain protected, and that transaction records are not altered unnoticed.

[[Encrypted Communication Channel|Encrypted channels]], certificates, access controls, and secure application design protect different parts of the exchange. No single control establishes trust in the entire commercial process.
