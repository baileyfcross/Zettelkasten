# Method of Moments

The method of moments determines parameters by making selected sums computed from a fitted curve agree with corresponding sums from the observations. For a polynomial model, equations are formed from sums of values and sums weighted by powers of the argument.

The number of matched moments equals the number of unknown constants. This produces a direct fitting system, although the resulting criterion is different from minimizing the [[Residual Sum of Squares]].
