# Emergent Biological Function

Emergent biological function is a capability that arises from interactions among cells, materials, and environmental feedback without being specified as one direct design step. Living components can reorganize and adapt after fabrication.

The possibility requires monitoring and revision because a construct may become stronger, more connected, or behaviorally different from the state originally reviewed for safety.
