# Sound Wave Amplitude

Sound wave amplitude describes the magnitude of pressure variation in a waveform and is associated with perceived loudness. Digital samples record amplitude at successive moments.

Playback gain scales the signal, while distance attenuation and occlusion can reduce the effective level reaching the listener. Excessive amplitude can exceed the available range and distort through clipping.
