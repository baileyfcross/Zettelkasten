# Ellipsoid

An ellipsoid with positive semiaxes $a$, $b$, and $c$ has equation

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}+\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1.$$

Its coordinate-plane sections are ellipses, and equal semiaxes reduce it to a [[Sphere Equation|sphere]]. It can be parameterized by scaling the usual spherical-coordinate parameterization independently along the three axes.
