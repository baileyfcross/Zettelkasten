# Phoneme

A phoneme is a language-specific sound category that can distinguish words. Actual pronunciations vary, but phoneme sequences provide an intermediate representation between written words and acoustic observations.

A [[Pronunciation Model]] maps a word to one or more phoneme sequences. The [[Acoustic Model]] then estimates how well observed speech matches those sound units.
