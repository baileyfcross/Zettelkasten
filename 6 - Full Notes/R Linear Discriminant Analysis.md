# R Linear Discriminant Analysis

R linear discriminant analysis classifies observations by estimating class means under a shared within-class covariance structure. The resulting discriminant directions are linear combinations of predictors that emphasize separation among the labeled groups.

Predicted classes and posterior probabilities should be evaluated on observations not used to fit the rule. Strongly non-normal predictors, unequal covariance structures, or a poorly estimated covariance matrix can make the linear boundary unreliable.
