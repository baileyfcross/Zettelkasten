# AI Standardization

AI standardization creates shared technical and procedural requirements for designing, testing, documenting, and operating intelligent systems. Standards can turn broad principles into repeatable practices for safety, traceability, privacy, or transparency across organizations.

Standards are not identical to laws, though regulation may require or recognize them. Their legitimacy depends on who develops them and whose risks they represent. Industry expertise can improve technical detail, but concentrated control can favor business convenience over the public interest. Effective standards therefore need transparent governance, appropriate stakeholder input, and consequences when required practices are ignored.
