# Intelligence Explosion

An intelligence explosion is the proposed feedback process in which an intelligent system improves its own capabilities, then uses the improved capabilities to make still better improvements. Repeated self-improvement could produce a rapid transition from human-level intelligence to [[Superintelligence]].

This idea supplies the mechanism in many accounts of a [[Technological Singularity]]. It depends on strong assumptions: that general intelligence can be built, that the system can redesign relevant parts of itself, and that improvements will continue rather than meet physical, computational, or organizational limits. These assumptions make the scenario important to analyze without treating it as an established forecast.
