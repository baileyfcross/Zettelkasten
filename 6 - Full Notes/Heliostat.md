# Heliostat

A heliostat is a guided mirror that follows the Sun and continually redirects its beam toward a stationary central receiver. A field of individually steered heliostats concentrates sunlight at the tower of a [[Solar Tower Power Plant]]. Mirror reflectivity, aiming accuracy, shading, and intercepted fraction affect how much radiation reaches the receiver; the field's large collection area alone does not determine useful heat.
