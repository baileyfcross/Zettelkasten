# Weighted Residual Finite Element Formulation

A weighted residual finite element formulation substitutes an approximate field into a differential equation and forces the remaining residual to be orthogonal to selected weighting functions. It applies even when a convenient physical energy functional is unavailable.

Choosing the element shape functions as weights gives the Galerkin form. Integration can lower derivative requirements and introduces natural boundary terms before local equations are assembled.
