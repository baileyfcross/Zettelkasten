# Geodetic GPS Surveying

Geodetic GPS surveying uses carrier and code observations at known or coordinated stations to determine relative positions far more precisely than a standalone consumer fix. Long observation intervals and differential processing can reduce common satellite, clock, and atmospheric errors to centimeter scale. The resulting measurements support control networks, crustal-motion studies, boundary work, and refinement of the geoid and coordinate datums used to relate orbital positions to the physical Earth.
