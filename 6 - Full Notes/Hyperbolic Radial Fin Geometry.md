# Hyperbolic Radial Fin Geometry

A hyperbolic radial fin varies its thickness along a profile defined by a hyperbolic relationship and extends around a central axis. The profile can be parameterized in a work plane and revolved to create the three-dimensional solid.

The changing thickness redistributes material between the base and outer radius. Profile continuity, minimum thickness, volume, and surface measurements are necessary checks before comparing the design with constant-thickness radial fins.
