# Complex Inner Product

The standard complex inner product uses conjugation in one argument, such as $\langle u,v\rangle=\bar u^Tv$. This ensures $\langle v,v\rangle$ is real and nonnegative.

It defines length and orthogonality for complex vectors. With real entries it reduces to the [[Euclidean Inner Product]].
