# Local Optimum in K-Means

A local optimum in K-means is a partition that cannot be improved by the algorithm's immediate reassignment steps even though another distant partition may have lower total squared error.

Because the search follows the starting configuration, the method offers no general signal that its local solution is global. Multiple starts and comparison of final objective values are standard safeguards.
