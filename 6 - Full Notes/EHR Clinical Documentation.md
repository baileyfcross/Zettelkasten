# EHR Clinical Documentation

EHR clinical documentation captures clinicians' observations, assessments, plans, and reports. It preserves expressive detail that fixed fields cannot easily represent, but much of that detail appears as free text with abbreviations, negation, uncertain statements, and institution-specific formatting.

The record therefore has two complementary analytic forms. Structured fields support direct counting and filtering, while narrative documentation often requires a [[Clinical NLP Pipeline]] to recover problems, findings, treatments, and context. Treating one form as a complete substitute for the other can miss clinically important evidence.
