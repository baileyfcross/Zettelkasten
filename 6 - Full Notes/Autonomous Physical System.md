# Autonomous Physical System

An autonomous physical system uses processed information to act in the material world without requiring a person to approve every operation. Connected vehicles, industrial devices, and other automated equipment join data analysis to physical consequences.

Governance must therefore examine sensing, processing, control, and accountability as one chain. An error in [[Automated Information Collection]] or interpretation can become movement, restriction, or damage rather than remaining a mistaken record.
