# Pressurization Cycles and Structural Fatigue

Every climb and descent cycles the fuselage between lower and higher pressure differential. Repeated stress can initiate and grow fatigue damage at average stresses well below the material's tensile strength, especially near stress concentrations. Aircraft that fly many short sectors can therefore accumulate structural cycles faster than older long-haul aircraft with fewer flights.
