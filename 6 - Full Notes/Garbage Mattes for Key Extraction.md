# Garbage Mattes for Key Extraction

A garbage matte quickly removes portions of a shot that lie well outside the subject before a fine key is attempted. It need not follow every hair or moving contour; it only reduces the area where the keyer must distinguish the subject from its screen. A narrow band of screen can remain near the subject for the key to resolve. In the book's crowd-shadow example, a roto garbage matte first isolates the performer before the blue-screen key is pulled. This coarse-to-fine division saves detailed keying work.
