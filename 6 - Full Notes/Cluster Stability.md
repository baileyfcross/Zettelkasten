# Cluster Stability

Cluster stability is the degree to which a grouping persists after reasonable changes to the analysis. Cases can be added or removed, features perturbed, starting configurations varied, or samples regenerated and the resulting partitions compared.

A stable result is less likely to be an accident of one sample or initialization. Stability does not prove substantive meaning, but instability is an important warning.
