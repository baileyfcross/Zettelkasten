# Game Music Work-for-Hire Agreement

A game music work-for-hire agreement transfers ownership of commissioned music from the composer to the developer or publisher. The client becomes the legal owner, while the composer generally warrants that the work is original and that the relevant composition, recording, and performer rights can be transferred.

This differs from a [[Game Music Licensing Agreement]], which grants defined permission while allowing some ownership to remain with the composer. A work-for-hire contract may also restrict soundtrack revenue, royalties, or even portfolio use, so retained rights, credits, and promotional permissions must be stated rather than assumed.
