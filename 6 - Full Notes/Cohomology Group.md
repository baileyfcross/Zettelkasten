# Cohomology Group

The n-th cohomology group of a [[Cochain Complex]] is the kernel of the outgoing differential divided by the image of the incoming differential. It measures failure of exactness at degree n.

The [[Ext Functor]] and [[Local Cohomology]] are defined as cohomology of complexes built from resolutions or localization data.
