# Irrotational Flow

Irrotational flow has zero curl of the velocity field, meaning fluid particles have negligible local angular velocity. This property permits velocity to be represented as the gradient of a scalar potential.

When the fluid is also ideal and incompressible, the potential satisfies a Laplace-type equation. Irrotationality can hold outside regions where vorticity is generated even though the flow follows curved paths.
