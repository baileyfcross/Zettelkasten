# Quadratic Interpolation Search

Quadratic interpolation search fits a parabola through three objective values and uses the parabola's extremum as the next trial point.

The bracket is updated and the process repeated. When the objective is locally close to quadratic, it can converge faster than [[Golden-Section Search]], but the fitted extremum must remain suitable for maintaining progress.
