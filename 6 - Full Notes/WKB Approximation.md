# WKB Approximation

The WKB approximation constructs semiclassical solutions of the one-dimensional Schrodinger equation when the potential changes slowly compared with the local wavelength. The wavefunction is written with an amplitude and a rapidly varying phase determined by the local classical momentum.

In classically allowed regions the solution oscillates, while in forbidden regions it grows or decays exponentially. The approximation fails near turning points, where connection formulas are needed to join the regional solutions and obtain quantization conditions.
