# Synchronous Play

Synchronous play requires participants to be connected during the same period. Actions may be real-time or turn-based, but each player's progress depends on the others being present.

The design must handle waiting, late arrival, and sudden absence. Session length and scheduling become part of accessibility because participation requires overlapping time.
