# Ethernet

Ethernet is a local-area networking method developed by Robert Metcalfe and David Boggs at Xerox PARC. It lets computers share a communication medium while coordinating transmissions and recovering from collisions.

High local speed allowed workstations to share files, printers, and servers without behaving like dumb terminals. Ethernet helped make client-server computing practical.
