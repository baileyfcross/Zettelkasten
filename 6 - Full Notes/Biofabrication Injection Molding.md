# Biofabrication Injection Molding

Biofabrication injection molding fills a shaped cavity with cells suspended in a hydrogel precursor and solidifies the mixture under cell-compatible conditions. It rapidly produces millimeter- or centimeter-scale parts with the mold's external geometry.

The approach is robust but usually creates homogeneous material and offers limited control over internal microscale architecture or multiple materials.
