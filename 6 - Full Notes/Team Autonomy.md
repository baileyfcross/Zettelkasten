# Team Autonomy

Team autonomy is the ability to deliver changes within an owned business capability without waiting continually for other teams. It improves when a cross-functional team owns complete [[Bounded Context]]s rather than one technical layer shared by every feature. Autonomy does not eliminate alignment: teams still agree on outcomes and boundary contracts. It reduces coordination queues, context switching, and unfinished work, supporting [[Loose Coupling and High Alignment]] at the organizational level.
