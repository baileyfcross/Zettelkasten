# Sound Occlusion and Obstruction

Sound obstruction occurs when geometry blocks the direct path between an emitter and listener but reflected paths remain, while occlusion more completely encloses or isolates the source. Games approximate these conditions with ray casts and changes to volume or frequency content.

The distinction lets an audio system produce different effects for a pillar between two points and a sound sealed behind a wall. Exact acoustic simulation is usually replaced by efficient perceptual cues.
