# Gamma Distribution

The gamma distribution is a continuous probability family restricted to positive values. Its shape and rate parameters can represent varying degrees of right skew and tail weight, making it suitable for quantities that cannot be negative.

The source introduces it while modeling gene-specific variability, whose distribution is positive and heavy-tailed rather than normal. The chi-squared and exponential distributions arise as special cases under particular parameter choices.
