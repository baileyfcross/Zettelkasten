# Primal and Dual Linear Programs

Every linear program has an associated dual. A primal maximization problem corresponds to a dual minimization problem: primal constraints become dual variables, primal variables become dual constraints, and the coefficient matrix is transposed.

Constraint directions determine signs of dual variables, while sign restrictions on primal variables determine directions of dual constraints. Applying the construction twice returns the original problem, so the labels primal and dual describe a relationship rather than a permanent hierarchy.
