# Phong Reflection Model

The Phong reflection model approximates surface lighting by combining ambient, diffuse, and specular contributions. Diffuse response depends on the angle between light and surface normal, while the specular term produces a view-dependent highlight.

It is a bidirectional reflectance model used to calculate how lights affect an object. Its components are efficient enough for real-time use but simplify the physical behavior of light.
