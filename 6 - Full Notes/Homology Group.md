# Homology Group

The n-th homology group of a [[Chain Complex]] is Hₙ = ker(dₙ)/im(dₙ₊₁). It consists of cycles modulo those cycles already explained as boundaries.

Homology vanishes at a position exactly when the complex is exact there. A [[Short Exact Sequence of Complexes]] produces a [[Long Exact Sequence in Homology]] that relates the homology of all three complexes.
