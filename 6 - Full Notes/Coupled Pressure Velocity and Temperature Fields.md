# Coupled Pressure Velocity and Temperature Fields

Fluid pressure and velocity satisfy mass and momentum conservation, while temperature satisfies energy conservation. In a nonisothermal model, advection carries thermal energy and temperature-dependent properties feed back into the momentum equations.

Boundary conditions must be compatible across all three fields: an inlet may specify flow and temperature, an outlet may constrain pressure, and walls impose both velocity and thermal behavior. Solver convergence then reflects the combined system rather than any single equation alone.
