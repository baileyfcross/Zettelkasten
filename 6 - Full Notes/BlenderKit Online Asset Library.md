# BlenderKit Online Asset Library

BlenderKit connects Blender to an online catalog of models, materials, brushes, and add-ons. Its viewport-facing interface allows assets to be searched, imported, and in some cases uploaded without first building a separate local library by hand.

An online catalog accelerates furnishing and population of environments, but imported content still needs inspection for scale, materials, licensing, and suitability. Availability and integration also depend on the installed add-on version rather than Blender's core asset system alone.
