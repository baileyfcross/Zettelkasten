# Algebraic Connectivity

Algebraic connectivity is the second-smallest eigenvalue of an undirected graph's [[Graph Laplacian]]. It is also called the Fiedler value.

A disconnected graph has algebraic connectivity zero, while a positive value indicates that the graph is connected. Its magnitude supplies spectral information about how difficult the graph is to separate.
