# Orbiting Panning and Zooming in Blender

The 3D Viewport is a two-dimensional window onto a three-dimensional scene. Orbiting rotates the apparent world around a fixed point, panning shifts the view laterally, and zooming changes how much of the scene is visible.

Thinking of the scene as moving in front of the viewer clarifies these controls. Navigation changes the point of view, not the scene objects themselves, so it can be used freely while modeling or inspecting spatial relationships.
