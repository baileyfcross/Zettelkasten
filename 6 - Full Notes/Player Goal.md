# Player Goal

A player goal is a desired state that organizes attention and action during play. Goals can operate over immediate, short-term, and long-term timescales and may be supplied explicitly by the game or created by the player.

Goals give consequences meaning and allow [[Feedback]] to be interpreted as progress, setback, or new opportunity. Nested goals connect moment-to-moment [[Core Gameplay Loop|core loops]] to broader [[Progression System|progression]].
