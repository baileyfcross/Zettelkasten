# Hierarchy of Challenges

A hierarchy of challenges organizes the goals a player pursues from completing the whole game down through levels, missions, sub-missions, and the immediate atomic challenge. Several levels of the hierarchy can be active in the player's mind at once.

The hierarchy gives local actions a larger purpose. It also helps designers find a goal that lacks supporting steps or a low-level task that does not contribute to meaningful progress.
