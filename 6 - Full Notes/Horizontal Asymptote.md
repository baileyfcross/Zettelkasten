# Horizontal Asymptote

The line $y=L$ is a horizontal asymptote of $f$ when $f(x)$ approaches $L$ as $x$ tends to positive or negative infinity. It describes end behavior, not a barrier: a graph may cross the line at finite inputs. A function can have different horizontal asymptotes in its two infinite directions.
