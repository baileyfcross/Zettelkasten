# Point Space-Time Time-Series View

A point space-time time-series view coerces the measurements at each location into a separate temporal line. Superposition reveals network-wide timing and the range of station behavior, especially when thin transparent lines soften overplotting. The view does not preserve geographic proximity and individual paths may be hard to identify. It therefore complements [[Point Space-Time XT Plot|XT plots]] and map slices rather than replacing them.
