# BeiDou Navigation Satellite System

BeiDou is China's satellite-navigation system, initially known as COMPASS. Its early regional architecture mixed geostationary and inclined geosynchronous satellites to maintain coverage over China and surrounding areas, then expanded toward a global service that also uses medium-Earth-orbit satellites. BeiDou supports civil and military users and illustrates how a hybrid orbit design can join strong regional availability with worldwide coverage.
