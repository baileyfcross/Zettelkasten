# Topic Count Selection

Topic count selection chooses how many latent topics a model should estimate. The count is a modeling decision: too few topics merge distinct themes, while too many can fragment a coherent subject into unstable or redundant components.

Held-out [[Topic Model Perplexity]] can narrow the plausible range, but the minimum does not guarantee the most interpretable solution. Comparing nearby counts and examining representative terms and documents lets domain knowledge test the statistical recommendation.
