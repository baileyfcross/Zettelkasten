# Biofabrication Ethics Vignette

A biofabrication ethics vignette is a concrete imagined scenario used to examine the values and uncertainties around a possible technology. A case such as unusually strong engineered muscle makes downstream questions about enhancement, access, safety, and responsibility discussable.

Vignettes do not predict the future; they expose assumptions and help participants locate boundaries before a real decision becomes urgent.
