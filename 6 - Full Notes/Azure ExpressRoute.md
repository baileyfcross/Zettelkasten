# Azure ExpressRoute

Azure ExpressRoute provides private connectivity from customer or colocation networks into Microsoft’s network through a connectivity provider. It offers planned bandwidth, routing, resilience, and quality characteristics better suited to substantial hybrid estates than an internet VPN. A circuit is not a complete recovery design: provider locations, peering, redundant connections, route propagation, encryption requirements, and backup paths must be considered separately.
