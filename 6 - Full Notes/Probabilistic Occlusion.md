# Probabilistic Occlusion

Probabilistic occlusion represents uncertainty about whether a real or virtual surface is in front instead of forcing every pixel into a perfectly known binary decision. Tracking error, model error, and uncertain boundaries can all contribute to the probability.

The composite can use that uncertainty to soften or otherwise manage ambiguous regions. This avoids presenting an inaccurate hard edge as if the registration and scene model were exact.
