# Local Maximum

A local maximum is a point whose objective value is no less than values at sufficiently nearby feasible points. It may differ from the absolute maximum over the full domain.

A differentiable interior local maximum is normally a [[Stationary Point]]. A negative-definite [[Hessian Matrix]] supplies a sufficient second-order condition for a strict local maximum.
