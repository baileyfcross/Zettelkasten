# Structured Data Storage

Structured data storage keeps records under an explicit schema of fields, types, relationships, and constraints. Relational tables are the primary example and support queries and consistency checks across related information.

Structure is valuable when relationships and invariants matter across transactions. It can impose migration and coordination costs when record shapes change frequently or write distribution matters more than global relational constraints.
