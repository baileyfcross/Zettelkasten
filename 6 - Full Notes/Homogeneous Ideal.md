# Homogeneous Ideal

A homogeneous ideal is an ideal generated by [[Homogeneous Element]]s. Equivalently, whenever it contains an element, it contains each homogeneous component of that element.

Quotienting a [[Graded Ring]] by a homogeneous ideal preserves a grading. Extension and contraction along graded homomorphisms also preserve homogeneity under the source's conditions.
