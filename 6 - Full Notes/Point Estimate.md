# Point Estimate

A point estimate is a single value calculated from sample data to approximate an unknown [[Population Parameter]]. A sample mean estimates a population mean, and a difference between sample means estimates a population difference.

The value alone does not communicate how much it would vary across repeated samples. Pairing it with a [[Standard Error]] or [[Confidence Interval]] makes the uncertainty around the estimate visible.
