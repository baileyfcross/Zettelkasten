# Physical Dimension and Measurement Unit

A physical dimension identifies the kind of measurable quantity, while a unit names a scale used to state its value. The book's base-unit table pairs length with meter, time with second, and mass with kilogram. A length can therefore be recorded in different units without becoming a different kind of quantity. Derived quantities combine dimensions algebraically, which is why their unit expressions can be checked by [[Dimensional Homogeneity in Equations]]. See [[Seven SI Base Units]].
