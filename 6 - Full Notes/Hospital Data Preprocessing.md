# Hospital Data Preprocessing

Hospital data preprocessing converts operational records into consistent examples for analysis. It resolves missing or impossible timestamps, duplicates, inconsistent codes, noisy attributes, outliers, and fields whose meaning changed over time.

Cleaning choices should be recorded because removing a rare event or imputing a value can change predictions and may hide a real workflow problem.
