# Global Outlier

A global outlier is an observation that is inconsistent with the overall distribution of a data set. An exceptionally tall building can be unusual among all buildings regardless of what stands beside it.

Its reference group is the complete population under analysis, which distinguishes it from a [[Spatial Outlier]] defined by a local neighborhood. Changing that population can change the judgment: professional basketball players may be global height outliers among all adults but ordinary within a league. Detection therefore requires an explicit comparison set and confidence rule.
