# America Online

America Online grew from earlier attempts to connect game-console and personal-computer users. It offered friendly access, e-mail, chat rooms, and curated content through a private service.

By the late 1990s AOL connected tens of millions of individuals and increasingly bridged them to the Internet. Its community-oriented model helped normalize household networking.
