# Single-Similarity Drug Interaction Prediction

Single-similarity drug interaction prediction infers a candidate interaction from one notion of resemblance, such as chemical similarity between drugs or sequence similarity between targets. The premise is that similar entities may share interaction partners.

A single view is simple and interpretable but can miss relationships expressed in another data type. Structural similarity does not guarantee the same biological effect, and target similarity can be misleading when functional context differs. Predictions should therefore be understood as evidence from one representation rather than a complete interaction model.
