# Independent Replication Method Review

Independent replication method review asks contributors outside the replication team to inspect the planned design before data collection. Original authors can also review whether the proposed procedure reproduces important features of their study.

Concerns are addressed where possible and otherwise recorded in advance with their evidential basis. This reduces avoidable design deficiencies and makes post hoc explanations easier to distinguish from genuine prior warnings. The review strengthens a [[Replication Protocol Standardization|standardized protocol]] while keeping the replication team responsible for the final independent implementation.
