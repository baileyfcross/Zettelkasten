# Planar Perspective Rectification

Planar perspective rectification transforms an oblique view of a flat surface into a front-facing view by estimating the plane's projective geometry. Parallel lines, right angles, or known shapes can supply the constraints needed to compute the transformation.

Rectification can make signs, documents, license plates, and other planar details easier to inspect. It reorganizes sampled information but cannot recover detail that blur, occlusion, or insufficient resolution never recorded; enhancement should not be mistaken for reconstruction of lost evidence.
