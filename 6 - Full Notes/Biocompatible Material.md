# Biocompatible Material

A biocompatible material performs its intended role without producing unacceptable harm to the contacting cells or tissue. Compatibility depends on chemistry, degradation products, stiffness, surface behavior, dose, duration, and the biological setting.

For biofabrication, the material must also tolerate manufacturing conditions that preserve cell viability and function.
