# Cruise Performance Modeling

Cruise performance relates engine speed, pressure altitude, and temperature to outputs such as brake horsepower, true airspeed, and fuel flow. A regression surface can reveal their combined trends and support interpolation inside the handbook's sampled range. The model is useful for planning only when configuration, weight assumptions, and units remain consistent with [[Pilot Operating Handbook Performance Data]].
