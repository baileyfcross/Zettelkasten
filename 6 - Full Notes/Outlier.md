# Outlier

An outlier is an observation that deviates enough from other observations to suggest it may have arisen from a different mechanism. It can be an error, a rare valid case, or evidence of a distinct process.

Declaring a point anomalous requires an explicit reference context. Global distance, local density, statistical probability, and cluster membership can produce different judgments.
