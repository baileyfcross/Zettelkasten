# Floor Live Load Reduction

Floor live load represents movable occupancy effects rather than permanent construction. Building-load provisions permit reduction for members with sufficiently large influence areas because it is unlikely that the maximum specified occupancy load will cover every part of a large supported area at the same time. The reduction depends on the member's tributary or influence area and its role in the framing system.

Reduction is bounded and is not permitted for every occupancy or loading condition. Assembly uses, heavy storage, and other cases with a credible full-area load may require the unreduced value. A member can also have multiple influence areas for different effects. The designer should apply reduction at the correct level—beam, girder, or column—without reducing permanent loads or overlooking concentrated live-load requirements.
