# Multivariate Polynomial Division

Multivariate polynomial division reduces a polynomial by an ordered list of divisors using a fixed [[Monomial Order]]. At each step, a divisible leading term is cancelled; otherwise that term is moved to the remainder.

Unlike one-variable division, the remainder can depend on divisor order unless the divisors form a [[Gröbner Basis]]. A Gröbner basis makes zero remainder a reliable test for ideal membership.
