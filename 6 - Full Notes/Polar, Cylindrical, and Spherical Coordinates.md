# Polar, Cylindrical, and Spherical Coordinates

Polar coordinates locate a planar point by radius and angle. Cylindrical coordinates add height, while spherical coordinates use a radius and two angles to locate a point in three dimensions. Converting these descriptions to Cartesian components makes chart geometry, Earth locations, and the vector operations in [[Navigation Vectors and Ground Velocity]] easier to compute.
