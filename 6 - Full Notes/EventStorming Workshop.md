# EventStorming Workshop

An EventStorming workshop brings domain experts and people who will build the solution into one active modeling session. It needs a large continuous surface, abundant notes and pens, a visible color convention, and enough room for participants to move. The session should favor discovery over presentation: people write and place facts themselves, compare competing flows, and leave unresolved questions as [[EventStorming Hotspot]]s. Its model can later guide deeper workshops, prototypes, and context analysis.
