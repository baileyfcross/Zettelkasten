# Derived Temporal Feature

A derived temporal feature converts a date or time into an analytically useful component such as year, month, weekday, or season. The transformation can expose periodic or long-term structure that a raw timestamp does not express directly.

Derivation rules must match the relevant calendar, timezone, and domain definitions.
