# Jobs as Units of Computational Value

A job is a task that someone considers valuable, and computational design asks how a computer might help perform it. Framing value in terms of jobs keeps attention on what a user or community is trying to accomplish rather than on computation for its own sake.

Some jobs are well-defined enough for [[Automation]], while others involve concerns that cannot be reduced to fixed procedures. Computational thinking can still assist the latter through models, tools, and informed human-computer interaction.
