# M-9 Gun Director

The M-9 gun director was an analog electronic system that aimed antiaircraft weapons from changing tracking data. Wire wound around shaped forms stored trajectory relationships, and feedback continually revised the gun’s direction.

Used with the proximity fuse, it was effective against German V-1 weapons. Its goal-directed behavior anticipated automatic control ideas later implemented in digital software.
