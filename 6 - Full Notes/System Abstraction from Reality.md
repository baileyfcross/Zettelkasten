# System Abstraction from Reality

System abstraction from reality selects a few recognizable relationships from a complex real activity and omits details that do not serve the game. Players can supply or ignore missing detail as long as the retained objects and rules communicate a coherent impression.

Abstraction is not limited to simplification. A designer may also alter reality—such as removing completed rows of shapes—to strengthen the activity's most compelling pattern. The relevant question is therefore not whether a system is realistic in every respect, but whether its chosen rules focus attention on the interaction and feeling the game is meant to produce.
