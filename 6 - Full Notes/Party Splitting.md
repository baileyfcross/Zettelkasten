# Party Splitting

Party splitting occurs when one political organization divides into separate parliamentary groups. It is a group-level change that can happen while individual legislators are also [[Party Switching|switching parties]].

Official group counts and co-sponsorship communities need not divide in the same way. Dense collaboration may persist across formal splits, making session-level networks necessary to compare institutional labels with behavior.
