# Simultaneous Right-Hand-Side Changes

When several right-hand-side values change together, their combined effect can be tested with a 100 percent rule. Each actual increase or decrease is divided by the corresponding allowable change in that direction, and the resulting percentages are added.

A total no greater than 100 percent guarantees that the current basis remains feasible and its dual values can still price the objective change. A larger total is inconclusive: the basis may fail, but direct recalculation is needed to know.
