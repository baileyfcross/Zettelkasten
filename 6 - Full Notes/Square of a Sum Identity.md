# Square of a Sum Identity

The square of a sum identity is

$$(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2.$$

It follows by distributing ((a+b)(a+b)). Geometrically, a square of side (a+b) divides into squares of areas (a^2) and (b^2) plus two rectangles of area (ab).

The middle term is essential: exponentiation does not distribute over addition. The identity can expand a squared binomial, recognize a perfect-square trinomial, or support mental arithmetic by choosing a nearby convenient value.
