# Game Music Project Contract

A game music project contract defines the work, schedule, compensation, expenses, delivery obligations, ownership transfer, credit, confidentiality, warranties, termination, and other conditions governing a composer-client relationship. Exhibits can add a milestone schedule, asset list, payment dates, technical requirements, and revision limits.

Specific scope lowers the risk of conflicting expectations. The agreement should identify the number or duration of assets, included revisions, overage terms, and what happens when production changes. When feature creep appears, the composer should raise it before exceeding the contract and use [[Game Music Contract Negotiation]] to revise scope or compensation.
