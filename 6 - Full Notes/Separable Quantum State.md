# Separable Quantum State

A pure multi-qubit state is separable when it can be factored into a tensor product of states for its subsystems. For two qubits, the amplitudes must admit coefficients $a_0,a_1,b_0,b_1$ such that

$$
|\psi\rangle=(a_0|0\rangle+a_1|1\rangle)\otimes(b_0|0\rangle+b_1|1\rangle).
$$

Expanding the product gives algebraic consistency conditions among the four joint amplitudes. If those equations have a solution, the joint description contains no pure-state entanglement; if they do not, the state is an [[Entangled Quantum State]].
