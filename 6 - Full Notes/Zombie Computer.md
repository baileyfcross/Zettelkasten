# Zombie Computer

A zombie computer is a compromised device that operates under an attacker's remote direction, often without its owner's awareness. The device can send spam, scan other targets, deliver malware, steal information, or generate disruptive traffic.

Large numbers of zombies can be coordinated as a [[Botnet]]. Detecting unexpected outbound communication and resource use is therefore as important as blocking inbound attacks.
