# Augmentation Placement

Augmentation placement describes where generated output appears relative to the user and physical environment. Information may be attached to a world location, an object, the user's body, a display surface, or a moving region controlled by the user.

Placement affects meaning as well as visibility. A world-fixed label identifies a place, an object-fixed overlay follows its referent, and a view-fixed control remains available but no longer claims a physical location.
