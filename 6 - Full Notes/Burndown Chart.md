# Burndown Chart

A burndown chart plots estimated work remaining against time in a fixed interval. Its trend helps a team see whether its present rate is likely to reach the end of a Sprint or release with the intended scope completed.

The chart is a forecast and conversation trigger, not an individual performance measure. Since the end date is fixed, the team can adapt scope or approach when the trend exposes a mismatch.
