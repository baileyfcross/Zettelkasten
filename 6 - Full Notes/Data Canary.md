# Data Canary

A data canary is a check whose correct result is known in advance and whose failure warns that a larger analysis may be wrong. Examples include verifying that percentages sum to 100 percent, that distribution counts equal the number of objects, or that two independent formulas calculate the same average.

Canaries add deliberate redundancy where an unfamiliar data set offers no intuitive way to recognize a bad answer. Passing a canary does not prove that every calculation is correct, but repeated checks make silent errors easier to detect before their results guide balancing or production decisions.
