# Gradient of a Scalar Field

The gradient converts a scalar field into a vector field of its spatial rates of change. In Cartesian coordinates, $\nabla f$ contains the partial derivatives of $f$ with respect to each coordinate.

At a point, the gradient points toward the steepest increase of the scalar and its magnitude gives that maximum directional derivative. It is perpendicular to the scalar field's level surface, which makes it a natural link between potential functions and conservative force fields.
