# Narrative Interpretation

Narrative interpretation is the audience's construction of meaning from arranged events, images, rules, and omissions. A story comes into being not only through what its author provides, but through the connections a player makes while experiencing it.

Games make this process especially visible because players act inside the system and may encounter different fragments. Even when mechanics remain fixed, different players can understand what those mechanics express in different ways.
