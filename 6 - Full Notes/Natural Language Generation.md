# Natural Language Generation

Natural language generation converts a symbolic response into words suitable for the user and situation. It decides what information to express, how to organize it, and which linguistic form to use.

A typical pipeline includes a [[Sentence Planner]], [[Microplanner]], and [[Lexicalization]] stage. The resulting text can be displayed or passed to [[Text-to-Speech]] for spoken delivery.
