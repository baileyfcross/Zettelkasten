# Weblog

A weblog is a Web publication arranged as dated entries, typically with the newest material first and links to related sites. Publishing tools let writers post regularly without editing raw HTML.

Blogs lowered the technical barrier to writing on the Web and developed audiences around frequent personal commentary. Social platforms later absorbed some of their conversational role.
