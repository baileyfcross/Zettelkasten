# Verification versus Validation in Thermal Modeling

Verification asks whether the mathematical model was implemented and solved correctly. Equation checks, conservation checks, solver convergence, and discretization studies address mistakes or numerical error between the intended equations and the computed answer.

Validation asks whether those equations and assumptions represent the physical system well enough for the intended decision. Analytical benchmarks and experiments such as [[Comparing Thermal Simulation with Infrared Measurements|thermal imaging]] supply independent evidence; a converged solution can still fail validation.
