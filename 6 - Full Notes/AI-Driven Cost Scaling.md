# AI-Driven Cost Scaling

AI-driven cost scaling reduces or relocates capacity when demand and service objectives permit. An agent can identify traffic lulls, recommend smaller node types, schedule workloads on cheaper infrastructure, or reverse an earlier performance-driven scale-out.

Cost information must be interpreted together with expected outcomes, workload constraints, and reliability evidence. Scheduled reviews can propose changes, while approval or policy controls prevent savings actions from silently violating performance, availability, or data-location requirements.
