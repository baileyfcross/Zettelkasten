# Limits of Responsible Innovation

The limits of responsible innovation appear when ethical design methods assume that a proposed technology will be built and ask only how to make it acceptable. Under that assumption, ethics can become a lubricant for development or a public justification for business as usual.

Some applications may be too unreliable, harmful, or illegitimate to deploy. A genuine ethical process must preserve the option to pause, reject, or redirect a project. It must also acknowledge that values cannot always be fully articulated as design requirements and that consequences emerge through social use. [[Responsible Innovation]] is strongest when it retains this critical space.
