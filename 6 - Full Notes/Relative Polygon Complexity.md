# Relative Polygon Complexity

Low poly and high poly are relative descriptions rather than fixed polygon-count thresholds. A mesh considered detailed on older hardware may be inexpensive on newer systems, and a film asset may make a real-time game asset appear simple by comparison.

Complexity must be judged against target hardware, game type, asset role, camera distance, and the tools used to author it. The useful question is whether additional geometry improves the player's view enough to justify its processing and memory cost.
