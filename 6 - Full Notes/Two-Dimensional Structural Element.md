# Two-Dimensional Structural Element

A two-dimensional structural element represents an area with two translational degrees of freedom at each node. Triangular and quadrilateral forms support plane-stress or plane-strain analysis through the appropriate material matrix.

The element displacement vector generates strain through a derivative matrix and stress through the constitutive relation. Element geometry and interpolation control whether strain is constant or variable.
