# Capacitor Energy

A charged capacitor stores electric potential energy because work is required to separate charge onto conductors. The stored energy is

$$U=\frac12QV=\frac12CV^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}.$$

For a field-filled volume, the same energy can be regarded as stored in the electric field with density proportional to $E^2$.
