# Engine-Failure Aviate Navigate Communicate Sequence

An engine-failure response first preserves aircraft control and establishes the appropriate glide, then selects a reachable landing site and route, and only then communicates the emergency. Engine checks and restart attempts fit inside that priority without displacing control of the aircraft. The sequence turns the finite altitude represented by [[Power-Off Glide Distance]] into time for orderly action.
