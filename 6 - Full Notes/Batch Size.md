# Batch Size

Batch size is the amount of work grouped before it moves to the next stage or reaches integration. Smaller batches reduce speculative effort, shorten feedback, and reveal workflow or design problems before they affect a large inventory.

A batch cannot always be minimized without cost, but its size should be an explicit economic choice. In game production, smaller asset sets let teams test gameplay, quality, and production rates sooner.
