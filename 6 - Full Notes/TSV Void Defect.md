# TSV Void Defect

A TSV void defect is an unfilled region left during metal deposition inside a through-silicon via. It reduces the effective cross-sectional area available to carry current, increasing current density and accelerating [[Through-Silicon Via Electromigration]].

Because the defect exists before die bonding, stress or burn-in tests can expose it early. Capacitance or resistance sensing can distinguish a defective via and add it to the [[TSV Defect Fault Map]].
