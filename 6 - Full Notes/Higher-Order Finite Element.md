# Higher-Order Finite Element

A higher-order finite element uses a polynomial shape function of degree two or greater and introduces side or interior nodes as needed. It can represent rapidly varying fields or curved geometry more accurately than a first-order element.

Accuracy may be improved through mesh refinement, polynomial order, or both. Fewer higher-order elements can sometimes reach the accuracy of a much finer linear mesh at the cost of larger local matrices.
