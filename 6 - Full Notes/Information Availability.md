# Information Availability

Information availability means that authorized users can obtain information or a service in time for its intended purpose. It includes reach from required locations, readiness when requested, continuity during demand, and recovery after failure.

Availability commitments can distinguish planned downtime, service restoration time, and tolerable data loss. [[Recovery Time Objective]] and [[Recovery Point Objective]] give measurable targets for the last two concerns.
