# AI Purview and Performance

An intelligence can be described by both its purview and its performance. Purview is the range of domains in which it can operate; performance is how well it operates within each domain.

This separates generality from superiority. A narrowly scoped system can exceed every human at one task while lacking [[Artificial General Intelligence]], and a broadly capable system can remain mediocre in several areas. Because digital agents can have direct access to large stores of data and can be copied or accelerated, their pattern of abilities may also differ sharply from the uneven strengths of any individual person.
