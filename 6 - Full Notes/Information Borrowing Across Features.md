# Information Borrowing Across Features

Information borrowing across features uses the collection of related measurements to stabilize inference for each individual feature. A [[Hierarchical Statistical Model]] estimates the population pattern of feature parameters and uses it to regularize noisy feature-specific estimates.

The approach gains power from high-throughput structure while retaining feature-level results. It assumes the features share a meaningful parameter distribution, not that their observed values or biological effects are identical.
