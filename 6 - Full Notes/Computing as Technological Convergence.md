# Computing as Technological Convergence

Computing emerged from the convergence of calculation, data storage, automatic control, and communication. Each capability had a separate technical history before digital representation allowed them to operate within one programmable system.

The result is more than a collection of parts. A device becomes broadly useful when stored information can guide control, calculation can reshape that information, and communications can connect the process to distant people and machines.
