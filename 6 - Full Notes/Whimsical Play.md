# Whimsical Play

Whimsical play creates pleasure through silly actions, surprising consequences, bodily exuberance, or conceptual absurdity. Its rules often make an ordinary behavior strange enough that the sensation of performing it becomes the main event.

Whimsy can arise from constraint as readily as freedom. An awkward controller, deliberately unstable movement, or an implausible rule can produce a coherent experience when the resulting unpredictability supports the game's comic or euphoric character.
