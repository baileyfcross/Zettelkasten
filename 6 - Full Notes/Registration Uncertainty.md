# Registration Uncertainty

Registration uncertainty is the range of plausible locations for an augmentation when tracking, calibration, or the world model is imperfect. Presenting one exact position can falsely imply more certainty than the system possesses.

A visualization can encode uncertainty through a region, altered appearance, or another visual cue. The representation should help the user distinguish imprecise placement from the physical extent of the underlying object.
