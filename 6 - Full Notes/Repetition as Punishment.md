# Repetition as Punishment

Repetition as punishment requires a player to replay part of a game after failure. Returning to a [[Checkpoint]] can make the earlier sequence the price of another attempt without permanently removing the opportunity to continue.

Repetition can serve as practice when it returns the player to an unmastered challenge or an open situation that can unfold differently. Once the repeated actions are already mastered and fixed, it increasingly becomes rote time cost rather than useful learning.
