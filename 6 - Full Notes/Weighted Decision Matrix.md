# Weighted Decision Matrix

A weighted decision matrix compares alternatives by scoring each requirement and multiplying that score by the requirement's relative importance. The weighted results make explicit why one cloud service or vendor is preferred over another.

Weights distinguish critical factors from desirable ones, while scores record how fully each alternative satisfies them. The matrix supports judgment rather than replacing it, because poor criteria or unsupported scores merely give false precision to the decision.
