# Thermal Imaging Validation of Hot Tea

The hot-tea case compares modeled surface temperatures with thermal-camera observations of a cup cooling over time. Matching the geometry, initial state, ambient conditions, and observation locations turns a familiar system into a controlled validation exercise.

Differences between prediction and observation expose uncertain convection coefficients, material assumptions, and measurement limits. The exercise demonstrates that increasing geometric or multiphysics detail is useful only when [[Comparing Thermal Simulation with Infrared Measurements|independent evidence]] tests the result.
