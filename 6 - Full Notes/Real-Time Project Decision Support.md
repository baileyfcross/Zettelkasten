# Real-Time Project Decision Support

Real-time project decision support analyzes current messages, reports, task state, incidents, and resource signals to produce timely risk assessments and recommendations. It reduces the delay between a change in project conditions and management awareness.

Freshness does not ensure completeness or truth. The system should identify data timestamps, conflicting sources, confidence limits, and the decisions that still require accountable human judgment.
