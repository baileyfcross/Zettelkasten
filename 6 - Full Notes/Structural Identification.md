# Structural Identification

Structural identification learns aspects of a probabilistic model's decomposition from data. Instead of assuming every conditional dependency in advance, it compares candidate factorizations or graph structures.

Learned structure can reveal a compact representation that supports more efficient inference. It does not automatically establish causation, because equivalent dependencies and unmodeled variables may explain the same observations.
