# Images as Planes Add-On

Images as Planes imports an image or movie and creates a plane whose aspect ratio, UV mapping, and material correspond to the source. It reduces the setup needed for reference cards, billboards, or other image-backed geometry.

For a modeling reference, an unlit historical material setting kept the image independent of scene lighting, and disabling selection in the Outliner prevented accidental movement. The resulting plane remains scene geometry, unlike a purely editor-level background image.
