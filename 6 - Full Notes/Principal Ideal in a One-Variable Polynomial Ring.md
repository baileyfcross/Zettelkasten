# Principal Ideal in a One-Variable Polynomial Ring

Every ideal in K[x], where K is a [[Field]], is generated by one polynomial. Choosing a nonzero element of least degree and applying the [[Polynomial Division Algorithm]] shows that every other ideal element has zero remainder on division by it.

Thus K[x] is a principal ideal domain. This property supports polynomial greatest-common-divisor and Bézout calculations.
