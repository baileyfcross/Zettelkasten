# Rees Algebra

The Rees algebra of an ideal I is R(I) = ⊕_{n≥0} Iⁿtⁿ inside R[t]. It packages every power of I into one [[Graded Ring]] while retaining multiplication among the powers.

Quotienting by the ideal generated by t in the appropriate presentation yields the [[Associated Graded Ring]]. Rees algebras connect filtrations, blowup-style geometry, and implicit equations.
