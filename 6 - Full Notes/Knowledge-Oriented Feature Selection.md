# Knowledge-Oriented Feature Selection

Knowledge-Oriented Feature Selection, or KOFS, integrates heterogeneous sources by producing a feature ranking from each internal knowledge representation and then aggregating those rankings. It distinguishes external knowledge from the converted internal forms actually used by ranking algorithms.

KOFS can combine sample categories, sample geometry, feature relations, and feature functions without forcing every source into one similarity matrix. Its ranking weights can be learned, and the similarity-fusion [[MSFS Framework]] becomes a restricted case in which sample similarity is the only internal representation.
