# Tweet Text Feature

A tweet text feature derives from the textual content of a post. N-grams, parts of speech, named entities, hashtags, mentions, URLs, punctuation, and repetition provide complementary views of meaning and style.

Short length and informal language make sparsity and normalization important. Features should preserve task-relevant markers rather than treating every platform token as ordinary prose.
