# 3D Object Tracking for Replacement

A moving object's pose may need to be solved separately from the photographed camera when it is to be replaced by CG. The book describes using visible markers or recognizable features, deriving the object's changing orientation, and attaching the new model to that solution. From the object's point of view, some motion can be treated as an inverse camera movement. The render still needs lighting and any foreground occlusion: a hand-roto matte may be necessary to put a photographed object back in front of the CG replacement.
