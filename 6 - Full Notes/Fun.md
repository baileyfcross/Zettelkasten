# Fun

Fun is a voluntary, generally nonconsequential, positively valenced form of sustained engagement. It may arise through sensory, cognitive, social, emotional, or cultural interaction as long as the demands fit the individual's current [[Interactivity Budget]].

Not every engaging game experience is fun in a simple pleasurable sense, but fun experiences are engaging. A design can aim for tension, sadness, or reflection while still supporting meaningful action and continued attention.
