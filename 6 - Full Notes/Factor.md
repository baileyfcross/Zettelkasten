# Factor

A factor is a number or expression multiplied with another to produce a product. In

$$12x^2=3x\cdot4x,$$

(3x) and (4x) are factors of (12x^2). A factor must divide the product exactly within the number or expression system being used.

Recognizing factors permits cancellation in fractions and supports [[Polynomial Factorization]]. A common factor divides every term of a sum and can be taken outside parentheses through the [[Distributive Property]]. Terms joined by addition are not themselves cancellable factors until the expression is factored.
