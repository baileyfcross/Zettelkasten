# Neuromuscular Junction-on-a-Chip

A neuromuscular junction-on-a-chip cultures motor neurons and skeletal muscle in connected microenvironments. Neurites grow toward the muscle and form bioelectrical connections through which neural stimulation produces muscle contraction.

Using patient-derived neurons can model diseases whose origin lies in communication between tissue types rather than in muscle alone.
