# Scrum Principles

Scrum is organized around empiricism, emergence, collaboration, [[Timebox|timeboxing]], prioritization, and self-organization. Together these principles let a team make decisions from observable results while keeping work bounded and directed toward value.

They explain why Scrum practices are structured as they are: a working increment enables inspection, an ordered backlog focuses effort, and a self-managing team adapts the implementation as knowledge emerges.
