# Polynomial Kernel Classifier

A polynomial kernel classifier measures similarity with a powered affine dot product. The implicit feature space includes interactions among original variables up to the chosen degree.

Increasing the degree allows more curved boundaries but also increases sensitivity to scale and raises the risk of fitting accidental interactions. Offset, degree, and coefficient parameters jointly determine which combinations dominate the resulting geometry.
