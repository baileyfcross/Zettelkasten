# Faithfully Flat Module

A faithfully flat module is flat and also detects nonzero modules or failures of exactness after tensoring. Tensoring with it preserves and reflects the relevant algebraic information.

Faithful flatness lets properties such as regular sequences be checked after scalar extension. It is stronger than ordinary [[Flat Module|flatness]], which preserves exactness without necessarily detecting whether an object vanished.
