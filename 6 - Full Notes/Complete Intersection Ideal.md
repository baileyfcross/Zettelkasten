# Complete Intersection Ideal

A complete intersection ideal of height s is an ideal generated by a [[Regular Sequence]] of length s. The number of defining equations matches the codimension measured by prime height.

This equality gives the ideal especially controlled homological behavior. Localizing the condition at every prime containing the ideal gives a [[Locally Complete Intersection Ideal]].
