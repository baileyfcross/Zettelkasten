# AI Energy Consumption

AI energy consumption is the electricity used to train models, operate them, store and move data, and support the devices and infrastructure around them. Environmental cost depends on both the amount of energy and how that energy is produced.

Efficiency improvements can reduce consumption for a given task, but expanded use can increase total demand. Assessment should therefore compare the complete system with realistic alternatives and include ongoing operation rather than focusing on a single computation. AI can also optimize energy use elsewhere, so its net value depends on the relationship between resource cost and the benefit actually achieved.
