# Heuristic Evaluation

Heuristic evaluation is an analytic method in which several usability experts independently inspect a [[User Interface]] using general or domain-specific design guidelines. Representative users are not required.

The experts' findings are combined, ranked, and used to prioritize redesign. The method can expose major problems quickly and inexpensively, especially early in development. Its effectiveness depends on the quality and relevance of the heuristics and the expertise of the evaluators.

For a novel [[3D User Interface]], heuristic evaluation can precede user-based [[Formative Evaluation]] as part of an iterative evaluation sequence.
