# Ultrasonic Friction Modulation

Ultrasonic friction modulation vibrates a touch surface at an ultrasonic frequency so a moving fingertip experiences less friction. The vibration itself is not heard or felt directly; the perceptible event is the reduction in lateral resistance as contact time and the supporting air film change.

Piezoelectric actuators can create micrometer-scale motion that substantially reduces friction. Because this technique only lowers resistance, combining it with [[Electrovibration]], which raises friction, can widen the continuum of textures available to a [[Surface Haptics]] display.
