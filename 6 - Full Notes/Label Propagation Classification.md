# Label Propagation Classification

Label propagation classification spreads information from labeled nodes through a graph to nearby unlabeled nodes. Edge weights determine how strongly connected cases encourage compatible labels.

Repeated propagation seeks a smooth assignment while retaining the known labels as anchors. Its success depends on homophily or another valid relationship between connectivity and class; misleading edges can transmit errors across large portions of the network.
