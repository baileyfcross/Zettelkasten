# Hadoop Ecosystem

The Hadoop ecosystem is the collection of storage, resource-management, query, scripting, ingestion, coordination, and analytical tools built around distributed Hadoop clusters. Its components specialize rather than forcing one engine to solve every data task.

That flexibility creates integration and tuning work: formats, metadata, schedulers, serialization, security, and engine versions must agree across the pipeline.
