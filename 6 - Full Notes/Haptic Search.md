# Haptic Search

Haptic search is the active exploration of a collection to find a target without relying on vision. The searcher reduces complexity by attending to properties likely to distinguish the target, such as the cold, hard feel of keys among softer objects in a bag.

Search time depends on whether the chosen feature produces a [[Haptic Pop-Out Effect]]. Salient material properties can be detected across several contacts with little dependence on the number of distractors, while geometric features such as edge orientation often require serial examination. The best search strategy therefore follows the strengths of touch rather than copying visual search.
