# Renewable Energy Supply

Renewable energy supply is time-varying electricity generated from sources such as solar or wind. Its availability may not align with the timing of data-center workloads.

Planning must forecast both generation and demand, then decide when to execute flexible work, store surplus energy, buy from the grid, or curtail output. Forecast error makes reserve capacity and robust scheduling important.
