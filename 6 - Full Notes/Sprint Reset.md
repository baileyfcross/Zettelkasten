# Sprint Reset

A Sprint reset ends and replans a Sprint when its goal has become obsolete or impossible. Incomplete backlog items return to the Product Backlog and the team begins planning again from the changed reality.

A reset is deliberately rare because it discards coordination and creates overhead. Ordinary discovery should be handled by adapting the Sprint plan while preserving the goal.
