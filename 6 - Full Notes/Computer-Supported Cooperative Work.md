# Computer-Supported Cooperative Work

Computer-supported cooperative work studies how computer systems can support collaborative activities and their coordination.

Collaboration contexts can be classified by time and place. Participants may work synchronously or asynchronously, and they may be collocated or remote. These dimensions affect the communication, awareness, and coordination mechanisms the system must provide.

CSCW is closely related to [[Social Computing]]. In a shared [[Virtual Environment]], it may use [[Telepresence]] so participants in different physical locations can work in the same spatial task area.
