# Personal Health Device Data

Personal health device data are measurements produced by consumer or personal-use healthcare terminals and sensors. They can extend observation beyond institutions and provide frequent records of activity or physiological state.

Device accuracy, user behavior, changing hardware, and incomplete context affect interpretation. Continuous collection also creates privacy and governance questions because intimate information may flow through commercial systems.
