# Computational Thinking Beyond Sequential Steps

Many definitions of computational thinking assume a von Neumann machine that executes a sequence of instructions. Emerging systems can compute through energy minimization, chemical evolution, or learned signal transformations without presenting a programmer with an ordered instruction list.

A broader definition focuses on designing computations and interpreting information processes. It can include new machines while still requiring explicit representations, controllable setup, and a way to interpret results.
