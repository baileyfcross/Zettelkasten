# Deuterium

Deuterium is a hydrogen isotope whose nucleus has one proton and one neutron. The source identifies it as a naturally available fusion reactant obtainable from water. Paired with [[Tritium]], it can undergo the [[Deuterium-Tritium Fusion Reaction]] favored in the book's reactor discussion. Its abundance does not by itself solve the problems of plasma heating, confinement, and thermal-to-electric conversion.
