# Natural Language Understanding

Natural language understanding converts a word sequence into a symbolic representation an assistant can act on. It tries to identify the user's [[Intent]], the relevant [[Entity|entities]], and the relationships among them.

NLU operates after [[Automatic Speech Recognition]] in a spoken assistant. A correct transcript can still be misunderstood, because recognizing words and interpreting their purpose are different problems.
