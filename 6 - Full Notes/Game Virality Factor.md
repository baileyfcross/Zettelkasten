# Game Virality Factor

A game virality factor estimates how many new users each existing user brings into the game. A simple form multiplies invitations sent per user by the proportion of invitations that convert.

A factor above one implies self-amplifying growth only while the assumptions remain stable; real populations saturate and conversion changes. Virality is a result of players choosing to share and others choosing to join, not a substitute for a sustainable experience or business model.
