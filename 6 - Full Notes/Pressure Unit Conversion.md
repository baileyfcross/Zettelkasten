# Pressure Unit Conversion

Pressure and stress are represented in the book by force per area, such as pound-force per square inch or newtons per square meter. Its psi-to-kPa example converts the force with $\mathrm{N/lbf}$, converts the square-inch denominator by an inverse area factor, then changes pascals to kilopascals. Each step has a distinct dimensional role. Converting pound-force without the squared area would not yield pressure. See [[Force Unit Conversion]] and [[Area Unit Scaling]].
