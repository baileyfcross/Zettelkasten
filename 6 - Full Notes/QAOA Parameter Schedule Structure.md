# QAOA Parameter Schedule Structure

A depth-$p$ QAOA circuit has phase angles $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_p$ and mixer angles $\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_p$. Finding these parameters can be difficult because variational landscapes may contain broad flat regions or complicated local structure.

Several cited analyses find regular parameter families that resemble digitized evolution schedules. Easy instances can resemble smooth adiabatic schedules, while harder cases may use diabatic or bang-bang structure. This connection supplies informed initializations and low-dimensional parameterizations, but it does not remove instance-dependent optimization.
