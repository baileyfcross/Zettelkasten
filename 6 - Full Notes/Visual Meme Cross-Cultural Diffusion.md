# Visual Meme Cross-Cultural Diffusion

Visual memes can cross linguistic boundaries by using recognizable poses, facial expressions, layouts, and image-text conventions. Their forms may be easier to identify than jokes whose meaning depends entirely on a particular language.

Visuality does not make a meme culturally neutral. A portrait, gesture, or stereotype can carry local meanings, and captions still require interpretation. Cross-cultural success occurs when the template is legible enough to preserve continuity while remaining open to localized content and stance.
