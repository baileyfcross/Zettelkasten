# Strong-Named Assembly

A strong-named assembly has an identity formed from its simple name, version, culture, and public-key information. The assembly manifest records this identity alongside its files, types, and dependencies, allowing the runtime to distinguish assemblies that share a simple name.

Strong naming historically supports side-by-side assembly versions and identification in .NET Framework deployment mechanisms such as the Global Assembly Cache. It extends the metadata identity of a [[.NET Assembly]]; the compiled implementation remains [[Intermediate Language]] loaded by the runtime.
