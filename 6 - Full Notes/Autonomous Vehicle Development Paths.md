# Autonomous Vehicle Development Paths

Autonomous vehicles can emerge through two broad development paths. A software-centered approach treats the car as a physical peripheral around a data and navigation platform, while an automotive approach progressively integrates sensors, actuators, and assistance functions into an existing vehicle architecture.

The paths prioritize different expertise, risks, and business models. A clean platform can rethink controls and interfaces but must master vehicle engineering; incremental integration benefits from mature manufacturing but inherits old constraints. Early choices create path dependence, influencing later safety assumptions, suppliers, regulations, and ownership models.
