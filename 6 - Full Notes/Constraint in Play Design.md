# Constraint in Play Design

A play-design constraint limits the actions, objects, space, information, or resources available to a player. The limit creates a problem to interpret and work within, turning unrestricted activity into a field of meaningful alternatives.

Constraint is productive when it clarifies what matters while leaving room for choice. A restriction that removes every viable response collapses the [[Play Space]], whereas a carefully placed limit can make strategy, skill, or expression legible.
