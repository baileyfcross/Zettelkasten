# Roblox Premium Payout

Roblox Premium payouts reward an experience for engagement from Premium subscribers under the platform program described by the source. This creates a revenue path based on sustained play rather than a direct item purchase alone.

Because eligibility and formulas are platform policies, they should be treated as version-dependent rather than encoded as permanent assumptions. The durable design lesson is to create an experience worth returning to and to interpret payout results alongside visits, play time, purchases, and updates in [[Roblox Experience Analytics]].
