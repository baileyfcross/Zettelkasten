# Multilayer Basement Wall Heat Transfer

The basement case models heat movement through layered wall and soil regions under several combinations of sources, prescribed temperatures, transient or stationary analysis, and single- or multilayer construction. Each scenario isolates the effect of one modeling choice.

Periodic boundaries represent repeating lateral sections, while profiles through the depth show how material interfaces redistribute temperature. Comparing scenarios makes the [[Thermal Boundary Condition|boundary formulation]] as visible as insulation conductivity in determining predicted heat loss and hot spots.
