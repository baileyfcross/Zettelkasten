# Vanishing Line

A vanishing line is the image of a plane's line at infinity. Two distinct vanishing points formed by directions that lie within the same scene plane determine this line and therefore encode the plane's perspective orientation.

The line is useful for checking whether sets of parallel edges belong to a common ground, wall, or object plane. It also supports perspective rectification. As with individual vanishing points, weak or incorrectly identified line evidence can make the estimate unreliable.
