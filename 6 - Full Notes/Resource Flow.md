# Resource Flow

Resource flow describes how quantified entities enter, move through, change ownership within, convert inside, and leave an internal economy. Mapping sources, drains, stores, traders, and converters exposes the structure behind changing totals.

A flow diagram can reveal mutual dependencies, feedback loops, exploits, and stalled production before they are buried in implementation. An entity that never changes or transfers is not functioning as a resource.
