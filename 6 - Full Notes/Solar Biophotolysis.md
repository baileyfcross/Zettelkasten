# Solar Biophotolysis

Biophotolysis is a sunlight-and-water hydrogen-production route that uses the ability of green algae to release hydrogen. The source specifies an oxygen-free, sulfur-free environment for its example. The biological reactor's light, growth, and gas-handling conditions distinguish it from electrode-based [[Solar Photoelectrolysis]]. It is a proposed production method, not a way for hydrogen to appear without solar input.
