# Thermal Diffusivity from Material Properties

Thermal diffusivity is the ratio of thermal conductivity to density times specific heat capacity, $\alpha=k/(\rho c_p)$. It characterizes how rapidly a temperature disturbance spreads through a material relative to the material's capacity to store energy.

Because it is derived from three other properties, a thermal model can compute it rather than require it as an independent input. Laser-flash analysis can measure its temperature dependence by heating one face of a specimen and tracking the opposite face's transient response.
