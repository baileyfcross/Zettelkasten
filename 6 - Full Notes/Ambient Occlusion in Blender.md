# Ambient Occlusion in Blender

Ambient occlusion approximates the subtle darkening found where surfaces meet, fold, or block indirect light. Those contact shadows reveal creases and small geometric detail that otherwise may look flat.

Eevee uses ambient occlusion as a screen-space effect, while Cycles already calculates broader global illumination and usually has less need for the approximation. Factor and distance settings control its strength and reach.
