# Freelance Technical Proposal

A freelance technical proposal demonstrates understanding of a prospective client's particular problem before presenting a solution. A concise structure can restate the pain point, outline an approach and relevant technologies, point to comparable evidence, and request a conversation about requirements.

A generic inventory of skills gives the client little reason to believe the proposal fits their system. Research, honest limits, and a relevant [[Technical Project Case Study]] are stronger than overpromising. The proposal opens discovery; the detailed obligations belong in a later [[Statement of Work Scope Boundary|statement of work]].
