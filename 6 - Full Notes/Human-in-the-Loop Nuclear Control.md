# Human-in-the-Loop Nuclear Control

SAGE was described as semiautomatic to emphasize that a person would remain responsible for decisions involving nuclear weapons. The term marked a deliberate boundary between machine calculation and human authority.

False alarms caused by hardware faults, software errors, and mistaken test data showed how fragile that boundary could be. Human presence helps only when interfaces and procedures give people time and information to judge correctly.
