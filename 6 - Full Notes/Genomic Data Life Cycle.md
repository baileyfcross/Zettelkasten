# Genomic Data Life Cycle

The genomic data life cycle connects acquisition, transfer, storage, analysis, sharing, reuse, and preservation. Decisions at each stage affect later reproducibility, cost, privacy, and scientific interpretation.

Keeping sequence files is insufficient without metadata, reference versions, processing parameters, provenance, access rules, and stable identifiers for derived results.
