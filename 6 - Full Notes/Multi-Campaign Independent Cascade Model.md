# Multi-Campaign Independent Cascade Model

The multi-campaign independent cascade model represents harmful and corrective campaigns spreading at the same time. Instead of maximizing one cascade, the intervention chooses seeds for the good campaign that minimize eventual misinformation adoption.

The described model assumes that a user cured or immunized by the corrective campaign cannot later be reinfected. Competition between cascades turns timing, network position, and seed budget into parts of the containment problem.
