# Exact Differential Equation

A first-order equation written $M(x,y)dx+N(x,y)dy=0$ is exact when it is the total differential of a scalar function. The compatibility condition is that the cross partial derivatives satisfy $\partial M/\partial y=\partial N/\partial x$ in the region of interest.

Solving the equation means reconstructing a potential function whose partial derivatives are $M$ and $N$. The implicit relation that this potential equals a constant then gives the family of integral curves.
