# Axial Strain

Axial strain is the change in a member's length divided by its original length. It is dimensionless and records relative elongation or contraction rather than absolute displacement.

Within a finite element, axial strain is obtained by differentiating the interpolated displacement field. For a two-node linear bar with constant properties, that derivative and the resulting strain are constant inside the element.
