# Surface Radiation Coupling

Surface radiation coupling adds radiative heat flux to the boundary energy balance of a solid or solid-fluid thermal model. The same temperature field that results from conduction and convection determines emission, while absorbed radiation changes that field in return.

Exterior boundaries may exchange with an ambient, and mutually visible interior boundaries may require [[Surface-to-Surface Radiation|radiosity and view factors]]. Shared dependent variables and consistent emissivity definitions prevent separate physics interfaces from applying contradictory temperatures.
