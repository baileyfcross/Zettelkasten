# Stag Hunt

The Stag Hunt is a coordination game in which mutual cooperation produces the best shared outcome, but choosing the cooperative action alone produces the worst individual result. A safer independent action yields less value but protects the player if the other does not cooperate.

The game has two pure [[Nash Equilibrium|Nash equilibria]]: both cooperate or both choose safety. It distinguishes payoff superiority from strategic security and shows why rational players can settle on an inferior equilibrium when they cannot trust one another.
