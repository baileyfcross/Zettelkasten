# Thinking Ahead of the Aircraft

Thinking ahead of the aircraft means anticipating the next event and at least one event beyond it. Frequencies, weather, configuration changes, checklists, and alternatives can then be prepared before workload peaks. This habit turns good practice into a continuously updated forecast of the aircraft's state rather than a sequence of reactions after each demand arrives.
