# Satellite Communication System

A satellite communication system relays information between separated ground locations through one or more spacecraft. Early experiments tested passive reflectors, low-orbit store-and-forward designs, and active repeaters before geostationary satellites became the dominant architecture for continuous long-distance service.

Low-orbit systems such as Telstar could connect continents only during short passes. A geostationary relay remains over the same region and permits fixed antennas and continuous links. International organizations and commercial firms turned this capability into a global network for telephone traffic, television, entertainment, and data, making orbital infrastructure an engine of globalization.
