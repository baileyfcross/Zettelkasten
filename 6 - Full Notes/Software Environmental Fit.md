# Software Environmental Fit

Software achieves environmental fit when it aligns seamlessly with established practices and social norms. Users can incorporate it into their work without reconstructing their entire way of acting.

An ATM illustrates the principle by preserving familiar banking transactions in a new interface. Fit turns functional capability into practical usefulness by respecting the environment in which the capability is exercised.
