# Split-Complementary Color Balance

A split-complementary scheme begins with one base hue and replaces its direct complement with the two hues adjacent to that complement. It preserves noticeable contrast while distributing it across a less confrontational relationship.

The base hue can establish the environmental field while the two opposing hues separate different accents or gameplay categories. Value and saturation control keep the three colors hierarchical instead of equally dominant.
