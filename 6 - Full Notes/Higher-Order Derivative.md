# Higher-Order Derivative

A higher-order derivative is obtained by differentiating a function repeatedly. The second derivative measures how the first derivative changes, and successive derivatives continue this process with notation such as $f''(x)$ or $d^ny/dx^n$.

Higher derivatives reveal local shape and physical evolution. The sign of the second derivative distinguishes upward from downward curvature, while equations of motion often use second derivatives to represent acceleration and still higher derivatives can appear in approximations or field models.
