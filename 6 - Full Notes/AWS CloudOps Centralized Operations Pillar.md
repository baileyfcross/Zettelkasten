# AWS CloudOps Centralized Operations Pillar

The centralized-operations pillar lets a small number of operational teams apply controlled actions across many accounts, regions, applications, and resources. Central views and automation improve consistency at scale, but access must preserve account boundaries and least privilege. The goal is a shared operational plane for inventory, incidents, patching, and remediation rather than unrestricted administrative access to every workload.
