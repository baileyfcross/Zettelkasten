# Taylor Series

A Taylor series represents a sufficiently differentiable function near a point using its derivatives there. Truncating the series produces a polynomial approximation whose remainder measures the omitted higher-order behavior.

First-order terms provide a local linear approximation. Second-order terms introduce the [[Hessian Matrix]] for multivariable functions and motivate Newton-type optimization and root-finding methods.

In physical approximation, expanding about a chosen point expresses a function as successive derivative corrections. The useful interval depends on convergence, and a truncated polynomial is reliable only when omitted higher-order terms remain small on the scale of interest.
