# Robotics Component Spillover

Robotics often advances by reusing components developed for larger markets. Mobile-phone cameras, game-console depth sensors, wireless networking, microcontrollers, graphics processors, batteries, gears, and materials can become affordable robot parts because another industry already produces them at scale.

This spillover works in both technical and economic terms: a component may solve a robotics problem only after its cost, size, power use, and reliability have improved elsewhere. The resulting robot is an interdisciplinary assembly whose progress depends on surrounding ecosystems, not only on breakthroughs labeled as robotics research.
