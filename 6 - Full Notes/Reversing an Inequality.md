# Reversing an Inequality

Reversing an inequality changes (<) to (>) or (\leq) to (\geq), and conversely. It is required when both sides are multiplied or divided by a negative number.

For (-2x\leq-10), division by (-2) gives (x\geq5). The reversal occurs because multiplication by a negative reflects ordered points across zero on the [[Number Line]]. Forgetting it produces the opposite solution set. Adding or subtracting a negative number does not itself trigger reversal; the rule concerns multiplication or division by a negative value.
