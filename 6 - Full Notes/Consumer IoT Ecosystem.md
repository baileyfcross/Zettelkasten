# Consumer IoT Ecosystem

A consumer IoT ecosystem combines devices, apps, platforms, networks, cloud services, and outside data sources into a coordinated environment for everyday activity. Its value grows when devices share context: a wearable can collect a signal, a phone can display it, a cloud service can analyze it, and another app can incorporate the result.

This multiplier effect distinguishes an ecosystem from a pile of gadgets. Compatibility, account management, automation rules, and data movement determine whether the parts reinforce one another or become separate islands. The same integration that creates convenience also increases dependence on platform providers and expands the surface for privacy and security failures.
