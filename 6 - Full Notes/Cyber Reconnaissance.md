# Cyber Reconnaissance

Cyber reconnaissance is the information-gathering stage of the [[Cyber Kill Chain]]. An attacker studies potential targets, technologies, people, relationships, and exposed services to find a promising path for later action.

Reconnaissance may combine public information with active probing. Reducing unnecessary exposure and monitoring unusual discovery behavior can make targeting harder or reveal an operation before malware is delivered.
