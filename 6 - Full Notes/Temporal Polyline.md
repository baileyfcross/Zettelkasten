# Temporal Polyline

A temporal polyline connects successive observations in a scatterplot according to their time order. The path shows how a pair of variables evolves, revealing direction, loops, reversals, and distinct trajectories that an unconnected point cloud conceals. Color can identify groups, and selected year labels can establish direction. Dense crossings remain difficult to follow, so [[Year-Panel Comparison]] or animation may complement the static path.
