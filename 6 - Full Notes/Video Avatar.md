# Video Avatar

A video avatar represents a remote participant with captured image content placed inside a shared virtual or augmented space. The representation may be a textured plane, a segmented body image, or video mapped onto reconstructed geometry.

Unlike a fully synthetic avatar, it preserves real appearance and nonverbal behavior from the camera. Its viewpoint, scale, segmentation, and placement determine whether it conveys presence without conflicting with the surrounding scene.
