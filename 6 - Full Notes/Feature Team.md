# Feature Team

A feature team is a cross-discipline group able to deliver a player-facing feature across the components and crafts it requires. Its backlog and Sprint Goals are organized around integrated value instead of one technical layer.

Feature teams reduce handoffs and support [[Whole Game Focus]], but they require shared architecture, standards, and access to specialized knowledge to avoid duplicating or diverging foundational work.
