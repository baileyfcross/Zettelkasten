# Logistic Regression Model Presentation

A logistic regression model presentation reports odds ratios and confidence intervals for the exposure and adjusted covariates. It may place unadjusted, partially adjusted, and fully adjusted models side by side to show how the estimates change.

Reference groups must be named, absent terms marked, and intervals shown on the odds-ratio scale. A tidy exported model table reduces transcription errors and makes it easier to assemble the journal-ready layout.
