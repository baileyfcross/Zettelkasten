# Exact and Derived Conversion Factors

The book distinguishes factors fixed precisely by definition or agreement from derived factors obtained by calculation or decimal truncation. Its app marks a miles-to-feet factor as Exact and a liquid-pint-to-cubic-meter factor as Derived. This status affects how much precision can be claimed from the factor; a displayed string of decimal places should not be confused with accuracy of a measured input. The distinction is tied to [[Significant Digits in Unit Conversion]].
