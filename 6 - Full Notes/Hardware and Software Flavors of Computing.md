# Hardware and Software Flavors of Computing

Computational thinking divided early into hardware and software flavors. Computer engineers emphasized physical circuits and machine architecture, while software designers and theorists emphasized programs, languages, algorithms, and formal properties.

The division reflected institutional homes as well as technical focus. Reliable systems nevertheless depend on their interaction because software abstractions ultimately execute through hardware behavior.
