# Eigenvector

An eigenvector of a matrix is a nonzero [[Vector]] whose direction is unchanged by multiplication with that matrix. The result equals the original vector multiplied by a scalar called its [[Eigenvalue]].

When PageRank converges, multiplying the final [[PageRank Vector]] by the Google matrix returns the same vector. It is therefore the matrix's leading eigenvector with eigenvalue one.

For a chosen eigenvalue $\lambda$, eigenvectors are the nonzero solutions of the [[Homogeneous Linear System]] $(A-\lambda I)x=0$. Together with the zero vector they form the corresponding [[Eigenspace]], so every nonzero scalar multiple is also an eigenvector.
