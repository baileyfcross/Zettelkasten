# Bartlett Test

Bartlett's test evaluates whether several groups have equal variances. A small p-value provides evidence against [[Homogeneity of Variance]] and may motivate an unequal-variance or nonparametric method.

The test is sensitive to non-normal data and to very large sample sizes. It should therefore complement graphical inspection and substantive judgment rather than automatically determine the analytic method.
