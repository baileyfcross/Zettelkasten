# Predicate Logic

Predicate logic extends [[Propositional Logic]] by representing properties and relations that become propositions when applied to entities. A one-place predicate P associates each entity in its domain of discourse with the proposition P(a), while predicates with several places express relations among several entities.

Variables in an open statement remain free until a [[Universal Quantifier]] or [[Existential Quantifier]] binds them. This lets logic express general claims that propositional variables alone cannot preserve.
