# Choice Overload in Games

Choice overload occurs when the number or complexity of options exceeds the player's ability or willingness to compare them. Additional alternatives can then reduce confidence and satisfaction instead of increasing [[Player Agency]].

Designers can manage overload by grouping options, revealing them progressively, supplying meaningful comparison criteria, or limiting the decision to alternatives that produce distinct consequences. The aim is not the fewest options, but a choice set the player can actually reason about.
