# Discrete Copula Quantum Generator Benchmark

The discrete copula circuit with probability-integral-transformed data is the most consistently successful quantum configuration across two- and three-dimensional mixed-Gaussian, X-shaped, O-shaped, and stock-return datasets. Both QCBM and QGAN variants often achieve good KL divergence with a single circuit block, and additional blocks give little improvement.

The clearest case is the three-dimensional X distribution: a copula QGAN reaches KL $0.029$ with 54 parameters, while the best listed classical GAN reaches $0.089$ with 9,475 parameters. In two dimensions, the same quantum configuration obtains the best reported X and stock-return KL values, while classical models remain strongest on some mixed-Gaussian and O cases.

The experiment shows parameter-efficient training fit on low-dimensional simulated circuits. It does not assess generalization, real-device noise, total training resources, or a complete classical frontier, so it is architecture evidence rather than a practical quantum advantage demonstration.
