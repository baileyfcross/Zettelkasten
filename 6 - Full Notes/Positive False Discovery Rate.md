# Positive False Discovery Rate

The positive false discovery rate is the expected [[False Discovery Proportion]] conditional on the procedure selecting at least one feature. It removes the zero-discovery cases that automatically contribute a zero proportion to the ordinary false discovery rate.

This conditional quantity is useful when an analysis fixes a candidate threshold and expects a nonempty list. Estimating it can use the observed distribution of p-values and an estimate of how many null hypotheses are true.
