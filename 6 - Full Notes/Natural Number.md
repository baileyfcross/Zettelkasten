# Natural Number

A natural number is a member of the ordered collection

$$\mathbb{N}=\{0,1,2,3,\ldots\}.$$

Natural numbers are used to count objects and identify positions in an order. Addition and multiplication of two natural numbers produce another natural number, so these operations satisfy the [[Closure Property]] on this set. Subtraction and division can leave it: (3-5) is not natural, and neither is (3/5). Extending natural numbers with opposites produces the [[Integer]]s.
