# Face Validity of Clusters

Face validity of clusters is an expert judgment that the grouping appears plausible in the application domain. A panel can examine representative cases, defining attributes, visualizations, and exceptions.

Face validity is valuable when external labels are unavailable, but it remains subjective. It should complement stability and quantitative validation rather than replace them.
