# Platform Build-or-Buy Decision

A platform build-or-buy decision compares creating a capability with adopting an existing commercial or open-source solution. Buying includes free software because adoption still transfers some design control to an external product and creates integration, upgrade, and dependency obligations.

The decision should evaluate differentiation, fit, security, support, team skills, time, operating cost, composability, and long-term maintenance without assuming engineering from scratch is inherently more flexible. The people who will operate the result need to participate. A decision tree makes the assumptions discussable, but team judgment and lifecycle cost determine the answer.
