# Evaluation as Design Diagnosis

Evaluation converts observed play into a diagnosis of why the experience diverged from or fulfilled the design intent. It distinguishes evidence about what happened from a proposed change to the rules, interface, presentation, or content.

A diagnosis should be tested against [[Game Design Values|design values]] and more than one possible remedy. Jumping from a player's symptom to the first suggested feature treats input as an order rather than information for the next iteration.
