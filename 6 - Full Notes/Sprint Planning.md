# Sprint Planning

Sprint Planning establishes a [[Sprint Goal]], selects feasible [[Product Backlog Item|Product Backlog Items]], and creates an initial plan for delivering them. The Product Owner clarifies value and priorities while the Development Team determines what it can accomplish and how.

The plan is a forecast based on capacity and past results. It can be adapted during the Sprint as long as the goal remains viable.
