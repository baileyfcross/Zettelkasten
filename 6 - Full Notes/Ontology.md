# Ontology

An ontology is an explicit organization of concepts, properties, and relationships in a domain. For language understanding, it provides a shared structure into which phrases can be mapped.

An [[Intent Schema]] is a practical, task-oriented form of conceptual organization. Rich ontologies can support inference, but creating and maintaining them is labor-intensive and limits the easy scaling of hand-built understanding systems.
