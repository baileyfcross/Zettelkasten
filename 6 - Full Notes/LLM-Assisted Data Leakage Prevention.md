# LLM-Assisted Data Leakage Prevention

LLM-assisted data leakage prevention classifies sensitive content and interprets access or transfer patterns to identify activity that may expose protected information. It can recognize meaning across documents and communication that simple pattern matching misses.

The prevention system itself processes sensitive material and therefore needs strict access, retention, isolation, and audit controls. Model output should be combined with deterministic data rules and human review before disruptive blocking or disciplinary action.
