# Blender Edge Loops and Rings

An edge loop follows a connected path through mesh topology. A closed loop returns to its beginning, while a terminating loop stops at a pole or another interruption in the surrounding edge flow.

Loops make coherent surface regions easier to select and refine. Edge rings cross the mesh in the complementary direction, and loop cuts can introduce new rings whose position can be slid while preserving the broader form.
