# Signed Area Enclosed by a Parametric Curve

For a positively oriented simple closed planar curve, the enclosed signed area can be computed from its parameterization by

$$A=\frac12\oint(x\,dy-y\,dx).$$

The expression sums the oriented areas of infinitesimal triangles formed with the origin. Reversing the traversal reverses the sign, while the magnitude remains the geometric area for a [[Jordan Curve]].
