# Grease Pencil Layers and Materials

Grease Pencil appearance comes from object materials rather than being stored only in the drawing brush. Material slots act like a palette of defined stroke and fill treatments.

Its layers form an ordered stack in which higher layers can cover lower ones. Layers can be tinted, assigned pass indices or view layers, and controlled separately, joining familiar drawing organization with Blender's rendering and compositing structure.
