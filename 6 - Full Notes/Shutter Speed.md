# Shutter Speed

Shutter speed is the duration for which a camera gathers light during an exposure. A longer duration records more light but also integrates movement over more time; a shorter duration reduces motion blur but admits less light.

The visible sharpness of moving objects can therefore be checked against the exposure time stored in metadata. The inference depends on the unknown speed and direction of motion and on camera movement, so it supplies a constraint rather than a unique reconstruction.
