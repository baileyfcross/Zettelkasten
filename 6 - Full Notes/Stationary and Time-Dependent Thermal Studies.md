# Stationary and Time-Dependent Thermal Studies

A stationary study solves the state in which time derivatives vanish, making it appropriate for equilibrium operating conditions. A time-dependent study advances from an initial condition and records how temperature, flow, and stored energy evolve.

The choice follows the question rather than a preference for shorter runs. A stationary model cannot report warm-up time or transient overshoot, while a time-dependent model may use a stationary solution as an efficient initial state for later changing conditions.
