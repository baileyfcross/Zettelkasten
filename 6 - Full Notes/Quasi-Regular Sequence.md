# Quasi-Regular Sequence

A sequence generating an ideal I is quasi-regular on M when every homogeneous polynomial relation that evaluates into I^{t+1}M has all its coefficients in IM. The definition is independent of the order of the sequence.

Every [[Regular Sequence]] is quasi-regular. The condition describes how the sequence behaves in the [[Associated Graded Ring]] and controls relations among successive ideal powers.
