# Party-Based Interaction Model

A party-based interaction model gives the player control over a small group of characters that usually remain together but may separate briefly. It is common in role-playing games.

The interface must make selection, orders, inventory, formation, and individual status manageable without losing the group as a coherent unit. Aerial views and point-and-click navigation often support this scope.
