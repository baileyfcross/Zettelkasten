# Amazon DataZone

Amazon DataZone provides a business-oriented environment for cataloging, discovering, requesting, sharing, and governing data across organizational teams. Producers publish data products with business context, and consumers find and request access through governed workflows. DataZone complements technical catalogs by making ownership and intended use visible; it does not replace the underlying storage, transformation, permissions, or quality systems that produce and protect the data.
