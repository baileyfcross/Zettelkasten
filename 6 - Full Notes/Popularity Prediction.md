# Popularity Prediction

Popularity prediction estimates the future attention received by a post, story, topic, or event. Models can use content features, early user behavior, lifecycle patterns, and the topology through which information travels.

The output may be a ranking, a class, or a numerical count. Its usefulness depends on timely forecasts and evaluation against later observations rather than fit to the training history alone.
