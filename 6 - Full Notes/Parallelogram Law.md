# Parallelogram Law

The parallelogram law states

$$\|u+v\|^2+\|u-v\|^2=2\|u\|^2+2\|v\|^2.$$

The vectors $u+v$ and $u-v$ form the diagonals of the parallelogram determined by $u$ and $v$. Expanding both squared lengths with the [[Dot Product]] proves the identity by cancellation of the mixed terms.
