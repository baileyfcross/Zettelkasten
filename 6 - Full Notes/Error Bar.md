# Error Bar

An error bar displays the uncertainty attached to a plotted data point. Horizontal and vertical bars can represent uncertainty in either experimental variable. Their extent helps judge whether a proposed line is consistent with the observations and supports construction of steepest and shallowest plausible gradients rather than treating every plotted coordinate as exact.

In R, error bars are added from explicit lower and upper endpoints rather than inferred from the plotted estimate. This keeps standard deviations, standard errors, and confidence intervals visually similar but semantically distinct; the caption must identify which quantity and interval level the endpoints represent.
