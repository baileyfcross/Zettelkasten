# Distal Attribution

Distal attribution is the learned experience that sensory-substitution stimulation represents an external object rather than merely a pattern on the body. A trained user may feel that a camera-derived tactile pattern on the tongue, back, or abdomen describes something located ahead in the environment.

The shift shows that perception is organized around the source and use of information, not permanently tied to the receptor surface receiving it. Reliable sensorimotor coupling and practice are critical: the user must learn how changes in movement and scene produce changes in the [[Tactile Vision Substitution System|tactile display]].
