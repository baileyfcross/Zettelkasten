# Rumor Cascade

A rumor cascade is the branching sequence of users and transmissions through which an unverified claim spreads. Its depth, breadth, timing, and participating communities describe propagation separately from the claim's truth value.

Rumors and non-rumors can travel through the same network, so a large cascade is not proof of falsity. Comparisons must control for topic, audience, observation window, and platform behavior.
