# Statistical Software Licensing

Statistical software licensing determines who may install, run, and distribute an analytic environment and which components are available. Proprietary systems may tie procedures, seats, renewal dates, or support to paid agreements, while open-source systems allow use without a license fee.

Licensing is part of analytic feasibility. A method that depends on an unavailable component cannot be reproduced by a collaborator, and a workflow affordable in a university may be impractical for a nonprofit or public-health organization outside that license.
