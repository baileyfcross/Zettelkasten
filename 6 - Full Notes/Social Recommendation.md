# Social Recommendation

Social recommendation augments a recommender system with relationships or interactions among users. Friendship, following, trust, membership, and communication can add evidence beyond a person's direct history with items.

A useful model distinguishes [[Individual Preference]] for content from [[Interpersonal Influence]] exerted by senders. Combining them explains why the same item may be adopted by one receiver and rejected by another.
