# Photovoltaic Battery Capacity

Battery capacity in an [[Autonomous Photovoltaic Plant]] must cover demand during a chosen number of low-sun days without exceeding allowable discharge depth. The source's sizing inputs are daily load, reserve factor, autonomy days, battery voltage, discharge limit, and efficiency. A battery's rated ampere-hours alone do not describe usable delivered energy, because charging and discharging incur losses.
