# Microsoft Foundry Model Catalog

The Microsoft Foundry Model Catalog is the discovery surface for foundation and specialized models available to a project. Models can be filtered by collection, provider, capability, inference task, and fine-tuning support, then inspected for lifecycle, benchmarks, licensing, Responsible AI guidance, modalities, and suitable use cases.

The catalog narrows a large supply of models into candidates; it does not choose the production model automatically. A team must relate catalog metadata to its own task, data residency, context, latency, cost, safety, and deployment constraints. Shortlisted models should then be compared and evaluated against representative application data.
