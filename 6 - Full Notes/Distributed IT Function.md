# Distributed IT Function

A distributed IT function embeds technology capability in business units rather than concentrating every operational decision in one central department. Local teams can select and use cloud services close to the needs they support.

A shared leadership role still defines standards, governance, and audit expectations, while finance and procurement can manage billing and contracts. Distribution improves responsiveness only when the organization preserves common security, integration, and service-management practices.
