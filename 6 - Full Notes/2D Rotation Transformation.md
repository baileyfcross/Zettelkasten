# 2D Rotation Transformation

A two-dimensional rotation moves a point through an angle about the origin while preserving its distance from the origin. With the book's row-vector convention, the sine and cosine terms are arranged so a positive angle produces counterclockwise rotation and a negative angle produces clockwise rotation.

The formulas follow from angle addition: x prime equals x cosine theta minus y sine theta, while y prime equals x sine theta plus y cosine theta. Rotation about another point is built by translating the chosen center to the origin, rotating, and translating back.
