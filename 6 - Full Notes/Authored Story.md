# Authored Story

An authored story is a deliberately shaped narrative carried by a game. Its creator arranges plot, characters, context, rules, images, words, animation, and sound to communicate particular events or ideas.

Authorship can range from a fixed story shown alongside play to a story expressed through the playable system itself. It does not exclude player action, but the available material and paths are intentionally provided by the creator.
