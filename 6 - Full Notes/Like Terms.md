# Like Terms

Like terms have the same variable factors raised to the same corresponding powers. Their order can differ because multiplication is commutative, so (4a^2b^3) and (7b^3a^2) are like terms.

Only their [[Numerical Coefficient]]s may differ. Terms such as (4a) and (4a^2), or (4ab^2) and (4ab^3), are unlike because at least one exponent differs. Matching structure permits [[Combining Like Terms]]; superficial similarity in letters is not enough.
