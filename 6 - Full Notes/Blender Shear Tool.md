# Blender Shear Tool

Shear offsets points along one axis in proportion to their position along another, slanting a form while parallel layers remain parallel. In interactive use, the current view and orientation determine the apparent horizontal shear direction.

The operation changes angles without applying a simple rotation. Choosing the correct axis, orientation, and pivot is essential when producing leaning walls, skewed supports, or stylized low-poly forms with controlled proportions.
