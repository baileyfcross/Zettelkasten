# Roblox Hinge and Spring Constraints

A HingeConstraint lets two attached parts rotate around one shared axis. Its attachment axes must align, and limits can restrict the permitted angle. A SpringConstraint instead applies a restoring force based on its length and stiffness, with damping controlling how quickly oscillation loses energy.

These constraints describe different mechanical relationships: hinges define rotational freedom, while springs continuously respond to displacement. Both depend on correct [[Roblox Attachments and Constraints]], and both can produce unstable or surprising results when dynamic parts begin in an intersecting or badly aligned state.
