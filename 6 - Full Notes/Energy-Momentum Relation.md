# Energy-Momentum Relation

The energy-momentum relation connects a particle's total energy, spatial momentum, and invariant mass:

$$E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4.$$

It follows from the invariant Minkowski norm of [[Four-Momentum]]. Energy and momentum change between inertial frames, but the combination $E^2-p^2c^2$ remains equal to $m^2c^4$.

For a particle at rest, $p=0$ and the equation reduces to [[Mass-Energy Equivalence|$E=mc^2$]]. For a massless particle, it reduces to $E=pc$, allowing nonzero energy and momentum even when invariant mass is zero.

The relation also provides a direct way to calculate [[Invariant Mass]] from measured energy and momentum, including the invariant mass of a multiparticle system whose constituents need not be at rest.
