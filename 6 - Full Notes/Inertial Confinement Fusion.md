# Inertial Confinement Fusion

Inertial confinement fusion compresses a small deuterium-tritium fuel pellet with a brief, intense laser pulse. The fuel reaches extreme density and temperature and reacts during the short interval before it expands. No sustained magnetic chamber is needed for that event, unlike [[Magnetic Fusion Confinement]]. A power plant would still have to repeat the process and collect useful heat from the reactions.
