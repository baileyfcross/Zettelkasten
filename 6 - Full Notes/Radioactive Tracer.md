# Radioactive Tracer

A radioactive tracer is an isotope incorporated into a biologically active substance so its distribution can be detected outside the body. The tracer should emit sufficiently penetrating radiation, have a half-life long enough for the procedure but short enough to limit dose, and participate in the process under investigation without substantially altering it.
