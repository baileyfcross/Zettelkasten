# Legendre Polynomial

Legendre polynomials are polynomial solutions of Legendre's differential equation. Indexed by a nonnegative integer, they are mutually orthogonal on the interval from $-1$ to $1$ with unit weight.

They arise when Laplace-type equations are separated in spherical coordinates and the solution has axial symmetry. Their recurrence, parity, and orthogonality properties make them a natural basis for angular dependence and multipole expansions.
