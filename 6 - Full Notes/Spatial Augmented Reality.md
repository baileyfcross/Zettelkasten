# Spatial Augmented Reality

Spatial augmented reality places the display technology in the environment rather than carrying it on the user's head or body. Stationary screens or projectors present augmentations at fixed physical locations and can support several viewers without requiring each person to wear a display.

Projected forms place light directly on physical surfaces, making the object itself part of the display. Their usable geometry, brightness, shadows, and occlusion depend on the projector, object, user, and environmental arrangement.
