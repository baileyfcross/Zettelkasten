# Patient Operation Time Consumption Model

A patient operation time consumption model predicts how long a particular patient will occupy a treatment task. The source's model uses an optimized random forest trained from historical records containing timestamps, patient characteristics, and treatment details.

Individual duration estimates become inputs to queue waiting-time and route recommendations rather than direct clinical diagnoses.
