# Uncertain Associative Classification

Uncertain associative classification mines class association rules when items or attribute conditions have probabilistic presence. Expected support replaces the integer count used with certain transactions.

Rules must remain frequent and class-informative after uncertainty is integrated. Treating every possible item as present exaggerates support, while thresholding probabilities too early discards graded evidence supplied by the uncertainty model.
