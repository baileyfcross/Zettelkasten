# Cabin Climb-Rate Control

Cabin pressure is changed gradually so the cabin reaches its selected altitude near, but normally before, the aircraft reaches cruise or landing altitude. A rate that is too low can let pressure differential exceed its limit during a rapid aircraft climb, while an unnecessarily high rate increases occupant discomfort. Setting the rate therefore coordinates aircraft vertical speed, target cabin altitude, and a timing buffer.
