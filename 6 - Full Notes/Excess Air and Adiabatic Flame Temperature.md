# Excess Air and Adiabatic Flame Temperature

Adiabatic flame temperature is the maximum temperature predicted when combustion exchanges no heat with its surroundings and reactant and product enthalpies balance. The source's octane calculations show that adding excess air lowers this temperature because more gas shares the released energy. The result informs material selection and connects [[Fuel-Air Mixture Control]] to engine temperature and efficiency.
