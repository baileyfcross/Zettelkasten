# Kotlin Jump Expressions

Kotlin uses `break` to leave a loop, `continue` to skip to its next iteration, and `return` to leave a function or selected functional context. These expressions interrupt the ordinary top-to-bottom flow rather than merely computing another value.

Labels can identify the loop or return target when control structures are nested. A labeled jump makes the destination explicit, but it also signals that the surrounding flow has become more complex and should be kept narrow enough to remain understandable.
