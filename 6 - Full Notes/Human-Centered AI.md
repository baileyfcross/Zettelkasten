# Human-Centered AI

Human-centered AI directs the design and governance of artificial intelligence toward human dignity, agency, rights, and well-being. It rejects systems that make people serve technical optimization and requires that important automated processes remain answerable to those they affect.

The approach is valuable when it protects people from manipulation or unaccountable power, but its scope is contested. A strictly human-centric policy can neglect animals, ecosystems, and the material conditions on which human life depends. [[Anthropocentrism in AI Ethics]] identifies this limit, while [[Sustainable AI]] expands the account of responsible design beyond immediate human users.
