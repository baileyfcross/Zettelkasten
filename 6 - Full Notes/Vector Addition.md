# Vector Addition

Vector addition combines corresponding components: $(u+v)_i=u_i+v_i$. In an abstract [[Vector Space]], the operation must be closed, associative, commutative, and possess a [[Zero Vector]] and additive inverses.

Geometrically, addition composes displacements. Algebraically, it combines vectors used in a [[Linear Combination]].
