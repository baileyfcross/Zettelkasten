# Fixed-Ratio Reinforcement Schedule

A fixed-ratio reinforcement schedule gives a reward after a known number of responses. Continuous reinforcement is the special case in which every response is rewarded.

The schedule tends to produce a burst of activity followed by a pause after the reward. An RPG that grants a level after every tenth monster uses a fixed ratio: progress is predictable, but engagement can dip immediately after the threshold is reached.
