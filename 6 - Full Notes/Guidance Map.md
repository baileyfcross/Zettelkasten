# Guidance Map

A guidance map is a map, radar, route overlay, compass, or similar system that explicitly directs the player toward an objective. It is especially useful when the game world is too large or unfamiliar for a player to infer a reliable route from the environment alone.

Strong guidance can also suppress spatial learning. A player who follows a highlighted path may watch the indicator instead of forming a [[Player Mental Model]] of landmarks and connections. Designers should decide whether rapid arrival or environmental knowledge is more important and tune the precision of the guidance accordingly.
