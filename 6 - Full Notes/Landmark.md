# Landmark

A landmark is an easily distinguishable object used to maintain spatial orientation, estimate distance or direction, and build landmark and route knowledge.

A global landmark is visible from much of the environment and supplies broad directional guidance. A local landmark supports decisions at a particular point. Effective landmarks are placed where they can be noticed and are visually distinct in color, texture, lighting, shape, or size.

Landmarks make environments more legible and support [[Wayfinding]], [[Spatial Knowledge]], and the construction of a [[Cognitive Map]].
