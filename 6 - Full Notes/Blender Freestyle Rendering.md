# Blender Freestyle Rendering

Freestyle is Blender's line-based nonphotorealistic rendering system. It derives contour strokes from three-dimensional surfaces and can combine those strokes with Eevee or Cycles shading to produce technical drawings, cartoons, or hand-drawn effects.

Global controls establish absolute or resolution-relative line thickness. More detailed line-set and line-style controls then decide which edges become strokes and how those strokes look.
