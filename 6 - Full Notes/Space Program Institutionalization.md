# Space Program Institutionalization

Space program institutionalization occurs when temporary projects create permanent agencies, laboratories, contractors, facilities, skills, budgets, and political constituencies. Once established, these organizations can sustain space activity after the crisis or competition that originally funded them has ended.

Institutionalization preserves expertise and enables new science and services. It can also make program choice path-dependent: centers seek missions that maintain their workforces, contractors defend production lines, and elected officials protect local jobs. The result helps explain both the durability of spaceflight and the continuation of expensive projects whose original rationale has weakened.
