# Strongly Connected Attribute Values

Two categorical values from different attributes are strongly connected when their observed co-occurrence exceeds a multiple of the support expected under independence. Sets are strongly connected when all relevant cross-pairs meet that condition.

CACTUS uses maximal strongly connected sets with sufficient support as the basis for cluster projections and candidates.
