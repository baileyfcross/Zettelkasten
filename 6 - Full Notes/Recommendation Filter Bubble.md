# Recommendation Filter Bubble

A recommendation filter bubble forms when personalization repeatedly selects information close to a user's observed interests and thereby limits exposure to conflicting, challenging, or unfamiliar material. The system's view of relevance can become self-reinforcing because the options it withholds cannot generate counterevidence.

The risk is not personalization alone but an evaluation objective that rewards immediate agreement without valuing breadth. [[Recommendation Serendipity]], diversity constraints, and deliberate exploration can introduce relevant difference, although each may trade short-term engagement for a healthier information environment.
