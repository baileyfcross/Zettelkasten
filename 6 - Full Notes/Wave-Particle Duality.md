# Wave-Particle Duality

Wave-particle duality describes quantum entities through both wave-like interference and particle-like localized detection. Which behavior becomes evident depends on the experimental arrangement. The two descriptions are not classical alternatives carried simultaneously in ordinary form; quantum theory uses a state amplitude to predict probabilities for discrete outcomes.
