# Synthesis Flow

A synthesis flow turns an accelerator description into implementable hardware through analysis, scheduling, logic generation, placement, routing, and verification. Reports estimate timing, area, memory use, and achievable frequency before deployment.

Iteration is essential because an algorithmically correct design may miss performance or resource goals once interfaces and physical constraints are included.
