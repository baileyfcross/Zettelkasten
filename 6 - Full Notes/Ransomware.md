# Ransomware

Ransomware denies access to data or systems and demands payment for restoration. It commonly encrypts files, but the coercive objective can also involve locking systems or threatening to disclose stolen information.

The attack can harm [[Cybersecurity Availability]] and [[Cybersecurity Confidentiality]] at the same time. Reliable isolated backups, restricted privileges, patching, filtering, network segmentation, and practiced recovery reduce both the opportunity for infection and the attacker's leverage.
