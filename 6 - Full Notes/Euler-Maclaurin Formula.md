# Euler-Maclaurin Formula

The Euler-Maclaurin formula connects a finite sum with an integral plus endpoint corrections involving derivatives and Bernoulli numbers. In quadrature form, it refines the trapezoidal approximation by adding terms from odd-order derivatives at the interval endpoints.

Truncating the correction series yields successively higher-order formulas when endpoint derivatives are available. The same identity can estimate sums from integrals or estimate integrals from regularly spaced sums.
