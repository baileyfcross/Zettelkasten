# Pretending

Pretending is the mental act of accepting a notional reality while knowing that it differs from ordinary reality. It lets an object, action, or rule acquire a special meaning during play.

Together with [[Play]], goals, and rules, pretending is one of the source's essential elements of a [[Game]]. Entering the [[Magic Circle]] is the social commitment to maintain that shared pretense.
