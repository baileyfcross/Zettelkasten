# Electromagnetic Constitutive Relation

An electromagnetic constitutive relation connects field quantities through material properties. Permittivity relates electric displacement to electric field, permeability relates magnetic flux to magnetic field, and conductivity relates current density to electric field.

The relations may be linear or nonlinear, isotropic or anisotropic, and homogeneous or position dependent. Maxwell's equations cannot determine a material-specific field without these properties.
