# Free-Body Diagram

A free-body diagram isolates one object and shows every external force acting on it as a vector. Internal forces and forces the object exerts on other bodies are omitted. Resolving the displayed forces into components makes the [[Resultant Force]] explicit and provides the starting point for equilibrium or acceleration equations.
