# Reaction Photoshop

A reaction Photoshop places a recognizable person or object from a source image into new visual contexts. The cutout functions as a reusable reaction whose meaning changes through juxtaposition with each inserted setting.

The Pepper-Spraying Cop meme exemplifies the genre by moving the officer's spraying gesture into historical paintings, political scenes, and absurd situations. Each version comments on the source while also forming an argument about authority, disproportion, or indifference through the chosen background.
