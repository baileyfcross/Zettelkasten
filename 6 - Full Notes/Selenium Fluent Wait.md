# Selenium Fluent Wait

A Selenium fluent wait is a configurable explicit wait with a maximum timeout, a chosen polling interval, exceptions to ignore during polling, and an optional failure message. It is useful when a condition has irregular timing or transient lookup failures.

The flexibility should serve an observed need. For a straightforward visibility or clickability check, a normal [[Selenium Explicit Wait]] with a predefined expected condition is simpler and easier to maintain.
