# Polyhedral Angle

A polyhedral angle is formed by several plane angles that share a vertex and meet consecutively along common edges. Its faces bound a solid angular region around that vertex.

For a convex polyhedral angle, the sum of the face angles is less than $2\pi$. This restriction limits how regular polygonal faces can meet at a vertex and is central to classifying [[Platonic Solid|Platonic solids]].
