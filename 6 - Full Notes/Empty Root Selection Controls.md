# Empty Root Selection Controls

An Empty can become the root parent of a complex object hierarchy and serve as its single viewport control. Making the child hierarchy nonselectable leaves the visible Empty as the safe handle for moving or keyframing the assembled object.

The control prevents accidental animation of an internal child and keeps high-level motion on a clearly named root. Its display shape and size can be chosen to surround the asset and remain easy to recognize without rendering.
