# Wave Superposition

The principle of superposition states that when compatible waves overlap, the resultant disturbance is the vector sum of their individual disturbances. In-phase contributions reinforce through constructive interference, while opposite-phase contributions cancel through destructive interference. After overlapping in a linear medium, the component waves continue with their original forms.
