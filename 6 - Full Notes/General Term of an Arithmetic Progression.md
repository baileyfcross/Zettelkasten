# General Term of an Arithmetic Progression

The general term of an arithmetic progression gives its value at position (n) without listing earlier terms. If the first term is (a_1) and the [[Common Difference]] is (d), then

$$a_n=a_1+d(n-1).$$

The factor (n-1) appears because no difference has yet been added at the first position. For (2,7,12,17,\ldots), the formula is (2+5(n-1)). Setting this expression equal to a proposed value tests whether that value occurs at a natural-number position.
