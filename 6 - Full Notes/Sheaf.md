# Sheaf

A sheaf is a [[Presheaf]] whose sections are determined locally and whose compatible local sections glue uniquely to a section on their union. It turns local algebraic data into coherent global data.

The [[Structure Sheaf]] on a spectrum assigns localized rings to basic open sets. A [[Quasi-Coherent Sheaf]] similarly arises from localizing a module.
