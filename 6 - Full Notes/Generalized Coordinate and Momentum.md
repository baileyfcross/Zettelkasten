# Generalized Coordinate and Momentum

A generalized coordinate is any independent variable that specifies a system's configuration while respecting its constraints. It need not be a Cartesian distance; angles or other convenient parameters may describe the same physical state more efficiently.

The generalized momentum conjugate to a coordinate is the derivative of the Lagrangian with respect to that coordinate's velocity. If the Lagrangian does not depend explicitly on a coordinate, its conjugate momentum is conserved.
