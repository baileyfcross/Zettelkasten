# Computational Thinking as Information Interpretation

Computational thinking also explains phenomena by interpreting them as information processes. A scientist may ask what transformations, states, or exchanges could reproduce an observed process, then investigate that interpretation through a [[Computational Model]].

This explanatory face does not require claiming that nature literally is a computer. It treats computation as a productive lens for forming models, running [[Simulation|simulations]], and discovering patterns that theory or experiment alone may not reveal.
