# Virus Component Model

The virus component model separates a [[Computer Virus]] into an infection mechanism, a trigger, and a payload. The infection mechanism finds and modifies hosts, the trigger determines when another behavior begins, and the payload performs the intended effect.

This decomposition prevents replication from being mistaken for the virus's objective. Two viruses can use similar infection methods while carrying very different triggers and payloads, or share an effect while spreading differently.
