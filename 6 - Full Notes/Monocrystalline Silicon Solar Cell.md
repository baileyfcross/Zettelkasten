# Monocrystalline Silicon Solar Cell

A monocrystalline silicon cell is cut from a grown single crystal, then doped to make a [[P-N Junction]] and given conductive contacts. The single-crystal wafer process is material- and manufacturing-intensive, but the source presents these cells as suitable for medium and large photovoltaic plants. Enclosed cells are connected in a [[Solar Module]] rather than used as isolated wafers.
