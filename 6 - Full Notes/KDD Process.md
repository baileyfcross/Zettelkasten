# KDD Process

The KDD process begins by understanding the application and end-user goals, then selects a target data set, cleans and preprocesses it, reduces or projects its representation, chooses a method and algorithm, performs mining, interprets the patterns, and incorporates the knowledge.

Loops among these stages are expected. A poor pattern may reveal the need for different data, attributes, parameters, or even a revised question.
