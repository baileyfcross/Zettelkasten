# Pearson Chi-Squared Test

The Pearson chi-squared test compares observed cell counts in a [[Contingency Table]] with the counts expected if its categorical variables were independent. Its statistic increases as observed counts depart from those expectations.

The p-value is evaluated with degrees of freedom determined by the table dimensions. Sparse expected cells can undermine the approximation, in which case a [[Fisher Exact Test]] may be more appropriate.
