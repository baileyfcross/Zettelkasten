# Consumption-Based Pricing

Consumption-based pricing charges for the computing resources actually used over a period. Common measures include storage capacity, processing power, memory, and network bandwidth.

The model aligns naturally with infrastructure and platform services because their resource consumption is visible. It is less meaningful for a consumer of information or a business process, who normally cares about the delivered information or transaction rather than the processors used behind it.
