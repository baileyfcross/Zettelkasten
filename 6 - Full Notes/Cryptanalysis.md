# Cryptanalysis

Cryptanalysis studies a cryptographic system by looking for weaknesses that reveal protected information or violate an intended security property. It may work from ciphertext alone or exploit additional knowledge such as plaintext-ciphertext pairs, a small key space, statistical structure, or implementation behavior.

Its constructive role is to show where a design fails and what must be improved. The [[Caesar Cipher Exhaustive Search]] exploits a tiny key space, the [[Kasiski Examination]] looks for repetition caused by a recurring Vigenere key, and the [[Known-Plaintext Attack on Hill Cipher]] uses the cipher's linear structure.
