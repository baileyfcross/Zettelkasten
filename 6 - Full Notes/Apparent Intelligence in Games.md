# Apparent Intelligence in Games

Game artificial intelligence is judged by whether behavior appears purposeful, responsive, and suitable for play rather than by whether it reproduces general human intelligence. A simpler system can be more effective when it is predictable enough to learn yet varied enough to remain believable.

Pathfinding, behavior states, and planning therefore serve the designed experience. Their computation must fit within the time available for a frame and may deliberately expose patterns the player can understand.
