# Interactive Music Transition

An interactive music transition bridges a source cue and a destination cue by closing one musical thought and introducing the next. It can be a crossfade or a separately composed passage whose rhythm, harmony, tempo, and instrumentation make a state change sound intentional.

A synchronized transition waits for a musically valid beat, bar, or phrase boundary, improving continuity at the cost of response time. A nonsynchronized transition can begin immediately but may conflict with the current musical context. A [[Game Music Transition Matrix]] helps expose which routes need dedicated material and how much transition content the system must support.
