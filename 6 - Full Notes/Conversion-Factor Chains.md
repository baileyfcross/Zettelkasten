# Conversion-Factor Chains

When no convenient direct factor is used, several compatible ratios can be multiplied in sequence. The book converts nautical miles to fathoms through kilometers and meters: $\mathrm{nmi}\times\mathrm{km/nmi}\times\mathrm{m/km}\times\mathrm{ftm/m}$ leaves only fathoms. The intermediate units cancel regardless of the values carried by their factors. Writing the units alongside each ratio exposes a reversed factor or missing step before a calculator result is accepted. See [[Unit Conversion Factor]] and [[Dimensional Homogeneity in Equations]].
