# Clinical Prediction Model

A clinical prediction model estimates an outcome, risk, or prognosis from patient variables. Statistical models, decision trees, neural networks, Bayesian models, and other learning methods provide different ways to connect predictors with clinical endpoints.

Model complexity should follow the decision problem and data rather than novelty alone. The prediction target, observation window, class imbalance, censoring, and cost of errors all affect design. Performance measured on training data is insufficient; calibration, discrimination, and validation on appropriately separated cases determine whether the estimate can transfer to practice.
