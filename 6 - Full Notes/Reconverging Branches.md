# Reconverging Branches

Reconverging branches are narrative paths that separate after a choice and later return to a shared plot line. They preserve some variation in the journey while limiting the amount of permanently divergent material a [[Branching Story]] requires.

Reconvergence can support different details, perspectives, or routes to one outcome. If branches return without acknowledging meaningful differences, however, the earlier choice may feel unsupported or merely cosmetic.
