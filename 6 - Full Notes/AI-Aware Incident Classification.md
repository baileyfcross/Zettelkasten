# AI-Aware Incident Classification

AI-aware incident classification supplements ordinary business-impact severity with the significance of model or agent behavior. A prompt injection that reaches a prohibited intent but is blocked before a write may cause little immediate damage, yet it proves an attack path exists and deserves investigation and regression coverage.

A useful scale distinguishes unauthorized destructive execution, successful injection stopped by a guardrail, out-of-scope model behavior without an attack, and quality degradation detected by evaluation. Each class maps to containment, evidence preservation, escalation, repair deadlines, and restart conditions rather than relying on an ad hoc judgment during the event.
