# K-Means Convergence

K-means convergence occurs when an allocation pass causes no material membership or centroid changes, or when improvement falls below a tolerance. At that point the objective cannot be improved by the algorithm's current local update rule.

Convergence establishes stability of one run, not global optimality. A different initialization can converge to a different partition with a better or worse objective value.
