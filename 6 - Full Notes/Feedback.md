# Feedback

Feedback is information that helps a user understand system state, the result of an operation, or progress on a task. It can come from the system, the environment, or the user's own body.

Sensory feedback may be visual, auditory, haptic, chemical, proprioceptive, or kinesthetic. Reactive feedback comes from operating the interface, instrumental feedback comes from controls and tools, and operational feedback communicates the system's result.

Useful feedback closes the [[User-System Loop]] and supports an accurate [[Conceptual Model]]. Multiple channels can reinforce one another, but they should maintain [[Feedback Compliance]] in space and time.
