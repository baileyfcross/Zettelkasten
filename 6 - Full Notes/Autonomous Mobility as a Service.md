# Autonomous Mobility as a Service

Autonomous mobility as a service treats transportation as access to a moving vehicle rather than ownership of a mostly idle asset. A shared fleet can route the nearest suitable vehicle, combine riders, reposition during low demand, and choose charging or refueling times algorithmically.

Higher utilization could reduce the number of privately owned cars and the land devoted to parking, while changing insurance, financing, maintenance, and transit intermediaries. The benefit depends on pricing and routing incentives: empty repositioning or exclusive rides can add traffic even when individual ownership falls.
