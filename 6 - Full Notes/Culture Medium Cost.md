# Culture Medium Cost

Culture medium cost can dominate the price of cell-based products because large cell populations repeatedly consume nutrient and signaling mixtures. The source cites estimates in which medium accounts for more than half and potentially nearly all tissue-production cost.

Food applications can reduce purity requirements and explore recycling, but reused medium must not accumulate harmful waste or contamination.
