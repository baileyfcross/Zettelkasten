# Simple Pendulum

A simple pendulum is a point mass suspended by a light inextensible string. For small angular displacements, $\sin\theta\approx\theta$, making the restoring force proportional to displacement and the motion approximately harmonic. Its period is $T=2\pi\sqrt{l/g}$, independent of bob mass and approximately independent of amplitude only in the small-angle regime.
