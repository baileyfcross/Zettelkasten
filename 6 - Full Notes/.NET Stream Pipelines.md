# .NET Stream Pipelines

The `System.IO.Pipelines` model organizes high-performance I/O around a producer that writes bytes and a consumer that reads them. Buffers are managed cooperatively so applications perform less manual allocation, copying, and bookkeeping than traditional stream loops often require.

Pipelines are particularly useful for protocols and servers that parse partial data arriving over time. They do not eliminate the need to handle boundaries and completion, but they provide a disciplined buffer lifecycle that reduces error-prone I/O boilerplate.
