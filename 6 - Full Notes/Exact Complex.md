# Exact Complex

A complex is exact at degree n when the image of the incoming differential equals the kernel of the outgoing differential. It is an exact complex when this holds at every degree.

Equivalently, every [[Homology Group]] of a chain complex vanishes. Exactness says that every cycle is already a boundary, so the complex contains no residual homological obstruction.
