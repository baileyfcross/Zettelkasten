# Human Cell Donation Consent

Human cell donation consent specifies what a donor understands and permits regarding collection, culture, genetic analysis, sharing, commercialization, and future uses of their cells. An open-ended cell line may support research far beyond the original sample.

Meaningful consent must address foreseeable categories of use and governance for later uncertainty rather than treating a one-time signature as unlimited transfer.
