# Single-Link Clustering

Single-link clustering defines the distance between two clusters by the nearest pair of observations across them. It is also called nearest-neighbor linkage.

This rule can recover elongated or irregular chains because one close bridge is enough to join groups. The same property can cause chaining, where otherwise distinct dense groups connect through intermediate points.
