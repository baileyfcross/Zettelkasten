# Workflow Evolution Provenance

Workflow evolution provenance preserves the sequence of changes by which one workflow version became another. Instead of keeping only snapshots, a change-based history can record additions, deletions, parameter edits, and structural rewiring.

This history lets researchers return to earlier states, compare branches of exploration, and understand why a published version differs from a preliminary one. It complements [[Prospective Provenance]] and [[Retrospective Provenance]] by explaining the development path between designs and runs. A preserved history can also support analogous edits and extraction of reusable workflow patterns.
