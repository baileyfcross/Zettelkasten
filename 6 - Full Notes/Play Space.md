# Play Space

Play space is the set of states and traversable paths made possible by a game's tokens, rules, and systems. It is the possibility space within which players choose actions and pursue goals.

A broad play space contains several viable routes whose consequences differ. A dominant strategy or heavily scripted path collapses that space, reducing [[Meaningful Decision|meaningful decisions]], engagement, and replay value.
