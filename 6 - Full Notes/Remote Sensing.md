# Remote Sensing

Remote sensing collects information about a target without the measuring instrument touching it. A working system joins three elements: an object or area to observe, a sensor that detects a signal, and a platform such as a table, aircraft, drone, or satellite that carries the sensor.

The measurement alone is not the final product. Signals must be stored, processed, interpreted, and related to a purpose. Satellite imagery, a weather radar, and a baby monitor all fit the same pattern even though their scales differ. Their usefulness depends on the sensor's capabilities, the platform's coverage, and the analyst's ability to distinguish observation from inference.
