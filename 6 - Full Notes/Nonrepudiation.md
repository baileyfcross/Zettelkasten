# Nonrepudiation

Nonrepudiation supplies evidence that the source of information or an online action can be validated later. It reduces the ability of a sender to deny sending a message or of a participant to deny completing a recorded transaction.

The source describes it as a combination of authentication and integrity. [[Digital Signature|Digital signatures]] and [[Message Authentication Code|message authentication codes]] can bind message evidence to protected keys, while delivery and transaction records preserve the relevant context.
