# Texture Paint Brush Falloff

Brush falloff defines how influence changes from the center of a paint dab to its edge. A hard profile preserves a sharp boundary, while a gradual curve blends the stroke into surrounding pixels.

Custom falloff curves support asymmetric or stylized profiles, and pressure can modify the effective strength during a stroke. Falloff works with radius, texture, and mask, so its visible result must be evaluated as part of the full brush stack.
