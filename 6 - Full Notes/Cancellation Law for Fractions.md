# Cancellation Law for Fractions

The cancellation law for fractions states that for nonzero natural numbers (n) and (k),

$$\frac{mk}{nk}=\frac{m}{n}.$$

The factor (k) scales both the number of selected parts and the number of parts in a unit, so the represented proportion remains unchanged. The law permits a common factor to be removed from the numerator and denominator when finding a [[Fraction in Lowest Terms]]. It cancels factors, not separate terms joined by addition or subtraction.
