# Human-Robot Partnership Continuum

The human-robot partnership continuum replaces a binary choice between human and robot with combinations of biological and mechanical sensing, cognition, and action. At different points, a person may direct a tool, receive robotic assistance, share control, or rely on a mostly autonomous system.

The continuum makes questions of capability and responsibility more useful than arguments over labels. Which partner sets goals, interprets ambiguity, supplies force, observes consequences, and can override the other? Prostheses, navigation aids, surgical systems, teleoperation, and shared-control vehicles all occupy different positions rather than fitting one uniform category.
