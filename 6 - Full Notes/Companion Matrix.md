# Companion Matrix

The companion matrix of a monic polynomial places its negated coefficients in one boundary row or column and uses a shift of ones in the remaining entries. Its characteristic polynomial is the polynomial from which it was constructed.

Polynomial roots therefore become eigenvalues of the matrix. For distinct roots, corresponding eigenvectors have a Vandermonde pattern, connecting the companion construction to the [[Characteristic Polynomial]] and polynomial root computation.
