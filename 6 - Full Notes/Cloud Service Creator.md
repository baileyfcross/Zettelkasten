# Cloud Service Creator

A cloud service creator designs and builds a service that can be supplied through a [[Cloud Service Provider]]. Its work includes marshaling and optimizing the resources, components, interfaces, and management practices that underpin the offering.

The creator and provider may be the same entity, but separating the roles clarifies the supply chain. The creator produces a reusable capability; the provider contracts, orchestrates, and delivers that capability to a consumer.
