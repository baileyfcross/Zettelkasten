# MHD Topping Cycle

An MHD topping cycle extracts electrical power while combustion gas is still very hot and conducting, then sends its remaining heat to a steam or gas-turbine bottoming plant. Conductivity drops as gas cools, so extending the MHD channel alone cannot use all of its thermal energy. Combining stages uses a wider temperature range and raises total fuel utilization over either stage alone.
