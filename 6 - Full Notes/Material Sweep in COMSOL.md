# Material Sweep in COMSOL

A material sweep solves the same geometry and physics using multiple candidate materials assigned to a selected domain. It supports comparison of conductivity, heat capacity, density, and other property sets without reconstructing separate models.

The results express both the material differences and any temperature dependence contained in their definitions. Fair comparison requires identical conditions and a common output such as maximum temperature, heat loss, or heating time from [[COMSOL Derived Values|derived values]].
