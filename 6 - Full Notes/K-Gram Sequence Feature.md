# K-Gram Sequence Feature

A k-gram sequence feature represents an ordered contiguous pattern of exactly k elements. Its value may indicate presence, count occurrences, or weight the pattern within a sequence.

Increasing k captures more context but expands the candidate space and makes individual patterns rarer. Support constraints and feature selection help control this combinatorial growth.
