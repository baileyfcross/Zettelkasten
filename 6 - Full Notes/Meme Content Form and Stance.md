# Meme Content Form and Stance

Internet memes can be compared across three dimensions. Content is the idea or subject conveyed, form is the physical or perceptible embodiment of the message, and stance is the creator's position toward the text, its audience, and the situation being represented.

Separating the dimensions explains how versions can belong together while changing substantially. A remake may preserve the audiovisual form but replace its subject, repeat a topic in a new medium, or imitate an attitude while altering both content and form. The framework makes meme families comparable without assuming exact copying.
