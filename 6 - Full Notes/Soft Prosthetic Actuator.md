# Soft Prosthetic Actuator

A soft prosthetic actuator uses deformable, often fluidically powered material to create finger motion and compliant contact. The structure can conform to an object's shape and integrate rigid supports or embedded sensors where precise load transfer is needed.

Compliance reduces the need to command every contact geometry exactly and can distribute pressure more safely than a rigid linkage. The actuator must still satisfy the [[Prosthetic Hand Weight Constraint]], generate useful force, survive repeated bending, and provide sensor signals that support [[Prosthetic Sensory Feedback]] rather than merely produce motion.
