# Frustration in Games

Frustration in games occurs when challenge exceeds the player's present skill or understanding, often producing repeated failed attempts with little visible progress. The player pushes into the system and repeatedly receives the equivalent of an uninformative refusal.

Frustration can be temporary and meaningful when attempts are short, feedback is clear, and practice reveals improvement. It becomes wasteful when failure consumes time without helping the player revise a strategy or build mastery.
