# Indian Regional Navigation Satellite System

The Indian Regional Navigation Satellite System is an independently controlled service focused on India and nearby ocean regions rather than uniform global coverage. Its planned architecture combines geostationary satellites with inclined geosynchronous satellites, keeping useful transmitters visible over the service area. A regional constellation can therefore provide strategic autonomy with fewer satellites than a worldwide medium-orbit system.
