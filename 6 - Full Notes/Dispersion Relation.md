# Dispersion Relation

A dispersion relation specifies how angular frequency $\omega$ depends on wavenumber $k$ for waves in a medium. It determines both the phase velocity $\omega/k$ and the group velocity $d\omega/dk$.

When $\omega$ is directly proportional to $k$, all frequency components travel with the same speed and a composite profile can retain its shape. A nonlinear relation gives different component speeds, causing a wave packet to spread or change as it propagates.
