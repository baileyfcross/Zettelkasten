# Electromagnetic Continuity Equation

The electromagnetic continuity equation expresses conservation of electric charge. The divergence of current density equals the negative time rate of change of volume charge density.

In steady or quasi-static conditions where charge does not accumulate, current density is divergence-free. The equation is implicit in Maxwell's equations and constrains physically valid source distributions.
