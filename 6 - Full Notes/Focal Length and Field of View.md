# Focal Length and Field of View

Focal length is the distance between the projection center and sensor in the pinhole model, while field of view is the angular extent captured by the camera. For a fixed sensor, increasing focal length magnifies projected objects and narrows the field; decreasing it widens the scene and reduces projected size.

These linked quantities constrain whether apparent object sizes and perspective are compatible with a claimed camera setup. Cropping can imitate a narrower view without reproducing every optical consequence, so image dimensions and metadata must be considered together.
