# Story as System

Story as system conveys narrative meaning through the structure of a game's rules and the experience of acting within them. Resistance, available verbs, consequences, and recurring interactions can express what a situation feels like without relying only on words or images.

The designed system makes some actions important and others impossible, implicitly describing how its world works. Players discover that message by pushing into the rules and interpreting the responses they receive.
