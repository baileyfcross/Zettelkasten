# Free Fall

An object is in free fall when gravity is the only significant force acting on it. Near Earth's surface and over modest height changes, its acceleration is approximately constant and downward, independent of mass when air resistance is neglected. Apparent weightlessness in orbit is continuous free fall rather than absence of gravity.
