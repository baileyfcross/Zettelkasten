# Open Review

Open review makes scientific criticism and discussion visible to a wider community rather than confining it to a private exchange among selected reviewers and authors. Readers can inspect the evidence and contribute feedback when they have relevant expertise.

This model blurs the boundary between reader and reviewer and treats authority as grounded in the quality of a contribution. Openness does not remove the need for editorial judgment or quality control; it exposes that process to inspection. Access to code, data, and methods makes the review capable of examining reproducibility rather than only the paper's narrative.
