# Emergent Story

An emergent story is a story about a particular experience of playing a game. It arises as the player learns rules, makes decisions, pursues goals, encounters resistance, and interprets what happened.

The same system can produce different stories for different players or sessions. The range and novelty of those stories depend on the relationships among rules and on how much room the [[Possibility Space]] gives players to develop their own strategies and purposes.
