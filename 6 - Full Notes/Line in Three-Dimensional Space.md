# Line in Three-Dimensional Space

A line through a point with [[Position Vector]] $a$ and nonzero direction vector $u$ has the vector equation

$$r(t)=a+tu.$$

Each coordinate is therefore an affine function of the same parameter. Two spatial lines may intersect, be parallel, or form [[Skew Lines]] when they are nonparallel but lie in different planes and never meet.
