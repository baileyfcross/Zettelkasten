# Neighborhood-Regularized Logistic Matrix Factorization

Neighborhood-regularized logistic matrix factorization, or NRLMF, predicts drug-target interactions by combining logistic latent-factor probabilities with local drug and target similarities. It learns a [[Shared Drug-Target Latent Space]] while encouraging close biological neighbors to receive nearby representations.

The model also uses [[Confidence-Weighted Interaction Observation]] and a smoothing step for drugs or targets with no known training interaction. These components address different weaknesses: global factorization captures broad structure, regularization preserves local similarity, and smoothing handles cold-start representations.
