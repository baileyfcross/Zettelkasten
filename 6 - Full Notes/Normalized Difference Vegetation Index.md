# Normalized Difference Vegetation Index

The Normalized Difference Vegetation Index, or NDVI, combines visible and near-infrared reflectance into a normalized value between minus one and one. Healthy green leaves absorb visible light for photosynthesis while their cell structure reflects near-infrared energy, so stronger vegetation tends to produce a higher index value.

Normalization reduces variation caused by overall illumination and makes observations from different times easier to compare. NDVI is a compact indicator rather than a direct inspection of every plant: farmers and researchers interpret its changes over time to monitor crop vigor, dense foliage, drought, and possible stress.
