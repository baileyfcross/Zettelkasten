# System Behavior

System behavior is an action by which a part changes its own state, affects another part, or communicates a resource or message. Behaviors are the active connections that turn a collection of parts into loops.

One part can perturb another, but the receiving part determines its own response. Keeping that response local supports encapsulation, [[Modular Behavior]], and the possibility of [[Emergence]].
