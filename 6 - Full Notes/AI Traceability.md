# AI Traceability

AI traceability is the ability to follow how an automated system was built and how a particular outcome developed. It includes records of data collection and selection, model training, versions, human approvals, inputs, outputs, and later interventions.

Traceability supports investigation across the [[Data Science Pipeline]] and helps address the [[Problem of Many Hands]] and [[Problem of Many Things]]. A trace does not by itself decide who is responsible, but it preserves the evidence needed for explanation and audit. The required detail should match the consequences of the application and respect privacy and security.
