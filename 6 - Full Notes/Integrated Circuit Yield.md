# Integrated Circuit Yield

Integrated-circuit yield is the proportion of fabricated chips on a wafer that pass testing. Dust, material defects, or process variation can disable individual circuits, making yield a major determinant of cost.

Manufacturers improve yield through clean environments, process control, testing, and design choices. A brilliant circuit is commercially weak if too few working copies emerge from production.
