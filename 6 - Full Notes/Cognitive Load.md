# Cognitive Load

Cognitive load is the amount of mental work required by a task or situation. It is shaped by external task demands, such as difficulty and priority, and by internal limits in [[Attention]] and [[Information Processing]]. Skills and well-formed mental structures can reduce the effort needed.

An interface raises cognitive load when users must remember transient information, divide attention, interpret unclear mappings, or control a secondary task such as [[3D Travel]] while performing a primary task.

Evaluation can combine subjective workload ratings with task-performance or physiological measures. Self-report is useful but is stronger when correlated with objective evidence.
