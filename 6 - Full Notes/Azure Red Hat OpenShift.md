# Azure Red Hat OpenShift

Azure Red Hat OpenShift is a jointly operated managed OpenShift platform on Azure. It adds OpenShift’s opinionated enterprise tooling, security model, developer workflow, and ecosystem on top of Kubernetes while Microsoft and Red Hat share platform support. The additional conventions can standardize large estates, but they also reduce flexibility and require OpenShift skills; the choice should follow organizational platform strategy rather than treating it as interchangeable with AKS.
