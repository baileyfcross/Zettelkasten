# Vehicle Platooning

Vehicle platooning links a line of vehicles so they maintain coordinated speed and short following distances behind a lead vehicle. Sensors and wireless communication let following vehicles react more quickly and consistently than independent human drivers.

Close spacing can reduce aerodynamic drag, improve fuel economy, and increase roadway capacity. The design still needs safe joining and leaving, reliable communication, a clear transfer of control, and a response to a failing leader or disconnected follower. Platooning is a partial automation model between individual driver assistance and a fully independent shared fleet.
