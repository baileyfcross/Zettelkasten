# Jacobian Matrix

The Jacobian matrix of $f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m$ collects the first partial derivatives, with entry

$$J_{ij}(x)=\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}(x).$$

It is the standard-basis matrix representation of the linear derivative $Df(x)$, not a separate derivative. Under composition, the [[Multivariable Chain Rule]] becomes matrix multiplication of the corresponding Jacobians.
