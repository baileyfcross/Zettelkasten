# Viral-Memetic Spectrum

The viral-memetic spectrum classifies popular digital content by the balance between one widely copied item and a family of user-created versions. Purely viral circulation concentrates attention on a source, while strongly memetic circulation distributes attention across derivatives.

Between the endpoints are founder-based memes, whose original remains a focal point, and egalitarian memes, whose variants matter more collectively than any single source. The spectrum avoids a false binary and directs attention to how audiences participate, not only how many views an artifact receives.
