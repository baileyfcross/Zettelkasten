# Solar Tower Power Plant

A solar tower plant uses a field of tracking [[Heliostat]]s to direct [[Beam Solar Radiation]] to one high-mounted [[Solar Central Receiver]]. The receiver heats steam, air, liquid metal, or another primary fluid; its heat then feeds a power cycle. Field reflectivity, interception, receiver loss, and heat transport determine useful power. It is a point-focus alternative to line-focus parabolic troughs.
