# Operational Level Agreement

An operational level agreement states the service commitment between an internal supplier and an internal consumer in the same organization. For example, an IT department can agree with a business department on the availability and delivery of an internal computing service.

It resembles a [[Service Level Agreement]] but applies within the organizational boundary. When an internal service depends on an external provider, its operational objectives should be compatible with the external commitment it receives.
