# Graph-Structured Feature Selection

Graph-structured feature selection uses an arbitrary network of relationships among variables rather than assuming that features are independent or arranged in a tree. Edges may encode correlation, interaction, physical connection, or domain knowledge.

A graph-aware objective can favor connected or mutually consistent subsets while preserving predictive discrimination. Because graph structure is more flexible than a hierarchy, it also introduces more difficult optimization and greater sensitivity to errors in the supplied relationships.
