# Journal Table 1

A journal Table 1 summarizes the analytic sample and the distribution of exposures and candidate confounders. For a categorical outcome it commonly reports frequencies and proportions overall and by outcome group; for a continuous outcome it commonly adds group means and standard deviations.

Bivariate test results may help readers understand group differences. The table should be generated from analysis objects or structured data whenever possible, because manually copying values from console output into a spreadsheet creates avoidable transcription risk.
