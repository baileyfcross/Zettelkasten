# Algorithmic Due Process

Algorithmic due process applies procedural fairness to decisions made or supported by automated systems. A person affected by a consequential decision should know that automation was used, understand the relevant basis, receive a comprehensible reason, and have a real opportunity to challenge errors.

The requirement connects [[Right to Explanation]] with institutional remedy. Disclosure without appeal leaves the person's position unchanged, while an appeal without adequate information cannot test the system's reasoning. Due process also requires a responsible human or organization with authority to review and correct the outcome rather than treating the model as final.
