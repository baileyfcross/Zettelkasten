# Redirected Walking

Redirected walking maps a physically curved path to an apparently straight or differently shaped path in a virtual environment. The display slowly rotates or otherwise adjusts the scene below the threshold at which the user is likely to notice, causing ordinary balance corrections to steer the body within a limited real area.

The technique lets [[3D Travel]] cover a virtual space larger than the tracked room while retaining actual walking as the locomotion input. It depends on continuous [[3D Tracking]] and enough free space for the induced path. If the manipulation becomes noticeable or directs the user too close to a wall, the illusion and safety benefit both fail.

Redirected walking supplements rather than replaces [[XR Physical Boundary Safety]]. Boundaries, obstacles, bystanders, and tracking loss still require explicit handling.
