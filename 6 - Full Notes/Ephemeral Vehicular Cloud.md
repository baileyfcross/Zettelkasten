# Ephemeral Vehicular Cloud

An ephemeral vehicular cloud forms from vehicles that remain together only briefly, such as traffic at an intersection or a moving group. Short and uncertain membership limits setup time and the amount of state an application can safely place on any participant.

Long-running big-data work is a poor fit when vehicles leave faster than virtual machines can migrate or results can be replicated.
