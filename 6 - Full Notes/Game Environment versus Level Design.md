# Game Environment versus Level Design

Game-environment design establishes the world's visual character through architecture, objects, lighting, color, and mood. It makes the setting readable and emotionally coherent rather than merely filling empty space with decoration.

Level design emphasizes the challenges, routes, rules, and pacing the player encounters in that setting. The disciplines overlap because spatial appearance can guide action, but an attractive environment does not automatically create a playable level.
