# Scrumban

Scrumban combines Scrum's goal-oriented Sprint cadence with Kanban's visualization and work-in-progress controls. A team can reserve a board area for Sprint work while using flow policies for production, support, or other continuously arriving items.

The combination is useful when exploratory feature work and predictable content flow coexist. Its practices should solve observed coordination problems rather than accumulate as process decoration.
