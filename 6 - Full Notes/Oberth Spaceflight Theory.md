# Oberth Spaceflight Theory

Oberth spaceflight theory was Hermann Oberth's detailed argument that liquid rockets could make interplanetary travel technically feasible. His 1923 book presented more advanced spaceflight concepts than were then widely available outside Russia and helped give Central European advocates a common technical foundation.

Popularizers carried the ideas beyond specialists, contributing to societies, experiments, and a transnational exchange of publications. Oberth's work therefore linked engineering analysis to the [[Spaceflight Movement]], even though universities initially rejected the dissertation from which the book developed.
