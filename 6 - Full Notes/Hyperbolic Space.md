# Hyperbolic Space

Hyperbolic space is a non-Euclidean metric space with negative curvature. In two dimensions, the circumference and area available at a given radius grow exponentially rather than at the polynomial rate of Euclidean space.

That expansion resembles the growth of nodes with distance from the root of a tree. It makes hyperbolic space a useful latent geometry for representing hierarchical and scale-free [[Graph|networks]].
