# UML Activation

A UML activation is the narrow rectangle drawn on a lifeline while that participant performs or controls an operation. Its top marks the start of the active execution interval and its bottom marks the end.

Nested activations can show one operation calling another, including a recursive call on the same lifeline. They make the distribution of control more explicit than message arrows alone.
