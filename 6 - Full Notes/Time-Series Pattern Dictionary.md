# Time-Series Pattern Dictionary

A time-series pattern dictionary represents a series by counts or weights of recurring local patterns. Windows are often normalized, discretized, and converted into symbolic words before their occurrences are accumulated.

The dictionary discards exact timing while preserving which shapes recur, producing a bag-of-patterns representation. Window length, discretization, and vocabulary construction control the balance between invariance and detail.
