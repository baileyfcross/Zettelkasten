# Multi-Agent Process Control

Multi-agent process control determines how tasks are allocated, ordered, retried, escalated, and combined. Sequential, hierarchical, consensus, and coordinator-based patterns provide different tradeoffs between predictability, independence, and synthesis cost.

Process control belongs in explicit orchestration rather than being left entirely to free-form model decisions. Timeouts, stop conditions, partial-result handling, and validation prevent one failed or looping agent from blocking the whole system.
