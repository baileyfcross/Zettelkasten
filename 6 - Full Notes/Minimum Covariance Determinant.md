# Minimum Covariance Determinant

The minimum covariance determinant is a robust estimator based on the subset of observations whose covariance matrix has the smallest determinant. Mean and covariance estimates from that compact subset are less influenced by extreme points.

Mahalanobis distances computed from the robust estimates can reveal observations hidden by inflated full-sample spread. Iterative subset improvement reduces the determinant.
