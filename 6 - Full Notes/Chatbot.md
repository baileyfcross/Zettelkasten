# Chatbot

A chatbot is a conversational program whose primary interface is typed or spoken dialogue. Some chatbots are open-ended social systems, while others are narrow agents that answer questions or complete transactions.

The category describes the interaction form, not a guaranteed level of intelligence. A chatbot may rely on scripted rules, statistical [[Intent Classification]], learned generation, or a combination of these methods.

The Bot Framework project describes a chatbot as a conversational state machine whose framework manages service and channel plumbing. Message activities enter through the bot adapter, language understanding selects an intent, and the bot chooses and sends an appropriate reply.
