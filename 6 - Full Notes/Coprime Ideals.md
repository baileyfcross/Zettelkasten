# Coprime Ideals

Two ideals I and J are coprime when I + J is the whole ring. Equivalently, there are elements a ∈ I and b ∈ J with a + b = 1.

For coprime ideals, intersection and product agree: I ∩ J = IJ. Pairwise coprimality is also the condition that makes the canonical map to a product of quotient rings surjective, a ring-level form of the Chinese remainder principle.
