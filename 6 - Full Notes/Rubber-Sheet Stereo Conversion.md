# Rubber-Sheet Stereo Conversion

The rubber-sheet method maps an extracted image onto a subdivided flat plane and uses a grayscale depth map to displace that plane in 3D. Levels or grade reshape how far different features protrude. Two virtual cameras then record the slightly different left and right views needed for stereo presentation. Unlike a simple horizontal 2D displacement, the second view comes from viewing an actual deformed surface. The result still depends on accurate extraction and the authored depth map, as explained in [[Roto-Gradient Depth Maps for Stereo]].
