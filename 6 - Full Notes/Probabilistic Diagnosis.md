# Probabilistic Diagnosis

Probabilistic diagnosis infers hidden causes from observed effects. A generative description specifies how candidate causes produce evidence, and a Bayesian question reverses that relationship through conditioning and marginalization.

No separate inverse model is required when the joint description is available. Priors distinguish explanations that fit the same observation but were not equally plausible beforehand.
