# Jacobson Radical

The Jacobson radical of a ring is the intersection of all its [[Maximal Ideal]]s. An element a belongs to it exactly when 1 − ab is a [[Ring Unit]] for every ring element b.

The Jacobson radical controls finite generation through [[Nakayama's Lemma]]. In a [[Local Ring]], it is simply the unique maximal ideal.
