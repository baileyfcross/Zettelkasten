# AI Transparency

AI transparency is the availability of relevant information about an automated system, including where it is used, how data were selected, what goal was optimized, and how human decisions shaped its operation. Transparency makes hidden power and assumptions open to scrutiny.

More disclosure is not automatically more understanding. Publishing source code may be useless to an affected person and can conflict with privacy, security, or legitimate proprietary interests. Transparency should be tied to an audience and purpose. [[Explainable AI]] interprets a decision, while [[Algorithmic Auditability]] gives qualified reviewers the evidence needed to evaluate a system.
