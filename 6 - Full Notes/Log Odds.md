# Log Odds

Log odds are the natural logarithm of the ratio between the probability of an event and the probability of no event. Logistic regression models this unbounded quantity rather than modeling probability directly.

A logistic coefficient is an additive change in log odds, which is difficult to interpret clinically. Exponentiating it yields an [[Odds Ratio]], converting the comparison to a multiplicative scale with a no-association value of one.
