# Blender Status Bar Context

Blender's status bar changes its guidance according to the active tool and operation. Its left side can show available mouse and keyboard actions, while its right side can expose scene statistics and system information selected from the status-bar context menu.

The bar is therefore a live explanation of context rather than a static footer. Watching it during an unfamiliar transform or tool invocation can reveal controls that are not obvious from the viewport alone.
