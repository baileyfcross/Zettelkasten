# Solar Radiation Threat to GPS

Solar activity threatens GPS through both spacecraft exposure and signal propagation. The satellites operate in a radiation environment that can damage electronics, while sunspot cycles and coronal mass ejections disturb the ionosphere through which their signals travel. Monitoring spacecraft and space weather permits protective action and warning, but the intensity and timing of major events remain uncertain. Resilience therefore requires hardened hardware as well as models of atmospheric delay.
