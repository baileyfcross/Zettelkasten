# Blender Edge and Vertex Slide Tools

Edge Slide moves a selected edge along the surrounding surface while preserving its connection to adjacent faces. Vertex Slide similarly moves vertices along connected edges rather than freely through space.

Sliding adjusts edge spacing and local proportions without changing the mesh's component count. Even and clamp options can constrain the motion, which is useful when repositioning support loops or refining the surface flow described by [[Blender Mesh Modeling and Modifiers]].
