# Flight-Test Preparation Checklist

Flight-test preparation starts before the test day with syllabus review, route and weight planning, weather and advisory checks, document organization, fuel coordination, and an independent walkaround. A written checklist protects attention when stress makes memory unreliable. Arranging for the instructor to attend the debrief also turns the result into a concrete training plan.
