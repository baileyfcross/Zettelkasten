# Distance Attenuation

Distance attenuation reduces the level of a spatial sound as the [[3D Sound Emitter]] moves farther from the [[3D Sound Listener]]. The falloff curve defines how quickly the sound changes between its near and far ranges.

The chosen curve is often tuned for gameplay clarity rather than strict physical realism. Important cues may remain audible farther away, while ambient sounds can fade more aggressively.
