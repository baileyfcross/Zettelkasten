# Stationarity Feature

A stationarity feature measures whether a time series maintains stable statistical behavior over time. Tests and summary statistics can indicate changes in level, variance, or other distributional properties.

Stationarity matters because many temporal models assume that their governing relationships do not drift. A feature encoding departures from that assumption can distinguish series and guide the choice of forecasting or transformation methods.
