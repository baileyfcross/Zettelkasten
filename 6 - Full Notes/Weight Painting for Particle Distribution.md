# Weight Painting for Particle Distribution

Particle systems can read painted vertex weights to control where particles are placed or emitted. High-weight areas can contribute strongly to a distribution, while zero-weight areas can be excluded.

The approach replaces a uniform emitter with an artist-directed scalar field. It is useful for effects such as concentrating hair on a scalp or restricting dynamic particles to selected regions of an object.
