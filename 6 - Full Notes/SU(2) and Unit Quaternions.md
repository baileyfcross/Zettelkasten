# SU(2) and Unit Quaternions

The unit quaternions are quaternions of norm one. Under multiplication they form a group isomorphic to $SU(2)$, the group of complex $2\times2$ unitary matrices with determinant one.

A unit quaternion can parameterize a spatial rotation, with its scalar and vector parts containing a half-angle and rotation axis. Because opposite unit quaternions represent the same ordinary three-dimensional rotation, $SU(2)$ double-covers the spatial rotation group.

The source makes this relationship concrete through the [[Quaternion Matrix Representation]] and Pauli-matrix form of a quaternion. It also notes that the complexified generator algebra of the [[Lorentz Group]] can be reorganized into two commuting $SU(2)$-like parts, although their physical interpretation is not simply two independent classical quaternion spaces.
