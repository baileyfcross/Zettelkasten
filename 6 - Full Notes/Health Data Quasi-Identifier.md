# Health Data Quasi-Identifier

A health data quasi-identifier is a field that may not name a patient by itself but can identify one when combined with other information. Age, sex, geography, admission details, and other demographic attributes can form a distinctive record.

Removing direct identifiers therefore does not guarantee anonymity. A link attack can match quasi-identifiers in a released health dataset to an external source and recover a person's sensitive record. Privacy assessment must consider realistic combinations and background knowledge, not inspect each field in isolation.
