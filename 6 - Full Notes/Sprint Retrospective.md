# Sprint Retrospective

A Sprint Retrospective inspects how a team created value and identifies a small improvement to try in the next Sprint. A simple format asks what to stop, start, and continue, then assigns and tracks the chosen action.

The event focuses on the working system, relationships, tools, and practices. It turns [[Continuous Improvement]] into an explicit recurring responsibility.
