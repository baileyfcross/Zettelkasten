# Player Retention

Player retention is the proportion of players who return after their first experience, commonly observed at later day-based checkpoints. It indicates whether the game continues to offer understandable goals and reasons to reengage.

Retention is interpreted through [[Cohort Analysis]] because day-to-day totals alone mix players at different stages. Weak early retention can point to problems in the [[First-Time User Experience]], balance, or the enduring core loop.
