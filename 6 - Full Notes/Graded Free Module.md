# Graded Free Module

A graded free module is a direct sum of degree-shifted copies of its graded base ring. Equivalently, it has a basis of homogeneous, linearly independent generators.

The shifts preserve each generator's degree information. Graded free modules form the terms of a [[Graded Free Resolution]] and allow Hilbert functions to be computed from generator degrees.
