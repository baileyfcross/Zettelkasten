# Mode Shape

A mode shape is the relative displacement pattern associated with a natural frequency. It is the eigenvector of the structural stiffness-mass problem and is defined up to an arbitrary scale.

Plotting mode shapes reveals which portions of a structure move together or remain near nodal locations. Modal vectors provide a basis for interpreting and approximating more general dynamic response.
