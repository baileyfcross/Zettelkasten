# Stefan-Boltzmann Radiation Law

Any body above absolute zero emits thermal radiation. The net radiative heat rate between a surface and its surroundings is proportional to area, emissivity, the Stefan-Boltzmann constant, and the difference of their absolute temperatures raised to the fourth power.

The fourth-power dependence makes radiation increasingly important at high temperature and makes the governing equation nonlinear. Correct use requires Kelvin temperatures and credible [[Surface Emissivity and Absorptivity|surface optical properties]], not merely the temperature difference used in a convection relation.
