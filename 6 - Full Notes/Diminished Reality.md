# Diminished Reality

Diminished reality removes a selected real object from an augmented view and replaces its image region with a plausible view of what lies behind it. The system identifies the region of interest, obtains or reconstructs the hidden background, and composites that background over the object.

The hidden area may be observed from another time or viewpoint or modeled from surrounding evidence. Registration, changing illumination, and moving backgrounds determine whether the removal remains convincing.
