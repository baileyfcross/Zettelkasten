# Orthogonal Complement

The orthogonal complement of a subspace $W$ is $W^\perp=\{v:\langle v,w\rangle=0\text{ for every }w\in W\}$. It is itself a subspace and intersects $W$ only in the zero vector.

In a finite-dimensional inner-product space, $V=W\oplus W^\perp$ and $\dim W+\dim W^\perp=\dim V$. Every vector therefore splits uniquely into a component in $W$ and an orthogonal residual.
