# Narrative Ambiguity

Narrative ambiguity leaves events, motives, relationships, or meanings partly unresolved so the audience must interpret them. In games, ambiguity can also result from hidden systems and from players discovering different amounts of authored material.

Ambiguity opens space for several plausible understandings rather than supplying one final explanation. It is most productive when the available evidence supports interpretation, not when essential information is missing by accident.
