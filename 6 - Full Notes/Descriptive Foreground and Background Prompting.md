# Descriptive Foreground and Background Prompting

Descriptive foreground and background prompting specifies both the main subject and the surrounding scene for text-to-image generation. The source recommends naming the medium, number and position of people or animals, prominent objects, colors, mood, perceived time of day, and environmental details instead of relying on a short subject label.

Foreground detail constrains what should receive attention, while background detail reduces the space the model must fill with assumptions. The prompt should include only details supported by the intended concept; vivid specificity can still fabricate a scene when it is derived from incomplete source material. Iterating on medium and composition is therefore an artistic control, not evidence that the generated image accurately depicts an event.
