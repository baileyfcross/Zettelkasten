# Open Design Principle

The open design principle says security should not depend on attackers being ignorant of how a system works. Protection should rest on controlled secrets such as keys and on mechanisms that remain sound when the design is public.

Public scrutiny can expose weaknesses and permits independent evaluation. Security through obscurity may add friction, but it cannot substitute for a defensible architecture.
