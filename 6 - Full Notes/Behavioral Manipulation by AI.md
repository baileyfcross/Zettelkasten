# Behavioral Manipulation by AI

Behavioral manipulation by AI uses profiling, prediction, and adaptive presentation to steer choices in ways that primarily serve the system's operator. Personalized messages can exploit a person's vulnerabilities while concealing the purpose or method of influence.

The difference between assistance and manipulation depends on autonomy, transparency, and whose goals control the intervention. A recommendation that supports a user's chosen end differs from one designed to capture attention or produce compliance. At scale, manipulation can also affect democratic debate by targeting groups with different messages and weakening a shared basis for public judgment.
