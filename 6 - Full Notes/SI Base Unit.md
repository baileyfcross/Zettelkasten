# SI Base Unit

An SI base unit is one of the independently defined units from which other SI units are constructed. The seven base quantities are length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance, and luminous intensity, measured by the meter, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, and candela. Mechanical measurements chiefly combine length, mass, and time.
