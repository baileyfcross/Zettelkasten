# Between-Cluster Separation

Between-cluster separation measures how distinct one cluster is from other clusters. A strong solution combines tight within-cluster relationships with enough distance between groups that the boundaries have a meaningful interpretation.

Separation can be defined through individual points, cluster centers, extreme cross-cluster pairs, or density gaps. Different definitions motivate methods such as [[Single-Link Clustering]], [[Complete-Link Clustering]], and centroid-based partitioning.
