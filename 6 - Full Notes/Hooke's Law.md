# Hooke's Law

Hooke's law states that deformation is directly proportional to applied force within the linear elastic region. For a spring, $F=-kx$, where the negative sign marks the restoring direction. The law fails beyond the proportional limit, and sufficiently large loading can produce permanent deformation or fracture.
