# AI Ethics Education

AI ethics education makes moral and social analysis a normal part of learning to build, purchase, govern, or use intelligent systems. Technical competence without awareness of bias, privacy, responsibility, and social context can multiply existing risks through routine decisions.

Education should work in both directions. Engineers need practice connecting design choices to human consequences, while people in the humanities and social sciences need enough technical understanding to evaluate what AI can and cannot do. The goal is not a separate checklist course, but a professional understanding in which doing AI responsibly includes ethical judgment.
