# Bottom-Up Network Communities

Bottom-up network communities emerged from hobbyists and ordinary users connecting bulletin boards, forums, and commercial services outside government research networks. Their priorities included conversation, identity, entertainment, and informal exchange.

The modern networked world formed when these social systems collided with top-down academic and military infrastructure. Protocols supplied interconnection, while communities supplied much of the demand and culture.
