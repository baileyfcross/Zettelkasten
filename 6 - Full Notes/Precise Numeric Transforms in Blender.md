# Precise Numeric Transforms in Blender

A transform can receive a numeric value immediately after it begins. Axis constraints and repeated axis keys distinguish global from local directions, allowing exact movement, rotation, or scale without dragging a gizmo.

Blender's advanced numeric input can also evaluate expressions after the equals key, including arithmetic, constants, and simple functions. The Sidebar's Transform panel provides another place to enter exact location, rotation, scale, and dimensions.
