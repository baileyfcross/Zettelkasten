# Suppression of Parentheses

Suppression of parentheses removes grouping symbols while preserving the value of an expression. A plus sign before a group leaves its terms unchanged, while a minus sign negates each term:

$$-(a+b)=-a-b,$$

$$-(a-b)=-a+b.$$

More generally, a factor outside a group is distributed to every internal term. After parentheses are removed, [[Combining Like Terms]] can simplify the result. Parentheses are not redundant when they determine the base of a power or distinguish a product from a sum.
