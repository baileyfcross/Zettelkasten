# Player Value Contribution

A player value contribution is a form of benefit a game receives from its audience. Direct payment is one form, but players can also contribute word of mouth, social visibility, ratings, user-created content, a larger matchmaking pool, or market-scale numbers such as downloads and active users.

These contributions are not automatically compatible. A design optimized for frequent purchases may disrupt flow, while one optimized for content creation or player interaction must provide suitable tools and a sustained community. Choosing one or two primary contributions early helps align the game's systems, business model, and audience promise.
