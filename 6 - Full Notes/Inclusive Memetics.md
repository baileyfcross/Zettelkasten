# Inclusive Memetics

Inclusive memetics treats any information that can be copied through imitation as a meme, without insisting on a strict separation between ideas and their material expressions. Mental representations, spoken performances, and digital artifacts can all participate in the same copying process.

The breadth avoids choosing prematurely between mentalist and behavior-driven schools, but it risks making the term so inclusive that it loses analytical precision. The operational definition of an [[Internet Meme]] narrows the field by requiring a recognizable group of related digital items that circulate through user awareness and transformation.
