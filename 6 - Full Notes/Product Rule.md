# Product Rule

The product rule differentiates a product of functions:

$$(fg)'=f'g+fg'.$$

Both factors may change, so simply multiplying their derivatives is generally wrong. The formula follows by adding and subtracting an intermediate product in the difference quotient and separating the resulting limit into two parts.
