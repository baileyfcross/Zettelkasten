# Activity Tracker

An activity tracker is a device or application that records a person's physical activity, commonly by using motion or location sensors to estimate steps, movement, exercise, or sleep. It usually converts repeated sensor samples into summaries intended for monitoring goals and trends.

The summary is an interpretation rather than a direct view of activity. Algorithms decide how a movement becomes a step, how inactivity becomes sleep, and which time interval appears on the display. Different devices can therefore produce different values from the same routine.

Trackers are sold for personal wellness but may also be subsidized by employers, connected to insurance programs, or used in research. The institutional setting changes whether participation is voluntary and who can act on the record.
