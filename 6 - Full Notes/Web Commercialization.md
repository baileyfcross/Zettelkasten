# Web Commercialization

Web commercialization transformed an open research information system into a platform for retail, advertising, finance, and subscription services. Secure browsers, user sessions, household access, and relaxed network policies made transactions practical.

Companies such as Amazon and eBay demonstrated new business models while speculative investment produced the dot-com bubble. The market crash removed many firms without reversing commercial use of the Web.
