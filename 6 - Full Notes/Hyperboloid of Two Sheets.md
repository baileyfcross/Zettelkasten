# Hyperboloid of Two Sheets

A hyperboloid of two sheets can be written as

$$\frac{z^2}{c^2}-\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1.$$

The equation requires $|z|\geq c$, leaving a gap around the central plane and producing two disconnected sheets. Horizontal sections on either sheet are ellipses, while vertical coordinate-plane sections are hyperbolas.
