# Kirchhoff's Voltage Law

Kirchhoff's voltage law states that the algebraic sum of potential changes around a closed circuit loop is zero. Energy gained per unit charge from sources equals energy transferred per unit charge in components. A chosen traversal direction and consistent sign convention convert the loop into an equation for unknown currents or voltages.
