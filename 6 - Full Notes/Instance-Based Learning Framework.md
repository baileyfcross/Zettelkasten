# Instance-Based Learning Framework

An instance-based learning framework stores training observations and defers most generalization until a test case arrives. It identifies a relevant local neighborhood and predicts from the labels or locally fitted models within it.

This lazy strategy avoids committing to a global boundary, allowing different parts of the feature space to behave differently. Its accuracy and cost depend on the representation, distance function, neighborhood size, and efficiency of retrieving nearby cases.
