# Thermionic Boiler-Tube Integration

A boiler riser tube receives strong radiation from combustion gases and can be fitted with thermionic emitter and collector surfaces. This extracts some electricity from high-temperature heat before the remaining energy drives a steam plant. The source fills the electrode gap with ionized cesium vapor. The integration is a topping approach, not a replacement for the boiler's fluid and downstream turbine cycle.
