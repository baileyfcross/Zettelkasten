# Collective Data Ownership

Collective data ownership assigns control or claims to a group rather than only to an individual holder. It becomes relevant when a dataset describes a community, gains value from many contributors, or creates harms that no member can address alone.

The model raises questions about group definition, representation, decision authority, and benefit sharing. Its purpose is to match governance to collective production and effects that individual contracts may miss.
