# Blender Quad View

Quad View divides one 3D Viewport into top, front, and right orthographic views plus a user-perspective view. It provides the familiar four-view layout of many 3D applications without manually splitting and configuring four separate areas.

The arrangement can be toggled from the View menu or with Ctrl+Alt+Q. Returning to the full view preserves the angle from the perspective quadrant, allowing detailed inspection and measured alignment to coexist.
