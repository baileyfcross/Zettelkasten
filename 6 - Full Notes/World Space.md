# World Space

World space is the shared coordinate system in which game objects have positions and orientations relative to the overall scene. A model reaches world space when its local vertices are transformed by the object's world transform.

Lighting, collision, and relationships among separate objects are often evaluated in this common space. Rendering later converts world-space geometry into [[Camera Space]].
