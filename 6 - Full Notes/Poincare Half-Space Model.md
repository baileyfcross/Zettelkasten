# Poincare Half-Space Model

The Poincare half-space model represents hyperbolic points above a Euclidean boundary using one positive coordinate and the remaining ordinary coordinates. Its shortest paths appear as vertical lines or semicircles perpendicular to the boundary.

It is isometric to other models of [[Hyperbolic Space]], so a transformation can change the representation while preserving hyperbolic distances and geometric relationships.
