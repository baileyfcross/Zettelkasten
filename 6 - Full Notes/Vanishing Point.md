# Vanishing Point

A vanishing point is the image location toward which projections of parallel lines in a three-dimensional direction converge. It may lie far outside the visible frame when the lines are nearly parallel to the image plane.

Lines belonging to the same physical direction should yield a common point under perspective projection. Disagreement can expose incompatible geometry in a composite, but inaccurate line selection and nearly parallel image lines make the intersection unstable, so the estimated uncertainty matters.
