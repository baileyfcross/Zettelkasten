# Image Metadata Preservation Policy

A derivative workflow needs an explicit policy for metadata rather than either copying or stripping everything. Orientation, color profiles, copyright, captions, and business records may be required, while location data, embedded thumbnails, or editor history may be private or unnecessary.

The policy can differ between the archival master and public derivatives. Removing selected fields reduces bytes and exposure, but indiscriminate removal can break color, ownership, layout, or legal requirements. See [[Lossless JPEG Metadata Optimization]] and [[Image Color Profiles]].
