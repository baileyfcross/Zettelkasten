# Net Energy Cost Savings

Net cost savings are the present value of conventional-energy costs avoided by a new system minus its added investment and operating costs, adjusted for residual value. The source treats the changes relative to the existing alternative, not the entire gross cost of both systems. A positive result supports substitution under the chosen price escalation and discount assumptions; [[Net Present Value]] provides the common time basis.
