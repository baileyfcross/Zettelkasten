# Online Correlation Clustering

Online correlation clustering groups evolving time series according to the strength and consistency of their recent relationships. It updates compact statistics or assignments as new observations arrive instead of recomputing every pair over the complete history.

A sliding window or fading mechanism determines which past behavior remains relevant. The method must separate meaningful collective change from transient noise and adapt when formerly correlated series diverge.
