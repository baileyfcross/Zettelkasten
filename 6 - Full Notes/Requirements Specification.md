# Requirements Specification

A requirements specification records requested capabilities, constraints, or expected behavior, but it is an incomplete substitute for direct domain learning. It can freeze assumptions, emphasize features over the underlying [[Problem Space]], and omit tacit knowledge that experts consider obvious. Treating it as an input to [[Knowledge Crunching]] rather than as unquestionable truth allows conversations, prototypes, and models to reveal contradictions and clarify what outcome the software must support.
