# Direct Sum of Vector Spaces

A vector space $V$ is the direct sum $U_1\oplus\cdots\oplus U_k$ when every vector in $V$ can be written uniquely as $u_1+\cdots+u_k$ with $u_i\in U_i$. The subspaces must span $V$ without sharing nonzero directions that would make the representation ambiguous.

For two subspaces, the condition is $V=U+W$ together with $U\cap W=\{0\}$. More generally, each summand must intersect the sum of the others only in the [[Zero Vector]].
