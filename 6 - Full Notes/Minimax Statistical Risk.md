# Minimax Statistical Risk

Minimax statistical risk is the smallest worst-case risk achievable by any estimator over a specified parameter class. The outer worst case prevents a method from appearing strong only on easy signals, while the infimum over estimators defines an information-theoretic benchmark.

For coordinate-sparse regression, the characteristic prediction scale is proportional to sparsity times $\log(p/D)$ and noise variance. Matching upper and lower bounds show that an estimator is rate-optimal up to constants. Minimax risk therefore helps locate the [[High-Dimensional Estimation Frontier]] rather than merely rank two available algorithms.
