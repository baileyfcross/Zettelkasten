# Rand Index

The Rand index compares two partitions by examining every pair of observations. It counts agreement when both partitions place the pair together or both place it apart, then divides by the total number of pairs.

The index can rise as the number of clusters increases and does not correct for chance agreement. Adjusted variants address those limitations.
