# Simulation as a Third Mode of Science

Computer simulation introduced a third mode of scientific work alongside theory and experiment. It lets researchers execute models whose equations are too complex for closed-form solutions and observe the resulting behavior.

A simulation can function as an experimental platform when physical experiments are inaccessible, dangerous, slow, or expensive. Its evidence remains dependent on the model, numerical method, parameters, and validation against the world.
