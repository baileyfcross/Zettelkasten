# Biological Material Responsiveness

Biological material responsiveness is the ability of living material to sense conditions and change its form or function. Muscle strengthens with repeated loading, skin initiates repair after injury, and cells alter behavior in response to chemical, electrical, and mechanical signals.

This adaptiveness motivates biofabrication but also makes final behavior less fixed than that of an ordinary manufactured part.
