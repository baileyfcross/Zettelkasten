# Designer Loop

The designer loop is the cycle in which a designer changes a game, observes players interacting with it, compares their experience with the design intent, and revises the design. It acts as a [[Balancing Loop]] around the [[Game-Player System]].

The loop cannot close through rules or a simulation alone; it requires actual player interaction. [[Prototype|Prototypes]] and [[Playtesting]] supply the evidence by which the designer reduces the gap between intent and experience.
