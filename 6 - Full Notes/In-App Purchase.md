# In-App Purchase

An in-app purchase is a real-money transaction made from within a game to obtain content, features, currency, convenience, or continued play. It connects the internal economy to an external payment system.

The item offered determines the design effect. Cosmetic purchases may preserve competitive rules, while functional advantages can create [[Pay-to-Win]] pressure and alter how every player evaluates progress.
