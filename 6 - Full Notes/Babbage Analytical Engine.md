# Babbage Analytical Engine

The Analytical Engine was Babbage’s design for a general-purpose mechanical computer. Punched cards supplied instructions, while separate input, processing, memory, and output units supported selection, looping, and arithmetic operations.

Although never completed, its organization anticipated major elements of later machines. The design showed how one mechanism could perform many computations when controlled by different programs.
