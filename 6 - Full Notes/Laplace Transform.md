# Laplace Transform

The Laplace transform maps a function on the nonnegative time axis to a complex parameter by integrating it against an exponential decay factor. The factor can make growing or oscillating functions transformable within a suitable region of convergence.

Time derivatives transform into algebraic powers of the parameter together with terms containing initial values. This property incorporates initial conditions directly and makes the transform especially effective for linear initial-value problems and piecewise forcing functions.
