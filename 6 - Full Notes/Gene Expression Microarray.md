# Gene Expression Microarray

A gene expression microarray measures many transcripts by hybridizing labeled sample material to probes arranged on a chip. The resulting intensities provide a high-dimensional expression profile for each biological sample.

Raw intensities include technical effects from preparation, scanning, and probe behavior, so comparisons require normalization and quality control. Microarrays measure predefined probes rather than discovering every sequence, but their matrix-like output supports differential expression, clustering, classification, and biomarker analysis across many genes at once.
