# Biofabrication Scale-Up

Biofabrication scale-up expands a laboratory process to produce far greater quantities with controlled cost and quality. Larger bioreactors, automated handling, reliable cell banks, process monitoring, and contamination control must replace repeated manual fabrication.

Biological variability and transport limits mean that simply enlarging every vessel can change cell behavior, so scale often requires redesign rather than multiplication.
