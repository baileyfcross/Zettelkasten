# Lasso Oracle Inequality

A Lasso oracle inequality bounds prediction error by the best sparse approximation error plus a variance term proportional to support size and the logarithm of ambient dimension. It states that Lasso can nearly adapt to an unknown sparse representation without enumerating all supports.

The guarantee requires the penalty to dominate the largest design–noise correlation and depends inversely on the [[Compatibility Constant]]. A highly collinear design can make that constant small and the bound weak. The result is consequently both a performance guarantee and a precise statement of the design conditions under which sparsity is useful.
