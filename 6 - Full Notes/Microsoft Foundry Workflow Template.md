# Microsoft Foundry Workflow Template

Microsoft Foundry workflow templates provide starting structures for common orchestration patterns. A blank workflow offers full control, a sequential workflow passes work through a fixed order, a human-in-the-loop workflow pauses at approval points, and a group-chat workflow coordinates specialized agents.

The choice follows the process rather than the sophistication of the model. Linear transformations favor sequential execution; regulated or high-risk decisions need review checkpoints; distinct expert roles may justify a group chat; and an unusual process may need a blank canvas. Templates accelerate setup but remain editable designs whose nodes, handoffs, timeouts, and failure behavior require explicit validation.
