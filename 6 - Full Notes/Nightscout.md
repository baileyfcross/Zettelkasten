# Nightscout

Nightscout is an open-source, do-it-yourself project that makes continuous glucose-monitor data available on more convenient displays such as a phone or watch. It was developed by patients and families who needed the information to fit daily activities and caregiving situations that the original device did not address well.

Changing the display can materially change care without changing the underlying measurement. A parent can monitor a child at a sleepover, and an adult can check a watch while using both hands or driving instead of repeatedly handling a separate monitor.

The project demonstrates [[Patient Expertise]] and the value of data access. It also crosses clinical and nonclinical systems, raising questions about reliability, security, regulation, and responsibility for a user-created modification.
