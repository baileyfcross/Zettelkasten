# Online TSV Current-Direction Balancing

Online TSV current-direction balancing counters long periods of current in one direction by applying or scheduling current in the reverse direction. Alternating flow allows partial atomic redistribution and can slow electromigration compared with an equivalent unidirectional stress.

A mitigation circuit monitors accumulated directional activity for a defective TSV and acts before imbalance exceeds a chosen threshold. It cannot repair the original manufacturing defect; it extends usable lifetime by reducing the defect's electromigration acceleration.
