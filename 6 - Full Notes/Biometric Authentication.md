# Biometric Authentication

Biometric authentication uses a measured physical or behavioral characteristic as evidence of identity. Fingerprints, facial features, voice, and other traits belong to the inherent category in [[Authentication Factor Categories]].

Biometric matching is probabilistic rather than a perfect equality test, so systems must manage false acceptance and rejection. Because a biometric characteristic is difficult to replace after exposure, it is often stronger as one factor in [[Multi-Factor Authentication]] than as a standalone secret.
