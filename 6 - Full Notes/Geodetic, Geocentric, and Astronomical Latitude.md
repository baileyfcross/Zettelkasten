# Geodetic, Geocentric, and Astronomical Latitude

Geocentric latitude uses the line from a surface point to Earth's center, astronomical latitude uses the local direction of gravity, and geodetic latitude uses the normal to the reference ellipsoid. Because Earth is oblate rather than perfectly spherical, these directions generally differ away from the Equator and poles. Unqualified latitude conventionally means geodetic latitude.
