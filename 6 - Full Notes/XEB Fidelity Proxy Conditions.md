# XEB Fidelity Proxy Conditions

XEB approximates many-body fidelity when circuit scrambling equilibrates correlations faster than weak noise removes them. With independent, spatially homogeneous errors of rate $\epsilon$, the leading correction stays small in a regime of the form $N\epsilon f(c)\ll 1$, where $f(c)$ depends on the circuit architecture and gate ensemble. XEB, fidelity, and the probability of no error then have approximately the same leading behavior.

This is a conditional calibration result rather than an identity. The independence and homogeneity assumptions must be checked experimentally, and the approximation worsens when the error rate outruns equilibration or when errors are localized. [[Particle-Density Discrepancy between XEB and Fidelity]] gives a statistical-mechanics account of why these departures usually make XEB overestimate fidelity.
