# Bomb Disposal Robot

A bomb-disposal robot lets an operator inspect, manipulate, or detonate a suspected explosive from a safer distance. Tracked mobility, cameras, chemical or radiation sensors, and an articulated arm allow the robot to enter buildings, caves, roadsides, or other spaces before a person does.

The robot does not remove risk; it relocates and changes it. Communication can fail, debris can trap the platform, manipulation remains difficult, and the device may be sacrificed. Its value comes from placing replaceable machinery between a human team and the most dangerous portion of the task.
