# Luck-Influence Test

A luck-influence test examines how close players must be in ability before an outcome becomes unpredictable. If experts consistently defeat novices but similarly skilled players trade wins, the game is influenced by luck while still allowing ability to dominate across a large skill gap.

Individual matches do not provide enough evidence for this classification. Designers need many games among players of varied ability and can use rankings or ladders to estimate skill. Long-run win trends then reveal how much randomness obscures or preserves the relationship between player quality and results.
