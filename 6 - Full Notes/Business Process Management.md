# Business Process Management

Business process management is the collection of methods, tools, and technologies used to design, enact, analyze, optimize, and control business processes. It treats recurring work as an explicit flow that can be measured and improved.

Cloud business-process services depend on this discipline because automation is valuable only after the process, participants, information, and desired outcome are understood. A workflow may contain both automated and human stages.
