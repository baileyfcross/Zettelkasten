# Helmholtz Equation

The Helmholtz equation has the form $\nabla^2u+k^2u=0$ and describes the spatial part of a field with a single harmonic time dependence. It results when the time-dependent wave equation is separated using an oscillation at fixed angular frequency.

Its solutions depend on geometry and boundary conditions: trigonometric modes suit rectangular domains, Bessel functions suit cylindrical symmetry, and spherical Bessel functions with spherical harmonics suit spherical symmetry. Allowed values of $k$ form the normal-mode spectrum.
