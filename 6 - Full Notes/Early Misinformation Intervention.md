# Early Misinformation Intervention

Early misinformation intervention tries to identify malicious accounts or claims before they create a large cascade. Detection from account construction and behavior can act before the first harmful message is widely forwarded.

Earlier action limits exposure but uses less evidence and risks more false positives. An intervention policy must balance that classification uncertainty against the speed and potential damage of diffusion.
