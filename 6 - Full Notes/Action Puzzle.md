# Action Puzzle

An action puzzle combines discovery of a solution with real-time or dexterous execution. The player may understand exactly what must happen yet still need practice to perform the sequence, timing, or movement correctly.

This separates conceptual difficulty from execution difficulty. A design can become more puzzle-like by narrowing the gap between intent and action—for example, by slowing or pausing time during planning—or more action-oriented by demanding precise performance after the insight is known. Feedback should reveal which part caused failure.
