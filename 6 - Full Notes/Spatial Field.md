# Spatial Field

A spatial field treats a variable as potentially defined at every location in a region, even when it is observed only at a finite set of samples. Elevation, temperature, and solar radiation are common examples. A raster discretizes the field into cells as [[Raster Data]], while [[Point-Surface Interpolation]] estimates unsampled locations from point observations. Field maps emphasize spatial pattern rather than individual named objects.
