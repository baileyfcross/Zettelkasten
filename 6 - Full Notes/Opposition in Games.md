# Opposition in Games

Opposition is whatever pushes back against a player's attempt to reach a goal. It may come from rules, the game world, computer-controlled agents, other players, or trade-offs within the player's own plans.

Opposition creates uncertainty and gives [[Meaningful Decision|decisions]] stakes. It supports engagement when it challenges the player's model and skills without creating an unrecoverable or inherently unfair state.
