# Transitive Closure

The transitive closure R* of a [[Binary Relation]] R relates a to b when a finite sequence of one or more R-steps leads from a to b. It is the smallest transitive relation containing the original relation.

If R records direct flights between cities, R* records every destination reachable through a sequence of flights. The construction converts local adjacency into multi-step reachability.
