# Target Audience Playtest

A target-audience playtest recruits people who are likely to seek the intended kind of experience. It asks whether a sufficiently developed prototype fulfills its promise for the players the project means to serve.

This test becomes useful after the core experience exists; using it too early can make participants evaluate missing finish instead of the design question. Audience fit supplies evidence without turning majority preference into the game's creative direction.
