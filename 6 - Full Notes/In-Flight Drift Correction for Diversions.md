# In-Flight Drift Correction for Diversions

A diversion track must be checked against landmarks and elapsed time after the new heading is established. Before halfway, the [[Double-Error Drift Correction]] can return the aircraft to track in the same elapsed time; after halfway, the [[Opening-Closing Angle Drift Correction]] aims directly toward the destination. If the destination bearing is known reliably, direct tracking can replace both approximations.
