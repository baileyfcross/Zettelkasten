# Patient-Generated Pharmacovigilance

Patient-generated pharmacovigilance uses online posts and community reports to identify experiences associated with medications. It can capture symptoms, quality-of-life effects, and treatment concerns that may not reach formal reporting systems.

The evidence is noisy and self-selected. Product exposure, dosage, timing, and medical confirmation may be missing, while copied or general statements can resemble personal reports. Text mining should identify who experienced what and when, deduplicate messages, and treat the result as a safety signal requiring corroboration.
