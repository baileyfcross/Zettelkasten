# Free-to-Play Game

A free-to-play game permits continued play without an initial purchase while encouraging optional payment for speed, access, convenience, content, or service quality. It lowers the entry barrier and depends on converting part of a large audience into paying customers.

The model shapes progression and rewards because friction can become a sales mechanism. Designers must distinguish enjoyable pacing from repetitive delay created mainly to pressure payment.
