# Gravitational Field Strength

Gravitational field strength is force per unit test mass, $g=F/m$. Around a spherical mass $M$, its magnitude is $GM/r^2$ and its direction is inward. It is also the local acceleration of freely falling matter when other forces are negligible, giving it equivalent units of $\mathrm{N\,kg^{-1}}$ and $\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.
