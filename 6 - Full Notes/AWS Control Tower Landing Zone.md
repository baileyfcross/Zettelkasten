# AWS Control Tower Landing Zone

An AWS Control Tower landing zone is a governed multi-account environment built from AWS Organizations and related services. It establishes account structure, identity access, centralized logging, and preventive or detective controls as a baseline for new workloads.

Account vending applies the baseline repeatedly rather than configuring every account by hand. The landing zone accelerates consistent governance, but its controls and organizational units still need to reflect the enterprise's risk and operating model.
