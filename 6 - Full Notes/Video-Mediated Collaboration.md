# Video-Mediated Collaboration

Video-mediated collaboration shares live imagery of a participant or task environment with a remote collaborator. AR can register virtual objects, marks, or pointing cues with the video so guidance refers directly to visible physical features.

A fixed camera, participant-held camera, or head-mounted view provides different context and control. Delay, limited field of view, viewpoint choice, and alignment of annotations affect how well the remote user can understand the scene.
