# Head-Worn Display

A head-worn display is a [[Visual Display]] worn on the user's head so the imagery remains aligned with the eyes as the head moves. Opaque versions can immerse a user in [[Virtual Reality]], while see-through forms can overlay information for [[Augmented Reality]].

Head tracking lets the system render from the user's changing viewpoint. Because the display turns with the head, it can provide a 360-degree [[Field of Regard]] even when its instantaneous [[Field of View]] is much smaller.

Design tradeoffs include resolution, weight, comfort, optics, depth-cue conflicts, and [[Latency]].
