# Technology Jumping

Technology jumping occurs when an industry moves from a maturing technology to a new one that can sustain rapid improvement. Successive waves—from punched cards to vacuum tubes, transistors, and silicon—can preserve an overall growth trend even when each substrate reaches limits.

Organizations watch for the inflection point where the current technology slows and a competitor’s alternative accelerates. The jump requires investment and redesign because new physical mechanisms often demand new architectures and skills.
