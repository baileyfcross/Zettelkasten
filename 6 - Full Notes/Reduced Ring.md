# Reduced Ring

A reduced ring is a ring whose [[Nilradical]] is zero. Equivalently, it has no nonzero [[Nilpotent Element]]s.

Every ring R has a canonical reduced quotient R/N, where N is its nilradical. This quotient preserves the prime-ideal structure while eliminating elements whose powers vanish.
