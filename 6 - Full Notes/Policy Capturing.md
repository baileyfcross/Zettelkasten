# Policy Capturing

Policy capturing models a person's judgments from the observable attributes of the cases being evaluated. Predictor variables describe each profile and a criterion variable records the judge's overall rating.

A regression equation makes the implicit weighting policy explicit. Its fit indicates whether the judge applied a consistent relationship across the evaluated cases.
