# JPEG Ghost

A JPEG ghost is a regional difference pattern that appears when an already compressed image is recompressed across a range of candidate qualities. Pixels originating from a previous JPEG save respond differently near their earlier quality level than pixels with another compression history.

Mapping average absolute differences can reveal a pasted region whose effective quality differs from its surroundings. The result indicates inconsistent prior compression, not the exact editing operation, and naturally mixed or repeatedly processed files can complicate interpretation.
