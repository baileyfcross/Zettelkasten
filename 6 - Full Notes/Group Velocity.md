# Group Velocity

Group velocity is the speed of the slowly varying envelope formed by nearby wave frequencies. For a continuous dispersion relation it is $v_g=d\omega/dk$, obtained as the limit of the beat-pattern ratio $\Delta\omega/\Delta k$.

In a dispersive medium it can differ from [[Phase Velocity]] and generally depends on frequency. The group velocity describes the propagation of the wave group and provides an estimate of the speed with which its energy is carried.
