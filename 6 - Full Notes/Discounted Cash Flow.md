# Discounted Cash Flow

Discounted cash flow values a future payment by reducing it according to the time until it occurs and a selected discount rate. The method reflects time preference, financing cost, and investment alternatives.

Long-delayed benefits from a big-data component may contribute less present value than smaller benefits delivered earlier, even if their nominal totals are equal.
