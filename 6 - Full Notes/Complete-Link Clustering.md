# Complete-Link Clustering

Complete-link clustering defines the distance between two clusters by their most distant cross-cluster pair. It is also called furthest-neighbor linkage.

A merge is acceptable only when all members remain comparatively close, so the method tends to produce compact groups. It can be sensitive to extreme points because the largest pairwise separation controls each comparison.
