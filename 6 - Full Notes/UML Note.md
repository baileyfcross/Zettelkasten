# UML Note

A UML note is a free-form annotation attached to a diagram element or placed near the behavior it explains. It can record a constraint, assumption, clarification, or other information that the standard symbols do not express economically.

Notes should support the model rather than replace missing relationships or messages. Concise annotations preserve readability while making important qualifications visible to the reader.
