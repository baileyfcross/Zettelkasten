# Speech-to-Text Service

A speech-to-text service exposes [[Automatic Speech Recognition]] through a network interface. A device sends captured audio to a remote system and receives a transcript that another assistant component can interpret.

Centralized services can use large models and compute resources, but they introduce latency, connectivity, and privacy concerns. Local [[Wake Word]] detection can limit transmission to audio that follows a likely activation.
