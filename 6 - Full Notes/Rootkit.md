# Rootkit

A rootkit is a collection of techniques or software that preserves privileged, concealed access to a compromised system. It can alter or evade ordinary system reporting so that malicious processes, files, or connections are harder to observe.

Rootkits attack confidence in the host's own evidence, making [[System Integrity Verification]] especially important. Recovery may require inspection from a trusted environment or complete restoration rather than relying only on the affected operating system.
