# Perspective Projection

Perspective projection maps a three-dimensional scene point onto a two-dimensional sensor along a ray through the camera center. In the pinhole model, image position is proportional to focal length and lateral scene position and inversely proportional to depth.

The relation explains why nearby objects appear larger and why parallel scene lines may converge in an image. Photogrammetric forensic methods use these invariants to test whether objects, shadows, reflections, and camera parameters could belong to one coherent scene.
