# Excel Formula-Based Conditional Formatting

Excel formula-based conditional formatting applies a visual style when a formula evaluates to true. The formula is written relative to the active cell of the selected range, and Excel adjusts relative references as the rule is evaluated across that range.

Anchoring determines whether every cell tests its own value, a value from the same row, or one fixed threshold. Mixed references are therefore central to correct rules. Conditional formatting should reveal meaningful states rather than decorate the sheet: the underlying values and formula remain the evidence, while color or style makes exceptions, deadlines, or patterns easier to notice.
