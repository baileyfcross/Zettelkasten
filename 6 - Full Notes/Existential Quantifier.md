# Existential Quantifier

The existential quantifier ∃ turns an open statement P(x) into the proposition ∃x P(x), which is true when at least one entity in the domain of discourse makes P true.

Negating an existential claim produces a universal denial: ¬∃x P(x) is [[Logically Equivalent]] to ∀x ¬P(x). An existence claim requires only one witness, whereas its negation must rule out every candidate.
