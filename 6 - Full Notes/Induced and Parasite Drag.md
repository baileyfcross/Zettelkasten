# Induced and Parasite Drag

Induced drag is the drag associated with producing lift and is especially important at lower speeds and higher lift coefficients. Parasite drag combines form, skin-friction, and interference effects and grows strongly as speed increases. Their opposing speed trends create a minimum in total drag that helps define efficient cruise and glide conditions.
