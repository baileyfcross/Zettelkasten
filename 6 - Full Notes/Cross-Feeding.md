# Cross-Feeding

Cross-feeding is a balancing structure that lets different player skills or systems satisfy the same objective. A player may defeat a boss through combat execution or compensate for weaker execution by investing time in finding powerful equipment.

Cross-feeding increases agency and supports personal stories because players can route effort through the systems they prefer. It also makes scenario balance harder: one route may dominate and render the others functionally useless. It is therefore most appropriate in sufficiently rich, interconnected games where each supported path can be tested as a genuinely viable alternative.
