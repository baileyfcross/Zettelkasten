# Base Point of a Parametrization

A base point of a projective parametrization is a parameter value at which every defining homogeneous polynomial vanishes. The coordinate formulas therefore fail to determine an image point there.

Base points carry algebraic and geometric multiplicities. Their contributions reduce the degree of a generically one-to-one parametrized surface from the square of the polynomial degree, linking local intersection data to global implicit geometry.
