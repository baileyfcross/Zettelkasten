# Total Cost of Ownership

Total cost of ownership includes the continuing costs required to obtain, operate, support, maintain, and eventually replace or leave a technology, not only its purchase or license price. Application packaging, distribution, updates, staffing, integration, and backup can materially change a comparison.

For a cloud service, subscription and usage charges must be considered alongside migration, network, support, dependency, data export, and continuity costs. A low entry price does not by itself establish low ownership cost.
