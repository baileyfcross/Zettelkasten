# Auslander-Buchsbaum Formula

For a finitely generated module M of finite projective dimension over a Noetherian local ring R, the Auslander-Buchsbaum formula is pd_R(M) + depth(M) = depth(R).

The formula balances homological complexity against regular-sequence depth. A module requiring a longer projective resolution must have correspondingly less depth when the ambient ring remains fixed.
