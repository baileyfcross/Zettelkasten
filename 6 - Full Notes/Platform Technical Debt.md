# Platform Technical Debt

Platform technical debt is the future operating, maintenance, security, or change cost created by current architecture and implementation choices. It includes deliberate shortcuts, vendor and open-source dependencies, custom integration glue, retained data, obsolete components, and once-sensible decisions that no longer fit the platform’s scale or users.

Debt is not proof of failure; some of it is the cost of learning and delivering value before every uncertainty is resolved. It becomes harmful when the team cannot see, own, or prioritize it and spends increasing time on toil instead of product improvement. The debt ledger should connect each obligation to evidence, risk, an owner, and a review point.
