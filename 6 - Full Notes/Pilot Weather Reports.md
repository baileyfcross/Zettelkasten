# Pilot Weather Reports

A pilot weather report supplies direct observations from an aircraft already encountering the atmosphere. It can report turbulence, icing, cloud bases, visibility, or other conditions that fixed stations and forecasts may not resolve along a route. These observations provide practical evidence for evaluating [[SIGMET and AIRMET Advisories]] and forecasts.
