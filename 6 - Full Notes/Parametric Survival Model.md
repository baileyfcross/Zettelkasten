# Parametric Survival Model

A parametric survival model assumes that event times follow a specified distribution, such as exponential, log-normal, Weibull, gamma, or log-logistic. The distribution supplies a complete mathematical form for survival and hazard over time.

This structure can yield efficient, interpretable estimates when the assumption is credible, but different distributions can reverse conclusions when the fit is poor. Fitted curves and information criteria should guide [[Survival Model Distribution Selection]].
