# Attribute Weighting for Clustering

Attribute weighting for clustering assigns different influence to features in the proximity or objective function. It is useful when domain knowledge says some differences matter more than others.

Weights directly change neighborhood geometry and cluster boundaries. They should be justified, tested for sensitivity, and reported as part of the model.
