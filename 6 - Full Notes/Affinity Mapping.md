# Affinity Mapping

Affinity mapping groups individual observations or ideas by perceived relationship so patterns can emerge from a large set. Participants move items into clusters and name the themes the clusters represent.

The technique is useful during retrospectives and problem exploration because it lets structure arise from the evidence before the group chooses a cause or solution. It supports convergence without discarding minority observations too early.
