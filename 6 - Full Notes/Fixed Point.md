# Fixed Point

A fixed point of a function $g$ is a value $p$ satisfying $g(p)=p$. An equation $f(x)=0$ can often be rearranged into an equivalent fixed-point equation.

The rearrangement matters because different choices of $g$ can have different convergence behavior. [[Fixed-Point Iteration]] succeeds locally when the mapping contracts distances around the fixed point.
