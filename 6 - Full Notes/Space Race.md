# Space Race

The Space Race was the Cold War competition in which the United States and Soviet Union used space achievements to signal technological capability, military strength, and political legitimacy. It began publicly with announced scientific-satellite projects and intensified after [[Sputnik 1]] demonstrated Soviet orbital launch capability.

The race rewarded visible firsts, especially human flights, because audiences could interpret them without understanding the underlying engineering. That logic accelerated schedules and spending far beyond what scientific demand alone would have supported. The competition built launch vehicles, agencies, factories, tracking networks, and operational experience that later sustained science and infrastructure after its most intense phase ended.
