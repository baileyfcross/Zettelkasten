# Thermal Result Scale Selection

The color range of a thermal plot controls which differences become visible. An automatic full range faithfully shows global extrema but can compress meaningful variation elsewhere, especially when a small hot region dominates the maximum.

A restricted scale can reveal the operational temperature distribution, but saturated regions must be disclosed. Presenting a full-scale view beside a focused view, as in several case studies, lets [[COMSOL Temperature Contours|temperature contours]] communicate both extremes and local structure.
