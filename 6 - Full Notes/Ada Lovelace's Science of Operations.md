# Ada Lovelace's Science of Operations

Ada Lovelace described programs for the [[Babbage Analytical Engine]] and recognized that its symbols could stand for more than numerical quantities. Any information with a suitable symbolic representation could in principle be processed by the machine.

She called the study of such procedures the science of operations. The insight reframed the computer as an information machine rather than only an automatic calculator.
