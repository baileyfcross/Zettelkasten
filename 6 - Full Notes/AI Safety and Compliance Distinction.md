# AI Safety and Compliance Distinction

AI safety concerns whether a system avoids harmful, unreliable, or uncontrolled behavior, while compliance concerns whether its design and operation meet applicable laws, regulations, contracts, and organizational requirements. The two overlap, but neither guarantees the other: a safe-seeming system may lack required records or controls, and a formally compliant process may still produce poor outcomes.

Teams should maintain separate evidence for each purpose and connect it where appropriate. Safety evaluation, red teaming, guardrails, and incident handling address behavior; access records, approvals, data boundaries, documentation, and auditability support compliance. Keeping the distinction visible prevents a passing safety metric from being treated as complete governance.
