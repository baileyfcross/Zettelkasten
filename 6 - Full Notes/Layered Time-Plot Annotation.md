# Layered Time-Plot Annotation

A layered time-plot annotation adds explanatory marks behind or above the primary [[Time Plot]] without replacing the observed series. Background blocks can distinguish years, translucent regions can flag threshold conditions, and lines or labels can mark averages and extrema. Layer order matters: broad contextual regions should generally be drawn first so they do not hide the data. [[Time-Series Panel Function|Panel functions]] provide one way to control that order.
