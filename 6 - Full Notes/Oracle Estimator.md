# Oracle Estimator

An oracle estimator is the member of a candidate collection with the smallest true risk. Its index depends on the unknown signal or data-generating distribution, so the oracle cannot normally be computed from observed data.

The oracle serves as a benchmark rather than an algorithm. A selection or aggregation procedure is successful when its risk is close to the oracle risk after paying a controlled complexity cost. The oracle model need not be literally true: it may simply give the best bias–variance tradeoff among deliberately approximate models.
