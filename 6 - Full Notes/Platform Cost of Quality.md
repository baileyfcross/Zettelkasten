# Platform Cost of Quality

Platform quality has a continuing cost in engineering time, operations, licenses, support, and internal cost sharing. A highly integrated and reliable platform may be excellent for its primary workloads yet too expensive for a small service or too restrictive for an exceptional system.

Quality should therefore be matched to use cases rather than maximized uniformly. When the shared platform’s entry cost exceeds the value it provides, teams are pushed toward shadow platforms and duplicate tooling. A tiered or composable design can preserve strong defaults while avoiding a single expensive quality level for every workload.
