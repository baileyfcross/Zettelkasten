# Translation-Resistant Meme

A translation-resistant meme depends so heavily on language, cultural knowledge, or local convention that moving it to another audience destroys much of its effect. Puns, phonetic jokes, and densely situated references are especially difficult to reproduce.

Resistance does not make circulation impossible, but it raises the amount of adaptation required. A creator may replace the original reference with a culturally proximate one or preserve only the formal structure. The degree of resistance helps explain why visually simple memes often travel farther than verbally intricate jokes.
