# Information Systems Model Construction

Information systems model construction converts validated observations and a conceptual account into a usable formal or statistical representation. The process begins by confirming that the data are the intended data, their values are correctly specified, and they are complete, accurate, and fit for the proposed use.

The researcher then selects variables and a model form, performs preliminary analyses, chooses the design and methodology, checks the fitted model, and extracts the equation or rule that represents the result. These steps are iterative: diagnostics can require revised variables, transformed measurements, or a different structure.

Verification and maintenance continue after construction. The implementation must correspond to its specification, and the model must be reassessed when its environment, data-generating process, or intended decision changes.
