# Cultural Proximity in Meme Diffusion

Cultural proximity is the degree to which an audience shares the language, references, values, genres, and social knowledge needed to interpret a meme. Greater proximity generally lowers the effort required for recognition and reuse.

Diffusion therefore follows cultural as well as technical connections. A globally accessible artifact may remain locally unintelligible, while a translated or adapted version can bridge the gap. Cultural proximity explains why cross-border reach is selective rather than an automatic consequence of Internet availability.
