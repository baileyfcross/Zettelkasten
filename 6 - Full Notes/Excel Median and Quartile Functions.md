# Excel Median and Quartile Functions

Excel MEDIAN returns the middle value of an ordered dataset, splitting observations into two halves. Quartile functions return boundaries that divide an ordered dataset into four parts. These measures describe position without being pulled as strongly by extreme observations as an arithmetic mean.

The median and quartiles are useful for skewed distributions and for comparing the center and spread of groups. Their interpretation still depends on the data selected and the quartile convention used by the chosen Excel function. A workbook should not mix differently defined quartile variants without recording that choice.
