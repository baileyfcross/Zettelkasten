# Particle Motion Forces and Lifetime

Particles acquire movement from velocity, gravity, resistance, turbulence, and other force or motion parameters. Size, weight, and lifetime change how long an element remains and how it responds in the simulation. The book builds a fountain by driving particles upward, spreading their path, adding downward gravity, and then adjusting the starting velocity to restore its height. Variability breaks the unnatural uniformity of identical particles. These controls act after the birth timing described in [[Particle Emitter Birth and Limit]].
