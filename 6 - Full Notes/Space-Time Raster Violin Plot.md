# Space-Time Raster Violin Plot

A space-time raster violin plot combines a density-shaped silhouette with box-plot summaries for the cells belonging to each temporal group. It reveals changes in central tendency, spread, and multimodality, such as recurring clear-sky and overcast modes. The shape depends on density bandwidth and ignores cell locations. [[Space-Time Raster Small Multiples]] are therefore needed to connect the distribution back to geography.
