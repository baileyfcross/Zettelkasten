# Subjective Probability

Subjective probability represents the state of knowledge of a reasoner about a proposition or variable. A distribution is conditioned on preliminary knowledge, so two reasoners with different information can rationally assign different probabilities.

Subjective does not mean arbitrary. Assignments and updates must follow the probability rules consistently, and disagreement with observations can reveal that the preliminary knowledge or model structure should be revised.
