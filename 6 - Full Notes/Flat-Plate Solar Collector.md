# Flat-Plate Solar Collector

A flat-plate collector admits sunlight through a transparent cover, absorbs it on a dark plate, and transfers heat through tubes to a working fluid. Insulation and casing reduce losses. It is commonly fixed and tilted toward the equator, can use both beam and diffuse radiation, and suits lower-temperature tasks such as water or space heating. Higher fluid temperature increases its heat loss.
