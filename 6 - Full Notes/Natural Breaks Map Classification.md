# Natural Breaks Map Classification

Natural breaks map classification chooses interval boundaries around clusters and gaps in the observed distribution, commonly through [[Fisher-Jenks Classification]]. It can reveal structure hidden by equal-width or equal-count intervals because each class is internally compact. Since its breaks depend on a particular dataset, two maps may use different numeric meanings for the same colors. Shared breaks are necessary when maps must be compared directly.
