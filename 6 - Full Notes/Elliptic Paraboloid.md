# Elliptic Paraboloid

An elliptic paraboloid has a standard form such as

$$z=\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}.$$

Horizontal sections $z=c>0$ are ellipses, while vertical coordinate-plane sections are parabolas. Because the right side is nonnegative, the standard surface lies on or above its vertex plane.
