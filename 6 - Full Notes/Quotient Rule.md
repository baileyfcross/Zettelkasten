# Quotient Rule

For differentiable functions with $g(x)\ne0$, the quotient rule is

$$\left(\frac{f}{g}\right)'=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}.$$

It can be derived from the [[Product Rule]] by writing $f=(f/g)g$. Domain restrictions remain important because the derivative cannot restore points excluded by a zero denominator.
