# Parallax as a 3D Matchmove Cue

An image-space track can be sufficient when a flat replacement follows a surface without substantial depth change. If the camera moves around an object and reveals formerly hidden sides, nearby and distant features move differently: this parallax exposes a flat-card solution. A 3D camera solve places the replacement at the correct depth and lets its perspective change with the filmed camera. The distinction helps choose between [[Four-Point Corner Pin Tracking]] and [[3D Camera Solve from Feature Tracks]].
