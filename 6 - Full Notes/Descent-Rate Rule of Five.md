# Descent-Rate Rule of Five

Multiplying groundspeed in knots by five estimates the feet-per-minute descent rate for a path near three degrees. At 150 knots, the rule gives about 750 feet per minute, and the rate should fall as groundspeed decreases on approach. The exact geometric multiplier is slightly larger, so the rule intentionally provides a quick cockpit approximation.
