# Magnetostatic Field

A magnetostatic field is a time-independent magnetic field produced by steady current. It is governed by the static forms of Ampere's law and magnetic Gauss's law.

Scalar magnetic potential can be used in current-free regions, while a vector magnetic potential represents magnetic flux density more generally. Material permeability links magnetic field intensity and flux density.
