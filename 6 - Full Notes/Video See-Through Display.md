# Video See-Through Display

A video see-through display captures the physical world with a camera and electronically composites that image with generated imagery. The user views the completed image on an otherwise conventional display rather than looking directly through an optical combiner.

The digital pipeline gives the renderer control over both real and virtual pixels, which supports pixel-level registration, occlusion, and common image processing. Camera and display delay, limited resolution, distortion, and a viewpoint offset between camera and eye can weaken the experience.
