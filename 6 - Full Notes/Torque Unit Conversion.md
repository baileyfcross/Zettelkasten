# Torque Unit Conversion

A moment of force carries force multiplied by distance. Pryor converts pound-force feet to newton meters by applying a pound-force-to-newton factor and a feet-to-meters factor. Both original units cancel, leaving $\mathrm{N\,m}$. This differs from pressure, which divides force by area, even though both may begin with pound-force. A unit string such as lbf·ft should be kept intact until each part has been converted. See [[Force Unit Conversion]] and [[Pressure Unit Conversion]].
