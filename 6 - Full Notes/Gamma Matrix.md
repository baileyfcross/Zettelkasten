# Gamma Matrix

Gamma matrices are matrices used to express the Dirac equation in a spacetime-covariant form. Their anticommutators equal twice the spacetime metric, a relation that ensures squaring the Dirac operator yields the relativistic second-order wave operator.

Different matrix representations are physically equivalent when they satisfy the same algebra. Products of gamma matrices organize relativistic bilinear quantities and connect spinor components to scalar, vector, and other transformation properties.
