# Scrum of Scrums

A Scrum of Scrums is a cross-team coordination event focused on dependencies, integration, and obstacles that affect more than one team. Representatives bring information and decisions back to their teams rather than reporting upward for individual control.

The event should resolve or route cross-team problems. Repeating each team's ordinary status creates meeting overhead without improving the whole game.
