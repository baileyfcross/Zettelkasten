# Cloud Critical Success Factor

A cloud critical success factor is a condition that must be satisfied for an adopted service to deliver its intended value. It converts an important requirement into a focus for design, measurement, ownership, and review.

Success factors can concern interoperability, resilience, security, support, financial viability, or process readiness. They should be specific enough to guide a decision and be tested throughout adoption rather than declared only after deployment.
