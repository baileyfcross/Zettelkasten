# RMS Voltage

Root-mean-square voltage is the DC voltage that would produce the same average power in a resistor as a varying supply. For a sinusoid, $V_{rms}=V_0/\sqrt2$ and $I_{rms}=I_0/\sqrt2$. Average AC power for an in-phase resistive load is $V_{rms}I_{rms}$.
