# Crowdsourced Research Modularity

Crowdsourced research modularity decomposes a large scientific objective into contributions that individuals or small teams can complete with limited dependence on one another. Completed modules are later combined into the larger study.

Independent replication teams illustrate the pattern: delay in one study need not halt every other team. Modularity also lets volunteers control the scale and nature of their commitment, helping close the [[Replication Incentive Gap]]. It succeeds only when interfaces, quality standards, and aggregation rules are explicit enough that separately produced modules remain comparable.
