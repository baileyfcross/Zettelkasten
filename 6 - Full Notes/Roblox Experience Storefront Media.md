# Roblox Experience Storefront Media

A Roblox experience's icon, thumbnails, and trailer form its storefront presentation. The icon often creates the first impression in a crowded discovery surface, while thumbnails and video explain the fantasy, activity, and visual quality after a player opens the listing.

Media should show recognizable gameplay and set accurate expectations rather than promise features the experience does not contain. Clear imagery, readable composition, and a consistent identity improve the path from discovery to play. [[Roblox Experience Analytics]] can reveal whether changes in presentation correspond with visits and engagement.
