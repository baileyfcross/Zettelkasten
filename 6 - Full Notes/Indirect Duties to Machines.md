# Indirect Duties to Machines

Indirect duties to machines are obligations concerning how people treat artificial systems even when the systems themselves have no rights or experiences. Abusing a humanlike robot may cultivate cruelty, distress observers, or normalize harmful patterns of behavior toward beings that resemble social partners.

On this account, the immediate beneficiary of the duty is human character or social life rather than the machine. It offers a reason to regulate treatment without first proving [[Machine Sentience]]. The approach differs from [[Relational Moral Status]], which treats the relationship with the machine itself as capable of grounding moral consideration.
