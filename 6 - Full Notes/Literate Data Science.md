# Literate Data Science

Literate data science applies literate programming to the full analytic argument: data provenance, transformations, exploration, modeling, validation, and interpretation. The report becomes an executable record rather than a detached summary.

Reproducibility still depends on available data, packages, versions, and controlled randomness.
