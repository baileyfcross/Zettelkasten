# Context as a Design Value

A context design value identifies who will play, where and when play will occur, how people will encounter the game, and why they may choose to participate. These conditions affect session length, explanation, controls, privacy, social behavior, and expectations.

Treating context as a value keeps the imagined use situation visible during iteration. A game designed for a noisy public event requires different signals and commitments from one intended for solitary, repeated play.
