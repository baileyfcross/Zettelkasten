# Auralization

Auralization renders the sound field of a source in space to simulate a binaural listening experience. It attempts to recreate how sound travels and reflects through an environment, making it useful for reverberation.

Wave-based methods solve or approximate the sound-wave equation. Ray-based methods follow paths from source to listener but simplify effects associated with wavelength. Ambisonics records a spherical directional sound field, while wave-field synthesis approximates virtual sources by combining many physical loudspeakers.

Auralization is one route to producing [[Spatial Audio]] for an [[Auditory Display]].
