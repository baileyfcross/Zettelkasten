# Rank Factorization

If an $m\times n$ matrix $A$ has rank $r$, a rank factorization writes $A=CF$, where $C$ is $m\times r$ with full column rank and $F$ is $r\times n$ with full row rank.

One construction chooses a basis of the column space for the columns of $C$; each column of $A$ then supplies one coordinate column of $F$. The factorization is not unique because an invertible $r\times r$ change of basis can be inserted between the factors.
