# Unique Semantic Identifier

A unique semantic identifier names a scientific concept or document component without relying on its display text or physical position. References can use the identifier to connect atoms to bonds, values to dictionary definitions, computations to functional forms, and results to their intended targets.

Stable identifiers make a [[Semantic Scientific Document]] internally navigable and allow separately maintained resources to share meaning. They also support validation: a reference can be checked for existence and type. Human-friendly labels may still be displayed, but the machine-readable identity should remain unambiguous when wording or document layout changes.
