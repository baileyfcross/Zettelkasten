# Epistemic Condition for Moral Responsibility

The epistemic condition for moral responsibility holds that a responsible actor must know, or be in a position where they should know, the morally relevant features and likely consequences of an action. Opaque and complex AI systems can make this knowledge difficult to obtain.

Lack of actual knowledge is not always an excuse. A designer, purchaser, or operator may have a duty to investigate foreseeable risks and understand the limits of a system before relying on it. [[Explainable AI]], testing, documentation, and professional education help actors meet this condition and identify when uncertainty is too great for deployment.
