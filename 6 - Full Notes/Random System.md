# Random System

A random system produces outcomes that are not determined in advance, although they may fall within a defined probability range. Its unpredictability comes from chance rather than from sensitivity in a deterministic rule set.

This distinguishes it from a [[Chaotic System]]. Games use [[Randomization]] to stand in for systems that are too detailed to model and to introduce uncertainty into [[Meaningful Decision|meaningful decisions]].
