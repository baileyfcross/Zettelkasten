# Clearing Denominators

Clearing denominators multiplies every term on both sides of an equation by a common multiple of the denominators. Using the least common multiple usually keeps coefficients smallest.

The method converts a fractional equation into an equivalent form without fractions. Every term must receive the multiplier, including constants and terms outside visible fractions. Denominators containing variables require restrictions because the multiplier may be zero at excluded values. [[Cross Multiplication]] is a compact special case for an equation consisting of one fraction on each side.
