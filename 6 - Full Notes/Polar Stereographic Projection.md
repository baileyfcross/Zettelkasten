# Polar Stereographic Projection

The polar stereographic projection maps the globe to a plane tangent at a pole from a projection point at the opposite pole. Parallels become concentric circles and meridians radiate as straight lines. It is conformal and makes great-circle routes nearly straight near the pole, while scale increases as the chart extends toward lower latitudes.
