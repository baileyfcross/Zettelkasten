# Depth Matte Control of Atmosphere

A grayscale depth matte can limit an effect according to apparent distance rather than a hand-drawn region alone. The book applies color and density through such a matte to stage atmosphere, then adjusts its levels or gamma to move the effect's near, middle, and far boundaries. This is a compositing control image, not necessarily a physically measured depth map. Changing its contrast can reshape where haze appears without moving the scene cards themselves. See [[2.5D Atmospheric Cards]].
