# Operational AI ROI Measurement

Operational AI return on investment compares observed changes in work and service outcomes with the full cost of building and running the system. Measures can include mean time to resolution, toil hours, recurring-incident rate, change failure rate, accepted recommendations, infrastructure, model usage, evaluation, training, and review effort.

The baseline must be captured before deployment and the comparison repeated after rollout. Counting generated summaries or model calls rewards activity rather than value; a useful result is a sustained operational improvement whose labor and risk savings exceed its platform and governance costs.
