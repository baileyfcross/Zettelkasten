# Biohybrid Gripper

A biohybrid gripper uses contractile tissue to bring flexible jaws or edges together around an object. Sustained high-frequency stimulation can keep muscle contracted and maintain the grip.

Soft living actuation may suit delicate samples, but controlled release, force calibration, fatigue, sterility, and the device's dependence on culture conditions limit practical deployment.
