# Limits to Growth

Limits to growth is a system pattern in which a [[Reinforcing Loop]] initially drives rapid expansion, but a limiting resource activates a [[Balancing Loop]]. Growth then slows into an S-shaped path or overshoots and crashes.

The pattern matters in progression and economy design because apparently stable growth can conceal a constraint that emerges only later. Recognizing the limiting stock or flow allows the designer to shape the transition intentionally.
