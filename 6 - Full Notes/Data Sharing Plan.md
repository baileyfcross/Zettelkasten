# Data Sharing Plan

A data sharing plan decides how research data will be prepared, described, licensed, deposited, and made accessible before the project produces its final publication. Early planning matters because large or expensive datasets may be impractical to reacquire and difficult to transfer after the fact.

The plan should address suitable repositories, formats, metadata, persistent links, privacy or confidentiality limits, and the intended release time. It can also specify a [[Public Domain Dedication for Research Data]] or other terms. Treating sharing as part of study design reduces the chance that legal, technical, or partnership constraints make later release impossible.
