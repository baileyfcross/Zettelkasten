# Hidden Labor of AI

The hidden labor of AI is the human work concealed by descriptions of an intelligent system as autonomous. People collect and label training examples, correct outputs, moderate harmful content, maintain infrastructure, and perform small tasks that make automated services possible.

Invisibility affects ethical judgment because the system appears cleaner and more capable when labor conditions are removed from view. Workers may be geographically distant, poorly paid, or exposed to disturbing material while users interact with a polished interface. Evaluating AI therefore requires following the full production chain and recognizing [[Digital Labor]] as part of the system.
