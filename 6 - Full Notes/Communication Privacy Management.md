# Communication Privacy Management

Communication privacy management is a theory of how people treat private information as something they own or control through disclosure rules and negotiated boundaries. Sharing creates co-owners who may have different expectations about later access and distribution.

Mobile commerce complicates these boundaries because applications, platforms, advertisers, and brokers can participate in an exchange. Privacy turbulence occurs when the parties apply incompatible rules or information crosses a boundary unexpectedly.
