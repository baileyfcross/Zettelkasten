# Coarticulation

Coarticulation is the overlap of articulatory movements for neighboring speech sounds. A sound therefore changes acoustically depending on what comes before and after it.

This context dependence makes [[Continuous Speech Recognition]] harder than matching isolated word templates. An [[Acoustic Model]] must learn how phonetic realizations vary within real sequences.
