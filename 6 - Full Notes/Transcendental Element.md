# Transcendental Element

An element of an extension is transcendental over K when no nonzero polynomial in K[x] has it as a root. It therefore satisfies no algebraic relation with coefficients in K.

The book contrasts familiar algebraic radicals with transcendental real numbers such as e and π over the rationals.
