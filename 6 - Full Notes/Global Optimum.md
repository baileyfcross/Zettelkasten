# Global Optimum

A global optimum is the best solution among every permitted solution to a problem. It differs from a [[Local Optimum]], which is best only compared with directly neighboring alternatives.

An exact algorithm may guarantee the global optimum but require impractical computation. A heuristic or [[Approximation Algorithm]] can instead seek a good solution efficiently, with the tradeoff stated through bounds or observed performance.
