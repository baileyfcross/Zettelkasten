# Limited Agent Rollout

A limited agent rollout exposes a new assistant or integration to a controlled user group, traffic share, or task scope before broad release. The smaller boundary limits the impact of routing mistakes, permission problems, unsafe responses, and misunderstood workflows while producing realistic evidence that predeployment tests cannot fully supply.

The rollout should have entry criteria, monitored quality and operational metrics, feedback channels, incident ownership, and explicit expansion or rollback rules. Logs and user corrections should feed evaluation datasets. Gradual exposure is valuable only when observations lead to decisions rather than functioning as an indefinite unlabeled production state.
