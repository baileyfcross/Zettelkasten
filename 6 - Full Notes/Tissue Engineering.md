# Tissue Engineering

Tissue engineering combines living cells, supporting materials, fabrication, and conditioning to restore or replace damaged biological tissue. The engineered construct must reproduce enough structure and function to survive, integrate, and improve the target condition.

Different tissues impose different requirements for geometry, stiffness, electrical behavior, mechanical loading, vascularization, and cell source.
