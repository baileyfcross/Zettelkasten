# Slowly Adapting Mechanoreceptor

A slowly adapting mechanoreceptor continues firing while the skin remains indented or stretched. Its response preserves information about ongoing pressure and deformation instead of signaling only the transition into contact.

Slowly adapting units contribute to the perception of edges, coarse texture, shape, motion direction, and joint movement produced by skin stretch. Small-field units provide fine local detail, while other units respond over larger regions. This sustained channel complements [[Fast-Adapting Mechanoreceptor|fast-adapting receptors]], which emphasize vibration and changes in contact.
