# Service Delivery Cloud Usage Model

A service delivery cloud usage model gives business units direct access to providers selected from a centrally maintained [[Cloud Service Catalog]]. The business units manage their own service agreements and bills, while central IT establishes choices and performs integration.

The model loosens the operational coupling that can drive [[Shadow IT]]. It balances local agility with shared standards, but depends on the catalog and oversight keeping unsuitable services outside the approved set.
