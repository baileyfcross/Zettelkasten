# Support of a Module

The support Supp(M) of an R-module is the set of prime ideals p for which the localization M_p is nonzero. It identifies where the module remains visible on the ring's spectrum.

For a finitely generated module, support consists exactly of primes containing Ann(M). Every [[Associated Prime]] belongs to the support, and every support prime contains an associated prime.
