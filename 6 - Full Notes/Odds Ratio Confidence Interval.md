# Odds Ratio Confidence Interval

An odds ratio confidence interval exponentiates the lower and upper confidence limits of a logistic coefficient. It communicates both the plausible magnitude of the association and the precision of the estimate on the odds-ratio scale.

An interval that includes one does not support a nonzero association at the corresponding two-sided level. Reporting the interval is generally more informative than reporting only a p-value because it shows which effect sizes remain compatible with the model.
