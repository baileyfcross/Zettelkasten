# Industrial Internet of Things

The Industrial Internet of Things connects machines, sensors, software, communications, and analytics within business and infrastructure operations. It makes equipment observable and controllable as part of a larger process, supporting factories, farms, utilities, transportation, health facilities, and supply chains.

Industrial IoT emphasizes operational outcomes such as uptime, safety, quality, resource use, and end-to-end visibility. Its communications can connect machines to other machines, people, or handheld devices. Because decisions may control expensive or dangerous equipment, dependable data, low-latency processing, security, and integration with legacy systems are essential parts of the architecture.
