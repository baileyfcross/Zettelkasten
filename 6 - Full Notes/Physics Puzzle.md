# Physics Puzzle

A physics puzzle asks the player to use a simulated physical system to move objects, redirect forces, or reach a target. The available solution space emerges from properties such as momentum, gravity, collision, friction, and constraints rather than from a list of scripted answers.

Consistency is central because the player learns by predicting the simulation. The design should expose enough causal feedback for failed experiments to improve that model. Reliable physics middleware makes these puzzles practical, but the designer must still constrain the environment so the possibility space remains understandable.
