# Unity Prefab Override

A Unity prefab override is a property or structural difference recorded on a [[Unity Prefab Instance]] relative to its source prefab. Overrides let one placement vary without discarding the benefits of a shared template.

Useful overrides are intentional, such as a different color, position, or gameplay value. Accidental overrides make maintenance harder because they can mask later prefab changes. Unity therefore exposes override information so an author can inspect differences, apply a change back to the prefab, or revert the instance to inherited values.
