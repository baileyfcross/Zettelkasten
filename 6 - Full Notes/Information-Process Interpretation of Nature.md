# Information-Process Interpretation of Nature

An information-process interpretation describes a natural phenomenon through representations, states, transformations, and exchanges. It can make processes in biology, physics, chemistry, and cognition available for computational modeling.

The interpretation is productive when it reproduces observed behavior or generates testable predictions. It should not be confused with proof that the natural system is literally a digital computer.
