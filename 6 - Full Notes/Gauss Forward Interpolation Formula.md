# Gauss Forward Interpolation Formula

Gauss's forward formula is a central-difference interpolation formula for an argument just to the right of a chosen central value. It alternates differences drawn from the center and entries immediately above it in the difference table.

Because the origin is near the target, its normalized displacement remains small and the terms often decrease more rapidly than in an endpoint formula. For a target on the opposite side of the center, the [[Gauss Backward Interpolation Formula]] is the natural counterpart.
