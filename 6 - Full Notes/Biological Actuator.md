# Biological Actuator

A biological actuator converts cellular energy into mechanical force and motion. Engineered muscle is attractive because it produces high force for its volume and responds to electrical, neural, chemical, or optical stimulation.

Unlike an electric motor, living muscle needs nutrients and compatible surroundings but can remodel itself in response to loading and damage.
