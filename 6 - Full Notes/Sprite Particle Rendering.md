# Sprite Particle Rendering

Instead of rendering heavy volumes for every particle, an image or footage sprite can be attached to each simulated point. The book uses cloud-image sprites on a fountain-like particle path to make rocket exhaust and a spiraling missile trail. Their speed and visual detail make them useful when the camera view preserves the billboard illusion. A camera move that reveals the cards' flatness or an effect whose debris must spin in volume needs [[Geometry-Instanced Particle Debris]] instead.
