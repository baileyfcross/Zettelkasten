# Newton-Cotes Formula

A Newton-Cotes formula integrates a polynomial interpolating function values at equally spaced points. The number of subintervals and retained polynomial degree determine the weights.

Special cases include the [[Trapezoidal Rule]], [[Simpson One-Third Rule]], [[Simpson Three-Eighths Rule]], [[Boole Rule]], and [[Weddle Rule]]. Composite forms apply a low-degree rule repeatedly, avoiding one unstable high-degree interpolant across a long interval.
