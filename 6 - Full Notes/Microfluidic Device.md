# Microfluidic Device

A microfluidic device manipulates microliter-scale or smaller fluid volumes through networks of microscopic channels, valves, mixers, and pumps. Precisely controlled flow can deliver different media, cells, chemicals, and mechanical stimuli to separate regions.

The small scale suits tissue models and screening, although it does not directly solve fabrication of large replacement tissues.
