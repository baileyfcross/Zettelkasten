# Limit of a Sequence

A sequence has limit $L$ when its terms can be made as close to $L$ as desired by going sufficiently far along the sequence. The limit describes long-run behavior and need not be one of the terms. Convergent approximations in the [[Method of Exhaustion]] use this idea to determine a geometric quantity.
