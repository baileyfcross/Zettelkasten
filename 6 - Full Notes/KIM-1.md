# KIM-1

The KIM-1 was a single-board development system built around the MOS 6502. It offered a keypad, small display, memory, and interfaces sufficient for learning and experimentation.

MOS expected an engineering market, but hobbyists bought it as a compact computer. The response demonstrated demand for accessible systems below the price and complexity of established minicomputers.
