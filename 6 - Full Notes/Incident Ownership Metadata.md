# Incident Ownership Metadata

Incident ownership metadata identifies the team responsible for a service, workload, cluster, or business capability. It can be attached through infrastructure tags, deployment labels, catalog entries, or other semantic context and carried into logs, metrics, traces, and events.

Without ownership, newly detected incidents accumulate in a fallback operations queue and increase noise. With it, routing and access control can be automated, and an incident explanation can name the people able to validate the cause or perform the remediation.
