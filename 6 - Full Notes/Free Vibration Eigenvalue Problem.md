# Free Vibration Eigenvalue Problem

The free vibration eigenvalue problem balances elastic restoring forces against inertia with no external forcing. A nontrivial displacement vector exists only when the determinant of the stiffness matrix minus squared frequency times the mass matrix vanishes.

Solving the determinant condition yields natural frequencies; substituting each value back gives its mode shape. The finite element matrices turn the continuous vibration problem into a generalized algebraic eigenproblem.
