# Shortest Vector Problem

The shortest vector problem asks for a nonzero lattice vector having minimum norm relative to a supplied basis. Its computational form asks for that minimum vector or distance, while its decisional form asks whether the minimum distance lies at or below a given threshold.

Two-dimensional pictures make the task look like a nearest-dot search, but cryptographic lattices can have thousands of simultaneous components. That dimensional growth is what turns the geometric definition into a hardness assumption. The source connects a polynomial-factorization form of this problem to the [[NTRU Cryptosystem]] and treats it as a foundation for [[Lattice-Based Cryptography]].
