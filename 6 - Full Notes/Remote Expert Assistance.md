# Remote Expert Assistance

Remote expert assistance lets a knowledgeable participant observe another person's task environment and provide guidance from a different location. Video, audio, pointing, annotations, or virtual demonstrations can place instructions directly in the worker's view.

The expert needs enough spatial context to refer to real components accurately, while the local participant needs guidance that does not obscure the work. The system should make authorship and timing of remote cues clear.
