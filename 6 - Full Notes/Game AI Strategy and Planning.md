# Game AI Strategy and Planning

Game AI strategy and planning selects actions over a longer horizon than immediate movement or local behavior. It may allocate resources, choose objectives, coordinate units, or evaluate candidate plans against changing world state.

The system does not need exhaustive optimality to appear intelligent. Real-time strategy opponents often combine authored priorities, evaluation functions, and limited search so decisions arrive within the game's computational budget.
