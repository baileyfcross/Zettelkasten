# Dynamic Narrative

Dynamic narrative is story created through the course of play. It can arise from a player's route through authored branches, from simulation and system state, or from cooperation and conflict among human players.

The designer supplies conditions rather than every resulting sequence. A family history formed in a simulation or a dramatic comeback in a competitive match can be narratively meaningful without having been written as a fixed plot. Dynamic narrative is therefore a direct bridge between [[Emergent Game Narrative]] and the concrete systems that enable it.
