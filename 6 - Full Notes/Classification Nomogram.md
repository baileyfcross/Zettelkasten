# Classification Nomogram

A classification nomogram displays how predictor values contribute to a class score or probability along aligned scales. A reader traces an observation's values, accumulates their contributions, and maps the total to the prediction.

The form is especially useful for additive models because it exposes both direction and magnitude of each feature's effect. Complex interactions are harder to represent without losing the nomogram's direct readability.
