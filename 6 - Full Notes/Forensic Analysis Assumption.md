# Forensic Analysis Assumption

A forensic analysis assumption is a condition that must hold for an image test to produce an interpretable result. A geometric method may require identifiable parallel lines, a sensor method may require unsaturated pixels, and a compression method may depend on a particular prior encoding history.

Analysts must state and test these conditions before interpreting an anomaly. If an assumption is violated, a negative result may be meaningless and a positive result may be a false alarm. Careful application therefore includes judging whether the photograph is suitable for the technique, not merely running the detector.
