# High-Missingness Variable

A high-missingness variable has too few observed values to support reliable use without strong assumptions. Its apparent relationship with the target may reflect who was measured rather than the quantity itself.

The choice to exclude, retain, or impute it should consider the missingness mechanism, intended use, and validation performance.
