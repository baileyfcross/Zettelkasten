# Frequent Pattern Mining

Frequent pattern mining finds structures whose support exceeds a chosen threshold. Apriori explores candidates through downward-closure logic, while FP-growth and vertical methods reorganize the search to avoid costly candidate generation.

Frequency ensures repeated evidence but not relevance to a target. Very low thresholds cause pattern explosion, whereas high thresholds can discard rare but important structures.
