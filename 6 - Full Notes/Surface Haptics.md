# Surface Haptics

Surface haptics creates virtual tactile effects on a physical flat surface such as a touch screen. Rather than building mechanically raised features, a display changes the lateral resistance felt by a moving fingertip so visual buttons, sliders, edges, or textures gain a touchable quality.

The main variable-friction approaches are [[Ultrasonic Friction Modulation]], which lowers friction, and [[Electrovibration]], which raises it. Both require tracking the finger and changing the surface signal as it moves. Moisture, skin structure, contact force, and motion alter the result, so rendering must account for the user–surface interaction.
