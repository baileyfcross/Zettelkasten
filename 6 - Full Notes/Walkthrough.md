# Walkthrough

A walkthrough is an informal review in which an author explains a small code change and one or two reviewers read it to decide whether the change is correct. It is suited to a narrowly scoped correction rather than a new method or a large block of code.

If the reviewer finds a problem, the author revises the change and repeats the check. The technique supplies a second perspective with little ceremony, but larger or riskier work needs the preparation and broader viewpoints of a [[Peer Code Review]] or [[Code Inspection]].
