# Blender Workspace Presets

A Blender workspace stores an arrangement of editors suited to a kind of work. Built-in presets are grouped around general 3D work, 2D animation, sculpting, visual effects, and video editing, and new workspaces can be added from the workspace tabs.

Choosing the right preset reduces repeated setup. A modeling workspace can expose mesh-editing tools, while a compositing or video-editing workspace can foreground the editors needed near the end of production.
