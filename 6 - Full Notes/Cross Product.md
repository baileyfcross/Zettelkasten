# Cross Product

The cross product $u\times v$ is a vector perpendicular to the plane spanned by three-dimensional vectors $u$ and $v$, with direction set by the right-hand rule. Its magnitude is $\|u\|\|v\|\sin\theta$, equal to the area of their parallelogram. It vanishes for parallel vectors.
