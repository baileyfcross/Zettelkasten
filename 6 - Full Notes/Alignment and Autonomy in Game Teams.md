# Alignment and Autonomy in Game Teams

A game team needs alignment on design values, constraints, and responsibilities while preserving autonomy in how members solve their assigned problems. Alignment without autonomy creates a bottleneck; autonomy without alignment produces incompatible local decisions.

Shared criteria and clear interfaces let specialists exercise judgment without asking permission for every detail. Regular playable reviews reveal when independent work is drifting away from the whole experience.
