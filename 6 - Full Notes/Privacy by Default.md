# Privacy by Default

Privacy by default configures a system so that a person receives meaningful privacy protection without having to discover and change permissive settings. Only data needed for the stated purpose should be collected, exposed, retained, or made broadly accessible by default.

The approach complements [[Privacy by Design]] by turning an architectural commitment into the initial behavior experienced by users.
