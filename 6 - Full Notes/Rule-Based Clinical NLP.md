# Rule-Based Clinical NLP

Rule-based clinical NLP uses dictionaries, patterns, and explicit linguistic or domain rules to identify and interpret information in medical text. A rule may combine a concept term with nearby context cues, section position, or grammatical structure.

Rules are inspectable and can encode specialist knowledge with little labeled data. They can also become brittle when documentation style changes or many exceptions accumulate. Their evaluation should therefore include reports from different authors and settings, and a hybrid system may combine their precision with statistical methods that generalize across surface variation.
