# Captive Power Plant

A captive power plant is an on-site installation sized to meet the electricity needs of a particular industry or service organization. The source gives interruption-sensitive factories, hospitals, and hotels as examples. Local generation avoids a long transmission line and some associated reliability losses, but the operator must supply its own plant capacity, fuel, and maintenance. Diesel and small thermal plants are possible arrangements.
