# AI Safety Certification

AI safety certification is a formal process for showing that an AI system meets specified safety requirements before or during use. Certification can include testing, documentation, independent review, operating limits, and recurring evaluation when the system or its environment changes.

The model draws on safety-critical industries where visible investigation and certification infrastructures support public trust. A certificate is only as strong as its standards, evidence, independence, and enforcement. Learning systems also make one-time approval insufficient when later data or updates can change behavior. Certification should therefore be connected to monitoring and [[Algorithmic Auditability]].
