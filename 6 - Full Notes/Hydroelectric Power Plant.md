# Hydroelectric Power Plant

A hydroelectric plant stores water at height and conducts it through penstocks toward a turbine. Water's potential energy becomes directed flow and turbine rotation, which drives an electrical generator. The source notes that the arrangement depends on a suitable site and water resource and can require a dam. It has a mechanical conversion stage despite using renewable input energy.
