# Texture Paint Brush Texture Mapping

A brush texture modulates the color or strength within a texture-paint dab. It can introduce grain, stamps, procedural variation, or repeated surface detail that would be slow to draw by hand.

View Plane, Tiled, 3D, Random, and Stencil mapping attach that texture to different coordinate frames. Offset, scale, and rotation determine placement, so the same source image can behave like a screen pattern, object-space material, random dab, or positioned stencil.
