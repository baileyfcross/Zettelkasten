# Unsupervised Feature Relevance

A feature is relevant for unsupervised learning when it helps expose structure in the observations even though no class labels identify the desired groups. Relevance may mean preserving local neighborhoods, separating latent components, or supporting a compact and stable partition.

The difficulty is circular: clusters are used to judge features, while features determine the clusters that can be found. Practical methods therefore state an explicit structural criterion and compare candidate subsets or weights under that criterion.
