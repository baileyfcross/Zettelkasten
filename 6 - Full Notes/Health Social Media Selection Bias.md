# Health Social Media Selection Bias

Health social media selection bias occurs because the people, events, and experiences visible online differ systematically from the population of interest. Platform demographics, internet access, willingness to disclose, posting frequency, and moderation all influence what is observed.

More data does not remove this bias. A large collection can estimate the behavior of its contributors very precisely while still misrepresenting patients who are absent or quiet. Responsible analysis defines the observed population, uses user-level rather than post-level counts when appropriate, and compares findings with external health data.
