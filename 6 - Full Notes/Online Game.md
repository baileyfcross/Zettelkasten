# Online Game

An online game is a multiplayer distributed game whose participants' machines communicate over a network. Online describes the connection arrangement rather than a particular genre.

Separate screens allow private information and individual viewpoints, but the design must handle transmission delay, arrival, departure, failure, security, communication, and the social consequences of remote play.
