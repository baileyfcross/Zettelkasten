# Component Team

A component team owns a shared subsystem or technical component used by multiple features. It concentrates scarce expertise and provides a coherent foundation, but its output may not be independently valuable to a player.

Because feature teams depend on it, component work needs transparent ordering and close integration. Otherwise queues and local optimization delay the whole game.
