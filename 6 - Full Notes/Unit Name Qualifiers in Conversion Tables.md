# Unit Name Qualifiers in Conversion Tables

A familiar unit name may refer to more than one defined scale, so a conversion table must preserve its qualifier. Pryor's alphabetical catalog distinguishes U.S. and Imperial gallons, U.S. dry and liquid quarts, ordinary and U.S. Survey feet, several British thermal unit definitions, computer and printer picas, and different kinds of year. Choosing a factor from the wrong variant changes the result without producing an obvious dimensional error: both ratios may still cancel correctly. The qualifier is therefore part of the unit, not optional decoration. See [[Unit Conversion Factor]] and [[Volume Unit Scaling]].
