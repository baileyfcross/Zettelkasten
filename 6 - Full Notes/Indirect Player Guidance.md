# Indirect Player Guidance

Indirect player guidance influences behavior without presenting itself as an instruction. Constraints, goals, interface shape, environmental composition, sound, avatar appearance, and non-player characters make some actions or destinations feel more natural than others.

The method preserves a sense of agency because players perceive themselves as choosing. It works only when cues are legible enough to influence attention and expectation. [[Playtesting]] with newcomers is essential because a cue that feels obvious to its designer may be invisible, ambiguous, or point toward an unintended interpretation.
