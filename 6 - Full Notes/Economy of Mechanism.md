# Economy of Mechanism

Economy of mechanism is the security principle that a design should remain as small and simple as practical. Fewer mechanisms and interactions make behavior easier to understand, test, and review.

Complexity creates places for defects and hidden assumptions to accumulate. Simplicity does not mean omitting necessary controls; it means avoiding needless machinery that enlarges the attack and failure surface.
