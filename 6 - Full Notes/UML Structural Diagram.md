# UML Structural Diagram

A UML structural diagram describes the relatively stable organization of a system: its classes, objects, components, packages, deployment nodes, or other elements and their relationships. It answers what exists and how those parts are arranged.

The [[UML Class Diagram]] is the structural view most directly connected to object-oriented code. Behavioral diagrams complement it by showing how the represented parts participate in actions and interactions.
