# Differential Analyzer

A differential analyzer is an analog computer that solves differential equations by linking mechanical integrators. Vannevar Bush developed influential versions at MIT that represented mathematical relationships through rotating disks and shafts.

The machine supported scientific calculation and wartime firing tables. Its physical modeling approach also shaped proposals for electronic computers, even as digital numerical methods ultimately became dominant.
