# Audio Reverb

Audio reverb is a digital signal processing effect that simulates the many delayed reflections produced by an acoustic environment. Its decay, density, and tonal qualities can make the same sound suggest a small room, large hall, or open space.

Games can associate reverb settings with regions and blend them as the listener moves. The effect communicates environmental character without modeling every physical reflection.
