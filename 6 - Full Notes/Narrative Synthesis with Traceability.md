# Narrative Synthesis with Traceability

Narrative synthesis assembles selected text units into a larger storyline rather than only breaking a story apart for analysis. The book proposes controlling the target length, using mapped transitions to make adjoining passages coherent, and retaining a route back to each component text.

That traceability can matter for revision, interactive branches, and attribution or digital rights. A geometric or hierarchical support tool can suggest structure, but authors still judge whether the assembled sequence carries an intelligible dramatic meaning for its audience.
