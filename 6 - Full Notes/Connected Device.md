# Connected Device

A connected device is an object that exchanges data over the Internet or a private network. Connectivity does not by itself make the object part of the [[Internet of Things]]: it may communicate only within a closed proprietary system. It becomes part of a broader IoT ecosystem when its data and behavior can participate in services involving other devices, applications, or data sources.

The interface may be a conventional app, but it can also be a switch, voice command, wearable, smart television, or an automated rule. This diversity makes [[Internet of Things Interoperability]] important because devices that can connect technically may still be unable to understand or control one another.
