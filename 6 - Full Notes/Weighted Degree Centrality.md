# Weighted Degree Centrality

Weighted degree centrality sums the weights of all edges incident to a node. In a [[Tag Co-Occurrence Network]], it measures the total volume of a tag’s pairwise co-occurrences rather than merely the number of distinct neighboring tags.

The measure often tracks raw popularity, so it may reveal little beyond frequency. Comparing it with [[Betweenness Centrality]] or [[Eigenvector Centrality]] can distinguish widely used nodes from bridges or nodes connected to other influential nodes.
