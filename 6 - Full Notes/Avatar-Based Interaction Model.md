# Avatar-Based Interaction Model

An avatar-based interaction model lets the player influence the world through one represented character, vehicle, or entity. The player's reach is limited to the avatar's location and permitted actions.

The interface must support movement, orientation, object use, and awareness from that situated position. Camera and navigation choices are tightly coupled because the player often cannot see the whole world at once.
