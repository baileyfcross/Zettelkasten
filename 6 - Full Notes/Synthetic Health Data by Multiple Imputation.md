# Synthetic Health Data by Multiple Imputation

Synthetic health data by multiple imputation replaces sensitive values with draws from statistical models fitted to the original records. The released dataset preserves modeled relationships without publishing the original patient-level values directly.

Utility depends on whether the imputation models capture the relationships users need, while privacy depends on how much individual information the fitted models and draws reveal. A synthetic dataset can look realistic yet distort rare groups or downstream coefficients. It should be evaluated with the planned analyses and with explicit disclosure-risk tests.
