# Particle Interactions and Behaviors

A simple particle may move according to emission and forces without considering its neighbors. A behavioral particle system can additionally use collision, self-interaction, attraction, avoidance, deflection, or crowding to produce organized motion such as a flock or swarm. The extra relationships matter when the effect depends on elements affecting one another rather than merely passing through space. The book treats these as controls layered on top of basic [[Particle Motion Forces and Lifetime]], not as a replacement for emitter timing or rendering choices.
