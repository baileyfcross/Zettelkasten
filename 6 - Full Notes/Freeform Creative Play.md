# Freeform Creative Play

Freeform creative play lets players create within a supported domain with few game-imposed rules. The software, interface, hardware, and available materials still set boundaries even when there is no required outcome.

The pleasure comes from making, exploring possibilities, and sharing results rather than overcoming a prescribed challenge. Useful tools, reversibility, and legible constraints matter more than a victory condition.
