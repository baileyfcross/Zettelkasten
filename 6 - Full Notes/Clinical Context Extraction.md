# Clinical Context Extraction

Clinical context extraction determines how a concept mention is qualified in a report. A disease or finding may be negated, historical, hypothetical, experienced by a family member, or stated with uncertainty rather than asserted as a current patient condition.

This context changes the analytic meaning of the same words. Rules can search around a mention for cues, while learned methods can model wider dependencies. The output should keep the concept and its modifiers together so an [[Electronic Phenotyping Algorithm]] does not turn “no evidence of pneumonia” into a positive case.
