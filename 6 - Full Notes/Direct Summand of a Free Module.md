# Direct Summand of a Free Module

A module P is a direct summand of a free module when there is another module Q such that P ⊕ Q is free. This means P can be included in a free module and recovered by a projection.

The condition characterizes [[Projective Module]]s. It permits projective modules to inherit many free-module properties without requiring them to possess a basis themselves.
