# Polynomial

A polynomial is an algebraic expression formed from constants and variables raised to nonnegative integer powers, combined through addition, subtraction, and multiplication. In one variable it can be arranged as

$$a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_1x+a_0.$$

Its components are terms, and its highest nonzero exponent determines the [[Polynomial Degree]]. Polynomials can be expanded, combined, multiplied, divided with a possible remainder, and rewritten through [[Polynomial Factorization]]. Negative powers or variable denominators instead produce a [[Rational Expression]].
