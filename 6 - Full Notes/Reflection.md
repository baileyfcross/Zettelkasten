# Reflection

Reflection occurs when a wave returns from a boundary rather than transmitting completely. The incident ray, reflected ray, and surface normal lie in one plane, and the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection. Depending on boundary conditions, the reflected disturbance may also undergo a phase reversal.
