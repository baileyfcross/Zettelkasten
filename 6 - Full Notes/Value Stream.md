# Value Stream

A value stream is the complete sequence through which an idea becomes value delivered to a user. A value-stream map records both active stages and the waiting between them, revealing where elapsed time actually accumulates.

Improving one stage may not improve delivery if another queue dominates [[Lead Time]]. The whole stream, including approval, testing, deployment, and measurement, is the unit of optimization.
