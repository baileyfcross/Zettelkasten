# Checksum

A checksum is a value computed from data and later recomputed or compared to detect whether the data changed. The sender or storage system supplies both the data and an expected value, and the receiver checks whether its result agrees.

Checksums can reveal corruption from storage or a noisy transmission channel and can contribute to integrity checking. The strength and trustworthiness of the scheme depend on its algorithm and on protecting the expected value from the same unauthorized modification.
