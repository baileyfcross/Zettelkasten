# Polynomial Root Adjoining Theorem

The polynomial root adjoining theorem states that every irreducible polynomial over a field K has a root in some extension field. One construction uses the quotient K[x]/⟨p(x)⟩, where the coset of x becomes a root.

This turns a desired polynomial relation into field arithmetic.
