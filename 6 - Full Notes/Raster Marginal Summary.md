# Raster Marginal Summary

A raster marginal summary aggregates cell values along rows or columns and plots the result beside a [[Quantitative Raster Map]]. It reveals broad variation by latitude or longitude that may be difficult to isolate in the full two-dimensional pattern. The summary function may be a mean, median, or another statistic, and missing cells must be handled consistently. Margins reduce spatial detail, so they complement rather than replace the map.
