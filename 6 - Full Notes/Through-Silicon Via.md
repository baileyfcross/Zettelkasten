# Through-Silicon Via

A through-silicon via, or TSV, is a vertical conductor that connects dies in a three-dimensional integrated circuit. Stacking shortens global interconnects and can integrate dies built with different processes, but critical buses then depend on the electrical and mechanical quality of these vertical links.

Fabrication and bonding can introduce voids, pad misalignment, or surface contamination. Those defects reduce effective conductive area, raise resistance or current density, and make TSVs a central target for [[Through-Silicon Via Electromigration]] analysis.
