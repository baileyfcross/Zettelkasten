# Law of Sines

For a triangle with sides $a,b,c$ opposite angles $A,B,C$, the law of sines states

$$\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}.$$

It solves triangles when an angle-side opposite pair is known. In the ambiguous side-side-angle case, the sine value may correspond to two supplementary angles.
