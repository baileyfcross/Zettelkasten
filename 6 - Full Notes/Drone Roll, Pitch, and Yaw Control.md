# Drone Roll, Pitch, and Yaw Control

Roll and pitch arise when rotor thrust is made unequal across the corresponding body axes, tilting the total thrust vector. Yaw arises from an imbalance in the opposing rotor reaction torques. Horizontal acceleration requires a deliberate tilt so part of thrust acts sideways, followed by an opposite tilt as the vehicle approaches the desired position.
