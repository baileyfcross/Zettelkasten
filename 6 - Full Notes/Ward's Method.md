# Ward's Method

Ward's method merges the two clusters that cause the smallest increase in within-cluster error sum of squares. It treats the loss of information from a merge as the objective to minimize.

The greedy rule often produces compact, roughly spherical clusters. Like other agglomerative methods, it cannot undo an earlier merge even if a later global comparison suggests a different structure.
