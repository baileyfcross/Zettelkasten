# GPS Master Control Station

The GPS master control station is the central operational site that combines observations from the tracking network into estimates of satellite orbit and clock state. It plans updates, monitors constellation health, and directs uploads through ground antennas. Backup control facilities reduce dependence on the primary site, and newer satellites can maintain useful predictions longer between uploads, improving continuity if ground contact is interrupted.
