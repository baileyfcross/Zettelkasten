# Fusion Coulomb Barrier

Positively charged nuclei repel one another electrically, so fusion reactants must acquire enough kinetic energy to approach closely and react. The source treats this as a central reason terrestrial fusion needs extremely high plasma temperature. Overcoming the barrier is necessary but not sufficient: the fuel also must be confined long enough and at enough density to release useful net energy.
