# Cloud-Enabled Business Scalability

Cloud-enabled business scalability is the ability to expand a product, service, or organization rapidly by obtaining computing, information, and automated processes without building every supporting system first. Network delivery also gives a small organization access to geographically broad markets.

Technical capacity is only one constraint. Business processes, support, security, supplier relationships, and revenue must scale with demand or the cloud merely moves the bottleneck elsewhere.
