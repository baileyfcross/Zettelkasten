# Kelley-Salisbury Method

The Kelley-Salisbury method iteratively estimates standardized multiple-regression weights from a predictor correlation matrix and a vector of predictor-criterion correlations. It adjusts the coefficient associated with the largest residual validity until the trial validities converge.

The procedure is systematic and easy to program. In the book's treatment, it can obtain a solution even when predictors are statistically dependent.
