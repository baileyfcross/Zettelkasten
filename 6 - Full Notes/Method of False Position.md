# Method of False Position

The method of false position, or regula falsi, is a [[Bracketing Method]] that replaces the graph between two opposite-sign endpoints by a secant. The secant's intercept becomes the next root approximation.

After evaluating the function there, the endpoint having the same sign is replaced so the interval continues to bracket a root. The method converges linearly and often improves more quickly than [[Bisection Method]], although one endpoint can remain fixed for many iterations.
