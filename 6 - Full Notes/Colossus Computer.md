# Colossus Computer

Colossus was an electronic binary machine built at Bletchley Park to process encrypted German teleprinter traffic. Vacuum tubes provided high-speed storage and logical processing, while configurable circuits adapted it to codebreaking tasks.

Its focus on text rather than numerical arithmetic complicates claims about the first computer. Wartime secrecy also kept its design from public histories for decades.
