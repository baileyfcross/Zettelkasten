# AI Software Bill of Materials

An AI software bill of materials records the models, packages, libraries, and other supplied components that contribute to an AI system. It gives security, compliance, operators, and customers an inventory for assessing exposure when a vulnerability, malicious package, or licensing concern is discovered.

CycloneDX emphasizes vulnerability identification, while SPDX emphasizes licensing and software-supply-chain information. An AI inventory extends conventional SBOM practice to model artifacts and should be regenerated, timestamped, stored, and connected to continuous scanning rather than treated as a one-time report.
