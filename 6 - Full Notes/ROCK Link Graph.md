# ROCK Link Graph

A ROCK link graph has one vertex for every record and an edge between two vertices when they have at least one common-neighbor link. The similarity threshold controls the graph's density.

Raising the threshold makes neighbor relations rarer, often increasing the number of connected components. The graph supplies the structure used during agglomerative merging.
