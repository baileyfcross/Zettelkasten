# Variable Role

A variable role records how a column participates in analysis, such as identifier, input, target, risk, or ignored field. Separating role from storage class prevents a numeric identifier from being mistaken for a quantitative predictor.

Role metadata makes model formulas, exclusions, and downstream scoring more systematic.
