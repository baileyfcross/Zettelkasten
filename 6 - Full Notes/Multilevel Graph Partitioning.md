# Multilevel Graph Partitioning

Multilevel graph partitioning repeatedly contracts vertices or edges to create a hierarchy of smaller graphs, partitions the coarsest graph, and then projects and refines the solution while uncoarsening.

Coarsening makes a global cut problem manageable and can reveal large-scale structure before local details. Contraction must preserve important connectivity, and refinement is needed because coarse decisions create boundary errors at finer levels.
