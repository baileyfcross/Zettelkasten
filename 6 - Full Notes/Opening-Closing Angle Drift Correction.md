# Opening-Closing Angle Drift Correction

After the halfway point, returning to the original track before proceeding is inefficient. The pilot estimates the opening angle from the departure track and the closing angle from the present position to the destination, then combines them into the heading correction. The method aims directly at the endpoint while compensating for the accumulated displacement.
