# Machine Sentience

Machine sentience is the hypothetical capacity of an artificial system to have subjective experiences, including pleasure or suffering. If a machine were sentient, its experiences could support [[Machine Moral Patiency]] independently of whether it resembled a human or could explain itself.

Behavior alone makes the question difficult because a machine may convincingly display pain, attachment, or preference through programmed responses. The ethical problem is therefore epistemic as well as metaphysical: people must decide how much uncertainty is acceptable before granting consideration. Current emotional reactions to robots do not prove sentience, but they show why the issue can become socially important before it is scientifically resolved.
