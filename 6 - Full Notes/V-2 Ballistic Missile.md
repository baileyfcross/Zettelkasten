# V-2 Ballistic Missile

The V-2 was a German long-range ballistic missile and the first large liquid-propellant rocket to fly beyond the atmosphere. As a weapon it was expensive and strategically ineffective, but its engine, guidance, production, and flight experience represented a revolutionary step toward the intercontinental missile and space booster.

Its technical achievement was inseparable from the Nazi war program and coerced underground production. After Germany's defeat, the United States and Soviet Union captured hardware, documents, and personnel, then used them to establish postwar test programs. The V-2 thus became a direct bridge between wartime weapons development and early [[Sounding Rocket|rocket-borne science]].
