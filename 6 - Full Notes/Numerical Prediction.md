# Numerical Prediction

Numerical prediction estimates a quantitative outcome such as visits, votes, revenue, or an influence score. Unlike ranking or classification, the distance between predicted and observed values is meaningful.

Correlation-based evaluation measures whether both values move together, while error-based evaluation measures their direct disagreement. A model can correlate strongly yet still be systematically miscalibrated, so the two views answer different questions.
