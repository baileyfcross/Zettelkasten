# Model-Free Occlusion

Model-free occlusion estimates which real image regions should cover virtual content without relying on a complete prebuilt geometric model of the environment. Image segmentation, depth observations, or motion evidence can supply a visibility mask at runtime.

The approach supports changing or previously unknown scenes, but it inherits uncertainty from the observed imagery. Stable segmentation and temporal coherence are necessary to keep the composite boundary believable.
