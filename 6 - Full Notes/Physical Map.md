# Physical Map

A physical map depicts landscape and natural features such as terrain, water, and relief. In the source's layered construction, elevation and sea-depth rasters combine with administrative boundaries, labels, and other context. [[Hill Shading]] can make landform visible, but every layer must share compatible coordinates and an intentional visual hierarchy. The result explains the physical setting rather than encoding one thematic statistic.
