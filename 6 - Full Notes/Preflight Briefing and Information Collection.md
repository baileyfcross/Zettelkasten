# Preflight Briefing and Information Collection

A flight briefing reviews the route, weather, NOTAMs, aerodrome conditions, aircraft state, planned exercises, takeoff and landing details, and relevant skills before departure. Longer flights may require preparation days in advance and information about fuel, fees, services, medical access, and alternates. The debrief afterward closes the learning and accountability loop.
