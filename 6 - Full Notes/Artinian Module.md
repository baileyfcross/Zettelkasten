# Artinian Module

An Artinian module satisfies the [[Descending Chain Condition]] on submodules. Every decreasing sequence of submodules eventually becomes stationary.

A module has finite [[Module Length]] exactly when it is both Artinian and Noetherian. Over a Noetherian ring, an Artinian module has finitely many associated primes, all of which are maximal.
