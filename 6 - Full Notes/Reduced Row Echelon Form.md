# Reduced Row Echelon Form

Reduced row echelon form adds to [[Row Echelon Form]] the requirement that every leading one be the only nonzero entry in its column.

Every matrix has a unique reduced row echelon form, although different sequences of [[Elementary Row Operation|elementary row operations]] may reach it. The result makes basic variables and free variables directly visible.
