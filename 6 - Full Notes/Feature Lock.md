# Feature Lock

Feature lock is the point after which no new game features may be introduced. The remaining schedule is reserved for completing, debugging, testing, balancing, and polishing the agreed design.

The transition is often driven by the time required to finish reliably rather than by a proof that the design is perfect. Adding scope after feature lock threatens both quality and release timing.
