# Ionizing Radiation

Ionizing radiation transfers enough energy to remove electrons from atoms or molecules. Alpha particles ionize densely but penetrate weakly, beta particles have intermediate behavior, and gamma rays penetrate strongly while interacting less frequently. Biological risk depends on absorbed energy, radiation type, tissue sensitivity, exposure route, and time.
