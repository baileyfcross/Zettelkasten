# Roblox Soundtrack Playback

A soundtrack uses music to establish mood, pace, and continuity across a Roblox experience. A looping Sound can maintain background music, while scripts can change tracks or volume as the player moves between menus, exploration, combat, and other states.

Music should leave room for dialogue, feedback, and environmental cues rather than competing with every other sound. Transitions are more convincing when they match gameplay boundaries and avoid abrupt restarts. Shared balancing through [[Roblox Sound Group]] helps the soundtrack remain distinct from [[Roblox Ambient Sound]] and short effects.
