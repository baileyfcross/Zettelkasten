# 2.5D Atmospheric Cards

A partly transparent card colored from the distant atmosphere in a plate can be placed in front of an element to simulate haze at its depth. Multiple cards give separately staged elements different atmospheric densities. If all cards affect every layer, they accumulate on one another and alter the already photographed background. The book instead limits each card with its corresponding element's alpha, so its color and opacity affect only that element. See [[Atmospheric Perspective for CG Elements]].
