# Roblox Inverse Kinematics

Inverse kinematics positions the end of a joint chain toward a target and lets the system solve intermediate joint rotations. In Roblox animation authoring, it can help place a hand or foot while preserving a connected limb instead of rotating every joint independently.

The solved pose is a starting point rather than a guarantee of natural motion. Joint limits, target placement, body balance, and surrounding keyframes still need review. IK is most useful when combined with deliberate [[Roblox Pose Keyframes]] and a rig whose hierarchy satisfies [[Roblox Animation Rig Requirements]].
