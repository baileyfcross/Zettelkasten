# Character Modeling Method Selection

Character modeling method selection chooses among box modeling, polygon-by-polygon construction, sculpting followed by [[Retopology in Blender|retopology]], automatic topology generation, and modifier-assisted construction according to the form and the artist's control needs.

No method has to govern the entire asset. A model can begin from a primitive, use individual polygons around the face, pass through sculpting for broad form correction, and retain mirrored or subdivided regions through modifiers. The useful criterion is whether each representation makes the next decision easier.
