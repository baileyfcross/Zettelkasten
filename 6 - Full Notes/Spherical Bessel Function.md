# Spherical Bessel Function

Spherical Bessel functions are radial solutions associated with wave and Helmholtz equations in spherical coordinates. They are related to ordinary Bessel functions of half-integer order and are indexed by a nonnegative angular-momentum integer.

The first-kind function remains finite at the origin, whereas its companion second-kind solution is singular there. Their oscillatory radial behavior combines with spherical harmonics to describe three-dimensional waves and quantum states with spherical symmetry.
