# Legislative Re-Election Strategy

A legislative re-election strategy is a pattern of action intended to preserve a lawmaker's political career. Network hypotheses ask whether changing centrality, internal party ties, or cross-group collaboration distinguishes successful candidates.

The examined co-sponsorship case found no simple path shared by re-elected legislators, though combinations of intermediate centrality and homophily appeared in association rules. Exogenous factors remain necessary when the network is only one part of electoral survival.
