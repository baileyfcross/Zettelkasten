# Game Camera

A game camera determines what portion of the game world the player can see and from what point of view. It may show the whole world, follow a protagonist, remain fixed, or present the world through a first-person perspective.

Camera design changes the perceived relationship between player, character, and world. An overhead view can frame play as strategy, while a first-person view makes the protagonist the lens through which the world is perceived.
