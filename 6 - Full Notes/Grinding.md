# Grinding

Grinding is the repetition of already mastered actions for the sake of accumulating rewards or resources. Its main demand is usually time, patience, and endurance rather than deeper understanding of the game's rules.

Grinding can let a player trade time for reduced future difficulty, such as by increasing an avatar's numerical power. It can also become filler that disrespects the player's time when prolonged repetition is required mainly to delay progress or sustain engagement.
