# TSV Bonding Surface Contamination

TSV bonding surface contamination is a particle or residue trapped at the interface between bonded pads. It blocks part of the conductive contact, increasing resistance and reducing the effective area through which current can flow.

Resistance sensing after bonding can detect severe contamination. The reliability concern is not only immediate contact quality: current crowding around the obstruction can shorten electromigration lifetime.
