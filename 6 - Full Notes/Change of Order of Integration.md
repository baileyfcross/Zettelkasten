# Change of Order of Integration

Changing the order of integration rewrites an iterated [[Double Integral]] so the other variable is integrated first. Over a rectangular region with constant limits the exchange is usually direct, but a region bounded by curves requires new limits describing the same set from the other direction.

A sketch of the region prevents the limits from being changed mechanically. The inner limits must trace one complete slice at a fixed outer variable, and the outer limits must cover all such slices; a useful change of order can turn a difficult inner antiderivative into an elementary one.
