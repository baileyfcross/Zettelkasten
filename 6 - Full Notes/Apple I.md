# Apple I

The Apple I was Steve Wozniak’s 6502-based personal computer design, sold initially as an assembled circuit board. Users supplied the case, power supply, keyboard, and display.

Its elegant economy emerged from close knowledge of available components. The machine led Wozniak and Steve Jobs to found Apple and develop a more complete consumer system.
