# Automated Deployment

Automated deployment encodes the repeatable operations that install an artifact into an environment. The pipeline selects the package, applies the environment's configuration, deploys it to the target service, and records the result.

Automation reduces manual variation and makes frequent releases practical. Approval and promotion policy can remain deliberate even when the mechanical deployment steps are automated.
