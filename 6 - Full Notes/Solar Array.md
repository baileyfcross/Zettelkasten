# Solar Array

A solar array interconnects [[Solar Module]]s to supply a chosen voltage, current, and power. Series-connected modules raise operating voltage; parallel-connected branches increase output current. The source sizes these counts from nominal module ratings and required generator voltage and current. Array output also reflects mismatch, wiring, temperature, and the fraction of total exposed area actually covered by cells.
