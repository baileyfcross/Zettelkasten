# Human Exceptionalism in AI

Human exceptionalism in AI is the belief that a morally decisive boundary separates humans from machines. The boundary may be grounded in consciousness, reason, emotion, embodiment, free will, or participation in human social life.

AI makes the belief difficult to apply because machines can imitate individual capacities without clearly possessing the whole form of life associated with them. Rejecting exceptionalism does not require treating every machine as a person. It instead requires explaining why a particular capacity or relationship matters and recognizing that nonhuman beings may have value without duplicating humanity.
