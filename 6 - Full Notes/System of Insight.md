# System of Insight

A system of insight derives actionable understanding from historical or aggregated data. Warehouses, data lakes, analytical engines, notebooks, and business-intelligence tools can participate in the pipeline from stored events to reports or models. Its data is often copied or transformed from operational systems, so freshness, lineage, quality, and the interpretation of metrics determine whether the insight can support a decision.
