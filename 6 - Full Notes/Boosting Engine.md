# Boosting Engine

A boosting engine is a [[Reinforcing Loop]] in which a source produces a resource and the player chooses between spending it on an immediate action or investing it to increase future resource flow.

The investment-versus-action trade-off creates long-term strategic decisions. Without sufficient [[Braking Engine|brakes]], the loop can run away, stall through deadlock, or converge on a dominant strategy that collapses the [[Play Space]].
