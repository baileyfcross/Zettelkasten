# X-Ray Attenuation

X-ray attenuation removes photons from a beam through absorption and scattering. For a uniform material, transmitted intensity follows $I=I_0e^{-\mu x}$. Photoelectric absorption provides strong material-dependent image contrast at lower energies, while Compton scattering reduces contrast and contributes unwanted exposure.
