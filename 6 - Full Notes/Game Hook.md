# Game Hook

A game hook is a call to action that combines a perceivable opportunity with enough motivation for the player to act. It gives the player a reason to enter or continue an interactive loop.

An effective hook supports an understandable early goal and timely positive [[Feedback]], allowing the player to begin a [[Player Mental Model]]. It must lead into the real [[Core Gameplay Loop]] rather than substitute spectacle for the game's enduring activity.
