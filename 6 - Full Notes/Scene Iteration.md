# Scene Iteration

Scene iteration is the repeated cycle of constructing a [[Game Scene]], playing it, identifying problems, and revising its arrangement. Each draft tests whether the scene communicates its purpose and supports the intended choices.

Iteration treats a first layout as a hypothesis rather than a finished answer. Changes should solve observed problems while preserving the established rules and relationships that make the scene intelligible.
