# Homoscedasticity

Homoscedasticity means that the variance of regression errors is constant across fitted values and predictor levels. It supports the conventional standard errors and significance tests of ordinary linear regression.

A residual plot with changing vertical spread suggests heteroscedasticity. Large samples do not make the pattern disappear, although the substantive consequence depends on its severity and the inferential method used.
