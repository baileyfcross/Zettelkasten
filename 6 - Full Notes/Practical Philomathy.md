# Practical Philomathy

Practical philomathy is the pursuit of varied knowledge combined with applying it to a concrete project. For a game designer, broad interests become useful when ideas from mathematics, psychology, art, economics, programming, and other domains are transferred into design decisions.

Consuming many games alone does not provide this breadth, just as eating many meals does not make someone a chef. The designer cultivates both curiosity and the ability to connect outside knowledge to the system and player experience being built.
