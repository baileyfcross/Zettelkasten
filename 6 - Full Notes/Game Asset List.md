# Game Asset List

A game asset list inventories the visual, audio, textual, and other produced materials required by the current design. Each item can record its purpose, owner, status, dependencies, and the prototype or final experience in which it must appear.

Deriving the list from design schematics ties production work to player-facing needs. It also reveals when a proposed interaction depends on more content than the project's time and resources can support.
