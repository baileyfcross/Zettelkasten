# Identity Under Emulation Duplication

Duplicating an emulated mind creates two successors with the same memories and organization at the copying event. As soon as they receive different inputs or take different actions, their histories and personalities begin to diverge.

Both copies have an equal continuity claim, which defeats any simple rule that the predecessor becomes exactly one of them. The problem suggests that survival may consist in psychological or causal continuity without preserving uniqueness. It also connects metaphysical identity to concrete disputes about [[Property Rights Across AI Copies]] and [[Responsibility Across AI Copies]].
