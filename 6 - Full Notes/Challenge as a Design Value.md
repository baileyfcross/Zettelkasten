# Challenge as a Design Value

A challenge design value names the kind of resistance the game should present. The desired challenge may be physical, intellectual, strategic, social, interpretive, emotional, or directed at the player's assumptions about a subject.

This statement helps the team reject difficulty that is merely incidental. A demanding control scheme is valuable only if mastering that form of action belongs to the intended experience.
