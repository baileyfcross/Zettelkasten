# Biomanufacturing Cost

Biomanufacturing cost includes cells, media, scaffolds, equipment, facilities, labor, quality control, failed batches, storage, and transport. A successful proof of concept may be unaffordable because it uses small bespoke batches and research-grade materials.

Automation and purchasing scale can lower cost, but sterility and biological quality requirements prevent every industrial shortcut used with passive materials.
