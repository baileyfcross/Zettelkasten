# Gauss's Law

Gauss's law states that the electric flux through a closed surface is

$$\oint E\cdot dA=\frac{Q_{enc}}{\varepsilon_0}.$$

Only enclosed charge contributes to the net flux. With sufficient symmetry, the law converts a surface integral into a direct calculation of electric field strength for spherical, cylindrical, or planar charge distributions.
