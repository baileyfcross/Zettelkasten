# Event-Driven Game Music Sequencer

An event-driven game music sequencer triggers short prerecorded cells from a common metronomic clock. Because the cells begin on quantized subdivisions and can overlap while their natural decays finish, the engine can rearrange performed audio with finer timing than cue-level [[Horizontal Resequencing]].

Gameplay events choose which cell or phrase should sound next, allowing detailed changes without relying entirely on synthesized MIDI instruments. The approach preserves the character of live recordings but requires every cell to fit the shared timing grid and a sequencer that understands legal combinations, synchronization, and overlap.
