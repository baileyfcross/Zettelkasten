# Video Story Clustering

Video story clustering first segments a stream into coherent story units and then groups units describing the same event or topic. Visual shots, speech transcripts, audio cues, captions, and temporal proximity provide complementary evidence.

Temporal continuity aids segmentation, while multimodal similarity aids cross-story grouping. Repetition and evolving events complicate the boundary between a continuation of one story and a newly emerging but related topic.
