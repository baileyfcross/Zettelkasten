# Color Histogram

A color histogram counts how image pixels are distributed among color bins. It is a compact global descriptor, and adaptive binning can allocate resolution to the colors that actually occur.

Because it discards pixel positions, the histogram can tolerate small viewpoint or lighting changes but cannot say where a color appears. Images with different spatial arrangements may therefore receive similar histograms.
