# Agent Human Intervention Rate

Agent human intervention rate measures how often operators must approve, correct, override, or rescue an agent run. Broken down by task, risk level, model version, and tool, it reveals where apparent automation still transfers substantial cognitive work to people.

The measure is not minimized blindly. High-risk actions should retain deliberate approvals, while unexpected intervention in a low-risk, well-defined workflow may indicate weak context, poor planning, or brittle tooling. It is most informative alongside task success and guardrail outcomes so fewer interventions are not mistaken for safer or better behavior.
