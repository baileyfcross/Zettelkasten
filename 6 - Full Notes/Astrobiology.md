# Astrobiology

Astrobiology studies the origin, distribution, and possible forms of life in the universe. Earlier U.S. work used the name exobiology, a field that initially had little direct extraterrestrial evidence and relied heavily on studies of early-Earth chemistry and the environmental requirements of life.

Planetary missions made the search experimental. Viking's Mars landers carried miniaturized biological packages whose results were negative or ambiguous and likely reflected nonbiological soil chemistry. Later discoveries of terrestrial extremophiles widened ideas about habitable environments and encouraged investigation of subsurface water and icy moons rather than only Earth-like surface conditions.
