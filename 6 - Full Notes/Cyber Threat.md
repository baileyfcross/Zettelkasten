# Cyber Threat

A cyber threat is a circumstance or actor capable of causing harm to an information system or the assets it supports. A threat represents potential danger; it becomes an attack when someone takes action to exploit an opportunity.

Keeping threats distinct from [[Cyber Vulnerability]] and [[Security Risk]] makes analysis clearer. A system may contain a weakness without an active adversary, while a threat may exist without a usable path into that system.
