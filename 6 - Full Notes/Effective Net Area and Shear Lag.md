# Effective Net Area and Shear Lag

Net area subtracts material removed by bolt holes from a tension member's gross cross-section. A straight failure path crosses the relevant holes, while a staggered path receives an adjustment for its diagonal length. The critical net section is the path that yields the smallest resistance, not necessarily the visually shortest line through the connection.

Effective net area further reduces net area with a shear-lag factor when the connection engages only part of the shape. Stress is highest near connected elements and takes distance to spread into outstanding elements. Longer connections and more complete attachment improve participation, while a single connected angle leg or one connected part of a tee can have significant lag. Welded members have no bolt-hole deduction but may still experience shear lag.
