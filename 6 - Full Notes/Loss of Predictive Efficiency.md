# Loss of Predictive Efficiency

Loss of predictive efficiency is the drop in the overall R-squared that occurs when two individual or grouped judgment policies are replaced by one combined equation. JAN chooses the merge producing the smallest drop at each stage.

A sharp increase in loss can signal that substantively different policy groups are being forced together. It therefore supplies a possible stopping criterion for the hierarchy.
