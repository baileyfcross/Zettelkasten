# Neural Mapping for Brain Emulation

Neural mapping for brain emulation records the components and connections needed to initialize a simulation. A connectome supplies the wiring pattern among neurons, but structure alone may not reveal synaptic strengths, transmitter effects, membrane states, or other variables that alter signaling.

Destructive slice-and-scan methods trade the survival of the original brain for very high spatial detail. Less destructive proposals include fluorescent activity recording, molecular labels, and speculative microscopic probes. Every method embodies a decision about resolution: omitting a causally important feature can make an otherwise detailed [[Whole Brain Emulation]] behaviorally wrong.
