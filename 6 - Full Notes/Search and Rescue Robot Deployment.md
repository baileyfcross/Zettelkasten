# Search and Rescue Robot Deployment

Search-and-rescue robot deployment includes transport, unpacking, setup, battery changes, instructions, maintenance, communication, and integration with an incident team. A capable machine can still be rejected if responders cannot operate it quickly under pressure.

The equipment may sit unused for months and then face mud, heat, dust, water, or cold without access to power or online manuals. Design must therefore favor clear controls, field-replaceable parts, robust storage, offline documentation, and realistic training. Operational readiness is a property of the entire [[Search and Rescue Robot]] system, not only its laboratory mobility.
