# Mechanics Teaching Sequence

A mechanics teaching sequence introduces a new action in isolation, expands the situation in which it is used, adds danger gradually, and finally requires competent execution under greater difficulty. Early stages remove time pressure or punishment so experimentation is safe.

The sequence turns instruction into escalating practice. Each step changes one demand while preserving the mechanic being learned, allowing a designer to observe where understanding or execution breaks down. Mastery becomes evidence produced through play rather than a claim inferred from reading a prompt.
