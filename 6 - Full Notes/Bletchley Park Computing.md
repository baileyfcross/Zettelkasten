# Bletchley Park Computing

Bletchley Park combined mathematicians, linguists, operators, engineers, and specialized machines to decrypt wartime communications. Bombes attacked Enigma traffic, while Colossus processed a different electronic cipher stream.

The work shortened the war but remained secret afterward. That secrecy limited commercial transfer and obscured the contribution of text-processing machines to computing history.
