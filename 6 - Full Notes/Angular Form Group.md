# Angular Form Group

An Angular form group collects named form controls into one reactive structure. Its value is assembled from child values, and its status reflects the validation state of the group and its members.

Grouping lets a component submit or inspect a form as one model while retaining field-level errors. An [[Angular Form Builder]] provides a shorter syntax for constructing groups and controls.
