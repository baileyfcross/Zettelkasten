# Gameplay Engine

A gameplay engine is a systemic loop that reinforces or balances the same kind of [[Resource]]. It differs from a [[Game Economy]], which gains value by exchanging resources, and a [[Game Ecology]], which balances through exchange.

Engines can add capability through a [[Boosting Engine]] or regulate it through a [[Braking Engine]]. These structures often sit inside larger mechanics and progression systems rather than appearing alone.
