# Density-Based Outlier

A density-based outlier lies in a region substantially sparser than the relevant surrounding data. It is judged relative to local concentration rather than one global distance threshold.

This perspective handles variable-density data better than a simple radius rule, but estimating neighborhood density and selecting its scale can be computationally demanding.
