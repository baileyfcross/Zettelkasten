# HyperMap Embedding

HyperMap embeds a scale-free network in the [[Poincare Disk Model]] by approximately maximizing the likelihood of its observed links. It orders nodes by degree, associates that order with arrival time, assigns radial coordinates, and estimates angular coordinates from local likelihoods.

Unlike distance-preserving embedding, HyperMap tries to reconstruct a plausible hyperbolic growth process. The inferred coordinates can also predict missing links because nearby non-neighbors receive a higher connection probability.
