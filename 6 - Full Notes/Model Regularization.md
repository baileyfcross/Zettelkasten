# Model Regularization

Model regularization fits observed data while imposing a cost or prior preference against unnecessary complexity. It prevents the learner from treating every small fluctuation in a sample as structure that must be reproduced.

The tradeoff is deliberate: a slightly less exact training fit can produce better [[Generalization in Machine Learning|generalization]] by reducing sensitivity to noise. Regularization may shrink parameters, eliminate unneeded ones, or otherwise favor simpler structures; excessive strength can instead cause [[Model Underfitting|underfitting]].
