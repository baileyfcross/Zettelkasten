# Principal Ideal Domain

A principal ideal domain is an [[Integral Domain]] in which every ideal is generated by one element. Divisibility behaves enough like the integers to support greatest common divisors, Bézout identities, and prime factorizations.

Every ascending chain of ideals in a PID stabilizes. These properties make invertible row and column operations sufficient to reduce matrices to [[Smith Normal Form]].
