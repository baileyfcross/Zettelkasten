# Historical Project Decision Support

Historical project decision support uses prior schedules, outcomes, risks, staffing patterns, and decisions to inform a current choice. Language models can summarize comparable cases and extract patterns from unstructured reports and communication.

Past correlation is not a guarantee under new market, team, or technical conditions. Recommendations should identify the comparison set, relevant differences, and missing evidence so decision makers can judge transferability.
