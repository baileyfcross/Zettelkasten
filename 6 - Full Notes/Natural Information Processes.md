# Natural Information Processes

A natural information process is a phenomenon interpreted through states, representations, transformations, and exchanges of information. Examples include biological regulation, cognitive activity, and physical interactions modeled computationally.

Such an interpretation is valuable when it yields testable explanations or productive simulations. The meaning of the represented information remains tied to the scientific domain rather than supplied by the computer.
