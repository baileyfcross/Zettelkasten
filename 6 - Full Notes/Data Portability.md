# Data Portability

Data portability is the ability to move records from one service to another and continue using them. It requires more than possession of a file: the data must be available in a documented format whose structure and meaning another system can interpret.

Portability reduces switching costs and limits dependence on a single platform. It lets a person preserve a history, compare services, use a specialized analysis, or leave a provider whose terms have changed. Without it, the value accumulated through a person's effort can become a tool for keeping that person in a service.

Open formats, [[Personal Data Export]], and interoperable interfaces support portability. Privacy remains relevant during transfer because wider movement creates additional recipients and security responsibilities.
