# Ideal of Linear Type

An ideal I is of linear type when the natural map Sym(I) → R(I) from its [[Symmetric Algebra of an Ideal|symmetric algebra]] to its [[Rees Algebra]] is an isomorphism.

In this case, all defining equations of the Rees algebra follow from the linear syzygies of the generators. The source relates this property to torsion in the symmetric algebra and to approximation-complex acyclicity.
