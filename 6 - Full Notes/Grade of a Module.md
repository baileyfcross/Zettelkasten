# Grade of a Module

For a finitely generated module M over a Noetherian ring, grade(M) is the smallest i for which Extⁱ_R(M,R) is nonzero. It also equals the depth of R with respect to Ann(M).

Grade measures how far the module's support lies from being detected by ordinary Hom. The source relates it to depths of localized rings at primes in [[Support of a Module|Supp(M)]].
