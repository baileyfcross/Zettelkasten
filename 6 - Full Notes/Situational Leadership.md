# Situational Leadership

Situational leadership adjusts the balance of direction and support to a team's capability and confidence. A leader may direct an inexperienced group, coach developing competence, support capable people who need confidence, and delegate to a mature team.

The goal is not permanent control but the least intervention that helps the team succeed and grow toward self-management.
