# Loglinear Time Complexity

Loglinear time complexity, written $O(n\log n)$, combines a linear factor with a logarithmic one. Its growth is greater than [[Linear Time Complexity]] but far lower than common quadratic methods at large input sizes.

Efficient comparison-based sorting methods such as [[Quicksort]] in expectation and [[Merge Sort]] operate in loglinear time. They avoid comparing every item with every other item by partitioning or merging structured subproblems.
