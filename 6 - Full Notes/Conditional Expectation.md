# Conditional Expectation

A conditional expectation is the mean of an outcome among cases with specified predictor values, written as $E(Y\mid X=x)$. Under squared-error loss, it is the prediction that minimizes expected error for those predictor values.

For a binary outcome coded zero and one, the conditional expectation equals the conditional probability of class one. This connects regression-style estimation to [[Bayesian Classification]] and other class-prediction methods.
