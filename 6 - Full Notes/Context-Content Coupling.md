# Context-Content Coupling

Context-content coupling analyzes linguistic forms together with the setting and relationships that give them meaning. It contrasts with methods that treat each word as an independent datum detached from order, genre, and social use.

Preserving the coupling can reveal semantic and pragmatic functions that frequency alone misses. It also makes analysis more demanding because variation must be investigated rather than removed as noise.
