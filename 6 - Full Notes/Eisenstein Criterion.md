# Eisenstein Criterion

Eisenstein's criterion proves an integer polynomial irreducible over the rationals when a prime divides every nonleading coefficient, does not divide the leading coefficient, and its square does not divide the constant term.

A change of variable can sometimes place a polynomial into a form where the criterion applies.
