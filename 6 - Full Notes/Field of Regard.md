# Field of Regard

Field of regard is the amount of physical space around a user in which a [[Visual Display]] can present imagery. It is measured in degrees of visual angle.

It differs from [[Field of View]], which is the portion visible at a single instant. A cylindrical display may offer a 360-degree horizontal field of regard. A tracked [[Head-Worn Display]] can also provide a 360-degree field of regard because the display remains in front of the eyes as the user turns, even when its instantaneous field of view is much smaller.

Field of regard is one component of [[Display Fidelity]].
