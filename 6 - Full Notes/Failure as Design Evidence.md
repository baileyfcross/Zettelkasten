# Failure as Design Evidence

A failed prototype is productive when it reveals that an assumption about action, comprehension, technology, or experience was wrong. Discovering that mismatch early gives the team information while the design is still cheap to change.

The useful response is neither to hide the failure nor to treat it as proof that the entire idea is worthless. The team identifies what the result teaches, revises the question, and runs another cycle.
