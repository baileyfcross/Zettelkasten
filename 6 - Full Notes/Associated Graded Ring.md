# Associated Graded Ring

The associated graded ring of a filtered ring is gr(R) = ⊕Rₙ/Rₙ₊₁. Each graded piece records one layer of the filtration after deeper layers are collapsed.

For the [[I-Adic Filtration]], gr_I(R) = ⊕Iⁿ/Iⁿ⁺¹. This converts local or filtered behavior into homogeneous algebra that can be studied with graded methods.
