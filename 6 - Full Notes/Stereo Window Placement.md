# Stereo Window Placement

Aligning the two eye images at a particular subject establishes the depth that seems to coincide with the physical screen, or stereo window. Aligning a distant subject makes nearer material appear in front of the screen; aligning a nearer subject makes more of the scene appear behind it. The book compares several alignments of three depth-staged models to show the difference. Dramatic foreground pop-out is a creative option, not a default; excessive separation can strain the viewing experience. See [[Stereo Parallax and Convergence]].
