# LLM-Assisted Phishing Detection

LLM-assisted phishing detection analyzes message language, sender context, requests, links, and social-engineering patterns to identify suspicious communication. Semantic analysis can recognize manipulative intent even when an attack avoids a known phrase or template.

Detection should be combined with sender authentication, URL and attachment analysis, reputation, and user reporting. A model's judgment alone can block legitimate mail or miss a technically convincing attack.
