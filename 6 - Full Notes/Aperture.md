# Aperture

Aperture is the adjustable opening that controls how much light passes through a camera lens. A wider opening admits more light, while a narrower opening admits less and therefore requires compensation through shutter time, sensitivity, or scene illumination.

Aperture also influences [[Depth of Field]]. This makes it forensically useful: a recorded f-number can be compared with how quickly objects before and behind the focus distance become blurred, provided the analyst also considers focal length and subject distance.
