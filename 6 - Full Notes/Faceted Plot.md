# Faceted Plot

A faceted plot repeats the same graphic for subsets of data in aligned panels. Shared scales and encodings make group-specific patterns easier to compare without combining every observation in one crowded display.

Free scales may reveal local variation but weaken direct comparison, so their use should be explicit.
