# Helidon Health Checks

Helidon Health Checks is Helidon MP's compatible implementation of the MicroProfile Health specification. A compliant application receives health endpoints and default responses automatically, and application code can add liveness, readiness, and startup procedures.

The checks report operational state rather than business data. Their separation tells an orchestrator whether a process must be replaced, may temporarily stop receiving work, or has not yet completed initialization.
