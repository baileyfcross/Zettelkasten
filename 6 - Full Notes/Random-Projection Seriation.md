# Random-Projection Seriation

Seriation gives observations a one-dimensional order that may serve as a starting point for hierarchy construction. The book takes several random projections and averages them; in its tested settings, this consensus correlates closely with row marginal sums.

A direct marginal sum is cheap, but distinct vectors can share it. A projection can separate some such ties while retaining a simple scalar representation. Neither order is guaranteed to be the unique or optimal hierarchy, so the subsequent tree and its proximity behavior need comparison with the original multivariate data.
