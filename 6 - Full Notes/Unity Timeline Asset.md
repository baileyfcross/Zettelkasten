# Unity Timeline Asset

A Unity Timeline asset sequences clips on parallel tracks to coordinate animation, audio, activation, and other time-based behavior. Tracks bind abstract timeline content to objects in a scene, while clips define what happens over particular intervals.

A Playable Director component connects a timeline asset to its scene bindings and controls playback. Clips can overlap for blending or be mixed on tracks, allowing cinematics and scripted sequences to be assembled visually rather than encoded as one long procedural script.
