# Unitary Quantum Gate

A quantum gate is represented by a unitary matrix $U$ satisfying $U^\dagger U=UU^\dagger=I$. Unitarity preserves vector norms and inner products, so valid closed-system state transformations preserve total probability.

Every unitary has inverse $U^\dagger$, making the gate reversible. A gate on $k$ qubits is a $2^k\times2^k$ matrix, but a circuit normally specifies a large computation with a sequence of small local gates instead of materializing the full matrix.
