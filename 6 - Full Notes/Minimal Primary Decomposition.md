# Minimal Primary Decomposition

A primary decomposition is minimal or irredundant when its associated prime radicals are distinct and no component can be removed without changing the intersection.

Every submodule that has a primary decomposition also has a minimal one. The minimal associated primes are intrinsic, while components belonging to [[Embedded Prime]]s can show more dependence on the chosen decomposition.
