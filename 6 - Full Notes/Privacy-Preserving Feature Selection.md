# Privacy-Preserving Feature Selection

Privacy-preserving feature selection identifies useful variables while limiting disclosure about sensitive samples or features. Mechanisms may perturb statistics, distribute computation, or provide formal privacy guarantees.

Privacy changes the evaluation problem because exact relevance scores can themselves leak information. The method must balance predictive utility, selection reliability, computational cost, and the strength of protection.
