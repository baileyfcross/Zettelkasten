# User Story

A user story describes desired value from a user's point of view, commonly as “As a role, I want a goal, so that a reason.” It is a concise placeholder for a conversation, not a complete specification.

The form keeps attention on who benefits and why. [[Acceptance Criteria]] add testable detail when the story approaches implementation, while the Development Team retains freedom to discover the solution.

Within architecture work, a story is one input to requirements analysis: its concise value statement can reveal behavior, but performance, security, interoperability, and other cross-cutting constraints may need separate specification.

The current source describes the story through card, conversation, and confirmation. The card is a movable placeholder, the conversation develops shared understanding and may split or estimate the work, and confirmation supplies examples and acceptance criteria that demonstrate whether the intended value was delivered.
