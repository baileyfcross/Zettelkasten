# Unity Audio Source

A Unity Audio Source is the component that plays an audio clip in a scene. It controls properties such as volume, pitch, looping, automatic playback, priority, and the balance between non-positional 2D sound and distance-based 3D sound.

The source is heard through an Audio Listener, normally attached to the player's camera. Scripts can start, stop, delay, or replace clips, while routing the source to an [[Unity Audio Mixer]] gives shared control over related sounds.
