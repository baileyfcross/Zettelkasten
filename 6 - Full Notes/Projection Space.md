# Projection Space

Projection space is the coordinate representation produced when camera-space geometry is transformed for perspective or orthographic viewing. Perspective projection makes apparent size decrease with distance and prepares coordinates for clipping and normalization.

After the perspective divide and viewport mapping, projected positions become [[Screen Space]] coordinates used by the rasterizer.
