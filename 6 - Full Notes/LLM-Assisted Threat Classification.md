# LLM-Assisted Threat Classification

LLM-assisted threat classification interprets security events, reports, and unstructured evidence to assign a threat category and summarize its characteristics. Broad language representations can connect new descriptions with known behaviors and help analysts triage large event volumes.

Classification should retain the source signals and confidence because an incorrect label can misroute response. Deterministic indicators, threat intelligence, and analyst review remain necessary for high-impact cases.
