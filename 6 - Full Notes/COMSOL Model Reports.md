# COMSOL Model Reports

COMSOL reports assemble definitions, geometry, materials, physics equations, conditions, mesh, study settings, and results into a structured document. Brief, intermediate, complete, and custom templates vary how much of the model tree they include.

Automatic generation reduces clerical work but does not decide which assumptions and findings matter. A useful report connects the [[Thermal Management Goals|model objective]] to settings, verification evidence, plots, and derived values so that another analyst can review the reasoning rather than only the interface state.
