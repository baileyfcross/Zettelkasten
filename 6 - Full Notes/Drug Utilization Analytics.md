# Drug Utilization Analytics

Drug utilization analytics examines prescribing, dispensing, consumption, cost, and outcomes to evaluate how medicines are used. Large healthcare datasets can reveal repeated patterns across institutions and populations.

The analysis can support efficiency and management, but codes and clinical context must be reconciled before comparisons are credible. Commercial sensitivity and patient privacy also affect access to the underlying records.
