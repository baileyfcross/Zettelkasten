# Radio Time Signal

A radio time signal distributes a common clock reference to distant receivers. Ships and aircraft could use broadcasts such as WWV to set chronometers without physically transporting a clock. High-frequency signals traveled far by reflecting from the ionosphere, but changing propagation paths introduced timing uncertainty. Satellite navigation preserves the idea of broadcast time while using higher-frequency signals and explicit corrections for propagation delay.
