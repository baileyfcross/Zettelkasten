# Digital Service Integration

Digital service integration connects an assistant's conversation layer to systems that retrieve information or perform actions. Examples include calendars, weather services, messaging systems, commerce platforms, and smart-device controls.

The [[Dialog Manager]] converts an understood request into a service call, handles the result or error, and passes a symbolic response to [[Natural Language Generation]]. Conversation is therefore an interface to capabilities that usually live outside the assistant itself.
