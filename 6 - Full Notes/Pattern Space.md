# Pattern Space

A pattern space is the set of structured patterns permitted by a representation and its constraints. Itemsets, sequences, subgraphs, and other pattern families create different spaces with different matching rules.

The space is usually enormous, so support thresholds, structural restrictions, and task-specific bounds determine which candidates can be enumerated. Its definition limits both expressiveness and computational cost.
