# Microarray Data Clustering

Microarray clustering organizes genes or samples from large matrices of expression measurements. Gene clusters may reveal coordinated response across conditions, while sample clusters may suggest biological subtypes.

Measurements are noisy, dimensions greatly outnumber samples, and preprocessing choices can dominate proximity. Biological interpretation therefore combines statistical stability with functional or experimental evidence rather than treating clusters as definitive pathways.
