# Extracellular Matrix

Extracellular matrix is the network of proteins and other molecules surrounding cells in tissue. It provides physical support, adhesion sites, mechanical cues, and biochemical signals that influence cell survival, organization, and function.

Biofabrication uses natural matrix materials or synthetic mimics to recreate selected aspects of this environment around cultured cells.
