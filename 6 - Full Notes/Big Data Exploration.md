# Big Data Exploration

Big Data exploration is the interactive examination of large, varied datasets to discover distributions, relationships, anomalies, and questions worth deeper analysis. Efficient exploration helps analysts move from raw volume to hypotheses and interpretable evidence.

Because complete inspection may be impossible, tools use summaries, indexing, sampling, and visualization. Those shortcuts must remain visible so a convenient view is not mistaken for the full dataset.
