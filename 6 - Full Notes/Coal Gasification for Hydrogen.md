# Coal Gasification for Hydrogen

Coal or heavy oil can undergo partial oxidation at high temperature to form a gas mixture from which hydrogen is recovered. The source describes converting accompanying carbon monoxide to carbon dioxide in catalytic stages, then scrubbing out CO2. This route produces hydrogen from a carbon-containing primary fuel and has different cost and energy yield from [[Steam Methane Reforming]] or [[Water Electrolysis]].
