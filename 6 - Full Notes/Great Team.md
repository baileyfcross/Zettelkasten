# Great Team

A great team aligns complementary skills around a shared purpose, communicates openly, makes decisions together, and accepts shared accountability for valuable results. Members can disagree safely while remaining committed to the team's outcome.

Team quality is not merely the sum of individual talent. Trust, a compelling goal, manageable size, and repeated delivery create the conditions in which collective capability grows.
