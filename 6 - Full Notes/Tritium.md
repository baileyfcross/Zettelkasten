# Tritium

Tritium is a hydrogen isotope whose nucleus contains one proton and two neutrons. It is a reactant in the [[Deuterium-Tritium Fusion Reaction]], but the source notes that it is not abundant as a natural fuel supply. A reactor can make more by exposing lithium in a [[Tritium Breeding Blanket]] to fusion neutrons, so fuel recovery becomes part of the plant design.
