# Human-in-the-Loop Recommender System

A human-in-the-loop recommender system lets an algorithm narrow or rank candidates while a person applies contextual judgment before the recommendation reaches its recipient. The person's decision and the user's response can then become feedback for later model updates.

Stitch Fix illustrates the division: models identify clothing likely to fit a client's profile, while a stylist interprets taste, aspiration, and social context that the feature representation may miss. The arrangement is not simply a [[Hybrid Recommender System]] of two algorithms; it assigns calculation and judgment to different participants so each can compensate for the other's blind spots.
