# Internet of Services

The Internet of Services is the collection of networked services that monitor, support, bill for, or otherwise act on behalf of connected devices. A printer service can track supplies and usage; a streetlight service can detect failed lamps and organize maintenance.

The device supplies observations or accepts control, while the service performs the surrounding business process. This complements a network of connected things by giving their data an operational purpose.
