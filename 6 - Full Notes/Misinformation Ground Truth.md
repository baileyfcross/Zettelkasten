# Misinformation Ground Truth

Misinformation ground truth is the reference labeling used to train or evaluate detection. Labels may come from manual annotation, fact-checking judgments, honeypots, or lists of suspended accounts.

Each method changes cost, collection time, realism, and class distribution. A large label set is not automatically representative, especially when it contains only accounts already caught by a platform or baited by an artificial profile.
