# Resultant Force

The resultant force is the vector sum of all external forces acting on an object. A zero resultant produces translational equilibrium or constant velocity; a nonzero resultant produces acceleration according to [[Newton's Second Law]]. Forces may be added graphically or resolved into perpendicular components before their component sums are recombined.
