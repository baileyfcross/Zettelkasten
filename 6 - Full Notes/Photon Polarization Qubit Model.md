# Photon Polarization Qubit Model

Photon polarization supplies a physical model of a qubit. Horizontal and vertical polarization can be chosen as an orthonormal basis, so an arbitrary polarization is written as a normalized complex superposition of those two states.

A polarization filter then acts as both a basis choice and a measurement device. A photon is transmitted or absorbed with probabilities determined by the squared amplitudes, and a transmitted photon leaves in the filter's polarization state. The example connects the abstract [[Qubit State Vector]] to [[Quantum Measurement Projection]].
