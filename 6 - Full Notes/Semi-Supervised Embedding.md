# Semi-Supervised Embedding

A semi-supervised embedding maps data into a lower-dimensional space that preserves unsupervised geometry while drawing must-linked or same-labeled points together and separating incompatible ones. Clustering is then performed in the learned representation.

The balance between structural preservation and supervision determines whether the embedding generalizes beyond the guided examples. Excessive constraint weight can collapse local geometry into the few relationships that were labeled.
