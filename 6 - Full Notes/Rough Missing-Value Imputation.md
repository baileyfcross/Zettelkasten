# Rough Missing-Value Imputation

Rough missing-value imputation fills gaps with a simple representative value, such as a numeric median or a common category, to let an exploratory model run. It is a pragmatic diagnostic step rather than a substitute for a principled missing-data analysis.

The resulting model should be labeled accordingly because simple fills can shrink variation and distort relationships.
