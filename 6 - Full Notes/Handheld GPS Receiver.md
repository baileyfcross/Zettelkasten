# Handheld GPS Receiver

A handheld GPS receiver integrates an antenna, signal-processing electronics, processor, display, maps, and portable power into a field navigation tool. Microelectronics made equipment once weighing hundreds of kilograms small enough for soldiers, hikers, boaters, and survey crews to carry. Its displayed location is only the final layer: satellite geometry, atmospheric conditions, map datum, signal obstruction, and any augmentation determine how the coordinate should be interpreted.
