# Essential Boundary Condition Enforcement

Essential boundary condition enforcement prescribes selected nodal unknowns before solving the finite element equations. In structural problems these are imposed displacements; in field problems they may be specified temperatures or potentials.

The elimination method removes the prescribed row and transfers its column contribution to the right-hand side. Penalty and multipoint-constraint methods preserve other useful matrix or relationship structures for software implementation.
