# Inequality Symbol

An inequality symbol states the ordering relationship between two quantities:

- (a<b): (a) is less than (b)
- (a>b): (a) is greater than (b)
- (a\leq b): (a) is less than or equal to (b)
- (a\geq b): (a) is greater than or equal to (b)

The strict symbols exclude equality, while the inclusive symbols permit it. On a graph, that difference is shown through an [[Open Endpoint]] or [[Closed Endpoint]]. Reversing the order of the two expressions also reverses the symbol: (a<b) is equivalent to (b>a).
