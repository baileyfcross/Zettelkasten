# ThemeRiver

A ThemeRiver is a flowing stacked-area display whose combined thickness is centered symmetrically around the horizontal axis. The total width shows the global sum, while colored bands show the components' changing contributions. Centering creates a visually balanced shape but removes the stable baseline already missing from most internal layers of a [[Stacked Area Time-Series Plot]]. It favors broad patterns and relationships over exact component values.
