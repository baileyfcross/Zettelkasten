# Skill-Based Play

Skill-based play centers satisfaction on developing and applying a capacity that improves with practice. Active skill concerns timing, coordination, dexterity, and other embodied performance; mental skill concerns memory, inference, calculation, planning, or pattern recognition.

A skill becomes part of the game only when its consequences are perceptible. Clear [[Feedback]] lets players connect practice to improved performance and distinguish a demanding challenge from arbitrary failure.
