# Culture Jamming

Culture jamming appropriates familiar signs, advertisements, brands, or media conventions and alters them to criticize the institutions and values they normally support. Recognition of the original gives the intervention immediate rhetorical leverage.

Internet memes extend this tactic through rapid, distributed remix. A participant can reuse a public image or slogan, shift its stance, and circulate the result to a networked audience. The method exposes the constructed nature of dominant messages while offering an accessible form of political commentary.
