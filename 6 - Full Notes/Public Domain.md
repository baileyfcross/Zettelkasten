# Public Domain

The public domain contains works and inventions that are no longer protected by an exclusive intellectual-property right or were not eligible for that protection. A public-domain work can be reproduced, changed, performed, or incorporated into a new product without permission or compensation to a former rights holder.

Public availability is not the same as public domain. A source file posted online may still be copyrighted, while a protection that expires places the protected subject into a domain of unrestricted use that does not later revert to exclusivity.
