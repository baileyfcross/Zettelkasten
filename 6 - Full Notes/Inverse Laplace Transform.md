# Inverse Laplace Transform

The inverse Laplace transform recovers a time-domain function from its transformed expression. In practical calculations, algebraic decomposition and a table of transform pairs often replace direct evaluation of the complex inversion integral.

Partial fractions separate rational transforms into standard terms associated with exponentials, polynomials, or oscillations. Poles of the transformed function encode the system's characteristic modes and therefore help determine growth, decay, and stability in the recovered solution.
