# Soft Robotic Gripper

A soft robotic gripper uses compliant materials that deform around an object rather than requiring a rigid finger to match its geometry exactly. Passive conformation distributes contact and allows one gripper to handle a wider range of fragile or irregular shapes.

Compliance simplifies some grasp-planning demands but makes shape and force sensing more difficult because the structure itself stretches and bends. Flexible sensors and embedded electronics must distinguish commanded deformation from external contact. A [[Variable-Stiffness Robotic Gripper]] can add rigidity after conforming when a secure hold is required.
