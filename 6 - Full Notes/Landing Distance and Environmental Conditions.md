# Landing Distance and Environmental Conditions

Landing performance distinguishes ground roll from total distance over an obstacle. Temperature, pressure altitude, wind, runway slope, surface condition, braking, flap setting, and aircraft weight determine how much runway is required. A calculation should therefore be paired with current conditions and the braking information in [[Runway Friction and Crosswind Limits]].
