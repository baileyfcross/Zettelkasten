# GPS Full Operational Capability

GPS full operational capability marked the transition from a developing constellation to a service able to meet its specified worldwide military mission. Achieving it required enough operational satellites and spares, a mature ground-control network, production receivers, and validated performance rather than merely a successful prototype. The declaration in the mid-1990s also created a stable platform for rapidly expanding civil and commercial reliance.
