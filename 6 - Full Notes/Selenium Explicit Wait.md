# Selenium Explicit Wait

A Selenium explicit wait applies a timeout to one [[Selenium Expected Condition|specific condition]] for a particular element or browser state. A WebDriverWait polls until the condition succeeds and then continues immediately rather than consuming the entire timeout.

Explicit waits are suited to dynamic content because they can wait for visibility, clickability, selection, text, titles, URLs, alerts, or frames. If the condition remains false, the wait ends with a timeout failure.
