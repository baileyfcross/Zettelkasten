# FOIL Method

The FOIL method is a mnemonic for multiplying two [[Binomial]]s. It names the First, Outside, Inside, and Last term products so that every term of one factor is multiplied by every term of the other.

For ((a+b)(c+d)), the four products are (ac), (ad), (bc), and (bd). Their sum is then simplified by combining like terms. FOIL is not a separate algebraic law and does not generalize by its name to polynomials with more terms; the underlying rule is the [[Distributive Property]].
