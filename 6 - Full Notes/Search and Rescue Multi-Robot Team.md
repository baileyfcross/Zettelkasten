# Search and Rescue Multi-Robot Team

A search-and-rescue multi-robot team combines platforms whose perspectives and movement complement one another. An aerial robot can survey a broad area and warn ground units about hazards, while small ground robots enter rubble and local sensors build a detailed map.

Multiple robots can work in parallel, relay communication, and preserve capability when one unit fails. They also increase coordination and operator load. The team needs shared locations, task allocation, collision avoidance, and a rule for how many machines one person can supervise without losing situational awareness.
