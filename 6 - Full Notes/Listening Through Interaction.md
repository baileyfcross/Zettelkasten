# Listening Through Interaction

Listening through interaction is a form of play in which actions help the player understand an experience the game is expressing rather than determine or optimize its outcome. The player participates, but the system may deliberately offer limited agency.

Not every interactive conversation needs to let the player redirect events toward a preferred ending. Fixed or constrained responses can ask the player to attend to how a situation works and what it feels like from a perspective the creator has shaped.
