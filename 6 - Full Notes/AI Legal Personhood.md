# AI Legal Personhood

AI legal personhood is the proposal to give certain artificial systems a legal status through which rights, duties, property, or liability could be assigned. Legal personality is an institutional tool and does not by itself prove consciousness or [[Moral Status of AI]].

The proposal is controversial because it may help organize transactions while also giving human actors a way to avoid responsibility. If a machine is treated as the liable person but owns few resources and cannot understand punishment, harmed people may receive less protection. Any legal fiction must therefore be evaluated by the accountability structure it creates, not by the sophistication of the machine alone.
