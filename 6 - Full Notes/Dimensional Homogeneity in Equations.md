# Dimensional Homogeneity in Equations

The units on both sides of a physical equality must resolve to coherent dimensions. This check is independent of whether the numerical arithmetic was keyed correctly. In the book's velocity example, an inch-per-second value becomes centimeters per second by multiplying by centimeters per inch; the inch units cancel while seconds remain. An expression that ends in centimeters when a velocity was expected has failed before its number can be trusted. See [[Conversion-Factor Chains]] and [[Algebraic SI Derived Units]].
