# CLICK Clustering

CLICK, Clique Clustering using K-partite graphs, represents categorical attribute values as parts of a graph and connects strongly related values across attributes. It detects maximal multipartite cliques as candidate clusters.

Preprocessing builds the graph and clique matrix, detection finds strongly connected cliques, and postprocessing validates and merges supported candidates.
