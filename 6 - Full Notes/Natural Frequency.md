# Natural Frequency

A natural frequency is a rate at which a structure can vibrate freely after disturbance. It is obtained from the nontrivial solutions of the stiffness-mass eigenvalue equation and is commonly reported in radians per second or hertz.

Geometry, stiffness, material density, and boundary conditions determine the values. Mesh refinement improves the finite element approximation, especially for higher frequencies.
