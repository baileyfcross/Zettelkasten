# Behavior-Driven Development

Behavior-driven development expresses software expectations in terms of observable behavior and examples. Tests describe what a unit or interface should do rather than concentrating only on its internal implementation.

The practice is related to [[Test-Driven Development]] but emphasizes behavior-oriented language and scenarios. Jasmine test suites use descriptive grouping and expectations to state client behavior.
