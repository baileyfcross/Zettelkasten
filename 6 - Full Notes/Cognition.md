# Cognition

Cognition is the collection of processes that generate and use knowledge. It governs memory, language, and thought and is closely connected to [[Perception]] and [[Attention]].

Perceived stimuli are interpreted using representations and stored knowledge so that a person can reason, choose an action, and perform a task. In a [[3D User Interface]], cognition is especially important for complex tasks such as [[Wayfinding]], where the user must maintain [[Situation Awareness]], consult a [[Cognitive Map]], and make decisions while moving.

Interfaces can fail cognitively when they demand more attention or [[Working Memory]] than the user can supply.
