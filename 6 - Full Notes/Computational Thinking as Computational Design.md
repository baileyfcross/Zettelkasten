# Computational Thinking as Computational Design

Computational thinking includes the practiced ability to design computations that let a [[Computer]] perform a valuable job. It joins an understanding of [[Algorithm|algorithms]], representations, and machines with judgment about whether the resulting system will work reliably in its actual setting.

This design face is broader than writing code. It asks how a desired capability can be made machine-realizable, how errors and limits will be handled, and how the result will serve the people who depend on it.
