# Game Handicap

A game handicap adjusts rules or victory conditions to compensate for differences in player skill. It gives a less experienced participant a credible chance without requiring both players to face an identical task.

A handicap should preserve agency and be understandable to everyone involved. It balances participants, whereas a difficulty mode ordinarily changes the player's relationship to the environment.
