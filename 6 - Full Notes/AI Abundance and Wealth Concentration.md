# AI Abundance and Wealth Concentration

AI-driven productivity can make goods and services cheaper while concentrating ownership of the systems that produce them. Abundance in total output therefore does not guarantee an equitable distribution of income, power, education, or meaningful activity.

If less paid labor is required, society can distribute leisure and creative opportunity broadly or divide into a small group that owns and directs productive systems and a dependent majority. The technical gain does not select between these outcomes. Institutions governing ownership, compensation, education, and social value determine whether automation expands freedom or reinforces hierarchy.
