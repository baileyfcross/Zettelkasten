# Time-Interval Clinical Pattern

A time-interval clinical pattern represents events that have duration and records the relationships among their intervals. This is useful for conditions, treatments, or abstracted states that cannot be reduced to a single timestamp.

The pattern can encode whether one interval occurs before, overlaps, contains, starts, or finishes another through an [[Allen Temporal Relation]]. Mining searches for relational structures that recur across patients. Temporal abstraction and minimum support reduce continuous records to comparable patterns, but their thresholds determine which clinical variation is preserved.
