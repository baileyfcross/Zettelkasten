# Crowdsourcing

Crowdsourcing distributes a task or information request to a large group of participants, often through an open online platform. Aggregated contributions can label data, report conditions, generate ideas, or solve problems at a scale unavailable to one organization.

Quality varies with expertise and incentives, so validation, redundancy, reputation, and adversarial resistance are part of the system design.

Connected devices can make participation partly or fully automatic. Phones can contribute location and speed to traffic routing, while networked thermometers can report measurements that reveal the emergence and movement of illness. This reduces the delay of manual reporting and lets maps adjust as conditions change, but it also makes consent, sampling bias, sensor accuracy, and the distinction between anonymous and reidentifiable data part of the crowdsourcing design.
