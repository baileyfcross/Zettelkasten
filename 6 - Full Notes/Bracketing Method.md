# Bracketing Method

A bracketing method begins with endpoints $a$ and $b$ for which a continuous function has opposite signs, so the intermediate value property guarantees at least one [[Root of an Equation]] in the interval.

Each iteration replaces one endpoint while preserving the sign change. [[Bisection Method]] halves the bracket, whereas the [[Method of False Position]] uses the intercept of a secant through the endpoints. Maintaining the bracket gives reliability that open iterations such as the [[Secant Method]] do not guarantee.
