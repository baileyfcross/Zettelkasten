# Parts on the Garage Floor

Parts on the Garage Floor is a development dysfunction in which disciplines polish separate components across many iterations but postpone assembling a playable whole. Local task progress hides uncertainty about integration and the actual experience.

The remedy is to select thinner slices that combine design, code, art, audio, and testing into playable value each Sprint. Integration then becomes a source of continuing knowledge rather than an end-stage crisis.
