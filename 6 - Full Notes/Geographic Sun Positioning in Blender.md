# Geographic Sun Positioning in Blender

The Sun Position add-on derives solar orientation from geographic coordinates, date, time, and time-zone offset. It can therefore reproduce or animate natural-light direction for a specified place instead of relying on an artist's guessed rotation.

This is particularly useful for architectural studies where shadow direction is evidence about a real site. The add-on can coordinate a Blender sun with an environmental lighting setup so the direct and indirect cues agree.
