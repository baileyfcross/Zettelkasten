# Principle of Least Privilege

The principle of least privilege gives each process or user only the permissions needed for its current task. Limiting authority confines the damage possible from errors, misuse, or compromise.

Privileges should be narrow in scope and duration rather than granted permanently for convenience. Applying the principle requires understanding actual workflows so restrictions do not force unsafe workarounds.
