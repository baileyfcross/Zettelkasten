# Training Epoch

A training epoch is one complete pass through every example in a [[Training Dataset]]. During the pass, the learning procedure produces outputs, measures loss, and updates the model's parameters.

Training commonly uses multiple epochs so later passes begin from parameters improved during earlier ones. Too many passes can make the model conform too closely to the training examples, producing [[Overfitting]] and weaker results on a [[Test Dataset]].
