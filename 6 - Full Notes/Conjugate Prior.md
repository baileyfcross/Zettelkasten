# Conjugate Prior

A conjugate prior belongs to a distribution family that is preserved when it is updated by a corresponding likelihood. The posterior has the same functional form as the prior with revised parameters.

Conjugacy makes Bayesian identification analytically convenient and supports compact sequential updates. It is a modeling choice for tractability rather than evidence that the selected family must describe reality exactly.
