# Wireframe Modifier Rendering

The Wireframe modifier converts mesh edges into renderable geometry. Disabling its Replace Original option keeps the underlying surface and places the generated wires over it, allowing a render to show both form and topology.

A material offset can assign the wires a distinct material, while thickness and offset control their width and separation from the surface. An unlit or emissive treatment keeps topology legible rather than allowing scene illumination to obscure it.
