# Memes as Democratic Subversion

Memes can support democratic subversion by letting ordinary users appropriate elite images, challenge official narratives, and circulate criticism through accessible cultural forms. Humor and ambiguity can lower the threshold for participation and sometimes help expression pass around controls.

Their democratic potential is conditional rather than automatic. The same templates can reproduce stereotypes, exclude outsiders, or support opposing causes, and access to circulation remains unequal. Political value comes from the practices and power relations surrounding a meme, not from remix technology by itself.
