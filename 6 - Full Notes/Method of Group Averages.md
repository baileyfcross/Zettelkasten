# Method of Group Averages

The method of group averages estimates constants by dividing ordered observations into nearly equal groups. For a two-constant law, the mean coordinates of two groups are substituted into the model to form two equations for the constants.

Nonlinear laws may first be transformed into a [[Law Reducible to Linear Form]]. The method is quick but the result depends on how the observations are grouped and is generally less precise and unique than least squares.
