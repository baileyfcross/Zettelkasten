# Aviation Weather Data in IWXXM

IWXXM represents aviation meteorological information in a software-readable form built on XML and geographic markup. Its structured fields allow systems to exchange observations, forecasts, and advisories without treating them as unstructured text alone. This makes standardized products such as [[METAR and SPECI Reports]] easier to validate, route, and combine digitally.
