# Satellite-Based Augmentation System

A satellite-based augmentation system uses surveyed reference stations to monitor GNSS errors across a region, processes those observations into corrections and integrity information, and rebroadcasts the result through geostationary satellites. The user receives both the original navigation signals and a regional assessment of their quality. This architecture extends differential positioning over a wide area and supports safety-critical operations without requiring a local correction transmitter at every site.

The Wide Area Augmentation System applies this architecture to improve GPS accuracy and operational assurance for aviation across its coverage area. Compatible consumer receivers may also benefit, but the system's defining contribution is not merely a smaller position error; it is the regional monitoring and communication layer added to the original constellation.
