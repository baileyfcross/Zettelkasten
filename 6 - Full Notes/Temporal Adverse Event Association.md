# Temporal Adverse Event Association

A temporal adverse event association examines whether an outcome occurs unusually often within a time interval related to an exposure, such as a drug. It extends a simple co-occurrence table by preserving when the exposure and event happened.

Timing helps separate plausible exposure-event relationships from unrelated background events, but it does not establish causality. Reporting patterns, repeated exposures, disease severity, and other treatments can confound the association. The comparison window and baseline should be chosen before inspecting the strongest signal.
