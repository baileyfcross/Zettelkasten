# Political Meme Functions

Political memes perform three broad functions: persuasion or advocacy, grassroots action, and public expression or discussion. A meme can promote a candidate or cause, coordinate participation, or give citizens a reusable way to state and contest positions.

The functions frequently overlap. A personalized image may express one person's experience, recruit sympathetic viewers, and contribute to a collective campaign at the same time. This makes political memes important not merely as messages but as templates through which participation is organized.
