# Iterative Partition Clustering

Iterative partition clustering alternates between assigning observations to groups and updating group representatives. A pass continues until no beneficial reallocation remains or another convergence rule is satisfied.

The procedure searches a large space through local improvements. Repeating it from distinct initial partitions helps reveal sensitivity and can produce a better final objective value.
