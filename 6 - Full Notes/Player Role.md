# Player Role

The player role describes who or what the player is within the game and what activities that position permits. It might be one avatar, a party leader, a manager, a vehicle operator, or a shifting set of responsibilities.

Clarifying the role helps explain goals, actions, rules, and perspective. If the role changes, the game must communicate why it changed and how the player should adapt.
