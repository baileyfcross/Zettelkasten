# Internet of Things Digital Divide

The Internet of Things digital divide is unequal access to the devices, connectivity, knowledge, and institutions needed to benefit from connected services. As health, education, work, transportation, and household management incorporate IoT systems, being offline can mean losing not only convenience but early warnings, learning resources, lower costs, and opportunities.

The divide can compound itself. Connected populations generate the data used to design products and policies, while people excluded by cost, broadband availability, technical skill, disability, or geography become less visible in those datasets. A service can therefore widen inequality even when it performs well for its represented users.
