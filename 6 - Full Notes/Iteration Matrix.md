# Iteration Matrix

An iteration matrix expresses a linear update as $x^{(k+1)}=Tx^{(k)}+c$. The error after successive steps is controlled by powers of $T$.

A stationary iteration converges for every starting vector when the [[Spectral Radius]] of $T$ is less than one. Matrix norms can provide easier sufficient tests.
