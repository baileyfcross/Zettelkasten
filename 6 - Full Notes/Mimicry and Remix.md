# Mimicry and Remix

Mimicry reproduces a text's performance or structure through a new enactment, while remix manipulates an existing artifact by editing, combining, or recontextualizing its components. Both practices turn an audience member into an active participant in a meme's development.

The distinction is analytical rather than absolute: a lipsynch video mimics a performance, whereas a recut trailer remixes footage, yet many artifacts do both. Together they are the principal engines of memetic variation, producing recognizable continuity while allowing new creators to insert identities, jokes, and arguments.
