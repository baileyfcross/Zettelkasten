# Rendering from the Blender Video Sequencer

Rendering an edited sequence uses Blender's animation-output process, but its final deliverable is commonly a video file rather than an intermediate frame sequence. Output settings establish the codec, format, dimensions, frame range, and destination.

The Sequencer contributes its assembled strips, effects, and transitions to the render. A short test output can verify that image and audio settings behave as intended before the complete sequence is encoded.
