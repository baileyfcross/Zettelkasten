# Egocentric Social Bot Detection

Egocentric social bot detection represents an account together with its immediate social neighborhood. Features describe the focal user, nearby accounts, and the connections or interactions among them.

The local network supplies context for otherwise ambiguous behavior. Its usefulness depends on the availability and authenticity of neighbor data, which adversaries may also manipulate.
