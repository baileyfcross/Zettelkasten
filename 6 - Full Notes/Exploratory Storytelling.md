# Exploratory Storytelling

Exploratory storytelling distributes optional narrative material through a game space. Players may discover books, objects, conversations, environmental clues, or other fragments while deciding how thoroughly to investigate.

Because these elements can be missed, they usually deepen setting, character, or backstory rather than carry every necessary plot event. Their optional nature makes the player's route and attention part of how the authored material is experienced.
