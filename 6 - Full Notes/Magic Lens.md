# Magic Lens

A magic lens is a movable interface region that changes how information inside its boundary is displayed. In AR, a tracked handheld or virtual lens can reveal hidden structure, apply a filter, or present additional detail while the surrounding view remains unchanged.

The lens position and orientation become spatial input. Its boundary makes the transformation local and understandable, although holding, aiming, and coordinating the lens with another task can impose physical effort.
