# Business Process as a Service

Business process as a service delivers a business function through an automated or partly manual workflow hosted as a cloud service. It is a high abstraction level that can compose infrastructure, platforms, applications, information, people, and processes behind one outcome-oriented interface.

Candidate functions include payroll, tax processing, training, examinations, and health monitoring. A dependable BPaaS defines its integration points, workflow measures, service commitments, and per-use or other operating price because it replaces part of the consumer's operating process rather than merely supplying software.
