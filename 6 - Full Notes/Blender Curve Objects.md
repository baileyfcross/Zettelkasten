# Blender Curve Objects

Curve objects describe paths and outlines with control points rather than fixed polygon faces. Bezier and NURBS-style splines can produce smooth forms whose resolution remains adjustable through curve settings.

Bevel depth can turn a path into a cable, rail, or other tubular form, and a profile can shape its cross-section. Curves remain efficient to edit but may need conversion to a [[Blender Mesh Objects|mesh]] before certain modifiers, direct topology edits, or game-engine exports.
