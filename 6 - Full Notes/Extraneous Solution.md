# Extraneous Solution

An extraneous solution satisfies a transformed equation but not the original one. It can arise from a nonreversible step such as squaring both sides, multiplying by an expression that may be zero, or clearing a denominator whose zero is excluded. Substitution into the original equation is therefore part of solving radical and rational equations.
