# Relative Error

Relative error divides [[Absolute Error]] by the magnitude of the exact value: $|x-x^*|/|x|$, when $x\neq0$.

It expresses error as a proportion of the quantity being approximated. Multiplying by 100 gives percentage error, while the number of correct significant digits can be related to a sufficiently small relative error.
