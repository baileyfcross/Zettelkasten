# Local Outlier Factor

Local Outlier Factor scores a point by comparing its local neighborhood density with the densities of its neighbors. A substantially lower density produces a larger indication of outlier tendency.

The method depends on the number of neighbors and distance definition. It addresses varying densities but requires many neighborhood computations.
