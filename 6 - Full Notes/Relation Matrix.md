# Relation Matrix

Given generators of a module and generators of the kernel of the corresponding surjection from a free module, a relation matrix records the coefficients of those kernel generators. Its rows encode relations among the selected module generators.

This is a row-oriented form of a [[Presentation Matrix]]. Multiplying on the left or right by invertible matrices changes the chosen relation or module generators while preserving the isomorphism type of the presented module.
