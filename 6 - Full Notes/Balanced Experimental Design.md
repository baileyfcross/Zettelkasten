# Balanced Experimental Design

A balanced experimental design distributes experimental units across combinations of factors so that one factor is not determined by another. Balance makes separate effects easier to estimate and often improves precision.

In the source's example, aligning every treatment with only one sex creates perfect confounding and a rank-deficient design matrix. Representing both sexes within every treatment restores independent columns and makes both effects estimable.
