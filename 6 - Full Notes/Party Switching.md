# Party Switching

Party switching occurs when a legislator leaves an original political group for another or joins a newly created group. The decision may reflect career strategy, institutional bargaining, party weakness, or changing alliances.

Temporal community analysis can reveal a lawmaker's network position moving between groups before or alongside an official switch. The inference requires repeated snapshots because an aggregate term-wide graph hides the transition.
