# Cloud Tax Planning Service

A cloud tax planning service composes current tax information, personal or business data, calculation applications, scenario comparison, and reporting into a coordinated process. Information retrieval should be automated so the workflow uses the applicable rules available at the time.

Human judgment can remain in the process when choosing among future scenarios. The service illustrates how [[Information as a Service]] and [[Software as a Service]] can become building blocks for [[Business Process as a Service]].
