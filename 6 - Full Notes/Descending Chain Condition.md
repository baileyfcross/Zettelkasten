# Descending Chain Condition

The descending chain condition says that every decreasing sequence of subobjects eventually stops changing. A module satisfying this condition on submodules is an [[Artinian Module]].

Together with the [[Ascending Chain Condition]], it prevents indefinitely refining or enlarging submodule chains. A module satisfies both conditions exactly when it has finite length.
