# Vehicle-Based Data Center

A vehicle-based data center provides conventional computing and storage services from coordinated resources hosted in vehicles. Unlike a fixed facility, its capacity changes as participants arrive and leave.

Sufficient residency time and interconnection bandwidth are the source's key conditions for supporting big-data applications. Scheduling, replication, and migration translate those physical conditions into usable service continuity.
