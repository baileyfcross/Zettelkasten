# Raw and Derived Attributes

A raw attribute is recorded directly from a source; a derived attribute is calculated from other fields. Derivation can encode a meaningful interaction that no single raw field expresses. The book uses body mass index, calculated from weight and height, to illustrate how a combined measure may relate more strongly to a study target than either input alone.

Derived features still need domain scrutiny. A useful calculation for one setting may be unhelpful elsewhere, and an inappropriate transformation can hide differences. Feature design is part of representing the problem, not just a technical optimization after data collection.
