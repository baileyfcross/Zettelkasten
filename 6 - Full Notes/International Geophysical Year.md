# International Geophysical Year

The International Geophysical Year was a coordinated scientific campaign from July 1957 through December 1958 focused on the polar regions, atmosphere, oceans, magnetic field, ionosphere, and near space. Both the United States and Soviet Union publicly connected their first satellite plans to it.

The campaign created a legitimate international scientific setting for launching instruments while Cold War institutions also valued the military and prestige implications. It extended earlier sounding-rocket work into satellites that could observe for long periods and helped make space science a durable multinational activity.
