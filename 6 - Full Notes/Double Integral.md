# Double Integral

A double integral accumulates a scalar function over a two-dimensional region. It can represent area, mass, probability, or another distributed quantity and is commonly evaluated as an iterated integral with limits describing the region.

Changing coordinates can adapt the integration to the geometry. In polar coordinates the area element becomes $r\,dr\,d\theta$; the additional factor is the Jacobian that accounts for how coordinate cells change size under the transformation.

Describing the region by its boundary inequalities determines the order and limits of iteration. A general [[Change of Variables in Multiple Integrals|change of variables]] replaces the polar factor with the absolute Jacobian determinant of the chosen coordinate map.
