# Shape-Based Time-Series Clustering

Shape-based time-series clustering groups sequences by the form of their variation after accounting for shifts, scale differences, noise, or timing offsets. Similarity is defined over whole temporal patterns rather than unordered observations.

Normalization and alignment determine which transformations are treated as irrelevant. A centroid or prototype must also respect sequence structure; an ordinary coordinate-wise mean can blur peaks when members are misaligned.
