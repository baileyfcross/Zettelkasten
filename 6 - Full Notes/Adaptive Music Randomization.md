# Adaptive Music Randomization

Adaptive music randomization varies playback by choosing among alternative tracks, segments, entry points, or timing values. It can make a repeated game state sound less predictable even when the underlying asset collection is finite.

Unbounded randomness does not guarantee musical variety; it can repeat the same choice, create stylistic contradictions, or weaken a dramatic sequence. Useful randomization therefore applies exclusions, weights, recent-history rules, or context-sensitive pools. These constraints complement a [[Game Music Rule Set]] by controlling chance at the level of authored assets.
