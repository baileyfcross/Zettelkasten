# Harmonic Response Analysis

Harmonic response analysis determines steady vibration amplitude when applied loads vary sinusoidally with time. The response depends on excitation frequency relative to the structure's natural frequencies.

Stiffness, mass, and damping govern magnitude and phase. The analysis reveals resonant regions and how periodic forcing is transmitted through the structure.
