# Roblox Skybox

A Roblox skybox surrounds the world with six images that represent the distant faces of an enclosing cube. It establishes the horizon and far background without requiring reachable geometry.

The six images need to form a coherent visual environment at their seams. A skybox does not replace scene lighting or atmospheric depth; it is the remote backdrop seen through those systems. Coordinating it with [[Roblox Atmosphere]] and [[Roblox World Lighting]] makes the environment read as one place rather than a foreground scene pasted over an unrelated background.
