# Kanban

Kanban is a method for visualizing, measuring, and managing the flow of work. A [[Kanban Board]] makes states and queues visible, while limits on [[Work in Progress]] expose constraints and encourage finishing before starting more.

Unlike Scrum, Kanban does not require a Sprint timebox or replace existing roles. It is especially useful for production and support work that arrives continuously or follows a recurring flow.

The current source distinguishes continuous flow from sprint selection and connects the board to lead time, work in progress, and swimlanes. Limiting how many cards occupy a state turns an accumulating queue into a visible signal that the team should finish or unblock work before pulling more.
