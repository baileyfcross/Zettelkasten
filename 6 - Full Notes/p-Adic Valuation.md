# p-Adic Valuation

For a chosen prime p, the p-adic valuation of a nonzero integer is the exponent of the highest power of p dividing it. For a rational numerator divided by denominator, the valuation is the numerator's exponent minus the denominator's. The associated p-adic norm is p raised to the negative valuation.

This reverses ordinary magnitude intuitions: a number divisible by a very high power of p is small in the p-adic norm. The resulting non-Archimedean distance expresses closeness through shared divisibility or prefix structure and supports a hierarchical topology.
