# Minimum Viable Product

A minimum viable product is the smallest coherent set of features that can be delivered to users and provide real value. Limiting scope lets a team design, build, test, and release a working slice while using feedback to decide what to add next. The book's inventory application begins with enough commands to record and inspect stock, postponing broader features until their need is clearer; "minimum" describes scope, not an excuse to omit necessary quality.
