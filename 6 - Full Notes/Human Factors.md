# Human Factors

Human factors are the capabilities, characteristics, and limitations of the person using a system. They include the body acting, the senses perceiving, and the brain thinking.

Human-factors-driven design studies what people can comfortably and reliably do, then uses that knowledge to pursue safe, efficient, and effective interaction. In a [[3D User Interface]], relevant areas include [[Perception]], [[Cognition]], [[Attention]], and [[Physical Ergonomics]]. These factors are interconnected, so evaluation may need to isolate particular issues without forgetting their relationships.

Human factors form a foundation for [[User-Centered Design]] and [[Usability Engineering]].
