# Political Meme

A political meme is a group of related digital artifacts that uses imitation and transformation to engage public issues, political actors, identities, or events. Its circulation combines cultural participation with political expression.

Political memes need not resemble formal campaign communication. Humor, popular culture, personal testimony, and visual remix can carry arguments while inviting others to adapt them. Their significance lies in how people use shared formats to persuade, coordinate, criticize, and debate rather than in whether institutions recognize the activity as politics.
