# Computer Mouse

A computer mouse converts hand movement and button presses into selection and positioning on a graphical display. Douglas Engelbart developed the device while investigating ways to augment human intellect.

The mouse made pointing at visible interface objects faster than typing many commands. Its design links fine motor control to direct manipulation of digital representations.
