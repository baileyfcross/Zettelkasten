# Horizontal Business Process

A horizontal business process supports many kinds of organizations without defining their industry-specific product. Payroll, human resources, billing, auditing, and training are examples of functions needed across different business models.

Commonality makes these processes strong candidates for standardization and [[Business Process as a Service]]. A provider can spread the cost of a repeatable workflow across consumers while exposing the interfaces each organization needs.
