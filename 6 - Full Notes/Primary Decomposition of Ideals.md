# Primary Decomposition of Ideals

A primary decomposition writes an [[Ideal]] as a finite intersection of [[Primary Ideal]]s. In a [[Noetherian Ring]], every ideal has such a decomposition.

Taking radicals of the primary components reveals the associated [[Prime Ideal]]s, while the primary components retain information analogous to prime powers. The construction generalizes unique prime-power decomposition from integers to broader commutative rings.
