# Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity. Average acceleration is $\Delta v/\Delta t$, and instantaneous acceleration is $a=dv/dt$. It can result from a change in speed, direction, or both. The area under an acceleration-time graph gives the corresponding change in velocity.
