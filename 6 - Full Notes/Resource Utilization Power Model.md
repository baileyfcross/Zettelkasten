# Resource Utilization Power Model

A resource utilization power model predicts energy consumption from observed use of processors, memory, storage, and network interfaces. Coefficients approximate how much each resource adds to a machine's baseline demand.

Such a model is useful for online control when direct component measurements are unavailable. Its accuracy depends on workload type, hardware state, and interactions that a simple linear estimate may omit.
