# Resampling Periodicity

Resampling periodicity is the repeating pattern of prediction relationships created when an interpolation grid passes through an original pixel lattice. At some output positions the value depends strongly on a particular arrangement of neighbors, and that arrangement recurs at intervals determined by the scale or rotation.

A detector estimates local linear dependencies and searches their strength for periodic structure. A localized response can expose a transformed insert, whereas an image-wide response may simply record benign resizing during publication.
