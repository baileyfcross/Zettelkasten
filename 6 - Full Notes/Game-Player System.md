# Game-Player System

The game-player system treats the game and the player as interacting subsystems of one functional whole. The game supplies a dynamic model and feedback; the player supplies intentions, decisions, actions, and learning.

Neither subsystem alone creates the played experience. Their reciprocal exchange forms [[Interactivity]], produces [[Engagement]], and supplies the higher-level whole observed and changed through the [[Designer Loop]].

This exchange can be understood as a [[Game as Conversation|conversation]]. The creator speaks through rules and context, the player responds through verbs and choices, and the system's resistance and feedback shape what each action means.
