# Local Cohomology Change of Rings

For a Noetherian R-algebra S, an ideal I of R, and an S-module M, local cohomology computed over R with support in I agrees with local cohomology over S with support in the extended ideal IS.

The result lets the ambient ring change without changing the supported cohomological information. Under flat base change, local cohomology also tensors compatibly with the new ring.
