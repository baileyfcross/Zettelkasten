# Four-Current

Four-current combines electric charge density with three-dimensional current density into one [[Four-Vector]]. In time-first notation it can be written $J=(c\rho,\mathbf{j})$, with sign and component order adjusted to the chosen convention.

Its components mix under a [[Lorentz Transformation]]. A charge distribution that is static in one frame has only a density component, while a moving observer also assigns it a current. This is the source-side counterpart of the frame-dependent split between electric and magnetic fields.

The four-divergence of $J$ is zero, which is the covariant [[Electromagnetic Continuity Equation]]. It states that changes in charge density are balanced by current flowing into or out of a region and expresses conservation of [[Electric Charge]] in every inertial frame.
