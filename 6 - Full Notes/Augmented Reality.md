# Augmented Reality

Augmented reality is an approach that uses displays, [[3D Tracking]], and other technologies to enhance a user's view of the real environment with synthetic objects or information.

AR design must account for both physical and virtual content. Display limits, changing lighting, inaccurate registration, and occlusion can make virtual information hard to interpret. Interaction should make it clear how real and virtual objects relate, while controls should avoid hiding important parts of the environment.

AR is part of the broader [[Mixed Reality]] continuum and uses a [[3D User Interface]] when tasks occur directly in a three-dimensional spatial context.
