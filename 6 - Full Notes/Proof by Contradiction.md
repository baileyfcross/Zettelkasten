# Proof by Contradiction

Proof by contradiction establishes a proposition p by assuming ¬p and deriving a statement known to be false, often a conjunction q ∧ ¬q. Because valid deductions from true assumptions remain true, the contradiction shows that the temporary assumption cannot hold.

The method can expose global impossibilities that a [[Direct Proof]] does not easily reveal, as in arguments that irrational numbers or infinitely many primes must exist.
