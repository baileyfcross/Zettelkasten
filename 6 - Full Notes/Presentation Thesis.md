# Presentation Thesis

A presentation thesis is the single claim or idea the audience should understand when the presentation ends. It gives every example, image, and spoken explanation a common purpose and provides a test for removing material that does not support the point.

The thesis should be explicit enough that the presenter can state it briefly, but the slides need not repeat the speech. A presentation is a live argument, not a document read aloud.
