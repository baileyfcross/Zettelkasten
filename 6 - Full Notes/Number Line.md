# Number Line

A number line represents ordered numbers as points on a straight line. Values increase to the right and decrease to the left, with zero separating positive and negative directions.

The representation gives geometric meaning to several algebraic ideas. Addition can be understood as directed movement, an [[Opposite Number]] as reflection across zero, and [[Absolute Value]] as distance from zero. Inequality solutions can be graphed as rays or segments, with filled and open endpoints recording whether boundary values belong to the solution set.
