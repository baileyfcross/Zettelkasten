# Reducing an Equation to Quadratic Form

Reducing an equation to quadratic form transforms an equation that does not initially look quadratic into [[Standard Form of a Quadratic Equation]]. This may require clearing denominators, expanding products, moving all terms to one side, and combining like terms.

For an equation containing (x) in a denominator, [[Cross Multiplication]] can create an (x^2) term. Values excluded by the original denominator must be recorded before transformation. Once the quadratic form is obtained, factorization or the quadratic formula produces candidates that are verified in the original equation.
