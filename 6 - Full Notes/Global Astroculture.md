# Global Astroculture

Global astroculture is the worldwide circulation and local adaptation of spaceflight imagery, advocacy, entertainment, and scientific meaning. Superpowers promoted missions through tours, publications, films, and visits, while science-fiction franchises and later national programs spread space themes far beyond Europe, Russia, and the United States.

The process is not a uniform export of one culture. Audiences could interpret achievements as human rather than bloc-specific, and new space nations connected imported technology with their own status and aspirations. At the same time, early visions often carried Euro-American assumptions about conquest, settlement, gender, and racial hierarchy that global participation could reproduce or challenge.
