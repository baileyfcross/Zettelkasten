# Storage Redundancy

Storage redundancy replicates cloud data across more than one failure boundary. Copies may be placed on separate machines, in separate facilities, or in another geographic area depending on the selected durability level.

Replication reduces the likelihood that one hardware or regional event destroys the only copy, but no service promises absolute safety. Greater separation and resilience generally increase cost, so the required failure tolerance must be chosen explicitly.
