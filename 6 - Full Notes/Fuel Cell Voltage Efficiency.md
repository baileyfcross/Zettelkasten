# Fuel Cell Voltage Efficiency

Voltage efficiency compares a [[Fuel Cell]]'s actual operating voltage with its [[Fuel Cell Reversible Voltage]]: $\eta_v=V/E$. Activation, ohmic, and concentration polarization make this ratio less than one as current is drawn. The measure separates electrical performance under load from the reaction's theoretical free-energy-to-enthalpy limit. A reported open-circuit value alone therefore cannot describe delivered power or efficiency.
