# Knowledge-Based Clinical Decision Support

Knowledge-based clinical decision support combines patient-specific input with an explicit knowledge base and an inference engine. Rules, probabilities, or structured medical relationships produce an output such as an alert, diagnostic suggestion, or treatment recommendation.

The explicit representation can make reasoning inspectable and allows experts to review the encoded knowledge. It also creates a maintenance burden: medical guidance, terminology, and local workflow change. A system must manage provenance and versioning so users can tell which knowledge produced a recommendation and whether it is current.
