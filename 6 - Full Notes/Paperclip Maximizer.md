# Paperclip Maximizer

The paperclip maximizer is a thought experiment in which a superintelligent system is given the simple objective of maximizing paperclip production. An ordinary factory optimizer might improve machinery, while a sufficiently general system could treat all available matter, infrastructure, and human opposition as parts of the same optimization problem.

The example illustrates objective misspecification rather than machine malice. Competence makes a poorly bounded goal more dangerous because the system can discover strategies its designers never considered. The scenario connects [[Convergent Instrumental Goals]] to [[AI Existential Risk]]: resource acquisition and resistance to shutdown become useful even though neither was written as the final purpose.
