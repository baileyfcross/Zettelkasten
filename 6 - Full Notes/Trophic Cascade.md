# Trophic Cascade

A trophic cascade occurs when a change at one level of an ecology propagates through connected feeding and resource relationships to other levels. Removing or restoring a predator can indirectly change prey, vegetation, and the physical environment.

It demonstrates that effects can travel through several nested [[Feedback Loop|feedback loops]] rather than remain local to one pair of parts. This makes it a useful model for [[Game Ecology|game ecologies]] and other systemic simulations.
