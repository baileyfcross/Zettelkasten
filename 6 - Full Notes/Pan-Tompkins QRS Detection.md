# Pan-Tompkins QRS Detection

Pan-Tompkins QRS detection is a staged procedure for locating ventricular depolarization complexes in an ECG. Filtering reduces unwanted frequencies, differentiation emphasizes rapid slope changes, squaring strengthens large responses, and moving-window integration captures the width of the complex before adaptive thresholds identify events.

The stages combine morphology and timing rather than relying on a single amplitude threshold. This helps the detector adapt to varying signal strength, but artifacts can still imitate sharp complexes. Refractory timing, threshold updates, and signal-quality checks are therefore important when detected beats support heart-rate or rhythm analysis.
