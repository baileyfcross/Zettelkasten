# Game Music Audio Design Document

A game music audio design document records the score's creative direction, dramatic goals, cue structure, interactive plan, technical constraints, and implementation expectations. It gives composers, designers, programmers, and producers a shared description of what the music system is meant to accomplish.

The document grows from preproduction decisions rather than from finished tracks. It can incorporate the results of [[Game Music Spotting]], identify control inputs and delivery formats, and connect the score to the broader game design. Keeping it current reduces the risk that composition and implementation proceed from different assumptions.
