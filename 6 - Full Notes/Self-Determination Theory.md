# Self-Determination Theory

Self-determination theory explains intrinsic motivation through three broad needs: autonomy, mastery, and relatedness. People seek some control over their actions, evidence that their actions can produce competence and outcomes, and connection with others.

Games can support autonomy through consequential choice, mastery through learnable challenge, and relatedness through meaningful social connection. Expected contingent rewards can undermine these needs when they replace the player's own reason for engaging.
