# Unguided Alternative Clustering

Unguided alternative clustering seeks several distinct high-quality solutions without a user specifying what the alternatives should mean. Diversity penalties or sequential objectives discourage each solution from repeating structures already found.

The approach can reveal hidden views for exploration, but mathematical distinctness does not guarantee domain relevance. Comparing features, prototypes, and stability helps determine whether an alternative is interpretable rather than merely different.
