# Hazard Ratio

A hazard ratio is the exponentiated coefficient from a Cox proportional hazards model. It compares the instantaneous event hazard for a predictor level with that of the reference, conditional on the other model terms.

A value of one represents no hazard association, while values above or below one indicate higher or lower hazard. Its confidence interval must be interpreted under proportional hazards and should not be described as a simple probability or risk ratio.
