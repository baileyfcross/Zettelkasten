# Solar Air Mass

Air mass compares the atmospheric path length traversed by a sunlight beam with the path when the Sun is directly overhead. For ordinary solar zenith angles, the source uses $AM=1/\cos\theta_z$; it is one at sea-level zenith and grows as the Sun lowers. A longer path generally increases atmospheric attenuation, helping explain changing radiation and photon flux at a site.
