# 2.5D Depth Cards and Parallax

Two-dimensional images can be placed on cards at different depths in a three-dimensional scene. A virtual camera then produces different apparent sizes and movement for the cards, including motion parallax and depth-of-field changes. This is more convincing than scaling several copies while leaving them on one flat layer, as the aircraft example in the book demonstrates. The illusion has limits: a large camera orbit can reveal that a card has no side or volume. A matte painting must choose camera motion that respects those limits.
