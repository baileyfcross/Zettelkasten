# Physical Quantity as Value and Unit

A physical quantity joins a numerical value to a unit, such as 43 meters. The number gives magnitude only after the unit specifies what scale is being used. Pryor begins his unit-conversion explanation with this pairing: a conversion changes the numerical representation and unit while preserving the intended measured quantity. An unlabeled 43 cannot be compared reliably with another length or entered safely into a compound physical equation. See [[Physical Dimension and Measurement Unit]] and [[Unit Conversion Factor]].
