# Online Analytical Processing

Online analytical processing provides interactive operations over multidimensional data, including paging, pivoting, sorting, filtering, drill-down, and drill-up. It supports fast exploration of aggregated organizational information.

OLAP can reveal candidate trends or segments through direct manipulation. Data mining extends that exploration with algorithms that infer patterns or models not specified as a sequence of manual queries.
