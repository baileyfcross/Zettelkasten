# Stylized Augmented Reality

Stylized augmented reality gives real and virtual imagery a shared artistic treatment instead of pursuing photographic realism. The camera image may be transformed into a drawn, painted, or otherwise simplified representation while generated objects use compatible rendering.

A common style can hide small photometric differences and communicate intentional visual meaning. Geometric registration and temporal consistency still matter, because a coherent style does not correct content that slides or flickers relative to the world.
