# Spatial Resolution

Spatial resolution describes how finely an [[Output Device]] can distinguish or present information across space.

For a [[Visual Display]], resolution depends on both pixel count and screen size rather than pixel count alone. Viewing distance also affects perceived resolution because individual pixels become less distinguishable at greater distances. For a [[Haptic Display]], spatial resolution is the minimum spatial separation between stimuli the device can present, which should be matched to the sensitivity of the body location being stimulated.

Higher resolution can improve detail, but its value depends on the user, task, viewing or contact conditions, and other parts of [[Display Fidelity]].
