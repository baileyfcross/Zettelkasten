# Cloud Auditing

Cloud auditing examines whether a cloud service and its use conform to agreed policies, contracts, standards, and controls. It relies on evidence such as configurations, access records, service reports, bills, test results, and remediation history.

When several services interoperate, they should be subject to compatible audit expectations and common oversight. A gap at one provider can undermine the combined process even if every other component is reviewed carefully.
