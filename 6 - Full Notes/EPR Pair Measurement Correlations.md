# EPR Pair Measurement Correlations

The Bell state

$$
|\Phi^+\rangle=\frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

is an entangled pair whose computational-basis outcomes are individually random but perfectly correlated. Measuring the first qubit yields $0$ or $1$ with equal probability and projects the pair to $|00\rangle$ or $|11\rangle$, making the second outcome match.

The correlation is not a communication channel because neither observer controls the local random result. Its significance is that the joint state predicts relationships that cannot be represented as two independent pure qubit states.
