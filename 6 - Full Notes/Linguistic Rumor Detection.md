# Linguistic Rumor Detection

Linguistic rumor detection classifies questionable information from words, phrasing, sentiment, or other natural-language features. It can identify patterns associated with uncertainty, support, denial, or sensational presentation.

Language features complement graph-based evidence because they inspect the claim rather than only its route. Their performance depends on topic, language, platform, and the labels used during training.
