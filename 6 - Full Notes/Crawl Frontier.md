# Crawl Frontier

A crawl frontier is the collection of discovered URLs that a [[Web Crawler]] has not yet visited. Adding newly found links and tracking visited pages prevents endless revisits while depth, domain, date, or content rules determine which candidates remain eligible.

The frontier encodes the practical scope of a collection. Its ordering and filters affect which pages are reached first and which may never be observed before a crawl stops, so those decisions belong in the collection record.
