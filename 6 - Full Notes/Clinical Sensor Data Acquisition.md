# Clinical Sensor Data Acquisition

Clinical sensor data acquisition collects measurements from devices whose sampling rates, interfaces, reliability, and placement may differ. Sources include bedside physiological monitors, wearable activity sensors, environmental devices, and human observations.

The acquisition design determines what later analysis can know. Sampling too slowly can hide a transient event, clocks that are not synchronized can invent or erase temporal relationships, and a detached sensor can resemble an abnormal patient. Metadata about device state, units, location, and time must therefore travel with the measurements.
