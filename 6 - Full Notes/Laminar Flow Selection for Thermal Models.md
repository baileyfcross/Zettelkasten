# Laminar Flow Selection for Thermal Models

A laminar flow model assumes orderly viscous motion without resolving turbulent fluctuations. For internal pipe flow, Reynolds number compares inertia with viscosity and provides a regime check; the book's pipe examples keep the value below about 2,300.

Because density and viscosity may vary with temperature and pressure, a velocity that begins in the laminar range may not remain there throughout a heated system. [[Thermal Model Verification and Sensitivity|sensitivity checks]] should therefore examine the regime criterion across the solved property range.
