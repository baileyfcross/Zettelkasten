# TEM Threshold Bond Dimension

The TEM threshold bond dimension $\chi^*$ is the smallest dimension that exactly represents the first-order Taylor expansion of the inverse-noise map. For the studied brickwork Clifford circuits, the number of propagated local-noise terms crossing a matrix-product-operator link grows with the link's spacetime causal area, producing the empirical scaling $\chi^*\approx L^2/2$.

Increasing $\chi$ below this threshold rapidly improves the compression error because first-order noisy directions are still being recovered. Beyond it, improvements slow because only second-order and smaller corrections remain. The threshold is independent of qubit count while a causal cone expands normally, but can cross over toward $NL$ scaling when a very deep circuit's cone becomes a width-limited stripe.
