# CAF Operations Perspective

The CAF operations perspective develops the capabilities needed to run cloud workloads and deliver agreed service outcomes. It includes observability, incident and problem management, change and release management, configuration, availability, continuity, and operational automation.

Migration changes how systems are operated as well as where they run. Teams should define ownership, service objectives, runbooks, telemetry, and learning loops so operational knowledge evolves with the platform instead of remaining tied to former infrastructure practices.
