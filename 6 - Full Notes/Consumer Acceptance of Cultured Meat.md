# Consumer Acceptance of Cultured Meat

Consumer acceptance of cultured meat is willingness to try, buy, and regularly use a product made through cell culture. Perceived naturalness, safety, ethics, environmental benefit, health, price, taste, and familiarity all influence the decision.

The source notes that framing the product mainly as high technology can produce more resistance than presenting practical benefits or connections to familiar cultured foods.
