# Data Quality

Data quality is the fitness of data for the decision or analysis it supports. Accuracy, relevance, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and adequate context determine whether a large collection can produce useful conclusions.

Volume does not compensate for poor quality. Errors and irrelevant observations can be amplified by aggregation, while uncertain provenance makes it difficult to explain or correct an outcome.

For cluster analysis, quality is inseparable from representation. Unrelated features distort proximity, incompatible data types can invalidate a method, and stale domains or unenforced business rules can corrupt results. The source emphasizes accuracy, reliability, timeliness, relevance, and completeness as complementary dimensions maintained through ongoing cleansing.
