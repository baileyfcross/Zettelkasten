# Stokes Theorem

Stokes theorem equates the circulation of a vector field around a closed boundary curve with the surface integral of the field's curl across any smooth surface spanning that curve. It turns a line integral into a measure of accumulated local rotation.

The curve and surface orientations must follow the right-hand rule. The result shows that, when the necessary conditions hold, changing the spanning surface does not change the boundary circulation because each surface has the same oriented edge.
