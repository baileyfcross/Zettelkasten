# Axially Loaded Member

An axially loaded member is a rod-like structural component whose applied forces act along its longitudinal axis. Under the small-deformation assumptions used in the primer, the member primarily develops normal stress and axial displacement.

A one-dimensional bar mesh models this behavior with translational nodal degrees of freedom. Constant, varying, and stepped cross sections change how stiffness and stress are distributed along the member.
