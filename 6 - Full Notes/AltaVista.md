# AltaVista

AltaVista was a fast, focused search engine created within Digital Equipment Corporation to demonstrate its Alpha processor. Its clean interface and rapid results made it popular with knowledgeable Web users.

DEC treated the service primarily as support for hardware rather than as a valuable information business. Competitors overtook it as Web search became a central industry.
