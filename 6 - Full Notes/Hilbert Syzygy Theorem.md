# Hilbert Syzygy Theorem

The graded Hilbert syzygy theorem states that every finitely generated graded module over K[x₁,…,xₙ] has a finite graded free resolution of length at most n.

Any two minimal graded resolutions are isomorphic. Thus polynomial rings impose a uniform bound on how many successive layers of [[Syzygy Module]]s are needed to describe a finitely generated module.
