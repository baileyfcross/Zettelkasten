# Adequacy and Fluency Evaluation

Adequacy evaluates how much information from a source or professional reference is preserved in a machine translation. Fluency evaluates whether the target sentences are well formed, readable, and natural in context. Separating them prevents smooth but inaccurate output from receiving the same judgment as faithful but awkward output.

Human raters can apply the two criteria more consistently than a single impression of overall quality, yet the scores remain subjective. Reviewers differ in what errors they tolerate and how they weigh missing information, grammar, terminology, and style. The method is therefore informative but expensive and difficult to reproduce at the scale of frequent system development.
