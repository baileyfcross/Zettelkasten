# Content-Driven Game

A content-driven game relies primarily on authored sequences of levels, events, dialogue, or other material to carry the player forward. Its experience tends to follow a limited set of intended paths.

Because each additional experience requires more authored content, the approach can create a content treadmill and limited replayability. It contrasts with a [[Systemic Game]], though most games combine authored content with systemic structure.
