# Water Electrolysis

Water electrolysis uses electrical energy to separate water into hydrogen at the cathode and oxygen at the anode. The source describes alkaline, membrane, and high-temperature steam versions and gives the net reaction as $2H_2O+energy\rightarrow2H_2+O_2$. If electricity comes from intermittent solar or wind supply, produced hydrogen can shift some of that energy into a stored fuel for later use.
