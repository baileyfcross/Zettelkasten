# Homogeneity of Variance

Homogeneity of variance is the assumption that comparison groups have the same population variance. It underlies the pooled form of the [[Student t-Test]] and standard one-way ANOVA calculations.

Group standard deviations, box plots, residual plots, and formal tests can inform the judgment. Formal variance tests can detect trivial departures in very large samples, so statistical significance should be considered alongside the size and practical consequence of the difference.
