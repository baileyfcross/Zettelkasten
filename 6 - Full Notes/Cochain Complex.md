# Cochain Complex

A cochain complex is a sequence C⁰ → C¹ → C² → ··· whose consecutive maps compose to zero. Its indices increase in the direction of the maps, unlike a [[Chain Complex]].

The quotient of cocycles by coboundaries gives a [[Cohomology Group]]. Injective resolutions and local cohomology are naturally expressed through cochain complexes.
