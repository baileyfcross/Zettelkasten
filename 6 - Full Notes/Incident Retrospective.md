# Incident Retrospective

An incident retrospective reconstructs the timeline, contributing conditions, available signals, responses, and recovery after a live failure. Its purpose is to strengthen the system, not identify a person to blame.

Useful outcomes include clearer detection, safer deployment, new tests, reduced response time, and changes to organizational conditions. The review turns operational failure into shared product knowledge.
