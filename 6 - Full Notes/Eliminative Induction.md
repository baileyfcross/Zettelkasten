# Eliminative Induction

Eliminative induction compares cases under varied circumstances to rule out candidate explanations and isolate relationships that may be causal. It seeks informative differences rather than relying only on the repeated presence of a pattern.

The approach better resembles experimental and observational scientific practice than simple enumeration. Its conclusions remain revisable as new variations and evidence test previously unexamined alternatives.
