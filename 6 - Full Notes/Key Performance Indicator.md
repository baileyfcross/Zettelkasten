# Key Performance Indicator

A key performance indicator is a selected measure used to judge whether a product change advances an important outcome, such as participation or retention. It turns a product hypothesis into evidence that can be observed after deployment.

A KPI is a proxy, not the purpose of the game. Optimizing a short-term number without context can harm player value or hide delayed effects, so metrics require interpretation and multiple forms of feedback.
