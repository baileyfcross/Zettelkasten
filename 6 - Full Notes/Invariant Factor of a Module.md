# Invariant Factor of a Module

Invariant factors are the nonunit diagonal entries in a [[Smith Normal Form]], ordered by divisibility. They determine the cyclic quotient components in the decomposition of a finitely generated module over a principal ideal domain.

Although relation matrices depend on chosen generators, invariant factors do not. They therefore provide canonical data for comparing module presentations and, over a polynomial ring, for analyzing a linear operator.
