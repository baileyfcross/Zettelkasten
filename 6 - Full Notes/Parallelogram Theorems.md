# Parallelogram Theorems

In a parallelogram, opposite sides are equal, opposite angles are equal, adjacent angles are supplementary, and the diagonals bisect one another. A diagonal separates the figure into congruent triangles, allowing these properties to be proved from parallel-line angle relations and [[Triangle Congruence]]. Converse tests use the same properties to establish that a quadrilateral is a parallelogram.
