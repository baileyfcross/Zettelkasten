# Planar Process

Jean Hoerni’s planar process fabricated flat silicon transistors beneath a protective oxide surface. The geometry allowed devices and their interconnections to be patterned across a wafer.

This manufacturing method made Robert Noyce’s integrated-circuit design practical. It became a foundation of modern semiconductor production.
