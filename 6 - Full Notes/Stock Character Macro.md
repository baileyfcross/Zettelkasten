# Stock Character Macro

A stock character macro is an image macro organized around a recurring persona with predictable traits. Each caption presents a new situation that confirms, complicates, or reverses the character's established behavioral script.

The genre creates a compact symbolic universe: knowing the character lets an audience infer the expected joke before reading every word. Successful variants balance consistency with novelty, using a stable portrait and persona to classify recognizable social behavior across many situations.
