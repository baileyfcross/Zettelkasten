# Human Spaceflight Political Economy

Human spaceflight political economy is the interaction among prestige, public budgets, regional employment, agency survival, contractors, and mission goals in sustaining crewed programs. Scientific return is only one part of the justification.

Facilities and workforces built during Apollo and the missile race created constituencies that favored follow-on projects. Agencies sometimes understated initial cost to win approval, distributed work among centers and partners, or retained vehicle elements to protect jobs. These arrangements preserve capability but can also make a program expensive, slow, and resistant to cancellation when its stated destination or schedule changes.
