# Newton's Third Law

Newton's third law states that when body A exerts a force on body B, body B simultaneously exerts an equal and opposite force on body A. The pair acts on different bodies, so the forces do not cancel on either object's [[Free-Body Diagram]]. The law underlies momentum conservation in isolated interactions.
