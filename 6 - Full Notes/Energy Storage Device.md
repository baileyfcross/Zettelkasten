# Energy Storage Device

An energy storage device moves electricity across time by charging during surplus and discharging during shortage or high price. In a green data center, storage can smooth variable [[Renewable Energy Supply]] and reduce peak grid demand.

Capacity, charge and discharge rates, efficiency losses, degradation, and initial state constrain how much flexibility the device actually provides.
