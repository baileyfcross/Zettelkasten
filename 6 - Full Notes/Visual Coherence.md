# Visual Coherence

Visual coherence is the perceptual agreement that makes real and virtual parts of an augmented image appear to occupy one scene. Position, depth ordering, illumination, image formation, and style must provide compatible evidence rather than revealing two unrelated layers.

The AR rendering pipeline acquires a model of the real scene, establishes geometric and photometric registration, composites real and virtual imagery, and presents the result. Errors in any stage can break the apparent relationship.
