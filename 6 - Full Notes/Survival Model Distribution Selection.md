# Survival Model Distribution Selection

Survival model distribution selection compares the observed event-time experience with plausible parametric curves and considers whether a semiparametric Cox model is more defensible. Visual overlays, AIC, and hazard diagnostics provide complementary evidence.

The choice can materially change coefficient direction and interpretation. When reasonable distributions disagree or event times are unreliable, analysts should treat the instability as evidence about the limits of the analysis rather than select the preferred conclusion.
