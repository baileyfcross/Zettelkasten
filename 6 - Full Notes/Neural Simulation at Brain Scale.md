# Neural Simulation at Brain Scale

Neural simulation at brain scale must update enormous numbers of interacting units while preserving the timing and communication that make their collective behavior possible. The task is naturally parallel, but communication, memory movement, and synchronization can become as limiting as arithmetic.

Conventional processors and graphics processors can simulate portions of this workload, while neuromorphic hardware seeks a closer match to neural organization. More computation does not remove uncertainty about the correct model: scaling a biologically incomplete neuron or synapse model can produce a larger simulation without producing a faithful [[Whole Brain Emulation]].
