# Hyperboloid of One Sheet

A hyperboloid of one sheet can be written in standard form as

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}-\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1.$$

Horizontal sections are ellipses whose semiaxes grow with $|z|$, so the surface remains connected through its narrow central section. Vertical coordinate-plane sections are hyperbolas.
