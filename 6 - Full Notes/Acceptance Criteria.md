# Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria are testable conditions that clarify when a user story provides its intended behavior or value. They capture important details discovered through conversation without turning the story into an implementation script.

Criteria help the Product Owner, developers, and testers form a shared understanding before work begins. They complement the team's general [[Definition of Done]] with conditions specific to one item.
