# Thermoelectric Material Selection

Selecting generator legs means seeking a large [[Seebeck Coefficient]], good electrical conduction, and low thermal leakage at the intended hot-side temperature. The source favors suitable p-type and n-type semiconductors over ordinary metals and also requires strength, corrosion resistance, and compatible conducting bridges. Doping and geometry can improve the [[Thermoelectric Figure of Merit]], but thermal stress can still limit service life.
