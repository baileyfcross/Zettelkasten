# Ethics by Design

Ethics by design integrates ethical requirements into a technology's architecture and development process. Instead of adding a policy statement after completion, a team turns values into choices about data, permissions, constraints, interfaces, logging, testing, and human oversight.

The method can improve [[AI Traceability]], privacy, safety, and accountability, but a value must be interpreted before it can be engineered. Design cannot eliminate conflicts among principles or decide the legitimate purpose of a system by itself. Ethical governance must therefore connect technical features to participation, institutional rules, and the continuing ability to challenge deployment.
