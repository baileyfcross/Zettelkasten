# Liferay Portal Social Model

Liferay supplies portal concepts for users, sites, organizations, roles, content, and modular applications. Its social model adds relationships, activities, and collaboration to these managed portal identities and spaces rather than treating social interaction as a detached public feed.

Because the portal already knows its members and permissions, social features can participate in enterprise governance. An activity or relationship can be visible within the appropriate site, and an application can reuse the portal's authentication and authorization instead of building a parallel account system. [[Liferay Social Collaboration]] covers the participant-facing features, while [[Liferay Sharing and OpenSocial Gadgets]] covers reuse and outward sharing. Together they exemplify [[Enterprise Social Portal Integration]].
