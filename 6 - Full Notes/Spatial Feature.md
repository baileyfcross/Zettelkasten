# Spatial Feature

A spatial feature is a geographic entity represented by geometry together with descriptive attributes. A feature may be a point, line, polygon, or a multipart collection of these forms. In the [[Simple Feature]] model, each row of a data frame represents one feature and a geometry list-column stores its shape. Mapping joins the feature's attributes to visual properties while its geometry determines position and extent.
