# Definition of Done

A Definition of Done is the shared quality threshold every completed increment must satisfy. It can cover integration, testing, asset validation, performance, platform behavior, and any other conditions needed for stakeholders to inspect the result honestly.

The standard is negotiated and strengthened as the product matures. It makes remaining debt visible but does not claim that every increment is ready for final commercial deployment.
