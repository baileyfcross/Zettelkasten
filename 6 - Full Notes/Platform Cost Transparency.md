# Platform Cost Transparency

Platform cost transparency connects infrastructure and service charges to the products, teams, environments, and usage patterns that caused them. It lets engineering, finance, procurement, and business stakeholders discuss value and tradeoffs with shared evidence rather than treating the cloud bill as an unexplained central expense.

Transparency depends on consistent allocation metadata, provider billing data, ownership, and utilization signals. Reports should expose meaningful units and trends without overwhelming users with provider detail. The goal is not public blame or indiscriminate reduction; it is timely feedback that lets teams balance reliability, performance, speed, and cost in their designs.
