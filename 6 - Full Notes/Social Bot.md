# Social Bot

A social bot is an automated or partially automated account that participates in an online social system. Bots can provide benign services or manipulate attention, conversation, and apparent consensus.

Detection is difficult because behavior ranges from simple automation to coordinated, human-like activity. Feature engineering combines account, content, temporal, and network evidence rather than relying on a single visible cue.
