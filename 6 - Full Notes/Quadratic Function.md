# Quadratic Function

A quadratic function has the form $f(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ with $a\ne0$. Its graph is a parabola whose opening direction is determined by the sign of $a$, whose vertex gives an extremum, and whose real roots are the horizontal-axis intersections.

Evaluating the expression over an R vector produces the curve coordinates, allowing roots and the vertex to be checked against algebraic calculations. The coefficient meanings belong to the chosen coordinate scale, so transformations of $x$ change their numerical values.
