# Chain Complex

A chain complex is a sequence of modules and homomorphisms ··· → Cₙ₊₁ → Cₙ → Cₙ₋₁ → ··· in which every consecutive composite is zero.

The zero-composite condition makes every [[Boundary in a Chain Complex]] a [[Cycle in a Chain Complex]]. Their quotient is the [[Homology Group]], which measures the failure of the complex to be exact.
