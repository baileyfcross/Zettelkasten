# Five Whys

Five Whys is a root-cause technique that repeatedly asks why an observed condition occurred, following the causal chain beneath the initial symptom. Five is a prompt for sufficient depth, not a mandatory stopping point.

The method works best with evidence and multiple perspectives. Used as an interrogation or a search for a person to blame, it conceals system causes instead of revealing them.
