# Historical Automaton

A historical automaton is a self-moving mechanical representation of life created before electronic robotics. Clockwork figures, mechanical animals, and staged chess players demonstrated movement and apparent intelligence through gears, stored sequences, or concealed human assistance.

These machines joined engineering to spectacle. Vaucanson's mechanical duck, for example, appeared to eat and digest even though its intake and output were mechanically separated. Such artifacts reveal that audiences have long judged artificial creatures by performed behavior, not internal mechanism. They form an important precursor to the definitional uncertainty in [[Robot Definition Boundary]].
