# Unity GameObject

A Unity GameObject is the basic entity placed in a scene. Cameras, characters, lights, scenery, and empty organizational objects share the same container identity; their actual capabilities come from the [[Unity Component|components]] attached to them.

Every GameObject has a [[Unity Transform]], while optional components supply rendering, collision, physics, behavior, audio, or custom data. This composition model avoids defining a different deep class hierarchy for every combination of capabilities and makes behavior visible and editable in the [[Unity Inspector]].
