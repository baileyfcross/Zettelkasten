# Runway Friction and Crosswind Limits

Runway condition indices describe braking capability on dry, wet, snowy, slushy, or icy surfaces. Reduced friction lengthens stopping distance and can lower the crosswind that can be handled without losing directional control. The aircraft's demonstrated crosswind component is evidence from flight testing, not a universal guarantee, so surface condition and pilot capability still shape the decision.
