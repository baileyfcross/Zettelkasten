# Area Under the ROC Curve

Area under the ROC curve summarizes how often a classifier ranks a randomly chosen positive case above a randomly chosen negative case across thresholds. A value near one indicates strong separation, while one-half corresponds to chance ranking.

AUC does not specify an operating threshold or encode asymmetric error costs, so it complements rather than replaces decision-specific measures.
