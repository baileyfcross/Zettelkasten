# Gröbner Basis

A Gröbner basis is a finite subset of a polynomial ideal whose leading terms generate the ideal's [[Initial Ideal]] for a chosen [[Monomial Order]]. It generates the original ideal as well.

Reduction by a Gröbner basis gives a decisive ideal-membership test: a polynomial lies in the ideal when its remainder is zero. [[Buchberger's Algorithm]] constructs a basis by resolving incompatible leading-term reductions.
