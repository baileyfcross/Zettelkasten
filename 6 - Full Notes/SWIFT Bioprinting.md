# SWIFT Bioprinting

Sacrificial writing into functional tissue, or SWIFT, prints a removable ink inside a dense cell-and-matrix support material. Removing the ink leaves hollow channels through which culture medium can be perfused.

The method addresses the oxygen and nutrient limits of thick constructs by building a vascular-like transport network into the living tissue.
