# Phonetic Transcription

Phonetic transcription maps normalized words to a sequence of speech sounds for synthesis. It must handle ordinary vocabulary, names, abbreviations, and words whose pronunciation depends on context.

The transcription guides the [[Speech Synthesizer]] in producing the segmental content of the utterance. Prosodic choices such as [[Intonation]] determine how those sounds unfold over time.
