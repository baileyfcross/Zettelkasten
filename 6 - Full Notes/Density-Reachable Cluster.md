# Density-Reachable Cluster

Density reachability defines a cluster through chains of sufficiently populated neighborhoods. A core observation has enough neighbors within a fixed radius, and another observation is density-reachable when a sequence of core-mediated steps connects them.

This relation allows dense regions of arbitrary shape to become clusters while isolated observations remain noise. A single radius and minimum-neighbor threshold can struggle when different parts of the dataset have substantially different densities.
