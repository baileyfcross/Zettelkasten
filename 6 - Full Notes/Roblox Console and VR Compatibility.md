# Roblox Console and VR Compatibility

Console and virtual-reality players interact with Roblox through different displays, controllers, focus systems, and physical expectations. An experience should avoid assuming a mouse, keyboard, or touch screen and should expose actions through platform-appropriate input paths.

Interfaces need navigable focus and legible placement, while camera behavior must remain comfortable and controllable. VR requires special care with scale, motion, and view changes that may disorient the player. Compatibility testing complements [[Roblox Mobile UI Compatibility]] and turns cross-platform support into an input and presentation design problem, not merely a publishing checkbox.
