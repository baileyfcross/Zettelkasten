# FinOps

FinOps is a collaborative operating practice for making cloud value and cost visible to engineering, finance, and business stakeholders. It replaces a purely after-the-fact bill review with shared ownership, timely allocation data, forecasts, and decisions that balance performance, reliability, speed, and expenditure.

The practice is iterative rather than a one-time cost-cutting project. The [[FinOps Lifecycle]] makes spending understandable, identifies worthwhile improvements, and embeds their execution into normal engineering work. Unit economics and business outcomes matter more than indiscriminately minimizing the total bill.
