# Hilbert Series

The Hilbert series of a bounded-below graded module is the formal series P_M(t) = ΣH_M(n)tⁿ. Its coefficients are the values of the [[Hilbert Function]].

For finitely generated graded modules over Noetherian graded rings, the source shows that this series is rational. Free resolutions express it as an alternating combination of shifted free-module series.
