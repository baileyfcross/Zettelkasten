# Polarization

Polarization confines the oscillation of a [[Transverse Wave]] to a particular direction or pattern in the plane perpendicular to propagation. A linear polarizing filter transmits one electric-field component. Malus's law gives transmitted intensity $I=I_0\cos^2\theta$ for polarized light incident on an analyzer at relative angle $\theta$.
