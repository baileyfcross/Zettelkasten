# Virtual and Potential Temperature

Virtual temperature is the temperature dry air would need in order to have the same pressure and density as a parcel of moist air. Potential temperature is the temperature a parcel would reach if brought adiabatically to a reference pressure of 1,000 millibars. These transformations make air parcels comparable despite differences in moisture or pressure and support analysis of [[Atmospheric Lapse Rates and Stability]].
