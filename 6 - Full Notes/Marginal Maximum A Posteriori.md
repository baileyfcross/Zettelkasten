# Marginal Maximum A Posteriori

Marginal maximum a posteriori inference first sums or integrates designated free variables and then maximizes the resulting marginal posterior over searched variables. It differs from maximizing all variables jointly and discarding some afterward.

The distinction matters because marginalization rewards an assignment supported by a large region of alternatives, whereas joint maximization may favor one sharp configuration with little surrounding probability mass.
