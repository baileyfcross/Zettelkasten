# Data as a Nonrivalrous Good

Data are nonrivalrous because one party can use a copy without preventing another party from using the same information. Copying therefore differs from taking a physical object whose possession is exclusive.

Nonrivalry complicates the justification and scope of a [[Data Ownership Right]]. Exclusive control may create incentives or bargaining power, but it can also restrict socially useful reuse without being necessary to preserve the original holder's use.
