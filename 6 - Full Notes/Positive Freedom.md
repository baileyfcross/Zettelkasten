# Positive Freedom

Positive freedom is the practical ability to act as a self-directing person, not merely the absence of an immediate prohibition. It concerns the conditions that make meaningful agency possible.

Privacy can support positive freedom by protecting the development and exercise of autonomous purposes. This connects data protection to the structure of personhood rather than treating it only as secrecy or control over isolated facts.
