# Expansion-Reduction Feature Engineering

Expansion-reduction feature engineering first generates a broad collection of transformed variables and then removes weak or redundant candidates. Expansion increases representational possibilities; reduction controls dimensionality and overfitting.

The approach is simple and parallelizable, but exhaustive expansion becomes infeasible with many functions or composition levels. Guided generation and early filtering are therefore central to scale.
