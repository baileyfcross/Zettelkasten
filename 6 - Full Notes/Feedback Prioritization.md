# Feedback Prioritization

Feedback prioritization clusters and ranks requests using project objectives, urgency, affected users, market signals, risk, and expected value. Language models can extract themes from large volumes of unstructured feedback and connect them to candidate work.

The ranking criteria should be explicit and periodically reviewed. Popularity, emotional language, or data volume can otherwise crowd out strategically important minority needs or compliance obligations.
