# Complicated Process

A complicated process connects many parts in a linear or sequential chain. It may be difficult to understand or execute, yet its later behavior does not loop back to change the conditions that produced it.

This separates complication from complexity. A [[Complex System]] contains feedback loops, so its history and interactions reshape future state in ways a merely linear process does not.
