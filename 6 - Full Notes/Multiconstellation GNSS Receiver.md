# Multiconstellation GNSS Receiver

A multiconstellation GNSS receiver observes satellites from more than one navigation system, such as GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, or BeiDou. More visible transmitters can improve availability and geometry in urban canyons, at high latitudes, or where part of the sky is blocked. Combining systems requires compatible radio bands, signal processing, coordinate frames, and time-offset handling; the receiver must reconcile those differences rather than simply count satellites.
