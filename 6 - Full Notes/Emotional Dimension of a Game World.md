# Emotional Dimension of a Game World

The emotional dimension specifies both the feelings present among characters and, more importantly, the feelings the designer hopes the world will evoke in the player. Mood emerges from events, stakes, art, sound, pacing, and the player's relationship to the world.

A coherent emotional design aligns these elements rather than relying on a story statement alone. The intended response must also fit the audience and the actions the game repeatedly asks the player to perform.
