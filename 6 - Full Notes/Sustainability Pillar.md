# Sustainability Pillar

The sustainability pillar seeks to minimize the environmental impact of running a cloud workload. It encourages measuring impact, maximizing utilization, matching resources to demand, choosing efficient services and regions where requirements permit, and reducing unnecessary storage or computation.

Efficiency improvements often align with cost and performance goals, but sustainability remains a distinct design objective. Teams should define measurable proxies, avoid idle capacity, and revisit architecture as hardware, services, and workload patterns improve.
