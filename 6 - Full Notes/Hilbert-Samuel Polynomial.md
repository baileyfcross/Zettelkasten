# Hilbert-Samuel Polynomial

For an ideal of definition I in a Noetherian local ring and a finite module M, the length ℓ(M/IⁿM) agrees with a polynomial in n for all sufficiently large n. This is the Hilbert-Samuel polynomial.

Its degree is the [[Dimension of a Module]]. Its leading coefficient, after normalization by a factorial, defines the [[Hilbert-Samuel Multiplicity]].
