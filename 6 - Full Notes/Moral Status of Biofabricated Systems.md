# Moral Status of Biofabricated Systems

The moral status of a biofabricated system concerns whether and why its interests deserve ethical consideration. Being composed of living cells does not automatically make an object an organism or a subject of experience.

Capacity for pain, awareness, autonomous goals, biological integration, dependence on support, and similarity to protected beings may become more relevant as constructs grow more complex.
