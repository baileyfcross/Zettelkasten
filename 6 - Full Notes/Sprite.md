# Sprite

A sprite is a two-dimensional image rendered as a discrete game object or visual element. Its texture, position, scale, orientation, and drawing order determine how it appears in the scene.

Animated sprites select successive source images over time, often from a [[Sprite Sheet]]. Sprites remain the primary visual building block of many 2D games even though modern displays and assets are much larger than their early counterparts.
