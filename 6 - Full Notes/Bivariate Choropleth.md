# Bivariate Choropleth

A bivariate choropleth encodes two attributes within the same regional fill. The source combines hue for a categorical variable with transparency or lightness for a quantitative variable, producing a two-dimensional legend. The technique can reveal joint spatial patterns but quickly becomes difficult to decode, so category and value levels should be limited. Separate [[Small-Multiple Choropleth|small multiples]] may be preferable when either variable needs detailed reading.
