# Reputation System Attack

A reputation system attack manipulates aggregated trust evidence rather than improving the underlying behavior. Newcomer attacks repeatedly discard bad identities, self-boosting raises an attacker's score, bad-mouthing lowers a target, and on-off behavior alternates honesty with abuse.

Robust models consider rater reliability, time, uncertainty, collusion, and the cost of creating a fresh identity.
