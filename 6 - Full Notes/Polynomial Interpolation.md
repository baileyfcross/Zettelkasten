# Polynomial Interpolation

Polynomial interpolation constructs a polynomial that passes through given data points. For $n+1$ distinct input values, there is a unique [[Interpolating Polynomial]] of degree at most $n$.

[[Lagrange Interpolation]] and [[Newton Divided Difference Interpolation]] express the same polynomial in different forms. Numerical behavior can deteriorate at high degree or with poorly placed nodes.
