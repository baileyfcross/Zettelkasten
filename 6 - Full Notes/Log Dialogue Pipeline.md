# Log Dialogue Pipeline

A log dialogue pipeline turns raw operational telemetry into a bounded exchange in which engineers can ask for patterns, correlations, and evidence-backed explanations. Collection and normalization occur first; time-window chunking, metadata, retrieval, and model interpretation then compress the relevant material without replacing the original records.

Useful prompts include host, service, incident window, and required evidence, while structured output separates observations from hypotheses and recommended checks. The purpose is faster human investigation, not conversational novelty: every conclusion should remain traceable to logs, metrics, traces, or approved documentation.
