# AWS CloudOps Provisioning and Orchestration Pillar

The provisioning and orchestration pillar delivers application infrastructure in a repeatable, immutable, and governed form. [[AWS CloudFormation Stack|AWS CloudFormation]], [[AWS Service Catalog]], [[AWS Proton]], and the [[AWS Cloud Development Kit]] operate at different abstraction levels, from resource templates to approved products and platform interfaces. Versioned definitions make environments reproducible and reviewable while reducing configuration differences caused by manual setup.
