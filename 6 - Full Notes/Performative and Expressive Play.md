# Performative and Expressive Play

Performative play turns playing into an act for an audience, whether spectators are physically present, online, or represented by other participants. Expressive play lets players communicate identity, style, feeling, or authorship through choices inside the system.

The two forms can overlap without being identical. A private customization can be expressive without an audience, while a public feat can be performative even when it offers little personal authorship.
