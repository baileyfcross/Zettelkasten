# Technical Replicate

A technical replicate repeats measurement of the same biological material to characterize variability introduced by preparation, instruments, or measurement procedures. It does not represent an independently sampled biological unit.

Comparing technical with [[Biological Replicate|biological replicates]] helps separate measurement noise from genuine variation among organisms or specimens. Treating technical replicates as independent biological samples would overstate the effective sample size.
