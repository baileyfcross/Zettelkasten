# Beam Deflection

Beam deflection is transverse displacement of the member's reference axis under loading. Its magnitude and shape depend on load placement, support conditions, elastic modulus, section moment of inertia, and span.

A finite element solution gives deflection at nodes and interpolates values within each beam element. Comparison with analytical solutions helps assess whether the mesh resolves distributed loading adequately.
