# Wilcoxon Rank Sum Test

The Wilcoxon rank sum test compares two independent groups after replacing their combined measurements with ranks. It is equivalent to the Mann-Whitney test and avoids basing the comparison directly on sample means and standard deviations.

This rank-based construction can be less sensitive to extreme observations than a [[Student t-Test]]. It evaluates a distributional ordering rather than automatically testing equality of means, so interpretation depends on the shapes of the two group distributions.
