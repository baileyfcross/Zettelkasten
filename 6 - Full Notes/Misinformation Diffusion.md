# Misinformation Diffusion

Misinformation diffusion is the network process through which inaccurate claims move from senders to spreaders and receivers. Exposure affects belief and trust but does not guarantee that every contacted person adopts the claim.

Network connectivity, local leaders, communities, and forceful individuals alter the eventual consensus. This makes belief-exchange models a useful complement to epidemic-style information-spreading models.
