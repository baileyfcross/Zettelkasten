# Administrative Censoring

Administrative censoring ends observation at a prespecified study boundary, such as age 30, age 50, or a fixed number of days after an index date. Participants without the event by that boundary receive the cutoff or their earlier last-observed time.

Changing the boundary changes both the event indicator and follow-up time. A window should be selected for scientific relevance and credible exposure timing rather than chosen after inspecting which cutoff gives the strongest result.
