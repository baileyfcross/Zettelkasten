# Third-Party Service Integrator Model

A third-party service integrator model appoints a specialist intermediary to coordinate cloud services and providers on the organization's behalf. The integrator can manage service relationships, integration, contracts, reporting, and consolidated billing.

Central IT then deals primarily with the integrator while retaining overall oversight. The model reduces some internal coordination work but adds dependency on the integrator's ability to represent the organization's requirements accurately.
