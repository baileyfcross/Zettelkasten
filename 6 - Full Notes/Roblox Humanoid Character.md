# Roblox Humanoid Character

A Humanoid gives a Roblox character model standard player-like behavior and state, including health, movement, jumping, naming, death, and animation-related properties. Character rigs commonly use R6 or R15 structures, which differ in their number of body parts and articulation.

Scripts can read Humanoid properties and connect to its events to implement damage, respawning, movement changes, and character-specific rules. Because the Humanoid expects a compatible model hierarchy, custom characters must preserve the required parts and joints. Their motion assets are prepared through [[Roblox Animation Rig Requirements]].
