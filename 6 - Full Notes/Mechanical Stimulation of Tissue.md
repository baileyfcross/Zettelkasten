# Mechanical Stimulation of Tissue

Mechanical stimulation of tissue applies load, stretch, pressure, or fluid-driven force during culture. Cells sense these cues and can change alignment, matrix production, differentiation, and functional strength.

Conditioning an engineered meniscus, heart, or bone construct with physiologically relevant loading helps its internal organization approach the tissue it is intended to replace.
