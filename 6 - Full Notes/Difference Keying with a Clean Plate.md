# Difference Keying with a Clean Plate

Difference keying compares a shot containing the subject with a matching clean plate of the same scene without the subject. The differences provide a candidate matte even when the background is not a conventional chroma screen. This depends on the two images aligning in camera, lighting, and exposure; unrelated changes can be mistaken for foreground. The clean plate also has another use in [[Wire Removal with Clean Plates]], where it supplies pixels behind an object removed by a roto window.
