# Animal-Derived Serum

Animal-derived serum is a blood fraction added to culture medium because it contains proteins, attachment factors, hormones, and growth signals that support many cell types. Its complex composition makes it broadly useful.

Serum varies between donor animals and production batches, complicates reproducibility, and maintains reliance on animals, which motivates [[Serum-Free Medium]].
