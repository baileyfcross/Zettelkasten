# Attention Guidance

Attention guidance is the use of composition, motion, threat, contrast, sound, and established rules to lead a player toward important information. It lets the design make something noticeable without removing control of the [[Game Camera]].

Guidance is strongest when the cue already matters to play. A familiar indicator of incoming fire can turn the player's view toward both the threat and a nearby event, preserving the relationship between the player's decision and what is seen.
