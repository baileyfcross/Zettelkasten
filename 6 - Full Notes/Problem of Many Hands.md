# Problem of Many Hands

The problem of many hands arises when an outcome results from the contributions of so many people that individual responsibility becomes difficult to identify. An AI system may involve data collectors, labelers, model developers, managers, vendors, operators, and institutional decision makers across a long history.

Distributed contribution does not eliminate accountability. It requires explicit roles, records of important choices, and responsibility matched to each actor's authority and knowledge. Organizational responsibility is especially important when no single participant can understand the whole system but the organization chooses to deploy and benefit from it.
