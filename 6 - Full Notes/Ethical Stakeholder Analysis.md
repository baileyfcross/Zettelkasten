# Ethical Stakeholder Analysis

Ethical stakeholder analysis begins by stating the decision, listing direct and indirect stakeholders, identifying rights, duties, legal constraints, risks, and possible actions, and then comparing how each action affects the people and institutions involved.

The analysis makes hidden consequences and conflicts visible before a conclusion is chosen. Professional codes, [[Deontological Ethics]], and [[Consequentialism]] provide different tests for the alternatives; disagreement among them is evidence that the tradeoff needs explicit justification rather than concealment.
