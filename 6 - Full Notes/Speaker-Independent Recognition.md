# Speaker-Independent Recognition

Speaker-independent recognition is designed to transcribe speech from people who were not individually enrolled with the system. Training must cover a broad range of voices, accents, ages, speaking styles, and recording conditions.

This breadth makes the task harder than [[Speaker-Dependent Recognition]]. Its success depends strongly on a representative [[Training Dataset]] and an [[Acoustic Model]] able to generalize across speakers.
