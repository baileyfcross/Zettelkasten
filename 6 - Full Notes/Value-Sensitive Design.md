# Value-Sensitive Design

Value-sensitive design is a method for identifying the human values affected by a technology and incorporating them throughout design. It examines direct and indirect stakeholders, investigates how values appear in the context, and connects those findings to technical requirements.

The method helps make apparently neutral design choices discussable, but it faces two limits. Values can conflict, and people may not be able to state all of their practical ethics as explicit rules. It also often assumes the technology will be built. [[Limits of Responsible Innovation]] arise when the ethically important question is whether development should stop.
