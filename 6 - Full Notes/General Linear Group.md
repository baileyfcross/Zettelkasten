# General Linear Group

The general linear group $GL(V)$ is the set of all [[Invertible Linear Transformation|invertible linear transformations]] from a vector space $V$ to itself, with composition as its operation. The identity transformation is the group identity and every member has an inverse.

After choosing a basis of an $n$-dimensional space, $GL(V)$ corresponds to the group of nonsingular $n\times n$ matrices. It is generally noncommutative because matrix composition depends on order.
