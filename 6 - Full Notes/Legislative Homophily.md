# Legislative Homophily

Legislative homophily is the tendency for lawmakers with shared attributes to form political ties. Similar ideology, party, geographic origin, seniority, or prior collaboration can increase the likelihood of co-sponsorship.

Homophily helps explain network cohesion but does not prove why a particular tie formed. Comparing internal and external links can show whether observed collaboration remains mostly inside an attributed group.
