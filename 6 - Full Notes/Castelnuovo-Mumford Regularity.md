# Castelnuovo-Mumford Regularity

Castelnuovo-Mumford regularity of a graded module is the supremum of j−i for which the graded Betti number β_{i,j} is nonzero. It summarizes how generator and syzygy degrees grow through a minimal resolution.

The source also characterizes regularity through vanishing degrees of local cohomology and Ext. These equivalent views connect computational resolutions to geometric and cohomological behavior.
