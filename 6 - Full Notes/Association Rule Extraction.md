# Association Rule Extraction

Association rule extraction turns each frequent itemset into candidate antecedent-consequent splits and retains those meeting the minimum-confidence threshold. Every nonempty proper subset can serve as an antecedent.

Confidence relationships can prune weaker candidates during generation. The final rules should still be interpreted for redundancy, prevalence, and practical meaning.
