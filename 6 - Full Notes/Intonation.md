# Intonation

Intonation is the pattern of pitch movement across an utterance. Along with loudness and duration, it signals phrasing, emphasis, sentence type, attitude, and conversational intent.

A [[Text-to-Speech]] system must predict intonation from text and context rather than pronounce every word with a flat contour. Poor prosody can make otherwise correct synthetic speech difficult or unpleasant to interpret.
