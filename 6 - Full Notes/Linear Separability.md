# Linear Separability

Linear separability means that classes can be divided without error by a line, plane, or higher-dimensional hyperplane. A single perceptron learning rule is guaranteed to find such a boundary when one exists under its assumptions.

Problems such as exclusive-or are not linearly separable. They require a representation or model with nonlinear capacity, such as a multilayer network.
