# AWS Data Lake Governance

AWS data-lake governance assigns ownership, definitions, access policy, lineage, quality expectations, retention, and audit evidence to shared data. It connects technical metadata with business meaning so consumers can tell who produced a dataset, how it may be used, and whether it is trustworthy. Governance is not a single service: Lake Formation, cataloging, logging, encryption, quality checks, and organizational processes enforce different parts of the contract.
