# URL

A uniform resource locator identifies where and how a resource can be accessed. The same addressing form can point to material on a distant server, a nearby machine, or local storage.

URLs give the Web a flat navigational surface across geography and hardware. This convenience creates a discovery problem because location alone does not explain a resource’s quality or relevance.
