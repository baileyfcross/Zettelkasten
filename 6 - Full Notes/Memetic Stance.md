# Memetic Stance

Memetic stance is the position encoded by a meme version toward its subject, its source, its audience, and the act of communication. It includes participation structure, the key or emotional tone of the utterance, and the communicative function the artifact performs.

Stance can unite artifacts even when their wording or imagery changes. A remake may sincerely endorse a model, mock it, parody it, or use it to address a new audience. Tracking these choices reveals whether imitation is compliant or subversive and helps explain [[Stance Change in Meme Translation]].
