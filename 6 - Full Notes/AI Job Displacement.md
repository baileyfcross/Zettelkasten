# AI Job Displacement

AI job displacement occurs when automated systems reduce demand for human labor in particular tasks or occupations. The effect is not determined by technical capability alone; costs, organizational choices, policy, and the creation of new roles shape whether work is removed, transformed, or redistributed.

The ethical issue includes the speed and distribution of transition. Gains may flow to owners while workers bear unemployment, retraining, or loss of security. Evaluation should therefore examine who benefits, which groups are most vulnerable, and whether institutions provide meaningful alternatives rather than treating aggregate productivity as a complete measure of improvement.
