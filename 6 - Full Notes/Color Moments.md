# Color Moments

Color moments summarize the distribution of values in each color channel with statistics such as the mean, variance, and higher-order shape. They replace a full histogram with a small numerical feature vector.

The compactness is useful when global color composition matters, but moments lose multimodal and spatial detail. Their adequacy depends on whether coarse distributional shape separates the images in the task.
