# Limits of Performance Model Extrapolation

Interpolation estimates a value between conditions represented in the source data, while extrapolation predicts outside that range. Extrapolation is more likely to fail because the fitted trend may change beyond the observed domain or omit a limiting mechanism. Aircraft performance models should therefore stay within the data and assumptions of [[Pilot Operating Handbook Performance Data]] whenever operational decisions are involved.
