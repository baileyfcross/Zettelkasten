# Integrated Customer View

An integrated customer view assembles compatible information about a person or account from otherwise separate applications. Sales, payments, call-center contacts, credit information, and external records may each contribute only one part of the picture.

Joining these sources requires matching identities and reconciling field meanings before modeling. A unified view can support better analysis, but it also increases privacy risk because combining ordinary records may reveal more about a person than any individual source disclosed.
