# Meeting Knowledge Capture

Meeting knowledge capture turns recordings, transcripts, and shared screens into searchable summaries, decisions, questions, and candidate action items. It reduces the need for every stakeholder to attend synchronously and preserves context for later review.

The captured record should distinguish what was said from what the model inferred, identify speakers where appropriate, and obtain confirmation for decisions and assignments. Sensitive meeting content needs retention and access rules before indexing.
