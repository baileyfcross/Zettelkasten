# Difference Equation

A difference equation relates values of a dependent sequence at different integer arguments. For example, $y_{n+2}-3y_{n+1}+2y_n=0$ constrains every new term through earlier terms.

It is the discrete analogue of a differential equation. A solution is a sequence satisfying the recurrence together with any specified initial or boundary values, and the [[Shift Operator]] provides a compact operator notation for linear cases.
