# Clinical Decision Support Regulation

Clinical decision support regulation addresses the legal and safety obligations of software that influences patient care or processes patient data. The required scrutiny depends on what the system does, how directly it affects a decision, and the harm that failure could cause.

A model's technical accuracy is only one part of safety. Data protection, validation, human oversight, update control, and evidence that the system benefits rather than harms patients also matter. Mobile and continuously learning systems complicate this assessment because their platform, inputs, or behavior can change after initial evaluation.
