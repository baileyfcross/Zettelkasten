# Double Koszul Complex

A double Koszul complex combines exterior powers and symmetric powers with two compatible Koszul-style differentials. One direction uses the original sequence, while the other uses formal variables.

Taking cycles, boundaries, or homology along one direction produces [[Approximation Complex]]es. The construction relates relations among ideal generators to symmetric and Rees algebras.
