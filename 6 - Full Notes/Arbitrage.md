# Arbitrage

Arbitrage is buying a resource in one place and selling it elsewhere for a higher price. It can create legitimate trading play when distance, time, risk, or limited capacity makes the difference costly to exploit.

An uncontrolled repeatable arbitrage loop creates currency from price mismatches and can rapidly destabilize a [[Game Economy]]. Designers must evaluate every conversion path and use [[Price Boundary|price boundaries]] or other constraints to prevent runaway gain.
