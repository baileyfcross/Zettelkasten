# Machine-to-Machine Communication

Machine-to-machine communication is the direct exchange of data and instructions among computing devices or equipment without a person mediating each interaction. A machine can report its state, receive a command, coordinate with another machine, or trigger an automated process.

M2M is a foundation of the [[Industrial Internet of Things]] because it lets production, logistics, utilities, and robotic systems respond continuously rather than wait for manual inspection. It differs from human-to-machine and machine-to-smartphone communication in where control and interpretation occur. Removing the person from the immediate loop increases speed but makes rules, authentication, fault handling, and safe operating limits more important.
