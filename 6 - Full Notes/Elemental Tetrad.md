# Elemental Tetrad

The Elemental Tetrad analyzes a game through mechanics, aesthetics, story, and technology. None of the four elements is inherently primary: a change in one can constrain or reshape the others, so coherent design depends on their relationships.

The framework resembles a development-team view of the artifact, connecting mechanics with design, aesthetics with art and sound, story with writing, and technology with engineering. It mainly describes what is built into the game; the [[Layered Tetrad]] extends it to behavior during play and effects in culture.
