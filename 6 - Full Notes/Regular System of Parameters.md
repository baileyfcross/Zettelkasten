# Regular System of Parameters

A regular system of parameters is a [[System of Parameters]] that generates the maximal ideal of a [[Regular Local Ring]]. Its length equals the ring's Krull dimension.

The images of its elements form a basis of m/m² over the residue field, and the elements form a [[Regular Sequence]]. Initial portions reduce the dimension one step at a time while preserving regularity.
