# Vertex Degree

The degree of a vertex is the number of edges adjacent to it. It summarizes how many direct connections a [[Vertex]] has within a [[Graph]].

Degree can determine whether particular routes are possible. In the Königsberg bridge graph, more than two vertices had odd degree, preventing an [[Eulerian Path]]. It also constrains coloring: the largest vertex degree helps bound the number of colors required for [[Edge Coloring]].
