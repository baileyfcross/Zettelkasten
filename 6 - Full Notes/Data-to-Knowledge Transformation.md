# Data-to-Knowledge Transformation

Data-to-knowledge transformation is the progression from recorded observations to structured information, interpreted patterns, and actionable understanding. Each stage adds context and judgment rather than simply increasing the amount of data.

The book uses this progression to explain why cleaning, exploration, modeling, validation, and communication are all parts of [[Data Science]].
