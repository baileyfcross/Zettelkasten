# Platform Contribution Model

A platform contribution model lets users propose, build, review, and maintain improvements to shared capabilities instead of making the central team the sole source of change. It borrows the community practices of open-source projects: visible roadmaps, documented interfaces, contribution guidance, review, communication, and shared ownership.

Contribution does not remove governance. The platform team still protects architectural principles, compatibility, security, and supportability, while contributors bring domain knowledge and evidence about unmet needs. This collaboration keeps the product connected to its users, expands capacity, and prevents feedback from ending at feature requests that only the central team can act on.
