# Five Lemma

The Five Lemma concerns a commutative diagram with two exact rows of five objects. If the outer vertical maps have the needed injectivity or surjectivity and the adjacent middle maps are isomorphisms, then the central map is an isomorphism.

The result combines the two directions of the [[Four Lemma]]. It is frequently used to transfer an isomorphism through related exact sequences.
