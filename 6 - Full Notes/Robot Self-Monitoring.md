# Robot Self-Monitoring

A robot must sense its own condition as well as the external world. Internal monitoring can include temperature, battery state, motor load, component orientation, wheel rotation, network connection, and the status of safety systems.

This telemetry consumes processing and communication resources, but without it the robot cannot estimate where it is, detect degradation, or choose a safe response to failure. Self-monitoring is analogous to biological regulation: it keeps the platform within operating limits while [[Hierarchical Robot Control]] uses the resulting feedback to adjust physical action.
