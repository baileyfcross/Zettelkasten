# Working Agreement

A working agreement records the behaviors a team chooses for collaboration, communication, meetings, conflict, and quality. Because members create it together, the agreement makes expectations explicit and gives the team a reference for addressing recurring friction.

It is revised as the team learns rather than imposed once as policy. Useful statements describe observable behavior and connect to the team's shared purpose.
