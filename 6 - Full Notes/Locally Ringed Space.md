# Locally Ringed Space

A locally ringed space is a topological space equipped with a [[Sheaf]] of rings whose stalk at every point is a [[Local Ring]]. A morphism combines a continuous map with compatible sheaf maps that induce local homomorphisms on stalks.

Every [[Affine Scheme]] is a locally ringed space. The condition ensures that the sheaf records functions with a meaningful notion of value and nonvanishing near each point.
