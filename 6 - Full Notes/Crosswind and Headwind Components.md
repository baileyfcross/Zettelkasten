# Crosswind and Headwind Components

Wind can be resolved relative to a runway or course into perpendicular crosswind and parallel headwind or tailwind components. The crosswind component is wind speed times the sine of the relative angle, while the headwind component uses the cosine. Because weather wind is true and runway heading is magnetic, [[True, Magnetic, and Compass Heading Conversion]] must precede a direct comparison.
