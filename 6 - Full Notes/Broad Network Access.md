# Broad Network Access

Broad network access is the cloud characteristic that makes a service reachable through a network from different locations and suitable client devices. It separates access to a service from physical presence at the infrastructure that runs it.

The breadth of access depends on the deployment boundary. A [[Public Cloud]] is normally reached over the Internet, while a [[Private Cloud]] may restrict access to a private network. Increasing reach also increases the importance of authentication, secure channels, and endpoint protection.
