# Spatiotemporal Data

Spatiotemporal data associate observations with both geographic positions and times. Their structure depends on which combinations exist: every feature may be observed at every time in a [[Full Space-Time Grid]], only selected lattice combinations may appear in a [[Sparse Space-Time Grid]], measurements may be irregular, or positions may form trajectories. Visualization must decide whether to preserve space, time, or both in each view.
