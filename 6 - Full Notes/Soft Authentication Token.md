# Soft Authentication Token

A soft authentication token is a software-generated credential used to demonstrate possession of an enrolled device or secret. It commonly produces a time-limited or challenge-based value that supplements [[Password Authentication]].

As a possession factor in [[Multi-Factor Authentication]], it can prevent a password alone from granting access. Its protection still depends on device security, enrollment integrity, recovery procedures, and resistance to real-time interception.
