# Viral Sharing and Memetic Remaking

Viral sharing distributes a cultural item while memetic remaking modifies it. Sharing can communicate taste, affiliation, or emotion without altering the artifact; remaking adds a new performance, text, image, or arrangement to the circulating family.

The contrast reflects two models of communication. Viral transmission emphasizes movement of information, whereas memetic participation also creates ritual connection through repeated cultural action. Comments and recommendations still add meaning around a viral item, but derivatives incorporate the response into the artifact itself.
