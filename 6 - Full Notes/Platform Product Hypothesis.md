# Platform Product Hypothesis

A platform product hypothesis connects a proposed capability to an observable improvement for a defined user group. It states the pain being addressed, the intervention the platform will provide, and the evidence that would show the intervention worked.

The baseline must be measurable before the [[Thinnest Viable Platform]] is introduced; otherwise later adoption or performance data has nothing reliable to compare against. Ticket volume, lead time, incident work, compliance violations, adoption, and qualitative feedback can all serve as evidence when their definitions are stable and their collection does not distort user behavior.
