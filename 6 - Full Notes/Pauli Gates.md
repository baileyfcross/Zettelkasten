# Pauli Gates

The Pauli $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ gates are single-qubit unitaries corresponding to rotations by $\pi$ around the Bloch sphere's coordinate axes. $X$ swaps $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$, $Z$ changes the phase of $|1\rangle$, and $Y$ combines a bit flip with phase.

Each Pauli gate is self-inverse: $X^2=Y^2=Z^2=I$. Their simple algebra makes them basic building blocks for circuit construction, Hamiltonian descriptions, error models, and observables.
