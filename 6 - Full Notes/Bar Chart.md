# Bar Chart

A bar chart displays frequencies or proportions for categorical levels as lengths measured from a common baseline. The shared scale makes comparisons more accurate than the angle-based comparisons of a [[Pie Chart]].

Readable bars require clear category names, an appropriate order, and an axis that communicates counts or proportions without unnecessary scientific notation. Color may distinguish levels, but the labels should still carry the meaning.

The book compares ordinary, filled, dodged, stacked, and horizontal bar arrangements in R. The choice determines whether the display emphasizes absolute totals, proportions, subgroup comparisons, or readable category labels.
