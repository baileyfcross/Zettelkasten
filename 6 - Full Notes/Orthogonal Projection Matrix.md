# Orthogonal Projection Matrix

If the columns of a full-column-rank matrix $A$ span a subspace $W$, then $P=A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T$ projects vectors orthogonally onto $W$. The residual $x-Px$ lies in the [[Orthogonal Complement]] of the column space.

The matrix is symmetric and idempotent: $P^T=P$ and $P^2=P$. These properties express that projecting twice has the same effect as projecting once and that the range and residual directions are perpendicular.
