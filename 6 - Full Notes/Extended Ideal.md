# Extended Ideal

Given a [[Ring Homomorphism]] f: R → T and an [[Ideal]] I of R, the extended ideal Iᵉ is the ideal of T generated by f(I). It includes all finite T-linear combinations of images of elements of I.

Under [[Ring Localization]], extension sends I to S⁻¹I. Extending and then contracting can enlarge an ideal when elements annihilated by denominators become indistinguishable.
