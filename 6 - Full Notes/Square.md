# Square

The square of a quantity is its second power:

$$a^2=a\cdot a.$$

The name reflects the area of a geometric square with side length (a). Squaring a real number produces a nonnegative result, so ((-a)^2=a^2). Parentheses distinguish the square of a negative value from the opposite of a square. A quantity that equals the square of another number is a perfect square, and reversing the operation uses a [[Square Root]].
