# Blender Outliner

The Blender Outliner presents a hierarchical view of data stored in a blend file, including scenes, collections, objects, and other datablocks. It complements the spatial [[Blender 3D Viewport and Object Operations|3D Viewport]] by making ownership and organization visible even when an item is hidden, difficult to select, or outside the current view.

The Outliner can select, hide, disable selection for, exclude from rendering, delete, or unlink items. Filters control which kinds of data appear, and collections can be created from the editor. This makes the Outliner both a navigator and an administrative surface for complex scenes rather than merely a list of object names.

Even in the simpler Blender 2.77 workflow, the Outliner provides a dependable way to find and select an object that is obscured or difficult to click in the 3D view. Its list represents scene membership rather than the current camera angle, so spatial occlusion does not hide an object's identity.
