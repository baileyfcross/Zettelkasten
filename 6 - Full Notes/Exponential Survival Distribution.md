# Exponential Survival Distribution

The exponential survival distribution assumes a constant hazard rate over time. Its simplicity yields a direct parametric model, but the implied event process is restrictive for risks that rise, fall, or change with age.

An exponential fitted curve should be compared with the nonparametric survival experience. Visible departure or contradictory conclusions relative to other plausible models are reasons to reject the assumption or report substantial sensitivity.
