# Cellular Agriculture

Cellular agriculture produces food or consumer materials from cultured cells rather than raising and harvesting an entire animal. It adapts cell expansion, scaffolding, differentiation, and bioreactor methods to products whose requirements differ from medical implants.

The field aims to improve resource use and animal welfare while meeting strict expectations for safety, cost, sensory quality, and scale.
