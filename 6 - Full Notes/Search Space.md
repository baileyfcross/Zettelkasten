# Search Space

A search space is the domain of possible values or candidates within which a [[Search Algorithm]] looks for a solution. Information gained during the search can remove portions that can no longer contain an acceptable answer.

In [[Binary Search]], comparing the target with the middle ordered item eliminates at least half of the current search space. The process repeats until a match is found or the space becomes empty.
