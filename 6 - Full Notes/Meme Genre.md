# Meme Genre

A meme genre is a socially recognized pattern for performing a recurring communicative action. It supplies conventions for what creators should preserve, what they may replace, and how an audience should interpret the result.

Genres stabilize participation without freezing it. A reaction Photoshop, image macro, or rage comic becomes intelligible because users recognize its formula, yet each instance adapts the formula to a new situation. Genre analysis therefore links the formal features of meme artifacts to the communities and purposes that sustain them.
