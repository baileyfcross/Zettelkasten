# Health Insurance Data

Health insurance data describe enrollment, claims, payments, covered services, and related administrative events. They can reveal patterns of service use and support analysis of cost and efficiency across populations.

Claims reflect reimbursement processes rather than a complete clinical history. Analysts must distinguish what was billed from what was medically observed and protect information whose disclosure could affect individuals.
