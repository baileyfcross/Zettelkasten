# Ghosting Visualization

Ghosting visualization makes selected foreground structure partly transparent or selectively removed so an occluded target can be seen in context. Object-space methods modify a three-dimensional scene representation, while image-space methods operate on projected regions or buffers.

The technique should preserve enough boundary and depth evidence for the viewer to understand which surfaces remain in front. Excessive transparency can make both foreground and hidden information difficult to interpret.
