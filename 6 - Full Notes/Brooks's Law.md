# Brooks's Law

Brooks's Law states that adding people to a late software project can make it later. New members require onboarding, create additional communication paths, and consume the attention of experienced people before they contribute effectively.

The effect is strongest when work is highly interdependent and cannot be partitioned cleanly. Addressing constraints, scope, and coordination may improve delivery more than increasing headcount.
