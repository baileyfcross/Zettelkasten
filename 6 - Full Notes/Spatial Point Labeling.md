# Spatial Point Labeling

Spatial point labeling places names or values near mapped locations while attempting to prevent collisions with symbols and other labels. Automated placement can search alternative positions around a point, but dense maps may still require prioritization or omission. Labels should attach unambiguously to their locations and remain subordinate to the quantitative encoding. Interactive [[Spatial Tooltip|tooltips]] offer an alternative when permanent labels would cover the map.
