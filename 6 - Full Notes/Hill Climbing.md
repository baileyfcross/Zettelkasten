# Hill Climbing

Hill climbing is a problem-solving approach that repeatedly moves from the current solution to a better neighboring solution. The metaphor treats the desired optimum as a peak and each allowed change as a step on the terrain.

Because it uses only local improvement, hill climbing can stop at a [[Local Optimum]] or plateau that is lower than the [[Global Optimum]]. Escaping may require returning to an earlier state or using a strategy that accepts a move without immediate improvement.
