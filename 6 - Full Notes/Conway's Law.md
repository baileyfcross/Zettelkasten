# Conway's Law

Conway's Law observes that a system's design tends to reproduce the communication structure of the organization that creates it. Teams separated by technology, discipline, or feature area often produce corresponding seams in the product.

Organizational design is therefore also product design. Cross-discipline feature teams and shared whole-game goals can reduce fragmentation that a component-centered hierarchy would otherwise embed.
