# Improper Fraction

An improper fraction is a [[Fraction]] whose [[Numerator]] is at least as large as its positive [[Denominator]]. Its value is at least one, such as (9/2).

An improper fraction may be rewritten as a mixed number, so (9/2=4+1/2). Algebra usually favors the improper form because it behaves as one quotient under multiplication, division, and symbolic manipulation. A mixed form can make size intuitive, while the improper form makes operations and cancellation more direct.
