# Reliability Pillar

The reliability pillar concerns a workload's ability to perform its intended function correctly and consistently. It covers foundations, architecture, change management, and failure management, with emphasis on automatic recovery, tested procedures, horizontal scaling, and sufficient capacity.

Reliable architecture assumes components will fail and constrains the effect. Health checks, multi-zone design, backups, controlled deployment, and recovery objectives must be tested together rather than inferred from the availability of individual services.
