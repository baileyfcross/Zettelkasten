# Relative Difficulty

Relative difficulty compares a challenge's absolute difficulty with the power the game gives the player to meet it. Health, equipment, abilities, information, allies, and permitted actions can all change that power.

The same enemy can therefore be difficult for a weak character and easy for a developed one. Progression must coordinate challenge growth with the player's changing capabilities.
