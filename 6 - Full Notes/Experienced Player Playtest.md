# Experienced Player Playtest

An experienced-player playtest examines the game after participants have learned its basics over repeated or extended sessions. It exposes long-term strategy, progression, balance, repetition, exploits, and whether early interest develops into sustained engagement.

These tests answer different questions from first-use sessions. A design may onboard clearly yet become shallow, or initially confuse players while supporting rich mastery later.
