# Electrical Resistivity and Conductivity Units

The book presents resistivity and conductivity as separate electrical conversion categories. Its resistivity example changes ohm-circular-mil per foot into nanoohm-meters, requiring resistance, area, length, and metric-prefix factors. Its conductivity example changes mho per centimeter into siemens per meter, converting the inverse-length denominator as well as the named conductance unit. The expressions Ω·m and S/m identify different target quantities; the shared electrical setting does not justify applying one category's factor to the other. See [[Conversion-Factor Chains]].
