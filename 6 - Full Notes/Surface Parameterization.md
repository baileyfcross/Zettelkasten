# Surface Parameterization

A surface parameterization represents points on a surface by a two-variable map $r(u,v)=(x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v))$. Two parameters are needed because a surface has two independent directions of local motion, whereas a [[Parametric Curve]] needs only one.

A useful parameterization must cover the intended part of the surface with suitable parameter ranges and orientation. Cylinders can use an angular parameter plus height, while surfaces of revolution naturally use one parameter along the generating curve and another around the axis.
