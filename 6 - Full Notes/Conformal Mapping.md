# Conformal Mapping

A conformal mapping preserves angles and their orientation locally wherever its complex derivative is nonzero. Analytic functions therefore provide transformations that can reshape a two-dimensional domain while retaining local geometric relationships.

The mapping can turn a difficult boundary geometry into a simpler one where a harmonic problem is easier to solve. After solving in the transformed plane, the inverse mapping carries the result back to the original domain; the Schwarz-Christoffel transformation is an important polygonal example.
