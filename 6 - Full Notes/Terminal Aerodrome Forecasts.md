# Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts

A Terminal Aerodrome Forecast describes expected weather in the vicinity of an aerodrome over a stated validity period, commonly 24 or 30 hours. It communicates prevailing and changing wind, visibility, cloud, and significant weather conditions for planning. Its forecast role complements the observed conditions in [[METAR and SPECI Reports]].
