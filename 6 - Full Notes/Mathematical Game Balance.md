# Mathematical Game Balance

Mathematical game balance uses equations, probability, and forward-looking models to set relationships among costs, benefits, power, and progression. It helps expose strongly unbalanced parts before extensive play data exists.

Models are tools rather than complete answers: they cannot decide the desired experience or eliminate all interaction effects in a complex game. Their predictions still need designer judgment and [[Playtesting]].
