# Unequal Group Power Calculation

An unequal group power calculation allows comparison groups to have different sample sizes. One group's size may be fixed by disease prevalence, recruitment limits, or the available records while the calculation solves for the other.

Imbalance generally supplies less information than the same total sample divided evenly. Planning should therefore distinguish the number of available events from the total number of observations and avoid assuming that a large dataset automatically makes every subgroup comparison well powered.
