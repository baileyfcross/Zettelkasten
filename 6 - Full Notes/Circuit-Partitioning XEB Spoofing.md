# Circuit-Partitioning XEB Spoofing

A circuit-partitioning spoofing algorithm omits selected entangling gates that cross chosen subsystem boundaries. The altered circuit factors into smaller pieces that can be simulated independently, producing a full approximate output distribution without reproducing the intended global entanglement.

The omitted gates are localized, correlated deviations rather than homogeneous device noise. XEB can remain sizable because the separated subsystems retain probability correlations with the ideal circuit and their contributions are approximately additive. This creates an adversarial counterexample to treating a high [[Linear Cross-Entropy Benchmark]] score as proof of faithful global simulation or quantum advantage.
