# Zero Matrix

A zero matrix has zero in every entry and may be square or rectangular. It is the additive identity for [[Matrix Addition]], so $A+0=A$.

Multiplying any compatible matrix by a zero matrix gives a zero matrix. In the equation $Ax=0$, the right side is a zero vector and the system is a [[Homogeneous Linear System]].
