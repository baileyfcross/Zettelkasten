# Fraudulent Healthcare Provider

A fraudulent healthcare provider exhibits a sustained pattern of deceptive billing or service behavior. Provider-level analysis aggregates many claims to reveal unusual rates, code combinations, patient mixes, or temporal changes that are weak at the transaction level.

Comparison requires appropriate peers because specialties and practice settings legitimately differ. A provider should not be judged against an unrelated population simply because its volume is high. Risk scores are strongest when they combine peer-normalized features with episode, patient, and network evidence.
