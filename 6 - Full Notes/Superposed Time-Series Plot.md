# Superposed Time-Series Plot

A superposed time-series plot draws several comparable series in one panel against a shared time axis and value scale. It reveals collective motion and departures from the group, but many opaque lines quickly become unreadable. Thin strokes and [[Transparency for Time-Series Overplotting]] can shift attention from individual paths to density. Superposition is appropriate only when the variables share units or have been transformed into a common comparison scale.
