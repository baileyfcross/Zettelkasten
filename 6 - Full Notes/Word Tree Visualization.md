# Word Tree Visualization

A word tree visualization expands the phrases that precede or follow a selected word, branching by the sequences found in a corpus. Branch size can indicate how often a continuation occurs while retaining more local context than a [[Word Cloud]].

The tree helps test why a frequent term appears in positive or negative material and can expose recurrent constructions hidden by aggregate counts. Its interpretation still depends on the selected root, direction, pruning, and subset of documents displayed.
