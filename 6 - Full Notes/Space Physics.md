# Space Physics

Space physics studies the physical environment above the dense atmosphere, including solar activity, cosmic rays, the ionosphere, magnetic fields, radiation belts, and the flow of particles through near-Earth space. Early investigations used V-2s and sounding rockets before satellites allowed longer observations.

The field had immediate practical connections to communication, missile operations, and polar routes because solar activity changes the ionosphere and radiation environment. Explorer observations associated with James Van Allen revealed radiation belts around Earth, showing how instruments in orbit could discover structures that brief ground and rocket measurements could not map completely.
