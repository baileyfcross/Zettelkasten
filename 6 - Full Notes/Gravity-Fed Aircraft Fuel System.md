# Gravity-Fed Aircraft Fuel System

A high-wing light aircraft can feed fuel from wing tanks toward the engine by gravity. Tank and cap vents admit replacement air and help equalize tank levels; if a vent becomes blocked by ice or debris, reduced flow can cause fuel starvation. Fuel selection and vent integrity therefore remain important through takeoff, climb, descent, and landing.
