# Inner Product

An inner product assigns a scalar $\langle u,v\rangle$ to two vectors while satisfying conjugate symmetry, linearity, and positivity. It generalizes the geometric dot product.

It defines length by $\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v,v\rangle}$ and orthogonality by a zero inner product. Different inner products can impose different geometries on the same vector space.
