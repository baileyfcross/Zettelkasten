# Entity Relationship

An entity relationship states how instances of one persistent entity are associated with instances of another. A city-to-country model, for example, gives each city one country while a country exposes a collection of its cities.

In [[Entity Framework Core]], foreign-key and navigation properties make that relationship available to application code. Conventions or explicit configuration determine how it is represented in the database schema.
