# Minimum-Norm Solution

A minimum-norm solution is the solution of an underdetermined system with the smallest Euclidean length. It removes ambiguity among infinitely many exact solutions by choosing the one closest to the origin.

This vector is orthogonal to the null space and lies in the row space of the coefficient matrix. A [[Pseudoinverse]] computes it directly.
