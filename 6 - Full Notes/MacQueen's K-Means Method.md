# MacQueen's K-Means Method

MacQueen's K-means method processes observations in sequence, compares each with all current centroids, and immediately reassigns it when another centroid is nearer. The losing and gaining centroids are updated after the move.

Immediate updates let later observations respond to the newest state. The processing order can therefore influence the path to convergence.
