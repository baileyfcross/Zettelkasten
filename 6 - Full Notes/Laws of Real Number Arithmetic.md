# Laws of Real Number Arithmetic

Real-number arithmetic is governed by closure, commutative and associative laws, distributivity, identities, and inverses. These laws justify symbolic rearrangement: addition and multiplication may be regrouped, multiplication distributes over addition, and nonzero values have multiplicative inverses. They supply the structural basis for [[Equivalent Equations]] and algebraic simplification.
