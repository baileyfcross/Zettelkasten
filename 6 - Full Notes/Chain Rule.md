# Chain Rule

The chain rule differentiates a composition:

$$(f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x).$$

It multiplies the outer function's rate of change, evaluated at the inner output, by the inner function's rate of change. In [[Leibniz Notation]], the relationship resembles $dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$.
