# Big Bang Evidence

Evidence for a hot, dense early universe includes cosmic expansion described by [[Hubble's Law]], the cosmic microwave background, and the observed abundance of light nuclei. Expansion implies that matter was closer together in the past. The model describes the evolution of spacetime and its contents from an early hot state, not an explosion into preexisting empty space.
