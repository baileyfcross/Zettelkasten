# Botnet

A botnet is a collection of compromised devices controlled together by an attacker. Each [[Zombie Computer]] contributes network access and computing resources, allowing the operator to distribute activity across many apparently unrelated systems.

Botnets support denial-of-service attacks, spam, credential abuse, malware delivery, and surveillance. The [[Mirai Botnet]] illustrates how weakly protected Internet-connected devices can turn a large population of ordinary products into attack infrastructure.
