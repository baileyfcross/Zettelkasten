# Fuzzy DBSCAN for Uncertain Data

Fuzzy DBSCAN adapts density reachability when an object's position or pairwise relationship is uncertain. Neighborhood membership and core status can be expressed by degrees or probabilities rather than deterministic yes-or-no tests.

The method retains density-based handling of irregular shapes and noise while exposing borderline uncertainty. Its output depends on how uncertain distributions are converted into neighborhood evidence and how fuzzy reachability is aggregated.
