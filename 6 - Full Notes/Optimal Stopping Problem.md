# Optimal Stopping Problem

An optimal stopping problem asks when to stop examining sequentially arriving options in order to maximize a reward or minimize a cost. Each decision must account for the danger of accepting too early and the danger of passing the best option.

The defining constraint is that rejected choices cannot later be recovered. The [[Secretary Problem]] gives a concrete strategy: reserve an initial sample for comparison, then accept the first later candidate that exceeds that benchmark.
