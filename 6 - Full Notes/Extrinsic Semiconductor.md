# Extrinsic Semiconductor

An extrinsic semiconductor has controlled impurities that add allowed levels within the [[Semiconductor Band Gap]]. Donor impurities provide electrons near the conduction band, producing [[N-Type Semiconductor]] material. Acceptor impurities create electron deficiencies near the valence band, producing [[P-Type Semiconductor]] material. This is the electronic result of [[Semiconductor Doping]], not a different base crystal.
