# Linear Model Power Calculation

A linear model power calculation uses an effect-size measure for explained variance together with numerator and denominator degrees of freedom, significance level, and target power. It aligns planning more directly with regression than a two-group t-test approximation.

The numerator degrees of freedom reflect the tested predictors, while the solved denominator degrees of freedom imply the required residual information and sample size. Adding parameters without increasing effect can raise the required sample.
