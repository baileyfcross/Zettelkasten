# Logistic Model Convergence

Logistic model convergence occurs when the iterative fitting algorithm reaches stable parameter estimates under its numerical criterion. Output such as the number of Fisher-scoring iterations helps show whether the calculation completed normally.

Failure to converge may reflect a badly scaled or unsupported specification, sparse categories, collinearity, or [[Complete Separation]]. A reported coefficient should not be interpreted until the cause of a convergence warning is understood.
