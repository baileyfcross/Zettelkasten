# Day-for-Night Compositing

Turning a daytime plate into night is more than making the whole image blue and dark. The book separately controls the sky, desaturates and lowers contrast, places gradients where upper structures would receive less light, and shapes color channels toward a night look. It then restores believable local sources: window mattes add warm light and blurred glow, while additional mattes create reflected light pools. Lights should not spill indiscriminately into the sky. The scene must retain a readable hierarchy of darkness and illumination.
