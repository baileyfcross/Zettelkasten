# Flow-Balance Equations in Queueing

Flow-balance equations express that, in steady state, the total probability flow into a queueing state equals the total flow out. They relate the long-run proportion of time spent in neighboring occupancy states.

Solving and normalizing these equations yields state probabilities. Operational measures such as blocking probability can then be derived from the probability of the capacity state.
