# Internal Rate of Return

Internal rate of return is the discount rate at which a project's [[Net Present Value]] is zero. For an energy-system investment, yearly fuel savings, operation and maintenance, loan payments, equipment life, and initial capital all affect its cash-flow sequence. The source compares the resulting rate with cost of capital; risk can raise that required capital return. IRR is a rate test, not an amount of energy delivered.
