# Thermo-Fluid Sciences in Flight

Flight science combines thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and heat transfer. Thermodynamics describes energy, temperature, heat, and work; fluid mechanics describes pressure and motion in air; and heat transfer explains conduction, convection, advection, and radiation. Together they connect atmospheric conditions to [[Aerodynamics and Flight Forces]], engine operation, and aircraft performance.
