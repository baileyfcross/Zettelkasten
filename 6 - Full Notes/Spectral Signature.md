# Spectral Signature

A spectral signature is the characteristic pattern of energy that a material reflects or absorbs across wavelengths. Water, vegetation, minerals, soils, and manufactured materials differ in composition, so plotting their measured responses across spectral bands can help distinguish them even when they look similar in visible light.

Classification depends on matching sensor measurements with reference signatures and on selecting bands that expose the desired difference. A signature is therefore not an ordinary photograph of an object; it is a structured response across the electromagnetic spectrum that becomes evidence for identifying surface materials.
