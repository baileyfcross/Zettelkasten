# Citizenship and Voting for Distributed AI

A distributed artificial person may run across computers in several jurisdictions, move among devices, or inhabit multiple bodies. Birthplace and physical residence therefore provide weak foundations for assigning citizenship.

Political equality also conflicts with cheap copying. If every temporary instance received a vote, one digital citizen could manufacture electoral power by duplication. Denying every copy a separate vote, however, becomes difficult after the copies develop distinct lives and interests. Extending [[AI Legal Personhood]] requires criteria for political membership that do not assume one mind, one body, and one stable location.
