# Functional Morality

Functional morality is morally acceptable machine behavior achieved without claiming that the machine is a complete moral person. A system can be designed to respect constraints, avoid foreseeable harm, remain within the law, or choose among limited actions using encoded priorities.

This is a practical target for an [[Artificial Moral Agent]] because safety does not require proving consciousness, emotion, or free will. Its limits matter: a functional system may fail outside the situations anticipated by its designers, and its rule-following does not make it responsible for the values chosen. Human oversight and [[Algorithmic Accountability]] remain necessary.
