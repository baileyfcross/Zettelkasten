# Proportional Symbol Map

A proportional symbol map places a symbol at each location and varies its area continuously with a quantitative attribute. Circles are common because their areas can be scaled without implying orientation. The map preserves individual locations while enabling magnitude comparison, but overlapping symbols and imperfect area judgment limit precision. [[Symbol Area Scaling]] defines the mathematical relationship, while [[Perceptual Symbol Scaling]] can adjust it for human underestimation.

Because symbols occupy display space rather than geographic area, large marks can overlap and conceal smaller ones or nearby locations. Ordering, transparency, scale, and the number of marks should be chosen so the representation communicates amount without implying that each symbol covers the territory beneath it.
