# Hyperbolic Paraboloid

A hyperbolic paraboloid has a standard form such as

$$z=\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}.$$

Vertical sections in the two coordinate directions are parabolas opening in opposite senses, while the level section at $z=0$ is a pair of intersecting lines. This opposing curvature gives the surface its saddle shape.
