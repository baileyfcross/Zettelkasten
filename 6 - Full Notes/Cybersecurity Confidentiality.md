# Cybersecurity Confidentiality

Confidentiality keeps information secret from parties that are not authorized to receive it. Access restrictions can limit who reaches the information, while [[Encryption]] converts it into an unreadable representation for anyone without the required key.

Confidentiality resembles privacy but is framed as a security property of information and communication. It must be protected while data is stored, transmitted, and processed, because disclosure at any state defeats the goal.
