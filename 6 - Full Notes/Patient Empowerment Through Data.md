# Patient Empowerment Through Data

Patient empowerment through data is an increase in a patient's practical ability to understand, manage, and influence care by accessing and using personal health records. It can let a patient recognize a pattern, adapt a display to daily life, formulate a better question, or participate more fully in a treatment decision.

Empowerment depends on control rather than collection alone. A device that records the body but withholds the measurements, or a dashboard that gives an unexplained score, can increase institutional knowledge without increasing the patient's agency.

The language of empowerment can also conceal transferred work. If the patient must collect, clean, interpret, and coordinate data without support, the technology may shift labor and cost while leaving authority unchanged. The outcome should be evaluated in the person's actual care relationship.
