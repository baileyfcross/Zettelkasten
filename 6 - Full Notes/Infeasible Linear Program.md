# Infeasible Linear Program

An infeasible linear program has no point satisfying all of its constraints and sign restrictions. In two dimensions, the constraint half-planes have no common region; algebraically, no feasible basis can be established.

Infeasibility is different from an [[Unbounded Linear Program]]: the former has no feasible solution at all, while the latter has feasible solutions but no finite optimum. A positive [[Artificial Variable]] that cannot be removed at the end of a feasibility procedure is evidence of inconsistency.
