# Prime Characteristic

A nontrivial integral domain with positive characteristic must have prime characteristic. If the characteristic factored, the corresponding nonzero multiples of the identity would multiply to zero.

Finite fields therefore have prime characteristic even when their number of elements is a higher prime power.
