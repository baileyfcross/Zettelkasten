# Decision-Level Multimedia Fusion

Decision-level multimedia fusion trains separate classifiers for different modalities and combines their predicted labels or probabilities. Each model can use features and algorithms suited to its own signal.

Late fusion is robust when a channel is missing and permits modality-specific calibration, but it cannot learn low-level interactions discarded before combination. Voting, weighted scores, or a learned combiner determine the final class.
