# Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling

Dynamic voltage and frequency scaling changes a processor's clock rate and supply voltage to trade performance for power consumption. Lower settings can reduce instantaneous power but lengthen execution.

The energy-minimizing setting depends on whether reduced power outweighs the extra runtime. Parallel configurations should therefore be compared by both completion time and total energy.
