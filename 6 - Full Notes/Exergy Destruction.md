# Exergy Destruction

Exergy destruction is the portion of incoming [[Exergy]] that does not appear in a useful product because of irreversible processes or losses. The source writes a balance as input exergy equals useful-product exergy plus destroyed or lost exergy. Locating that term in a collector, heat exchanger, or power stage shows where design changes can preserve more energy quality, even when a first-law energy balance already closes.
