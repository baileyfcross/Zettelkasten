# Slope of a Line

The slope of the line through $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2)$ is $m=(y_2-y_1)/(x_2-x_1)$ when $x_1\ne x_2$. It measures vertical change per unit of horizontal change. Positive and negative slopes distinguish rising and falling lines, zero describes a horizontal line, and a vertical line has undefined slope.
