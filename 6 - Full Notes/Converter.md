# Converter

A converter transforms one kind of [[Resource]] into another resource or object. The input is consumed by the conversion while the output is created.

Converters may have one input and output or form long production chains with multiple requirements and by-products. Additional conversion detail is valuable only when it produces meaningful decisions rather than needless [[Cognitive Load]].
