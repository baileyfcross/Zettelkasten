# Repeating Decimal

A repeating decimal has a finite block of digits that continues indefinitely, such as

$$0.090909\ldots=0.\overline{09}.$$

Every repeating decimal is a [[Rational Number]]. To convert one to a fraction, let a variable equal the decimal, multiply by powers of ten until two forms share the same infinite tail, and subtract. For (x=0.\overline{09}), (100x-x=9), so (99x=9) and (x=1/11). A terminating decimal is also rational because trailing zeros make it a repeating form.
