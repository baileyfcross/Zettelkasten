# Predicate Logic as Mechanical Symbol Processing

Predicate logic extends propositional logic with quantifiers for statements about all or some members of a domain. Its formal syntax and inference rules allow expressions to be transformed through mechanical symbol-processing steps.

This resemblance to a programming language was historically important: reasoning could be studied as a process performed by rule. [[The Decision Problem|The later decision problem]] asked whether one universal mechanical procedure could decide the truth of every proposition in such a system.
