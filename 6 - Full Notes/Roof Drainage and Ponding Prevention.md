# Roof Drainage and Ponding Prevention

A steel-framed roof should be laid out with positive slope, reliable primary drains, and independent secondary drainage paths that limit water depth if the primary system is blocked. Drain and scupper elevations, deck direction, member camber, construction tolerances, and expected deflection all influence where water actually moves rather than where drawings imply it will move.

Ponding prevention combines geometry and stiffness. Flexible framing can create low points that deepen under accumulated rain, increasing load and further deflection. Adequate beam, girder, joist, and deck stiffness helps arrest this feedback, but stiffness cannot replace drainage capacity. Coordination with roofing and plumbing preserves overflow paths and avoids saddles or curbs that trap water after the structural analysis is complete.
