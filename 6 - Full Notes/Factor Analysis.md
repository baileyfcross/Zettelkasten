# Factor Analysis

Factor analysis models correlations among observed variables through a smaller collection of [[Hidden Factor|hidden factors]] and feature-specific noise. Each observation is represented as a weighted combination of the common factors plus an independent residual.

The source connects factor estimation to leading principal components under suitable assumptions. For gene-expression data, the factors can summarize shared sample structure that ordinary independent-error linear models fail to represent.
