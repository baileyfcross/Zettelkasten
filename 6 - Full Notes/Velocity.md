# Velocity

Velocity is the amount of relatively estimated work a team completes within a Sprint. An average over several Sprints helps forecast how much similarly estimated backlog may fit into a future release.

Velocity is evidence for the team's own planning, not a commitment or a performance ranking. Comparing it across teams or rewarding higher numbers encourages estimate inflation rather than greater delivered value.
