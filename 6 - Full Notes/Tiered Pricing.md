# Tiered Pricing

Tiered pricing assigns different prices to defined bands of spending, volume, or service quality. A larger number of users may receive a lower price per user, while a stricter service-level tier may carry a higher charge.

The boundaries and measurement period determine which tier applies. This approach can reward scale or let consumers choose among service commitments without requiring the provider to create a completely different offering for every customer.
