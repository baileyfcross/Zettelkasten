# Long Exact Sequence in Homology

A [[Short Exact Sequence of Complexes]] yields a long exact sequence linking the homology groups of its three complexes. Connecting homomorphisms move from Hₙ(C″) to Hₙ₋₁(C′).

This sequence transfers vanishing and isomorphism information across complexes. It is the homological analogue of the kernel-cokernel sequence produced by the [[Snake Lemma]].
