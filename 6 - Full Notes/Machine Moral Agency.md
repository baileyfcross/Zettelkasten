# Machine Moral Agency

Machine moral agency is the capacity attributed to an artificial system when its behavior can be assessed in moral terms. A weak account asks whether the system interacts with its environment, acts with some autonomy, and adapts its behavior. A stronger account requires understanding, intention, freedom, and the ability to answer for a choice.

Current systems may satisfy functional criteria without meeting the stronger conditions for responsibility. Calling them agents can describe their causal role, but it should not transfer [[Algorithmic Accountability]] away from the people and institutions that design, deploy, and benefit from them.
