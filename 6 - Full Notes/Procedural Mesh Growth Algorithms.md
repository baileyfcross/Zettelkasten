# Procedural Mesh Growth Algorithms

A mesh-growth algorithm repeatedly chooses a region, selects its components, and extrudes or transforms them according to a rule. Random choices can produce organic variation, while deterministic rules can construct repeatable architectural, mathematical, or scientific forms.

The geometry is produced by the procedure rather than stored as a finished asset. Parameters such as bounds, direction probabilities, step size, and iteration limits define a family of models. Reliable growth depends on selection by geometry and careful mode management because every iteration changes the topology on which the next decision operates.
