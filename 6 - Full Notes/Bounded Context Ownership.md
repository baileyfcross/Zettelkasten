# Bounded Context Ownership

Bounded context ownership assigns responsibility for one [[Bounded Context]] to no more than one cross-functional team. One team may own several contexts, but splitting one context among several teams recreates continuous coordination over its language and internal model. Ownership includes the code, data, rules, and operational decisions inside the boundary. It turns [[Conway's Law]] into a design consideration: the organizational communication structure should reinforce, rather than undermine, the intended system boundaries.
