# Direct View Storage Tube

A direct view storage tube is a line-based display whose persistent phosphorescent coating retains a drawn image for a long period. Because a line can be drawn directly between arbitrary screen points and remains visible without continual refresh, the display is effectively flicker-free.

Persistence also creates the principal limitation: an individual entity is difficult to change or erase. Updating the picture generally requires flooding the screen to erase everything and then redrawing the complete image. Drawing is slow and animation is impractical, which distinguishes the storage tube from the buffer-driven [[Random Scan Display System]].
