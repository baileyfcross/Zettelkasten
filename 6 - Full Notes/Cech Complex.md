# Cech Complex

The Čech complex for elements x₁,…,xₙ is built from localizations of a ring or module at products of those elements. Its terms combine one-element and multiple-element localizations with alternating maps.

The cohomology of this complex computes [[Local Cohomology]] supported in the radical of the generated ideal. It provides a direct localization-based model without first constructing an injective resolution.
