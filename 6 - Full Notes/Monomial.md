# Monomial

A monomial is a [[Polynomial]] consisting of one [[Algebraic Term]], such as (7x^3) or (-2a^2b). Its coefficient and variable powers are multiplied rather than separated by outer addition or subtraction.

Monomials multiply by multiplying coefficients and applying the [[Laws of Exponents]] to matching bases. A monomial can be divided term by term into a polynomial when each quotient is defined. A common monomial factor is often the first structure removed during [[Polynomial Factorization]].
