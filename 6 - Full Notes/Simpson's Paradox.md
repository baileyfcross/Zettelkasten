# Simpson's Paradox

Simpson's paradox occurs when an association observed in aggregated data reverses or disappears after the data are stratified by a third variable. Unequal group composition lets the stratifying variable create a misleading overall comparison.

The source uses university admissions to show that an apparent gender difference was driven by application patterns across departments with different admission rates. The example demonstrates why correlation alone does not establish a direct causal relationship.
