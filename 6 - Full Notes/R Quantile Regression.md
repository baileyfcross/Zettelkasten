# R Quantile Regression

R quantile regression models a specified conditional quantile of a response rather than its conditional mean. Fitting several quantiles can show whether a predictor has different associations near the lower, middle, and upper parts of the response distribution.

Coefficient uncertainty and nested-model comparisons require methods appropriate to quantile loss rather than ordinary least-squares formulas. The selected quantile must be stated because the same predictor can have meaningfully different effects across the distribution.
