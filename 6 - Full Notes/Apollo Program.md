# Apollo Program

The Apollo Program was NASA's effort to land astronauts on the Moon and return them safely before the end of the 1960s. It joined the Saturn launch vehicles, crewed spacecraft, guidance, tracking, mission control, new facilities, and a large contractor network into a single prestige objective.

Apollo 11 achieved the first landing in July 1969, followed by additional scientific expeditions. The program demonstrated extraordinary national capability but depended on a level of Cold War spending that did not persist. Its success also produced enduring imagery and institutions while making later human-spaceflight proposals appear slow by comparison to an exceptional geopolitical project.
