# Random Surfer Model

The random surfer model interprets PageRank transitions as the choices of a person moving from page to page. Normally the surfer follows an outgoing [[Hyperlink]], with each available link assigned a probability.

At a [[Dangling Node]], the surfer can jump to any page. The fuller model also permits [[PageRank Teleportation]] from ordinary pages with a chosen probability, preventing the surfer from becoming trapped in a [[Link Sink]].
