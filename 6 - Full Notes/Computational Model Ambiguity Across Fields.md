# Computational Model Ambiguity Across Fields

The term computational model has different meanings across disciplines. In science it often denotes equations describing a physical process; in computer science it often denotes an abstract machine capable of executing programs.

Collaboration improves when participants make the intended meaning explicit. A scientific model may be implemented by a simulation running on an abstract machine, linking the two senses without making them identical.
