# Cloud Service Catalog

A cloud service catalog is an organized list of services available to a defined group of consumers. Each entry can state what the service does, who supplies it, its price, its contract, and the service levels expected from it.

The catalog turns cloud procurement into a governed choice rather than an untracked individual purchase. A central IT function or [[Cloud Service Broker]] can maintain approved entries while allowing business users to select services suited to their needs.
