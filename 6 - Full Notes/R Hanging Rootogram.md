# R Hanging Rootogram

An R hanging rootogram compares observed frequency counts with the frequencies expected from a fitted distribution. Square-root scaling stabilizes the visual contribution of large counts, and hanging the bars from the expected curve makes patterned departures easier to see around a common baseline.

Runs of positive and negative deviations can reveal skew, tail mismatch, or overdispersion that a single goodness-of-fit statistic obscures. The expected counts must come from the fitted model being assessed.
