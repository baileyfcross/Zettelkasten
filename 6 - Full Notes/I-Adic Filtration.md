# I-Adic Filtration

The I-adic filtration of a ring or module is the descending chain formed by powers Iⁿ or submodules IⁿM. Multiplication respects the indices because IᵐIⁿ = Iᵐ⁺ⁿ.

Its layers define an [[Associated Graded Ring]], while the [[Rees Algebra]] stores the powers before taking successive quotients. The [[Artin-Rees Lemma]] controls how this filtration intersects a submodule.
