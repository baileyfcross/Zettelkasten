# Personal Digital Assistant

A personal digital assistant is a handheld computer organized around personal information such as contacts, appointments, notes, and tasks. It emphasizes quick interaction, portability, and synchronization rather than desktop-scale software.

PDAs created a bridge between organizers and mobile phones. Their interface and application ideas survived even after smartphones absorbed the category.
