# Document Topic Distribution

A document topic distribution assigns a probability or proportion to each latent topic for one document. It preserves the idea that a message can combine several themes instead of belonging to exactly one cluster.

High-probability documents provide useful examples for interpreting a topic and checking whether the model’s statistical pattern is coherent. The distribution can also support search, similarity, and downstream classification after the model has been fitted.
