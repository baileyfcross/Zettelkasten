# Complex Number

A complex number has the form $z=a+bi$, where $a$ and $b$ are real and $i^2=-1$. The values $a$ and $b$ are its real and imaginary parts.

Complex numbers extend the real line to a plane and permit polynomial roots and matrix eigenvalues that are not real. Addition combines corresponding parts, while multiplication uses the rule for the [[Imaginary Unit]].
