# Human-Robot Warehouse Partnership

A human-robot warehouse partnership divides fulfillment according to complementary strengths. Mobile robots move shelves or racks through a controlled floor, while people identify, grasp, and pack individual products whose shapes and arrangements demand flexible vision and hands.

The system reduces worker walking without requiring the robot to solve every picking problem. It also changes the workplace: software coordinates inventory location, robot traffic, and human stations as one process. Performance should therefore be evaluated at the level of the combined workflow, not by comparing an isolated robot with an isolated employee.
