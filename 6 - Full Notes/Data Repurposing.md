# Data Repurposing

Data repurposing occurs when information collected for one purpose is later used for another. Combining records can reveal sensitive patterns or support decisions that the person could not reasonably anticipate when the information was first provided.

Repurposing weakens consent when permission for the original context is treated as permission for every future analysis. It can also create a chilling effect: people alter ordinary behavior because they cannot know how their traces may be interpreted later. [[Privacy by Design]] therefore considers limits on purpose, retention, access, and combination throughout a system's life.
