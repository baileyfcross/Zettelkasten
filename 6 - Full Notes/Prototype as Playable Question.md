# Prototype as Playable Question

A prototype is most useful when it poses a specific question that can be answered through play. Its features and fidelity should be limited to what is necessary to expose that uncertainty.

Treating the artifact as a question prevents polish from masquerading as knowledge. A narrow prototype can be discarded after the answer changes the design, while an unfocused miniature game makes it difficult to know which assumption a test confirmed or challenged.
