# PID Control for Multirotor Drones

A proportional controller responds to present error, a derivative term responds to the error's rate of change, and an integral term accumulates persistent past error. Combined PID control can reduce steady offset while damping overshoot and oscillation. In a multirotor, the resulting commands change individual rotor speeds to achieve the desired position and attitude described by [[Drone Rotation Matrices]].
