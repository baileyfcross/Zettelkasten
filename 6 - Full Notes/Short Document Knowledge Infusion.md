# Short Document Knowledge Infusion

Short documents contain too few observed words for reliable direct similarity. Knowledge infusion enriches their representation with related concepts, categories, entities, or statistics learned from a larger corpus.

The added context can connect texts that use different surface vocabulary. It can also import bias or irrelevant associations from the external knowledge structure, so enrichment should remain traceable and task appropriate.
