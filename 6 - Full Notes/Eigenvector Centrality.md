# Eigenvector Centrality

Eigenvector centrality assigns a node more importance when it connects to other highly important nodes. The recursive definition is solved as an eigenvector problem, so a connection to a central neighborhood contributes more than an equal connection to peripheral nodes.

Its ranking can differ sharply from frequency or [[Betweenness Centrality]]. In a co-occurrence graph, the measure emphasizes membership in a mutually prominent core rather than bridge position.
