# Rare Event Poisson Approximation

A rare event Poisson approximation replaces a [[Binomial Distribution]] with a [[Poisson Distribution]] when the number of trials is large, the success probability is small, and their product remains a moderate expected count.

The approximation captures the asymmetric distribution near zero that a normal approximation cannot represent well. Its Poisson rate equals the binomial expected count, preserving the relevant event scale while simplifying calculation.
