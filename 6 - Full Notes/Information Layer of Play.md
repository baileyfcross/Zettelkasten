# Information Layer of Play

The information layer is the player's interpretation of sensory signals as facts about the game state. A perfect information space exposes everything relevant, while an imperfect one deliberately hides some facts through secrecy, fog, uncertainty, or another participant's private knowledge.

More information is not automatically better. The design should reveal enough for intentional decisions while withholding only what supports inference, surprise, bluffing, discovery, or another chosen quality of play.
