# Contrast Pattern Mining

Contrast pattern mining finds patterns whose occurrence differs substantially between groups or classes. Growth rates or related measures emphasize differential support rather than raw prevalence alone.

The resulting features can be highly discriminative even when they are not globally frequent. Small denominators and multiple testing can exaggerate apparent contrast, so reliability constraints matter.
