# Line Segment Plane Intersection

Line segment-plane intersection substitutes a segment's parametric equation into the plane equation and solves for the segment parameter. A value between zero and one places the hit between the two endpoints.

A negative value lies behind the start, and a value greater than one lies beyond the end. A near-zero denominator indicates that the segment direction is parallel or nearly parallel to the plane.
