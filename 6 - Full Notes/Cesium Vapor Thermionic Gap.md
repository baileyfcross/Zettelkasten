# Cesium Vapor Thermionic Gap

Ionized cesium vapor can fill the narrow space between thermionic electrodes instead of leaving a high vacuum. The conducting plasma helps reduce the [[Space-Charge Barrier]] that impedes electron transport to the collector. The source describes this arrangement for boiler-tube and reactor integrations. It adds material and operating-control requirements, so the absence of rotating parts does not make the generator simple to build.
