# Blender Shrink and Flatten Tool

Shrink/Flatten moves selected mesh components inward or outward relative to their normals. Positive and negative offsets can contract a region or expand it without relying on one global transform axis.

The operation is useful for adjusting thickness-like spacing on an irregular surface. Offset-even and proportional-editing settings affect whether the movement remains consistent across differently oriented components and how far its influence spreads.
