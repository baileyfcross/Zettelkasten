# Platform Readiness Assessment

A platform readiness assessment asks whether an organization has a recurring coordination problem large enough to justify a shared product. Strong signals include multiple infrastructure environments, demanding security or compliance controls, developers overloaded by undifferentiated operational work, and applications whose many components require consistent orchestration.

Warning signs include a small estate of simple or commercial applications, no internal development organization, an already overextended infrastructure team, or no budget for the skills needed to operate the platform. The assessment prevents [[Platform Engineering]] from becoming a fashionable solution in search of a problem and makes the intended value explicit before architecture begins.
