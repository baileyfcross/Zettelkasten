# Denominator

The denominator is the lower, nonzero component (n) of a [[Fraction]] (m/n). It specifies the number of equal parts into which a unit is divided and serves as the divisor in (m\div n).

The condition (n\neq0) is essential because division by zero is undefined. Fractions with different denominators can be compared, added, or subtracted by converting them to a [[Common Denominator]]. Increasing the denominator while preserving the same numerator generally makes each represented part smaller.
