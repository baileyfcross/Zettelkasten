# Kernel of Ring Homomorphism

The kernel of a ring homomorphism consists of source elements mapped to zero. It is an ideal because it is closed under subtraction and multiplication by arbitrary source elements.

The homomorphism is injective exactly when its kernel is the zero ideal.
