# TypeScript Access Modifier

A TypeScript access modifier declares how a class member may be used. `public` permits external access, `private` limits access to the class, `protected` permits the class and its descendants, and `readonly` prevents reassignment after initialization.

These constraints are enforced by the TypeScript compiler rather than by the generated JavaScript at runtime. They communicate intended boundaries and help the compiler detect inappropriate use during development.
