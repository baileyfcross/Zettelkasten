# Cloud Profit Margin

Cloud profit margin is the portion of business value left after the costs of delivering a cloud-supported product or service. Shared provider scale, reusable services, and lower up-front infrastructure can move the break-even point and reduce some delivery costs.

The margin is not guaranteed by adopting cloud technology. Service price, integration work, support, risk, demand, and the revenue actually enabled must all be included when comparing cloud delivery with an owned environment.
