# Rhumb Lines and Great Circles

A rhumb line crosses every meridian at a constant bearing, making it operationally simple to follow. A great-circle arc follows the intersection of Earth with a plane through its center and gives the shortest surface route between two points. Projection choice determines which of these paths looks straight, as contrasted by [[Mercator Projection for Constant Bearings]] and [[Lambert Conformal Conic Projection]].
