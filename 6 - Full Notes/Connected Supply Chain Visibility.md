# Connected Supply Chain Visibility

Connected supply chain visibility uses identifiers, sensors, location systems, and networks to observe goods and equipment as they move from source to destination. It can report not only where a shipment is but also whether temperature, vibration, humidity, or another condition remained within acceptable limits.

This turns periodic status reports into an end-to-end operational stream. Organizations can detect spoilage, delays, misrouting, or inventory gaps earlier and coordinate production and delivery more precisely. Visibility remains conditional on sensor coverage and data sharing across organizational boundaries; an unobserved transfer or incompatible system creates a blind spot in the supposed complete picture.
