# P-Type Semiconductor

A p-type semiconductor has acceptor impurity levels near the valence band. Electrons can move into those levels, leaving mobile electron vacancies called holes that behave as positive charge carriers. It is one side of a photovoltaic [[P-N Junction]]. P-type behavior depends on the type and concentration of impurities introduced through [[Semiconductor Doping]].
