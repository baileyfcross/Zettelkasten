# Deployment Risk

Deployment risk is the possibility that releasing a change will interrupt service, expose incomplete behavior, corrupt data, weaken security, or otherwise harm users and operations. It depends on change scope, evidence, reversibility, environment, and team readiness.

Frequent delivery can reduce the size of each change, but automation does not eliminate consequence. Review, staged validation, monitoring, flags, and recovery planning address different parts of the risk.
