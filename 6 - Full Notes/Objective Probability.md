# Objective Probability

Objective probability interprets probability as a property or model of the external phenomenon rather than as a representation of a reasoner's knowledge. Under this view, randomness or noise is attributed to the world being modeled.

The book contrasts this with subjective probability, which makes preliminary knowledge explicit. The interpretive choice changes how uncertainty, unknown information, and model error are explained even when the same probability calculations are used.
