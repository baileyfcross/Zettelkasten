# Constant Strain Triangle

A constant strain triangle is a three-node linear triangular element for two-dimensional structural analysis. Linear displacement interpolation makes the strain and stress constant within each element.

The element is geometrically flexible and easy to mesh, but a coarse collection cannot reproduce steep stress gradients well. Refinement is especially important near holes, corners, and concentrated loads.
