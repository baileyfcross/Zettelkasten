# Security-Convenience Imbalance

A security-convenience imbalance occurs when usability and protection are treated as competing absolutes. Controls that impose too much friction encourage workarounds, while effortless access can leave valuable systems exposed.

The source frames effective security as a deliberate balance: protections should match the asset and threat while remaining practical enough that users follow them. The [[Human Security Layer]] therefore depends on usable procedures as well as awareness.
