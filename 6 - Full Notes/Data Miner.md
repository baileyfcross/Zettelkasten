# Data Miner

A data miner searches prepared datasets for predictive or descriptive patterns using statistical and machine-learning methods. The role must distinguish stable structure from artifacts of a particular sample through independent validation.

The book positions data mining between conventional analysis and the broader synthesis expected of a [[Data Scientist]].
