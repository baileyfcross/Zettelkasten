# Socially Layered Reward Functions

Human motivation cannot be reduced neatly to a fixed list of biological rewards. Language, education, imitation, institutions, and relationships transform what people pursue, allowing learned moral and cultural concerns to guide behavior alongside more immediate drives.

This layered character complicates attempts to reproduce human motivation in an AI. A compact [[Reinforcement Learning Reward Function]] may specify an outcome, but it does not automatically recreate the developmental and social processes through which people revise their values. Human-like conduct therefore depends on more than maximizing a static signal.
