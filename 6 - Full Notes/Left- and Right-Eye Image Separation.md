# Left- and Right-Eye Image Separation

Stereoscopic imagery begins with two slightly different horizontal viewpoints of the same scene. The left view must reach the left eye and the right view the right eye; if both eyes receive an identical image, the intended binocular depth is absent. The separation can be provided by a physical barrier, color filters, or another display method. The principle remains the same despite the different devices used to present the views. The offset produces the [[Stereo Parallax and Convergence]] relationship that the viewer interprets as depth.
