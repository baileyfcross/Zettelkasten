# Ground Speed on a Desired Track

When the desired ground track is fixed, the aircraft must crab so the cross-track part of the wind is cancelled by part of its air-velocity vector. The remaining along-track components determine groundspeed. A pure crosswind can slightly reduce groundspeed because some true airspeed is spent holding the track rather than advancing along it.
