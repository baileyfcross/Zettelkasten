# Linear Isomorphism

A linear isomorphism is a bijective [[Linear Transformation]]. It preserves the full vector-space structure and has a linear inverse, so the two spaces are structurally identical even when their elements are represented differently.

Finite-dimensional vector spaces over the same field are isomorphic exactly when they have the same dimension. In equal dimensions, injectivity, surjectivity, and invertibility of a linear map are equivalent.
