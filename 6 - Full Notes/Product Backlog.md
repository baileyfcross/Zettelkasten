# Product Backlog

A Product Backlog is an ordered, continually revised list of everything currently believed necessary for a product. It can contain features, content, tools, defects, technical work, and experiments expressed as [[Product Backlog Item|Product Backlog Items]].

The backlog supports emergence by holding more detail near the top and larger, less certain items deeper down. It is a planning instrument, not a promise that every recorded item will be built.
