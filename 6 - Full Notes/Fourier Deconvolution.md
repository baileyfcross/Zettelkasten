# Fourier Deconvolution

Fourier deconvolution attempts to recover an input that has been blurred by a known response. After transforming the measured output and response, the input spectrum is obtained formally by dividing the output spectrum by the response spectrum.

An inverse transform then reconstructs the estimated original signal. The method is simple algebraically because of the [[Convolution Theorem]], but frequencies where the response is zero or very small cannot be recovered stably by unqualified division.
