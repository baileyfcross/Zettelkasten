# Significance Level

The significance level is the prespecified probability threshold used to control false rejection of a null hypothesis under its assumptions. A conventional value such as 0.05 becomes one input to power and sample-size planning.

Lowering the significance level while holding other inputs fixed requires more information or a larger effect to maintain the same [[Statistical Power]]. It should be set before viewing the results rather than moved until a preferred conclusion becomes significant.
