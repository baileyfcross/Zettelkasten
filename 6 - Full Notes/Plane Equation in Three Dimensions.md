# Plane Equation in Three Dimensions

A plane with normal vector $n=(a,b,c)$ through $(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ consists of points satisfying

$$a(x-x_0)+b(y-y_0)+c(z-z_0)=0.$$

Equivalently, it has equation $ax+by+cz=d$. The normal vector is perpendicular to every displacement vector lying in the plane.
