# AWS Consolidated Billing

AWS consolidated billing combines charges from member accounts under an organization's management account while preserving per-account usage visibility. Aggregated consumption can qualify for volume pricing that isolated accounts would not reach individually.

Central payment does not require workloads to share an account or identity boundary. Cost-allocation practices still need to attribute spend to the responsible team, environment, or product so a combined bill does not hide local consumption.
