# Second-Order Design

Second-order design specifies the tokens, rules, state space, and systems from which play emerges rather than scripting one exact path through the experience. The designer directly creates conditions and indirectly shapes the player's journey.

Its success depends on whether the resulting [[Play Space]] offers multiple viable paths and [[Meaningful Decision|meaningful decisions]]. The designer evaluates those indirect results through iterative prototyping, [[Playtesting]], and the [[Designer Loop]].
