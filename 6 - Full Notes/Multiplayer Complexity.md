# Multiplayer Complexity

Multiplayer complexity is the uncertainty and strategic richness created when several human minds act within the same game system. Players do not merely respond to rules; they anticipate, surprise, cooperate with, and communicate through actions to one another.

A simple system may remain predictable even with two people, but strongly interacting choices can produce distinctive [[Emergent Story|emergent stories]]. Human intentions make the possibility space difficult to reproduce with fixed computer behavior alone.
