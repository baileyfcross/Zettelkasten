# CS Principles Education

CS Principles is an introductory approach organized around broad computing ideas rather than mastery of one programming language. It was developed to connect high-school Advanced Placement study with accessible university entry courses.

The approach widens participation by including data, algorithms, systems, creativity, and social implications. Its breadth works best when students also practice constructing and evaluating computations.
