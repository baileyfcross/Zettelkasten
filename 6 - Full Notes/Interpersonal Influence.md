# Interpersonal Influence

Interpersonal influence measures how another user's relationship and prior interactions affect a receiver's decision. A sender who is a close friend, authority, or consistently successful recommender may receive more weight.

Influence differs from [[Individual Preference]] because it concerns who supplied the item rather than what the item contains. Social models can learn both factors and combine them when estimating adoption.
