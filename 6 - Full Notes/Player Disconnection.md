# Player Disconnection

Player disconnection occurs when a participant leaves or loses network contact during an online session. The game may not be able to distinguish a deliberate exit from equipment or connection failure.

Possible responses include forfeiture, pausing, replacement by an automated player, reconnection windows, or continuing with fewer participants. The rule must protect the remaining players without punishing unavoidable failure excessively.
