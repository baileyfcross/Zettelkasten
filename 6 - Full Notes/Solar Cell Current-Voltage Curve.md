# Solar Cell Current-Voltage Curve

A solar-cell current-voltage curve records how output current changes as operating voltage changes under a particular illumination level. It marks open-circuit voltage, short-circuit current, and an interior operating point where their product is greatest. Comparing curves at different radiation levels shows why the available electrical output of a [[Solar Cell]] changes with sunlight. See [[Solar Cell Maximum Power Point]].
