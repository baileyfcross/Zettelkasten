# Rocketry Technology Transfer

Rocketry technology transfer is the movement of designs, hardware, people, and practical knowledge among countries and institutions. Early advocates circulated books and correspondence openly; after World War II, victorious powers seized V-2 components and recruited or compelled German specialists; later programs licensed or adapted foreign spacecraft and engines.

Transfer is not simple copying. The receiving institution must rebuild production, testing, materials, guidance, and organizational experience around the imported element. National programs therefore often presented achievements as domestic while relying on transnational foundations. The process connects [[Project Paperclip]], Soviet postwar institutes, allied exchanges, and later international cooperation.
