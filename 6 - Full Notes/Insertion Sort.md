# Insertion Sort

Insertion sort processes items one at a time and inserts each new item into its proper position among those already ordered. It resembles maintaining a sorted hand of playing cards as cards arrive.

Its $O(n^2)$ [[Time Complexity]] is not optimal for large arbitrary collections, but it is an [[Online Algorithm]]: it can maintain a valid sorted result without knowing how many later items will arrive.
