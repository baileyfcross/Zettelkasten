# Production Mechanism

A production mechanism makes a resource available to a player. It may introduce the resource directly through a source or use a building, character, or facility to gather or convert inputs into usable output.

Production dependencies can create growth, scarcity, or deadlock. The designer must trace required inputs, capacity, timing, ownership, and what happens when a supply is interrupted.
