# Stoichiometric Aviation Fuel Combustion

Stoichiometric combustion supplies the theoretical minimum oxygen needed to oxidize a fuel completely. For an octane model of aviation gasoline, the ideal products are carbon dioxide, water, and nitrogen carried with the air. Too little oxygen promotes incomplete products such as carbon monoxide, while additional air changes temperature and composition as described in [[Excess Air and Adiabatic Flame Temperature]].
