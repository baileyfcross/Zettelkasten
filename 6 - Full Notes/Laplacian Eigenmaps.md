# Laplacian Eigenmaps

Laplacian Eigenmaps is a nonlinear dimension-reduction method that builds a neighborhood graph and embeds nearby observations close together in a lower-dimensional space. Eigenvectors of a graph Laplacian define the coordinates.

The method is designed to preserve local manifold structure rather than all pairwise distances. Its result depends strongly on how neighborhoods and edge weights are constructed.
