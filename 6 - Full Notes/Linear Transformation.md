# Linear Transformation

A linear transformation $T:V\to W$ preserves vector addition and scalar multiplication: $T(cv+w)=cT(v)+T(w)$. Once its values on a basis of $V$ are known, its value on every vector is determined.

Finite-dimensional linear transformations correspond to matrices after bases are selected. The [[Kernel of a Linear Transformation]] records inputs sent to zero, while the [[Image of a Linear Transformation]] records attainable outputs.
