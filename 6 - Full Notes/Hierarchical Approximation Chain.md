# Hierarchical Approximation Chain

A hierarchy can represent one observation as successive approximations. Begin with the global smooth of a dendrogram Haar transform, then add the signed details on that observation's path. Each added branch distinction gives a finer representation until the original vector is recovered.

The book relates this sequence to a partial order of information: an ancestor-level description is less specific than a descendant-level one. Its simplified face example uses the chain to propose a way to generate an object from a group average and a short sequence of refinements; the practical value depends on the chosen hierarchy and representation.
