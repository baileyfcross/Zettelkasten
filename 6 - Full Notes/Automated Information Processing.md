# Automated Information Processing

Automated information processing transforms, combines, analyzes, or classifies collected information through computational procedures. Its legal significance depends on the content of the data and the decisions or actions produced, not simply on the speed or scale of computation.

Machine-learning systems can make the relationship between input, purpose, and outcome difficult to explain. Accountability therefore requires attention to the training process, analytical assumptions, and downstream use of results.
