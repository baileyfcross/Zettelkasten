# Eigenvalue Problem

An eigenvalue problem seeks a nonzero vector $x$ and scalar $\lambda$ satisfying $Ax=\lambda x$. The matrix transformation changes the vector's magnitude or sign but not its line of direction.

Rearranging gives $(A-\lambda I)x=0$. Nontrivial solutions occur only for roots of the [[Characteristic Equation]], and their vectors form an [[Eigenspace]].
