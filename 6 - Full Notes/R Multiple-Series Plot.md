# R Multiple-Series Plot

An R multiple-series plot draws several related sequences against a common horizontal coordinate. Each series needs a consistent encoding through color, line type, or symbol, and the common scale must support meaningful comparison across the displayed values.

The first plotting call establishes the coordinate system, after which additional series and a legend are added. Crowded lines or incomparable ranges can hide rather than reveal structure, in which case small multiples are preferable.
