# Utility Pricing

Utility pricing meters use of a service and charges the consumer according to a defined unit. The payment is usually recurring, and the measured unit may be infrastructure consumption, transactions, allocated access, or another observable quantity.

[[Consumption-Based Pricing]], [[Transaction-Based Pricing]], and [[Subscription-Based Pricing]] are utility-oriented approaches in the book. The chosen unit should follow the value and cost drivers of the service closely enough to be understandable to both parties.
