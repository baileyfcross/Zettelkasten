# Tuned Stub

A tuned stub is a length of transmission line or waveguide connected to an active circuit at only one end. Its unconnected end may be open-circuited or short-circuited, and its length determines how it interacts with the field in the main path.

In the book's waveguide model, each stub is a hollow rectangular cavity coupled through an aperture in the main waveguide wall. The stub is open where it meets the waveguide and short-circuited at its outer end. Adjusting its mechanical length also changes its electrical effect.

Several stubs can be distributed along a guide to provide more control over matching. Their number and placement trade tuning flexibility against fabrication cost and ease of adjustment.
