# Mathematical Induction

Mathematical induction proves a statement for every integer from a starting point by establishing a base case and showing that one valid case forces the next. The induction hypothesis is the temporary assumption used in the second step.

The book uses induction for statements about repeated products, polynomial roots, and finite factorizations.
