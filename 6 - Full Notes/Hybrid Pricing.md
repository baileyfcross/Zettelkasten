# Hybrid Pricing

Hybrid pricing combines two or more pricing mechanisms in one cloud-service agreement. A subscription may include volume tiers, or a fixed monthly amount may permit usage within agreed limits while an outcome component rewards additional value.

Combining models can balance predictable spending, affordability, risk transfer, and incentives. It also makes comparison harder, so each component's measurement, thresholds, and contractual term should remain explicit.
