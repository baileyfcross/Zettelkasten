# EHR Laboratory and Vital Sign Data

Laboratory systems and bedside measurements contribute quantitative observations to an [[Electronic Health Record]]. Examples include test results, pulse, respiration rate, blood pressure, temperature, body mass index, and related reference information.

These values require more than numeric storage. Units, normal ranges, specimen context, measurement time, and repeated observations must remain attached to the result. Mixing kilograms with pounds or treating multiple readings as independent patient records can create a clinically meaningless signal, so harmonization is part of the analysis rather than a cosmetic cleanup step.
