# Surface Emissivity and Absorptivity

Emissivity measures how effectively a real surface emits radiation relative to an ideal black body, with values between zero and one. Absorptivity describes the fraction of incident radiation absorbed rather than reflected or transmitted.

Both properties depend on surface condition and may depend on wavelength, direction, and temperature. They influence [[Stefan-Boltzmann Radiation Law|radiative exchange]] and infrared temperature measurement, so coatings, oxidation, or an assumed gray-surface approximation can materially change a thermal result.
