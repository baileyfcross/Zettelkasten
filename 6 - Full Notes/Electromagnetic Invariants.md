# Electromagnetic Invariants

Electromagnetic invariants are combinations of electric and magnetic field components that remain unchanged when observers change inertial frame. The separate electric and magnetic fields generally mix under a [[Lorentz Transformation]], so their individual magnitudes are not universal.

In the book's simplified two-component matrix treatment and units with $c=1$, the determinant gives the invariant combination $E^2-B^2$. A boost changes $E$ and $B$ together while the unit determinant of the boost matrix preserves this difference.

In the four-dimensional formulation, invariant information is obtained from contractions of the [[Electromagnetic Field Tensor]]. Such quantities help distinguish what belongs to the underlying electromagnetic field from what belongs only to one observer's electric–magnetic decomposition.
