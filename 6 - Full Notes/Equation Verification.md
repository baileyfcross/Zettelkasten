# Equation Verification

Equation verification substitutes a proposed solution into the original equation and checks whether both sides evaluate to the same value. If (x=11) is proposed for (3x-5=28), then (3(11)-5=28), so it verifies.

Checking the original form matters because later simplified forms may have lost domain restrictions or gained extraneous candidates. Verification also catches arithmetic and sign errors without repeating the entire solution method. A value that fails the check is not a [[Solution of an Equation]], even if it satisfied an intermediate line.
