# Localization at a Prime Ideal

Localization at a [[Prime Ideal]] p uses the multiplicatively closed set R\p. The resulting ring R_p is a [[Local Ring]] whose unique maximal ideal is pR_p.

This construction studies behavior near one prime. A module or ring property is often global precisely when it holds after localizing at every prime, which motivates the notion of a [[Local Property]].
