# Peripheral Nerve Cuff Feedback

Peripheral nerve cuff feedback uses an implanted electrode wrapped around a remaining nerve to deliver controlled electrical pulses from a prosthetic sensor system. Stimulation can evoke a sensation referred to a particular region of the missing hand.

This makes the feedback pathway more anatomically meaningful than an arbitrary vibration elsewhere on the body. It still requires safe long-term electrodes, stable nerve contact, selective stimulation, and encoding that relates sensor magnitude to perceived contact. The interface is one component of [[Prosthetic Sensory Feedback]], not a complete replacement for the many receptor types of a natural hand.
