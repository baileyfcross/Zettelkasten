# Electronic Health Record

An electronic health record integrates longitudinal patient information from administrative, laboratory, radiology, pharmacy, nursing, and clinical documentation systems. Unlike a single encounter form, it can connect observations, orders, results, diagnoses, procedures, and medications around the patient and the healthcare provider.

Its value for analytics comes from this breadth, but the same integration creates heterogeneity. Structured codes, free text, images, and measurements with different units must be reconciled before a record can support reliable [[Electronic Phenotyping Algorithm|electronic phenotyping]] or clinical research.
