# Output Device

An output device is a physical device that communicates information from a computer to a user. It is also called a display and may stimulate vision, hearing, touch, smell, taste, or the sense of balance rather than only showing images.

The computer generates or models information and the output device converts it into a perceptible form. In a [[3D User Interface]], the choice of output device affects which [[Interaction Technique]]s are practical. Its [[Display Fidelity]], [[Latency]], resolution, ergonomics, and sensory coverage can all change task performance and [[User Experience]].
