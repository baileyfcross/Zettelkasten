# Astrofuturism

Astrofuturism presents spaceflight as the desirable or inevitable future of humanity. It joins technical forecasts with images of orbital stations, Moon bases, Mars settlements, and expansion across the solar system.

The idea helped early advocates turn rockets from weapons or curiosities into vehicles of progress. During and after the Space Race, it sustained expectations that each milestone would lead quickly to the next. The repeated failure of those timelines to materialize weakened the optimism without eliminating it. Astrofuturism remains culturally powerful even when political funding and human biology make the proposed future uncertain.
