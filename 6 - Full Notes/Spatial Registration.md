# Spatial Registration

Spatial registration aligns corresponding virtual and physical information within a shared geometric frame. A registered augmentation remains attached to its referenced object or location as the user changes viewpoint.

The alignment depends on tracking, calibrated camera and display relationships, object models, and the complete chain of coordinate transformations. Static calibration error and runtime measurement error appear directly as displacement between the virtual content and the physical target.
