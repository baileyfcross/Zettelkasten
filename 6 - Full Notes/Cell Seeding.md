# Cell Seeding

Cell seeding places living cells onto or within a scaffold. Density, uniformity, attachment, cell type, and the route by which cells enter the construct affect later survival and organization.

Too few cells may fail to establish supportive contacts, while excessive density can exhaust nutrients before vascular or perfusion systems are available.
