# L'Hopital's Rule

L'Hopital's rule evaluates certain quotient limits with the indeterminate form $0/0$ or $\infty/\infty$. Under the rule's differentiability conditions, the limit of $f(x)/g(x)$ can be replaced by the limit of $f'(x)/g'(x)$ when the latter exists or has the required infinite behavior.

The numerator and denominator are differentiated separately, not by the quotient rule. Other indeterminate forms must first be transformed into an eligible quotient, and repeated application is justified only while the conditions continue to hold.
