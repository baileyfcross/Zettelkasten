# Depth Cue

A depth cue is information the visual system uses to infer the spatial structure and distance of objects. Depth cues support [[Object Selection]], [[3D Manipulation]], and [[Navigation]].

Static monocular cues include relative size, height relative to the horizon, occlusion, linear and aerial perspective, shadows, lighting, and texture gradients. Motion can provide parallax. The eyes also supply cues through [[Accommodation]], [[Vergence]], and binocular disparity.

Cues can reinforce or contradict one another. A display that presents inconsistent depth information can cause false spatial judgments, discomfort, or reduced performance, so cue support is an important part of [[Display Fidelity]].
