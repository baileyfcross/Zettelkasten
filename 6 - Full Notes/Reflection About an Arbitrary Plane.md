# Reflection About an Arbitrary Plane

Reflection about an arbitrary plane is constructed by aligning that plane with a principal coordinate plane. First translate a known point on the plane to the origin. Then rotate the plane's normal onto a principal axis, which makes the plane coincide with the perpendicular principal plane.

Apply the elementary reflection that negates the coordinate normal to that plane, then undo the rotations and translation. If the plane equation is known, its coefficients determine a unit normal and therefore the required direction cosines. The composite preserves all points on the reflection plane while exchanging points on opposite sides.
