# Business Translator

A business translator combines data literacy with industry and functional expertise so analytical findings can be incorporated into everyday processes. The role connects technical teams with the people responsible for operational choices.

Translation includes framing a useful question, interpreting model output, and explaining what action the evidence supports. It addresses an organizational barrier that better algorithms alone cannot solve.
