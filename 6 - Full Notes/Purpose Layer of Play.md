# Purpose Layer of Play

The purpose layer answers why a player is participating. The purpose may be to win, explore, socialize, perform, master a skill, encounter an authored experience, or pursue a self-created goal.

Purpose organizes the other experience layers by determining which signals, facts, and actions matter. A designer can propose a purpose through goals and framing, but players may adopt another purpose that the game's possibility space permits.
