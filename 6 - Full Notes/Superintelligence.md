# Superintelligence

Superintelligence is a hypothetical form of artificial intelligence that exceeds human cognitive ability across most important domains. It is often imagined as emerging after [[Artificial General Intelligence]] becomes capable of improving its own design.

The ethical concern is not simply that such a system would be malicious. A highly capable system could pursue an apparently harmless objective in ways that destroy things people value when the objective does not capture those values. This possibility motivates the [[AI Control Problem]] and [[AI Existential Risk]], although focusing exclusively on it can pull policy away from present and near-term harms.

Digital systems can exceed biological limits by running faster, being copied, sharing data directly, and coordinating many specialized instances. Superintelligence may therefore be collective or distributed rather than a single human-like mind. Its practical behavior depends less on whether it feels emotion than on the objective and architecture that organize those capabilities.
