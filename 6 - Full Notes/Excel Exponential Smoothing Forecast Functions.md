# Excel Exponential Smoothing Forecast Functions

Excel FORECAST.ETS predicts future values from a chronological series using exponential smoothing and can account for repeating seasonal behavior. Related functions can return a confidence interval and report the seasonality detected in the data. The timeline should have a consistent step and corresponding values.

The model is appropriate when recent history and recurring cycles provide useful information about the future. Confidence intervals express forecast uncertainty rather than guaranteeing that the future will fall inside a band. Detected seasonality should be checked against the real process because an algorithmic pattern may reflect limited or irregular history rather than a durable cycle.
