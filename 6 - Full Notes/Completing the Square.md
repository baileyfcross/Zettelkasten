# Completing the Square

Completing the square turns a quadratic expression into a [[Perfect Square Trinomial]]. For

$$x^2+bx,$$

add

$$\left(\frac{b}{2}\right)^2$$

to obtain

$$x^2+bx+\left(\frac{b}{2}\right)^2=\left(x+\frac{b}{2}\right)^2.$$

When solving an equation, the same quantity must be added to both sides. The method converts the unknown into a square that can be isolated and reversed with a square root. It also supplies the underlying derivation of the [[Quadratic Formula]].
