# Biochemical Differentiation Cue

A biochemical differentiation cue is a soluble or matrix-bound signal that guides an immature cell toward a specialized lineage. Growth factors, hormones, and medium composition can activate developmental pathways associated with cartilage, muscle, bone, or other tissues.

The identity, concentration, timing, and combination of cues determine both efficiency and the maturity of the resulting cells.
