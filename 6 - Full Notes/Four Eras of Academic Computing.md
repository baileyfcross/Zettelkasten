# Four Eras of Academic Computing

Academic accounts of computing passed through four broad emphases: phenomena surrounding computers, programming as art and science, computing as automation, and pervasive information processes. Each era expanded what counted as central computational work.

The eras did not simply replace one another. Programs, machines, automation, and natural information processes remain active perspectives whose relative importance shifts with technology and practice.
