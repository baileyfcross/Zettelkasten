# Divide and Conquer

Divide and conquer solves a problem by breaking it into smaller instances, solving those instances, and combining their results. The decomposition repeats until each subproblem is simple enough to solve directly.

Repeated halving explains why logarithms frequently appear in algorithm analysis. [[Binary Search]] keeps only one half of its search space, while [[Merge Sort]] solves both halves and combines their ordered results with a [[Merge Operation]].
