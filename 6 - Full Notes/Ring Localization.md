# Ring Localization

Ring localization forms a ring S⁻¹R of fractions r/s whose denominators lie in a [[Multiplicatively Closed Set]] S. Every element of S becomes a [[Ring Unit]], while fractions are identified when a further element of S clears their difference.

The construction is characterized by the [[Localization Universal Property]]. It supports local study, preserves quotient relationships, and corresponds geometrically to restricting functions to an open region of a spectrum.
