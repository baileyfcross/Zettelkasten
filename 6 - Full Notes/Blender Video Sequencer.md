# Blender Video Sequencer

Blender's Video Sequencer is a nonlinear editor for arranging video, image, sound, and Blender-scene material. It is especially suited to animators making quick edits or planning a larger production inside the same project environment.

The Video Editing workspace combines the Sequencer with previews, file access, properties, and timeline controls. Although it omits some tools expected in a dedicated professional editor, it can complete substantial animation projects.
