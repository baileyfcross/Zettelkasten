# Variable-Mass Rocket Equation

The variable-mass rocket equation follows from momentum conservation when a vehicle expels mass with a velocity relative to the vehicle. Because the rocket is not a closed fixed-mass system, the momentum carried away by the exhaust must be included explicitly.

In the absence of external forces and for constant exhaust speed, integrating the equation relates the change in rocket velocity to the logarithm of the initial-to-final mass ratio. This logarithmic dependence makes propellant fraction central to achievable velocity change.
