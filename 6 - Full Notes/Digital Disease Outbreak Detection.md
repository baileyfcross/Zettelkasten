# Digital Disease Outbreak Detection

Digital disease outbreak detection uses changes in online behavior as early evidence of infectious-disease activity. Search queries, website access logs, blogs, and social posts may rise when people experience symptoms or seek information before formal surveillance reports are complete.

The signal is indirect. News coverage, seasonal interest, automated accounts, and changes in platform use can produce similar increases. A detection system should therefore establish a baseline, account for location and time, and compare digital indicators with independent epidemiological evidence before interpreting a spike as an outbreak.
