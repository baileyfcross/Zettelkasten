# LLM-Assisted Threat Intelligence Sharing

LLM-assisted threat intelligence sharing extracts indicators, behaviors, relationships, and summaries from reports across organizations and formats. It can normalize language and make relevant intelligence easier for defenders to search and compare.

Sharing requires provenance, confidence, classification, and handling restrictions so generated summaries do not launder rumors into facts or expose protected sources. Structured standards and analyst approval support reliable exchange.
