# Dynamic Mechanics

Dynamic mechanics are the procedures, strategies, interactions, emergent behaviors, and outcomes produced when players act within [[Inscribed Mechanics]]. They describe what the formal system does in use rather than merely what its rules say.

This distinction makes exploitative or unintended strategies part of the design's consequences. Players who optimize an incentive are responding to the system available to them, even when their behavior conflicts with the designer's hoped-for style of play. Revision should therefore address the incentive structure instead of treating every unexpected strategy as player misconduct.
