# Blender Transform Gizmos

Transform gizmos provide visible controls for moving, rotating, and scaling selected objects. Their colored axes connect manipulation to the active transform orientation, making the allowed direction or plane explicit.

Gizmos are useful when visual feedback is more important than speed. They complement Blender's hotkey workflow and accept modifiers for snapping or fine adjustment, so interactive placement can still be controlled precisely.
