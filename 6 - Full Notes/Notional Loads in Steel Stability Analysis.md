# Notional Loads in Steel Stability Analysis

Notional loads are small lateral forces applied at building levels to represent the destabilizing influence of initial out-of-plumbness and other geometric imperfections. They are proportional to gravity load at the level and act in the direction that produces the unfavorable frame response for the load combination being checked.

Their purpose is not to model wind or earthquake; it is to keep a stability analysis from assuming a perfectly straight, perfectly centered structure. Notional loads participate in second-order response, so gravity force acting through their induced displacement produces additional moments. Depending on the stability method and drift level, they may be added to other lateral loads or used in prescribed combinations. Their placement must cover both principal directions and critical frame lines.
