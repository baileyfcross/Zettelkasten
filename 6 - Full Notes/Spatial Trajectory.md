# Spatial Trajectory

A spatial trajectory is an ordered sequence of locations associated with successive times, representing the movement of an entity through space. Its position and time are linked rather than forming all possible combinations of independent spatial and temporal indexes. The `spacetime` package uses STT and [[STTDF Object|STTDF]] for simple trajectories. Speed or direction can be derived only after accounting for time intervals and the coordinate system.
