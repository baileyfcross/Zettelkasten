# Filler Word

A filler word is a spoken token or phrase that contributes little to the requested action, such as a hesitation or conversational preface. Natural speech contains these forms even when a formal command grammar does not expect them.

An NLU system can model or ignore fillers so they do not obscure the [[Intent]] and [[Entity|entities]]. Treating every word as equally semantic makes a voice interface unnecessarily brittle.
