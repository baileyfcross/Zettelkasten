# Viewpoint Offset

Viewpoint offset is the displacement between the viewpoint that captures or generates an image and the eye position from which the user interprets it. In handheld and video see-through systems, the camera is commonly located away from the user's actual eye.

Nearby geometry makes the discrepancy especially noticeable because perspective changes rapidly with viewpoint. Image warping can compensate when sufficient scene geometry is known, but otherwise the offset can distort spatial judgments and hand-eye coordination.
