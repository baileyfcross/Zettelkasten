# Simplifying Rational Expressions

Simplifying a [[Rational Expression]] rewrites its numerator and denominator as products and cancels common nonzero factors. For example,

$$\frac{x^2-1}{x^2-2x+1}=\frac{(x-1)(x+1)}{(x-1)^2}=\frac{x+1}{x-1}.$$

Only complete factors can be cancelled; terms connected by addition cannot be removed independently. Values that made the original denominator zero remain excluded after simplification. The factored form therefore reveals both cancellation opportunities and domain restrictions.
