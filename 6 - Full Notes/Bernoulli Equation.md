# Bernoulli Equation

For steady, incompressible, nonviscous flow along a streamline,

$$p+\frac12\rho v^2+\rho gh=\text{constant}.$$

The terms are pressure, kinetic energy per unit volume, and gravitational potential energy per unit volume. At equal height, faster flow is associated with lower static pressure, provided energy losses are negligible.
