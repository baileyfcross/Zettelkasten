# Four Lemma

The Four Lemma gives conditions under which one vertical map in a [[Commutative Diagram]] with exact rows is injective or surjective. The required hypotheses combine neighboring injective and surjective maps.

Its role is structural: exactness lets an element or preimage be chased across the diagram until the missing property follows. The [[Five Lemma]] strengthens this pattern for a five-object diagram.
