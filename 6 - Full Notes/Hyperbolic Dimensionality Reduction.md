# Hyperbolic Dimensionality Reduction

Hyperbolic dimensionality reduction maps high-dimensional points into a lower-dimensional [[Hyperbolic Space]] while controlling distance distortion. Reducing to two or three dimensions can make the same coordinates useful for analysis and visualization.

Landmark methods and randomized transforms reduce the cost of processing large datasets. The technique is especially suited to hierarchical data because low-dimensional hyperbolic space can preserve tree-like expansion more naturally than a flat plane.
