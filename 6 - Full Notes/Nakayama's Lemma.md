# Nakayama's Lemma

Nakayama's lemma states that if M is a finitely generated module and I lies in the [[Jacobson Radical]], then IM = M implies M = 0. More generally, M = IM + N implies M = N.

Over a [[Local Ring]], a basis of M/mM over the [[Residue Field]] lifts to a minimal generating set of M. The lemma converts information after reduction modulo the maximal ideal into information about the original module.
