# Ultrametric Inequality

An ultrametric is a distance satisfying the strong triangle inequality: the distance from x to z is no greater than the larger of the distances from x to y and y to z. It is more restrictive than the ordinary triangle inequality.

For any three points, the two largest pairwise distances are equal. The corresponding triangle is equilateral or isosceles with a small base. This geometry is why nested cluster relationships can be represented by a rooted tree rather than by arbitrary overlap among neighborhoods.
