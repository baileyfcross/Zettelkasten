# Practical Boundary of Automation

The practical boundary of automation depends on both algorithms and available machines. A procedure may exist in principle yet require exponential time, making its result unreachable at meaningful input sizes.

New hardware and improved algorithms can move this boundary, so practical automability changes over time. Undecidable problems remain outside it regardless of processing speed.
