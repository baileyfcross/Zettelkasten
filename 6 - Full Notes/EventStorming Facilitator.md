# EventStorming Facilitator

An EventStorming facilitator prepares the space and notation, keeps participation active, and protects the workshop from circular arguments. When a new group hesitates, the facilitator can place an initial event to demonstrate the action and invite correction. The facilitator watches time and energy, reinforces consistent colors, and marks unresolved disagreements as [[EventStorming Hotspot]]s rather than deciding domain questions personally. The role enables discovery; it does not make the facilitator the owner of the model.
