# Two-Dimensional Cell Culture

Two-dimensional cell culture grows adherent cells as a layer on a flat glass or plastic surface. Coatings and patterned adhesion molecules control where cells attach and how different cell populations interact.

The method is accessible and observable but does not reproduce the three-dimensional matrix, geometry, transport, and mechanical conditions cells experience inside tissue.
