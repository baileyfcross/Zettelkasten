# Clinical Decision Support Alert Fatigue

Clinical decision support alert fatigue occurs when users receive so many low-value or repetitive warnings that they increasingly dismiss or ignore them. A system can therefore reduce safety even when each alert rule appears defensible in isolation.

Alert design should consider severity, specificity, timing, and whether the recipient can act. Monitoring override patterns can identify poorly targeted rules, but a high override rate must be interpreted with clinical context. The goal is not maximal alert volume; it is reliable attention to the small set of warnings that materially improve a decision.
