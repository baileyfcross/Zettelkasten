# No Silver Bullet in Software Engineering

There is no single technology or method that removes the essential complexity of software design. Better languages, tools, and hardware reduce accidental difficulty, but designers must still understand and organize the system’s core concepts.

Progress therefore comes through incremental growth, reuse, prototyping, feedback, and cultivation of skilled designers. Claims of a universal shortcut should be tested against the conceptual work the proposed solution leaves unresolved.
