# Motor Control

Motor control governs the selection and execution of physical responses. Open-loop control follows an internal movement plan, while closed-loop control uses sensory [[Feedback]] to make corrections during the movement.

Performance depends on stimulus-response compatibility, device [[Latency]], visual feedback, coordination, and the tradeoff between speed and accuracy. Aimed movement often has a fast ballistic phase toward a target followed by a corrective phase that brings the motion onto the target.

[[Fitts's Law]] models how target distance and size affect the time required for this kind of movement.
