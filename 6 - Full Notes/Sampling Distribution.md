# Sampling Distribution

A sampling distribution is the probability distribution of a statistic across repeated samples drawn by the same procedure. It describes how an estimator varies even though the population parameter remains fixed.

Statistical theory and simulation both approximate this distribution. Its center indicates systematic bias, while its spread is the statistic's [[Standard Error]] and provides the basis for tests and confidence intervals.
