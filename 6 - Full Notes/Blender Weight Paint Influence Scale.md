# Blender Weight Paint Influence Scale

Weight Paint assigns each affected vertex a numerical influence from zero to one and visualizes the value through a color scale. Blue represents no influence, red represents maximum influence, and intermediate colors show proportional values.

The colors are an editing visualization rather than surface appearance. Their purpose is to make a hidden control field legible before that field drives deformation, simulation, particles, or a modifier.
