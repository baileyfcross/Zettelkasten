# Attribute Number Granularity

Attribute number granularity is the amount of numeric resolution available for distinguishing game states or objects. Small whole numbers are easy for players to remember and calculate, but a narrow scale can crowd distinct objects together and leave no room for later additions. Very large numbers permit fine distinctions but obscure consequences.

The useful scale is therefore just large enough to express the required variety. It should also fit the frequency of player calculation, the range of random outcomes, and any familiar real-world measurements. Internal calculations may use decimals while the interface presents rounded whole values that players can interpret.
