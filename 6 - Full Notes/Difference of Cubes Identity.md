# Difference of Cubes Identity

The difference of cubes identity is

$$a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2).$$

The sign in the linear factor matches the original difference, while every term in the quadratic factor is positive. Expanding the product cancels the mixed terms and leaves (a^3-b^3).

For example, (x^3-8=(x-2)(x^2+2x+4)). The identity is distinct from the [[Cube of a Difference Identity]]: (a^3-b^3) has two terms, whereas ((a-b)^3) expands to four.
