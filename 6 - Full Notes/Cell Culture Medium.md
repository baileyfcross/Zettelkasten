# Cell Culture Medium

Cell culture medium is the liquid environment that supplies cultured cells with water, glucose, salts, vitamins, amino acids, and other factors. Formulations are selected for the metabolism and signaling needs of a particular cell type.

Cells release acidic waste into the medium, so color indicators and scheduled replacement help maintain pH and nutrient availability.
