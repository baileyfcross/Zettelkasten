# Grease Pencil Modifiers

Grease Pencil modifiers apply broad procedural changes to a drawing without editing every stroke point. Like mesh modifiers, they are managed in the modifier stack and remain adjustable until applied.

Layer and material controls can restrict where a modifier acts. Modifiers can automate visual effects and also support rigs that animate a drawing through controls rather than requiring every frame to be redrawn.
