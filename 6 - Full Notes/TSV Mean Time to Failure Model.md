# TSV Mean Time to Failure Model

A TSV mean time to failure model estimates electromigration lifetime from fabrication and operating conditions. The model relates lifetime inversely to current density and exponentially to temperature, while current density depends on capacitance, supply voltage, switching activity, frequency, and conductive area.

A defect that reduces area can therefore have a disproportionate effect on lifetime. The estimate is useful for comparing [[TSV Void Defect]], [[TSV Bonding Pad Misalignment]], and [[TSV Bonding Surface Contamination]] cases, but its parameters remain technology-specific.
