# Optogenetic Muscle Control

Optogenetic muscle control activates genetically modified muscle with patterned light. Illuminating one side of a symmetric robot creates an asymmetric contraction and directional movement, while a moving light pattern can generate a coordinated wave.

The method enables remote spatial control but requires genetic modification, optical access, compatible wavelengths, and careful exposure levels.
