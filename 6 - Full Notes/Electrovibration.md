# Electrovibration

Electrovibration increases the friction felt by a dry moving finger through electrostatic attraction to an energized conductive surface covered by an insulating layer. Modulating voltage amplitude and frequency changes the shear force and can produce perceived edges and textures on a flat display.

The effect requires finger motion and normally applies the same signal across the energized region unless the surface is divided into separately controlled areas. Skin moisture and contact properties change its strength. Unlike an [[Electrotactile Display]], electrovibration does not pass stimulating current through the skin to activate nerves directly.
