# Network-Based Misinformation Detection

Network-based misinformation detection uses who transmits information to whom and the resulting graph structure. Abnormal patterns, such as sending similar material to many strangers, can reveal suspicious behavior even when individual messages appear ordinary.

Graph constraints can also preserve similarity between connected users in a reduced feature space. Care is required because malicious accounts may deliberately follow regular users to disguise their structure.
