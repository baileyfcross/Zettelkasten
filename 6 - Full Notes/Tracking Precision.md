# Tracking Precision

Tracking precision describes how closely repeated measurements of the same physical quantity agree with one another. It varies by sensor type and may differ among the position and orientation degrees of freedom.

Random measurement error limits precision. Filtering can make readings more consistent, but may increase computational cost and latency; a steadier augmentation is not useful if it lags noticeably behind motion.
