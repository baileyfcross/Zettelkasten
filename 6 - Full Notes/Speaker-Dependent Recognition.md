# Speaker-Dependent Recognition

Speaker-dependent recognition is trained or adapted for a particular person's voice. Because the system can specialize to one speaker's pronunciation and vocal characteristics, it may recognize a limited vocabulary more accurately with less general data.

The specialization is a constraint: performance may fall sharply for other users. [[Speaker-Independent Recognition]] instead aims to cover variation across many speakers.
