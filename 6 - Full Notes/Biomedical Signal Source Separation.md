# Biomedical Signal Source Separation

Biomedical signal source separation decomposes multichannel measurements into latent components that may correspond to different physiological sources or artifacts. Independent component analysis uses statistical independence and non-Gaussianity to estimate components without directly observing the original sources.

The approach can separate maternal and fetal cardiac activity or isolate functional patterns in multichannel recordings. The recovered components are not automatically labeled or clinically meaningful: their order, scale, and sign are ambiguous, so signal shape, spatial distribution, and domain knowledge must be used to interpret them.
