# User-Generated Content

User-generated content is digital material created and shared by individual participants rather than only by a platform owner or professional publisher. Social posts, reviews, images, videos, crowdsourced reports, and recommendations can collectively form a major big-data source.

Low publishing friction increases variety and scale, but anonymity, uncertain provenance, and mixed incentives make quality and trust difficult to establish.
