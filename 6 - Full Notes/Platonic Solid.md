# Platonic Solid

A Platonic solid is a convex polyhedron whose faces are congruent regular polygons and for which the same number of edges meet at every vertex.

If each face is a regular $n$-gon and $m$ faces meet at a vertex, their interior angles must sum to less than $2\pi$. This condition leaves only five possibilities: the tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron.
