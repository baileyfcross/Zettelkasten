# Blender Camera Projection Types

Blender cameras can use perspective, orthographic, or panoramic projection. Perspective reproduces converging depth cues, orthographic projection removes perspective convergence for flat technical views, and panoramic projection captures a much wider surrounding field.

Projection is distinct from lens and focus settings. Focal length changes perspective framing, while depth of field controls the range that appears sharp; the output settings then determine the rendered frame's dimensions and format.
