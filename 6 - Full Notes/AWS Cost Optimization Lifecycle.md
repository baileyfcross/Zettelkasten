# AWS Cost Optimization Lifecycle

AWS cost optimization is a continuing cycle of visibility, ownership, measurement, action, and verification. Teams first allocate and inspect spending, then remove idle resources, right-size capacity, choose suitable pricing commitments, and redesign inefficient workload components.

Optimization is not a one-time reduction exercise because demand, prices, services, and architectures change. [[AWS Cost Explorer]], budgets, tags, and architecture reviews provide feedback, while business value and reliability constraints prevent savings from becoming the only objective.
