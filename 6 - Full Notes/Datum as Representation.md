# Datum as Representation

A datum is a recorded representation of some aspect of the world, not the aspect itself. A row may stand for a customer, transaction, or event, but the selected fields capture only the properties that collection systems could observe and that analysts chose to retain.

This distinction matters when interpreting results. A model learns relationships among recorded attributes; omitted circumstances and measurement choices remain outside its view. Treating the dataset as a partial abstraction encourages domain checks before a statistical pattern is converted into a claim about people or causes.
