# Trivial Ring

The trivial ring has one element, so its additive and multiplicative identities coincide. It satisfies the ring axioms but is excluded from definitions that require a nonzero ring.

Its characteristic is one, a case separated from the zero or prime characteristics of nontrivial finite domains.
