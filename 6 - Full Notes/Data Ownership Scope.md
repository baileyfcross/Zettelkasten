# Data Ownership Scope

Data ownership scope specifies the powers, limits, duration, subject matter, and parties bound by a proposed data right. A right might cover access, copying, transfer, commercial use, alteration, or remedies without granting every power associated with physical property.

Scope must also address whether the right sits on top of existing law or replaces other protections. Overbroad exclusivity can obstruct reuse, while a narrow right may fail to correct the problem offered as its justification.
