# Null Distribution

A null distribution is the probability distribution of a test statistic when the [[Null Hypothesis]] is true. It describes the variation expected from sampling or random assignment without the effect under investigation.

The position of the observed statistic within this distribution determines a [[P-Value]]. The distribution may come from mathematical theory, a [[Parametric Simulation]], or a [[Permutation Test]], depending on the design and available assumptions.
