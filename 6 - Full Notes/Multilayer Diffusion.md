# Multilayer Diffusion

Multilayer diffusion spreads information, behavior, disease, or failure through within-layer and interlayer connections. Participation in several networks creates routes unavailable in any one layer.

Threshold, cascade, and epidemic models can be extended to this setting, but their behavior may change because exposures and dependencies cross layer boundaries. Aggregating layers can conceal those mechanisms.
