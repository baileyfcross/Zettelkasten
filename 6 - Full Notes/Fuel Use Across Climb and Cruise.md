# Fuel Use Across Climb and Cruise

Total airborne fuel use combines the fuel consumed while climbing with the flow accumulated during cruise. Pressure altitude, outside temperature, engine speed, and flight duration all influence the result, and ground-operation fuel must be added separately. Segmenting the calculation makes deviations easier to monitor through [[In-Flight Fuel Monitoring]].
