# Cultured Meat Water Use

Cultured meat water use includes process water, cleaning, cooling, culture medium, and water embodied in agricultural or industrial inputs. Avoiding animal drinking water and feed crops can reduce demand compared with some livestock systems.

A complete estimate must include facility sanitation and upstream ingredient production rather than counting only liquid present in the bioreactor.
