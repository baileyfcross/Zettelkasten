# F2 Mesh Fill Add-On

The F2 add-on accelerates face construction by inferring the next face from a small selected boundary and the cursor's location. Repeated presses can extend a strip of new faces while following nearby topology.

Its inference saves repetitive edge selection but can choose the wrong direction in ambiguous areas. The artist still needs to guide the cursor, inspect each extension, and correct topology rather than treating the add-on as an autonomous remesher.
