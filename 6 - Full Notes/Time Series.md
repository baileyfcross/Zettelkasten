# Time Series

A time series is a sequence of observations associated with an ordered [[Time Index]]. Its ordering makes change, persistence, cycles, and exceptional periods part of the data rather than incidental row order. A series may be [[Regular Time Series|regular]] or irregular, univariate or multivariate. In R, indexed representations such as a [[zoo Time Series Object]] keep observation values and their temporal coordinates connected.
