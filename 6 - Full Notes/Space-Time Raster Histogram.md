# Space-Time Raster Histogram

A space-time raster histogram groups cell values by a time-derived condition and displays their frequency distributions in separate panels. Monthly histograms can reveal seasonal shifts, spread, skewness, or multiple modes that maps alone do not summarize. Because cells are pooled across geography, the display loses location. It therefore complements [[Space-Time Raster Small Multiples]] rather than explaining where each part of the distribution occurs.
