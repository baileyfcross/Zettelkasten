# Story Point

A story point is a team-specific unit for the relative size and complexity of a Product Backlog Item. It combines the effort, difficulty, and uncertainty perceived by the people who will do the work.

Story points are not hours and should not be compared across teams. Their planning value comes from relating a team's completed points to its own historical [[Velocity]].
