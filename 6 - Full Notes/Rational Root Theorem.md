# Rational Root Theorem

For an integer polynomial, every rational root in lowest terms has numerator dividing the constant coefficient and denominator dividing the leading coefficient. This produces a finite list of candidates.

Failure of every candidate can prove a quadratic or cubic irreducible over the rationals.
