# Weak Duality Theorem

Weak duality states that the objective value of any feasible maximization solution is no greater than the objective value of any feasible solution to its minimization dual. Each dual feasible point therefore supplies an upper bound on the primal maximum, and each primal feasible point supplies a lower bound on the dual minimum.

If a feasible primal and dual pair have equal objective values, neither can be improved without violating this bound. They are therefore optimal for their respective problems.
