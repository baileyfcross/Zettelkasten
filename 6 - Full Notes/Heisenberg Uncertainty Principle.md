# Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

The Heisenberg uncertainty principle places a lower bound on simultaneous position and momentum spreads:

$$\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq\frac{\hbar}{2}.$$

This is not merely instrument disturbance; it reflects the structure of quantum states. A narrowly localized wave packet requires a broad range of component wavelengths and therefore momenta.
