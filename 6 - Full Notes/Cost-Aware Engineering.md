# Cost-Aware Engineering

Cost-aware engineering makes the financial effect of technical choices visible early enough to influence design. Teams request the capacity, duration, service level, and retention they need rather than receiving unconstrained provider access and learning the consequence from a later invoice.

An internal platform can encode defaults, limits, automatic shutdown, and right-sizing while showing an estimate and actual usage back to the owner. This preserves self-service but replaces the assumption of infinite resources with informed choice. Cost becomes one quality attribute alongside security, performance, reliability, and developer experience rather than a separate finance intervention.
