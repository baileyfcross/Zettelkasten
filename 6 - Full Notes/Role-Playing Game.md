# Role-Playing Game

A role-playing game lets the player control one or more characters through adventures or quests, usually with substantial choices about abilities, equipment, and development. Growth in power is common but not logically required.

The genre typically combines exploration, resource management, tactical conflict, character progression, and story. Computer role-playing games implement the world's rules and bookkeeping in software.
