# Jacquard Punched-Card Control

The Jacquard loom used chains of punched cards to determine which threads were lifted on each line of a woven pattern. Its physical card sequence acted as external, replaceable control information for the machine.

This separation between mechanism and instructions anticipated [[Software]]. Changing the card deck changed the produced pattern without rebuilding the loom.
