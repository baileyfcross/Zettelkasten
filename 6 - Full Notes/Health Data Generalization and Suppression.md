# Health Data Generalization and Suppression

Health data generalization replaces precise quasi-identifiers with broader categories, while suppression removes selected values or records. A specific age can become an age band and a detailed location can become a larger region, making records less distinctive.

The method reduces linkage risk by creating groups of similar records, but the protection is purchased with information loss. Broad categories can hide clinically relevant variation and bias subgroup analyses. The transformation should therefore be chosen against intended uses and evaluated for both disclosure risk and analytic utility.
