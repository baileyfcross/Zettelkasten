# Cultured Meat Nutrition

Cultured meat nutrition concerns the protein, amino acids, fats, vitamins, minerals, and other dietary components in engineered tissue. Culture and scaffold composition provide an opportunity to tune these quantities rather than simply copy one animal product.

An improvement must remain compatible with growth, sensory properties, cooking, safety, and credible nutritional measurement.
