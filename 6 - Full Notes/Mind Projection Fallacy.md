# Mind Projection Fallacy

The mind projection fallacy occurs when uncertainty in a modeler's knowledge is projected onto the external phenomenon as though the uncertainty were a property of the thing itself. A probability assignment then appears to describe inherent randomness rather than a conditional state of information.

Keeping preliminary knowledge explicit avoids this confusion. The distribution states what can be inferred under a description, while the phenomenon may contain unmodeled structure that different evidence could reveal.
