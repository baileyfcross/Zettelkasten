# Cloud Price Flexibility

Cloud price flexibility is the ability to change pricing arrangements as demand, volume, and circumstances evolve. A consumption model may suit a small or irregular workload, while a volume, tiered, or performance model may become more valuable as use grows.

The consumer can seek a different plan from the same provider or move to another provider when interoperability permits. The value comes from avoiding a permanently fixed cost structure that no longer matches the workload.
