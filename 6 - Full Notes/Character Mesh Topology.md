# Character Mesh Topology

Character mesh topology is the arrangement of vertices, edges, and faces over an animated figure. Two meshes can describe the same visible shape yet behave differently under [[Blender Subdivision Surface Modifier|subdivision]] and skeletal deformation because their connections and edge directions differ.

Production topology favors mostly even quads, avoids unnecessarily long faces, follows anatomical or stylized forms, and adds structure where expression or articulation needs it. Triangles and n-gons are not universally forbidden, but they should be placed where curvature and deformation will not expose pinching.
