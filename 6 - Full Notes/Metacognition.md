# Metacognition

Metacognition is thinking about one's own thinking. It makes assumptions, habits, and interpretive frames available for inspection rather than leaving them implicit.

For a designer, metacognition supports movement between [[Reductionism]], [[Holism]], and [[Systems Thinking]]. It also helps the designer reason about how a player forms a [[Player Mental Model]] while recognizing the limits of the designer's own perspective.
