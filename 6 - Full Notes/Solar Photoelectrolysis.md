# Solar Photoelectrolysis

Solar photoelectrolysis illuminates electrodes that decompose water, liberating hydrogen and oxygen at opposite electrodes. The source presents it as a solar-driven alternative to supplying all splitting energy from an ordinary external electrical source. Output follows available sunlight, so timing and collection conditions matter. It is distinct from [[Solar Biophotolysis]], which uses living algae rather than illuminated electrode reactions.
