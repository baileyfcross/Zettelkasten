# Gut-on-a-Chip

A gut-on-a-chip cultures intestinal epithelial cells under flow and cyclic deformation that mimic aspects of peristalsis. The cells can form villus-like folds and a selective barrier.

Introducing selected microbes allows the platform to study how microbiota, movement, and the epithelium interact without attempting to recreate the entire digestive system.
