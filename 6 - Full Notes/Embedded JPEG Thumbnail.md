# Embedded JPEG Thumbnail

An embedded JPEG thumbnail is a small preview stored in an image's metadata. A camera typically creates it from the full image through a device-specific sequence of resizing, filtering, enhancement, and JPEG encoding, with characteristic dimensions and compression choices.

The preview is a second representation of the captured scene and can preserve evidence overlooked during editing. If the full-size pixels change while the thumbnail remains untouched, the two may depict different content; if software regenerates it, its dimensions and encoding may instead reveal the new pipeline.
