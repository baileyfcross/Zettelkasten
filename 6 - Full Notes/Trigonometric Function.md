# Trigonometric Function

Trigonometric functions relate an angle to ratios in a right triangle and, through the unit circle, extend those relationships to all real angles. Sine and cosine give periodic coordinates, tangent is their ratio where defined, and the reciprocal functions describe the inverse side ratios.

R's trigonometric functions expect [[Angle Measure in Radians|radians]]. Evaluating them over a sequence and plotting the output reveals period, amplitude, zeros, and phase relationships that isolated calculator values can hide.
