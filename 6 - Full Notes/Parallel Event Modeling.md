# Parallel Event Modeling

Parallel event modeling allows many workshop participants to write and place [[Domain Event]]s at the same time. Unlike a meeting controlled by one speaker or diagrammer, it gives quieter or differently specialized participants a direct way to contribute. Clusters often form around separate areas of knowledge; sparse connections between those clusters can become early evidence for a [[Context Map]]. The model is reconciled afterward through discussion, ordering, and clarification rather than by preventing divergent ideas from appearing.
