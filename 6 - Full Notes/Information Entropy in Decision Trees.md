# Information Entropy in Decision Trees

Information entropy measures the class uncertainty present in a decision-tree node. It is zero when all examples share one class and grows as the class distribution becomes more mixed.

A candidate split is evaluated by the weighted entropy of its child nodes. A useful attribute produces children that are less ambiguous than their parent.
