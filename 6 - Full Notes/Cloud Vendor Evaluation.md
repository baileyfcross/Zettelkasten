# Cloud Vendor Evaluation

Cloud vendor evaluation compares providers against the consumer's defined functional, nonfunctional, and business requirements. It examines service maturity, features, interoperability, security, resilience, capacity, price, support, continuity, and the transparency of reports and service levels.

The comparison should include the consequences of leaving as well as the experience of joining. A provider that scores well on current features may still be unsuitable if data cannot be exported or an equivalent service cannot take over.
