# Freelance DevOps Niche

A freelance DevOps niche is a focused service position built around a recurring client problem, such as CI/CD automation, Terraform adoption, Kubernetes operations, DevSecOps, or cloud cost optimization. It replaces a vague claim to do every kind of DevOps work with a specific reason a client should seek that consultant.

The niche guides which projects enter a [[GitHub Technical Portfolio]], which case studies supply proof, and which communities are relevant for reputation building. It can broaden after credibility is established, but an initial focus makes proposals more concrete and reduces competition based only on hourly price.
