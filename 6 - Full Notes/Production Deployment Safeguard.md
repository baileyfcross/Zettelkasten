# Production Deployment Safeguard

A production deployment safeguard is a control that prevents or limits a release when required evidence, authority, timing, or system health is missing. Examples include protected environments, approvals, health checks, and bounded rollout policies.

Safeguards should be automated where the decision is objective and explicit where judgment is required. A control that is routinely bypassed becomes documentation of intended safety rather than actual protection.
