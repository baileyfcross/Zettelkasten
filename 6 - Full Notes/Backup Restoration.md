# Backup Restoration

Backup restoration reconstructs files or a complete system from retained backup sets after loss or corruption. A full scheme may require one set, a differential scheme a full set plus the latest differential, and an incremental scheme a baseline plus its ordered chain of increments.

Recovery time depends on locating, transferring, decrypting, verifying, and applying every required set. Regular restore tests provide evidence that stored backups are complete and usable rather than merely present.
