# Anthropocentrism in AI Ethics

Anthropocentrism in AI ethics places human beings and human interests at the center of moral evaluation. It can protect human dignity against systems that prioritize efficiency, but it may also assume that resemblance to humans is the main route to intelligence, agency, or moral worth.

The assumption affects both machines and the environment. It can exclude unfamiliar artificial beings from consideration and neglect the effects of AI on animals, ecosystems, and future conditions for life. [[Posthumanism]] challenges this hierarchy, while [[Human-Centered AI]] attempts to retain protection for human agency within practical governance.
