# Electrocardiogram Signal

An electrocardiogram records electrical activity associated with the heart through electrodes placed on the body. Its repeating waveform includes components whose timing and morphology can be used to estimate heart rate and detect abnormal cardiac behavior.

The measured signal is a mixture of cardiac activity and artifacts. Power-line interference, respiration-related baseline wander, electrode contact changes, and body motion can obscure the waveform. ECG analysis therefore combines signal cleaning with event detection, such as locating QRS complexes before deriving beat-to-beat measurements.
