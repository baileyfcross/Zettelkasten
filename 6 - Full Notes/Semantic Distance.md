# Semantic Distance

Semantic distance is the gap between feedback presented by a system and the meaning a player must derive from it. Clear, contextual signals shorten this distance; obscure or indirect signals make the player spend more effort interpreting state.

Reducing semantic distance helps [[Feedback]] update the [[Player Mental Model]] quickly. Together with low [[Articulatory Distance]], it keeps the interactive loop focused on the game rather than on operating the interface.
