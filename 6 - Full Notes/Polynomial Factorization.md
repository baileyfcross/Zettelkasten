# Polynomial Factorization

Polynomial factorization rewrites a polynomial as a product of simpler [[Factor]]s. It is the reverse of [[Polynomial Expansion]]:

$$ab+ac=a(b+c).$$

Methods include removing a common factor, grouping terms to expose a repeated factor, recognizing a special identity, and factoring a quadratic trinomial. A complete factorization applies methods repeatedly until the remaining factors cannot be decomposed further over the chosen number system. Multiplying the factors verifies that the original polynomial is recovered.
