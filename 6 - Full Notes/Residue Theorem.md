# Residue Theorem

The residue theorem states that a contour integral around isolated singularities equals $2\pi i$ times the sum of their residues, with the sign determined by contour orientation. The function must be analytic on the contour and away from the enclosed singularities.

It reduces a global integration problem to local coefficient calculations. With a suitable contour and decay conditions, it can also evaluate difficult real integrals by relating them to residues of a complex extension.
