# Gesture Interaction

Gesture interaction interprets a meaningful movement of the hand, body, or tracked object as a command or spatial input. A gesture may identify an action, indicate an object, describe a path, or manipulate a value.

Recognition must distinguish intentional input from ordinary movement and provide feedback about what was understood. Combining gesture with speech can let one modality name an action while the other supplies spatial arguments.
