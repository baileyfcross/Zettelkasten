# Lipdub

A lipdub is a video in which participants appear to perform a prerecorded song by synchronizing their lips and actions to the track. The original music supplies a stable scaffold while the performers, location, choreography, and camera movement provide variation.

Many lipdubs showcase a school, workplace, or other community in a continuous group performance. Their memetic quality comes from accessible mimicry: participants do not need to compose new music, but can use bodily performance to express collective identity and local creativity.
