# Control Storage Calculation and Communication

Control, storage, calculation, and communication are four historical streams that converged in digital computing. Control selects operations, storage preserves encoded state, calculation transforms it, and communication moves it across distance.

A programmable networked computer coordinates all four. The convergence explains why computing grew beyond arithmetic into a general infrastructure for information.
