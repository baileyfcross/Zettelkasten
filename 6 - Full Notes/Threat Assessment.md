# Threat Assessment

A threat assessment identifies the kinds of harmful events, actors, or conditions to which an organization and its information may be exposed. It considers attacks from outside, misuse from inside, compromised clients, interrupted providers, and failures along the data journey.

The result informs [[Security Risk]] and the selection of preventive, detective, and recovery controls. Threats should be reassessed when a cloud deployment changes its users, locations, service providers, interfaces, or stored data.
