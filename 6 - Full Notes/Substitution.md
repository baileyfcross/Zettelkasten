# Substitution

Substitution replaces a variable with an equivalent value or expression. It is the central step in [[Expression Evaluation]] and [[Equation Verification]]. Every occurrence governed by the assignment must be replaced consistently.

Grouping protects structure during replacement. If (x=-2), substituting into (x^2) gives ((-2)^2=4), not (-2^2=-4). An expression may also be substituted for a variable, allowing a general identity to generate a related one; replacing (b) by (-b) in the square-of-a-sum identity produces the square-of-a-difference identity.
