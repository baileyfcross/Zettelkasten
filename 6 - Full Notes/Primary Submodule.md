# Primary Submodule

A proper submodule N of a finitely generated module M is primary when rm ∈ N with m ∉ N forces a positive power of r to send all of M into N.

The radical of the ideal (N:M) is a [[Prime Ideal]] p, and N is called p-primary. The quotient M/N is then a [[Primary Module]].
