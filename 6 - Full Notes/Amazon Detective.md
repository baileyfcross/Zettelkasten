# Amazon Detective

Amazon Detective organizes security telemetry into relationships among principals, resources, network activity, and observed events. Its visual context helps investigators trace behavior around a finding and compare current activity with historical patterns.

Detective supports investigation after a signal has been raised, whereas services such as [[Amazon GuardDuty]] generate threat findings. The service helps form and test an incident hypothesis, but responders still decide containment, evidence preservation, remediation, and recovery actions.
