# Color-Space Keyer Tolerance

A color keyer defines a region of color values to treat as background. A simple two-dimensional keyer chooses a color and tolerance in a limited color-space view; a three-dimensional keyer can define a volume of related values. The chosen region must include screen variation without consuming subject colors. Narrow tolerance leaves fragments of the screen, while broad tolerance damages the foreground. This is why the color and lighting decisions in [[Choosing a Chroma Key Color]] matter before any keyer setting is changed.
