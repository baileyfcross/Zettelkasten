# Relative Humidity and Saturation

Relative humidity compares the actual water-vapor content or pressure with the saturation value at the same temperature. Because saturation capacity changes with temperature, air can approach saturation through cooling even when no water vapor is added. The result links [[Dry-Bulb and Wet-Bulb Temperature]] to dew point, fog, cloud, and icing risk.
