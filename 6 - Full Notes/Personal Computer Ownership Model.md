# Personal Computer Ownership Model

The personal-computer model gives an individual direct control over a machine rather than allocating brief access through a shared mainframe. Owners can experiment, customize, play games, and run interactive applications without an operator’s permission.

This desire was underestimated by advocates of time-sharing utilities. Falling semiconductor costs and hobbyist culture made ownership practical and socially compelling.
