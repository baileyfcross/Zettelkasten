# Competing Fairness Criteria

Competing fairness criteria arise when different plausible definitions of [[Algorithmic Fairness]] recommend different decisions. A policy might seek identical treatment, equal rates of error, equal access to benefit, correction for prior exclusion, or the best total outcome. Satisfying one can undermine another.

This conflict cannot always be resolved by better calculation because it concerns what justice requires. Technical teams and policymakers must make the value choice explicit, identify who benefits or loses under each criterion, and include affected people in the decision. A single fairness score can otherwise conceal a political judgment inside a model specification.
