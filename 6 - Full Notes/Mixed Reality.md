# Mixed Reality

Mixed reality is a set of approaches in which real and virtual information are combined in different proportions. It includes both [[Virtual Reality]] and [[Augmented Reality]].

A system can be located on a continuum whose extremes are a purely real environment and a purely virtual one. Its position on that continuum may change as the user interacts. The concept is useful because interfaces can share components and problems even when they mix physical and synthetic content differently.
