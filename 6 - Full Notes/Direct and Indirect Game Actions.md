# Direct and Indirect Game Actions

A direct game action changes an object or playspace through the player's immediate operation, such as moving a piece or aiming an avatar. An indirect action produces effects through a mediated system, delayed consequence, delegated unit, or chain of rules.

The distinction concerns causal distance, not importance. Indirect actions can create strategy by requiring the player to predict how a present decision will propagate through the [[Game State Snapshot|game state]].
