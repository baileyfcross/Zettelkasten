# Vector Potential

A vector potential is a vector field whose curl produces another vector field. A divergence-free field can often be written in the form $\mathbf{F}=\nabla\times\mathbf{A}$, making the zero divergence automatic because the divergence of a curl vanishes.

The potential is not unique: adding the gradient of a scalar leaves its curl unchanged. This freedom is important in electromagnetic descriptions, where different vector potentials can represent the same magnetic field.
