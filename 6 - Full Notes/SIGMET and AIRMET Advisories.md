# SIGMET and AIRMET Advisories

SIGMETs warn of significant meteorological hazards such as severe turbulence, severe icing, or dangerous convective activity. AIRMETs cover weather of lesser intensity but still operational importance, including the Sierra, Tango, and Zulu categories. They supplement aerodrome forecasts by describing hazards across a wider flight-information region.
