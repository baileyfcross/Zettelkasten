# Linguistic Boundary

A linguistic boundary appears where a term's meaning changes between business capabilities. “Product” can refer to marketing content, inventory stock, purchasing terms, or warehouse handling, each with different properties and behavior. Treating those meanings as one object causes ambiguity and unrelated change. Identifying the boundary is a strong first method for finding a [[Bounded Context]] because it shows where one [[Ubiquitous Language]] stops being precise and another must begin.
