# Playtest Plan

A playtest plan specifies the design question, suitable prototype and participants, time and place, facilitator procedure, and method of recording evidence. It prevents the session from becoming unstructured play whose results cannot be interpreted.

Planning also identifies what not to explain, which behaviors to observe, and how discussion will follow play. The plan should be narrow enough that the team knows what the session can and cannot establish.

The environment should minimize distractions and support consented recording when possible. The plan can pair open questions about salient frustrations or strengths with specific quantitative measures that are comparable across participants.
