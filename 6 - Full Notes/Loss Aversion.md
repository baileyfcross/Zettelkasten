# Loss Aversion

Loss aversion is the tendency for losing an amount to have greater emotional impact than gaining an equal amount. Equivalent changes therefore do not feel symmetric when one is framed as giving something up.

Game systems can motivate return by threatening the loss of crops, progress, or previously granted power, but the resulting pressure may be unpleasant. A designer can often preserve uncertainty or catch-up effects by granting positive events rather than imposing a direct loss.
