# Newton's Second Law

Newton's second law states that resultant force equals the rate of change of momentum, $F=dp/dt$. For constant mass it reduces to $F=ma$, so acceleration is in the direction of the resultant force, proportional to that force, and inversely proportional to mass. The momentum form also applies when mass changes.
