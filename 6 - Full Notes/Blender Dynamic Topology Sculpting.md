# Blender Dynamic Topology Sculpting

Dynamic topology, or Dyntopo, changes mesh density during sculpting. The brush can add or remove geometry where it works, allowing a local area to receive detail without subdividing the whole object.

Dyntopo favors freeform creation over clean production topology. Its result often becomes a high-resolution reference from which a more orderly mesh is later constructed.
