# Solar Collector Heat Removal Factor

The heat removal factor describes how effectively a collector's fluid extracts energy from its heated absorber. The source multiplies both absorbed-radiation gain and heat-loss terms by this factor in a [[Flat-Plate Solar Collector]] useful-heat equation. It depends on flow and collector design, so a coating's optical absorption alone does not determine delivered heat. Collector performance curves combine it with optical efficiency and heat-loss coefficient.
