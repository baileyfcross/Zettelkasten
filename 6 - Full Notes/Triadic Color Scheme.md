# Triadic Color Scheme

A triadic scheme selects three hues spaced evenly around the color wheel. The geometric balance supplies variety without abandoning a clear underlying relationship.

In a game environment, one hue usually carries most of the scene while the other two serve secondary structures and accents. Adjusting saturation and value prevents the palette from reading as three equally loud signals.
