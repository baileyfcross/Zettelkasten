# COMSOL Work Plane

A COMSOL work plane supplies a local two-dimensional construction frame inside a three-dimensional geometry. Profiles drawn on it can be extruded, revolved, or swept into solids, and the plane can partition selected domains.

The plane can be aligned with a principal coordinate plane, offset by distance, passed through a vertex, or defined by three points. Its local origin and axes determine profile orientation, so reversing axis order can change the resulting solid even when the plane occupies the same location.
