# Agile Values

Agile values guide decisions when a prescribed process does not fit the situation. They favor people collaborating around working results and adapting from evidence, while still recognizing a supporting role for tools, documents, contracts, and plans.

A practice is not agile merely because it uses short schedules or familiar terminology. It must help the team deliver value, expose reality, and respond constructively to what is learned.
