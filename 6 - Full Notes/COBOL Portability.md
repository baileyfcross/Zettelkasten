# COBOL Portability

COBOL was designed as a common language for business data processing with readable, English-like statements. United States government procurement rules encouraged manufacturers to support it across otherwise incompatible computers.

The language achieved an early form of program portability when nearly the same program ran on machines from different vendors. Readable words did not eliminate the complexity of maintaining large programs over time.
