# Karush-Kuhn-Tucker Conditions

The Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions extend [[Lagrange Multiplier Method|Lagrange multiplier reasoning]] to inequality constraints. They combine primal feasibility, multiplier sign restrictions, stationarity, and complementary slackness.

Complementary slackness connects each multiplier to its constraint: an inactive inequality has zero multiplier, while a positive multiplier can occur only for an active constraint. Under convexity conditions, the conditions can also be sufficient for optimality.
