# Platform Principles

Platform principles are durable guardrails for deciding how capabilities and quality attributes will be delivered. A capability states what the platform provides, and an attribute describes a property such as security; a principle guides recurring choices about how the team will realize them.

Useful principles shorten repeated debates without prescribing every implementation. A principle such as self-service first can direct interface, automation, and ownership decisions while leaving individual components replaceable through [[Platform Composability]]. Principles should be few, documented, and tested against the platform’s stated purpose rather than treated as slogans.
