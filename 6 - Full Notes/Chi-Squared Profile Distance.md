# Chi-Squared Profile Distance

A profile divides a row's occurrence counts by that row's total, so long and short texts can be compared by their relative word usage. Correspondence analysis weights the squared difference between two row profiles by the inverse mass of each column. A difference on a rare attribute therefore contributes more than an equal difference on a common one.

This chi-squared distance is appropriate to the frequency table that begins the analysis. The complete factor-space representation preserves it as Euclidean distance, making otherwise awkward weighted relationships easier to inspect and cluster.
