# Electrical Power

Electrical power is the rate of electrical energy transfer, $P=VI$. For an ohmic resistor, substitution gives $P=I^2R=V^2/R$. A real source's internal resistance dissipates some power inside the source, so its terminal voltage and delivered load power depend on current.
