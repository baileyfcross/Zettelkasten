# Graph Coordinate Bisection

Graph coordinate bisection assigns vertices coordinates from a geometric embedding and splits them with a line or hyperplane chosen to balance the parts. The embedding turns a combinatorial partition problem into a spatial one.

The method can produce fast balanced cuts when coordinates preserve graph locality. A poor embedding may place strongly connected vertices far apart, so coordinate construction and cut refinement are central to solution quality.
