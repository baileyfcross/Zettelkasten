# Business Continuity Plan

A business continuity plan defines how essential operations will continue through a disaster, emergency, attack, provider outage, or other major disruption. It connects service priorities with alternate people, processes, locations, systems, and communication paths.

For a critical cloud service, the plan should address loss of the provider as well as failure inside the consumer's own application. Tested interoperability with secondary services is necessary if work or data must move across provider boundaries.
