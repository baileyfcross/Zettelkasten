# Spatial Data Science

Spatial data science discovers interesting, useful, and nontrivial patterns in large geographic or spatial data sets. It extends data-science techniques by treating location, distance, neighborhood, and movement as part of the evidence rather than as optional descriptive fields.

Its methods differ from ordinary statistics because nearby observations often influence one another and different places may follow different distributions. The field uses prediction, hot spots, outliers, and colocation to support decisions, then tests those patterns with statistical confidence, domain review, and [[Ground Truthing]].
