# Human-Robot Interaction Metrics

Human-robot interaction metrics attempt to measure not only the person and robot separately but the quality of their combined work. Relevant measures include task success, productive time, operator workload, trust, intervention frequency, communication quality, and the fit between autonomy and the task.

A metric focused only on robot performance can miss confusion or excessive human overhead, while self-reported trust alone can reward an overconfident interface. Evaluation should connect capabilities, roles, and outcomes. The system succeeds when the partnership is safe, understandable, effective, and appropriate to the people affected by it.
