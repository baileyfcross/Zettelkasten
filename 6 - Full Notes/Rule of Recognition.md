# Rule of Recognition

A rule of recognition supplies criteria for identifying which norms count as valid law within a legal system. Officials use it to distinguish authoritative rules from other commands, customs, or moral claims.

It is a secondary rule because it governs the status of primary obligations rather than prescribing ordinary conduct directly. Its role helps explain how a legal order can be analyzed as a structured practice.
