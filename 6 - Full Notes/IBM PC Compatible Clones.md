# IBM PC Compatible Clones

IBM PC compatible clones reproduced the interfaces and behavior needed to run software written for IBM’s machine. Standard processors and third-party components made hardware imitation easier than in earlier mainframes.

Compatibility expanded consumer choice and drove prices down, while reinforcing MS-DOS as the common platform. IBM could not maintain exclusive control over the architecture it popularized.
