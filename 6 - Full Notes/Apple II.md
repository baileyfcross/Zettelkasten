# Apple II

The Apple II combined a capable personal computer with attractive packaging, color graphics, expansion slots, and professional marketing. Its design made computing approachable beyond the kit-building hobbyist community.

Applications such as VisiCalc gave the machine practical business value, while games and graphics broadened consumer appeal. The product established Apple as a major personal-computer company.
