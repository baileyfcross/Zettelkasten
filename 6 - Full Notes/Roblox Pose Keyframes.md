# Roblox Pose Keyframes

A keyframe records the poses of animated joints at a particular moment on the Roblox animation timeline. The motion between keyframes is interpolated, so the animator defines important extremes and transitions rather than manually specifying every rendered frame.

Timing controls more than total duration: moving two keys closer speeds the transition, while spacing them apart slows it. Strong blocking begins with the poses that communicate the action most clearly, followed by refinement of secondary joints. [[Roblox Animation Easing and Priority]] changes how the interpolated motion feels and how it combines with other animations.
