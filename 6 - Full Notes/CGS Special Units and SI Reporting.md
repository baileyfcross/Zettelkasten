# CGS Special Units and SI Reporting

Centimeter-gram-second systems give special names to units such as erg for energy, dyne for force, gauss for magnetic flux density, and poise for dynamic viscosity. The book supplies their SI values but says these special-name CGS units are not accepted for general use in commerce with SI. That is a reporting convention in the cited SI context, not a claim that the quantities cannot be converted or discussed. Centimeter, gram, and second themselves are also SI-defined units. See [[Algebraic SI Derived Units]].
