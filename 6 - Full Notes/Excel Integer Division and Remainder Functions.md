# Excel Integer Division and Remainder Functions

Excel QUOTIENT returns the integer portion of a division, while MOD returns the remainder. Together they decompose a quantity into complete groups and a leftover amount. This supports packaging, cyclic schedules, unit breakdowns, and tests for even or odd numbers.

The divisor must be nonzero, and signs should be tested when negative inputs are possible. MOD is particularly useful for repeating patterns: row or column numbers can be reduced to positions within a cycle. QUOTIENT and MOD answer structural questions about division rather than replacing ordinary division when the fractional ratio matters.
