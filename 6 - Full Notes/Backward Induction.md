# Backward Induction

Backward induction solves a sequential game by starting at the final decision nodes, selecting the rational action at each one, and replacing those branches with their resulting payoffs. The process moves toward the initial choice until the complete strategy is determined.

A [[Game Tree]] makes the order and contingencies visible. The method can expose threats or promises that will not be carried out when their decision point arrives, which is central to a [[Commitment Problem]].
