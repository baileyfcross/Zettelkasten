# Image-Based Lighting

Image-based lighting uses captured images of the surrounding illumination as the light environment for rendering virtual objects. The directional color and intensity stored in that representation produce reflections and shading related to the physical scene.

A light probe or reconstructed environment map supplies the observations. The capture must be registered to the scene, and its angular and intensity range must be sufficient for the material effects being simulated.
