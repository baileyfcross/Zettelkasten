# Dataset Abstraction

Dataset abstraction is the reduction of people, objects, or events to selected variables and records that a model can process. The dataset is a map of reality rather than reality itself: it preserves some distinctions, removes others, and reflects the purpose and methods of its construction.

Treating the abstraction as complete can turn measurement choices into apparently objective facts. A model may be precise about the representation while missing qualities the representation excluded. Ethical data practice therefore asks who defined the categories, what context was lost, and whether the abstraction remains suitable when data are reused for a different decision.
