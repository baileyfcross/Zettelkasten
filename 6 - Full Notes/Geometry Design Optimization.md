# Geometry Design Optimization

Geometry design optimization varies dimensions or shape while evaluating an objective under physical and manufacturing constraints. Size optimization changes parameter values, shape optimization moves boundaries, and topology optimization changes where material or connected structure exists.

The objective must be tied to a measurable outcome such as mass, temperature, pressure loss, stress, or cost. A geometrically novel result remains an engineering candidate until its mesh, physics, interfaces, and manufacturability have been checked.
