# Robotic Tactile Sensor

A robotic tactile sensor measures contact phenomena such as pressure, deformation, vibration, or tangential force at a robot's surface. Capacitive, magnetic, piezoresistive, piezoelectric, and optical technologies provide different compromises among sensitivity, flexibility, size, and durability.

Fingertip placement is demanding because the sensor must survive repeated collision and abrasion while preserving fine signals. Useful touch also requires interpretation: a controller must combine distributed measurements to estimate contact area, slip, and interaction force. Hardware sensitivity without a responsive grasp controller does not yield stable manipulation.
