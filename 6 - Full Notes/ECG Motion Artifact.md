# ECG Motion Artifact

An ECG motion artifact is a disturbance caused by electrode movement, changing contact, muscle activity, or patient motion rather than cardiac electrical activity. It is often nonstationary and can overlap the frequencies of the desired waveform, making simple fixed filtering inadequate.

Adaptive methods can use a correlated reference or recent signal behavior to estimate and subtract the disturbance. The central difficulty is avoiding removal of true cardiac change. Quality indicators and multi-lead comparison help distinguish a shared physiological event from a local electrode problem.
