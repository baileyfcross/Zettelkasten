# Direct Proportionality

Two variables are directly proportional when $y=kx$ for a constant $k$. Multiplying $x$ by any factor multiplies $y$ by the same factor. A graph of $y$ against $x$ must therefore be a straight line through the origin, with gradient $k$; a straight line with a nonzero intercept is linear but not directly proportional.
