# Spatial Distortion

Spatial distortion deliberately bends, compresses, or displaces parts of a represented scene to reveal otherwise occluded information. Unlike a physically faithful view, it sacrifices local geometric accuracy to preserve access to both the target and its context.

The deformation should remain understandable and stable enough that users can relate the changed view back to the physical environment. Smooth transitions can help communicate how the distorted arrangement was derived.
