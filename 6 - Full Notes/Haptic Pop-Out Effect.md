# Haptic Pop-Out Effect

A haptic pop-out effect occurs when a target property is sufficiently salient that it can be detected without serially examining every item. Roughness, coldness, and hardness can pop out during [[Haptic Search]], reflecting the haptic system's efficiency at processing material properties.

Pop-out can be asymmetric: a rough item may stand out among smooth items even when a smooth item does not stand out equally among rough ones. Features such as edge orientation usually lack this advantage and require item-by-item exploration, making response time grow with the number of distractors.
