# Model Uncertainty

Model uncertainty is the range of outcomes or explanations left open by incomplete knowledge. In the book's Bayesian interpretation, uncertainty is not an intrinsic character of a variable; it describes what the modeler can state about its value given the current description and evidence.

A probability distribution measures this uncertainty. New observations can concentrate or shift the distribution, while remaining incompleteness prevents the update from becoming absolute certainty in general.
