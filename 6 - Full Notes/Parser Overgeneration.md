# Parser Overgeneration

Parser overgeneration occurs when a grammar or robust parser accepts inputs outside the intended language and assigns them plausible-looking structures. Permissive rules that improve coverage can therefore increase false interpretations.

The risk is especially high when [[Robust Parsing]] skips unmatched material. Confidence scoring, domain constraints, and confirmation through a [[Clarification Question]] can limit the consequences.
