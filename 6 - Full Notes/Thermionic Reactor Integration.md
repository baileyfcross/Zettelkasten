# Thermionic Reactor Integration

Nuclear-reactor fuel elements can provide the high-temperature side of a [[Thermionic Generator]]. A hot fuel-associated emitter is surrounded by a cooler collector, with an ionized gap to carry electrons and reduce space charge. Electrical power is extracted directly from some heat near the reactor source. Cooling, radiation environment, electrode durability, and material protection still determine whether the arrangement is practical.
