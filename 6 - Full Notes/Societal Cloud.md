# Societal Cloud

A societal cloud serves people connected by a common geography, language, institution, hobby, industry, or civic interest. It can provide shared communication, documents, information, analysis, or services within that community boundary.

Aggregated and de-identified data can support group-level analysis, while the service can broker more individualized capabilities for members. The shared purpose must not be treated as blanket permission to expose personal information.
