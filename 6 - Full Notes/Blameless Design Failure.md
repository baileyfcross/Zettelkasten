# Blameless Design Failure

Blameless design failure treats an unsuccessful prototype or decision as information about assumptions, coordination, and the system of work rather than as evidence that one collaborator is deficient. This posture makes it safer to expose uncertainty early.

Accountability remains: the team still identifies what happened and changes its practice. Removing personal blame helps the review focus on conditions that can be improved in the next iteration.
