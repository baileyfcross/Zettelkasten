# Primary Decomposition for Linear Operators

If the minimal polynomial factors into relatively prime powers $\mu_T(x)=\prod_i p_i(x)^{r_i}$, primary decomposition splits the space into the direct sum $V=\bigoplus_i\ker p_i(T)^{r_i}$. Each summand is an [[Invariant Subspace]].

The decomposition isolates the behavior associated with one irreducible factor at a time. When the factors are linear, these summands are generalized eigenspaces and provide the primary blocks used to construct [[Jordan Canonical Form]].
