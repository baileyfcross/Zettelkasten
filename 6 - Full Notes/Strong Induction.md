# Strong Induction

Strong induction proves P(k + 1) under the assumption that every earlier case P(0), P(1), …, P(k) is true. With a suitable base case, this establishes P(n) for every natural number.

The stronger induction hypothesis is natural when the next object is assembled from components of several smaller sizes, as in prime factorizations or recursively structured trees. It has the same proving power as ordinary [[Mathematical Induction]].
