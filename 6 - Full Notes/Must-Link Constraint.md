# Must-Link Constraint

A must-link constraint states that two observations should belong to the same cluster. It can encode domain knowledge, repeated measurements of one entity, or direct analyst feedback.

Must-link relations are transitive when treated as hard equivalences, so a few constraints can create a large connected component. Contradictory or noisy constraints may require soft penalties rather than absolute enforcement.
