# Beam Bending Stress

Beam bending stress varies across a section in proportion to bending moment and distance from the neutral axis. Its maximum magnitude occurs at the extreme fiber for the usual linear elastic beam model.

Finite element beam results supply moment values, while section geometry converts those moments into stress. Mesh resolution affects how accurately peak internal forces are located under distributed loads.
