# Exact Bayesian Inference

Exact Bayesian inference computes the requested posterior without deliberately approximating its probability values. It applies symbolic transformations or exhaustive numerical operations implied by the model's decomposition.

Exactness can be practical when conditional independences keep intermediate distributions small. Densely connected models or many free variables can make the required sums and integrals prohibitively expensive.
