# Pin Fin Geometry

A pin fin is a slender extended surface, commonly built from a circular cross section extruded or represented by a cylinder. Its defining parameters include diameter or radius, height, placement, and the geometry of its attachment to a base.

Repeated pin arrangements can be generated with array transforms, but spacing becomes part of the physical model when neighboring thermal or flow fields interact. Measurements of volume and exposed area support comparisons with other fin forms.
