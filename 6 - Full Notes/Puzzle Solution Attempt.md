# Puzzle Solution Attempt

A puzzle solution attempt turns an insight into an action whose result can confirm, refine, or reject it. A failed attempt may return the solver to exploration because the theory was wrong, or it may show that the theory was incomplete and needs a more precise execution.

In action puzzles, the correct idea can still require repeated practice because intention and physical performance are separate challenges. Designers should know whether failure is testing reasoning, timing, dexterity, or all three; otherwise the feedback may teach the wrong lesson.
