# Eliminating Intuition from Routine Computation

The historical effort to mechanize calculation aimed to remove intuition from routine execution. Experts translated their practiced judgments into explicit steps so that repetitive work could be performed consistently by less experienced people or by machines.

This did not make intuition worthless. Expert intuition remained essential for finding the core of a problem, choosing a method, noticing anomalies, and deciding when a formal routine no longer fit the situation.
