# Regular Localization

If a Noetherian local ring is regular, then its localization at every prime ideal is again regular. A finite free resolution localizes to a free resolution, so the relevant projective-dimension bound survives.

This theorem makes regularity stable under moving to a smaller neighborhood of the spectrum. It supports the definition of a [[Regular Ring]] through all prime localizations.
