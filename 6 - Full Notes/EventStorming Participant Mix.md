# EventStorming Participant Mix

An EventStorming participant mix combines people who know how the business operates with people who will design and implement the software. Multiple experts are valuable because knowledge may be fragmented or contradictory, and people from adjacent functions can expose handoffs that one group overlooks. Too homogeneous a group merely repeats its existing assumptions. [[Parallel Event Modeling]] lets specialized participants contribute simultaneously while the shared wall reveals where their event clusters connect.
