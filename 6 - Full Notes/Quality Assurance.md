# Quality Assurance

Quality assurance improves the practices that prevent defects and create quality throughout development. QA specialists help define acceptance criteria, testing strategy, automation, regression coverage, and the conditions by which work is done.

Quality remains a whole-team responsibility. Embedding QA in planning and implementation supplies earlier feedback than inspecting a nearly finished game at the end.
