# Character Back Story

A character back story is the fictional history that precedes the events shown in the game. It can explain attitudes, relationships, skills, fears, and goals that influence present behavior.

Only details that support believable action or useful dramatic context need to reach the player. An extensive private history is not a substitute for characterization visible through choices, dialogue, animation, and consequences.
