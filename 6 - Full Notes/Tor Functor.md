# Tor Functor

Torᵢᴿ(M,N) is the i-th homology of P• ⊗_R N, where P• is a [[Projective Resolution]] of M. The resulting module is independent of the chosen resolution up to isomorphism.

Tor is symmetric in M and N over a commutative ring. Positive-degree Tor measures the failure of tensor product to preserve exactness and vanishes for [[Flat Module]]s.
