# Systemic Depth

Systemic depth is the presence of multiple meaningful levels of interacting subsystems within a design. It comes from nested loops and production chains rather than simply adding unrelated features.

Depth expands possible relationships while recurring structures can scaffold learning from one level to the next. This can make a game difficult to master without making it needlessly difficult to learn, an aspect of [[Systemic Elegance]].
