# Linear Equation in One Variable

A linear equation in one variable can be written in the form

$$ax+b=c,$$

where (a\neq0) and (a), (b), and (c) are real numbers. The unknown appears only to the first power.

Solving reverses the operations surrounding the variable: subtract (b) from both sides and divide by (a), giving (x=(c-b)/a). More complicated-looking equations may become linear after distributing, combining like terms, or clearing denominators. [[Equation Verification]] checks the resulting value.
