# Game Developer Playtest

A game developer playtest uses peers who understand how games are designed and built. These testers can identify systemic patterns, production risks, and familiar design problems earlier than a general audience might articulate them.

Their expertise can also distort the evidence because developers tolerate roughness and infer missing conventions. Their observations should answer an early design question rather than substitute for tests with the intended players.
