# Organoid-on-a-Chip

An organoid-on-a-chip combines stem-cell self-organization with the controlled flow and stimulation of a microfluidic device. The organoid supplies complex tissue development, while the chip regulates transport and exposes measurable environmental conditions.

The integration can improve maturation and experimental control but inherits variability from organoids and interface complexity from microfluidics.
