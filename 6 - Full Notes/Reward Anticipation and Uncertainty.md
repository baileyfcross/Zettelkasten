# Reward Anticipation and Uncertainty

Reward anticipation gives value to the period before an outcome arrives. A cue can become engaging because it signals a future reward, and uncertainty about which reward will appear can intensify attention even when the eventual prize is unchanged.

Games combine anticipation with [[Variable-Ratio Reinforcement Schedule|variable-ratio reinforcement]], visible near-success, and the [[Goal-Gradient Effect]] to sustain action. Designers should distinguish enjoyment of anticipation from value in the reward itself, because the latter can fall sharply once obtained.
