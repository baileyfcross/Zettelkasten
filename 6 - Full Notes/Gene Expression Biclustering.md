# Gene Expression Biclustering

Gene-expression biclustering searches for a subset of genes that behaves coherently across a subset of experimental conditions. It does not require the same gene relationship to hold across the entire matrix.

Biclusters can overlap because a gene may participate in different processes under different conditions. Objectives may seek constant, additive, multiplicative, or order-preserving patterns, so the coherence model shapes the biological claim.
