# Cryptology

Cryptology is the broader study of secret communication. It contains the defensive work of designing cryptographic systems and the offensive or evaluative work of [[Cryptanalysis|cryptanalysis]]. The two sides are interdependent: an encryption rule is useful only when analysis shows what information an adversary can recover from its ciphertexts, keys, or implementation.

The field builds larger protocols from lower-level mechanisms such as encryption functions, hashes, key-generation procedures, and random-number generators. Historical systems such as the [[Caesar Cipher]] make this design-and-attack relationship easy to observe before it is expressed through modern mathematical assumptions.
