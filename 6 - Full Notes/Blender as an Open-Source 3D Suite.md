# Blender as an Open-Source 3D Suite

Blender is a free and open-source suite for producing still images and animation from three-dimensional geometry. Its uses extend beyond film and television into games, industrial design, scientific visualization, and architecture.

Open source means that the program's code can be inspected and modified rather than remaining available only to its original vendor. This lets a worldwide community contribute improvements and makes Blender especially accessible to students, independent artists, freelancers, and small studios.
