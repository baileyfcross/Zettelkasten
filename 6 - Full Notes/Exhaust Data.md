# Exhaust Data

Exhaust data are traces generated as a by-product of a primary activity rather than gathered chiefly for analysis. Application logs, network traffic, website activity, and sensor streams can add signals that operational transaction systems did not capture explicitly.

Their volume does not imply analytical value. The traces may be low-density, inconsistent, or difficult to integrate with structured records. They may also disclose sensitive behavior despite seeming indirect. A project should ask what these traces represent, how they were produced, and whether their use is appropriate before modeling them.
