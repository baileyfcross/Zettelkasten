# CoreMedia Social Media Hub

CoreMedia Social Media Hub gathers content from social services for use within a managed digital experience. Editors can discover relevant external material and incorporate selected items into a site rather than treating an uncontrolled live feed as ordinary first-party content.

Curation is the key boundary. External posts have their own authorship, rights, availability, and moderation context, and they can change or disappear after selection. A portal therefore needs provenance, review, presentation, and fallback behavior for imported items. Provider adapters also need maintenance as APIs change. The hub complements [[CoreMedia Elastic Social]], which manages participation around the enterprise's own content, and illustrates the inbound side of [[Enterprise Social Portal Integration]].
