# Biohybrid Robot

A biohybrid robot uses living tissue to contribute to sensing, control, or response while operating as an engineered machine. Current examples commonly attach muscle to flexible skeletons so contraction creates movement.

The robot's material can mature, fatigue, strengthen, or heal, making biological state and environmental support part of its control problem.
