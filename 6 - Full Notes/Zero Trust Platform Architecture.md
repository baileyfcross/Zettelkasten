# Zero Trust Platform Architecture

Zero trust platform architecture treats network location and prior access as insufficient proof of trust. Each human, workload, tool, and automation path must present identity and receive only the authorization required for the current interaction.

The model combines strong authentication, narrowly scoped permissions, encrypted communication, segmentation, credential rotation, and continuous audit. It fits platform self-service when identity and policy are encoded behind workflows rather than delegated as unrestricted infrastructure access. Verification should continue across source, pipeline, registry, cluster, and service boundaries because compromise at one stage must not imply authority everywhere else.
