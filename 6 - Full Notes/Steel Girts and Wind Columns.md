# Steel Girts and Wind Columns

Girts are horizontal members that support exterior wall cladding against wind pressure and deliver reactions to columns or other vertical supports. Wind columns are secondary vertical members that reduce girt or cladding span and transfer lateral wall load into roof and foundation levels. Their loads may reverse as pressure changes to suction.

These members are often governed by deflection because excessive movement can damage brittle cladding or open joints before steel strength is exhausted. Eccentric cladding attachment can introduce torsion, and sag rods or bridging may provide construction and weak-axis stability. Connections must accommodate intended vertical building movement while still transferring wind. Corrosion exposure and moisture details are especially important near exterior envelopes.
