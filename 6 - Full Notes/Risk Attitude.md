# Risk Attitude

Risk attitude describes how a decision maker values uncertainty beyond its [[Expected Value]]. A risk-neutral chooser follows expected value, a risk-averse chooser accepts a lower expectation to avoid a severe outcome, and a risk-loving chooser accepts a lower expectation for access to a large possible gain.

The same player can display different attitudes across contexts, as illustrated by buying insurance while also buying lottery tickets. A [[Risk-Reward Decision]] should therefore account for payoff shape and perceived consequences, not only the average return.
