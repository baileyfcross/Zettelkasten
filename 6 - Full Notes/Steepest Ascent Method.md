# Steepest Ascent Method

The steepest ascent method iteratively moves in the direction of the [[Gradient]], which gives the greatest first-order increase of a differentiable objective.

A one-dimensional search chooses how far to move along that direction. Recomputing the gradient creates a new direction at each point, and the method stops when improvement or gradient magnitude is sufficiently small.
