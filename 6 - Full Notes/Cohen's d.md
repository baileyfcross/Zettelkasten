# Cohen's d

Cohen's d is a standardized difference between two means, expressed relative to a measure of within-group variability. It puts differences measured on different continuous scales into comparable standard-deviation units.

The statistic can supply the effect argument for a two-sample power calculation. It should not be confused with the unstandardized difference in means, and the choice of pooled or alternative standardization must match the design and software function used.
