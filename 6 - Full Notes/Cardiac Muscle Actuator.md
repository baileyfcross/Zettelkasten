# Cardiac Muscle Actuator

A cardiac muscle actuator uses cardiomyocytes whose electrically coupled contractions produce rhythmic force. Cells patterned on a flexible film can bend or coil the substrate as they beat.

Spontaneous synchronized activity suits pumps and cyclic swimmers, while external pacing changes frequency. Continuous beating makes cardiac tissue less suited to actions that must remain off for long periods.
