# Solving Quadratics by Factoring

Solving a quadratic by factoring first rewrites the equation in standard form, factors the polynomial, and applies the [[Zero Product Property]]. For example,

$$x^2+2x-3=0$$

factors as ((x+3)(x-1)=0), so (x=-3) or (x=1).

The method is efficient for a [[Factorable Quadratic]], but it depends on setting the product equal to zero. Factoring an expression without first moving all terms to one side does not directly create the separate linear equations.
