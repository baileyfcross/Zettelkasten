# Human Spaceflight

Human spaceflight carries people beyond the atmosphere in vehicles that provide life support, guidance, safe acceleration, protection, and return or long-term habitation. It adds biological and safety requirements that make a mission much more expensive and complex than sending a robotic instrument.

People can perform flexible maintenance, experiments, and public representation, but political meaning has often outweighed direct scientific necessity. Human missions became the most visible signals of national capability during the Space Race and continued afterward through stations, international partnerships, emerging national programs, and private ventures.
