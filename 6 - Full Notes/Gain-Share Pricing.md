# Gain-Share Pricing

Gain-share pricing gives a provider an agreed portion of improvement beyond a baseline. The consumer compares actual performance with historical or standard performance and shares some of the resulting gain through a predetermined formula.

The model rewards the provider for exceeding expected service levels and can be combined with penalties for underperformance. A credible baseline and a clear method for calculating the gain are essential.
