# Matrix Representation of a Linear Transformation

Given ordered bases $\mathcal B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ for $V$ and $\mathcal C$ for $W$, the matrix of $T:V\to W$ has column $j$ equal to $[T(b_j)]_{\mathcal C}$. It therefore satisfies $[T(v)]_{\mathcal C}=A[v]_{\mathcal B}$.

This construction is a one-to-one correspondence between linear maps and matrices of the appropriate size. Composition of transformations becomes [[Matrix Multiplication]], with the matrix of $U\circ T$ equal to the product of their coordinate matrices.
