# Dice Sum Distribution

A dice sum distribution counts the ordered combinations that produce each total when two or more dice are added. Although each die is uniform, the sums are not: middle totals have more producing combinations than extreme totals.

For two six-sided dice, seven is more likely than two because six ordered pairs produce seven while only one produces two. Designers should model the combination space rather than treating every visible total as equally likely.
