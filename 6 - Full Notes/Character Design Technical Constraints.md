# Character Design Technical Constraints

Character design technical constraints are implementation limits considered while the visual concept is still inexpensive to change. Long simulated hair, layered clothing, tiny accessories, high polygon counts, large textures, or complex transparency can create costs in modeling, rigging, simulation, rendering, and real-time playback.

Constraints should shape rather than merely diminish the design. Choosing modeled hair for a stylized character, enlarging important details, or simplifying unseen surfaces can preserve the intended identity while keeping the asset achievable for its medium and schedule.
