# Degrees of Freedom

Degrees of freedom are the independent dimensions in which a body can move. They describe the possible motion of an [[Input Device]], an articulated body part, or a virtual object.

A tracker commonly reports six degrees of freedom: translation along the x, y, and z axes and rotation as yaw, pitch, and roll. The number of degrees a device can sense indicates some of its capacity, but control integration also matters. Six values controlled together by one coordinated movement behave differently from values split across several controls.

Reducing unnecessary degrees of freedom can make [[3D Manipulation]] or [[3D Travel]] more precise and easier to control.
