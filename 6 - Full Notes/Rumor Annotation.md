# Rumor Annotation

Rumor annotation assigns structured labels to messages or events so propagation and veracity can be studied. Labels may distinguish rumor from non-rumor, record disputed or unknown truth values, and identify supporting, questioning, or correcting reactions.

Annotation converts qualitative judgments into training and evaluation data. Its reliability depends on clear definitions, evidence available to annotators, and agreement among independent judgments.
