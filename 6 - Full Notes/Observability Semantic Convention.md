# Observability Semantic Convention

An observability semantic convention gives common names and meanings to entities, attributes, and events. Stable fields for services, environments, versions, clusters, deployment stages, and owners let independently produced signals describe the same system in compatible terms.

Conventions matter especially in disposable cloud-native infrastructure, where instance names change after restarts or scaling. Dashboards, access rules, retention policies, and AIOps analysis can then operate on durable metadata rather than on one ephemeral host or Pod name.
