# Normalized Cut Relaxation

Normalized cut evaluates each group's boundary relative to the total edge weight incident on that group. It therefore balances separation against graph volume and is sensitive to connectivity rather than group size alone.

Its discrete optimization is relaxed to a generalized or normalized Laplacian eigenproblem. The final partition remains an approximation whose quality depends on how continuous eigenvectors are discretized.
