# Ethical Black Box

An ethical black box is a recorder built into an autonomous system to preserve data about its sensors, internal state, decisions, and actions. Like an aircraft recorder, it is intended to help investigators reconstruct what happened after an accident or harmful event.

The device supports [[AI Traceability]] without making the decision model itself fully interpretable. Its ethical effectiveness depends on what it records, how long records are kept, who can access them, and whether the evidence leads to accountability. Recording can also create privacy risks, so the investigation benefit must be designed together with data protection.
