# Primary Cell

A primary cell is isolated directly from living tissue and retains many characteristics of its source environment. This biological relevance makes primary cultures valuable for modeling healthy or diseased function.

Primary cells have limited capacity to divide, differ among donors, and may lose specialized behavior outside the body, restricting the scale and repeatability of fabrication.
