# AR Actor

An AR actor is a real or virtual entity that participates in an authored augmented experience. An actor has an identity and may carry geometry, appearance, state, behavior, or relationships to other actors and physical anchors.

Treating both real and virtual entities as actors lets the author define interactions across the real-virtual boundary. A tracked physical object can therefore trigger or constrain behavior just as a generated character can.
