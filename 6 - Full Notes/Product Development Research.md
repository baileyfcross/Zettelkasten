# Product Development Research

Product development research investigates and creates an information-system solution through a cycle of analysis, synthesis, implementation, and evaluation. The artifact is both an intervention in a practical problem and a source of evidence about the principles embodied in its design.

System analysis identifies the problem and goals, clarifies the mission, decomposes required functions and tasks, and evaluates possible methods and means. System synthesis selects and integrates a solution, implements it, and tests whether the resulting product meets the stated requirements and constraints.

Customer and user feedback should enter throughout the cycle rather than only after completion. Evaluation can expose mistaken requirements as well as implementation defects, causing the project to revisit its problem formulation and design assumptions.
