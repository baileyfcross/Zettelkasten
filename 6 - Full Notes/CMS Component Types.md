# CMS Component Types

CMS components are reusable structured pieces that can be composed into larger content items. A component may encapsulate a heading, image, quotation, contact card, or another recurring combination of fields and rendering rules.

Components give editors flexible composition without abandoning structure. Their contracts should remain focused because an overly general component shifts meaning into informal editor conventions and makes templates harder to maintain.
