# Cloud Health Monitoring Service

A cloud health monitoring service collects measurements from personal or wearable devices, analyzes them, and alerts an individual or nominated caregiver when defined conditions occur. The workflow joins sensing, transmission, storage, analysis, and notification.

Aggregated anonymized measurements can also support population-level analysis, while individual data remains subject to strong privacy and consent boundaries. A prediction or threshold alert supports attention; it does not replace clinical judgment.
