# Machine Consciousness

Machine consciousness can mean either outward cognitive capacities associated with consciousness or the presence of subjective experience. The outward sense includes awareness of the world, integration of information, reflection, and perhaps self-awareness; the phenomenal sense asks whether there is something it feels like to be the machine.

An [[Artificial General Intelligence]] may need many outward capacities to model its situation and coordinate action without possessing human-like experience. The ethical distinction matters because functional competence can make a system persuasive, while the capacity for felt pleasure or pain would support a stronger claim to [[Machine Moral Patiency]].
