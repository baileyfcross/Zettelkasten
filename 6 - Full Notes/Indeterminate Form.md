# Indeterminate Form

An indeterminate form is a symbolic outcome such as $0/0$, $\infty/\infty$, $0\cdot\infty$, $1^\infty$, or $\infty^0$ that does not determine a limit by direct substitution. Different functions can produce the same form while approaching different values.

The form signals that the competing rates must be analyzed rather than treated as ordinary arithmetic. Algebraic simplification, a series expansion, a known comparison limit, or [[L'Hopital's Rule]] can expose the actual limiting behavior.
