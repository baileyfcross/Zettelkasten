# Conditioned Scatterplot

A conditioned scatterplot divides observations into panels according to one or more conditioning variables and plots the same relationship within each subset. Time-derived factors such as month expose how an association changes seasonally. Additional panels may distinguish alternative statistics or transformations. Consistent scales support comparison, while transparency, rugs, and smoothers can reveal distribution and shape within each panel without collapsing the subsets into a single average relationship.
