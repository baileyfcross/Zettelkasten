# Two-Dimensional Heat Conduction

Two-dimensional heat conduction allows temperature to vary over an area. Triangular or quadrilateral elements model regions with multiple boundaries, insulation, prescribed temperature, and convection.

The formulation solves nodal temperatures across the surface and then recovers directional gradients and fluxes. Two-dimensional modeling is needed when lateral boundary effects cannot be represented by a one-dimensional path.
