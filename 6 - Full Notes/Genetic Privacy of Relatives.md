# Genetic Privacy of Relatives

Genetic privacy of relatives recognizes that one person's genomic information reveals probable facts about biologically related people. A donor's decision to sequence or publish a cell line can expose family risks and ancestry without relatives participating.

Individual consent therefore has relational effects that data-access and disclosure policies should acknowledge.
