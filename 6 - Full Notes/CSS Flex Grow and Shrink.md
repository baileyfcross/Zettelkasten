# CSS Flex Grow and Shrink

The `flex-grow` factor determines how positive free space is divided among flex items. A factor represents a proportion of the sum of growth factors, so items with larger factors receive more of the remaining space.

The `flex-shrink` factor participates when the line is too small. Shrinkage is scaled by both the factor and the item's flex base size, preventing a small item and a large item with equal factors from losing the same raw width.
