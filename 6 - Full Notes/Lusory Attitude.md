# Lusory Attitude

The lusory attitude is a player's voluntary willingness to accept a game's arbitrary rules and inefficient means for the sake of the activity. A person agrees to pursue a goal through the permitted actions even when an easier real-world method exists.

This attitude turns restriction into play. It depends on consent to the challenge rather than ignorance of alternatives, and it can disappear when rules feel incoherent or participation ceases to be voluntary. Designers cultivate it by making the constrained activity itself meaningful, legible, and worth sustaining.
