# Cost-Constrained Architecture

Cost-constrained architecture treats budget as a design boundary alongside behavior and quality. Compute capacity, managed services, storage, data transfer, licensing, development effort, and operational skill all contribute to the feasible solution.

The least expensive component does not necessarily produce the least expensive system. An architect compares total delivery and operating consequences while preserving the qualities that stakeholders have identified as essential.
