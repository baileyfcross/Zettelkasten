# Dream Text Ultrametricity

The book maps collections of dream reports and their words into a full Euclidean correspondence-analysis factor space, then counts approximately ultrametric triangles among text-unit locations. In its case studies, dream reports show a higher measured tendency than several literary and technical text collections.

The coefficient is sensitive to corpus selection, text segmentation, word encoding, dimensionality, and the angular rule used to count triangles. It characterizes relationships in written reports after conscious narration; it is not a direct measurement of the dreamer's unconscious neural activity.
