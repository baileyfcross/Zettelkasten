# Fuel Cell Electrolyte

The electrolyte in a [[Fuel Cell]] transports ions between its electrodes while electrons take the external circuit. Its composition must remain approximately invariant as ions are consumed at one electrode and replaced at the other. Polymer proton membranes, alkaline solutions, solid oxides, and molten carbonates move different ions and support different operating conditions. Slow ionic transport can also contribute to concentration losses.
