# Fair Use

Fair use is a contextual limitation on copyright that weighs the purpose and character of a use, the nature of the copyrighted work, the amount used relative to the whole, and the effect on the work's potential market or value.

Teaching, criticism, commentary, reporting, scholarship, parody, and research can support a fair-use argument, but the label is not an automatic exemption. A small, transformative, noncommercial use that does not substitute for the original is more defensible than copying the expressive core into a competing product.
