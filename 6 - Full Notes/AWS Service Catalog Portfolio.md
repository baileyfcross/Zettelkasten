# AWS Service Catalog Portfolio

An AWS Service Catalog portfolio groups products with their configuration constraints and access permissions. Portfolios let platform teams distribute different approved offerings to different users, groups, accounts, or organizational contexts without duplicating each product definition. The portfolio is therefore a governance boundary around a catalog: it controls who can see and launch which products and under what restrictions.
