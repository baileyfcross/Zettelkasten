# Boss-Fight Puzzle

A boss-fight puzzle requires the player to learn an opponent's recurring attacks and reactions, infer a vulnerability, and execute the action that exploits it. The encounter combines observation, pattern recognition, experimentation, and often demanding timing.

A common three-stage structure lets the first successful hit reveal the solution, the second confirm the insight, and the third demonstrate mastery. Variations should increase pressure without invalidating what the player learned. Otherwise the encounter becomes trial-and-error memorization rather than a readable puzzle.
