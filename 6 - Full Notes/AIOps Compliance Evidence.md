# AIOps Compliance Evidence

AIOps compliance evidence shows that an operational AI followed applicable controls, policies, and record-retention requirements. It can include prompts, responses, tool calls, approvals, identity and access decisions, guardrail results, data sources, model versions, and the actions ultimately taken.

Security and compliance overlap but are not identical: a secure implementation can still miss an industry record requirement. Durable evidence must therefore live outside the model's transient context window and remain accessible for audit without exposing more sensitive data than the control requires.
