# Game Probability Model

A game probability model enumerates possible outcomes, identifies which cases are equally likely, and computes the proportion that satisfy the event of interest. Probability becomes a counting problem when the sample space is defined correctly.

Independent joint events multiply, conditional probability narrows the relevant cases, and mutually exclusive alternatives add. The difficult part is usually not arithmetic but representing the cases without omission, overlap, or a false assumption of uniformity.
