# Firth Logistic Regression

Firth logistic regression uses a penalized likelihood to reduce small-sample bias and obtain finite estimates when ordinary logistic regression encounters separation. The correction prevents empty or perfectly predicted cells from driving coefficients to infinity.

It is a modeling remedy, not permission to ignore why separation occurred. Analysts should still examine sparse categories, study design, and the stability of the resulting confidence intervals before presenting the adjusted odds ratios.
