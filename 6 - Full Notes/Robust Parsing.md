# Robust Parsing

Robust parsing tries to recover useful meaning even when an utterance does not fully match a grammar. It can allow gaps or “holes” for unknown material while preserving recognized phrases and slots.

This tolerance helps with disfluencies and unexpected words, but excessive flexibility causes [[Parser Overgeneration]]. The parser must balance accepting natural variation against inventing unsupported interpretations.
