# Manchester Baby

The Manchester Baby was an experimental electronic computer that successfully executed a stored program in 1948. Its purpose was to demonstrate a new high-speed memory technology and the feasibility of internal program storage.

The machine was not built as a complete production system. Its historical significance lies in proving that the stored-program principle could operate in electronic hardware.
