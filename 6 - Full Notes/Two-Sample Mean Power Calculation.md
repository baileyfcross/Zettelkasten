# Two-Sample Mean Power Calculation

A two-sample mean power calculation relates the number per group to a standardized mean difference, significance level, desired power, and one- or two-sided alternative. It is aligned with a two-sample t-test design.

If every input but one is specified, the remaining quantity can be solved. The output must be read carefully because many implementations report the required sample for each group rather than the combined total.
