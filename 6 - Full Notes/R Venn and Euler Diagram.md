# R Venn and Euler Diagram

An R Venn diagram represents every possible intersection among a small number of sets, while an Euler diagram may omit empty intersections and seek regions whose areas reflect the observed relationships. Both depend on computing set memberships and intersections correctly before drawing shapes.

These diagrams become difficult to read as the number of sets grows and their geometry may only approximate the intended areas. They are best used for a few sets when the presence and size of overlaps are the central message.
