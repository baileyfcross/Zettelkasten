# Moist-Air Mixing Ratios

Humidity ratio expresses water-vapor mass relative to dry-air mass, while mixing ratio is commonly used to describe the same underlying partition of a moist-air sample. Unlike relative humidity, these ratios do not directly state proximity to saturation. They provide a conserved and quantitative moisture variable for [[Thermodynamic Weather Diagrams]].
