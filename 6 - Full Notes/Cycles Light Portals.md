# Cycles Light Portals

In the Cycles version covered by the book, an area lamp marked as a portal guided environment-light sampling through an opening such as a window. The portal did not act as an ordinary emitter; it described where important incoming light was likely to enter.

Matching the portal's size and placement to the opening could reduce noise or improve quality for a given sample count in enclosed scenes. The feature and interface are version-specific and should not be assumed unchanged in current Blender releases.
