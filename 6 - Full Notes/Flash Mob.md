# Flash Mob

A flash mob is a coordinated public performance in which participants assemble, carry out a prearranged action, and disperse. Digital communication supports the organization, while recordings allow the event to circulate beyond those who witnessed it in person.

The genre joins networked coordination to embodied collective action. Surprise arises from inserting synchronized behavior into an ordinary setting, and later groups can imitate the structure with different dances, messages, or places. The documented performance becomes both an event and a model for repetition.
