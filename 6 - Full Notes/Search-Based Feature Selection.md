# Search-Based Feature Selection

Search-based feature selection treats a candidate subset as a point in a combinatorial space and explores that space for a good evaluation score. Complete, sequential, and randomized strategies trade optimality guarantees against feasible runtime.

The evaluator may be independent of a predictor or tied to one. Because the number of subsets grows exponentially, practical search relies on heuristics, pruning, stopping rules, or sparse optimization.
