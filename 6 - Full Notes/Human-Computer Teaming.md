# Human-Computer Teaming

Human-computer teaming assigns complementary parts of a task to people and machines. Computers contribute speed, search, and consistent calculation, while people contribute context, judgment, meaning, and the ability to respond to unforeseen situations.

A team is not automatically better than either member alone. Performance depends on interface design, division of authority, shared awareness, and whether human intervention occurs at a point where it can still affect the outcome.
