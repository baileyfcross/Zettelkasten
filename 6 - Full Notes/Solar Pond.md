# Solar Pond

A solar pond uses a vertical salt-concentration gradient to keep warm dense water at the bottom instead of letting it circulate to the cooler surface. Its middle nonconvective layer suppresses heat loss while the bottom layer stores solar heat. Hot lower water can drive an organic-fluid power cycle or provide process heat, and cool upper water can serve the condenser. The pond is both collector and storage medium.
