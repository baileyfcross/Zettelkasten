# Composition Series

A composition series is a finite chain 0 = M₀ ⊊ M₁ ⊊ ··· ⊊ Mₖ = M in which every quotient Mᵢ/Mᵢ₋₁ is a [[Simple Module]].

The number k is the [[Module Length]]. If one composition series exists, every composition series has the same length, and every submodule chain can be extended to one.
