# Heated Seat Transient Thermal Model

The heated-seat case represents layered seat materials and embedded resistive wires with three inputs: constant heat flux, time-varying heat flux, and prescribed time-dependent wire temperature. The alternatives distinguish uncontrolled heating from scheduled or thermostat-like behavior.

Point histories and lines through the cushion show that the wire temperature rises before the occupant-facing surface responds. This delay makes transient exposure, material layering, and [[COMSOL Probe Time Histories|probe placement]] central to comfort and safety assessment.
