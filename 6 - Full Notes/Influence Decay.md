# Influence Decay

Influence decay models the reduction in a topic's effect as time passes after reports or interactions. A decay function can weight recent activity more strongly while retaining accumulated influence from earlier periods.

The rate need not be constant; it can change with attention and reporting frequency. Including decay helps a popularity model avoid treating old and current evidence as equally predictive.
