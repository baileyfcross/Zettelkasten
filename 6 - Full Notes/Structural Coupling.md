# Structural Coupling

Structural coupling occurs when recurrent interactions mold two or more systems into congruent structures. The systems remain distinct but adapt in response to one another as parts of a larger system.

A game and player become structurally coupled as the player learns the [[Game Model]] and the game responds to the player's actions. This relationship supports engagement when their models remain close enough for prediction without eliminating uncertainty.
