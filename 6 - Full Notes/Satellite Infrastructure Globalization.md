# Satellite Infrastructure Globalization

Satellite infrastructure globalization is the reciprocal relationship between orbital systems and increasing integration among world regions. Communications satellites spread television, entertainment, news, and business links, while navigation and observation supported transportation, markets, weather knowledge, and shared environmental study.

Globalization also changed the systems themselves. More nations developed spacecraft, international organizations coordinated services, corporations operated across borders, and people and expertise circulated beyond the national programs created during the Cold War. The result is a transnational infrastructure that remains shaped by unequal national power and military origins.
