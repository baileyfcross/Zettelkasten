# Generator Trivialization under Quantum Gate Symmetrization

A generator is trivialized when group twirling maps it to zero. Under a one-qubit symmetry represented by $X$, for example, both $Y$ and $Z$ change sign under conjugation, so averaging each operator with its conjugate cancels it.

Trivialization depends on the starting gateset, not only on the transformations the original gates could collectively express. A universal Euler decomposition built from $Y$ and $Z$ can lose every trainable gate after this projection even though nontrivial symmetry-compatible rotations generated by $X$ exist.

The remedy is to inspect the projected generators and, when necessary, choose a different initial parametrization that spans the commutant. [[Quantum Gate Symmetrization by Group Twirling]] guarantees equivariance of what survives, not that a useful gateset survives.
