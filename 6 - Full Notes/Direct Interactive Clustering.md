# Direct Interactive Clustering

Direct interactive clustering lets an analyst alter assignments, prototypes, constraints, parameters, or local relationships and immediately recomputes the affected solution. The interface makes domain judgment part of the optimization loop.

Effective interaction should expose why the solution changed and preserve a history of interventions. Otherwise a visually pleasing final partition may be impossible to reproduce or distinguish from arbitrary manual editing.
