# Internal Cluster Validity Index

An internal cluster validity index evaluates a clustering from the observed data, proximity matrix, and fitted structure without using known class labels. It may measure compactness, separation, or preservation of pairwise relationships.

Internal validity shows how well a solution satisfies a stated geometric criterion. It cannot independently establish that the geometry has the intended real-world meaning.
