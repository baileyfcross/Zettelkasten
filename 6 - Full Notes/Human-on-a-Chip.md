# Human-on-a-Chip

A human-on-a-chip is the proposed integration of multiple organ models into a connected system that reproduces important body-wide signaling and drug distribution. Fluid connections could reveal effects that appear only after one tissue transforms a compound used by another.

Major barriers include missing organ models, scaling relationships, and the lack of one culture medium that supports all tissues simultaneously.
