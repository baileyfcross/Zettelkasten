# Rumor Propagation

Rumor propagation is the movement of an unverified claim as people publish, forward, criticize, verify, or retract it. Online participants act as both producers and consumers, permitting rapid transmission to targeted audiences.

The process varies with network structure, user actions, truth status, and uncertainty. A propagation model describes how nodes change state, while content analysis addresses what the message says.
