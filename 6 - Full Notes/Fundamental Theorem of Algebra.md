# Fundamental Theorem of Algebra

The fundamental theorem of algebra states that every nonconstant polynomial with complex coefficients has at least one complex root. Repeated application of the [[Factor Theorem]] shows that a degree-$n$ polynomial factors into $n$ linear factors over the complex numbers when multiplicity is counted. Real coefficients make nonreal roots occur in conjugate pairs.
