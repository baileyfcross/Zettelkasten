# Bra-Ket Notation

Dirac notation writes a column state vector as a ket $|\psi\rangle$ and its complex-conjugate transpose as a bra $\langle\psi|$. The inner product $\langle\psi|\phi\rangle$ is a scalar amplitude, while the outer product $|\phi\rangle\langle\psi|$ is an operator.

An outer product can be read operationally: $|\mathrm{out}\rangle\langle\mathrm{in}|$ maps the normalized input state to the output state and annihilates vectors orthogonal to the input. Sums of these terms provide a direct way to construct gate operators from basis-state mappings.
