# Projective Resolution

A projective resolution of a module M is an exact sequence ··· → P₂ → P₁ → P₀ → M → 0 in which every Pᵢ is a [[Projective Module]].

It replaces M by modules with a lifting property, allowing functors to be applied and their failure of exactness measured. The shortest possible resolution length is the [[Projective Dimension]].
