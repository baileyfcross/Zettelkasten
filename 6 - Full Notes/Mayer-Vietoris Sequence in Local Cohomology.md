# Mayer-Vietoris Sequence in Local Cohomology

For ideals I and J, local cohomology modules with support in I, J, I+J, and I∩J fit into a long exact Mayer-Vietoris sequence.

The sequence assembles information supported on two closed subsets from their union and intersection. It is derived from the corresponding short exact relationship among the Čech complexes or support functors.
