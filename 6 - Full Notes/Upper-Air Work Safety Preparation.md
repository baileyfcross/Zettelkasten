# Upper-Air Work Safety Preparation

Upper-air exercises require the aircraft to be in the appropriate loading category, adequate altitude and area, security from conflicting traffic, satisfactory engine state, and a deliberate lookout. Personal readiness, cockpit organization, secured equipment, suitable clothing, and accessible emergency items reduce avoidable distraction. Preparation makes the maneuver the only novel task rather than one hazard among many.
