# Rule Introduction

Rule introduction is the first designed encounter through which a player learns what a rule or object means. That encounter establishes expectations that will influence every later use of the element.

A clear introduction isolates the important behavior, gives the player a reason to interact, and makes consequences observable without imposing an excessive setback. New objects are safest to combine only after their individual behaviors and relationships to existing [[Game Verb|verbs]] are understood.
