# K-12 Computational Thinking Movement

The K–12 computational thinking movement sought to make foundational computing available to every student. It gained momentum after 2006 through teacher training, curriculum frameworks, coding activities, and a revised Advanced Placement pathway.

Its public success also narrowed many descriptions of computational thinking toward beginner programming. A complete account must connect those accessible foundations with professional design, machines, software systems, and computational science.
