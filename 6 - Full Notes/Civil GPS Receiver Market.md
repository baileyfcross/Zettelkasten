# Civil GPS Receiver Market

The civil GPS receiver market emerged when open signals, improved microelectronics, and mass production reduced equipment from specialized military hardware to compact commercial devices. Surveyors and marine users formed early high-value markets, followed by hikers, motorists, aircraft operators, and embedded systems. Civil demand also changed policy: widespread economic dependence made intentional degradation or withdrawal of the service increasingly costly even though the constellation remained publicly funded.
