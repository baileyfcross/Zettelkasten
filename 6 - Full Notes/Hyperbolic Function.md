# Hyperbolic Function

Hyperbolic sine and cosine are combinations of the natural exponential: $\sinh x=(e^x-e^{-x})/2$ and $\cosh x=(e^x+e^{-x})/2$. Their ratio defines hyperbolic tangent, with reciprocal forms defining the remaining hyperbolic functions.

They resemble trigonometric functions but satisfy identities with characteristic sign changes, including $\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1$. Their exponential construction makes them natural solutions of differential equations and useful in mathematical physics, quantum theory, and relativity.
