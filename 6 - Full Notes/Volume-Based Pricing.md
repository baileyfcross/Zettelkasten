# Volume-Based Pricing

Volume-based pricing changes the charge according to the quantity of service delivered. The volume may be users, storage, bandwidth, processing power, transaction rate, or another measured capacity.

The contract must define precisely whether it counts allocated, average, peak, or concurrent volume. Because demand changes over business cycles and events, both measurement and reporting determine whether the model remains fair and predictable.
