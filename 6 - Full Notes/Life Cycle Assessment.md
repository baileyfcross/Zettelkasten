# Life Cycle Assessment

Life cycle assessment estimates environmental effects across raw-material production, manufacturing, operation, cleaning, packaging, storage, transport, use, and disposal. It prevents one favorable stage from standing in for the complete system.

For an emerging technology, results depend heavily on projected facilities, energy sources, batch yields, media inputs, waste, and the conventional product chosen for comparison.
