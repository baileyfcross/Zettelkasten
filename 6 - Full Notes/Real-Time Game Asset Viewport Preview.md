# Real-Time Game Asset Viewport Preview

The Blender 2.7x workflow could configure its game-oriented viewport with GLSL materials, ambient occlusion, lights, shadows, and visible topology to approximate an engine preview. The goal was not final rendering but early detection of how a model might read under real-time constraints.

Three-point lighting and material shading made form legible, while wire display revealed polygon structure. Because Blender and a target engine still differ, the preview was a diagnostic approximation that needed confirmation after actual import.
