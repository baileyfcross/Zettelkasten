# Player-Killing

Player-killing is nonconsensual combat in an online world, often involving a stronger participant attacking a weaker one for possessions or harassment. It differs from mutually accepted competitive play.

The design must decide where combat is allowed, how consent works, what can be lost, and what protection or redress exists. Unrestricted player-killing can drive away new or noncompetitive players.
