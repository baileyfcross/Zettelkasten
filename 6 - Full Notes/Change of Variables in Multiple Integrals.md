# Change of Variables in Multiple Integrals

A differentiable one-to-one coordinate map $x=g(u)$ transforms a multiple integral by multiplying the transformed integrand by the absolute [[Jacobian Determinant]]:

$$\int_D f(x)\,dx=\int_\Delta f(g(u))\,|\det J_g(u)|\,du.$$

The determinant compensates for the local change in area or volume. A useful substitution maps an awkward domain to simpler bounds or makes the integrand easier to evaluate.
