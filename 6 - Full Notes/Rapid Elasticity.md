# Rapid Elasticity

Rapid elasticity is the ability to add resources when demand rises and release them when demand falls. It uses [[Horizontal Scalability]] in both directions so that capacity can follow a changing workload instead of remaining fixed at its anticipated peak.

Elasticity depends on [[Resource Pooling]], automation, and sufficiently fast provisioning. It can protect service availability during a surge and reduce idle capacity afterward, but it does not remove the need to measure workload behavior or set safe scaling rules.
