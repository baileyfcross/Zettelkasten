# Truss Member

A truss member is a two-force structural component connecting two joints. Its local response is governed by axial rigidity, length, and the extension projected along the member's axis.

In a two-dimensional model, direction cosines transform this local axial behavior into global x and y components. The sign of recovered axial stress distinguishes tension from compression.
