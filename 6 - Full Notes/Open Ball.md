# Open Ball

An open ball of radius $\varepsilon>0$ centered at $a\in\mathbb{R}^n$ is

$$B_\varepsilon(a)=\{x\in\mathbb{R}^n:\|x-a\|<\varepsilon\}.$$

It generalizes an open interval in one dimension, an open disk in two dimensions, and the interior of a sphere in three dimensions. Open balls provide the local neighborhoods used to define [[Open Set in Euclidean Space|open sets]], limits, and continuity.
