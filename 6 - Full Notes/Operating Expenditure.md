# Operating Expenditure

Operating expenditure is recurring spending for a service or resource used during ordinary operations. A cloud consumer can pay monthly or according to usage instead of purchasing all anticipated computing capacity as a capital asset.

This can make spending follow actual demand and preserve capital for other opportunities. It does not automatically make a service cheaper, because the contractual term, ongoing price, integration, support, and exit costs still contribute to the comparison.
