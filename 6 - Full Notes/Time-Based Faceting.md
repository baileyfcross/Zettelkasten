# Time-Based Faceting

Time-based faceting uses years, months, seasons, or other intervals to partition a plot into repeated panels. It turns time into a conditioning variable rather than an axis, making the same spatial or statistical relationship comparable across periods. The panels need a stable layout and scales to support visual comparison. Too many periods produce tiny panels, so aggregation or selecting representative intervals may be preferable.
