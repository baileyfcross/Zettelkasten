# Transient Response Analysis

Transient response analysis determines structural motion under arbitrary time-dependent loading. Unlike modal or harmonic analysis, it follows how displacement, velocity, and acceleration evolve through a specified time history.

Time-step size and integration method must resolve both the load variation and important structural modes. Initial conditions and damping influence the computed response.
