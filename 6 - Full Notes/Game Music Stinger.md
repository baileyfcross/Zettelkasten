# Game Music Stinger

A game music stinger is a short musical accent triggered by a discrete gameplay event. It can announce success, danger, discovery, or another state change without replacing the longer cue that is already playing.

Synchronized stingers wait for a beat or bar so they align rhythmically, while unsynchronized stingers play immediately for tighter visual response. Because an event can occur over many harmonic contexts, a stinger may use percussion, neutral harmony, or a pentatonic collection to reduce clashes with the underlying score.
