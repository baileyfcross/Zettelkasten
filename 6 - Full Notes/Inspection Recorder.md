# Inspection Recorder

An inspection recorder captures each reported defect, its severity, and its classification during the meeting. The recorder also collects preparation and meeting measures needed for the inspection report.

Accurate recording lets the group continue searching rather than debating memory or assigning repairs on the spot. The resulting defect list becomes the author's rework input and the moderator's evidence for follow-up verification.
