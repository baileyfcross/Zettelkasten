# Continuous Production Deployment

Continuous production deployment automatically releases every change that satisfies the delivery pipeline's policies. It removes a standing manual release decision after automated build, test, analysis, and environment checks succeed.

The practice is more demanding than continuous integration or delivery to a staging boundary. It requires strong confidence in evidence, small reversible changes, monitoring, and the ability to respond rapidly to unexpected production behavior.
