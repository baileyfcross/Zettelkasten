# Average-Comparison Time Plot

An average-comparison time plot places a series beside a relevant mean and may also show their difference. A long-term seasonal average can expose unusually high or low periods that the raw [[Time Plot]] alone makes hard to judge. The reference and deviation should be visually distinguishable, and a separate screen for the difference can prevent the baseline from obscuring the original values. A [[Horizon Graph]] can compactly display those deviations.
