# Cooperation

Cooperation is a form of play in which participants act together toward shared goals. Their actions can be complementary, coordinated, or mutually supportive rather than directed against one another.

A cooperative game still needs challenges and meaningful decisions. The design must make joint action matter so that one participant does not perform all important work while the others merely observe.
