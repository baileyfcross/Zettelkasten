# Logistic Growth

Logistic growth begins approximately exponentially but slows as the population approaches a carrying capacity. The growth rate is proportional both to current population and to the remaining fraction of capacity, producing an S-shaped trajectory under constant parameters.

R can compare the unrestricted exponential curve with the bounded model and show how initial size, intrinsic rate, and carrying capacity change the path. The capacity represents an environmental assumption and should not be treated as an immutable constant outside the modeled conditions.
