# Horizon Graph

A horizon graph divides positive and negative deviations into magnitude bands, mirrors the negative bands, and overlays the bands within a compact strip. Hue distinguishes sign while color intensity distinguishes magnitude. This compression lets many time series appear as vertically aligned [[Time-Series Small Multiples]] without losing broad changes. Exact values are difficult to recover, so a horizon graph is best for pattern comparison rather than precise reading.
