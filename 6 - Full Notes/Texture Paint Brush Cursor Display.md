# Texture Paint Brush Cursor Display

The texture-paint cursor previews brush radius, outline, falloff, and texture or mask information before pixels are changed. Its display can communicate both the active tool and the area likely to receive influence.

Cursor appearance does not guarantee the final pixel footprint because UV resolution, surface angle, occlusion, and pressure still intervene. It is a placement aid whose feedback should be checked against the resulting image in both 3D and 2D views.
