# Vector Field Map

A vector field map represents a quantity that has both magnitude and direction at locations across a region, such as wind. Each cell or sample contains two components that can be transformed into speed and orientation. An [[Arrow Plot]] shows local vectors discretely, while a [[Streamline Map]] emphasizes the continuous flow structure. Sampling density and symbol scale must avoid clutter without erasing important variation.
