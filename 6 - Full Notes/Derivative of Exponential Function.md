# Derivative of Exponential Function

The natural exponential function is characterized by

$$\frac{d}{dx}e^x=e^x.$$

More generally, $d(a^x)/dx=a^x\ln a$ for $a>0$. When the exponent is itself a function, the [[Chain Rule]] gives $d(e^{g(x)})/dx=e^{g(x)}g'(x)$.
