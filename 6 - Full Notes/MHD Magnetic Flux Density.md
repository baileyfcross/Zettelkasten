# MHD Magnetic Flux Density

Magnetic flux density measures the field strength across the conducting flow of a [[Magnetohydrodynamic Generator]]. A stronger field raises induced voltage and potential electrical output, but conventional windings consume power and require cooling. The source discusses superconducting windings as a way to cut magnet resistance losses at the cost of cryogenic equipment. Field strength is therefore both a performance and plant-design decision.
