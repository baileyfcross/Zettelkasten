# Aircraft Pressurization Valves

An outflow valve meters how quickly conditioned air leaves the pressure vessel and thereby regulates cabin pressure. Positive-pressure relief protects against excessive internal differential, negative-pressure relief prevents damaging external overpressure, and a dump valve rapidly releases cabin pressure when commanded. The valve system must coordinate rather than rely on the inflow source alone.
