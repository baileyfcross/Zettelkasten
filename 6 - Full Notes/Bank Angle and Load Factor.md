# Bank Angle and Load Factor

Banking tilts the lift vector, so only part of it remains vertical. A level turn therefore requires greater total lift, and its load factor rises approximately with the secant of bank angle. The higher wing loading raises stall speed, which is why steep bank close to approach speed narrows the margin described by [[Stall Speed as a Function of Bank and Flaps]].
