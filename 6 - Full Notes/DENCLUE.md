# DENCLUE

DENCLUE models overall data density as the sum of influence functions centered on individual observations. Clusters are associated with density attractors reached by following the gradient of that estimated density, subject to a minimum-density condition.

The formulation provides a mathematical connection between density estimation and clustering and can handle irregular shapes and noise. Bandwidth and density thresholds control which modes survive and whether nearby high-density regions are joined.
