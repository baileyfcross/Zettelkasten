# Chain Map

A chain map between chain complexes is a family of homomorphisms fₙ: Cₙ → C′ₙ that commutes with the differentials. Applying a differential before or after the appropriate f gives the same result.

Because it sends cycles to cycles and boundaries to boundaries, a chain map induces homomorphisms on [[Homology Group]]s. Maps of cochain complexes similarly induce maps on cohomology.
