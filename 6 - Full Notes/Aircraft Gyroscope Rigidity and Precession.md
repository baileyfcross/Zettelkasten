# Aircraft Gyroscope Rigidity and Precession

Gyroscopic rigidity is the tendency of a spinning rotor to preserve its axis orientation. It increases with rotor mass, effective radius, peripheral mass concentration, and rotational speed. When torque tries to redirect the axis, the gyro responds through precession at right angles to the applied influence, providing the physical basis for attitude and heading instruments.
