# Monochromatic Environment Palette

A monochromatic palette develops one hue through variations in value and saturation. It creates strong visual unity and can establish a distinct atmosphere without relying on many competing hue relationships.

Because hue contrast is limited, form and navigation must be carried by lightness, saturation, silhouette, texture, and lighting. A small contrasting accent can identify an interactive object while preserving the overall monochromatic impression.
