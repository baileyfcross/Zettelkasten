# Lean Production

Lean production improves the complete stream by which game content moves from need to playable integration. It reduces waste, batch size, queues, and handoffs while using measured cycle time to expose constraints.

The objective is reliable delivery of valuable assets, not maximum utilization of every specialist. Small batches bring production, gameplay, and quality evidence back before large volumes are committed.
