# Exponential Scaling in Hyperbolic Space

Exponential scaling in hyperbolic space means that circumference and area grow exponentially with radius. Small neighborhoods near the center look nearly flat, while equal-area regions appear increasingly compressed near the boundary of a disk visualization.

This growth mirrors the number of nodes found at successive depths of a branching tree. The correspondence helps explain why [[Hyperbolic Space]] can represent hierarchical and scale-free networks with low dimensionality.
