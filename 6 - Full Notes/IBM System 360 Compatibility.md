# IBM System 360 Compatibility

IBM designed the System/360 as a family whose models shared a common instruction architecture. Customers could move software from a smaller business system to a more powerful scientific or commercial machine while preserving their investment.

The compatibility strategy unified previously separate product lines and helped IBM dominate the mainframe market. Achieving it required enormous investment in processors, peripherals, operating systems, and programming support.
