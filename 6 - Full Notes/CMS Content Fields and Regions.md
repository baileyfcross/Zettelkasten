# CMS Content Fields and Regions

A content field stores one typed value, while a region groups fields or components into a meaningful part of a page or post. Together they describe the editable schema presented to authors and the structured values available to templates.

Field types should reflect the intended data rather than force every value into unstructured text. Well-named regions communicate editorial purpose and let presentation evolve without erasing the semantics of the stored content.
