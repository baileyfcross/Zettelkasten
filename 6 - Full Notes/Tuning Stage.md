# Tuning Stage

The tuning stage is the final design stage in which the team polishes and balances an already defined game without adding new features. Values, timing, difficulty, presentation, and usability can still change.

Restricting new scope lets implementation, debugging, and testing converge. Tuning is therefore different from elaboration: it adjusts the existing design rather than expanding what the product promises.
