# Oblique Mercator Route Alignment

An oblique Mercator reorients the cylinder so the line of tangency follows a selected great-circle corridor rather than the Equator or a meridian. Distortion is minimized along that oblique reference, allowing a long route to be represented inside the chart's useful band. The method is a projection equivalent of rotating coordinates to fit the path of interest.
