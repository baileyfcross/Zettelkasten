# Volumetric Heat Capacity

Specific heat capacity measures energy required to raise a unit mass by one degree, while density converts that mass-based quantity to energy storage per unit volume. Their product $\rho c_p$ is the volumetric heat capacity used in transient heat equations.

A high value lets a region absorb more energy for the same temperature rise. Together with conductivity it determines [[Thermal Diffusivity from Material Properties|thermal diffusivity]], separating a material's ability to store thermal energy from its ability to redistribute it.
