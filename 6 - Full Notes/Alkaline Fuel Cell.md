# Alkaline Fuel Cell

An alkaline fuel cell uses an alkaline [[Fuel Cell Electrolyte]] such as potassium hydroxide. In the source's hydrogen-oxygen arrangement, hydroxide ions move from the oxygen electrode toward the fuel electrode while electrons pass through the load. Low weight and relatively low operating temperature made this family attractive for spacecraft, although its need for clean reactants and high cost constrain other applications.
