# Angular Template-Driven Form

An Angular template-driven form defines much of its structure and validation through directives in the HTML template. Angular derives the form model from that markup and synchronizes it with component data.

The approach is concise for small forms, but complex rules and dynamic behavior can become distributed through the template. An [[Angular Reactive Form]] instead builds the form model explicitly in TypeScript.
