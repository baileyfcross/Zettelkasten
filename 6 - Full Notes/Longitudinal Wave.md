# Longitudinal Wave

A longitudinal wave has oscillations parallel to its direction of energy transfer. In a material medium, alternating compressions and rarefactions move through the substance while individual particles oscillate about equilibrium. Ordinary sound in air is longitudinal and cannot be polarized because it has only one oscillation direction.
