# Unity Tilemap Collider

A Unity Tilemap Collider 2D derives collision shapes from the occupied cells of a [[Unity Tilemap]]. It turns painted level structure into physical boundaries without requiring a separate hand-placed collider for every tile.

Many individual tile outlines can be expensive or create unwanted seams. Combining the tilemap collider with a Composite Collider 2D can merge adjacent geometry into larger boundaries, improving efficiency and producing smoother physical interaction for [[Unity 2D Physics|2D bodies]].
