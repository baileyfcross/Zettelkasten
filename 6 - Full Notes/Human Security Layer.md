# Human Security Layer

The human security layer addresses the decisions, habits, and social interactions through which people affect a system's security. Training, clear procedures, careful identity verification, and usable controls reduce the chance that a person becomes the easiest route around technical defenses.

Because attackers can exploit trust through [[Social Engineering]], this layer belongs inside [[Defense in Depth]] rather than outside the technical system. Security design must account for how people actually work.
