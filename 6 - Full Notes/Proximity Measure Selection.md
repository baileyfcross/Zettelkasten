# Proximity Measure Selection

Proximity measure selection matches the definition of resemblance to the data type, scale, and expected cluster structure. Euclidean distance favors compact geometry, Manhattan distance changes the influence of coordinate differences, and categorical data require matching or co-occurrence measures.

Results should be compared across plausible choices. A strong clustering under an unsuitable metric is not strong evidence about the application.
