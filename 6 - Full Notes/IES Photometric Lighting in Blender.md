# IES Photometric Lighting in Blender

IES profiles encode measured or manufacturer-supplied photometric distributions for real lighting fixtures. The Extra Lights add-on described in the source exposes natural, incandescent, LED, and fluorescent lights that use these profiles for more realistic emission patterns.

This replaces an idealized point or cone with a fixture-specific distribution. In the Blender 3.0 workflow covered by the book, IES-textured lights are a Cycles capability rather than an Eevee one.
