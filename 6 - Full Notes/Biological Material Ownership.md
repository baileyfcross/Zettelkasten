# Biological Material Ownership

Biological material ownership concerns control and benefit when donated cells are expanded, modified, patented, or incorporated into valuable products. The physical sample, derived cell line, genetic information, manufacturing process, and finished construct may involve different claims.

Governance should distinguish legal possession from continuing obligations of consent, privacy, attribution, access, and benefit sharing.
