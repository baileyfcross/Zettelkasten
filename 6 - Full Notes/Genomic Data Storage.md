# Genomic Data Storage

Genomic data storage preserves sequences, alignments, variants, annotations, phenotypes, and workflow results at scales that can grow faster than local research infrastructure. Compression and tiered storage reduce cost, but access patterns and reuse determine what must remain readily available.

Retention decisions should distinguish irreplaceable raw observations from derived files that can be reproducibly regenerated.
