# Closure Property

A collection is closed under an operation when applying that operation to any allowed inputs always produces another member of the same collection. For natural numbers (a) and (b), both (a+b) and (ab) are natural numbers, so (mathbb{N}) is closed under addition and multiplication.

Closure depends on both the operation and the number system. Natural numbers are not closed under subtraction or division, because results such as (3-5) and (3/5) leave the set. [[Integer]]s repair closure under subtraction, while [[Rational Number]]s also accommodate division by nonzero values.
