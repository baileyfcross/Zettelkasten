# Tree-Structured Feature Selection

Tree-structured feature selection incorporates hierarchical relations among variables. A feature may represent a broad category whose descendants encode progressively narrower alternatives, so a valid subset should respect ancestry or descendant constraints.

Using the hierarchy can stabilize selection and keep the result interpretable at an appropriate level of detail. The selection rule must specify whether choosing a child requires its ancestors or whether choosing a parent substitutes for its descendants.
