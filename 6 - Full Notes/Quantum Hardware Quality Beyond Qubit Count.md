# Quantum Hardware Quality Beyond Qubit Count

Qubit count alone does not measure a quantum processor's computational usefulness. A device also needs accurate and sufficiently general controls, especially multi-qubit operations; adequate coherence; useful operation and measurement speed; stable behavior; and calibration that can be completed before the system drifts.

Connectivity and native gate set determine the compilation overhead for an algorithm, while error rates and coherence determine how much of the compiled circuit survives. Comparing processors therefore requires workload-level capability rather than treating every physical qubit as an interchangeable unit of computation.
