# Ideal Gas Equation

The ideal gas equation combines the empirical gas laws:

$$pV=nRT=Nk_BT.$$

Pressure $p$, volume $V$, absolute temperature $T$, and amount of gas are linked through the molar gas constant $R$ or Boltzmann constant $k_B$. Real gases approach ideal behavior at low density and sufficiently high temperature.
