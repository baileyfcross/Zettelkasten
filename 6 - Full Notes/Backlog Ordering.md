# Backlog Ordering

Backlog ordering arranges Product Backlog Items by their combined value, cost, risk, knowledge, and dependency effects. The sequence expresses what should be considered next rather than assigning each item an isolated priority label.

High-value or high-learning work often moves upward, while low-value detail remains coarse. Dependencies can change the order, but they should be challenged rather than treated as automatic justification for component-first development.
