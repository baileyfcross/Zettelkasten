# Cross-Border Health Data Flow

Cross-border health data flow occurs when medical or health-related information is transferred, stored, or processed outside the jurisdiction where it was collected. Collaborative research and distributed services can create such movement.

Regulation must address permission, security, receiving-country protections, accountability, and remedies for disclosure. Health data remain sensitive even when a transfer serves a beneficial analytical purpose.
