# Solar Cell Maximum Power Point

The maximum power point is the voltage-current combination on a [[Solar Cell Current-Voltage Curve]] for which $P=VI$ is greatest. It is not the open-circuit or short-circuit endpoint, because power at either endpoint is zero. The source obtains it by plotting power against voltage or current; its coordinates provide the usable peak rating for a cell or module under stated radiation.
