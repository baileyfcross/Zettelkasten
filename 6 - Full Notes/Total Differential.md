# Total Differential

The total differential gives the first-order change of a multivariable function when all independent variables change. For $f(x,y)$ it is $df=f_xdx+f_ydy$, with one term for each coordinate direction.

It is the linear part of the local change and therefore approximates $f(x+dx,y+dy)-f(x,y)$ when the increments are small. The coefficients of the coordinate differentials are the corresponding partial derivatives, connecting local approximation to the gradient.
