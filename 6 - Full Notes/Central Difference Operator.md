# Central Difference Operator

The central difference operator uses values placed symmetrically about a center. With half-step notation,

$$\delta f(x)=f\left(x+\frac h2\right)-f\left(x-\frac h2\right).$$

Odd central differences are attached to half-index locations and even differences to integer locations. Their symmetric placement often produces faster-decreasing interpolation coefficients and more accurate derivative formulas than one-sided differences.
