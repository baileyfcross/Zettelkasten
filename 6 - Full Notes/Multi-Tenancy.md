# Multi-Tenancy

Multi-tenancy is the use of a shared cloud resource or service by multiple consumers. The shared resource may be software, storage, a platform, or virtualized infrastructure, while each consumer experiences an appropriately separated service.

Sharing improves utilization and spreads costs across tenants. It also makes isolation a central requirement: one tenant's activity or data must not improperly affect the availability, confidentiality, or integrity experienced by another.
