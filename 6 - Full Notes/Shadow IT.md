# Shadow IT

Shadow IT is technology acquired or operated by a business unit without the knowledge or standard management of the organization's IT function. It often arises when official delivery is too slow or restrictive for an immediate business need.

Easy cloud purchasing lowers the threshold for creating shadow services. The resulting agility can carry untracked security, support, integration, duplication, and continuity risks, so governance should address the unmet demand that caused it rather than only prohibit the service.
