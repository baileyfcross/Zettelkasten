# Characteristic Polynomial

The characteristic polynomial is $p(\lambda)=\det(A-\lambda I)$. Its degree equals the size of the square matrix, and its roots are the eigenvalues counted with multiplicity.

Its coefficients connect eigenvalues to matrix invariants: the sum of eigenvalues equals the [[Matrix Trace]], while their product is related to the [[Determinant]].
