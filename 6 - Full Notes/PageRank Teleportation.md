# PageRank Teleportation

PageRank teleportation gives the [[Random Surfer Model|random surfer]] a nonzero probability of jumping to any page instead of following an outgoing link. The remaining probability is assigned to normal movement through the web's link structure.

This adjustment prevents rank from becoming trapped in a [[Link Sink]] and makes every destination reachable in the transition model. Combining link-following and teleportation probabilities produces the [[Google Matrix]].
