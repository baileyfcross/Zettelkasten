# Aligning the Camera to the View

Aligning the active camera to the current viewport converts an exploratory view into a renderable composition. The artist first navigates to a useful angle and then makes the camera adopt that position and orientation.

This is faster than reproducing the view by transforming the camera manually. It also separates two decisions: informal navigation finds the framing, and camera alignment commits that framing to the scene's output viewpoint.
