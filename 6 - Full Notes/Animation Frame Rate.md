# Animation Frame Rate

Animation frame rate is the number of map states shown per second. It converts the sampling sequence into viewing time: a higher rate compresses the temporal story and may hide changes, while a lower rate can make motion feel discontinuous. The source's raster movie concatenates frames at six per second. Rate should be chosen together with data interval, transition smoothness, and the viewer's need to inspect individual states.
