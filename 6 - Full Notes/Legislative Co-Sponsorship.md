# Legislative Co-Sponsorship

Legislative co-sponsorship is a lawmaker's public support for another legislator's proposal. Repeated acts create ties that can reflect ideology, shared origin, committee collaboration, seniority, social identity, or strategic alliance.

Because the action is recorded with a bill, date, and participants, it supports longitudinal network analysis. Its interpretation still depends on the rules that govern which proposals can be introduced and supported.
