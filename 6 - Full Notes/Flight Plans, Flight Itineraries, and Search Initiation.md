# Flight Plans, Flight Itineraries, and Search Initiation

A flight plan filed with a flight service organization or an itinerary left with a responsible person creates an external record of the intended route and arrival time. If the flight is not closed within the defined interval, that record supports search-and-rescue initiation. Opening, updating, and closing it are therefore operational safety actions rather than administrative paperwork alone.
