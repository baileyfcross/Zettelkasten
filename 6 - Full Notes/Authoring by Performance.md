# Authoring by Performance

Authoring by performance records a person carrying out an intended spatial action and converts that demonstration into reusable AR content or behavior. Motion, timing, speech, pointing, and interaction with physical objects can all supply authoring input.

The approach lets creators express situated behavior in the environment where it will occur. The system must decide which details belong to the reusable specification and which were incidental to the recorded performance.
