# Fowlkes-Mallows Index

The Fowlkes-Mallows index measures the overlap of pairs assigned to the same cluster in two partitions. It divides common positive pairs by the geometric mean of the positive-pair counts in each solution.

The normalization balances precision-like and recall-like aspects of cluster recovery. It is one of several external indices compared through simulation in the source.
