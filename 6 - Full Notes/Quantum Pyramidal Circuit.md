# Quantum Pyramidal Circuit

A quantum pyramidal circuit arranges independently parameterized [[Reconfigurable Beam Splitter Quantum Gate|RBS gates]] in alternating nearest-neighbor layers. A square $n$-qubit pyramid has depth $2n-3$ and $n(n-1)/2$ angles, exactly matching the degrees of freedom of an $n\times n$ special orthogonal matrix.

The structure needs only nearest-neighbor connectivity and preserves excitation number. With a [[Unary Amplitude Encoding|unary input]], its action is precisely [[Unary-Subspace Orthogonal Matrix Multiplication]]. Rectangular variants expose fewer output qubits and use $(2n-1-d)d/2$ parameters for an $n\times d$ column-orthogonal transformation.
