# Logarithmic Scale

A logarithmic scale places equal visual intervals at equal multiplicative ratios rather than equal additive differences. It compresses quantities spanning several orders of magnitude and turns a constant exponential factor into an approximately straight trend.

Interpreting the axis requires stating its base and units because a one-unit change may represent multiplication by ten, $e$, or another factor. Richter magnitude, pH, and stellar magnitude illustrate how a familiar reported difference can encode a much larger physical ratio.
