# Prosthetic Hand Weight Constraint

The prosthetic hand weight constraint is the severe mass limit imposed by comfort and daily wear. Although a biological forearm and hand can weigh several kilograms, users may reject an artificial limb that approaches even half that mass because its load is carried through a socket rather than integrated living tissue.

Every motor, battery, sensor, transmission, and structural element competes within this budget. A design that reproduces more motions can become less usable if weight causes fatigue or discomfort. Functional benefit must therefore be evaluated at the system and wearer level rather than by dexterity alone.
