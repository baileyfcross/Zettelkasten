# Dimension Reduction for Clustering

Dimension reduction for clustering maps high-dimensional observations into fewer features before grouping them. Removing redundant or noisy dimensions lowers distance-computation cost and can make cluster structure easier to distinguish.

The transformation can also erase a small but important separation, so its preservation objective should match the geometry used by the clustering method.
