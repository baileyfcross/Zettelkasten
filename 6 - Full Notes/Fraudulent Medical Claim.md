# Fraudulent Medical Claim

A fraudulent medical claim contains a deliberately false or misleading request for reimbursement, such as a service not delivered, a misrepresented procedure, or an improper combination of codes. Detection compares the claim with patient, provider, and episode context.

Bayesian and non-Bayesian methods can estimate suspicion from categorical and numeric features, but confirmed labels are scarce and behavior changes. A claim score should prioritize investigation rather than automatically deny care. Explanatory features help reviewers determine whether the anomaly reflects deception, a coding error, or an unusual legitimate service.
