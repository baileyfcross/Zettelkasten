# Density and Mass Concentration Units

Density and the book's mass-concentration examples both have mass divided by volume, though the labels reflect different applications. Pryor converts lb/ft³ to kg/m³ and lb/gal to g/L. The mass factor belongs in the numerator; the volume ratio is inverted to cancel the original denominator. A numerically correct mass conversion alone therefore cannot give the right density. The unit structure also distinguishes these measures from mass alone. See [[Volume Unit Scaling]] and [[Conversion-Factor Chains]].
