# Regression F-Statistic

The regression F-statistic compares the variability explained by a linear model with its unexplained variability, accounting for their degrees of freedom. Its p-value tests whether the included slope coefficients are jointly zero under the model assumptions.

A significant overall F-test does not imply that every predictor is important or that the model fits well in an absolute sense. Individual coefficients, residual diagnostics, and the size of explained variation remain necessary.
