# Low-Resolution Test Renders in Blender

A test render uses reduced resolution or preview shading to evaluate a recent change before committing to expensive final output. It shortens the feedback loop between adjusting a model, material, light, or camera and seeing the result.

Frequent small tests expose problems while they are still cheap to correct. Final quality settings can be restored after composition and appearance are stable.
