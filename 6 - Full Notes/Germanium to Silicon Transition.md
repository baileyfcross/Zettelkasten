# Germanium to Silicon Transition

The first transistor used germanium, but the industry shifted toward silicon as processing methods improved. Silicon was harder to work initially, yet it supported stable oxide layers and fabrication techniques suited to integrated circuits.

The transition shows that the best laboratory material is not always the best manufacturing platform. A material’s compatibility with insulation, patterning, scale, and yield determines its long-term value.
