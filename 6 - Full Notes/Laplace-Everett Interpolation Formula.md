# Laplace-Everett Interpolation Formula

The Laplace-Everett formula is a central interpolation formula expressed with the two neighboring tabular values and even differences only. It is obtained by reorganizing the Bessel expansion to eliminate odd differences.

Using even differences can be convenient when the target lies between the two central entries and those columns are accurately tabulated. The formula assumes equally spaced arguments and inherits the truncation behavior of the underlying interpolation polynomial.
