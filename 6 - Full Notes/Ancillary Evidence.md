# Ancillary Evidence

Ancillary evidence is information that is not itself the state being estimated but changes how a measurement should be interpreted. Visibility, operating conditions, or sensor context can alter the distribution of a reading given the state.

The sensor model therefore conditions on both the state and the ancillary variable. Incorporating that clue prevents a reading gathered under poor conditions from being treated as though its reliability were unchanged.
