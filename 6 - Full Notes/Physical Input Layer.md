# Physical Input Layer

The physical input layer is the bodily action and [[Input Device|input mechanism]] through which a player performs a [[Game Verb]]. A digital action may be represented onscreen, but the player actually presses, holds, releases, points, swipes, or moves a control.

Different inputs provide different degrees and rhythms of control. A binary key suits discrete grid movement, while a mouse supports continuous motion on two axes. The design should use the properties of its input method and make the physical performance reinforce the pace and character of the represented verb.
