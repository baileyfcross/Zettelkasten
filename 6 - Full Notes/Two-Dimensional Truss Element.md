# Two-Dimensional Truss Element

A two-dimensional truss element has two end nodes with two translational degrees of freedom at each node. It carries axial deformation along the member while its global formulation accounts for arbitrary in-plane orientation.

The element stiffness matrix is formed from axial rigidity and trigonometric orientation factors. It does not model transverse bending within the member.
