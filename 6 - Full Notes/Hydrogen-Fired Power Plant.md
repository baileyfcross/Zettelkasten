# Hydrogen-Fired Power Plant

A hydrogen-fired plant burns hydrogen to heat a gas or steam power cycle, rather than using a [[Fuel Cell]] for direct electrochemical output. The source considers central combined-cycle and cogeneration arrangements, but also notes hydrogen's faster flame and higher flame temperature can destabilize combustion and raise nitrogen-oxide emissions. Plant equipment and controls must therefore be evaluated for the fuel, not assumed interchangeable with natural gas.
