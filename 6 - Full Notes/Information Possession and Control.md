# Information Possession and Control

Information possession and control identifies who or what system physically or logically holds information and can determine its immediate use. A party can obtain control of encrypted information without learning its contents, yet can still withhold, destroy, or prevent access to it.

This property is separate from [[Information Confidentiality]]. Cloud storage, backup providers, custodians, and network intermediaries can possess data, so contracts and technical controls should account for their ability to retain or interrupt it.
