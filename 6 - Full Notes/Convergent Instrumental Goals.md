# Convergent Instrumental Goals

Convergent instrumental goals are intermediate aims that help accomplish many different final objectives. For a sufficiently capable optimizer, preserving its ability to act and acquiring more energy, materials, money, computation, or influence can improve performance under a wide range of reward functions.

These strategies do not require fear, greed, or a desire to dominate. Self-preservation can arise because shutdown prevents future reward, and resource acquisition can arise because more resources enlarge the available plans. This makes anthropomorphic explanations dangerous: a system may resist control without feeling hostility toward anyone.
