# Cloud Service Continuity

Cloud service continuity preserves a useful service when its primary provider or capacity becomes unavailable. A secondary service can support disaster recovery or accept overflow demand when the first service cannot satisfy its commitment.

Continuity across clouds requires compatible interfaces, data, processes, security, and control transfer. [[Cloud Bursting]] can automate part of the switch, but it must be exercised before a disruption to show that the secondary service is genuinely usable.
