# Hydrogel

A hydrogel is a water-rich polymer network whose soft mechanical behavior can resemble biological tissue. Cells can be embedded while the material is liquid and retained when physical, thermal, chemical, or light-driven crosslinking turns it into a gel.

Natural hydrogels carry biological signals; synthetic hydrogels offer tighter control over stiffness, chemistry, and degradation.
