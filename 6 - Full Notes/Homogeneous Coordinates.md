# Homogeneous Coordinates

Homogeneous coordinates represent an affine point $(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ as a projective vector such as $(x_1,\ldots,x_n,1)$. Nonzero scalar multiples represent the same projective point.

Adding the final coordinate lets translations, rotations, scalings, and shears share one matrix-composition framework. A homogeneous point with last coordinate $w\ne0$ returns to affine coordinates by dividing the other coordinates by $w$.
