# Proof by Contrapositive

A proof by contrapositive establishes p → q by directly proving the equivalent implication ¬q → ¬p. It is useful when assuming p does not expose a path to q but the negation of q has a concrete structural form.

The technique depends on the logical equivalence between a [[Conditional Proposition]] and its contrapositive. It differs from [[Proof by Contradiction]], which assumes the negation of the entire claim and derives an impossibility.
