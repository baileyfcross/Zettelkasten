# Scrum

Scrum is an empirical framework in which a small [[Scrum Team]] creates a usable [[Product Increment]] during a fixed [[Sprint]]. Its roles, events, and artifacts make the state of the product and the way of working visible enough to inspect and adapt.

Scrum does not prescribe the engineering or creative techniques used inside a Sprint. It creates a cadence and accountabilities within which a team can discover and improve those techniques.
