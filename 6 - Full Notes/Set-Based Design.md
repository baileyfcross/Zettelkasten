# Set-Based Design

Set-based design begins with several feasible alternatives and narrows them as evidence accumulates. Design, art, and technology explore overlapping possibility sets, eliminating options that fail shared constraints while delaying commitment to one solution.

The apparent duplication buys protection against committing early to a path that later proves infeasible. Small parallel experiments are most valuable where uncertainty and the cost of late reversal are high.
