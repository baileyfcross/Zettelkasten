# Shared Drug-Target Latent Space

A shared drug-target latent space represents every drug and target with a low-dimensional vector. The dot product of a drug vector and a target vector is transformed by a logistic function to estimate their interaction probability.

The factors summarize global structure in the interaction matrix, but sparse rows or columns may be poorly identified. [[Drug Neighborhood Regularization]], [[Target Neighborhood Regularization]], and [[Drug-Target Neighborhood Smoothing]] add local similarity evidence.
