# Software Crisis

The software crisis named the recurring pattern of projects delivered late, over budget, unreliable, unsafe, or not delivered at all. As programs grew into products, techniques for individual programmers could not manage their complexity and long operational lives.

The NATO conferences of 1968 and 1969 promoted software engineering as a response. The crisis shifted attention from merely producing code to creating dependable systems through disciplined design, testing, organization, and maintenance.
