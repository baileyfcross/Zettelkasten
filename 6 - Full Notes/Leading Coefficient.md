# Leading Coefficient

The leading coefficient of a nonzero polynomial is the coefficient of its highest term under the selected ordering. In one variable, this is the coefficient attached to the term whose exponent equals the [[Polynomial Degree]].

In several variables, the leading coefficient depends on a [[Monomial Order]]. Together with the leading monomial it determines the leading term used to build an [[Initial Ideal]] and a [[Gröbner Basis]].
