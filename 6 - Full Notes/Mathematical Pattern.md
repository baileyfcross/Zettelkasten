# Mathematical Pattern

A mathematical pattern is a regular relationship among values, positions, or operations that can be expressed with a general rule. Searching for a pattern involves comparing cases, identifying what remains constant, and proposing a [[Law of Formation]].

Algebra turns the proposed regularity into a formula that can be tested beyond the examples that suggested it. A list increasing by five may lead to an [[Arithmetic Progression]], while repeated doubling may lead to a [[Geometric Progression]]. Observing several matching cases supports a conjecture, but the rule must also explain why the relationship continues.
