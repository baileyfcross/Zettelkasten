# AIOps Cost-Benefit Analysis

AIOps cost-benefit analysis assigns measurable value to the operational problem and compares it with implementation, infrastructure, licensing, training, and opportunity costs. Incident count, mean time to resolve, engineer hours, contract loss, and customer retention can turn a vague efficiency claim into a testable case.

Benefits may be direct, indirect, intangible, or competitive, while costs can be known, hard to estimate, or tied to alternatives not chosen. The analysis should use local numbers and continue after deployment so a technically successful system is not assumed to be economically valuable.
