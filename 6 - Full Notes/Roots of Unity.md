# Roots of Unity

The $n$th roots of unity are the complex solutions of $z^n=1$. Using polar form, they are $e^{2\pi i k/n}$ for integers $k$ from zero through $n-1$.

Their points are equally spaced around the unit circle and form the vertices of a regular $n$-gon. Multiplying roots adds their angles modulo $2\pi$, so the set is closed under multiplication and its powers cycle periodically.
