# Session Layer

The session layer of the OSI model manages the continuing conversation between two applications. It establishes, coordinates, and ends a session and describes whether communication can proceed in simplex, half-duplex, or full-duplex form.

A session is a logical interaction above raw delivery. Its state must define which side may transmit, how an interrupted conversation resumes, and when the exchange is complete.
