# Knowledge Crunching

Knowledge crunching is the repeated collaborative work of acquiring, comparing, and refining [[Domain Knowledge]]. Conversations, observation, documents, prototypes, and workshops can reveal different parts of the domain, especially when knowledge is fragmented across an organization. The purpose is not merely to collect nouns; it is to understand behavior, rules, and exceptions well enough to revise the model. [[EventStorming]] intensifies this process by making competing explanations visible on one shared surface.
