# Uncertain Data Classification

Uncertain data classification predicts labels when an attribute is represented by a range, set, or probability distribution rather than one exact value. Measurement error, imputation, privacy transformations, and indirect sensing can all create this representation.

A classifier must aggregate over possible realizations or derive statistics of the uncertainty distribution. Replacing every uncertain value by its mean can discard variation that changes split, distance, density, or likelihood decisions.
