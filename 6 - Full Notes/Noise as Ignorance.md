# Noise as Ignorance

Noise can be understood as the effect of influences that a model ignores rather than as a mysterious substance added to the phenomenon. A die throw, for example, depends on many forces and conditions that are excluded from a practical predictive model.

What counts as signal or noise therefore depends on the description and question. Improving the available knowledge can move a formerly ignored influence into the model, changing both the predicted distribution and what remains unexplained.
