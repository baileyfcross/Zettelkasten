# Propositional Logic

The logical perspective on [[Proposition]] and how we can combine or manipulate them 
This thought process has an application to the design of [[Electronic Circuits]]

Propositional logic deliberately ignores the internal subject matter of a statement and keeps only its truth value. This abstraction permits [[Propositional Variable|propositional variables]] and logical operators to express arguments whose validity does not depend on the particular statements substituted into them.
