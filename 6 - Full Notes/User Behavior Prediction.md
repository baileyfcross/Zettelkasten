# User Behavior Prediction

User behavior prediction uses actions such as posting, commenting, sharing, browsing, visiting, or voting to forecast later activity. Because these behaviors change over time, models often represent them as rates or time series.

Early behavior may predict final content attention or behavior outside the platform, such as revenue. The model must account for platform-specific actions and the visibility mechanisms that expose users to content.
