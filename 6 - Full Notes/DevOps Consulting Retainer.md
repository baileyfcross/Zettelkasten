# DevOps Consulting Retainer

A DevOps consulting retainer gives a client access to an agreed amount of continuing maintenance, support, or improvement capacity each month. It sits between a fixed project and unbounded hourly availability, creating recurring revenue for the consultant and predictable access for the client.

The agreement should still define response expectations, included work, unused capacity, and how additional requests are priced. A retainer is most suitable after the consultant understands the system and the parties have established trust; it should not become an informal promise to absorb unlimited scope.
