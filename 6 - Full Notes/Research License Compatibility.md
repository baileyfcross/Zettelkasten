# Research License Compatibility

Research license compatibility asks whether code, data, text, and other components can be combined and redistributed while satisfying every applicable license. Two individually open components can still impose conflicting conditions on a derivative work.

Compatibility should be checked before a [[Research Compendium]] or [[Research Object]] is assembled for release. Software licenses, article licenses, and data dedications serve different kinds of material, so one blanket label may be inappropriate. Recording component-level provenance and terms prevents a reproducible package from becoming legally unusable.
