# Structure Sheaf

The structure sheaf O_X on X = Spec(A) assigns the localization A_f to each basic open set where f does not vanish. Restriction maps are the corresponding localization homomorphisms.

Its [[Sheaf Stalk]] at a prime p is A_p, a [[Local Ring]]. The pair (Spec(A),O_X) is an [[Affine Scheme]] and carries both the topology of prime ideals and their local algebra.
