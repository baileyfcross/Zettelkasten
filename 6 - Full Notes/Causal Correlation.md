# Causal Correlation

A causal correlation reflects a relationship in which changing one factor, under the relevant conditions, changes another. Such a relationship can support intervention and, when the conditions persist, prediction.

Not every statistical association has this status. Establishing causal meaning requires evidence about variation and structure rather than the magnitude of correlation alone.
