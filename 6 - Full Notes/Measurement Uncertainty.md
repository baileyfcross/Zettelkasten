# Measurement Uncertainty

Measurement uncertainty states a defensible interval around a reported value rather than claiming unattainable exactness. It may be written as an absolute uncertainty, such as $x\pm\delta x$, or as a fractional or percentage uncertainty. When measurements are combined, their uncertainties must also be propagated so the result reflects the limitations of the original data.
