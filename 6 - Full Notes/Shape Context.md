# Shape Context

Shape context represents a point on an object boundary by a histogram of the relative locations of other boundary points. Log-polar bins preserve coarse information about both distance and direction.

Comparing these local histograms supports point correspondence and overall shape matching. The descriptor gains tolerance to small deformations through binning, while normalization can address scale and other geometric variation.
