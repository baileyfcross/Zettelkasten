# Aircraft Center-of-Gravity Calculation

Aircraft center of gravity is calculated by summing each item's moment about a reference datum and dividing total moment by total weight. Passengers or components at the same arm can be combined, while equipment changes require their weight and position to be recorded. The result must fall inside the approved envelope because it changes stability, control authority, and [[Center of Pressure and Center of Gravity]] relationships.
