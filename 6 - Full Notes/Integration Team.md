# Integration Team

An integration team maintains the ability to assemble and verify a whole game from the work of many teams. It can coordinate builds, shared environments, cross-feature testing, and visibility of integration health.

It should enable frequent integration rather than become the only group responsible for making incompatible work fit at the end. Every contributing team retains responsibility for integratable output.
