# Outcome-Based Cyber Defense

Outcome-based cyber defense organizes protection around the harmful results an adversary seeks rather than around an endless list of tools and attack labels. The source highlights outcomes such as service denial, data theft, and deception.

This view asks which essential properties must survive and which observable events precede their loss. [[Denial-of-Service Outcome Resilience]], [[Extrusion Detection]], and [[Cyber Deception Outcome]] then become complementary strategies tied to specific consequences.
