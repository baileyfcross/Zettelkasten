# Ultrametric Distance Matrix

An ultrametric distance matrix records the tree-derived distance for every pair of leaves. Reordering its rows and columns consistently can reveal nested blocks: low within-cluster values collect near the diagonal and higher values separate larger branches.

The book demonstrates this with iris measurements, a Ward dendrogram, and the matrix read from that dendrogram. The matrix is more than a visualization aid: its ordered block structure is a numerical fingerprint of hierarchical relationships and can guide seriation of observations.
