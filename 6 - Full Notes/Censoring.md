# Censoring

Censoring occurs when an observation's exact event time is not observed, although the subject is known to remain event-free up to a particular time. Right censoring is common when follow-up ends before the event.

Censored observations still contribute information to the risk set until their censoring times. Survival methods rely on assumptions about how censoring relates to event risk, so systematic loss to follow-up can bias the estimated curve.
