# One-Sided Limit

A one-sided limit considers inputs approaching a point from only one direction. The left-hand limit uses $x<a$, and the right-hand limit uses $x>a$. A two-sided [[Limit of a Function]] exists only when both one-sided limits exist and are equal, making the distinction essential at jumps, endpoints, and piecewise boundaries.
