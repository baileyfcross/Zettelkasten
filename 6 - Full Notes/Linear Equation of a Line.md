# Linear Equation of a Line

A nonvertical line with slope $m$ and vertical intercept $b$ has equation $y=mx+b$. If a point $(x_1,y_1)$ and the slope are known, the point-slope form $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$ describes the same line. A vertical line instead has equation $x=c$ for a fixed horizontal coordinate.
