# Feedback Compliance

Feedback compliance is the spatial and temporal agreement among the forms of [[Feedback]] a user receives.

Directional compliance means a virtual object moves in the same direction as the physical control. Nulling compliance means returning a device to its initial pose also returns the controlled object to its corresponding pose. Temporal compliance means feedback arrives without a disruptive delay; [[Latency]] is a common violation.

When visual information conflicts with proprioceptive, kinesthetic, vestibular, or other cues, performance can degrade. Strong conflicts can also produce disorientation and [[Cybersickness]].
