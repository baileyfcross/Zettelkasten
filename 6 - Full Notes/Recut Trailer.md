# Recut Trailer

A recut trailer edits footage from an existing film into a preview that implies a different genre, mood, or story. Selection, sequencing, music, titles, and voice-over reframe familiar scenes without requiring new filmed material.

The genre makes editing conventions visible by showing how packaging can transform interpretation. A comedy can appear as horror or a thriller as romance when cues are rearranged. The joke depends on the audience recognizing both the source and the grammar of movie trailers.
