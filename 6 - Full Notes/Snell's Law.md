# Snell's Law

Snell's law relates refraction angles and refractive indices at a boundary:

$$n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2.$$

Frequency remains continuous across the boundary, while wave speed and wavelength change. A wave bends toward the normal when it enters a medium with larger refractive index and lower phase speed.
