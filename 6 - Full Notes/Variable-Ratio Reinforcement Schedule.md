# Variable-Ratio Reinforcement Schedule

A variable-ratio reinforcement schedule gives a reward after an unpredictable number of responses that averages around a target ratio. A monster with a fixed chance to drop rare loot uses this schedule because the player cannot know which defeat will pay out.

Variable ratios tend to produce high and persistent response rates with less rapid extinction than fixed schedules. That persistence makes them powerful but also raises ethical concerns when uncertainty is used to keep players repeating costly actions.
