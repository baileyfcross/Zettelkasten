# Diversion Planning and Recovery Legs

A diversion redirects a flight from its original destination to a temporary alternative. The route that later returns from that alternative to the original plan is the recovery leg. A sound diversion recalculates altitude, course, time, fuel, weather, and communication before committing to the new path.
