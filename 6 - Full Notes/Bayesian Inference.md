# Bayesian Inference

Bayesian inference computes the probability distribution requested by a question from a probabilistic description. Known values condition the joint distribution, free variables are marginalized, and the remaining values are normalized over the searched variables.

The calculation can be applied in any direction supported by the joint model. Its difficulty depends chiefly on the size of the searched and marginalized spaces and on how effectively the decomposition exposes conditional independence.
