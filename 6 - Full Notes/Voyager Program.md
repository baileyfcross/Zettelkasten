# Voyager Program

The Voyager program sent two robotic spacecraft through the outer solar system using a rare planetary alignment and gravity-assisted flybys. The missions returned close observations of Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune and continued outward on trajectories toward interstellar space.

Voyager demonstrates the leverage of long-lived robotic exploration. Careful trajectory design allowed a modest number of spacecraft to reach several distant worlds that humans could not practically visit, while durable instruments and communications extended scientific return across decades. The program helped establish planetary science as a continuing enterprise beyond the original Space Race.
