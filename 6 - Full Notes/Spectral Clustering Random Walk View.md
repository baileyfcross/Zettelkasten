# Spectral Clustering Random Walk View

The random-walk view interprets a normalized similarity matrix as transition probabilities on a graph. A good cluster is a region in which a random walker tends to remain because internal transitions are strong relative to outgoing ones.

Low-frequency eigenvectors describe slowly mixing divisions and supply coordinates for clustering. This interpretation connects spectral partitions to metastable states, while also showing why degree normalization changes the preferred solution.
