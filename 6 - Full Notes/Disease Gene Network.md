# Disease Gene Network

A disease gene network represents genes or molecular products as nodes and biological relationships as edges in the context of a disease. It can combine interaction, regulation, coexpression, or pathway evidence to move beyond one-gene explanations.

Network analysis can identify modules and central components associated with a phenotype, but heavily studied genes may appear important because the underlying knowledge base is uneven. Edge type and evidence should remain explicit, and a topological hub should not be treated automatically as a safe or causal drug target.
