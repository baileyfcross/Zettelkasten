# Enterprise AI Adoption Playbook

An enterprise AI adoption playbook turns scattered experimentation into a controlled operating model. It defines permitted and prohibited use cases, approved tools, guardrail requirements, human approvals, audit and logging standards, compliance enforcement, and incident-handling procedures.

The playbook also assigns responsibilities. DevOps engineers review output and enforce least privilege; security teams define safeguards and policy; platform teams maintain automation boundaries; leadership sets risk tolerance. Clear ownership prevents a governance failure from becoming an argument about who was supposed to notice or intervene.
