# Prodigy Online Service

Prodigy combined dial-up information services with preloaded graphics and advertising. Local graphics compensated for slow modems and gave sponsors a way to subsidize user costs.

Heavy use of e-mail and discussion consumed connection time without generating equivalent ad exposure. Attempts to censor forums also produced conflict with subscribers.
