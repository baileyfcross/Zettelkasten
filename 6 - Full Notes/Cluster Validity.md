# Cluster Validity

Cluster validity is the assessment of whether a clustering accurately represents meaningful structure. It asks whether within-cluster relationships, between-cluster separation, and the selected number of groups are supported by evidence.

Validity can be evaluated against known labels, from the data and proximities alone, or by comparison with alternative solutions. The criteria must match the method and intended interpretation.
