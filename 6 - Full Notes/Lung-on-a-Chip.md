# Lung-on-a-Chip

A lung-on-a-chip places airway and vascular cell layers on opposite sides of a porous flexible membrane. Air exposure, fluid flow, and cyclic stretching reproduce parts of the alveolar-capillary interface and the mechanical action of breathing.

The device can test inflammatory or toxic responses while making cell interactions and physical forces directly controllable.
