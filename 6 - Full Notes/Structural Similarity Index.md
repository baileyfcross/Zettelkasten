# Structural Similarity Index

Structural similarity compares local luminance, contrast, and structure instead of reducing all pixel differences to squared error. It is intended to better reflect the way visible relationships within an image matter to human observers.

SSIM is still a model rather than a universal truth. Its score depends on scale and evaluation choices, and a single aggregate can conceal a damaging local artifact. It is most useful as one input to quality selection alongside visual inspection and operational goals. See [[Image Quality Tier Selection]].
