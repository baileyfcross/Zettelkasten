# Feature Area Team

A feature area team coordinates several teams working within a broad area of the game. The grouping gives related work a coherent product goal and Product Ownership layer while preserving small delivery teams beneath it.

Feature areas should follow meaningful product boundaries and evolve as the game changes. Permanent silos can reproduce separate games inside one release.
