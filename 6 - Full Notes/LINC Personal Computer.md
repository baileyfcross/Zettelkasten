# LINC Personal Computer

The LINC was a small interactive computer developed by Wesley Clark’s team for laboratory researchers. Demonstrated in 1962, it placed computation directly beside specialized scientific equipment rather than behind a remote operator.

The machine anticipated personal computing by giving one user control of a responsive system. Its medical audience accepted an approach that many mainframe engineers considered impractical.
