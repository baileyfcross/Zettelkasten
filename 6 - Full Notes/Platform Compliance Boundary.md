# Platform Compliance Boundary

A platform compliance boundary identifies which platform components, data flows, people, and external services fall under a regulatory or organizational control set. It lets the platform team translate frameworks into concrete requirements for access, evidence, retention, encryption, isolation, and change management.

Compliance and security overlap but are not identical: a system can satisfy a checklist while remaining insecure, or use strong controls without producing the evidence a framework requires. The boundary should therefore support both substantive protection and auditable proof, while making clear which application-level or physical controls remain outside platform ownership.
