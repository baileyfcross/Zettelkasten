# Guess Two-Thirds Game

The guess two-thirds game asks each participant to choose a number from zero to one hundred, with the prize going to the guess closest to two-thirds of the group average. Repeated elimination of impossible rational choices drives the theoretical equilibrium to zero.

Experimental players rarely converge immediately on zero. Many stop after one or two levels of reasoning, showing that a model requiring repeated induction can predict an equilibrium that actual participants do not reach.
