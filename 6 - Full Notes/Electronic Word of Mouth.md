# Electronic Word of Mouth

Electronic word of mouth is the online exchange of opinions and experiences about products, services, organizations, or events. Reviews and recommendations can spread beyond direct acquaintances and strongly influence later decisions.

The influence attracts manipulation through fake reviews, coordinated promotion, or malicious criticism. Credibility depends on evidence about contributors, relationships, timing, and patterns of behavior.
