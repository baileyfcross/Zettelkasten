# Platform Purpose

Platform purpose is the concise statement of whose problem the platform solves and what improvement it intends to create. It precedes the technical architecture because a list of preferred tools cannot determine whether the platform should optimize onboarding, compliance, delivery speed, operational insight, cost, or another user outcome.

Purpose gives [[Platform Principles]] a direction and defines the hypothesis that a [[Thinnest Viable Platform]] can test. A team can use it to reject attractive features that do not serve current users, choose success measures, and explain why the platform deserves continuing investment.
