# Reactive Extensions for .NET

Reactive Extensions, commonly called Rx, provide operators and supporting types for composing .NET observable sequences. The book uses `System.Reactive` to convert arrays into observables and then merge, filter, and map their notifications. Rx builds on the `IObservable<T>` and `IObserver<T>` contract, replacing hand-written event wiring with reusable stream transformations.
