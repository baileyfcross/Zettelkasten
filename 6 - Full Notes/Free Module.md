# Free Module

A free R-module is a direct sum of copies of R. Its standard generators form a basis, so every element has a unique finite expression as a linear combination of those generators.

Every free module is a [[Projective Module]]. Free modules provide the terms in a [[Projective Resolution]] and make module homomorphisms representable by matrices.
