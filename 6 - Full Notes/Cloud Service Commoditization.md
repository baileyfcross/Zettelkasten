# Cloud Service Commoditization

Cloud service commoditization turns computing capabilities into standardized services that can be selected and purchased with less specialist knowledge. Reusable offerings, measurable units, common patterns, and provider competition make infrastructure and software resemble other utilities.

Standardization can lower cost and speed procurement, but it can also concentrate supply and hide meaningful differences. Interoperability and transparent service commitments are required before apparently comparable services can be substituted safely.
