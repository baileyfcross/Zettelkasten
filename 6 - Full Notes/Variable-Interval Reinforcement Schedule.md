# Variable-Interval Reinforcement Schedule

A variable-interval reinforcement schedule rewards the first qualifying response after an unpredictable amount of time that averages around a target interval. The player cannot concentrate effort around a known deadline.

This pattern tends to produce steady activity: more continuous than a fixed ratio, less frantic than a variable ratio, and less vulnerable to rapid extinction than a fixed interval. It suits behaviors the designer wants distributed across time.
