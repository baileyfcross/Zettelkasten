# Occupy 99 Percent Meme

The Occupy 99 Percent meme used photographs of people holding handwritten accounts of economic hardship, usually ending with identification as part of the 99 percent. The shared format connected diverse personal experiences to a broad critique of inequality.

Its structure exemplified connective action: participants retained their own voices while becoming visibly part of a collective claim. The handwritten signs, faces, and domestic settings emphasized authenticity, and the repeatable format made the campaign easy to join, recognize, and answer with countermemes.
