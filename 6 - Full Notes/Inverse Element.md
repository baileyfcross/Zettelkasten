# Inverse Element

The inverse of a group element a is the unique element a⁻¹ satisfying aa⁻¹ = a⁻¹a = e. Under additive notation it is written -a.

The inverse of a product reverses order: (ab)⁻¹ = b⁻¹a⁻¹.
