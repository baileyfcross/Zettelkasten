# Roblox Experience Analytics

Roblox analytics summarize how players find, use, and spend within an experience. Measures can include visits, play time, device or platform distribution, Robux revenue, Premium payouts, and sales of passes or developer products over different time ranges.

A metric becomes useful when connected to a decision or event. Comparing periods before and after an update, storefront revision, localization change, or performance improvement helps distinguish plausible effects from raw totals. Analytics cannot explain every cause, but they provide feedback for [[Roblox Update and Notification Strategy]], [[Roblox Experience Storefront Media]], and cross-platform planning.
