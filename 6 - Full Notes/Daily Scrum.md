# Daily Scrum

The Daily Scrum is a roughly fifteen-minute Development Team event for inspecting progress toward the [[Sprint Goal]] and coordinating the next day of work. Members surface progress, intended work, and impediments that affect the team's plan.

It is not a status report to a manager or a place to solve every problem. Detailed problem-solving follows with only the people who need to participate.
