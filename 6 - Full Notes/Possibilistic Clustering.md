# Possibilistic Clustering

Possibilistic clustering treats membership as the degree to which an observation exemplifies each cluster and does not require memberships across clusters to sum to one. An observation can be typical of several groups or atypical of all of them.

Dropping the probability constraint makes the method more mode-seeking and can distinguish a boundary point from a remote point that is equally divided only because every option fits badly.
