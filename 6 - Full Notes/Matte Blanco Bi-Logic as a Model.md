# Matte Blanco Bi-Logic as a Model

Matte Blanco's bi-logic distinguishes asymmetric, conscious reasoning from a symmetric mode associated with unconscious processes. Murtagh proposes Euclidean or ordinary metric geometry as a model of the former and nested, ultrametric hierarchy as a mathematical representation of the latter.

The mapping is a conceptual model, not a demonstrated identity between a person's mind and a clustering algorithm. It makes the theory's claims about equivalence classes and generalization inspectable as tree relations, while empirical evidence and interpretation remain separate questions.
