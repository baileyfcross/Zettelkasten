# Cloud Service Encapsulation

Cloud service encapsulation places one or more cloud services inside a larger service boundary. Consumers of the larger service can depend on what a component does without needing every detail of how that component is built.

The book distinguishes two forms: [[Cloud Service Aggregation]] for comparable repeated components and [[Cloud Service Composition]] for distinct cooperating components. Both can improve reuse and provisioning speed when their interfaces are standardized.
