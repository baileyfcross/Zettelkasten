# Responsibility Across AI Copies

Responsibility across AI copies asks how liability should be assigned when a digital person is duplicated after an action. Both descendants remember and are causally continuous with the actor, but their later conduct, knowledge, and attitudes can diverge.

Punishing every copy equally treats shared memory as sufficient for responsibility; punishing only one requires an arbitrary choice unless a legal rule identifies a continuing bearer. The dilemma becomes sharper when one copy confesses and another conceals the act. Digital agency therefore challenges the assumption that one past action always maps to one enduring responsible person.
