# Quantum Supremacy Verification Strategy

A computation designed to exceed direct classical simulation cannot be validated by rerunning the full task classically at scale. Verification therefore triangulates from smaller instances that can be simulated, separable pieces of the large computation, and structured variants that are easier to check.

Agreement in those accessible regimes supports calibration and modeling but does not logically prove every inaccessible output. The benchmark must also resist classical methods that target the score without faithfully simulating the circuit. Verification is therefore part of the advantage claim rather than an afterthought to runtime.
