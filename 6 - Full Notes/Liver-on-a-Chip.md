# Liver-on-a-Chip

A liver-on-a-chip maintains small liver-like tissues in a controlled platform for studying metabolism and toxicity. Patterned hepatocyte colonies, supporting fibroblasts, and fluid delivery help preserve functions lost in simple hepatocyte culture.

Testing known safe and toxic compounds provides evidence that the model can remove harmful drug candidates before human trials.
