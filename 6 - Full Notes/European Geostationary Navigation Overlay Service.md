# European Geostationary Navigation Overlay Service

The European Geostationary Navigation Overlay Service is Europe's regional satellite-based augmentation system. Like WAAS, it derives corrections and integrity information from a network of known ground sites and distributes them through geostationary satellites. EGNOS improved GPS-based accuracy and operational assurance over Europe before Galileo was fully deployed, showing that augmentation and an independent core constellation solve related but different problems.
