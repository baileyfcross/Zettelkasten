# Displacement

Displacement is the vector change in position from an initial point to a final point. It records both magnitude and direction and therefore differs from distance traveled, which is a scalar path length. The gradient of a displacement-time graph is [[Linear Velocity]], while the area under a velocity-time graph gives displacement change.
