# Injective Dimension

The injective dimension of a module is the least length of a finite [[Injective Resolution]], or infinity if none exists. An injective module has injective dimension zero.

For a finitely generated module of finite injective dimension over a Noetherian local ring, the source relates that dimension to the depth of the ring through Ext vanishing.
