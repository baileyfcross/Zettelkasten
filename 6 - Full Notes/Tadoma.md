# Tadoma

Tadoma is a method by which a deaf-blind reader places a hand on a speaker's face and neck to perceive speech through touch. The thumb senses airflow at the lips, fingers follow lip and jaw motion, and other contact detects vibration of the larynx.

These simultaneous cues form a rich haptic representation of articulation. Exceptional, extensively trained users can understand speech at high levels, demonstrating the information available through natural facial movement. The method is difficult to master and requires intimate contact, which limits its practicality despite outperforming many artificial tactile speech displays.
