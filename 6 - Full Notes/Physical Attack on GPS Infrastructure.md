# Physical Attack on GPS Infrastructure

A physical attack on GPS infrastructure could target spacecraft, control facilities, tracking stations, or communication links. Medium-Earth-orbit satellites are harder to reach than low-orbit vehicles, and constellation spares mean that disabling one satellite does not disable the service. Secured and redundant control sites add another layer. These measures raise the cost and visibility of an attack, but they do not make a global system invulnerable.
