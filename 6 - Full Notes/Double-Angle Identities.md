# Double-Angle Identities

Setting the two angles equal in the [[Angle Addition Identities]] gives

$$\sin 2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta$$

and

$$\cos2\theta=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta.$$

The [[Pythagorean Trigonometric Identity]] rewrites the cosine formula as $2\cos^2\theta-1$ or $1-2\sin^2\theta$.
