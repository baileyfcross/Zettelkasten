# Moment of Inertia

Moment of inertia measures rotational resistance to angular acceleration. For point masses about an axis, $I=\sum m_ir_i^2$, so mass farther from the axis contributes more strongly. It depends on both total mass and its distribution, and it plays the rotational role that mass plays in linear dynamics.
