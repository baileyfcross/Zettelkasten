# Consumer Privacy Paradox

The consumer privacy paradox is the gap between expressed concern about privacy and behavior that still discloses personal information. Mobile users may accept tracking because a service offers convenience, access, social value, status, or a monetary benefit.

The gap does not necessarily prove that privacy is unimportant. Choices can be constrained by unclear notices, limited alternatives, trust, habits, or difficulty evaluating future uses of data.
