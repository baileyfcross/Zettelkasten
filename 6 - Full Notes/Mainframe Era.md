# Mainframe Era

The mainframe era of the 1950s and 1960s centered computing in large, air-conditioned installations operated by specialists. Banks of magnetic tape, card readers, printers, and consoles surrounded expensive processors that organizations kept continuously busy.

IBM dominated this market, but UNIVAC and other vendors competed. Mainframes supported scientific calculation and stable business workloads long before interactive personal computing became common.
