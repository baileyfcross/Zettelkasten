# Fuel Cell Cogeneration

Fuel-cell cogeneration supplies electrical power and useful heat from the same fuel-cell installation. Electrical reaction work is bounded by [[Gibbs Free Energy]], while remaining heat can warm buildings or serve industrial processes. The source especially associates phosphoric-acid, molten-carbonate, and solid-oxide cells with such systems. Total utilization can be much higher than electricity-only efficiency when heat demand is nearby and simultaneous.
