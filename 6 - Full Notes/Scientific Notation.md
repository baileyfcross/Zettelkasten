# Scientific Notation

Scientific notation writes a nonzero decimal as $a\times10^n$, where $1\leq |a|<10$ and $n$ is an integer. The exponent records how far the decimal point moves. Multiplication combines coefficients and adds exponents, making the notation especially useful for quantities that are much larger or smaller than ordinary decimal displays handle clearly.
