# Music Copyright in Games

Game music can involve separate rights in the musical composition and in the sound recording of a performance. A composer who owns both cannot necessarily transfer a clean recording unless hired performers have also granted the needed performance rights through contracts or releases.

The project's agreement determines whether those rights are licensed or assigned through a [[Game Music Work-for-Hire Agreement]]. Publishing and performance royalties are separate questions from ownership of the delivered file, so the parties should identify which rights, uses, territories, durations, and royalty shares the agreement actually covers.
