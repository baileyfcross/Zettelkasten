# Cross-Discipline Team

A cross-discipline team contains the capabilities needed to deliver an integrated result. In games, programmers, designers, artists, audio specialists, and testers coordinate around playable features instead of passing isolated components between departmental queues.

This structure reduces [[Handoff|handoffs]], aligns priorities, and reveals constraints early. It does not erase specialties; it makes the whole team's result the primary unit of progress.
