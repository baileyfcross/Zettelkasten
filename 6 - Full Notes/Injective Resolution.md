# Injective Resolution

An injective resolution of a module M is an exact sequence 0 → M → I⁰ → I¹ → ··· in which every Iⁱ is an [[Injective Module]].

Applying Hom into this cocomplex and taking cohomology constructs Ext groups. The minimum possible finite length of such a resolution is the [[Injective Dimension]].
