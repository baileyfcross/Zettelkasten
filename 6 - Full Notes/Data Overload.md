# Data Overload

Data overload occurs when a visualization presents more information than a viewer can effectively perceive, organize, or use. In AR, the physical scene is already information-rich, so every annotation competes with real objects and other augmentations.

Cluttered content can obscure the task and impair understanding even when every individual item is relevant. Spatial and knowledge-based filtering, layout, and interaction should control what appears for the current user, location, and purpose.
