# Analogous Environment Palette

An analogous palette uses neighboring hues on the color wheel. Their shared components create harmony, making the scheme useful for environments that need a continuous mood or gradual transition.

One hue can dominate while the others support variation, preventing the scene from becoming visually flat. Value and saturation differences remain important for separating objects and routes even when the hues themselves are closely related.
