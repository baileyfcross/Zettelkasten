# Emotional Resonance

Emotional resonance occurs when game events connect with the player's empathy, memory, or sense of consequence. Even simple names, facial expressions, or persistent individual traits can make abstract system states feel like the experiences of characters.

Authored context can give systemic choices emotional weight, while events produced through play can make characters feel personal without a fixed plot. Irreplaceability and accumulated history often strengthen that attachment.
