# Space Debris

Space debris consists of dead satellites, spent rocket stages, fragments, and other human-made objects that remain in orbit without serving an active mission. Even small pieces can cause severe damage because orbital encounters occur at very high relative velocity.

Risk depends on altitude and traffic. Debris in lower orbits can eventually fall because of atmospheric drag, while objects higher in low Earth orbit persist longer and cross many inclined paths. Collisions create additional fragments, making the problem cumulative and connecting every operator's behavior to the safety of a shared orbital environment.
