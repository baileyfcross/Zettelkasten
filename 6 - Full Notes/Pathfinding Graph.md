# Pathfinding Graph

A pathfinding graph represents traversable space as nodes connected by edges. Nodes stand for locations or regions, edges represent allowed movement, and edge costs estimate the expense of traveling between them.

A grid, collection of [[Path Node|path nodes]], or [[Navigation Mesh]] can all produce a graph searched by the same general algorithm. Representation determines route quality, memory use, and search cost.
