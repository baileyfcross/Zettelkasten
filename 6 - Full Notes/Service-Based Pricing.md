# Service-Based Pricing

Service-based pricing derives the charge from the benefit or commitment delivered to the consumer rather than only the provider's underlying resource consumption. Scope, service levels, transferred delivery risk, and money saved can therefore influence the amount.

The book groups fixed, volume, and tiered approaches in this category. The model is strongest when both parties can define the service boundary and measure the unit on which the charge depends.
