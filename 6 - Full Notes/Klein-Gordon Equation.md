# Klein-Gordon Equation

The Klein-Gordon equation is a relativistic wave equation for a spin-zero field. It follows by promoting the relativistic energy-momentum relation to differential operators acting on a scalar wavefunction.

Because it is second order in both time and space, it treats spacetime derivatives symmetrically and admits positive- and negative-frequency solutions. Its conserved current does not provide a positive-definite single-particle probability density, motivating a field interpretation.
