# Cumulative Distribution Function

A cumulative distribution function gives, for every value, the probability or proportion of observations less than or equal to that value. It compresses an ordered collection into a monotone description of where its values lie.

Differences between two cumulative values give the probability of an interval. A [[Quantile]] reverses the relationship by starting with a cumulative probability and returning the corresponding value.
