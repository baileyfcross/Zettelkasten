# Minicomputer

A minicomputer was a smaller, less expensive computer designed for direct use in laboratories, departments, and industrial systems. It occupied the space between centralized mainframes and later microprocessor-based personal computers.

Companies such as DEC built minicomputers around transistor and integrated-circuit technology. Their interactive use and customer-developed applications prepared users for personal computing.
