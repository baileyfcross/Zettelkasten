# Electric Motor

An electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical rotation. Opposite sides of a current-carrying coil in a magnetic field experience forces that form a torque. In a simple DC motor, a split-ring commutator reverses coil current every half-turn so the torque continues in the same rotational sense.
