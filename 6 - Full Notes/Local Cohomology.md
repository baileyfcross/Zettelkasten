# Local Cohomology

Local cohomology Hⁱ_I(M) is the cohomology obtained by applying [[Global Sections with Support|Γ_I]] to an injective resolution of M. It measures module behavior supported on the closed set defined by I.

The modules depend only on the radical of I and can be computed through a [[Cech Complex]]. They participate in long exact sequences, change-of-rings isomorphisms, and Mayer-Vietoris sequences.
