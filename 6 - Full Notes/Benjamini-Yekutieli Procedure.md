# Benjamini-Yekutieli Procedure

The Benjamini-Yekutieli procedure is a [[Step-Up Multiple Testing Procedure]] that controls the [[False Discovery Rate]] under broad dependence by reducing the nominal level with a harmonic correction $\sum_{i=1}^{m}1/i$.

Its generality comes at a power cost. The correction grows with the number of tests and can make the rejection threshold smaller than alternatives designed for independence or positive dependence. It is useful when dependence cannot be characterized well enough to justify the more powerful [[Benjamini-Hochberg Procedure]].
