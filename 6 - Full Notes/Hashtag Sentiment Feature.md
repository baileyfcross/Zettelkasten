# Hashtag Sentiment Feature

A hashtag sentiment feature records the presence of hashtags as additional predictors beside ordinary text terms. Hashtags can name a topic, stance, campaign, or evaluative phrase whose meaning would be lost if punctuation removal simply discarded the hash-prefixed token.

The feature space can be represented as a sparse presence matrix and joined to a [[Document-Term Matrix]]. Rare and rapidly changing hashtags still require selection or regularization so they do not create unstable predictors.
