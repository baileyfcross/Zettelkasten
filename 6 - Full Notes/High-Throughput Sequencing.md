# High-Throughput Sequencing

High-throughput sequencing reads very large numbers of DNA fragments in parallel. It lowers the cost and time per base while producing massive collections of short reads and associated quality measurements.

The raw output requires alignment or assembly, quality control, variant analysis, and substantial transfer and storage capacity before it becomes biological evidence.
