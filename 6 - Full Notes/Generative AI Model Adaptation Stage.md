# Generative AI Model Adaptation Stage

The generative-AI model adaptation stage aligns a pretrained model with a use case through fine-tuning, transfer learning, LoRA, prompt engineering, distillation, or related techniques. Kubernetes coordinates the containerized jobs and accelerator resources used by the chosen method.

Adaptation output includes more than weights: data versions, configuration, evaluation, logs, and environment metadata are required for review. Automated workflows can run distributed training and tuning, but acceptance gates must remain tied to task and safety criteria.
