# Fly-By Animation

A fly-by animation changes the viewer's virtual position and orientation around a static or changing three-dimensional scene. The source constructs a textured Earth and defines a sequence of geographic locations for the camera to visit. Unlike a [[Raster Map Animation]], the animated attribute may be the view itself rather than the data. Smooth paths, recognizable landmarks, and controlled speed preserve spatial orientation during the flight.
