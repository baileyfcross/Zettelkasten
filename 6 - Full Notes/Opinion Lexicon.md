# Opinion Lexicon

An opinion lexicon lists words or expressions with an associated polarity or sentiment score. A basic classifier can sum the values of matched entries and label a text positive, negative, or neutral according to the resulting balance.

This method can operate without labeled training documents, but it ignores many compositional effects unless explicit rules handle them. Coverage and word meaning vary by domain, so a general lexicon may miss specialized expressions or assign the wrong orientation.
