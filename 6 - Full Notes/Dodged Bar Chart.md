# Dodged Bar Chart

A dodged bar chart places subgroup bars side by side on a common baseline. This arrangement makes comparisons between subgroup magnitudes easier than stacked segments, though it can become crowded with many categories.

Consistent ordering and a readable legend are essential for tracing each subgroup across the chart.
