# Microsoft Defender for Identity

Microsoft Defender for Identity monitors signals from the identity environment to identify suspicious authentication, reconnaissance, credential use, and directory activity. Sensors associated with domain controllers observe behavior that ordinary endpoint antivirus may not interpret as an identity attack, and the cloud service correlates those observations into alerts and investigation context.

The service detects patterns; it does not make weak administrative design safe. Sensor coverage, connectivity, identity inventory, alert ownership, and response procedures must be maintained for findings to be useful. Baselines can distinguish normal service-account or administrative behavior from anomalies, but privileged-account separation and timely remediation remain necessary. Defender for Identity succeeds the older Advanced Threat Analytics approach and fits into a broader Microsoft Defender security ecosystem.
