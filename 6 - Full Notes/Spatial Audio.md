# Spatial Audio

Spatial audio presents sound so that it appears to originate from a location or direction in three-dimensional space. It uses the listener's auditory localization abilities to provide an aural sense of the environment.

One approach records separate left- and right-ear signals in a real setting. Another filters a monaural source through head-related transfer functions for a desired location, which supports interactive placement but must address individual differences and missing reverberation. [[Auralization]] instead models how sound propagates and reflects through an environment.

Spatial audio can support [[Wayfinding]], reveal off-screen events, and strengthen [[Presence]].
