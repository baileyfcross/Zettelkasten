# AI for Climate Action

AI for climate action applies pattern recognition, modeling, monitoring, and coordination to environmental problems. Systems can analyze complex environmental data, improve energy management, support smart grids or agriculture, and help implement reductions in harmful emissions.

These capabilities make AI a tool rather than a complete climate policy. Models do not decide how costs should be distributed, which goals deserve priority, or how conflicts among communities and nonhuman interests should be resolved. The environmental benefit must also be compared with [[AI Energy Consumption]] and material infrastructure. Useful computation complements political action and practical wisdom instead of replacing them.
