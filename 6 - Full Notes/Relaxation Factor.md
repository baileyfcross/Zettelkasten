# Relaxation Factor

A relaxation factor $\omega$ controls how strongly an iterative method accepts a newly computed correction. The update combines the previous value with the candidate value.

In [[Successive Over-Relaxation]], $\omega=1$ gives the [[Gauss-Seidel Method]], $0<\omega<1$ gives under-relaxation, and $1<\omega<2$ gives over-relaxation. The best value depends on the problem.
