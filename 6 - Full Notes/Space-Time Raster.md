# Space-Time Raster

A space-time raster is a sequence of aligned raster layers whose cells describe a field at successive times. It forms a three-dimensional array with two spatial dimensions and one temporal dimension. A [[Space-Time Level Plot]] shows selected snapshots, while [[Hovmoller Diagram|Hovmoller diagrams]] or time-series reductions aggregate one spatial direction. No single two-dimensional display preserves every relationship in the full array.
