# Personal Data

Personal data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable natural person. The definition reaches beyond names and direct identifiers: a record can become personal when it can be connected to someone through available means or combined with other information.

In big-data systems, identifiability is contextual. Seemingly anonymous observations may reveal a person through patterns, linkage, or inference, so governance must examine the complete processing environment rather than each field in isolation.
