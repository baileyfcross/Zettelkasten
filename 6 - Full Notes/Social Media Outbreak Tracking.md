# Social Media Outbreak Tracking

Social media outbreak tracking follows the evolution of a suspected disease event after initial detection. It examines changes in volume, location, symptoms, and topics to estimate how public concern or illness-related reports move over time.

Tracking is distinct from detecting a first anomaly: the vocabulary and affected places can shift as the event develops. The analyst must separate epidemiological change from media response and platform dynamics. Combining the stream with established surveillance sources helps determine whether a changing online pattern corresponds to disease transmission or merely attention.
