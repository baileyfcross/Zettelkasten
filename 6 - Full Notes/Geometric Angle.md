# Geometric Angle

A geometric angle measures the rotation between two rays, lines, directions, or surfaces about a shared origin or intersection. The selected unit and reference direction determine how the same orientation is represented numerically.

Angles enter geometry through sketches, transforms, coordinate systems, work-plane orientation, and boundary relationships. Confusing degrees with radians or reversing the reference direction can create a formally valid but incorrectly oriented model.
