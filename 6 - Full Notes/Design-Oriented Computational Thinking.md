# Design-Oriented Computational Thinking

Design-oriented computational thinking constructs virtual worlds in which people can achieve meaningful purposes. It combines technical knowledge with sensitivity to user practices, concerns, history, identity, and assessments of quality.

It includes software engineering rather than replacing it. Correctness and reliability establish a foundation, while contextual inquiry and iterative feedback determine whether the system creates value.
