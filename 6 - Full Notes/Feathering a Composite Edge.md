# Feathering a Composite Edge

A hard-edged matte can reveal where two photographed plates meet. Feathering or alpha blur turns that abrupt boundary into a gradual mixture and can hide minor differences in the edge position. The book's sky-augmentation example begins with a large rectangular mask that visibly cuts across the sky; a modest edge feather makes the join less conspicuous. Feathering does not repair wrong light direction, color, or perspective, so those cues must also be checked. See [[Sky Augmentation versus Extraction]].
