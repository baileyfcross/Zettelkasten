# Responsible AI Ownership Model

A responsible AI ownership model assigns named responsibility for policies, data, prompts, evaluation, guardrails, deployment approval, incident response, and user feedback. Without clear owners, safety issues can fall between model teams, application developers, security, legal, and business stakeholders even when every group assumes someone else is monitoring them.

Ownership should include decision rights and escalation paths, not only task lists. The organization needs to know who can approve a threshold, accept a known limitation, halt a deployment, investigate an incident, or revise a policy. Microsoft Foundry can centralize artifacts and evidence, but accountability remains an organizational design choice.
