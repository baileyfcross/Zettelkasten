# Grade of Membership

Grade of membership is a value from zero to one expressing how strongly an observation belongs to a fuzzy cluster. Larger values normally indicate greater compatibility with the cluster prototype.

It is not automatically a probability. Fuzzy membership can allow simultaneous belonging to several clusters, whereas a probabilistic assignment describes uncertainty over mutually exclusive alternatives.
