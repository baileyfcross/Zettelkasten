# Factor Loading

A factor loading is a coefficient connecting a latent factor to an observed variable or unit in a [[Factor Analysis]] model. Its magnitude and sign describe how strongly that factor contributes to the corresponding measurement pattern.

Loadings make a hidden direction interpretable by showing which variables move together with it. Their sign and scale can change with an equivalent rotation or factor convention, so interpretation focuses on relative patterns rather than an arbitrary orientation.
