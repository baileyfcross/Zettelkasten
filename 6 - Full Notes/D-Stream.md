# D-Stream

D-Stream maps incoming points to grid cells, updates cell densities over time, and periodically clusters adjacent dense cells. Density decay lets cell states change among dense, transitional, and sparse as the stream evolves.

The grid makes updates independent of the total number of past observations and can describe irregular regions through connected cells. Fixed resolution and periodic adjustment can delay or distort rapidly changing fine-scale patterns.
