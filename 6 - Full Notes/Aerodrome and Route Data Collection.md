# Aerodrome and Route Data Collection

Route preparation collects runway, taxiway, circuit, frequency, fuel, service, parking, and operating-hour information for departure, destination, and plausible alternates. It also records terrain, landmarks, restrictions, reporting points, variation, and nearby settlements along the path. These details make [[Pilotage and Dead Reckoning]] and diversion decisions possible when electronic tools fail.
