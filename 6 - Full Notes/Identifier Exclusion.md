# Identifier Exclusion

Identifier exclusion removes record keys and near-unique labels from model inputs while retaining them for joins and auditing. Identifiers can let a flexible learner memorize training cases without discovering a transferable relationship.

The field remains valuable operationally, so exclusion from a formula should not mean deletion from the governed dataset.
