# Homogeneous and Inhomogeneous Differential Equations

A linear differential equation is homogeneous when its forcing side is zero and inhomogeneous when a nonzero function remains. The homogeneous equation describes the system's free modes, while the inhomogeneous term drives a response.

Linearity permits solutions of the homogeneous equation to be added and scaled. For an inhomogeneous equation, the full solution is the sum of the general homogeneous solution and any one particular solution that produces the forcing term.
