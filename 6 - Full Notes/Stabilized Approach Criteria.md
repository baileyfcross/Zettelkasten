# Stabilized Approach Criteria

A stabilized approach keeps configuration, speed, power, vertical path, sink rate, and checklists within the aircraft and operator's defined limits by a specified altitude. Those conditions must then remain stable through landing. If they are not achieved or are lost, a go-around is the planned corrective action rather than an exceptional failure.
