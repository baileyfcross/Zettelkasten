# Atomic Challenge

An atomic challenge is the immediate, indivisible obstacle facing a player during play. It is the lowest level in a [[Hierarchy of Challenges]] and is not composed of smaller subchallenges.

Atomic challenges combine into larger tasks such as encounters, sub-missions, and levels. Their sequence and overlap determine the moment-to-moment texture and difficulty of gameplay.
