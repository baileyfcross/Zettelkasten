# Active and Passive Remote Sensing

A passive remote sensor measures energy already present in the environment, such as sunlight reflected from a surface. An active sensor supplies its own energy toward a target and measures the returned signal. A camera without a flash is passive, while a camera using a flash illustrates the active pattern.

The distinction affects when and what a [[Remote Sensing|remote-sensing system]] can observe. Passive instruments depend on available illumination or emitted energy; active systems such as radar and lidar can control the transmitted signal but require additional power and must interpret how that signal interacted with the target.
