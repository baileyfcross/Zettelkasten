# Normal Distribution

The normal distribution is a symmetric continuous distribution determined by its mean and standard deviation. Its bell-shaped density concentrates values near the mean while assigning decreasing probability to increasingly distant tails.

R can evaluate its density, cumulative probability, quantiles, and pseudorandom draws. Simulated histograms approach the model's shape as sample size grows, but a normal generator does not justify assuming that an observed scientific variable is normal.
