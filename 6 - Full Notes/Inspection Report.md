# Inspection Report

An inspection report records the defects found, their types and severities, the size of the reviewed artifact, and the preparation and meeting effort. It is distributed promptly so the author has an authoritative rework list and the organization can evaluate the review process.

The measures are not a substitute for correction. Their value is to connect defect discovery with follow-up and to improve future estimates about how much material can be reviewed and how much rework similar artifacts require.
