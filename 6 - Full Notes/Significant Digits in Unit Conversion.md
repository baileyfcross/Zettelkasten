# Significant Digits in Unit Conversion

The meaningful digits of a converted result should reflect the precision known for the original measurement, not simply every digit produced by multiplying factors. Pryor's app applies a factor, chooses a significant-digit count, and rounds the result as separate steps. An exact defined factor does not improve the quality of the input observation, while a truncated derived factor may add its own limitation. The book's app also offers an override when its inference from entered digits does not represent the measurement's real accuracy. See [[Conversion Resolution versus Accuracy]].
