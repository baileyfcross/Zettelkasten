# Truss

A truss is a load-bearing structure formed from slender members connected at joints idealized as pins. Under this idealization, members transmit axial tension or compression rather than bending moment.

Finite element analysis represents each member with a truss element and enforces equilibrium through shared joint degrees of freedom. Loads and support constraints are applied at the nodes.
