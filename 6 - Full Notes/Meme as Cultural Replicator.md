# Meme as Cultural Replicator

A meme is a unit of culture that spreads from person to person through copying or imitation. The concept treats cultural circulation as an evolutionary process in which variants compete for attention, some are selected, and some persist long enough to be copied again.

This analogy highlights variation, competition, selection, and retention, but it does not require culture to behave exactly like biology. In digital culture, the people who copy a meme also interpret and redesign it, so [[Human Agency in Meme Diffusion]] remains central even when a replicator vocabulary is useful.
