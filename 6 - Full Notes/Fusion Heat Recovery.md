# Fusion Heat Recovery

Fusion reactions release energy in particles and reactor surroundings; a plant must transfer that heat to a usable working fluid before producing electricity. The source describes neutron heating around a tokamak and coupling a proposed fusion reactor to vapor power cycles, including topping and steam stages. Fusion is therefore the heat source, not a direct electrical cell, and blanket cooling and fuel recovery are linked system functions.
