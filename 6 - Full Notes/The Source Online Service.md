# The Source Online Service

The Source was an early commercial online service that gave personal-computer users access to information and social interaction by modem. Its centralized system anticipated an online utility for consumers.

Although it closed in 1989, its experiments exposed both the demand and the operational difficulties of mass online communities. Later services reused lessons from its successes and mistakes.
