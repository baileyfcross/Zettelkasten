# Phase Velocity

Phase velocity is the speed and direction with which a point of constant phase travels. For a harmonic wave written $A\cos(\omega t-kx-\phi)$, the phase speed is $\omega/k$.

It describes the motion of individual crests or other fixed-phase features, not necessarily the speed of an envelope or energy transport. When the relation between frequency and wavenumber is not proportional, the phase velocity varies with frequency and the medium is dispersive.
