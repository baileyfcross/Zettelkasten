# WYSIWYG

WYSIWYG means that a document’s screen display closely matches its printed result. Bitmapped graphics, fonts, layout software, and laser printing made this correspondence possible.

The approach gives users immediate control over formatting without reasoning through printer codes. It became a defining feature of graphical word processing.
