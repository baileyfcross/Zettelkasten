# Most Plausible Value

When more linear observational equations are available than unknown quantities, the equations may be inconsistent because of measurement error. The most plausible values are the unknowns that minimize the sum of squared equation residuals.

Differentiating that sum with respect to every unknown produces a square system of [[Normal Equation|normal equations]]. Solving it gives one compromise estimate that satisfies all observations as nearly as possible in the least-squares sense.
