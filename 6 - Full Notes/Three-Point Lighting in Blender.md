# Three-Point Lighting in Blender

Three-point lighting arranges key, fill, and back lights around a subject. The key establishes the main direction, the fill moderates its shadows, and the back light separates the subject from the background.

The arrangement is a starting point rather than a rigid formula. Changing relative angle and intensity can move the scene from even studio illumination toward stark shadows, silhouettes, or other dramatic effects.
