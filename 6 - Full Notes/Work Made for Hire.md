# Work Made for Hire

Work made for hire is work whose ownership is assigned to an employer or commissioning party under the applicable employment or contract arrangement. In software employment, agreements commonly allocate to the company the intellectual property created for the job or with company resources.

Ownership should not be inferred casually in a startup, class team, or research collaboration. Contributors need an explicit agreement about what each person brings, how jointly created software is owned, and what rights remain for work made independently outside the agreed scope.
