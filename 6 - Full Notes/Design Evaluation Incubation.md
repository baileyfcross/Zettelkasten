# Design Evaluation Incubation

Design evaluation incubation is a deliberate pause between reviewing evidence and selecting a solution. It gives collaborators time to detach from immediate disappointment, integrate observations, and let alternative interpretations emerge.

The pause is bounded reflection rather than avoidance. The team returns with the evidence and design values still visible, then proceeds to generate and compare responses.
