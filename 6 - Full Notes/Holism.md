# Holism

Holism seeks to understand an object as an integrated whole rather than as a collection of isolated parts. It is useful for recognizing large-scale patterns and the qualities of a complete experience.

A holistic view without careful analysis can invent relationships or mistake correlation for causation. [[Systems Thinking]] combines it with [[Reductionism]] so that claims about a whole remain grounded in actual parts and interactions.
