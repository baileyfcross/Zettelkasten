# R ARIMA Model

An R autoregressive integrated moving-average model represents an ordered series through autoregressive terms, differencing, and moving-average terms. The three orders describe dependence on past values, the amount of differencing used to seek stationarity, and dependence on past innovations.

Model fitting should follow inspection of the time order and correlation structure rather than treating the observations as exchangeable rows. Residual diagnostics test whether meaningful temporal dependence remains after the fit.
