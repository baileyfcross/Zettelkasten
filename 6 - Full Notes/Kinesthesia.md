# Kinesthesia

Kinesthesia is the sense of limb position, movement, and muscular force. [[Muscle Spindle|Muscle spindles]] report muscle length and its rate of change, skin stretch and joint receptors contribute position information, and [[Golgi Tendon Organ|Golgi tendon organs]] plus [[Corollary Discharge|corollary discharges]] contribute force information.

The term is sometimes treated as equivalent to proprioception, although proprioception may be defined more broadly to include vestibular information about posture and balance. Kinesthetic signals combine with touch during [[Haptic Sensing]], allowing the nervous system to interpret both contact with an object and the movement used to explore it.
