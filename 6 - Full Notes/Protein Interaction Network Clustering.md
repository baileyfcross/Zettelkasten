# Protein Interaction Network Clustering

Protein interaction network clustering seeks densely or functionally connected groups of proteins in a graph whose edges represent observed interactions. Such groups may correspond to complexes, pathways, or broader functional modules.

Interaction data contain missing and spurious edges, and biologically meaningful modules can overlap. Edge confidence, auxiliary annotations, and robustness across graph perturbations help qualify any functional interpretation.
