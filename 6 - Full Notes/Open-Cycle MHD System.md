# Open-Cycle MHD System

An open-cycle MHD system burns fuel to make a hot gas stream, adds an ionizing seed, accelerates the gas through a nozzle, and extracts direct current in an [[MHD Generator Duct]]. Exhaust heat preheats incoming oxidant; seed recovery and pollution control precede discharge. Unlike a [[Closed-Cycle MHD System]], the working gas is not continuously recirculated through the same loop.
