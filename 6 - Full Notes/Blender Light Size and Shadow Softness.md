# Blender Light Size and Shadow Softness

The apparent size of a light source affects the softness of its shadows and specular highlights. Increasing the radius of a point light or the dimensions of an area light broadens the source, producing more gradual shadow boundaries.

A spot light adds cone size and edge-blend controls, while a sun uses angular diameter rather than physical scene distance. This means shadow character should be adjusted through the parameter appropriate to each light type rather than by applying one universal setting.
