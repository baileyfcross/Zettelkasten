# Magnetic Quantity Unit Conversion

Magnetic field strength, magnetic flux, and magnetic flux density have different unit expressions and separate tabs in Pryor's converter. Its examples convert oersted to A/m, maxwell to nanoweber, and gauss to millitesla. The maxwell example passes through webers and a nano- prefix; the gauss example passes through teslas and a milli- prefix. Each chain preserves a different physical quantity, so the three conversion factors should not be mixed simply because all are magnetic. See [[Algebraic SI Derived Units]].
