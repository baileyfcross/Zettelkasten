# Characteristic Zero

A ring has characteristic zero when no positive multiple of its multiplicative identity equals zero. The integers, rationals, and reals have characteristic zero.

For an integral domain, characteristic zero means the embedded copy of the integers does not collapse modulo a positive integer.
