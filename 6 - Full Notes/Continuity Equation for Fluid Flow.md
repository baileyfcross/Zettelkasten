# Continuity Equation for Fluid Flow

The continuity equation expresses conservation of mass in a flowing fluid: $\rho Av$ has the same value through every cross-section of a steady flow tube. For an incompressible fluid, density is constant and the relation becomes $A_1v_1=A_2v_2$. Fluid therefore moves faster through a narrower section.
