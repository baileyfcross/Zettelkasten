# Closure Theorem for Elimination

The closure theorem states that the variety of an elimination ideal is the smallest [[Affine Variety]] containing the projection of the original variety. It is therefore the [[Zariski Closure]] of that projected image.

When the original variety is nonempty, points outside a smaller exceptional subvariety can be lifted back to the original solution set. The theorem connects symbolic variable elimination with geometric projection.
