# Articulatory Distance

Articulatory distance is the gap between a player's intended action and the physical action required to express it through an interface. A direct, well-learned mapping has a shorter articulatory distance than an indirect or arbitrary one.

Reducing this distance lowers [[Cognitive Load]] in the input side of the [[User-System Loop]]. Its feedback-side counterpart is [[Semantic Distance]], the effort needed to interpret the system's response.
