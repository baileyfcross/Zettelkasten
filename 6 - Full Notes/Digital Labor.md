# Digital Labor

Digital labor is human work performed through or for data-driven platforms, including producing behavioral data, labeling examples, moderating content, and completing small online tasks. It often remains hidden behind descriptions of an AI system as autonomous.

Users can also supply economically valuable traces through ordinary participation without understanding their role in production. Ethical analysis asks who performs this work, who owns its products, how workers are compensated, and which risks are transferred to people with little bargaining power. [[Hidden Labor of AI]] is the portion most likely to disappear from the public story of automation.
