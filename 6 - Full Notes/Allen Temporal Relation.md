# Allen Temporal Relation

An Allen temporal relation describes the qualitative arrangement of two intervals. Relations include before, meets, overlaps, contains, starts, finishes, equals, and their inverses.

The vocabulary preserves more structure than merely sorting events by start time. For example, a treatment that overlaps an abnormal laboratory state differs from one that begins after it ends. A [[Time-Interval Clinical Pattern]] can be represented by the relations among all included intervals, enabling algorithms to compare longitudinal records without requiring identical durations.
