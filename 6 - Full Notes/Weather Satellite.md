# Weather Satellite

A weather satellite observes clouds, storms, atmospheric conditions, and related Earth systems from orbit. Polar spacecraft can eventually view the entire globe, while geostationary spacecraft repeatedly monitor the same hemisphere.

The first experimental systems had to prove that cloud imagery could improve meteorology. They quickly increased warning time for hurricanes over open oceans and revealed large weather structures unavailable to ground observers. Military operators also valued cloud-cover predictions for planning reconnaissance, so civilian forecasting and national-security requirements developed through related technology.
