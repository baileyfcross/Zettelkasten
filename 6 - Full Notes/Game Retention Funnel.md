# Game Retention Funnel

A game retention funnel counts how many players reach successive steps in an experience. Large drops between adjacent steps identify where progression, comprehension, technical performance, or perceived value may be preventing continued play.

The funnel locates a problem but does not explain its cause. Designers combine the drop-off with playtests, cohort comparisons, and qualitative evidence before changing the game, because optimizing a measured step can harm the broader experience if the metric is treated as the goal.
