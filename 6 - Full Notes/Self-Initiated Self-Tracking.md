# Self-Initiated Self-Tracking

Self-initiated self-tracking begins with a person's own question or purpose. The person chooses what to record, how to record it, and what would count as useful learning. This differs from [[Employer-Imposed Tracking]], where another institution defines participation and may control the consequences.

Initiative does not make the practice independent of outside influence. Devices offer preselected measures, communities suggest methods, and cultural ideals shape which goals appear desirable. A self-initiated project preserves more room to revise the question, abandon an unhelpful measure, or decide that tracking is not worth its [[Tracking Burden]].
