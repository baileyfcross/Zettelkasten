# Orthogonal Basis

An orthogonal basis consists of nonzero basis vectors whose pairwise [[Inner Product|inner products]] are zero. Orthogonality guarantees linear independence and lets each coordinate be computed independently by projection.

Dividing every basis vector by its norm produces an [[Orthonormal Basis]]. For an orthogonal basis $(v_i)$, the coefficient of $v_i$ in $v$ is $\langle v,v_i\rangle/\langle v_i,v_i\rangle$.
