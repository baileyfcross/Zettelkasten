# AWS Artifact

AWS Artifact is a self-service portal for accessing AWS compliance reports and managing certain agreements. It provides documents such as independent audit reports and certifications that customers may need for assurance, risk assessment, or evidence collection.

An AWS report describes controls and scope on the provider side; it does not automatically make a customer's workload compliant. The customer must map evidence to its own requirements and demonstrate that its configurations, processes, and use of AWS satisfy the remaining responsibilities.
