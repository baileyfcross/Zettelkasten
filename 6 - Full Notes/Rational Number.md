# Rational Number

A rational number is a number expressible as a quotient of two integers,

$$\frac{a}{b},\qquad b\neq0.$$

Rational numbers include integers and positive or negative [[Fraction]]s. They are closed under addition, subtraction, and multiplication, and under division by a nonzero rational number. In positional decimal notation, every rational number has an expansion that either terminates or eventually repeats. Conversely, a terminating or [[Repeating Decimal]] represents a rational number.
