# CSS Main-Axis Distribution

The `justify-content` property distributes flex items along a flex line's main axis after flexible lengths and automatic margins have been resolved. Items can collect at the start, end, or center, or share remaining space between or around them.

The property controls leftover space rather than calculating each item's flexible base size. Its physical direction follows [[CSS Flex Direction]] and the writing mode.
