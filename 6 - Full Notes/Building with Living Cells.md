# Building with Living Cells

Building with living cells treats cells as functional construction materials rather than only as objects of biological study. A fabricated system must support their survival, organization, and activity after its initial shape has been made.

Unlike passive components, cells can grow, communicate, adapt, and remodel their surroundings, so manufacturing and later operation cannot be cleanly separated.
