# Locally Complete Intersection Ideal

An ideal is locally a complete intersection of height s when its localization at every prime containing it is generated by a [[Regular Sequence]] of length s.

The condition allows different local generating sequences while requiring complete-intersection behavior at every relevant point. It is a local version of a [[Complete Intersection Ideal]].
