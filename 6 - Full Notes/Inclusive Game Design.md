# Inclusive Game Design

Inclusive game design broadens participation by removing unnecessary barriers while preserving a coherent identity and purpose. It differs from trying to make one game universal through an unrelated mixture of features.

Designers should research the chosen audience, question binary demographic assumptions, and avoid exclusionary material that does not serve the core experience. Inclusion is a disciplined fit between people and design, not a promise that everyone will like the same game.
