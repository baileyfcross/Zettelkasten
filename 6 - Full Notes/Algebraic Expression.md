# Algebraic Expression

An algebraic expression is a structured combination of numbers, variables, operation symbols, and grouping symbols. Terms are separated at the outermost level by plus or minus signs; for example, (18a+3b-5) has three [[Algebraic Term]]s, while (18ab^2c) has one.

An expression does not assert equality by itself. It represents a quantity that can be simplified, expanded, factored, or evaluated. These transformations must preserve its value for every permitted assignment to its [[Variable]]s. Its visible form can change while the represented quantity remains equivalent.
