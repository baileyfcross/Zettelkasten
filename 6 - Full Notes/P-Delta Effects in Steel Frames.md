# P-Delta Effects in Steel Frames

P-Delta effects are additional moments created when axial compression acts through a displaced geometry. Large-scale P-Delta response comes from gravity load acting through story drift, while member-level P-delta response comes from axial force acting through curvature between a member's ends. Both increase demand beyond a first-order analysis based on the undeformed structure.

The effect grows with axial load, flexibility, unbraced length, and frame drift. It may be captured by a second-order analysis or by specification-approved amplification of first-order moments when the required conditions are satisfied. Ignoring it can overstate stiffness and understate beam-column demand. Stability design must also include geometric imperfections and stiffness reductions so the analysis does not depend on an unrealistically perfect frame.
