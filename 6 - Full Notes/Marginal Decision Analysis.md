# Marginal Decision Analysis

Marginal decision analysis compares the cost and benefit of the next unit rather than the average across all units. An attractive average price can conceal that an additional bundle is dominated by combining smaller, cheaper units.

For game economies, the marginal cost of each additional unit of virtual currency should be inspected across bundle sizes. A large bundle whose next units cost more than units in smaller bundles creates a dominated purchase even if its total quantity is highest.
