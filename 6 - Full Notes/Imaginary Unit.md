# Imaginary Unit

The imaginary unit $i$ is defined by $i^2=-1$. Its powers repeat in a four-step cycle: $i, -1, -i, 1$.

It supplies the second coordinate of a [[Complex Number]]. A number $a+bi$ is real when its coefficient of $i$ is zero and purely imaginary when its real part is zero.
