# Thermal Equilibrium

Two systems are in thermal equilibrium when no net heat transfer occurs between them. They then have the same temperature, even if their materials or stored internal energies differ. The zeroth law makes this relation transitive and permits a thermometer in equilibrium with a system to represent its temperature.
