# Fan-In Incident Synthesis

Fan-in incident synthesis collects completed specialist results and passes them to a coordinating agent or deterministic function. The coordinator receives the incident identity plus structured deployment, health, and runbook analyses rather than the entire raw evidence set again.

Its purpose is controlled reconciliation: combine supported findings into an executive summary, likely cause, immediate action, rollback recommendation, validation plan, and stakeholder message. The synthesis is still untrusted until required fields and policy constraints are validated.
