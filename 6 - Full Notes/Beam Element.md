# Beam Element

A beam element connects two end nodes and represents flexural behavior along its length. In the primer's planar formulation, each node carries transverse displacement and slope.

The interpolation must represent both displacement and its derivative at the endpoints. The resulting element matrix relates nodal forces and moments to those displacement and rotation degrees of freedom.
