# Engine Replacement and Center-of-Gravity Shift

A replacement engine changes both mass and its arm from the aircraft datum. In the Wilga conversion, the longer engine moved its mass forward of the firewall and increased the bending moment even though the engine itself was lighter. Additional support struts, fuel placement, and the final [[Aircraft Center-of-Gravity Calculation]] were needed to reconcile power with structural and balance limits.
