# Crew Resource Management and PACE

Crew resource management makes expertise and concern usable across cockpit authority gradients. PACE escalates communication through Probe, Alert, Challenge, and Emergency when a safety issue is not resolved. The sequence preserves a clear pilot-in-command role while preventing deference or vague hints from silencing information needed for a safe decision.
