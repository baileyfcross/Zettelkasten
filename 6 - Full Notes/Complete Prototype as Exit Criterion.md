# Complete Prototype as Exit Criterion

A complete prototype is an exit criterion because a design cannot be validated solely from isolated mechanics or documents. The team needs a playable representation of the whole experience to observe how its parts interact across the full arc.

The artifact may still use provisional assets, but it must include every major system and transition whose relationship could invalidate the design during production.
