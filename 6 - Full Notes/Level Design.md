# Level Design

Level design arranges space, challenges, events, objects, resources, atmosphere, and pacing to produce a particular segment of player experience. It turns general game rules and world content into moment-to-moment play.

The work is cross-disciplinary. Layout must coordinate with story, art, audio, programming, core mechanics, and the actions the player has already learned.
