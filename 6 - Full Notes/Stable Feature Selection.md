# Stable Feature Selection

Stable feature selection seeks subsets that remain similar when the training data are perturbed. Stability matters when selected variables are interpreted scientifically or used to guide costly follow-up work.

Several correlated features may be predictively interchangeable, causing unstable choices even when accuracy is consistent. Stability measures and ensemble selection reveal this uncertainty rather than treating one fitted subset as uniquely correct.
