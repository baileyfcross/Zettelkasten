# TVP Evolution

TVP evolution is the evidence-led transition from a [[Thinnest Viable Platform]] to a broader platform product. A capability that was good enough for early users may become inadequate as adoption, scale, regulation, or critical user journeys change.

The team revisits the original hypothesis and journeys to decide which features to expand, replace, or retire. Adoption, support burden, reliability, and user feedback guide the next increment. This preserves the TVP’s learning advantage without turning its initial omissions and shortcuts into permanent architecture.
