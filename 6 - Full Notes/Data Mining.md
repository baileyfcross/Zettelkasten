# Data Mining

Data mining applies algorithms from statistics, computer science, and machine learning to extract useful patterns from large data collections. Outputs can include classifications, clusters, associations, sequences, correlations, trends, and predictive models.

The algorithmic search is one stage within [[Knowledge Discovery in Databases]]. A pattern becomes useful knowledge only after it is checked for relevance and validity and connected to the goals and practices of its domain.
