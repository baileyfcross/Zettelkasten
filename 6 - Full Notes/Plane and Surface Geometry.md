# Plane and Surface Geometry

A plane is a flat two-dimensional set extending through three-dimensional space, while a surface is a two-dimensional boundary that may curve. Planes often provide construction frames or idealized domains; surfaces enclose solids, define interfaces, and carry boundary conditions.

Three non-collinear points are sufficient to define a plane. A usable surface model additionally requires consistent connectivity and orientation so that the analysis tool can distinguish adjoining regions and, when appropriate, infer an enclosed volume.
