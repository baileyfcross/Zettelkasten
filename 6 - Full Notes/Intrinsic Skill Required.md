# Intrinsic Skill Required

Intrinsic skill required is the amount of ability needed to overcome a challenge when time pressure is removed. It isolates the precision, reasoning, memory, or coordination demand from the stress of having to act quickly.

Separating skill from time pressure helps tune difficulty. A task can require sophisticated execution but little stress, or simple execution under severe stress.
