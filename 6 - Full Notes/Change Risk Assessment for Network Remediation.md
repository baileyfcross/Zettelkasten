# Change Risk Assessment for Network Remediation

Change risk assessment evaluates a proposed network remediation before execution. It identifies affected devices and services, prerequisites, blast radius, confidence, maintenance requirements, success checks, and a feasible rollback path.

The assessment should combine policy and topology evidence rather than relying on a model's verbal confidence. High-impact or weakly reversible changes demand stronger approval and testing. Even a low-risk recommendation remains bounded by the identity and permissions of the tool that would carry it out.
