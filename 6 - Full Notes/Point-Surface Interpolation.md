# Point-Surface Interpolation

Point-surface interpolation estimates a continuous [[Spatial Field]] from measurements at discrete [[Spatial Point Data|points]]. The resulting raster can reveal broad spatial pattern and supply predicted values between stations, but its appearance depends on the interpolation model, sampling arrangement, and output grid. The source demonstrates [[Inverse Distance Weighting]] while noting that selecting and validating an appropriate interpolation method is a separate analytical problem.
