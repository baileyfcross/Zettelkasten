# Blender Mesh Rip Tools

Rip Region separates selected vertices or edges from one side of their surrounding faces while retaining geometry on the other side. Rip Edge provides a related split focused on an edge-based selection.

Ripping opens a seam without deleting the selected surface outright. The side chosen by cursor movement matters, so the artist should inspect neighboring faces and normals after the operation before continuing topology edits.
