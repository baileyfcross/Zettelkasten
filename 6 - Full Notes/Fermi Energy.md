# Fermi Energy

Fermi energy is the highest occupied energy of a solid's free electrons at absolute zero. These electrons do not behave like an ordinary classical gas: only a limited number can occupy each quantum state. At a higher temperature, some electrons near the Fermi level enter higher available states. This energy distribution helps explain electronic transport and [[Thermionic Emission]].
