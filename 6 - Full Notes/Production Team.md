# Production Team

A production team creates recurring game content through a comparatively stable workflow after mechanics, tools, and budgets have matured. Visual flow, small batches, and measured cycle time help it deliver assets predictably.

The team differs from an exploratory feature team because much of its uncertainty lies in flow and volume rather than discovering a new mechanic. Production evidence should still feed back into design and scope.
