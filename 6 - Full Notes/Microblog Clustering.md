# Microblog Clustering

Microblog clustering groups brief, rapidly arriving posts using textual, temporal, social, and sometimes location signals. Individual messages are sparse and informal, while the underlying events and topics may change quickly.

Aggregating related terms or users can strengthen weak content evidence, and fading weights can track emerging conversations. Spam, repeated messages, and popular background topics must be controlled so they do not dominate the clusters.
