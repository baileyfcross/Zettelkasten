# AR Label Placement

AR label placement chooses a readable image position for a label while preserving its connection to a registered object. Candidate layouts must avoid overlap with other labels, important scene content, and display boundaries.

Leader lines can retain the object relationship when the label cannot sit directly on its target. Optimization and image-guided placement balance legibility, distance, interference, and temporal coherence as the view changes.
