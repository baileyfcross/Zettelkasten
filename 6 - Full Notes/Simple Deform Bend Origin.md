# Simple Deform Bend Origin

The Bend mode of Blender's Simple Deform modifier curves a sufficiently subdivided mesh around an axis. An Empty can serve as the modifier origin, making the bend center and orientation controllable independently of the object's own origin.

The mesh needs enough segments to describe the curve; otherwise the result folds in coarse steps. Moving or rotating the origin controller provides a nondestructive way to explore where and how the bend occurs.
