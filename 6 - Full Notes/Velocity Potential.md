# Velocity Potential

A velocity potential is a scalar field whose spatial gradient gives the velocity components of an irrotational flow. Solving for one nodal scalar can therefore replace direct solution of multiple velocity components.

In an incompressible source-free region, the potential satisfies Laplace's equation. Finite element differentiation of the interpolated potential recovers element velocities after the nodal values are known.
