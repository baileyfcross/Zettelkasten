# Standing Wave

A standing wave forms when similar waves traveling in opposite directions superpose. Fixed nodes have zero amplitude, while antinodes have maximum amplitude, and adjacent nodes are separated by half a wavelength. The pattern transfers no net energy along the medium and appears only at frequencies compatible with the boundary conditions.
