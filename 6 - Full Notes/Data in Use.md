# Data in Use

Data in use is active information being read, changed, calculated, or otherwise processed by a user, application, or system. It may be protected during storage and transmission yet become exposed when an authorized process makes it usable.

Authorization, process isolation, monitoring, endpoint security, and careful handling of temporary copies help protect this state. [[Data Loss Prevention]] must include active use rather than treating encryption at rest as complete protection.
