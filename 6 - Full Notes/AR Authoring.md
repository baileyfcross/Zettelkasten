# AR Authoring

AR authoring defines the content, semantics, behavior, and interaction of an augmented-reality experience. It goes beyond producing individual models by arranging actors, stages, story, interactions, and setup into an application the user can experience.

An authoring system should expose real-world relationships without requiring every creator to implement tracking and rendering infrastructure. Hardware abstraction and reusable workflows keep content decisions separate from changing device details.
