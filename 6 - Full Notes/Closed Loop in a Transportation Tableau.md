# Closed Loop in a Transportation Tableau

A closed loop begins at an empty transportation cell, moves horizontally and vertically through occupied cells, and returns to the start. Direction changes occur only at occupied corner cells, no cell is repeated, and each involved row or column contributes paired corners.

Alternating plus and minus signs around the loop describes a shipment adjustment that preserves all supply and demand totals. Such loops support transportation improvement tests and determine how an entering cell can replace a leaving basic cell.
