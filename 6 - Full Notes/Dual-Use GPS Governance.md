# Dual-Use GPS Governance

Dual-use GPS governance balances a military need for protected, high-integrity positioning with civilian dependence on an open global utility. Operational responsibility remained with the Air Force, while national oversight evolved toward a civil-military executive committee with Defense and Transportation leadership. Decisions about degradation, signal access, funding, and interference cannot be purely technical because denying an adversary may also disrupt aviation, communications, commerce, and foreign users.
