# Blender Sculpt Brushes

Sculpt tools act through a brush cursor that conforms visually to the mesh surface. Dragging over the object applies the active tool, and a pressure-sensitive tablet can provide more natural input than a mouse.

Different sculpt tools add, subtract, smooth, flatten, inflate, grab, rotate, or otherwise reshape geometry. Symmetry can mirror a stroke across an object's local axis so both sides develop together.
