# Cabin Altitude and Pressure Differential

Cabin altitude expresses interior pressure as the altitude at which that pressure would occur in the standard atmosphere. As the aircraft climbs, outside pressure falls and the difference between cabin and ambient pressure grows. The airframe's maximum approved differential therefore limits the combination of aircraft altitude and lowest maintainable cabin altitude.
