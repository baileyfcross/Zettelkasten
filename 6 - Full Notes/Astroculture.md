# Astroculture

Astroculture is the heterogeneous collection of images, artifacts, media, institutions, stories, and practices through which societies give outer space meaning. It includes real missions and technologies but also astronomy, science fiction, spirituality, alien-life speculation, advocacy, and popular heroes.

Astroculture helps make expensive technical programs imaginable and publicly significant. It is not a single coherent ideology: national prestige, human expansion, scientific humility, environmental concern, and commercial entertainment can all use the same space image differently. The plural forms that developed across countries are connected through globally circulating media and shared mission events.
