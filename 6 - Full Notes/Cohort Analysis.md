# Cohort Analysis

Cohort analysis groups players by a shared starting period or other relevant condition and compares their behavior over time. It reveals whether changes in progression, session length, purchasing, or retention differ between groups.

Aggregated cohorts are usually more informative than isolated individual behavior. The grouping and measurements must match a real design question so that analytics remain interpretable and actionable.
