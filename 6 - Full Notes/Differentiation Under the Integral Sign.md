# Differentiation Under the Integral Sign

Differentiation under the integral sign exchanges a derivative with an integral over another variable. For fixed limits and a sufficiently regular integrand $f(x,t)$, the derivative with respect to $x$ can be moved inside as the integral of $\partial f/\partial x$.

The method treats the integration variable as fixed during partial differentiation and can evaluate parameter-dependent integrals that are difficult to integrate directly. Its validity depends on the interchange being justified; the elementary rule does not automatically cover singular integrands or moving limits.
