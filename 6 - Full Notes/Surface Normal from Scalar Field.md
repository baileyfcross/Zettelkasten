# Surface Normal from Scalar Field

A surface written implicitly as $f(x,y,z)=k$ has the gradient $\nabla f$ as a normal direction wherever the gradient is nonzero. Any tangent displacement stays on the same [[Level Surface]], so its dot product with the gradient is zero.

A unit normal is obtained by dividing the gradient by its magnitude. The two signs give opposite orientations, and the physical problem determines which is outward or otherwise preferred.
