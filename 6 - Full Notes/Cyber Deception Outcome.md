# Cyber Deception Outcome

A cyber deception outcome occurs when an attacker causes users or systems to accept false information, identities, or states as genuine. The harm is not limited to secret theft or interrupted service; corrupted trust can redirect decisions while systems appear available.

Authentication, integrity verification, provenance evidence, and independent confirmation help resist this outcome. Framing deception explicitly extends [[Outcome-Based Cyber Defense]] beyond confidentiality and availability alone.
