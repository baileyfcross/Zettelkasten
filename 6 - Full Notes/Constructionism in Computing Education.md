# Constructionism in Computing Education

Constructionism holds that learners develop knowledge by making and acting within meaningful worlds of practice. In computing education, students construct programs and models, encounter consequences, and refine their understanding through activity.

Seymour Papert connected this approach to Logo and self-directed projects. The teacher facilitates exploration rather than treating computational knowledge as a list of facts to transmit.
