# International Space Station

The International Space Station is a modular low-Earth-orbit laboratory assembled through U.S., Russian, European, Japanese, and Canadian contributions. It emerged when the troubled U.S. Freedom project merged with planned Russian capability after the Soviet collapse.

Continuous occupation began in 2000. The station has supported long-duration physiology, medicine, materials work, Earth observation, and other experiments while demonstrating that separately built national modules and systems can operate together. Its technical and diplomatic achievements coexist with immense cost and limited scientific return relative to a more narrowly designed laboratory.
