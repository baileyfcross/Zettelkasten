# Operational AI Bias Risk

Operational AI bias risk arises when training, tuning, sampling, or current context makes one cause or action appear more likely than the evidence supports. An agent repeatedly exposed to a familiar explanation can leap to it during a new incident and recommend an unnecessarily destructive response.

Balanced data, accuracy evaluation, explicit policy, preserved evidence, and human approval help expose the bias. Sanitization and sampling also need review because removing sensitive data can unintentionally remove the counterexamples that would have corrected a skewed conclusion.
