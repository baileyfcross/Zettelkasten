# EHR Data Quality Challenge

An EHR data quality challenge arises when records created for patient care and administration are reused as an analytic dataset. Missing values, inconsistent units, duplicate entries, changing codes, and uneven documentation can all distort the apparent patient history.

The absence of a record is especially ambiguous: it may mean that an event did not occur, was measured elsewhere, was omitted, or was stored in narrative form. Analysts must profile completeness by variable and setting, trace fields to their workflow, and test how alternative interpretations affect an [[Electronic Phenotyping Algorithm]] or predictive model.
