# Legendre Transformation

A Legendre transformation replaces dependence on a variable with dependence on its conjugate slope. It constructs a new function by subtracting or adding the product of the original variable and the derivative conjugate to it, according to convention.

In mechanics, transforming the Lagrangian's velocity dependence into momentum dependence produces the Hamiltonian. The operation preserves the underlying dynamics while changing which variables are treated as independent.
