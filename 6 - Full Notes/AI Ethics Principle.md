# AI Ethics Principle

An AI ethics principle is a high-level value used to guide the development and use of intelligent systems. Common proposals emphasize human dignity, autonomy, beneficence, prevention of harm, justice, privacy, safety, accountability, transparency, and sustainability.

Principles give diverse actors a shared vocabulary, but agreement on words can conceal disagreement about meaning and implementation. For example, fairness may require equal treatment or correction of prior inequality. A principle becomes operational only when it is connected to methods, responsibilities, evidence, and consequences in a particular context. [[Ethics by Design]] and regulation are two different routes from aspiration to practice.
