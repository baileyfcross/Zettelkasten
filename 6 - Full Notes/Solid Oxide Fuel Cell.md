# Solid Oxide Fuel Cell

A solid oxide fuel cell uses a ceramic oxide-ion conducting [[Fuel Cell Electrolyte]] and porous metal-ceramic electrodes. Oxygen-side ions travel through the ceramic and react with hydrogen or carbon monoxide at the fuel electrode. The source describes high-temperature operation and use in industrial or cogeneration plants, where usable exhaust heat can complement the electrical output. It contrasts with low-temperature polymer-membrane cells.
