# Airborne Ultrasound Tactile Display

An airborne ultrasound tactile display focuses and spatially modulates acoustic radiation pressure so a person feels contact-like pressure without touching hardware. An array of emitters can place pressure patterns in free space and allow a hand to explore a virtual shape.

The method avoids a worn or surface-mounted actuator and has been investigated for touchless controls. The resulting sensation is gentle pressure rather than the full force and texture range of a grounded [[Haptic Display]]. Its usable resolution and strength depend on array geometry, focusing, and hand position.
