# Cauchy Integral Theorem

Cauchy integral theorem states that the contour integral of an analytic function around a closed curve is zero when the function is analytic throughout the enclosed region and the domain satisfies the required conditions.

The theorem permits continuous deformation of integration paths without changing the result, provided no singularity is crossed. It is the foundation for Cauchy's integral formula and for evaluating contours by isolating the singularities that obstruct such deformations.
