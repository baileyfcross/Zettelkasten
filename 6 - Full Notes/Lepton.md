# Lepton

A lepton is an elementary particle that does not participate in the strong interaction. The charged leptons are the electron, muon, and tau, each paired with a neutral neutrino; every lepton has a corresponding antiparticle. Lepton-family numbers and electric charge constrain allowed particle transformations.
