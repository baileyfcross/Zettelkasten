# Triple Integral

A triple integral accumulates a scalar function throughout a volume. Written as an iterated integral, its limits encode the boundaries of the three-dimensional region and its integrand can describe density, charge, probability, or another field.

Coordinate systems should match the symmetry of the domain. Cylindrical and spherical coordinates introduce volume-element Jacobians, such as $r$ in cylindrical coordinates and $r^2\sin\theta$ in spherical coordinates, so that transformed coordinate cells represent the correct physical volume.

The order of integration determines which projections and bounding surfaces must be described first. Changing that order can simplify the limits without changing the accumulated volume when the integrability conditions hold.
