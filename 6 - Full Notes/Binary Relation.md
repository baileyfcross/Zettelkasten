# Binary Relation

A binary relation from A to B is a [[Subset]] of the [[Cartesian Product]] A × B. Writing a R b means that the ordered pair (a, b) belongs to the relation R.

A relation on one set can be reflexive, symmetric, antisymmetric, or transitive. Particular combinations define partial orders and equivalence relations, while [[Transitive Closure]] extends a relation to capture reachability through one or more steps.
