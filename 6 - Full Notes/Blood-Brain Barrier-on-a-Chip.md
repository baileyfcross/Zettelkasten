# Blood-Brain Barrier-on-a-Chip

A blood-brain barrier-on-a-chip recreates the interface that controls movement from circulating blood into brain tissue. Endothelial cells line a vessel-like channel while pericytes, astrocytes, or other neural support cells surround it.

The model can investigate barrier dysfunction and whether a therapeutic molecule can cross without relying only on static membrane assays.
