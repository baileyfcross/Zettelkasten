# QAOA Speedup Evidence Boundary

QAOA has provable or analytically tractable behavior on selected problems, and special parameter schedules can recover square-root query complexity for unstructured search. Low-depth circuits can also be hard to sample classically under standard complexity assumptions.

These facts do not establish a general practical speedup for combinatorial optimization. Approximation improvements can inspire better classical algorithms, parameter setting remains difficult, and hardware depth, noise, compilation, and classical optimization belong in the comparison. The lecture therefore presents QAOA as a NISQ heuristic to evaluate rather than a proven universal optimizer.
