# Gravitational Time Dilation

Gravitational time dilation means that clocks deeper in a gravitational field run more slowly than clocks at higher gravitational potential. The effect follows from curved spacetime and is distinct from motion-based [[Time Dilation]]. Satellite navigation must correct for both effects to preserve timing and position accuracy.

In a weak field, the frequency shift can be approximated from the change in gravitational potential. A photon climbing away from a massive body is observed at lower frequency and longer wavelength, while an exact strong-field calculation uses the spacetime metric rather than treating the photon as losing classical mechanical energy.
