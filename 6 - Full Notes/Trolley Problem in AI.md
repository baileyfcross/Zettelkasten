# Trolley Problem in AI

The trolley problem in AI applies a forced-choice moral dilemma to an autonomous system, often asking how a self-driving vehicle should distribute unavoidable harm. It is useful for revealing conflicts among principles and for asking who is authorized to encode a choice.

Its artificial simplicity can misrepresent real engineering. Actual safety decisions involve uncertain perception, probabilities, design trade-offs, maintenance, law, and opportunities to prevent the dangerous situation before a final collision choice exists. Ethical design should therefore not reduce [[Functional Morality]] to selecting a victim in a stylized scenario.
