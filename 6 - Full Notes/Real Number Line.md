# Real Number Line

The real number line identifies every [[Real Number]] with a unique point on an infinite directed line. Values increase to the right and decrease to the left, so geometric position represents numerical order. [[Rational Number]]s occupy fractional positions, while [[Irrational Number]]s fill the line without being expressible as integer quotients.
