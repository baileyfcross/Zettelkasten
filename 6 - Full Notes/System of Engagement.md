# System of Engagement

A system of engagement provides channels through which an organization interacts with employees, customers, partners, or other external parties. Web interfaces, mobile applications, APIs, bots, communications services, and notification systems can all serve this role. Engagement components emphasize reach and experience, but they usually depend on systems of record for authoritative data and systems of intelligence for personalized or predictive behavior.
