# Eccentrically Loaded Weld Groups

An eccentric load on a weld group produces direct stress plus moment-induced stress about the group's centroid. In an elastic method, direct force is distributed over total effective throat area and torsional shear varies with distance from the centroid according to the weld group's polar property. Vector combination identifies the critical location along the weld.

An instantaneous-center approach can estimate ultimate strength when weld elements deform nonlinearly and redistribute load. Some groups also experience shear combined with tension normal to the joint. Shape matters: spreading weld farther from the centroid increases moment resistance, while a balanced layout reduces peak demand. The connected base metal, weld access, start-stop locations, and restraint-induced stress remain necessary checks beyond the weld-group calculation.
