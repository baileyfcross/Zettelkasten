# Beamforming

Beamforming combines signals from multiple microphones to emphasize sound arriving from a chosen direction and suppress competing directions. Differences in arrival time and level across the array provide the spatial evidence.

For [[Far-Field Speech Recognition]], beamforming can improve the target speaker's signal before feature extraction. In a [[Social Robot]], the estimated direction can also guide orientation and attention behavior.
