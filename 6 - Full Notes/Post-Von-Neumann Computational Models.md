# Post-Von-Neumann Computational Models

Post-von-Neumann computational models seek alternatives to a processor repeatedly fetching instructions from separate memory. Quantum, neural, reversible, DNA, and memristive systems exploit different physical processes and favor different classes of problems.

Each architecture demands its own computational thinking. A representation natural for one machine may be awkward or meaningless for another, so architecture selection becomes part of problem formulation.
