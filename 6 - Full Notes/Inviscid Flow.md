# Inviscid Flow

Inviscid flow neglects viscosity and the associated frictional shear effects. It is an idealization rather than a property of a real fluid, but it can describe regimes where viscous effects are not the focus.

Combined with incompressibility and irrotationality, the assumption leads to potential flow. Boundaries are then enforced through normal-velocity or potential conditions rather than no-slip viscous behavior.
