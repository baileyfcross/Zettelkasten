# Data Warehouse

A data warehouse is a subject-oriented, integrated, time-varying, and generally non-updatable collection designed to support management decisions and business intelligence. It consolidates data from otherwise separate operational sources.

A common, high-quality repository gives data mining a consistent view of organizational history. It does not remove the need to understand provenance, definitions, or changes in source systems.
