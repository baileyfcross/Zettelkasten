# Continuity at a Point

A function is continuous at $x=a$ when $f(a)$ is defined, $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)$ exists, and that limit equals $f(a)$. Equivalently, the left and right limits both meet the function value. Failure of any condition creates a removable, jump, or infinite discontinuity.
