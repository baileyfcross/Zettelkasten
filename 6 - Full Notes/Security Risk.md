# Security Risk

Security risk expresses the likelihood and consequence of a security breach in relation to a threat and the system's vulnerability to it. The book presents a simple quantitative view in which risk grows as either threat level or vulnerability level grows.

A risk assessment helps prioritize controls rather than treating every possible event as equally urgent. The assessment must follow the data, users, systems, and providers across the complete cloud service boundary.
