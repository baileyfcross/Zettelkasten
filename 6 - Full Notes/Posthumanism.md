# Posthumanism

Posthumanism questions the assumption that human beings are the central measure of intelligence, agency, and moral value. In AI ethics, it resists judging every nonhuman system only by how closely it imitates human thought or behavior.

This perspective can make room for unfamiliar forms of agency and for the interests of animals, ecosystems, and other nonhumans. It also emphasizes that people are already constituted through relationships with technologies. However, dissolving a sharp human-machine boundary must not obscure domination or exploitation. Power still determines whose interests are represented when a system is designed and whose lives bear its costs.
