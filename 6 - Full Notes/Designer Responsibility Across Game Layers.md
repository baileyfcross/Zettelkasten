# Designer Responsibility Across Game Layers

A game designer is responsible not only for the material deliberately placed into a game but also for reasonably foreseeable behavior and cultural effects produced by its systems. [[Dynamic Mechanics]] can reward conduct that conflicts with stated intent, and community-scale consequences can influence players and public attitudes.

This does not mean a designer controls every interpretation. It means that surprise does not erase causation. Respect for players requires testing incentives, considering how compelling systems affect time and money, and treating pro-social or harmful outcomes as design evidence rather than external accidents.
