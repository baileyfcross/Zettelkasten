# Cultured Meat Hygiene

Cultured meat hygiene controls microbial contamination, chemical residues, handling, storage, and processing throughout food production. A closed manufacturing environment can avoid some farm-associated pollutants and pathogens but introduces bioreactor and culture-media risks.

Commercial protocols need validated sanitation, monitoring, traceability, temperature control, and food-safe final processing rather than relying on laboratory asepsis alone.
