# Co-Located Collaboration

Co-located collaboration occurs when participants share the same physical environment while working with related augmented content. Face-to-face speech, gesture, body orientation, and physical objects remain available alongside the digital channel.

The AR system should preserve these natural cues rather than isolating each person behind a private interface. Tracking and shared coordinates allow virtual objects to occupy the common workspace consistently for all participants.
