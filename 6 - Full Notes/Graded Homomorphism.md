# Graded Homomorphism

A graded homomorphism of degree d sends the degree-n component of a graded ring or module into the degree-(n+d) component of the target. A degree-zero map preserves degree exactly.

Graded isomorphisms require a homogeneous inverse. Degree shifts convert a degree-d map into a degree-zero map, which simplifies graded resolutions and comparisons.
