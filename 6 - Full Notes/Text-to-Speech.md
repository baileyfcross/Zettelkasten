# Text-to-Speech

Text-to-speech converts written text into an acoustic speech signal. A synthesis pipeline normalizes text, derives pronunciations, predicts prosody, and generates the waveform played to the user.

TTS is the output counterpart to [[Automatic Speech Recognition]] in a voice assistant. Its naturalness depends on intelligible segmental sounds as well as appropriate [[Intonation]], rhythm, and emphasis.
