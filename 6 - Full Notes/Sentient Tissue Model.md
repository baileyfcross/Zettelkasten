# Sentient Tissue Model

A sentient tissue model is a hypothetical or future construct complex enough to have subjective experiences such as pain. A sensory neuron can detect a damaging stimulus without proving conscious experience, while integration with sufficiently complex neural processing raises a harder question.

Researchers can reduce uncertainty by limiting unnecessary neural complexity and defining review thresholds before such capabilities are demonstrated.
