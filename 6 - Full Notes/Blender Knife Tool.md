# Blender Knife Tool

The Knife tool draws arbitrary cuts across a mesh surface, adding vertices, edges, and faces where the cut crosses existing geometry. It is often a preparatory tool rather than the final modeling step.

For example, a cut can define the outline and cracks of a hole before the enclosed faces are extruded or deleted. The tool is valuable when the required topology does not follow an existing loop or a regular subdivision pattern.
