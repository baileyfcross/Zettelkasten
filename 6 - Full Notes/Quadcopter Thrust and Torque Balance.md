# Quadcopter Thrust and Torque Balance

A quadcopter uses four rotor thrust vectors mounted on a rigid frame. Opposite pairs rotate in opposing directions so their reaction torques can cancel in a steady hover, while total upward thrust balances weight. Changing selected rotor speeds breaks those balances to command the motions in [[Drone Roll, Pitch, and Yaw Control]].
