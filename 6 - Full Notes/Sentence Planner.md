# Sentence Planner

A sentence planner selects and organizes the information that a generated response should communicate. It can decide which facts matter, their order, and whether several facts should be combined or summarized.

The plan sits between the [[Dialog Manager]]'s symbolic action and surface realization. A [[Microplanner]] then chooses more detailed structures for expressing the selected content.
