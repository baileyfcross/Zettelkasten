# Green and Gold Open Access

Green open access makes an accepted or peer-reviewed manuscript available through self-archiving, such as an institutional or subject repository. Gold open access publishes the article directly in an openly accessible journal.

Both routes expand reading access, but reproducible communication also depends on reuse rights and machine-usable formats. A freely readable article may still restrict redistribution, text mining, or adaptation. [[Copyright in Research Articles]] and explicit licensing therefore remain relevant, while standardized full text can let the literature itself become a computational research resource.
