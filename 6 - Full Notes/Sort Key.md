# Sort Key

A sort key is the feature or combination of features used to compare [[Data Record|records]] and determine their order. The key must provide an ordering that the [[Sorting Algorithm]] can apply.

A key may be atomic, such as one identifier treated as a single value, or composite, such as sender followed by delivery date. Composite keys order tied values from an earlier field by consulting the next field.
