# Servant Leadership

Servant leadership treats the growth and effectiveness of people and the whole system as the leader's primary responsibility. It emphasizes listening, care, foresight, community, systems thinking, and removing conditions that prevent good work.

Serving does not mean avoiding standards. A servant leader expects capable people to pursue ambitious results, permits responsible mistakes, and creates the trust needed for honest learning.
