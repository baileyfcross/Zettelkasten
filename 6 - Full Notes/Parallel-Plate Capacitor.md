# Parallel-Plate Capacitor

An ideal parallel-plate capacitor consists of conducting plates of area $A$ separated by distance $d$. Ignoring edge effects, its capacitance is $C=\varepsilon A/d$, and the field between the plates is approximately uniform with $E=V/d$. Increasing plate area or permittivity raises capacitance; increasing separation lowers it.
