# Unique Selling Point

A unique selling point is a meaningful way a game is attractively distinct from alternatives already familiar to its audience. A concept commonly identifies a small set of these differences rather than claiming novelty in every feature.

The point must matter to the [[Target Audience]] and be visible in the proposed experience, not merely in internal technology. It helps explain why a player or stakeholder should choose this game in a [[Game Concept Document]] or [[Game Pitch]].
