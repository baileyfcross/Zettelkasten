# IBM 701

The IBM 701 was the company’s first large stored-program electronic computer, introduced primarily for scientific, aerospace, and defense customers. Modified cathode-ray tubes supplied high-speed memory.

IBM initially marketed it as a defense calculator, reflecting the government funding behind early demand. Its success helped move the dominant punched-card supplier into electronic computing.
