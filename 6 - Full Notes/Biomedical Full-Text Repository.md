# Biomedical Full-Text Repository

A biomedical full-text repository makes complete articles or reports searchable, extending retrieval beyond bibliographic records and abstracts. Methods, results, tables, and discussion sections can contain evidence that is not represented in metadata.

Full text increases both recall and noise. A query term may occur in a cited work, background statement, or negated result rather than in the article's own finding. Section structure and field-aware ranking help distinguish central content from incidental mentions, while access rights determine which documents can actually be delivered.
