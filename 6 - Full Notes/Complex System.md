# Complex System

A complex system contains interacting parts whose effects loop back and alter later system behavior. Unlike a [[Complicated Process]], it is not adequately described as a long linear sequence.

Feedback makes complex systems history-dependent, adaptive, and capable of [[Emergence]]. Their behavior is best understood by combining part-level detail with a view of loops, the whole, and the surrounding context through [[Systems Thinking]].
