# Tanning Engineered Tissue

Tanning engineered tissue stabilizes and finishes a collagen-rich biofabricated sheet using processes adapted from leather production. Salts, plant-derived compounds, chromium, aldehydes, oils, washing, coloring, and drying alter durability and feel.

Because the input lacks hair, flesh, and much of a natural hide's structure, the process can omit some steps but must compensate for lower thickness or collagen content.
