# Flowboard

A flowboard is a hybrid of a flowchart and storyboard used to document a game's structure. It shows gameplay modes, shell menus, and the conditions that move the player among them.

The diagram communicates experience flow more clearly than a list of screens. It also reveals unreachable modes, missing return paths, and transitions that ask the player to change controls or expectations without adequate explanation.
