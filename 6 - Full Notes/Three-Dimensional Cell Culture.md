# Three-Dimensional Cell Culture

Three-dimensional cell culture surrounds or organizes cells in a volume rather than restricting them to one flat layer. Cell-cell contact, matrix interaction, diffusion, and mechanical support can more closely resemble native tissue.

Thicker constructs are harder to image and feed, and passive diffusion creates a limit on how far oxygen and nutrients reach living cells.
