# SUSE Customer Center Entitlement

SUSE Customer Center associates an organization, subscriptions, registered systems, and the software products or extensions those subscriptions permit. An entitlement is therefore the service-side basis for receiving supported repositories and updates, not merely a local package setting.

Deployment planning should confirm that the correct registration code and product rights are available for the intended machine. When a repository is missing, diagnosis should distinguish entitlement and registration from local Zypper configuration: changing a local URL cannot grant a product the account does not possess.
