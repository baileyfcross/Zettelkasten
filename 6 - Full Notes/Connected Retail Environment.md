# Connected Retail Environment

A connected retail environment uses cameras, shelf sensors, RFID, beacons, geofencing, smartphones, smart displays, and analytics to link physical shopping with digital information and services. It can locate inventory, guide a shopper, change a price display, deliver a contextual offer, support augmented-reality preview, or automate checkout.

In an automated grocery store, entry authentication, cameras, and shelf-weight sensors associate selected goods with a shopper and charge an account when the person leaves. The convenience depends on extensive observation, making transparency, error correction, and limits on behavioral tracking central design issues rather than separate policy concerns.
