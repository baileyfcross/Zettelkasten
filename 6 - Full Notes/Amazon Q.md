# Amazon Q

Amazon Q is a generative-AI assistant family designed to help people work with organizational information, software development, and AWS tasks. Its responses can be grounded in connected enterprise sources or development context, depending on the product and configuration.

The assistant can accelerate explanation, search, and drafting, but generated output should be reviewed before it changes systems or informs important decisions. Access to underlying data must follow organizational permissions so conversational convenience does not bypass information boundaries.
