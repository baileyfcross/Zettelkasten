# Tr Character Translation

The `tr` utility translates, deletes, or squeezes characters read from standard input. Character sets can express case conversion, delimiter replacement, deletion of unwanted characters, or compression of repeated whitespace.

Tr operates on characters rather than structured fields or arbitrary multi-character strings. It therefore belongs early in simple normalization pipelines, while record-aware transformations require tools such as sed or awk.
