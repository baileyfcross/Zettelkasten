# Speech Recognition Grammar

A speech recognition grammar specifies the words and phrase patterns a recognizer should consider in a constrained interaction. Restricting the search space can improve accuracy when the next valid responses are predictable.

A [[Directed Prompt]] can establish such expectations for the user and system. Broad assistants need more open statistical models because their possible utterances cannot be enumerated compactly.
