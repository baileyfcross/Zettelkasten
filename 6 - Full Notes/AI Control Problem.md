# AI Control Problem

The AI control problem asks how humans could ensure that a highly capable autonomous system continues to pursue acceptable goals. The difficulty is that a formal objective may not include every value, exception, or context its designers intended. A system that optimizes the objective effectively may therefore create destructive side effects without disobeying its instructions.

The problem is often discussed in relation to [[Superintelligence]], but a practical version already appears whenever people cannot predict, understand, or interrupt an automated decision process. Present governance adds human oversight, constraints, testing, and [[Algorithmic Accountability]] rather than assuming that technical optimization alone guarantees alignment.

Simple containment proposals fail against a system able to model its overseers. Distributed execution weakens a physical off switch, linguistic access can support persuasion, and an [[Oracle AI Containment Failure|oracle]] can disguise a plan that expands its own powers. Control therefore depends on both limited capabilities and an objective that remains acceptable when the system discovers strategies its designers did not anticipate.
