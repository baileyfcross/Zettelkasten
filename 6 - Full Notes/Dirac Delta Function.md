# Dirac Delta Function

The Dirac delta is a generalized function concentrated at one point. It vanishes away from that point, has unit integral over any interval containing it, and is defined most usefully by its sampling property $\int f(x)\delta(x-a)dx=f(a)$.

The delta represents an idealized point source, impulse, or sharply localized distribution. It also appears in completeness relations, where a continuous collection of basis functions reconstructs the identity operator through an integral rather than a discrete sum.
