# Project Knowledge Integration

Project knowledge integration connects requirements, decisions, research, work items, reports, and team experience so participants can retrieve and synthesize information across the project lifecycle. Language models can summarize and answer questions over these heterogeneous records.

The integration needs source identity, timestamps, access control, and links to original artifacts. Otherwise a fluent synthesis can erase disagreement, mix obsolete and current decisions, or disclose information outside its intended audience.
