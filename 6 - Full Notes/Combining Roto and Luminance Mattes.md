# Combining Roto and Luminance Mattes

A hand-drawn shape can isolate the general region of a subject while a luminance key resolves fine detail within it. The book uses this division for difficult edges such as hair, where tracing every strand by hand would be inefficient. The broad roto limits the keyer's search to a useful area, and the resulting mattes are combined into one foreground extraction. This is different from [[Garbage Mattes for Key Extraction]] only in degree: the roto may participate in the final edge instead of merely discarding distant material.
