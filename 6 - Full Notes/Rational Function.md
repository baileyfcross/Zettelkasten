# Rational Function

A rational function is a quotient $f(x)=p(x)/q(x)$ of polynomials with $q(x)\ne0$. Zeros of the denominator are excluded from its domain and may produce holes or [[Vertical Asymptote]]s. Comparing polynomial degrees helps determine end behavior and possible [[Horizontal Asymptote]]s.
