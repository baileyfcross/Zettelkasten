# Liferay Member Map Portlet

A member-map portlet presents portal members geographically by combining profile location data with a map view. It turns a directory into a spatial discovery tool that can help participants find nearby colleagues or understand the distribution of a community.

The feature also exposes the privacy implications of combining identity and location. The portal should define which location granularity is stored, who can view it, whether participation is optional, and how stale data is corrected or removed. A technically successful map can still violate user expectations if address-level details become more visible than they were in a profile. The example therefore joins [[Liferay Social Collaboration]] to the privacy concerns in [[Enterprise Social Privacy Exposure]].
