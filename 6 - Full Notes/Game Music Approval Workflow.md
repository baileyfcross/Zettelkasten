# Game Music Approval Workflow

A game music approval workflow defines who reviews music, how feedback is delivered, and when an asset is considered final. The composer and producer need an agreed path for creative direction, revisions, technical review, and acceptance so conflicting comments do not create uncontrolled rework.

On projects with many assets, every response should leave a durable record tied to the correct version. A shared sheet, ticket system, or asset-management tool can distinguish material awaiting review, requiring changes, or approved for the build. This paper trail connects creative feedback with [[Game Music Asset Tracking]].
