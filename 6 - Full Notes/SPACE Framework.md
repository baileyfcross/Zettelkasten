# SPACE Framework

The SPACE framework examines developer productivity through satisfaction and well-being, performance, activity, communication and collaboration, and efficiency and flow. The dimensions deliberately prevent one observable activity, such as commit count, from standing in for the whole outcome of software work.

SPACE can be applied at individual, team, and organizational levels, but measurement at those levels raises privacy and interpretation concerns. Platform teams should prefer aggregate signals, consult data-protection stakeholders, and combine activity data with feedback and delivery outcomes. [[DORA Metrics]] can inform the flow dimension, while the [[Developer Experience Framework]] adds a more direct account of how developers perceive their work.
