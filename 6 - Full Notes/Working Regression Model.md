# Working Regression Model

A working regression model is the provisional specification produced after an initial round of candidate-variable assessment. It retains the exposure required by the hypothesis and the confounders that met statistical or prespecified empirical criteria.

The model is deliberately not final. A second round attempts to reintroduce discarded covariates, testing whether their behavior changes in the presence of the assembled working set before a [[Final Regression Model]] is chosen.
