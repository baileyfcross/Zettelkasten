# Team Resource Visibility

Team resource visibility means that collaborators share a realistic understanding of available time, skills, tools, money, and attention. A design decision made without those constraints merely postpones the conflict until implementation.

Making capacity explicit supports informed tradeoffs and fair commitments. It also allows the team to reduce scope or change a prototype before hidden overload becomes missed work or resentment.
