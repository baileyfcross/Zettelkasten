# System Sink

A system sink removes a [[Resource]] from the modeled system. What happens afterward lies beyond the chosen [[System Boundary]], so a sink is sometimes also called a drain.

Sinks regulate resource accumulation and are essential to many [[Balancing Loop|balancing loops]]. Too few sinks can produce [[Economic Inflation]], while sinks that drain too aggressively can contribute to [[Economic Stagnation]].
