# Semilocal Ring

A semilocal ring is a ring with finitely many [[Maximal Ideal]]s. Its [[Jacobson Radical]] is the finite intersection of those maximal ideals.

A [[Local Ring]] is the special case with one maximal ideal. Semilocality retains enough finiteness to let several local components be handled together while distinguishing their separate residue fields.
