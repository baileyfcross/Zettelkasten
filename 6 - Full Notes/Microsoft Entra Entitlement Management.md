# Microsoft Entra Entitlement Management

Microsoft Entra Entitlement Management governs how users request and receive collections of access. It organizes resources into catalogs and publishes them through [[Microsoft Entra Access Package|access packages]] whose policies define eligible requestors, approvals, expiration, and review requirements.

This replaces a series of independent group, application, and site assignments with one lifecycle-aware entitlement. Connected organizations describe external communities that may request access, while sponsors and approvers retain business accountability. The package is only the grant mechanism: regular [[Microsoft Entra Access Review|access reviews]], meaningful expiration, and removal when the business relationship ends are what prevent a convenient onboarding bundle from becoming permanent excess access.
