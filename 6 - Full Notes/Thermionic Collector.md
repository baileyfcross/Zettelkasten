# Thermionic Collector

The collector is the cooler anode that receives electrons crossing from the [[Thermionic Emitter]]. Electrons give up energy at its surface, so collector heat must be rejected while they return through the external load. Its temperature and surface work function affect the useful voltage. The source discusses cesium-coated collector materials as one approach, alongside the need to control the gap's [[Space-Charge Barrier]].
