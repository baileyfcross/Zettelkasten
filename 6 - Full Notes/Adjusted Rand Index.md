# Adjusted Rand Index

The adjusted Rand index compares pair assignments in two clusterings after subtracting the agreement expected by chance under a stated partition model. It equals zero at expected agreement and reaches one for identical assignments.

Chance correction makes it more informative than the ordinary [[Rand Index]] when cluster counts or sizes differ. Its value still depends on the assumptions used to define random partitions.
