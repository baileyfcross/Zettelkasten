# Dynamic Brain Mapping

Dynamic brain mapping supplements anatomy with measurements of activity over time. Neural systems depend not only on which cells are connected but also on changing electrical states, chemical conditions, and plastic synaptic effects.

A static scan may therefore underdetermine the initial state of an emulation. Recording activity while controlled stimuli or behaviors occur can help connect structure to function and estimate otherwise hidden parameters. The closer an emulation aims to reproduce a particular individual at a particular moment, the more consequential these dynamic details become.
