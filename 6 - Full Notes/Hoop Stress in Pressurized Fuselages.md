# Hoop Stress in Pressurized Fuselages

When cabin pressure exceeds outside pressure, a cylindrical fuselage is loaded circumferentially by hoop stress. Frames and longitudinal stringers reinforce the pressure vessel against expansion, while the structure is much less tolerant of external pressure that can cause buckling. Windows and other geometric discontinuities must avoid sharp stress concentrations.
