# Survival Probability

Survival probability is the probability of remaining free of the event beyond a specified time. It decreases as events occur and is estimated while accounting for censored observations that leave the risk set.

It differs from simply subtracting overall event prevalence from one. At each event time, the calculation conditions on those still at risk, so both the numerator and denominator evolve throughout follow-up.
