# Comparing Thermal Simulation with Infrared Measurements

Infrared validation compares simulated surface temperatures with thermal-camera observations made under matching geometry, initial temperature, ambient conditions, and elapsed times. Point histories and spatial patterns reveal different kinds of disagreement and should both be considered.

Camera accuracy, pixel resolution, emissivity settings, reflections, and uncertain convection coefficients limit the comparison. Agreement within those bounds supports the modeled behavior, while mismatch can direct revisions to [[Thermal Imaging Calibration|measurement treatment]], properties, or boundary conditions.
