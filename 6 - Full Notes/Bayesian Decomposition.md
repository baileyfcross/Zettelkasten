# Bayesian Decomposition

A Bayesian decomposition expresses a joint probability distribution as a product of smaller conditional distributions. Each factor follows from a conditional-independence assumption encoded by the modeler.

The decomposition must balance three goals: reduce dimensionality enough for tractable inference, retain dependencies needed for a relevant model, and produce elementary distributions that can be specified or learned. It is an algebraic representation more general than requiring every factor to contain one child variable.
