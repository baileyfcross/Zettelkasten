# Interest Point Detector

An interest point detector locates image positions with distinctive local structure that can be found again under changed viewing conditions. Corners and blob-like regions are useful because their location is better constrained than a uniform patch or straight edge alone.

AR tracking needs detectors that balance repeatability, localization accuracy, computation, and invariance to scale or viewpoint. Each detected point becomes the center of a feature descriptor used for matching.
