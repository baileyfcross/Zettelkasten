# Vendor Lock-In

Vendor lock-in is the dependency that makes leaving a provider costly or operationally difficult. It can arise from provider-specific application frameworks, data formats, interfaces, skills, contracts, integration, and the work required to recreate an environment elsewhere.

The risk can be reduced through open formats, independent backups, portable application and data architecture, and tested alternatives. Complete avoidance may sacrifice valuable provider capabilities, so the dependency should be understood and accepted deliberately.
