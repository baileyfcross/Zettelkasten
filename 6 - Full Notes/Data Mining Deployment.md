# Data Mining Deployment

Data mining deployment turns an evaluated pattern or model into a maintained part of an information or decision system. It includes a deployment plan, monitoring and maintenance, reporting, and review of the discovery process.

Deployment also exposes models to changing data and business rules. Updating and revalidation are therefore continuing responsibilities rather than one-time implementation tasks.
