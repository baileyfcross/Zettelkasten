# Multilayer Network Synchronization

Multilayer network synchronization is the coordination of dynamic states across connected layers. Coupling within and between networks determines whether initially different processes converge, remain separated, or destabilize one another.

A supra-adjacency or related spectral representation can expose how interlayer strength changes synchronizability. The complete multilayer structure matters because layer aggregation may predict different behavior.
