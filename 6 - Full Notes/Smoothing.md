# Smoothing

Smoothing revises an estimate of a past hidden state by using observations that occurred after that state. Later evidence can distinguish past explanations that were difficult to separate at the time.

It is the backward-looking counterpart to prediction in a time-indexed Bayesian model. Smoothing therefore requires retaining or reconstructing information beyond the single current filtered belief.
