# Buchsbaum-Eisenbud Exactness Criterion

The Buchsbaum-Eisenbud criterion tests exactness of a finite complex of finitely generated free modules using ranks of consecutive maps and depths of their determinantal ideals.

At each position, adjacent ranks must add to the rank of the middle free module, and the ideal of the expected minors must contain a regular sequence of sufficient length. These conditions convert exactness into computable algebraic data.
