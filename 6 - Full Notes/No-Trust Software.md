# No-Trust Software

No-trust software is experienced as dangerous, exploitative, or unreliable. It may crash, carry malware, ransom data, or manipulate users, and its producers show little discipline toward dependability, safety, or security.

People may still use it because of deception, convenience, or compromised websites. The category demonstrates that adoption alone is not evidence of design quality or informed consent.
