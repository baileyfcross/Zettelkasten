# Local Action

Local action is behavior that operates on nearby parts at approximately the same organizational level and within the same operational context. It supplies an effect but lets the receiving part determine its own response.

This locality avoids brittle dependencies across distant areas of the [[System Hierarchy]]. When combined with [[Modular Behavior]], it allows distributed interactions to generate [[Emergence]] without a central script controlling the whole.
