# Genomic Biomarker

A genomic biomarker is a measured gene, variant, expression pattern, or molecular signature associated with diagnosis, prognosis, treatment response, or another clinical state. A signature may combine several weak features into one score.

High-dimensional discovery can produce an apparently accurate marker by overfitting. Feature selection must be performed inside validation, and the marker should add useful information beyond established clinical variables. Analytical reproducibility, biological plausibility, and performance in an independent population are separate requirements for translation to care.
