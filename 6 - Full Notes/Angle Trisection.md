# Angle Trisection

General angle trisection asks for a straightedge-and-compass construction dividing any angle into three equal parts. Certain angles can be trisected, but no construction works for all angles.

Trisecting a 60-degree angle would require solving an irreducible cubic whose relevant coordinate is not constructible.
