# Stereo Panning

Stereo panning distributes a sound between left and right output channels according to its direction relative to the listener. A source on one side contributes more strongly to the corresponding channel.

Panning supplies a directional cue even without a full surround system. It is combined with [[Distance Attenuation]] and other spatial effects rather than replacing them.
