# Precision Approach Guidance and Minima

A precision approach supplies both lateral and vertical guidance for instrument approach and landing. Its category specifies operational minima involving decision altitude or height, runway visual range, and visibility. Higher capability categories reduce some restrictions only when the aircraft, ground equipment, crew, and procedures all meet the required standard.
