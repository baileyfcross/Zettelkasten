# Context-Rule Cohesion

Context-rule cohesion is the degree to which a game's presentation supports the behavior of its rules. When objects act according to the metaphors assigned to them, players can transfer prior expectations and predict new interactions.

When presentation suggests one behavior but the system produces another, the player may doubt the entire [[Player Mental Model]]. Cohesion can be strengthened through consistent imagery, motion, sound, timing, and [[Feedback]].
