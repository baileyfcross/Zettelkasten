# Blender Internal and Cycles Complementarity

In Blender 2.7x, Blender Internal and Cycles were not simply a weak and strong version of the same renderer. Their material, texture-painting, viewport, and lighting features differed, so a workflow could select the engine whose capabilities best fit a stage or desired result.

This is a historical production distinction because Blender Internal was later removed. The reusable lesson is to compare renderer feature support, not assume that a more physically complete renderer automatically offers the best authoring workflow for every task.
