# Image Metadata

Image metadata are descriptive records stored alongside pixel data. They may identify the camera make and model, lens and exposure settings, capture time, geographic coordinates, editing software, copyright information, and an embedded preview.

Metadata support forensic reasoning when fields agree with one another and with the visible photograph. They are not intrinsically trustworthy: software can edit or remove them, online services often strip them, and a forger can copy plausible values from another file. Their evidentiary value lies in specific consistencies and contradictions.
