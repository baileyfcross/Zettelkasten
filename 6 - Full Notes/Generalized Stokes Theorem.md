# Generalized Stokes Theorem

For an oriented smooth manifold $M$ with consistently oriented boundary $\partial M$, the generalized Stokes theorem states

$$\int_{\partial M}\omega=\int_M d\omega.$$

It unifies boundary-to-interior integration laws. [[Green's Theorem]] is its planar one-form case, [[Stokes Theorem]] is its surface-curl form, and the [[Divergence Theorem]] is its three-dimensional flux form.
