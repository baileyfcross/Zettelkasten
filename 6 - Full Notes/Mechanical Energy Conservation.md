# Mechanical Energy Conservation

Mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic and potential energy. It remains constant when only conservative forces transfer energy within the system. Friction can reduce mechanical energy by transferring it to internal or thermal forms, but total energy is still conserved: energy is neither created nor destroyed, only transferred or transformed.
