# Cyclic Quadrilateral

A cyclic quadrilateral has all four vertices on one circle. Opposite interior angles are supplementary because each is an inscribed angle intercepting arcs that together complete the circle. Conversely, a quadrilateral with supplementary opposite angles can be inscribed in a circle.
