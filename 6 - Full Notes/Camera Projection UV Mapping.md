# Camera Projection UV Mapping

Camera-projected UV mapping flattens selected faces from the active camera's view so a rendered or photographed image aligns with the model from that viewpoint. It is effective for camera-matched proxy geometry and fixed compositions.

The mapping encodes one view rather than complete surface coverage. Large camera movement reveals stretching and unseen regions, so the method is best for limited parallax, background reconstruction, or shots whose intended viewpoint is already known.
