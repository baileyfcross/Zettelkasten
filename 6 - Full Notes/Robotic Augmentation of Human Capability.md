# Robotic Augmentation of Human Capability

Robotic augmentation extends human force, cognition, sensation, or presence through a physical computing system. Prostheses and exoskeletons support movement, surgical mechanisms translate hand motion into precise instrument control, and remote robots let a person sense or act at a distance.

Augmentation should be evaluated by the resulting partnership. Added capability can also introduce weight, power dependence, training, unequal access, and uncertainty about control. The goal is not to make the machine resemble a person, but to create a combined system that achieves something neither partner could perform as safely or effectively alone.
