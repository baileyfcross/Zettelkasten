# HCL Social Media Publisher

HCL Social Media Publisher connects managed web content to configured social channels so selected material can be published beyond the portal. The feature joins editorial workflow with social distribution instead of requiring an author to recreate the same item independently on every service.

Publishing needs more than a destination button. The system must manage provider credentials, transform content to platform constraints, preserve approvals, identify the intended account and audience, and record success or failure. Removal and later edits may not propagate automatically to every copy, so the portal should treat external posts as distinct published artifacts. This feature is an outbound specialization of [[HCL Digital Experience Social Integration]] and a governed form of [[Enterprise Social Portal Integration]].
