# Risk Chart

A risk chart orders cases by predicted probability or score and compares accumulated positives, errors, or value across the ranked population. It shows how model performance translates into a limited review or intervention budget.

The chart is most useful when accompanied by a baseline and a clear definition of the risk or benefit accumulated.
