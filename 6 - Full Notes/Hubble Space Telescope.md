# Hubble Space Telescope

The Hubble Space Telescope is a large Earth-orbiting observatory launched in 1990 to conduct astronomy above the atmosphere. Its main mirror had been ground precisely to the wrong shape after a redundant optical test was removed to save money, producing blurred images and damaging NASA's credibility.

The instrument remained scientifically usable, and a 1993 Space Shuttle servicing mission installed corrective optics. Later repairs and upgrades made Hubble exceptionally productive. Its history shows the value of serviceability and the danger of eliminating independent verification from a complex system even when each component appears precise.
