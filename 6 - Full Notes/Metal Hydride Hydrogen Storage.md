# Metal Hydride Hydrogen Storage

Some metals and alloys chemically bind hydrogen as solid hydrides. Heating the material later releases hydrogen for use, letting fuel be stored or transported in solid form. The source lists several candidate compounds but notes the heavy mass of hydride relative to recovered hydrogen. Bond strength, release heat, and material weight therefore matter beside storage safety and volume.
