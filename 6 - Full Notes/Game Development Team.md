# Game Development Team

A game development team is a coordinated system of people whose design, programming, art, sound, writing, production, testing, and other skills produce a game. Its effectiveness depends more on shared vision and interaction than on merely assembling expert parts.

Successful teams maintain mutual respect, clear roles, open communication, accountability, local authority, and room to learn from mistakes. They address risks and destructive behavior while avoiding extended crunch and unnecessary design thrashing.
