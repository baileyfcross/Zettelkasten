# Copyright

Copyright protects an original work of authorship once it is fixed in a tangible form of expression. For software, it covers the particular source-code expression and gives the holder exclusive rights such as reproduction, distribution, adaptation, and public display subject to legal limitations.

Copyright does not give ownership of every idea, fact, process, or mathematical rule described by the work. This is why independently implementing an algorithm differs from copying its source text, although licenses, patents, contracts, and other obligations may still affect the implementation.
