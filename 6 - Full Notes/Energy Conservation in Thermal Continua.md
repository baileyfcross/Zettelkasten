# Energy Conservation in Thermal Continua

For any identifiable portion of a continuum, energy entering plus energy generated must equal energy leaving plus energy stored. Applying the balance to progressively smaller elements produces the differential heat equation that relates spatial heat-flux variation to thermal storage and volumetric generation.

The storage term depends on mass, [[Volumetric Heat Capacity|heat capacity]], and the rate of temperature change. At steady state it vanishes, but internal sources and boundary fluxes must still balance; in a transient, their mismatch changes the local temperature field.
