# Association Rule Mining

Association rule mining searches for combinations of categorized attributes that frequently accompany an outcome. Support records how much of the dataset contains a rule, confidence records how often the outcome occurs with it, and lift compares that association with independence.

In legislative analysis, session-level centrality and homophily values can be categorized and tested for combinations associated with re-election. A discovered rule is an empirical association rather than proof of a causal career strategy.
