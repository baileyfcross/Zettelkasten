# Tragedy of the Commons

The tragedy of the commons is a system pattern in which individuals acting in their own short-term interest deplete a limited shared resource. Each actor receives a concentrated benefit while the cost is distributed across the group.

The pattern can create conflict and strategic play when the shared [[Resource]] and its depletion are legible. Without meaningful coordination or balancing mechanisms, the common stock can collapse even though no participant intended that collective outcome.
