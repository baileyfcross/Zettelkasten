# Sparse Autoencoder

A sparse autoencoder adds a constraint that encourages only a small proportion of hidden units to be active for any input. The network may therefore use a wide hidden layer without learning a trivial identity representation.

Sparse activation promotes selective, parts-like features and can improve interpretability. The strength and form of the sparsity penalty determine the balance between accurate reconstruction and specialized codes.
