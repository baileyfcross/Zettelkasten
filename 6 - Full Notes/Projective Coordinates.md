# Projective Coordinates

Projective coordinates write a point as $[x_0:\cdots:x_n]$, where multiplying every coordinate by the same nonzero scalar leaves the point unchanged. The notation emphasizes that the coordinates name an equivalence class.

An affine chart is obtained by selecting representatives with a chosen coordinate equal to one. Points for which that coordinate is zero lie on the [[Hyperplane at Infinity]].
