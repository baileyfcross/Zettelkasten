# Reductionism

Reductionism explains something by dividing it into smaller parts and analyzing those parts. This analytic move is valuable for identifying attributes, state, and mechanisms.

Taken too far, reductionism ignores how parts interact and can therefore miss [[Feedback Loop|feedback loops]] and [[Emergence|emergent]] properties. [[Systems Thinking]] retains part-level analysis while recombining it with [[Holism|holistic]] attention to the operating whole.
