# Inverse Distance Weighting

Inverse distance weighting estimates an unsampled location as a weighted average of nearby observations, giving closer points more influence than distant ones. The distance exponent controls how quickly influence declines. The method produces a [[Point-Surface Interpolation|surface]] without explicitly modeling spatial covariance, so it can create local bull's-eye patterns and provides no automatic account of prediction uncertainty. Its output should be interpreted in light of sampling density.
