# Phrase-Synchronized Game Music

Phrase-synchronized game music coordinates gameplay or visual action with a meaningful musical boundary. Instead of changing immediately, the system schedules an event for the next beat, bar, cadence, or phrase so the score can complete a coherent gesture.

This creates a tension between musical timing and interaction timing. Waiting improves continuity but can delay the visible response; changing at once preserves responsiveness but may damage the phrase. A successful design therefore requires the music engine and gameplay system to share timing information and to decide which kinds of events can tolerate latency.
