# Hom Module

Hom_R(M,N) is the module of all R-linear [[Module Homomorphism]]s from M to N. Addition and scalar multiplication are defined pointwise.

This construction turns mappings themselves into an algebraic object. It is paired with the [[Tensor Product of Modules]] through the tensor-Hom relationship and extends to the derived [[Ext Functor]] when exactness fails.
