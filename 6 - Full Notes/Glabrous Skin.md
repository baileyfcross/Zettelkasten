# Glabrous Skin

Glabrous skin is hairless skin on the palms and soles. On the hand it is relatively thick, anchored to underlying tissue at flexure lines, and covered by papillary ridges that help stabilize grasp, transmit contact forces toward receptors, and house sweat-gland openings.

Its receptor population differs from hairy skin and includes mechanoreceptors specialized for spatial detail, pressure change, sustained indentation, and high-frequency vibration. This organization makes the fingertips highly sensitive while still allowing the palmar surface to bend around objects during [[Active Touch]].
