# Beam Bending Moment

Beam bending moment is the internal couple associated with flexural deformation. Through the beam's moment-curvature relation, it depends on flexural rigidity and the curvature of the deflection field.

Element end moments are obtained from the beam stiffness relation after nodal degrees of freedom are known. Bending-moment diagrams expose critical regions and support reaction moments.
