# Dirac Equation

The Dirac equation is a relativistic wave equation that is first order in both time and spatial derivatives. It acts on a multicomponent spinor and uses gamma matrices whose anticommutation relations reproduce the relativistic energy-momentum relation.

Its solutions naturally include spin and both positive- and negative-energy branches. The equation supplies a conserved current with a nonnegative time component, while its nonrelativistic limit connects to two-component spin dynamics.
