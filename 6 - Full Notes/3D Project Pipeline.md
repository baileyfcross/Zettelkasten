# 3D Project Pipeline

A 3D project pipeline divides work into preproduction, production, and postproduction. Preproduction plans and tests the intended result, production creates the primary assets and action, and postproduction assembles, corrects, and finishes the material that will be delivered.

The boundaries depend on the project. In an animated film, simulation and compositing may overlap asset and shot production, but the distinction remains useful because early decisions are cheaper to revise than completed models, animation, or renders.
