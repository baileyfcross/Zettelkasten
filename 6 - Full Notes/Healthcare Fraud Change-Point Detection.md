# Healthcare Fraud Change-Point Detection

Healthcare fraud change-point detection searches a provider's time series for a sustained shift in billing or service behavior. The provider can serve as its own historical baseline, making the method sensitive to new behavior even when peers vary widely.

A change may reflect a scheme, but it can also come from a new specialty, policy, contract, patient population, or coding system. The detected point should therefore trigger contextual investigation. Features and time windows must be chosen so seasonal volume or one short surge does not masquerade as a lasting structural shift.
