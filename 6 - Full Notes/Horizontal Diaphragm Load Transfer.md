# Horizontal Diaphragm Load Transfer

A roof or floor diaphragm collects lateral force over an area and transfers it in its own plane to vertical frames or walls. Flexible diaphragms distribute primarily by tributary area, while rigid diaphragms distribute according to the relative stiffness and position of the vertical resisting elements. Real diaphragms lie on a spectrum and must possess adequate strength, stiffness, and connections for the assumed behavior.

Openings, reentrant corners, discontinuous frame lines, and offsets disturb direct transfer. In-plane shear travels through deck and its fasteners, perimeter chords resist the tension-compression couple associated with diaphragm bending, and collectors gather force into localized frame or wall lines. Connections between each component are part of the lateral path, so nominal deck strength alone does not establish diaphragm adequacy.
