# yearmon Time Index

The `yearmon` class represents a calendar month as a numeric year plus a fractional month. It gives monthly observations an ordered [[Time Index]] while retaining meaningful printed labels and conversions. Unlike assigning an arbitrary day to every month, it expresses the actual granularity of a [[Regular Time Series]]. It also supports selecting monthly windows and aggregating finer observations to months.
