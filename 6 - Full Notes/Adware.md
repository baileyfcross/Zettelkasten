# Adware

Adware displays or injects advertising through software, sometimes while tracking user behavior or changing browser and system settings. Not all advertising-supported software is malicious, but deceptive installation, unwanted persistence, and undisclosed collection place harmful adware within the malware family.

Its effects can include reduced performance, loss of privacy, and exposure to further unsafe content. Clear consent and controlled software installation help distinguish legitimate monetization from abuse.
