# Iterative Development

Iterative development revisits a product repeatedly, using what each working version teaches to revise its design. Each iteration contains a small pass through analysis, design, implementation, integration, and testing instead of assigning those activities to one long sequence.

For games, this converts uncertainty about feasibility and fun into evidence through a playable build. Iteration is therefore a learning structure as well as a delivery cadence.
