# Aircraft Weight Definitions

Aircraft planning distinguishes standard and basic empty weight, gross weight, takeoff weight, useful load, dry operating mass, traffic load, zero-fuel mass, and maximum takeoff or landing limits. Each term includes and excludes different items, so substituting one for another changes the result. Correct definitions are the basis for [[Maximum Traffic Load Constraints]] and center-of-gravity calculations.
