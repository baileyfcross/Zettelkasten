# Maximum Traffic Load Constraints

Maximum traffic load is constrained simultaneously by maximum takeoff mass, maximum landing mass, and maximum zero-fuel mass. The allowable load is the smallest value left after subtracting dry operating mass and the fuel applicable to each limit. Identifying the binding constraint matters because adding fuel, passengers, or cargo can shift which structural or operational limit controls.
