# Gameplay Mode

A gameplay mode is a coherent configuration of available gameplay, camera model, and interaction model at a particular time. A significant change in any of those elements means the player has entered another mode.

Modes limit complexity by offering an appropriate subset of actions and information. Their transitions must be understandable because the player needs to know what can be done, how the world is viewed, and how control now works.
