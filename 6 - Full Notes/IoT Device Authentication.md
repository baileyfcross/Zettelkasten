# IoT Device Authentication

IoT device authentication verifies that a device, gateway, user, application, or service is the identity it claims before permitting communication or control. Digital certificates such as X.509 can bind a public key to an identity and allow participants to validate one another across a network.

Authentication is more demanding than a one-time setup because large fleets contain physically exposed devices, multiple vendors, long service lives, and changing ownership or authorization. A secure system must provision identities, protect credentials, revoke compromised devices, and distinguish a legitimate endpoint from a counterfeit or commandeered one throughout the device lifecycle.
