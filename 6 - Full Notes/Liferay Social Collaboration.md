# Liferay Social Collaboration

Liferay's collaboration features bring user profiles, relationships, activity, messaging, blogs, wikis, forums, and shared content into portal sites. These features allow a workplace or community to make contribution and discussion part of the same environment used for managed applications and information.

The value comes from integration with portal membership and permissions. Participation can be scoped to a site or organization, and social activity can point back to governed portal resources. Administrators still need moderation, privacy, and retention policies because enterprise identity makes contributions attributable without making every contribution appropriate for every audience. [[Liferay Portal Social Model]] supplies the identity and site foundation, while [[Liferay Member Map Portlet]] is a focused example of presenting the member community.
