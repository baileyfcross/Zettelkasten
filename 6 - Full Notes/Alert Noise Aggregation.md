# Alert Noise Aggregation

Alert noise aggregation groups abnormal signals that arise from the same underlying problem into one incident. Dependencies, shared resources, timing, and external events show when several service, database, and infrastructure alerts are symptoms of one causal chain.

Aggregation is stronger than suppression because it preserves evidence while changing how it is presented and routed. It reduces duplicate paging and gives responders a coherent incident whose scope, cause candidates, impact, and ownership can be investigated together.
