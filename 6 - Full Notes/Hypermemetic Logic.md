# Hypermemetic Logic

Hypermemetic logic is a condition in which rapid copying, variation, and circulation become expected features of digital culture rather than exceptional events. A successful item is not merely passed along; it invites visible derivatives, comparisons, and contests among versions.

Digital networks intensify older cultural practices by making artifacts easy to duplicate, edit, search, and distribute. The result is a public environment where copying is both a communication method and a mode of cultural participation, and where the relationships among remakes become part of a meme's meaning.
