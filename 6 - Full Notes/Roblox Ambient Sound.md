# Roblox Ambient Sound

Ambient sound gives a Roblox environment an audible sense of place. Wind, machinery, crowds, wildlife, or water can play continuously or intermittently, with positional sources attached to parts when the player should hear direction and distance.

A global ambient bed creates broad atmosphere, while several localized emitters make movement through the world audible. Range, rolloff, volume, and overlap should be tested so sources blend without becoming a constant wall of sound. [[Roblox Sound Group]] can control the ambient category separately from music and interface effects.
