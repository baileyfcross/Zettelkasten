# Modular Behavior

Modular behavior is a simple, general-purpose action that performs one function without depending on unnecessary context. It can be reused and combined with other behaviors in many situations.

Highly scripted behaviors are narrow and brittle because they encode a particular sequence and purpose. Generic behaviors operating through [[Local Action]] combine more freely, making [[Emergence]] and a broader [[Play Space]] possible.
