# Synchronizing Sun Lights with World Environments

A directional sun and a World environment can contradict each other if their brightest directions do not match. Blender's Sun Position workflow can align a sun object with either the brightest location in an HDRI map or the sun represented by a procedural Sky Texture.

Synchronization makes the cast shadow, background, diffuse illumination, and reflections describe one coherent lighting situation. After alignment, rotation, date, time, and strength remain artistic or site-specific controls.
