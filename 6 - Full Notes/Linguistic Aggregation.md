# Linguistic Aggregation

Linguistic aggregation combines related pieces of information into a more compact utterance. Instead of repeating a full sentence for every result, a generator can coordinate facts, group shared properties, or summarize a list.

Aggregation improves efficiency but requires attention to clarity and cognitive load. The [[Sentence Planner]] must decide which details can be combined without obscuring distinctions the user needs.
