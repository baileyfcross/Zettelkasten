# Tensor-Hom Adjunction

The tensor-Hom adjunction identifies R-linear maps M ⊗ N → P with R-linear maps M → Hom_R(N,P). A bilinear rule in two inputs can therefore be represented either by a tensor map or by a family of linear maps.

This correspondence follows from the universal property of the [[Tensor Product of Modules]]. It connects tensor constructions with the [[Hom Module]].
