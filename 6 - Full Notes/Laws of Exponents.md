# Laws of Exponents

The laws of exponents summarize how repeated factors combine. For appropriate real values and integer exponents,

$$a^ma^n=a^{m+n},$$

$$\frac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}\quad(a\neq0),$$

$$(a^m)^n=a^{mn},$$

$$(ab)^n=a^nb^n.$$

Each law follows by counting and regrouping factors. The common-base requirement matters: exponents cannot be added merely because two powers are multiplied. These rules also explain the [[Zero Exponent]] and [[Negative Exponent]] conventions and support simplification of polynomial and rational terms.
