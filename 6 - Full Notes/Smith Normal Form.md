# Smith Normal Form

Smith normal form is a diagonal form for a matrix over a [[Principal Ideal Domain]]. Its nonzero diagonal entries $a_1,\ldots,a_m$ are ordered so that $a_i$ divides $a_{i+1}$.

Invertible row and column operations produce matrices $P$ and $Q$ with $PAQ$ in this form. The diagonal entries become [[Invariant Factor of a Module|invariant factors]] and expose the cyclic quotient summands of the module presented by $A$.
