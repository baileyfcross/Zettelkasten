# UML Composition Relationship

Composition is a strong whole-part association in which the part's identity or useful lifetime belongs to the whole. UML marks the whole end with a filled diamond.

An order and its order items illustrate the relationship: the items are meaningful as constituents of that order rather than independently owned objects. Composition therefore communicates stronger ownership than [[UML Aggregation Relationship|aggregation]].
