# Coordinate Vector Relative to a Basis

If $\mathcal B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ is an [[Ordered Basis]] and $v=c_1b_1+\cdots+c_nb_n$, the coordinate vector $[v]_{\mathcal B}$ is the column $(c_1,\ldots,c_n)^T$. Uniqueness follows from the basis being linearly independent.

Coordinates depend on the selected basis, while $v$ itself does not. A [[Change-of-Basis Matrix]] converts one coordinate vector into another representation of the same vector.
