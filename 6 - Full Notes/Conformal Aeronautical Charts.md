# Conformal Aeronautical Charts

A conformal, or orthomorphic, projection preserves local angles and small shapes by matching scale in perpendicular directions at a point. It does not preserve area globally, so large regions can still be substantially distorted. Correct local bearings make conformality especially valuable for navigation even though scale must be checked across the planned route.
