# IoT Privacy Aggregation Risk

IoT privacy aggregation risk arises when individually ordinary sensor records become sensitive after they are combined. A single location, heart-rate, purchase, device, or timestamp may reveal little, while a sequence joined with other logs can expose identity, routines, health, relationships, and behavior.

Removing an obvious identifier does not necessarily make the data anonymous. Cell-tower records, toll events, payment transactions, device logs, and social activity can be aligned to reidentify a person. Privacy protection must therefore consider possible linkage and inference across the entire data ecosystem, not only whether one database contains a name or static address.
