# Linear Equation

A linear equation in one unknown can be written $ax+b=0$ with $a\ne0$. Its single solution is $x=-b/a$. Solving consists of reversible arithmetic steps that isolate the unknown, so maintaining [[Equivalent Equations]] at each step preserves the solution set.
