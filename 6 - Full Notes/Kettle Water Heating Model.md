# Kettle Water Heating Model

The kettle case reduces a rotationally symmetric vessel to an axisymmetric model, omitting the handle and spout to save computational effort. It combines solid conduction, laminar water flow, gravity-driven convection, electric heat input, and surface radiation.

The free water surface and exterior walls require different flow, convection, and radiation conditions. Revolving the solved two-dimensional data set produces a three-dimensional view, while streamlines and [[COMSOL Temperature Contours|temperature contours]] show both circulation and heating.
