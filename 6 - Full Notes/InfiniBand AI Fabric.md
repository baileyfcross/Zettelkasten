# InfiniBand AI Fabric

An InfiniBand AI fabric provides a purpose-built, high-bandwidth and low-latency interconnect for high-performance computing and large AI clusters. Native [[Remote Direct Memory Access|RDMA]] reduces CPU and operating-system involvement in transfers, making the fabric suitable for tightly synchronized multi-node training.

The performance benefit comes with specialized adapters, switches, topology, and operating knowledge. Selection should use the workload's effective communication needs, budget, and team capability rather than treating a fabric generation name as a complete design.
