# Information Credibility

Information credibility is the degree to which a claim or source should be considered trustworthy. In social media it can be estimated from message content, source behavior, propagation patterns, and the responses of other users.

Credibility is not equivalent to popularity: repeated sharing can amplify unreliable material. Useful systems expose uncertainty and combine independent evidence rather than treating one network metric as proof.
