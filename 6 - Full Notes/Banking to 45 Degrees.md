# Banking to 45 Degrees

Banking to 45 degrees chooses a plot aspect ratio so the absolute orientations of its line segments are centered near forty-five degrees. The method can improve perception of slopes and changes in a [[Time Plot]], because neither horizontal nor vertical movement dominates the display. For a long series, the computed panel may become impractically shallow; [[Cut-and-Stack Time Plot|cutting and stacking]] can retain the chosen aspect ratio while using page space efficiently.
