# Differential Rendering

Differential rendering computes how virtual content changes the appearance of a modeled real scene rather than replacing the real image with a fully synthetic rendering. A rendering with virtual contributions is compared with a rendering of the real-scene model alone, and their difference is applied to the camera image.

This preserves observed real detail while adding effects such as virtual shadows and reflected light. Its quality depends on sufficiently accurate geometry, materials, illumination, and registration.
