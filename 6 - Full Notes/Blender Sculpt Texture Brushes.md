# Blender Sculpt Texture Brushes

A texture can modulate a sculpt brush, replacing a uniform stroke with patterned surface detail. Existing textures from Texture Properties or the Texture Node Editor can be assigned through the brush's Texture panel.

Rake rotates the texture to follow the direction of the stroke, reducing repetitive stamped patterns. Textured brushes are therefore useful for adding detail while retaining the speed of broad brush-based sculpting.
