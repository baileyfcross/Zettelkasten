# Standard Form of Linear Program

A linear program in standard form expresses its constraints as equations and requires its variables to be nonnegative. Inequalities are transformed using [[Slack Variable|slack]] or [[Surplus Variable|surplus variables]].

Standard form supplies the algebraic structure used by the [[Simplex Method]] and provides a consistent basis for constructing a dual problem.
