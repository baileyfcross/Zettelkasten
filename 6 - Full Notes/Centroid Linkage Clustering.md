# Centroid Linkage Clustering

Centroid linkage clustering represents each cluster by the mean of its member coordinates and compares clusters through the distance between those centroids.

The summary is efficient and intuitive for numerical data, but it can hide cluster spread or shape. Merging changes both centroids, so the sequence need not behave like nearest- or furthest-neighbor linkage.
