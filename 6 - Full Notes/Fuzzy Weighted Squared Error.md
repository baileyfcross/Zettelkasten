# Fuzzy Weighted Squared Error

Fuzzy weighted squared error sums the squared distances from observations to cluster centers after weighting each contribution by its membership. It evaluates how well a fuzzy partition fits its prototypes.

The measure can compare a current set of clusters with a proposed merge or drive the iterative updates in fuzzy C-means. Its meaning still depends on the scale and distance used.
