# Kotlin Loops and Ranges

Kotlin provides `while` and `do-while` loops for condition-controlled repetition and `for` for traversing an iterable value. A `do-while` evaluates its condition after the body, so the body executes at least once.

Ranges provide a compact sequence for many `for` loops. Inclusive ranges, downward progressions, and step sizes describe which values are visited without manually updating a counter. Arrays and collections can be traversed directly when the elements, rather than their indexes, are the real subject of the loop.
