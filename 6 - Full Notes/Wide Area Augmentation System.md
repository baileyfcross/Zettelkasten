# Wide Area Augmentation System

The Wide Area Augmentation System is the United States regional augmentation service developed chiefly for civil aviation. Ground stations at known positions compare GPS observations with their surveyed locations, central processing estimates wide-area corrections and integrity status, and geostationary satellites transmit the information to aircraft. WAAS improves horizontal and vertical accuracy while providing timely confidence information needed for approach guidance.
