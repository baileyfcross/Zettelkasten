# Follow Active Quads UV Mapping

Follow Active Quads extends the UV orientation and proportions of an active quad across connected quad topology. It is useful for strips, grids, walls, roads, and other surfaces whose edge flow should become an orderly island.

The active face supplies the starting reference, so its shape and UV placement affect the entire result. Length, average, or even spacing choices determine whether the layout preserves edge proportions or regularizes the grid.
