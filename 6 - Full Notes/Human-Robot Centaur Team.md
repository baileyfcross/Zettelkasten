# Human-Robot Centaur Team

A human-robot centaur team combines human judgment with machine speed, memory, calculation, or control so the pair outperforms either partner alone. The design depends on a deliberate division of work rather than asking one side to imitate all capabilities of the other.

Strong teams make machine recommendations legible, preserve channels for correction, and train people to rely on automation without surrendering attention. They also anticipate interactions between human error and algorithmic failure. [[Human-Robot Comparative Advantage]] identifies the strengths to combine, while the centaur model turns those strengths into an operating partnership.
