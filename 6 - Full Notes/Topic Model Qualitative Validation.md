# Topic Model Qualitative Validation

Topic model qualitative validation inspects leading terms and high-probability documents to decide whether a latent topic forms a coherent, useful theme. A domain specialist may recognize relationships that a generic numerical score cannot evaluate.

The review can reveal names, artifacts, or mixed themes that call for preprocessing changes or another topic count. Because iterative inspection informs the next model, the final report should distinguish exploratory refinement from an independent evaluation.
