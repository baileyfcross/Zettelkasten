# Crowdsourcing

Crowdsourcing distributes a task or information request to a large group of participants, often through an open online platform. Aggregated contributions can label data, report conditions, generate ideas, or solve problems at a scale unavailable to one organization.

Quality varies with expertise and incentives, so validation, redundancy, reputation, and adversarial resistance are part of the system design.
