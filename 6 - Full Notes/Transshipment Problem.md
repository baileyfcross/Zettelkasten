# Transshipment Problem

A transshipment problem extends the transportation model by allowing goods to pass through intermediate points or to move among sources or destinations before reaching final demand points. Flow conservation at each intermediate node requires inbound quantity to equal outbound quantity.

The model can be converted to an expanded transportation table and solved with transportation methods. Its additional routing freedom can reduce total cost, but it introduces transfer-node balances that direct origin-to-destination shipping does not need.
