# Player-Centric Game Design

Player-centric game design evaluates decisions through the experience of a representative member of the intended audience. The designer asks whether each feature entertains and serves that player rather than favoring the team's convenience or personal preferences.

The approach begins only after the target audience is defined. It is not a claim that every possible player must be satisfied, but a discipline for keeping the chosen player visible throughout development.
