# Power

A power is an expression (a^n) formed from a [[Base of a Power]] and an [[Exponent]]. For positive integer (n), its value is the product of (n) copies of (a).

The second and third powers have special names: a [[Square]] and a [[Cube]]. Powers compress repeated structure and make patterns such as growth in a [[Geometric Progression]] easy to express. They follow the [[Laws of Exponents]], which permit products, quotients, and nested powers to be simplified without expanding every repeated factor.
