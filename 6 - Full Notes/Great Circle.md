# Great Circle

A great circle is the intersection of a sphere with a plane through the sphere's center. Its radius equals the sphere's radius, and its arcs give the shortest surface paths between non-antipodal points. The sides of a [[Spherical Triangle]] are arcs of great circles rather than straight segments.
