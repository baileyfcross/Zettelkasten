# Search Result Ranking

Search result ranking orders pages that match a query so the most useful candidates appear first. Content matching alone cannot distinguish authoritative pages from trivial or misleading pages when all contain the requested terms.

[[PageRank]] contributes a structural signal by treating links from other pages as endorsements whose weight depends on the importance of the source. A complete search engine may combine this signal with query content and other information.
