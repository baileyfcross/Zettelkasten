# Coprime Polynomials

Polynomials are coprime when they have no common nonconstant factor. Over a [[Field]], this is equivalent to the ideal they generate being the entire [[Polynomial Ring]].

The [[Bezout Identity for Polynomials]] then expresses 1 as a polynomial combination of the inputs. A zero [[Polynomial Resultant]] detects the opposite situation for two positive-degree polynomials: the presence of a common factor.
