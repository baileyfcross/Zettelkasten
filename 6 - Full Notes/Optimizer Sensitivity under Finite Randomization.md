# Optimizer Sensitivity under Finite Randomization

An RC-ZNE VQE run can improve its best energy estimate while becoming less consistent across random initial parameters. In the finite-randomization simulations, the distribution of converged energies sometimes widens after mitigation, especially for LiH, and its spread differs between Powell and BOBYQA optimization.

The variability arises from the interaction among the optimizer, the initial point, the noise model, and noisy objective estimates generated by finite randomized ensembles. It is therefore not enough to assess mitigation only by the median result or by one successful run; the minimum, median, and trial-to-trial spread answer different questions.

In the [[Infinite-Randomization Pauli-Channel Limit]] with exact expectation values, the spread is almost eliminated by combined RC and ZNE. This comparison indicates that the number of random compilations and the shot allocation are optimizer hyperparameters as well as measurement settings.
