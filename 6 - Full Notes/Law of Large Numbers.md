# Law of Large Numbers

The law of large numbers states that the average of repeated independent observations approaches their expected value under appropriate conditions as the trial count grows. It explains why a simulated success proportion tends toward the underlying probability even though short runs can vary widely.

The student companion visualizes this convergence by simulating batting outcomes at increasing numbers of at-bats. The path need not move monotonically toward the target; the declining scale of fluctuations is the relevant long-run behavior.
