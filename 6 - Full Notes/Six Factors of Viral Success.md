# Six Factors of Viral Success

Six factors commonly support viral success: positivity, strong high-arousal emotion, effective packaging, prestige, advantageous positioning, and opportunities for participation. They make an item pleasant or urgent to share, easy to understand, socially credible, visible at the right time, and connected to audience action.

No factor guarantees diffusion, and the factors interact with network structure. Prestige and positioning may produce exposure while emotional intensity motivates forwarding. Simplicity, humor, and participation can also help memetic success, but the features that maximize unchanged sharing are not identical to those that invite creative transformation.
