# Great-Circle Distance from Vector Products

Two geographic locations can be represented by radius vectors from Earth's center using their latitudes and longitudes. The normalized dot product gives the cosine of the central angle between those vectors. Converting that angle to degrees and multiplying by 60 nautical miles per degree yields the corresponding great-circle distance.
