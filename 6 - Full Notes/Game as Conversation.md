# Game as Conversation

Game as conversation is a model in which the creator structures a system and the player responds through action. The game presents rules, resistance, and opportunities; the player uses [[Game Verb|verbs]], makes choices, and receives [[Feedback]].

The creator may not be present when play occurs, but design still shapes what can be said and heard. Different games can invite open response, focused interpretation, competition, or a quieter form of listening.
