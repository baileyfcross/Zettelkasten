# Multivariable Limit

The statement $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L$ for $f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m$ means that every sufficiently small nonzero displacement from $a$ makes $f(x)$ arbitrarily close to $L$ in norm.

Unlike a one-dimensional approach, a point in several dimensions can be approached along infinitely many directions and curves. Agreement along a few paths is therefore necessary but not sufficient; differing path values prove that the limit does not exist.
