# GLONASS

GLONASS is the Russian global navigation satellite system developed by the Soviet Union as a counterpart to GPS. Its original design distinguished satellites mainly by carrier frequency rather than the code-division scheme used by GPS. The constellation deteriorated after the Soviet breakup and was later restored to comparable navigation performance. Modern receivers can combine GLONASS with other constellations, and newer generations move toward code-based signal separation.
