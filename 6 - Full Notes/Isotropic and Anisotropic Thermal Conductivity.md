# Isotropic and Anisotropic Thermal Conductivity

An isotropic material uses the same thermal conductivity in every direction. An anisotropic material requires directional values, so conductive heat flux need not align simply with a scalar conductivity times the temperature gradient.

Transversely isotropic materials share properties within a plane but differ normal to it, while orthotropic materials have three distinct values along orthogonal axes. Rolled sheet and layered structures therefore require orientation-aware inputs in [[Finite Element Thermal Modeling|finite-element models]].
