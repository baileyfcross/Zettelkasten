# Core Resource

A core resource is a resource the player most depends on, seeks to maximize, or must avoid losing to continue play. It is closely tied to the game's central goals and [[Core Gameplay Loop]].

Identifying a core resource gives balancing work a common basis for comparing costs and benefits. Subsidiary resources support or modify it, while a hidden aggregate may sometimes serve as a shadow resource when several visible values contribute to one outcome.
