# Prototype Decision Record

A prototype decision record connects an artifact to the design question it explored, the evidence gathered during play, and the decision made afterward. It prevents the team from remembering only whether a prototype felt successful.

Recording rejected alternatives and remaining uncertainty keeps later iterations from repeating a test without understanding why the earlier result was accepted, revised, or discarded.
