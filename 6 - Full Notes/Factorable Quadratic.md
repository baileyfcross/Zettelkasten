# Factorable Quadratic

A factorable quadratic is a quadratic polynomial that can be expressed as a product of lower-degree factors over the number system being used. For example,

$$x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3)$$

over the integers.

Factorability depends on the allowed coefficients. The polynomial (x^2-2) has no factorization into integer-coefficient linear factors, although it factors using irrational coefficients. When convenient factors are unavailable, a quadratic equation can still be solved through [[Completing the Square]] or the [[Quadratic Formula]].
