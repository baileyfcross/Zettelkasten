# Classification Prediction

Classification prediction assigns an observation to one of a finite set of categories, such as popular versus unpopular or interested versus uninterested. The result is evaluated by comparing predicted and actual classes.

A [[Confusion Matrix]] separates correct and incorrect outcomes for each class. Accuracy alone can mislead on imbalanced data, so positive-class precision, recall, specificity, and their combined score may also be needed.
