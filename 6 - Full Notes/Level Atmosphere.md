# Level Atmosphere

Level atmosphere is the mood created by the specific arrangement of environment art, lighting, sound, events, and pacing. The level designer assembles assets so they feel coherent with the game world and intended emotion.

Atmosphere should also support play. Visual and audio choices must preserve navigation, threat recognition, and important feedback instead of obscuring them for decoration.
