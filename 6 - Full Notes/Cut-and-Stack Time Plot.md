# Cut-and-Stack Time Plot

A cut-and-stack time plot divides a long time axis into overlapping segments and places those segments in aligned rows. It preserves an aspect ratio selected through [[Banking to 45 Degrees]] without producing an extremely wide or shallow figure. Small overlaps help the reader continue a trajectory across row boundaries. The layout improves local slope perception, but the discontinuous horizontal arrangement makes very long-range timing comparisons less immediate.
