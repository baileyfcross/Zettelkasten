# Projective AR Display

A projective AR display casts generated imagery onto physical surfaces. The projector and target geometry must be calibrated so rendered pixels land on the intended locations, turning walls, objects, or work areas into situated displays.

Projection allows unencumbered viewing and can preserve natural collaboration around a shared object. It cannot make surfaces darker, is sensitive to ambient light and material reflectance, and may be blocked by users or other objects that cast shadows.
