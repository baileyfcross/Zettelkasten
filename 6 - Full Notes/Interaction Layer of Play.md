# Interaction Layer of Play

The interaction layer is the player's understanding of what can be done and how an action changes the game. It joins perceived [[Affordance|affordances]], controls, rules, and [[Feedback]] into a usable relationship between intention and result.

Good interaction reduces avoidable effort between deciding and acting. Consistency lets previous learning carry forward, while navigability helps the player locate a coherent path through the possibility space.
