# Whole Brain Emulation

Whole brain emulation is the construction of a computer simulation that reproduces the functional activity of a particular biological brain. The proposed workflow has three stages: map the relevant structure and state, simulate their causal dynamics, and connect the simulation to an environment through a body or interface.

The proposal assumes that behavior depends on physical processes that can in principle be recorded and reproduced. It does not require a complete verbal theory of cognition before simulation begins, but it does require knowing which biological details are causally important at the chosen level of fidelity.
