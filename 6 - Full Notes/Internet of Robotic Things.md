# Internet of Robotic Things

The Internet of Robotic Things joins autonomous machines with connected sensors, external devices, databases, edge processors, and cloud services. A robot observes its surroundings, combines onboard data with information from the wider environment, computes an appropriate response, and acts in the physical world.

Connectivity expands what the machine can know beyond its own sensor suite. A delivery robot can use positioning and maps while detecting nearby obstacles locally, and industrial robots can share operating knowledge or request remote data when needed. The design must allocate time-critical perception and control to the edge because an unsafe movement cannot wait for an uncertain cloud round trip.
