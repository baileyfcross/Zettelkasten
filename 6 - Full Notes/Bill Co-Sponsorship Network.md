# Bill Co-Sponsorship Network

A bill co-sponsorship network links legislators who jointly present or support proposed legislation. Legislators are nodes, and shared sponsorship supplies an observable relationship of collaboration or support.

The network can be built for a whole term or separated into legislative sessions. Its centrality and community patterns may reveal cohesion, cross-party relationships, splitting, and switching, while remaining an imperfect proxy for private motives.
