# Chance and Uncertainty in Play

Chance is deliberate randomization, while uncertainty is the broader condition of not knowing what will happen. Hidden information, another player's choice, variable skill, or a complex system can create uncertainty without random numbers.

Chance supports play when players can still decide how to prepare for, interpret, or respond to its results. If an outcome is random and no decision can affect its meaning or consequences, the player becomes an observer of the draw rather than an agent within it.
