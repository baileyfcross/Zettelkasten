# Spaceflight Prestige Signaling

Spaceflight prestige signaling uses a mission to demonstrate a state's scientific, technical, industrial, and organizational capability. An orbital launch, human first, or lunar landing is visible evidence that the same society can coordinate advanced systems, even when the mission has little direct military use.

Prestige helped governments justify costs that science or commerce alone could not support. It also favored spectacular milestones over less visible infrastructure and made human spaceflight especially attractive. The signal persists after the Cold War: established and emerging powers continue programs partly because abandoning them could imply a loss of status.
