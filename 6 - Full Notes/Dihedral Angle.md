# Dihedral Angle

A dihedral angle is the region of space bounded by two half-planes that share an intersection line. The shared line is the edge, and the two half-planes are the faces.

Its measure is the angle between two rays, one in each face, drawn from the same point on the edge and perpendicular to that edge. This rectilinear angle is independent of the chosen point and relates the construction to the [[Angle Between Planes]].
