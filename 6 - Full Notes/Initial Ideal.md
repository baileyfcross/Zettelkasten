# Initial Ideal

The initial ideal of a polynomial ideal I is generated by the leading monomials of all polynomials in I under a fixed [[Monomial Order]]. It converts a general polynomial ideal into a monomial ideal.

A finite subset is a [[Gröbner Basis]] precisely when its leading terms generate the initial ideal. This reduces membership and remainder questions to finitely many leading divisibility tests.
