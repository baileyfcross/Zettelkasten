# Roblox Asset Identifier and Moderation

Roblox-hosted images, audio, meshes, animations, and other assets receive unique identifiers that experiences use to reference their content. The identifier separates the in-game object from the uploaded file, allowing Studio properties and scripts to point to a shared online asset.

Uploaded assets can require moderation before they become available, and ownership or usage rights can limit where they work. An experience should therefore test the final published identifiers rather than assuming every local preview will remain valid. The same system supports [[Roblox Decal and Texture Mapping]], [[Roblox Mesh Import]], and [[Roblox Sound Object]].
