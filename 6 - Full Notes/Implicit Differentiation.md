# Implicit Differentiation

Implicit differentiation finds a derivative from a relation between variables without first solving explicitly for the dependent variable. Both sides are differentiated with respect to the independent variable, and every occurrence of the dependent variable is handled with the [[Chain Rule]].

The resulting equation is then rearranged for the desired derivative. This method is especially useful for inverse functions and curves whose defining equation is simpler than any explicit branch, but the final expression may still need to be rewritten in terms of the original independent variable.
