# Social Phishing

Social phishing personalizes a deceptive message with information drawn from social profiles, relationships, or recent activity. Familiar names and plausible context make the request appear more credible than a generic phishing attempt.

Reducing exposed personal details, warning about unusual requests, authenticating sensitive actions, and detecting compromised accounts limit the value of this contextual targeting.
