# Power Series Solution of a Differential Equation

A power series method assumes the unknown solution can be expanded about a point as $y=\sum a_n(x-x_0)^n$. Substituting the series and its derivatives into the differential equation lets equal powers of the independent variable be matched.

The resulting recurrence relation determines coefficients from a small set of initial ones. This method constructs local solutions when elementary closed forms are unavailable and often reveals special functions as the natural solutions of physical differential equations.
