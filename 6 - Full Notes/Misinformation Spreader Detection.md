# Misinformation Spreader Detection

Misinformation spreader detection identifies accounts responsible for generating or amplifying deceptive content. It combines profile, behavior, sentiment, content, and social-link features to distinguish malicious users from ordinary participants.

Detecting the account can expose many messages at once, but supervised methods require credible labels. Suspension lists, manual annotation, and [[Social Honeypot|social honeypots]] each introduce different delays and sampling biases.
