# Diagonal Five-Point Formula

The diagonal five-point formula approximates a Laplace-equation value by the average of its four diagonal neighbors rather than its left, right, upper, and lower neighbors.

It is less accurate than the [[Standard Five-Point Formula]] but can provide convenient starting values when the diagonal nodes are already known. Those estimates can then be refined with the standard stencil or relaxation.
