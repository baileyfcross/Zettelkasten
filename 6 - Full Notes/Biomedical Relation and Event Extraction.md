# Biomedical Relation and Event Extraction

Biomedical relation and event extraction identifies how entities participate in processes described by scientific text. A relation can connect two entities, while an event can include a trigger, participants, roles, and nested biological processes.

This richer representation distinguishes a list of recognized names from an account of what happened. Syntax, semantic roles, and domain knowledge help determine whether a protein activates, inhibits, or merely co-occurs with another entity. Complex sentences and coreference remain major sources of error, so extracted structures should retain links to their textual evidence.
