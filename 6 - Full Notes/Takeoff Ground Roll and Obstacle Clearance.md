# Takeoff Ground Roll and Obstacle Clearance

Takeoff performance separates the ground roll from the total distance needed to clear an obstacle such as a 50-foot screen height. In the source's Cessna 172M analysis, both distances increase with temperature and pressure altitude under fixed assumptions about flap, throttle, surface, slope, and wind. Any change to those assumptions requires the corresponding approved correction rather than reuse of the baseline value.
