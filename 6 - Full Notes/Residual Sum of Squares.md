# Residual Sum of Squares

The residual sum of squares is $\sum_i(y_i-\hat y_i)^2$, the total squared vertical discrepancy between observed and fitted values.

Squaring prevents positive and negative residuals from cancelling and penalizes larger discrepancies more strongly. [[Least Squares Approximation]] selects parameters that make this quantity as small as possible.
