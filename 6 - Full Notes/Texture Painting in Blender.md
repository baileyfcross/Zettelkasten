# Texture Painting in Blender

Texture Paint mode applies color directly to an image while the artist paints on the corresponding 3D mesh. A usable UV layout connects each brush stroke on the model to a location in the flat texture.

Painting on the model is effective for blocking color and placing detail in context. The image and UV guide can later be moved to a dedicated two-dimensional graphics program when more specialized painting tools are needed.

Blender's Texture Paint workspace keeps the 3D Viewport and Image Editor connected to the same canvas. Brush, blend, stroke, texture, mask, falloff, symmetry, and slot settings determine how a stroke is generated and where its pixels are written.
