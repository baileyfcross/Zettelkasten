# Correlation Matrix

A correlation matrix arranges pairwise correlation coefficients for several variables in a symmetric table. The diagonal contains perfect self-correlations, while the off-diagonal cells summarize the strength and direction of each pairwise linear or rank relationship.

Missing-data rules and the chosen correlation method affect every entry. A matrix can identify redundancy or possible [[Regression Collinearity]], but it cannot determine causation or replace examination of the underlying scatter plots.
