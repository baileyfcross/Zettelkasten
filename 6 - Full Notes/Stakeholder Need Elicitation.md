# Stakeholder Need Elicitation

Stakeholder need elicitation draws goals, frustrations, assumptions, and operating conditions from the people affected by a system. Interviews, observation, workshops, and prototypes help reveal needs that are difficult to express as ready-made features.

Elicitation precedes commitment to a particular implementation. The resulting evidence feeds [[Requirements Analysis]], where conflicting statements and hidden constraints can be resolved before they harden into architecture and project work.
