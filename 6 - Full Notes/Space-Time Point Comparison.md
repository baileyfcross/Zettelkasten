# Space-Time Point Comparison

Space-time point comparison examines how fixed locations differ in level, variability, and timing. Map panels can reveal whether nearby stations behave differently over selected dates, an [[Point Space-Time XT Plot|XT plot]] exposes persistent station bands across the year, and time-series lines reveal common peaks. A defensible comparison checks the same scales and separates lasting location effects from short-lived network-wide events.
