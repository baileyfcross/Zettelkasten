# Precision Drawing Tools Add-On

Precision Drawing Tools, or PDT, adds CAD-oriented construction operations to Blender. It supports accurate two-dimensional drawing and the development of technical or architectural models from planar starting geometry.

PDT is complementary to measurement rather than a replacement for it: construction commands establish exact relationships, while tools such as [[MeasureIt Add-On]] make the resulting dimensions visible for checking.
