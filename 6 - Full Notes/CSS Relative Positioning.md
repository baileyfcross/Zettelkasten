# CSS Relative Positioning

A relatively positioned box first receives its ordinary position in normal flow and is then visually shifted by offsets. The space reserved at its original location remains unchanged, so neighboring boxes do not move into the gap or respond to the shifted position.

Relative positioning can also establish a [[CSS Containing Block]] for absolutely positioned descendants. It therefore supports local coordinate systems even when the ancestor itself is not visibly offset.
