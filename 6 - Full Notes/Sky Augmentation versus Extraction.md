# Sky Augmentation versus Extraction

A flat, nearly uniform sky may be improved by laying a new sky plate over it and masking that plate just above the horizon, rather than extracting every foreground boundary. Edge feather and reduced opacity let the two skies blend. A more complex sky or horizon demands roto, paint, or a procedural extraction to separate scene elements. In either case, the new sky's sun direction and atmospheric color must agree with the ground plate; the book flips a cloud plate when its light direction conflicts with shadows. See [[Feathering a Composite Edge]].
