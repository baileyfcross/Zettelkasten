# Rule-Based Access Control

Rule-based access control grants or denies access according to conditions defined by policy. A rule may test time, location, system state, network origin, or another contextual property surrounding the attempted operation.

The decision is based on the rule's current truth rather than only the claimant's fixed role. It can be combined with [[Role-Based Authorization]] when both job responsibility and environmental conditions matter.
