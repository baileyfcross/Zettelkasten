# Digital Puzzle Design Process

A digital puzzle design process moves from inspiration to simplification, construction tools, explicit rules, multiple levels, testing, sequencing, and presentation. Simplification identifies the essential tricky skill, removes irrelevant detail, regularizes pieces where useful, and gives the mechanic controls suited to its medium.

Construction tools should make repeated level creation inexpensive. Playtesting then corrects mistaken assumptions about difficulty, after which levels are ordered so each concept appears alone before increasing in challenge and mixing with prior concepts. Visual, audio, and interface polish comes after the rules and sequence are demonstrably workable.
