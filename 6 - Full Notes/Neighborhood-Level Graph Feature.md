# Neighborhood-Level Graph Feature

A neighborhood-level graph feature summarizes the local subgraph surrounding a node. Degree, neighbor attributes, local density, and small motif counts are common examples.

Local features are relatively inexpensive and often interpretable. They may fail to distinguish nodes whose immediate neighborhoods look alike but whose positions in the full network differ.
