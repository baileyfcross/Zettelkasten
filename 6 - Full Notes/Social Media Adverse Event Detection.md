# Social Media Adverse Event Detection

Social media adverse event detection searches patient-generated posts for mentions that connect a medical product with an unwanted effect. It can surface experiences that are not promptly submitted through formal reporting systems.

A post rarely supplies the controlled evidence needed to establish causality. The author, exposure, dose, timing, and outcome may be uncertain, and duplicated stories can exaggerate frequency. The method is best used to generate signals for investigation, with entity recognition and context analysis separating personal reports from general discussion.
