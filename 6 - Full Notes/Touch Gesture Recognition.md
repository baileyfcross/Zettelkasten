# Touch Gesture Recognition

Touch gesture recognition interprets one or more touch points over time as higher-level actions such as taps, swipes, pinches, or rotations. Meaning comes from position, movement, duration, and relationships among simultaneous touches.

The recognizer abstracts raw contact events so gameplay can respond to an intended gesture. It must also distinguish gestures that share similar early motion until enough evidence is available.
