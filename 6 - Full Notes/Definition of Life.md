# Definition of Life

A definition of life commonly combines autonomous metabolism and growth, response to environmental stimuli, and reproduction. No single definition is universally accepted, especially for systems that possess only some of these properties.

Biofabricated tissues metabolize, grow, and respond, yet usually depend on external support and cannot reproduce the complete engineered object, placing them near a conceptual boundary.
