# Asymmetric Game

An asymmetric game gives players different starting conditions, rules, roles, resources, or victory conditions. Fairness depends on the total opportunities each side receives rather than identical components.

Asymmetry can create distinctive strategies and cooperation, but it is harder to tune. A unique ability becomes unfair when its advantages are not offset by meaningful costs, limitations, or opposing tools.
