# GPS Fleet and Asset Tracking

GPS fleet and asset tracking couples a passive navigation receiver to a communication link that reports position, speed, or events to another party. Dispatchers can coordinate vehicles and emergency services can locate crashes, but the same capability enables employers or service providers to monitor individuals. The tracking function is therefore not performed by GPS alone: GPS supplies location, while a cellular or radio transmitter converts that private local estimate into remotely observable data.
