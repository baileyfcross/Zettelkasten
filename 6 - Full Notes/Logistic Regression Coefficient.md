# Logistic Regression Coefficient

A logistic regression coefficient is the adjusted change in outcome log odds associated with a one-unit predictor change. For an indicator variable, it compares the focal category with the omitted reference group while holding other model terms fixed.

The sign indicates whether the odds rise or fall, but the exponential is usually easier to communicate as an [[Odds Ratio]]. Its standard error supports Wald tests and confidence intervals.
