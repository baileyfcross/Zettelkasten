# Environmental Justice of AI

The environmental justice of AI concerns who receives the benefits of intelligent systems and who bears their environmental costs. Energy production, resource extraction, polluted air, discarded hardware, and climate risk are not distributed equally across places or populations.

Wealth can also buy escape from harmful environments through cleaner locations, private infrastructure, or protective technology. An application that benefits affluent users while transferring material damage elsewhere cannot be judged by efficiency alone. Justice requires tracing production and disposal, including affected communities in decisions, and comparing the project with lower-technology alternatives that may be easier to maintain and share.
