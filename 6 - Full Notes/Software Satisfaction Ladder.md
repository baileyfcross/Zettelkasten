# Software Satisfaction Ladder

The software satisfaction ladder ranges from no trust through cynical use, promise fulfillment, environmental fit, avoidance of negative consequences, and delight. Each level demands broader attention to the customer’s experience and future.

Ascending the ladder expands design from basic correctness toward practices, breakdowns, and evolving concerns. Higher levels cannot be specified entirely in advance because they depend on relationships between producers, users, and changing contexts.
