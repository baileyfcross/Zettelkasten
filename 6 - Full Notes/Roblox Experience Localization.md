# Roblox Experience Localization

Localization adapts a Roblox experience for players who use different languages and cultural conventions. Translatable interface and game text should be captured in a localization table rather than embedded unpredictably throughout scripts and images.

Translation may be automatic, community-supported, or manually managed, but every result needs contextual review. Text expansion, fonts, dates, names, and culturally specific imagery can require layout or content changes beyond word substitution. Localization therefore connects authoring to [[Roblox Responsive GUI Layout]] and to the global audience measured by [[Roblox Experience Analytics]].
