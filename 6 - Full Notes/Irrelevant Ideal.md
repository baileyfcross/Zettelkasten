# Irrelevant Ideal

For a nonnegatively graded ring generated in degree one, the irrelevant ideal R₊ is the direct sum of all positive-degree components. It is generated by the degree-one part.

Despite its name, it controls projective geometry and graded local behavior. Graded Nakayama arguments reduce a module modulo the homogeneous maximal ideal containing R₊.
