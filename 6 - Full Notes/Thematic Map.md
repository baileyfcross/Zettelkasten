# Thematic Map

A thematic map emphasizes the spatial distribution of an attribute rather than primarily helping a reader navigate. Disease prevalence, election results, rainfall, and land cover can be organized geographically so patterns and regional differences become visible.

Different encodings answer different questions. A [[Choropleth Map]] shades regions, a [[Proportional Symbol Map]] preserves individual locations while varying symbol size, a [[Cartogram]] changes geographic area, and a [[Flow Map]] represents movement between places. The map's classification and symbols are part of the analytical claim, not a neutral wrapper around the data.
