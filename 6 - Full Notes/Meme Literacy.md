# Meme Literacy

Meme literacy is the ability to recognize a meme's template, references, conventions, and expected transformations. It enables a viewer to understand not only an isolated joke but also how a version relates to the larger family from which it emerged.

Creating an effective derivative demonstrates subcultural knowledge. The participant must preserve enough of the formula for recognition while altering it in a meaningful way. Literacy therefore acts as cultural capital: insiders can decode layered references and judge whether a contribution follows, extends, or deliberately violates the genre.
