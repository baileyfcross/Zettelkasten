# Measured Cloud Service

A measured cloud service records the amount of a service or resource that a consumer uses. Measurements can include storage, processor capacity, memory, bandwidth, users, transactions, or another unit appropriate to the service.

Measurement supports usage reporting, capacity management, and pricing. Transparency matters because a consumer needs to relate changing consumption to both service performance and cost instead of discovering the result only after billing.
