# Microsoft Foundry Model Comparison

Microsoft Foundry model comparison places shortlisted models side by side across benchmarks, accepted inputs and outputs, context limits, training cutoff, endpoint options, and features such as streaming, reasoning summaries, tool calls, fine-tuning, and image input.

Comparison exposes incompatibilities that a single quality score hides. A model may be accurate but lack the required modality, deployment type, data policy, or tool interface. The decision should first eliminate candidates that cannot meet legal and architectural requirements, then balance quality, latency, throughput, cost, and operational support among the remaining models.
