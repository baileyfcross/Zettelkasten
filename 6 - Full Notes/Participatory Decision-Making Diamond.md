# Participatory Decision-Making Diamond

The participatory decision-making diamond separates group work into a divergent zone, a groan zone, and a convergent zone. Participants first generate different observations, then organize and wrestle with ambiguity, and finally narrow toward an action.

Protecting the middle prevents a group from jumping directly from the loudest idea to a solution. Tools such as [[Affinity Mapping]] and root-cause analysis help the group move through ambiguity constructively.
