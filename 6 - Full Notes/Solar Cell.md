# Solar Cell

A solar cell is an individual semiconductor device that uses a [[P-N Junction]] to deliver electrical power from incident sunlight. Light generates carriers in the junction, while contacts take the resulting current to an external circuit. Cells have material-dependent voltage and illumination- and area-dependent current. They can be wired into a [[Solar Module]] for higher useful voltage or current.
