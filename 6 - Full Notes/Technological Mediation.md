# Technological Mediation

Technological mediation is the way a technology helps shape how people perceive the world, choose actions, and relate to others. An AI system is not merely a neutral object between a user and a goal; its classifications, recommendations, and interface make some interpretations and actions easier than others.

Because mediation changes both experience and practice, ethics should enter during design rather than only after harm occurs. The relevant unit is the relationship among people, technology, and context. This view connects [[Posthumanism]] with [[Ethics by Design]] while preserving attention to the power built into concrete systems.
