# Surface Work Function

Work function is the energy required for an electron inside a solid to escape the attractive barrier at its surface. The source gives different values for different metals and uses it to explain why heating an emitter releases only some electrons. Together with temperature, it controls [[Thermionic Emission]] rate; emitter and collector work functions also affect the voltage of a [[Thermionic Generator]].
