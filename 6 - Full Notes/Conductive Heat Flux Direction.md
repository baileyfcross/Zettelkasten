# Conductive Heat Flux Direction

The temperature gradient points toward the direction of greatest temperature increase, while conductive heat flux points oppositely. In three-dimensional Cartesian space, separate temperature derivatives in $x$, $y$, and $z$ define the components of this vector relationship.

Total conductive power through a surface uses the component normal to that surface. Thus an $x$-directed heat flux crosses a plane parallel to $y$ and $z$, a geometric relationship that must be preserved in boundary selections and [[COMSOL Point Line and Surface Evaluations|surface evaluations]].
