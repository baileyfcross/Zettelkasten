# Cube

The cube of a quantity is its third power:

$$a^3=a\cdot a\cdot a.$$

The name corresponds to the volume of a geometric cube with edge length (a). Unlike a square, a cube preserves the sign of a real number because it contains an odd number of factors: ((-a)^3=-a^3). Reversing the operation uses a [[Cube Root]]. Cubes also form reusable expansion and factorization patterns such as the [[Sum of Cubes Identity]].
