# CAF Security Perspective

The CAF security perspective builds organizational capabilities for confidentiality, integrity, availability, threat management, identity, vulnerability management, infrastructure protection, data protection, and incident response in the cloud.

Security is integrated into adoption rather than appended after workloads move. The perspective connects policy and risk requirements with preventive and detective controls, continuous assurance, and response practices under the [[AWS Shared Responsibility Model]].
