# Local Identity in Global Meme Remakes

Global meme remakes express local identity by inserting national, regional, institutional, subcultural, political, or personal markers into a recognizable international template. Participation announces both membership in a global conversation and difference within it.

Gangnam Style derivatives demonstrate this dual movement: creators reused a globally legible song and dance while showcasing cities, professions, institutions, causes, or communities. The global template provided visibility, and local substitution supplied the reason to make another version.
