# Reproducible Experiment Specification

A reproducible experiment specification states the actual or derivable inputs, the hardware and software context, and the executable steps that transform those inputs into results. Each part matters because an omitted dataset, dependency, configuration, or operation can prevent a later run from representing the original experiment.

The specification may be expressed through a workflow, a [[Research Compendium]], or captured execution records. Its adequacy is judged along several independent dimensions, including [[Depth of Reproducibility]], [[Portability of Reproducibility]], and [[Coverage of Reproducibility]].
