# Game Macrosystem

A game macrosystem is a high-level structure formed by several integrated lower-level systems. Its behavior concerns the game as a whole—for example, whether difficulty across combat, rewards, progression, and player performance stays competitive or accessible—rather than the tuning of one object.

Macrosystem design may combine flat balance, reinforcing or balancing feedback, dynamic adjustment, player-selected difficulty, and [[Cross-Feeding]]. Because these methods can operate in different modes or layers of one game, their local effects must be evaluated in terms of the experience produced by the complete system.
