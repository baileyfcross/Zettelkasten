# Kanban Board

A Kanban board maps work items across columns that represent workflow states. Cards and queues reveal where work is active, waiting, blocked, or completed, allowing a team to inspect the entire flow at a glance.

The board should reflect the real process rather than an idealized one. Dates, buffers, and work limits can be added when they help measure [[Cycle Time]] or expose a [[Bottleneck]].
