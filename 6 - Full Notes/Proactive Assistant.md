# Proactive Assistant

A proactive assistant initiates a suggestion, warning, or action based on predicted user needs and changing context. It may use schedules, location, external events, and prior behavior to decide when help could be valuable.

Proactivity increases utility but also the risk of interruption, mistaken inference, and unwanted surveillance. The assistant needs conservative policies and transparent control over the [[Personal Context]] it uses.
