# Solar Thermal System Simulation

A solar-thermal system model combines collection, storage, fluid circulation, distribution, controls, and auxiliary heat rather than rating a collector alone. Weather and load change over minutes or hours, while useful delivery totals may be needed over months or years. The source recommends numerical simulation because component equations are nonlinear and continually changing; repeated time-step calculations reveal whole-system behavior.
