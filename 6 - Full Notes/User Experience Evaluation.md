# User Experience Evaluation

User experience evaluation is the process of assessing or measuring aspects of a person's [[User Experience]] with an artifact. It may consider [[Usability]], usefulness, emotional impact, comfort, or qualities specific to an environment, such as [[Presence]].

No single measure captures the whole experience. Evaluators combine evidence such as system metrics, task speed and accuracy, observations, interviews, questionnaires, and physiological measures. The appropriate combination depends on the question, users, task, context, and stage of design.

Evaluation should inform iterative design rather than occur only after implementation is complete.
