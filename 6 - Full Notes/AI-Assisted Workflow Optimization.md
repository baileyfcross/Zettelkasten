# AI-Assisted Workflow Optimization

AI-assisted workflow optimization examines process descriptions, project records, handoffs, and feedback to identify redundant steps, delays, and opportunities for automation. It can propose a revised flow and explain how the change relates to observed bottlenecks.

Optimization should preserve control, quality, and compliance goals that a timing metric may not reveal. A proposed change is best piloted with measurable outcomes and rollback rather than applied automatically to the whole organization.
