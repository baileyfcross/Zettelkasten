# Union of Sets

The union of sets contains every element that belongs to at least one of them. Finite unions combine listed collections, while indexed unions combine a family of sets.

A union of substructures need not itself be a substructure unless the family satisfies additional nesting conditions.

For two sets, A ∪ B = {x | x ∈ A ∨ x ∈ B}; its definition mirrors logical disjunction and includes elements that occur in either set or both.
