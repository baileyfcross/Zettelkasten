# Angular Similarity Coordinate

An angular similarity coordinate represents how alike network nodes are in a hyperbolic embedding. Nodes with nearby angles share latent characteristics, while their radial coordinates separately express popularity or hierarchy.

Combining angle with the [[Radial Popularity Coordinate]] lets [[Hyperbolic Distance]] balance similarity and prominence. Link likelihood generally increases as the resulting distance decreases.
