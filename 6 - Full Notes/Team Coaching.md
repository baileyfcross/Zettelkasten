# Team Coaching

Team coaching helps a group understand its goals, relationships, practices, and impediments so it can solve its own problems. The coach supplies observation, questions, facilitation, and process knowledge without taking ownership of every solution.

Coaching aims to increase the team's capacity for reflection and self-management. It cannot repair structural conditions that the organization refuses to change.
