# Cohesion

Cohesion is the degree to which the responsibilities of a module or class belong together. A highly cohesive class presents services at a consistent level of abstraction and concentrates on one closely related purpose.

Low cohesion often appears when a class has several unrelated reasons to change or its interface accumulates operations that serve different clients. Splitting those responsibilities can clarify the design, reduce the setup needed for testing, and make each part easier to reuse.
