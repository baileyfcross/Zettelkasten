# Gameplay

Gameplay is the set of challenges a game presents and the actions it permits the player to take, both to overcome those challenges and to perform other enjoyable activities. It is the active relationship between player and rules.

A setting, story, or visual style does not specify gameplay by itself. Designers must state what the player repeatedly does, what resists those actions, and how the system responds.
