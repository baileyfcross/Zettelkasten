# Submodule

A submodule N of an R-module M is a subset closed under addition and multiplication by scalars from R. Equivalently, it contains every R-linear combination of its elements.

An [[Ideal]] is precisely a submodule of a ring viewed as a module over itself. A submodule can be divided out to form a [[Quotient Module]] and can appear as the kernel or image in an exact sequence.
