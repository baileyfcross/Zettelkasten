# Game Process

A game process is a mechanic that operates over time rather than completing at one instant. Movement, production, regeneration, decay, and a spreading environmental effect are processes.

A process needs a start condition, update rule, timing model, and stopping condition. Its rates and dependencies often determine the behavior of an internal economy.
