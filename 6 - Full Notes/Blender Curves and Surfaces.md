# Blender Curves and Surfaces

Curves and surfaces are defined by mathematical functions and control points rather than by storing every visible vertex. This can produce precise shapes with compact underlying data.

They still must be tessellated into display geometry, so a mathematically exact curve can appear faceted at a low display resolution. Bézier handles and weighted NURBS control points provide different ways to shape the result.
