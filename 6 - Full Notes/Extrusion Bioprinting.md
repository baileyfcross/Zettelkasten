# Extrusion Bioprinting

Extrusion bioprinting deposits a continuous filament of cell-compatible material through a nozzle and builds a three-dimensional structure layer by layer. Nozzle path, pressure, gelation, and ink rheology determine shape fidelity and cell stress.

The method can build comparatively large tissues and channels, but soft hydrogels may buckle or collapse without temporary support.
