# Blender Cloud Texture Library

At the time covered by the book, Blender Cloud offered a public-domain texture library and a subscription workflow that could expose assets through a Blender add-on. It joined external asset discovery with direct use inside the authoring application.

The durable idea is integrated asset access, not the permanence of a particular catalog or plan. Availability, licensing statements, and add-on compatibility should be verified for the version and date of actual use.
