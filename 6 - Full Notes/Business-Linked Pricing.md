# Business-Linked Pricing

Business-linked pricing ties a cloud-service charge to a business performance indicator rather than a purely technical service metric. The intent is to reflect the service's contribution to the organization's business model.

Its main difficulty is attribution: a business result usually depends on several people, processes, and external conditions. The parties therefore need a defensible relationship between the chosen indicator and the service before using it as a payment basis.
