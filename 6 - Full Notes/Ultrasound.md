# Ultrasound

Ultrasound is sound with frequency above the upper range of ordinary human hearing, conventionally above 20 kHz. Its shorter wavelength supports fine spatial resolution, while reflection depends on changes in acoustic impedance between materials. Coupling gel reduces the strong reflection that an air gap would otherwise produce at a transducer boundary.
