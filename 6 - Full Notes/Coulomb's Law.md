# Coulomb's Law

Coulomb's law gives the electrostatic force between point charges:

$$F=\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2}.$$

The force acts along the line joining the charges and is attractive for unlike signs and repulsive for like signs. Multiple forces combine vectorially through superposition.
