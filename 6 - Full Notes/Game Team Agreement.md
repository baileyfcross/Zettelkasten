# Game Team Agreement

A game team agreement records how collaborators will communicate, make decisions, handle credit, share files, meet deadlines, resolve conflict, and respond when commitments cannot be met. The team creates it together before pressure makes those expectations implicit.

The agreement is revisable evidence of consent, not a punishment mechanism. Revisiting it gives the group a neutral reference when working habits begin to harm trust or the project.
