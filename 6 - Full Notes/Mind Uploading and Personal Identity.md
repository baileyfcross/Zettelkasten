# Mind Uploading and Personal Identity

Mind uploading proposes that a person's mental organization could continue in a computational substrate through [[Whole Brain Emulation]]. A behaviorally faithful copy could remember the same history, display the same personality, and insist that it is the original person.

Functional continuity is evidence for survival only if personal identity follows the brain's causal organization rather than its biological material. The proposal becomes harder when copying is possible: two equally faithful successors cannot both be numerically identical to one unique predecessor in the ordinary sense. Uploading therefore turns identity from an abstract question into a practical decision about survival.
