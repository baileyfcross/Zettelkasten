# Presentation Artifact Separation

Presentation artifact separation gives spoken notes, projected visuals, and take-home documentation different forms. The speaker's private notes support delivery, slides show only what the audience needs to see now, and a separate handout preserves details for later use.

Trying to make one slide deck perform all three jobs produces dense text that competes with speech and invites the audience to read ahead. Separate artifacts let the live presentation remain legible without discarding useful documentation.
