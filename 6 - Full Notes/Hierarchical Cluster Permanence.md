# Hierarchical Cluster Permanence

Hierarchical cluster permanence is the rule that once two entities or groups merge, they remain joined at all later agglomerative levels. The merged cluster becomes a building block for subsequent decisions.

This property makes the result a true nested hierarchy, but it also propagates early mistakes. Hierarchical clustering does not perform the later reallocation used by iterative partition methods.
