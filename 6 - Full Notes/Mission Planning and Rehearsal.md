# Mission Planning and Rehearsal

Mission planning and rehearsal uses simulation to let individuals or teams practice a specific operation before facing its real consequences. A scenario can reproduce terrain, objectives, equipment, communications, and team roles while allowing instructors to change conditions or repeat a difficult segment.

Immersive rehearsal is particularly useful for spatial and coordinated tasks such as movement formations, emergency response, room clearing, parachute operations, or distributed team exercises. Tracking head, body, and equipment motion supports after-action analysis, while communication channels and shared viewpoints help reveal coordination failures that individual practice would miss.

The simulation augments rather than replaces field training. Its value lies in safe repetition, cost control, and deliberate variation, and it depends on whether the environment and interaction constraints reproduce the decisions and [[Situation Awareness]] demands of the mission.
