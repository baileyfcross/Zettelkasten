# Partition Clustering

Partition clustering produces one division of the data rather than a complete nested hierarchy. It usually optimizes an objective over all observations or repeatedly improves a current assignment.

Partition methods are attractive when constructing a full dendrogram is too expensive. Their main challenges are choosing the number of clusters, selecting an initial configuration, and avoiding poor local optima.
