# Alembic Geometry Cache Interchange

Alembic is an open interchange format designed for baked computer-graphics data. It supports polygon meshes, subdivision surfaces, parametric curves, NURBS patches, particles, transform hierarchies, and cameras.

The format emphasizes evaluated geometry and animation caches rather than an editable construction history. Textures may require separate reassignment after import, so geometry fidelity and material portability must be checked independently.
