# Lexicalization

Lexicalization selects the words and grammatical forms used to realize a planned meaning. It resolves choices such as vocabulary, agreement, number, and gender while preserving the intended content.

In a generation pipeline, lexicalization follows a [[Sentence Planner]] and [[Microplanner]]. Even template systems perform a limited form of lexicalization when they insert and format variable values.
