# Color Correlogram

A color correlogram measures how often pixels of specified colors occur at chosen spatial distances from one another. It adds local or global spatial relationships to ordinary color-frequency information.

This makes the descriptor more discriminating than a color histogram when arrangement matters. The added distance and color-pair dimensions also increase storage and computation, so practical variants often compress the representation.
