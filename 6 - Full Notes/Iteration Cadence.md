# Iteration Cadence

Iteration cadence is the rate at which a team can turn a design question into a prototype, observe play, evaluate evidence, and try a revision. A short cadence exposes mistaken assumptions before they accumulate dependencies and sunk cost.

Speed does not mean skipping reflection. The cycle remains useful only when each pass records its question, evidence, and decision well enough for the next pass to build on what was learned.
