# Finite Differences of a Polynomial

For a polynomial of degree $n$ tabulated at equal spacing $h$, each forward difference lowers the degree by one. Its $n$th difference is constant, and every higher difference is zero.

If the leading coefficient is $a$, then $\Delta^n f=a n!h^n$. Conversely, a table with constant $n$th differences can be represented by a polynomial of degree $n$, which underlies polynomial interpolation from tabulated data.
