# Markov Localization

Markov localization estimates a robot's position from a sequence of actions and observations. A motion model predicts where the robot may have moved, while a sensor model corrects that belief using perceived features.

Representing location as a probability distribution permits multiple competing positions to remain possible. Repeated Bayesian updates concentrate or redistribute that belief as new evidence arrives.
