# Predictive Feature Relevance

Predictive feature relevance is the degree to which a variable helps distinguish observations belonging to different classes. With labels available, relevance can be estimated through association, dependence, distance, or class-separation criteria.

A relevant feature contributes information about the target rather than merely varying across cases. The source emphasizes that selecting discriminative features can improve generalization, reduce computation and storage, and leave the original variables interpretable.

In the book's workflow, relevance is examined alongside redundancy and operational availability. A highly ranked field can still be unsuitable if it is an identifier, leaks the target, is mostly missing, or cannot be obtained when predictions are made.
