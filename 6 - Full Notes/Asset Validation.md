# Asset Validation

Asset validation automatically checks game assets before or while they enter a build. Rules can detect naming errors, invalid geometry, and violations of memory, polygon, or platform budgets.

The rule set grows from defects the team actually encounters. Early automated rejection prevents malformed assets from becoming slower integration or runtime failures.
