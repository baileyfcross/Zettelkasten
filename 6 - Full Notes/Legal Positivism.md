# Legal Positivism

Legal positivism explains law through social rules and institutional practices rather than making legal validity depend directly on moral merit. A legal order identifies which norms count as law through criteria accepted and used by its officials.

The framework permits a distinction between what the law is and whether it is morally justified. That separation makes it possible to ask whether privacy is merely enacted protection or an [[Intrinsic Limit of Law]] needed for a viable legal order.
