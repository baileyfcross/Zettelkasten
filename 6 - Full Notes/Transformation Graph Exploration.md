# Transformation Graph Exploration

Transformation graph exploration chooses which derived dataset nodes to expand and evaluate. Breadth-oriented, depth-oriented, and global policies allocate a finite search budget differently across the graph.

Local exploration can refine a promising branch, while broader exploration reduces the risk of early commitment. Caching, duplicate detection, and performance estimates make the traversal more efficient.
