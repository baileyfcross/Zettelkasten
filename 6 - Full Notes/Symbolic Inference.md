# Symbolic Inference

Symbolic inference manipulates probability expressions before assigning numerical values. It applies identities such as normalization, factorization, and marginalization to simplify a Bayesian question algebraically.

The result may be a closed form or a smaller expression for later numerical evaluation. Symbolic work is especially valuable when the same model form will be queried repeatedly with different parameter values.
