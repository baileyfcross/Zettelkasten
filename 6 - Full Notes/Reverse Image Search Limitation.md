# Reverse Image Search Limitation

Reverse image search is limited by what a provider has indexed and by how strongly an uploaded query resembles stored images. A failed search does not show that a photograph is original; the source may be private, newly published, heavily edited, or absent from the index.

Uploading a sensitive image also discloses evidence to the search provider and may create privacy or investigative risks. The method is therefore most informative when it finds a credible match, while a nonmatch must remain an inconclusive result.
