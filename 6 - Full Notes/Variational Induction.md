# Variational Induction

Variational induction infers causal structure from systematic changes in circumstances and effects. It develops the logic behind the [[Method of Difference]] while allowing evidence to accumulate across imperfect observations and experiments.

A useful method should reason largely from facts, distinguish causal factors from background conditions, and revise confidence as new variations are observed. Its goal is reliable prediction and manipulation, not merely description of repeated correlations.
