# Wire Removal with Clean Plates

To remove a visible support rig, roto its changing footprint as a window and reveal a matched clean plate behind it. The clean plate must be tracked or animated with the original shot, and the roto edge feathered enough to hide a seam. The book's bungee example also repairs harness marks on the performer with cloning, copied and warped body material, and a small amount of restored motion blur. One replacement plate cannot solve every occlusion; some areas require independent paint or reconstruction. See [[Difference Keying with a Clean Plate]].
