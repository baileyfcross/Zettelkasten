# R Mathematical Plot Annotation

R mathematical plot annotation uses expression syntax to place Greek letters, subscripts, superscripts, fractions, and formulas in titles, axes, and labels. The annotation is parsed as mathematical notation rather than printed as an ordinary character string.

The notation should communicate the quantity actually plotted and remain readable at the final export size. Complex expressions are most useful when they replace ambiguous plain text, not when they decorate a graph with unnecessary symbolic detail.
