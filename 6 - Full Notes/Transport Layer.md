# Transport Layer

The transport layer delivers data between endpoint processes and determines what reliability, ordering, flow management, segmentation, and connection behavior the transport protocol supplies. TCP and UDP represent different choices within this responsibility.

Transport does not decide the business meaning of an HTTP request or the route between networks. It supplies the delivery service on which those higher-level interactions depend.
