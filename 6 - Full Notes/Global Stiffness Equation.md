# Global Stiffness Equation

The global stiffness equation expresses the assembled finite element problem in matrix form, commonly as a global characteristic matrix times a nodal unknown vector equals a global load vector.

It describes the behavior of the full discretized domain rather than a single element. After boundary conditions are imposed, solving the reduced or modified system yields the unknown nodal field values.
