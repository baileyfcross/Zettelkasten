# Image Formation Pipeline

The image formation pipeline begins with the scene and its illumination, continues through camera optics and a light-sensitive sensor, and ends with in-camera processing and file encoding. Each stage imposes physical, geometric, or statistical regularities on the resulting pixels.

[[Digital Image Forensics]] works backward through this pipeline. A composite may preserve plausible objects while disrupting their shared light, perspective, interpolation correlations, sensor noise, or JPEG history. Modeling the stages separately helps an analyst choose evidence that is relevant to the suspected alteration.
