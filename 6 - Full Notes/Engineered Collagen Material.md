# Engineered Collagen Material

An engineered collagen material is built from collagen produced or organized outside an animal's intact tissue. Fibroblasts can secrete collagen into layered sheets that are harvested and processed into a structural consumer material.

Fiber organization, density, crosslinking, remaining cells, moisture, and thickness determine whether it achieves the strength and feel of natural hide.
