# Evidence

Evidence is the set of variable values treated as known when a probabilistic question is instantiated. Conditioning on that information restricts inference to joint assignments compatible with the observation.

In Bayes theorem, the evidence probability also serves as the normalization across competing hypotheses. Evidence may be hard, fixing a value, or soft, supplying a distribution that expresses uncertain observation.
