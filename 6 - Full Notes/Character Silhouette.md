# Character Silhouette

A character silhouette is the outer shape by which a game character can be recognized without interior detail. Different silhouettes let players distinguish behaviors even when color, texture, or facial features are hard to see.

Silhouette matters most when several moving characters share a scene and rapid identification affects choice. Hair, limbs, weapons, shields, and proportions can all make a role legible while reinforcing [[Game Character Design|characterization]].
