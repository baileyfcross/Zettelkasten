# Instructional Scaffolding

Instructional scaffolding supplies the amount of support a learner needs at the current stage and removes that support as competence grows. The help is temporary structure, not a permanent replacement for player judgment.

A well-scaffolded game introduces mechanics gradually, gives immediate practice, and uses evidence of skill to reduce prompts or increase challenge. Too little support overwhelms novices; support that remains after it becomes redundant can create the [[Expertise Reversal Effect]].
