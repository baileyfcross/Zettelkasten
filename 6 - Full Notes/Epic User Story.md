# Epic User Story

An epic is a user story too large or uncertain to complete within one Sprint. It identifies a meaningful area of value while postponing premature detail.

As it moves toward implementation, the epic is explored and divided through [[Story Splitting]]. The resulting smaller stories should preserve useful player-facing outcomes rather than becoming isolated discipline tasks.
