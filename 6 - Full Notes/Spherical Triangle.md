# Spherical Triangle

A spherical triangle is a region on a sphere bounded by three arcs of [[Great Circle]]s. Its side lengths are measured as central angles, and its vertex angles are angles between the corresponding great-circle planes. Unlike a Euclidean triangle, its interior-angle sum is greater than $180^\circ$.
