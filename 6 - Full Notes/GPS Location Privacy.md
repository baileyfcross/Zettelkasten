# GPS Location Privacy

GPS satellites broadcast timing and orbital information; they do not receive or store a user's location. Privacy risk arises when a receiver is coupled to a communicating device whose apps, employer, service provider, or government records and analyzes the resulting position. The distinction separates the positioning infrastructure from the organizations that collect a location trace.

Location can enable emergency alerts, navigation, fleet management, and personal safety while also exposing travel, work routines, associations, and behavior. Meaningful governance must therefore address collection, retention, sharing, consent, and legal access rather than treating the mere calculation of coordinates as the whole privacy problem.
