# Linear Regression Confidence Interval

A linear regression confidence interval gives a range of coefficient values compatible with the data and model at a stated confidence level. It is formed from the estimate and its standard error under the fitted model's sampling assumptions.

An interval containing zero does not support a nonzero association at the corresponding two-sided significance level. Its width communicates precision more directly than a p-value and should be interpreted in the outcome's units.
