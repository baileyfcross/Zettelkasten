# Lorentz Force Law

The Lorentz force law gives the force on a charge as the sum of electric force and the cross product of its velocity with magnetic flux density. A volume form applies the same relationship to charge and current distributions.

It connects electromagnetic fields to mechanical action on matter. Device models use it to translate solved fields into forces on conductors, charges, or magnetic components.

The velocity-dependent magnetic term shows directly why the split into electric and magnetic force is frame-dependent. An observer moving with a charge can see a purely electric force where another observer sees both components. Covariantly, the [[Electromagnetic Field Tensor]] acting on [[Four-Velocity]] produces the electromagnetic [[Four-Force]].
