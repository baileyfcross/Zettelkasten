# Kinetic Theory of Gases

Kinetic theory models an ideal gas as many tiny particles in rapid random motion, with negligible long-range interactions and elastic collisions. Pressure results from momentum transfer when particles strike container walls. The model connects macroscopic variables in the [[Ideal Gas Equation]] to microscopic mass, number density, and mean-squared speed.
