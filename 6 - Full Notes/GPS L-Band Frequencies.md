# GPS L-Band Frequencies

The original GPS design broadcasts on two L-band carriers centered at 1575.42 MHz and 1227.6 MHz. The signals pass through the ionosphere rather than reflecting from it, but their travel times are delayed by an amount that depends on frequency. A dual-frequency receiver can compare the delays and estimate the ionospheric correction. Common carriers are shared among satellites by assigning distinguishable pseudorandom codes.
