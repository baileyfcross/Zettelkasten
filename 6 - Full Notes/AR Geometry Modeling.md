# AR Geometry Modeling

AR geometry modeling creates a digital geometric representation while the physical target remains visible in the modeling view. The creator can compare virtual points, planes, volumes, or surfaces directly with the object or environment they represent.

This immediate reference supports reconstruction in place, but mobile input is less precise than a desktop modeling setup. High-quality tracking, geometric constraints, and semi-automatic fitting can reduce the burden of specifying exact spatial values.
