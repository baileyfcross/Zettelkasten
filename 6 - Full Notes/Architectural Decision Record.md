# Architectural Decision Record

An architectural decision record captures the context, options, chosen approach, consequences, and reasoning behind a significant design choice. It preserves why the decision made sense at the time so future owners can distinguish an intentional tradeoff from an accidental implementation detail.

The record complements current architecture diagrams, which show what exists and how data moves but may not explain rejected alternatives or assumptions. ADRs support onboarding, audit, technical-debt review, and later replacement decisions. They should be versioned near the architecture they govern and updated through new decisions rather than silently rewritten as history changes.
