# Surface of Revolution

A surface of revolution is generated by rotating a profile curve around an axis. One parameter selects a point along the profile and a second parameter gives the rotation angle, producing a two-dimensional parametric surface from a one-dimensional generator.

If the profile lies in a plane containing the axis, its radial distance determines the circular path traced during rotation. Lines and conic curves generate familiar cylinders, cones, spheres, and related shapes, while a free-form spline produces a more general rotational body. A full turn closes the surface around the axis.
