# Capacitance

Capacitance measures how much charge a pair of conductors stores per unit potential difference: $C=Q/V$. Its SI unit is the farad. Capacitance depends on conductor geometry and the dielectric medium rather than on the instantaneous charge or voltage while the system remains linear.
