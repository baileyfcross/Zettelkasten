# AWS Organizational Unit

An AWS Organizational Unit is a logical group of accounts inside AWS Organizations. OUs can represent environment, business function, security posture, or another governance dimension, and they may be nested to form a hierarchy.

Policies attached to an OU inherit to member accounts and child OUs. The grouping should therefore express a real control boundary rather than merely copy an organizational chart, because moving an account can change the guardrails that apply to it.
