# Grace Hopper's Compiler Work

Grace Hopper led work on systems that made UNIVAC programming accessible through reusable routines and higher-level notation. Her career emphasized that computers should serve nonspecialists rather than preserve a priesthood of machine-code experts.

These early compiler efforts automated assembly of program components and helped establish programming languages as an interface between human intentions and hardware instructions.
