# Kicked-Heisenberg Error-Mitigation Benchmark

The paper proposes a one-dimensional brickwork Floquet circuit for a kicked isotropic Heisenberg model with inhomogeneous external fields. A correlated-pair initial state breaks translation symmetry at one central pair, and the target quantities include global parity, a separated two-qubit observable, and a connected correlator.

At interaction strength $J=\pi/4$, the entangling operation reduces to a swap-like dual-unitary evolution, so the state and selected observables remain analytically verifiable even as a generic matrix-product-state simulation becomes expensive. For 122 qubits and 30 Floquet steps, this point exposes the observable error caused by compression and supplies an anchor for testing the resource comparison in [[Universality-Based Practical Quantum Advantage]].
