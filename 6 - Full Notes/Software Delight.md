# Software Delight

Software delight occurs when a product creates valuable possibilities beyond what users expected. It reflects a producer’s understanding of the customer’s world and can inspire strong loyalty and advocacy.

Delight is contextual and temporary. Once a capability becomes familiar, expectations rise, so sustaining it requires continuing attention to changing practices rather than a one-time feature.
