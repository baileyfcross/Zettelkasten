# Beam

A beam is a structural member whose principal loading acts transverse to its longitudinal axis. Its response includes bending deflection, rotation, internal shear, and bending moment.

Beam theory reduces the three-dimensional member to a line model with cross-sectional properties. Finite element beam formulations approximate the transverse displacement field between nodes.
