# Quaternion Algebra

The quaternion algebra consists of numbers $q=a+bi+cj+dk$ with three imaginary units satisfying $i^2=j^2=k^2=-1$ and ordered products such as $ij=k$ while $ji=-k$. Multiplication is associative but not commutative.

Quaternion conjugation reverses the signs of the three imaginary components. Multiplying a quaternion by its conjugate produces the real norm $a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2$, so every nonzero quaternion has a multiplicative inverse. This makes the quaternions a four-dimensional real division algebra.

Unit quaternions form a rotation group closely related to [[SU(2) and Unit Quaternions|SU(2)]]. The source uses their antisymmetric component structure and a [[Quaternion Matrix Representation]] to compare quaternion rotations with the antisymmetric [[Electromagnetic Field Tensor]].
