# Position Vector

The position vector of a point is the [[Vector]] drawn from the coordinate origin to that point. Its components are the point's coordinates, so the point $(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ corresponds to the vector $(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ once an origin and coordinate basis have been chosen.

Position vectors turn geometric relations among points into vector algebra. In particular, subtracting the position vector of $A$ from that of $B$ gives the displacement from $A$ to $B$.
