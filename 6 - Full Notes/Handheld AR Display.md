# Handheld AR Display

A handheld AR display uses a mobile screen and camera as a movable video see-through window onto an augmented scene. Device tracking determines the camera pose, the captured image supplies the real view, and registered virtual information is composited on the screen.

Handheld devices are widely available and support touch input, but the user must hold and aim the screen. The display covers only a small portion of the surrounding field, and the camera's viewpoint differs from the user's eyes.
