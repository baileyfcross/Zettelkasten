# Web Content Management System

A web content management system lets editors create, organize, revise, and publish site content through an application rather than by changing source code. It typically separates structured content from templates that determine how that content is rendered.

CMS design balances editorial flexibility with a controlled information model. Content types, fields, media, permissions, routes, and presentation templates establish what editors can express and how reliably the site can deliver it.
