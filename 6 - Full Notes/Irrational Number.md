# Irrational Number

An irrational number is a real number that cannot be expressed as a quotient of two [[Integer]]s. Its decimal expansion is infinite and nonrepeating.

For example, (sqrt{2}) is irrational, so any finite calculator display of it is an approximation rather than an exact equality. The same distinction applies to (pi): values such as (3.14) or (22/7) can be close, but they are not equal to it. Rational and irrational numbers together form the [[Real Number]]s.
