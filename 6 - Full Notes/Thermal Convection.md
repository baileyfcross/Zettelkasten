# Thermal Convection

Thermal convection transfers energy between a solid surface and a surrounding fluid. The heat-flow rate is proportional to surface area, heat-transfer coefficient, and the difference between surface and ambient temperature.

In a finite element model, convection contributes both a coefficient to the thermal matrix and an ambient-temperature term to the load vector. It is a boundary condition rather than volumetric conduction.
