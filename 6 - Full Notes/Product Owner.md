# Product Owner

The Product Owner communicates the product vision and orders the [[Product Backlog]] to maximize the value produced by the team. This requires continual decisions about value, cost, risk, knowledge, and the needs of [[Stakeholder|stakeholders]].

The role is accountable for clear priorities and outcomes, not for prescribing how developers perform the work. A single coherent ownership point prevents competing local priorities from fragmenting the product.
