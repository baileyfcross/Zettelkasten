# Decision-Oriented Design Meeting

A decision-oriented design meeting has a defined purpose, prepared questions, appropriate participants, and an explicit record of conclusions and actions. It uses synchronous time for issues that need interaction rather than for reporting information that could be shared asynchronously.

Closing the meeting requires identifying what was decided, what remains open, who will act, and when the result will be reviewed. Without that conversion, discussion creates motion but not progress.
