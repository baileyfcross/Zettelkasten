# Cooperative Play Structures

Cooperative play asks participants to coordinate toward a shared outcome. Symmetrical cooperation gives players similar capabilities, asymmetrical cooperation divides capabilities by role, and symbiotic cooperation makes each participant depend on contributions others cannot supply.

A shared goal alone does not guarantee cooperation. The design must create interdependence, information exchange, or complementary action so that collaboration changes what the group can achieve.
