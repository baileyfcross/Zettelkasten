# Automated Information Collection

Automated information collection acquires data through networked services, sensors, transactions, or other systems with little direct human intervention. It determines which people, events, and environments become available for later analysis.

Legal evaluation should ask what is collected, from whom, under what authority, and with what notice. Collection is a distinct activity even when it is tightly coupled to [[Automated Information Processing]] or an [[Autonomous Physical System]].
