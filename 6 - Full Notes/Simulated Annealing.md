# Simulated Annealing

Simulated annealing is a randomized optimization method that always accepts an improving proposal and sometimes accepts a worsening proposal according to a temperature-dependent probability. Early acceptance of uphill moves helps the search escape a local minimum.

A cooling schedule gradually lowers the temperature so worsening moves become less likely and the search concentrates near promising states. Proposal scale, initial temperature, cooling rate, and stopping rule jointly determine the balance between global exploration and local refinement.
