# GRIDCLUS

GRIDCLUS partitions the data space into rectangular cells, identifies cells with sufficient support, and connects adjacent dense cells into clusters. Operating on cell summaries can reduce dependence on the number of individual records.

Grid resolution determines which local patterns are visible. Cells that are too coarse merge distinct structures, whereas cells that are too fine fragment density and increase the number of units the algorithm must manage.
