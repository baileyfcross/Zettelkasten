# Proximity Measure

A proximity measure quantifies the relationship between two observations or clusters. Similarity measures grow as objects become more alike; dissimilarity measures such as distance grow as they become less alike.

Euclidean, Manhattan, Minkowski, Chebyshev, power, and percent-disagreement measures emphasize different geometry or data types. Since clustering is built on proximity, the chosen measure determines which neighborhoods and shapes an algorithm can recognize.
