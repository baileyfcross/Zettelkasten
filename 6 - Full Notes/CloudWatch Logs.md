# CloudWatch Logs

CloudWatch Logs collects log events into log streams and log groups, where they can be retained, searched, filtered, and used to derive metrics. AWS services and installed agents can send application, operating-system, and service logs to this centralized store.

Retention settings control storage duration, while metric filters and subscription filters can turn selected events into alerts or downstream processing. Logs provide detailed evidence, but they are most useful when timestamps, identifiers, and structured fields make related events traceable.
