# A-B Playtest

An A-B playtest assigns different versions of one design element to separate participant groups and compares the resulting behavior. Holding other conditions stable helps distinguish the effects of competing interventions.

The method can be expanded with a group receiving both interventions and a control receiving neither. Its conclusions depend on adequate samples and careful measurement; a small numerical difference is not automatically a reliable design effect.
