# Data-Ink Ratio

The data-ink ratio compares the marks that communicate information with the total marks in a visual display. Decorative dimensions, borders, textures, and effects that do not clarify relationships lower the ratio and compete with the message.

Removing non-data ink usually strengthens a chart or slide, although a small amount of decoration can support comprehension or attention. The practical test is whether an element helps the audience see the evidence or merely consumes visual space.
