# Latitude-Nut Gyro Compensation

A latitude nut applies a deliberate real-wander tendency that opposes the expected apparent drift from Earth's rotation at a selected latitude. The adjustment can make a directional gyro accurate in that region but becomes imperfect after substantial north-south travel. It is one term in the broader error budget described by [[Earth-Rate and Transport Wander]].
