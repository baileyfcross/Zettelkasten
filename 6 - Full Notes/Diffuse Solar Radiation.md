# Diffuse Solar Radiation

Diffuse radiation is sunlight redirected by atmospheric molecules, vapor, dust, or clouds rather than arriving as a single direct beam. Ground-reflected diffuse light can also strike a tilted collector. It contributes to total [[Terrestrial Solar Radiation]], and a [[Flat-Plate Solar Collector]] can use it. Because it lacks one incoming direction, a mirror or lens cannot focus it like [[Beam Solar Radiation]].
