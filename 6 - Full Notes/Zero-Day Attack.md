# Zero-Day Attack

A zero-day attack exploits a vulnerability before defenders have had an effective opportunity to correct it or build a reliable known-pattern defense. The exploit can therefore operate during the interval between discovery, recognition, and remediation.

Because [[Signature-Based Threat Detection]] may not recognize a new technique, layered controls, anomaly detection, least privilege, segmentation, and rapid response help limit the damage of zero-day exploitation.
