# Story-Game

A story-game is a game in which choosing how an authored story proceeds is the primary verb. The player reads or observes a situation, selects from available responses, and follows the resulting [[Branching Story|branch]].

The form descends from gamebooks and can make unavailable options meaningful as well as available ones. Even when choices are constrained, they let players express preferences and search for the version of the story they want to experience.
