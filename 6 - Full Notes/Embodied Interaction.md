# Embodied Interaction

Embodied interaction is interaction with computer systems that exist within physical and social reality and make use of that fact. It draws on people's existing experience with physical artifacts, space, time, and social conversation.

Meaning is created, manipulated, and shared through engaged interaction with these embodied phenomena. [[Tangible User Interface|Tangible interfaces]] embody digital information in physical objects, while [[Social Computing]] uses computer systems as a medium or focus for social relationships.

The concept is closely related to [[Activity Theory]] because both treat interaction as something situated in a larger real-world context.
