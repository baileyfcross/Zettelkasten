# Characteristic of a Ring

The characteristic of a nontrivial ring with identity is the least positive integer n for which n copies of the identity add to zero, if such an integer exists. If none exists, the characteristic is zero.

It is the additive order of the ring identity and controls how integer arithmetic is represented in the ring.
