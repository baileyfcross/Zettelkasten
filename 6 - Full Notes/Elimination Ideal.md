# Elimination Ideal

For an ideal I in K[x₁,…,xₙ], the ℓ-th elimination ideal is I ∩ K[xₗ₊₁,…,xₙ]. It contains exactly the consequences of I that do not involve the first ℓ variables.

Computing a Gröbner basis in lexicographic order exposes this intersection through the [[Elimination Theorem]]. Elimination ideals describe projections and produce implicit equations from parametrizations.
