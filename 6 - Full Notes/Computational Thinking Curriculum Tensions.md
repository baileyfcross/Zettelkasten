# Computational Thinking Curriculum Tensions

Computational thinking curricula face recurring tensions over programming languages, definitions of algorithms, use of machines, domain transfer, natural information processes, assessment, and the place of user satisfaction. Many disagreements arise because participants address different skill levels or purposes.

A productive curriculum makes these choices explicit rather than presenting one narrow framework as the whole field. It balances teachable foundations with pathways toward professional and domain-specific practice.
