# Graded Nakayama's Lemma

For a finitely generated graded module over a graded local ring, homogeneous elements generate the module when their images generate the quotient by the homogeneous maximal ideal. The dimension of that quotient over the residue field gives the minimal number of homogeneous generators.

If M = N + mM for a graded submodule N, then M = N. In particular, mM = M forces M = 0.
