# UML Association Relationship

A UML association represents a meaningful structural connection between classifiers whose instances know about or relate to one another. A solid line connects the classes, and navigability arrows can indicate which direction can follow the relationship.

Association is the general relationship; [[UML Aggregation Relationship|aggregation]] and [[UML Composition Relationship|composition]] add whole-part semantics. The model should use the weakest relationship that accurately states the intended ownership and lifetime.
