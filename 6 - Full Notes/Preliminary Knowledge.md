# Preliminary Knowledge

Preliminary knowledge is the information a modeler contributes before learning from the current dataset. In Bayesian programming it includes the choice of variables, the decomposition of their joint distribution, and the mathematical forms assigned to the factors.

A prior over parameters is only one small part of this knowledge. Changing the variables or conditional-independence assumptions can alter the model more fundamentally than changing a numerical prior.
