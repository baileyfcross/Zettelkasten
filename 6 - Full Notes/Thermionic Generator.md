# Thermionic Generator

A thermionic generator heats an emitter until it releases electrons, guides them across a narrow vacuum or ionized-vapor gap, and collects them on a cooler electrode. The electrons return through an external load, producing electrical power. It has no moving conversion parts, but high emitter temperature, [[Space-Charge Barrier]], heat rejection, and electrode materials govern its practical efficiency.
