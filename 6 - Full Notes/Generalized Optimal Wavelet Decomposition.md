# Generalized Optimal Wavelet Decomposition

Generalized optimal wavelet decomposition selects wavelet-processing choices to produce a useful representation for forecasting. In the source's framework, it is combined with [[Reinforcement Learning]] so the procedure can search for decomposition and thresholding decisions that improve predictive performance.

The method illustrates automated analytics as a coordinated pipeline: transform the signal, suppress noise, fit a learner, and compare in-sample and out-of-sample results.
