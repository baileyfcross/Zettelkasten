# Kinship Model of Personal Data

The kinship model of personal data treats data as the product of an enduring relationship rather than as a commodity that one party completely gives away. A self-tracking record remains connected to the person whose body or activity it describes and to the organization whose technology helped produce it.

The metaphor emphasizes reciprocal rights and responsibilities. The person may retain claims to access, explanation, or limits on harmful reuse, while the organization may have duties of security, stewardship, and maintenance. Those obligations can continue even when contractual possession sits with one party.

This model follows from [[Personal Data Co-Production]] and is especially useful when an exclusive ownership rule would ignore either the intimate subject of the data or the infrastructure that shaped the record.
