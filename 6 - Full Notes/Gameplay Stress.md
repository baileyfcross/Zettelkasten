# Gameplay Stress

Gameplay stress is the time pressure placed on a player while attempting a challenge. It increases difficulty by reducing the opportunity to plan, check work, or recover from an error.

Stress is distinct from intrinsic skill. Changing a timer, enemy pace, or response window can alter stress without changing the underlying action the player must perform.
