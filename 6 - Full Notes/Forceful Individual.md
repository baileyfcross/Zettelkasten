# Forceful Individual

A forceful individual is a node whose belief changes little during social exchange while strongly affecting regular neighbors. The asymmetry models a persistent authority, leader, or committed source rather than equal peer influence.

Local placement can matter more than global centrality. A forceful node inside or between weakly connected communities may shape their consensus differently from a broadly connected influencer.
