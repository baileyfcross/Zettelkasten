# Space-Time Pattern Comparison

Space-time pattern comparison asks whether locations share temporal levels, fluctuations, peaks, or seasonal behavior while also considering their geographic arrangement. A [[Space-Time XT Plot]] can expose persistent high and low stations, a [[Spatiotemporal Time-Series Plot]] shows common timing, and map panels locate the differences. The comparison should distinguish a network-wide event from a local anomaly and an enduring level difference from a brief excursion.
