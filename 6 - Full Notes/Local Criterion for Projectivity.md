# Local Criterion for Projectivity

A finitely presented module P is projective if and only if P_p is projective over R_p for every prime ideal p; equivalently, it is enough to test every maximal ideal. Under the source's Noetherian finite-generation hypotheses, the localized modules are free.

The criterion makes projectivity a [[Local Property]]. Finite presentation ensures that maps and relations behave well enough under localization to recover the global splitting.
