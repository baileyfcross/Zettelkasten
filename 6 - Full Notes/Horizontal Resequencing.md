# Horizontal Resequencing

Horizontal resequencing changes an interactive score by selecting which musical segment plays next on a timeline. A [[Game Music Control Input]] can branch from the current cue to another cue, crossfade between pieces, or insert a composed transition as the player's situation changes.

The technique gives each state its own tempo and harmonic design, but moving between states must preserve continuity. Immediate crossfades respond quickly while potentially interrupting a phrase; branching waits for a legal exit point; transitional forms bridge the cues more deliberately. Repeated routes also require enough variation that the same transition does not reveal the system too clearly.
