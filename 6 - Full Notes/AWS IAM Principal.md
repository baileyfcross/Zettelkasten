# AWS IAM Principal

An AWS IAM principal is an entity that can make an authenticated request. It may be the root user, an IAM user, an assumed role session, a federated identity, or an AWS service acting through an authorized role.

Permissions should attach to the role or group appropriate to the task rather than to a long-lived individual key. Identifying the principal is the first step in evaluating whether an action is allowed and recording who or what performed it.
