# Personalized Political Expression

Personalized political expression frames public issues through an individual's story, identity, humor, or creative style. Rather than repeating an organization's official slogan unchanged, participants make politics legible through their own lives.

Meme templates enable this personalization while retaining collective visibility. The Occupy testimonial format, for example, allowed different people to describe debt, unemployment, education, or family circumstances while closing with a shared identification. Personal variation and common structure reinforced one another.
