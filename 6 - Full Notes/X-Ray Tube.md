# X-Ray Tube

An X-ray tube accelerates electrons through tens of kilovolts toward a metal target, commonly tungsten. Rapid deceleration produces a continuous bremsstrahlung spectrum, while inner-shell transitions add characteristic lines. Most electron energy becomes heat rather than X-rays, so target material, cooling, filtration, voltage, and current shape safe operation.
