# Cauchy Integral Formula

Cauchy's integral formula recovers the value of an analytic function inside a closed contour from its values on the contour. For a point $z_0$ inside the curve, the kernel $1/(z-z_0)$ gives an integral proportional to $f(z_0)$.

Differentiating the formula yields contour expressions for every derivative of the function. Thus boundary data determine the analytic function throughout the interior, illustrating the strong rigidity produced by complex analyticity.
