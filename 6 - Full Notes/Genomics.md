# Genomics

Genomics studies the complete genetic material of organisms, including sequence, variation, organization, expression, and relationships among genes. It differs from studying one gene in isolation by treating the genome as an interconnected system.

High-throughput instruments and public projects make genomics data-intensive, connecting biological questions to large-scale storage, transfer, analysis, and provenance.
