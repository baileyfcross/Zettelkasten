# Capital Expenditure

Capital expenditure is money committed up front to acquire or create an asset that will be used over time. In computing, it includes purchasing servers, storage, networking, facilities, and other capacity before the organization knows exactly how much demand will occur.

Owning capacity can provide control, but unused headroom ties up funds and aging equipment can outlast the workload it was bought for. Cloud services often replace part of this commitment with [[Operating Expenditure]].
