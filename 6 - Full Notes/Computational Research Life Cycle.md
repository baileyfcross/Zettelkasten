# Computational Research Life Cycle

The computational research life cycle connects individual exploration, collaboration, production-scale execution, publication, and education. Reproducibility weakens when each phase uses disconnected tools and results are transferred manually between prototypes, production programs, figures, and manuscripts.

An integrated practice preserves code, data, decisions, and outputs while work moves forward and backward through these phases. Version control, [[Dependency-Aware Research Execution]], tests, and a [[Computational Lab Notebook]] reduce the gaps. Reproducibility is therefore a property designed into daily work from exploration onward, not an archive assembled only at submission time.
