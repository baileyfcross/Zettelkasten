# Hierarchical Haar Wavelet Transform

A Haar transform on a binary dendrogram replaces child vectors with a parent smooth and signed detail. The smooth summarizes the two children, while the detail records their difference. Repeating this at successive merges gives one global smooth and a collection of branch-level details.

Following the correct route from root to leaf and adding or subtracting its details exactly reconstructs the original observation in the book's construction. The hierarchy is therefore not only a display of groups; it is an operational multiscale encoding of the data.
