# Puzzle Insight

Puzzle insight is a candidate understanding that reorganizes what the solver has learned and points toward a solution. It may arrive as certainty, as an intuition about the relevant direction, or as recognition that an assumed rule or boundary was never actually required.

An insight is not yet proof. It becomes useful when the puzzle permits a [[Puzzle Solution Attempt]] that tests its prediction and provides interpretable feedback. Designs that hide the relationship between attempt and result make genuine insight difficult to distinguish from guessing.
