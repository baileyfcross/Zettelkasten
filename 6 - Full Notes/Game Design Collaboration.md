# Game Design Collaboration

Game design collaboration treats the design as knowledge produced with programmers, artists, audio specialists, and testers through a working game. Designers communicate known vision and important unknowns, then participate directly in the team discovering feasible behavior.

Assigning a designer to each core-mechanic team shortens conversations and lets implementation evidence reshape the design. Documents support this collaboration when they record current knowledge rather than speculative detail.
