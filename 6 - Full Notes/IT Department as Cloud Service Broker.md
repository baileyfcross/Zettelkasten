# IT Department as Cloud Service Broker

An IT department acting as a cloud service broker curates available services, negotiates or oversees provider relationships, integrates capabilities, and helps business units choose approved offerings. Its focus moves from owning every system to governing a service portfolio.

This role can give departments more autonomy while retaining shared standards for security, interoperability, reporting, and continuity. The catalog must respond to real needs or users may create [[Shadow IT]] outside the broker's view.
