# Integration by Parts

Integration by parts follows from the product rule and rewrites an integral as $\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$. It is useful when differentiating one factor makes it simpler while the other factor can be integrated directly.

The choice of $u$ and $dv$ determines whether the transformation is productive. Repeated integration by parts handles products involving powers, exponentials, or trigonometric functions and can also produce recurrence relations among related integrals.
