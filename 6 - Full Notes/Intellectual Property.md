# Intellectual Property

Intellectual property is a legal category for intangible products of human creativity and invention. Because the same expression or method can be used by several people without being physically consumed, law grants limited exclusive rights intended to reward creation while eventually preserving public access.

Different protections cover different interests. Copyright protects a fixed expression such as source code, patents can protect qualifying inventions or processes, and material whose protection has ended or never applied may be used from the [[Public Domain]].
