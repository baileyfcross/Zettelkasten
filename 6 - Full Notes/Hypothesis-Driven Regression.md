# Hypothesis-Driven Regression

Hypothesis-driven regression fits a model to evaluate a relationship specified before the outcome analysis. The exposure and outcome remain central, while candidate covariates are included to address plausible confounding rather than to maximize prediction alone.

At least one representation of the exposure stays in every model iteration so the final model can answer the hypothesis. If its adjusted estimate is not statistically or substantively meaningful, the appropriate conclusion is that the data do not support the proposed relationship.
