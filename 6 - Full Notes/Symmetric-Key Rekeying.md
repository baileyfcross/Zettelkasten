# Symmetric-Key Rekeying

Symmetric-key rekeying replaces a shared encryption or authentication key according to an agreed update process. Regular change limits the amount of data exposed if one key is compromised and can support stream-by-stream verification.

Resource-constrained sensing systems need updates that are faster than public-key operations while remaining synchronized and resistant to lost, replayed, or reordered messages.
