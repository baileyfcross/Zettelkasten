# Cylindrical Surface

A cylindrical surface is formed by all lines parallel to a fixed direction that pass through a generating curve. The parallel lines are its generators, so the surface need not have a circular cross section.

In a Cartesian equation, a missing coordinate often signals a cylinder whose generators are parallel to that coordinate axis. A [[Surface Parameterization]] can combine one parameter along the generating curve with another along the generator direction.
