# Locally Free Module

A module is locally free when its localization M_p is a free R_p-module at each relevant prime ideal p. The rank may be read from these localized free modules.

For a finitely generated module over a Noetherian ring, local freeness at every prime characterizes projectivity. This connects [[Projective Module]]s to the pointwise structure of the ring's spectrum.
