# Genomic Data Acquisition

Genomic data acquisition converts biological samples into digital measurements through sequencing, genotyping, or related assays. Experimental design, sample handling, instrument settings, controls, and quality scores influence every later analysis.

Metadata must preserve these conditions so technical variation can be distinguished from biological variation.
