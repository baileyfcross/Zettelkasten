# Mean Difference Operator

The mean operator $\mu$ averages values on the two sides of a central location:

$$\mu=\frac12\left(E^{1/2}+E^{-1/2}\right),$$

where $E$ is the [[Shift Operator]]. It accompanies the [[Central Difference Operator]] in central interpolation formulas, allowing adjacent odd or even differences to be represented at a common center.
