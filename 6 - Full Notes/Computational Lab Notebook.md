# Computational Lab Notebook

A computational lab notebook records commands, explanations, code, results, figures, and decisions close to the moment of investigation. Unlike an unstructured shell history, it selects and organizes the steps needed to understand how an analysis developed.

An interactive notebook can preserve executable cells and their outputs, while environment snapshots can retain broader state. Neither should become the only authoritative source for long-lived software: reusable logic can move into tested modules, and the notebook can retain the narrative of exploration. This connects daily work in the [[Computational Research Life Cycle]] to later communication.
