# Aitken Interpolation

Aitken interpolation builds higher-order interpolants recursively from lower-order ones. It combines approximations based on overlapping subsets of the data until the value associated with all selected nodes is obtained.

The method evaluates an [[Interpolating Polynomial]] at a desired point without first expanding all polynomial coefficients, making the recursive table itself the computation.
