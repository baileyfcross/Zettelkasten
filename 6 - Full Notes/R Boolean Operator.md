# R Boolean Operator

R Boolean operators negate logical values or combine conditions through conjunction and disjunction. Element-wise forms evaluate corresponding entries across vectors, while short-circuit forms evaluate a single control-flow condition and may skip the second expression.

Parentheses should make compound conditions visible because comparison and Boolean precedence can otherwise obscure the intended test. A combined condition is only as well-defined as its treatment of missing logical values.
