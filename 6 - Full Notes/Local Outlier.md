# Local Outlier

A local outlier is unusual relative to its immediate neighborhood even if it is not extreme with respect to the full data set. Dense and sparse regions can therefore use different effective standards.

Local methods compare a point's neighborhood density or reachability with those of nearby points. They reveal anomalies that global averages can hide.
