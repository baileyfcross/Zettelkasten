# Excel Loan Payment Function

The Excel PMT function calculates the periodic payment required for a loan or annuity under a constant interest rate and fixed number of periods. Its inputs include the periodic rate, number of payments, present value, and optional future value and payment timing.

A monthly loan calculation should divide an annual rate into a monthly rate and express the term as a number of monthly periods. The returned sign reflects the direction of cash flow relative to the borrowed amount. PMT describes the modeled principal-and-interest payment; additional charges such as taxes, insurance, and fees require separate treatment.
