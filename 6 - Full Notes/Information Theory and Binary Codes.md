# Information Theory and Binary Codes

Information theory gave a mathematical basis for encoding, transmitting, and recovering messages. Binary digits became a common measure because two distinguishable states can express alternatives and combine into larger symbol sets.

The theory developed alongside early digital computers rather than as a consequence of them. Together they made reliable communication and machine representation parts of the same digital system.
