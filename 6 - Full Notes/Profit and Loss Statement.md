# Profit and Loss Statement

A profit and loss statement estimates revenue, costs, and the resulting profit or loss over a period. For a game project, it makes assumptions about sales, price, platform shares, development expense, operation, marketing, and other costs explicit.

Because important values are uncertain, the statement should be tested across several plausible scenarios rather than presented as a single forecast. Profitability also does not guarantee solvency when the timing of receipts and expenses creates a [[Project Cash Flow]] shortage.
