# Marginalization Rule

The marginalization rule removes a variable by summing its joint probabilities across every possible value, or by integrating when the variable is continuous. For discrete $Y$, $P(X)=\sum_y P(X,Y=y)$.

In a Bayesian question, free variables are not assumed irrelevant; their possible values are accumulated. The computational difficulty often comes from the size of this summed or integrated space.
