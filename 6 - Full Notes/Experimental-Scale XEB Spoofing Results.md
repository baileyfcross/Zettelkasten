# Experimental-Scale XEB Spoofing Results

An unoptimized partitioning algorithm combined with top-$k$ post-processing reached XEB $1.85\times10^{-4}$ for Google's 53-qubit depth-20 circuit, or 8.26 percent of the reported experimental value, in about 0.6 seconds on one V100 GPU. It reached $8.18\times10^{-5}$ for USTC's 56-qubit depth-20 circuit, or 12.4 percent of the experiment, also in about 0.6 seconds.

For USTC's 60-qubit depth-24 circuit, the algorithm reached $7.75\times10^{-6}$, or 2.12 percent of the experimental XEB, in about 1.5 seconds. These results did not reproduce the full experimental scores, but they showed that a benchmark-specific classical shortcut could capture a nontrivial fraction with desktop-scale resources. The relevant verification question is therefore not whether XEB is hard for exact simulation, but how high a score the strongest targeted classical strategy can reach.
