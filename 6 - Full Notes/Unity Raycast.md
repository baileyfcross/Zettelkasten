# Unity Raycast

A Unity raycast queries the physics world along a directed line and reports whether it intersects a collider. It can support aiming, selection, line-of-sight tests, ground checks, and other interactions that do not require a persistent volume.

A ray has an origin and direction, and practical queries often include a maximum distance or layer mask. The result can identify the hit object and location. Because raycasts test [[Unity Collider|colliders]], an object's visible mesh alone does not make it detectable.
