# Azure Recovery Objective Model

The Azure recovery objective model separates target and measured recovery. Recovery Time Objective states the acceptable service-restoration duration, and Recovery Point Objective states acceptable data loss. Recovery Time Actual and Recovery Point Actual record what a test or real outage achieved. Comparing actuals with objectives turns availability claims into evidence and exposes whether backups, replication, automation, dependencies, and operational procedures satisfy the business requirement.
