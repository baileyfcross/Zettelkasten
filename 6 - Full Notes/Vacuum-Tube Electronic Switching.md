# Vacuum-Tube Electronic Switching

Vacuum tubes switch or amplify currents through electrons moving inside an evacuated enclosure. Replacing mechanical contacts with electronic motion allowed calculation and logic to operate hundreds of times faster.

Tubes consumed power, generated heat, and failed frequently, making large systems difficult to maintain. Even so, their speed enabled ENIAC, Colossus, and the first generation of electronic computers.
