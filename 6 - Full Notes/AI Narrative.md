# AI Narrative

An AI narrative is a recurring story that frames what artificial intelligence is likely to become and how people should respond to it. Stories of obedient servants, rebellious creations, machine domination, or technological salvation shape public expectations before a specific system is evaluated.

Narratives can direct attention and policy. A future-oriented story about [[Superintelligence]] may highlight the [[AI Control Problem]], while also distracting from harms caused by present systems. Narratives differ across cultures, so treating one familiar Western story as a universal forecast can hide alternative relationships between humans and technology.
