# Raster Time Slicing

Raster time slicing selects one or more layers from a [[Time-Indexed Raster Stack]] according to their temporal positions. Each slice is a spatial snapshot, so comparing slices reveals movement or change without mixing cells from different times. A few slices can be shown as small multiples, while a long sequence may require [[Monthly Raster Aggregation]] or animation. Shared color limits are necessary for magnitude comparison across slices.
