# Ocean Modifier Surface Generation

The Ocean modifier procedurally generates an animated water surface from parameters such as time, scale, choppiness, and resolution. Because the surface remains modifier-driven, its motion and detail can be adjusted without manually editing waves into a base mesh.

Higher resolution improves detail at a computational cost, while excessive choppiness can produce implausible or self-intersecting forms. The parameters must be balanced for the intended shot scale and viewing distance.
