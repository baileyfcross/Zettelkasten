# Prime Ideals Under Localization

The [[Prime Ideal]]s of S⁻¹R correspond exactly to prime ideals of R that are disjoint from the [[Multiplicatively Closed Set]] S. A source prime extends to a localized prime, and contracting returns the original prime.

Any prime meeting S becomes the whole localized ring because it contains an element made invertible. This correspondence explains why localization restricts the spectrum to primes compatible with the chosen denominators.
