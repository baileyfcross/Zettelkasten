# Electron Energy Band

When many atoms form a crystalline solid, interactions spread their individual electron energy levels into bands of allowed states. The upper occupied band is the valence band; the next available band is the conduction band. Their occupancy and separation explain why a material conducts readily, acts as an insulator, or needs excitation to conduct as a semiconductor. See [[Semiconductor Band Gap]].
