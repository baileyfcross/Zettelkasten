# Fourier Series

A Fourier series represents a periodic function as a sum of constant, sine, and cosine terms at integer multiples of a fundamental frequency. Orthogonality over one period determines each coefficient independently.

The expansion turns complicated periodic behavior into simple harmonic components. Its convergence at a discontinuity approaches the midpoint of the jump under standard conditions, while finite truncations can display persistent oscillatory overshoot near that jump.
