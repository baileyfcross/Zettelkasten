# Quantile-Based Grouping

Quantile-based grouping divides a continuous variable at estimated percentiles, such as quartiles. The cutpoints are first calculated from valid observations, then used to assign each value to an ordered category.

This approach produces groups tied to the observed distribution rather than fixed clinical thresholds. It can expose nonlinear or threshold relationships, but it also discards within-group information and makes the resulting categories sample-dependent.
