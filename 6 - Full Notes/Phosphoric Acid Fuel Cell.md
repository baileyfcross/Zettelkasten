# Phosphoric Acid Fuel Cell

A phosphoric acid fuel cell uses phosphoric acid as its [[Fuel Cell Electrolyte]] and operates at a higher temperature than a polymer-membrane cell. The source presents it for buildings, small power plants, and combined heat-and-power supply. Electricity-only efficiency and total useful-energy efficiency are distinct: reclaiming its heat raises the latter, but does not turn that heat into extra electrical output.
