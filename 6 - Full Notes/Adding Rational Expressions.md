# Adding Rational Expressions

Adding or subtracting rational expressions requires a shared denominator, just as with numerical fractions. Factor the denominators, construct a [[Common Denominator]] containing the needed factors, rewrite each numerator, combine the numerators, and simplify if possible.

For denominators (x-3) and (x+2), the product ((x-3)(x+2)) is a common denominator unless factors are already shared. Domain restrictions from every original denominator remain in force. Expanding the combined numerator may expose [[Like Terms]], while factoring may expose later cancellation.
