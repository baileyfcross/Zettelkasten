# Big Ball of Mud

A Big Ball of Mud is a software structure whose boundaries and responsibilities have become unclear through accumulated features, cross-references, and expedient changes. It is not caused by a database alone; it grows when distinct parts of a business are placed in one model and their interactions remain implicit. The result is hard to explain, test, or change locally. [[Linguistic Boundary]] discovery and explicit [[Bounded Context]]s provide a path toward separating meaningful system parts.
