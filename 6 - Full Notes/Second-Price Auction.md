# Second-Price Auction

A second-price auction awards the item to the highest secret bidder but charges the second-highest bid. Under the rational model, bidding one's actual value is an equilibrium because bidding higher risks paying more than the item is worth and bidding lower risks losing a profitable purchase.

Real auction behavior often departs from that strategy through overbidding and last-second changes. The gap illustrates why a rational incentive analysis is a useful baseline but not a sufficient prediction of player behavior.
