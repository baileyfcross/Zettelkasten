# DevOps AI Return on Investment

DevOps AI return on investment compares measurable engineering outcomes before and after controlled adoption. Relevant measures include workflow authoring time, infrastructure iteration speed, failure recovery time, log-triage duration, documentation effort, suggestion acceptance, modification rates, and rework.

Faster output is not sufficient if quality, security, or compliance regresses. Licensing, compute, training, review, and correction costs belong in the comparison, and cost is best measured per successful accepted outcome rather than per request. A controlled pilot establishes a baseline, tests value, adjusts guardrails, and scales only when the improvement is defensible.
