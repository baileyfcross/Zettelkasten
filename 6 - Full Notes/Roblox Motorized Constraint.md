# Roblox Motorized Constraint

A Roblox hinge can be configured as a motor so it actively drives rotation rather than merely permitting it. Angular velocity establishes the target rotational speed, and motor torque determines how strongly the constraint tries to reach that target.

A motorized assembly still relies on its mechanical setup. The two attachments need a shared straight axis, the moving geometry must be unanchored, and the supporting geometry must provide a stable reference. The motor therefore adds powered behavior to the structure established by [[Roblox Attachments and Constraints]] rather than replacing careful alignment.
