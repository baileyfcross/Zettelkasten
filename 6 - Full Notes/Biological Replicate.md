# Biological Replicate

A biological replicate is an independently sampled organism, tissue, culture, or other biological unit exposed to the study conditions. Variation among these units includes genuine biological heterogeneity as well as measurement error.

The source finds substantially greater gene-expression variability among biological replicates than among [[Technical Replicate|technical replicates]]. Inference about a biological population must therefore model the biological level rather than relying only on repeated measurements of one specimen.
