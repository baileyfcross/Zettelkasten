# Market Luck in Game Development

Market luck in game development is the irreducible role of timing, visibility, audience attention, and other uncertain conditions in commercial success. Good design and sustained work matter, but they do not make success deterministically reproducible.

Survivorship and selection bias make successful teams more visible and invite observers to treat their path as a formula. A responsible plan studies successes without assuming that copying their steps removes market uncertainty, and it protects the developer from relying on one favorable outcome.
