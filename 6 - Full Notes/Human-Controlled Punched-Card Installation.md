# Human-Controlled Punched-Card Installation

Before stored programs, people controlled information-processing installations by moving card decks, changing switches, plugging wires, and deciding which operation came next. The machinery automated local steps while humans maintained the overall sequence.

This arrangement exposed automatic control as a missing component. Electronic computers absorbed the coordinating plan into programs, reducing the need for an operator to direct every transition.
