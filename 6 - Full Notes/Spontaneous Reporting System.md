# Spontaneous Reporting System

A spontaneous reporting system collects voluntary reports of suspected adverse drug events from clinicians, patients, manufacturers, and other reporters. It can reveal rare or unexpected problems after a product reaches a large population.

The data does not directly provide incidence. Underreporting, publicity, duplicate reports, missing exposure counts, and uncertainty about causality all affect the observed frequencies. Signals from the system identify drug-event pairs for follow-up; they should not be interpreted as event rates or definitive causal conclusions.
