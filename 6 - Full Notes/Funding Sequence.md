# Funding Sequence

A funding sequence is the ordered plan for financing and running the subprojects in a portfolio. It must respect dependencies and available capital while determining when each component's costs and benefits occur.

Comparing feasible sequences can reveal that beginning with an early-value component funds later work or that delaying a costly dependency destroys expected return.
