# Blender Sculpting Workspace

Blender's Sculpting workspace opens a 3D Viewport already configured in Sculpt mode. It reduces setup and gives the brush-based tools more room than a general-purpose layout.

Sculpting usually benefits from a denser starting mesh than Blender's default cube because the brush needs geometry to displace. The workspace supplies the environment, while the artist still chooses a mesh density and topology strategy appropriate to the intended detail.
