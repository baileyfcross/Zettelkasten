# Fuzzy Cluster Center

A fuzzy cluster center is a prototype computed from observations weighted by their membership degrees. Strong members influence the center more than weak members.

The center may not be an observed point. Fuzzy objective-function methods alternate between estimating these prototypes and updating memberships from the distances to them.
