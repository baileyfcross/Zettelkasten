# Memeplex

A memeplex is a group of mutually supporting memes that tend to circulate together. Religious systems, political ideologies, and other cultural formations can persist not because every component is independently compelling, but because the components reinforce one another as a package.

The concept shifts analysis away from isolated fragments and toward linked cultural systems. Online, recurring formats, conventions, jokes, and community expectations can similarly form clusters that make each element easier to recognize and reproduce, connecting the memeplex idea to [[Meme Genre]] and [[Meme Literacy]].
