# Vector Reflection

Vector reflection produces the direction obtained when an incoming vector bounces across a surface normal. It reverses the component pointing into the surface while preserving the component parallel to it.

With a normalized normal, the reflected vector can be derived from the incoming vector and their [[Dot Product]]. The operation supports effects such as ricochets, mirrored directions, and idealized light reflection.
