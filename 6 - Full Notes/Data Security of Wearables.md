# Data Security of Wearables

Data security of wearables protects the signals, stored records, accounts, and transmissions produced by devices worn on or near the body. Weak wireless design can let an unauthorized party detect a device or follow its location, while companion applications can send data to several domains without the wearer understanding the recipients.

Security is a prerequisite for privacy but not a substitute for it. Encryption and authentication can prevent unauthorized access, yet a fully secured service can still violate [[Contextual Integrity]] by making an unexpected authorized disclosure.

Protection should cover the entire path from sensing through phone, network, cloud storage, analysis, and export. A privacy policy alone is inadequate when the technical design permits inexpensive tracking or uncontrolled third-party transmission.
