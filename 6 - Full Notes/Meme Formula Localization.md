# Meme Formula Localization

Meme formula localization preserves the relational structure of a meme while replacing elements that do not fit the receiving culture. A creator may retain a two-part joke, character type, layout, or rhetorical reversal but substitute locally recognizable people and situations.

The Israeli adaptation of the Successful Black Man macro into Akiva illustrates this process. The visual and caption pattern remained recognizable, while the character and social cues were recast for local audiences. Localization thus reproduces a grammar rather than merely translating a finished sentence.
