# Exact Sequence

A sequence of modules and homomorphisms is exact at a module when the image of the incoming map equals the kernel of the outgoing map. It is exact when this equality holds at every position.

Exactness records both what enters and what becomes zero. It is stronger than merely requiring consecutive maps to compose to zero and is preserved by operations such as module localization.
