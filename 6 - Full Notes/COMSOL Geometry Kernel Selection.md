# COMSOL Geometry Kernel Selection

COMSOL geometry kernel selection chooses the engine that represents and operates on geometric entities. The built-in COMSOL kernel supports the base workflow, while an optional CAD kernel can preserve and manipulate forms imported through compatible CAD interfaces.

The kernel affects which primitives, conversions, repairs, and interoperability paths are available. Converting between kernels may change entity identities or topology, so dependent selections and physics assignments require review.
