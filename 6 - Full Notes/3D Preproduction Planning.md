# 3D Preproduction Planning

3D preproduction converts a desired result into references, tests, design decisions, and a sequence of manageable tasks before expensive asset creation begins. It exposes missing skills and technical risks while they can still be explored with quick prototypes.

Planning does not eliminate production problems, but it reduces avoidable rework and gives the team a visible measure of progress. A useful plan stays concrete enough to guide modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, and delivery without pretending that later discoveries will never require revision.
