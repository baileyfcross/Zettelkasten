# Excel Workday and Month Date Functions

Excel EDATE shifts a date by a number of months, and EOMONTH returns the last day of a shifted month. WORKDAY advances or retreats by working days, while NETWORKDAYS counts working days between two dates. Workday functions exclude weekends and can also exclude a supplied holiday range.

These functions encode calendar rules more reliably than adding a fixed number of days. The holiday range should contain genuine date values and remain stable when formulas are copied. Month-end calculations are useful for reporting periods and due dates, while workday calculations suit schedules whose elapsed calendar days differ from operational days.
