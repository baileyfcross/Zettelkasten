# Relational Moral Status

Relational moral status treats moral consideration as something formed through social interaction rather than derived only from hidden internal properties. A robot can enter patterns of care, address, dependence, and recognition that change how people understand what may be done to it.

The approach explains why status can differ across settings and cultures and why people may respond morally to a machine without evidence of [[Machine Sentience]]. It also creates a risk of projection: designers can manufacture cues that invite attachment. Ethical analysis must therefore examine both the lived relationship and the power of those who designed its terms.
