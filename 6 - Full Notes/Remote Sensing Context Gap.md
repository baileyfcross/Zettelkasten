# Remote Sensing Context Gap

A remote sensor records properties that its instrument can detect, but those measurements may omit the social or causal context needed for interpretation. An image can show that a forest was cleared without revealing ownership or intent, and nighttime light can indicate electricity use without directly measuring population.

This context gap makes plausible visual inference unsafe when the missing variable matters. Analysts should combine imagery with domain knowledge, other data sources, and [[Ground Truthing]] rather than treating sensor output as a complete account of a place. Higher resolution may reveal more detail while leaving the underlying explanation just as uncertain.
