# Synthetic Media

Synthetic media are images, audio, video, or text generated or altered so that fabricated content can appear authentic. AI lowers the effort required to imitate people, invent events, and automate persuasive communication.

The ethical harm includes more than a single deception. When fabricated media become common, genuine evidence can also be dismissed as fake, weakening trust in communication and public institutions. Bots can amplify the effect by simulating social agreement or overwhelming discussion. Disclosure of artificial identity and methods for verification help, but the broader problem concerns the social conditions under which people decide what to believe.
