# Dollar Auction

The dollar auction sells a prize while requiring both the highest and second-highest bidders to pay their final bids. Once two bidders are committed, each next bid can appear cheaper than quitting and losing the amount already offered, so escalation can pass the prize's value.

The local decision to bid again can be rational even though entering the contest was dangerous. The game exposes how sunk commitments and changing marginal comparisons can create a path to a collectively bad outcome.
