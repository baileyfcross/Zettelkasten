# Otto Cycle Engine Efficiency

The ideal Otto cycle models a spark-ignition piston engine with compression, heat addition through combustion, expansion, and exhaust-related heat rejection. Compression and expansion are treated as reversible adiabatic processes, and the enclosed pressure-volume area represents net work. Its ideal efficiency increases with compression ratio and depends on the ratio of specific heats, although real engines also incur friction, heat loss, and incomplete combustion.
