# Photoelectric Effect

The photoelectric effect emits electrons from a material when incident radiation exceeds a threshold frequency. Einstein modeled the light as photons of energy $hf$, giving maximum electron kinetic energy $K_{max}=hf-\phi$. Frequency controls electron energy, while intensity above threshold primarily controls the number of emitted electrons.
