# Hyperbolic Distance

Hyperbolic distance measures separation under a negatively curved geometry rather than ordinary Euclidean distance. Its formula depends on the chosen coordinate model, but isometries preserve the same underlying distance relationships.

For embedded networks, one distance can combine a node's radial popularity and angular similarity. Thresholding the distance can infer correlations or links, while comparing it to graph distance evaluates an embedding's distortion.
