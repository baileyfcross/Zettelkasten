# Personalized Advertising

Personalized advertising selects messages using information about an individual's behavior, characteristics, or inferred interests. Mobile and cross-platform records let advertisers tailor content more closely than broad audience categories allow.

Personalization can increase relevance, but it also depends on tracking and data exchange that consumers may not understand. The privacy issue concerns the full profile and reuse pathway, not only the final advertisement shown.
