# Random Row Inspection

Random row inspection samples records from across a dataset for manual review. Unlike only viewing the head or tail, it can reveal malformed values or unexpected categories located elsewhere in the file.

The sample supports, but does not replace, systematic validation of dimensions, classes, ranges, and missingness.
