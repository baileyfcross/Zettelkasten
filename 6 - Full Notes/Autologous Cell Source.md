# Autologous Cell Source

An autologous cell source uses cells taken from the same person who will receive the engineered tissue. Immune recognition is generally more favorable than with cells from an unrelated donor.

The source may still be limited by patient health, harvest size, expansion time, cost, and whether the required cell type can be obtained or differentiated reliably.
