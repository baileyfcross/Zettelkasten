# Quantile

A quantile divides an ordered distribution according to a cumulative probability. Quartiles are quantiles at 0.25, 0.50, and 0.75, while arbitrary probability values yield corresponding [[Percentile|percentiles]].

Quantiles are robust descriptions of position and useful inputs to [[Quantile-Based Grouping]]. Their calculation must specify how missing values are handled, because an unremoved missing value can prevent the estimate or silently change the analyzed subset.
