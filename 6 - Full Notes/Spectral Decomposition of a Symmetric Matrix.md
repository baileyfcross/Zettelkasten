# Spectral Decomposition of a Symmetric Matrix

Every real symmetric matrix has a factorization $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$, where $Q$ is orthogonal and $\Lambda$ is a real diagonal matrix. The columns of $Q$ are orthonormal eigenvectors, and the diagonal entries of $\Lambda$ are their eigenvalues.

This decomposition is an [[Orthogonal Diagonalization]]. It separates the transformation into independent orthogonal directions and shows directly why a symmetric matrix has real eigenvalues.
