# Profile Privacy

Profile privacy concerns personal attributes associated with an online account, including fields the user supplied and characteristics inferred from activity. Hiding a field is insufficient when posts, groups, locations, or friends make it predictable.

Defenses combine audience controls, data minimization, encryption, inference testing, and clear settings that help users understand indirect disclosure.
