# Permutation Matrix

A permutation matrix contains only zeros and ones, with exactly one one in each row and each column. Left multiplication permutes rows; right multiplication permutes columns.

Pivoting can therefore be recorded algebraically with a permutation matrix. An LU factorization with row exchanges is commonly expressed as $PA=LU$.

Complete sets of permutation matrices are closed under multiplication and provide concrete representations of finite groups. The multiplication relations, rather than the visual identities of the matrices, determine the represented group. For example, the two $2\times2$ permutation matrices represent the order-two [[Cyclic Group]].
