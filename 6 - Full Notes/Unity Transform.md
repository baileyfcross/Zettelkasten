# Unity Transform

A Unity Transform is the mandatory component that stores a GameObject's position, rotation, and scale. It also establishes parent-child relationships in the Hierarchy, causing a child's spatial state to be interpreted relative to and move with its parent.

Transform hierarchy provides both spatial composition and organizational structure. It can assemble a character, weapon, camera rig, or modular environment from separate objects, but nested transforms also compound scale and rotation. Predictable coordinate spaces therefore depend on deliberate parenting and consistent local transforms.
