# Data Center Energy Trading

Data center energy trading coordinates purchases, sales, or exchanges of electricity when local generation and storage do not exactly match computational demand. A center may buy during a deficit or sell surplus renewable production when prices justify it.

The decision couples market prices with workload forecasts, storage limits, carbon goals, and the cost of violating service commitments.
