# Responsive Image Art Direction

Art direction changes the composition, crop, or even subject of an image for different layouts. A narrow viewport may need a tight crop that keeps the important subject legible rather than a uniformly shrunken wide scene.

Because the pixel content differs, art direction requires author-controlled source alternatives, commonly ordered in a `picture` element. It is distinct from offering the same composition at several resolutions. See [[Art Direction Versus Resolution Switching]] and [[Picture Element Source Selection]].
