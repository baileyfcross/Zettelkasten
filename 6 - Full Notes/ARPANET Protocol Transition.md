# ARPANET Protocol Transition

ARPANET initially used a protocol designed for its limited network, then switched to TCP/IP in 1983 as scale and interconnection needs grew. The transition separated reliable message handling from movement of individual packets.

Changing a live network required participating hosts to adopt a coordinated standard. The successful migration created a foundation on which many different networks could interoperate.
