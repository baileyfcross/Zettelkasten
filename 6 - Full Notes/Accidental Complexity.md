# Accidental Complexity

Accidental complexity is difficulty introduced by the solution rather than by the underlying domain. Frameworks, databases, infrastructure, integration, and optimization can all add it. Some is unavoidable, but confusing it with [[Essential Complexity]] causes teams to spend their attention on tools instead of the business problem. [[Domain Model Isolation]] helps contain accidental complexity by keeping transport and persistence requirements outside the core model whenever possible.
