# CAF Business Perspective

The CAF business perspective aligns cloud transformation with strategy and measurable business outcomes. It addresses capabilities such as strategic intent, portfolio management, innovation, benefits realization, and the business case for change.

Its stakeholders include leaders who own products, finances, and organizational direction. The perspective asks why the organization is adopting cloud and how success will be measured, preventing a migration from becoming an infrastructure exercise detached from customer or mission value.
