# Speech Recognizer Training

Speech recognizer training estimates model parameters from recorded utterances paired with transcriptions. Effective data include many speakers, accents, devices, noise conditions, and speaking styles relevant to the intended use.

The transcript provides word labels, while the audio supplies acoustic evidence. Coverage matters because a recognizer cannot reliably learn variation missing from its [[Training Dataset]].
