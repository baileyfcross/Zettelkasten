# Retaining Pristine RGB During Keying

A keyer may produce a useful alpha while degrading the RGB colors at the same time. Instead of taking both outputs unquestioningly, use the extracted matte to cut the subject from the original, pristine foreground footage. This separates the question of where the subject is visible from the question of what its pixels should look like. Spill and color corrections can then be applied deliberately. The book demonstrates this again in its stereo-conversion example after a blue-screen extraction. See [[Alpha Channel as Compositing Matte]] and [[Suppressing Chroma Spill]].
