# Truncation Error

Truncation error arises when an infinite process is replaced by a finite one, such as stopping a series, derivative approximation, or iterative method after limited terms or steps.

It differs from [[Round-Off Error]], which comes from finite number representation. Increasing the number of terms reduces truncation error but may increase the amount of rounding accumulated.
