# Blender Sculpt Brush Controls

A sculpt brush's radius controls the area affected, while strength controls how strongly a stroke changes the surface. Pressing F adjusts radius visually, and Shift+F adjusts strength without leaving the 3D Viewport.

The same settings appear in Active Tool Properties, the Sidebar, and the Topbar. Radius and strength can also respond to tablet pressure, letting hand pressure vary the size or intensity of a stroke.
