# R Bland-Altman Agreement Plot

An R Bland-Altman agreement plot places the difference between two quantitative methods against their mean for each paired subject. The average difference estimates systematic bias, while limits based on the spread of differences describe the range in which most method disagreements are expected.

Agreement is not the same as correlation: two methods can track one another closely while differing by an unacceptable amount. Trends or changing spread across the measurement range also warn that one constant set of limits is inadequate.
