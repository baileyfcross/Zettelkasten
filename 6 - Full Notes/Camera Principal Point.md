# Camera Principal Point

The camera principal point is where the optical axis intersects the image sensor, usually near the image center. Three vanishing points produced by mutually perpendicular scene directions form a triangle whose orthocenter identifies the principal point under the pinhole model.

Because an image has only one principal point, independent orthogonal structures should agree on its location. Inconsistent estimates can reveal composited geometry, while uncertain perpendicularity, lens distortion, or cropping must be accounted for before drawing that conclusion.
