# Contracted Ideal

Given a [[Ring Homomorphism]] f: R → T and an ideal J of T, the contracted ideal Jᶜ is the inverse image f⁻¹(J) in R. It contains exactly the source elements whose images lie in J.

For [[Ring Localization]], every ideal of the localized ring is the extension of its contraction. Prime ideals of the localization correspond to source primes disjoint from the denominator set.
