# Pythagorean Theorem

In a right triangle with legs $a$ and $b$ and hypotenuse $c$,

$$a^2+b^2=c^2.$$

The theorem relates Euclidean length to squares and underlies the [[Distance Between Points]] formula. Its converse also holds: if the squared side lengths satisfy this equation, the angle opposite the longest side is a right angle.
