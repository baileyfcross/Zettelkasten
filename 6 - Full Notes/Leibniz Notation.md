# Leibniz Notation

Leibniz notation writes the derivative of $y=f(x)$ as $dy/dx$. Although it is not an ordinary fraction, the notation emphasizes the relationship between changes in dependent and independent variables. It makes rules such as the [[Chain Rule]] readable and extends naturally to higher derivatives such as $d^2y/dx^2$.
