# Line Integral of a Differential Form

A line integral integrates a one-form along an oriented [[Parametric Curve]]. Substituting the parameterization and its differentials reduces the expression to an ordinary integral over the parameter interval.

Parameterizations with the same orientation give the same value, while reversing orientation changes the sign. For a closed curve the integral is often written with a loop symbol and represents quantities such as [[Circulation Integral|circulation]].
