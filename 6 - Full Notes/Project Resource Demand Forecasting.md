# Project Resource Demand Forecasting

Project resource demand forecasting estimates future peaks and valleys in people, infrastructure, budget, or specialist needs from the work plan and comparable project history. Forecasts can expose upcoming contention early enough to change scope, sequence, or staffing.

The result depends on the accuracy of estimates and historical comparability. Scenario ranges are more useful than a single precise number when requirements, delivery rate, or external dependencies remain uncertain.
