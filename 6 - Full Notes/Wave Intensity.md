# Wave Intensity

Wave intensity is power transferred per unit area. For a harmonic wave in a fixed medium, intensity is proportional to the square of amplitude, so doubling amplitude multiplies intensity by four. For energy spreading uniformly from a point source, the growing spherical area produces an [[Inverse-Square Law]] with distance.
