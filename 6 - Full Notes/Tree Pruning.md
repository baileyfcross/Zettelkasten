# Tree Pruning

Tree pruning removes branches or subtrees that add complexity without improving reliable classification. It aims to discard patterns caused by noise or sampling accidents.

Pruning can occur during growth or after a larger tree is built. A separate validation set, a statistical test, or a minimum-description criterion can guide the decision.
