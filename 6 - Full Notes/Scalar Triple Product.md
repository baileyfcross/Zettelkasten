# Scalar Triple Product

The scalar triple product is $u\cdot(v\times w)$. Its absolute value is the volume of the parallelepiped spanned by the three vectors, and its sign records orientation. Cyclic permutations preserve the value, while interchanging two vectors reverses the sign. A zero value means the vectors are coplanar.

In components, the scalar triple product equals the determinant whose rows or columns are the three vectors. This makes its geometric volume interpretation and its alternating behavior direct consequences of determinant properties.
