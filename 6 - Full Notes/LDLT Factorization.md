# LDLT Factorization

An LDLT factorization writes a symmetric matrix as $A=LDL^T$, where $L$ is unit lower triangular and $D$ is diagonal.

It separates diagonal scaling from triangular structure and avoids square roots required by [[Cholesky Decomposition]]. The form is useful when symmetry is available even when the matrix is not positive definite.
