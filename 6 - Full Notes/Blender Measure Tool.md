# Blender Measure Tool

The Blender Measure tool creates viewport rulers for distances and angles. A ruler can be repositioned, converted into an angular measurement, deleted, or copied as a numeric value; snapping it to vertices, edges, and faces makes the reading correspond to model geometry.

Precision mode helps refine placement, but the ruler remains a viewport aid rather than a constraint on the mesh. It is most useful for checking whether modeled dimensions agree with a plan or reference.
