# Multimedia Feature Extraction

Multimedia feature extraction converts raw text, images, audio, or video into numerical descriptions that a classifier can compare. Image color and texture, acoustic measurements, motion, and accompanying words expose different aspects of the same item.

Preprocessing must respect the modality's scale and temporal or spatial structure. A classifier cannot recover distinctions that the extracted representation discarded, so feature design is part of the predictive model.
