# Electron Diffraction

Electron diffraction occurs when electrons pass through a crystal or aperture with structure comparable to their [[de Broglie Wavelength]]. The resulting interference pattern demonstrates that matter propagates with wave-like probability amplitudes. Changing accelerating voltage changes electron momentum and therefore the diffraction spacing.
