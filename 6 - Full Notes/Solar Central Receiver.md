# Solar Central Receiver

A central receiver intercepts the concentrated beams of a [[Solar Tower Power Plant]] and transfers their energy through absorber surfaces to a heat-transfer fluid. The source describes tube panels with headers, allowing high flux and thermal expansion. Rays that miss the target, reflection, wind convection, thermal radiation, and conduction to supports subtract from useful heat even when the heliostat field aims correctly.
