# Summary Statistic

A summary statistic reduces a distribution to a numerical feature such as its count, mean, standard deviation, median, range, skewness, or standard error. Different measures describe different aspects of center, spread, and shape.

No single statistic fully describes a variable. Means and standard deviations can be misleading for strongly skewed data, so they should be read alongside [[Quantile|quantiles]] and visualizations such as histograms or box plots.
