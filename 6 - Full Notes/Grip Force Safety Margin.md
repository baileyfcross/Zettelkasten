# Grip Force Safety Margin

Grip force safety margin is the difference between the force used to hold an object and the minimum force at which it would begin to slip. The margin must be large enough for stability but not so large that the hand wastes effort and fatigues.

The nervous system adjusts grip for object weight and skin–surface friction using signals from [[Cutaneous Mechanoreceptor|cutaneous mechanoreceptors]]. When incipient slip occurs, grip force can increase automatically within tens of milliseconds, often before conscious perception. Numbness or thick gloves remove that feedback and produce excessive, poorly optimized force.
