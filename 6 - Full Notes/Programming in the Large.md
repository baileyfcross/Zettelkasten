# Programming in the Large

Programming in the large concerns systems made of many interacting programs, versions, users, and development teams. Architecture, interfaces, coordination, deployment, maintenance, and organizational communication become as important as individual code.

Methods that succeed for a thousand-line program may fail at a million lines. Large-scale work requires design and management practices that control dependencies and preserve reliable behavior as many people change the system.
