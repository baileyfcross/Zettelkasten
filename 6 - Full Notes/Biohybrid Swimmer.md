# Biohybrid Swimmer

A biohybrid swimmer converts tissue contraction into thrust through a flexible body, fin, or tail. Cardiac cells provide rhythmic bending, while body shape determines how those deformations push against the surrounding liquid.

Adding a second tail or coordinating waves across a fin can greatly change speed and steering without changing the underlying cell type.
