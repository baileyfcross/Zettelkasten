# Internet Backbone Topology

Internet backbones are high-capacity long-distance networks that carry traffic among regional and local networks. The topology evolved from a small mesh of research machines into hierarchical connections spanning the globe.

Fiber-optic links and large server centers replaced slow leased lines and isolated mainframes. End devices reach a local network, then a regional provider, and eventually one or more backbones.
