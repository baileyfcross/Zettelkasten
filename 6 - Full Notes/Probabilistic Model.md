# Probabilistic Model

A probabilistic model represents knowledge about variables and their relationships with a joint probability distribution. It makes uncertainty explicit and supports conditional questions when observations do not determine one certain conclusion.

The model is defined by its chosen variables, dependencies, forms, and parameters. Inference may be mathematically correct while the answer remains unsuitable if those modeling choices omit a relationship important to the phenomenon.
