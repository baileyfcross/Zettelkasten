# Prompting Center of Excellence

A prompting center of excellence converts scarce performance, security, FinOps, delivery, or observability expertise into reusable agent instructions and self-service interactions. Engineers can ask for the specialist's analysis without placing the specialist directly on every team.

Prompt-based access is an intermediate scaling model. As common needs become visible, the center can move proven procedures into autonomous agents that deliver advice at the time of a code review, test, release, or operational risk rather than waiting for someone to know what to ask.
