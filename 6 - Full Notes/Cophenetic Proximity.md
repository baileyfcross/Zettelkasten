# Cophenetic Proximity

Cophenetic proximity between two observations is the dendrogram level at which they first become members of the same cluster. Collecting these values produces a cophenetic proximity matrix.

Comparing that matrix with the original proximities measures how faithfully the hierarchy preserves pairwise relationships. The [[Cophenetic Correlation Coefficient]] is one such comparison.
