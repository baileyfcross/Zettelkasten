# Selective Solar Absorber Coating

A selective absorber coating takes in much short-wavelength sunlight while emitting relatively little long-wavelength thermal radiation. Applied to a [[Solar Collector]] plate, it helps retain captured heat at operating temperature. The source also requires durability against moisture, corrosion, and thermal shock. A simply black surface may absorb sunlight well but radiate much of its own heat away, so absorptance and emittance both matter.
