# Personal Data Export

Personal data export is the ability to retrieve a copy of records from a device or service in a form the person can store and inspect. It protects against the loss of a long personal history when a product changes, a subscription ends, or a company disappears.

An export is useful only when its contents are intelligible. Time stamps, units, sampling intervals, derived fields, and omissions should be documented, and the format should be open enough for another tool to read. A screenshot or summary score may preserve an appearance without preserving the observations needed for new analysis.

Export is a foundation for [[Data Portability]], but portability also requires the destination to understand and use what was exported.
