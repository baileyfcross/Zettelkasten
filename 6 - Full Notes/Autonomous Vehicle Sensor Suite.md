# Autonomous Vehicle Sensor Suite

An autonomous vehicle combines sensors that answer different questions about the road and the vehicle itself. Cameras recognize markings and visible objects, radar measures range and relative motion, lidar produces geometric distance data, GPS and maps provide larger-scale location, and wheel or inertial sensors track the vehicle's own movement.

No instrument is sufficient alone. Weather, glare, occlusion, jamming, map age, and hardware failure create different blind spots, so integration must compare evidence and degrade safely. [[Lidar-Based Autonomous Driving]] supplies dense environmental geometry, while [[Autonomous Vehicle Pose Estimation]] corrects that geometry for the vehicle's changing orientation.
