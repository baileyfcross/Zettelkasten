# Quasi-Zenith Satellite System

Japan's Quasi-Zenith Satellite System is a regional system designed to complement GPS rather than replace it globally. Its orbital arrangement keeps at least one satellite high in the sky over Japan for much of the time, improving reception where buildings or terrain obstruct lower-elevation signals. QZSS also functions as a local augmentation channel, concentrating resources on regional availability and accuracy.
