# Planar-Tracked Digital Makeup

A broad planar track can follow a face region while smaller roto shapes attached to that track identify the precise area for a color or texture change. The book demonstrates this with a lip-color alteration and a tattoo: a modified still acts as a source patch that is moved and deformed with the tracked region. If the patch begins to distort over time, additional corrected stills can be supplied at intervals. Tracking carries most of the motion, but the precise matte and color still need inspection. See [[Film Colorization with Roto Layers]].
