# Unity Particle Modules

Unity particle modules divide an effect into configurable responsibilities. The main module establishes lifetime, speed, size, color, gravity, simulation space, and overall limits; emission controls rates and bursts; shape determines where particles originate; and the renderer determines how they appear.

Other modules vary velocity, force, color, size, rotation, noise, trails, and collision over time or speed. Many values can use constants, curves, or randomized ranges. This modular structure turns one [[Unity Particle System]] into a reusable composition of independent behaviors.
