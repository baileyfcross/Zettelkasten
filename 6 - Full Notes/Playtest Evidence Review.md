# Playtest Evidence Review

A playtest evidence review organizes observed strengths, breakdowns, comments, behaviors, bugs, and emergent ideas before the team chooses revisions. It looks for recurring patterns while preserving important outliers relevant to the design question.

The review compares outcomes with [[Design Values]] rather than with an undefined notion of improvement. A surprising behavior may be a defect, a new possibility, or irrelevant depending on the experience the game is meant to create.
