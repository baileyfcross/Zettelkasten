# Hilbert Polynomial

The Hilbert polynomial is the polynomial that agrees with the [[Hilbert Function]] of a finitely generated graded module for all sufficiently large degrees.

Over a standard graded polynomial ring, its degree is one less than the [[Dimension of a Module]] when the module is not Artinian. Its leading coefficient records the module's multiplicity after factorial normalization.
