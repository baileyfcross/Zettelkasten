# Lift and Drag Coefficients

Lift and drag are commonly expressed as one-half air density times speed squared times reference area times a dimensionless coefficient. The coefficients collect the effects of airfoil shape, angle of attack, and flow state, allowing forces to be compared across speed and density conditions. Their changing ratio is central to [[Power-Off Glide Distance]] and aircraft performance.
