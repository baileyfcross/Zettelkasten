# Spatial and Frequency Image Domains

The spatial domain describes an image by pixel values at positions. A frequency-domain representation instead describes how strongly patterns of different spatial frequencies contribute to an area.

Slow changes correspond to low frequencies, while sharp edges and fine texture create higher-frequency components. Transform codecs exploit this separation because many natural-image blocks concentrate energy in relatively few low-frequency coefficients. Quantization can then reduce less perceptually important detail, as in [[JPEG Discrete Cosine Transform]].
