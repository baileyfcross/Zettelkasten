# AWS Savings Plan

An AWS Savings Plan exchanges a commitment to a consistent amount of eligible compute usage per hour for discounted rates relative to on-demand pricing. Different plan types provide different degrees of flexibility across compute services, instance families, and regions.

The commitment can lower predictable baseline cost, but unused committed spend reduces its value. Historical utilization and future architecture plans should be examined before purchase so the commitment reflects durable demand rather than a temporary peak.
