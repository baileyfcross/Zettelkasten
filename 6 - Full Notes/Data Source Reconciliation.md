# Data Source Reconciliation

Reconciliation resolves differences among sources before their records are treated as one dataset. Customer or patient systems may use different identifiers, units, rating scales, text encodings, or definitions for the same apparent field. Merely copying their tables into one repository does not produce a trustworthy common view.

An analyst must decide which record is authoritative, map alternate representations to agreed values, and investigate ambiguous matches. The work is partly technical and partly semantic: a standardized field is useful only when its meaning remains comparable across sources.
