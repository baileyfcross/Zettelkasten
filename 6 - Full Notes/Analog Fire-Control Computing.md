# Analog Fire-Control Computing

Analog fire-control systems represented equations of motion directly through electrical or mechanical relationships. Continuously changing radar and tracking inputs adjusted a computed firing solution in real time.

These systems succeeded before general digital computers were practical. Their design raised lasting questions about feedback, automatic control, and how human operators should interact with fast machinery.
