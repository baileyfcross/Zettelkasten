# Isolated Speech Recognition

Isolated speech recognition expects separate words or short commands with clear boundaries. Pauses make it easier to segment the signal and compare each utterance with a constrained vocabulary.

Early recognizers often used this mode because it reduced variability. [[Continuous Speech Recognition]] must instead locate word and sound boundaries inside naturally connected speech.
