# Player Pace Preference

Player pace preference describes the rhythm and kind of temporal demand a target player enjoys. “Fast” and “slow” are insufficient on their own: speed chess, an athletic game, and a reflex shooter are fast in different ways, while a stealth game may intentionally alternate methodical buildup, explosive action, and cooldown.

A useful audience profile therefore identifies whether the pace is turn-based or continuous, thoughtful or reflexive, steady or escalating, and how much downtime it permits. This helps avoid testing a game with players whose preferred mode of attention conflicts with the intended experience.
