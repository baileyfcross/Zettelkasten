# Modularity-Based Community Detection

Modularity-based community detection searches for groups with more internal edges than expected under a comparison model. A fast greedy procedure repeatedly merges communities to improve modularity and provides a computationally practical partition for a large graph.

Optimizing the score does not guarantee a uniquely meaningful grouping. Very large communities can combine several recognizable themes, so the partition should be inspected and compared with alternatives such as [[Infomap Community Detection]].
