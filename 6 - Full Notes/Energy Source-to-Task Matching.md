# Energy Source-to-Task Matching

Source-to-task matching selects the quality and temperature of energy actually needed by a service. The source pairs low-temperature flat-plate solar heat with water heating, space heating, or crop drying; producing shaft work needs higher-temperature heat from greater concentration. Collecting very high-grade heat for a low-grade task can waste resources. [[Exergy]] analysis clarifies the mismatch beyond a simple energy-total comparison.
