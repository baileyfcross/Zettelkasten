# Direct Methanol Fuel Cell

A direct methanol fuel cell feeds liquid methanol to an electrode without first reforming it into a separate hydrogen supply. Its polymer electrolyte passes protons, and the net reaction with oxygen produces water and carbon dioxide. This fuel-handling distinction makes it different from a [[Hydrogen-Oxygen Fuel Cell]], even though both produce current by transporting ions internally and electrons through a load.
