# Personally Identifiable Information

Personally identifiable information is information that can distinguish or trace an individual's identity or that is linked or linkable to that individual. Examples include identity numbers, birth details, biometric records, and medical, educational, financial, or employment information.

A collection can become identifying through combination even when one field appears harmless in isolation. Cloud services handling this information need explicit privacy, access, retention, transmission, location, and deletion rules.
