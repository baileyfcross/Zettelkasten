# Optogenetics

Optogenetics genetically equips cells with light-sensitive proteins so illumination changes their electrical or biochemical activity. Light can be directed in space and time more precisely than an electric field spread through culture medium.

In biohybrid robotics, optogenetic muscle turns a focused pulse into a localized contraction used for steering or selective actuation.
