# Electric Charge

Electric charge is a fundamental property that occurs in positive and negative forms. Like charges repel and unlike charges attract. Charge is conserved and quantized in multiples of the elementary charge for ordinary isolated particles, although quarks carry fractional elementary charges within composite particles. Its SI unit is the coulomb.

Charge is Lorentz invariant: changing inertial frame does not change the total charge assigned to a particle or isolated system. [[Charge Density Transformation|Charge density]] is not invariant, because the volume containing a moving collection contracts along the direction of motion. The invariance of total charge is expressed covariantly by conservation of the [[Four-Current]].
