# External Image Asset Licensing

External images can serve as textures, modeling references, concept art, backgrounds, billboards, or environment lighting. A useful search result is not automatically a usable asset: each source applies its own license, attribution requirements, resolution limits, and account conditions.

The role of the image also affects selection. Turnaround references support shape study, tileable textures support surfaces, and HDR environment maps support ambient illumination; substituting one category for another can create avoidable technical and legal problems.
