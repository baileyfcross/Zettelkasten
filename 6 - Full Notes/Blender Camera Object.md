# Blender Camera Object

A Blender camera object defines the viewpoint from which a scene is rendered. It has a transform like other objects, but its lens and projection settings determine how three-dimensional positions are framed and projected into the final two-dimensional image.

Only the active camera supplies the render view, although a scene can contain several cameras for alternative shots. The object can be positioned directly, aligned to an exploratory viewport, animated, or controlled with constraints. Aperture and depth-of-field choices further shape the shot, so camera setup participates in composition and perceived scale rather than serving only as an export switch.
