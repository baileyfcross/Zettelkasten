# Hovmoller Diagram

A Hovmoller diagram places time on one axis, a spatial coordinate or zone on the other, and an aggregated field value in color. It reduces one spatial dimension of a [[Space-Time Raster]], making propagation, seasonality, and persistent bands visible in a single panel. The aggregation direction and function must be stated because distinct geographic patterns can produce the same reduced matrix.
