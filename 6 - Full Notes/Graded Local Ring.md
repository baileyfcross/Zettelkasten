# Graded Local Ring

A graded local ring has a unique maximal homogeneous ideal. That ideal need not be maximal among all ideals; it is maximal within the homogeneous ideals.

Every homogeneous element outside it is invertible in the graded sense. This structure supports [[Graded Nakayama's Lemma]], which identifies minimal homogeneous generating sets after reduction modulo the homogeneous maximal ideal.
