# Biot-Savart Law

The Biot-Savart law gives the magnetic field contribution from a current element:

$$dB=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{I\,d\ell\times\hat r}{r^2}.$$

Integrating along the conductor produces the total field. The cross product fixes direction through the right-hand rule, while geometry determines how contributions reinforce or cancel.
