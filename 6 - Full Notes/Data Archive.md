# Data Archive

A data archive preserves information that is no longer used regularly but must be retained for historical, regulatory, or compliance reasons. Search and retrieval over long retention periods matter more than immediate operational restoration.

An archive differs from a backup, whose purpose is rapid recovery of changing day-to-day data. Backup retention is commonly measured in weeks or months, while archival retention can extend for years or decades.
