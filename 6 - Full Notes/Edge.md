# Edge

An edge is a connection between vertices in a [[Graph]]. It can represent a bridge, relationship, hyperlink, dependency, possible move, or any other relation retained by the graph model.

Edges may be undirected, directed, repeated in a [[Multigraph]], or assigned numerical values in a [[Weighted Graph]]. A sequence of connected edges forms a [[Graph Path]], and many graph algorithms operate by deciding which edges to traverse or select.
