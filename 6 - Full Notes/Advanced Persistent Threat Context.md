# Advanced Persistent Threat Context

Advanced persistent threat context combines observations over time to understand a capable adversary that maintains access, adapts techniques, and pursues a sustained objective. Individual alerts may look minor, but their sequence can reveal reconnaissance, lateral movement, command activity, and data collection.

Preserving context supports [[Cyberattack Attribution]], [[Cyber Kill Chain]] analysis, and adaptive defense. It shifts attention from isolated events to the continuing relationship among actor, target, technique, and outcome.
