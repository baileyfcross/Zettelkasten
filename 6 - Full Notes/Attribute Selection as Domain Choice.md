# Attribute Selection as Domain Choice

Attribute selection decides which facets of an instance a model may use to distinguish cases. It should be guided by the intended question and domain knowledge, then revised through data exploration and evaluation. Irrelevant fields can create accidental associations; redundant fields can complicate interpretation without adding useful information.

The book contrasts customer-churn projects in two countries where a phone-handset attribute was useful in one and useless in the other because companies offered different upgrade incentives. Predictive relevance thus depends on the local business process, not just on the field name or an algorithm's feature score.
