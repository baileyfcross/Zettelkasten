# Speech Recognition Search

Speech recognition search finds the most plausible word sequence given acoustic evidence, pronunciations, and language probabilities. It must compare many hypotheses without exhaustively enumerating every possible sentence.

The search balances how well a sequence fits the [[Acoustic Model]] with how plausible it is under the [[Statistical Language Model]], using the [[Pronunciation Model]] to connect words to sound units.
