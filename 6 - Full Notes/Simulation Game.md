# Simulation Game

A simulation game models a process, activity, or system so the player can operate or explore it. Vehicle simulations emphasize control of a machine, while construction and management simulations emphasize building and managing processes under constraints.

The design abstracts reality selectively. Accuracy matters only to the degree needed for the intended experience, audience, and challenge; unnecessary detail can make the simulation less playable without making it more informative.
