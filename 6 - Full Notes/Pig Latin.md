# Pig Latin

Pig Latin is the procedural dataflow language used by [[Apache Pig]]. Each statement applies a transformation to relations made from bags, tuples, and fields, allowing a pipeline to load, filter, group, order, and store large datasets.

The compiler converts the script into logical and physical operator plans and then into MapReduce jobs. Its concise syntax improves expressiveness, but external control flow is normally needed for iterative algorithms.
