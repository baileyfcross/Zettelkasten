# AR Stage

An AR stage is the spatial and situational setting in which authored actors and interactions operate. It includes relevant physical geometry, coordinate frames, anchors, display conditions, and the locations where events may occur.

Unlike a wholly virtual scene, the stage must accommodate an environment the system does not completely control. Setup and calibration connect the authored representation to the particular physical place where it is performed.
