# Data Science Project Sponsorship

Project sponsorship gives a data science effort an organizational owner who cares about the business process and use of its results. The book emphasizes support from senior business management, not simply an IT mandate to keep systems operational.

A sponsor can define the decision to improve, secure cooperation across domain and technical teams, and support repeated use after the first demonstration. Treating data science as a one-off box-ticking exercise leaves even an accurate model without a durable pathway to value.
