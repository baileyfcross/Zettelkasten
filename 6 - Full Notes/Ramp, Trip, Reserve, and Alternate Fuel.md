# Ramp, Trip, Reserve, and Alternate Fuel

Ramp fuel covers all fuel onboard before departure, including start, taxi, takeoff, trip, and planned reserves. Contingency fuel addresses variation in route or consumption, alternate fuel supports a diversion or missed approach, final reserve protects the last safe endurance margin, and extra fuel covers special circumstances. The categories prevent one undifferentiated total from hiding why each portion is required.
