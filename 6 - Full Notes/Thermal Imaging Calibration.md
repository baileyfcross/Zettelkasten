# Thermal Imaging Calibration

An infrared camera converts received radiative signals into apparent temperature using internal assumptions about emissivity and instrument response. Calibration against a black-body source of known temperature and emissivity checks that conversion over the measurement range.

User calibration can support an experiment, but it does not replace manufacturer adjustment of the camera's internal parameters. Credible [[Comparing Thermal Simulation with Infrared Measurements|simulation comparison]] must also account for resolution, accuracy, surface emissivity, viewing geometry, and ambient reflections.
