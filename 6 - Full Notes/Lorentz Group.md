# Lorentz Group

The Lorentz group is the group of linear transformations that preserve the Minkowski spacetime interval. Its proper orthochronous part connects inertial frames without spatial reflection or time reversal and contains three spatial rotations and three [[Lorentz Boost|boosts]].

The generators satisfy noncommutative relations. Commutators of rotation generators produce rotations, commutators of rotations with boosts produce boosts, and the [[Commutator of Lorentz Boosts|commutator of boosts in different directions]] produces a spatial rotation. These relations encode the fact that the order of non-collinear changes of velocity matters.

Lorentz covariance means a physical law retains its form under every transformation in the group. Four-vectors and tensors are constructed so their components transform together, while scalar contractions such as the [[Invariant Spacetime Interval]] remain unchanged.
