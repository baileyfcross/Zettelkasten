# Bounded Interval

A bounded interval lies between two finite endpoints. Examples include

$$-1\leq x\leq2$$

and

$$-1<x<2.$$

Its length is the upper endpoint minus the lower endpoint, regardless of whether either boundary is included. Although the length is finite, the interval contains infinitely many [[Real Number]]s. Endpoint symbols determine membership: brackets or filled points include a boundary, while parentheses or open points exclude it.
