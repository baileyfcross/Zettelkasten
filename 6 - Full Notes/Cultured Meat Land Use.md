# Cultured Meat Land Use

Cultured meat land use is the land required for production facilities and the agriculture supplying media, energy, and material inputs. Growing only desired tissue can avoid pasture and feed production associated with maintaining an entire animal.

The magnitude of savings depends on the livestock system and on whether required sugars, proteins, and other inputs displace land use elsewhere.
