# Searched Variable

A searched variable is a variable whose conditional probability distribution is requested by a Bayesian question. At least one searched variable is required because it defines what the inference is trying to determine.

The answer retains the searched variable's alternatives and normalizes probability across them for the instantiated evidence. A variable can be searched in one question and known or free in another.
