# Jacobi Eigenvalue Algorithm

The Jacobi eigenvalue algorithm repeatedly applies plane rotations to a real [[Symmetric Matrix]] to eliminate off-diagonal entries.

Each step produces an orthogonally similar matrix, preserving eigenvalues while moving the matrix toward diagonal form. The accumulated rotations also produce the eigenvectors.
