# Unity Tile Palette

A Unity Tile Palette is an authoring collection of tile assets used to paint a [[Unity Tilemap]]. It works like a visual set of reusable brushes: the author selects a tile or region and applies it to cells in the active map.

A palette does not itself become the level. It organizes the available tile choices, while the tilemap stores placements in the scene. Keeping related terrain, decoration, and obstacle tiles coherent in palettes makes repeated world construction faster and less error-prone.
