# AR Annotation Authoring

AR annotation authoring creates information and attaches it to a physical object, feature, or location. The author selects a referent, establishes a registered anchor, and supplies content such as text, graphics, media, or a link.

Annotation requires less geometric precision than reconstructing the entire object, which makes it suitable for end-user contribution. The saved anchor must still be recognizable later so the situated annotation can return to the intended place.
