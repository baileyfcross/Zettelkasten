# Lotus 1-2-3

Lotus 1-2-3 combined spreadsheet calculation, charting, and data functions for the IBM PC. Its speed and practical business features helped make the PC a standard office machine.

Like VisiCalc on the Apple II, it was a platform-defining application. Users bought computers to obtain the capability supplied by the software.
