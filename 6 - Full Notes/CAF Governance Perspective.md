# CAF Governance Perspective

The CAF governance perspective coordinates cloud initiatives with decision rights, risk, compliance, finance, data oversight, and portfolio accountability. It establishes how the organization sets guardrails and evaluates whether transformation remains aligned with obligations and priorities.

Cloud speed makes governance more important, not less. Policies should be translated into reusable controls, account structures, budgets, and evidence so teams can act quickly inside known boundaries rather than waiting for case-by-case approval.
