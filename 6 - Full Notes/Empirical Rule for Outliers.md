# Empirical Rule for Outliers

The empirical rule states that approximately 68.3, 95.4, and 99.7 percent of normally distributed observations lie within one, two, and three standard deviations of the mean. A point beyond three standard deviations is therefore rare under normality.

Using the rule for outliers assumes the distribution is approximately normal and that the estimated mean and standard deviation have not been badly distorted by the same extreme cases.
