# Playtest Observation Log

A playtest observation log separates what happened during a session from the team's later interpretation and response. During play, the observer records the player, location in the game, and exact behavior or comment. Afterward, the team identifies the underlying issue, severity, and proposed solution.

This structure protects evidence from being rewritten as an immediate diagnosis. Repeated observations can be grouped under one issue, while reproduction steps, assignment, status, and effort estimates can turn the log into an action list. A single comment is a clue; a recurring pattern is stronger design evidence.
