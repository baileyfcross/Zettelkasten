# Wedge Product

The wedge product combines differential forms while recording orientation. It is bilinear and associative, and exchanging two one-forms reverses the sign:

$$dx_i\wedge dx_j=-dx_j\wedge dx_i.$$

Consequently, $dx_i\wedge dx_i=0$. Evaluated on vectors, an elementary wedge product gives an oriented determinant component of the parallelogram or higher-dimensional parallelotope they span.
