# BlackBerry Mobile Email

The BlackBerry made corporate e-mail available on a handheld mobile device. Its compact keyboard and persistent messaging connection turned text communication into a central function of the phone.

The product helped converge computing and cellular networks before modern touchscreen smartphones. Its popularity showed that mobile data could matter more to many users than voice calls.
