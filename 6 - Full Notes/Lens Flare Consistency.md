# Lens Flare Consistency

Lens flare consists of bright artifacts produced when a strong source reflects and scatters within a camera lens. In many cases the flare elements, image center, and projected light source lie on a common line determined by the optical system.

This relationship can constrain whether a visible sun or lamp occupies the position implied by the flare. Cropping, complex lens designs, added effects, or an off-frame source can change what is visible, so flare should corroborate other lighting evidence rather than stand alone.
