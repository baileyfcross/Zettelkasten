# Punched Cards as Machine Control

A punched card can encode control instructions as well as data. Jacquard looms used hole patterns to select threads, while later computing installations used cards to determine operations and the sequence of jobs.

The distinction between data and control depends on interpretation rather than the physical card. This anticipated the stored-program insight that one encoded pattern may be treated as information or as an instruction.
