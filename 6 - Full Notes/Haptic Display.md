# Haptic Display

A haptic display is an [[Output Device]] that simulates physical interaction with virtual objects through touch and proprioception. Depending on its design, it can produce force, vibration, pressure, temperature, or combinations of these sensations.

Tactile feedback stimulates receptors in the skin, while kinesthetic feedback acts through muscles and joints. Effective haptic interaction requires a fast loop between user motion and returned force or touch; excessive [[Latency]] can make feedback unstable or unrealistic.

Important characteristics include [[Spatial Resolution]], temporal resolution, range, safety, attachment effort, weight, and freedom of movement. No single haptic device is best for every task.

AR haptics can exploit contact with real objects as passive feedback or add active tactile and force effects. Because the physical environment already resists motion, a design must align generated feedback with visible and tangible surfaces rather than treating touch as an independent channel.
