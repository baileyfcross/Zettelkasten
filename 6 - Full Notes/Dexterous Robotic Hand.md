# Dexterous Robotic Hand

A dexterous robotic hand uses several articulated fingers, multiple controlled joints, and force or displacement sensing to perform more varied grasps and manipulations than a simple industrial gripper. An opposing digit and independent joint motion expand the stable grip set.

Accurate joint positioning and fast motors do not by themselves create dexterity. The controller must plan contact, regulate force, detect slip, and adapt to uncertainty. [[Robotic Tactile Sensor|Robotic tactile sensors]] remain a limiting component because fingertip devices must combine sensitivity with resistance to repeated impact, abrasion, and deformation.
