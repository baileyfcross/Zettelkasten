# Integration of Rational Functions

Integrating a rational function begins by comparing the polynomial degrees. If the numerator is not lower in degree than the denominator, polynomial division separates a polynomial part from a proper rational remainder.

The proper remainder can then be decomposed into simpler fractions associated with factors of the denominator. Those terms reduce to standard logarithmic, inverse-trigonometric, or elementary power integrals, with repeated or irreducible factors determining the needed form of the decomposition.
