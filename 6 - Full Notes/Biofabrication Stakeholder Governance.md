# Biofabrication Stakeholder Governance

Biofabrication stakeholder governance brings researchers, clinicians, patients, donors, consumers, workers, policy makers, ethicists, journalists, and the public into decisions about research and deployment. Each group sees different risks, needs, and distributions of benefit.

Expert self-governance can establish technical safeguards, but durable legitimacy requires participation from people affected outside the laboratory.
