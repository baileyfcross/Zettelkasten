# Cell Culture Microcarrier

A cell culture microcarrier is a small bead that provides an attachment surface for adherent cells suspended in a stirred bioreactor. Large numbers of beads expose far more growth area than the vessel wall alone.

Harvesting may require separating cells from the beads, dissolving the carrier, or making it edible so it can remain in a cultivated-meat product.
