# Numerical Representation for Computation

Computation begins by representing quantities or phenomena with numbers, equations, symbols, or models in an agreed form. The representation determines which operations become possible and which features of the original situation are retained or omitted.

Digital computation extends this practice by encoding information into machine-processable data. A useful representation therefore connects meaning in the domain to transformations the selected machine can reliably perform.
