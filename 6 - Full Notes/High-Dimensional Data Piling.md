# High-Dimensional Data Piling

Data piling is the concentration of point-cloud relationships that the book studies in very high-dimensional, sparse settings. In its experiments, distances and orientations become more uniform, while a cloud can be approximated increasingly well by its average or marginal profile.

This is not a universal promise that higher dimension makes every task easy. The phenomenon depends on the data distribution, sparsity, and how the number of observations grows relative to attributes. The book exploits the concentration to justify simple projections and searches for irregularities against a highly symmetric background.
