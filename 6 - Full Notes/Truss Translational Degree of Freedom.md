# Truss Translational Degree of Freedom

A truss translational degree of freedom is a nodal displacement component along a global coordinate axis. A planar truss node normally has one x translation and one y translation.

Supports prescribe selected components, while connected members couple the remaining components through their oriented stiffness. Consistent ordering of these translations controls assembly of the global matrix.
