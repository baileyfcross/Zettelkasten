# Trustworthy AI

Trustworthy AI is the policy goal of building systems whose operation and governance justify reliance. It combines technical qualities such as safety and reliability with ethical and institutional qualities such as fairness, privacy, accountability, respect for autonomy, and intelligibility.

Trust should be earned rather than manufactured through reassuring design or branding. A user may understand a system and reasonably decide not to trust it. Demonstrating trustworthiness therefore requires evidence, [[Algorithmic Auditability]], clear responsibility, and remedies when commitments fail. The aim is not maximum trust, but well-calibrated trust supported by enforceable practices.
