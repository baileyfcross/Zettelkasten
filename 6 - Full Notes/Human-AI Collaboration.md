# Human-AI Collaboration

Human-AI collaboration divides activity between machine pattern recognition and human judgment, experience, or responsibility. It can use automation to extend human capability without assuming that either partner should perform the whole task.

The arrangement is ethical only when the human role is substantive. A person who must approve opaque outputs at machine speed may provide the appearance of oversight without real control. Good collaboration preserves the knowledge, time, authority, and feedback needed to question the system. It also recognizes that practical wisdom and contextual understanding cannot simply be delegated to statistical prediction.
