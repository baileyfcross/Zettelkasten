# Image of a Linear Transformation

The image of $T:V\to W$ is $\operatorname{im}T=\{T(v):v\in V\}$. It is a [[Vector Subspace]] of the codomain containing every output the transformation can produce.

The image is spanned by the images of any basis of $V$. Its dimension is the rank of $T$, so surjectivity means the image equals the entire codomain.
