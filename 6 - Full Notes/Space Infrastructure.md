# Space Infrastructure

Space infrastructure is the network of satellites, ground stations, launch systems, control centers, communications links, data processing, and institutions that provides continuing services from orbit. It supports weather observation, communications, navigation, timing, reconnaissance, early warning, science, and military command.

The system is often invisible because users interact with a forecast, map, broadcast, or synchronized device rather than the spacecraft. Its value makes it durable beyond the political competition that first financed it. The same dependence creates systemic risk: ground failures, signal interference, orbital collisions, or war can disrupt services shared by governments, businesses, and daily life.
