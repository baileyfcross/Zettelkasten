# Spreadsheet Sheet Separation

Spreadsheet sheet separation places data objects with substantially different attribute sets on different sheets. Weapons and armor may share a cost field, for example, but their remaining schemas differ enough that combining them into one giant table makes entry, filtering, analysis, and export harder.

A well-structured game workbook also separates functional roles: help, reference inputs, object data, analysis or visualization, and disposable scratch work. The result is a visible flow from instructions and controlled inputs through data to calculated outputs, without mixing permanent records with temporary experiments.
