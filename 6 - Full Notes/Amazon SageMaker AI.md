# Amazon SageMaker AI

Amazon SageMaker AI is a managed platform for preparing data, building or selecting machine-learning models, training them, and deploying inference endpoints or batch jobs. It brings multiple stages of the machine-learning lifecycle into a governed AWS environment.

Unlike task-specific AI services such as [[Amazon Rekognition]] or [[Amazon Comprehend]], SageMaker supports teams that need more control over models and training workflows. Data quality, evaluation, monitoring, security, and cost remain essential even when the infrastructure is managed.
