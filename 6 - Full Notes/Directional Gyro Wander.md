# Directional Gyro Wander

Gyro wander is a change between the indicated axis and the reference direction. Real wander comes from imperfections such as imbalance, friction, wear, or uneven rotor mass, while apparent wander comes from the observer's movement and Earth's rotation even if the gyro axis itself is ideal. A directional gyro must therefore be checked against the magnetic compass and periodically calibrated.
