# Presheaf

A presheaf assigns an algebraic object to every open set and a restriction map whenever one open set is contained in another. Restrictions compose consistently and act as the identity when an open set is restricted to itself.

A [[Sheaf]] is a presheaf satisfying additional locality and gluing conditions. Presheaves organize information that varies over a space even before those conditions are imposed.
