# CAF People Perspective

The CAF people perspective prepares culture, leadership, skills, structure, and workforce practices for cloud transformation. It considers how roles change, how teams learn, and how leaders create incentives and communication that support new ways of working.

Technology can be available before an organization is able to use it effectively. Skills assessment, training, communities of practice, organizational design, and change management therefore belong to the adoption plan rather than being treated as consequences after migration.
