# Heat Equation

The heat equation states that the time rate of change of temperature is proportional to its spatial Laplacian. The same mathematical form describes diffusion of particles or other conserved quantities under simple conditions.

Separated solutions consist of spatial eigenfunctions multiplied by exponentially decaying time factors. Higher spatial-frequency modes decay faster, so an initially irregular distribution becomes smoother and approaches the equilibrium pattern selected by its boundary conditions.
