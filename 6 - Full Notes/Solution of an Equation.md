# Solution of an Equation

A solution of an equation is a value for the [[Unknown Variable]] that makes both sides equal when substituted into the original equation. An equation may have one solution, several solutions, no solution, or be an identity with every permitted value as a solution.

A proposed result is not complete until its domain restrictions are respected. Multiplying, dividing, factoring, or cross multiplying can hide excluded values or introduce candidates that do not satisfy the original form. [[Equation Verification]] distinguishes valid solutions from such extraneous candidates.
