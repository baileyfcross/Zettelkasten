# Unambiguous Computational Steps

A computational step must specify an operation precisely enough that different competent executors obtain the same result. Replacing vague instructions with elementary arithmetic, comparison, and symbol manipulation makes a method reproducible and eventually mechanizable.

Unambiguity is a source of trust, not a guarantee of correctness. The steps can still embody a faulty model or be connected in the wrong order, so the entire procedure and its assumptions must also be examined.
