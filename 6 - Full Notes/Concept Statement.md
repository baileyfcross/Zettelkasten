# Concept Statement

A concept statement expresses a game's central experience and differentiator in one or two brief, accurate sentences. It should be short enough to remember and repeat while still communicating what the player does and why the game is distinctive.

The statement anchors a [[Game Concept Document]] and opens a [[Game Pitch]]. Refining it with other people reveals where the underlying concept is unclear or where listeners form a different mental model than intended.

The source calls this a high concept statement and limits it to roughly two or three sentences. Its job is to communicate the most important aspects quickly, leaving platform, audience, progression, and production detail to the larger concept document.
