# Gamified Self-Tracking

Gamified self-tracking adds game-like structures to recording and behavior, such as points, badges, levels, competitions, challenges, or rewards. These elements are intended to turn an abstract goal into visible progress and encourage repeated participation.

The design frames what counts as success. A step leaderboard favors a count that is easy to compare, while a streak makes uninterrupted consistency more important than context or recovery. The same mechanism can motivate one person and discourage another whose illness, work, or disability makes the target inappropriate.

Gamification is therefore not a neutral layer on top of data. It directs attention, establishes norms, and may serve a platform's need for engagement as much as the user's underlying purpose.
