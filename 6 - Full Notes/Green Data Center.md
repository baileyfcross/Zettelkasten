# Green Data Center

A green data center manages computing capacity with explicit goals for energy efficiency and environmental impact. It coordinates server utilization, cooling, workload timing, power sources, and sometimes on-site renewable generation.

The objective is not merely to minimize electricity use. A practical design balances performance commitments, cost, carbon emissions, reliability, and the variability of supply and demand.
