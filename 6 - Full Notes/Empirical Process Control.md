# Empirical Process Control

Empirical process control directs work using observed results rather than relying on a complete predictive model. Transparency makes the current product and process visible, inspection compares them with the goal, and adaptation changes the next action.

It is appropriate when creative and technical uncertainty prevents reliable long-range prescription. A working [[Product Increment]] supplies stronger evidence than progress reports about unfinished components.
