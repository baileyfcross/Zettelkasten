# Amazon S3 Storage Lens

Amazon S3 Storage Lens aggregates storage-usage and activity metrics across buckets, accounts, organizations, and regions. Its dashboards and recommendations reveal object counts, bytes, access patterns, protection settings, and opportunities to remove or tier unused data. Storage Lens observes the estate rather than changing it automatically; owners must interpret the metrics and apply lifecycle, replication, encryption, or cleanup policies deliberately.
