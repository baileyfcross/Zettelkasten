# Suppressing Chroma Spill

Light reflected from a blue or green screen can tint the subject, especially at its edges. Removing the screen in the alpha does not automatically remove this contamination from RGB. The book's blue-screen stereo example uses a hue correction to isolate and reduce blue spill after the initial key, then uses the alpha to retrieve the performer from original footage. Spill correction is therefore a color operation coordinated with, but separate from, [[Retaining Pristine RGB During Keying]].
