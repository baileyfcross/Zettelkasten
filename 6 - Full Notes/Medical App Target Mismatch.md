# Medical App Target Mismatch

Medical app target mismatch occurs when a health technology is designed and marketed to people who are easiest to reach rather than those with the greatest health need. Consumer applications often assume a younger, healthier, wealthier, and more technically confident user even when chronic illness, age, or limited access creates the stronger potential benefit.

This mismatch shapes features, evidence, and business strategy. A tool built around fashionable fitness goals may not support medication complexity, accessibility, intermittent connectivity, caregiving, or communication with a clinic.

Correcting it requires involving intended communities before the product and measure are fixed. A cheaper technical substitute is not automatically an equitable solution if it ignores the social setting or displaces access to necessary professional care.
