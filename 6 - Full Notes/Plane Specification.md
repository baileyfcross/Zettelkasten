# Plane Specification

Plane specification identifies a flat surface in the physical environment for modeling or content placement. The plane can be derived from several measured points, fitted to observed data, or selected from reconstructed geometry.

Once established, it supplies a position, orientation, and two-dimensional surface coordinate system. Augmentations can then be constrained to a wall, floor, tabletop, or planar object rather than positioned freely in space.
