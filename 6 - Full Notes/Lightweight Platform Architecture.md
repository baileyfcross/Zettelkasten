# Lightweight Platform Architecture

A lightweight platform architecture supplies the smallest durable set of components and resource commitments needed for supported outcomes. It avoids global availability, replication, retention, and integration levels whose environmental and operating costs exceed the user requirement.

Lightweight does not mean fragile. The platform reduces unnecessary consumption, reuses components and hardware where practical, and makes replacement possible through composability. Architecture decisions consider lifecycle energy, idle capacity, operational effort, and eventual retirement alongside performance and reliability, producing a foundation that can grow without beginning at maximum scale.
