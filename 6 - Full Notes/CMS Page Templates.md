# CMS Page Templates

A CMS page template converts structured content into a delivered page. It chooses the layout and renders the fields, regions, and components defined by the page's content type.

Keeping templates separate from stored content lets a site change presentation without rewriting every content item. Templates must nevertheless honor the content contract and handle optional or missing values predictably.
