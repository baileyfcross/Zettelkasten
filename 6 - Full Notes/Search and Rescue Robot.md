# Search and Rescue Robot

A search-and-rescue robot enters dangerous or inaccessible environments to map damage, detect hazards, locate survivors, deliver sensors or supplies, and sometimes manipulate debris. Air, ground, surface, and underwater platforms serve different scales and conditions.

Disaster environments resist standardization: rubble, water, ash, metal, dust, failed networks, and rearranged landmarks challenge sensing and movement. A useful rescue robot is therefore not just a rugged vehicle. It is part of a response system that includes [[Search and Rescue Robot Deployment]], trained operators, local responders, maps, communication, and procedures for acting on discoveries.
