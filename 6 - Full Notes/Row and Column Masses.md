# Row and Column Masses

After dividing a table by its grand total, each row mass is its share of all recorded occurrences and each column mass is an attribute's corresponding share. These marginal distributions provide empirical weights for the two clouds of points used in correspondence analysis.

Mass is not simply a display size. It helps define the chi-squared profile distance and the moment of inertia around a cloud's center. A text segment and a word thus enter the factor mapping through both a conditional profile and a weight derived from their frequency in the whole table.
