# Nonsingular Matrix

A nonsingular matrix is a [[Square Matrix]] with nonzero [[Determinant]]. It has a unique [[Matrix Inverse]], full rank, and exactly one solution to $Ax=b$ for every compatible right-hand side.

Nonsingularity also means the matrix can be reduced to the [[Identity Matrix]] and expressed as a product of [[Elementary Matrix|elementary matrices]].
