# Enterprise Professional Social Networks

An enterprise professional social network organizes profiles and relationships around employment, recruiting, expertise, and business activity. A profile often functions as a digital curriculum vitae, while company pages, professional contacts, job discovery, and industry publishing connect individuals with organizations.

The source contrasts globally oriented LinkedIn with the more European concentration of XING and notes that specialized services can grow by acquiring job or expatriate communities. For software integration, professional identity and organization context matter as much as ordinary posts. Platform-specific access can be provided through tools such as an [[Social Platform Java Client Selection|appropriate Java client]], but the application must still separate a provider's account from its own authorization model.
