# Topic Model Inference

Topic model inference estimates the posterior distributions of latent topic assignments from the words observed in a corpus. The generative story specifies how documents could arise, but the hidden document-topic and topic-term variables must be approximated from data.

Sampling and variational optimization offer different approximations and can produce somewhat different themes. Reproducible comparison requires fixed seeds, recorded controls, and inspection of both numerical fit and topic coherence.
