# Online Game Player Expectations

Online game player expectations are beliefs about what a continuing paid or supported world owes its community. Players may expect fairness, profitable effort, protection before harm, fast problem resolution, and a presumption of honest behavior.

No service can satisfy every expectation. Operators must communicate rules, limits, support commitments, and the consequences of player conduct while recognizing that taking payment creates an ongoing obligation.
