# Replication Power Planning

Replication power planning estimates how many observations are needed for a replication to detect an effect of a stated size with a chosen probability. In the project described by the book, teams calculated sample sizes for several power targets and selected the highest feasible design subject to a minimum standard.

Adequate power reduces the chance that a true effect appears absent merely because the replication was too small. It does not guarantee success or validate the original effect-size estimate. Power assumptions belong in the registered [[Replication Protocol Standardization|protocol]] so the design can be evaluated before the outcome is known.
