# Human Metaphors in Robotics

Robotics language frequently maps machine functions onto human bodies and minds: sensors become senses, processors think, manipulators become hands, and automated responses become decisions. These metaphors make complex systems approachable, but they can exaggerate resemblance and hide important differences in mechanism.

Biomimicry is one design strategy, not the only route to effective machines. Aircraft do not fly by copying birds exactly, and future machine intelligence need not reproduce a human brain. Evaluating a robot by the task and environment it serves can reveal better designs than treating human form or cognition as the universal benchmark.
