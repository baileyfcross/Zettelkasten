# Rank-Nullity Theorem

For a linear transformation $T:V\to W$ with finite-dimensional domain, $\operatorname{rank}T+\operatorname{nullity}T=\dim V$. Rank is the dimension of the [[Image of a Linear Transformation|image]], and nullity is the dimension of the [[Kernel of a Linear Transformation|kernel]].

The theorem accounts for every independent input direction: each either survives as an independent output direction or belongs to the kernel. For an $m\times n$ matrix, it gives $\operatorname{rank}A+\dim\ker A=n$.
