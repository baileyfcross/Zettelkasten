# Haptic Rendering

Haptic rendering computes the physical interaction between a device's virtual representative and objects in a simulated environment. Collision detection identifies contact, a force-response model calculates stiffness, friction, deformation, or impact, and the [[Haptic Display]] returns that response to the user.

The loop needs high update bandwidth and low latency—often around a kilohertz for stable force feedback—because the hand can detect rapid mechanical changes. Visual realism cannot compensate for an unstable force loop, although coordinated visual and auditory cues can strengthen or bias the perceived material property.
