# Deep Boltzmann Machine

A deep Boltzmann machine is an undirected graphical model with multiple layers of hidden variables. Interactions across layers let it represent complex dependencies and learn hierarchical latent features.

Unlike a directed stack, inference involves jointly explaining observations through the hidden layers. This expressiveness makes both inference and training computationally demanding and dependent on approximation.
