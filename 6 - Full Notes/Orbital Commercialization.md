# Orbital Commercialization

Orbital commercialization is the expansion of profit-seeking firms into satellite communications, Earth imaging, launch services, and other activities in near-Earth space. Communications became the earliest and largest commercial sector because orbital relays could sell continuing services to large terrestrial markets.

Commercialization did not remove the state. Governments funded foundational launch and sensor technology, assigned spectrum and orbital positions, purchased services, and maintained military and navigation systems. It changed ownership and incentives within an infrastructure that remained dependent on public regulation and earlier national investment.
