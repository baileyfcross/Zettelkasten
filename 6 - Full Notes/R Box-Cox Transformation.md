# R Box-Cox Transformation

An R Box-Cox transformation searches a family of power transformations for a positive response variable. A likelihood profile helps identify exponents that make the fitted model's residual behavior closer to its variance and normality assumptions, with the logarithm arising as the limiting zero-power case.

The transformation changes the response scale and therefore the interpretation of effects and predictions. It should be judged through model diagnostics and domain meaning rather than used only to make the marginal outcome look normal.
