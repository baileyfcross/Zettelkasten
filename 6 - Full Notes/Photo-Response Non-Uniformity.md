# Photo-Response Non-Uniformity

Photo-response non-uniformity is the stable, pixel-by-pixel variation in a sensor's sensitivity to light. Manufacturing differences cause nominally identical elements to respond slightly differently, producing a weak pattern proportional to scene brightness.

The pattern is largely unique to one physical sensor, even among cameras of the same model. Estimating it reliably usually requires averaging noise residuals from roughly ten to twenty suitable, evenly illuminated images so scene content and random noise cancel.
