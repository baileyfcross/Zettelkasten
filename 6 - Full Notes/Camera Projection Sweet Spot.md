# Camera Projection Sweet Spot

A photograph projected onto simple scene geometry looks most accurate from the viewpoint of the fixed projection camera. A separate animation camera can move through the scene, but an extreme off-axis position stretches textures and reveals gaps. The book calls the original viewpoint the sweet spot and recommends distributing a camera move around it rather than ending at its most distorted extreme. Newly revealed regions behind foreground geometry need clean-plate textures. See [[Digital Matte Painting with 2.5D Cards]].
