# Heat as Energy in Transit

Heat is energy crossing a boundary because a temperature difference exists. It is not a substance stored inside an object; the stored quantity is internal energy, while heat describes the transfer that changes that energy.

The spontaneous direction is from higher temperature to lower temperature until [[Thermal Equilibrium and Temperature Difference|thermal equilibrium]] is approached. A heat-transfer model therefore needs both a temperature field and a boundary through which energy may enter or leave.
