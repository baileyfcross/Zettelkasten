# Arrange-Act-Assert

Arrange-Act-Assert divides a test into three responsibilities. Arrange creates the subject and inputs, Act performs the behavior being tested, and Assert compares the observed result with the expected result.

The structure makes setup distinct from the single operation under examination and its verification. It can be applied to both ASP.NET Core controller tests and Angular component tests.
