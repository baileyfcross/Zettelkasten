# Design-Build-Test Loop

The design-build-test loop is an iterative production cycle in which a design is made tangible, tested, evaluated, and revised. All three activities overlap throughout development even though their relative emphasis changes over time.

Early iterations focus on concept and fast prototypes, production shifts toward implementation, and later work emphasizes testing and correction. Keeping the loop short makes uncertainty visible before it becomes expensive and supplies evidence for the [[Designer Loop]].
