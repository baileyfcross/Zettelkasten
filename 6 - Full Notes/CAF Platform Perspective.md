# CAF Platform Perspective

The CAF platform perspective designs and delivers the cloud technology foundation on which workloads run. It covers architecture, data, application development, infrastructure, migration, and the engineering capabilities needed to build reusable platforms.

The perspective turns organizational requirements into environments, connectivity, account structure, deployment mechanisms, and workload patterns. A well-designed platform reduces repeated setup while preserving enough flexibility for product teams with different needs.
