# Fourier Transform

The Fourier transform represents a nonperiodic function as a continuous superposition of complex exponential frequencies. It maps a signal or field from its original variable into a frequency or wave-number description.

Differentiation in the original domain becomes multiplication by frequency in the transformed domain, which can turn differential equations into algebraic equations. Localization and spectral spread are complementary: a sharply confined function generally requires a broad range of Fourier components.
