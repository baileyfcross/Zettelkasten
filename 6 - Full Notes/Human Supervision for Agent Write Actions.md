# Human Supervision for Agent Write Actions

Human supervision for agent write actions places review at the point where an AI can change code, configuration, permissions, production state, or data. Read-only investigation can be automated earlier because an incorrect conclusion is less immediately destructive than an incorrect write.

Supervision may take the form of a proposed pull request, an explicit approval gate, or a bounded workflow whose consequential step remains human-controlled. Autonomy can expand after dry runs, runbooks, telemetry, and guardrails demonstrate dependable behavior in the specific environment.
