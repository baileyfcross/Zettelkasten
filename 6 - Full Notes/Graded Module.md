# Graded Module

A graded module over a graded ring decomposes as M = ⊕Mₙ so that RᵢMⱼ is contained in Mᵢ₊ⱼ. Members of Mₙ are homogeneous elements of degree n.

Graded modules make generator and relation degrees visible. Their growth is measured by a [[Hilbert Function]], and their relations can be organized in a [[Graded Free Resolution]].
