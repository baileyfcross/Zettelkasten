# Mirrored Walk Cycle Blocking

Mirrored walk cycle blocking creates the contact and passing states for one side of the body, then copies them with left and right controls exchanged to construct the opposite half-step. A final copy of the first pose closes the loop.

This reduces the number of unique poses needed for a basic symmetrical gait while keeping timing explicit in the Dope Sheet. The mirrored result is a starting structure, not a demand for perfect symmetry; offsets and curve edits can later add personality and remove mechanical repetition.
