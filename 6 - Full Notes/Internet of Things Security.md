# Internet of Things Security

Internet of Things security addresses networks of connected sensors, appliances, controllers, and other specialized devices. Their scale, varied ownership, physical exposure, and uneven maintenance make familiar weaknesses difficult to manage consistently.

An insecure device can endanger its owner and become infrastructure for attacks elsewhere. The [[Mirai Botnet]] demonstrates why unique credentials, safe defaults, updates, segmentation, and monitoring must be designed across the device ecosystem.

IoT security must also protect physical behavior and a long, heterogeneous lifecycle. Devices can combine different operating systems, firmware, apps, radios, clouds, and manufacturers, and their data may cross public infrastructure before being stored or reused elsewhere. Authentication, encryption, closed or protected ports, updates, and monitoring are therefore complementary controls; none alone can secure the entire path from sensor to action.
