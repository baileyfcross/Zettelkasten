# Skin Stretch Display

A skin stretch display applies tangential movement rather than pressing normally into the skin. Directional stretch can cue left or right motion, rotational stretch can indicate orientation, and changing rate or direction can mimic shear, friction, impact, and inertial effects.

Because real object manipulation produces tangential forces, skin stretch can communicate geometric and mechanical properties with compact hardware. It has been integrated into controllers and wearable devices, but the effect must remain aligned with the visible or intended movement; contradictory stretch can create a compelling but misleading haptic interpretation.
