# IoT Reliability and Fail-Safe Design

IoT reliability and fail-safe design aims to keep connected physical systems predictable when devices, software, data, communications, or people fail. A small error can propagate across many endpoints, and a failure in transportation, medicine, utilities, or industrial control can cause physical harm rather than a mere loss of information.

Resilience can require redundant communication paths, multiple sensing methods, local caching, peer-to-peer operation, and a safe behavior when the Internet or cloud is unavailable. Reliability also includes detecting bad data and failed sensors, because uninterrupted delivery of an incorrect reading is not successful operation. The fallback should limit harm and make the degraded state visible to the people responsible for recovery.
