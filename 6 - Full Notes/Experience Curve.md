# Experience Curve

The experience curve describes the tendency for the real cost of producing a product or service to decline as cumulative experience doubles. Learning, standardization, scale, and innovation allow the same result to be produced with less expense or effort.

For cloud services, increasing provider and consumer experience can reduce both service costs and the technical skill or time required to use a capability. Different services follow different rates of improvement, so the effect should not be treated as one universal percentage.
