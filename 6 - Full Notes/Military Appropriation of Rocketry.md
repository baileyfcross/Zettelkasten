# Military Appropriation of Rocketry

Military appropriation of rocketry is the transfer of rocket research from open advocacy and small experiments into secret state weapons programs. In Germany and the Soviet Union, totalitarian consolidation and rearmament gave military institutions control over people, facilities, and priorities that had previously served the spaceflight movement.

Military funding accelerated engines, guidance, production, and testing far beyond what enthusiasts could support. It also redirected the goal from exploration to ballistic delivery and embedded the technology in coercive labor and war. After 1945, Cold War missile competition continued to supply the boosters and institutions from which early space programs emerged.
