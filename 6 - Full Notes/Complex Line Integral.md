# Complex Line Integral

A complex line integral accumulates a complex function along an oriented curve in the complex plane. Parameterizing the curve as $z(t)$ turns the contour integral into an ordinary integral of $f(z(t))z'(t)$.

In general, the result depends on the path as well as its endpoints. When the integrand is analytic on a suitable simply connected domain, closed-contour integrals vanish and integrals between fixed endpoints become path independent.
