# Stakeholder Participation in AI Governance

Stakeholder participation in AI governance gives people affected by a system a role in defining its purpose, risks, rules, and evaluation. Relevant participants include developers and users as well as people who are classified, monitored, displaced, or exposed to consequences without choosing the system.

Participation supplies practical knowledge that expert-only policy can miss and gives democratic legitimacy to choices about shared futures. It is meaningful only when participants can change an outcome, not merely comment on a completed plan. Particular effort is needed to reach groups with less institutional power and those likely to carry the greatest risks.
