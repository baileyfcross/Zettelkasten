# Hessian Matrix

The Hessian matrix of a twice-differentiable function collects its second partial derivatives: $H_{ij}=\partial^2f/(\partial x_i\partial x_j)$. When mixed partials agree, the Hessian is a [[Symmetric Matrix]].

At a [[Stationary Point]], a positive-definite Hessian indicates a strict [[Local Minimum]], and a negative-definite Hessian indicates a strict [[Local Maximum]]. An indefinite Hessian is associated with a [[Saddle Point]].
