# Divergence of a Vector Field

The divergence of a vector field is a scalar that measures the field's net outward tendency near a point. In Cartesian coordinates it is the sum of the partial derivative of each vector component with respect to its corresponding coordinate.

Positive divergence indicates local source-like behavior, negative divergence indicates sink-like behavior, and zero divergence describes a locally solenoidal field. Its integral over a volume equals the outward flux through the boundary surface.
