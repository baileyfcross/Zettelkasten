# Isoprofit and Isocost Line

An isoprofit or isocost line contains every point with the same value of a [[Linear Objective Function]]. Changing that value translates the line parallel to itself because its coefficient ratio, and therefore its slope, stays fixed.

For maximization, the line is moved in the direction of increasing profit until it has its final contact with the feasible region. For minimization, it is moved toward lower cost. Contact along an entire feasible edge indicates [[Alternative Optimal Solutions]].
