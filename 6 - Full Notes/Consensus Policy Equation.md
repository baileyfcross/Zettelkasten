# Consensus Policy Equation

A consensus policy equation combines the ratings of a sufficiently similar group of judges into one regression relationship. It expresses how the group collectively weights the profile attributes when predicting the criterion.

The equation should be formed only after examining the loss produced by merging individual policies. If no defensible joint policy exists, the analysis should preserve multiple policy groups or use an explicit arbitration process.
