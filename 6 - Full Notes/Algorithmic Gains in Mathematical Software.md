# Algorithmic Gains in Mathematical Software

The performance of scientific computation improved through algorithms as well as faster hardware. Numerical analysts devised more efficient and stable methods for evaluating models, controlling round-off, and organizing calculations.

Algorithmic improvement can multiply available computing power without changing the machine. It also shows why scientific progress cannot be attributed to [[Moore's Law]] alone: representation and method determine how effectively hardware is used.
