# OODA Loop

The OODA loop cycles through Observe, Orient, Decide, and Act. Observation gathers changing evidence, orientation interprets it in context, decision selects a response, and action changes the environment that will be observed next.

For live games, a shorter reliable decision loop lets a team learn and respond faster than a competitor or a slower internal release process.
