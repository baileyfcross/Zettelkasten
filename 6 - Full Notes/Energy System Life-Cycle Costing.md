# Energy System Life-Cycle Costing

Life-cycle costing compares alternative energy systems by bringing all expected costs and residual value to present time. It includes initial investment, yearly operation and maintenance, repair or replacement, conventional-energy purchases, and eventual salvage. The source's decision rule selects the lower total present-value cost when alternatives supply the needed service. Comparing purchase price alone ignores long-running cost differences.
