# Computational Experiment Platforms

A computer simulation becomes an experimental platform when researchers can manipulate conditions, collect generated data, and observe consequences within a model. It can reach regimes unavailable to physical laboratories.

Results are experiments on the model, not direct observations of nature. Their credibility depends on verification of the implementation and validation of the model against independent evidence.
