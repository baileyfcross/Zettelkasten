# Neuron Activation

Neuron activation is the production of an output signal by a neuron. A biological neuron becomes more likely to fire as relevant input signals accumulate, while an [[Artificial Neuron]] calculates a numerical output from its weighted input and activation function.

Activation need not be a binary event in an artificial network. A [[Sigmoid Activation Function]] produces values between zero and one, a [[Tanh Activation Function]] ranges from negative one to one, and a rectifier passes positive values while suppressing negative ones.
