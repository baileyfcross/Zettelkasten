# Linear Momentum

Linear momentum is the vector $p=mv$, the product of mass and velocity. Its SI unit is $\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$, equivalent to a newton-second. The total momentum of a system changes only through external impulse, making momentum especially useful for analyzing collisions, explosions, and propulsion.
