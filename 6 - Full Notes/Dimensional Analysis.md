# Dimensional Analysis

Dimensional analysis represents a physical quantity through powers of fundamental dimensions such as mass $M$, length $L$, time $T$, current $I$, and temperature $\Theta$. Every valid physical equation must be homogeneous: corresponding terms have the same dimensions. This requirement can detect algebraic errors and constrain the possible form of a relationship, although it cannot determine dimensionless numerical constants.
