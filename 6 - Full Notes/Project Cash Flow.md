# Project Cash Flow

Project cash flow is the time-ordered series of expenditures and receipts associated with an initiative. Development, infrastructure, operation, and maintenance create outflows, while savings or revenue create inflows.

Representing the timing explicitly makes it possible to compare funding sequences and calculate [[Net Present Value]] instead of relying on undiscounted totals.
