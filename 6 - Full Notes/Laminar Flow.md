# Laminar Flow

Laminar flow occurs when fluid particles follow smooth, orderly streamlines that remain approximately parallel. Neighboring layers slide without large-scale mixing, so viscous effects dominate. It is favored by lower speed, smaller length scale, and greater kinematic viscosity; increasing the Reynolds number eventually destabilizes this organized regime.
