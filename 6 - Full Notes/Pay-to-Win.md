# Pay-to-Win

Pay-to-win is a monetization design in which spending real money provides a competitive gameplay advantage unavailable on equal terms to nonpaying players. Wealth outside the game changes the effective rules of competition.

The source treats this as a violation of fair multiplayer play. Asymmetric roles can still be fair, but a purchased advantage produces an arms race based on spending rather than skill or decisions.
