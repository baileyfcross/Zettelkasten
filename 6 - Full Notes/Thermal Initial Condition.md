# Thermal Initial Condition

An initial condition defines the temperature field from which a time-dependent thermal solution begins. It may be uniform, vary by domain, or come from a preceding study that has established a physically meaningful state.

Initial temperature affects the early transient and can influence nonlinear solver convergence. It is not a substitute for a [[Thermal Boundary Condition|boundary condition]], which governs interaction over time; the initial condition specifies the state at the starting instant only.
