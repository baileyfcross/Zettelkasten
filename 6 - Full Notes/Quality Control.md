# Quality Control

Quality control identifies defects after they have entered a product. Playthroughs, inspections, and defect reports provide evidence about whether an increment meets expected behavior and quality.

QC is necessary but cannot substitute for [[Quality Assurance]], which changes the process to prevent recurrence. Delaying control until late development makes defects more expensive and obscures their causes.
