# Mercator Projection for Constant Bearings

The Mercator projection represents meridians and parallels as straight lines arranged so a rhumb line appears straight. A pilot or navigator can therefore follow a constant bearing along that line. A great circle usually curves on this chart, so the shortest long route requires changing bearings or transferring points from another projection.
