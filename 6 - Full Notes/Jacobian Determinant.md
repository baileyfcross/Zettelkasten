# Jacobian Determinant

For a differentiable map between Euclidean spaces of the same dimension, the Jacobian determinant is $\det J_f(x)$, the determinant of its [[Jacobian Matrix]].

Its magnitude gives the local factor by which the map scales oriented volume, while its sign records whether orientation is preserved or reversed. A nonzero value is the local nonsingularity condition used in [[Change of Variables in Multiple Integrals]].
