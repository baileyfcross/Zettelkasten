# Rule of Change

A rule of change specifies who may introduce, alter, or repeal legal rules and which procedures make those changes valid. It allows a legal order to adapt deliberately rather than depending only on slow shifts in custom.

As a secondary rule, it constrains the creation of new powers and duties. In data-intensive environments, it also frames how legal protections can respond to new forms of observation and automated action.
