# Transparency for Time-Series Overplotting

Transparency reduces the visual weight of each line in a [[Superposed Time-Series Plot]]. Where many trajectories overlap, accumulated opacity creates darker regions that reveal common behavior; isolated paths remain lighter. This is useful when the group pattern matters more than identifying every series. It does not eliminate occlusion or supply identities, so a highlighted subset or interactive selection may still be necessary for detailed inspection.
