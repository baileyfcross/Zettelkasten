# Structural Symmetry Reduction

Structural symmetry reduction models only a repeated portion of a body when geometry, material, and loading are symmetric. Appropriate displacement constraints on the symmetry lines reproduce the omitted portions.

A plate with a centered hole and symmetric tension can be reduced to one quarter of the full domain. The reduction lowers computational cost while retaining the local stress field when boundary conditions are applied correctly.
