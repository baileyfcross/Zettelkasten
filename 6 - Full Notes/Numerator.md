# Numerator

The numerator is the upper component (m) of a [[Fraction]] (m/n). It records how many of the equal parts determined by the denominator are being taken and serves as the dividend in the equivalent division (m\div n).

A numerator may be larger than the [[Denominator]], producing an [[Improper Fraction]]. In fraction multiplication, numerators multiply with numerators. In addition or subtraction, numerators can be combined directly only after the fractions have been expressed with a [[Common Denominator]].
