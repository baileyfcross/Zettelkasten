# Cosmic Evolution

Cosmic evolution is a naturalistic account that connects the history of the universe, formation of stars and planets, origins of life, and biological evolution into one continuous timescale. It places human existence within billions of years of physical and biological change.

Carl Sagan popularized this perspective after the peak of the Space Race, relating robotic planetary science and the search for extraterrestrial life to questions about humanity's place and future. The idea broadened space culture beyond a near-term program of human expansion, although its influence on general belief is difficult to measure.
