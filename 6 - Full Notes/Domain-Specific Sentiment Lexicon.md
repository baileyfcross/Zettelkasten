# Domain-Specific Sentiment Lexicon

A domain-specific sentiment lexicon assigns polarity using the vocabulary and meanings of a particular field. Terms that appear neutral in general language may signal optimism or risk in financial discussion, while an ordinary positive word may carry a different implication in that setting.

Adapting the lexicon can improve both [[Lexicon Coverage]] and polarity accuracy, but the resource may not transfer to another domain or communication style. Its evaluation should therefore use in-domain messages and report how unmatched documents are treated.
