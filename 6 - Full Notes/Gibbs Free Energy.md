# Gibbs Free Energy

Gibbs free-energy change expresses the part of a reaction's energy that can become reversible electrical work under the stated conditions. The source relates it to enthalpy and entropy by $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$. For a [[Fuel Cell]], $-\Delta G$ sets the maximum work per mole of reaction, while the remainder appears as heat rather than electrical output.
