# Single-Slit Diffraction

Single-slit diffraction spreads a wave after it passes through an aperture comparable to its wavelength. Destructive minima for slit width $a$ satisfy $a\sin\theta=m\lambda$ for nonzero integer $m$. The central maximum is twice the angular width of the neighboring maxima, and narrowing the slit broadens the pattern.
