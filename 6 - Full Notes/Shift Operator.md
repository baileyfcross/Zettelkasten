# Shift Operator

The shift operator $E$ advances a function by one tabulation interval: $Ef(x)=f(x+h)$. Its inverse shifts backward, $E^{-1}f(x)=f(x-h)$.

It connects the main finite-difference operators through identities such as $\Delta=E-1$, $\nabla=1-E^{-1}$, and $\delta=E^{1/2}-E^{-1/2}$. These operator relations let one difference formula be rewritten in another notation.
