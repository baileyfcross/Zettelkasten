# Tautology

A tautology is a [[Compound Propositions|compound proposition]] that is true under every possible assignment of truth values to its variables. A contradiction is false under every assignment.

Two propositions are [[Logically Equivalent]] exactly when their biconditional is a tautology. Tautologies therefore express laws of logic that remain valid regardless of what the component propositions mean.
