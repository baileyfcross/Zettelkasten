# Law of Cosines

For a triangle with angle $C$ between sides $a$ and $b$, the law of cosines is

$$c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C.$$

It handles side-angle-side and side-side-side data. When $C$ is a right angle, $\cos C=0$, so the formula reduces to the [[Pythagorean Theorem]].
