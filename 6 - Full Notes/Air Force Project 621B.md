# Air Force Project 621B

Project 621B was an Air Force satellite-navigation proposal descended from a three-dimensional extension of LORAN. It explored pseudorandom spread-spectrum ranging and a mixture of geostationary and highly elliptical satellites, with timing tied strongly to ground facilities. The program's coding work entered GPS, while the unified design rejected its orbit and ground-clock arrangement in favor of the Navy's medium-orbit constellation with atomic clocks aboard the satellites.
