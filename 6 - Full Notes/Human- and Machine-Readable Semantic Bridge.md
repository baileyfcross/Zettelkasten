# Human- and Machine-Readable Semantic Bridge

A human- and machine-readable semantic bridge lets researchers author concise documents while expanding them into the explicit, repetitive form required for computation. Named substitutions can replace complex expressions, and transclusion can insert reusable definitions or data from other files.

The bridge avoids forcing people to write enormous machine-oriented documents while preserving a complete executable representation. Expansion must remain inspectable because the compact form can hide important detail. The result is a [[Semantic Scientific Document]] whose human layer communicates intent and whose expanded layer supports deterministic parsing, validation, and execution.
