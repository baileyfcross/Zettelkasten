# CompuServe

CompuServe offered consumers dial-up access to databases, messaging, forums, and other centralized online services. Users paid setup and connection charges to reach its mainframe-based system.

The service predated general household Internet access and used a hub-and-spoke architecture. It helped establish expectations for online information and communities later absorbed by the Web.
