# Graded Free Resolution

A graded free resolution of a graded module M is an exact complex ··· → F₂ → F₁ → F₀ → M → 0 in which every Fᵢ is a [[Graded Free Module]] and every differential respects degree.

The degree shifts in each Fᵢ record generator and relation degrees. A [[Minimal Free Resolution]] removes redundant summands and makes the resulting graded Betti numbers intrinsic.
