# Iterated Game

An iterated game repeats a strategic interaction so that earlier behavior can affect later choices. The prospect of retaliation, forgiveness, or reputation can support cooperation that would not appear in a one-shot version.

If a repeated [[Prisoner's Dilemma]] has a commonly known final round, [[Backward Induction]] predicts defection in that round and then unravels cooperation back to the beginning. Real players often behave differently, showing the limits of a strictly rational model.
