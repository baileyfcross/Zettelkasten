# Conic Section

A conic section is a curve obtained by intersecting a plane with a double cone. Circles, ellipses, parabolas, and hyperbolas appear as different orientations of that plane. In Cartesian coordinates each is represented by a quadratic equation in two variables, connecting its geometric construction to algebraic analysis.
