# Law of Formation

A law of formation is a rule that generates the members of a pattern or [[Sequence]]. It can describe how to obtain each term from earlier terms or give a direct formula from the term's position.

For (2,7,12,17,\ldots), the recursive law adds five each time, while the direct law gives the (n)th term as (2+5(n-1)). A proposed law should reproduce every listed case and specify which positions are allowed. Algebraic notation makes the rule reusable without enumerating the infinitely many terms it can generate.
