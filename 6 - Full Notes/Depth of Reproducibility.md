# Depth of Reproducibility

Depth of reproducibility describes how much of an experiment's material is available. A shallow release may provide only figures or final outputs; progressively deeper releases add scripts, input data, intermediate data, configurations, workloads, source code, build instructions, or an executable environment.

Greater depth usually gives a reproducer more ways to inspect and reconstruct the work, but no single level is sufficient for every purpose. A usable assessment therefore states which layers are present instead of treating reproducibility as a binary label. Depth complements [[Portability of Reproducibility]] and [[Coverage of Reproducibility]].
