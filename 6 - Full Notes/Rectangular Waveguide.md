# Rectangular Waveguide

A rectangular waveguide is a hollow conducting channel with a rectangular cross-section that carries microwave electromagnetic fields between an input and a load. Its enclosed cavity guides the signal while the conductive walls define the field boundaries.

In a computational model, the hollow interior is represented as a dielectric domain, such as vacuum, and the surrounding metal is represented through conductive boundary properties. End faces become [[Waveguide Port Excitation|ports]], while added cavities or discontinuities can reshape the transmission response.

The physical dimensions and operating frequency must be treated together because a waveguide component is designed for a wavelength-specific range. The book models one as the main body of a [[Three-Stub Tuner]].
