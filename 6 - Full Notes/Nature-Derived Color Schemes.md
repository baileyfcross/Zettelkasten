# Nature-Derived Color Schemes

A nature-derived color scheme samples relationships already observed in landscapes, plants, animals, weather, or other real scenes. Such references can provide a coherent starting palette without requiring the artist to begin from a formal wheel construction.

The sampled colors still need adjustment for the game's lighting, mood, and readability. Their relative hue, saturation, and value matter more than copying isolated swatches, and controlled contrast may be added to guide player attention.
