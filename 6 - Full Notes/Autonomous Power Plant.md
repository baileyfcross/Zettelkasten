# Autonomous Power Plant

An autonomous power plant uses local energy sources to provide electricity at or near its point of demand. The source lists solar, wind, biomass, and small run-of-river hydro as possible inputs, often assembled into a hybrid with standby generation. Local supply avoids long-distance transmission loss, while intermittent sources require backup, storage, or both. See [[Autonomous Photovoltaic Plant]] for one specific arrangement.
