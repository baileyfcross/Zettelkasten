# Elliptic Partial Differential Equation

An elliptic partial differential equation has second-derivative coefficients whose classification corresponds to the elliptic case. Laplace and Poisson equations are the principal examples in mathematical physics.

Elliptic equations usually describe equilibrium or steady spatial configurations rather than propagation through time. Boundary values around a region determine the interior solution globally, so changing data on one portion of the boundary can influence the field throughout the domain.
