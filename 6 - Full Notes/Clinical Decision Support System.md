# Clinical Decision Support System

A clinical decision support system uses patient data and medical knowledge or learned patterns to assist diagnosis, treatment, ordering, and other care decisions. It may generate reminders, alerts, ranked possibilities, or recommendations at the point of care.

Decision support augments rather than replaces the clinician. Its benefit depends on accurate inputs, appropriate reasoning, and integration into the workflow where action can occur. Reliability, usability, and explanation are as important as the underlying algorithm because a technically correct suggestion can fail if it arrives late or cannot be understood.
