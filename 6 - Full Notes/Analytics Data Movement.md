# Analytics Data Movement

Moving records from a database to a separate analytics server and loading scored results back can dominate a modeling workflow's time. The book describes an experiment in which extracting and preparing data consumed most of model-building time, while extraction and saving consumed most of scoring time for the tested datasets.

This bottleneck explains interest in in-database analysis and distributed processing near stored data. Reducing movement also reduces uncontrolled copies, but it must be balanced against the capabilities and constraints of the chosen storage environment.
