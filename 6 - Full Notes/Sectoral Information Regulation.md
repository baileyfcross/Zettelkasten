# Sectoral Information Regulation

Sectoral information regulation applies different rules to information practices in fields such as government, defense, commerce, industry, and science. Each sector has distinct purposes, powers, evidence needs, and consequences for affected people.

A sectoral approach helps identify specialized duties, but it can fragment oversight when the same data move between institutions. Analysis must therefore follow both the original setting and later transfers or combinations.
