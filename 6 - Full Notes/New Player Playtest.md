# New Player Playtest

A new-player playtest observes someone encountering the game without prior knowledge of its controls, rules, or goals. It reveals whether the opening experience supports discovery, forms an accurate mental model, and communicates enough information for independent action.

Because a person can be new only once, coaching destroys the evidence. The facilitator should note hesitation and interpretation before explaining what the design was supposed to mean.
