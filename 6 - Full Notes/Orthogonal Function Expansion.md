# Orthogonal Function Expansion

An orthogonal function expansion expresses a function as a weighted sum of basis functions whose pairwise inner products vanish. Orthogonality isolates each coefficient: multiplying by one basis function and integrating removes all contributions from the others.

The inner product may include a weight function determined by the differential equation and interval. Fourier functions and families such as Legendre, Laguerre, Hermite, and Bessel functions provide bases suited to different geometries and boundary conditions.
