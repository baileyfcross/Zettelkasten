# Emulation Speed and Subjective Time

The subjective pace of a digital mind can depend on the rate at which its simulation is executed. More computing resources could let an emulation experience and complete more cognitive cycles during one hour of external time, while limited resources could slow it down.

This separates biological time from computational time. Accelerated emulations could perform research, deliberate, or form societies at a pace that makes outside events appear slow. The advantage is not necessarily greater intelligence per cognitive step, but a larger amount of thought and coordination before biological observers can respond.
