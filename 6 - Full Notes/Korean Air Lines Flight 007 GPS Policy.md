# Korean Air Lines Flight 007 GPS Policy

The destruction of Korean Air Lines Flight 007 in 1983 connected a navigation tragedy to GPS civil-access policy. After the airliner strayed into Soviet airspace and was shot down, President Ronald Reagan announced that GPS would be made available for civilian navigation once operational. The event did not create GPS or immediately equip aircraft, but it supplied a decisive public rationale for opening a military positioning system to global civil users.
