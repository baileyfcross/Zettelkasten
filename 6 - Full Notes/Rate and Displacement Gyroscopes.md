# Rate and Displacement Gyroscopes

A displacement gyro indicates an angular position such as pitch, roll, or heading, while a rate gyro indicates angular change per unit time. A restricted single-gimbal rate gyro can support turn indication, whereas freer gyros provide stable attitude or directional references. Pneumatic or electric power keeps the rotor spinning, but each installation introduces characteristic limits and errors.
