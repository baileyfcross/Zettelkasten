# Game Ecology

A game ecology is a system dominated by balancing loops in which parts exchange resources while constraining one another. The parts may each contain reinforcing loops, yet their aggregate interaction tends toward metastable dynamic balance.

Ecologies need not simulate biology: combat, inventory, factions, and social competition can share this structure. A healthy ecology is neither frozen nor runaway; it remains resilient and perturbable by [[Meaningful Decision|meaningful player decisions]].
