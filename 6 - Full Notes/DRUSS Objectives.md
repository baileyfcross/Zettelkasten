# DRUSS Objectives

The DRUSS objectives define production software as dependable, reliable, usable, safe, and secure. They broaden success beyond functional correctness to include continuity of service, predictable behavior, human use, prevention of harm, and resistance to misuse.

These properties interact and can conflict. Meeting them requires architectural principles, empirical testing, human-centered evaluation, and operational monitoring rather than a single proof or development technique.
