# Challenge Tolerance Profile

A challenge tolerance profile expresses how much failure a target audience is willing to experience in pursuit of success. One practical representation is an expected failure-to-success ratio: a high ratio describes an audience willing to repeat difficult attempts, while a low ratio describes players seeking frequent progress or reward.

Because success and failure can be measured in playtests and telemetry, the profile is both an audience assumption and a testable design target. Observed results that differ from the intended ratio indicate that difficulty, feedback, or audience selection may need adjustment.
