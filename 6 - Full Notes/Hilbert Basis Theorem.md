# Hilbert Basis Theorem

The Hilbert basis theorem states that if R is a [[Noetherian Ring]], then R[x] is also Noetherian. Iterating the result shows that a polynomial ring in finitely many variables over a field is Noetherian.

Consequently every polynomial ideal has a finite generating set, and every finitely generated algebra over a field is Noetherian. This finiteness is essential for Gröbner-basis computation.
