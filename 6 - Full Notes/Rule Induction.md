# Rule Induction

Rule induction extracts conditional if-then relationships from data, often using statistical significance, tree paths, or frequent co-occurrence. The result is an interpretable set of conditions associated with outcomes or classes.

Induced rules summarize observed regularities rather than guaranteed causal laws. Their support, accuracy, stability, and domain plausibility must be assessed before operational use.
