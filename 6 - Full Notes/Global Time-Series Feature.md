# Global Time-Series Feature

A global time-series feature is computed from the complete observed sequence rather than a particular subsequence. Examples include distributional statistics, entropy measures, autocorrelation summaries, and fitted-model parameters.

Global features give a compact overview and are often inexpensive to compare. They can nevertheless hide short, class-defining events, so they are frequently combined with interval or pattern-based features.
