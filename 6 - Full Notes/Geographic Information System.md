# Geographic Information System

A geographic information system, or GIS, captures, stores, analyzes, manages, and visualizes spatial data together with descriptive attributes. It can combine measurements from remote sensing, surveys, and digitized maps, reconcile coordinate systems, perform spatial analysis, and produce maps suited to a question.

A GIS is therefore more than drawing software. Database storage supplies the geographic layers, while projections, symbols, colors, and generalization rules turn selected results into a cartographic display. It often serves as a gateway to [[Spatial Database Management System|spatial databases]] and spatial-analysis libraries, connecting data management with human interpretation.
