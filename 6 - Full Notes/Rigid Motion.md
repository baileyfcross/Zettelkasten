# Rigid Motion

A rigid motion preserves the distance between every pair of points. In three-dimensional affine space it can be written $T(p)=Rp+t$, where $R$ is a rotation matrix and $t$ is a translation vector.

The rotational part is an [[Orthogonal Matrix]] with determinant one. Composing rotations and translations changes position and orientation without changing an object's shape or size.
