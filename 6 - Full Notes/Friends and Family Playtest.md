# Friends and Family Playtest

A friends-and-family playtest exposes an early game to people outside the team in a relatively safe social setting. It helps the designer practice running sessions and reveals assumptions that internal familiarity concealed.

Personal relationships can make criticism gentler or make participants unusually eager to please. Behavioral observation is therefore especially important when spoken reactions may be shaped by support for the creator.
