# Liquid Hydrogen Storage

Liquid-hydrogen storage cools hydrogen to cryogenic temperature and holds it in well-insulated tanks. The source gives a boiling point near -253°C and notes the liquid's high energy per mass but much lower energy per volume than gasoline. Cooling and insulated transport equipment are necessary, so comparing fuels by mass alone hides important tank-volume and handling costs.
