# Constraint-Guided Alternative Clustering

Constraint-guided alternative clustering uses analyst feedback to define how a new solution should depart from or preserve aspects of an existing one. Constraints can identify relationships that the alternative must join, separate, or ignore.

Guidance narrows the vast space of possible alternatives and makes the result more task aligned. Inconsistent constraints or an overly restrictive reference can eliminate every good solution, so feasibility and tradeoffs should be exposed.
