# Bayesian Information Criterion

The Bayesian information criterion scores a fitted model by balancing its maximum likelihood against a penalty that grows with the number of parameters and the amount of data. It discourages improved fit purchased through needless complexity.

In structure learning, the criterion permits candidate decompositions to be ranked with one numerical objective. It is an asymptotic approximation, so its score is evidence for comparison rather than a complete posterior over models.
