# Cyber Kill Chain

The cyber kill chain models an intrusion as a sequence: [[Cyber Reconnaissance]], [[Cyber Weaponization and Delivery]], [[Cyber Exploitation and Installation]], [[Cyber Command and Control]], and [[Cyber Actions on Objectives]]. The stages describe how an attacker moves from preparation to the intended effect.

Defenders can use the model to place opportunities for detection and disruption throughout an operation. Breaking any necessary stage can prevent later objectives even if an earlier control failed.
