# Engineered Bone Tissue

Engineered bone tissue uses bone-forming cells or stem cells in a scaffold stiff enough to reproduce important mechanical cues. Composite materials can combine biological hydrogels with ceramic particles or strong fibers.

Loading, vascularization, mineral deposition, scaffold degradation, and matching the surrounding bone's strength are central to repairing a large defect.
