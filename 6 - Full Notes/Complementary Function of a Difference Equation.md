# Complementary Function of a Difference Equation

The complementary function is the general solution of the associated [[Homogeneous Difference Equation]]. For constant coefficients, it is assembled from the roots and multiplicities of the auxiliary equation.

It carries the arbitrary constants needed to satisfy the recurrence's initial or boundary values. In a forced problem, it is added to the [[Particular Integral of a Difference Equation]] to form the complete solution.
