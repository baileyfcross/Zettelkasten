# AR Authoring Plug-In

An AR authoring plug-in adds tracking, registration, and augmented display capabilities to an existing multimedia or game-development environment. Authors retain the host tool's asset, animation, scripting, and scene-editing features instead of adopting an isolated AR editor.

The inherited ecosystem can accelerate content production, while the plug-in must map the host's virtual scene assumptions to cameras, trackers, and physical anchors. Testing in the real environment remains necessary.
