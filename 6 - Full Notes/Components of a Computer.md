# Components of a Computer

A computer combines mechanisms for calculation, information storage and retrieval, automatic control, input-output, and increasingly communication. Programmability coordinates these mechanisms so one physical system can perform many different tasks.

No single component alone defines modern computing. The historical breakthrough came from integrating them around a digital representation that could treat instructions and data through compatible machinery.
