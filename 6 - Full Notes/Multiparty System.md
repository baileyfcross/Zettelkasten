# Multiparty System

A multiparty system allows several political parties to compete for and hold legislative seats. No organization may control an absolute majority, so governing, opposition, and minority groups depend on negotiation and shifting coalitions.

Network interpretations developed for two-party legislatures may not transfer directly. Party weakness, electoral rules, executive power, and low re-election can change what co-sponsorship and switching mean.
