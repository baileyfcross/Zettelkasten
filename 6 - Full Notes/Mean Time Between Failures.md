# Mean Time Between Failures

Mean time between failures is the expected operating interval separating disruptive failures. Large platforms can have a shorter effective interval because any one of many components may fail.

Checkpoint and scheduling policies use the estimate to balance protective overhead against expected lost work. A lower interval generally favors more frequent checkpoints and more explicit recovery planning.
