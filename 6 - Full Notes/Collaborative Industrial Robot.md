# Collaborative Industrial Robot

A collaborative industrial robot is designed to work within a human workflow rather than behind the safety cage typical of a powerful fixed robot. Lower force, contact sensing, easier programming, and flexible task assignment make it practical for smaller organizations and changing production.

Collaboration does not mean the robot possesses general social understanding. Safety still depends on speed, payload, tooling, workspace, and the tasks assigned to nearby people. The design goal is to let the robot take repetitive handling while workers contribute judgment and adaptability, extending the role of an [[Industrial Robot]] without copying the complete human job.
