# Layered Tetrad

The Layered Tetrad is a framework for analyzing how four game elements—mechanics, aesthetics, narrative, and technology—change across three contexts. The [[Inscribed Layer]] contains what developers author, the [[Dynamic Layer]] arises when people play, and the [[Cultural Layer]] covers the game's reciprocal relationship with communities and society.

Unlike a definition of games, the framework is a design lens. It asks how a single decision propagates across elements and layers: an inscribed reward rule may generate an unexpected play strategy, which may then influence community norms or public meaning. This makes it useful for anticipating experience and responsibility beyond the shipped artifact.
