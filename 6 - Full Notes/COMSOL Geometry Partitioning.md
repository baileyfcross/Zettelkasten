# COMSOL Geometry Partitioning

COMSOL geometry partitioning divides an object, face, edge, or domain with another geometric entity such as a work plane. The new subregions make it possible to apply different material properties, mesh controls, sources, contacts, or boundary conditions within one connected model.

Partitioning should follow an analytical need. Unnecessary regions add entities and mesh constraints, while a missing partition can make a localized condition impossible to assign precisely.
