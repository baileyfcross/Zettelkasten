# Point of View as a Design Value

A point-of-view design value specifies what the player can see, hear, or feel and the cultural or representational standpoint from which the game presents its world. It includes sensory style as well as the perspective embedded in selection and framing.

Point of view is therefore more than camera position. Choosing abstraction, detail, voice, scale, and omitted information determines whose understanding becomes normal inside the game.
