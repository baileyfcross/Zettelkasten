# Deterministic Policy Enforcement for AI

AI can identify a possible vulnerability or explain a compliance concern, but policy enforcement should remain deterministic. Branch protection, required status checks, environment approvals, static analysis, schema validation, and policy engines decide whether work may proceed.

This separation prevents a persuasive model response from waiving a rule or approving its own output. Suggestions are evidence for a reviewer; enforced controls are executable organizational decisions. When a policy changes, the change should be versioned and reviewed rather than emerging implicitly from prompt behavior.
