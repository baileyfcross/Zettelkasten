# Design Revision Decision

A design revision decision chooses which evaluated response will enter the next prototype. It weighs fit with design values, expected effect, cost, risk, dependencies, and what the revision will make possible to learn.

The decision should also record what is deliberately not being changed. This protects a focused test from accumulating unrelated improvements that obscure whether the chosen response worked.
