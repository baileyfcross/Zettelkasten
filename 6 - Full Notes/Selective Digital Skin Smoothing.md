# Selective Digital Skin Smoothing

Digital skin smoothing can use a lightly blurred copy of the plate limited to feathered roto shapes over relatively smooth skin regions. The book avoids eyes, nose, mouth, ears, and hair so their detail does not disappear. After blurring removes local texture and grain, matching noise is added back to the treated region to help it blend with the original shot. Excessive blur would replace aging detail with an obviously soft patch, so both the matte placement and grain restoration are essential. See [[Matching Moving Film Grain]].
