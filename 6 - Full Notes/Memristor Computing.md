# Memristor Computing

Memristor computing uses circuit elements whose resistance depends on the history of current that has passed through them. The same physical structure can combine storage with computation, reducing movement between processor and memory.

This organization may ease the [[Processor-Memory Bottleneck]] and support neural-style workloads. Programming it requires thinking in terms of material state and signal dynamics rather than only sequential instructions.
