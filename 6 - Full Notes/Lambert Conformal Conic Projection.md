# Lambert Conformal Conic Projection

The Lambert conformal conic projection places a cone over or through the Earth and develops that cone into a plane. Meridians become radial lines and parallels become concentric arcs. Across a selected midlatitude region, a great-circle route is nearly straight and scale varies slowly, making the projection useful for sectional and route charts.
