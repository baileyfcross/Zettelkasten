# Symptom Debugging Through Tracking

Symptom debugging through tracking records symptoms, possible triggers, and possible sources of relief to find patterns that a diagnosis alone does not explain. It is especially useful for intermittent conditions such as migraines, allergies, fatigue, sleep problems, or variable pain that occur mainly outside a clinical visit.

The method begins with close descriptions of what was felt and what happened shortly before it. Candidate causes can then be narrowed, and possible interventions can be tested through an [[N-of-1 Experiment]]. A long timeline may reveal how treatment, sleep, food, activity, stress, and symptoms move together.

The goal is often practical relief rather than proof of a final biomedical cause. That distinction permits exploratory learning while avoiding the claim that a personal correlation is a universal explanation.
