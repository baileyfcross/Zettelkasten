# Particle Emitter Birth and Limit

An emitter defines where particles begin, how quickly they are born, and the total number that may exist. A birth rate of one per frame spreads a fixed limit across time; a birth rate equal to the limit releases the whole burst on one frame. That difference helps distinguish a continuous fountain from an explosion. A nozzle determines the initial arrangement, while generation can be triggered by time, keyframe, or an event such as a collision. Later appearance depends on [[Particle Motion Forces and Lifetime]].
