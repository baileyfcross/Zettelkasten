# Shared Resource Anomaly Correlation

Shared resource anomaly correlation connects symptoms in different services to contention in infrastructure or a common dependency. Several workloads may share a cluster, network path, database, queue, or identity service, so a noisy neighbor can degrade an application without appearing in its own code.

Topology and traces show which components share or call the affected resource, while synchronized anomalies establish the evidence trail. Grouping the dependent symptoms around that resource reduces duplicate incidents and points response toward the condition that can resolve them together.
