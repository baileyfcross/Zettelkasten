# Body Area Network

A body area network connects wearable or implanted sensors around a person to a local coordinator and onward communication infrastructure. Physiological and motion measurements can be sent to nearby devices, medical databases, emergency contacts, or intelligent health applications.

The book describes intra-body communication, an intermediate personal network, and communication beyond the body as distinct tiers. This separation helps manage energy, range, and responsibility. Reliability and privacy matter because a small low-power sensor chain may carry data used for real-time diagnosis or emergency alerting.
