# Backward Difference Operator

The backward difference operator compares a tabulated value with the preceding one:

$$\nabla f(x)=f(x)-f(x-h).$$

Its powers provide higher backward differences. Backward formulas are naturally anchored near the end of a table, in contrast with the beginning-oriented [[Forward Difference Operator]].
