# Star Network Topology

A star network topology connects peripheral nodes to a central hub rather than directly to one another. Communication between peripheral devices passes through that central node.

Individual peripheral links can fail without disconnecting every other node, and the shape is straightforward to extend. The hub remains a shared dependency whose failure can interrupt the whole network.
