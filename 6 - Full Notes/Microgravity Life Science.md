# Microgravity Life Science

Microgravity life science studies how living organisms respond to the near-weightless conditions of orbital free fall. In human programs, the question shifted from whether people could survive brief flight to how bones, muscles, fluids, and other systems adapt during weeks, months, or years.

Long missions revealed calcium loss and other changes that require exercise and additional countermeasures. Space stations provide repeated observations and experiments, but they also show the limits of extrapolation: evidence from low Earth orbit does not remove the added radiation and partial-gravity uncertainties of long journeys to the Moon or Mars.
