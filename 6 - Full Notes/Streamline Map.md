# Streamline Map

A streamline map draws curves that are tangent to a [[Vector Field Map]] at each point. The curves reveal continuous flow structure, convergence, and circulation more readily than isolated arrows. Their spacing and seeds are graphical choices rather than measurements, and a streamline does not necessarily trace the path of a physical particle through a changing field. Direction cues and a magnitude encoding may be added when shape alone is insufficient.
