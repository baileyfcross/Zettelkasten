# Lossy and Lossless Image Compression

Lossless compression allows the original encoded sample values to be reconstructed exactly. It is appropriate when exact pixels, sharp synthetic edges, or repeated editing matter, but it cannot discard visually insignificant information to achieve very high compression.

Lossy compression deliberately removes or coarsens information to reduce size. Its effectiveness depends on placing error where viewers are less likely to notice it. Repeated lossy encoding can accumulate damage, while lossless optimization can reorganize or remove nonessential data without another quality loss. See [[JPEG Quantization Tradeoff]].
