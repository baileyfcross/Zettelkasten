# Gabor Filter

A Gabor filter responds to image structure at a particular spatial scale and orientation. A bank of filters produces local energy measurements that capture oriented texture and edge patterns.

Multiple scales and directions make the representation expressive, but also enlarge the feature vector. Pooling or selecting filter responses can retain the most useful texture information while limiting dimensionality.
