# Inscribed Narrative

Inscribed narrative is the pre-authored story material contained in a game. It includes premise, characters, setting, dialogue, plot events, lore, and scripted branches prepared by the development team.

It is broader than a linear plot but narrower than every story associated with play. The path a player takes through branching content and the stories produced by interacting systems are [[Dynamic Narrative]], while fan fiction and public stories about the game belong to [[Cultural Narrative]].
