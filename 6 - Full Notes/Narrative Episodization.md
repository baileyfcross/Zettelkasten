# Narrative Episodization

Episodization divides a larger narrative into meaningful, ordered passages before analyzing or composing it. The book applies semantic maps and sequence-constrained hierarchies to scenes, sub-scenes, and tweet segments, using changes between adjacent passages to propose where one episode gives way to another.

For interactive or cross-platform stories, such segmentation can support authoring decisions without dictating a single plot. The level of resolution matters: a scene, dramatic beat, tweet, or text block exposes different kinds of transition and different degrees of audience interpretation.
