# AWS CloudOps Monitoring and Observability Pillar

The monitoring and observability pillar measures application and infrastructure health so operators can detect, understand, and respond to abnormal behavior. Metrics, logs, traces, events, dashboards, and alarms answer different questions and should be connected to service objectives and response actions. Collecting telemetry without ownership or thresholds creates data, not an operating capability.
