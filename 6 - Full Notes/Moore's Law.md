# Moore's Law

Moore's law describes the long-running tendency for the component capacity of silicon chips at a given price to double on an approximately two-year cycle. The computing industry coordinated design and manufacturing advances around this expectation.

It is a technology trend rather than a physical law and cannot continue indefinitely on one substrate. [[Technology Jumping]] explains how a new technology curve can preserve broader progress when the current one approaches its limits.
