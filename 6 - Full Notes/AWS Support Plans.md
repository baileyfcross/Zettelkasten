# AWS Support Plans

AWS support plans provide increasing levels of technical guidance, response speed, advisory checks, and account relationship services. The Basic plan supplies account and billing help plus limited resources, while paid Developer, Business, Enterprise On-Ramp, and Enterprise plans address progressively more demanding operational needs.

Plan selection should follow workload criticality and the expertise or response relationship required. A technical account manager is associated with the Enterprise tier, but no support plan transfers responsibility for sound architecture, monitoring, backups, or incident preparation.
