# Power-Off Glide Distance

Power-off glide distance converts altitude into a reachable horizontal radius under a specified speed, flap setting, propeller state, wind, and aircraft configuration. For the source's Cessna 172M example, the relationship is approximately linear over the plotted range, but the aircraft handbook remains authoritative. Wind and a different lift-to-drag ratio move the actual landing area away from a simple circle.
