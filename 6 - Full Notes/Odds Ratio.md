# Odds Ratio

An odds ratio compares the odds of a binary outcome between a focal predictor state and its reference. It equals the exponential of a logistic regression coefficient when the predictor changes by one unit.

An odds ratio of one indicates equal odds, values above one indicate higher odds, and values below one indicate lower odds. Odds are not probabilities or risk ratios, so the distinction becomes important when the outcome is common.
