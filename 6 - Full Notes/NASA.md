# NASA

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration is the U.S. civilian space agency created in 1958 by expanding the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics. Its formation consolidated major nonmilitary space projects and gave the United States a public institution capable of competing in the Space Race.

NASA developed a network of field centers, contractors, laboratories, and political constituencies. The [[Apollo Program]] rapidly enlarged that system, while later budgets forced it to preserve capabilities through the Space Shuttle, space stations, science missions, and commercial contracts. Its history shows that an agency's facilities and workforce can become reasons for continuing programs as well as means for carrying them out.
