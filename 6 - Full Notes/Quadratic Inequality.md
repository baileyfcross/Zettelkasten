# Quadratic Inequality

A quadratic inequality asks where a quadratic polynomial is positive, negative, nonnegative, or nonpositive. Its real roots divide the [[Real Number Line]] into sign intervals. Testing one point in each interval, or reading the parabola's position relative to the horizontal axis, determines which intervals satisfy the comparison.
