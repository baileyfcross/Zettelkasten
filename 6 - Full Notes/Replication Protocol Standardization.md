# Replication Protocol Standardization

Replication protocol standardization gives independent teams a common template for defining measures, procedures, sampling, analysis, documentation, and reporting. The shared structure makes results easier to aggregate while leaving room for details required by each target study.

A protocol should be finalized and registered before data collection. Contact with original authors can recover missing details, and unresolved concerns can be recorded in advance instead of invoked only after an unexpected result. Standardization works with [[Independent Replication Method Review]] to improve quality without pretending that every replication can be identical to the original.
