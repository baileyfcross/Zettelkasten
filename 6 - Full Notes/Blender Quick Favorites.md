# Blender Quick Favorites

Quick Favorites is a user-defined menu for operators that are used often but do not justify a custom hotkey. Pressing Q opens the menu in the current context.

An operator is added by finding it in a Blender menu, right-clicking it, and choosing Add to Quick Favorites. Items can be removed from the Quick Favorites menu the same way, allowing the list to evolve with the current kind of work.
