# Fuel Cell

A fuel cell makes electrical power from a continuing electrochemical reaction between supplied fuel and oxidant. Ions pass through an electrolyte, while electrons travel through an external load. In the [[Hydrogen-Oxygen Fuel Cell]], the net material product is water. A cell needs reactant supply, adequate electrode reaction area, and ionic conduction; it is not simply a battery storing a fixed charge internally.
