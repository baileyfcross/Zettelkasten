# IoT Messaging Middleware

IoT messaging middleware coordinates data exchange among devices, storage systems, applications, and services. It can register devices, maintain visibility into the connected environment, route messages, and accommodate streams whose records vary greatly in size, frequency, persistence, and urgency.

The middleware separates application behavior from the details of every endpoint. This is valuable in a heterogeneous [[Internet of Things Architecture]], where some devices emit tiny intermittent readings and others produce continuous video or telemetry. The middleware still depends on suitable [[IoT Application Protocols]] to express how particular devices publish, request, queue, or maintain messages.
