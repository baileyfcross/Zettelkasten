# R Cohen Kappa

R Cohen kappa measures agreement between two categorical raters beyond the amount expected from their marginal category frequencies. An unweighted coefficient treats all disagreements alike, while weighted versions can give larger penalties to more distant disagreements in ordered categories.

The coefficient depends on category prevalence as well as observed agreement, so it should be reported with the underlying table. For more than two raters, a multi-rater extension such as Fleiss kappa answers a different design question.
