# Actionable Insight

An actionable insight is a discovered pattern that supports a decision an organization can actually take. Novelty and predictive strength alone are insufficient: the result must arrive early enough, be understandable to the people responsible, and connect to a feasible intervention.

For example, predicting customer departure after a customer has already left offers little value. A useful churn prediction gives the service provider time and a practical way to respond. This makes the decision process part of the data science problem, not an afterthought attached to a model.
