# Normalized Floating-Point Number

A normalized floating-point number places its first nonzero digit at a fixed position in the mantissa. This removes redundant representations and uses the available digits efficiently.

The allowed mantissa length limits precision, while the exponent range limits magnitude. Values outside the range overflow or underflow, and values between representable numbers require [[Rounding]] or [[Chopping]].
