# Amazon CloudWatch Metric

An Amazon CloudWatch metric is a time-ordered series of numerical observations identified by a namespace, metric name, and dimensions. AWS services publish operational metrics, and applications can publish custom measurements that represent workload-specific behavior.

Statistics aggregate metric values over a chosen period, allowing operators to see trends or evaluate thresholds. A metric becomes actionable when connected to a [[CloudWatch Alarm]], scaling policy, dashboard, or investigation procedure.
