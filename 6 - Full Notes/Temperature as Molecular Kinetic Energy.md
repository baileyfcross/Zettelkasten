# Temperature as Molecular Kinetic Energy

Temperature is an intensive measure related to the average molecular kinetic energy of matter. It differs from heat, which is energy transferred because of a temperature difference. In atmospheric analysis, absolute temperature influences density, the [[Density Altitude and Aircraft Performance]], and the local speed of sound.
