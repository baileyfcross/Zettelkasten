# Checkpoint

A checkpoint is a stored point in a game's progress to which the player returns after a setback. It may be created automatically by the system, visibly marked in the world, or chosen by the player through saving.

Checkpoint placement controls the amount of [[Repetition as Punishment|repetition]] attached to failure. A nearby checkpoint supports focused practice, while a distant one makes previously completed play part of the cost of another attempt.
