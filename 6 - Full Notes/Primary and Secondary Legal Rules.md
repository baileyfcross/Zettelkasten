# Primary and Secondary Legal Rules

Primary legal rules require or forbid conduct, while secondary legal rules determine how primary rules are identified, changed, and applied. Their combination explains how a legal system can move beyond a set of disconnected obligations.

The secondary structure includes the [[Rule of Recognition]], [[Rule of Change]], and [[Rule of Adjudication]]. Treating privacy as a secondary rule places it within the conditions that govern how legal power itself is exercised.
