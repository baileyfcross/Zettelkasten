# AWS Pay-as-You-Go Pricing

AWS pay-as-you-go pricing charges for measured service consumption instead of requiring customers to purchase all infrastructure capacity in advance. The exact unit varies by service and may include runtime, requests, stored bytes, provisioned capacity, or transferred data.

Variable pricing converts some capital expenditure into operating expenditure and makes capacity easier to add or release. It does not make every workload automatically inexpensive: unused resources, inefficient architectures, data transfer, and poorly understood price dimensions can still produce waste.
