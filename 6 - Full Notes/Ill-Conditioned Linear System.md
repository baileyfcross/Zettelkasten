# Ill-Conditioned Linear System

An ill-conditioned linear system is one whose solution changes greatly under small changes in coefficients or the right-hand side. Its [[Matrix Condition Number]] is large.

Ill-conditioning is a property of the problem, not simply of the chosen algorithm. Pivoting, stable factorizations, and [[Iterative Refinement]] can reduce additional numerical damage but cannot remove the underlying sensitivity.
