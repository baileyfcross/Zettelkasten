# Primary Module

An R-module M is p-primary when its set of associated primes is exactly {p}. Its zero divisors are therefore controlled by one [[Prime Ideal]].

For a Noetherian module, p-primary behavior can be characterized through injection into M_p together with annihilation of each element by some power of p. Primary modules are the quotient pieces arising from [[Primary Submodule]]s.
