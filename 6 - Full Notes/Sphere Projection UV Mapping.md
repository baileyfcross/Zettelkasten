# Sphere Projection UV Mapping

Sphere Projection maps vertices according to their angular position around a spherical center. It provides a natural starting layout for globes, balls, domes, and other rounded assets.

Like a geographic map, the projection introduces distortion near its poles and requires a longitudinal cut. Rotating the mesh or projection direction can move those problem areas to less visible parts of the asset before island cleanup.

The book uses Sphere Projection on a UV sphere as a direct introduction to the relationship between three-dimensional surface vertices and a flattened UV layout. Opening the UV/Image Editor after projection makes the generated angular mapping visible before an image is assigned.
