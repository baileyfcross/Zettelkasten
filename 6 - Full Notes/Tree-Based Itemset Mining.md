# Tree-Based Itemset Mining

Tree-based itemset mining compresses transactions or candidate prefixes into a shared branching structure. Common prefixes reuse nodes, and traversal extends only branches that can still satisfy support and constraint requirements.

In distributed uncertain mining, local trees find candidate patterns before global evidence is combined, reducing unnecessary transmission of raw transactions.
