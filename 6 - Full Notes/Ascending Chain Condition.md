# Ascending Chain Condition

The ascending chain condition says that every increasing sequence of subobjects eventually stops changing. For ideals, a chain I₁ ⊆ I₂ ⊆ ··· is stationary when some stage equals every later stage.

A ring satisfies this condition on ideals exactly when it is a [[Noetherian Ring]]. A module satisfying it on submodules is called Noetherian, linking chain behavior to finite generation.
