# Essential Complexity

Essential complexity comes from the business problem itself. It cannot be removed without reducing the problem's scope or discarding a rule that matters to the domain. The goal of [[Domain Knowledge]] is not to pretend this complexity does not exist, but to make it explicit enough that a model can protect the right constraints. This differs from [[Accidental Complexity]], which is introduced by the chosen solution and can often be simplified or replaced.
