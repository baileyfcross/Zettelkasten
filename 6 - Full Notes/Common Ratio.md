# Common Ratio

The common ratio of a [[Geometric Progression]] is the constant factor relating each nonzero term to the preceding one. For terms (a_1,a_2,\ldots),

$$r=\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}.$$

A ratio greater than one in magnitude produces growing magnitudes, while a magnitude between zero and one produces decay. A negative ratio alternates signs. Together with the first term, the ratio determines the progression and the [[General Term of a Geometric Progression]].
