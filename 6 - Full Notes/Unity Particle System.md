# Unity Particle System

A Unity Particle System emits many lightweight particles to create effects such as fire, smoke, sparks, rain, fog, or magical trails. Particles are often camera-facing billboards because many instances can be displayed more cheaply than equally detailed three-dimensional objects.

The system controls emission and lifetime, while optional [[Unity Particle Modules]] shape motion and appearance. Several nested systems can form one coordinated effect, such as separate flame and smoke layers. Max-particle limits and offscreen simulation choices make visual ambition a performance decision.
