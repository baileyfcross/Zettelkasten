# Physical and Temporal Separation

Physical separation assigns sensitive work to different hardware, creating a strong boundary because the activities do not share the same machine. Its equipment and coordination costs make it impractical for many ordinary applications.

Temporal separation reuses hardware but schedules protected tasks at different times. It reduces simultaneous interference while relying on the system to clear or protect state left behind between execution periods.
