# Quantum Circuit Model

A circuit-model quantum computation initializes a register, applies a time-ordered sequence of one- and two-qubit operations, measures selected qubits, and uses classical control to choose steps and interpret results. The circuit diagram is a compact factorization of one large unitary transformation.

Gates describe abstract operators rather than the physical pulses of a particular machine. Hardware compilation must later decompose them into supported native interactions, route qubits, and schedule operations without changing the intended transformation.
