# Rumor Centrality

Rumor centrality ranks candidate sources by the number or likelihood of diffusion orderings consistent with an observed infected subgraph. The most central candidate is treated as the source estimate under the assumed spreading model.

Its guarantees depend on conditions such as a tree-like network and homogeneous transmission. Recovery, cycles, partial observations, and multiple sources require extensions or different estimators.
