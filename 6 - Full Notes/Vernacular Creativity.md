# Vernacular Creativity

Vernacular creativity is everyday cultural production practiced outside formal artistic institutions. Internet users employ accessible tools and familiar conventions to make jokes, performances, images, and commentaries that circulate among peers.

Its importance lies less in technical polish than in participation and social recognition. Meme genres function as shared resources that let ordinary creators contribute quickly, while small stylistic decisions express identity and competence. The resulting work blurs the boundary between media consumption and production.
