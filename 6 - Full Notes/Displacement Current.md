# Displacement Current

Displacement current is the changing-electric-field term Maxwell added to Ampère's law. Without it, taking the divergence of the curl equation would force the divergence of ordinary current to vanish even where charge density changes, conflicting with charge conservation.

The corrected relation makes the current source include both conduction current and a term proportional to $\partial\mathbf{E}/\partial t$. This allows the curl of the magnetic field to remain consistent with the [[Electromagnetic Continuity Equation]].

The term also couples time-varying electric and magnetic fields. Together with Faraday's law, it leads to the [[Electromagnetic Wave Equation]], in which a changing electric field sustains a changing magnetic field and vice versa as the disturbance propagates through empty space.
