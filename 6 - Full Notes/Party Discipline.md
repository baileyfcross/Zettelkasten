# Party Discipline

Party discipline is the consistency with which legislators remain aligned with their political organization. In a network, persistent within-party relationships and the absence of member departures can provide evidence of cohesion.

Discipline is shaped by institutional incentives and cannot be inferred from one aggregate graph alone. Session-level community patterns help distinguish stable alignment from temporary cross-party collaboration.
