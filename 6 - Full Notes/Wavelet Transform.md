# Wavelet Transform

A wavelet transform decomposes a signal into components associated with different scales or frequency ranges while retaining localized information. Approximation components capture broader movement and detail components capture finer variation.

This multiscale representation can separate structure from noise before forecasting. The choice of wavelet and decomposition depth determines which patterns are exposed to later learning.
