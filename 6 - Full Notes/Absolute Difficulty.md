# Absolute Difficulty

Absolute difficulty combines the intrinsic skill required by a challenge with the stress produced by time pressure. It evaluates the challenge itself without considering the particular power or experience of the current player.

Separating its components helps designers tune precisely. A challenge can become harder by demanding a more complex action, by allowing less time, or by changing both.
