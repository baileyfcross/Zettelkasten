# Vehicle Residency Time

Vehicle residency time is the duration a vehicle remains available to a vehicular cloud. A workload must either finish within that interval or move and replicate its state before departure.

Longer and more predictable residence improves capacity planning and makes virtual-machine setup worthwhile; uncertain short stays raise churn and recovery overhead.
