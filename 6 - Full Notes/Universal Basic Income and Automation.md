# Universal Basic Income and Automation

Universal basic income and automation are linked by the proposal to provide people with a regular income independent of employment when technology reduces the availability or necessity of paid work. The policy could separate basic security from a labor market transformed by AI.

Income alone does not answer every ethical problem. Work can provide meaning, relationships, structure, and recognition, while unpaid care already contributes value without adequate reward. The proposal therefore belongs within a broader discussion of [[Meaningful Work Under Automation]], inequality, public services, and how productivity gains should be distributed.
