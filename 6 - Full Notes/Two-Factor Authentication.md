# Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication requires two different kinds of evidence before accepting an identity. A password can be combined with a one-time value delivered or generated through another device or channel.

The second factor reduces reliance on a single compromised credential. It strengthens [[Authentication]] but does not replace [[Authorization]], which still determines the operations the authenticated user may perform.
