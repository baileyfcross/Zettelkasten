# Temperature-Dependent Fluid Properties

Fluid density, viscosity, conductivity, and heat capacity can vary substantially with temperature; gas density may also depend strongly on pressure. These changes alter both the energy equation and the pressure-velocity solution.

Interpolation tables or analytic functions should cover the simulated range and define deliberate extrapolation behavior. In a pipe, changing viscosity and density also changes Reynolds number, linking material data to [[Laminar Flow Selection for Thermal Models|flow-regime selection]].
