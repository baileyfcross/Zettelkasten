# Isosceles Triangle Theorem

The isosceles triangle theorem states that angles opposite equal sides are equal. Its converse states that equal angles have equal opposite sides. A proof can bisect the vertex angle to construct two congruent triangles, making the equality of the base angles a consequence of corresponding parts.
