# Moral Status of AI

The moral status of AI concerns where an artificial system belongs within moral relationships. It combines two distinct questions: whether the system can be a [[Machine Moral Agency|moral agent]] whose actions can be evaluated, and whether it can be a [[Machine Moral Patiency|moral patient]] toward which others have duties.

These questions need not have the same answer. A machine might reliably follow moral constraints without deserving praise or blame, or people might have reasons not to abuse a robot even if it lacks experience. Moral status can be based on internal capacities, socially ascribed relationships, or the effects that treatment of machines has on humans.
