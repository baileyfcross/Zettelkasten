# Capacitor Charging

In a series RC circuit connected to a constant supply, capacitor voltage rises as $V_C=V_0(1-e^{-t/RC})$ while current falls as $I=(V_0/R)e^{-t/RC}$. The time constant $RC$ sets the pace: after one time constant the capacitor has reached about 63 percent of its final charge.
