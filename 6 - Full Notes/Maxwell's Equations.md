# Maxwell's Equations

Maxwell's equations relate electric and magnetic fields to charge and current. Their differential form is suited to finite element treatment because it produces field equations over a bounded domain, while their integral form expresses circulation and flux over contours, surfaces, and volumes.

Together with material relations and boundary conditions, they define macroscopic electromagnetic problems. Static, quasi-static, source-free, and time-harmonic cases follow by simplifying their source or time-derivative terms.
