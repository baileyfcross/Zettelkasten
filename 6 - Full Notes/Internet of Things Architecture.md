# Internet of Things Architecture

Internet of Things architecture joins several functional layers rather than relying on one uniform stack. Physical devices sense or act; local links such as Bluetooth, ZigBee, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or cellular networks move data; IP and transport protocols address and connect systems; messaging and application protocols coordinate exchange; and storage, analytics, and applications turn streams into decisions.

The architecture must bridge legacy and modern systems while meeting different requirements for range, bandwidth, energy use, latency, availability, and security. An [[IoT Gateway]] may translate between device networks and the Internet, while [[IoT Edge Computing]] or [[IoT Fog Computing]] can keep time-sensitive processing close to the devices. The resulting system is therefore selected and composed for a particular environment rather than copied from a single universal design.
