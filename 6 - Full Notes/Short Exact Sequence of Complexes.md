# Short Exact Sequence of Complexes

A short exact sequence of complexes 0 → C′ → C → C″ → 0 is exact at every degree and its maps commute with the differentials. Each component sequence 0 → C′ₙ → Cₙ → C″ₙ → 0 is short exact.

The interaction between componentwise exactness and the differentials creates connecting maps, producing a [[Long Exact Sequence in Homology]].
