# Active Listening

Active listening gives full attention to another person's meaning and checks understanding through silence, open questions, and paraphrase. The listener observes verbal and nonverbal information instead of preparing an immediate rebuttal.

In team coaching, this exposes assumptions and gives participants room to think. Accurate reflection builds shared understanding without requiring the listener to agree with every conclusion.
