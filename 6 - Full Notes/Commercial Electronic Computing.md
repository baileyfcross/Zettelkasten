# Commercial Electronic Computing

Commercial electronic computing transformed wartime prototypes into machines sold for recurring business and scientific work. Vendors had to provide reliability, service, peripherals, programming support, and a path from established punched-card operations.

The transition did not happen overnight. Customers adopted electronic systems when their practical utility and dependability exceeded the familiar equipment already embedded in their organizations.
