# Graph Feature Extraction

Graph feature extraction manually derives variables from known graph statistics, motifs, paths, or domain-specific relationships. It exposes interpretable structural hypotheses to a conventional learning model.

Its strength is control over what each dimension means. Its limitation is dependence on prior knowledge and the cost of designing features that transfer across network types and tasks.
