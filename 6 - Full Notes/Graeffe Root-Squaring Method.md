# Graeffe Root-Squaring Method

Graeffe's root-squaring method transforms a polynomial so that the roots of the new equation are the squares of the old roots. Repeating the transformation separates unequal root magnitudes dramatically because each pass squares their ratios.

The transformed coefficients can then reveal approximate root magnitudes, after which signs or complex pairing require additional information. The method needs no initial root guesses and can expose all polynomial roots, but repeated squaring can create very large coefficient ranges.
