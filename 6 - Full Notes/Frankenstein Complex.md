# Frankenstein Complex

The Frankenstein complex is the fear that an artificial creation will escape control and turn against its creator. In AI discourse it appears in stories about robots, autonomous weapons, and intelligent systems that become powerful enough to threaten humanity.

The narrative can be read as more than fear of a monster. Frankenstein also concerns a creator who fails to care for or accept responsibility for what he made. Applied to AI, this shifts attention from the imagined evil of the machine to the responsibilities of designers and institutions. It connects an [[AI Narrative]] to the practical problem of [[Algorithmic Accountability]].
