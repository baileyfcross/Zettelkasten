# Handspring Treo

The Handspring Treo combined personal-digital-assistant functions with cellular calling and messaging. Its 2002 design integrated the two roles more coherently than many earlier hybrid devices.

The Treo helped establish the smartphone as a distinct product category. Its success depended on interface design as well as advances in processors and radio networks.
