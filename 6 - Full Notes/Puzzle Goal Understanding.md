# Puzzle Goal Understanding

Puzzle goal understanding is the solver's ability to state what condition would count as success. Without a clear goal, experimentation lacks direction and many players will not begin, even when the underlying mechanism is interesting.

The immediate goal and the ultimate goal can be different. A game may track a large mystery while continually revealing smaller questions that keep the next action intelligible. Some puzzles deepen the goal during [[Puzzle Exploration]], but each stage still needs a sufficiently clear target to support reasoning and feedback.
