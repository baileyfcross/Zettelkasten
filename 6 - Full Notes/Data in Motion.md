# Data in Motion

Data in motion is information being transported between endpoints over a communication channel. It can be exposed to interception, alteration, substitution, loss, or delivery to an unintended party while it moves.

Secure channels, endpoint authentication, encryption, checksums, and appropriate message delivery controls protect different aspects of the transfer. The protection should persist across every network boundary in the data's end-to-end journey.
