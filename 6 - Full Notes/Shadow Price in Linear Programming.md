# Shadow Price in Linear Programming

A shadow price is the optimal dual value attached to a limited resource. Within the range where the current basis remains valid, it measures the change in the optimal objective value produced by one additional unit of that resource.

The interpretation is local, not unlimited. Once a right-hand-side change forces a different basis, the old shadow price need not describe further units, so it must be paired with the [[Right-Hand-Side Sensitivity Range]].
