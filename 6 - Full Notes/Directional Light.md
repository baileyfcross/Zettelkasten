# Directional Light

A directional light models illumination whose rays share one direction and whose source is treated as infinitely far away. Unlike a [[Point Light]], it has no meaningful position and does not change direction across the scene.

Sunlight is a common use because the distance to the source is enormous relative to the game world. Surfaces respond according to their orientation toward the light direction.
