# Hydrostatic Pressure

Hydrostatic pressure is the pressure increase caused by the weight of fluid above a point. For a uniform fluid of density $\rho$ in a uniform gravitational field, the excess pressure at depth $h$ is $\Delta p=\rho gh$. It depends on depth and density rather than the vessel's shape.
