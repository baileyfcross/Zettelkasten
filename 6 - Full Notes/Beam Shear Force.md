# Beam Shear Force

Beam shear force is an internal transverse resultant that balances applied loads along a beam. It is related to changes in bending moment and appears among the element end forces.

Finite element post-processing recovers shear from the solved nodal displacement and slope vector. Its sign and discontinuities depend on the adopted convention and concentrated loads.
