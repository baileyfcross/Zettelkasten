# Implicit Domain Concept

An implicit domain concept is a rule, role, distinction, or operation that participants rely on without naming clearly. Early conversations are especially vulnerable because developers fill gaps with assumptions while experts omit facts that feel obvious to them. Making the concept explicit adds it to the [[Ubiquitous Language]] and gives it a visible place in the model. Precise verbs are as important as nouns because behavior often reveals distinctions that a data glossary misses.
