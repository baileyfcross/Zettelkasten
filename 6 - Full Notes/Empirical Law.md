# Empirical Law

An empirical law is an equation inferred from observed pairs of variables rather than derived solely from a theoretical model. It summarizes their measured relationship and can be used to estimate values between or near the observations.

The proposed functional form comes from a scatter pattern, experience, or physical reasoning. [[Curve Fitting]] then determines the constants so the equation represents the data as closely as the chosen criterion permits.
