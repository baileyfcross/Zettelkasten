# Unity Audio Mixer

A Unity Audio Mixer is an asset that routes Audio Sources through named groups for collective balancing and effects. Groups can represent categories such as music, dialogue, ambience, or sound effects, allowing one control to influence many sources.

Routing separates local playback from global mix policy. An [[Unity Audio Source]] still chooses and plays a clip, but its output group determines shared volume and processing. This makes it easier to rebalance a complete soundscape than editing every source independently.
