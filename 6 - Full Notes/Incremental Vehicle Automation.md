# Incremental Vehicle Automation

Incremental vehicle automation builds autonomy by integrating capabilities already present in conventional cars. Antilock braking, traction control, lane warnings, adaptive following, parking assistance, cameras, positioning, and electronic steering or throttle each automate a bounded part of driving.

As sensors and control systems share more information, the vehicle can assume broader tasks without being redesigned as a completely new machine. This path can make adoption gradual, but it also inherits legacy interfaces and unclear handoff points. A takeover is especially difficult when a driver has been passive and must suddenly resume control, so responsibility and handoff evidence also affect [[Autonomous Vehicle Liability]].
