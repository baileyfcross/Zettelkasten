# Opinionated Platform

An opinionated platform deliberately narrows the supported solution space by selecting integrated defaults and a preferred way to perform common work. This can speed adoption, improve consistency, and produce a production-ready path because fewer alternatives must be understood and combined.

The same opinion creates switching cost and may fit simple or unusual workloads poorly. Architects should distinguish visible vendor-wide opinions from small hidden constraints that emerge only after deep adoption. A strong opinion is safest when the platform remains composable, the user benefit is explicit, and exceptions have a known cost.
