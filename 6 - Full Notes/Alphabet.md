# Alphabet

An alphabet is a finite, nonempty set whose elements are called symbols. Formal language theory commonly denotes an alphabet by Σ and constructs [[String|strings]] as finite sequences drawn from it.

The alphabet fixes the available symbols but not the legal sequences. A [[Formal Language]] selects whichever strings over the alphabet belong to the language.
