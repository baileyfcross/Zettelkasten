# DNA Computing

DNA computing represents candidate solutions with molecular strands and uses biochemical operations to transform and select them. Early demonstrations encoded paths through a graph and evolved a mixture toward strands representing valid or short routes.

Its operations are massively parallel but governed by chemistry rather than a CPU instruction cycle. Effective design therefore requires molecular biology as well as computational abstraction.
