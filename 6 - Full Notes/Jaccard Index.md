# Jaccard Index

The Jaccard index for clustering compares the number of observation pairs placed together in both partitions with the number placed together in at least one. Pairs separated by both solutions do not increase the score.

This focus on shared positive membership can be useful when co-clustering is more informative than mutual separation. The value ranges from no shared pairs to identical positive assignments.

The same intersection-over-union principle can compare neighborhood sets. In feature-selection evaluation, the neighbors of each sample under the selected-feature kernel are compared with its neighbors under the target similarity matrix, and the scores are averaged. This measures discrete neighborhood preservation rather than agreement between complete cluster partitions.
