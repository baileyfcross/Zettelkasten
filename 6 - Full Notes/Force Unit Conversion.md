# Force Unit Conversion

Force is not the same quantity as mass even when an inch-pound unit name includes pound. The book gives separate mass/weight and force conversion tabs and converts pound-force to newtons using a mass ratio together with the standard acceleration used in the definition of the force factor. Its derived-unit table expresses $\mathrm{N}$ as $\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}$. Keeping the force dimension visible prevents a pound-force value from being treated as a kilogram value. See [[Pressure Unit Conversion]] and [[Algebraic SI Derived Units]].
