# Module

A module over a [[Commutative Ring]] R is an abelian additive structure whose elements can be multiplied by scalars from R. Scalar multiplication distributes over both module addition and ring addition and respects ring multiplication and identity.

Modules generalize vector spaces by allowing scalars that need not form a [[Field]]. Ideals are submodules of R viewed as a module over itself.
