# Scheduled Aircraft Maintenance Intervals

Aircraft maintenance is organized by both operating time and calendar interval. Oil changes, periodic inspections, engine overhaul limits, first-aid and emergency-locator checks, compass calibration, and pitot-static or transponder certification occur on different schedules. Airworthiness therefore requires tracking each applicable interval rather than relying on a single annual inspection.
