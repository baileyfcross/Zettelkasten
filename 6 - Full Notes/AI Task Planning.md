# AI Task Planning

AI task planning uses requirements, dependencies, priorities, schedules, and resource information to propose a work breakdown and sequence. Historical projects can inform likely duration, risk, and staffing patterns for similar work.

The plan remains a recommendation because records may omit tacit knowledge and current constraints. Project leaders should review dependencies, estimates, ownership, and acceptance criteria before turning generated tasks into commitments.
