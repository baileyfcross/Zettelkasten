# Power Rule

The power rule states

$$\frac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}.$$

For positive integers it follows from expanding the difference quotient, and the rule extends to rational powers wherever the corresponding real function is differentiable. Combined with the sum rule, it differentiates any [[Polynomial]] term by term.
