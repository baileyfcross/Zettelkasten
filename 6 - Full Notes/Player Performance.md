# Player Performance

Player performance is the particular way a player realizes a designed [[Game Scene]] through timing, movement, strategy, and choice. The designer shapes the scene and provides its vocabulary, but the player determines the exact performance.

Meaningful performance requires enough liberty for individual decisions to matter. At the same time, choices remain bounded by available [[Game Verb|verbs]] and rules, allowing a scene to have a recognizable shape without prescribing every action.
