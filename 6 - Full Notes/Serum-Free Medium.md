# Serum-Free Medium

Serum-free medium replaces animal serum with a defined or otherwise controlled mixture of nutrients and signaling factors. Known composition can improve reproducibility and reduce ethical, contamination, and supply concerns.

No single formulation supports every cell type as effectively as serum, and purified growth factors can make defined media expensive at manufacturing scale.
