# Polynomial Matrix

A polynomial matrix has polynomial entries, equivalently a finite polynomial whose coefficients are ordinary matrices of the same size. Its degree is the largest degree appearing among its entries.

Polynomial matrices can encode parameter-dependent rank conditions. Applying [[Smith Normal Form]] over a one-variable polynomial ring exposes invariant factors, while a [[Generalized Companion Matrix]] can linearize the associated rank problem.
