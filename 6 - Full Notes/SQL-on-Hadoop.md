# SQL-on-Hadoop

SQL-on-Hadoop systems expose relational queries over data stored in the Hadoop ecosystem. They translate SQL into distributed operations while attempting to preserve familiar schemas, joins, aggregations, and business-intelligence access.

Systems differ in whether they use MapReduce, a long-running query engine, or Spark; whether they rely on Hive metadata; and how they trade interactive latency against fault tolerance and throughput.
