# Reciprocal Conversion Factors

A factor for one direction of a compatible conversion can be inverted for the reverse direction. Pryor's fuel-efficiency example uses a kilometers-per-mile ratio in the numerator but the reciprocal of liters-per-gallon in the denominator, so gallons cancel and liters remain below kilometers. Bidirectional conversion still requires choosing the correct orientation for each unit appearing in a compound expression. A reciprocal does not by itself handle scales with an offset, such as absolute Fahrenheit and Celsius readings. See [[Temperature Readings versus Intervals]].
