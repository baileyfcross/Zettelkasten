# Industrial IoT Situational Awareness

Industrial IoT situational awareness emerges when many sensors provide a high-resolution, current view of an operating environment. Measurements embedded in roads, structures, soil, machinery, water systems, or animals expose spatial and temporal relationships that isolated inspections cannot show.

The combined view supports action: a farm can vary irrigation from soil moisture and weather, a city can coordinate traffic signals, and engineers can prioritize bridge repairs from measured structural condition. Situational awareness is not raw sensor density. It requires a model that relates observations to assets, risks, and decisions while exposing uncertainty and missing coverage.
