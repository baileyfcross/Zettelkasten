# Iterative Convergence Criterion

An iterative convergence criterion determines when an approximation process should stop or whether it will approach the desired solution. A practical rule compares successive approximations or the residual against a chosen tolerance.

A theoretical criterion may use the [[Spectral Radius]] or a norm of the [[Iteration Matrix]]. Small step-to-step change should be interpreted with care because it does not always guarantee a small solution error.
