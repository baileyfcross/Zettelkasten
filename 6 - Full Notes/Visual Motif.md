# Visual Motif

A visual motif is a recurring shape, color, material, or image that represents a consistent rule or class of objects. Once the player learns one instance, later appearances can communicate their likely function without explicit instruction.

Motifs form a visual vocabulary and shape expectations. A motif can also develop by preserving its core meaning while appearing in new interactions, but inconsistent use risks visual miscommunication.
