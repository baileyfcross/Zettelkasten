# Mentalist Memetics

Mentalist memetics treats memes as ideas or informational patterns residing in the mind. Observable texts, images, performances, and artifacts are vehicles through which those internal representations become visible and move between people.

This approach distinguishes a meme from any single material copy, but it creates an empirical problem: analysts cannot directly observe the mental entity they claim is replicating. Internet meme research therefore often favors digital artifacts that can be collected and compared, while still recognizing that shared interpretations help connect those artifacts.
