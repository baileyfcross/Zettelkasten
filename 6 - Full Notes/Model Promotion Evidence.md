# Model Promotion Evidence

Model promotion evidence is the recorded basis for allowing a trained artifact to move toward production. It connects code, data, configuration, framework and hardware environment, experiment metrics, validation results, approval, model version, and the identity of the artifact selected for serving.

A registry makes versions identifiable but does not decide that one is acceptable. Promotion requires explicit quality and operational gates, and the evidence must support rollback to a previously approved version when production behavior degrades.
