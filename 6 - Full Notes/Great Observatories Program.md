# Great Observatories Program

The Great Observatories program grouped four major NASA space telescopes designed to examine the universe in complementary wavelength regions. Hubble observed mainly visible and ultraviolet light, while Compton, Chandra, and Spitzer covered gamma rays, X-rays, and infrared radiation.

The program treated the electromagnetic spectrum as a coordinated scientific strategy rather than expecting one instrument to answer every question. Packaging the missions together also helped build political support across long development periods. Their results demonstrate why observations at different energies reveal different physical processes and why access above the atmosphere changes astronomy.
