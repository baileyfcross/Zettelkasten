# Sphere Equation

A sphere with center $(h,k,l)$ and radius $r$ is the set of points satisfying

$$(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2+(z-l)^2=r^2.$$

The equation fixes the three-dimensional distance from every surface point to the center, extending the planar [[Circle Equation]] by one coordinate.
