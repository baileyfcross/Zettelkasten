# Coordinate Rotation

A coordinate rotation expresses the same points relative to a new orthonormal basis. The coordinates change, but an orthogonal rotation preserves Euclidean distances and total sum of squares.

The source uses rotation to align a new axis with the direction of strongest shared variation in paired measurements. Once aligned, a small number of rotated coordinates can approximate the data more effectively than the original axes.
