# COMSOL Derived Values

Derived values calculate extrema, averages, integrals, or evaluated expressions from a selected solution data set. They turn a temperature or flux field into measurable outputs such as maximum temperature, total heat rate, or average domain temperature.

A derived value remains meaningful only when its expression, entity dimension, selection, and unit are compatible. Storing results in tables supports direct comparison across materials, times, or parameter values and provides numerical evidence alongside [[COMSOL Temperature Contours|visual contours]].
