# Truss Joint Equilibrium

Truss joint equilibrium requires the vector sum of member forces, applied loads, and reactions at a joint to vanish. Separate balance equations are written in the global coordinate directions.

Analytical joint equilibrium provides a useful check on a finite element solution. The assembled stiffness equations enforce the same balance through the nodal force and displacement relationships.
