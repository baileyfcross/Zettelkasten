# Reinforcing Loop

A reinforcing loop amplifies the direction of change already occurring in a system. The reinforced quality may be desirable or harmful: growth, learning, disease spread, and accumulating advantage can share the same structure.

In games, reinforcing loops support gain and [[Progression System|progression]], but an unchecked loop can produce a rich-get-richer outcome and collapse the [[Play Space]]. [[Balancing Loop|Balancing loops]] can preserve meaningful competition by limiting runaway divergence.
