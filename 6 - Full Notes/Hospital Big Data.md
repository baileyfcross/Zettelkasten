# Hospital Big Data

Hospital big data includes clinical, administrative, treatment, timing, demographic, and operational records accumulated through everyday care. It is large, high-dimensional, heterogeneous, and often noisy because it was collected for many immediate purposes rather than one later model.

Operational analytics can extract queue and duration patterns, but interpretation must respect clinical meaning, provenance, privacy, and missing observations.
