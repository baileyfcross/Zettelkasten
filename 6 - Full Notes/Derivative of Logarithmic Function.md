# Derivative of Logarithmic Function

For $x>0$, the natural logarithm satisfies

$$\frac{d}{dx}\ln x=\frac{1}{x}.$$

This follows from the inverse relationship between logarithm and exponential functions. With a differentiable inner function, the [[Chain Rule]] gives $d(\ln g(x))/dx=g'(x)/g(x)$ wherever $g(x)>0$.
