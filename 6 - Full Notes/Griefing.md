# Griefing

Griefing is intentional harassment of another player or community outside the accepted social norms of an online game. It ranges from disruptive annoyance to threats or abuse with serious real-world consequences.

Mechanics, reporting, moderation, identity controls, and community standards all affect its cost and visibility. A technically permitted action can still be griefing when its purpose is to prevent others from participating.
