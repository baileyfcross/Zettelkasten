# Artificial Skin Sensor

An artificial skin sensor embeds distributed sensing within a compliant covering so a prosthetic or robotic hand can detect contact while deforming around objects. Flexible and stretchable electronics are promising because rigid packages do not conform naturally to soft fingers and curved surfaces.

The design challenge is multipurpose, durable sensing: natural skin tolerates liquids, temperature change, stretch, impact, and abrasion while reporting pressure, slip, vibration, and temperature. Artificial skin must balance those modalities with repairability, wiring, calibration, and the mechanical protection required for repeated manipulation.
