# Game Music Spotting

Game music spotting is the preproduction process in which the composer and design team decide where music belongs, what role it serves, and how it should react. Because the game may be incomplete, they work from scripts, design documents, maps, artwork, prototypes, gameplay mechanics, level structure, and expected pacing.

The process establishes style, instrumentation, narrative and emotional goals, score organization, control inputs, production needs, and a schedule. Its practical output is a [[Game Music Cue Sheet]] and a plan that can be incorporated into the [[Game Music Audio Design Document]].
