# Regenerative Fuel Cell

A regenerative fuel-cell arrangement uses available energy to split water into hydrogen and oxygen, then later combines those gases in a [[Fuel Cell]] to make electricity and water again. This makes the reactants a way to store energy across time, rather than a one-way fuel feed. The source illustrates sunlight driving the splitting step, with produced water recirculated for another cycle.
