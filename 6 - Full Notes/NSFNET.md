# NSFNET

NSFNET was a National Science Foundation network created to connect researchers with supercomputer centers and one another. It adopted TCP/IP and welcomed general academic traffic rather than serving only one discipline.

Regional networks connected to its national backbone, allowing the system to expand beyond ARPANET. NSFNET became the principal bridge from a government research network to the broadly accessible Internet.
