# Structure Theorem for Finitely Generated Modules over a PID

The structure theorem states that a finitely generated module over a [[Principal Ideal Domain]] decomposes as a direct sum of cyclic quotients $R/(a_i)$ and a finite-rank free part, with $a_i$ dividing $a_{i+1}$.

A relation matrix reduced to [[Smith Normal Form]] gives this decomposition directly. Its nonzero diagonal entries supply the torsion invariant factors, and zero columns account for free summands.
