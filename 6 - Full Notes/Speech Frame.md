# Speech Frame

A speech frame is a short segment of audio treated as approximately stable for acoustic analysis. Speech recognizers commonly analyze overlapping intervals on the order of about ten milliseconds.

Each frame is transformed by [[Speech Feature Extraction]] into measurements such as filter-bank energies. A sequence of frames lets an [[Acoustic Model]] track how speech evolves over time.
